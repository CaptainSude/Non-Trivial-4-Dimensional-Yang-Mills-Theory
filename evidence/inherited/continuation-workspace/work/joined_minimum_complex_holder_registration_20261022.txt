1. THE RESPONSE THEOREM IS STRONGER THAN C1 ADMISSION

Write ell_r=L^r eta for the physical scale of an actual determining
stratum. Fix 0<beta<beta_0<1. The relevant dimensionless local norm is

  ell_r |A| + ell_r^2 |nabla A|
              + ell_r^(2+beta) [nabla A]_beta.             (1)

The Holder seminorm in the propagator theorem is covariant with respect
to its specified background, with a local cutoff and the actual graded
distance. It must not be silently replaced by a norm in unrelated axial
gauges chosen independently for different factors.

CMP102, Variational Problem and Background Fields, printed279 Theorem1,
(9), gives (1) <= C_beta M epsilon_1 for a REAL minimum on a cube in
the stated local mixed-grade class. The associated cube construction is
spelled out in Sect.F, printed300--301, (144)--(150). Its conclusion is
at the minimum's OWN local determining grade. It is not a uniform
proper-n statement for any cube in a subsequently changed array.

More usefully, the analytic response is also Holder controlled. Printed
308, (190), bounds the functional derivative of the Landau increment
H(B), simultaneously in value, first covariant derivative, Holder first
derivative, D*D, and Laplacian norms. The first three output factors are

  ell_r^-1, ell_r^-2, C_beta ell_r^(-2-beta),

times ell_(r')^-d exp[-c d(y,y')] for an input row of grade r'. Local
cutoff norms appear explicitly in the third factor. Summing against the
native input measure ell_(r')^d and using the weighted exponential-kernel
bounds yields the corresponding graded version of (1). This is an
actual smoothing estimate, not a consequence of arbitrary C1 input
admission. Proposition9 on printed309 explicitly extends H analytically
to complex B and to arbitrary regular external U satisfying B99
(3.35)--(3.38), and retains (190).

B99, printed422--423, (3.133) and Theorem3.12, gives the same covariant
Holder norm for H,H1. The exclusion concerning a covariant Laplacian of
G does not exclude the Holder first-derivative norm. The latter is one
of the retained norms. The complex-background and random-walk results
used here are Corollaries3.5--3.8 and Theorems3.7--3.10.

B116 printed5--7, (1.11)--(1.21), is especially important for the actual
localizers: the localized operators and nonlinear fixed points are

4. ONE-FINER BUFFER: A QUALIFIED GEOMETRIC FACT

LI179(1.11)--(1.13) leaves Omega''_j=Omega_j for j<=h. Let n<=h and
let D be an ACTUAL proper-n cube whose complete near and averaging read

 dist(x,(Omega_(n-1))^c) >= c M R_(n-1) L^(n-1) eta.       (4)

The constant c is the fixed native separation constant. Ordinary birth
of the n-row alone is not a substitute for this preceding-pair premise:
the preceding pair may already have been changed by R. Completed later
resets only enlarge the old Omega_(n-1), so a buffer actually obtained
this way survives for that retained birth record. Choosing the fixed
weak floor so that
c R_min > C_near L puts the COMPLETE near read inside
Omega''_(n-1). Thus the original B'' constraints on this read have no
grade below r=n-1. For i<=r this is a useful input to a nested-average
constraint-fiber proof. The case i=n has only a fixed one-step age and
can be kept separately. Initial grade-zero cases retain their actual

There is an essential qualification: B119256 explicitly states its MR
separation for domains created by ordinary renormalization steps, NOT
