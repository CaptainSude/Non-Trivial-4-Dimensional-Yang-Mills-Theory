2. EXACT ENDPOINT RUNG

The full homogeneous absolute hierarchical return is

 Gamma_j(x)=Gamma_(j+1)(y) u_j(y) P_j(U_j;y,x),

where y is the T^j ancestor, u_j is the native relative return, and P_j
is the ORDERED product of ordinary averaged-field paths. For an endpoint
x in T^(j-1), all lower hierarchy paths are empty. Hence exactly

 Gamma_j(x)=Gamma_(j+1)(y) u_j(y)
             P_(j-1)(M^(j-1)U_j;y,x).                (1)

The order in (1) is essential. This is a one-rung formula only on the
specified endpoint lattice, not a replacement for the full fine-point
formula. The rung has at most d(L-1) links of M^(j-1)U_j.

The anchor u_j(y) still has its complete depth-j read. Recursing the
finite ordinary-average reads and all native relative-return means
enlarges the fine read by at most C sum_(m<j)L^m <= C_L L^j.
Thus the ENTIRE rung plus anchor read, including every path, inverse,
mean and collar, has diameter C_L L^j. It may meet several adjacent
native blocks. It does not directly read an older-scale frame region:
the only old frame value in (1) is the carried Gamma_(j+1)(y).

The response U_j is nonlocal in its inputs. Substituting it into this
finite outer read adds its full exponentially decaying response tails;
it does not produce a compact complete input read. All such tails must
remain in the derivative envelope.

This proves the precise frame premise used in Sections4--5 of the
finite-prefix local-tube note. Its boundedness is supplied by the B98
common-chart return estimate and the ordinary-average estimate. Cauchy
on the reserved field chart bounds its directional derivatives without
summing all fine coordinates inside the block.

An important distinction: in this SAMPLED state the zero-noise frame
map still interpolates from the coarse endpoints using U_(j+1), and
therefore has an O(1) background derivative. It is not I+O(delta_j).
The full-fine identity Gamma_j(x)=Gamma_(j+1)(x)R_j(x), with R_j-1
noise-proportional, assumes the child value Gamma_(j+1)(x) is already
retained. Both statements are consistent. The sampled-state O(1)
derivative is sufficient for a C^t L^(ct) bound.

 ||D_q F|_p|| <= C M_F rho_packet^-1

5. THE ACTUAL ROW/COLUMN COUNT

Use one final metric d_t(p,q)=dist(p,q)/(M L^t). The same fixed positive

 ||M_j||_(mu,row)+||Btilde_j||_(mu,row)<=C_L,

8. CONCRETE REAL TRUNCATION IN ONE FIXED-REFERENCE CHART

Let R_t=M L^t, and let P be the COMPLETE terminal source seed. For each
integer n choose a real scalar cutoff chi_n with values in[0,1], equal

(n+1)R_t. On the lattice choose it with

 |nabla_t chi_n|<=C/M.

The derivative is in the normalized grade-t units. A graph-distance
cutoff with a fixed Lipschitz profile suffices. On a finite torus take

 B^[n]=chi_n B,
 J^[n]=Jbar+chi_n(J-Jbar),
 U^[n]=exp(i xi_t B^[n])Wbar.                       (7)

The exact finite-difference product rule therefore gives

 ||B^[n]||<=||B||,
 ||nabla_Wbar B^[n]||
       <=||nabla_Wbar B||+(C/M)||B||.              (8)
