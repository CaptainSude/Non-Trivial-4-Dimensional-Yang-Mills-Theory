1. REQUIRED TOP REGISTRATION

For every grade j, U_j^Gamma_j must satisfy the absolute native
hierarchical conditions through depth j. This is already how the
descending frame is constructed, but it must also be true for the
starting pair U_t,Gamma_t. Arbitrary bounded holomorphic Gamma_t would
not suffice for the statement below, or for a locality assertion.

One admissible top convention is the canonical absolute axialization
of U_t with specified coarse anchors (for example1). Its hierarchical
path formula is the same as below. Its complete read has diameter
O(L^t) in original fine units. If other anchors are used, their own
locality/read certificate is required explicitly.

Use the original lattice bonds throughout, and let xi_j=L^-j. For
x=x0,x1,...,xj=y the nested centers define the ordered hierarchy

  P_j(F;y,x)=P_(j-1)(M^(j-1)F;xj,x_(j-1)) ...
                                      P_0(F;x1,x0).   (1)

This uses the actual B109 endpoint means and ordinary averages, not
newly chosen single paths. Endpoint covariance gives the same formula.

2. EXACT INCREMENT, WITH ALL ORDERS FIXED

Let W=U_(j+1), F=U_j=exp(i xi_j A_j)W. Let u_j be the native B98
relative return. The usual frame construction gives

  Gamma_j(x)=Gamma_(j+1)(y) u_j(y) P_j(F;y,x).      (2)

Because W^Gamma_(j+1) satisfies the absolute hierarchy also at every
level below j+1, its depth-j conditions imply

  Gamma_(j+1)(x)=Gamma_(j+1)(y) P_j(W;y,x).         (3)

Combining(2),(3) yields the exact RIGHT increment

  Gamma_j(x)=Gamma_(j+1)(x) R_j(x),
  R_j(x)=P_j(W;y,x)^-1 u_j(y) P_j(F;y,x).          (4)

The placement of u_j between the two paths is essential. In particular
one may not replace(4) by u_j times an unordered path ratio.

At A_j=0, F=W and the native relative return u_j=1. Thus

  R_j(0,W)=1                                      (5)

IDENTICALLY in W, including complex W in the common chart. This is
stronger than merely observing that its value is small on real fields.

4. BACKGROUND DERIVATIVES ALSO CARRY THE NOISE FACTOR

Work with normalized relative-background coordinates in a fixed

5. COMPLETE READ OF ONE INCREMENT

Define Rread_j(x) as the UNION of every fine bond, current-independent
background path, partial ordinary-average read and B98 return read
appearing in(4). Include all native endpoint means and their path
families, the recursive averages used by them, and all fixed collars.
No part of P_j(W), P_j(F), u_j or an inverse/adjoint branch is omitted.

This is a deterministic finite read in the fine variables (W,A_j).
There is a constant C_read depending only on the fixed native geometry
such that

  Rread_j(x) subset {z:dist_fine(z,x)<=C_read L^j}.  (10)

Proof: one ordinary averaging level has a fixed finite coarse-link
read. If its input mesh is L^m fine units, this enlarges the previous
read by at most C L^m. Recursing m levels costs the geometric sum
sum_(n<=m)L^n<=C_L L^m. Each primitive hierarchy endpoint has only
d(L-1) coarse links. Taking their union over m<j gives another such
geometric sum. B98's u_j product has these same hierarchy/path/average
reads. Enlarging by the fixed collars changes only C_read. The result
may occupy several neighboring ancestor blocks; it is not asserted to
be exactly one block.

The increment has no direct read of Gamma_(j+1) other than the explicitly
carried factor in Gamma_j=Gamma_(j+1)R_j. Its dependence on older data
through W and A_j must still be expanded. The response A_j has the
native exponentially decaying operator-word tails at scale L^j, so
substituting it into(10) creates those tails around this complete local
read; it does not make the read finite in the original top/noise data.

The original cavity source must retain its entire fixed tree/filling
packet before this propagation starts. Expanding only the nominal
plaquette or a reduced boundary read would give a different source.

6. LIMIT AND USE

Equations(4),(8),(9),(10) are a rigorous frame refinement. They remove
