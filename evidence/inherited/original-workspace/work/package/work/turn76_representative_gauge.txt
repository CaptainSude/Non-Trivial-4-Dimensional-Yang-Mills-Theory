2. EXACT FRESH-REPRESENTATIVE FREEDOM, WITH ITS NECESSARY HYPOTHESIS

Fix one actual finite prepared branch h and all source-independent
cutoffs, maps, measures and reference normalizers. Let omega contain
every still-real variable, including private old variables and the
retained coordinate/gauge choices. Let iota_h(omega) be the COMPLETE
actual read of one fresh insertion. Suppose analytic representatives
F and Fnew obey

             F(iota_h(omega))=Fnew(iota_h(omega))             (2.1)

for every admitted real omega, not merely at zero retained noise.
Perform the actual exact localization of each complete insertion,
retaining every term, scalar, source root and boundary compensation.
Then all original finite source jets obtained by inserting either
representative in that branch agree.

PROOF. The unnormalized integrands agree pointwise by(2.1). A finite
source polynomial, or exp(sF+tH) at a finite regulator, is unchanged
when each whole insertion is replaced under(2.1); cross products and
scalars are included. An exact localization is an equality for that
whole analytic function. Its admitted absolute convergence permits
resummation/integration. Subsequent source-free normalized native
operations are linear on the unnormalized coefficient algebra, so
they preserve this equality. The original denominator remains the
same. Normalized logarithmic jets agree after the exact common
normalization. No source-retuned kernel or divided raw mass appears.

Individual localizers need not evaluate physical points; their sum
recovers the original insertion before integration. Accordingly the
proof requires recomputing ALL affected localizers and boundary files.
Changing only the retained interior terms is a different operation.
The theorem supplies no bound on the difference of the two carrier
catalogues, and does not identify their independent-pair germs.

This is more than a formal liberty in the present source: (1.59)--
(1.60) and the subsequent four comparisons are linear in the whole
input coefficient. They apply with source labels held as spectators.
At a mixed jet one retains the complete products/covariance terms;
one does not separately replace only their favorable factors.

4. WHY HOMOGENEOUS NULLNESS DOES NOT MAKE ALL R DIFFERENCES ZERO

For the TURN75 core the specified birth representative is
F_i(U,J)=u_i(M_i^ord U). It is current-independent, and agrees with the
coarse physical u_i at every HOMOGENEOUS birth reconstruction. But
Pi_o in(3.2) is an adaptive OLD retraction. Some of its determining
rows may be finer than i or may be retained fixed boundary data.
It need not land on the homogeneous birth minimum graph Pi_i.

A minimal strictly convex constraint calculation shows the exact
logical issue. Take S(x,y)=(x^2+y^2)/2, birth constraint M_i(x,y)=x,
so Pi_i(x,y)=(x,0). The analytic function N(x,y)=y^2 vanishes on
every birth minimum. If an adaptive problem also retains y as data,
its minimum map is the identity. N at that actual endpoint is y^2,
not zero. There are no zero modes, topology or nonlinear failures in
this example. The missing assertion is the constraint refinement
or equality of read graphs, not analyticity or smallness.

Likewise, masking a sum is not an operation on its total: even the
identically zero periodic sum sum_x[J_(x+1)-J_x] leaves a boundary
flux when only x in a proper interval is kept. Recomputing the full
localization of zero gives zero, but reproducing the old masked
representation requires its compensating boundary flux. That term
cannot simply be dropped because the unmasked total was zero.

These examples test inferences, not the Yang--Mills law. The actual
counterpart is explicit in Large-II printed373: domains intersecting
Z are omitted from the new interior action and retained elsewhere.
Equation(1.65) guarantees admission of those reads, not(2.1) for an
arbitrary homogeneous replacement. Thus fresh representatives are
allowed, but physical equality must be verified on the correct graph.

5. AN EXACT EOM FACTORIZATION OF A NULL BIRTH

The following uses the actual chart hypotheses of
offshell_native_retraction_turn5; it does not add them by fiat to an
arbitrary native response. Fix a common linearized birth-constraint
fiber with gauge-fixed coordinates p, stationary p_*, and action
s(p)=f.p+(1/2)p.Lp+V(p). Suppose G=(L|fiber)^(-1) and
||G V''||<=theta<1 throughout the common analytic segment. Then

 r(p)=d_fiber s(p),
 Sigma_p=(I+G int_0^1 V''(p_*+t(p-p_*))dt)^(-1)G,
                    p-p_*=Sigma_p r(p).                       (5.1)

Let N(p,J) be an analytic COMPLETE source total which vanishes at
(p_*,J_*) for every admitted birth datum. Put deltaJ=J-J_* and

 A_p=int_0^1 D_p N(p_*+t delta p,J_*+t deltaJ)dt,
 A_J=int_0^1 D_J N(p_*+t delta p,J_*+t deltaJ)dt.

The fundamental theorem of calculus gives the exact identity

            N(p,J)=<r(p),Sigma_p^t A_p>+<deltaJ,A_J>.          (5.2)

Use transpose, not conjugate transpose, on the holomorphic domain.
The bound on the first multiplier is in the appropriate dual norm,
not automatically a stronger velocity/divergence norm. If the actual
inverse, vertices, N and segment share the complete support algebra,
the Neumann series and Cauchy on an inner margin retain every source
root and inverse path. This is not a statement about independently
cut walk pieces. A physical-current-only variant requires the exact
gauge-force identity on the same slice; otherwise that force remains
in r. No quantum integration-by-parts zero insertion follows.

For the constant-action g^2 coefficient of TURN74/75, N can be its
complete coefficient minus the physical rank scalar (with the cutoff
tail kept separately). The rank statement supplies N(p_*,J_*)=0 on
the physical birth graph. Formula(5.2) is a valid local factorization
where the common chart hypotheses hold. It neither gives extra powers
of g nor makes N vanish on a different adaptive constraint graph.
If the norm of N is O(g^2), its multipliers have that amplitude times
the stated inverse/Cauchy constants. General varying-profile births
do not obey the constant-action rank identity; apply(5.2) only after
proving their own complete physical nullness for every profile.

6. WHICH ADAPTIVE ROWS CAN PRODUCE THE PHYSICAL FORCE DEFECT

There is a useful exact cancellation before estimating(5.2). At a
physical adaptive stationary point write the constrained equation

                  dS(U)=sum_b DM_b(U)^t lambda_b.             (6.1)

Work on an admitted common birth chart and include all fixed boundary
rows. If a row factors as M_b=f_bi composed with M_i, then for every
birth-fiber tangent z, DM_i(U)z=0 implies DM_b(U)z=0. Therefore

 d_fiber,i S(U)[z]
       =sum_(b NOT factoring through M_i) lambda_b DM_b(U)z.  (6.2)

Ordinary coarser rows satisfy the factorization by the actual nested
averaging law, when the full endpoint/block and boundary conventions
apply. Finer retained rows and nonmatching boundary rows must stay in
the right side. No assumption that their multipliers vanish is made.
If a different gauge slice is used, its gauge-force contribution must
be added, unless the admitted horizontal tangent annihilates it.

Combining(6.2) with the physical part of(5.2) gives a defect sourced
only by these nonfactoring rows and propagated by the actual supported
inverse/multiplier. Each expanded carrier retains a path from the
source to such a row. This is a genuine first-connection statement
when the complete algebra bounds are available, not point support of
the inverse. Its primary analogue is precisely Large-II(1.55): side
criticality kills the matching constraint part, and the surviving
Q_B^* theta_1 H_B^* J and theta J terms retain the thin changed layer.

The physical substitution in that assertion is important. Define
N_phys(p)=N(p,J_W(p)) and apply the FTC proof to THIS full function.
Its derivative is D_p N+D_J N composed with D J_W, so the current
difference in(5.2) is absorbed by the chain rule, not discarded.
On the common admitted segment,

 N_phys(p)=<r(p),Sigma_p^t int_0^1 D N_phys(p_*+t delta p)dt>.

The complete D J_W reads and its actual norm must accompany this
multiplier. For an independent current J there is an additional
N(p,J)-N(p,J_W(p)) term. Its current-segment factorization is exact
when that segment is admitted, but it has NO automatic boundary
support. Thus(6.2) does not by itself boundary-localize an arbitrary
independent-current extension. A fresh representative through the
actual common retraction in(3.3) supplies a physical current; an old
prescribed free-current read cannot simply be relabelled that way.

For a LOCAL birth retraction with an artificial outer boundary D one
must instead write

       N(U)=N(Pi_i^D U)+[N(U)-N(Pi_i^D U)].                   (6.3)

The second term has the force factorization. The first is a genuine
comparison of the local Dirichlet and homogeneous birth graphs; it
is not identically zero. Its paths to the artificial boundary must
be proved by the complete minimum/response comparison. A weak local
completion may supply that estimate, but source support locality alone
does not. This isolates the exact remaining comparison required to
turn the factorization into a native boundary norm estimate.

7. RUNNING-RADIUS HOMOGENEOUS q RETURN: A QUANTITATIVE COROLLARY

This section concerns a mark ALREADY represented as a complete strong
homogeneous coarse family, with the same total gauge/Ward, neutral
source interpolation and lattice covariance as TURN70. Its scalar
vacuum is stored separately. It does not assume every B/R output is
such a mark.

Let a_j=g_j ell_j^p, ell_j=1+|log g_j|, and let the retained native
noise obey T_j=o(ell_j^p). The same proof works with fixed constants
multiplying a_j. The unmarked core retains its larger fixed b0 domain.
Choose the same finite q, allowing a fixed output/input radius ratio
Lambda, before the weak floor. TURN75's deterministic chart obeys

 ||Bhat_q|| <= C R^(-q) B_out + C_q sum g_l T_l.               (7.1)

For B_out=Lambda a_(j+q), bounded signed steps of gamma=g^(-2) give
a_(j+q)/a_j=1+O(q/gamma_j) at weak coupling. Thus(7.1) is strictly
inside a_j after choosing q and the floor. A fixed larger ratio can
also be paid by q for a first freshly restricted source birth.

The exact q joint density is still TURN75(4.A), with ALL conditional
normalizers and cutoff masses. It does not depend on the source
radius. In(4.C) the source derivatives cost respectively a_j^(-1)
and a_j^(-2); the source-free scores use b0 and retain uniform fixed
polynomial-mark estimates. Consequently

 ||(L_(j,q)^C-T_(j,q))r||_(a_out)
       <= C_q [sum_(l=j)^(j+q-1) g_l^2/a_j^2] ||r||_(a_j).   (7.2)

The coupling auxiliary parity is unchanged. No small complex-g disc
is substituted for the polynomial-moment proof. Under the same
bounded-step profile the bracket is
delta_j<=C_q ell_j^(-2p), which tends to zero. The convergence admission
still uses the unmarked numerical smallness g_l T_l^(d_q), with fixed
d_q, and the source noise margin g_l T_l/a_j; these are distinct tests.

The classical contraction is uniform in this changing radius. To see
where its powers go, a degree-m coefficient has Cauchy cost a_j^(-m);
the prefix evaluated on the output ball has size C a_(j+q)/L. Their
product is C^m(a_(j+q)/a_j)^m L^(-m). The fixed degrees2--4 therefore

The degree>=5 phase remainder starts with the fifth power of that

The support core/tail reserve and Ward argument do not depend on the

 |beta_out T r-beta_in r|
            <= C a_j^(-2)||r||_(a_j) exp(-c N_out).           (7.3)

Keep the same physical reference with norm <=C a_(j+q)^2. For the

Choose q so the classical q contraction has a strict margin. Then

with kappa<1 fixed. The marginal feedback from its quantum part is

the explicit C exp(-cN_out)t_j. Scalar vacua are not in the contracted
tag norm. This is the normalized tag calculation requested for healed

If an actual forcing satisfies ||forced_tag||<=C g_j^(K+2) ell_j^(2p),

An arbitrary-power raw estimate can furnish such a bound only after

The coupled sequence and summability of its marginal feedback are

Recomputing the complete localization permits fresh representatives
under(2.1), and the common old R retraction is a source-attached example
valid for all retained variables. This removes an artificial demand
for equality of two off-pair extensions. It does NOT establish(2.1)

module. Large-II's own same-coefficient comparison is compatible with
this distinction and must not be rewritten as global stationarity of
its glued fields.

The running-radius corollary closes the q analytic/contraction consumer
for marks already in the homogeneous Ward class. The decisive remaining
typing question is which freshly emitted boundary, healed and source
collision coefficients admit that class or a raw-owned dated envelope,
with all original roots, scalars, source calibration and normalization
retained. No full original-law source estimate or YM4 limit is asserted.
