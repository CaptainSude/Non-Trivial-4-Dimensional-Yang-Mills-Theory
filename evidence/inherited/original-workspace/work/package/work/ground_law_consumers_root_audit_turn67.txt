6. EXACT SEPARATED-SOURCE CONSUMER

Producer: separated_source_spectral_target_turn67.txt.

For gamma=g^-2, T=K/(2a gamma), Q=4gamma S/a, the log-coupling
operator derivatives are H'=Q-T=2Q-H and H''=H.
On the actual ground vector, centered H' is twice centered Q.
Thus E_tautau=E-8chi_Q and
chi_Q=(E-gamma E_gamma'-gamma^2 E_gamma'')/8.
The kinetic second derivative cannot be dropped.

The finite slab uses the SAME unsourced normalized ground
vector on both ends. Its magnetic-source curvature is

 J_Q(T)=2 int [T/E-(1-exp(-TE))/E^2]rho_Q(dE).

Consequently

 (1/2)[J_Q(T+2delta)-2J_Q(T+delta)+J_Q(T)]
 =int exp(-TE)[(1-exp(-delta E))/E]^2rho_Q(dE).

This is exactly the reflected norm of a centered insertion
integrated over[T/2,T/2+delta]. For log coupling, Duhamel gives
K_tautau=4J_Q(T)-TE, so the factor becomes1/8.
An earlier-window norm bounds the later-window energy by
R_Q(t-s,delta)/(2es), with0<s<t.

The fixed-domain analytic family justifies these identities at
each regulator. It does not prove a common source disc, uniform
