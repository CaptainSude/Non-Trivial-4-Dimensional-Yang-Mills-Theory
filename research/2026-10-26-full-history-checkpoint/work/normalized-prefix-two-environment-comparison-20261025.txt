1. EXACT HYBRID IDENTITY

Put mu^a=product_(j=t-1 down to0)q_j^a and F^a=mu^a f^a, with f^a
the corresponding whole-prefix source pullback. Define

 G_j^0(x_j;x_>j)=integral_(x_<j) f^0 product_(m<j)q_m^0.

Empty integrals are interpreted literally. Finite product telescoping
and integration of the lower noises give EXACTLY

 F^1-F^0=mu^1(f^1-f^0)
 +sum_j integral_(x_>j) product_(m>j)q_m^1
                                  [(q_j^1-q_j^0)G_j^0].   (1)

Each upper product is a normalized positive law. Lower kernels in
G_j^0 use family0's deterministic state at the SAME upper noise labels;
the upper hybrid distribution does not change that map. No convexity
of the full multi-date joint density is needed.

2. HAMILTONIAN INTERPOLATION ON THE SAME PRODUCT

At fixed upper noise let H_s=(1-s)H_j^0+sH_j^1 and normalize exp(-H_s)

Let v_j(i) bound |partial_i G_j^0| uniformly, and let d_j(k) bound
|partial_k(H_j^1-H_j^0)| uniformly over x_j and all upper noises.
Exact normalized differentiation yields

 (q_j^1-q_j^0)G_j^0
       =-integral_0^1 Cov_(q_s)(G_j^0,H_j^1-H_j^0)ds.

Consequently (1) proves

 |F^1-F^0|<=sup|f^1-f^0|+sum_j sum_(i,k)v_j(i)C_j(i,k)d_j(k). (2)

The backgrounds need not share a global interpolation chart or sector.
Only Hamiltonians on the SAME product are interpolated. No intermediate
gauge field, minimum, or current is constructed. The auxiliary q_s do
not replace either endpoint denominator in the exact identity (1).
