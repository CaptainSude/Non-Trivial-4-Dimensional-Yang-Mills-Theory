FIXED ENDPOINT CELLS FOR ALL HISTORICAL SCAFFOLDS
25 October 2026. Root; geometric supplier, not full source stability.

This note removes a possible ultraviolet-depth dependence from the cell
choice in fixed-cell-common-source-chessboard-consumer-20261025.txt.
It is conditional only on the explicitly stated aligned native grids and
the completed source coefficient covariance/locality. It does not prove
the nonlinear source family or its partition-function upper bound.

1. ONE CELL SCALE CAN CONTAIN ALL HISTORICAL PARTITION PERIODS

Fix a terminal band Y<=x_N<=Y+B_* and bounded steps |x_(j+1)-x_j|<=B.
Let all native spatial R_j be powers of the same blocking integer L and
obey R_j<=C_R[1+log(x_j)]^r, with r and C_R fixed. Let M=L^m be fixed.
The physical length, in date-N units, of a date-i MR_i partition cube is

                      p_i=M R_i L^{i-N}.                (1)

For t=N-i, bounded steps imply x_i<=Y+B_*+Bt, hence

 p_i<=C M L^{-t}[1+log(Y+B_*+Bt)]^r.                    (2)

The supremum over nonnegative integer t is finite and depends only on
Y,B_*,B,L,r,C_R,M. Exponential L^{-t} dominates the logarithm. Choose
an integer K, independently of N and the ultraviolet cutoff, such that
the right side is at most M L^K. Since every R_i is an L power,

            ell=M L^K,       ell/p_i is an integer L power.  (3)

Thus translation by ell in date-N units preserves EVERY historical MR_i
partition. The ordinary M/L block grids divide it as well. Increasing K
by a fixed integer makes room for the buffered support D_0 required by
the packing/chessboard argument. Compatible ambient periods can be chosen
as arbitrarily large integer multiples of this fixed cell length.

Use the original aligned cell-reflection convention. If the native block
grids are centered with odd L, the quotient in (3) is odd, so the outer
cell faces have the same midpoint alignment as the subdividing block
faces. For a different parity convention use its common admissible
reflection tiling, enlarging the fixed cell by at most a fixed factor
when that convention permits it. No reflection positivity is asserted
for a tiling that is not a reflection-positive bare Wilson cell algebra.

For source coefficients constructed by the actual covariant words,
translations/reflections of this fixed aligned cell commute with the
entire scaffold, including young MR grids. Consequently their completed
vacuum sum has the covariance needed in consumer H3. This inference
requires coefficient covariance from the construction: geometric support
alone does not imply it.

2. BUFFER LOCALITY WITH FIXED POLYNOMIAL/LOGARITHMIC COSTS

Suppose a complete source carrier at date i meeting both D_0 and a face
must have tree distance at least c eta L^{N-i}/P(x_i)-C0, where eta>0
is the fixed face buffer. Here P can be a fixed logarithmic power; take
P=1 for the fine-M carrier norm. Suppose its rooted coefficient bound
has at most a fixed polynomial cost (1+x_i)^D P_1(x_i), apart from the
exponential tree weight. The face-crossing sum is then bounded by

 C sum_(t>=0) L^{4t}(1+Y+B_*+Bt)^D P_1(Y+B_*+Bt)
      exp[-b eta L^t/P(Y+B_*+Bt)].                       (4)

This series converges. Indeed the logarithm of its polynomial prefactor
is O(t+log(1+t)), while L^t divided by any fixed logarithmic power of
Y+B_*+Bt grows exponentially in t. Its sum depends on the fixed terminal
band and buffer, but not on ultraviolet depth or ambient volume.

Each scalar source coefficient must vanish when all source values on
its COMPLETE read carrier vanish; subtract its source-zero value to
ensure this. Then a coefficient contributing to the face error must
meet the actual source support as well as the face. Source-independent
vacua cancel before (4) is used. Counting each translated copy gives
at most the number of copies, which is canceled by the chessboard root.
The bound is on the entire supported weight, not on a chosen list of
bumps or a sum of separate source normalizations.

In particular a fixed polynomial birth/radius cost does not by itself
destroy consumer H4. The actual complete-carrier exponential estimate,
source-zero cancellation and original aligned covariance are still
essential supplier hypotheses.
