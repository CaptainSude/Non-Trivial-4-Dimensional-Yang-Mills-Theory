For a frozen real source-free joint history, the transported bounded

Thus the ORIGINAL integrated moments, including each original reflected

 E[F G|Z] = E[F|Z]E[G|Z] + Cov(F,G|Z),                       (1)

with the appropriate conjugation for sesquilinear forms. The covariance
term and all further contacts are indispensable.

There is an even simpler OS warning. In a reflection-positive product
law with independent positive and negative halves, a positive-half
mean-zero variable X is OS-null. If X^2=1, then X^2 is NOT OS-null.
Consequently the OS null space is not a pointwise-product ideal. Equality
of individual reflected state vectors cannot by itself define products
of the corresponding limiting field representatives.

For bounded probes |F_i|<=1, their whole marked source is entire and its

varying complex background nor a local carrier expansion of the posterior.

4. NEW RIGOROUS OBSTRUCTION TO UNLIMITED HALF-RESTRICTED SMOOTHING

Lemma. Fix ONE finite nondegenerate Wilson lattice at finite beta, with
reflection-invariant probability mu. Let F have nonzero centered OS
seminorm q>0. There cannot exist smooth representatives F_n such that

 (a) F_n remain in the positive-half algebra and have the same centered
     OS vector as F;
 (b) mu(F_n)=mu(F);
 (c) E_mu|grad_FULL F_n|^2 <= A/n for all n>=1,

where A is finite at this fixed lattice and grad_FULL differentiates ALL
original links on which F_n depends.

Proof. The configuration manifold is a finite product of compact connected
SU(2)'s. The Wilson density is smooth and strictly positive. Haar Poincare
and the finite upper/lower density ratio imply a finite constant C_mu with

 Var_mu(f) <= C_mu E_mu|grad_FULL f|^2.

For example the elementary density-comparison constant can be taken no
larger than C_Haar exp(2 beta N_plaquettes). Its possibly enormous size is
irrelevant at a FIXED lattice. Reflection invariance and Cauchy--Schwarz
give |OS(f-mu f,f-mu f)|<=Var_mu(f). Therefore

               0<q<=C_mu A/n for every n,

which is impossible. This proves the lemma. It does not prohibit a
particular large but finite n depending on the cutoff; it proves that
the three claimed properties cannot all survive arbitrary iteration.

What changes for a kernel restricted to an interior region D? Its updates
leave outside variables fixed. The relevant conditional expectation
E[F|U_(D^c)] is generally a nonconstant function of the boundary links.
The Fisher/spectral argument then controls derivatives in the UPDATED
interior variables, not all derivatives of that boundary-conditioned
function. In differentiating an unupdated boundary link, the inner
conditional observable also depends on that link; the proof that only
the outer Gaussian density is differentiated no longer applies.

This can already be seen in the exact conditional heatbath projection
Q_D F=E[F|U_(D^c)]. If D and its interaction collar are inside the positive
half, it preserves the original OS vector by conditioning the reflected
test. Its interior derivatives are zero after one step, but its boundary
dependence retains the signal. Iterating it changes nothing.

Thus positive-half restriction is a legitimate way to keep support in
that half, but it does not import the full-gradient bound (3) or enlarge
the native complex domain for free. A Dirichlet-boundary source estimate
on the remaining boundary germ is the precise missing estimate. Even a
three-dimensional fine boundary has O(a^-3) links, so the bare ell2
majorant for angles O(a b) still costs O(a^-1 b^2), unless a new
source-specific cancellation is proved.

5. AN ACTUAL WILSON COUNTERTEST FOR THIN BLOCKED PLAQUETTES

Independently rechecked the self-contained proof in
physical_coarse_variance_turn66.txt, Sections1--4. For an edge-simple
SU(2) loop C of length ell, select at least ell/19 edges no two of which
share an action plaquette. Condition on all other links. The chosen
links are exactly independent tilted Haar variables with staple strength
0<=kappa<=6 beta. In spin 1/2 and spin1, the conditional representation
averages have operator norm at most exp[-c/(1+beta)].

One elementary proof of the uniform gap uses t=Tr(U)/2 and the tilted
S^3 density: E(1-t^2)>=c/(1+kappa). Then
1-c_(1/2)>=E(1-t^2)/2 and 1-c_1=(4/3)E(1-t^2).
Each selected edge appears once in the loop, so conditioning multiplies
these contractions without any independence assumption about the rest.
Writing W=Tr U(C)/2 and eta=exp[-c ell/(19(1+beta))] gives

 |E(W|outside)|<=eta,
 |E(W^2|outside)-1/4|<=3eta/4.                              (5)

The second identity uses (Tr U)^2=1+chi_spin1(U). Any reflected loop
outside the selected positive-time edges is measurable in the conditioned
outside. Thus its centered reflected covariance is at most 2eta in
absolute value, and original reflection positivity makes the self-pair
nonnegative.

For a fixed physical thin square, ell is of order a^-1. On the present
logarithmic bare trajectory, a(1+beta_a)->0. Hence

 Var(W_a)->1/4,       OS(W_a-EW_a,W_a-EW_a)->0.               (6)

This is an ORIGINAL-law example with a genuinely positive ordinary
variance but no surviving reflected state. HS smoothing preserves the
vanishing state exactly. A raw blocked plaquette formed solely by fine
path products is such a thin loop and fails as a nonzero OS witness.
The theorem does not cover a genuinely averaged ordinary-block plaquette,
a thick/flowed observable, or a renormalized contour. Those are different
probes and require their own estimates.

6. WHY COMPACTNESS OR COARSE LINKS ALONE DO NOT FINISH THE TASK

Bounded loop/compact-link observables on a countable fixed grid algebra
admit subsequences of their moments. One can keep one diagonal subsequence
for all finite products and pass their finite positivity identities.
This only yields a state on that specified abstract compact algebra.
It does not identify its dependence on continuum positions/shapes, make
the translation representation strongly continuous, recover local
curvature fields, prove O(4) covariance, or identify Yang--Mills dynamics.
Equation(6) shows that even the most literal path-holonomy candidate may
have only ultraviolet ordinary fluctuations and a trivial centered OS
sector. Nonlinear block averages are not automatically consistent under
changes of resolution or equal to original posteriors after R redraws.

For the energy route, the obstacle is different. The established original
Wilson variance note proves

 Var[c_a sum_p f_p(1-Tr U_p/2)]
       >= C_f |c_a|^2 a^-4 beta_a^-2.

A fixed nonzero endpoint calibration therefore has no uniform ordinary
L2 norm. Subtracting a deterministic mean changes no variance. This does
not rule out separated correlations or a properly contact-renormalized
distribution; it prevents an unqualified random-distribution compactness
claim based on raw energy self-moments.

The inherited tensor_all_order_continuum_turn2.txt is a CONDITIONAL
consumer. Its chosen OS-II route asks for a common all-order bound such
as C^n(n!)^B with a fixed linearly growing seminorm order after the stated
collision-flat extension. Separate order-two or per-order bounds do not
prove that producer. Other reconstruction routes may use other hypotheses,
but they still require an actually constructed common observable state.

7. SHORTEST SOUND NEXT MODEL ESTIMATE

Do not spend more work proving generic bounded-source transport or
generic smoothing. Those identities are already available. Choose ONE
original probe family and keep its exact whole-product marks through the
full T/R history. Prove a nonzero reflected comparison with its actual
calibration and all complementary/boundary/contact contributions retained.

For the existing scalar-energy program, the concrete next task in
original_observable_next_consumer_audit_20261022.txt remains a useful
minimal milestone: four separated original covariances at two positive
times, compared with the calibrated Maxwell benchmark with an error
strictly smaller than its reflected difference. The current canonical
coupling theorem helps choose the same weak original laws; it does not
provide the inhomogeneous profile/source tangent or the outer covariance.
The full original two-source output, including the conditional covariance
from (1), is the missing model estimate. It requires no false uniform
raw-energy L2 bound.

If a bounded probe is preferred, use a genuinely averaged/thick/flowed
fixed-resolution observable, not the thin path-product plaquette ruled
out above. Define it on ALL original compact configurations and prove its
complete reflection-half read, nonzero original reflected signal, and
source-specific native own-domain/carrier bound. The HS theorem can then
supply a smooth representative, but it cannot supply these three inputs.

Only after such a milestone should the same construction be extended to
a separating local gauge-invariant observable algebra and all required
products/contacts. A scalar two-point witness can establish a nonzero
dynamical channel; it cannot identify the full non-Abelian theory or
exclude all free/generalized-free descriptions. The pinned hints say so
explicitly. Interaction must be formulated in that same identified
observable algebra, not inferred from a nonlinear composite's cumulant.
