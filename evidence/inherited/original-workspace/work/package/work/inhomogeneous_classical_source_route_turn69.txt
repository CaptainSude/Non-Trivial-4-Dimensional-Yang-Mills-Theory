1. WHY A WEAKER PROPAGATOR MAY SUFFICE

The exact one-mark recursion on the selected map is

 u_(j+1)=T_j u_j+D_E F_j[u_j]+B_j(f).

The audited results give, in fixed norms,

 ||D_E F_j[u]||<=C g_j^2 ||u||,
 ||B_j(f)||<=C g_j^4||f||+C g_j^2 ell_j^alpha[f]_alpha.

The constant source has its own already bounded exact solution
C_j=alpha_j'(0)S_j+u_j^constant from TURN49. Its marginal scalar
stays near one. For a variable profile, it may be more effective
to compare the true mark with a LOCAL MODULATION of this entire
C_j, including its quantum correction, rather than with only the
weighted classical action.

If an exact local modulation operator M_j(f) and a controlled
representative change are available, the difference would be
forced by commutators

 (T_j+D_EF_j)M_j(f)-M_(j+1)(f)(T_j+D_EF_j),

plus local null-family changes of representatives. Complete-read
locality suggests a factor ell_j^alpha[f]_alpha. This must be
proved in the chosen quotient; the notation alone does not do it.

A classical propagator bounded by C L^(epsilon(k-j)), with one
fixed epsilon<alpha, would already be enough to sum these
geometric source differences, if quantum perturbation adds only
the factor exp[C sum g_l^2]. Along the weak trajectories this
last factor is at most polynomial in the elapsed age and Gamma.
Geometric decay L^(-(alpha-epsilon)age) then beats it.

One must use the protected CONSTANT solution in this argument.
Summing a generic g_j^4 force after a polynomially growing
propagator need not converge for arbitrary polynomial exponent.
It is not legitimate to skip the modulation/commutator step.

2. A CONCRETE SUBCLASS: INDIVIDUALLY INVARIANT CARRIERS

Consider an input family E_H on the current lattice whose EACH
coefficient is analytic on the b link-angle ball and invariant
under local gauge transformations along paths remaining in that
domain. Let its carrier norm have a positive exponential reserve.
These are stronger hypotheses than invariance of the summed total.

For the direct classical prefix of endpoint ratio L, the imported
complete C^(1,alpha) bound gives current link angles O(b/L) and
plaquette curvature O(b/L^2). The following split is promising.

SMALL CARRIERS: |H|<=c L, with c a sufficiently small fixed number.
A tree gauge on the vertices of H, using actual paths and a
contractible local filling in the smooth background, has residual
link angles bounded schematically by

                   C b |H|^2/L^2.

The whole interpolating gauge path has angles at most
C b |H|/L. Thus it stays in the original b ball after choosing c
small. Individual invariance then licenses evaluating E_H on the
tree-gauged field. Semisimple global color invariance kills the
first Taylor derivative of E_H at the identity; its normalized
value begins at degree two.

The elementary coordinate majorant with at most D|H| coordinates
has small parameter t_H=C |H|^2/L^2 and gives a bound of the form

 ||E_H|| times
       C |H|^6 L^(-4) exp[C |H|^3/L^2].

For |H|<=cL, the exponential is at most exp[C c^2 |H|], payable
from a fixed small fraction of the input carrier reserve. The
polynomial |H|^6 is paid from another fixed fraction. There are
O(L^4) fine source roots per final physical cell, so the L^-4
factor has exactly the needed dimension-four cancellation.

LARGE CARRIERS: |H|>cL.
Use the ungauged prefix bound O(b/L), so the original analytic
domain remains valid. The input exponential tail should yield
L^4 exp(-c' L), bounded uniformly in L, after the usual composition
and root-sum costs. Winding input carriers belong in this tail
when output periods stay above a fixed admissible threshold.

The small-carrier filling need not be assigned its number of FINE
plaquettes as output support. Its physical diameter is O(|H|/L),
so in this split it lies in a fixed final-cell collar. The complete
field norm is essential to avoiding a spurious fine-coordinate sum.

3. WHAT A COMPLETE PROOF OF THAT SUBCLASS STILL NEEDS

The preceding estimates give a plausible uniform scalar/coordinate
majorant. A finished theorem must additionally construct:

 (a) the actual covariant tree/filling map and a complete supported
     expansion with all original read paths retained;
 (b) coherent common-domain bounds for the whole gauge path;
 (c) output carrier and rerooting estimates at the SAME prescribed
     output norm for arbitrary endpoint ratio L;
 (d) exact periodic compatibility and the large-carrier/winding
     remainder, rather than only pointwise bounds;
 (e) enough margin to repeat the same fixed norm construction,
     with no radius/exponent loss after each step.

The existing complete-field classical prefix theorem supplies
important inputs to (a)--(c), but this note does not carry out
the full inherited nonlinear carrier summation. Accordingly even
the subclass theorem is recorded as a proposal, not as proved.

If a genuinely uniform direct theorem were established, an
adapted finite-future norm could be defined by taking a supremum
of the direct classical transports, with an optional L^-epsilon
weight. Uniform equivalence to the input carrier norm is required
before this definition is useful. Merely defining such a norm
does not prove finiteness or control the quantum derivative in it.

4. THE PRECISE GAP TO THE PRESENT FINITE QUOTIENT

The existing finite quantum induction chooses near-minimal
representatives of a quotient by equality of normalized TOTALS.
Their totals are gauge invariant. Their individual coefficients
need not be invariant. The source derivative identity (4.2) of
regular_source_transport_turn69 also gives a small representative
whose individual terms generally fail gauge invariance.

Therefore one cannot tree-gauge each of those coefficients by
the argument in Section 2. Gauge invariance of a sum does not
authorize a different gauge transformation in each summand.

TURN47 supplies individually invariant ACTUAL canonical birth
carriers on its stated coherent domain. That does not by itself
give individually invariant, uniformly small representatives
after every quotient minimization or local cancellation.

The needed interface is a controlled invariant relocalization:
either prove the classical bound directly for the full quotient
of invariant totals, or show that a small class has a comparably
small individually invariant carrier representative on one
fixed domain. For the modulation route one also needs:

 a locally weighted null family whose TOTAL is zero can be
 represented with norm O(ell^alpha[f]_alpha), retaining the
 appropriate gauge identities and fixed support budget.

The audited derivative identity proves this last smallness in
the ordinary analytic carrier quotient, but not in the stronger
individually invariant-carrier norm. This distinction is the
specific obstacle, not a missing scalar constant-source estimate.

A position-dependent finite tensor of marginal curvature terms
may be the correct normal form. The old single scalar beta
extraction assumes translation/hypercubic symmetry and is not
already that normal form.
