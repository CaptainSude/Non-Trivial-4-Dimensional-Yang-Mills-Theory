TURN76 -- THE LAST ADAPTIVE-TO-HOMOGENEOUS READ IS NOT IN THE FOUR-MAP CHAIN
20 October 2026. history47. Source-specific follow-up; prior files unchanged.

RESULT

The supplied Large-II formulas establish the same-coefficient comparison
between the old and new ADAPTIVE reconstructions. They do not replace
the new determining set outside a source collar by an ordinary homogeneous
one. The omitted operation is substantive even before a free current is
introduced. Common-OLD retraction preserves the correct physical endpoints,
but preserves their adaptive constraints as well.

There is a precise additional obstruction to the proposed matched-inverse
shortcut. A constrained inverse can annihilate its constraint adjoint at
every decoupling parameter, yet still couple two regions whose ordinary
precision has been disconnected. The two-coordinate calculation in Section4
proves this. Thus exact constraint matching does not alone give the
factorization/first-hit property required by the native localization.
This is an obstruction to that inference, not a counterexample to an
appropriately constructed new native comparison.

The physical-current, independent-current and source-scalar distinctions
remain essential. No full source transport or new continuum bound follows
from this note.

Let D be one actual fresh finite source coefficient, including a raw
scalar or mixed coefficient, after its complete native integration and
connected resummation. Suppress no live arguments. On admitted physical
reads its proposed fresh decomposition is exactly

 D(Psi_ad(d)) = D(Psi_hom(W(d)))
              + [D(Psi_ad(d))-D(Psi_hom(W(d)))].             (3.1)

Psi includes the physical current, gauge/frame and all remaining source
and boundary data. This is the useful chronological formulation in
turn76_fresh_source_typing_gauge: make this comparison before declaring
a new independent-current extension. The last bracket is still an
actual quantity; analyticity does not make it vanish.

The common-OLD map Pi_o=P_o M_o, when admitted on the complete old datum,
fixes the two physical native endpoints because their determining maps
factor in the required direction. Composing an ENTIRE fresh source
bracket with Pi_o before localization is then a legitimate new extension.
Its chain rule retains all used paths. But Pi_o lands on the old adaptive
graph. It need not equal the homogeneous birth retraction Pi_i. In

This exact freedom cannot change an already frozen old source germ.

For a local born-fiber retraction Pi_i^D the elementary identity is

 N(U)=N(Pi_i^D U)+[N(U)-N(Pi_i^D U)].                       (3.2)

The second bracket is accessible to the force/current factorization

4. WHY MATCHING A SADDLE INVERSE IS NOT YET A LOCALIZER

For real finite matrices with independent constraint rows and a positive
precision on ker Q, let the inverse of the saddle matrix be

 S=[ Delta  Q^* ; Q  0 ],    S^(-1)=[ G  B ; B^*  C ].

Multiplication on the right gives exactly G Q^*=0. The same statement
holds for a parameter family S_s wherever it is invertible. Thus root's
suggestion is algebraically correct: a jointly matched saddle inverse
can preserve the constraint identity at every s.

It does NOT follow that G_s factorizes over components of a decoupled
Delta_s. Consider two scalar sites with Delta=I_2 and the single row
Q=(1,1). There is no ordinary precision edge between the sites, but

 G = I_2-Q^*(QQ^*)^(-1)Q
   = (1/2)[ 1 -1 ; -1 1 ].                                (4.1)

In particular G_12=-1/2 while GQ^*=0. A marked response can cross the
separation through the retained constraint row. The inverse exists and
has a positive constrained gap, so neither a singularity nor a lost
gauge mode causes the failure. The complete constraint footprint must
be treated as a connection in the decoupling graph.

One can instead change Q_s, but this is a different operation with
additional forcing. For S_s(x_s,lambda_s)=(f_s,b_s), differentiation
gives

 S_s (dot x_s,dot lambda_s)
 = (dot f_s-dot Delta_s x_s-dot Q_s^* lambda_s,
                         dot b_s-dot Q_s x_s).             (4.2)

The multiplier/read terms in (4.2) cannot be omitted. Changing a row,
splitting it into several constraints, or changing its fixed datum can
also change the fiber and normalization. Those changes require the
actual native construction, not just the identity G_s Q_s^*=0.

This matters for the proposed adaptive comparison: a coarse determining
row is a block average with a complete footprint, possibly large in the
current units. Keeping it in Q can join two regions across a proposed
cut. Removing or modifying it invokes(4.2), the row's full support and
its actual old-scale domain. The simple finite-dimensional example does
not disprove a localization that explicitly retains all these objects;
it disproves deriving that localization from the block inverse alone.

5. THE DECOUPLED CURRENT DEFECT AND THE PRIMARY CONSTRUCTION

At an undecomposed physical endpoint suppose J=Q^*lambda and G(1)Q^*=0.
If only the inverse walk is decoupled while the row/current is left
unchanged, the exact identity is

             G(s)Q^*lambda=[G(s)-G(1)]Q^*lambda.            (5.1)

It need not vanish. Every difference walk contains a changed parameter,
but that hit can be an auxiliary decoupling boundary rather than an
actual bad row. To obtain an actual owner one needs the protected-domain
geometry and the complete native current rewriting, not merely decay
of the two kernels separately.

Large-II(1.55) is a specific successful rewriting: it first uses the
actual matching constraints and side criticality, and retains the layer
forcing (2.1). It does not claim that every independently cut G(s) is
the upper inverse block of a saddle problem with the same Q. Nor does
it supply such a construction for the new homogeneous endpoint in(3.1).
There is therefore no contradiction with the published same-read
boundary estimates. Applying those estimates to an additional endpoint
requires the corresponding layer/constraint identity and its domains.

The older archive warning about the forced current chart is relevant:
the variational chart used in the indicated construction omits the
-GJ term. A small change in the independently admitted J does not itself
prove stationarity, or local support of a stationarity residual.
Equation(5.1) and the retained layer term show the missing algebra
explicitly rather than invoking an unspecified nonlocality concern.

6. WHAT THE ACTUAL LOCAL DOMAIN DOES AND DOES NOT PROVIDE

Large-I printed190--191 supplies, on each prescribed proper cube, a
gauge in which the REAL factor has the stated potential/derivative
bound. The complex perturbation A', independent J and the derived
local-minimum/current reads have their own simultaneous bounds.
The proper cubes have specified finite sides in their own scale.

This is useful local input for a proposed weak completion. It is not
a single common gauge on an arbitrarily enlarged protected region;
the cube gauges and boundary corrections must be reconciled. Extending
one local potential to zero outside its cube, even if weak, produces
an artificial boundary and a NEW reconstruction. The source's derived
minimum/current clauses and the full complex homotopy would still
have to be checked. Its first connection is initially to that artificial
boundary, not automatically to the actual Z component.

In particular, the existing source-domain and four-map statements do
not give the missing endpoint in(3.1) by a cutoff of the potential.
This is a precise absence in the inspected estimates, not a proof that
no such completion exists. No topological or global SU2 counterexample
is asserted here.

7. FRESH HEALING DOES NOT REMOVE THE DISTINCTION

Large-II printed390 assigns a fresh R' coefficient to the bulk only
after its complete support avoids the surviving large-field region.
It then has full d_k decay and exp[-p0(g_k)] smallness. It is still
described on the native independent-(U,J) domain, with covariance (2.32)

L^[-(k-j)] M R_j <= L. Full decay alone is not homogeneous Ward typing.

The exact original-source four jets, their raw masses, all scalar
terms and any live exterior arguments must be retained during this
assignment. A fixed-branch complete constant-action Gaussian rank
identity is valid if the inserted action is exactly the action defining
the adaptive constrained Hessian, including crossing terms. It does
not identify a carried source with that action, or make its separately
localized carriers scalar. This narrow consumer is correctly stated
in turn76_fresh_source_typing_gauge Section6.

8. DISPOSITION

The meaningful new conclusion is the explicit separation between
constraint annihilation and support decoupling, with(4.1)--(4.2) showing
why a matched inverse does not by itself finish the proposed proof.
The literal missing comparison is(3.1), or equivalently the first term
in(3.2) together with its actual physical-current and frame data.

The existing native machinery already controls the adaptive-to-adaptive
part and offers the correct model: keep all matching determining rows,
rewrite the stationary current before localization, and retain the
constraint-layer forcing. A new adaptive-to-homogeneous construction
must establish that same mechanism for its changed rows and admissible
source contour. The inspected formulas have not supplied that extra
endpoint. No existing theorem is silently renamed to provide it.

No full sourced native iteration, source-susceptibility bound for the
original Wilson law, nonzero physical limit, or YM4 construction is
claimed. The completed earlier comparison and homogeneous running-radius
consumer remain valid at their previously recorded scopes.
