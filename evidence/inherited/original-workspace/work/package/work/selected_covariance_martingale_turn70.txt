      +Cov_(nu_n)(X_n(f_n),X_n(h_n)).                 (R1)

All source-only constants cancel from these covariances. The raw

The selected law here is the normalized positive sector already
identified in projected_product_sector_turn48 Sections 3,6,7 and

1. THE ACTUAL POSITIVE FIBERS AND COMPLETE NORMALIZERS

Write lambda_j for its actual reference measure. It includes the

Let q_j(du|v) be the positive WEIGHTED COAREA fiber kernel of the
actual step from u in S_j to v in S_(j+1). It includes the admitted

The phase and incoming inherited density are not counted twice:
q_j is defined so that the actual incoming raw density rho_j obeys

       rho_(j+1)(v)=int rho_j(u) q_j(du|v).            (1.1)

Here rho_j includes its complete accumulated vacuum factor. When

Let chi_n(v) be the ACTUAL terminal weak-domain restriction, and let

The terminal reference measure and restriction must be the ones used

No lower bound uniform in depth or volume is asserted.

Where rho_(j+1)>0 define

  K_j(du|v)=rho_j(u)q_j(du|v)/rho_(j+1)(v).            (1.3)

Values on zero-mass states are immaterial. These are normalized

2. ONE ORIGINAL SOURCE AT EVERY STAGE

Choose real vertex profiles f_n,h_n on a fixed final physical grid.
Let F and H be their continuous tensor multilinear interpolants.
On every finer grid set

          f_j=I_(R^(n-j)) f_n,   h_j=I_(R^(n-j)) h_n. (2.1)

For different ultraviolet depths with the same terminal grid and

Let F_f(u_0),F_h(u_0) be the corresponding literal weighted ORIGINAL

  X_0(f_0,u_0)=F_f(u_0),
  X_(j+1)(f_(j+1),v)
       =int X_j(f_j,u) K_j(du|v).                    (2.2)

This is exactly the derivative at zero of the raw sourced density

Do not replace the raw recursion (2.2) by a recursion setting

3. REVERSE MARTINGALE PROOF

On (1.4) let G_j be the sigma-field generated by u_j,...,u_n.
These form a decreasing sequence. Kernel factorization gives

  X_j(f_j,u_j)=E_sel[F_f(u_0)|G_j].

The finite original Wilson actions are bounded at a finite
regulator, so all needed second moments exist. More general
insertions would require their actual second-moment hypothesis.

Define the increments
  D_j^f=X_j(f_j,u_j)-X_(j+1)(f_(j+1),u_(j+1)).

Then E[D_j^f|G_(j+1)]=0. Increments at distinct dates, and each
increment against the terminal variable, are orthogonal. Expanding
F_f=X_n(f_n)+sum_j D_j^f, and likewise F_h, gives

 Cov_sel(F_f,F_h)
  =sum_j E_sel[D_j^f D_j^h]
                            +Cov_(nu_n)(X_n(f_n),X_n(h_n)).

Conditioning the increment product on G_(j+1) gives precisely
Cov_(K_j)(X_j(f_j),X_j(h_j)). This proves (R1).
For complex profiles the bilinear identity still holds; absolute
bounds can use the corresponding Hermitian second moments.

The positivity used here is the actual path law, not positivity
of signed cluster activities. It is not necessary that every
localized conditional-covariance coefficient have either sign.

This is a ONE-STEP conditional covariance. An effective two-source

        sup_v |Phi_n(f,v)|<=2 C_X ||f||_(l1).          (6.1)

       <=4 C_X^2 ||f_n||_(l1)||h_n||_(l1).            (6.2)

7. EXACT LIMITATIONS

The upper bound is for the literal weighted original action with
the stated source normalization. Multiplying it by a cutoff-dependent
operator renormalization multiplies these estimates accordingly.
A physical composite calibration, including any required subtraction
or multiplicative renormalization, remains to be supplied.

The bound does not show that covariances at different UV depths
converge. It only controls the finely born contribution inside
each regulator and bounds the terminal term. Comparing the
terminal laws and source classes across regulators is separate.

The terminal covariance may vanish, and signed separated local
contributions may cancel. There is no nonzero lower bound.
The selected conditioning/window construction is not shown here
to preserve reflection positivity, even though its symmetry
and real positivity were admitted. Reflection-invariant selection
alone would not imply reflection positivity.

Finally, Z_sel/Z_0 need not be bounded below. A covariance estimate
in this normalized selected sector is not an estimate in the full
Wilson law. Complementary histories and their original-source
transport remain outside this theorem.
