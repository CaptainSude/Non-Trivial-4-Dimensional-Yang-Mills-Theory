1. PHYSICAL HAMILTONIAN AND THE UNGAUGED TRIAL

The fixed finite graph has connected configuration manifold SU2^E.
The positive elliptic kinetic coefficient and bounded smooth Wilson
potential give a unique positive ground eigenfunction. Gauge invariance
of the operator and positivity/normalization of that eigenfunction make
it gauge invariant. Hence the full and physical spectral bottoms agree.
The ungauged product trial is therefore a legitimate upper bound on the
physical ground energy. No gauge-average normalizer has been omitted.

The Pauli/2 metric is four times the round unit-quaternion metric:
Delta u0=-3u0/4 and |grad u0|^2=(1-u0^2)/4. These give exactly
t E_t(1-u0^2)=3c(t) and the one-link trial kinetic form 3t c(t)/16.
This matches the Hamiltonian convention g^2 K/(2a)+4S_sp/(g^2 a).

at most 18ng^2, including at positive flow time.
