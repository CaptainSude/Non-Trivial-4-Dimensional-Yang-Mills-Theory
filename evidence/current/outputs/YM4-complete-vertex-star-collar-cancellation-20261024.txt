1. THE MINIMAL COMPLETE COLLAR AND ITS ORIGINAL RAW LAW

Use a finite periodic SU(2) Wilson lattice containing a nonwrapped
closed cube B=[0,n]^4, n>=3, in fine lattice units. Let V_int be its
strictly interior vertices and let

 E_int={unoriented links with at least one endpoint in V_int}.

The inclusion of spokes is essential. A gauge transformation at any
vertex of V_int now changes ONLY integrated links. Define P_int as
plaquettes containing an E_int link. Every such plaquette lies in B.
Let Gamma be the complete graph of edges on the cubical boundary of B.
The actual boundary read of P_int is a subset of Gamma; unused edges
of Gamma are retained as an explicit harmless read enlargement.

For v on Gamma and u on E_int, put

 H_B(u;v)=sum_(p in P_int) h(U_p),
 h(W)=1-Tr(W)/2,
 Z_B(v)=integral exp[-beta_W H_B(u;v)] dHaar(u),
 Q_B(v,du)=Z_B(v)^(-1)exp[-beta_W H_B(u;v)]dHaar(u),
 beta_W=c_W/g0^2.                                        (1)

All Haar measures are normalized original group measures. Z_B>0
for EVERY real v. This is exactly the original Wilson conditional
law given all links outside E_int. Its read is Gamma. The actual
exterior law is

 pi_B(dv,deta)=Z_B(v) exp[-beta_W H_ext(v,eta)]
                         dHaar(v,eta)/Z_original.          (2)

Thus every scalar is present once; this does not substitute a global
small-field probability or a locally renormalized exterior measure.

Original P0/P1/Q1 partitions may be reassembled as their exact
identities before forming this conditional law. The normalized
averaged-gauge insertions may likewise be undone in the original
gauge-invariant source integral. Integrating the original coarse
variables removes their normalized group deltas. These operations
recover(1), since they recover the original Wilson measure.
This sentence does NOT claim that a finite chosen list of old atoms
can be summed while all native coarse V1 and gauge-fixed exterior
coordinates stay held. The construction disintegrates the resulting
original UNFIXED group measure. It keeps no previous fine/coarse
record atom. A finite local attempt to retain all those old outputs

For original plaquettes p,q fully inside V_int, set
F_p=c_a f_p h(U_p), alpha_p=|c_a f_p|, and similarly F_q.
Their complete source loops are integrated, and |F_p|<=2alpha_p.

3. AN EXPLICIT ZERO-FREE BOUNDARY TUBE FOR THE COMPLETE KERNEL

Let N_partial be the number of plaquettes in P_int reading a boundary

 |beta_W[H_B(u;w)-H_B(u;1)]|

Inverse group matrices are continued holomorphically, not conjugated.

literal action formula. Choose

 rho_B=c/[1+beta_W max(1,N_partial)]                       (5)

with c fixed small enough that |exp[-Delta H]-1|<=1/4.

 |Z_E(w)-Z_E(1)|<=Z_E(1)/4.                               (6)

In particular both FULL and SMALL normalizers are nonzero in this

The original interior marks have no direct boundary factors in these

 |f(A)-f(0)|<=C cap(f) (a/rho_B)^2.                       (8)

To state an exact curvature/read price, let A_partial(B) be the

No age is refreshed: h_e is the actual fine-to-current ratio of this

A lower bound on Z_B is obtained by integrating each independent

                 exp[-C_B(1+b^2/g0^2)].                   (12)
