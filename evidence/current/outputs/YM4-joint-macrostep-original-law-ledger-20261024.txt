1. RAW TRANSFERS BEFORE ANY SCALAR OR COUPLING REARRANGEMENT

Let V_j denote the actual field at grade j, V_0=U. Retain a fixed
finite homogeneous all-success sequence h through grade m: no owner
is removed, and every relevant ordinary small-field region is the
full torus. Let B_j be the native one-step group average from grade
j to grade j+1, and M_j=B_(j-1)...B_0, M_0=identity.

Define the UNNORMALIZED transfer R_j^h(dV_j;V_(j+1)) at the original
group-variable level. It contains the actual average delta, the
normalized averaged-gauge insertion, all native success and earlier
record predicates allocated at this step, and every exact source-free
factor. No factor is omitted because it depends only on V_(j+1).
Write, recursively,

 W_0(U)=the original Wilson Haar density,
 W_(j+1)(v)=integral R_j^h(dV;v) W_j(V),
 Q_j^h(dV|v)=R_j^h(dV;v) W_j(V)/W_(j+1)(v).             (1)

On a real admitted history with W_m(v)>0, the actual joint backward
law is therefore

 Q_(m-1)(dV_(m-1)|v)...Q_0(dU|V_1)
       =[W_0(U)/W_m(v)] product_(j=0)^(m-1)
                            R_j^h(dV_j;V_(j+1)).        (2)

This is an exact cancellation of every intermediate W_j. In coordinates
it cancels the inherited Z_(j-1) in the next numerator against the
preceding conditional denominator. It does NOT cancel a newly inserted
gauge normalization, a coarea determinant, an old success indicator or
the final W_m. Renormalized coupling/action notation is only an exact
rearrangement of these W_j; it creates no extra uncancelled quantum
potential in(2).

Native ledger locators: B119 printed249(1.15)--(1.16) gives the first
complete integral; printed253--254(1.26)--(1.30) places its exact

2. ELIMINATING THE INTERMEDIATE GROUP FIELDS

For normalized Haar measure and the corresponding central group delta,

 integral dV_j delta(B_(j-1)V_(j-1) V_j^-1) F(V_j)
                         =F(B_(j-1)V_(j-1)).

Applying this identity successively removes V_1,...,V_(m-1) from(2).
It leaves the FINAL composite constraint M_m U=v. On the same admitted
chart the joint source expectation is consequently

 T_(m,v)^h f =
 [integral dU delta(M_m U v^-1) f(U)
       exp(-S_W(U)) G_m(U) Theta_h(U;v) C_h(U;v)]
 /[the same integral with f=1].                         (3)

Here Theta_h is the product of EVERY original intermediate success/
prior/cut indicator evaluated at the actual V_j=M_j U. In particular
it retains the old approximate-field masks, the true current free-ball
tests in their original pivot convention, the averaged-gauge cutoffs,
and any coarse endpoint record restrictions. They have not become
one just because only one delta remains.

G_m denotes the actual product of smooth gauge penalties. In the
full-torus convention of B119(1.5), its factors are

 product_j z_j^(-n_j) exp[-G_j(M_j U)/g_j^2],             (4)

with their original counts n_j and coupling-dependent gauge constants.
The cutoff part of(1.5) is in Theta_h, not in the exponential in(4).
C_h records any remaining exact scalar allocation not already in
S_W, G_m or the Haar measure. One can choose the original unrearranged
group ledger so that only the corresponding explicit partition/gauge
constants occur here; if another convention is used, C_h is retained
literally. In particular no presumed cancellation of a stationary
classical scalar is needed. There is NO product of intermediate inverse
Z_j in(3): those have already telescoped by(2).

At this group stage there is also no additional change-of-variable
Jacobian for integrating V_j against its delta. The coarea determinant
appears when solving the final composite constraint in fine coordinates.
Multiplying that determinant by all the old coordinate coarea factors
again would double count. Conversely, starting from the coordinate
version of(2) requires undoing those coordinate changes with their
exact Jacobians before using the displayed group identity.

Gauge representatives matter. Each new gauge insertion can be moved
back to the finer history by its actual nested block lift. B109 printed
265(2.1)--(2.3), printed269(2.16), and B119 printed250 establish the
needed covariance and invariance of earlier cuts under THAT lift.
The earlier fields and their earlier backgrounds/pivots are transformed
together. Thus the group variables in(3) can be taken in one coherent
fine representative. A gauge-invariant incoming source is unchanged
by these lifts and still reads its same physical links P. For a general
noninvariant source the corresponding transformed source must be kept;
it is not automatically the original f(U|P). The actual canonical
rare source is invariant, so this qualification is compatible with
the current target.

Equation(3) is valid on the actual success-chart contribution; no
undefined group average on a rough exterior is introduced by writing
M_j there. A proposed enlarged continuation must separately remain
inside the analytic domain of all composite averages.

4. WHY THE FIRST-BARE LARGE-PRODUCT THEOREM CANNOT BE COPIED VERBATIM

Even after all effective Z_j cancel, the smooth density in(6) is NOT
the first-bare density of the earlier theorem. The hierarchy(4) remains,
and the final constraint is M_m rather than B_0. The inverse final
pivot already costs H^3. Pulling back G_j(M_j U) gives complete reads
on blocks of diameter up to H. The physical noise Hessian and local
force constants therefore require a new quantitative registration.

At flat, the intrinsic quadratic form before final free-coordinate
embedding has the identifiable form

 E_m(A)=c_W||dA||^2+
           sum_(j=0)^(m-1) c_G(g0/g_j)^2
                         ||ell_j DM_j A||^2,
                         DM_m A=0.                     (7)

ell_j is the ACTUAL linearized averaged gauge on grade j. For a
fine vertex gradient, gauge covariance gives

 ell_j DM_j(d phi)(x)=phi(y_j(x))-phi(x),                (8)

on grade-j vertices, with y_j their next block center. Thus the
hierarchy controls gauge-vertical directions; ignoring all but G0
would leave additional coarse gauge directions uncontrolled.
The complete final coarea/Haar logarithms also remain beyond(7).

A plausible finite proof of a quantitative gap uses a hard coordinate
tree in each H-block: remove a fine gradient with value0 at its center;
bound the remaining intra-block bonds by plaquette fillings; use the
positive private-interface average and DM_m A=0 to bound crossing
bonds; then telescope the vertex chain from a fine vertex through its
successive centers using(8). Each path/filling has polynomial H cost.
This indicates a bound of the form E_m(A)>=C_m^-1||A||^2, with
C_m=(C_L)^(O(m)) when the complete composition bounds are registered.

THIS NOTE DOES NOT USE THAT INDICATED GAP AS AN ESTABLISHED NATIVE

5. THE EXACT QUANTITATIVE INPUTS FOR A FINITE PROJECTION

In coordinates(5) a fixed auxiliary product K_S would already hold
the source arguments fixed; a second source rechart is unnecessary.
To apply the proved projection lemma to(6), the following are the
specific missing native estimates, rather than an unspecified
"macrostep regularity" assumption:

(a) A common real/complex chart for (5) and all M_j, with complete
    smooth density and final coarea defined on K_S. The interpolation
    of its flat quadratic and remainder must have a positive weighted
    gap throughout that product. A pointwise local-force estimate
    with the actual growing read and constants must imply a mean/tail
    under the containing law, not under a guessed Gaussian.

(b) An INNER inclusion K_s subset D_(h,v) verified for EVERY original
    predicate. It requires explicit bounds, for each j and each local
    comparison domain, on the actual current residual

     g_j^-1 log[(M_j U)(M_j U_(j+1)(M_(j+1)U))^-1]

    at its original tested free bonds, with the native nonlinear
    pivot convention, AND on the approximate-field and gauge masks.
    An estimate for U minus a new macro minimum is not this bound.
    A possible near-flat route integrates the ACTUAL old minimum first
    response from zero at its own determining data, then bounds all
    M_j U uniformly small. The running physical admission margins and
    each original center constant must be retained in that calculation.

(c) An OUTER inclusion D_(h,v) subset K_S in the SAME final chart.
    Recursively reconstructing each V_j from its original successful
    free variables and its actual center could give

       ||log U|| <= C_m[||log v||+sum_j g_j r_j].        (9)

    The constants and representative in(9) must be those of the actual
    native minimum/return maps. It does not follow from the final
    pivot inverse alone. It also cannot be inferred from a new

(d) A fixed real parameter box for v on which (a)--(c) all hold,
    and a finite holomorphic likelihood-ratio bound for the SAME

    source holomorphy is not the missing input.

6. GROWING-READ COSTS: A FINITE GAP IS NOT YET AN AGE-UNIFORM RESULT

Even the favorable prospective estimates have a significant numerical
gate. Suppose the full native registration above gives constants
C_m=C_0^m and, after the required chart/constraint conversions, only

 epsilon_m <= C N_I exp[-c r_min^2/C_m^K],               (10)

with fixed K>0, r_min=min_j r_j and N_I the actual integrated fine
coordinate count. This is a useful finite estimate for fixed m at a
sufficiently weak coupling. It is not a volume-independent estimate
if N_I is the whole ultraviolet torus.

Taking the smallest m with L^(3m)>N gives C_m^K comparable to
(1+N)^a up to fixed constants, a=K log(C_0)/(3 log L). A sufficient
threshold to CERTIFY a macrostep gain L^[-(4+sigma)m] using the
displayed conservative majorant (10) is

 r_min^2 >= C (1+N)^a [log(2+N_I)+(4+sigma)m log L].     (11)

This is a threshold for that majorant to prove the desired bound,
not an intrinsic necessary condition on the unknown actual tail.
At comparable weak couplings r_j=A1 log^p(g_j^-2), this left side
is only of order [log(g^-2)]^(2p). For a hybrid read growing as a
positive power of age, (11) therefore covers only a corresponding
early window unless a much better multiscale estimate is proved.
The incoming rare amplitude is multiplied by epsilon_m, but that
does not turn a failure of (11) into a new age contraction.

There is a separate real-admission gate. A crude small-field witness
for(b) based on absolute fine logs can force

            ||log v|| <= g_min r_min/C_m^K.             (12)

That box need not contain the actual NEXT ordinary image, whose
current fluctuations are of size g_m r_m. Weak coupling alone does
not remove the ratio C_m^-K when the couplings/radii are comparable.
Covariant comparison around the original centers may improve(12),
but that is exactly the native estimate that must be established;
one may not discard the outer complement of this smaller box.

Neither(10) nor(12) is asserted to be an intrinsic impossibility.
They show why the obvious finite-dimensional gap/absolute-smallness
route would be quantitatively insufficient for the current growing
hybrid read even if its finite estimates were completed. A graded
metric, a sharper original-center comparison or a supported source
decomposition may avoid these losses.
