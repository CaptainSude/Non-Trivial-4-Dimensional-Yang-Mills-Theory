ONE-OBSERVABLE SPECTRAL COMPACTNESS AND A DYNAMIC WITNESS
TURN71. Root derivation. 20 October 2026.

This is a conditional consumer theorem, not a Yang--Mills estimate.
Its purpose is to avoid requiring a full cross-cutoff Cauchy theorem
when a subsequential existence construction would suffice, and to
distinguish a surviving positive-energy state from a frozen mixture.

1. SETUP

For every cutoff a, let A_a be a nonnegative self-adjoint operator,
Omega_a a normalized ground vector, and v_a a centered physical
observable vector. No common Hilbert space is assumed.

Let rho_a(B)=||1_B(A_a)v_a||^2. At each fixed cutoff rho_a is finite.
The estimates below also work when this is only locally finite and
the stated positive-time transforms exist.

For a fixed time-window width delta>0 put

 b_delta(E)=(1-exp(-delta E))/E,   b_delta(0)=delta,

 R_a(t)=integral exp(-2tE)b_delta(E)^2 rho_a(dE).

This is the squared norm of exp(-t A_a)b_delta(A_a)v_a.
For the actual fixed-spatial Hamiltonian and a magnetic multiplication
observable it is also the reflected covariance of the centered time
window [t,t+delta]. Its finite-regulator source identity was derived
in TURN67 and attached to temporal Wilson limits in TURN68--69.
Nothing here extends those results uniformly in the spatial cutoff.

2. SUBSEQUENCE WITHOUT A CAUCHY ESTIMATE

Suppose for some t0>0 that R_a(t0)<=M<infinity uniformly along a
cutoff sequence a->0. Set

 nu_a(dE)=exp(-2t0 E)b_delta(E)^2 rho_a(dE).

These positive measures have total mass <=M. Regard them as measures
on the compact metrizable interval [0,infinity], including infinity.
They have a weakly convergent subsequence nu_a -> nu_hat. Let nu be
the restriction of nu_hat to finite energies.

For every t>t0 the function exp[-2(t-t0)E], assigned value zero at
infinity, is continuous on this compactification. Hence on the SAME
subsequence,

 R_a(t) -> R(t)
   := integral_[0,infinity) exp[-2(t-t0)E] nu(dE).

An atom of nu_hat at infinity is lost at these strictly later times.
Thus a uniform early-time upper bound alone already gives a nonnegative
Laplace-transform subsequential limit for this single observable.
It does not give convergence at the boundary t=t0.

For any k>=1 and t>=t0+eta,

 |partial_t^k R_a(t)|
   <= M sup_(E>=0) (2E)^k exp(-2eta E)
   = M [k/(e eta)]^k.

Weak convergence of the continuous derivative kernels and these
bounds give convergence of every derivative locally uniformly for
t>t0. This can also be proved by Arzela--Ascoli on compact time
intervals, with the pointwise Laplace limit fixing every subsequential
limit. No analyticity of a physical-source logarithm is required for
this implication.

This theorem does not produce the PREMISE R_a(t0)<=M. The available
selected-sector upper bound is not automatically such a bound for
the original Hamiltonian/Wilson physical source.

3. WHY A LATER POSITIVE NORM ALONE CAN LEAVE ONLY ZERO ENERGY

A later lower bound R_a(t1)>=m>0 prevents all weighted spectral mass
from escaping to infinity. It need not prevent collapse to zero.

For example take the two-state Markov Hamiltonian

 A_epsilon=(epsilon/2)[[1,-1],[-1,1]],
 Omega=(1,1)/sqrt(2),
 Q=diag(1,-1),  v=Q Omega.

It has a unique ground state at every epsilon>0, a positive
semigroup, and centered spectral measure delta_epsilon. Its stationary
reversible path law is reflection positive. Nevertheless

 R_epsilon(t)=exp(-2t epsilon)b_delta(epsilon)^2 -> delta^2

at every fixed t. The limiting centered state can lie in an enlarged
zero-energy sector. Finite-regulator uniqueness does not rule this out.

For a full QFT construction, vacuum clustering/ergodic-component
control is one way to address this. The following stronger finite-
cutoff criterion gives a directly dynamic witness instead. It is
sufficient, not asserted necessary for every observable choice.

4. A QUANTITATIVE FIXED POSITIVE-ENERGY BAND

In addition to R_a(t0)<=M, suppose for fixed t1>t0 and s>0,

 D_a:=R_a(t1)-R_a(t1+s)>=m>0.

Let alpha=2(t1-t0)>0 and beta=2s>0. Then

 D_a=integral q(E) nu_a(dE),
 q(E)=exp(-alpha E)(1-exp(-beta E)).

This q is nonnegative, bounded by one, and vanishes at BOTH zero
and infinity. Put

 epsilon=m/(4 beta M),
 Lambda=max(2 epsilon, alpha^-1 log(4M/m)).

The premises imply 0<m<=M. On E<epsilon, q(E)<=beta E, so that

 integral_(E<epsilon) q(E) nu_a(dE)<=m/4.

On E>Lambda, q(E)<=exp(-alpha Lambda), whence

 integral_(E>Lambda) q(E) nu_a(dE)<=m/4.

Therefore

 integral_[epsilon,Lambda] q(E) nu_a(dE)>=m/2,
 nu_a([epsilon,Lambda])>=m/2.

This is a FIXED physical energy band, independent of cutoff. Since
exp(-2t0 E)b_delta(E)^2<=delta^2, it also implies

 rho_a([epsilon,Lambda])>=m/(2 delta^2).

Passing to the weak subsequence from Section2, q is continuous on
[0,infinity], with q(0)=q(infinity)=0, so

 integral q(E)nu(dE)>=m.

The same low/high estimates give nu([epsilon,Lambda])>=m/2.
No choice of interval endpoints with zero atom is necessary for this
conclusion; it follows by the integral estimate.

The resulting single-observable spectral representation has a
nonzero positive-energy subspace. For example on L2(nu), multiplication
by E is a nonnegative self-adjoint generator and the vector 1 has
nonzero projection onto [epsilon,Lambda]. Its semigroup matrix
elements reproduce R(t0+u/2) for u>0.

This is an abstract one-observable limit, not a local four-dimensional
Yang--Mills theory: no common continuum algebra, spatial translations,
rotations, products, or gauge-theory identification follows.

5. EXACT ORIGINAL-SOURCE EXPRESSION FOR THIS CRITERION

For a fixed finite-spatial magnetic observable Q, define

 K_a(T,z)=log <Omega_a,
    exp[-T(H_a-z Q_a-E_a)] Omega_a>.

The endpoints are the UNSOURCED ground vector, and
K_a(T,0)=0. Write J_a(T)=partial_z^2 K_a(T,0).
TURN67's exact Duhamel/reflection identity is

 R_a(t)=(1/2)Delta_delta^2 J_a(2t),
 Delta_delta^2 J(T)=J(T+2delta)-2J(T+delta)+J(T).

Consequently the dynamic witness in Section4 is exactly

 D_a=(1/2)[Delta_delta^2 J_a(2t1)
                   -Delta_delta^2 J_a(2t1+2s)].

There are only finitely many required slab lengths. Any source scalar
affine in T cancels in each Delta_delta^2, provided the SAME
normalization is used at all these lengths. Source-dependent errors
not known to be affine cannot be discarded.

For the global logarithmic coupling source of TURN67, with magnetic
observable Q_gamma=4 gamma S_sp/a and gamma=g^-2, the factor 1/2
above is replaced by 1/8 for its source curvature. Local coupling
profiles have the separately retained energy-current correction;
this identity does not remove it.

These formulas specify the quantitative source information that
would suffice. No upper bound M or positive difference m uniform
in spatial cutoff has been proved for the actual required YM family.

6. ROLE IN THE EXISTENCE PROGRAM

Full-sequence uniqueness and cross-cutoff Cauchy estimates are not
logically necessary for a subsequential existence argument. One
can instead seek compatible limits and preserve positivity along
a common subsequence. The present theorem demonstrates this for
one physical spectral family and supplies a sufficient positive-
energy witness.

The full proof still needs estimates for the ORIGINAL law, a common
local observable algebra with the required products and continuum
symmetries, and an appropriate reconstruction. Neither a scalar
Laplace-transform limit nor compactness of unrelated correlations
performs those steps.
