RARE SOURCE FORCING WITH GENERIC MARGINAL FEEDBACK ON TUNED TRAJECTORIES
24 October 2026. Root. Conditional numerical consumer, not native admission.

PURPOSE

A new rare source need not satisfy the complete Wilson reference's
special O(g^4) marginal cancellation. A uniformly controlled O(g^2)
feedback gives only polynomial amplification along the actual tuned
trajectory. The actual logarithmic-radius rarity pays this factor,
as well as any fixed once-born inverse-coupling and read polynomial.
This note proves that statement for a full tensor and neutral-memory
recurrence. It does not prove that an original selector or neighborhood
projection satisfies the input recurrence or its microscopic census.

1. FINITE RECURRENCE WITH ALL REQUIRED INPUTS EXPLICIT

Let a_j>=0 bound the norm of the FULL source marginal tensor at date j.
Let w_j>=0 bound the new neutral birth, and P_j>=0 the prepared old
neutral sources AFTER the original four-dimensional root sum. Suppose

 P_j<=sum_(i<j)K(j-i)w_i,
 K(t)<=C_K r^t, 0<r<1,
 w_j<=C_w v_j(a_j+P_j)+b_j,
 a_(j+1)<= (1+C_a v_j)a_j+C_b v_j P_j+c_j,               (1)

with v_j=q_j+e_j, q_j=g_j^2, and e_j,b_j,c_j nonnegative. A kernel
bounded by a fixed polynomial times L^-sigma t has the displayed
geometric majorant after reducing sigma. All constants must be
uniform in the actual grade and regulator. These are hypotheses on
the SAME actual source construction. The exact flat scalar and any
mixed contact are retained separately, not discarded by (1).

The inhomogeneous b_j,c_j may include once-born polynomial source
radius/read prices. They may not include unaccounted repeated costs,
an exponential support-volume loss, or the original microscopic root
count which P_j explicitly assumes already handled. The feedback
coefficients in (1) must be bounded by v_j with fixed constants;
mere smallness of the raw incoming source does not prove that.

2. A SINGLE POSITIVE MEMORY VARIABLE

Define z_j=sum_(i<j) r^(j-i)w_i, so P_j<=C_K z_j and
z_(j+1)=r(z_j+w_j). Start z_0=0, or retain a specified nonnegative
initial memory with the same update. Set M_j=a_j+z_j. From (1),

 a_(j+1)<= (1+C_a v_j)a_j+C_b C_K v_j z_j+c_j,
 z_(j+1)<= r C_w v_j a_j+r(1+C_w C_K v_j)z_j+r b_j.

Choose A>=max(C_a+r C_w,C_K(C_b+r C_w)). Then r<=1 gives

 M_(j+1)<=(1+A v_j)M_j+f_j,  f_j=c_j+r b_j.             (2)

No one-step contraction of the original source is assumed here. The
strict r is its separately established static age kernel. Iterating
(2), with empty products one, gives the exact upper bound

 M_j<=M_i product_(l=i)^(j-1)(1+A v_l)
       +sum_(k=i)^(j-1) f_k product_(l=k+1)^(j-1)(1+A v_l).
                                                               (3)

It also bounds a_j and P_j<=C_K M_j. This argument accommodates a
finite-dimensional general invariant curvature tensor by taking its
norm; no unsupported scalar Wilson reduction is used.

3. THE TUNED TRAJECTORY GIVES POLYNOMIAL AMPLIFICATION

Use the actual endpoint theorem at horizon N:

 x_j=q_j^-1>=Y+lambda(N-j), Y=X-C_G>0, lambda>0.

For 0<=i<j<=N, monotonicity of the majorant as a function of backward
age, not monotonicity of x_j itself, gives

 sum_(l=i)^(j-1) q_l
 <= integral_(N-j)^(N-i) du/(Y+lambda u)
 =lambda^-1 log[(Y+lambda(N-i))/(Y+lambda(N-j))].        (4)

Consequently, if sum_l e_l<=E uniformly,

 product_(l=i)^(j-1)(1+A v_l)
 <=exp(AE)[(Y+lambda(N-i))/(Y+lambda(N-j))]^nu,
 nu=A/lambda.                                          (5)

Finite-period errors with a matched exponentially growing period
have bounded E; otherwise the actual error sum is an explicit input.
The estimates permit all signed elementary coupling fluctuations
allowed by the drift theorem. They do not choose new trajectories.

4. ACTUAL LOGARITHMIC-RADIUS RARITY PAYS THE AMPLIFICATION

Suppose x_j also obeys the established upper bound X+B(N-j), and
choose X>=2C_G+1. Then x_j is comparable, by fixed constants, to
u_j=Y+lambda(N-j). Suppose for fixed d,c>0 and beta>1,

 f_j<=C (1+x_j)^d exp[-c(log x_j)^beta].                 (6)

This includes a genuine tail exp(-cT_j^2) with
T_j=A1(log x_j)^p, p>1/2, multiplied by any FIXED polynomial in
inverse g_j, logarithmic read size and backward date. It does not
assert that every original complementary row has that tail.

Comparability gives, after increasing the weak floor,
f_j<=C' u_j^d exp[-c'(log u_j)^beta]. By (3)--(5), the
forcing contribution to M_N is bounded by

 C exp(AE) Y^-nu sum_(t>=1)
       (Y+lambda t)^(d+nu) exp[-c'(log(Y+lambda t))^beta]. (7)

For every prescribed H>0, the exponential eventually dominates
u^-(d+nu+H+2), because beta>1. Comparison with the integral of
u^-(H+2) then proves

 forcing contribution to M_N <= C_H exp(AE) Y^-H,        (8)

uniformly in N. The constants and weak floor can depend on H and the
fixed once-born prices, but not on the number of elapsed dates. A
rare initial M_0 with the same polynomial-tail cap also satisfies
the bound after its polynomial amplification in (5).

For the forcing born in the fine prefix N-j>=R+1, the same proof
gives, with any prescribed H and an adjusted constant,

 prefix contribution <=C_H exp(AE) Y^-nu
                                      (Y+lambda R)^-H. (9)

Thus this tail tends to zero for fixed terminal X as R grows. This
is a uniform numerical tail of the specified recurrences, not yet
convergence of different finite original probability measures.

5. WHY THE SOURCE CENSUS AND ACTUAL ADMISSION REMAIN ESSENTIAL

If the forcing instead carries an unremoved L^(4(N-j)) microscopic
count, its exponential growth is not paid by (6):
(log(Y+lambda t))^beta=o(t). Likewise replacing the feedback factor
1+Aq_j by a fixed factor greater than one would allow exponential
growth in t. Neither modification satisfies the theorem's intended
source inputs. Static h^4 or h^(4+alpha) after a proved same-read
preparation can cancel that count, but a normalized quantum average
is not a static evaluation and can generate a new scalar and tensor.

The benefit of (8) is specific: once an actual rare family is admitted
with its full tensor, an age kernel and O(g^2) quantum feedback, the
special complete-reference O(g^4) cancellation is unnecessary for
that family's smallness. Polynomial inverse-radius costs may be paid
once at the birth by the genuine rarity. Repeated fresh Cauchy prices
are not covered. Every original mass, selector and emitted contact
must still be attached to its own proved recurrence or kept separate.

This consumer is intended for the newly investigated original rare
source components. It does not promote the current one-step real
quantum estimate to an analytic all-scale source theorem. It does
not prove the original-law continuum limit or nontriviality.
