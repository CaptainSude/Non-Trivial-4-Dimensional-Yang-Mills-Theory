RESULT AND SCOPE

The first full-Omega normalizer ratio L0 is a real coarse-gauge-invariant
scalar. This does not supply the holomorphic field hypothesis of the
existing analytic extraction theorem. In fact, at flat coarse data its
actual intersecting old masks have a one-sided boundary-width formula.
On sufficiently large native periodic volumes at least one width is
nonzero: otherwise the actual local comparison minima would glue to a
finite-range global classical response, contradicting the exact flat
transverse block-average precision. Consequently L0 is not C1 at flat
data in at least one real coarse direction. The correction cannot be
inserted unchanged into the old analytic Ward module.

This is an obstruction to that attachment, not to Yang--Mills theory,
not to all possible scaling bounds for L0, and not to smoothness of the
complete original density after complementary old-mask sectors have
been recombined. The next log M1 is gauge invariant; its corner is NOT
asserted to persist through the next integration.

3. EXACT ONE-SIDED WIDTH FORMULA FOR THE ACTUAL OLD MASKS

Use the Pauli spectral convention of the domain note and set

 rho=2 arcsin(delta0),       R*=rho/g0.

The exact old factor at a free independent bond b is

                     B_rho(exp(i v_Db(beta))), D in D_b},

These are the actual (1.8) starred tests, with only the globally
specified average pivots deleted. Straightening changes only those
pivots; it changes neither (1) nor its independent coordinates.
At beta=0 all these comparison and global minima equal1, so
K_b(0)=B_(R*). Along a real physical coarse tangent beta=t h put

                  v_Db(t h)=t w_Db(h)+O(t^2).                 (2)

For a ray a=r n, |n|=1, the exact boundary equation is

 cos|v| cos(g0 r)+sin|v| (n_v dot n) sin(g0 r)=cos rho.

Its r derivative at (v,r)=(0,R*) is -g0 sin rho, and its t
derivative is sin rho (n dot w). Thus the single-center radial
velocity is n dot w/g0. The intersection has right velocity
min_D n dot w_Db/g0 and left velocity max_D n dot w_Db/g0.
All lists D_b are finite, so their min expansions are uniform in n.

Define the positive unnormalized boundary marginal

 m_b(n)=integral_(product_(c!=b) B_(R*))
          exp[-H0(R* n,A_-b;0)] dA_-b .                     (3)

It is continuous and strictly positive. H0 is the COMPLETE physical
action including Haar, gauge and nonlinear constraint terms, not a
Gaussian replacement. It and its physical beta derivative are smooth
on this finite common compact chart. Product radial integration and
dominated differentiation therefore give

 Z_Omega'(0+)-Z_Omega'(0-)
  = -(R*)^2/g0 sum_b integral_(S^2) m_b(n)
          [max_D n dot w_Db-min_D n dot w_Db] dn.              (4)

Equivalently the left side is the limit of
[Z_Omega(t h)+Z_Omega(-t h)-2Z_Omega(0)]/t, t down to0.
The complete smooth-action score cancels in that symmetric quotient.
The same formula for log Z_Omega has m_b divided by Z_Omega(0).
The fixed-cut log Z_s is C1, so it contributes zero to this quotient
for L0=log Z_Omega-log Z_s.

Every term on the right is nonpositive. It is strictly negative if
w_Db != w_Eb for any one actual tested b,D,E. Indeed the width is

The global-minimum derivative is a COMMON translation of all w_Db
