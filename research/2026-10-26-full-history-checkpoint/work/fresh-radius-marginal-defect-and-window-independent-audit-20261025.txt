4. CAUSAL WINDOW APPLICATION: USE A DETERMINISTIC TRIAL HORIZON

Section 5 of the defect note is correct as a consequence on an already
known stopped family. To use the defect bound to PROVE first passage,
do not assume a bound on an unconstructed stopping date. Choose first

 J_H=floor((sigma H+C0)/lambda)+1,
 ell_trial,H=T L^(H-J_H),

where 0<sigma<lambda and H is large enough that J_H<=H. These numbers
are known before constructing a single step. Suppose the actual defect
producer yields, for every already admitted state at j<J_H,

 |delta_j|<=D_H=P(H) exp[-c ell_trial,H/P(H)],                 (A4)

with fixed polynomial P. Since ell_trial,H grows exponentially in H,
C_W J_H D_H and J_H^2 D_H tend to zero. This statement is a named
finite/universal comparison premise, not an inference from decay of
the coefficients separately. Its state-uniformity must be checked.

On a constructed prefix before first passage, (A1)--(A2) use only
m_j<=C_W j D_H and the next bound (A4). For large H their extra error
C m_j+D_H is smaller than the fixed strict drift/increment margins.
The causal Gaussian argument therefore extends the prefix, as long as
the input is weak. Until passage the input exceeds X, and at passage
the output exceeds X-B>=Y. If no passage occurred by J_H, the resulting
stretch bound gives x_(J_H)<X, contradiction. Hence n_H<=J_H.

This proves the diagram is causal when (A4) is established on trial
prefixes. Afterwards (A3) gives an additional total numerical driver
O(J_H^2 D_H), tending to zero. No defect estimate based only on a
favorable future stopping geometry may be used in this induction.

Bounds such as x_j<=x_0+C0 and R_sp,j<=C(log H)^r are themselves valid
on each already admitted drift prefix, which is enough to use them
inside a causal proof of (A4). The FREE-NOISE cut R_cut and SPATIAL
