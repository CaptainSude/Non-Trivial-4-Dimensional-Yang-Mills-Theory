3. EXACT FINITE-PERIOD BOUNDARY COEFFICIENT

Let phi_k denote the same spatial function of the original slice at
time k. For 0<=k<=N,

 E_W[phi_k phi_0]
  =Tr(T^(N-k) Phi T^k Phi)/Tr(T^N)

 Z_T=Tr(T^N).                                          (3.1)

The prefactor in (1.1) cancels at this SAME source-free normalization.
The spectral sums are absolutely convergent. For example the terms are
nonnegative and the two-insertion trace is bounded by ||phi||infty^2
Tr(T^N), by Schatten Holder (also at endpoint k=0 directly).

Choose an integer d>=1 and k such that 2k+d<N. Pair terms i<j, ordered
with lambda_i>=lambda_j, and put r=lambda_j/lambda_i in(0,1]. The paired
numerator difference between k and k+d is exactly

 lambda_i^N |Phi_ij|^2
             (1-r^d)(r^k-r^(N-k-d)).                  (3.2)

It is nonnegative; it is strictly positive when r<1 and Phi_ij!=0.
The diagonal and equal-eigenvalue pairs cancel. By (2.2), at least one
pair i=0,j>0 is strictly positive. Thus

             E_W[(phi_k-phi_(k+d))phi_0]>0.            (3.3)

The means of the two terms are identical by stationarity, so this is
also their covariance with phi_0. For positive-half use impose the
stronger geometric restriction 0<k<k+d<N/2, with physical buffers.
The sign formula (3.2) itself only needs 2k+d<N.
