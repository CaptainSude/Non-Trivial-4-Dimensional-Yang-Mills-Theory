1. What the scalar admission actually uses

The repaired producer, source_producer_adversarial_trace Sections5--7,
puts an extra spatial-source family in a reserved g_i^kappa_R majorant.
Its one-history Cauchy radius has the factor g_k^kappa_R; it does not

No new cutoff exponent is chosen in response to K for this statement.

The R'' coordinate is different: its parent terms come from the actual
young-R s-interpolation. Keep the source's own old/young split. A parent
remaining in that interpolation has age at most

                  C0+C1 log log gamma_new.                  (1)

Terms beyond that range have their FLUCTUATION expressions in the old
regular E integral; they are not silently renewed as R''. The classical

       C delta_src(gamma_new) (1+log log gamma_new)^C.

3. Acyclic parameter order, and what is not closed by it

Fix the weaker admission exponent kappa_R, the analytic/geometric

 b_j^-2 ||R_j|| <= C gamma_j^(2-K/2) (log gamma_j)^D,          (4)

so any already proved finite-prefix, fixed-polynomial deletion bound

There remains a distinct infinite-age issue. Replacing the actual

such a propagator has been established, or if the needed loss exponent
itself grows with K through an untracked change of geometry.

Thus the exponent separation repairs the previously apparent source-
radius circularity for finite polynomial losses. It does not by itself
close the tensor phase-homogenization or full operator-fusion argument.
