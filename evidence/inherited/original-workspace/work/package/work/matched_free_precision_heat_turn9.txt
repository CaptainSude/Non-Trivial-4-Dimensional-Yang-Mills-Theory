SMOOTHING WITH THE EXACT BLOCKED FREE PRECISION
19 October 2026. Root derivation. Homogeneous Gaussian setting only.

WHY THIS CORRECTION IS NEEDED

The stationary full-array iteration of turn8 requires a flow preserving
the ACTUAL current measure. After a free Gaussian blocking that measure
has precision q_M, not the original nearest-neighbor Maxwell precision.
Using the latter without a score companion would already be wrong in
the free iteration. This note supplies uniform smoothing for the exact
homogeneous flat q_M family, using the explicit previously derived
symbols. It does not assert an interacting effective-action estimate.

1. THE UNIFORM PRECISION FAMILY

Let Q_M be the physical straight-string average with total factor M,
and q_M(B)=inf_(Q_M A=B)||d A||^2, with units chosen so its output
lattice spacing is one. Work on a compatible gauge/harmonic quotient;
unfixed noncompact constant harmonic fields are not assigned a Gaussian
inverse. All conclusions below concern curvature and are independent of
the selected minimizing gauge.

The explicit algebra in archive/ym4-research/work/
flat_covariance_decay_research.md proves that q_M(theta) is uniformly
bounded and holomorphic on one fixed complex strip, for every odd M,
including M=1. It also gives, for real theta!=0,

 q_M(B)=min_lambda (B+d^+ lambda)^* W_M^{-1}(B+d^+ lambda),
 W_M=diag(D_mu/P0),
 0<c_D<=D_mu<=C_D,
 P0=4M^2 sum_mu sin^2(theta_mu/(2M)).                        (1.1)

Put lambda_c(theta)=sum_mu |exp(i theta_mu)-1|^2. On the real
Brillouin cube, lambda_c<=P0<=C lambda_c. Hence (1.1) implies

 c lambda_c ||Pi_T B||^2<=B^*q_M B
                         <=C lambda_c ||Pi_T B||^2,        (1.2)

uniformly in M. Pi_T is the orthogonal complement of d^+ at nonzero
real momentum. Equation(1.2) follows by minimizing the comparison
scalar Euclidean norms; no commutation of W_M with Pi_T is assumed.
Also q_M d^+=0 and d^- q_M=0, identically on the analytic strip.

Add the analytic gauge term and define

                  G_M=q_M+d^+d^-.

For real theta it is Hermitian and

 c0 lambda_c I<=G_M(theta)<=C0 lambda_c I.                  (1.3)

The analytic uniform bound for q_M gives a uniform second derivative
bound for G_M on a fixed smaller strip by Cauchy. All constants below
are independent of the prior total blocking M.

2. COMPLEX HEAT BOUND WITHOUT A NORMALITY ASSUMPTION

Let z=xi+i zeta belong to that smaller strip. Hermiticity on the real
torus implies G_M(z)^*=G_M(conjugate z). Taylor's formula therefore
cancels the linear term in the Hermitian part, giving

 Re G_M(xi+i zeta)>=c0 lambda_c(xi) I-C1|zeta|^2 I.         (2.1)

The inequality concerns quadratic forms on complex vectors. It uses
the uniform second derivative bound on the intervening segment, not
a real-momentum lower bound extrapolated without a remainder.

For the ODE v'=-R^2 G_M(z)v, the energy inequality and Gronwall give

 ||exp[-T R^2 G_M(z)]||
        <=exp[-c0 T R^2 lambda_c(xi)+C1 T R^2|zeta|^2].     (2.2)

This argument works for a nonnormal complex matrix; diagonalization
with an uncontrolled condition number is not used.

In a final coarse Bloch fiber p, the fine OLD-lattice aliases have
z_l=(p+2pi l)/R, l in I_R^4. For |Im p|<=rho, (2.2) becomes

 ||exp[-T R^2 G_M(z_l)]||
                  <=C_(rho,T) exp[-c_T |Re p+2pi l|^2].   (2.3)

Choose the centered Brillouin representatives; their physical
derivative symbols u_l=R(exp(i z_l)-1) have size bounded by
C(|Re p+2pi l|+rho). Thus any FIXED number of physical output
differences can be inserted in (2.3), with an alias-square-summable
majorant uniform in M,R. A finite real overlap at Brillouin boundaries
can be handled by shifting centered aliases, as in the original
symbol gluing argument.

3. THE GAUGE TERM DOES NOT CHANGE THE CURVATURE FLOW

The products q_M d^+d^- and d^+d^- q_M vanish. These are analytic
identities, so the two terms commute on the complex strip. Since
the exterior derivative d_1 kills d^+,

 d_1 exp(-T R^2 q_M)=d_1 exp(-T R^2 G_M).                  (3.1)

This can be checked termwise in the entire matrix exponential. There
is no inverse of lambda_c or Pi_T in (3.1). The physical version has
d_eta=R d_1. Consequently all curvature smoothing estimates may use
the uniformly elliptic G_M while the actual drift remains q_M on
the physical quotient.

4. THE CORRECT BETWEEN-STAGE MINIMUM HAS A UNIFORM LOW NORM

Use three lattices of spacings 1/(MR), 1/R, and 1. Physical averages
obey Q_R Q_M=Q_(MR), with the literal nested straight-string convention.
One direct verification partitions the start-point average into M
subblocks and telescopes each long directional string into R strings;
the linear average sums every subblock start with its same normalized
weight. This statement uses flat linear averages, not nonlinear
path-ordered products.

Let H_(MR) be the explicit fine minimizing extension to final coarse
data from flat_minimizer_locality_research.md. Set

                     H_(M,R)=Q_M H_(MR).                   (4.1)

Nested minimization proves that H_(M,R) is an energy-minimizing lift
for the old precision q_M at spacing 1/R with prescribed Q_R data.
Indeed minimizing first at fixed Q_M A and then over its Q_R constraint
is the same infimum as imposing Q_(MR) A=B. The gauge representative in
(4.1) need not be the orthogonal conditional-mean gauge; its curvature
is the same as that of any Gaussian conditional minimizer.

Physical straight averaging is a contraction up to a fixed geometric
constant in volume-normalized L2: apply Jensen to its normalized
box/string averages and count the bounded translation multiplicity.
The aliases of H_(MR) already have a uniform L2 bound on a fixed
complex coarse strip. Q_M reads a neighborhood of physical size O(1/R)
and its normalized weights acquire only exp(C|Im p|/R) on that strip.
It follows that H_(M,R) has a uniform coarse-to-old-cell L2 symbol
bound there, independent of M and R. This needs no high derivative
regularity of the sharp reconstruction.

5. UNIFORM SMOOTHED CURVATURE SYMBOL AND ROOTED CONSEQUENCE

Define the ACTUAL free old-variable evolution at fixed final physical
time T>0 by exp[-T R^2 q_M] and put

                  A_(M,R)=d_eta exp[-T R^2 q_M] H_(M,R).

Use (3.1), then (2.3), and the alias L2 norm from Section4. For each
fixed number k of physical fine differences, Cauchy--Schwarz in aliases

 sum_l (1+|Re p+2pi l|)^{2(k+1)}
                         exp[-2c_T |Re p+2pi l|^2]<C_(k,T).

The bound holds on fixed physical output patches, including collars
crossing coarse-cell faces, on a common complex p strip. Hence contour
shifting gives exponential locality in the coarse INPUT cell, with
every fixed output derivative controlled uniformly in M,R.

This supplies the smoothing input needed by the multileg estimate
from turn8 for the actual successive free measures. In particular

subtraction, are not tied to a wrong stationary measure. A separate

6. EXACT MATCHED STATIONARITY AND LIMITS

On any compatible finite Gaussian gauge/harmonic quotient, the current
measure has covariance g^2 q_M^{-1} in its own link units. Its
Ornstein--Uhlenbeck flow with drift q_M is stationary for precisely
that measure. Under the physical rescaling used here its deterministic
part is exp[-T R^2 q_M]; noise and volume metric are rescaled together.
The conditional Wick identity therefore applies with the ACTUAL
fine and blocked covariances. There is no replacement of q_M by q_1.

This proves the homogeneous free matched-measure smoothing theorem.
It uses neither the unpublished interacting coupling trajectory nor
a claim about the full nonlinear blocked density. Holomorphy, locality
and ellipticity of the interacting full score remain unproved. A
compact active-region version for a nonlocal q_M needs its full
exterior read/tail and boundary smoothing estimates; global Fourier
locality does not make that version finite-range.
