1. The later collar argument supersedes the earlier open margin passage.

sum is <7 beta/4=.35, so f0(d)=4-sum_{d'<=d}c(d') stays in (3.65,4]. The argument

The arithmetic and monotonicity argument are valid on the stated premises.

The real-point lemma I142593ff separately controls crossing plaquettes. Those

They are level-one plaquettes, bounded by .013 alpha00; pinned plaquettes are

This is a real margin, not a claim that fixing raw links fixes their currents.

The collar displacement theorem I141733ff explicitly assumes its response and

In particular its use of an old N^Delta estimate must be replaced, where needed,
by the weak field/current companion estimate, not cited as already repaired.

2. Actual tower gaps bound the raw-bond mask in weak norms.

Let R be the coordinate projection on bonds with both endpoints outside Omega1;
M=I-R. Let ell_j=L^j eta. Raw I111998 fixes the observation stencil as
tildeDelta(y)=y+[-ell_j,ell_j]^4 for y of level j. The tower condition I12714ff is
dist(Omega_{j-1}^c,Omega_j)>Rgap mathfrakM ell_{j-1}.
Fix the harmless dimensionless floor Rgap mathfrakM>3L.

If j>=2, y belongs to Omega_j and Omega_{j-1} is contained in Omega1. Hence
dist(y,Omega1^c)>Rgap mathfrakM ell_j/L>3ell_j. The observation stencil and every
one-fine-step enlargement therefore lie in Omega1. They cannot see a jump of M.
Consequently every observation stencil seeing a mask jump has j<=1 and
ell_j/eta<=L. The same conclusion holds at an outer raw boundary, if the norm is
read only on the prescribed domain and fields have the same fixed extension.

The product formula
  nabla_U(MA)=M nabla_U A+(partial M) transported(A)
now gives N(MA)<=C(1+L)N(A). For the Holder norm with exponent beta, its extra
cost is bounded by C(1+L^(1+beta)): on the fine lattice the shortest nonzero
distance is eta, and only the first two levels see a discontinuous coefficient.
Exponential profile weights change by at most a fixed factor on this one-step
stencil. M is geometric, gauge covariant, and holomorphic (constant) in fields.
It enlarges no coordinate support; its differentiated stencil costs only eta.

This applies directly to the actual window towers: I12735ff gives the stronger
gap dist(square_{j-1}^c,square_j)>=M1 ell_j with M1>=8. General admissible towers
can use a once-for-all larger propagator gap R1 satisfying R1 mathfrakM>3L.
The qualification is eligibility: a graded family with neighboring levels
differing by one is not itself a proof of this tower gap. Each glued/predecessor
family must genuinely satisfy the admitted tower condition. No new height-
dependent or volume-dependent constant is introduced by the mask once it does.

At the all-ones decoupling point, Q has identity rows on raw bonds. For the
constrained symmetric propagator T, QT=0 implies RT=TR=0, so MTM=T. The raw
part of the exact lift H is the fixed raw datum. Thus a masked decorated
extension MT_sigma M and an exactly restored raw lift can preserve the true
operator at sigma=1 and retain the above weak estimates. This alone does not
prove that every nonlinear coefficient/decorated branch has been replaced in
a compatible way; it is the norm and endpoint part of that construction.

3. A frozen plaquette-star collar is not an identity-preserving free option.

Raw link equality does not freeze raw current. At a flat background, take a
Cartan-valued field with H_1(0)=0 and only H_2(eta e1)=hT nonzero locally.
Then (D*D H)_1(0)=hT/eta^2. The nonlinear Wilson current has the corresponding
sine term. Thus projecting H to zero on raw bonds does not justify independently
freezing J there at the physical endpoint.

The obstruction is already variational. In a transverse Abelian strip, let
A_2(x)=a_x T, a_0=a_{L+1}=0, and constrain the block mean L^-1 sum_{x=1}^L a_x=b.
The Maxwell action is proportional to sum_{x=0}^L(a_{x+1}-a_x)^2. Its unique
minimizer is a_x=6b x(L+1-x)/[(L+1)(L+2)], so a_1 is nonzero for b!=0.
The ordinary straight-path block average reduces to this mean in this commuting
transverse sector. Freezing the adjacent layer imposes an additional Dirichlet
condition and changes the minimization problem; small Wilson fields retain the
nonzero linear response by analytic perturbation. This illustrates the local
failure of the proposed inference; it is not a new global source construction.

The four completion layers are defined on the global chain (I73545ff), not on
strict interior supports. Their primitive data have local supports, while their
responses have tails. Shrinking every partition-of-unity cutoff to vanish on a
raw plaquette star loses the full sum=1 representation there unless the response
already vanishes there. No statement read establishes that stronger vanishing.
The dated-margin route avoids this unsupported alteration of the kernels and
their normalizations.

4. The forced chart must be substituted selectively.

P-stage and ordinary T-stage charts retain f=0 and block datum b. Their zero-b
response is zero, so the original localization at the stage datum and its dated
allowances are compatible with a weak current companion. Do NOT force these
maps with the unrelated independent background-current slot J.

The completion s=3 map is different. Its primitive forcing is calJ3, and its
potential datum is j3=T0_sigma calJ3 (Raw I73108--73140,77062,62585,62671).
The mixed budget already explicitly charges both block data and
Ksharp sup ell^3|calJ3|. In the forced chart the equation is
  P=H0 b+G[-calJ3+Theta H0b-V'(P)],
where G=(I-T0 Theta)^-1 T0 at sigma=1; thus the resummed affine term is -G calJ3.
Replacing this by -T0 calJ3 AFTER resummation would be incorrect.
The source's positive argument label G''J in CMP122-II(1.54) is an argument
convention, not a plus sign in the Euler equation; the latter has -J forcing.

The bounded companion contains the additional term -P0_sigma^T calJ3. Both its
weighted norm and its derivative kernels are bounded in the same mixed forcing
norm as the forced chart. This adds a fixed constant, not a new scale loss.
I142530--142552 already gives the off-component completion estimate in precisely
this mixed norm. Consequently its shape persists:
  Delta_a(y)/r_a(0)<=C_new sqrt(1+k) exp(-c R_k) exp(-c(k-1))
at a surviving raw collar point. Spend a fixed part of the exponential to bound
the polynomial, then enlarge K_J and K_W^0 once. The prescribed gamma ceiling
16 Pbar^0<=beta theta_S again yields the completion allowance.
The choice order is geometry and primitive constants first, these finitely many
new constants next, radius prefactors next, and the gamma ceiling last.

For a generic forced chart H(f,0) is not zero. The selective reading above is
essential; a blanket f=J substitution would invalidate block-only stage budgets.

5. Deep-core current suppression also needs only the weak companion.

Use the actual core data of Raw I4.22: on D2 the presented background equals the
real constrained minimizer U2 and its current equals J2 on the full current
stencil. In the deep block union D2circ, Q'' agrees with Q_{B2}; criticality gives

  f=f_out+r+Q''*lambda_D,
where r is confined to the fixed boundary collar and bounded by C_Q times the
original current. Set A_sigma=P0_sigma^T. Its exact all-ones identity is
A_1 Q''*=0. Therefore
  A_sigma f=A_sigma(f_out+r)+(A_sigma-A_1)Q''*lambda_D.
This is the same source decomposition as for G_sigma f. Every walk contributing
to A_sigma-A_1 must visit a decorated cube outside Lambda; it travels from the
deep core through the exempt region. The order-zero walk bound gives exponential
smallness there. No derivative or Laplacian of a forcing or propagator is used.

The remaining companion terms are bounded local operators or localized products
applied to P,H,X. Weighted Picard estimates propagate the same profile, after a
fixed rate reduction. The block datum b3 is localized at the source cut, and b4

The identity at sigma=1 is used only at sigma=1; it is not asserted after

6. Actual raw-collar geometry, and a weaker sufficient raw-rider repair.

This section supersedes the geometric question at the end of section 5. It
does NOT assert the stronger phi_flat/16 conclusion for arbitrary positive-level
or finer-birth consumers in I138384. The distinction is essential.

(a) Actual domains and complete stencils.
For a completing component C, h>=1 and Omega''1 cap C=Omega1 cap C. Thus
  Col(C)=(Omega1^~\Omega1) cap C subset Z1 cap C subset Zh cap C.
The actual prepared sequence, I53745--53820, gives
  Zh subset Z''h+1 subset ... subset Z''k subset Lambda.
Hence Col(C) subset Lambda, and in particular subset
  D2=(Z'h)^~2 cap Lambda.
This is stronger than simply assuming Col(C) subset Z'h.

The source's eight-minus-five-minus-two count, I91991--92023 (CMP119(3.2)--
(3.5), CMP122-I(1.11), p195), puts one full layer of side
  a=MR_{h+1} L^{h+1} eta
between Z'h and (Omega''h+1)^~2. The cut S=partial(Omega''h+1)^~ lies beyond
that layer, and the B_S of I138361 is literally a subset of S. Therefore the
collar avoids B_S. The same statement holds for its full finite current
stencils and its index-zero cubes: an index-zero cube has side <=10M eta,
and the union with the adjacent current stencils has Chebyshev reach at most
(10M+2)eta from a point in it. As h>=1 and R_{h+1}>=1,
a>=L^2 M eta. Thus L^2>12 suffices in the Chebyshev convention. If converting
the cube's diameter to Euclidean units first, use the sufficient bound
L^2>12 sqrt(d), with d=4. The usual odd choice L>=7 satisfies both. An extra
fixed cutoff or joined-set boundary collar of size O(M1 L^h eta) is also paid
inside this layer by the source fixed floor M/M1 and the large R_{h+1}; this
floor is imposed before the coupling ceiling. No fine-regulator loss occurs.

The joined-set formula CMP119(2.14) is
  B union Bj(Omega)
    =(B cap Omega^~-2) union (Bj(Omega) cap (Omega\Omega^~-2)).
Apply it to B2=B''k union Bh((Omega''h+1)^~c), CMP122-II(1.52).
On the collar and the above stencils/cubes B2 is exactly B''k, with exactly
the same low-level datum V''. As the preparation changes only levels >h,
the level-zero datum is V0. In particular every pinned plaquette of U2 is
the identical pinned plaquette of V0. This argument does NOT raise a raw
point to scale h+1 and does not pretend that rough retained data are smoothed.

(b) The direction of the map and the sufficient threshold.

  Col(C)=St(d^t)\St(d'),   d^t<d',   B_{d^t}=B''k.

(c) Controlling the base without a new weighted-current theorem for U2.

More economically, the existing real chart identity supplies a comparison.
Let Uprep be the actual prepared real carrier at any real parameter point of

  Uprep=exp(i eta H4^real) exp(i eta H3^real) U2.
The identity is used at all-ones decoupling and real data only. Its arbitrary

Uprep has the original pinned bound epsilon0<=.04 alpha00 and the actual

(e) Scope and exact input accounting.
The above closes the raw-rider comparison and its index-zero cube extension

(f) Uniform normalized size of the deep profile on these raw collars.
The scaled distance is the distance of the admitted graded determining set,
not unweighted Euclidean distance. Write Splus for the actual source collar
in I92473: the (2M1+3 blocks)-neighborhood of (Omega''h+1)^~2.
The eight-minus-five-minus-two construction, with the fixed M/M1 floor,

between Omega1,...,Omega_h. The admitted tower separation charges a fixed
positive amount c0 to each crossing in d_sc. It is immaterial that c0 may
be as small as a fixed multiple of M1/M: all geometry is fixed first.
The precise current-tower statements are I63585--63605 and I137105--137133:
consecutive levels, at least one own-scale M-cube in every stratum, and
d^wedge>=w_M(|n-ell|-1)^+. The latter includes admitted frozen/thin strata.
Thus, after enlarging the starting point to its current stencil or its

  d_sc(neighborhood(y),Splus)>=c0(h-1)-C0.
No claim of the stronger thick-shell age estimate for general pointwise law

Independently, the one remaining layer has scaled width at least checkR_k

  d_sc(neighborhood(y),Splus)>=checkR_k-C1.
