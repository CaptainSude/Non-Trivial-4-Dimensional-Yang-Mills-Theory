1. WHY A MINIMUM COMPARISON ALONE DOES NOT MODIFY THE LARGE LAW

Suppose an actual admitted local physical minimum U_act and a fresh
auxiliary minimum U_aux have identical determining data on a complete
inner region, with common old array, regularity/current caps and a
permitted common relative gauge. The existing common-shell theorem
then supplies, on the inner source read P,

 |jet(U_act)-jet(U_aux)|_P <=C epsilon exp(-mu d),           (1)

where d is distance to their first changed COMPLETE determining row.
Its proof permits arbitrary distant rough fixed rows and does not
require a globally near-identity potential. It does require BOTH
endpoints to be actual admitted minima; common complete ordinary,
incident, current and return data are part of that premise.

If the auxiliary coarse datum agrees with the original only on a
radius-n patch and is identity outside, the first changed row lies
at that patch boundary. The available d in (1) is O(n), even if the
original active fluctuation box has radius D>>n. A large physical box
does not move the first changed determining datum farther away.

There are three further gaps in the proposed direct substitution.

(a) Equation(1) is a comparison of minima and their specified jets.
It is not a comparison of the conditional measures exp(-H_act)/Z_act
and exp(-H_aux)/Z_aux with their actual earlier-mask domains. Their
current, gauge, pivot, Haar and original outer baseline channels all
remain. A source mean of a fluctuation law is not its source evaluated
at the stationary configuration.

(b) Zeroing coarse data just beyond the small patch creates a new
background there. If this lies INSIDE the large active action read,
it is not a dummy far row removable by the Dirichlet convention in
Section4A of the common-shell theorem. The exact local action-
difference equality in the remote-defect module uses agreement on
the WHOLE active/guard determining read W. It is false as an exact
identity when those data are changed inside that read.

(c) In a two-jet family varying the inner coarse data with arbitrary
colored outer coarse data held, constant-color invariance means
simultaneous rotation of both sets. It does NOT force the inner
derivative to vanish at inner theta=0 with the outer data nonzero.
The new flat-jet argument used JOINT flatness of the complete finite
auxiliary datum. One would need to bound the outer-induced linear
jet as well as the normalized law response.

A sufficient new comparison theorem would control the ACTUAL first
mean and raw pair under changed coarse determining data outside the
small patch by, for example,

 |m_act(F)-m_aux(F)|
       <=alpha_p P(g0^-1,n,D) exp(-mu n),                   (2)

uniformly on the entire original guarded earlier-mask law, including
the direct reconstructed source, normalizer, mask changes and all
current/frame terms. For source two-jets it must also control the
corresponding central first/second derivatives or the outer-induced
linear jet. Neither (1) nor the already proved retained-NOISE boundary
comparison supplies (2): that comparison holds the complete coarse
reference fixed. A complete relative-background containing-Hessian
and moving-mask score theorem would be a substantive extra input.

5. WHAT exp(-c r) CAN PAY, AND WHAT IT CANNOT

Write t=log(1/g0), r=K t^p with K fixed. For every fixed M,

        g0^(-M) exp(-c r)=exp[M t-cK t^p].                 (14)

It tends to zero faster than any fixed coupling power when p>1.
For p=1 it pays only powers below the fixed rate cK; for p<1 it
pays no positive fixed inverse-coupling power asymptotically. The
weaker registered p>1/2 condition alone does not imply the first
claim. No exponent is changed here to make (14) favorable.

For a requested age h=L^(-s), the boundary part of (7) needs
n>=C(s+r) to be exp(-c r) h^4. The range in which (11) proves a
rare patch is n<=c R_b=O(r). These two requirements are compatible
only for an O(r) age window (with constants paid). At s>>r, increasing
n to suppress the boundary makes (11) uninformative. It is not a
lower bound on the actual patch failure probability; it is a precise
limitation of this pair of estimates. The earlier exp(-c r^2) h^4
target would require n already O(r^2) at age zero and is outside
the useful range of (11).

The once-born per-root bound (13) does NOT by itself sum the original
microscopic labels at fixed physical volume. The unchanged source is
c_a sum_p f_p h_p with NO extra a^4. A bounded profile therefore has
an absolute root count of order a^(-4), not a current-cell count.
On an asymptotically free trajectory log(1/a) is of order g0^(-2),
whereas r is only polylogarithmic in g0^(-1). Thus the available bound
a^(-4) poly(g0,r) exp(-c r) is not uniform. Even exp(-c r^2) alone
would not pay that count. A matched dimension-four/current-image
gain, source cancellation, or an actual later owner/age payment is
still required. Formula(13) is a useful rare initial column, not the
missing all-scale original-source norm.
