6. Holomorphy

For a finite prefix and finite lattice, a descending composition of
the chosen holomorphic responses is holomorphic whenever each image
has a strict margin in the next admitted domain. The original finite
closed real noise product and closed complex localizer polydiscs are
compact, so strict uniform margins give an open complex neighborhood
of the full finite parameter product. No limiting argument is needed.

This does not provide a uniform Euclidean radius for arbitrarily many
source coordinates. Such a statement requires a source synthesis
norm. Nor does it imply nonvanishing of an integrated denominator,
bounded normalized complex reads, or finite-prefix carrier counting.
