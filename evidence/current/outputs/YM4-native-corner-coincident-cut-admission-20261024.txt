RESULT

pre-Q1 fiber with a codimension-two active-box corner and two distinct
P0 plaquettes whose cutoff functions depend on the SAME single active
free bond. Their cutoff spheres coincide at the chosen retained field
and split under an arbitrarily small retained free-bond variation.
All other original fine-curvature and gauge cuts can be made strict.

The construction below uses the actual admissible analytic group average,
not a replacement arithmetic averaging map. The initial coarse value is
chosen to be the actual coarse average of the constructed field, which
is I+O_L(epsilon0^2), and is then held fixed. Thus its smallness fits even
the stricter O(delta0) coarse box at the weak floor. Subsequent variations
use the actual local implicit pivot solution at that fixed coarse value.

This establishes a native obstruction to a uniform pairwise active
submersion/transversality premise. A conclusion about the differentiability
of the entire unlocalized earlier-mask integral additionally needs its
own analysis of all boundary terms; this note does not replace that
analysis by a generic two-ball model.

1. GEOMETRY AND AVOIDING THE ACTUAL PRIVATE PIVOTS

Let L=2m+1 with m>=3, as permitted by the native fixed-large-L choice.
Take a corner constraint block B={-m,...,m}^4 inside the active vertex
box C. The upper faces of C in directions1 and2 end at x1=m,x2=m.
C is a union of full constraint blocks, buffered in the other directions
as in the actual guard construction. All fields introduced below are
on internal links of B; the physical retained FREE links start at I.

Choose k in {0,...,m-1} and h in {-m+1,...,m-1}. The positive corner
tangential free bond is

       b_+ : (m,m,k,h) -> (m,m,k+1,h),

and let b_- be the corresponding direction3 bond at x1=-m. They are
intra-block links and hence are not private crossing pivots. The two
outward plaquettes p_1,p_2 at b_+ go in directions+1 and+2. Their other
three edges are retained; the two normal crossing edges per plaquette
must also be chosen not to be designated pivots.

Impose the same avoidance for the outward direction-1 and direction+2
plaquettes at b_-. Only three coarse interfaces can be involved:
the +/-1 interfaces of B and its +2 interface. There is exactly one
designated pivot on each. For a fixed interface, a pivot can lie on
one of these crossing edges for at most two candidate pairs (k,h),
because its direction3 coordinate must be k or k+1 and its direction4
coordinate must equal h. On the +2 interface it has at most one of
the transverse positions x1=+m,-m. Thus at most six candidate pairs
are forbidden. There are m(2m-1)>=15 choices. Choose any remaining pair.

This uses the actual pivots wherever they are placed, not a presumed
central-face convention. Avoidance of all four initially saturated
plaquettes will matter when the negative one is moved inward below.

4. REMOVE THE OTHER SATURATION USING THE ACTUAL PIVOT SOLUTION

Now hold V^0 fixed. Reduce the magnitude of the negative bond b_-
