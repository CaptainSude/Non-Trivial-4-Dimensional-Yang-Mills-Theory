5. FINITE COST CONSEQUENCES

For a proved delay t<=T log x and uniform (1),

                 N_t <= K0^t N0 <= x^(T log K0) N0.    (3)

The original L^(4t) positional census is included in K0^t and must
not be charged again. A rare exp[-c(log x)^beta] birth absorbs this
polynomial price when beta>1. Thus the stronger beta>2 threshold
was needed only for the previously conservative exp(C log^2 x)
bound, not for the present uniform value replay.

A power birth x^-p becomes at best x^(-p+T log K0), with its original
amplitude and strong support norm retained. This may be useful for
high-power running tags, but one must choose p after registering K0,
the delay T, any later feedback power, and the native parameter order.
No arbitrary fixed power absorbs an exp(C log^2 x) loss; (3) is the
substantive improvement. Future actual-kernel transport and birth
domain admission are not implied by this numerical observation.
