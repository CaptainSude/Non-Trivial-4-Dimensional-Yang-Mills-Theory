9. REAL SOURCE-ZERO CONTINUATION ON A WHOLE PRODUCT, WITH MASKS RETAINED

original free-noise balls, if the terminal curvature cap and physical

It is not a proof that those indicators are one on this product.

 kappa(W)=sup_p ||W(partial p)-1||,
