DIAGONAL SLAB ARRAYS BOUNDED BY COORDINATE CHESSBOARD
18 October 2026. Fixed-UV pressure consumer, then uniform insertion bound.

This proves the diagonal-chain version of rotation_slab_operator_consumer
(2.2) using ONLY the original coordinate-torus source producer. No source

1. PACKET AND LATTICE GEOMETRY

Use planar coordinates

 u=(t+x)/sqrt(2),   v=(t-x)/sqrt(2),

and transverse coordinates y,z. Let the packet profile be
phi_delta(u,v) f(y,z), where phi_delta=delta^-2 phi(./delta), the C1
shape phi is supported in a fixed square, and f is compact with fixed
C1 norm/support. Reflected or conjugate profiles have the same bounds.
Take 0<delta<=delta_0<=1. First work at bare mesh epsilon much smaller
than delta. Choose b an EVEN integer multiple of epsilon such that

 C_0 delta <= b <= (C_0+1)delta,
 Delta=sqrt(2)b,                                      (1)

with C_0 larger than a fixed multiple of the planar support radius.
The harmless rounded upper bound follows when epsilon<<delta. The
total slab padding Delta is O(delta), uniformly in epsilon.

Put m packet centers at u_l=(l+1/2)Delta, v_l=0,
l=0,...,m-1. In coordinate variables these are

 (t_l,x_l)=(b(l+1/2),b(l+1/2)).                        (2)

Choose fixed transverse intervals J_y,J_z, with integer-mesh lengths
B_y,B_z, containing supp f with positive fixed buffers. In the ordinary
COORDINATE rectangular grid of widths (b,b,B_y,B_z), packet l lies
strictly inside the cell

 C_l=[lb,(l+1)b] x [lb,(l+1)b] x J_y x J_z.            (3)

Its planar support occupies only a fixed small fraction of that cell.
These are m DISTINCT coordinate cells. On the entire closed cell C_l,

                  l Delta <= u <= (l+1)Delta.         (4)

This exact range is the endpoint separation mechanism. Any endpoint
observable whose complete support is in u<0 or u>m Delta reads no
variable in any array cell. Its transverse size is unrestricted.
In particular an OS polynomial translated to beyond the last slab,
and its reflected partner before the first slab, have this property
when their original supports are compact in the strict halfspace.

The usual doubling array can be chosen with packet centers at the slab
midpoints, so it has precisely (2). Diagonal reflection in u=l Delta
is an affine signed coordinate permutation with mesh-integral shift,
because b/epsilon is integral. It preserves the scalar lattice Wilson
observable and its bare sampling. Coordinate reflections used to
disseminate (3) need not preserve the packet's diagonal orientation;
the resulting orientations have the same support/C1 bounds.

2. ONE COMPLETE COORDINATE FOLD OF ONE PACKET

On a finite original C torus let

 d_epsilon,T(z;phi_l)=log mu_epsilon,T(exp[z O(phi_l)]),
 d_epsilon,T(0;phi_l)=0.                               (5)

Use that torus's OWN exact singleton normalization. The source identity
mu(exp[zO])=exp(E_UV(zphi_l)) h_l(z) and the fixed-support h bound give
|h_l(z)-1|<=1/2 on |z|<c delta^3, uniformly in position, cutoff and
sufficiently large torus. Thus (5) has the required analytic branch.
The branch respects exact bare translations, coordinate signed
permutations and conjugation. These covariance statements use invariance,
not reflection positivity of a diagonal finite-torus cut.

Let rho_delta=c_1 delta^(7/2), with c_1 fixed sufficiently small. Consider
the normalized single-cell function

 F_l=exp[z_l O(phi_l)-d_epsilon,T(z_l;phi_l)],
 |z_l|<=rho_delta.                                    (6)

Its complete coordinate chessboard dissemination contains N_cells
translated/reflected/conjugated packets. All their FULL supports are
separated by c delta: the planar cell buffers supply that distance in
t,x, and the transverse cell buffers supply a fixed distance in y,z.
Disjointness implies that the local source norm of the entire array is
the norm of one packet, up to a fixed constant; it does not carry a
factor N_cells. On a source scale of endpoint length a it is bounded by

 |z| M_a,  M_a=max(delta^-2,sqrt(a)delta^-3).            (7)

This lies in the admitted source class at |z|<=rho_delta. The square-
root scale factor is the actual W_j Lipschitz allowance; it is not
replaced by an unsourced stronger regularity bound.

Use the SAME local e_j(X;w) for the total array and for each singleton.
Subtracting all singleton values makes every coefficient reading at most
one packet cancel exactly. For a carrier meeting r_X>=2 packets, subtract
the linear Taylor term and use the reserved local source disc. The full
array's quadratic remainder is bounded by C |z|^2 M_a^2; the sum of its
r_X singleton remainders is bounded by C r_X |z|^2 M_a^2. Charge r_X to
the packets met, retain the tree crossing gain exp(-c delta/a), and use
the planar scale sum already proved in source-gradient Section17. It gives

 |E_UV(total)-sum_l E_UV(single_l)|
            <= C_f N_cells |z|^2 delta^-7
            <= C_f N_cells.                          (8)

This is an all-orders analytic remainder bound, not a truncation of the
source expansion. Carriers reading one packet cancel by locality even
when their fixed-scaffold coefficients vary with the packet position.
No small-grid covariance of those coefficients is asserted.

For each clone E_UV(single)-d(single)=-log h_single is bounded uniformly
on rho_delta. The full source stability bound gives
|h_total|<=exp(C V_stop). Therefore

 |mu_epsilon,T(Fold F_l)|
 <=exp[C_f N_cells+C V_stop].                         (9)

The physical final volume is exactly N_cells times the fine rectangular
cell volume b^2 B_y B_z. The latter is bounded by a constant depending
only on f, and is actually O_f(delta^2). Taking the chessboard root gives

                  ||F_l||_CB <= exp(C_f),             (10)

uniformly in delta, epsilon, l and the total number of cells. The full
fold is nonnegative by coordinate RP, but the estimate(9) used its
modulus and so applies to the required complex sources.

3. THE CHAIN AND ARBITRARY ENDPOINT POLYNOMIALS

Coordinate closed-cell chessboard Holder bounds the product of the m
distinct array-cell functions by the product of their norms(10), hence
by exp(C_f m). This uses only coordinate RP on the original C torus.
There is no enumeration of signs or array-cell patterns and no factor
depending on the number of possible empty cells.

Let A and its reflected/translated endpoint partner be finite polynomials
in local plaquette observables, supported beyond u=m Delta and before
u=0. At fixed epsilon, expand each into finitely many products of link
matrix entries. Assign each link to any closed coordinate cell containing
it, and group the factors by cells. By(4), these endpoint factors are
in cells DIFFERENT from all m array cells. Nongauge-invariant matrix-entry
factors and links on coordinate cell faces are allowed: coordinate
site-plane RP is the conditional square with the mirror links retained,
and its chessboard inequality applies to the closed-cell algebras.

Each endpoint-cell norm is at most its supremum norm. The number and
total coefficient size of the resulting products depend on epsilon,A,
delta, but not m. Translation of the positive endpoint by m Delta is
the mesh translation (mb,mb), so it changes neither this bound nor the
number of occupied endpoint cells. Thus

 |mu_epsilon,T[(product_l F_l) A_left A_right]|
                   <=C_(epsilon,A,delta) exp(C_f m).  (11)

The coefficient C_f is INDEPENDENT of A. This is the crucial operator-
norm requirement. If endpoint generating functions are preferred, their
ordinary Taylor series in finitely many fixed-UV plaquette variables
has an absolutely summable matrix-entry expansion on any fixed compact
source polydisc. The same argument yields(11) with endpoint exponentials
and a finite prefactor depending on that polydisc. No source-RG estimate
for the endpoint profiles is needed, and no absolute value or real-part
replacement is made inside a normalized array factor.

4. COMMENSURABILITY AND ORDER OF LIMITS

Take cubic side lengths which are even common multiples of b,B_y,B_z
and all required RG periods. The initial free integer N_T permits this.
A single sequence with free factor divisible by 2 k! at its kth member
eventually works for every fixed integer cell length and every fixed m.
Choose one common thermodynamic subsequence of states and local vacuum
coefficients. At fixed epsilon local observables are bounded, so their
MGFs converge uniformly on compact source sets; the singleton branch
(5) and the uniform bounds pass to the SAME state mu_epsilon.

The older two-frame fold geometry also permits these tori: for n=2a
it only needs fixed odd h,H,b_0 to divide a. If a=N_T ell^M, this holds
eventually. Its counts (2A,8A,4A,4A), A=a/b_0, remain even whether A
is odd or even. No use of odd a is necessary in the twisted-trace
comparison. Thus commensurability does not force a different family
when applying the separately audited same-state diagonal-RP argument.

First take the thermodynamic limit for each fixed epsilon. Only then
let m=2^r tend to infinity in the reflection doubling argument. Only
after the uniform operator bound is established is epsilon removed.
The endpoint prefactor in(11) disappears under the 2^-r root at fixed
epsilon. No uniform-in-epsilon endpoint norm is required.

5. CONSEQUENCE WHEN THE SAME STATE HAS DIAGONAL RP

If mu_epsilon has diagonal RP and translation invariance in the u
direction, the exact reflection-doubling identities in
rotation_slab_operator_consumer Section3 apply to(11). They yield a
bounded holomorphic normalized slab insertion for |z|<=rho_delta.
Cauchy at zero gives the centered linear insertion estimate

 ||B_delta(phi_delta f)|| <= C_f delta^-7/2.           (12)

The centering is the derivative of the exact d in(5). All self-source
counterterms were retained until that derivative. An arbitrary C1
planar alpha supported in a square of radius delta fits the same
geometry after increasing C_0 once. Normalize it as
alpha=c_alpha delta^-2 phi(./delta), with

 |c_alpha|<=C delta^2(||alpha||_infty+delta||grad alpha||_infty),

and a uniformly bounded C1 shape. By linearity(12) gives

 ||B_delta(alpha f)||
 <=C_f delta^(2-7/2)
       (||alpha||_infty+delta||grad alpha||_infty).     (13)

Its total padding is O(delta). This covers the small mollifiers needed
by the planar point-insertion lemma. The other diagonal orientation
uses u=(t-x)/sqrt(2) and the same coordinate cells with one index
reversed; all counts and endpoint separation statements are unchanged.
