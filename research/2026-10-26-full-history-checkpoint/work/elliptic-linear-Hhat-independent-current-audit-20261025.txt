ELLIPTIC LINEAR Hhat WITH AN INDEPENDENT CURRENT
25 October 2026. Scoped proof and audit of the proposed linear repair.

VERDICT: The proposed linear construction is valid on a reserved native
elliptic chart with sufficiently small independent current. It gives a
jointly holomorphic, covariant right inverse of the linearized average,
with the required potential, first derivative, P1 and covariant-Laplacian
bounds. It agrees with the physical linear Landau minimizer on the physical
subfamily. It does not assert the Landau condition off that subfamily.
Its localized version retains strong estimates and covariance, but does
not automatically retain the right-inverse identity away from s=1.
No nonlinear reconstruction or original scalar-law theorem is proved here.

All spaces are finite-dimensional native lattice spaces with the stated
boundary conditions and complete determining reads. Use the trace-bilinear
pairing and its transpose, denoted T. At real fields this agrees with the
usual real adjoint. At complex fields it is NOT Hermitian conjugation.

Write Q for the correctly normalized native linearized constraint, so
the desired constraint is QA=B. If the paper's unscaled Q_j is used,

normalization is fixed throughout; it is not changed between formulas.

Let

  Delta_a=Delta+DRD*+Q^T a Q,
  G0=Delta_a^-1,
  KQ=(Q G0 Q^T)^-1,
  H0=G0 Q^T KQ,
  GQ=G0-G0 Q^T KQ Q G0.                            (1)

The letter Delta in Delta_a denotes the Hessian operator used by B102;
Delta_U denotes the componentwise covariant Laplacian. They are distinct.
The native elliptic chart supplies the inverses in(1), their analyticity,

whose Laplacian estimates are expressly excepted in B99 Theorem3.12.

Exact algebra gives

  QH0=I, QGQ=0,
  GQ Delta_a Y=Y-H0 QY.                            (2)

2. AN EXPLICIT OPERATOR FOR THE PROPOSED BILINEAR FORM

For a site field lambda define the endpoint-sum operator

  (E_U lambda)(b)=lambda(b_-)+Ad_(U(b))lambda(b_+),
  T_U=E_U G' R D*.

Define S_J on arbitrary bond fields Y by

  2<A,S_JY>=<i[T_U A,Y],J>.                         (3)

The commutator and pairing on the right are summed over the native bonds.
Cyclicity of trace gives the exact factorization

  S_JY=(i/2)T_U^T[Y,J]
      =(i/2)D R G' E_U^T[Y,J].                     (4)

The second equality uses the analytically continued symmetric operators
R and G'. Formula(4), or the first equality without symmetry notation,
fixes the sign and factor1/2. It also shows why no derivative of J is
required: D acts AFTER R G', not on the product [Y,J].

3. SMALLNESS IN NATIVE NORMS

For transparency use B99's weighted sup norms |.|_(alpha), where a field
of norm1 behaves like (L^j eta)^alpha on the jth region. Let

  X0: |Y|_(-1),       F: |F|_(-3),
  gamma=|J|_(-3).

The product commutator obeys

  |[Y,J]|_(-4)<=C gamma |Y|_(-1).                   (5)

The endpoint-sum transpose preserves this weighted size up to a fixed
native constant. The global bounds(3.47) give D G' from weight-4 to
weight-3. The identity

  D R G'=D G'-D P G'

and the DP kernel bound(3.49) give the same estimate for D R G'. Thus

  |S_JY|_(-3)<=C_S gamma |Y|_(-1).                  (6)

This derivation uses only the compact-reference complex-C1 chart and
the independent sup/weighted current allowance. It does not assume
that J is a physical divergence, or assume a bound on a derivative of J.
In the homogeneous unit-block normalization, these are ordinary sup
norm bounds with the physical scale factors already absorbed.

Define the strong output norm

  ||Y||_X=max(|Y|_(-1),|nabla_U Y|_(-2),
              |Delta_U Y|_(-3),|P1 Y|_(-3)).

The elliptic G0 estimates, B102(129),(130), and the bounded constraint
maps imply

  ||H0 B||_X<=C_H ||B||_coarse,
  ||GQ F||_X<=C_G |F|_(-3).                        (7)

Indeed GQ=G0-H0 QG0, so its Laplacian estimate follows from the strong
leftmost G0/H0 estimates. The P1 estimate follows from the bounded
DPD* kernel(3.49), with the native P1 convention registered. This is an
elliptic estimate, not a claim about the excepted gauge-completed G1.

The constants in(6),(7) are uniform in depth and volume on the prescribed
native geometry and fixed strict chart. The underlying B99/B102 estimates
are published inputs; this note does not reprove their kernel theorems.

4. EXISTENCE, STRONG BOUNDS AND THE CONSTRAINT

Assume C_G C_S gamma<=rho<1, with a strict fixed rho, for example rho=1/2.
Then GQ S_J is a contraction on X0. Define

  Hhat=(I-GQ S_J)^-1 H0
       =sum_(n>=0)(GQ S_J)^n H0.                   (8)

The series converges in operator norm and defines the unique solution of

  Y=H0 B+GQ S_JY.                                 (9)

The weak bound is |Y|_(-1)<=C_H(1-rho)^-1||B||.
Use(9),(6),(7) once more to obtain

  ||Hhat B||_X
     <=C_H||B||+C_G C_S gamma C_H(1-rho)^-1||B||.  (10)

Thus contraction need only be proved in the weak norm; the strong norms
come from the left elliptic factor GQ. This avoids differentiating J.
Applying Q to(9) and using(2) gives

  Q Hhat B=B                                      (11)

for every independent pair in this unweakened elliptic chart. Joint
holomorphy in U,J follows by uniform convergence on slightly enlarged
strict charts of analytic finite-dimensional operator functions.

5. EXACT PHYSICAL MATCHING

Let H be the original physical LINEAR Landau minimizer, with physical
background current J and RD*H=0. B102 printed299,(138), states exactly

  Delta'_pi H=S_JH.                               (12)

The constrained equation for the original minimizer, with
Delta_pi=Delta-Delta'_pi, implies

  Delta_a HB=Q^T lambda+S_J HB,
  QHB=B,                                         (13)

where lambda includes the harmless constraint penalty contribution.
This is also read directly from its B99(3.126) constrained-Green formula
and RD*H=0. Multiply(13) by GQ and use(2):

  HB=H0 B+GQ S_J HB.

Uniqueness of(9) proves

  Hhat(U,Jphys(U))=H(U).                          (14)

No off-physical Landau or Ward identity was used to define Hhat. In
particular do NOT infer RD*Hhat=0 for arbitrary independent J from(14).
The construction provides a right inverse and the four strong norms;
its off-physical gauge condition is a separate question.

6. COVARIANCE

Let R_f(g) and R_c(g) be the actual fine/coarse endpoint adjoint actions.
The native operators D,G',R,Delta_a,Q intertwine these actions, as in
B99(3.33),(3.34) and ordinary relative-average covariance. Inverses and
products then give the corresponding intertwinings for H0,GQ. The trace
pairing is invariant even for complex g: R_f(g)^T=R_f(g)^-1.

The endpoint sum obeys E_(U^g) R_site(g)=R_f(g) E_U.
The bracket [Y,J] transforms pointwise. Therefore(4) gives

  S_(U^g,Ad_g J) R_f(g)=R_f(g) S_(U,J).

Applying this to every term of(8) proves

  Hhat(U^g,Ad_g J) R_c(g)=R_f(g) Hhat(U,J),         (15)

whenever both sides are in their declared charts. The identity itself
does not enlarge those charts or bound an arbitrary gauge.

7. LOCALIZED WORDS: WHAT IS PRESERVED

Expand the elliptic factors G0,G', the constrained projection and H0
using their native localized kernel representations; use the strong
leftmost G0/H0 bounds in the representations of GQ. The factor S_J is
given by(4): all current multiplications are local, and its remaining
factors are the already controlled D R G' endpoint kernel. Every factor
is covariant, and scalar cutoffs/weakening weights commute with gauge
endpoint actions.

For a fixed complex weakening radius, B116's walk majorants give fixed
inflated constants C_S(kappa),C_G(kappa),C_H(kappa). Choose the independent
current allowance so C_G(kappa)C_S(kappa)gamma<1. Then the analogous
Neumann word sum converges uniformly with the four strong output norms.
One must specify the weighting convention:

(a) Weaken the primitive factors and then sum their products. This gives
    the literal resolvent (I-GQ(s)S_J(s))^-1 H0(s).
(b) Flatten each entire operator word into its complete read, attach one
    weakening factor per encountered cube, and sum the weighted words.
    This gives a different s-family, generally without the factorized
    resolvent formula, but the same s=1 operator. Its absolute majorant
    is bounded by the factorwise majorant for |s|<=exp(kappa).

Both conventions preserve covariance and admit the strong Neumann
estimate when their actual complete words/collars satisfy the native
walk bounds. Do not mix their definitions in a source expansion.

For an explicit decay assertion, fix a rate delta' strictly smaller than
the common native rate delta. Use the corresponding exponentially
weighted kernel norm, including the native cell measures and scale
weights. The native convolution estimates give a fixed algebra constant;
absorb it in the norm. Equations(5)--(7) then give

  ||GQ S_J||_(delta')<=C_(delta') gamma.

Choose gamma so this is below1/2 as well as the earlier sup-norm cap.
Every n-fold word is bounded by a geometric majorant rho^n in this
weighted norm. The sum preserves decay delta', uniformly in volume and
depth. Strong output derivatives act on the LEFTMOST GQ (or H0 for
the zeroth term), for which the elliptic termwise estimates are available.
This gives the same decay for all four output slots. Scalar weakening
weights inflate the fixed word constants before this current cap is
chosen; they do not introduce an unbounded factor per prefix date.

B99 printed416, Theorem3.10, explicitly states its word estimates for
all norms on the left-hand sides of(3.42)--(3.47). This includes the
covariant Laplacian for the elliptic G0 used here. The later exception
for the gauge-completed G,G1 does not apply to this leftmost factor.
For the constrained factors, expand GQ=G0-H0 QG0 and H0=G0 Q^T KQ;
the coarse inverse uses the same localized-inverse construction as
B99(3.132), with the gauge-completion perturbation set to zero. The
strong output estimate always comes from this leftmost elliptic G0;
the remaining factors need only their native bounded/decaying kernels.

CRITICAL LIMIT: generic weakening does not preserve QGQ(s)=0 or
QH0(s)=I. Therefore QHhat_s=B is established at s=1, and for the
unweakened independent-pair operator(8), but is NOT automatic for general
s. This is sufficient for physical unweakened matching in the proposed
prefix map; any stronger localized constraint claim needs its own proof.

8. REMAINING NONLINEAR WORK

The linear repair does not by itself verify a replacement of H inside
B102's nonlinear D equation, V(80), Delta^(2), the corrected seed K,
or the native selected-current continuation. Those formulas use further
identities; off-physical RD*Hhat=0 must not be inserted. A nonlinear
rebuild must check its coefficients, first jet, quadratic remainder,
strong localized estimates, and agreement with the original physical
response separately. All source domains, complete determining images,
scalar factors and normalized denominators remain separate obligations.
