3. ANCHORED ANALYTIC NORMS IMPLY LOCAL CONDITIONAL JET BOUNDS

Write an analytic correction on a finite compatible regulator as

 F(w)=sum_H F_H(w_H),
 sup_I sum_(H contains I) exp(alpha|H|) ||F_H||_a <= K. (3.1)

Each I is a fixed J-cell with D_J scalar coordinates. Fix an active
finite set K of current links, let I_K be the cells it meets, and freeze
all other links to an admitted exterior e. Only carriers meeting I_K
can vary. Their complete sum F_K(x;e), including carriers extending
outside K, has absolute sup at most |I_K| K. Terms independent of x
are part of the conditional normalization, but no x-dependent long
carrier is deleted.

On |x|,|e|<=a/4, Cauchy and finite-dimensional norm conversion give

 ||D_x^r F_K|| <= C_(K,r) K a^(-r), r>=1.             (3.2)

An analytic chart x -> w(x;e) with fixed finite geometry gives the
same estimate on a smaller fixed fraction of a, provided its COMPLETE
range is inside the a product domain. Its constants may depend on K
and the chart, never on the earlier fine refinement.

For a direct row bound there is also no active-volume loss. Scalar
Cauchy gives |partial_i partial_j F_H|<=C a^-2||F_H||.
Sum j inside H first. Connectedness implies
dist(i,j)<=J(|H|+d). With mu=alpha/(2J),

 sup_i sum_j exp(mu dist(i,j)) |D_i D_j F|
 <= C_(J,alpha) K/a^2.                              (3.3)

The extra factor D_J |H| is paid by exp(-alpha|H|/2).
For r additional indices the corresponding polynomial |H|^(r+1)
is paid by the same spare exponent. This proves all fixed-order
rooted derivative bounds, not just a bound on an extensive total.
The contour derivative is taken before the positive carrier sum.

 product_(active links) d_H U_e

6. MATCHED PRECISION, WHITENING AND THE COMPLETE ACTION

exterior links. The exact flat precision is

 P_(M,K)=J_K^T q_(MR) J_K.                            (6.1)

It is the actual blocked Maxwell precision, not the original nearest-
neighbor Maxwell operator. The established uniform transverse

if a field supported in K is curl-free, it is a gradient with a
potential fixed on the connected exterior. Vanishing on the tree

the only such field is zero. Finite-dimensional Poincare on this

 lambda_K I<=P_(M,K)<=Lambda_K I,                    (6.2)

uniformly in M and in any ambient torus large enough to contain the
graph without a wrap. On a periodic active graph harmonic degrees
must instead be fixed or retained explicitly; they are not inverted.

The exact local regular effective action in tree coordinates is

 A_sf(x)=S_(MR)(exp(iJ_K x))
               -g^2 [F_K(J_Kx;1)+J_H(x)].           (6.3)

Put y=P_(M,K)^(1/2)x. The classical attachment turn10 gives

For identity exterior and a semisimple group, its first derivative

The real Hessian is symmetric because (6.3) is the actual real scalar

9. A DIRECT FIXED-BLOCK EXTENSION FOR LARGE RETAINED WINDOWS

There is a useful additional finite-block fact, without a new cutoff.
Fix the number n of retained coordinates and all chart geometry.
Assume the actual physical centered action-plus-gauge function

uniformly positive real retained Hessian at zero. The existing

Choose epsilon small enough that on |z|<=epsilon its Hessian differs

the gap, also in a smaller complex w ball.

Keep any ORIGINAL fixed retained rectangle with T>=1 and gT<=epsilon.

Haar factors and inherited difference E(U_w(gxi))-E(U_w(0)). Suppose

Quadratic coercivity gives, uniformly on a smaller complex w ball,

 Re[(f_w(gxi)-f_w(0))/g^2]>=c|xi|^2.                 (9.1)

The actual Jacobian/Haar factors are bounded above and away from zero

 |E(U_w(gxi))-E(U_w(0))|<=C(K_in g/a)|xi|.            (9.2)

This proves local logarithmic Hessian/tame bounds for this actual
