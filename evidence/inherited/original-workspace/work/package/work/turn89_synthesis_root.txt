1. The new estimate worth pursuing

spatial Wilson-flow time, fixed spatial smear and fixed observable

renormalized coupling. Put n_a a approximately L>0 and define

                  -(1/n_a) sum_(k=n_a)^(2n_a-1) phi_a(k).

The sufficient inequality is now entirely an ordinary original-law
real-source curvature:

 liminf_(a->0) Var_(full Wilson)(G_a)>0.                    TARGET

The full law, its original normalizer, and a common cutoff/volume sequence
are essential. The TARGET is not equal-time variance. It averages over a
fixed physical duration and subtracts the next equally long average.

If TARGET holds, choose v>0 strictly below its liminf, so Var(G_a)>=v
eventually. For n_a>=4/v, the exact finite Wilson
transfer spectrum has positive-pair mass at least v/4 in the physical band

 epsilon=v/(8L),  Lambda=max{2epsilon,8/(Lv)}.

After translating G_a to start at T0>0 and keeping T0+2L below the opposite
reflection plane with a fixed physical buffer, its original reflected
norm is at least

and the norm floor

3. What the exact auxiliary representation contributes

The exact two-Hubbard--Stratonovich joint has the original Wilson marginal
mu and the interacting auxiliary marginal nu. Conditional on the complete
real auxiliary record xi, the original links have independent tilted
Haar laws. Let m_t=E[phi_t|xi] and epsilon_phi=E Var(phi_t|xi).
Different original time slices have disjoint original spatial links, so

 Var_mu G_a = Var_nu[(1/n_a)sum_first m_t-(1/n_a)sum_second m_t]
                                              +2epsilon_phi/n_a,
 0<=2epsilon_phi/n_a<=1/(2n_a).

For every real source s the log partition functions also differ by at
most s^2/(4n_a), with the original one larger. This follows from exact
conditional independence and the bounded-variable exponential estimate;
no auxiliary Gaussian approximation or derivative interchange is used.

Consequently TARGET is equivalent to a positive limiting variance of the
same time-averaged CONDITIONAL MEANS under the full interacting nu.
All independent conditional link noise is paid by the vanishing contact
term. A useful calculation must therefore control this correlated mean
channel. Concentration of the individual conditional link laws is not
a lower bound on it.
