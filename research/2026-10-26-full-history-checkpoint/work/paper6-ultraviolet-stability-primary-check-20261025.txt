1. WHICH REFERENCE THIS IS

B119's introduction says that numbered references refer to the list in
B109, paper I. This paper is reference [16] there. B119 printed276
explicitly invokes its treatment of the R action, without the second
coupling-constant renormalization, and printed267 invokes its change of
variables. B119 printed264's [6] is instead the Higgs finite-volume upper
bound paper. An earlier speculative identification of [6] with paper6's

2. THE ACTUAL AGE GAIN

Printed266--267, (43)--(46), expands the old interaction in n>=2 local
curvature/gauge variables. Each such field factor gains two powers of
the scale under later minimizing-field transport. The root census has
dimension three in this paper. In the slowest n=2 case the field gain
is L^-4 per step, leaving a net L^-1 in dimension three.

The paragraph following (47) explicitly distinguishes dimension four:
the same quadratic term is marginal there. Its L^-4 field gain is
canceled by the four-dimensional root census. Coupling renormalization
is therefore required. The three-dimensional all-age estimate cannot
be imported as an unprojected neutral contraction in dimension four.

This supports the current decision to separate the scalar/tensor
marginal part of the homogeneous source ledger. A general all-age
R-like source sum with only logarithmically small quantum gain is not
closed by the three-dimensional geometric argument.

3. WHAT THE ORDINARY PREPARATION DOES SAY

Printed270, preceding (57), uses the old bounds for an arbitrary
configuration having the SAME regularity properties and bounds as the
specified minimizing field. It expands the old polynomial coefficients
through the actual nonlinear response and then localizes the resulting
graphs. Equations (57)--(59) retain the old root, old-scale factors,
response lines and complete union of vertex and walk supports.

That is useful evidence for a properly typed coefficient estimate.
It is not permission to evaluate a newly defined homogeneous hereditary
source function on a different mixed determining-tower domain. One
must still prove that the input lies in its actual domain, or construct
an analytic extension by explicit primitive formulas with a bound.

4. THIS IS AN INEQUALITY/TRUNCATION PROOF

The paper's Section B is explicitly an inductive assumption on approximate
effective actions, given by upper/lower inequalities (41),(47). Section C
expands interactions and cumulants through order six and bounds remainders
by a volume term; see printed270, (58), and the following paragraph.
This suffices for its three-dimensional ultraviolet stability conclusion.

It does not directly construct an exact nonlinear complex source family
with a common vacuum subtraction, nor its arbitrary mixed source jets.
Discarding those remainders would change the original generating function.
For the present goal the full B109/B116 convergent construction must be
used with any supplied mixed-domain source extension, retaining all
normalizers and scalar records. Paper6 provides primitive estimates and
a useful scope check, not the missing all-history source theorem by itself.
