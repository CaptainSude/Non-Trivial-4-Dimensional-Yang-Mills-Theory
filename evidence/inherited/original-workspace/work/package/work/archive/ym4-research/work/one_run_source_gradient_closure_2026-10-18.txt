2. A COHERENT CHANGE OF HOMOGENEOUS REPRESENTATIVE

Here is a precise way to control the ambiguity in anchoring a homogeneous
interaction. Fix a current cell lattice and its complete cell field slots.
Let A_H(B_H,z), B_H(B_H,z) be connected, vacuum-subtracted strict families,
holomorphic for |z|<R. Assume their scalar totals coincide for every
constant z and every sufficiently small finite-support field B. Put
D_H=A_H-B_H. Let c_H be an anchor in H. Work with a target norm
sup_x sum_{H contains x} exp(a|H|)||.|| and assume the input families have
the reserve a+log(3)+epsilon, epsilon>0, uniformly on |z|<R.
Let |w|<=R_0<R, with fixed margin m=R-R_0.

PROPOSITION. The anchored difference

       sum_H D_H(B_H,w(c_H))                            (2.1)

has a strict connected activity representation bounded by

 C_epsilon m^-1 Lip(w)
       ( ||A||_{a+log(3)+epsilon}+||B||_{same} ).        (2.2)

Distances and Lip here use the same current cell units. Its total vanishes
for constant w. The bound also holds with a separate exponential spatial
weight, paying the single diameter factor from that weight instead.

Proof. For S subset H define the anchored/Mobius component

 D_{H,S}(B_S,z)=sum_{T subset S}(-1)^{|S|-|T|}D_H(B_T,z),

where all cell slots outside T are zero. Then D_H=sum_{S subset H}D_{H,S},
||D_{H,S}||<=2^{|S|}||D_H||, and the empty component is zero. Each
D_{H,S} vanishes when any slot of S is zero. For any fixed nonempty S,
apply the finite difference in exactly the slots S to the equality of
the two total interactions, evaluated on fields supported in S. Absolute
local summability permits interchange with the H sum and gives

             sum_{H contains S}D_{H,S}(B_S,z)=0.         (2.3)

Choose a deterministic c_S in S. The left side of (2.1) therefore equals

 sum_{S nonempty} sum_{H contains S}
  [D_{H,S}(B_S,w(c_H))-D_{H,S}(B_S,w(c_S))].            (2.4)

All terms in (2.4) may retain carrier H, even though their actual field
support is S. The segment between the two source values stays in the
|z|<=R_0 disc. Cauchy on its m-neighborhood gives the difference bound
2^{|S|}m^-1||D_H|| Lip(w) diam(H), up to a fixed contour constant.
Summing over S costs sum_{S subset H}2^{|S|}=3^{|H|}. Connectedness gives
diam(H)<=C|H|, and the epsilon reserve pays this factor. This proves
(2.2), equality and local normal convergence. The proof uses complete
cell slots and no fine-coordinate dimension count. QED.

One may first prove this on finite tori, then pass to compact-field
limits using the same absolute estimates. The canonical anchor c_S
does not have to preserve lattice symmetry. Source support is explicitly
H, including both source evaluation anchors.

CAUTION. The bound is in the norm of BOTH representatives. If the literal
gauge-invariant source representative is bounded only by a small fixed E0,
while a quotient representative is bounded by K b_j^2, (2.2) gives
O(Lip(w) E0), not O(Lip(w) b_j^2). A small quotient norm alone cannot
remove this distinction. Also (2.4) does NOT make its individual pieces
gauge invariant; a separate invariant realization or total Ward argument
is required before using a theorem that needs piecewise invariance.
