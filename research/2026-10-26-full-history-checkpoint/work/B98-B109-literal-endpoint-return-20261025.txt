1. NOTATION THAT AVOIDS THE MULTIPLE OVERBARS

Fix depth k and fine spacing eta=L^(-k), with final lattice spacing one.

                         C_j=X_j W_j.                    (1)

2. EXACT COARSE-FRAME RECORD

Define b_0=I and

                  b_(j+1)=(r_j b_j)a_j,                 (4)

where r_j restricts site gauges to the actual next coarse centers.
Then for every j<=k,

                       M^j F=C_j^(b_j).                 (5)

Proof: use ordinary covariance M(V^g)=(MV)^(r_jg) and (3):

 M^(j+1)F=(M C_j)^(r_jb_j)
          =(C_(j+1)^(a_j))^(r_jb_j)
          =C_(j+1)^((r_jb_j)a_j).

This proves (5) inductively. It is B98(97)-(98), written for full fields.
In explicit pointwise order at a final coarse vertex y,

                  b_k(y)=a_0(y)a_1(y)...a_(k-1)(y),     (6)

with each earlier gauge restricted to that vertex. This is not an
unordered product and not a multiplicative-cocycle assumption for the
logarithmic mean.

3. THE LITERAL B98 RETURN GAUGE AND ITS ENDPOINT

B98(104)-(106) determines the gauge u that takes the regular relative
field X into its relative multiscale axial representative, subject to
the averaged normalization (81). It is NOT normalized by u(y)=I.
B98(87) and (99) give exactly

                     u(y)=b_k(y)^(-1), y in Omega^(k). (7)

For completeness this follows directly from the explicit product (106).
Let x_0=x, x_k=y, and x_j be the nested block centers. Let
P^0_(j+1)(x_(j+1),x) be the ordered background hierarchical transporter
from x_(j+1) down to x; it is the product of the W_j,...,W_0 endpoint
transports along the hierarchy. In the present notation, (106) reads

 u(x)= product_(j=k-1 down to0)
   Ad_(P^0_(j+1)(x_(j+1),x)^(-1))
       [ a_j(x_(j+1))^(-1) T_j(x_(j+1),x_j) ].          (8)

At a final coarse vertex x=y every nested center is y. Each endpoint
path is trivial, so T_j(y,y)=I and P^0_(j+1)(y,y)=I. Therefore (8)
reduces to a_(k-1)^(-1)...a_0^(-1)=b_k^(-1), proving (7).
The B98 averaged condition (81) is compatible with these nontrivial
point values; confusing the two conditions discards the frame record.

Combining (5) and (7) gives the exact ordinary endpoint of the returned
field:

 M^k(F^u)=(M^kF)^(r_ku)
          =(C_k^(b_k))^(b_k^-1)=C_k=X_k W_k.           (9)

B98 Proposition4 defines Q_k(W,eta A)=(1/i)Log X_k when X=exp(i eta A).
Consequently

 M^k[(exp(i eta A)W)^u]
                 =exp[i Q_k(W,eta A)] M^kW.            (10)

This is an equality of representatives and ordinary endpoints on the
native branch, not merely equality of gauge orbits. The unreturned
Landau-type representative has endpoint

 M^k[exp(i eta A)W]
                 = (exp[i Q_k(W,eta A)] M^kW)^(b_k).   (11)

Equations (10) and (11) must not be interchanged.
