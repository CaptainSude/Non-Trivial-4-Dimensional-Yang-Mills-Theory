2. A FREE CALCULATION RULES OUT THE WRONG GAUSSIAN REFERENCE

Use the actual homogeneous flat straight-string average Q_M with total
odd averaging factor M, in the conventions already verified in

on the appropriate gauge quotient. Its blocked Feynman covariance has
diagonal entries

                    [4M^2 sum_nu sin^2((theta_nu+2pi r_nu)/(2M))].

Consider theta=(t,0,0,0), 0<t<=pi, and a transverse component mu=2.
Every alias with r_nu nonzero for nu!=1 is killed by the centered block
average. The directional string in component2 equals1. Hence

                  sum_(r in I_M) csc^4((t+2pi r)/(2M)).     (2.1)

There is no gauge subtraction in this transverse entry. The elementary
identity obtained by differentiating the finite cotangent sum gives

 sum_(r in I_M) csc^4((t+2pi r)/(2M))
   =M^4 csc^4(t/2)-(2/3)(M^4-M^2)csc^2(t/2).

Consequently

 c_(M,2)(t)=1/[4sin^2(t/2)]-(M^2-1)/(6M^2),

 q_(M,22)(t)/q_(1,22)(t)
   =1/[1-(2/3)(1-M^-2)sin^2(t/2)].                    (2.2)

The ratio tends to1 as t tends to0, but at t=pi it is
3M^2/(M^2+2), tending to3 as M tends to infinity. Thus the mismatch
persists at zero coupling. A single running-coupling renormalization
cannot remove a momentum-dependent ratio.
