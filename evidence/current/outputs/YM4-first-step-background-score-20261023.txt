RESULT AND LIMIT

There is a complete finite background-score producer on the literal
FIRST, all-success, near-identity ordinary branch. Its quadratic form
is the fine-field Hessian in B119(1.16), not the later blocked precision
in B99(3.156). The former is a finite-block local analytic function of
the background and current. Together with the complete primitive it
gives a weighted noise/background mixed derivative with only a
polynomial cutoff-radius cost. A physical determining-data tangent
can then be composed through the actual first-step minimum response.

For a general later ordinary kernel, the existing fluctuation Hessian
theorem does not supply this derivative. Native positive action words
offer a concrete route for the interaction channels, but their complete
background-domain/read match and debt census must be proved. The later
precision, switch and inherited mixed-scale arguments cannot be dropped.
No general geometry-changing reset comparison is established here.

1. THE NORMALIZED SCORE THAT MUST ACTUALLY BE BOUNDED

Fix geometry, pivot labels, coupling, cut radii and old raw variables.
If b(t) varies the background in one common free chart, the matched law
is

 Q_t(dx)=Z_t^-1 1_KI(x) exp[-H_pre(x,psi_b(t)(a);b(t))] dx.         (1)

Here all x-dependent measure/coarea terms are in the complete action,
and K_I must stay the SAME original product cut. Retained current a is
held. Differentiating its original ratio gives

 d Q_t F_t/dt=Q_t dot F_t-Cov_Qt(F_t,S_t),
 S_t=partial_b H_pre[dot b]+H_pre,E'[partial_b psi_b(a) dot b].   (2)

Thus

 grad_x S_t=H_pre,Ib'' dot b
            +H_pre,IE''[partial_b psi_b(a) dot b].              (3)

In the decomposition H_pre=1/2 f^T D(b)f+V_all(f;b), the first term
includes (partial_b D)_I* f and grad_x partial_b V_all. The current
coordinate Hessian H_pre,ff'' does not bound these objects. Nor does
the bondwise C1 bound on D_a psi bound partial_b psi: the switch's H
field and any background-dependent cutoff/chart must be differentiated.

Scalar factors independent of x, including an exterior switch Jacobian,
cancel only through the covariance centering. Their contribution to the
joint outer law remains. A moving K_I would add its real boundary term;
changing the independent coordinate set may prevent (1) altogether.

5. COMPLETE FIRST-STEP MIXED DERIVATIVE

Let f=x+a, |f|_infty<=R, R>=1, and use a spare analytic noise cap 4R
with g_0 R below the fixed native threshold. At the all-success torus

only the fixed free-coordinate restriction.

bounded local analytic coefficients on the theta polydisc. Its
background read, both noise endpoints and their fixed contours lie
in a common finite block. Hence Cauchy and the original finite-degree
incidence bound give

 ||D_f D_theta [1/2 f^T D_0(theta)f]||_(nu,row and column)
                                                   <=C R.      (6)

This includes variation of C, Delta, the J_1 term and the gauge term.
There is no assumption that C or the precision is background constant.

For the complete first-step primitive P_0, the original supported
positive bound, uniform in the same background chart, is

 sup_z sum_(X whose complete read meets z)
   exp[kappa d(X)] sup_(|f|<=4R,theta-chart)|P_(0,X)|
                                      <=C g_0(R+R^3).           (7)

The root z may be a background or a free-noise block: B116's complete
locality is in U,J,B, with all three reads in the same localized
carrier. The finite first-step direct formulas yield the same claim.
Pure background constants may be subtracted since they have zero
noise derivative; this does not change the conditional law.

For a fixed noise coordinate i, first use noise Cauchy at radius
comparable to R, then background Cauchy in an arbitrary sup-unit
theta direction. Complex l-infinity/l-one duality gives the full
background row with cost C sup|P_X|/R. Reverse the two roles to obtain

exp[nu d(i,z)] for a fixed sufficiently small nu<kappa/C_read.
Thus

 ||P_0,f theta''||_(nu,row and column)<=C g_0(1+R^2).            (8)

No number of noise or background coordinates is multiplied into
this bound. The scalar directional duality and positive rooted word

Combining (6),(8) proves for the COMPLETE first-step action

 <=B_0(R),   B_0(R)=C[R+g_0(1+R^2)].                            (9)

For physical background coordinates the right side is B_0(R)/rho_*.
On R=C ell_0^p0 this is a polynomial in ell_0 at fixed rho_*.

not the free fluctuation vector f. The source has no inserted a^4 or

7. THE POSITIVE-WORD ROUTE FOR LATER INTERACTION CHANNELS

There is an alternative that avoids computing a mixed noise/background
Hessian. Suppose the actual PREintegration interaction has the already
constructed positive local word representation

 V(f;b)=sum_w V_w(f_(R_w),b_(T_w)),

with whole joint background domain and complete connected cover X_w.
A background directional Cauchy estimate produces its score word with
cap b_w times the normalized local tangent norm. The two-bounded-test
ordinary covariance theorem then bounds Cov(F,partial_b V_w), and
the full numerical diameter connects the source read to T_w. Word
and same-record hole sums must be performed with their actual reserve.

This is a concrete prospective use of the native positive action words:
the required new work is background-domain/read matching and its
positive weighted census, not a new source differentiability theorem.
Primitive P already has the joint U,J locality/analyticity. For
inherited E/B/R, the current physical Hessian notes explicitly freeze
the background and use exact physical minimum tangent curves. They
are not themselves positive independently localized background norms.

Some physical scalar first-tangent estimates can be reused for more
general determining-data directions, but every historical radius,
old-real slot, current stencil and boundary-anchor census must then
be retained. A second free-noise Cauchy estimate does not create a
missing spatial background arm or pay a historical density factor.

The full later ordinary Hamiltonian is not replaced by its first-step
