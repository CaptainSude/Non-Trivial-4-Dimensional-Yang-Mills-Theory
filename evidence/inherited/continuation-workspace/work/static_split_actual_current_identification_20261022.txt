RESULT

For a native E/R term, the physical static expression F_i(U_k^0)
uses the physical Wilson current of that whole field, in the source's
units. Its canonical independent-pair extension after LII(1.61)--(1.65)
uses a COMPOSITE OLD current component, denoted J^0 below. It is not
automatically the separate derived-minimum test J_(i,X)(M'(U)).

LII(1.65) takes values in an explicitly defined PAIR space. Its
independent-current clause therefore controls this J^0, with the
literal LI graded margins and L_0 factors. The scale factor in that
clause is cubic. The epsilon^2 derived-current comparison in the
separate v19 gate does not imply a universal loss for this direct
static pair composition.

This identifies and bounds the actual current component at domain level.
It does not establish compatible branchwise field Holder regularity on
all occupied contours, remove the actual allowance factors, or supply
the different current identity required by a new derived-near replacement.

1. WHAT THE NATIVE E/R FUNCTION MEANS

B109 printed261 defines

 J_i(U)=D_U^{xi_i *} xi_i^(-2) pi Im partial U             (1)

in (1.8), and states in (1.9) the physical equality

 E^(i)(X,g,U)=E^(i)(X,g,U,J_i(U)).                        (2)

The text explains that the second-order dependence occurs through (1),
and separates it into the independent variable J in the analytic
extension. B119259 following(2.27) imports this physical equality and
the same analytic/gauge properties for each localized E term. B119260
following(2.30) gives the R terms the same properties (i)--(iii).

Thus the literal physical F_i(U_k^0) notation does not declare its
current zero, and does not insert a new local minimum into its argument.
It abbreviates the term with the current of its displayed whole field,
rescaled to the born source units.

The domain has additional derived-minimum tests. B109262 (1.15)--(1.16)
defines U_p(M'(U)) and its physical current J_p(M'(U)) separately.
Printed263 explains that these tests are needed because subsequent
constructions sometimes SUBSTITUTE those pairs for (U,J). They are
not the definition of J in every original coefficient evaluation.

2. THE CURRENT OF A COMPOSED FIELD IS EXTENDED EXPLICITLY

B109272 (3.10) begins with the physical substitution

 U_old=exp(i xi_i A) U_new,
 J_old=J_i(exp(i xi_i A) U_new).                          (3)

Equation(3.11) expands the second expression into the whole-field
current of U_new, a second-order operator on A, and lower local terms.
The Landau/elliptic rewriting used by the actual H response then gives
the displayed canonical independent-pair function (3.13):

 E^(i)(X, exp(i xi_i A) U,
       s^3 J + Delta_U^(xi_i) A - P_1 A + F_1(U,A)),      (4)

where the paper writes s=L^(i-1) eta in that adjacent-step convention.
Equation(3.12) supplies the cubic change-of-units factor s^3. All terms
in (4), including the response/current and the actual gauge, are kept.
One cannot replace its last argument by J alone or discard the response
terms. On the physical graph it agrees with (3); off that graph it is
the canonical analytic extension, not an assertion that arbitrary
independent J equals the Euler current of arbitrary U.

The same distinction is visible on B109275 after(3.24): the paper says
that the shorthand E^(i)(X,U_i) should be written with the pair
(U_i,J_i) determined by the analytic function, as in(3.13). A prescribed
near-minimum substitution is an additional construction; it is not
silently present in every unprepared static row.

3. LII(1.65) CONTROLS THE COMPOSITE OLD PAIR

LII373--375 constructs the nested field using the actual joined arrays,
response functions, return gauges and localization parameters. It
introduces independent (U,J) in those expressions in the usual way.
Its final statement (1.65) maps the new pair domain into the old LI
space at the final preliminary stage n=N.

This value space is a PAIR space, not just a collection of bare fields.
LI190 explicitly defines its elements as configurations (mathbb U,
mathbb J). It lists separately

 mathbb U, U_(p,X)(M'(mathbb U)), mathbb J,
                           J_(p,X)(M'(mathbb U)).        (5)

The third entries of LI(1.64), (1.66), and (1.68) bound mathbb J;
their fourth entries bound the separate derived current. LI191 also
states, in the one-step value-space preservation argument, that there
is a corresponding statement for J. LII(1.65)'s value-space conclusion
therefore includes the composite old J^0 needed by (4). It is not a
claim only about the first field component.

For example, on the grade-r pieces of LI(1.64), the mathbb J allowance
is the printed margin times

       L_0^(2 max(0,r-k_0)) alpha_(0,r) (L^r eta)^(-3).   (6)

On the original grade-r pieces of LI(1.66) it is the printed margin
times

       L_0^(2(j-r)) alpha_(0,j) (L^j eta)^(-3),           (7)

with j=h+n, hence j=k at the final preliminary stage. LI(1.68) has
its analogous cubic-current entry, including the explicitly stated
top-domain factor. All these actual margins, subregions and allowance
factors must be retained.

Converting (6) to born-i units multiplies by (L^i eta)^3. Thus its
scale factor is

      L_0^(2 max(0,r-k_0)) alpha_(0,r) L^(-3(r-i)).       (8)

Likewise (7) becomes

      alpha_(0,j) L_0^(2(j-r)) L^(-3(j-r))
                                      L^(-3(r-i)).      (9)

This is cubic grading with its literal modified allowance. It is not
the derived-current allowance
alpha_(0,r) L^(r-i) L^(-3(r-i))=alpha_(0,r) epsilon^2
from B119(2.37) at p=i. No statement here replaces (8)--(9) by a
uniform running radius or suppresses a source-consumer price.

4. CRITICALITY DOES NOT SET THIS CURRENT TO ZERO

LII370 after(1.54) says J_(1,2) is not localized. It uses only the
constrained criticality condition pairing J_1 with variations whose
block averages vanish. LII371 (1.55) then expresses G''_k J_(1,2)
as a sum of localized/projection terms. It does not state J_1=0 or
J_(1,2)=0. A constrained minimum need not be an unconstrained critical
field. The direct static source and its current legs must remain.

5. MINIMAL CONSEQUENCE AND REMAINING CHECK

For the literal native E/R static composition, the pair-valued domain
inclusion supplies the canonical composite old-current bound. A direct
pair Ward estimate can use that current component, at the printed
allowances, without first replacing it by J_(i,X). Its remaining
analytic task is a compatible common-frame field estimate, including
the needed branchwise C1,alpha control on the full occupied localizer
contours and complete determining/read collars. Any allowance or
column price must still match the chosen source norm.

If instead the proof chooses a NEW derived-near representation, it
must match that representation's own inner current. B109(3.39)--(3.42)
provides a special cubic identity for its actual same-tower minimum;
generic B119(2.37) at p=i does not. The earlier v19 derived-current gate
remains valid for precisely that inference. It is not a universal
obstruction to every direct static pair map.

This trace applies to the native E/R extension just identified, and to
a marked coefficient only when its actual construction is certified to
use that same extension and fixed source-zero maps. A generic bounded
raw test, an arbitrary off-graph extension, or a source with additional
uncontrolled private arguments is not admitted by this identification.
