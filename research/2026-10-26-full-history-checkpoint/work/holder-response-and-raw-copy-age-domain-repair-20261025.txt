5. CONVERSION TO THE COMPACT-REFERENCE NORM

The image domain measures the relative field against Wbar, whereas (4)
is naturally U-covariant. Let U=exp(i xi B)Wbar, with |B|+|nabla_Wbar B|
bounded by the fixed small chart constant. No Holder assumption on B is
needed for the following conversion.

On any complete native test patch choose its EXISTING real reference
gauge Wbar=exp(i xi A0), with the native uniform C1 bound on A0. Real
gauge covariance preserves all relevant norms. In this patch the forward
covariant difference has the exact form

 D_U f=partial_xi f+M_U f_shift,
 M_U=(Ad_U-1)/xi.

The small C1 bounds on A0 and B give a uniform C1 bound on M_U and M_Wbar.
This follows from the convergent exponential/BCH series; every derivative
falls on a C1 field, and divided differences are uniformly bounded as
xi decreases. The difference M_U-M_Wbar is C1 bounded by C||B||_(C1).
Shortest-path products of these links over a distance r<=1 have norm
bounded by a fixed constant, and differ from the identity in this gauge
by O(r). Telescoping the products therefore converts covariant Holder
norms to ordinary Holder norms, and back, with uniform constants.

Finally D_Wbar A=D_U A+(M_Wbar-M_U)A_shift. The lattice product inequality

 [fg]_alpha <= C(|f|[g]_alpha+[f]_alpha|g|)

and the Lipschitz-to-Holder bound at r<=1 give

 ||A||_(C1,alpha;Wbar) <= C_alpha ||A||_(X_alpha;U).     (5)

All shifted endpoint and comparison-path reads stay in the registered
collar. This argument avoids any global gauge selection. For finite
own carriers it is applied on the overlapping native test patches;
the norms themselves are covariant, so no equality of independently
chosen gauges on different patches is assumed.

8. WHY THE ORIGINAL C1-ONLY CLASS IS INSUFFICIENT

Already in a fixed Cartan direction T, take flat Wbar=I and independent
J=0. For h=R^-1, in endpoint units set

 B_2(x)=b h sin(omega x_1/h) T, B_mu=0 for mu!=2,

with a fixed nonzero omega chosen so the unit old-scale difference does
not annihilate the sinusoid. On a compatible torus, or on a protected
interior patch with a fixed cutoff, |B|+|nabla B|<=C b and the actual
curvature is <=C b. Thus the C1-only pair class admits it at a fixed small
b, uniformly in h, and the flat reference satisfies every weighted test.
But [nabla B]_alpha is of order b h^-alpha.

Raw copy into old units gives A_old(y)=b h^2 sin(omega y_1)T. Its first
spatial Taylor remainder over an old-unit separation is O(b h^2), with
a nonzero lower bound at suitable points, not O(b h^(2+alpha)). The
failure is independent of how finely that old-unit region is sampled.

There is also a neutral local gauge-invariant quadratic test detecting
this failure. Let K_mu,nu(U;x) be the normalized antisymmetric plaquette
curvature (U(dp)-U(dp)^-1)/(2i eta^2), based at x. Form the trace-bilinear
square of

 Ad_(U(path x to x+e_rho)) K_mu,nu(U;x+e_rho)
                             -K_mu,nu(U;x),

and sum its translations/directions (or average within each owned unit
cell with native eta^4 measure). This is analytic and gauge invariant,
has zero vacuum, and its quadratic universal constant-curvature/Wilson
extractor vanishes: the displayed difference annihilates a constant
linearized curvature. Its carrier has fixed old-scale diameter and its
value is bounded on the fixed C1/curvature pair chart. On the commuting
oscillatory field it has density c b^2 h^4+O(b^4 h^8 eta^4) after averaging
over a fixed number of old periods, with c>0. The four-dimensional h^-4
root census leaves order b^2, with no h^alpha gain.

This is a counterexample to a universal neutral raw-copy age estimate
on the C1-only independent-pair class, if that estimate quantifies over
all such bounded local gauge-invariant coefficients. It is not a claim
that this oscillatory pair is a physical constrained minimum: its
physical current has uncontrolled high-frequency size, while the
independent current was chosen to be zero. The distinction is exactly
why physical-minimum smoothing cannot be silently applied to the old
independent-pair domain. Condition(6) removes this example and repairs
the regularity input by a genuine invariant image theorem.
