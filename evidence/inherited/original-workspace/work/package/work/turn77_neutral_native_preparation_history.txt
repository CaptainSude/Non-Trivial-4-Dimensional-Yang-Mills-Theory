minimum domain theorem nor a source derivative. The quantum input must
still be proved in that SAME catalogue; turn77_homogeneous_typing_gauge
addresses the native primitive core birth, not an arbitrary new lift.

identify a selected law with the full Wilson law, bound all mixed source
vacua, or give a nonzero continuum correlation.
