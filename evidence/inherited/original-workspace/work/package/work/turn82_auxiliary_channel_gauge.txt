TURN82 -- ORIGINAL-LAW HS SMOOTHING, FISHER CONTROL, AND THE REFLECTED CHANNEL
20 October 2026. gauge47. Finite-regulator results; no continuum claim.

RESULT
The exact two-HS augmentation gives a positive selfadjoint Markov operator
P on the ORIGINAL Wilson law. Its conditional-noise form is bounded by
the sum of original single-link heatbath forms. The actual auxiliary
current concentration also gives a volume-independent weighted
Lipschitz noise bound. More strongly, an exact Gaussian-score calculation
gives, in the product Pauli/2 metric,
  E_mu |grad P^(n+1) F|^2
       <= (18 beta+24) Var_mu(F)/(2n+2).                  (R1)
No derivative bound on the original bounded F is assumed here.

After reflection symmetrization, every buffered round changes a strictly
positive-half observable by an OS-null function. Thus O(a^-1) rounds give
an ORIGINAL-law, bounded, gauge-invariant representative of the SAME OS
vector with E|grad F_new|^2=O((beta+1)a), while retaining a fixed positive
time margin. This is a genuine finite-law estimate. The new representative
reads a four-dimensional time block, not one time slice. Consequently
(R1) is not the Hamiltonian one-slice Dirichlet criterion, algorithmic
rounds are not physical time, and this operation cannot create the
unproved nonzero continuum OS vector of the initial physical probe.

1. EXACT ORIGINAL AUGMENTATION AND METRIC

Use a finite nondegenerate four-dimensional periodic SU(2) Wilson lattice,
all periods at least4, with normalized Haar and
  h_p=1-Re Tr U_p/2, mu(dU)=Z^-1 exp(-beta sum_p h_p)dU.
For the reflection statements use an even temporal period and the usual
site-reflection-positive original Wilson law. A strict positive-half read
avoids both temporal fixed slices and links crossing them.

The two-HS identities are those explicitly proved in
work/turn81_external_source_history.txt Sections2 and5. Their underlying
primary method is Vairinhos--de Forcrand, JHEP12(2014)038,
arXiv:1409.8442, pp2--3. We use its exact positive augmentation, not a
weak-sector approximation. Denote the auxiliary variables by xi=(G,R),
their marginal by nu, and the original conditional lift by kappa_U.

For each plaquette let A_p and B_p be its two oriented length-two paths.
With independent standard complex 2x2 matrices Z_p,W_h of density
pi^-4 exp(-||Z||_F^2), the ACTUAL conditional lift given original U is
  G_p=m_p(U)+Z_p, m_p=sqrt(beta)(A_p+B_p)/2,
  R_h=n_h(U,G)+W_h,
  n_h(U,G)=sqrt(beta)U_h/2+G_p V_h^dagger.               (1.1)
Here U_h V_h is the corresponding half-path. The W_h are independent
given G and U; the displayed innovations may all be chosen independent.
The conditional G moments satisfy, uniformly in EVERY real U,
  ||m_p||_F^2=beta(2-h_p)<=2beta,
  E[||G_p||_F^2|U]<=4+2beta.                             (1.2)

Given real xi, all ORIGINAL links are independent:
  k_xi(dU)=product_e exp(Re Tr J_e^dagger U_e)dU_e/I(J_e),
  J_e=sum_first sqrt(beta)R_h+sum_second 2R_h^dagger G_p.
                                                               (1.3)
Orientations involving an inverse link are converted by the corresponding
dagger. Write U=q_0 I+i q_vec.sigma, q in S3, and define a(J) by
Re Tr J^dagger U=a(J).q. In particular a(cU)=2c q(U).
Equation(1.3) is a normalized conditional law, not an independently
chosen reference law. Its mixture is exactly mu, including Z.

For gradients use the invariant SU(2) metric in which i sigma_alpha/2
are orthonormal. If v_e is a link tangent in these coordinates,
  ||D_v U_e||_F^2=|v_e|^2/2, ||v||^2=sum_e |v_e|^2.      (1.4)
All constants below use this convention. Quaternion chord distance is
a different distance and is named explicitly in Section3.

2. POSITIVE DATA AUGMENTATION AND ORIGINAL HEATBATH COMPARISON

For real F in L2(mu), define
  C F(xi)=E[F(U)|xi],
  C* H(U)=E_kappa_U H(xi), P=C*C.                        (2.1)
The joint measure gives the stated adjoint. Conditional Jensen shows
that C is a contraction. Hence P is Markov, stationary, selfadjoint and
0<=P<=Id. This is the actual step U -> xi -> U', with U,U' conditionally
independent under k_xi. Its exact form is
  D_HS(F):=<F,(Id-P)F>_mu
        =E_nu Var_k_xi(F)=E[(F(U')-F(U))^2]/2.           (2.2)
In particular Var_nu(CF)=Var_mu(F)-D_HS(F). This identity alone says
nothing about the reflected covariance of CF or F.

Product-measure Efron--Stein at fixed xi gives
  Var_k_xi F <= sum_e E_k_xi,-e Var_k_xi,e F.
The right side integrated over nu equals
sum_e E Var(F|xi,U_(not e)). By the conditional-variance decomposition
with respect to U_(not e), this is no larger than
  D_HB(F):=sum_e E_mu Var(F|U_(not e)).
Therefore, exactly for the original Wilson law,
  D_HS(F)<=D_HB(F).                                      (2.3)
The heatbath convention in(2.3) is one rate-one update per link, summed
over all links. For a random-scan form divide D_HB by the link count.
The direction in(2.3) does not deduce a heatbath or gradient upper bound
from a small HS form.

For n>=0 the spectral theorem for 0<=P<=Id also gives
  D_HS(P^n F)<=Var_mu(F)/(2n+1),                          (2.4)
using sup_(0<=x<=1) x^(2n)(1-x)<=1/(2n+1). No spectral
gap or mixing time estimate is used.

3. ACTUAL CONDITIONAL NOISE: WEIGHTED LINK INFLUENCES

Let B be a real bounded original observable, and assume deterministic
quaternion-chord influences L_e satisfying, for all other link values,
  |B(U)-B(U with U_e replaced by V)|
       <= L_e |q(U_e)-q(V)|.                             (3.1)
For k_a(dq) proportional to exp(a.q)domega(q), set r=|a| and
c(r)=I_2(r)/I_1(r). Its mean is c(r)a/r, with mean0 at r=0.
For two independent samples q,q',
  E|q-q'|^2=2[1-c(r)^2].
The exact S3 covariance bounds proved independently in
work/turn82_current_tube_history.txt Section2 imply
  1-c(r)^2=Tr Cov(q)<=4/(1+r).                           (3.2)
Applying(3.1) in the product Efron--Stein formula before averaging gives
  D_HS(B)<=sum_e L_e^2 E_nu[1-c(|a(J_e)|)^2]
          <=4 sum_e L_e^2 E_nu(1+|a(J_e)|)^-1.           (3.3)
There is no conditional spectral-gap theorem hidden in this proof.

The actual current-concentration companion,
work/turn82_auxiliary_tails_tail.txt Section5, proves for the standard
three-first/three-second incidence convention
  P_nu{|a(J_e)|<2beta}<=p_beta,
  p_beta=min(1,192 exp(-beta/3200)),
and this bound holds uniformly conditional on ANY original U. Thus
  E_nu(1+|a(J_e)|)^-1<=(1+2beta)^-1+p_beta
                         <=C/(1+beta).                 (3.4)
The constant is absolute, not optimized. This proves the actual
volume-independent consumer
  D_HS(B)<=C/(1+beta) sum_e L_e^2.                        (3.5)
For 0<=B<=1 one may take the minimum with1/4. Its reflected convention
has exactly the same estimate after relabeling links. Section6 uses
only these two conventions, not an unverified incidence lower bound
for every possible auxiliary convention.

The surviving signal is Var_nu(CB); the loss in(3.3) is ordinary
conditional noise. Neither a large ordinary variance nor small loss
establishes a nonzero reflected norm. The separate Fisher estimate below
is stronger for producing a smooth representative because it requires
no prior control of the influences L_e of a nonlinear spatial flow.

4. AN EXACT ORIGINAL-LIFT FISHER BOUND

Differentiate kappa_U at fixed xi, in a deterministic tangent direction v.
The score is
  S_v=2 Re sum_p <Z_p,D_vm_p>_F
          +2 Re sum_h <W_h,D_U n_h[v]>_F.                (4.1)
In D_U n_h the variable G is held fixed. The score has mean0. Every
first/second cross term vanishes after conditioning on G and integrating
the mean-zero W. Different W scores also have zero cross covariance.
For a standard complex Gaussian, the variance of 2 Re<Z,A> is2||A||^2.
Consequently the Fisher quadratic form is exactly
  I_U(v):=E_kappa_U S_v^2
       =2 sum_p ||D_vm_p||_F^2
           +2 sum_h E_(G|U)||D_U n_h[v]||_F^2.           (4.2)
In particular one must not differentiate G(U) inside n_h while also
using the conditional-score decomposition(4.1).

Here is an explicit bounded-incidence estimate with no volume factor.
The derivative of A_p+B_p has four link contributions, each of norm
|v_e|/sqrt2. Cauchy--Schwarz and(1.4) give
  ||D_vm_p||_F^2 <= (beta/2)sum_(e in p)|v_e|^2.
Every four-dimensional edge belongs to six plaquettes, so its total
contribution to the first sum of(4.2) is at most6beta|v_e|^2.

For a half-path,
  D_U n_h[v]=(sqrt(beta)/2)D_v U_h+G_p D_v V_h^dagger.
Pauli/2 multiplication gives
||G_p D_v V_h^dagger||_F^2=|v_V|^2||G_p||_F^2/4.
Using ||x+y||^2<=2||x||^2+2||y||^2 and(1.2),
  2 E||D_U n_h[v]||_F^2
      <= (beta/2)|v_U|^2+(4+2beta)|v_V|^2.               (4.3)
Each edge occurs in six half-path incidences in total. The larger of
these two coefficients is4+2beta. Combining the two sums proves
  I_U(v)<=(18beta+24)||v||^2
          <=24(beta+1)||v||^2                            (4.4)
for EVERY real original U. Dagger-oriented traversals satisfy the same
norm estimates. No small-action event, moment of the Wilson density,
or physical source derivative is used in(4.4).

For bounded real F, CF is a fixed bounded function of xi. Differentiation
under kappa_U is justified by its Gaussian densities and finite moments,
uniformly on the compact original link manifold. Thus PF is smooth and
  D_v PF(U)=E_kappa_U[(CF-PF(U))S_v].                     (4.5)
Cauchy--Schwarz followed by(4.4), and then taking the supremum over a
SINGLE unit tangent v, gives
  |grad PF(U)|^2 <=(18beta+24) Var_kappa_U(CF).            (4.6)
Taking a separate coordinate estimate and summing would insert an
unnecessary dimension factor; the Fisher operator bound avoids it.

Integrating the variance and using C*C=P yields the exact consumer
  E_mu |grad PF|^2
      <=(18beta+24)(||CF||_nu^2-||PF||_mu^2)
      =(18beta+24)<F,P(Id-P)F>_mu.                      (4.7)
This is an averaged gradient estimate for the ACTUAL original law.
It uses the conditional law of auxiliaries given U, not a Gaussian
assumption about their interacting marginal nu.

5. ITERATION WITHOUT AN INPUT FLOW-DERIVATIVE ESTIMATE

Apply(4.7) with P^nF and use the spectral theorem:
  E_mu |grad P^(n+1)F|^2
     <=(18beta+24)<F,P^(2n+1)(Id-P)F>_mu
     <=(18beta+24) Var_mu(F)/(2n+2).                    (5.1)
The exact scalar maximum is
(2n+1)^(2n+1)/(2n+2)^(2n+2), which is bounded by1/(2n+2).
For 0<=F<=1, all iterates remain in[0,1] and Var_mu(F)<=1/4.
No statement of convergence to equilibrium uniformly in the regulator
is necessary. A finite graph's eventual mixing would not justify
letting the iteration count violate the support restrictions below.

6. FINITE READ COLLARS, REFLECTION SYMMETRIZATION, AND OS NULLNESS

For a complete original link read set S define H(S) to be the union of
all plaquette edges in plaquettes containing an edge of S. If F reads S,
then CF uses only the currents J_e for e in S. Each such current uses
G_p,R_h in those incident plaquettes. Formula(1.1) samples these variables
using only the original links in those SAME plaquettes and their local
Gaussian innovations. Therefore
  P F reads only H(S).                                  (6.1)
Repeated reads are contained in H^n(S). Gauge covariance of the exact
augmentation also shows that P preserves gauge invariance. Its source
integrals remain original observables, not auxiliary observables renamed
as original ones.

To avoid assuming that a fixed HS half-path convention is reflection
symmetric, write R F=F composed with theta for the LINEAR geometric
temporal pullback, without complex conjugation, and set
  Pbar=(P+R P R)/2.                                      (6.2)
This is a positive selfadjoint stationary Markov contraction, commutes
with R, and has the same read collar. The complex inner product is
<F,H>=E[conjugate(F)H], and OS(F,H)=<R F,H>. Thus the OS conjugation
occurs exactly once. The theorem below is stated for real observables;
the same convention gives its sesquilinear extension. Pbar can be represented by an
independent convention label L in{0,1}, followed by that convention's
exact HS augmentation. Given (L,xi), the original link law is still a
product. The label has no U derivative, so its Fisher quadratic form
is the average of the two Fisher forms. All of(2.2)--(2.4), (3.3)--(3.5),
and (4.4)--(5.1) therefore hold for Pbar with the stated constants.
We do NOT claim reflection positivity for all functions of this label:
reflection swaps conventions, and an odd label function need not have
a positive reflected form. Only the ORIGINAL-law proof below is used.

For ANY original F,H with disjoint complete original link reads,
conditional product independence in(1.3) gives
  <F,Pbar H>_mu=E_(L,nu_L)[(C_L F)(C_L H)]
               =E_mu[FH].                              (6.3)
For complex observables insert the usual conjugation in the first
factor. No disjointness of the auxiliary stars is needed for(6.3).
That stronger assumption would only be needed for an unrelated claim
of independence of two entire update randomizations.

Suppose F and Pbar F are both supported strictly in the original positive
half. For any positive-half K, the original reads of R K and F are
disjoint. Equation(6.3) says
  <R K,Pbar F-F>_mu=0.                                   (6.4)
Taking K=Pbar F-F is permitted by the support assumption. Thus Pbar F-F
has zero original OS seminorm. The original Wilson RP form makes this
an exact equality of OS quotient vectors; it is not merely an equality
of two positive numbers. Means are also unchanged by stationarity.
Inductively, whenever every intermediate H^j(S) remains in the strict
positive half,
  [Pbar^n F]_OS=[F]_OS,
  <R Pbar^n F,Pbar^m H>_mu=<R F,H>_mu                     (6.5)
for similarly buffered H,m. Centered Gram matrices are preserved too.
This argument uses the original measure and its conditional product
identity, not a presumed RP structure on every auxiliary function.

If a complete original read set is at physical time distance at least
d>0 from BOTH temporal fixed slices, the plaquette collar extends its
time range by at most a per round, with harmless endpoint conventions.
Taking n=floor(d/(4a)), for a sufficiently small compared with d, leaves
a fixed positive margin. Equations(5.1) and(6.5) give, for 0<=B<=1,
  E_mu |grad Pbar^n B|^2 <= C(beta+1)a/d,
  [Pbar^n(B-E_mu B)]_OS=[B-E_mu B]_OS.                   (6.6)
The norm in(6.6) is the full four-dimensional product link gradient.
The constant is independent of volume and does not depend on an input
Jacobian bound for B.

6A. STATES ARE PRESERVED; THE MAP IS NOT AN ALGEBRA HOMOMORPHISM

Pbar is not multiplicative. For example
Pbar(F^2)-(Pbar F)^2 is the conditional variance of F(U') given U
under the complete one-step update, and is generally nonzero. The
OS-null relation Pbar F-F therefore does NOT permit replacing every
same-side product by the product of its individually smoothed factors.
The OS null space is not being asserted to be a pointwise-product ideal.

For bounded original positive-half probes B_1,...,B_m the correctly
retained finite-source package is
  G_n(s;U)=Pbar^n exp(sum_i s_i B_i)(U).                  (6.7)
Its mixed jets are Pbar^n of the ORIGINAL products, including repeated
marks; they are not products of Pbar^n B_i. If the complete read halos
remain in the positive half, (6.5) applies to this whole source function
and to each jet, preserving their buffered reflected Gram forms and
their original means. Scalar-source analyticity at each finite lattice
follows from boundedness. A uniform complex ORIGINAL-field domain for
(6.7) is a separate continuation estimate; it is not supplied by the
OS identity alone. Thus this note constructs OS-equivalent state
representatives, not a new multiplicative continuum observable algebra.

7. THE ACTUAL SPATIALLY FLOWED MAGNETIC PROBE AND PHYSICAL LIMITS

One eligible input is the exact finite-regulator probe from
work/spatial_flow_probe_turn63.txt:
  B_(a,s)(t,x)=exp[-h |2 p(P_(12,s,a)(t,x))/(g_* a^2)|^2],
with fixed physical s>0, h>0 and g_*>0. Here p is the SU(2) quaternion
vector of the spatially flowed plaquette. Spatial Wilson flow uses only
the original spatial links at time t (or its declared spatial box), so
this is gauge invariant, lies in[0,1], and has exact single-time support.
The original scalar is kept; g_* is not silently replaced by bare g_a.

Equation(3.5) would require a bound on its GLOBAL link influences. The
linearized Maxwell comparison gives a squared coefficient sum of order
a/(g_*^2 s^(5/2)), as recorded in TURN64 magnetic_drift Section5, but that
is not an actual nonlinear influence bound. Compact-flow smoothness at
each finite lattice supplies finite influences, not the required uniform
scaling. Equation(6.6), in contrast, applies directly to this bounded
nonlinear input without importing its free comparison or a flow Jacobian
estimate. It produces a different original function in its exact OS class.

This improves the available ORIGINAL-law derivative estimate, while
leaving two important distinctions intact:
(i) The input probe's nonzero continuum reflected norm and calibration
    are still unproved. Exact OS preservation cannot create that norm.
(ii) Pbar^n B reads a four-dimensional time block of physical thickness
     O(na), rather than a single spatial slice. The earlier one-slice
     Hamiltonian energy criterion concerns a different function space
     and a different law. Its conclusion does not follow from(6.6).
For n of order a^-1, the bound is O(beta a), the appealing scale of the
previous gradient target, but its domain is the full isotropic Wilson
configuration. No isotropic-to-ground-law transfer has been supplied.

Similarly, the HS generator Id-Pbar and the heatbath generator in(2.3)
are algorithmic reversible generators. Their spectral parameter is not
physical temporal translation. Even replacing Pbar by
(1-epsilon)Id+epsilon Pbar rescales the algorithmic form while leaving
the Wilson law and its physical transfer matrix unchanged. Such a choice
cannot by itself prove a physical mass or spectral bound. The finite
read argument allows order a^-1 rounds within a fixed time buffer;
order a^-2 diffusive rounds do not preserve that buffer by this argument.
This is a limitation of the proved deterministic collar, not a theorem
that a sharper propagation estimate is impossible.

8. WHAT IS CLOSED AND WHAT REMAINS

Closed here for the exact finite original Wilson law: the HS/heatbath
form comparison; weighted conditional-noise estimate with actual current
moments; the dimension-free Fisher operator bound and its iterative
gradient consumer; and exact OS-nullness of buffered original-law
averaging, with gauge and reflection conventions explicitly retained.
The original partition function and every conditional normalizer are
already inside the common positive joint law throughout.

No native selected-sector map, raw-history truncation, or replacement
of a physical source by its reference redraw is used. Conversely, these
results supply neither spatial mixing of nu nor an original separated
source lower bound. The concrete next physical question is whether an
original bounded positive-half probe has a uniform nonzero OS channel,
or whether these OS-equivalent time-block representatives admit a
physical translation-energy estimate using their full block reads.
Neither conclusion is inferred from ordinary variance, current size,
or the algorithmic smoothing parameter.
