1. THE EXACT ORIGINAL DISINTEGRATION

Use the original finite periodic SU(2) Wilson measure with normalized
link Haar:

 dP_beta(U)=Z_beta^-1 exp[-beta sum_p h_p(U)] dU,

Each unoriented plaquette is counted once. C_W is its ACTUAL fixed
action normalization. The coherent source coefficient remains
alpha_p=|c_a f_p|, with the original four-corner profile and no extra
a^4. Neither beta nor c_a is renormalized by the construction below.

Choose a deterministic finite set I of integrated fine LINKS. Let P_I
be all plaquettes containing an edge of I and B the exterior edges
in these plaquettes. The remaining exterior links are Y; only Y_B
enters the conditional integral. Write

 H_I(x;y)=sum_(p in P_I) h_p(x,y),
 Z_I(y)=int exp[-beta H_I(x;y)] dx,
 nu_y(dx)=Z_I(y)^-1 exp[-beta H_I(x;y)] dx.                (2)

The source-zero exterior law is exactly

 dP_out(y)=Z_beta^-1 exp[-beta H_out(y)] Z_I(y) dy.         (3)

No Z_I is replaced by a success normalizer or an auxiliary completion.
All extra native gauge/partition insertions may be summed before(2),
since their original normalized positive masses are one. Equivalently
(2) is a disintegration of the original physical measure after that
reassembly. A native history label is retained only if it is literally
the fixed interior function specified below; the proof does not assign
an arbitrary cluster/localizer index a new positive law.

Let F,G,Traw be bounded Borel functions of x alone, with complete reads

Write |F|<=A, |G|<=B0 and |H|<=H0. These are true real sup bounds.

Examples are a prescribed P0 bad cube with all its tested plaquettes

5. TWO CAVITIES AND EXACT SEPARATED-SOURCE REPLACEMENT

Let I_1,I_2 be disjoint integrated link sets and assume no elementary
plaquette meets both. Condition on the original remaining links Y.
Then the actual action splits H_out(y)+H_1(x_1;y)+H_2(x_2;y), so

 nu_y(dx_1,dx_2)=nu_(1,y)(dx_1) nu_(2,y)(dx_2).           (17)

This is conditional independence from the finite-range original action,
not a clustering theorem. For interior sources F_i and marks E_i,
write a_i(y)=nu_(i,y)(F_i 1_(E_i)). Their cross conditional covariance
is EXACTLY zero, and

 Cov_P(F_1 1_(E_1),F_2 1_(E_2))=Cov_(P_out)(a_1,a_2).    (18)

The same equality holds after reinserting the two normalized interiors,
so the RHS may be evaluated under the ORIGINAL full measure P on the
boundary functions a_i. Its exterior density is the original product
Z_1 Z_2 exp(-beta H_out)/Z_beta. There is no new ensemble.

More generally a single cavity projection preserves all correlations
with tests measurable outside that cavity. For coherent sources with
separated compact physical supports, fixed-size cavities around each
microscopic source plaquette have this separation once the mesh is
small enough. Linearity then replaces each actual marked plaquette by
its boundary coefficient inside those separated correlations, with
the ORIGINAL coefficients c_a f_p and original sums over p,q. This is
a precise use of the lemma for original observables. It does not claim
an equality of the random insertions before correlation, or equality

Overlapping cavities or a plaquette joining them require the joint
cavity and its conditional contact(15); that contact is not zero.

boundary coefficients need not be independent under P_out and no

so joint original rarity can pay products; it is never replaced by
