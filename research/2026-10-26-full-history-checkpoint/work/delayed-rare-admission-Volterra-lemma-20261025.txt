DELAYED RARE-SOURCE ADMISSION ON A TUNED TRAJECTORY
25 October 2026. Root. Numerical and bookkeeping lemma, with native
source-registration hypotheses stated separately. This is not an
all-history theorem or a continuum construction.

1. DATA AND WHAT IS FACTORED OUT

For 0 <= i <= N let x_i = g_i^(-2), and assume the already established
finite-horizon trajectory bounds

 X + lambda (N-i) - C_G <= x_i <= X + B (N-i),
 |x_(i+1) - x_i| <= D,

with lambda,B,D,C_G fixed and X sufficiently large. Write Y=X-C_G.
A rare source born at date i takes an integer t_i >= 0 to enter its
fixed analytic domain, with t_i <= T log x_i for one fixed T. Absorb
any fixed additive delay into T by raising the weak floor.

The finite-entry theorem supplies, at each intermediate 0 <= s <= t_i,
an estimate of the form

 N_(i,s) <= A_i C (1+x_i)^d
   exp[-c (log x_i)^beta + C_1 (log x_i)^2
                       + C_2 log x_i log log x_i],                 (1)

where beta>2. Here A_i is the ORIGINAL source amplitude or its
specified profile norm. It has not been absorbed by rarity. In the
application A_i includes the original observable calibration. The
complete finite-entry root census L^(4s), seed price, inverse-coupling
prices and collar factors are already included in N_(i,s). Counting
the same L^(4s) again at admission would be an error.

This note also permits using a separately established real positive
conditional bound at an unfinished terminal date. Positivity cannot
be invoked for an arbitrary complex continued density or an isolated
signed cluster coefficient.

2. DELAY CHANGES THE RUNNING COUPLING BY A FIXED FACTOR

Choose the weak floor so that D T log u <= u/2 for every u>=Y.
For j=i+t_i <= N,

 |x_j-x_i| <= D t_i <= D T log x_i <= x_i/2.

Thus x_i/2 <= x_j <= 3x_i/2, and consequently

 t_i <= T log(2x_j).                                             (2)

All birth dates entering at a given j belong to the integer interval
[j-T log(2x_j),j]. Its cardinality is at most 1+floor(T log(2x_j)).
No monotonicity of t_i or of the signed coupling increments is used.

Since beta>2, the two positive logarithmic costs in (1) can be
absorbed by decreasing c. Fixed-factor comparability of x_i and x_j
then gives fixed d',c'>0 and C' such that

 N_(i,t_i) <= A_i C'(1+x_j)^d' exp[-c'(log x_j)^beta].               (3)

For arbitrary amplitudes the sum at date j is bounded by the right
side without A_i, times sum_{i:i+t_i=j} A_i. If A_i<=A uniformly in
the specified source norm, the additional fiber factor is O(log x_j).
It is another fixed polynomial price. This statement does NOT assert
that arbitrary microscopic source profiles have such a uniform A.

3. ADMISSION DATES ARE THE NEW AGES IN THE VOLTERRA CONSUMER

Suppose EACH admitted source, with its full marginal tensor and its
exact source-profile encoding, is registered in the recurrence of
YM4-rare-source-Volterra-stability-20261024.txt. In particular the
later prepared age kernel already includes the ORIGINAL remaining
four-dimensional source-position census and is bounded by C r^age,
0<r<1. Its age for this source starts at j=i+t_i, not at i.

With A_i<=A, (3) and the fiber bound imply a forcing bound

 f_j <= A C (1+x_j)^d'' exp[-c'(log x_j)^beta].                     (4)

The earlier Volterra lemma, applied from the ADMISSION date, gives
for every H>0 a total terminal contribution <= A C_H Y^(-H),
uniformly in N, provided the declared finite-period error sum is
bounded and the later feedback is 1+O(g_j^2). There is no additional
assumption of O(g_j^4) marginal cancellation for a generic rare source.

For completeness, a source entering at j has later amplification
bounded by C[(Y+lambda(N-j))/Y]^nu. Since j>=i this is no larger than
the corresponding birth-age upper bound. Thus delayed entry cannot
increase the numerical trajectory amplification. Delayed entry by
itself does not prove the spatial census in the hypothesis.

4. UNFINISHED TERMINAL DELAYS NEED NO ARTIFICIAL EXTRA RG STEPS

If i+t_i>N, put a=N-i. Then

 0 <= a < t_i <= T log x_i <= T log(X+B a).                        (5)

Choose X so that X>=2BT and 2BT log X<=X, and X>2. The function
F(a)=a-T log(X+B a) has F'(a)>=1/2 for a>=0, and

 F(2T log X) >= 2T log X - T log(2X) > 0.

It follows that a<2T log X. Hence there are at most
1+floor(2T log X) unfinished birth dates, and x_i is comparable to X.
Even the raw positional count during those remaining a dates obeys

 L^(4a) <= X^(8T log L).                                         (6)

Therefore a valid intermediate cap (1) at s=a, evaluated on its
admitted PHYSICAL terminal pair, sums to <= A C_H X^(-H) for every
H. Its root count is already in (1); (6) explains independently why
the unfinished physical horizon cannot create an exponential-in-N
loss. Do not multiply (1) by the same count again.

If only a real positive conditional cap is used instead, its complete
positional count must actually be supplied; (6) then pays that count.
This alternative requires a genuine original conditional expectation.
It is unavailable for an arbitrary signed or complex subcomponent.

5. EXACT PROFILE AND CALIBRATION CONDITIONS STILL TO BE REGISTERED

The elementary inequalities above do not license the following changes:

* Replacing the literal interpolated fine test profile by its value at
  one coarse root. Delayed roles must retain their exact profile
  functional, or an explicitly estimated residual source row.
* Replacing an asymmetric delayed role/history by a scalar Maxwell
  coefficient. Keep the full tensor until the complete original
  symmetry-related family and interpolation have been reassembled.
* Paying an unremoved L^(4(N-j)) future root census with logarithmic
  rarity. That census is exponentially larger than this rarity.
* Absorbing a cutoff-dependent calibration into constants C_H.
  Every conclusion carries A, and convergence of the calibrated
  observable requires its own matching/estimate.
* Inferring uniform convergence of different finite original laws
  from a uniform bound on their respective finite recurrences.

The new consequence is precise: once the actual later source consumer
and profile/census interface are registered, the logarithmic delay and
the finite terminal horizon introduce no new numerical obstruction.

6. STRONGER CONSEQUENCE WHEN THE VALUE COLUMN IS UNIFORM

Suppose the actual finite replay has the stronger value estimate
N_(i,s)<=K^s N_(i,0), K>=1 fixed, without inverse source-radius
prices. Such an estimate is being checked separately for the complete
fixed-domain homogeneous core; it is not granted for the actual
running-base law. Put d0=T log K. Then, for s<=t_i,

 K^s <= K^(T log x_i)=x_i^d0.                              (7)

The entire finite entry costs a fixed power of x_i. Accordingly a
birth envelope A_i x_i^d exp[-c(log x_i)^beta] with beta>1, rather
than beta>2, now retains the same logarithmic rarity after entry.
All date-fiber and terminal-delay proofs above are unchanged.

For a power-small birth N_(i,0)<=A_i C x_i^(-p), choose any fixed
epsilon>0 to pay the logarithmic admission-date multiplicity. Under
the same ACTUAL consumer/profile/census hypotheses, the aggregate
entry forcing is bounded by C A x_j^(-p+d0+epsilon). Put
s=p-d0-epsilon and suppose s>nu+1, where nu is the subsequent
Volterra amplification power. Comparability x_j~u_j=Y+lambda(N-j)
gives

 terminal forcing <= C A Y^(-nu)
                       sum_(a>=0)(Y+lambda a)^(-s+nu)
                  <= C' A Y^(-s+1).                       (8)

The elementary last inequality is the convergent integral comparison
for s-nu>1; the a=0 term is also bounded at Y>=1. The same power bound
holds for unfinished terminal delays using (7) and their O(log X)
number. Thus a sufficiently large power-small birth can pay a proved
uniform finite replay and subsequent polynomial feedback.

This implication does not assert that the native freely chosen rarity
power p can be made larger than d0+epsilon+nu+1 independently of all
constants determining K and nu. That parameter order must be checked.
Nor does it admit a general running-domain source: its field, current,
curvature AND compact-reference domain restrictions must be transported,
and every actual-base discrepancy must retain its original law/domain.
