6. THE MISSING SOURCE IF THE FIRST RECORD IS FORGOTTEN

To identify exactly the boundary of this result, let h run over all
actual first-step positive records and let W_h(V1) be their densities.
The full first conditional mean would be

 p1_full(V1)=sum_h pi_h(V1) p1^h(V1),
 pi_h(V1)=W_h(V1)/sum_k W_k(V1).                                  (17)

The corresponding full first mixed row contains both
sum_h pi_h c1^h and Cov_(pi)(p1^h,q1^h). A bound for the regular p1
in (3) is not a bound for (17).

Where a common smooth chart permits differentiation, the exact missing
branch-weight term is

 D p1_full
 =sum_h pi_h D p1^h
  +sum_h pi_h (p1^h-p1_full) D log W_h.                            (18)

Moving masks/domain changes can require additional genuine finite or
boundary comparisons instead of this formal derivative. The actual
failed-sector descendants and their direct source terms remain too.
Neither the first normalized score nor the second inner Hessian bounds
(18). Keeping h0 held avoids differentiating these mixture weights;
its density W_h0 is then indispensable in (6).

Likewise, integrating (14) over the actual outer (V2,h0,h1) law retains
both its actual branch probabilities and the covariance of the final
conditional means. Our estimate applies to the stated sector and does
not remove the other history sectors or that outer covariance. An
unrestricted first coarse law with the single function p1^h0 would be
the wrong source and the wrong normalization for this claim.

source. Its law response was not dropped at the first step, its first
