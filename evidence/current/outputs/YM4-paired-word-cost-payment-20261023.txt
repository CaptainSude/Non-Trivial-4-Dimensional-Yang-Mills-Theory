PAIRED ORDINARY WORDS: LOCAL ACTUAL-OWNER COST PAYMENT AND ITS INCIDENCE GATE
23 October 2026. New finite consumer; handoff originals and outputs unchanged.
Scope: original normalized real law, eligible actual removed owners, fixed
outside anchor sets, and literally compatible dated costs. No claim that the
whole fresh ordinary source class satisfies the required incidence gate.
Independent scoped PASS: paired_word_cost_payment_audit_20261023.txt;
the review includes the simultaneous local-operator tilt in Section 2.

RESULT

The fresh ordinary two-source column can be paid without an all-volume cost
moment on a sector where EVERY retained cost used by its words belongs to
actual eligible captured owners incident to a predetermined bounded outside
set. A local exponential cost moment follows from the existing native marked
operator proof, by interpolating only these local operation costs. It pays
arbitrarily many such owners, with the original C0 denominator and source
calibration. A simpler fixed-number-of-owners corollary uses the already
frozen single-mark theorem alone.

The full fresh class has not been matched to this sector. Two source arms
bound the multiplicity with which a shared dated slot is used; they do NOT
bound the number of retained owners met by a noisy localizer word. Existing
native ancestry anchors cover a required old lineage, explicitly not every
other retained read/filling slot. Untouched exterior slots remain outside
the current marked operation. The missing assertion is an actual compatible
capture/anchor incidence theorem, not another conditional covariance bound.

1. THE INPUT COLUMN AND THE ACTUAL PAYMENT SPACE

For the qualified fresh pair in outputs/YM4-fresh-ordinary-two-source-column-
20261023.txt, put

 m_uv = |Cov_Q(f_u,g_v)|,       d_uv = D_u+D_v,
 B = C A_p A_q exp[-nu d(P,Q)].                                      (1)

Its equation (6) is the pointwise supported-state inequality

                   sum_uv exp(-d_uv) m_uv <= B.                      (2)

All complete current reads, retained-hole fills, fixed seed conventions,
source prices, and source selectors are those of that theorem. A finite
constant debt independent of actual retained slots can be absorbed in B.
The source multiplier c_a is unchanged; for two original first directions
the product A_p A_q retains its actual |c_a|^2 factor. No a^4 or replacement
calibration is inserted.

To use an owner payment, (2), its compatible costs, and their proposed
capturing operations must be realized on ONE actual raw space. Fix the
current real q and all opened exterior y of a native reset. Let

 P_y(domega)=nu_y(domega)/C0(y)

be its complete physical branch law. Actual removed components H, their
private fields and histories are summed. For each eligible H use exactly
the original unresolved cost

              Q_H = sum_(actual private dated slots s in H) W_s,
              W_s=M^4 R_(j_s)^5 (d'_s+1).                            (3)

The once-only compatible ledger excludes healed slots. Captured pending
final-Y deposits and registered quiet forecasts are allowed precisely as
in cost_tilted_owner_intensity_first_capture, Sections 1--2. A retained
component outside H is not included merely because a source reads it.

The index in (2) can also denote an already emitted connected row transported
linearly through actual ordinary kernels while all its debt slots remain
literally held. The weighted positive sum contracts with constant one in
that open chart (real_marked_held_transport_known_capture, Section 1).
This preserves a stored numerical B; it does not preserve a small field
read. If a debt ceases to be held, or a changing selector enters the active
integration, the joint weighted law must be matched again. A label written
after averaging is insufficient. No nonlinear product or covariance
transport is inferred from this linear contraction.

2. A LOCAL EXPONENTIAL COST MOMENT FOR THE ORIGINAL RESET LAW

Let S be a finite set of outside current cells, with the native fixed
incidence collar. S is chosen from q,y BEFORE sampling the actual removed
branch. It may depend measurably on y. Define

 N_S = # {actual eligible removed H incident to S},
 Q_S = sum_(H actual eligible, H incident to S) Q_H.                  (4)

Each operation and dated cost is counted once in this actual compatible
sum. This is not the cost of all outer retained components.

Write rho_e=exp[-p0(g_e)/32]. For every fixed c>=0, chosen before the
common weak floor, there is a constant C_c such that

 E_(P_y) exp(c Q_S) <= exp[C_c |S| rho_e],                            (5)

 E_(P_y)[1_(N_S>=1) exp(c Q_S)]
             <= C_c |S| rho_e exp[C_c |S| rho_e].                    (6)

The constants are uniform over admitted exterior states and the lattice
volume. Dependence on the actual size |S| is displayed. Empty S gives zero
in (6). For bounded |S|, (6) is a genuinely local rare bound.

Proof of (5). On the unchanged raw private spaces interpolate, for
0<=theta<=1,

 T_(H,theta) G
    = T'_e(H)[exp(theta c 1_(H incident S) Q_H) G].                    (7)

All unmodified source-zero residual factors and old normalizers remain
inside the same complete bracket C(theta). This is an auxiliary generating
device. Exactly,

 C(theta)/C(0)=E_(P_y) exp(theta c Q_S),   C(0)=C0,
 d_theta log C(theta)=E_(theta)[c Q_S].                               (8)

These equalities use one joint branch integral, not products of separately
normalized histories. Positivity gives C(theta)>0 whenever the original
fiber is supported.

The fixed-geometry/history multipliers in (7) preserve the native sigma
comparison. Fix the common floor for cost coefficient c+1, including any
fixed prefactors. The fixed-cost replay used in the frozen cost-tilted
intensity then gives the same eligible all-test mass reserve uniformly
for every operator (7). The other operations retain their unmarked mass.
For the derivative at a specified incident operation,

 c Q_H exp(theta c Q_H) <= C_c exp[(c+1)Q_H].                         (9)

Thus its derivative insertion has the same all-test mass/sigma comparison
as a single cost-tilted mark, with a fixed prefactor absorbed by the floor.
This uniform replay is the only extension beyond the frozen first-moment
statement: ALL unmarked operators in its rooted proof may now be (7).
They obey the same bounds; neither that proof's positive primitive census
nor its absolute Ursell attachment requires them to equal the unmodified
operator exactly.

In particular the conservative 105/64 marked mass reserve and p0/32
geometry budget leave 7p0/64 beyond the native 3p0/2 requirement. The one
derivative mark has at most q choices in a q-operation primitive, paid by

                    q exp[-7p0 q/64] <= C rho_e.

An operation incident to a fixed s supplies the same real outside anchor.
The original relative path/retained-island rules are unchanged. The rooted
activity sum and its normalization clusters therefore bound the derivative
of the EXACT log C(theta), uniformly in theta and in which other operations
are tilted. Summing over s in S yields

                    d_theta log C(theta) <= C_c |S| rho_e.           (10)

Multiple incidence only increases this upper bound. Integrating theta
from zero to one proves (5). The convergent positive majorant just used
also justifies differentiation and passage through the finite-cutoff
private sums/integrals; finiteness of the desired moment is not assumed
as a separate input.

Proof of (6). The original unweighted owner count gives
P_y(N_S>=1)<=C|S|rho_e (its stronger exponent may be weakened). Since Q_S=0
when N_S=0,

 E[1_(N_S>=1)e^(cQ_S)]
     = E e^(cQ_S)-1+P(N_S>=1)
     <= exp(C_c |S|rho_e)-1+C|S|rho_e.

Use exp(x)-1<=x exp(x) and enlarge C_c. This also proves the c=0 case.

No whole-volume stability estimate or denominator lower bound occurs in
(5)--(6). The auxiliary tilted law in (8) is used only to prove a moment
of the ORIGINAL law P_y. Its existence does not alter the physical RG.

3. A PAID CAPTURE-COVERED ORDINARY PAIR COLUMN

Take any subfamily J(omega) of the pair terms in (2). Assume on the SAME
actual branch, whenever a selected term is nonzero,

 (i) N_S>=1, and
 (ii) d_uv <= c Q_S,                                                 (11)

with c fixed independently of date, cutoff and branch. These conditions
are concrete slot/owner incidence statements. The intended sufficient
match is that every debt slot used by either word is actually inside one
of the eligible owners incident to S, and the original geometric cost is
at most a fixed coefficient of that slot's W_s. Shared slots are charged
with their actual two-arm multiplicity, absorbed once in c.

Then, pointwise BEFORE integrating any branch or taking a supremum,

 sum_(uv in J) m_uv
   = sum_(uv in J) e^(-d_uv)m_uv e^(d_uv)
   <= B 1_(N_S>=1)e^(cQ_S).                                         (12)

Equations (6) and (12) prove the physical normalized estimate

 E_(P_y) sum_(uv in J) |Cov_Q(f_u,g_v)|
   <= C_c B |S| rho_e exp[C_c |S|rho_e].                             (13)

Here Q must be the actual ordinary disintegration used to produce these
rows on this branch, or the rows must have been legitimately transported
as in Section 1. In particular no new product law for Q and P_y is formed.

For |S|<=C_anchor, (13) is

 C A_p A_q exp[-nu d(P,Q)] exp[-p0(g_e)/32].                          (14)

The two-root decay was already supplied by the actual ordinary column;
there is no claim that the local owner mark independently has two-root
decay. The whole word sum was taken before the common positive density
comparison, so no count of alternative word-to-owner assignments is paid.
Branch-dependent assignments are harmless once (11) holds against this
common predetermined S and actual Q_S.

The distance in B is measured at the original ordinary emission grade.
If payment is at a later reset e, it remains a stored numerical factor;
it is not silently reinterpreted as a distance at e. The rarity rho_e
belongs to that actual payment reset. The theorem is for one payment
stage; summing possible future payment dates needs its own once-only
assignment and a controlled date sum.

Debt-free terms need no mark: their unweighted column is at most B.
Terms outside J retain their full debt and are not included in (13).
If B or |S| depends on y, retain their displayed product inside the actual
outer expectation; a deterministic uniform bound must be proved before
replacing it by a constant. At fixed deterministic B and bounded |S|,
integration over the actual output law preserves (13).

4. A SIMPLER COROLLARY USING ONLY THE FROZEN SINGLE-MARK THEOREM

Suppose each selected pair's complete debt is covered by at most k actual
eligible owners, each incident to the same pre-branch S, and

 d_uv <= c sum_(H in A_uv) Q_H,       1<=|A_uv|<=k.                   (15)

For fixed k, elementary convexity gives

 exp(d_uv)
  <= exp(c sum_(H in A_uv)Q_H)
  <= (1/k) sum_(H in A_uv)e^(kcQ_H)+(k-|A_uv|)/k
  <= sum_(H incident S)e^(kcQ_H).                                    (16)

For the last inequality one may alternatively use
sum_(A_uv)Q_H<=k max_(A_uv)Q_H; the largest exponential is bounded by
the full positive sum. Thus (16) does not require the interpolation in
Section 2. Sum (2) first and apply the frozen cost-tilted intensity with
tilt kc. The result is directly

 E_(P_y) sum_(uv in J)m_uv <= C_(kc) B |S| rho_e.                     (17)

For one actual owner shared by both arms its original Q_H occurs once
with the sufficient doubled coefficient. There is no second action
deposit. Equation (17) has one rarity factor; it neither assumes nor
claims two independent rare captures.

The hypothesis k=2 DOES NOT follow from source degree two. Each arm can
meet arbitrarily many actual retained islands. This simpler corollary
is useful only where a genuine bounded-owner capture allocation is known.

6. WHY THE NEW FRESH ORDINARY CLASS HAS NOT YET PASSED (11)

There are three distinct native facts; none may substitute for the next.

(a) WORD DEBT IS REAL, BUT GENERALLY EXTERIOR. The new ordinary column,
Section 2, makes D_u a fixed multiple of the actual compatible retained
read/filling cost, including the numerical bridge through each met island.
The fresh reset producer identifies the same debt as D_ret over actual
REMAINING outer holes after removed operations have been consumed. Its
recordwise D_ret<=Delta Q_open is not a current removed-owner bound.

(b) THE EXISTING SLOT MATCH EXPLICITLY RETAINS THIS SPLIT. In
first_touch_component_cost_slot_match, the compatible bridge union maps
to original dated W_s with bounded multiplicity. Its A_in slots inside
the current captured raw forest can be paid by replay. Its A_ext slots
remain untouched exterior debts until a genuine capture or a permitted
terminal real integral. In particular nu_j(H)<=C R_j^4(d'_j+1) matches
each debt to a real W_s, but does not put that slot inside another H.

(c) THE EXISTING ROOT ANCHOR COVERS ONE REQUIRED LINEAGE. Sections 3--4
of cost_tilted_owner_intensity_first_capture anchor the compiled record's
required old m-witness in a bounded predetermined current cover. The note
explicitly says this does not put all other held/private arguments in H.
The new ordinary localizer can reach other retained islands through its
full numerical cover X_u. The ordinary column pays that extension only
after retaining its wordwise island debt. Thus the required witness anchor
does not establish (11) for those other debt slots.

At the present reset, held retained costs are fixed in y while its removed
branch is still sampled. On the empty removed branch N_S=Q_S=0 even when
the held retained read has positive cost. The fresh ordinary theorem has
no rule that all such word terms vanish on that branch. Therefore the
pointwise domination (12) cannot be inferred from its current hypotheses.
This is an incidence mismatch on the actual disintegration, not a request
for a better constant in the marked estimate.

A useful sufficient NEW native assertion would be a disjoint complete-raw
partition assigning every charged pair term to a genuine capture stage
or a still-pending terminal sector, with:
  * every charged dated slot still unresolved and inside the assigned
    eligible owners at that stage;
  * those owners incident to a pre-branch source-rooted cover S with a
    proved bounded or source-summable size;
  * the entire positive weighted column (2) transported on that SAME
    joint law until payment, without unions of alternative normalized
    histories or newly uncharged selector/read costs.
The existing known-H theorem isolates precisely this missing rooted
capture assignment. The present local moment removes the need to bound
the number of captured owners once the bounded cover and full slot
incidence are proved. It does not prove that assignment.

A different sufficient route would be a local exponential cost estimate
for the actual RETAINED outer slots themselves under the complete outer
law. Sections 2--4 prove such an estimate for REMOVED eligible operations
only. The two populations cannot be interchanged by renaming Q_S.

For word-dependent large covers, a bound for each fixed S separately is
also insufficient if S is chosen after inspecting the removed branch.
One needs a pre-branch cover, or a deterministic positive envelope and
a summable cover/assignment census before averaging. The pointwise sum
(2) alone cannot justify an exchange of a branch-dependent cover supremum
with expectation. The actual localizer's full tree reserve may be useful
for that future census, but this note does not assert the needed common
raw matching.

7. SOURCE CENSUS AND THE EARLY-VOLUME OBSTRUCTION

The source-census notes retain the original calibration and count actual
root/grade records. They are numerical coefficient sums, not physical
cost moments. Here e denotes the ORDINARY EMISSION grade. For near-current
proper grades m in [e-B_e,e], their
two-arm census has the displayed factors

 (V_T+1)^2 L^[8(T-e+B_e)] (B_e+1)^2
             U^2(1+gamma_e+omega B_e)^(2D).                          (20)

For fixed physical separated supports, when the ACTUAL ordinary coordinate
roots satisfy d(P,Q)>=c d0 L^(T-e), a paid bounded-cover estimate with the
same B can be summed against (20): exp[-c nu d0 L^(T-e)] dominates its
polynomial, exponential-in-(T-e), and registered log-power preparation
prices. This implication requires either a direct matched payment at
that grade or an independently controlled sum over its assigned later
payments. A bound for one possible capture date does not prove that sum.
This is a genuine useful consequence IF source admission, read metric,
uniform kernel constants and the capture/cover/date gate are all matched.
The calibration U remains; older unrestricted proper grades are not added.

In contrast terminal_real_cost_tilted_stability bounds the complete actual
remaining-cost moment at an endpoint by exp[C(g_endpoint)(1+V_endpoint)].
It is UV uniform at a fixed endpoint and its fixed volume, but is not a
local volume-uniform bound. Treating an earlier date e as that endpoint
uses its actual V_e=L^[4(T-e)] V_T. Combining this available bound with
the ordinary two-root factor would produce an exponent of the form

 C(g_e) V_T L^[4(T-e)] - c nu d0 L^(T-e)
                       + 8(T-e) log L + preparation prices.         (21)

The supplied stability estimate states no decay in C(g_e) capable of making
(21) summable uniformly in the ultraviolet depth. This is a failure of
that proposed upper-bound route, not a proof of physical covariance
divergence. One cannot insert V_T in place of V_e or repay already healed
costs from a fictitious terminal slot. Transport to the one real terminal
law would require the complete compatible capture/remaining-debt ledger
identified in Section 6.

8. PRECISE GAIN AND LIMIT

The new paid statement is (13), with (5)--(6) its local actual-owner
producer; (17) is available directly from an already frozen producer.
Both keep the original real normalized law and original source prices.
They consume a positive ordinary pair column before its debt is replaced

one rarity factor and the column's existing two-root separation.

For the full newly constructed fresh ordinary column the concrete missing

its actual outer law. Conditional source locality, source degree two,
private ancestry of one required record, and the full-volume terminal
