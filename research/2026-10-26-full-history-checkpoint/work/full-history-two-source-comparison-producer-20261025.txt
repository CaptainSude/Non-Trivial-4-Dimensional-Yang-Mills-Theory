2. Exact raw-jet Duhamel, with no missing exterior covariance.

Here is a finite theorem independent of any RG interpretation. Let
P_j,Q_j be positive unital kernels from functions on S_j to functions
on S_(j+1), 0<=j<n. They must act on a COMMON state identification;
arbitrary kernels defined only on incompatible domains cannot be inserted.
Put E_j=P_j-Q_j, P_[b,a)=P_(b-1)...P_a, and similarly Q. Fix ONE terminal
probability nu. Define actual and comparison expectations by nu P_[n,0)
and nu Q_[n,0), respectively. Then for every integrable test u,

 P_[n,0)u-Q_[n,0)u
   =sum_(j<n) P_[n,j+1) E_j Q_[j,0)u.                 (4)

Proof: replace kernels from the fine end one at a time; the difference
of two successive products is the displayed summand. Positivity is not
needed for the identity, but allows absolute-value contraction afterward.

Choose c_F=nu Q_[n,0)F and c_G=nu Q_[n,0)G. Set

 f_j=Q_[j,0)(F-c_F), g_j=Q_[j,0)(G-c_G),
 h_j=Q_[j,0)((F-c_F)(G-c_G)+gamma_0).

Thus h_j=gamma_j+f_j g_j under the comparison recursion. Let

 a_F=sum_j nu P_[n,j+1)|E_j f_j|,
 a_G=sum_j nu P_[n,j+1)|E_j g_j|,
 a_H=sum_j nu P_[n,j+1)|E_j h_j|.                     (5)

For gamma0=0, exact centering and(4) give

 |Cov_P(F,G)-Cov_Q(F,G)| <= a_H+a_F a_G.              (6)

For a prescribed incoming gamma0, the same formula compares the complete
connected coefficient E gamma0+Cov(F,G). In particular it transports
incoming gamma linearly; it does not reset it to zero at an R step.

This is stronger in bookkeeping than separately bounding every later
posterior covariance: the exterior covariance in(3) is already included
in the raw product on the left of(6). It is not a claim that the terms
a_H are small. A large disconnected mean cannot be removed from those
terms without the exact centering or the corresponding compensating
first-mark terms.

One may instead use any deterministic centering constants. In that case
the exact formula also contains the known reference means multiplying
the first-mark differences. Choosing c_F,c_G as above gives the convenient
form(6). Constants may depend on the finite regulator; no uniform bound
