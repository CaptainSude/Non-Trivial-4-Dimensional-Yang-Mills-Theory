1. THE PRECISE CHANGE OF APPROACH

Fixing an inhomogeneous profile at the outset destroys the spatial
symmetries used by the old scalar extraction. Requiring an individually
gauge-invariant local representative of every quotient class creates
a different, unjustified requirement. Neither operation is necessary.

Instead keep

as a functional of both the field and a gauge-neutral scalar test
function. Impose gauge invariance for every f and simultaneous
translation and full signed-permutation covariance of f and A.
The source is not a new dynamical field.

The actual norm is

 ||E||_src=sup_i sum_(H contains i) exp(a|H|)
                                  sum_(x in H)||E_(H,x)||_b.

All source and field reads belong to the complete connected carrier H.
This is a sum of coefficient norms, not a supremum of the source-summed
functional. Quotient equivalence means equality of the field-normalized
total for EVERY f. Source vacua are recorded separately.

Evaluation C E=E(1,.) is then well defined and contractive. Define
the finite source extraction exactly by

 beta_src(E)=beta_old(C E).

This preserves the old finite constant-source channel without pretending
that source-anchored and field-anchored short lifts are identical.

8. WHAT IS STILL MISSING

The vanishing fine conditional-covariance tail is not a Cauchy theorem:
the transported coarser observables and their laws can still depend
on the ultraviolet cutoff. No nonzero separated lower bound follows,
since off-diagonal contributions can cancel and the terminal term
can vanish. Reflection invariance of selected windows does not imply
reflection positivity of the selected measure.

The bound uses the literal source sum_p f_p h_p. A cutoff-dependent
multiplicative calibration changes the estimate by the corresponding
factors. No inverse bare coupling or lattice power is silently inserted.

The full original Wilson law also contains the histories omitted by
the selected construction. The previous action-cell bad-bridge bound
does not identify its complement with this selected measure.

Full-law separated correlations, a nonzero surviving physical
correlation, compatible continuum observables, Euclidean symmetry
and reconstruction remain required. The isotropic selected/native
construction and the anisotropic Hamiltonian limits have not been
identified by a proved source-preserving comparison theorem.
