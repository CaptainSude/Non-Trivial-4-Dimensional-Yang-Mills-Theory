3. AN EXACT TWO-CUBIC CONTACT, INCLUDING ITS QUADRATIC CHANNEL

At the flat Gaussian reference let xi_A, A=1,2,3, be independent
three-color vectors with covariance sigma^2 I, and put

        T(m+xi)=det(m1+xi1,m2+xi2,m3+xi3).

Color-diagonal contractions within one determinant vanish. Hence

                        E T(m+xi)=det(m1,m2,m3).          (3.1)

But its COMPLETE normalized two-source coefficient is

 Var T(m+xi)=6 sigma^6
       +2 sigma^4 (|m1|^2+|m2|^2+|m3|^2)
       +sigma^2 (|m2 cross m3|^2+|m3 cross m1|^2
                                      +|m1 cross m2|^2). (3.2)

Proof: expand by noise degree. The degree-one terms have variances
sigma^2 times the displayed cross-product squares. The degree-two
terms have variances 2 sigma^4 |m_A|^2. Distinct noise subsets and
Gaussian chaoses are orthogonal. The determinant of three centered
vectors has second moment 3! sigma^6. This proves every coefficient.

No exponential source exists on a full real neighborhood for the
unbounded Gaussian cubic, but the nilpotent two-source identity is exact:

 log E[(1+z1 T)(1+z2 T)]
        =(z1+z2) ET+z1 z2 Var T,   z1^2=z2^2=0.          (3.3)

The pair (B,C) is cyclically complementary to A. This is the same

4. EXACT NORMALIZED WILSON SD ELIMINATION, WITH SPECTATOR SUPPORT

Let mu=Z^-1 exp(-S)dH, S=sum_p beta_p E_p, E_p=1-w(U_p), on a finite

Gamma_e(f,h)=sum_a X_a f X_a h, with exp(i t sigma_a/2) left derivatives.
For every smooth F,O, exact Haar integration by parts gives

 mu[(Gamma_e(S,F)-Delta_e F) O]=mu[Gamma_e(F,O)].          (4.1)

All expectation values are normalized under the SAME full Wilson law.
If the COMPLETE read of O avoids e, the right side is zero. This support
condition includes every common-base connector, not only the visible
plaquette interiors. The formula extends to products of separated
spectators without changing normalization.

Orient each incident action plaquette to traverse e once forward and
base it at e's initial vertex. Since w(P)=w(P^-1), this does not change
the action. Write its vector part p, and let Q be another such plaquette.
Then Gamma_e(E_p,E_q)=p.q/4 and Delta_e E_q=3w(Q)/4. Therefore

 mu[ O {sum_(p incident e) beta_p p.q-3w(Q)} ]=0         (4.2)

when O avoids e. Each p.q is its exact signed two-word difference.
For a spectator that reads e, the right side of (4.2) is instead
4 mu[Gamma_e(E_q,O)]. This is the contact and may not be dropped.

 Gamma_e(E_R,F)=[p0 r.q-det(r,p,q)]/4.                   (4.3)

       =mu[O {sum_R beta_R p0 r.q+3p.q}].               (4.4)

Every determinant is its exact signed triple-word difference. If O
