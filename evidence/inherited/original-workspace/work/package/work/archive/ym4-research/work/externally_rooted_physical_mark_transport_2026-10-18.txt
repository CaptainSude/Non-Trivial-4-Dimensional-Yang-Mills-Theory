1. CLASS AND PRECISE CLASSICAL INPUT

Let z be one external source cell on the OLD unit lattice, and write

 W_z(A)=sum_{H connected, z in H} W_{z,H}(A|_H).            (1)

The fields are actual ordinary physical link potentials; a bounded
complete block fiber can include their spatial derivatives. Each
coefficient is analytic on the independent block
b-ball. Let N_H be its number of fixed old blocks and D_H its diameter
in old physical units. Assume a fixed positive cardinality and diameter
reserve, with

 sum_H exp(a_in N_H+kappa_in D_H)
       sup_{||A||<b_in}|W_{z,H}(A)| <= M_z b_in^2.          (2)

The COMPLETE sum W_z is invariant under compact local physical gauge
transformations and has W_z(0)=0. This is the situation for the derivative
of a gauge-invariant generating action with respect to the scalar source
at z. It does not assert invariance of W_{z,H} separately.

Use the already constructed direct ordinary minimizing prefix P_h,
h=ell^(-q). It is the direct map, not q separately bounded compositions.
In final physical units its complete supported field norm and first
spatial derivative norm are <=C_a b_out, uniformly in q, refinement,
and compatible volume. The support constants retain a fixed positive
physical tree reserve. In old units this reads

 ||A||_support <=C_a h b_out,
 ||nabla_old A||_support <=C_a h^2 b_out, A=P_h(B).         (3)

These are the direct-prefix complete-fiber bounds from the classical
localization theorem and the ordinary-representative transfer. They
are not inferred from a C_q b composite bound. Fix the requested output
cardinality/tree weights first; their C_a is then independent of q.
The relevant tori have final side bounded below by one fixed positive
number, so a sufficiently small fixed final cube embeds in every torus.

Claim. For all sufficiently small h, the physical function T_h W_z=
W_z(P_h(B)) has a strict supported representation rooted at the final
cell containing hz, with

 ||T_h W_z||_(a_out,kappa_out;b_out) <= C M_z h^4 b_out^2. (4)

C is independent of q and of b_in,b_out, when b_out/b_in stays in a
fixed compact subinterval of (0,infinity). The whole output is again
gauge invariant. No source independent-(U,J) reentry is claimed or used.

2. PREPARE THE INPUT WITHOUT SPATIAL GAUGE AVERAGING

Subtract the constant of each W_{z,H}. Their sum is unchanged because
W_z(0)=0. Average each resulting coefficient over the CONSTANT compact
color group. This preserves its support, analytic ball, and norm, and
preserves the complete function W_z by its global-color invariance.
For a semisimple Lie algebra the averaged coefficient has zero linear
jet: an adjoint-invariant linear functional annihilates [g,g]=g.
Thus every coefficient now starts at total field degree two.

This step averages only constant color conjugations. It never averages
over a spatial gauge group on a small potential ball.

3. THE COMPACT ROOT GAUGE AND GLOBAL ADMISSION

Choose c>0 small enough that the final cube of radius 4c embeds in every
regulator. Fix a smooth scalar cutoff chi equal to one for |y|<=c and
zero for |y|>=2c, with fixed derivatives. Choose an old vertex z_0 at
bounded distance from z and set A_0,mu=A_mu(z_0). Define

 lambda_A(x)=chi(h(x-z_0)) sum_mu (x_mu-z_0,mu) A_0,mu,
 v_A(x)=exp(i lambda_A(x)).                              (5)

Take the sign convention U^v(x,y)=v(x)U(x,y)v(y)^-1.
On the old lattice the support radius of lambda is 2c/h. By (3),

 ||lambda||_support <=C b_out,
 ||nabla lambda||_support <=C h b_out,
 ||nabla^2 lambda||_support <=C h^2 b_out.                (6)

The coefficient carriers introduced by this gauge lie in a fixed final
cube about hz and connect to the supported coefficients of A(z_0).
Their final cardinality cost is fixed, not h^-4. The cutoff has no
input-field dependence. Products/exponentials are the same convergent
unital support-algebra products used for the comparison gauge.

For the global transformed link logarithm A^v, the analytic gauge-action
formula and (3),(6) imply

 ||A^v||_support <=C h b_out,
 ||nabla_old A^v||_support <=C h^2 b_out.                 (7)

Consequently the ENTIRE transformed field lies in a fixed fractional
part of the input b_in-ball when h is sufficiently small. The gauge
amplitude is O(b_out), even though its OLD support radius is O(1/h).
This is exactly why a cutoff at that radius is used.

For real B, v_A is a compact unitary gauge, and whole-mark invariance
gives W_z(A)=W_z(A^v). For complex B the same equality follows by
holomorphy on the common ball. Equivalently one can first use the
small complex gauge Ward identity. In neither argument is invariance
of an individual W_{z,H} used.

4. THE EXTRA ROOT-NEAR POWER, INCLUDING THE NONLINEAR TERMS

For H at old distance/diameter D from z, with h(1+D)<=c/2, chi=1
on its full fixed collar. Write A(x)=A_0+E(x). The complete supported
mean-value estimate from (3) gives

 ||E||_H <=C h^2(1+D)b_out.                              (8)

The transformed CONSTANT connection has no term linear in A_0:
lambda(x+eta e_mu)-lambda(x)=eta A_0,mu exactly in this region.
The exact matrix-exponential remainder, after division by eta, is
bounded by C(1+D)||A_0||^2 when ||lambda|| is on its fixed small ball.
Perturbing the middle link from A_0 to A_0+E costs C||E||. Thus

 ||A^v||_H <=C[||E||_H+(1+D)||A_0||^2]
             <=C h^2(1+D)b_out.                         (9)

The same formula controls the complete C1 fiber: spatial derivatives
cost ||nabla E||+||A_0||^2+||A_0||||E||, with constants on the fixed
lambda ball. There is no false omission of a D|A_0||E| term; that term
is absorbed by the bounded exponential/conjugation factor in the
Lipschitz estimate for E. The present theorem consumes ordinary-link
functions. It makes no independent-current extension claim: a small
norm on the physical minimizing graph would not control an arbitrary
analytic companion-current extension.

All bounds (8)--(9) hold in the supported algebra, not just after scalar
evaluation. To see this without a hidden coefficient cancellation,
represent A(x)-A(z_0) by its complete spatial-difference field along a
path of old length O(1+D), and use the displayed constant-connection
remainder before expanding products. Near paths occupy a fixed final
cube. The direct-prefix first-derivative family in (3) pays their length.

5. THE FULL ANALYTIC/CARDINALITY SUM

For a fixed H, insert the supported fields A^v into W_{z,H}. Put
alpha_H=C h^2(1+D_H)b_out/b_in in the near case and
alpha_H=C h b_out/b_in otherwise, using (7). In all cases alpha_H<=C h.
Expand multihomogeneously by the N_H OLD PHYSICAL BLOCKS, not by their
fine coordinates. Separate Banach polarization costs at most e per

 C N_H^2 alpha_H^2 exp(C N_H alpha_H)
              sup_{b_in}|W_{z,H}|.                      (10)

Indeed the full majorant is product_v(1-u_v)^-1, u_v<=C alpha_H;
remove its degree-zero and degree-one terms and use u_v<1/2. This is
bounded by C(sum_v u_v)^2 exp(C sum_v u_v). There is no entropy factor
exponential in the number of fine coordinates.

C_d(1+h N_H) blocks, before the fixed gauge cube is added. The direct
prefix's support propagator carriers have already been charged to C_a.

 h^2 exp[-kappa_in c/(8h)] <= C h^4.                    (11)
