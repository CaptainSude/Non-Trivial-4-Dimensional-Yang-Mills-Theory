2. ROOTED TAIL LEMMA

Let P be the torus side in CURRENT coarse units and w>=1 the actual
conversion to the kernel/localization metric. Let F have value norm

 |beta_1(F)-beta_2(F)|
       <=C a^-2 M(1+w)^p exp(-cP/w).                           (2)

The tensor version holds before scalar reduction. No P^4 factor
is required. The proof is a rooted sum: short terms cancel; every
other term has length ell>=c0 P. The fixed moment is bounded by
C(1+ell)^q. Reserve half the exponential to absorb this polynomial
and another part to pay exp(-cP/w). The remaining positive sum is
(T1). For ratios use n1/s1-n2/s2=(n1-n2)/s1+
n2(s2-s1)/(s1 s2). The reference lower bound proves(2).

A bound on final periodic support alone does NOT imply(T2). Even
