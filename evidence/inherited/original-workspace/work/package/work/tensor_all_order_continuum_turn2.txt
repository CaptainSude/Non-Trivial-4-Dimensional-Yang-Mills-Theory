1. WHAT IS ACTUALLY NEEDED FOR THE PRESENT RECONSTRUCTION ROUTE

Primary source: Osterwalder--Schrader II, CMP42 (1975), pp287--288,
Section IV.1, equation (4.1), and introduction pp282--283:
https://scispace.com/pdf/axioms-for-euclidean-green-s-functions-ii-with-an-appendix-4g1guiqrrx.pdf
DOI10.1007/BF01608978.
The stated E0' hypothesis has one fixed seminorm-order multiplier s
and a factorially bounded sequence of constants for the entire
hierarchy on the flat-collision Schwartz space. The practical bound is

 |S_n(F)| <= C^n (n!)^B p_(s n)(F),                         (1.1)

with C,B,s independent of n. Their stronger E0'' is unnecessary here.
The paper also gives a different reconstruction hypothesis; thus (1.1)
is sufficient for the selected route, not claimed logically necessary
for every possible QFT construction. Separate finite-order bounds do
not justify invoking this theorem. The distinction matters because the
analytic continuation of one correlation uses higher correlations.

The explicit existing os_s0_whitney_extension_2026-10-18.txt consumes
exactly a separated-product bound C^n(n!)^B d^(-A n), with one fixed A.
It produces (1.1) with a larger fixed factorial power and O(n)
seminorm order. Flatness at collisions pays a d^(-A n) singularity; it
does not erase an uncontrolled numerical C_n already present away
from collisions. For example fix a family of disjoint translated
Gevrey bumps with unit mutual separation. Their tensor seminorm of
order O(n) is at most exp[O(n log n)]. It cannot absorb exp(c n^p),
p>1, by merely invoking flatness.

The scalar source consumer in sourced_hierarchy_growth_audit Sections
6--9 is a valid improvement of its earlier exp(C n^6) estimate under
its stated source-producer hypotheses. Its shrinking chessboard
buffer is eta~1/n, its centered generating function is bounded by
exp[O(n log n)], and its square-free UV restoration costs a fixed
factorial power. It cannot be applied to tensor one-jets without
proving its joint source hypotheses. In particular a scalar uniform
source ball is not supplied by a tensor normalization matrix whose
bare norm grows polynomially with the cutoff coupling gamma.

2. THE STRETCHED-LOG OPTIMIZATION IS REAL BUT AVOIDABLE WITH GEOMETRY

The crude estimate

 sup_(gamma>=1) gamma^(D m) exp[-c(log gamma)^nu]
       = exp[C m^(nu/(nu-1))], nu>1,                       (2.1)

follows by setting u=log gamma and maximizing Dm u-cu^nu.
It is not an acceptable all-order OS bound for fixed nu. It is also
not an intrinsic obstruction to separated insertions: it discards
their scale of first joint localization.

Here is the exact elementary estimate needed instead. Let the
physical scales be geometric, a_k=a_* L^(-k), and suppose bounded
primitive scalar increments give

 gamma(a) <= C[gamma(d)+log(d/a)] for a<=d,
 gamma(d) <= C[gamma_*+1+log(1/d)] for 0<d<=1.             (2.2)

Fix lambda,D,c>0. Then for every integer m>=2,

 sum_(a<=d) a^(-lambda m) gamma(a)^(D m) exp[-c d/a]
 <= C^m d^(-lambda m) (m!)^ceil(lambda)
                    [gamma(d)+log(m+1)]^(D m)
 <= C_1^m (m!)^B d^(-A m),                               (2.3)

for fixed A,B. Any fixed polynomial anchor multiplicity can be
included by increasing lambda. Constants depend on fixed gamma_*,
L,c,D,lambda, but neither on the UV cutoff nor m.

Proof. Put u=d/a>=1. Spend half the exponential to sum the geometric
u sequence. With another quarter,
sup u^(lambda m) exp(-cu/4) <= (C m)^(lambda m).
For the last quarter, maximize
Dm log(G+log u)-cu/4, G=max(1,gamma(d)).
At an interior maximum u(G+log u)=4Dm/c, so u<=C m;
the endpoint u=1 obeys the same bound. Thus its supremum is at most
[C(G+log(m+1))]^(Dm). Stirling bounds the first factor by a fixed
factorial power. The inequalities log(m+1)<=C m and
G<=C(1+log(1/d))<=C/d finish the second line of (2.3).

When there is no engineering a^(-lambda m) factor, the age penalty
itself costs only exp[O(m log log(m+1))] at fixed d. This is stronger
than necessary. A crude polynomial bound in u also yields a fixed
factorial power and is enough for reconstruction.

The coarse scales d<=a<=1 cost at most
C^m d^(-lambda m) gamma(d)^(Dm) [1+log(1/d)], also bounded by
the last line of (2.3). There is no need to change the construction
or the terminal weak-coupling floor with m.

3. THE FIRST-DATE SHORTCUT IS FALSE FOR MULTISCALE COMPONENTS

A connected history component may contain a tiny ancient large-field
island at scale a_b near the first insertion, followed by a coarse
connection at scale d to the second insertion. Its earliest date is
b, but its physical connecting edge is only O(1) in d units. It need
not contain a path of length d/a_b in the a_b lattice. Consequently

 firstdate(Y)=b and Y meets two supports distance d apart
       does NOT imply a factor exp[-c d/a_b].              (3.1)

The one-mark census in tensor_original_source_turn2 (11.3) has already
summed the source positions and bounds everything by the earliest
gamma_b. Raising it to the mth power and attaching exp[-d/a_b] is
unjustified. The proper-row density bound is useful precisely before
this coarsening: each source arm must retain its own row and carrier.

The correct organization is:
 - dress each singleton source arm by its complete one-mark evolution;
 - charge old large-field islands met by that arm to its own native
   stretched-log reserve, costing a fixed constant for that arm;
 - only at the first genuine joint vertex use an m-mark local Taylor
   bound, dated at the scale of that joint vertex;
 - keep all fine-scale arms that allow separated sources to meet an
   earlier vertex, with their exponential localization weights.

The old island in the example is then a one-leg dressing, while the
joint vertex has coupling gamma(d), not gamma_b. If two distant
sources both reach the old island before coarsening, their retained
fine arms do cost exp[-c d/a_b], and (2.3) applies legitimately.

4. A PRECISE SUFFICIENT PRODUCER THEOREM

The following marked-forest statement is sufficient; a cutoff-uniform
positive anisotropic action or global ordinary source disc is not
required. It is stronger than the current first-jet result, but much
more precise than a request for generic all-order control.

Fix once the source-free RG construction and the 21-dimensional
linear tensor normalization. Use distinct square-free source labels
t_i with t_i^2=0 and physical profiles f_i supported in disjoint
balls. Require an EXACT expansion of their normalized density
coefficients into forests with these properties:

(F1) All one-label subtrees are resummed by the actual one-mark
     propagator, including its original vacuum, native R, and opened
     history terms. Its norm is bounded by a fixed constant times
     the specified single-label physical seminorm at its endpoint.

(F2) Each joint vertex reads all of its incoming source carriers on
     the literal prepared contour. At scale a, a vertex of incoming
     arity r costs at most C^r(r!)^b a^(-lambda r)
     gamma(a)^(D r), for fixed b,lambda,D, in compatible norms for
     the incoming subtrees. Its source carrier retains exponential
     tree decay at scale a, or an equivalent product of scale-labelled
     arm weights. These weights are not replaced by a census at the
     earliest large-field date. An unused fixed fraction pays carrier
     entropy and the unmarked history summation.

(F3) The sum of absolute majorants for scale-labelled skeletons,
     after the unmarked histories and singleton chains are resummed,
     costs at most C^n(n!)^b' times the vertex estimates. No C^l factor
     remains for an arbitrarily long one-label chain. Engineering
     weights telescope in these compatible subtree norms, leaving
     total degree O(n), not the sum of the numbers of descendant
     labels at all vertices. This distinction is essential: a binary
     comb has O(n^2) summed descendant counts, while its total incoming
     arity is <=2n-2. In particular the source labels are attached
     before component resummation.

(F4) The resulting coefficients are the SAME bare Wilson moments
     with the SAME normalization for all n. Vacuum restoration is
     exact. Centering deletes only the actual one-point means. The
     statement is uniform at one fixed terminal band for every n;
     a smaller cutoff epsilon_n for each fixed n is harmless, but a
     different weak-coupling floor or different model for each n is not.

For an unambiguous sufficient statement, require the following
already-resummed connected estimate as the quantitative conclusion
of (F2)--(F3). The unsummed skeleton formulation must establish this
scale power counting, rather than treating it as automatic. For
every label block S of size m>=2 and its
minimum support separation d_S, require

 |K_S| <= C^m(m!)^b product_(i in S)||f_i||_sharp
       times sum_a a^(-lambda m) gamma(a)^(D m)
                    exp[-c d_S/a]                         (4.1)

on fine scales a<=d_S, with the same bound without the exponential
on coarse scales. Here K_S is an exact connected coefficient after
the singleton subtrees have been resummed. Formula (4.1) is not an
assumption about cumulants of an unrelated Gaussian reference model.

Then (2.3) proves

 |K_S| <= C^m(m!)^B d_S^(-A m) product ||f_i||_sharp.

For centered full moments sum over partitions pi of [n] with blocks
of size >=2. Since product_(S in pi)|S|! <= n!, and the number of
partitions is at most n^n<=e^n n!, the sum is bounded by

 |S_n^centered(f_1,...,f_n)|
 <= C^n(n!)^(B+1) d^(-A n) product_i||f_i||_sharp.          (4.2)

The factor 21^n for tensor components is absorbed in C^n. The existing
explicit Whitney proof gives (1.1). This proves the consumer without
a zero-free full generating-function disc and without paying (2.1).

Why (F2) is plausible, but not yet proved by first jets: a local
holomorphic map on a coefficient ball has mixed derivatives bounded
by (Cm/r)^m, hence C^m m! times a fixed-power scale radius loss.
The actual local radius r_a~gamma(a)^(-D) therefore has exactly the
required factorial cost with D independent of m. However, one must
show this common local source map exists after the singleton
normalization and on the actual mixed contours; a bound for its first
derivative at zero does not establish it. One must also retain the
multi-source support arms through all later source-free preparations.

The public_argument_audit agent independently confirms that its
one-mark source localization retains original carriers before the
final census, but that the census alone does not establish (F1)--(F3).
Thus the all-order separation improvement is a proved conditional
consumer, not an unconditional all-order producer extracted from
the current one-mark note.

5. CONTINUUM AND ROTATIONS AFTER THE GROWTH ESTIMATE

Given compatible actual lattice limits and (4.2), choose one diagonal
subsequence on a countable dense family for ALL finite orders and
the finite component labels. Uniform fixed-order bounds extend it to
the specified test spaces. Symmetry, reality, and appropriate tensor
reflection positivity pass from the actual Wilson correlators.
For the tensor representation, reflection acts on F_(0i) with a
minus sign and on F_(ij) with a plus sign; the induced signs on Q_AB
must be included in the reflected test field. Treating all 21 entries
as reflection-even scalar fields is incorrect.

Continuous Euclidean rotations do not follow from hypercubic symmetry
or all-order growth. The scalar slab/rotation notes use extra analytic
and operator interfaces. Their tensor versions must identify the
same covariance representation and the same reconstructed hierarchy.
A nonzero tensor frame at a terminal lattice scale does not by itself
prove a nonzero continuum two-point norm; that requires a surviving
scale-independent observable estimate. Infinite volume, clustering
or phase selection are likewise separate consumers of the actual
hierarchy. None of these is supplied merely by (4.2).

6. THE 21 QUADRATIC FIELDS STILL DO NOT IDENTIFY THE FULL YM ALGEBRA

For SU(2), identify the six curvature components F_A at a point with
six adjoint vectors v_A in R^3. The 21 quadratic quantities are their
Gram entries v_A dot v_B. They are unchanged by every O(3) rotation
of all six vectors. Gauge conjugation gives only SO(3). The cubic
invariant v_A dot(v_B cross v_C), proportional to -i tr(F_A F_B F_C),
changes sign under an adjoint reflection. Hence it is not a function
of the Gram entries on a generic triple. Arbitrary curvature values
at one point can be realized by a local connection (choose its first
jet appropriately), so this is a genuine local invariant distinction.

Therefore reconstructing the entire quadratic tensor hierarchy gives
a tensor observable sector. Identifying it with the full YM local
observable algebra requires a theorem generating/recovering the
missing invariants, or a compatible extension to all invariant
curvature and covariant-derivative polynomials. The primary
Jaffe--Witten description asks for such local fields, not only F^2:
https://ncatlab.org/nlab/files/JaffeWitten-QuantumYangMills.pdf
This observation is independent of whether the user requests a mass
gap; it concerns what has been constructed even on the existence side.

7. BOUNDED WILSON OBSERVABLES: WHAT THEY DO AND DO NOT BUY

For a countable family of lattice-approximated loops, normalized SU(2)
traces lie in [-1,1]. Their joint laws therefore have subsequential
limits on the compact product [-1,1]^N. Products have norm <=1, so
no high-order moment growth problem occurs for these variables.
Continuous algebraic loop identities and fixed positive-half-space
reflection inequalities pass to such limits. This elementary compactness
statement by itself is far weaker than a continuum Yang--Mills theory:
it supplies no continuity as loops translate/rotate/deform, no local
nontriviality, no point-field regularity, and no identification of a
renormalized local curvature algebra. Renormalizing loops can also
remove the original uniform bound, which cannot then be reused.

There IS a bounded-algebra reconstruction theorem, so factorial point
field growth need not be a universal prerequisite for every route.
Schlingemann, hep-th/9802035, Definition2.3 and Theorem3.7, constructs
a Haag--Kastler net from a regular reflection-positive Euclidean
C*-net satisfying its time-zero condition. The paper explicitly
includes Wilson-loop-type objects:
https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9802035
https://www.esi.ac.at/preprints/esi498.pdf
To use it here one must construct that Euclidean net, prove the
regularity/time-zero hypotheses and nontriviality, and establish its
YM identification. Compactness of loop marginals does not prove
these assumptions. This is a possible alternative theorem target,
not a shortcut obtained from the present tensor first-jet estimates.

Positive flow time does not automatically repair this issue. The
standard gauge gradient flow smooths in Euclidean time as well as
space, so a flowed observable at positive coordinate time depends on
underlying fields on both sides of the reflection plane. It cannot
automatically be inserted in the original positive-time algebra.
The Luescher--Weisz finiteness theorem is perturbative to all orders
after ordinary four-dimensional renormalization, not a nonperturbative
construction or a reflection-positivity transfer theorem:
https://arxiv.org/abs/1101.0963

A direct countercheck is the free massive scalar in one Euclidean
time dimension. Heat flow multiplies its momentum covariance by
exp(-2t p^2). The resulting covariance C_t(s) is smooth, even, decays
at infinity, and C_t'(0)=0. If it were reflection positive as a local
time field, its positive-time covariance would be a positive Laplace
mixture int exp(-lambda s)d rho(lambda). Its right derivative at zero
is -int lambda d rho, which vanishes only if rho is concentrated at
lambda=0, making the covariance constant. This contradicts decay and
nonzero variance. Thus even the free-field heat flow does not preserve
the naive local-time reflection-positive field interpretation.

8. MINIMAL NEXT THEOREM FOR THIS ROUTE

The minimal concrete unresolved growth input is (4.1), or its
source-arm marked-forest producer (F1)--(F4), for the literal original
tensor-normalized Wilson construction on one fixed weak band. The
crucial geometric clause is the actual joining scale of multiple
source arms, not the earliest date of a completed mixed component.
Once this is established, the all-order factorial/flat-diagonal
regularity consumer is proved above and the exp(C n^p) loss is avoided.

This growth input still has to be combined with tensor Euclidean
covariance/rotation and nontriviality, then an observable-generator
or larger-field construction theorem to identify a full YM theory.
No scalar or quadratic-tensor sector alone establishes that last claim.
