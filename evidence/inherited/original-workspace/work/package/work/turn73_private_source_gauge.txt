TURN73. gauge47. 20 October 2026.

RESULT AND EXACT SCOPE

At the retained admitted native positive representation, a fixed
number of ORIGINAL fine-plaquette action marks can be inserted in
a complete raw component without dividing by its small mass.
There is a useful stronger statement: the number of marked fine
plaquettes can be the entire set of still-private original fine
plaquettes in that component. The initial large-field credit pays
this count, the bare inverse coupling, and any FIXED moment order,
uniformly in the subsequent number of RG steps.

This statement uses the strengthened fixed-fraction primitive-credit
ledger proved in native_tagged_history_propagation_turn54.txt,
Sections1--4, at its declared native parameter and admitted-prefix
scope. It does not certify all the other native inputs anew.

The qualification STILL-PRIVATE is decisive. Each of the four
original fine links of a marked plaquette must still be a literal
compact integration variable in that component's raw ancestry.
An original variable already integrated in a regular region, or
in a previously healed raw component, is not restored to this
class merely by appending its conditional distribution. Its source
response is then in coefficients and scalar normalizers which need
their own estimates.

Thus this closes a concrete private two-jet input, not a theorem
for the complete original weighted action. The split into private
and already-integrated parts is history dependent. The observable
inside each summand remains the original plaquette holonomy.
It is never replaced by the newly redrawn or reconstructed field.

2. WHICH FINE VARIABLES HAVE INITIAL BIRTH CREDIT

At the first regular blocking operation, original fine variables
in the regular integration region are integrated. The original
fine variables still present in raw slots lie in its complementary
large-field region and the declared fixed read collars.
The native initial geometric cover puts that region in the
bounded-layer enlargement of the initial fresh large domains;
this is the first-date instance of Large-II(1.76).

The exact pixels on printed381 show that(1.76) contains BOTH
the enlarged preceding retained region and the two-layer
enlargements of the fresh domains. The preceding term cannot
be dropped at a generic intermediate starting date.
For the original bare initialization, printed385 explicitly
treats j=1 and states

 Z subset union_i (Z_1^(i))^(~2),
 d'_1(Z)+1 <= 2(14)^d sum_i[d'_1(Z_1^(i))+1].             (2.0)

Thus the required initial absence of an independently supplied
old raw region is part of the source's base case, not inferred
by discarding the preceding-region term in(1.76).
If one starts instead with arbitrary incoming raw operators,
they need their own actual original ancestry and unused
primitive credits. They may not be counted as new initial
births. The result below is for the original bare ancestry.

Subsequent regular steps integrate more of these variables.
Coarsening, enlarging a region, or drawing a new reference field
does not create additional ORIGINAL fine link variables.
Whole-component healing integrates the corresponding old raw
slots. A former original variable subsequently represented by
a posterior is a passive reconstructed record, not an unintegrated
slot of the current T'_k.

Consequently a current raw component containing a literal
unintegrated original fine plaquette has initial raw ancestry.
Every still-private original fine read in this ancestry lies
in the corresponding initial large region, with its fixed
collars. This is stronger than merely projecting its position
into the large CURRENT component.

This chronology is the same complete-record convention used in
native_component_catalogue_turn62.txt, Sections3--4: former
regular integrations and healed coefficients are kept separately
when identifying the surviving raw lineage. It must not be
changed by assigning all the fine cells in a coarse component's
geometric preimage to its private operator.

Here is the size conversion needed below. A fixed-layer
enlargement of an MR_in cell increases the cell count by at
most a dimension-dependent constant. A native tree which meets
all cells of a connected domain gives

 #cells(F) <= C_d[1+d'_in(F)].

For example, split its edges at unit-cell scale; each segment
meets only a fixed number of neighboring cubes. The cells
touched at vertices are absorbed by the same bounded factor.
This is also the volume/tree conversion explicitly used on
printed380 before the large-field estimates.

Each such cell contains at most C_d(MR_in)^4 fine plaquettes.
Summing over the initial fresh domains and retaining their
bounded covering layers proves

 N_in(h) <= C_geo M^4 R_in^4 Q_in(h),                       (2.1)

where C_geo includes the fixed initial blocking-ratio conversion,
and N_in counts any subset of original fine plaquettes whose
COMPLETE four-link reads remain in the literal private initial
slots of X. A fixed complete-read collar changes C_geo only.
If Q_in=0, this private set is empty.

Neither L^(4k)|X| nor the number of all fine variables ever
integrated over the preimage of X is the count in(2.1).
Those much larger counts include fields which have already
become regular coefficients, and do not have this admission.

3. EXACT ORIGINAL PRIVATE SOURCE AND ITS FINITE JETS

Use SU(2), h(U_p)=1-Tr(U_p)/2, so 0<=h<=2.
Let beta_0=g_0^-2 be the original bare Wilson coefficient.
For a deterministic fine profile f and a fixed complete
history h, define the private source piece

 F_X(h,U_original)
   =beta_0 sum_(p in P_private(X,h)) f_p h(U_original,p).    (3.1)

Take P_private only from the admitted set in Section2. It
may be the entire such set, intersected with a prescribed
source support. A deterministic ownership rule prevents
counting one plaquette twice if several marked roles are
being decomposed. Define H_X similarly from a profile hprof.

The original plaquette is evaluated BEFORE its original
variables are integrated. Actual gauge changes preserve
its trace when all four reads and the inverse gauge records
are kept. A relative-coordinate substitution which would
require an additional nonprivate reconstruction is excluded
from this literal class unless its complete private identity
has independently been verified.

At any fixed finite regulator all these variables are compact
and the number of plaquettes is finite. Therefore

 T_X(s,t)G := T'_k(X)[exp(s F_X+t H_X)G]                   (3.2)

is entire in s,t for any bounded remaining private-record
test G, with all original cuts, references, couplings,
outer data and other factors held fixed. Exactly

 partial_s^m partial_t^n T_X(0,0)G
              =T'_k(X)[F_X^m H_X^n G].                   (3.3)

This is a derivative of the RAW operator, not of log T_X1
and not of a source-dependent normalization by its mass.

The pointwise bounds at a fixed history are

 |F_X| <= 2 beta_0 ||f||_infinity N_in(h),
 |H_X| <= 2 beta_0 ||hprof||_infinity N_in(h).              (3.4)

For an unscaled source omit beta_0. The variable choice in
(3.1) is substantive: a current fine approximation to U,
or a new reference plaquette, cannot be substituted in(3.4).

4. A DEPTH-UNIFORM PRIVATE MOMENT ESTIMATE

The native initial/adjacent-scale weak-coupling comparisons
give, with ell=1+log beta_0 and fixed constants,

       P_in >= a_P ell^p,       R_in <= a_R ell^r.         (4.1)

One can instead use the initial coupling directly if its
index is called0 rather than1. The finite adjacent-index
comparison changes only a_P,a_R. The stronger native
hierarchy retained in Section1 has p>6r in d=4; only
2p>1 is essential for fixed moments, and 2p>=4r makes
the displayed bound below simpler.

Combining(1.3),(2.1),(3.4), for j=m+n>=1 the marked
factor is bounded by the weaker ledger times

 ||f||_infinity^m ||hprof||_infinity^n
 [C beta_0 ell^(4r) Q]^j exp[-a ell^(2p) Q],               (4.2)

where Q=Q_in(h)>=1 and a>0 is fixed.
The factor is zero if Q=0. All constants are fixed before
the fine depth or the number of later steps is varied.

For j>=1,

 Q^j exp[-a ell^(2p) Q/2]
                <= [2j/(e a ell^(2p))]^j.

The other half of the exponential is at most
exp[-a ell^(2p)/2], since Q>=1. Thus the supremum of
(4.2) after removing the two profile norms is bounded by

 C^j[2j/(e a)]^j
 sup_(ell>=1) exp[j ell-a ell^(2p)/2]
                         ell^((4r-2p)j).                 (4.3)

For the retained hierarchy the last power is at most one.
The supremum is finite because 2p>1. More explicitly,
Young optimization gives a bound of the form

            B_j <= (Cj)^j exp[C j^(2p/(2p-1))],          (4.4)

after increasing the fixed constant for the finitely
many small j. No cutoff or current/fine scale ratio
appears in B_j.

Apply this bound at every COMPLETE continued history
before its scalar majorant is summed or replaced by a
supremum. The unchanged weaker ledger then proves

 |T'_k(X)[F_X^m H_X^n G]|
 <= B_(m+n) ||f||_infinity^m ||hprof||_infinity^n
       envelope_k(X) sup|G|,             m+n>=1.          (4.5)

There is a useful fixed-order strengthening. For every fixed
j>=1 and K>0, the same argument applied after multiplication
by beta_0^K replaces j ell in(4.3) by (j+K)ell. Its supremum
is still finite. Consequently

 |T'_k(X)[F_X^m H_X^n G]|
 <= B_(m+n,K) beta_0^(-K)
       ||f||_infinity^m ||hprof||_infinity^n
       envelope_k(X) sup|G|,             m+n>=1.          (4.6)

Constants depend on the fixed orders and K, not on the fine
depth. This uses the initial unused credit once and leaves
the SAME weaker current envelope. It is not an independently
repeatable penalty for every marked occurrence.

It is a total-variation/operator estimate on the actual
private-record integration. The test G can still depend
on those records. Its sup is taken only after its genuine
slot has been retained. For prepared products with their
own complete-history majorants, the same argument applies
before their original decay factors are summed; it does
not replace those products by an unrelated bound after
the native raw mass has been obtained.

In particular the one-mark, both one-mark species, and
same-factor two-mark arrays have bounds B_1,B_1,B_2 with
the SAME once-weakened current envelope. They can be used
in a two-mark connected expansion without an inverse
component mass or a new weakening for every age.

Source anchors remain their actual original plaquette
positions. Their complete initial read domains belong
to the raw ancestry contained in X. The fixed collars,
gauge records, and any actually used interaction
connectors remain in the carrier; the estimate does
not reduce a source to the root of X.

The external-field analytic comparison(1.73) can also
be retained if these marks, expressed in its integration
variables, are independent of the continued external
(U,J), or have an independently bounded holomorphic
continuation with the same budget. Literal closed fine
plaquettes unchanged by the private gauge substitutions
have the first property. No general holomorphy of a
posterior-reconstructed original observable is inferred.

5. WHY THE RESULT DOES NOT GIVE A FIXED SOURCE DISC

The direct exponential bound from(3.4) would cost

          exp[2|s| beta_0 C_geo M^4 R_in^4 Q].

Comparison with(1.3) by this method only admits

      |s| <= c P_in^2/(beta_0 M^4 R_in^4),                (5.1)

which tends to zero as beta_0 tends to infinity.
The finite-jet result(4.5) is stronger than using Cauchy
on this shrinking disc, but does not imply a nonzero
cutoff-independent complex source radius. The growth
allowed in(4.4) is faster than j! times a fixed power.

This is a statement about what these absolute estimates
prove, not a proof that the actual sourced operator
has no better analytic domain. A proof using the actual
positive bare action, instead of its compact supremum,
could improve the result. Such a proof must retain all
source-dependent normalizers and historical coefficients.

For a fixed number of bare, unscaled plaquette marks,
the simpler bounded-test inequality already suffices.
The improvement here is the canonical beta_0 factor
and the full private initial plaquette count in(2.1),
paid before the history geometry is erased.

6. POSITIVE ACTION SHIFTS: THE REAL OBSTRUCTION TO A LARGER CLAIM

At the original fine integral, a nonnegative source
beta_0 sum f_p h_p can of course be bounded against a
reserved fraction of beta_0 sum h_p when 0<=f<=1.
At a later prepared raw operator, however, changing only
the current Wilson and quadratic coefficients in(1.71)
does not perform that original insertion. The old fine
integrations have produced E_k, effective interactions,
determinants and normalizations. All their source
derivatives must remain.

This point is already explicit in the earlier exploratory
work/archive/ym4-research/work/
constant_source_shift_2026-10-18.txt, Sections3 and3A:
a frozen reference is not the reference recomputed at
the shifted coupling, and extraction of a shifted
current action leaves an old-minus-new boundary term.
That note's end-to-end source transport retained actual
localization and source-induction premises; it is not
used here as a completed source theorem.

A direct Gaussian calculation exhibits the normalizer
cost which a positive-action estimate must account for.
For n independent standard fluctuation coordinates and
their original canonical quadratic action Q=|xi|^2/2,

 E exp(sQ)=(1-s)^(-n/2),          Re s<1,
 partial_s log E exp(sQ)|0=n/2,
 partial_s^2 log E exp(sQ)|0=n/2.                          (6.1)

For Q centered by n/2,

 log E exp[s(Q-n/2)]
                 =(n/2)[-log(1-s)-s],                    (6.2)

whose second derivative is still n/2. A fixed reserved
positive fraction of Q bounds the integral but leaves
this determinant factor; centering only cancels its
first jet. Multiplying by a raw rare mass does not
license deleting these source scalar coefficients.

This is the exact normalization of regular Gaussian
fluctuations, not a counterexample to Yang--Mills.
Their number over a fixed coarse physical preimage
can grow as L^(4k). They are precisely among the
ALREADY-INTEGRATED variables excluded from Section2.
Their scalar and field-dependent source responses
cannot be covered by assigning that entire fine
preimage the private count N_in.

Where an exact local two-source representation is
available, a centered separated-source or slab second
difference can cancel its genuine contact/affine
scalar terms, as proved in
native_separated_source_attachment_turn68.txt, Section3.
That cancellation needs complete source reads and
actual matching coefficients. It must occur before
the absolute norm is taken. Equation(6.2) alone does
not supply the required localized interacting
cancellation or the final outer covariance bound.

7. WHAT HAS BEEN ATTACHED, AND WHAT REMAINS

The new attached estimate is(4.5), for ORIGINAL
still-private initial fine Wilson plaquette sources
and fixed derivative orders, at the retained native
complete-history and fixed-reserve scope.
It controls the raw signed jet measures against
bounded remaining tests. All unmarked scalar factors
and the marked scalar terms are retained.

The relevant sources are the history-owned part of
an original profile. Completing the original source
also requires:

 - the regular-integrated fine-field part and every
   scalar/source coefficient it produced;
 - parts whose original fields were integrated by
   earlier healing, with their actual carried marks;
 - plaquettes with nonprivate reconstructed reads;
 - the changes of conditional means and the final
   coarse covariance under the full original law.

A source read in a literal old private slot has now
been distinguished from each of these classes.
It is not renamed an arbitrary original source.

The fixed-jet bound can improve the private-component
input to a normalized two-mark cluster estimate.
It does not estimate the complete original
separated-source function or prove a nonzero
continuum witness. Full nontrivial YM4 existence
remains open.
