COMPLETE PREFIX WEAKENING OF NOISE TRANSPORT AND SOURCE READS
25 October 2026. New off-physical source-family construction and scope.

This note specifies a way to weaken the WHOLE homogeneous prefix at
one final spatial scale. It includes frame/noise transport and the
selected-current branches. The original physical map is recovered at
all weakening parameters equal1. No assertion is made that this is
the earlier canonical off-physical source family or its normalization.

The construction uses the already proved small-field whole-response
bounds. It also requires their COMPLETE primitive word/read records,
not just their numerical sup norms. The conditional source-catalogue
consequence at the end explicitly requires a uniform admitted source
bound on this newly constructed family.

1. FIX ONE FINAL SPATIAL PARTITION

Use original fine units. Let the final cells have side

  R_t=M_* L^t,

where M_* is a fixed sufficiently large geometric constant. Each cell
has a complex variable s(Delta), shared by ALL dates. Fix |s|<=S>1.
Choose S and M_* before the current and noise caps. Protect a connected
finite set of final cells containing the ENTIRE original source
tree/filling packet, including every nominally auxiliary filling read.

For every primitive operator word at grade j, retain its full connected
read closure: input/output localization domains, background/current
coefficient reads, inverse/projection branches, transposes, paths and
collars. Tag the word by the product of s(Delta) over final cells
intersecting that complete read. Use one tag per cell of the primitive
word, then the factor-word convention when composing words. Thus
multiplicities across different factors are retained and paid.

Do not multiply a regular output kernel by a point-dependent indicator
and call it a localized word. Its scalar tag is constant for the entire
native word/localization domain. This preserves its output derivative
estimates and its vanishing collars.

2. WHY THIS HAS A FIXED WORD COST

Let ell(omega) be the length, in original fine units, of a connected
tree meeting all primitive localization domains. Those domains have
diameter at most C M_native L^j. Choose M_* to dominate this constant.
A tubular-cover estimate for a connected tree gives

  n_final(omega)<=C_d[1+ell(omega)/R_t].            (1)

Indeed its thickening by a radius smaller than a fixed fraction of
R_t can be covered by C_d(1+ell/R_t) final cells. This includes domains
meeting a common corner; they cost only a fixed dimension constant.
The same estimate holds for the branched current/projection words.
Choose each added connected closure within this controlled-thickness
neighborhood of the native connecting tree, using fixed short connectors
for the original domains and collars. An arbitrary connected enlargement
is not allowed: it need not satisfy (1). The full primitive read must
fit this controlled closure before the cost (2) is asserted.

Consequently the scalar tag obeys

  S^n_final<=S^C exp[C log(S) ell(omega)/R_t].      (2)

The native word has a distance majorant exp[-delta ell/L^j], besides
its walk-count factors. Since j<=t, choosing M_* large enough compared
with log(S)/delta absorbs the second factor in(2), leaving a fixed
strict decay reserve. Short words cost at most the fixed factor S^C.
The resulting primitive constants are uniform in j,t and volume.

Subsequent current Neumann series and nonlinear fixed points use these
inflated constants. Their smallness caps are chosen AFTER S. This is
the same majorant logic as B116, applied to a coarser partition; it
does not introduce a new source-radius shrink at every date.

3. THE FRAME INCREMENT IS LOCAL ONLY BEFORE SUBSTITUTION

Register the absolute hierarchy at the top, with fixed coarse anchors
or an explicit local certificate for those anchors. The separate note
noise-proportional-frame-increment-locality-20261025.txt proves

  Gamma_j(x)=Gamma_(j+1)(x) R_j(x),
  R_j=P_j(U_(j+1);y,x)^-1 u_j(y) P_j(U_j;y,x),
  R_j(0,W)=1,  ||R_j^+/-1-1||<=C||A_j||.          (3)

As a function of its CURRENT fine arguments W=U_(j+1),A_j, this
outer formula has a finite complete read Rread_j(x) of diameter
O(L^j), including all partial averages and return paths. Hence it
intersects at most q_frame final cells, with q_frame fixed.

This is NOT the complete read in original inputs. Substitution of
A_j and W brings all their nonlocal response/current/frame tails.
Those branches retain their own primitive tags. The full read is the
union of the outer finite read AND every recursively substituted word
branch. No claim of finite O(L^j) support is made after substitution.

4. WEAKEN THE TRANSPORT OPERATOR, NOT AN ARTIFICIAL GROUP GAUGE

Only the evaluated noise argument needs the transport

  N_j=Ad_(Gamma_j^-1).

At unweakened parameters, (3) gives exactly

  N_j=(I+E_j)N_(j+1),
  E_j=Ad_(R_j^-1)-I,  ||E_j||<=C||A_j||.          (4)

For s!=1 define a bounded linear transport on the Lie algebra; it
need not remain the adjoint action of a group element. Let w_j(x;s)
be the product of tags of the COMPLETE OUTER read Rread_j(x), and set

  N_j(s;x)=[I+w_j(x;s) E_j(U_(j+1)(s),A_j(s);x)]
                                      N_(j+1)(s;x). (5)

All substituted fields on the right already carry their full earlier
tags. Formula(5) retains the frame/adjoint branches, but avoids an
unjustified group interpolation. It is analytic and exactly physical
at s=1. No inverse of a weakened N_j is used.

At the top, a canonical absolute hierarchy on U_t has a complete fine
read of diameter O(L^t). If Gamma_t is such a function of the fine
input, define

  N_t(s;x)=w_t(x;s) Ad_(Gamma_t(U_t;x)^-1),         (6)

where w_t tags its full read, including any variable coarse anchors.
Then ||N_t(s)||<=K0(S), a fixed constant, since the top read intersects
only a fixed number of final cells. If U_t itself is reconstructed
from coarser variables, all its response branches must also be
substituted and tagged. An arbitrary bounded but nonlocal Gamma_t
does not meet this assumption and supplies no locality theorem.

The actual original real noise x_j stays fixed. Evaluate the response
at x_j^sharp(s)=N_(j+1)(s)x_j, not at a changed integration variable.
The native response accepts arbitrary small complex Lie-algebra noise,
so it does not require N_(j+1)(s) to be an adjoint action off s=1.

5. TRANSPORT DEBT AND FIELD BOUNDS ON THE WHOLE S-POLYDISC

Write m_j=||N_j(s)|| and let ||g_j x_j||<=delta_j. The response bound
with the constants from Section2 gives

  ||A_j(s)||_four <= C_H(S) m_(j+1) delta_j.

Since |w_j|<=S^q_frame, (5) implies

  m_j<=m_(j+1)[1+C(S)m_(j+1)delta_j].              (7)

Choose M_N=2K0(S). If

  C(S) M_N sum_(j<t)delta_j<=log2,

descending induction gives m_j<=M_N throughout. This is a bound on
the actual transport, not a fictional frame norm at s!=1. Current and
noise caps are additionally chosen to fit the enlarged response chart.

The same BCH/curvature proof then gives the established geometric
field and current bounds with delta_j replaced by M_N delta_j and
fixed S-dependent constants. There is no factor S^{ct} detached from
the noise: every extra frame update multiplies I+O(delta_j), and its
multiplicities are paid by the total debt. The top costs K0(S) once.

6. THE SELECTED CURRENT AND LOCAL CANCELLATIONS

The entire current must be included. In particular P1_U A has a
nonlocal DPD* kernel and must be replaced by its completely tagged
operator words P1_s A. The native weak-field-to-current bound gives
||P1_s A||<=C(S)||A||, so the current recurrence remains valid.

The covariant-Laplacian and F1 terms have local complete stencils.
Tag a WHOLE stencil expression with its common complete-read tag.
Do not tag its large individual xi^-2 summands differently: that
would destroy their cancellation and could lose the uniform bound.
The tag is applied to the selected-current output, whose sup norm
is needed; no derivative of that tag is taken. The strong derivatives
of A itself still come from the regular output operator words.

The same rule applies to local nonlinear averaging rows and pivot
coefficients: evaluate the bounded native coefficient/inverse first,
tag its complete row read, and keep the actual nonlinear solved-pivot
map. At zero weakening a tagged inverse coefficient can vanish; one
does not try to invert that vanishing weakened coefficient. Pre-expanded
coarse inverses are used, as in the assembly.

7. EXACT ZERO-PARAMETER COMPONENT PROPERTY

Give every fine variable a fixed containing final-cell label. Enlarge
each primitive tag set to a connected closure containing all its
input/output labels and complete coefficient reads. When s=0 outside
an occupied set Y, a nonzero primitive word has its entire connected
read inside ONE component of Y. Hence it cannot connect two components.

Pointwise products and the analytic local nonlinearities preserve this
property when their whole local reads are tagged. The uniformly
contractive fixed-point series preserves it term by term. The identity
part of U_j=exp(i xi_j A_j)U_(j+1) and of(5) only carries data at the
same point. The top input U_t,J_t is point-local in these coordinates,
and (6) has its entire top transport read tagged.

Descending induction therefore proves: the value of the reconstructed
field/current at a root depends only on the inputs and weakening
variables in the occupied component containing its complete protected
root packet. All recursively substituted tails are accounted for by
their own tags. This is a property of the DECLARED new s-family; it
is not a property of a family that weakens only A_j.

8. A CONDITIONAL ROOTED CAUCHY CATALOGUE CONSEQUENCE

Suppose a fixed source f, including its entire filling packet, is
admitted on this whole S-polydisc image and satisfies

  sup_|s|<=S |f(U_0(s),J_0(s))|<=epsilon_source.    (8)

This source admission/bound must be supplied; field-value bounds alone
are not a source norm theorem. Fix s=1 on the protected connected root
set R. The finite multivariable fundamental-theorem expansion around
zero on the remaining cells writes the source at s=1 as a sum indexed
by activated cell sets. By Section7, any term with a component not
attached to R vanishes after its derivatives are taken.

For a connected Y containing R, each activated variable is differentiated
once and integrated over[0,1]. Cauchy on circles of radius r<S-1 gives

  ||f_Y||<=epsilon_source r^{-|Y\R|}.              (9)

The coefficient depends only on the COMPLETE data in Y. The number of
connected sets with n additional cells is at most C_R C_an^n on the
fixed-dimensional final-cell graph. Therefore, for any a with

  C_an exp(a)<r,

the rooted norm obeys

  sum_(Y connected,Y superset R) exp(a|Y|)||f_Y||
    <= C_R exp(a|R|) epsilon_source /
                                    [1-C_an exp(a)/r]. (10)

Choose S sufficiently large FIRST, then all field/current/noise caps.
This uses one final-scale source radius, not a contraction per date.
It is a catalogue of the raw whole-prefix source function, before
normalized noise integration or the old native reblocking ledger.

Restoring fine-root position counts for one final cell costs at most
C R_t^4=C M_*^4 L^{4t} in four dimensions. Additional fixed-order packet
derivative counts can introduce fixed powers of t or R_t, but not an
unjustified exponential in R_t^4. For t=O(log(1/g)), these fixed powers
are polynomial in1/g. This is the reason to retain connected complete
word reads instead of replacing them by filled balls with a tree-size
weight exponential in their volume.

9. WHAT IS NOT CERTIFIED HERE

The construction specifies the additional weakening work required for
frames, top transport, P1, local current stencils and all full branches.
Its application requires an explicit inventory showing that every
primitive of the chosen source family follows these rules. Numerical
map bounds alone do not establish that inventory. In particular(8)
must be verified for the actual cavity filling tube.

There is also a distinct domain interface: dependence only on data in
Y, proved on a global admitted input chart, defines a function on the
RESTRICTION of that chart to Y. It does not by itself extend the function
to every element of the abstract carrier-own-field domain U^c(Y).
Such an extension needs a bounded complete-read extension construction
or a separate intrinsic definition. A cutoff of a relative potential
can supply one on a specified fixed-reference chart with a sufficient
collar, but that is not automatically every native union-of-orbits
carrier domain. This note does not erase that distinction.

No normalized complex expectation, denominator lower bound, old
canonical scalar comparison, inherited near/far expansion, mixed-history
contact or continuum limit is proved. The Cauchy criterion(8)--(10)
separates a concrete complete-read locality statement from these other
obligations.
