At n=lambda R_b with sufficiently small fixed lambda, the negative
rate exceeds the positive filling entropy. Its exponent and the

padding must consume only a fixed spare fraction of n.

The remaining exp(-c r^2) terms, including their fixed inverse

Neither a late-age theorem nor a probability lower bound on patch

a polylogarithmic rare factor alone does not pay it.
