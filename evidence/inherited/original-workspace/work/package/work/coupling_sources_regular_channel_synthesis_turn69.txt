1. WHAT IS NOW ATTACHED TO THE ORIGINAL WILSON MODEL

The temporal construction previously handled bounded magnetic sources.
It now handles analytic changes of electric AND magnetic couplings on
every fixed finite spatial SU2 graph.

Let the sourced transfer operator be

 M_(delta,z)=exp(-delta V_z/2),
 beta_e(delta,z)=2/[delta kappa_e(z)].

The proof's new ingredient is the uniform additive estimate

 sup_(nu>=1)|I_nu(beta)/I_1(beta)
             -exp[-(nu^2-1)/(2beta)]| <= C/|beta|

in a fixed right complex sector at large |beta|. It follows by
comparing the exact Fourier integral to a Gaussian in L1. The
error is additive, so it remains valid at arbitrarily large spin;
there is no assertion of uniform relative error against a tiny
Gaussian coefficient.

With finitely many links this gives

 ||T_(delta,z)^n|| <= exp(C n delta)

on one complex source disc at the fixed spatial regulator.
Real-source transfer convergence supplies the limit on a real
interval. Holomorphic compactness and uniqueness then give
locally uniform complex convergence and every fixed source jet.
The uniform O(delta) electric error is used for stability;
it is not incorrectly summed to prove convergence.

All endpoint ground states and the baseline energy stay unsourced.
No sourced complex ground-state convergence is needed.

The original Wilson expectation differs from the normalized transfer
expression by an explicit source-dependent scalar:

The changed spatial action has half weights on each slab boundary
and full weights on its interior slices. The changed electric action

stay fixed. Thus this is a literal original Wilson source.

The scalar n L_delta generally diverges in the temporal limit.
At the SAME finite period and temporal spacing it satisfies

 Delta_h^2 log Z_orig,delta,N,n
                   =Delta_h^2 log F_delta,N,n

EXACTLY, because it is affine in the slab length n. No divergent
term is silently discarded. The second action derivative for a
nonlinear source parametrization is also affine in n and cancels.

Choosing the period after the temporal spacing controls the trace
remainder uniformly on the fixed source disc. With n delta -> T and

This closes the fixed-spatial electric/coupling source-jet gate left

The exact selected source recursion contains

Summing the birth terms alone does not control this recursion.
The existing contractive extracted space assumes translation and

The classical weighted-action identity does not itself give a
bounded propagator for every generated inhomogeneous error.

A possible next step is a bounded classical propagator on a
position-dependent marginal tensor space, combined with an exact
modulation of the already controlled constant-source solution.

There is also an independent full-history admission problem:
the fixed analytic selected-map discs are not already available
on every mixed native R/B domain. Old-source and denominator
factors remain in the exact replacement identity. Neither this
turn's birth bounds nor TURN68's simple action-cell bridge bound
removes those obligations.
