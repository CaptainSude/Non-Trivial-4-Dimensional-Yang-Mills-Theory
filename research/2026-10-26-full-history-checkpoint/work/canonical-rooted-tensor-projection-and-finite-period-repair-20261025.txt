CANONICAL ROOTED TENSOR PROJECTION AND FINITE-PERIOD REPAIR
25 October 2026. Root construction for the original marked source class.

SCOPE

This supplies a linear, representation-independent tensor projection for
an admitted source germ at EACH fixed original source label. It avoids
assuming that a covariant profile has a scalar affine tensor. It also
avoids assigning the projection to different output roots depending on
the localization carrier. Complete source norms, own-domain flat balls
and the source-root/arm reserve are the existing marked-column inputs.
The construction does not establish the full two-source original-law
comparison or a continuum state.

1. RETAIN THE ORIGINAL SOURCE LABEL BEFORE LOCALIZATION

At a current homogeneous date, let p be an original source label and let
F_p(U,J) be the WHOLE coherent source germ for that label, before choosing
one particular coefficient representation. An arbitrary original profile
forms sum_p f_p F_p. The label p includes an orientation/type if needed.
The actual source compiler and its normalizer are linear in F_p, and the
number f_p stays outside every field/noise operation.

Each coefficient H of a representation of F_p retains its complete read
and an arm to the current image of p. Let R(H,p) bound the resulting
current-scale diameter and rooted distance, including the fixed mother.
Suppose that the original positive norm has a spare exponential moment:

               sum_H exp(kappa R(H,p)) M_(H,p) <= M_p.       (1)

Use the actual coefficient/root measure in this formula; it is not a new
counting measure on the original fine links. Every own read has a common
flat-reference ball of radius r in its stipulated current C1,alpha field
and independent-current norm. Each coefficient has value at most M_(H,p)
on that ball. The physical source construction supplies intrinsic gauge
invariance and real type, or its already admitted compact gauge-average
coefficient representation. No gauge averaging of an arbitrary profile
or averaging of the original source labels is performed here.

Fix Y(p), a current reference cell determined only by p and the fixed
scaffold. It must NOT depend on H or on a chosen response/cluster graph.
If source-only tent allocation is used, fix its finitely supported weights
h_y(p) at this point, with sum_y h_y(p)=1; every occurrence of F_p uses
the SAME weights and reference cells. All statements below apply termwise
to that fixed allocation. A retained original source may have many graphs,
but it does not acquire a new source label after localization.

2. LITERAL TWENTY-ONE LOCAL REFERENCE FUNCTIONS

Index the six positive curvature planes by a. At each ORIGINAL fine
anchor v, let P_a(v) be the elementary loop in that plane based at v.
All six loops use the same gauge base. Put

 h_a(v)=1-(1/2)Tr P_a(v),
 K_a(v)=(P_a(v)-P_a(v)^(-1))/(2i),
 X_ab(v)=(1/2)Tr[K_a(v)K_b(v)],  a<b.                    (2)

These are literal original gauge-invariant functions. On SU(2) they are
real; on the admitted complex group they are holomorphic. K_a is
traceless. At an affine connection with curvature t F,

 [t^2]h_a(v)=(eta^4/4)Tr F_a^2,
 [t^2]X_ab(v)=(eta^4/2)Tr(F_a F_b).                      (3)

Thus the six h_a and fifteen X_ab are an affine basis for the real
symmetric 6 by 6 plane tensor, with quadratic form
q_T(F)=(1/4) sum_(a,b) T_ab Tr(F_a F_b).
No rotation symmetry of T is assumed. Global color invariance supplies
the invariant color pairing at degree two; it does not identify planes.

For a unit current reference cell Y sum each basis function over its
eta^-4 original fine anchors, assigning crossing loops by anchor and
adjoining their genuine halo. Denote the resulting tensor-linear map by
Omega_Y(T). Then its affine quadratic tensor is exactly T. For an M-cell,
divide by its actual current volume M^4 when normalizing the tensor;
retain that fixed factor everywhere in the associated density convention.

Let kappa_cap be the actual total curvature cap. Under the admitted
bound |log P_a|<=C eta^2 kappa_cap, every summand in (2) is
O(eta^4 kappa_cap^2). Hence

                 ||Omega_Y(T)|| <= C kappa_cap^2 |T|.    (4)

In the common all-cap classes kappa_cap/r is a fixed bounded hierarchy
constant, so this is C r^2 |T|. Otherwise keep the explicit factor
(kappa_cap/r)^2 in every projected bound below; a small flat extraction
ball alone does not make a larger curvature cap small.

This is a current-cell statement with the ORIGINAL fine census retained.
It does not bound the extensive sum over all cells by a constant.
The sum of the references over the L^4 child cells is exactly the parent
reference under raw copying, before normalizing to per-cell density.
This follows because the same original fine anchors/loops occur once.

3. A CANONICAL TOTAL-GERM TENSOR AT FINITE PERIOD

Let the current torus have side P much larger than the fixed source
mother. Choose one smooth scalar cutoff chi, equal to one in the ball
of radius 1/8 and zero outside radius 1/4. Use its periodic translate
about the actual physical anchor of p expressed in current coordinates,
without rounding it to a parent-cell center. Coordinates below use that
translate of the fundamental domain; the cutoff is zero near its seam.
It may be chosen invariant under the scaffold's signed permutations.

For a constant Lie-algebra-valued curvature F define

 a_F,mu(u)=-(1/2) sum_nu F_(mu nu) u_nu,
 A_(P,p),F(u)=chi(u/P) a_F(u),        J=0.                (5)

This is a legitimate smooth periodic finite direction, not a globally
affine torus gauge field. Restrict it to the actual fine bond positions.
For each finite P, small enough t lies in the global flat ball, so the
quadratic coefficient of F_p(exp(i eta t A_(P,p),F),0) exists. It is a
linear functional of the WHOLE germ F_p. Color invariance and polarization
identify a symmetric plane tensor, denoted E_(P,p)F_p, by

 [t^2]F_p(exp(i eta t A_(P,p),F),0)=q_(E_(P,p)F_p)(F).    (6)

It is sufficient to use one normalized color direction and polarize its
six plane amplitudes; color invariance supplies the other directions.
This tensor is real for a real-type source. Constants have zero tensor.
The first field/current derivative at the flat pair vanishes by color
invariance. Equation (6) is a second derivative, not an assertion that
a nonzero affine background belongs to an infinite-volume source domain.
The corner-loop references in (2) need not be separately covariant under
every Euclidean reflection about the parent cell. No such claim is used:
their tensors span the required space, and the exact source is preserved
by recombination. A separate symmetry claim for the projected pieces
would require its own covariant reference construction.

The cutoff direction has no seam jump. On a complete read H its actual
C1,alpha norm is bounded by C(1+R(H,p))|F|: near p it is affine, and
farther away the derivative of chi(u/P) is O(1/P), multiplied by a
potential of size O(P). The Holder seminorm has the same bound uniformly
in the fine mesh. Thus own-domain Banach Cauchy and (1) give

             |E_(P,p)F_p| <= C r^-2 M_p,                 (7)

with a fixed spent moment reserve and no P-dependent constant. It is
important to use a SMOOTH cutoff. A discontinuous periodic affine field
would introduce an avoidable fine-mesh seam derivative.

For P large enough that Y(p) and its loop halo lie in the inner affine
ball, (3) gives

                  E_(P,p) Omega_(Y(p))(T)=T              (8)

exactly, at this finite P. Consequently

 Pi_(P,p) F_p=Omega_(Y(p))(E_(P,p)F_p),
 N_(P,p) F_p=F_p-F_p(1,0)-Pi_(P,p)F_p                    (9)

are well-defined linear maps on coherent germs at p, and Pi^2=Pi.
If two representations have the same F_p for every independent pair,
they give exactly the same (9). Multiplication by any original number
f_p commutes with these maps. For fixed source-only tents replace
Omega_Y by sum_y h_y(p) Omega_y, whose tensor is still T by (8).

To bound the coefficient representation, apply (6) linearly to each
coefficient but use the SAME reference cell/tent for every H at p.
Adjoin that fixed cell to its complete arm/carrier. The original-root
containment and fixed mother reserve pay the enlargement. The bound
|E F_(H,p)|<=C r^-2(1+R(H,p))^2 M_(H,p), together with (4), restores
the projected value bound after the spare exponential pays the moment.
The tensor part and the neutral part recombine to F_p EXACTLY.

The cutoff can also be coherent across dates. Use the SAME physical
cutoff about the SAME original physical point p: P_j is the physical torus side
divided by the current cell length, hence P_(j+1)=P_j/L. Under the
declared raw-copy static map, a new potential pulls back as
A_old(u)=L^-1 A_new(u/L), with eta_new=eta_old/L and J=0 unchanged.
Formula (5) therefore pulls back EXACTLY as

 L^-1 A_(P_(j+1),p),F(u/L)=L^-2 A_(P_j,p),F(u).

Linearity of the potential in F gives the exact finite-period identity

       E_(j+1,p)(S_j F_p)=L^-4 E_(j,p)(F_p).              (9a)

This is the tensor coefficient for ONE retained source label. Summing
the L^4 child-cell reference densities gives the parent density and
cancels L^-4 for a homogeneous intensive source. Those two conventions
must not be confused. Gauge-return gradients, if part of the declared
raw-copy convention, leave this statement unchanged for the intrinsic
gauge-invariant source; the independent current remains zero.

4. DO NOT DECLARE THE ENTIRE FINITE-PERIOD REMAINDER NEUTRAL

The exact canonical map (9) is not by itself the hypothesis of the
unwrapped-affine neutral static theorem. Split the coefficient list into
short reads wholly within the inner affine ball and the remaining whole
reads. The latter must retain their full functions; denote their sum by L.
They have R(H,p)>=cP after adjusting the fixed mother constants.

For each short coefficient put T_(H,p)=E_(P,p)F_(H,p). Because (5) is
affine on its WHOLE read and on Y(p), the function

 N_(H,p)=F_(H,p)-F_(H,p)(1,0)-Omega_(Y(p))(T_(H,p))       (10)

has zero full unwrapped-affine quadratic tensor individually. It is an
eligible neutral birth under the direct C1,alpha static theorem, which
allows direct full tensor neutrality without a scalar symmetry premise.
Independent-current mixed and quadratic jets are retained, not erased.

The exact finite decomposition of (9) is

 N_(P,p)F_p = sum_short N_(H,p)
                    +(L-L(1,0))-Omega_(Y(p))(E_(P,p)L). (11)

The last whole function and its marginal correction are a separate
finite-period ledger. They are not given neutral contraction. With a
weaker reserved carrier exponent their combined norm is at most

                      C exp(-cP) M_p,                  (12)

or C(1+P)^m exp(-cP) M_p if the native whole-long estimate keeps the
moment polynomial explicit. Both are sufficient at fixed prefix.
There is no second subtraction of a long Wilson/tensor coefficient.
All vacuum scalars remain in the source ledger. If a complete read
wraps, it belongs to L as a WHOLE function, not to a clipped coefficient.

Equations (9)--(11) solve two distinct issues: exact quotient descent
uses the canonical total tensor; neutral decay applies only to the
short intrinsically tensor-neutral coefficients. A representation
change can alter these short/long lists, but not their recombined germ.

5. PHYSICAL AFFINE MATCHING AND PARAMETER ORDER

There is also a useful purely joint-field comparison BEFORE physical
matching. Let y be a current point root and suppose only original p at
bounded current distance from y occur in its fixed tent allocation.
For a short intrinsic coefficient whose whole read lies in the affine
cores about both p and y, the two directions A_(P,p),F and A_(P,y),F
differ there by a constant potential. Such a potential is a local
linear gauge gradient. The linearized gauge Ward identity at the flat
pair, with first derivative zero, annihilates its mixed Hessian against
every field/current direction. Hence

                         E_(P,p)F_H=E_(P,y)F_H

for these short coefficients. Use a contractible complete read and its
gauge extension; this is not a global torus constant-gradient claim.
The remaining whole coefficients reach distance cP from their original
root, so their difference has the same rooted-tail and Cauchy-moment
bound as (12). Therefore, for any fixed source-only weights h_y(p),

 sum_p h_y(p) E_(P,p) F_p
       =E_(P,y)[sum_p h_y(p)F_p]+delta_(P,y).             (13)

The defect tends to zero at each fixed prefix, uniformly in the current
root under the stated rooted norms. The SAME comparison applies after
Q, with the coefficient bounds of its complete unprojected source
column. A crude fixed-prefix bound on the separate original-p terms
is allowed for this exponentially small defect. It must be available
before and independently of a later improved uniform-template bound.

This qualification matters for the energy cone. A uniform template may
have zero neutral TOTAL germ after cancellation between original p's,
while the sum of norms of its separate p pieces is of order one. To
retain that cancellation, apply the quantum operator and common E_y to
the WHOLE current-root template. Equation (13) compares its tensor with
the exact original-p prescription. Carry delta as a finite-period
marginal error; do not give it neutral contraction or silently bound
the template by its larger separate-p envelope. No function-germ limit
is needed for this finite-prefix comparison.

For physical calibration one must still match (6) to the actual flat
minimum/current lift. The earlier complete B99 physical Hessian bridge
provides the relevant local comparison: on a truly affine polynomial,
the lifted field is that affine field up to the recorded gauge gradient,
and the linear physical current is zero. All AA, AJ and JJ Hessian
branches are retained; the source first derivative vanishes, so there
is no missing first-derivative/second-lift term.

Apply the exponential flat-lift kernels to the cutoff-affine field (5).
On a fixed inner fraction of the P ball its difference from the true

bounded by C_N (1+P)^m exp(-c_N P)|F| for each fixed prefix N. The same
is true of the current branch and recorded local axial return. On
source coefficients reaching that remote region use their own rooted
tail (1), rather than a pointwise affine-field assertion. Thus the
canonical joint tensor and the corresponding physical finite-cutoff
tensor differ by a fixed-prefix error tending to zero with P.

This is the tensor-polarized use of the previously checked flat lift,
not a new assertion of convergence of source functions as P tends to
infinity. No bound uniform in N is inferred for these lift constants.
For each finite prefix choose the compatible period afterward, paying

6. WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT CLOSE

The full-tensor projection (9), its original-profile linearity, and the
finite-period separation (11) are concrete constructions. They repair
the scalar and carrier-dependent-root shortcuts. The unprojected source
map still has to be the actual coherent normalized map and to satisfy
its claimed complete own-domain and protected-column bounds. Nothing
here replaces that map or any real old conditional law.

To obtain the energy theorem, one must use these maps to close the
coalescing tensor/profile/neutral norm, keep finite-period errors, and
prove the calibrated two-source comparison with its terminal and full
retained-history terms. Matrix invertibility of a uniform source alone
does not supply those local profile estimates or a positive signal.
