TURN72. 20 October 2026.

THE NONTRIVIAL FOUR-DIMENSIONAL CONTINUUM EXISTENCE PROOF IS INCOMPLETE.

1. THE EXACT QUESTION ADDRESSED

For the original positive probability P, the same physical observables
F,H on both sides, and a frozen selection weight 0<=w<=1, put

 p(s,t)=E_P[w exp(sF+tH)]/E_P[exp(sF+tH)].

The exact identity retained from TURN71 is

 Cov_P(F,H)-Cov_(wP/Pw)(F,H)=-partial_st log p(0,0).       (1.1)

A small selected mass is not itself a proof of a large correction.
Conversely, a useful selected covariance estimate does not control
the right side of (1.1). This turn computes or bounds that correction
in three specified settings, keeping the sources and normalizers.
The settings are not identified with one another.

2. ACTUAL NATIVE FIRST-CLASS LABEL SELECTION

Assume the complete positive native representation and original-marginal
identifications at the scope explicitly retained in TURN62. At a single
native R date, let N_S count the declared first-class component labels
meeting a set S of current cells. The hereditary insertion theorem has
deterministic activities with census

 Lambda_S=sum_(X meeting S) barlambda_X <= |S| epsilon_k.

Multiplying a family weight by exp(-theta N_S) preserves its insertion
inequality, with the marked insertion cost multiplied by exp(-theta).
It follows, with the tilted outer distribution included, that

 E_theta N_S<=exp(-theta)Lambda_S.

For bounded ORIGINAL observables F,H, differentiating their covariance
under this real count penalty and integrating theta from zero to
infinity proves

 |Cov_(P(.|N_S=0))(F,H)-Cov_P(F,H)|
       <=(5/4) osc(F) osc(H) Lambda_S,                    (2.1)
 P(N_S=0)>=exp(-Lambda_S).

This is an original-source estimate. It does not require replacing an
old observable by its formula on a new reference field. No denominator
has been discarded and no complex source-admission theorem is used.
There is also a deterministic soft-label version with
Lambda_S replaced by sum_X barlambda_X(1-r_X), 0<=r_X<=1.

If a complete finite mass-preserving continuation retains the already
born counts as passive records, their earlier marginals survive.
An all-date union bound and the exact covariance mixture identity give

 |Cov_(P(.|all selected absences))(F,H)-Cov_P(F,H)|
 <=(3/2) osc(F) osc(H) sum_k |S_k|epsilon_k,              (2.2)

when the selected event has positive probability. This is not a product
of unjustified conditional probability estimates.

The limitation is quantitative and explicit. For g_r^-2 comparable to
Gamma+b r, epsilon_r=exp[-c(log(Gamma+b r))^p], p>1, the rooted-lineage
sum is finite. But a fixed physical region contains O(L^(4r)) current
cells. The resulting census upper bound does not tend to zero. Also,
global source oscillations can themselves grow with the ultraviolet
cutoff. Neither (2.1) nor (2.2) uses source separation.

This does not prove that the actual error diverges. It proves that this
particular bound cannot close the physical estimate. Replacing global
oscillation by a local source sensitivity requires an actual comparison
of old-source laws across erasure, not just the unmarked label erasure.
Second-class regions and TURN70's projected windows remain different
selections.

3. THE EXACT PROJECTED GAUSSIAN CORRECTION

On an admitted fixed background, use the actual tangent Hessian and
projection K=H^(-1/2)P. The first two fluctuation jets of the fixed
physical sources have the ambient form

 F=c_F+g l_F.xi+(g^2/2)xi^t A xi,
 H=c_H+g l_H.xi+(g^2/2)xi^t B xi.

The pullbacks include the complete nonlinear chart second derivative.
For exactly affine-quadratic sources the following is an exact
covariance formula. For general analytic sources it is only the stated
Gaussian polynomial part, not the complete nonlinear order-g^4 answer.

Let the ambient q-component blocks be independent standard Gaussians,
selected by |xi_i|<=T. Define

 v=E_T|xi_i|^2/q,  m4=E_T|xi_i|^4/[q(q+2)],
 d=1-v,  kappa=m4-v^2.

Direct radial integration gives
 d=a_q T^q exp(-T^2/2)/(q c_T),
 m4=1-d(1+T^2/(q+2)),
 kappa=d(v-T^2/(q+2))<=0.

The exact full-Gaussian minus selected-Gaussian covariance is

 Delta_T =
   g^2 d l_F.l_H
   +(g^4/2)(1-v^2)Tr(AB)
   -(g^4 kappa/4)sum_i[tr A_ii tr B_ii+2tr(A_ii B_ii)].   (3.1)

It is positive semidefinite as a bilinear form on real affine-quadratic
sources. Its coefficients are O(exp(-T^2/4)), uniformly in the number
of blocks. With the actual complete source and background-read
catalogues, the contractions give

 ||Delta_T||_(k;two sources)
 <=C_q exp(-T^2/4)[g^2 L_F L_H+g^4 A_F A_H].             (3.2)

This norm has no hidden volume factor. Connected union carriers
contain both source anchors and give the displayed source-separation
factor in the detailed proof. The generally nonzero first-jet term
must remain for spatially varying profiles.

The selected Gaussian mass can be c_T^n and tend to zero. It cancels
from the mixed derivative because it is source independent, while
the source-dependent partition functions remain.

There is an additional important term under an outer background law.
Even when c_T^n is independent of that background, selection changes
the conditional means from c_F+g^2 Tr A/2 to c_F+g^2 v Tr A/2.
Their outer covariance changes too; the detailed proof gives its exact
formula. Therefore (3.2) is not the full outer correlation correction.

4. ACTUAL NONLINEAR WILSON BLOCKS

For a fixed SU(2) rectangular block with sufficiently weak Dirichlet
boundary, the original Wilson measure has a unique weak minimum in
boundary-rooted forest gauge and a global quadratic action bound.
It consequently has normalized Gaussian-scale distance moments and
tails, with its original partition function.

ARCHIVAL CREDIT: this bare coercivity mechanism was already proved
in the earlier fixed-box work. TURN37 strengthens it to explicit
polylogarithmically growing boxes and supplies a bare original-law
collar probability estimate and first-average admission. The present
fixed-box proof is a self-contained rederivation, not a newly discovered
global action theorem. Its added use is the following mixed-source
estimate for a frozen projected product window.

For a smooth source F let
 a_F=|dF(x_b)|, b_F=(1/2)sup||Hess F||,
 A_F(g)=g a_F+g^2 b_F,
where x_b is the actual conditional action minimum. The projected
window is defined using the physical local chart plus a mass-one
Gaussian normal extension, and is zero outside that chart.

The full nonlinear compact Wilson probability then satisfies

 |Cov_full(F,H|b)-Cov_selected(F,H|b)|
       <=C A_F(g)A_H(g)exp(-cT^2),                       (4.1)

for sufficiently large T and small gT. No interaction is evaluated at
arbitrarily large tangent coordinates and no Gaussian approximation
is substituted for the compact law.

At flat boundary, weighted plaquette actions have a_F=a_H=0, so
the bound is C g^4 exp(-cT^2). For the actions divided by g^2 it
is C exp(-cT^2). At nonflat weak boundary the correct dependence
is C(g|log b|+g^2)^2 exp(-cT^2); the boundary term cannot be dropped.

A boundary-tree filling argument transports this result in d=4 to
a gauge-invariant event controlling the boundary plaquette curvatures.
The boundary two-skeleton is simply connected. Every tree fundamental
loop is a finite product of conjugated boundary plaquette words, so
small boundary plaquettes give small links in that fixed tree gauge.
The metric window is gauge invariant; a projected window requires
its stated residual-conjugation covariance. With boundary curvature
threshold delta, normalized action sources retain the factor
(1+delta/g)^2.

The estimate is uniform in the surrounding volume conditional on the
admitted fixed boundary. Its constants depend on the block. The
TURN37 bare first-step boundary results remain available, but neither
those results nor (4.1) identify the chronological generated constrained
fibers with this bare conditional law. Averaging a conditional covariance
also requires retaining the covariance of conditional means.

5. A SUFFICIENT INTERACTING EXTENSION AND A TEST OF ITS HYPOTHESES

There is a volume-uniform all-orders extension for a specified
high-temperature model: a bounded-degree graph of Gaussian blocks,
globally small edge Mayer activities

 sup_(all real coordinates)|exp V_e-1|<=epsilon,

and quadratic-growth sources on a fixed number of sites. An interpolation
between the full and ball-truncated base probabilities gives one signed
Gaussian tail insertion in every difference term. The connected polymer
expansion retains the exact source normalizations and yields

 |Cov_full(F_A,H_B)-Cov_selected(F_A,H_B)|
 <=C M_F M_H exp(-cT^2) exp[-kappa dist(A,B)].            (5.1)

The proof and an independent check are appended. The actual selected
Yang--Mills remainder is only admitted on a bounded weak chart; it
does not satisfy this global activity hypothesis on the available
evidence. Already one Wilson plaquette has a Gaussian-relative
interaction exponentially large at tangent radius of order 1/g.
That observation does not prevent a global coercivity argument, but
it prevents invoking the bounded-Mayer theorem directly.

The compact positive analytic model
 S(b)=b^2(1-b^2)^2/2, Q(b)=b^2, -2<=b<=2
provides a precise test of a tempting shortcut. Its Gaussian-normalized
partition tends to 2 and its interaction is small on a logarithmic
window about zero. Nevertheless the full variance of Q tends to 1/4,
whereas the selected variance tends to zero: the other two wells carry
finite physical mass. This is not a Yang--Mills counterexample. It
shows why local analytic smallness plus partition stability cannot
replace the missing global comparison.

6. WHAT IS NEEDED TO REACH THE EXISTENCE CLAIM

The new estimates narrow the missing full-source comparison. They do
not eliminate it. A useful next producer must control both original
source legs, their coarse conditional means, and the complete omitted
histories with a spatially local bound that survives the ultraviolet
cell census. Possible inputs include a full constrained-fiber
coercivity/activity estimate or a connected marked native-history
estimate. A theorem for an unrelated bare block or Gaussian reference
cannot be substituted for that producer.

Even success on that upper-bound task would not finish the existence
proof. The retained obligations also include:

 - compatible renormalized local continuum observables and products;
 - a nonzero physical limiting witness, with a positive-energy
   criterion or another argument excluding a purely static remnant;
 - one common limiting law with the required positivity, locality,
   continuum symmetries and reconstruction hypotheses.
