Research continuation, TURN82, 20 October 2026

The nontrivial continuum four-dimensional Yang--Mills existence proof

Their full generating function and mixed moments are retained. The proof

This implements the useful exact-source lesson of the new QED3 outlines:

1. FINITE THEOREM, INCLUDING THE FULL SOURCE FAMILY

Let mu_beta be the SU(2) Wilson probability law on a finite periodic
four-dimensional lattice of spacing a, with nondegenerate periods at
least four and an even temporal period. Use the site reflection theta
with its two fixed temporal slices and the original Wilson reflection
positivity. Gradients use the product invariant metric for which
i sigma_1/2, i sigma_2/2, i sigma_3/2 are orthonormal on each link.

Let B_1,...,B_m be measurable gauge-invariant original observables,
0<=B_i<=1. Their COMPLETE original-link read set S must lie strictly in
the positive time half. Suppose its distance from BOTH fixed slices,
including link endpoints, is at least a fixed physical d>0.

There is an explicit positive selfadjoint stationary Markov contraction
Q on L2(mu_beta), constructed below, which preserves gauge invariance,
commutes with theta, and expands a read by one incident-plaquette collar.
Put

 n=floor(d/(4a)),
 B_i^[n]=Q^n B_i,
 H_n(s;U)=Q^n exp(sum_i s_i B_i)(U).                         (1.1)

For sufficiently small a relative to d, n>=1 and all these reads remain
strictly in the positive half with a fixed residual physical margin.
The following assertions hold for the exact finite original law.

(a) State and normalization preservation.
Writing R F=F composed with theta as a LINEAR geometric pullback and
OS(F,G)=E_mu[overline(R F)G], we have

 [Q^n F]_OS=[F]_OS,
 E_mu H_n(s)=E_mu exp(sum_i s_i B_i).                         (1.2)

The first identity applies to every bounded F with the declared buffered
read, including the full source exponential and each of its mixed jets.
It preserves centered reflected Gram matrices. It is not a claim that
Q is multiplicative or that the OS null space is a product ideal.

(b) Analytic ORIGINAL-link representatives at every real center.
For any real U^0 use the local complex exponential coordinates

 U_e(z)=exp(i sum_alpha z_(e,alpha) sigma_alpha/2) U_e^0.

There are numerical constants c,C>0, independent of beta, volume,
the number of reads, n and the input observables, such that H_n has a
holomorphic continuation on

 ||z||_2<c/sqrt(beta+1),
 |H_n(s,z)-1| <= exp(C(beta+1)||Im z||_2^2)(exp(R_s)-1),
 R_s=sum_i |s_i|.                                           (1.3)

Here the sum in the Hilbert norm may be restricted to the complete
n-collar read. On a possibly smaller fixed ball, (1.3) provides a
canonical holomorphic logarithm for a fixed R_s<R_0>0, with log H_n(0,z)=0.
The proof never evaluates a conditional source at complex auxiliary
records and never excludes a small-current event.

(c) Averaged real derivatives without derivatives of the input probe.
For every real bounded F and integer n>=1,

 E_mu |grad Q^n F|^2
       <= (18 beta+24) Var_mu(F)/(2n).                      (1.4)

For each fixed k>=1, there is a constant C_k independent of volume,
beta, read size and n for which

 E_mu ||D^k Q^n F||_op^2
       <= C_k(beta+1)^k Var_mu(F)/(2n).                     (1.5)

D^k denotes the Frechet derivative in the exponential chart based at
each real U, and op is its multilinear Hilbert operator norm. For k>1
this is not asserted to equal a covariant tensor derivative. It is not
a sum over all coordinate k-tuples. These distinctions matter.

For 0<=B<=1 and n as in (1.1), the consequence is

 E_mu ||D^k Q^n B||_op^2 <= C_k (beta+1)^k a/d.              (1.6)

In particular, if beta=O(log(1/a)), the right side tends to zero for
every FIXED k. This is a derivative statement in lattice-link coordinates;
it is neither convergence of the physical field to zero nor a proof of
physical nontriviality. The support is now a four-dimensional time block.

(d) All source contacts are retained.
At s=0,

 partial_(i_1)...partial_(i_r) H_n=Q^n(B_(i_1)...B_(i_r)),
 partial_i partial_j log H_n
       =Q^n(B_i B_j)-(Q^n B_i)(Q^n B_j).                    (1.7)

Repeated indices are allowed. Replacing H_n by exp(sum s_i Q^n B_i)
would lose these terms and is not part of the theorem. Each real raw
source product also lies in[0,1], so (1.4)--(1.6) apply to its smoothed
representative. This is a complete source package, not just separate
first derivatives.

2. PROOF OF THE CHANNEL AND REFLECTED-STATE IDENTITIES

Write a plaquette as U_p=A_p B_p^dagger using its two positive oriented
paths of length two. For each path h=(U_h,V_h), the exact two-HS positive

where every Z_p and W_h is an independent standard complex 2x2 Gaussian
matrix of density pi^-4 exp(-||Z||_F^2), conditional on the complete U.
Its marginal on U is precisely mu_beta. Given the REAL auxiliaries xi,
all original links are independent under their normalized tilted Haar
laws with currents

                 +sum_(second h at e) 2R_h^dagger G_p.      (2.2)

All scalar normalizers belong to this same joint law. Define
C F=E[F(U)|xi], K f=E[f(xi)|U], and P=KC=C*C. Conditional Jensen gives
0<=P<=I, P1=1 and mu_beta P=mu_beta. The read of PF is contained in
the union of edges of plaquettes incident to the original read of F:
(2.2) lists the auxiliary records used, and (2.1) lists their original
reads. Gauge covariance of the joint law implies gauge covariance of P.

A fixed path convention need not commute with reflection. Set

                 Q=(P+RPR)/2.                            (2.3)

It has all the preceding properties and commutes with R. Equivalently,
adjoin an independent fair choice between the two reflected conventions.
Given this label and the corresponding auxiliaries, original links are
still independent. No reflection positivity is assumed for arbitrary
functions of this label.

If F and G have disjoint COMPLETE original-link reads, product

 <F,QG>_mu=E[overline(CF)CG]=E[overline(F)G].                (2.4)

The middle expression includes the convention average. Auxiliary read

6. THE CONTINUUM REQUIREMENTS HAVE NOT DISAPPEARED

The finite smoothing theorem applies to a bounded function of an
original spatially flowed magnetic observable, even without its
nonlinear flow Jacobian estimate. The source must remain a specified
function of the original Wilson links, with its physical normalization
declared. A whole-slice spatial-flow read remains a whole-slice read;
the construction does not manufacture finite spatial support.

There are four essential limits to the present conclusion.

* The Hilbert complex radius is c/sqrt(beta+1), not a fixed complex
  radius in every coordinate on a growing physical region. The averaged
  operator jets are not coefficient-sum polymer norms. A reconstructed
  coarse-field pullback has its own possibly growing derivatives, and
  requires a proved spatially controlled source estimate.

* The smoothed observable occupies a four-dimensional time block.
  Its full product-link gradient is not a one-spatial-slice ground-law
  Dirichlet form. Q is an algorithmic averaging operator, not the physical
  time-transfer operator. Its spectral parameter is not physical energy.

* Reflection-state preservation cannot create a missing state. For a
  specified bounded physical probe, a concrete unresolved target is

     liminf_(a->0) OS(B_a-E B_a,B_a-E B_a)>0                (6.1)

  at fixed positive physical scales, with the required compatible volume
  limit. Large auxiliary currents, ordinary variance and Gaussian noise
  do not prove (6.1). The theorem preserves its value exactly at each
  cutoff and therefore also preserves the possibility that it vanishes.

* Even (6.1) would not finish the construction. Compatible continuum
  observables, limit and regularity estimates, Euclidean symmetries,
  identification with non-Abelian Yang--Mills, and reconstruction remain
  required. No all-history Bałaban source admission, full interacting
  auxiliary RG, or mass-gap conclusion is supplied here.

The strongest next use of these results is as a rigorously normalized
input to a PHYSICAL source estimate. Merely constructing further
algorithmic smoothing identities will not provide the missing signal.

6B. WHAT THE ACTUAL TRANSFER MATRIX ALREADY GIVES

A separate result in Appendix G removes a redundant continuity task.

from the HS smoothing Q in (1.1). The original partition scalar and

 A_F=T^q K_F T^q/sqrt(Tr T^(2N)),
 A_(tau_m F)=T^(q+m)K_F T^(q-m)/sqrt(Tr T^(2N)).

This is an actual cutoff-uniform physical time-continuity bound for

2. POSITIVE DATA AUGMENTATION AND ORIGINAL HEATBATH COMPARISON

For real F in L2(mu), define
  C F(xi)=E[F(U)|xi],
  C* H(U)=E_kappa_U H(xi), P=C*C.                        (2.1)
