STATUS: numerical implication below proved from the displayed inputs.
The conditional covariance estimate (1) is an INPUT, not proved here.
No full source catalogue, outer covariance or YM4 theorem is asserted.

Fix the endpoint physical length unit ell_T and let V denote volume
in those fixed endpoint units. At reset e<=T consider two prepared
source records p,q with actual proper grades

                 e-B_e<=m_p,m_q<=e,
       0<=B_e<=C_B(1+log gamma_e)^p_B,                  (0)

where B_e is a deterministic integer bound and C_B,p_B>=0 are fixed
independently of e,T. A fixed B is included. This also covers a window
reaching the actual h_e=e-Nprep_e when its registered preparation
schedule has Nprep_e bounded by a fixed power of log gamma_e: choose
B_e large enough for that schedule and any fixed additional offset.
It does not cover unrestricted older m. Keep the original source roots,
near preparation, calibration and exact conditional-law read rules.
Condition on the FULL q,y state used by the proposed theorem. Assume
its pointwise supported-state conditional covariance estimate

 |Cov_e(g_p,g_q)| <= C A_p A_q
   exp[C_0(1+R_e)^4-c_0 dist_e(S_p,S_q)^(1/4)].          (1)

Here A_p,A_q are the original prepared source amplitudes. S_p,S_q are
the ACTUAL MUTABLE source seed/read sets in that conditional theorem,
including every local source/flag/query collar which its interaction
or exclusion graph requires. Shared or global q,y arguments are fixed
under this conditioning and create no mutable edge merely by being
read by both sources. We do not require their elliptic field/held-Y
argument closures to be disjoint. Require separation d0>0 of the
mutable sets in fixed physical units and the numerical lower bound

 dist_e(S_p,S_q) >= c_geom(d0/ell_T)L^(T-e).              (2)

The fixed native cell size M can be included in c_geom. A metric which
contracts arbitrary retained islands does not acquire (2) from source
root separation alone. Thus (2), for the conditional theorem's exact
mutable root sets and metric, is part of this consumer's input.

Use the actual already-counted near-source census at fixed m,i:

 sum_(records at m,i) A_R
    <= C N_m Mhat_i L^[-alpha(m-i)],
 N_m <= C(V+1)L^[4(T-m)],
 Mhat_i <= U(1+gamma_i)^D,                               (3)

with alpha>0 and fixed D>=0. The constant includes the fixed-loss
r=m-s preparation, finite source-centred box choices, original safe
core radius and calibration. The complete H,z,role sum has already
been estimated before this census. There is no second auxiliary-root
or unrelated ambient-history count in (3).

Assume the native bounded-step upper comparison

 gamma_a<=gamma_b+omega(b-a), a<=b,
 gamma_a>=Gamma>=3,   R_e<=C_R(1+log gamma_e)^p_R,        (4)

with fixed omega,C_R,p_R. Only this upper comparison and the stated
common weak range are needed for the elementary sum.

For t=m-i>=0 and m in (0), (4) gives
gamma_i<=gamma_e+omega(B_e+t). Therefore

 sum_(i<=m) Mhat_i L^[-alpha(m-i)]
   <= C U(1+gamma_e+omega B_e)^D
          sum_(t>=0)(1+t)^D L^(-alpha t)
   <= C_(alpha,D,omega) U(1+gamma_e+omega B_e)^D.        (5)

Actual lower-age restrictions only reduce this sum. Apply (3)--(5)
to both sources, and then sum at most (B_e+1)^2 proper-grade pairs.
For d=T-e their complete product census is bounded by

 C U^2(V+1)^2 (B_e+1)^2 (1+gamma_e+omega B_e)^(2D)
                         exp[8(d+B_e)log L].            (6)

In particular the B_e density cost is NOT called a fixed constant.
Insert (1)--(2) into (6). With a defined below, the exact sufficient
date series before absorbing the preparation window is

 C U^2(V+1)^2 sum_(e<=T) (B_e+1)^2
     (1+gamma_e+omega B_e)^(2D)
  exp[8(T-e+B_e)log L+C_0(1+R_e)^4
                         -a L^((T-e)/4)].              (7a)

Put P=max(4p_R,p_B,1). The fixed schedule (0) implies
1+gamma_e+omega B_e<=C(1+gamma_e), since a fixed log power divided
by gamma_e is bounded on gamma_e>=3. Also log(B_e+1) is absorbed
by C(1+log gamma_e)^P, while 8B_e log L and C_0(1+R_e)^4 have the
same bound. Thus, using gamma_e<=gamma_T+omega d, (7a) is at most

 C U^2(V+1)^2 sum_(d>=0) (1+gamma_T+omega d)^(2D)
  exp[8d log L+C_1(1+log(gamma_T+omega d))^P
                         -a L^(d/4)],                 (7)

where a=c_0[c_geom d0/ell_T]^(1/4)>0. Extending the finite date sum
to all d>=0 is a positive upper bound, not an added source multiplicity.

This series divided by its displayed U^2 is finite uniformly in the
ultraviolet depth T. U retains the original calibration, possibly c_a;
no cutoff-uniform bound for that calibration is assumed here. To make
its endpoint dependence explicit, use
1+gamma_T+omega d<=(1+gamma_T)(1+omega d) and the fixed-power inequality
(u+v)^P<=C_P(u^P+v^P). This bounds (7) by

 C_(a) U^2(V+1)^2(1+gamma_T)^(2D)
                  exp[C_2(1+log gamma_T)^P],            (8)

where C_(a) is finite and independent of gamma_T,T and the cutoff:
its remaining summand is a polynomial in d times
exp[8d log L+C_2(1+log(1+d))^P-a L^(d/4)]. The negative term
eventually exceeds twice all positive terms. Thus the early-date
tail after factoring (8)'s endpoint dependence is bounded by
C exp[-(a/2)L^(d_min/4)] once d_min exceeds a fixed threshold.

Equation(8) divided by U^2 is ultraviolet uniform at fixed endpoint
coupling, separation and volume. The unnormalized source estimate is
uniform only if U itself has an independently proved uniform bound.
It does NOT assert a bound uniform as d0->0,
gamma_T->infinity, or V->infinity. In particular no weak-coupling
vanishing follows from this elementary estimate alone.

The restriction (0) matters: for older m outside that window, N_m contains the
additional factor L^[4(e-m)] on each arm, while (1)'s distance and
R_e remain at e. This proof provides no compensating older-proper
clock. Nor does it estimate the covariance of two conditional means
under the outer law, or an incoming mixed/contact source row. Those
are distinct consumers. Any actual preparation/dictionary price not
included in Mhat_i, and any mutable selector/query read which invalidates
(2), must remain explicit instead of being absorbed into C. Closing the
conditioning can produce an outer covariance through common q,y; the
present mutable-seed separation does not bound that term.
