6. EXACT COUPLING DEFECT AND ITS LIMIT

On the coupling of Section1 the empty branch has identical two reads.
Therefore

 (F_step-N_step)f
   = E_act[ 1_(X nonempty){f(Phi_X)-f(Phi_empty)} | new ]. (8)

In particular for a uniform pointwise bound |f|<=a,

 |(F_step-N_step)f|
     <= 2a Prob_act(X nonempty | new).                 (9)

This event is GLOBAL. To replace it by removal of a source-connected
owner requires the actual complete-read/localizer/CI estimate. Minima
have nonlocal responses; a distant component cannot be deleted from
(8) just by distance or a numerical label. Equations(8)--(9) alone
therefore do not provide the strong original-density rare-exit bound.

For linear raw maps the last-defect Duhamel identity is exact:

 F_(n:0)=N_(n:0)
     +sum_k N_(n:k+1)(F_k-N_k)F_(k:0),                (10)

where F_(b:a)=F_(b-1)...F_a, the N product uses the same convention,
and the sum is k=0,...,n-1; empty products are identities. The rare
difference in this orientation receives the FULL actual prefix, while
only real neutral maps follow it. Thus(10) does not require retyping
an auxiliary N-prefix as a complex input to a later rare exit.

Equations(8)--(10) here use the unrestricted N. On marked rows the same
linear telescoping identity holds with H in place of N, but then

             F-H = (F-N)+(N-H).

The second summand is the read-release/domain-exit remainder. It is not
automatically supported on X nonempty and has no rare-owner estimate
from(8). The actual source-zero body remains separate and unchanged in
this marked-row identity. An age estimate for H does not dispose of
this remainder or imply an age estimate for the unrestricted N product.

Moreover a mass-weighted estimate for a defect is not automatically
preserved by N or H. To apply(7) after a defect, its emitted complete record
must satisfy a pointwise weighted bound of the form(4) on the actual
remaining unresolved slots, with a coherent birth certificate. Neither
the global probability bound(9) nor a scalar rarity factor proves this.
The actual localized strong-norm defect consumer remains separate.

  <= C_(delta,D) V (1+gamma_T+A)^D exp(-delta A)

exp(-delta A)(1+gamma_T+A)^D

Two-arm mixed catalogues need their actual two-entry count and summed-
