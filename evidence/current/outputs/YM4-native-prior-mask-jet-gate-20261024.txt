2. PROVED INTERIOR SINGLE/PAIR SUBMERSION

Assume the relevant plaquettes, free variations and their complete
coarse-row pivot repairs are inside the active region. Fix one or two
distinct plaquettes p,q. Each has an edge absent from the other.
Vary one such edge of p in its left Lie-algebra tangent. The derivative
of the plaquette holonomy with respect to that edge is an invertible
adjoint transport. This can produce any desired radial derivative
of r_p; it leaves r_q unchanged. Correct every affected coarse row
using alternative crossing pivots outside BOTH plaquette boundaries.
Those corrections leave both plaquette holonomies unchanged. Repeat
for q. The resulting vector fields X_p,X_q satisfy exactly

 X_p r_p=1, X_p r_q=0, X_q r_p=0, X_q r_q=1,
             D(coarse average) X_p=0,
             D(coarse average) X_q=0.                       (1)

Their free-coordinate norms, first derivatives and divergences are
bounded by fixed-L constants and the harmless inverse positive radial
scale. Their support is a fixed-L enlargement of the original mask
reads. The coefficients are smooth near the positive cut, since both
the radial color direction and the private crossing coefficient are
smooth there. Bounds on a slightly thickened cutoff annulus are
uniform. This proves a lower bound for the one- and two-normal Gram
matrices in the actual fiber; it is not merely linearized rank at1.

Gauge masks have an exact, simpler transverse direction. An interior
fine gauge transformation at x, equal to1 at every coarse center,
preserves every plaquette norm and the coarse average. Covariance of
M_yx gives independent right multiplication at this one endpoint;
the other gauge variables with different endpoints are unchanged.
It therefore changes the chosen gauge radius freely. For a mixed
plaquette/gauge pair, subtract this fine gauge direction from the
plaquette variation to cancel its gauge-radius derivative. For two
gauge masks use their two endpoint directions. The same local bounds
follow, provided the complete gauge-orbit read is active.

No claim of independence for THREE or more masks is needed. The
Abelian Bianchi identity can make six cube-face normals dependent.
It does not invalidate(1) for distinct interior pairs.
