1. THE SAME PHYSICAL GROUND ENERGY CAN USE AN UNGAUGED TRIAL

Each plaquette has four distinct edges and |P_sp|=3n.
The metric is the product metric in which i x.sigma/2
has squared norm|x|^2. Thus it is four times the round
unit-quaternion metric, and one-link K has eigenvalues j(j+1).

For fixed finite graph,a,g, H_a is a uniformly elliptic
Schrodinger operator on the connected compact manifold X

kernel and the bounded Feynman--Kac weight give this directly.
The lowest eigenvalue is therefore simple with a strictly
positive smooth eigenfunction Omega, normalized in L2.

The gauge group acts by isometries and preserves S_sp.
Every gauge transform of Omega is another normalized positive

            inf_spec(H_a on L2(X))

A normalized ungauged product trial is consequently a legal

This is the Hamiltonian rigorously obtained at fixed spatial

For q=(u0,u) in S3=SU2 and t>0 define

The Pauli/2 metric gives

 |grad u0|^2=(1-u0^2)/4.

                 t E_t(1-u0^2)=3c(t).                  (2.2)

This retains the factor4 of the metric. Using the round-S3

3. THE ONE-LINK LOCALIZATION MOMENT AND PRODUCT POTENTIAL

Put h(q)=1-u0. Under psi_t^2 dHaar its density on[0,2]

             e^(-t h) h^(1/2)(2-h)^(1/2).

Let Gamma_t be the probability law on[0,infinity) with
density proportional to e^(-t h)h^(1/2), shape3/2 and
rate t. The preceding law is Gamma_t tilted by the
nonnegative decreasing function w(h)=(2-h)_+^(1/2).
For independent H,H' with law Gamma_t,

 Cov(H,w(H))
   =(1/2)E[(H-H')(w(H)-w(H'))]<=0.

Since E w(H)>0, division by this exact normalizer yields

              1-c(t)=E_t h<=E_Gamma H=3/(2t).          (3.1)

No asymptotic Gaussian replacement or missing tail is used.
For t=16/g^2 this in particular gives E_t h<=3g^2/32,
so the trial is localized on a link-angle scale of order g.
It nevertheless has full smooth support, avoiding a cutoff
derivative at a chart boundary.

Take the normalized product trial

                   Psi_t(U)=product_e psi_t(U_e).       (3.2)

Its squared law makes distinct links independent, with
E_t U_e=c(t)I and E_t U_e^-1=c(t)I. Since a plaquette uses

Summing the exact kinetic and potential expectations,

At each fixed graph the compact smooth flow exists for

There is also an elementary pointwise finite-lattice

left/right multiplication by its other links is an isometry;
therefore

 |grad_e h_p|^2=(2h_p-h_p^2)/4<=h_p/2.

A spatial edge meets four plaquettes. Cauchy--Schwarz
on their four gradient contributions and summation over
edges, each plaquette being counted four times, give

                 |grad S_sp|^2<=8 S_sp.                (4.2)

Apply(4.2) to Phi_s U and then use action monotonicity:

          E_(Omega^2)|grad S_sp(Phi_s U)|^2<=18n g^2.    (4.3)

The gradient in(4.1)--(4.3) is taken at the CURRENT flowed
configuration. It is not grad_U[S_sp(Phi_s U)], which
contains D Phi_s(U). This distinction is essential.

Translations preserve the Hamiltonian. By uniqueness
Omega^2 is translation invariant, and equivariance of
Phi_s preserves that property for its pushed law.
For any fixed spatial orientation on a rectangular torus,

                E h_(ij,x)(Phi_s U)<=(9/4)g^2.          (4.4)

On a cubic torus with isotropic action, permutation

For a set of m spatial plaquettes define the same discrete
