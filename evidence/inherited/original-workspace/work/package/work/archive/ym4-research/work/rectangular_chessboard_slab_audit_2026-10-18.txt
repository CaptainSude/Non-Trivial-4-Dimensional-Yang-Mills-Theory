RECTANGULAR CHESSBOARD BOUND FOR ONE PLANAR-PACKET CHAIN
18 October 2026. Working source-consumer proof.

SCOPE: all established chessboard/reflection estimates in this note
use COORDINATE mirrors. They do not supply tilted-frame slab bounds
or diagonal RP of a straight periodic-torus thermodynamic limit.
The latter is an additional open state-selection/shape issue; see
work/diagonal_torus_state_audit_2026-10-18.txt. In particular a diamond
tiling of a straight torus does not automatically have the positive
halfspace decomposition used by the rectangular proof below.

1. SETUP AND THE ORDER OF LIMITS

At each fixed bare spacing epsilon use the SAME source-free running
coupling sequence and fixed terminal weak band as in the source
producer. Let ell_cell be a fixed length compatible with every original
MR_j partition and every coordinate reflection centre. It may depend
on the fixed stopping band, but not on UV depth, chain length or torus
volume. The bound and nesting of these physical partition lengths are
proved in the one-run source note Section16(v).

Consider cubic periodic tori of side 2 ell_cell L^P with P increasing.
All coordinate reflection planes used below are lattice mirrors. If
necessary enlarge ell_cell once to meet the source parity/alignment
convention. Compactness of the compact-group product state space gives
a subsequential thermodynamic limit of the positive Wilson states at
this fixed epsilon. Take one diagonal subsequence for all local
continuous observables; the limit is translation invariant and has all
finite-local RP inequalities inherited from these periodic states.
This limit is taken BEFORE allowing arbitrarily long packet chains.

There is a compatible sourced normalization in the same limit. For
fixed epsilon the number of RG levels is finite. The local universal
vacuum functions e_j(X;v) are uniformly bounded and holomorphic on
fixed local source domains; each fixed carrier contains finitely many
bare source values. Montel compactness and a diagonal subsequence over
scales and countably many carriers give common limiting coefficient
functions, simultaneously with the Wilson state. Rooted exponential
tree bounds make the sum over carriers meeting a fixed compact source
uniformly convergent. Thus E_UV^infty(v) is the limit of the same
coherent finite-volume E_UV(v), and

 h(v)=exp[-E_UV^infty(v)] <exp A(v)>_infty.             (1.1)

This selection avoids assuming uniqueness of the thermodynamic state
or literal ambient-volume independence of every coefficient. Locality,
reflection covariance, reality and all coefficient bounds pass to the
limit. The coefficient and state subsequence is fixed once for all
local sources at this epsilon, not chosen separately for each m.

2. THE RECTANGULAR CHESSBOARD ESTIMATE IS THE SAME RP PROOF

Let C=product_mu[0,d_mu], d_mu=ell_cell L^{p_mu}, and choose P>=max p_mu.
The cubic torus of side 2 ell_cell L^P is tiled by translated copies of
C, with counts N_mu=2L^{P-p_mu}. Every N_mu is even; the N_mu need not
be equal. Let B=product_mu N_mu and V_C=product_mu d_mu.

Coordinate face reflections map this rectangular tiling into itself.
Their antilinear action on observables conjugates coefficients in odd
reflection parity. For an observable F supported strictly inside C,
reflection positivity gives, by repeated reflected Cauchy--Schwarz,

 |<F>| <= <product_{C'} F^[C']>^(1/B).                (2.1)

The expectation on the right is nonnegative. The proof in RawII's
chessboard-estimate lemma applies literally with N_mu in direction mu:
normalize a finite array by its chessboard values, take a maximizer,
and spread one cell's copies successively along each coordinate. A
reflected maximizer remains a maximizer by Cauchy--Schwarz; the final
array consists entirely of that cell's chessboard copies and has
normalized value1. The spreading is a separate one-dimensional even-
cycle operation in each coordinate. It never equates different N_mu,
uses a bound on aspect ratio, or replaces C by a containing cube.
The zero chessboard-value case follows by regularization/continuity.
Hence there is no dimension or anisotropy constant growing with m.

This is also the standard rectangular-block form: Biskup--Chayes--
Nussinov, arXiv/math-ph manuscript mp_arc03-445, Section6.1 explicitly
starts with rectangular V of side lengths a_1,a_2,a_3. The argument
here is the original gauge-link RP/chessboard proof, not a change of
model; the extra fourth coordinate is handled by one further fold.
Primary-source URL: https://web.ma.utexas.edu/mp_arc/c/03/03-445.pdf

3. PUT THE ENTIRE CHAIN IN ONE CELL

Let m>=1 planar packets have width delta, pairwise support separation
at least c_sep delta, centres along the time direction with consecutive
distances bounded above and below by fixed multiples of delta. Their
transverse smear f has fixed compact support, and their other planar
coordinate stays in a fixed-width delta strip. Thus the whole source
fits in a rectangular envelope of dimensions

 C(1+m delta), C(1+delta), C_f, C_f.

Choose a fixed physical buffer b>0. The spatial cell sides are fixed
powers of L times ell_cell, large enough to fit the fixed transverse
support and planar strip with buffer 2b. Choose the time side as the
SMALLEST allowed power times ell_cell above chain length+4b. Rounding
costs at most the fixed factor L, so

 V_C <= C_f,b (1+m delta).                            (3.1)

A common bare-lattice translation places the chain at distance at
least b from every cell face, provided epsilon is sufficiently small
compared with b. No translation proportional to the number of torus
cells is involved. At finite UV the plaquette stencil is also inside
the open cell when 2epsilon<b. Its source exponential is therefore an
admissible bounded complex observable for(2.1).

Write w=sum_l z_l f_{l,delta}, where each z_l is either z or conjugate z
(or is independently in that disc). Disjoint supports and vanishing
at their boundaries give

 ||w||_infty<=C_f |z|delta^-2,
 Lip(w)<=C_f |z|delta^-3,                             (3.2)

with NO factor m. Reflected copies vanish on whole face buffers; their
piecewise union w_ch has exactly the same supremum and Lipschitz
bounds, again with no number-of-cells factor. For

                  |z|<=c delta^(7/2),                (3.3)

and delta<=delta0, choose c so that (3.2) lies in the fixed W_N ball.
The actual requirement for this particular step is only O(delta^3);
(3.3) is the smaller radius needed by the slab route. This uses the
full local-source CLASS estimate, not a triangle-inequality source
polydisc sum_l |z_l| ||f_l|| that would introduce m.

4. EXACT BULK VACUUM CANCELLATION AND FACE COST

Use the SAME coefficient family E_UV and its reality/covariance on w
and w_ch. The normalized source identity and upper/floor stability give

 log |h(w)| <= C V_C + B^-1 Re E_UV(w_ch)-Re E_UV(w).  (4.1)

Here the volume factor is V_torus/B=V_C exactly. No lower bound at a
nonzero source is needed. If the disseminated expectation vanishes,
the chessboard estimate already makes the left side trivial.

Partition vacuum carriers into those whose complete read enlargement
is contained in an open cell and those meeting a cell face. On each
interior carrier source locality identifies its coefficient with the
appropriate reflected copy of the coefficient for w. Reflection
covariance and complex conjugation make their REAL parts identical.
Their B copies cancel EXACTLY in the difference of(4.1). Empty-source
cells of w contribute zero. This step uses the universal vacuum
family, not history-dependent boundary constants factored as scalars.

For a remaining carrier, nonzero source dependence requires that it
also meet a source packet. The packet-to-face distance is at least b,
up to the fixed plaquette enlargement. At physical scale a_j this
forces d_j(X)>=c b/a_j-C. Retain a tree exponent for that distance,
and use the other exponent for the rooted source tree sum. The number
of eligible roots per cell is bounded by

 C product_mu(d_mu/a_j+1) <= C V_C a_j^-4,             (4.2)

because all d_mu exceed the fixed coarsest localization length. This
crude volume count is enough; counting surface area is unnecessary.
Thus the two face sums in(4.1), after the factor1/B for w_ch, are at
most

 C V_C sum_j a_j^-4 exp[-c (b/a_j-C)_+] <= C_b V_C.   (4.3)

The scale sequence is geometric and bounded above by the fixed
stopping scale. The sum converges uniformly as epsilon decreases;
there is no UV-depth or m factor beyond V_C. Enlarging a carrier by
a fixed number of cells changes only C. Each crossing carrier is
assigned to one of its source roots, so (4.2) is an overcount, not an
assumption that the carrier is contained in the cell.

Combining(3.1),(4.1),(4.3) yields

 |h_m(z,conjugate z)| <= exp[C_f,b(1+m delta)]
                       <= exp(C' m)                 (4.4)

for m>=1, 0<delta<=delta0, |z|<=c delta^(7/2), uniformly in epsilon
sufficiently small relative to delta and in all compatible tori.
The same proof works for independent complex packet coefficients in
the common SUPREMUM disc; when writing h_m(z,conjugate z), it is the
restriction of this holomorphic multiparameter function. No claim of
holomorphy in z of an expression containing conjugate z is made.

5. LIMIT, PLACEMENT AND SCOPE

At fixed epsilon first pass(4.4) to the single selected thermodynamic
state and its coherent vacuum family from Section1. Compact sources
have bounded exponential insertions at fixed epsilon, and the local
vacuum sum convergence was already established. The resulting bound
holds for EVERY finite m in that one infinite-volume state. One may
therefore consider arbitrarily long chains after this limit without
running into a finite torus seam or changing states with m.

If the chain was translated to fit C, h in(4.4) is evaluated for the
TRANSLATED source using its own counterterm in the same fixed scaffold.
The original and translated raw expectations agree by Wilson
translation invariance. Their UV-subtracted h's need not agree.
For a restored full-moment or positivity argument use the translated
h together with its OWN mixed UV coefficients, then identify the raw
expectation with the original one. The uniform source estimates make
that legitimate; it is not an invariance assertion about E_UV.

The rectangular shape introduces no new source-domain, cell-count,
reflection-positivity or tree-summation condition beyond those checked
above. In particular its cost is exp(Cm), not exp(Cm^4). The argument
supplies the requested normalized remainder bound. Restoring all
mixed UV coefficients, controlling their chain sum, and deriving the
operator/spectral or angular consequence are separate steps; this
note does not infer them from(4.4) alone.


6. FIXED ENDPOINT POLYNOMIALS AT FIXED UV -- INSUFFICIENT FOR OPERATOR NORM

AUDIT CORRECTION. The argument in this section only proves a bound
C_epsilon,P exp(C_P m), because enlarging the rectangle's transverse
cross-section for an arbitrary endpoint polynomial P changes its
volume coefficient. Thus (6.5) below is valid only with C=C_P. The
claim that its exponential rate is independent of P is withdrawn.
It cannot by itself prove a uniform operator norm. The replacement
under development uses generalized bare-grid chessboard Holder:
one fixed-cross-section array cell, endpoint bond factors in other
closed cells, and their sup norms. See the independent slab note of
minimal_route for the completed replacement when available.

Add a finite list of endpoint source profiles q_1,...,q_d supported in
fixed-size regions at fixed offsets from the two ends of the chain.
Their number, individual norms and support sizes are independent of m;
the right endpoint may move with the end of the chain. Choose a fixed
endpoint parameter polydisc |lambda_l|<r_l so that their combined
sharp norm is at most one reserved fraction of r. Choose the array
constant c in(3.3) to use another reserved fraction. The whole weight
w_array+sum lambda_l q_l then belongs to the SAME W_N ball uniformly
in m. Enlarge the rectangle by fixed endpoint margins if needed:

                 V_C<=C(1+m delta+C_endpoint).        (6.1)

The proof of Sections2--4 consequently gives

 |h(w_array+sum lambda_l q_l)|
 <=exp[C(1+m delta+C_endpoint)]                     (6.2)

uniformly on that endpoint polydisc, for the same finite-volume and
thermodynamic-limit coefficient conventions.

At FIXED bare spacing epsilon the difference

 D_end(lambda)=E_UV(w_array+sum lambda_l q_l)
                                      -E_UV(w_array) (6.3)

has a uniform bound |D_end|<=C_epsilon,end, independent of m, z and the
volume. By locality only carriers meeting an endpoint support can
contribute. At each of the FINITELY MANY source scales their rooted
tree sum is bounded by the fixed number of cells meeting the endpoint
support times the uniform local vacuum bound. Endpoint locations do
not enter that count. Sum the finite scale range. This deliberately
allows the constant to diverge as epsilon->0. It is holomorphic on
the endpoint polydisc and its bound is uniform there; no separation
between an endpoint and the array is required.

The exact identity is

 e^-E_UV(w_array) <exp A(w_array+sum lambda_l q_l)>
                 = exp(D_end(lambda)) h(w_array+sum lambda_l q_l).
                                                               (6.4)

Apply finite-dimensional Cauchy in the endpoint parameters, not an
absolute-value replacement of the complex array by its real part.
It follows for every fixed endpoint polynomial P generated by these
profiles that

 |e^-E_UV(w_array) <P exp A(w_array)>|
                         <= C_epsilon,P exp(C_P m).  (6.5)

The array coefficients may alternate z and conjugate z. They are held
fixed in this Cauchy operation; analyticity is in the independent
lambda variables. Thus no invalid holomorphy in z/conjugate z is used.
The polynomial may be a finite sum and may include profiles at both
ends. At fixed epsilon a general compactly supported lattice-smeared
finite polynomial can be expressed as a finite polynomial in local
plaquette variables, so the assertion covers it with an epsilon-
dependent constant. Translation of an endpoint with its end of the
chain does not change that constant.

The order of limits in this attempted reflection-doubling argument is:
fix epsilon and the thermodynamic state, then let the chain length m
grow so constants C_epsilon,P independent of m disappear under m-th
roots. Only afterward may epsilon decrease. Interchanging those limits
would require an additional uniform endpoint estimate and is NOT used.
Even in the stated order, C_P in the exponential survives the m-th
root. The generalized chessboard replacement is needed to remove it.


7. THE BARE-GRID REPLACEMENT: PRESSURE AND ENDPOINT FACTORIZATION

This records the independently checked ingredients for the replacement
being assembled by minimal_route. The array lies in one time cell
[0,mD], with a fixed spatial cross-section determined only by its
transverse test f. Its first/last packets have c delta face buffers.
The endpoint polynomial has all its bonds strictly before0 or after
mD. This is the placement supplied by the positive-time endpoint
vectors in the reflection-doubling argument; it is a genuine support
condition, not a consequence of compactness alone.

Use cell faces THROUGH bare lattice sites. The Wilson site-reflection
positive form is valid for arbitrary complex functions of bonds in
the closed halfspace, without gauge invariance. It follows either
by conditioning on the boundary links in the decomposition
S=S_+ +theta S_+ +S_0, or by the explicit proof in RawII
8.7:lem-chessboard-torus(a), lines108696ff. The generalized complex
chessboard Holder inequality follows from the same reflected
Cauchy--Schwarz argument for arrays of closed-cell functions. Shared
boundary bonds cause no problem: functions on either closed side
remain in its positive-form algebra. The abstract statement is also
Frohlich--Israel--Lieb--Simon, CMP62(1978), Theorem4.1. Its hypotheses
are cyclicity and reflected Cauchy--Schwarz on an even cycle; unequal
even cell counts in different coordinate directions are allowed.
Primary preprint:
https://repo-archives.ihes.fr/FONDS_IHES/I_Prepublications/FROHLICH/1978-1980/P_78_224/P_78_224_web.pdf

Every fixed-UV endpoint polynomial is a finite sum of bond-matrix-entry
monomials. Split each monomial into products assigned to closed cells.
Each bond has both endpoints in some closed cell, including bonds
lying in faces. Since the endpoint times are outside the array cell,
none of these factors is assigned to that cell, irrespective of the
endpoint's transverse diameter. Their chessboard seminorms are bounded
by their ordinary sup norms. The finite sum of the products of these
bounds is a constant C_epsilon,A, independent of m. No absolute value
is moved inside the expectation of the complex array exponential.

The source-free thermodynamic sequence can be chosen ONCE so every
finite integer bare-cell length divides every sufficiently late torus
side with even quotient. For example take the freely chosen N_T to
contain twice k! and the fixed RG partition period. RawII682--715
allows arbitrary integer N_T; the partition conditions at767--882
do not bound it. Select the state and vacuum-coefficient subsequence
along this sequence. A new state for each m is unnecessary.

Here is the pressure bound which removes any need for the bare cell
grid to preserve the RG scaffold. For a single packet v_l let

 d_l=log <exp A(v_l)>, with the analytic branch d_l(0)=0.

This branch exists uniformly on |z|<=c delta^(7/2): the fixed-buffer
aligned-cell proof gives |h_l(z)|<=M on the larger disc |z|<=c0 delta^3,
and h_l(0)=1. Cauchy gives |h_l(z)-1|<=C delta^(1/2), after choosing
the smaller constant if necessary. Hence |log h_l|<=C and

                    d_l=E_UV(v_l)+log h_l.           (7.1)

The single-packet h estimate is uniform in placement. For each
placement use an aligned cell with origin a multiple of ell_cell and
fixed sufficiently large side, leaving a fixed buffer. The raw moment
and thus d_l transform exactly under every bare lattice isometry.
The scaffold-dependent E_UV and h need NOT transform separately.

For any separated union v=sum_l v_l of M packets, possibly reflected
and placed on this bare grid, the original source-tree bound gives

 |E_UV(v)-sum_l E_UV(v_l)| <= C M |z|^2 delta^-7.      (7.2)

A direct all-orders proof is short. At physical source scale a the
norm of the FULL union on its source domain is at most
u_a=C|z|max(delta^-2,sqrt(a)delta^-3); disjoint packet supports remove
the factor M. The local activity's source Taylor remainder after its
linear part is bounded by C u_a^2 exp(-kappa d_a(X)), by scalar Cauchy
on the full local source class. The linear parts add exactly. A
carrier reading only one packet cancels exactly in (7.2). Every
remaining carrier joins two packets and therefore has complete
carrier diameter at least c delta (epsilon is a sufficiently small
fraction of delta). Retain half the source-tree decay for this
distance. Rooting the union term at all packet supports is a harmless
overcount; root the singleton remainders similarly. Per packet the
number of possible roots is C delta^2 a^-4 for a<=delta and C a^-2
for a>=delta. Thus the scale sum is bounded by

 C|z|^2 sum_(a<=delta)
   [delta^-2 a^-4 +delta^-4 a^-3]exp(-c delta/a)
 + C|z|^2 sum_(a>=delta) a^-1 delta^-6
                  <= C|z|^2 delta^-7.               (7.3)

All scale sums are geometric up to the fixed stopping scale. No
expansion over source order or assumption on factorial constants is
hidden here. The linear cancellation is exact even when one carrier
reads many packets. For (7.2) summed over all reflected cell copies,
apply this proof to the complete array of M=mB packets. Its separation
and local source norm have the same constants.

Let W be exp[A(v_array)-sum_(l=1)^m d_l] in the single array cell.
Disseminate W to B cells with the usual complex conjugation. The
normalized source upper/floor estimate, (7.1), and (7.2) imply

 log ||W||_ch
 <= C V_cell + B^-1 Re[E_UV(v_dissem)-sum_copies d_l]
 <= C V_cell + C m
 <= C' m,                                           (7.4)

since V_cell<=C_f mD and D is a fixed multiple of delta (or a fixed
bounded allowed spacing), with fixed spatial cross-section. Exact
single-packet d copies are justified by raw Wilson invariance.
Only their real parts occur after conjugate dissemination. Equation
(7.2), rather than a covariance statement about counterterms, bounds
the first difference. Constants in (7.4) are independent of endpoints.

Generalized chessboard Holder and the endpoint factorization above
now give

 |<P W>| <= C_epsilon,P exp(C' m),                    (7.5)

with C' independent of P, its support diameter, epsilon and m. The
same conclusion holds for vacuum-subtracted array normalization:
by (7.1)--(7.2), replacing sum d_l by E_UV(v_array) costs at most
another exp(Cm). Equations(7.4)--(7.5) are the needed correction to
Section6. Endpoint constants can depend on epsilon because m tends
to infinity first in the one fixed-UV thermodynamic state.

Scope: these estimates consume the already established source
locality/stability and the exact packet separation/endpoint-time
placement. They do not by themselves prove the reflection-doubling
operator identity or its continuum limit; those are checked in the
slab-operator note. In particular no claim is made that general
endpoint supports can be put into separate cells without the stated
time separation.
