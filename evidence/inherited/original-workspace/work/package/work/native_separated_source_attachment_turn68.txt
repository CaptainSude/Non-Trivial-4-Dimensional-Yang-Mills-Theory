2. EXACT FINITE-LATTICE SLAB IDENTITY, WITH THE ORIGINAL DENOMINATOR

Fix any finite periodic original Wilson lattice having N time slices.
The couplings may be anisotropic but must be time-translation invariant.
Let Y_n be the time translate of a real bounded gauge-invariant
observable of one time slice; a spatial magnetic action, with its
complete physical normalization, is an example. At a fixed regulator
it is bounded even if its bound diverges along the cutoff sequence.
There is no replacement observable in this definition.

Write mu for the normalized ORIGINAL Wilson measure and

 C(r)=Cov_mu(Y_0,Y_r),       C(-r)=C(r).

The latter equality uses stationarity and commutativity of real
multiplication observables. Put, for 0<=m<=N,

 K_m(lambda)=log E_mu exp[lambda sum_(n=0)^(m-1)Y_n],
 J_m=partial_lambda^2 K_m(0).

The denominator of every expression is the SAME complete original
partition function. Direct expansion, including the diagonal, gives

 J_m = m C(0)+2 sum_(r=1)^(m-1)(m-r)C(r).              (2.1)

For integers h>=1, m>=0, m+2h<=N, define

 Delta_h^2 J_m=J_(m+2h)-2J_(m+h)+J_m,
 w_(m,h)(r)=(m+2h-r)_+-2(m+h-r)_++(m-r)_+.

Then EXACTLY

 (1/2)Delta_h^2 J_m
   =sum_(r=m+1)^(m+2h-1)
         [h-|r-m-h|] C(r).                            (2.2)

Proof: the m C(0) term has zero second difference. For every r>=1,
take the second difference of (m-r)_+ in (2.1). It is zero for
r<=m and r>=m+2h; on the remaining interval it is the displayed
nonnegative triangle. This is a finite algebraic calculation in the
original law, not a cluster-expansion approximation.

Thus a covariance contribution at separation r<=m is removed even
if its magnitude diverges with the ultraviolet cutoff. Removing it
after an absolute-value estimate would lose this exact cancellation.

For site reflection, take m odd and t0=(m+1)/2. If
B=sum_(u=0)^(h-1)(Y_(t0+u)-EY_0), then

 E_mu[(theta B)B]
   =sum_(u,v=0)^(h-1) C(m+1+u+v)
   =(1/2)Delta_h^2 J_m.                               (2.3)

The positive half must fit before the other periodic reflection
plane. For m even, the same identity uses the admissible bond/time
reflection plane and shifted mesh. This statement only uses a
reflection plane for which the original model has reflection
positivity. The centered B is the actual old-field observable.

With time spacing epsilon, set Y_n=epsilon Q_n. If m epsilon->T
and h epsilon->delta, (2.2) has the continuous triangular kernel.
Passage of the ORIGINAL finite products and mixed moments at fixed
spatial regulator is established separately in
separated_source_attachment_turn68.txt. In that scope it yields
the exact TURN67 identity

 R_Q(T/2,delta)=(1/2)Delta_delta^2 J_Q(T).              (2.4)

No spatial-cutoff limit is taken here.

3. WHAT EXACTLY CANCELS IN A LOCAL SOURCE CATALOGUE

There are three different cancellation statements.

3A. Literal diagonal/contact terms.

If the finite source action contains

 lambda sum_(n in I_m)Y_n
       +(lambda^2/2)sum_(n in I_m)Z_n,

then its log curvature is J_m+m E Z_0. The extra term cancels in
Delta_h^2. The same is true of an exactly matched scalar source
counterterm affine in m. No assumption that E Z_0 is uniformly
bounded is needed.

3B. Finite-range quadratic source terms.

On the infinite time lattice, suppose an exactly translated quadratic
term is indexed by two source-read times u,v. Summing its translates
whose two reads are in I_m produces (m-|u-v|)_+ times its coefficient
(with the corresponding ordering convention). Consequently its second
difference vanishes whenever |u-v|<=m.

On a finite time circle Z/N, retain both image terms. For an ordered
pair with displacement d in {1,...,N-1}, the exact translated pair
count is

                   (m-d)_+ +(m-(N-d))_+.              (3.1)

Thus a short pair of cyclic read range r cancels as a local affine
term only in an alias-free temporal embedding, for example
m+2h-1<N-r. Otherwise its opposite image may contribute near the
far end of the circle. Section2 remains exact without this extra
condition because its C(r) already contains the periodic aliases.
For the ground-limit use, the temporal circle is taken larger than
all fixed slab lengths and read ranges before its limit.

These cancellation statements hold for signed coefficients and
scalar/contact terms generated by an exact source expansion,
provided their actual two source reads and periodic images are kept.

In particular it is insufficient to know only the location of an
output carrier's declared root. A transported source may still read
an old field, a reference denominator, or another retained component.
The complete source-read set must be part of the coefficient label.

3C. A connected two-mark representation.

For a complete translation-covariant representation

 C(r)=sum_C c_C(0,r),

where each C contains the ACTUAL two source reads, a coefficient
surviving (2.2) must connect forward read times separated by more
than m. On a time circle their circular distance is bounded below
only by min(m+1,N-m-2h+1). Therefore a temporal diameter of at least
m+1 mesh units follows in infinite time, or for example under the
explicit finite-period restriction N>=2m+2h. Without this restriction,
keep the short periodic-image terms rather than inferring a long
native carrier from the forward lag alone.
This is the precise geometric consequence; the full carrier can
be larger. At finite cutoff the rearrangement is valid for finite
sums or absolutely convergent sums.

If a native construction is covariant only under its current block
translations, either retain the phases of the block grid or average
the translated construction. Slab endpoints may instead be chosen
on that grid. One may not discard grid-phase boundary coefficients
while asserting exact cancellation.

Neither this counting nor the primary action expansion proves the
required absolute summability of the ORIGINAL marked coefficients.
An exponential majorant for unmarked action coefficients is not
an exponential majorant for arbitrary inserted old-field statistics.

6. WHY WINDING/RARE-HISTORY PROPAGATION DOES NOT FINISH THIS SOURCE

A separated mixed contribution can arise in two ways:

 (i) a complete two-mark carrier already joins the two reads at a
     fine scale; or
 (ii) the two one-mark descendants travel separately through many
      steps and first join at their common physical scale.

Case(i) receives the exponential geometric factor in (5.2). This
is the setting in which a fixed physical separation can dominate
polynomial ultraviolet cell counts and the allowed dated response.

Case(ii) is not bounded by applying that fine-scale exponential to
each of the independent marks. Before their meeting, neither mark
has a long carrier. Their norms must be transported with the actual
physical normalization, mixing, scalar jets and common baseline.

The available general response costs rho_j^(-1)=C g_j^(-P). Even
with gamma_j=g_j^(-2) bounded by C(Gamma+n-j), a product over n
steps is permitted to grow like

       C^n product_(r=1)^n (Gamma+r)^(P/2),

whose logarithm is O(n log(Gamma+n)). An original local source
has no automatic initial exp(-c L^n) factor. The latter is what
made the tagged-winding estimates robust under this response.
At the physical meeting scale, a carrier spanning the two windows
has only order-one current-cell length. It cannot erase an already
uncontrolled product of one-mark norms.

This is a limitation of the AVAILABLE ESTIMATE, not a proof that
the true marked map grows at this rate. Improving it requires a
quantitative physical source/calibration theorem, rather than a
second use of support connectivity.
