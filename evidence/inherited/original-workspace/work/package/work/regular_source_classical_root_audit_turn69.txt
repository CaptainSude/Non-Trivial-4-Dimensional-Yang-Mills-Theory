1. THE FACTOR M^(-4) BELONGS TO EACH ORIGINAL PLAQUETTE

The imported complete-field bound controls A and its first physical
differences uniformly on a fixed complex input ball, with fine spacing
eta=1/M in current units. For a fine plaquette, its four link product
has logarithm Z_p with

 ||Z_p|| <= C eta^2 (||A||_(C^1)+||A||_(C^1)^2).

The term linear in Z_p has zero trace. In the SU2 convention used in
the producer, h_p=1-Tr exp(Z_p)/2; its holomorphic extension therefore
starts at quadratic order in Z_p. On a fixed small ball,

 |h_p| <= C eta^4 ||A||_(C^1)^2.

This is an analytic estimate, not just a real positivity argument.
At zero background the minimizer is trivial, so composition with
the supported minimizer has zero constant and linear coefficients.
Fixed-radius Banach coefficient estimates retain eta^4. The finite
fine-plaquette collar is interpreted as a complete output fiber;
no independent sum over its fine output coordinates is required.

The number of original fine plaquettes assigned to one current
four-dimensional cell is at most C M^4, including the fixed
orientation multiplicity. Thus their norm sum has a bound C b^2.
This cancellation uses dimension four. It is not an assertion that
one original density is itself O(1), nor that the entire physical
source region has a uniformly bounded extensive norm.
