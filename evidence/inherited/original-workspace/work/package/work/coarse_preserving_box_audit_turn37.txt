1. EXACT PRODUCT DISINTEGRATION

Use the actual bare positive law and separated cores of the annealed
box note. All spaces are finite products of compact groups or standard
Borel measurable images. Let omega be the full fixed exterior, and

  mu(domega,du)=mu_X(domega) product_i Q_i^omega(du_i),
  Y_i=A_i(omega,u_i).

Each A_i may read the common exterior but must read no other active
interior. Write lambda_i^omega for its pushforward and K_i^(omega,y_i)
for a jointly measurable regular conditional kernel. Then, for
mu_X-a.e. omega and product lambda_i^omega-a.e. y,

  mu(domega,dy,du)
   =mu_X(domega) product_i[lambda_i^omega(dy_i)
                           K_i^(omega,y_i)(du_i)].          (1.1)

Each K_i is supported on its fiber almost everywhere. This factorization
follows from the original conditional product and locality of the maps;
it does not assume smooth fibers, a coarea formula, or independent
unconditional coarse data. Null fibers can be assigned arbitrary kernels
without affecting any assertion. A datum reading two active interiors
would invalidate the displayed product and must instead be grouped.

Let B_i be the exterior-measurable bad-boundary event and W_i the
measurable original good window. On B_i^c set

  p_i(omega,y_i)=K_i^(omega,y_i)(W_i^c).

The original conditional tail gives the precise, sufficient estimate

       integral p_i d lambda_i^omega <= alpha on B_i^c.    (1.2)

No inference p_i<=alpha for individual fibers is legitimate.

2. REPLACEMENT AND NORMALIZATION

Fix 0<theta<1. Call a fiber active when B_i^c and p_i<=theta.
On active fibers replace K_i by K_i(.|W_i); leave all other fibers
unchanged. The denominator 1-p_i is then at least 1-theta. Consequently
the resulting law nu has density, relative to the SAME original law,

     dnu/dmu = product_i R_i,
     R_i=1_Wi/(1-p_i) on active fibers, and R_i=1 otherwise.

For almost every (omega,y), integral K_i R_i=1. Thus nu has exactly
the original joint law of (omega,Y_1,...,Y_m). This also preserves the
raw partition mass if the operation is applied to the original
unnormalized density. No boundary- or coarse-data-dependent normalizer
has been deleted: the factors (1-p_i)^-1 remain in the density.

The statement is equality of measures. It does not define a uniquely
preferred kernel at every exceptional datum, nor claim analytic
dependence of these kernels on coarse data.

3. COUPLING: TWO DIFFERENT CONDITIONING LEVELS

On each active fiber use the exact mixture

 K_i=(1-p_i) K_i(.|W_i)+p_i K_i(.|W_i^c).

It couples the old and new interiors so that a discrepancy mark D_i
has probability p_i; all inactive fibers have D_i=0. The unused bad
kernel at p_i=0 can be chosen arbitrarily. Given (omega,y), the marks
can be chosen independent across i and dominated by independent
Bernoulli(theta) marks.

After integrating y, conditional on omega the local data still factor
by (1.1), and the discrepancy probabilities are

  1_(B_i^c) integral p_i 1_(p_i<=theta) d lambda_i^omega
  <= alpha.

Thus independent Bernoulli(alpha) domination is available jointly with
the exterior AFTER coarse-data integration. It is not in general
available jointly with the revealed Y. This distinction is essential,
not merely a choice of proof.

For any selected set A of interiors, retaining omega and all Y in both
laws, the exact total variation distance is

  E_[omega,Y][1-product_(i in A)(1-1_active_i p_i)]
  <= |A| alpha.                                           (3.1)

Indeed on each conditioned fiber the new product law is the old product
law conditioned on the active windows. The same calculation gives

 D(nu||mu)=E_[omega,Y] sum_i 1_active_i[-log(1-p_i)]
          <= |A| alpha/(1-theta)                         (3.2)

when only interiors in A are replaced. The direction of entropy matters.

4. UNCONTROLLED MARKS AND JOINT PROBABILITIES

Let F_i=B_i^c intersect {p_i>theta} be the bad-fiber mark. On a good
boundary, F_i and D_i are disjoint. Their conditional probability,
after integrating the coarse datum, is bounded using the POINTWISE
inequality

   1_(p>theta)+p 1_(p<=theta) <= p/theta.

Hence it is at most alpha/theta. Given omega, these local marks are
independent across the distinct cores. If the original reflected
bad-boundary events obey mu(intersection_(i in J) B_i)<=beta^|J|,
expanding by their chosen subset proves

 P(B_i OR F_i OR D_i for every i in A)
             <= (beta+alpha/theta)^|A|.                  (4.1)

The saved producer proves the sharper bound: with
eta=min(1,alpha/theta), its exterior-conditional mark parameter is
at most eta+(1-eta)1_Bi. Expansion with the same boundary-array estimate
therefore gives

     P(all uncontrolled in A)
          <= [beta+(1-beta)eta]^|A|.                      (4.2)

This sharper argument passes and is optimal from these hypotheses.
The saved producer also correctly proves that replacement's ACTUAL
window defect has parameter beta+(1-beta)alpha, since its remaining
window mass on a good boundary is E[p 1_(p>theta)]. That mass plus
the removed discrepancy mass equals the original E[p] exactly.

This proof does not assert
independence or an iid domination theorem for the full uncontrolled
marks. It asserts their joint inclusion bound. Their connected-cluster
estimate then follows by the same bounded-degree animal count as in
the bare annealed note.

There is no need to add a second alpha term to (4.1); the elementary
inequality already includes the active-fiber discrepancy. Conversely,
using beta+alpha for the BAD-FIBER mark alone would generally be false.

5. NATIVE RELEVANCE AND EXACT LIMIT

The construction allows arbitrary measurable local coarse observations,
including nonlinear maps and singular fibers. It therefore genuinely
improves an unconstrained replacement: chosen coarse data and exterior
observables have exactly the SAME law, and bad fibers are left intact.
It does not approximate a hard constraint by a soft one.

The domain of the assertion remains the original bare joint measure.
Identification with a published native T or R kernel requires that
kernel to be the SAME positive disintegration, including branch flags,
gauge conventions, Jacobians and retained normalization factors. Native
read slots extending into other active interiors must be put in the
conditioning sigma-field or grouped before the product claim. Merely
sharing a block-average formula is not a full joint-law identification.

In particular, the hard-fiber counterexample in
polylog_box_native_matching_turn37, Section3, is consistent with this
result: it simply lies in a bad fiber. The new result avoids requiring
all fibers to be good. It does not prove the constrained-minimum,
complex-chart, source-derivative or global-fiber-gap estimates listed
in that note. An a.e. positive disintegration estimate cannot be
promoted to an analytic estimate at every prescribed native datum.

The saved producer's Section8 keeps the necessary branch qualifications:
total Borel completion of a partially defined average is allowed, but
an extra invalid-branch exclusion needs its own probability bound if
it is counted as uncontrolled. A finite retained multiplier has its
integral preserved; no weighted rarity estimate follows from that
identity. A singular delta or a selected null fiber is not thereby
identified. The original-law conditional product is not inferred for
later effective actions.

A finite-width collar containing the complete reads of a specified
first ordinary average is a plausible additional bare event. Its
separate geometry/branch-validity proof can refine this statement. No
general native validity conclusion is needed or assumed for the
disintegration theorem itself.

6. PHYSICAL-VOLUME LIMIT OF THE TV ESTIMATE

The exact coarse and exterior law preservation is GLOBAL, for all
chosen coarse variables in the finite lattice. Generic bounded fine
observables instead use the estimate |A|alpha; convergence for them
requires |A|alpha -> 0 or a sharper observable-specific bound.

In particular, under an asymptotically free relation
g^2 comparable to 1/log(1/a_uv), the available
alpha=exp[-c(log(1/g))^p] is only stretched exponential in log log(1/a_uv).
A fixed physical region contains order a_uv^-4/R^4 boxes when R is
polylog(1/g). The displayed |A|alpha bound need not tend to zero there.
The volume-uniform joint-inclusion/cluster estimates remain valid;
they must not be relabelled total-variation convergence on that region.
