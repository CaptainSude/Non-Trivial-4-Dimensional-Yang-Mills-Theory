1. FULL ORIGINAL SOURCE AND A COMMON ZERO-FREE DOMAIN

Let mu_a be any normalized original Wilson probability law, and let
B_1,...,B_m be measurable original observables with 0<=B_i<=1. For the
physical use they are gauge invariant and have complete reads in the
specified positive-time half. Define

 M_a(z)=E_mu_a exp(sum_i z_i B_i),   R=sum_i |z_i|.

At every regulator M_a is entire, and uniformly in regulator, beta,
volume, and the choices of B_i,

 |M_a(z)|<=e^R,     |M_a(z)-1|<=e^R-1.                  (1.1)

Indeed |sum z_i B_i|<=R and |e^w-1|<=e^{|w|}-1 pointwise.
For R<log 2, M_a lies in the open disk centered at1 with radius<1.
The logarithm with log M_a(0)=0 is therefore holomorphic there, and

 |log M_a(z)|<=-log(2-e^R).                              (1.2)

This follows from the absolutely convergent log(1+w) series. Cauchy
estimates on any fixed smaller polydisc give cutoff-uniform bounds on
every fixed finite source jet. The domain is the L1 source ball; this
does not give a fixed per-coordinate polydisc independent of m as
m tends to infinity. Raw moment derivatives at zero satisfy

 |partial^alpha M_a(0)|=E product_i B_i^{alpha_i}<=1.     (1.3)

No factorial is omitted in (1.3): these are derivatives, not power-series
coefficients. Cumulants are derivatives of log M and have their usual
order-dependent bounds; they are not all bounded by1.

The same argument is valid for every real positive conditional law.
It gives source-parameter analyticity even if its dependence on its
conditioning background is merely measurable.

2. FIXED POSITIVE HISTORY EVENTS AND NORMALIZED COMPARISON

Let P_a(dU,dh)=mu_a(dU)K_a(U,dh), where K_a is an ACTUAL probability
kernel, and freeze a measurable event E in this augmented law. Set
delta=P_a(E) and

 J_E(z)=E_P_a[1_E exp(sum z_i B_i(U))].

The exact estimates are

 |J_E(z)|<=delta e^R,
 |partial^alpha J_E(0)|<=delta.                          (2.1)

These estimates require neither raw ownership of B_i nor background
holomorphy; they retain the full original denominator. They apply to
a positive all-history event only if that joint positive law has
actually been identified. Signed walk decorations are not events.

For delta<1 let nu=Law(U|E^c), with its exact conditional normalizer.
The convex decomposition mu=(1-delta)nu+delta Law(U|E) implies, for
any B,H in[0,1],

 |Cov_mu(B,H)-Cov_nu(B,H)|<=3 delta.                     (2.2)

Proof: the cross moment changes by at most delta, and the product of
means changes by at most2delta. The same statement applies to B and
its reflected copy. It retains the mean correction; it is not just a
bound on an uncentered numerator. No RP assumption on nu is needed
for this numerical comparison. A bound on a different local bad event
does not identify nu with an all-good selected regulator.

There is also an exact uniform log-source consumer. Let M_nu be the
conditional generating function. For R<log2,

 |log M_mu(z)-log M_nu(z)|
     <=2 delta (e^R-1)/(2-e^R).                         (2.3)

Use M_mu-M_nu=delta(M_E-M_nu), subtract1 from each generating
function, and integrate the logarithmic derivative along their segment
inside the disk |w-1|<=e^R-1. Cauchy on a fixed smaller polydisc then
bounds each fixed mixed cumulant difference by a fixed constant times
delta. At delta=0 the formulas follow directly. The conditional law
is undefined at delta=1.

The same reasoning works for an actual retained weight 0<=w<=1 by
adding a Bernoulli label of conditional success probability w: delta
is then 1-Ew, and nu is the exactly normalized w-weighted law. This
does not make delta small. In particular the TURN71 coarse-reweighting
and rare-bridge counterexamples are not bypassed by (2.1)--(2.3).

 k_xi(dU)=product_e [e^{Re Tr J_e(xi)^dagger U_e}/I(J_e(xi))]dU_e.

Every factor is positive and normalized. In particular no D_beta occurs

 C_z(xi)=integral exp(sum_i z_i B_i(U)) k_xi(dU).          (3.2)

For EVERY real xi, including arbitrarily large auxiliaries, (1.1)--(1.2)

 M_mu(z)=E_nu_beta C_z,

Consequently the full mixed cumulant is

 Cov_mu(B_i,B_j)
   =E_nu Cov_kxi(B_i,B_j)+Cov_nu(m_i,m_j).               (3.4)

4. A LOCAL READ AND REFLECTED-CHANNEL CONSUMER

Suppose B reads only a set S of independent original links. Integrating
the other normalized factors in (3.1) shows that m_B and C_z for this
one probe depend only on currents J_e, e in S. Each J_e reads the finite
auxiliary plaquette-star explicitly given in (5.2) of the HS producer.
This is an exact read statement. If S is an entire spatial slice after
finite-lattice spatial flow, it does not become a bounded spatial box.

If the original link read sets of B and H are disjoint, (3.1) implies
conditional independence, even if the auxiliary stars overlap. Hence

 Cov_mu(B,H)=Cov_nu(m_B,m_H).                            (4.1)

Now require B to have complete original read in a strict positive half,
and require its complete auxiliary star to stay in that half too.
Choose the HS path conventions in the negative half by reflection of
the positive conventions. Then m_(theta B)=theta m_B and, provided
the original reflected read sets are disjoint,

 Cov_mu(theta B,B)=Cov_nu(theta m_B,m_B),   0<=m_B<=1.    (4.2)

For multiple probes, the same identity holds for the reflected Gram
matrix: negative original reads must be disjoint from positive ones;
the positive probes may overlap among themselves. The conditional
kernel is gauge covariant, so a gauge-invariant B gives a gauge-invariant
m_B under the induced auxiliary gauge action.

The half-space RP of the auxiliary marginal follows by applying the
root's local-augmentation theorem successively to the first and second
HS kernels on these admitted halves, or directly by factoring their
conditional coordinates given the original half. Crossing auxiliary
variables are excluded from strict-half reads and integrated out.
Thus (4.2) identifies the SAME centered original OS channel. This
reverse conditional map is not multiplicative for overlapping original
read sets. Ordinary same-side covariances still require (3.4).

For a physical limit, impose a fixed positive physical distance from
BOTH fixed slices of a periodic site reflection on the complete reads.
The additional one-plaquette star collar then fits for sufficiently
small spacing. A covariance identity for an interior probe is not a
claim about probes touching the reflection plane. No full-law source
comparison or lower bound is created by changing variables to xi.

4A. A UNIFORM COMPLEX CURRENT TUBE ABOUT EVERY REAL CONDITIONAL LAW

There is a further genuine, but specific, analyticity statement. Write
the real link coupling as l_e(J_e,U_e)=Re Tr J_e^dagger U_e. First choose
REAL coordinates for J_e and regard l_e as a real-linear function of
those coordinates. Complexify that linear function, not the operation
of taking Re or complex conjugates of new complex coordinates. Let
delta l_e(U_e) be the resulting complex linear perturbation and set

 L=sum_(e in S) sup_(U_e in SU(2)) |delta l_e(U_e)|.

For the normalized product kernel k_J on the relevant original link
set S, define

 D_J(delta)=E_kJ exp(sum_e delta l_e(U_e)),
 N_J(delta,z)=E_kJ exp(sum_e delta l_e(U_e)+sum_i z_i B_i(U)).

All B_i here read S. These finite integrals are entire in the indicated
complex current and source coordinates. Positivity of the REAL base
kernel gives, uniformly over every real current J,

 |D_J(delta)-1|<=e^L-1,
 |N_J(delta,z)|<=e^{L+R},
 |N_J(delta,z)-1|<=e^{L+R}-1.                           (4.3)

The first expression is precisely the perturbed product of compact
link integrals divided by its unperturbed product. Thus for L<log2,

 C_J(delta,z)=N_J(delta,z)/D_J(delta)
   is holomorphic and |C_J(delta,z)|<=e^{L+R}/(2-e^L).   (4.4)

It is entire in z on this current tube. On L+R<log2 its logarithm
with value0 at z=0 is the difference of the two zero-free logarithms,
log N_J(delta,z)-log D_J(delta). In particular the conditional means
and their finite current derivatives have uniform bounds on each
strictly smaller L-tube, by Cauchy. For example

 |m_B(J+delta)|<=e^L/(2-e^L),

where m_B uses the holomorphic normalized integral. The real case
still has the stronger 0<=m_B<=1. No analytic regularity of B as a
function of U is required for this conditional-current result; its
boundedness suffices for dominated integration.

This is a total L1 perturbation budget. For growing |S| it does not
give a fixed per-link complex radius. Moreover J_e(G,R) contains
bilinear terms R_h^dagger G_p: a fixed auxiliary-coordinate change
need not have bounded L on all unbounded real auxiliary records.
Composition on a desired complex auxiliary domain requires an actual
bound on these current increments. No estimate on the interacting
nu_beta marginal, its derivatives, or a native localized (U,J) map
follows merely from (4.3)--(4.4).
