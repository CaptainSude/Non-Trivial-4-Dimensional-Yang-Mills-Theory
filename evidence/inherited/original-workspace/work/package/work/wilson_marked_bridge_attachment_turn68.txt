ORIGINAL WILSON ATTACHMENT OF SOURCE-WEIGHTED BAD BRIDGES
TURN68. 20 October 2026. Root derivation; submitted for audit.

RESULT

The Hamiltonian marked-bridge theorem has a literal original
finite-Wilson realization along a suitable temporal diagonal.
The bridge event may use the ENTIRE finite periodic time board,
not only a prescribed finite observation window. Its centered
physical magnetic source contribution tends to zero at separated
supports at fixed physical spatial volume, or when the explicitly
retained boundary-cell count has an admitted polynomial growth
bound. Section6A extends the selectable-diagonal conclusion to
arbitrary finite spatial volumes by allowing a volume-dependent
time buffer. This result concerns the same time-averaged magnetic
action cells as the Hamiltonian theorem.

The proof does not transfer isotropic native RG estimates to an
anisotropic model. It uses the established actual Wilson temporal
limit, bounded magnetic source moments, and a finite-window
escape-event reduction. It does not bound the complementary
regular-field covariance.

1. REGULATORS AND ADMITTED INPUTS

Take a countable sequence of finite periodic spatial SU2 lattices
with spacings a->0, couplings g_a>0, and fixed spatial tile sides
l_i>=2. Retain the even spatial periods and dyadic tile counts
of hamiltonian_spacetime_moments_turn68.txt. Put V=l_1l_2l_3,
c=9V/2, C=exp(c). The ground action cells are

 X_(b,j)=(a g_a^2)^-1 int_(ja)^((j+1)a) S_b^cl(U_s) ds.

The proved joint ground-law bound is

 log E exp(sum t_c X_c)<=c sum t_c,    0<=t_c<=2.       (1.1)

Fix touching adjacency, with degree D<=80 and duplicate neighbors
removed. Its graph steps change the time-cell index by at most1.
The argument applies to any specified local adjacency with that
property and the same uniform degree bound.

At a fixed spatial regulator, the original anisotropic Wilson
transfer construction has beta_time=4a/(g_a^2 delta) and
beta_space=4delta/(g_a^2a). Its normalized trace and positive
ground transfer limit are those proved in TURN66.

The additive-source theorem in separated_source_attachment_turn68
gives convergence of all finite joint moments of bounded magnetic
time-integrals, first making delta small and then the original
finite time period large. All observables used below are gauge
invariant multiplication sources at a fixed spatial regulator.

2. A SINGLE THRESHOLD WITH THE REQUIRED CONTINUITY

Choose a number R0>c+log D. A real-valued random variable has
at most countably many atoms. There are only countably many
spatial regulators in the chosen sequence and countably many
ground action cells at each regulator. Therefore one can choose

 R in (R0,R0+1)

such that

          P_ground,a(X_c=R)=0                         (2.1)

for every cell at every chosen spatial regulator.

Set q=exp(c-R). Then Dq<1 uniformly. This is an existence
choice of threshold, not an assumed density theorem for
time-integrated actions. It does not require uniform control
of the probability of a thin interval around R.

For any finite cell set, a Boolean event made from comparisons
X_c>=R is continuous in the finite vector except on the union
of its threshold hyperplanes. By(2.1), that union has zero
limiting ground-law probability.

3. SOURCES AND A BUFFERED OBSERVATION SLAB

Take two real bounded compact spacetime profiles f_+,f_-, continuous
or bounded piecewise continuous in time, with disjoint physical
supports at strictly positive distance in
the periodic spatial geometry. Their suprema M_+,M_- are
uniformly bounded. At every cutoff assign each spatial
plaquette to one containing closed spatial tile.

Their ground integrated magnetic insertions are

 Q_+=4/(ag_a^2) int sum_p f_+(s,p)h_p(U_s) ds,
 Q_-=4/(ag_a^2) int sum_p f_-(s,p)h_p(U_s) ds.

Let A_+,A_- be the cells reading these source profiles. For
sufficiently small a they are disjoint, and

 |A_+|,|A_-|<=K a^-4,   dist_G(A_+,A_-)>=d0/a          (3.1)

with d0>0 after decreasing it to account for cell widths.
Constants are measured in fixed physical units.

Choose a finite time slab Lambda_a consisting of ALL spatial
tiles and a finite interval of time cells. It contains both
source supports and a fixed positive physical time buffer
around them. Its two endpoint time layers form a boundary
cell set B_a. The buffer can be chosen such that

 dist_G(A_+,B_a)>=b0/a,       b0>0.                    (3.2)

Let N_+,N_-,N_B denote these three cell counts. The source
counts have the bound in(3.1). At fixed physical spatial
volume, N_B<=K_B a^-3.

If the spatial volume also grows along the sequence, retain
N_B in the bound below. The stated a^-15 simplification
requires fixed spatial volume or a corresponding polynomial
volume bound. The theorem does not conceal a whole-volume
boundary count.

Within the finite induced graph on Lambda_a define two events:

 E_in: a bad-cell path joins A_+ to A_-;
 E_exit: a bad-cell path joins A_+ to B_a.

Both events depend on finitely many action cells. They may
be described using finitely many self-avoiding paths, and
are Boolean functions of X_c>=R.

4. SOURCE-WEIGHTED GROUND BOUNDS FOR THE TWO FINITE EVENTS

Define W=|(Q_+-EQ_+)(Q_--EQ_-)|. The source-mark estimate in
spacetime_marked_bridges_turn68.txt, including arbitrary
distinct source marks outside a prescribed bad path, gives

 E[W 1_E_in]
 <=16 M_+M_-(C+c)^2 N_+^2 N_-^2
          q(Dq)^(d0/a)/(1-Dq).                        (4.1)

The same argument with path starts in A_+ and ends in B_a gives

 E[W 1_E_exit]
 <=16 M_+M_-(C+c)^2 N_+^2 N_- N_B
          q(Dq)^(b0/a)/(1-Dq).                        (4.2)

Indeed, expand the two centered sources into N_+N_- distinct
marked pairs. For each such pair sum the prescribed-path
bound over N_+N_B possible start/boundary endpoints. The
marks need not lie on that path. The graph distance lower
bound and the self-avoiding path sum are unchanged.

At fixed physical spatial volume the prefactors are at most
order a^-16 and a^-15, respectively. All expectations and
source means in this section use the same actual Hamiltonian
ground law.

5. CONVERGENCE OF THE FINITE EVENT-WEIGHTED STATISTICS

Fix ONE spatial regulator a for this paragraph. Restrict temporal
meshes to delta_m=a/m, with m->infinity, so each action cell
contains an exact integer number of temporal mesh intervals.
Use the literal left-sampled original Wilson observables

 X_c^(delta)=(delta/(ag_a^2)) sum_(time steps in I_c) S_b^cl(U_j),
 Q_+^(delta)=4delta/(ag_a^2) sum_(j,p) f_+(j delta,p)h_p(U_j),

and similarly for Q_-^(delta). Cell endpoints and source
windows may be rounded as in the additive-source theorem.
For continuous or bounded piecewise continuous time profiles,
this is precisely the temporal approximation of the stated
magnetic integrals.

The finite vector consisting of all X_c, c in Lambda_a,
and Q_+,Q_- has uniformly bounded support at this FIXED a.
For example, h_p<=2 and the total time length in each cell
is a. Divergence of this bound as a->0 is allowed.

For each m choose a finite original period N_m AFTER delta_m,
long enough that the first m mixed moments of this finite
vector agree with its ground-chain moments to a tolerance
tending to zero. The finite list can include every monomial
of total degree at most m. The period may be constrained to
be a multiple of2m and to contain Lambda_a without wraparound.
It can also satisfy N_m delta_m->infinity.

The established source theorem and compact support imply that
the resulting finite-period Wilson vector converges in law
to the actual continuous-time ground vector. One direct proof
uses convergence of all polynomial moments, polynomial density
on the common compact box, and uniform bounds. Alternatively
one may use the locally uniform joint moment generators.

By(2.1), both event boundaries have zero measure for the
limiting vector. Consequently bounded weighted versions of
their indicators have convergent expectations. In particular,
with ORIGINAL finite-period centering,

 W_delta,N =
 |(Q_+^(delta)-E_delta,N Q_+^(delta))
  (Q_-^(delta)-E_delta,N Q_-^(delta))|,

we have along this fixed-a finite-period diagonal

 E_delta,N[W_delta,N 1_E_in^(delta)] -> E[W1_E_in],
 E_delta,N[W_delta,N 1_E_exit^(delta)] -> E[W1_E_exit]. (5.1)

The changing source means converge by the same first-moment
theorem and change these bounded weights uniformly on the
fixed-a compact box.

There is no need to prove atomlessness at every intermediate
temporal mesh. We apply convergence in law of the final
finite-period diagonal directly to the limiting continuity
sets. The countable threshold choice only concerns that limit.

6. WHY THIS CONTROLS BRIDGES ON THE ENTIRE FINITE TIME CIRCLE

For the finite original Wilson regulator, partition the complete
periodic time direction into cells of length a. This is possible
because N is a multiple of m when delta=a/m. Take the original
period large enough that Lambda_a is embedded without identifying
its two boundary layers or its external neighboring layers.

Let E_full^(delta,N) be the event of ANY bad-cell path on this
whole periodic board joining A_+ to A_-. A self-avoiding witness
path either stays inside Lambda_a, or has a first exit from it.
In the latter case the segment from its A_+ endpoint to the
last cell before that exit lies inside Lambda_a and ends in
B_a. Local touching adjacency cannot skip this boundary.
Thus the following pointwise implication is exact:

 E_full^(delta,N) subset
             E_in^(delta) union E_exit^(delta).       (6.1)

This step handles paths that make a long detour through the
rest of the time circle. It does not assert that they are
statistically independent of the observed slab.

Multiplying(6.1) by the nonnegative original centered weight
W_delta,N gives a bound by the two finite-window expectations
in(5.1). For each spatial a and any prescribed epsilon_a>0,
choose delta_a and then N_a along Section5's approximating
sequence so their summed error is at most epsilon_a.
Then, on the literal ORIGINAL finite Wilson law,

 E_delta_a,N_a[W_delta_a,N_a 1_E_full]
 <=16 M_+M_-(C+c)^2 q/(1-Dq)
    [N_+^2 N_-^2 (Dq)^(d0/a)
      +N_+^2 N_- N_B (Dq)^(b0/a)] + epsilon_a.         (6.2)

One may impose delta_a<=a^2 and a physical time period
N_a delta_a->infinity at the same time. Extra finite lists
of source jets can be included in the same choices.

At fixed physical spatial volume, setting
eta=Dq<1 and k=min(d0,b0)|log eta|>0 gives

 E_delta_a,N_a[W_delta_a,N_a 1_E_full]
                   <=K a^-16 exp(-k/a)+epsilon_a.      (6.3)

The temporal approximation error can be chosen arbitrarily
small at each a, including epsilon_a<=exp(-k/a).
No uniform temporal convergence rate is claimed.

Polynomial or logarithmic multiplicative calibrations may
be included in the fixed-a source list and error budget, and
are absorbed by the exponential bound if their sizes have
the stipulated polynomial/logarithmic upper estimates.
General unknown operator mixing is not covered.

6A. AN ADAPTIVE TIME BUFFER REMOVES THE SPATIAL-VOLUME RESTRICTION

For the existential temporal diagonal, one can improve the
preceding fixed-volume version. The number N_B of cells on
the two temporal boundary layers depends on the finite spatial
board, not on the duration of Lambda_a. Let eta=Dq<1 and choose
an integer buffer distance

 k_a >= d0/a + log(max(1,N_B))/|log eta|.               (6.4)

Enlarge the observation time slab so that the graph distance
from A_+ to its two boundary layers is at least k_a. One extra
time-cell margin absorbs integer rounding. All spatial tiles
remain included. Then

                   N_B eta^k_a<=eta^(d0/a).            (6.5)

The exit term in(6.2) is consequently bounded by the same
constant times

                   N_+^2 N_- eta^(d0/a),

whose polynomial prefactor is only O(a^-12) for the fixed
compact source supports. The inside term remains O(a^-16).
Thus a bound K a^-16 exp(-k/a)+epsilon_a holds on ANY countable
sequence of finite spatial volumes, provided the fixed source
geometry retains(3.1).

For each a the enlarged slab still contains finitely many
cells. The threshold choice covered all time cells from the
start, and the moment-convergence/continuity argument applies
unchanged to this larger finite vector. Choose delta and the
finite temporal period only after this slab has been chosen.

This extension does not give a uniform estimate at a prescribed
physical time period or buffer size. Its period may depend on
the spatial volume through(6.4), and its required temporal mesh
can be arbitrarily fine. It also does not establish a compatible
infinite-spatial-volume local theory. It proves precisely the
source-weighted bad-bridge bound on the selectable original
finite-regulator diagonal.

7. WHAT HAS AND HAS NOT BEEN ATTACHED

Equation(6.2) is a statement about original finite anisotropic
Wilson measures, including their actual denominator, original
source means, and all paths on the finite time circle.
Gauge-invariant action/source words in the full Hamiltonian
ground process agree with those in the physical ground sector.
No equality of gauge-coordinate path laws at finite temperature
is needed.

The theorem applies to a temporal diagonal that can be selected
after each spatial regulator. It is not a uniform estimate for
every relationship between spatial and temporal lattice spacings.
It is not an assertion about the original isotropic cutoff
family, or an admission of Bałaban's isotropic expansion on the
chosen anisotropic family.

The source-weighted contribution of the simple time-averaged
bad-action bridge event is now attached to the original Wilson
regularization. The event is still not a native history
predicate and does not prohibit short temporal spikes.

The complementary centered covariance, physical operator
renormalization, selection of a running coupling, compatible
continuum limits, full Euclidean symmetry and a nonzero
physical limit remain unproved. Suppression of this explicitly
specified contribution does not by itself prove YM4 existence.
