All p below are fixed. The real linear-tilt argument gives uniformly

For 2<=r<=4, let A_1,...,A_r have reads X_1,...,X_r. Form a minimum
spanning tree on these r labels with edge weights d(X_j,X_k). Let its

triangle inequality.

products of (2.2). Replace all crossing moments in the finite

 |Cum_mu(A_1,...,A_r)|
 <= C_r exp[-nu T_X/(r-1)] product_j N_(2r)(A_j).                (2.3)

Cumulants are unchanged by centering individual insertions. Reads that
overlap simply have zero corresponding distances and are covered by the

polynomial moments/gradient sums. It does not replace P by an uncharged
point. The proof also works at any other FIXED r, but only r<=4 is used.

Quasilocal native coefficients are handled by their positive complete-read

There are only finitely many insertions in (2.3); there is no new

7. WHY A NARROW ANALYTIC TUBE ALONE DOES NOT GIVE A RELATIVE g^2 BIRTH

The source derivative price is essential. Even a gauge-invariant example
can have unit real cap and a narrow analytic tube while its quantum change
is of order one. Near flat take a physical plaquette q(U)=1-tr U_p>=0 and
F_g(U)=exp[-q(U)/g^2]. It is gauge invariant, bounded by one on real fields,
and analytic. On a fixed multiple of a g^2 imaginary link tube its modulus

q(Phi(gx))=g^2 Q(x)+O(g^3 polynomial(x)) with nonzero Q>=0. Hence at a flat
background QF_g-F_g(flat) tends to E exp[-Q(x)]-1, which is nonzero.

This example diagnoses a value-only norm assertion; it is not an extra
source inserted into the original problem. Its derivative norm grows as
a power of g^-1 and is correctly charged by (0.1). The exponentially rare
amplitude of a genuinely rare input may pay that cost ONCE; arbitrary
bounded-Borel input normalization cannot. No property of the smooth
original Wilson plaquette requires this artificial narrow-tube example.

9. SCOPE OF THE ADVANCE

This establishes a new route to a finite, normalized quantum source
factor g^2 with polynomial source-read costs. The fourth-cumulant bound,

outer-sector complements, or pay the original UV root density through an
arbitrary suffix. No Yang--Mills existence/nontriviality conclusion follows.
