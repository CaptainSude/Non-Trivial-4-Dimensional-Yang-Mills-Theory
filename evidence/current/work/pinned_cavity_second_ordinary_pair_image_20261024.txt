6. WHY THIS DOES NOT YET IMPLEMENT AN ADMISSION DELAY

For the direct-ball route alone, write a_s for an effective old source
strong allowance after its direct/reconstructed margins have been paid.
Repeated use of the conservative argument above certifies at best

          a_(s+1)<=c_* a_s,  0<c_*<1,
          C_H delta_s <= c_* a_s.                     (15)

These are sufficient certificate inequalities, not optimal necessary
conditions on the true maps. Nevertheless they cannot be advertised
as the desired growing-radius preparation. With comparable weak
couplings over a finite initial stretch and a_0~tau, (15) permits at
most a number of steps of order

             log[(tau/delta_typical)]/|log c_*|        (16)

before this particular certificate ceases to include the noise. In
the actual first profiles tau/delta is only a fixed A0/A1 ratio (up
to adjacent variation), making this bound constant after constants
are fixed. Even optimistically comparing tau only to g0 leaves merely
O(log log(g0^-2)) steps. That is not the O(log(g0^-1)) delay required
to bring tau to a fixed physical core by geometric scaling.

Rarity can pay inverse-radius AMPLITUDE factors but cannot make (15)
true once the actual original noise leaves the domain. The possible
L^2 rescaling of physical curvature is not a proof of radius growth:
the actual old/current representative, near map, currents and primitive
reads must be transformed first, and all comparison constants retained.
CMP102 Theorem1 explicitly allows B3 to depend on d and L. Therefore
choosing L large does not prove L^2 dominates the direct-to-E loss.
No claim about the exact unread OCR prefactor in its (162) is needed.

The next decisive input is either a quantitatively EXPANDING complete
near/old pair image theorem with those actual constants, or a composed
kernel theorem which avoids iterating a shrinking domain. Neither follows
from the map lemma in Section3, from static curvature dilution, or from
the first coefficient's very small norm.

the true nonlinear current, the complete joint source weakening, the

identities by themselves do not prove this analytic image bound. This
route has not been ruled out by(15), because it does not iterate that
particular smaller-domain certificate; nor has it been established here.
