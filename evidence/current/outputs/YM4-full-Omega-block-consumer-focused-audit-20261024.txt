The maximal coupling in (12) concerns the entire protected marginal.
On its failure event, subtracting each law's own unconditional coordinate
mean and applying Cauchy--Schwarz gives the displayed sqrt(p) term.
No conditional second moment on that failure event is required.

For far blocks the common-tilt TV comparison uses osc(V_B)<=delta|B|<=1,
while changing the actual boundary coordinate j changes only potentials
containing j. Their sum has oscillation O(delta exp(-kappa d(j,B))).
Thus (9) has no O(delta|B|) floor. In (16) the mean-offset term is
bounded using a_B p/(1+s)<=C[exp(-nu k)+delta exp(-kappa k)]; the
unconditional variance terms give sqrt(p)/(1+s). These bounds retain
no spin-diameter factor.

For near blocks, the count O(ell^(d-1)(r+1)) and the protected complement
size O((r+1)^d) yield the leading ratio O((r+1)^(d+1)/ell). Taking
r=A log(ell+2), then fixed large ell, then delta small as (18), pays it.
The far shell sum is exponentially summable. The weighted version pays
exp(lambda(k+C ell)) from a fixed rate reserve and lambda=O(1/ell).
The exact N containing blocks erase a one-site disagreement, yielding
positive contraction after the summed births.

The hybrid cost's discrete unit term does not require changing the
underlying measurable spaces. Couplings and disintegrations remain on
the original compact standard-Borel coordinate spaces; the cost is
lower semicontinuous in their original topology. Coordinatewise gluing
and semigroup contraction may therefore be formulated there.

Finally, differentiating mu[(P_t F)G] gives minus the sum of conditional
covariances in (23). Independent same-boundary resamples have uniformly
bounded coordinate second moments for their differences, since their
means cancel. This proves (24), and each coordinate belongs to N blocks,
with at most N sites per block; integration of exp(-gamma t), gamma>=N/2,
gives (25). For bounded local G its coordinate profile is osc(G)1_T;
symmetry yields min(|S|,|T|) in (26). These read-size factors remain
explicit and are not silently replaced by a size-free constant.
