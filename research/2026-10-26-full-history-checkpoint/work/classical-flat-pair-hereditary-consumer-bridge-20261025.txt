9. STATIC AGE REPAIR FOR A RUNNING-BORN SOURCE

Suppose an old source is only admitted with relative field/C1 cap a1_i,
actual curvature cap a0_i, and current cap gamma_i. Take ONE complete
flat final-scale response pair at refinement M R_age, and view it in
the old M units on the same graph. Exact regrading gives

 old potential H_old=R_age^-1 H_final,
 old first difference=R_age^-2 nabla_final H_final,
 old scaled curvature=R_age^-2 curvature_final,
 old selected current=R_age^-3 J_final.                  (17)

The last equality is imposed on the declared selected polynomial, not
deduced from a weakened physical-current identity. Delta and P1 have
engineering order two, so applied to H they scale by R_age^-3; every
complete kernel uses its native measure. All copies retain the same
scalar weights under this coordinate relabeling. The local forcing
scales identically by its literal lattice formula.

Here the regraded P1 retains the SAME determining/gauge-fixing geometry
as the final-scale response, expressed in old coordinates. It is not
being identified with a separately reconstructed P1 for a different
old-scale constraint tower. At s=1 the scalar equality is supplied by
the physical minimum/current semigroup; at s!=1 this is the declared
localized extension of that scalar. Equality to a pre-existing different
off-graph old-scale localized family is neither required for this new
classical relocalization nor proved by engineering dimensions alone.

Thus the old own-domain image is admitted for

 b<=c min(r0,R_age a1_i,R_age^2 a0_i,R_age^3 gamma_i).    (18)

If every cap is bounded below by a common a_i, the conservative condition
b<=c min(r0,a_i R_age) suffices. Flat I satisfies every hereditary test.
The constant c includes the actual smaller direct-test caps and their
fixed ratios required by the old direct-to-full implication, applied
ONCE on the source's complete own determining tower. Merely meeting
the larger displayed bounds without this (iv) margin is not enough.
If the old source asks additionally for strong field slots, Delta/P1
also gain R_age^-3. The supported-family proof applies after this exact
regrading: it is not a sequence of B3 bounds.

The source radius can therefore become a fixed b after a logarithmic
STATIC classical age when a_i R_age is sufficiently large. The rooted
carrier compilation must retain the explicit R_age^4 old-root census,
its compression/halo constants and any inverse old-radius factors; it
is not a norm-one operation. These are the same complete support costs
which the high-jet transport proof is designed to record.

Equation(18) proves a static classical orbit image. It does NOT show that
every intermediate quantum image remains in the old source domain, or
that the actual old density is analytic on a fixed background ball.
An exact core-plus-running-tag ledger or an actual age-kernel theorem
would be needed to use it for a full quantum history.
