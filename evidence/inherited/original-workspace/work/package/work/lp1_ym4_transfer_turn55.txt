1. THE CHAMBER GEOMETRY REALLY SUPPLIES NORMAL CROSSINGS

README and LPO1 pp.13--15, I.a--I.b/F.a.

Let W=Z^3 semidirect B_3, where B_3 denotes the signed permutation
group. The region

                 0<x_3<x_2<x_1<1/2

is a fundamental alcove for this affine reflection action. Its volume
is (1/2)^3/3!=1/48. An interior v_0 has trivial stabilizer; the chamber
sites Wv_0 consequently have 48 points per photon lattice cell.

The four neighbors g s_i v_0 are reflections across the four walls of
gC_0. In the fundamental chamber, orthogonal projection onto each wall
lies in its wall face: zeroing x_3, averaging an adjacent ordered pair,
or setting x_1=1/2 preserves all other weak ordering inequalities.
The connecting segment lies in the two adjacent chamber closures.
Thus a fixed arrangement mirror can be crossed only through that
common wall. Every crossing chamber edge is normal to the mirror,
and its endpoints are exchanged by the reflection. An edge is not
merely assigned to the nearest mirror after the fact.

There is also a simple check on the photon projection pi. At v_0,
pi(v_0)=0. The first three simple reflections keep the nearest photon
site equal to 0; s_3 changes it to e_1. After applying any g, nearest
site projection is equivariant and remains unique. Hence chamber
hops project either to a single photon site or to a genuine nearest
neighbor photon link. Internal hops have A(x,x)=0.

This proves the basic crossing geometry from the stated chamber
definition. It does not establish the Poisson-summed photon Gram
factor, the complete fermionic reflection convention, or gluing.

The mirror family is exactly

 x_j=c, c in (1/2)Z;       x_j +/- x_k=c, c in Z.

The different offset grids are necessary: reflection in x_j=c is a
lattice map iff 2c is integral, whereas the diagonal formula requires
c integral. There are three coordinate and six diagonal families.

In four dimensions the same geometry has the alcove
0<x_4<x_3<x_2<x_1<1/2, five chamber neighbors, and 2^4 4!=384
sites per photon cell. Its mirror families number 4+12=16.
This is a geometric extension, not a construction of a nonabelian
four-dimensional gauge/fermion measure. For pure YM no such fermion
refinement is needed.

2. ORIENTATION, SPIN AND THE CLOSING CHARACTER CANNOT BE DROPPED

LPO1 p.13, I.a; pp.14--15, I.b/F.a; p.30, C.b.

The abelian curvature is an oriented two-cochain. Pullback includes
the induced orientation. For x_1=0, F_12 and F_13 change sign and
F_23 does not. For x_1=x_2, F_12 changes sign and F_13/F_23 are
exchanged, with the transformed locations understood. An observable
reflection is the ANTILINEAR operation

                  Theta A = conjugate(A composed with theta^*).

In particular Theta phi_beta=phi_(-theta^* beta). The minus sign here
comes from complex conjugation in addition to the tensor pullback.
Treating all plaquette components as scalars would invalidate the
reflected form and the characteristic-function restoration argument.

For the doubled three-dimensional Clifford matrices in the README,
gamma_n^2=I for a unit normal n. With P_+/-=(I+/-gamma_n)/2,

 I-rho gamma_n=(1-rho)P_+ +(1+rho)P_-.

This is positive when 0<rho<1. Multiplication of the crossing hopping
matrix by the reflection Clifford factor, with the appropriate
fermionic order convention, yields these positive coefficients.
The hopping matrix rho I-gamma_n itself is indefinite; it must not
be advertised as the positive Gram matrix. The reverse orientation
uses the corresponding I+rho gamma_n. The exact Berezin ordering
and reflection signs remain part of the finite fermion module.

The same strict range makes every matching hopping matrix invertible:
its eigenvalues are w_i(rho+/-1)/2. Thus rho=1 is not a harmless
endpoint in E.a's layer-elimination argument.

The doubled spin representation also accommodates improper spatial
maps. If J exchanges the two Pauli blocks, then J anticommutes with
all gamma_j, and J gamma_n implements the reflection of gamma_v
under conjugation. Its phase can be changed without affecting that
conjugation. This algebra checks why a spin lift is available; it
does not remove the Fock sign at a periodic seam.

For real M and symmetric edge weights, the two Pauli blocks of the
finite operator are adjoints, including the unitary link phases and
boundary characters. Thus det D=|det D_+|^2>=0. Strict positivity of
the integrated normalization still requires its finite-model input;
semidefiniteness alone is not a proof that the normalizer is nonzero.

On a cubic torus the antiperiodic character is
chi(n m)=(-1)^(m_1+m_2+m_3). Signed coordinate permutations preserve
it. But chi(n(e_1+e_2))=+1: a diagonal cross-section does NOT inherit
an independent antiperiodic character in every adapted direction.

F.a correctly retains the exact factor

        Tr[V_g^(-1) U_f^((1+chi(g))/2) S_r ... S_1].

Periodic chi(g)=+1 inserts Fock parity; antiperiodic chi(g)=-1 does
not. The transport V_g can also have a tangential part. Only the
reflected even fold on the straightened torus cancels the relevant
transport and yields a positive Schatten power.

The distinction D'_n versus D_n in the source's chain records an
actual change of fermion character. They cannot be identified just
because their underlying period lattices agree. For pure Wilson YM
there is no fermion character or Fock parity, so this middle step
disappears. The geometric tangential closing transport remains.

3. THE PRECISE PURE-WILSON DIAGONAL SUBLEMMA

This is independently checked here and already written in archived
Notebook 02, Section 2, equations (2.1)--(2.5).

Use SU(N) link variables and positive Wilson factor

             k_beta(U)=exp[(beta/N) Re Tr U], beta>0.

At an integer mirror x_1-x_2=c, no coordinate link crosses the plane
in its interior. A (1,2) plaquette based at height c has vertex
heights c,c+1,c-1,c. It is the only crossing plaquette type.
It splits into two length-two paths with the same boundary endpoints.
If their holonomies, in the same endpoint orientation, are A and B,
the weight is k_beta(AB^*).

Write u(A) for the real and imaginary matrix entries of A. Then
u(A).u(B)=Re Tr(AB^*) and |u(A)|^2=N. The absolutely convergent
feature expansion

 Phi_alpha(A)=[(beta/N)^|alpha|/alpha!]^(1/2) u(A)^alpha

has

 <Phi(B),Phi(A)>=k_beta(AB^*),    ||Phi(A)||^2=e^beta.

Thus the crossing factor is genuinely a positive Gram kernel.
Noncommutativity causes no factorization error: the two entire
ordered path holonomies are the arguments. Reflection exchanges
the paths, rather than arbitrarily reversing matrix factors.

Boundary links carry Haar measure once. Plaquettes in the mirror
carry square-root weights on the two adjacent slabs. Contracting
the feature indices restores each crossing plaquette exactly.
At fixed finite cross-section all weights and feature norms are
bounded, so these kernels are Hilbert--Schmidt. Gluing is exact,
and geometric reflection gives the adjoint.

Consequently the exact twisted slab trace and Schatten folds are
available for this pure-gauge geometry. A coordinate cubic torus
usually does not split into two halves at the selected diagonal:
the complement of a single diagonal cut need not separate it.
The twisted trace is not automatically a positive reflected form.
One still needs the adapted-period comparison.

The SU(N) Gram construction alone is not a proof of the whole
fixed-coupling infinite-volume theorem for arbitrary SU(N). The
archived local convolution/layer-reduction estimates were supplied
in explicit SU(2) form; their extension must be checked separately.

For nonabelian configurations the safe state space here is the
compact product G^E of oriented links, U_bar(e)=U_e^(-1). The
reflection acts geometrically on these links. A based plaquette
holonomy can acquire a conjugation when its basepoint changes and
is inverted when orientation reverses. There is no global abelian
identity dF=0 for Lie-algebra plaquette logarithms, nor an automatic
symmetry F -> -F. LP1's closed real two-cochain probability theorem
must not be substituted for this link-space statement.

4. WHAT RESTORES THE COMPARISON STATE -- AND WHAT DOES NOT

LPO1 F.b--c pp.16--17; M.b p.22; H.a--b pp.23--24;
E.a--d pp.25--28; C.a--b pp.29--30; S.a--c pp.34--36.

The logical transfer is sound at the explicit model inputs:

 (i) Full reflected arrays of a bounded real local O produce tilted
     adapted-torus laws with RP at a specified fixed wall.
 (ii) The pressure comparison gives nu(O)<=lambda_O(O) after volume
      tends to infinity and then the tilt tends to zero.
 (iii) Coordinate operator bounds plus local two-layer reduction
       restore even translations of lambda_O.
 (iv) Position O at the target wall, transport the expectation back,
      and use compact convex separation for every real determining
      local test. Different O may use different lambda_O.

Steps (iii) and "every real determining local test" are substantive.
A limiting tilted Gibbs/DLR state need not be translation invariant.
Taking t down to zero does not imply convergence to the original
state without uniqueness. Averaging auxiliary states over arbitrary
translations would move the RP wall and would not repair this step.

The general near-Hermiticity argument H.a does transfer:
if o(1)=1 and |o(X)|<=gamma ||X|| on a common local unital
*-algebra, then

 |o(X^*)-conjugate(o(X))|<=4 sqrt(gamma^2-1)||X||.

For self-adjoint X of norm at most one, write o(X)=u+iv and apply
the norm bound to X+is. Optimizing s yields |v|<=sqrt(gamma^2-1).
Taking the real and imaginary self-adjoint parts proves the formula.
No positivity assumption on this operator functional is smuggled in.
The limiting contractive unital functional is Hermitian. Reflection
as adjoint then gives the required two-layer invariance.

But these LP1 model-specific mechanisms do NOT directly transfer:

 * E.a uses the chamber graph's perfect matching and invertible
   fermionic hopping matrices, plus Berezin integration by parts.
 * E.c unfolds compact photon links with all integer fluxes and
   uses the exact quadratic Maxwell contour shift. The resulting
   character cancels an outer plane's abelian exponent. Nonabelian
   Wilson multiplication has no such linear cancellation formula.
 * H.b's two heat scales and electric-shift norm identity exploit
   abelian Gaussian convolution and its charge basis.
 * The all-flux sharp Gaussian bound supplies QED tightness and
   estimates for unbounded curvature insertions. It is not a YM
   Gaussian-domination theorem.

For pure compact Wilson YM, compactness replaces QED's Gaussian
tightness only at FIXED lattice spacing in link space. The archived
SU(2) proof supplies its different restoration mechanism: positive
electric-convolution eigenvalues, finite Peter--Weyl approximation,
and an explicit gauge-covariant local reduction to two slices.
See Notebook 02 Sections 3--4 and 7. Positivity of convolution by
itself does not prove that local reduction.

The convex separation theorem S.b checks directly: use the Hausdorff
topology on finite signed measures generated by the determining real
test space. Compactness of the RP class and strict separation would
produce a single violating test. In the compact-link specialization,
gauge-invariant local Peter--Weyl tests suffice within gauge-invariant
laws; finite endpoint gauge averaging preserves a separating
expectation. No common comparison state for all O is required.

RP passes to the complete open-side L2 space by finite-coordinate
approximation and the reflection isometry. LP1 explicitly excludes
plaquettes on or crossing the mirror. Any enlarged closed-side
claim must be justified by the corresponding boundary construction,
not obtained by changing this convention silently.

5. THE SAME-STATE RESULT DOES NOT REQUIRE AN RG SOURCE ALGEBRA

For each fixed beta, O is bounded on its finite compact link space.
The reflected auxiliary tilt exp[t sum O_c] is therefore a valid
finite probability tilt for every finite torus. Its required
operator and local reduction estimates have fixed-beta constants.
They need not be uniform in beta for the volume-first argument.

Thus, at the archived Wilson modules, this route removes the need
to propagate diagonal RP through every individual gauge-fixing,
cutoff, reblocking or auxiliary weak-field operation. It also
removes uniqueness as a symmetry input. RP is proved for the SAME
original infinite-volume Wilson limit, using auxiliary laws only
inside the domination/separation argument.

It does not turn our constant original-action source estimate into
the determining-test comparison. Here arbitrary bounded local O is
handled directly by the fixed-lattice Wilson measure and its
operator modules. Nor does it identify a retained native R output
with that original measure. If the construction only controls a
selected or insufficiently identified full law, that identification
gate remains exactly where frontier54 places it.

6. A CONCRETE FOUR-DIMENSIONAL ARBITRARY-SIDE EXTENSION

This is a conditional extension of the archived fixed-beta Wilson
argument using LP1 V.a--c, pp.31--33. Its new arithmetic and volume
accounting are explicit below. The imported inputs are exact Wilson
slabs/folds, the coordinate pressure limit, and the existing
fixed-beta amplification/restoration modules. This section does
not claim to re-prove those modules.

Fix beta and an admissible even cell width h, large compared with
the support of O. Take a companion m<=n with 4h dividing m.
In the orthogonal heights

           (x_1-x_2, x_1+x_2, x_3, x_4)

the successive original heights are (n,2n,n,n). Choose fold counts

           2k=(m/h, 2m/h, m/h, m/h).

At each step take 2k_a-1 regular slabs of height h and one empty
slab of the remaining height. The latter is positive for n>=m.
Its own straightened torus has normal period 2k_a times that
remaining height. Its possibly large volume is charged with its
Schatten exponent, not as an unweighted exceptional volume.

Repeated use of the exact one-step identity

       |T|=sum_j |T_j|/(2k_a)

shows that the final regular weighted volume is

       V_reg=(m-h)^3(2m-h)/2.                         (6.1)

The diagonal cell volume is |Q|=h^4/2, and its number of regular
copies is R=V_reg/|Q|. The final full adapted torus
D(m/2,m,m,m) has volume m^4 and 2m^4/h^4 cells.
The sum of ALL exceptional weighted volumes is exactly

                         V_exc=n^4-V_reg.             (6.2)

This follows either by telescoping the four volume identities or
by noting the successive retained weighted volumes:

 (m-h)n^3,
 (m-h)(2m-h)n^2/2,
 (m-h)^2(2m-h)n/2,
 (m-h)^3(2m-h)/2.

For the unnormalized Wilson weight of Section 3 there are six
plaquettes per four-dimensional site and

       -6 beta <= |T|^(-1) log Z_T <= 6 beta

on the admissible nondegenerate tori. Let bar f_beta be this common
upper bound and f_beta,infinity the coordinate pressure limit.
The same Jensen/convexity calculation as LP1 V.b gives

 mu_C(n)(O) <= mu_D(m),t(O) + (|Q|/t) Delta_beta(n,m,h),

 Delta_beta =
   f_beta(m/2,2m,m,m)-f_beta,infinity
   + (V_exc/V_reg)(bar f_beta-f_beta,infinity).          (6.3)

Full reflected arrays are used on the final torus. The original
translated copies have the same expectation by the original
Wilson measure's lattice symmetries. The empty slab has no source.
The weighted-volume identity is what prevents the exceptional
large tori from producing a nonvanishing pressure error.

For arbitrary integers n_i tending to infinity choose, for example,

 k(n)=max{k>=1:8 k!<=sqrt(n)},
 m(n)=8 k(n)! floor[n/(8 k(n)!)],

after defining the finitely many small cases harmlessly.
Then m<=n, n-m<sqrt(n), m/n->1, and every fixed positive integer
divides m eventually. Thus every fixed h is eventually admissible.
At fixed beta,

 V_reg/n^4=(m/n)^4(1-h/m)^3(1-h/(2m)) -> 1,

and all comparison sides grow. Consequently Delta_beta->0.
First take volume to infinity at fixed t>0, then t down to zero.
LP1's separation architecture and the archived Wilson restoration
modules then extend the SAME-state fixed-beta RP conclusion to
arbitrary cubic volume subsequences.

This comparison does not identify the original and companion
finite laws. It removes the divisibility restriction on the
original sequence by expectation domination. It requires no new
fermionic spin or flux estimate in the pure-gauge model.

The estimate is not uniform as beta tends to infinity. In particular,
the elementary exceptional-pressure bound itself grows with beta,
and convergence of the coordinate pressure has no ultraviolet rate
here. An arbitrary simultaneous volume/cutoff limit is not licensed.

7. EFFECT ON THE CURRENT YM4 PROGRAM

Concrete route choice: retain the original pure-Wilson regulator,
prove its same-state coordinate/diagonal RP at each fixed spacing
by the existing Wilson slab route, and use the unequal folds above
if arbitrary original volume sequences matter. Then take a
volume-first ultraviolet limit only after the required physical
observable bounds are obtained. Do not add chamber fermions merely
to recover this regulator symmetry.

This separates a symmetry obligation from the native large-field
continuation/normalization work. It can avoid demanding that each
auxiliary RG kernel or selected window preserve diagonal RP.
It does not eliminate:

 * identification of the actual original full Wilson law and its
   inserted numerators, including the missing whole-R identity;
 * common bare data and the required quantitative comparison of
   the physical observables controlled by the RG;
 * uniform ultraviolet tightness and convergence of the same
   reflected observable algebra;
 * Euclidean rotation symmetry beyond lattice signed permutations;
 * continuum reconstruction and nontriviality.

The sixteen B_4 mirror families generate lattice isometries with
signed-permutation linear parts, not arbitrary SO(4) rotations.
Having their RP inequalities at every cutoff does not prove full
continuum rotation invariance. Likewise fixed-spacing compactness
of G^E says nothing by itself about tightness of renormalized
curvatures or fields as the lattice spacing tends to zero.

LP1 therefore supplies a precise route clarification and a useful
arbitrary-volume refinement. It is not the missing full-measure
Yang--Mills existence argument.
