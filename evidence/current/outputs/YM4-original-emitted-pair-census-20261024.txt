4. A SHARP SUFFICIENT OLD-PROPER INTERFACE, WITH ITS COMPLETE SUM

The following is a proved CONSUMER conditional on its enumerated
initial source/slot match, not a claim that the full catalogue meets it.
It specifies a small concrete target rather than requiring a uniform
variance of the entire physical source.

Suppose actual emitted fresh pairs at b have source records (i,n,r),
i<=n<=b, with the following properties inside ONE actual compatible law:

 * The complete branchwise source, selector and old-real read are the
   already admitted fresh noisy input, in the original live-T realization.
 * Per original root, the complete value/seed prices obey (5), with
   u_i<=U(1+gamma_i)^D, and analogous prices for the second arm.
   Any extra guarded-seed factor is explicitly included in u_i P(n-i)
   or in the bounds below; it is not declared a uniform constant.
 * The pre-emission proper clock can be attached to the SAME initial
   canonical outer slots/full forest with fixed multiplicity, so the
   already paid (2) has the additional factor
       exp[-sigma(b-n_p)] exp[-sigma(b-n_q)].           (7)
   One-date slack changes only a constant. Both factors occur before
   the common outer integration. The required finite extra cost tilt
   is allowed in the fixed-floor choices of the payment theorem.
 * Each guarded current seed lies within a fixed number of b-cells of
   its original proper/source root; complete source-to-seed connectors
   are retained if this is not literally the chosen convention.
 * The original source profiles are separated by delta>0. The native
   bounded-step profile gives gamma_i<=gamma_T+omega(T-i).

Take sigma>4 log L. Write d=T-b, s=b-n, t=n-i. In one current b-cell,
there are at most C L^(4s) proper n-boxes and C L^(4t) original i-roots
per such box. Equations (5),(7) therefore bound one arm's total price
per current cell by

 C U sum_(s,t>=0) (1+gamma_T+omega(d+s+t))^D P(t)
          exp[-(sigma-4 log L)s] L^(-alpha t)
 <= C U (1+gamma_T+omega d)^D.                          (8)

The last inequality follows by factoring a fixed polynomial in s+t;
both remaining kernels are exponentially summable. If record counts
introduce fixed polynomials in s,t, the same proof includes them.
This uses the original two density factors L^(4s) and L^(4t). In
particular it does NOT replace an old i-root by a b-root at the start.

At current b the shell sum for the two-source exponential in (2)
then pays the second current position count. Splitting a fixed fraction
for physical separation gives

 sum_(all records at b) E_mu_b h_record
 <= C_T U^2 (1+V) L^(4d)
       (1+gamma_T+omega d)^(2D) exp[-c delta L^d].       (9)

Finite current-seed shifts only change constants. The sum of (9) over
d>=0 is finite, uniformly in the ultraviolet starting date. Thus the
complete i,n,b and ORIGINAL position sum is paid. This is the useful
form of an actual-suffix replacement for the old N/H sum, once the
fresh source and pre-emission cost annotation are really matched.

Extra seed prices matter. A factor exp[C K_(b,n)] per arm is harmless
if K_(b,n)<=K0+k1(b-n) with sigma>4 log L+C k1; a polynomial in any of
d,s,t is also harmless in (8). A factor exp[C L^(4(n-i))] is not paid
by (5). static_read_seed_positive_column avoids exactly that illegal
born-scale hull replacement. Its current guarded P has fixed size
only at its qualified whole-static-read scope, and it expressly retains
both the original i-root and proper n-box counts used in (8).

The necessity of a b-n payment is concrete. Keep a date b at fixed
physical scale near T and take n=i far in the UV. The Ward factor in
(5) is then one. There are L^[4(b-n)] old roots per current cell, while
current-seed separation supplies no increasing factor as n recedes.
Neither (2), a fixed current seed cost, nor current rarity at b sums
these roots. For two such arms both densities remain. This is an
obstruction to the PRESENT estimate, not a lower bound disproving the
actual Yang--Mills covariance; possible cancellations are not estimated
