HOMOGENEOUS COMPLEX PREFIX MAP WITH ORIGINAL NOISE COORDINATES
25 October 2026. Synthesis draft; independent review pending.

CLAIM AND SCOPE

Assuming (N1)--(N2) below for the correctly identified native response,
a finite homogeneous full-block ordinary prefix admits a common-frame
construction with independently selected currents and simultaneous
operator-weakening parameters. Original real
free-noise coordinates remain fixed. The map exists on fixed strict
top-pair caps provided the total physical noise size is sufficiently
small. Its bounds have geometric scale factors, with no fixed constant
raised to the prefix length in the field/current image estimate.

This is a MAP theorem, not a normalized-source catalogue or a complete
Yang--Mills construction. It preserves the original physical map on an
admitted unweakened real history. It does not assert that original
history masks are one on the entire constructed domain, or that those
masks have holomorphic continuations. All masks, scalar weights,
coarea factors and normalizers remain required by the original law.

There are two explicit native inputs to be audited in this synthesis:
(N1) the four-slot, independent-pair B116 response bound on the complete
direct/old-source intersection, in exactly the grade-j conventions;
(N2) equivariance of the complete localized response equations under
simultaneous fine background/current and coarse free-noise transport.
The exact free-pivot component of (N2) has a separate primary proof.
The covariant propagator/fixed-point component is specified below.
No statement for generalized determining-cube axial gauges is made.

PRIMARY MATCHING ISSUE FOUND DURING REVIEW: B102 (180) contains the
linear forcing Gtilde Delta^(2) H0 B, while B116 (1.4) omits it and
claims a quadratic A0. An explicit seed change K=(I+Gtilde Delta^(2))H0,
a=A0-Gtilde Delta^(2)H0 B reconciles the equations algebraically.
Its localized four-slot bound must be proved before the H0 notation

1. GEOMETRY, DATA, AND SPARE CAPS

Use original fine bonds throughout, with xi_j=L^{-j}, j=0,...,t.
Fix a compact reference Wbar with the complete real direct gauge-cube
tests at every grade, on fixed small caps, and the terminal fine
curvature bound. Retain all native primitive, block, current, average,
gauge and determining reads with their collars.

At the top choose an independent pair in the common frame

 U_t=exp(i xi_t B_t)Wbar,     J_t independent,

and a holomorphic frame Gamma_t. The original representative at the
top is U_t^{Gamma_t}; the current is transported simultaneously. It is
enough to take Gamma_t=1 when the top pair is already in this frame.
For physical matching require Gamma_t to be G-valued on the designated
real physical subfamily. Holomorphic invertibility alone is insufficient.
For physical top coarse data a B102 relative minimum chart and its
axial return supply the corresponding top parameterization; its full
chart must be retained rather than inferred from curvature alone.

Let b_t=max(||B_t||,||nabla_Wbar^{xi_t}B_t||),
k_t=xi_t^{-2}||dU_t-1||, c_t=||J_t||, and

 q_t=log max(1,||Gamma_t||,||Gamma_t^{-1}||).

All gauge/path matrix norms in this note are the unitarily invariant
operator norm, normalized by ||I||=1. Native Lie-field norms may use

The four-slot response constants and the fixed direct-to-full reserves

2. ORIGINAL COORDINATES AND ALIGNED RESPONSE

Suppose the common pair U_{j+1},J_{j+1} and frame Gamma_{j+1} have
already been constructed. At each free grade-j bond b define

 x_j^sharp(b)=Ad_{Gamma_{j+1}(b_-)^{-1}}x_j(b).          (1)

Only evaluation of a deterministic argument is involved. The integrated
variable is still x_j on its original real domain. No complex change
of integration variables, contour, or cutoff is made.

Put M_{j+1}=max(1,||Gamma_{j+1}||,||Gamma_{j+1}^{-1}||)
and delta_j^sharp=M_{j+1}^2 delta_j. Then

                  ||g_j x_j^sharp||<=delta_j^sharp.    (2)

Evaluate the literal private-pivot and localized minimum response at
(U_{j+1},J_{j+1},x_j^sharp,s_j):

 B'_j=g_j C_j x_j^sharp-h_j Dtilde_j(g_j C_j x_j^sharp),
 A_j=H0_j(s_j)B'_j+A0_j(s_j,H0_j(s_j)B'_j)
      -H_j(s_j)D_j(s_j,H0_j(s_j)B'_j+A0_j(...)).       (3)

The h_j in (3) is the private-pivot map. It is not an axial gauge.
Use the four-slot norm from B109 (3.14):

 ||A_j||, ||nabla_{U_{j+1}}^{xi_j}A_j||,
 ||P1_j A_j||, ||Delta_{U_{j+1}}^{xi_j}A_j||
                         <=C_H delta_j^sharp.          (4)

B116 (1.11)--(1.21) controls (3) on its independent-pair domain and
all simultaneous s_j. B109 (3.13)--(3.17) specifies the strong source
norm and old/current domain intersection. These are the required
native response inputs, not a claim about an arbitrary holomorphic map.

Set the common-frame output and SELECTED current to

 J_j=L^{-3}J_{j+1}+Delta_{U_{j+1}}^{xi_j}A_j

The current remains (5) when s_j is complex or zero. Recomputing the

3. EXACT FREE-PIVOT AND RESPONSE COVARIANCE

For a fine gauge g the free coordinates transform by
T_g x(b)=Ad_{g(b_-)}x(b). On a private row c with pivot p(c), its actual
coefficient obeys

 A_c(U^g)=Ad_{g(c_-)}A_c(U)Ad_{g(p(c)_-)}^{-1}.

The inverse row and embedding consequently obey

 h(U^g)=R_bond(g)h(U)R_row(g)^{-1},
 C(U^g)T_g=R_bond(g)C(U),       C=E-h L Q E.          (6)

The nonlinear row equation has a unique solution on its native chart.
Apply (6) to that equation to obtain

 B'(U^g,Ad_g J,T_g x)=R_bond(g)B'(U,J,x).              (7)

The independent current is transported, not recalculated. Details and
the native L^{-1} pivot coefficient are in the separate primary note
B109-literal-free-noise-pivot-covariance-20261025.txt.

For the minimum response, its covariant local functions, linear
operators H,H0,G and constraint C_nonlin intertwine the same endpoint
representations. Each localized random-walk word inherits this property:
internal gauge factors cancel, and its scalar weakening weights commute
with the endpoint representations. Uniqueness in the fixed-point
equations for D and A0 therefore gives covariance of (3), including
its complex s_j values. This is (N2). It must be checked for the literal
native operator conventions, not inferred merely from covariance of
the final physical scalar action. The same argument preserves the
four-slot operators and the selected companion (5).

4. HIERARCHICAL ABSOLUTE AXIAL FRAME

For j>=1 choose the homogeneous absolute native axial representative.
Its hierarchical endpoint-path conditions, B98 (64)--(69) with identity
background, are

 P_m(M^m F_orig;x_{m+1},x_m)=1,      0<=m<j.           (8)

Here x_0=x, x_j=y is the containing grade-j coarse center, and each
grade-m endpoint path has at most q_L=d(L-1) bonds. Use B109's modified
endpoint mean consistently; it has the same endpoint covariance and
the transferred bounds. The native homogeneous convention is traced
through B102's absolute Ax_k(...,1) and B109's axial minimum choice.

Let u_j be the literal B98 relative return for A_j over U_{j+1}.
Its coarse restriction is the inverse recorded coarse frame, not one.
The exact endpoint calculation and Proposition 7 give

 Q_j(U_{j+1},xi_j A_j) as the corrected endpoint,
 ||u_j^{+/-1}||<=exp(C delta_j^sharp),                 (9)

on the common chart. This holds for an arbitrary admitted A_j; at
s_j=1 the unweakened response also has Q_j(...)=B'_j.

Define the axial frame recursively along the hierarchy by

 Gamma_j(y)=Gamma_{j+1}(y)u_j(y),
 Gamma_j(x_m)=Gamma_j(x_{m+1})
                      P_m(M^m U_j;x_{m+1},x_m).       (10)

Endpoint covariance shows that U_j^{Gamma_j} satisfies (8), and its
grade-j ordinary endpoint is the one returned by u_j in the original
Gamma_{j+1} frame. On the real unweakened physical subfamily it is
therefore the actual native axial minimum, by the prescribed endpoint
and regular critical-orbit uniqueness. This eliminates the separate
unbounded-looking relative axial h algebraically; no estimate from
h|T^j=1 is asserted.

Write U_j=exp(i xi_j B_j)Wbar. B98 Proposition 6 on each truncated
partial average gives, for 0<=m<j,

 M^m U_j=(1+O(L^m xi_j b_j))M^m Wbar.                 (11)

The compact average M^m Wbar is unitary. Thus every short grade-m
factor and its inverse have norm at most exp(C_L L^m xi_j b_j).
The geometric sum of L^m xi_j is bounded by 1/(L-1), so (10) yields

 q_j<=q_{j+1}+C(delta_j^sharp+L^{-1}b_{j+1}),         (12)

after using b_j<=L^{-1}b_{j+1}+C delta_j^sharp.
The same estimate holds for inverses. It counts one short path per
grade, not a product of j identical worst-case bounds. B109's endpoint
mean version uses the explicit transfer of B98's averaging estimates.

5. THE COMPLETE DIRECT PAIR BOOTSTRAP

The common-frame-selected-pair-bootstrap note proves directly, with
BCH and a same-corner plaquette comparison,

 b_j<=L^{-1}b_{j+1}+C_b delta_j^sharp,
 k_j<=L^{-2}k_{j+1}+C_k delta_j^sharp,
 c_j<=L^{-3}c_{j+1}+C_c delta_j^sharp, j>=1.          (13)

The current line also applies at j=0 only if the optional bottom
selected current has been defined as described in Section 2.

The nonlinear current remainder and every P1/Laplacian slot are
retained. No fine C1 bound for the axial gauge is used: these are
estimates in the common Landau frame, with the fixed compact Wbar.

Solving the first recurrence and summing it over dates gives

 sum_{j=0}^{t-1} L^{-1} b_{j+1}
      <=b_t/(L-1)+C_b/(L-1) sum_{j=0}^{t-1}delta_j^sharp. (14)

Together with (12), uniformly for any tail of the prefix,

 q_j<=q_t+C_* b_t+C_* sum_{ell=j}^{t-1}
                                    delta_ell exp(2q_{ell+1}). (15)

Choose a fixed small q_*>0 inside the native frame reserves and require

 q_t+C_*b_t<=q_*/2,
 C_* exp(2q_*) sum_{ell<t}delta_ell<=q_*/2.             (16)

Descending induction in (15) gives q_j<=q_* for every j. Consequently
delta_j^sharp<=exp(2q_*)delta_j. Now (13) gives the explicit bounds

 b_j<=L^{-(t-j)}b_t
       +C_b exp(2q_*) sum_{ell=j}^{t-1}L^{-(ell-j)}delta_ell,
 k_j<=L^{-2(t-j)}k_t
       +C_k exp(2q_*) sum_{ell=j}^{t-1}L^{-2(ell-j)}delta_ell,
 c_j<=L^{-3(t-j)}c_t
       +C_c exp(2q_*) sum_{ell=j}^{t-1}L^{-3(ell-j)}delta_ell. (17)

In the last line j>=1 unless the optional bottom current is defined.

Choose the top caps and sup delta_j so these lie strictly within the
fixed one-step input reserves. The induction is noncircular: at a
given date, all later fields and frames have already been constructed;
(2),(4) then construct the next response and (13),(15) keep it inside
the reserved caps. Strict spare constants provide holomorphy around
the full original product and all indicated localizer contours.

At each grade apply B109 p263's direct-to-full criterion once on the
complete reads, choosing k_direct<=alpha0/(8B3) and the other direct
caps accordingly. This supplies its entire determining tower (iv)
for that pair and the fixed compact factor. B3 is not iterated in (13).
For the old/current intersection (3.16), reserve the old half-domain
as well: the same unchanged field has curvature scaled by L^{-2}
and potential by L^{-1} in old units. The INPUT independent J must
itself meet the current allowance of BOTH certificates; choose the
fixed c cap below their minimum. The L^{-3} factor belongs to the
OUTPUT selected current and does not admit an oversized input J.
Use the smaller of the two fixed certificates from the start. This
is not a repeated shrink of a source radius.

6. PHYSICAL IDENTIFICATION AND WHAT REMAINS OPEN

At real top physical data, s_j=1 and original real x_j, the frames and
responses are real compact/unitary. Formula (1), exact covariance (7)
and (N2), the exact Q endpoint, and (8)--(10) identify every reconstructed
field with its original native counterpart. This is pointwise in the
original x_j, not equality after replacing their integration law.
The selected current agrees with the physical current on this subfamily
by B109 (3.11)--(3.13); off it the declared selected current stays (5).

For t=O(log g_0^{-1}) and delta_j<=C g_0 log^p(g_0^{-2}) on an admitted
comparable weak trajectory, the total in (16) is
O(g_0 log^{p+1}(g_0^{-2})), hence tends to zero. Thus the frame condition
is compatible with the logarithmic delay; a fixed per-step small cap
alone would NOT have proved it for arbitrarily long prefixes.

The fine curvature consequence is

 ||dU_0-1||<=L^{-2t}k_t
        +C exp(2q_*) sum_{j<t}L^{-2j}delta_j.           (18)

This concerns the common representative. A fixed gauge-invariant
holomorphic cavity may be evaluated there when its complete local
tree/filling tube has been checked. Bounds for a finite packet use
the explicit compact factor and relative-potential bound as well as
(18), not just gauge invariance of an unknown domain.

Still absent: a connected source catalogue for the entire prefix,
normalized complex denominators on all its required contours, all
generated root/read counts and contacts, mixed-history reassembly,
observable calibration, the continuum/infinite-volume limit, and
Yang--Mills identification/interaction. The map theorem alone closes
none of these later obligations. Its complete localized source-image
application must also include the determining-cube replacements and
near/far response branches of the actual chosen expansion; the simple
whole-response s_j family does not automatically stand for those.
