TURN68. 20 October 2026. Root derivation; submitted for internal audit.

STATUS

This is an actual normalized Hamiltonian ground-path estimate.
It is not a Gaussian comparison or a native-reference probability.
It controls the part of a separated magnetic-source covariance
selected by a connected bad-action path between its support regions.
The complementary covariance is not estimated here.

INPUT AND MODEL

Use the finite periodic spatial SU2 Hamiltonian

 H=g^2 K/(2a)+4S/(g^2a),  S=sum_p h_p,
 h_p=1-Tr U_p/2,  K=-Delta

with the Pauli/2 metric, and its actual stationary ground-path law.
Spatial periods are even, tile sides l_i>=2, and N_i/l_i are powers
of2. Put V=l_1 l_2 l_3. Partition the entire time axis into intervals
I_j of length Delta=a. The spatial closed tiles and these intervals
form a spacetime board, finite periodically in space and infinite
in the time direction.

For a spacetime cell c=(b,j), define

 X_c=(a g^2)^-1 int_(I_j) S_b^cl(U_s)ds >=0.

The proved input in hamiltonian_spacetime_moments_turn68.txt is

 log E exp(sum_(c in A) t_c X_c)<=c_V sum_(c in A) t_c,
             0<=t_c<=2, c_V=9V/2,                    (0.1)

for EVERY finite set of cells A. In particular E X_c<=c_V.
The proof of that input uses the complete thermal trace,
space/time RP and a ground limit at fixed spatial graph.
It neither assumes independence nor exchanges the spatial
continuum limit with the thermal limit.

1. MIXED POLYNOMIAL MARKS ON A PRESCRIBED BAD SET

Choose a threshold R>=0 and put

 Bad_c={X_c>=R}, C_V=exp(c_V), q=exp(c_V-R).

Let A be any finite set of prescribed bad cells. Let J be any
finite set of marked cells, not necessarily contained in A,
and let m_j>=1 be integers. Then

 E[ product_(j in J) X_j^(m_j) product_(b in A)1_Bad_b]
       <= (product_(j in J) m_j!) C_V^|J| q^|A|.        (1.1)

PROOF. For x>=0, x^m<=m! exp(x). Also
1_{x>=R}<=exp(x-R). Thus the integrand is at most

 (product m_j!) exp(-R|A|)
       exp(sum_(j in J) X_j+sum_(b in A) X_b).

Every cell's exponent is0,1, or2, even when it is both marked
and bad. Applying(0.1) gives exactly(1.1).
All marks and the event are kept under the SAME original law.

Allowing thresholds R_b simply replaces q^|A| by
product_(b in A)exp(c_V-R_b). A nontrivial probability bound
requires q<1; no clipping of a marked bound is performed.

For two distinct marked cells i,j, expansion of the two centered
absolute majorants also gives

 E[(X_i+c_V)(X_j+c_V) product_(b in A)1_Bad_b]
                         <=(C_V+c_V)^2 q^|A|.          (1.2)

This holds even if neither marked cell lies in A. It follows
by applying(1.1) to the four terms separately. Coincident
marks use m=2, with the factor2! explicitly retained.

2. SELF-AVOIDING BAD PATHS

Give the board a fixed simple adjacency graph of degree at most D.
Touching cells have D<=3^4-1=80; face adjacency has D<=8.
Periodic duplicate neighbors and self-loops are removed.
Let d_G(i,j) be graph distance. Assume Dq<1.

If distinct cells i,j are connected by bad cells, there is a
self-avoiding bad path joining them. A path of m edges has
m+1 DISTINCT cells. There are at most D^m such paths from
i, including those which end anywhere, so this is also an
upper bound on paths ending at j.

Applying(1.1) or(1.2) to each path and summing gives

 E[X_i X_j 1_{i connected to j by bad cells}]
   <= C_V^2 q (Dq)^(d_G(i,j))/(1-Dq),                 (2.1)

 E[(X_i+c_V)(X_j+c_V)
                   1_{i connected to j by bad cells}]
   <= (C_V+c_V)^2 q (Dq)^(d_G(i,j))/(1-Dq).            (2.2)

No claim of independence of successive path cells is used.
An infinite bad cluster does not invalidate the union: connection
of two specified vertices always has a finite path witness.
The estimate itself suffices; an independent cluster-finiteness
theorem is unnecessary.

More generally, for ANY two distinct marked cells i,j, a prescribed bad
path from u to v has the same bound(1.2), whether or not i,j
lie on that path. Therefore the right side of(2.2), with
d_G(u,v), also bounds the expectation of (X_i+c_V)(X_j+c_V)
times the event that u and v are bad-connected.

3. PHYSICALLY NORMALIZED MAGNETIC SOURCES

Fix a deterministic ownership convention assigning each spatial
plaquette to ONE closed tile containing it. Such a tile exists,
and the choices may be made on the finite board once and for all.
Its owned action is bounded above by that tile's closed action.
No reflection covariance of this arbitrary convention is needed:
RP was applied to the enclosing closed-cell action, not to the
owned-source decomposition.

For a real bounded spacetime plaquette profile f, define

 Q_a(f)=4/(g^2 a) int_R sum_p f_p(s) h_p(U_s)ds.

This is the bare, canonically scaled magnetic-energy insertion
integrated in physical time. It is finite at every regulator.
It is not yet a renormalized continuum composite.

Write Q_a(f)=sum_(c in A_f) Q_c(f) using the ownership convention
and the time partition. Here A_f is the finite set of cells that
read the profile. If |f|<=M_f, then

 |Q_c(f)|<=4 M_f X_c,
 |Q_c(f)-E Q_c(f)|<=4 M_f(X_c+c_V).                    (3.1)

The factor4 is exact: the time integration in X_c already
contains(a g^2)^-1. There is no additional a^-4 multiplier
per cell. The number of cells in a fixed physical support
is instead O(a^-4) for fixed tile sizes.

For two profiles with disjoint cell supports A_+,A_-, put
q_c^+=Q_c(f_+)-E Q_c(f_+) and define q_d^- similarly.
Their actual centered covariance is the exact finite sum

 Cov(Q_a(f_+),Q_a(f_-))=E sum_(c in A_+,d in A_-) q_c^+ q_d^-.

Define its endpoint-connected contribution

 B_end=sum_(c in A_+,d in A_-) q_c^+ q_d^-
                          1_{c connected to d by bad cells}.

If all these cell pairs have graph distance at least r_a, then

 E|B_end|
 <=16 M_+M_- (C_V+c_V)^2 |A_+||A_-|
                      q(Dq)^(r_a)/(1-Dq).             (3.2)

This is an absolute bound on an explicitly defined signed
contribution. It does not assert that B_end is itself an OS norm.
The exact complement has the indicator of no such endpoint
connection, and is not estimated by(3.2).

4. THE WHOLE COVARIANCE RESTRICTED TO ANY SPANNING BAD BRIDGE

One can control a stronger event without forcing each marked
endpoint to belong to the bad path. Let E_bridge be the event
that some bad path joins a cell u in A_+ to a cell v in A_-.
Using the final assertion of Section2, first sum over u,v,
then over the source marks c,d. Equation(3.1) gives

 E[ |(Q_a(f_+)-EQ_a(f_+))(Q_a(f_-)-EQ_a(f_-))|
                                              1_E_bridge]
 <=16 M_+M_- (C_V+c_V)^2 |A_+|^2 |A_-|^2
                      q(Dq)^(r_a)/(1-Dq).             (4.1)

This bound deliberately overcounts paths and endpoints. The
overcount costs only the displayed polynomial number of cells.
It keeps the original denominator and the actual source means.

There is an exact decomposition of the centered covariance
into its E_bridge and E_bridge^c parts. Equation(4.1) controls
the first. It does not claim that different bad components are
independent, or that the second part equals a Gaussian covariance.

5. FIXED PHYSICAL SEPARATION

Take fixed compact physical source supports at positive distance
d>0 in the periodic spatial geometry, or in a common nonwrapping
embedding with that same separation. Use touching adjacency if
all geometric contacts are to be
included. One graph step moves at most a constant times a,
where that constant depends only on the fixed spatial tile sides.
After allowing for cells that meet the edges of the supports,
there is c_d>0 such that r_a>=c_d/a for all sufficiently small a.
Each |A_+|,|A_-| is at most C a^-4.

Choose R so that Dq<=eta<1 uniformly. Then(4.1) is at most

                  C a^-16 exp[-c_d |log eta|/a] ->0.  (5.1)

The sharper endpoint contribution(3.2) costs only a^-8.
Either bound absorbs any additional fixed power of a^-1
and any fixed logarithmic power in a multiplicative source
normalization. Such factors must actually be bounded by those
powers; arbitrary unknown renormalizations are not covered.

In particular choose a fixed R>c_V+log D. On a weak-coupling
sequence g_a->0, the bad event is

 (1/a)int_(I_j) S_b^cl(U_s)ds >=g_a^2 R.

Its good threshold tends to zero in the dimensionless
plaquette action. Alternatively R=R_a may tend to infinity,
provided the displayed q condition holds; if g_a^2 R_a->0
the threshold still remains weak in that sense.

These are TIME-AVERAGED action events. They do not prohibit
short spikes within a time cell and do not bound pointwise
temporal electric curvature. No pointwise small-field domain,
choice of gauge, or derivative estimate is inferred.

6. THE REMAINING CHANNEL IS A GENUINE OPEN ESTIMATE

The main new conclusion is an actual full-measure suppression
of source-weighted spacetime bad bridges at fixed physical
separation. The physical action normalization is retained.
It is stronger than an unmarked probability or a conditional

It does not show that the full separated covariance is nonzero.

The theorem supplies a concrete full-law large-field source
