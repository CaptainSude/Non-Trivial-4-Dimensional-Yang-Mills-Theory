1. Twisted action and the forest

The relative gauge action in (1.2) is correct for U=exp(A)t with
t fixed. For real SU(2) background t0, the discrepancy
b_e=|g_u-Ad_(t0_e)g_v| has b_e^2=4q(g_u t0_e g_v^-1 t0_e^-1).
Parallel transport along a forest path preserves the Frobenius
norm and fixes I. Hence a_v^2<=4d_v Q_F.

Summing b_e^2<=(a_u+a_v)^2<=2(a_u^2+a_v^2) yields exactly
Q0<=2 S_F Q_F<=C_F Q_F. The endpoint sum in (2.4) is at
most 4S_F Q_F<=2C_F Q_F, as claimed.

The triangular forest map solves
g_child=t0_e^-1 h_e^-1 g_parent t0_e.
Its factors are normalized-Haar-preserving. Reversing an edge
conjugates or inverts the relevant SU(2) relative element and
does not change q. Thus the forest integral is precisely w_s^m,
not a quantity with an unrecorded tree Jacobian or gauge volume.

When m=0, all g vertices are fixed to I. Then Q0=Q_F=0,
Psi_lambda(0,t0)=1 and w_lambda^m=1. The later estimates remain
valid, including nonzero field A.

2. Complex t perturbation

For B=t0_e g_v^-1 t0_e^-1, integrating
exp(sT)[T,B-I]exp(-sT) gives
|exp(T)Bexp(-T)-B|<=2e^2 tau |B-I| for tau<=1.
The trace of that difference is zero. Therefore multiplication
by g_u may be replaced by multiplication by g_u-I in its trace.

This supplies both vanishing factors and gives

 |Q_(0,t)-Q0|
 <= (C0 tau/4) sum_e(a_u^2+a_v^2)
 <= (C0/2) C_F tau Q0.

In particular, there is no additive error proportional to
lambda tau times graph volume. The error vanishes with Q0.
No commutation of the complex background and gauge variables
has been assumed.
