Result
------
The quantitative certificates(T) and(M) in Sections5--6 of
native_affine_multiplier_and_heat_operator_interface_20261024.txt hold
for the actual primitive B109 ordinary average on a fixed small physical
supremum ball. In particular they hold for its complete physical C1
inputs. Constants depend on the fixed primitive L, dimension and group,
but NOT on K=L^p, p, old homogeneous grade, or compatible lattice volume.

The proof does not assert that the raw primitive derivative has supremum
norm L. Its endpoint gauge term can give a larger linear norm. Instead,
an exact identity for all products of the flat derivative gives
||Q^n||_infty<=C_* L^n. A Duhamel argument treats the nonlinear defects,
whose scale weights are summable. This prevents a spurious C_L^p loss.

The input is U_0(A)=exp(i mu A), mu=L^-p, and U_j=M_L^j U_0.
All fields, endpoint contours, source directions and matrix branches are
the literal native ones. This concerns ordinary averages of prescribed
backgrounds. It does not identify a mixed history, change a sharp window,
or prove the heat comparison's additional energy/trace estimates.

1. Native local chart, conventions, and the fixed-L constants
-----------------------------------------------------------
Use B109 printed252--254(0.3),(0.5)--(0.12): centered odd L-blocks,
translation-covariant coordinate-order endpoint paths, the actual
analytic equivariant mean, and its anchored-relative-log average.
Its supplied primary is
 work/handoff/original-workspace/work/package/sources/supplied/balaban9.pdf.
The corresponding text is package/work/turn125_primary/b109_full.txt
under work/handoff/original-workspace/work/.

At every grade use unit coordinates on that grade. Bond and site spaces
have the maximum of a fixed finite-dimensional color norm for supremum
estimates; Hilbert estimates later use counting measure and the
trace-orthonormal color norm. An ordinary coarse bond reads the complete
two endpoint blocks, all actual endpoint paths, its translated strings
and its center reference string. Repeated reads are the literal recursive
unions of these reads. They are never cut off to a smaller source patch.

For complex theta in a fixed supremum ball ||theta||<delta_L, put

 F(theta)=Log[M_L(exp(i theta))]/i,
                     F(theta)=Q theta+N(theta).             (1)

Choose delta_L small enough that EVERY endpoint mean, path product,
anchored relative log and final log has its fixed native analytic branch
on a larger closed ball. There are finitely many input bonds per
primitive read, with number depending only on L. The same local formula
at every translate, and ordinary finite-dimensional Cauchy estimates on
this larger ball, give uniform local-to-supremum bounds

 ||N(theta)|| <= n_0 ||theta||^2,
 ||DN(theta)[v]|| <= n_1 ||theta|| ||v||,
 ||D^2N(theta)[v,w]|| <= n_2 ||v|| ||w||.                    (2)

The constants n_i may depend on L and on the chosen native mean. They
are independent of the number of blocks and remain valid for complex
inputs. Equation(2) uses the ACTUAL local analytic formula once at fixed
L, not analyticity of a large composite with an unspecified K-dependent
radius. F(0)=0 and Q=DF(0) are exact. The ensuing estimates ensure the
entire ordinary trajectory stays inside this common primitive chart.

2. An exact flat product bound: why no C_L^p occurs
-------------------------------------------------
Let S be the straight-string part of the flat ordinary average:

 (S a)_mu(y)=L^-4 sum_(r in B_L) sum_(s=0)^(L-1)
                                      a_mu(y+r+s e_mu).

It is a positive average followed by a sum of L translates, so
||S||_infty=L and ||S^j||_infty<=L^j. Let tau_y(r) be the arithmetic
mean of the actual endpoint path line integrals from y to y+r and set
psi(a)(y)=L^-4 sum_r tau_y(r). With the native signed gradient convention
d phi(y,y')=phi(y)-phi(y'), the exact flat formula is

                  Q=S+d psi.                              (3)

Every endpoint path has length |r|_1<=d(L-1)/2, whence
||psi||_infty<=c_psi=d(L-1)/2. Native endpoint gauge covariance gives

                  Q d=d r,                                (4)

where r restricts a fine site field to the ACTUAL coarse centers.
It is restriction, NOT the scalar block average. Thus ||r^k||_infty=1
and ||d r^k||_infty<=2, in the unit coordinates of the output lattice.

The product difference has the exact order

 Q^n-S^n=sum_(j=0)^(n-1) Q^(n-1-j)(Q-S)S^j
        =sum_(j=0)^(n-1) d r^(n-1-j) psi S^j.               (5)

The formula means the successive grade versions of these identical
operators; all domains and codomains are their corresponding lattices.
Combining(5) with the previous norms gives

 ||Q^n||_infty <= L^n+2 c_psi (L^n-1)/(L-1)
                <= C_* L^n,         C_*=1+d.               (6)

This proof also works on compatible tori: periodic translations and
restrictions have the same bounds. There is no volume sum. The equality
on constant inputs Q a=L a alone would NOT prove(6); the whole endpoint
gradient structure and the correct right factor S^j in(5) are needed.

3. Uniform complex nonlinear trajectory
---------------------------------------
Let p>=1, mu=L^-p, s_j=mu L^j for 0<=j<=p, and theta_0=mu A.
Take ||A||<=a. The exact recursion from(1) is

 theta_j=Q^j theta_0
                +sum_(l=0)^(j-1) Q^(j-1-l)N(theta_l).      (7)

Set B=2C_*. Choose a fixed a_*>0 so that

 B a_* <= delta_L/2,
 C_* n_0 B^2 a_* /[L(L-1)] <= C_*/2.                      (8)

There is no p in this choice. If ||theta_l||<=B a s_l for l<j, then
the nonlinear part of(7) is at most

 C_* n_0 B^2 a^2 sum_(l<j) L^(j-1-l)s_l^2
 =C_* n_0 B^2 a^2 (s_j/L) sum_(l<j)s_l
 <=C_* n_0 B^2 a^2 s_j^2/[L(L-1)].                       (9)

Together with the linear term C_* a s_j and s_j<=1, (8) closes the
induction with room to spare. Hence for EVERY complex A with ||A||<=a_*,

                 ||theta_j(A)||<=B a_* s_j,
          and in fact <=B ||A|| s_j,  0<=j<=p.             (10)

All intermediate means/logs have been defined on their native branch by
the induction itself. Thus (10) provides a K-uniform physical complex
input ball, not just estimates for a formally assumed trajectory.

For j<p the actual small-angle defects are summable:

 sum_(j<p)||theta_j|| <= B||A||/(L-1).                     (11)

The endpoint j=p is also controlled by(10), as needed to define the
final output; it is not included in the primitive-factor sum(11).

4. First and second physical-source derivatives
-----------------------------------------------
Fix A in the smaller closed physical ball, for instance ||A||<=a_*/2,
and complete directions A_1,A_2, with b_l=||A_l||. All derivatives below
are derivatives of the ACTUAL logarithm theta_j(A), not of an affine
surrogate. Set u_j=D theta_j[A_1], v_j=D^2theta_j[A_1,A_2].
Differentiating the finite recursion gives

 u_j=Q^j(mu A_1)
       +sum_(l<j)Q^(j-1-l) DN(theta_l)u_l,                (12)

 v_j=sum_(l<j)Q^(j-1-l){DN(theta_l)v_l
           +D^2N(theta_l)[u_l^(1),u_l^(2)]}.              (13)

There is no second derivative of theta_0=mu A. With x_j=||u_j||/s_j,
(2),(6),(10) give

 x_j<=C_* b_1+(C_* n_1 B a_*/L)sum_(l<j)s_l x_l.

Discrete Gronwall, using sum_(l<j)s_l<=1/(L-1), yields

             ||u_j||<=C_1 s_j b_1.                       (14)

Here C_1=C_* exp[C_* n_1 B a_*/(L(L-1))] is independent of p.
Using(14) in(13), the same argument with its increasing inhomogeneous
term proportional to sum_(l<j)s_l<=s_j/(L-1) gives

             ||v_j||<=C_2 s_j^2 b_1 b_2.                 (15)

For example C_2=C_* n_2 C_1^2 exp[C_* n_1 B a_*/(L(L-1))]
/[L(L-1)] is sufficient. This stronger square-scale bound implies the
requested <=C s_j b_1b_2, since s_j<=1. Uniform Banach Cauchy on the
larger ball from Section3 gives the weaker requested estimate as an
independent check, but(12)--(15) retain the sharper scale and exact maps.

For the physical norm in the interface,
 ||A||_phys,1=||A||_infty+mu^-1||nearest difference A||_infty,
one simply has b_l<=||A_l||_phys,1. Therefore(10),(14),(15) prove(T)
for every complete physical C1 input. No spatial derivative of A was
needed for this particular supremum trajectory theorem. Such derivatives
are still needed in the separate physical-vertex theorem.

The derivative defects are summable:

 sum_(j<p)||D theta_j[A_1]||<=C_1 b_1/(L-1),
 sum_(j<p)||D^2theta_j[A_1,A_2]||<=C_2 b_1b_2/(L^2-1).     (16)

The larger complex ball and strict margins ensure all these derivatives
have the complete original recursive source read. No Taylor coefficient
has been evaluated on a smaller, incompatible field domain.

5. Actual primitive moving-reference maps and their flat norms
-------------------------------------------------------------
At a primitive background U=exp(i theta), use exactly the maps of
Section5 of native_affine_multiplier_and_heat_operator_interface:

 B_U phi(y)=L^-4 sum_(x in B_L(y)) Ad_(P_L(U;y,x)) phi(x),
 Z_U v(y)=L^-4 sum_x D_v Log[P_L(exp(iv)U;y,x)P_L(U;y,x)^-1]/i,
 C_U=the left-relative derivative of the actual M_L,
                    Cbar_U=C_U-D_(M_L U) Z_U.              (17)

Here Cbar is the moving-reference corrected tangent; no derivative of a
fixed-identity corrected nonlinear map is substituted. Endpoint
covariance gives Cbar_U D_U=D_(M_L U) B_U. The total corrected tangent
is the chronological product of the primitive Cbar's at U_j; the total
B is the corresponding product of the primitive B's. Their exact
intertwining and C_total-Cbar_total=D_final Z_total are the algebraic
identities registered in that interface. This section estimates those
SAME products.

At flatness, Z_0=psi and Cbar_0=Q-d psi=S, by(3). Let P_0 be the
ordinary scalar block average. Its rows have disjoint fine supports,
and its 4D counting Hilbert norm is exactly L^-2. The straight map is
S=sum_(s=0)^(L-1)P_0 translation_(s e_mu), on each bond direction.
Therefore

           ||B_0||_(2->2)=L^-2,
           ||Cbar_0||_(2->2)<=L^-1.                        (18)

This is the actual four-dimensional volume normalization. It is not an
entrywise estimate with a hidden number of fine bonds.

At fixed L, every coefficient of B_theta,Cbar_theta has its complete
finite primitive read and is analytic on the chart of Section1. The
number of nonzero blocks per matrix row/column is bounded in L.
Entrywise differentiated Cauchy bounds followed by the finite Schur
bound thus yield, for F=B or Cbar and lambda=L^-2 or L^-1 respectively,

 ||F(theta)|| <= lambda[1+c_L||theta||],
 ||D_theta F(theta)[w]|| <=lambda c_L||w||,
 ||D_theta^2 F(theta)[w,z]||<=lambda c_L||w||||z||.          (19)

The first estimate uses the exact flat norm(18) plus a perturbation;
it is NOT replaced by lambda times a fixed constant greater than one
at every stage. Factors L in converting the finite Schur estimate to
the relative form(19) are absorbed in the fixed primitive c_L.

6. Product derivatives prove(M)
-------------------------------
For either primitive family write F_j(A)=F(theta_j(A)). Equations
(10),(14),(15),(19) imply

 ||F_j||<=lambda(1+C s_j a_*),
 ||D_A F_j[A_1]||<=lambda C s_j b_1,
 ||D_A^2 F_j[A_1,A_2]||<=lambda C s_j^2 b_1b_2.            (20)

In the last line both D_theta F times D_A^2theta_j and D_theta^2F
times the two first derivatives are included. Let F_tot=F_(p-1)...F_0.
Submultiplicativity, (11), and exact product Leibniz differentiation
give

 ||F_tot||<=lambda^p exp(C a_* sum_(j<p)s_j),
 ||D_A F_tot[A_1]||<=lambda^p C b_1,
 ||D_A^2 F_tot[A_1,A_2]||<=lambda^p C b_1b_2.              (21)

Indeed the one-insertion sum costs sum_j s_j, and the two-insertion
sums cost sum_j s_j^2+(sum_j s_j)^2, all bounded by(16). No inverse of
a factor is used, and no lower singular-value bound for an averaging
map is needed. This argument is valid on the same uniform complex ball.

For Cbar, lambda^p=L^-p=mu. For B, lambda^p=L^-2p=mu^2.
Writing J_A=mu^-2 B_tot(A)^T in the native counting pairings, (21) gives

 ||J_A||<=C,       ||D_A^r J_A[A_1,...,A_r]||<=C prod_l b_l,
 ||Cbar_tot||<=C mu,
 ||D_A^r Cbar_tot[A_1,...,A_r]||<=C mu prod_l b_l,
                                      r=1,2.              (22)

Transpose here is the native analytic bilinear transpose; its operator
norm has the same Hilbert bound. On real backgrounds the stronger exact
row identity B_tot B_tot^T=K^-4 Id gives ||J_A||=1. The complex estimate
in(22), rather than orthogonality alone, supplies the analytic bound.

All products keep the complete recursive source reads. In the native
homogeneous geometry the scalar hierarchical endpoint read lies in its
final block; the bond read lies in its two adjacent final endpoint
blocks with their actual incident bonds/contours. Equivalently one may
keep the literal recursive union; no estimate depends on deleting any
part of it. The uniform constants in(21)--(22) are operator constants,
not a sum over K^4 source coordinates.

7. What has and has not been proved
----------------------------------
This proves(T) and(M) for the literal ordinary trajectory and the
literal moving-reference maps. Their constants are uniform in K and
in the number of primitive steps. The elementary M_L construction
does not involve the old grade m, so these constants are uniform in m
as well. If another theorem asks for a smaller fixed classical analytic
margin, intersect a_* with that already established margin once.

It does NOT prove that every resulting endpoint belongs to every
original selected history or sharp-mask sector. It supplies no new
uniform large-block stationary inverse, changes no current/coarea
factor, and does not identify the complete heat operators with a
different reference. The remaining energy-sandwiched vertex, soft-pair
infrared cancellation and normalized trace estimates(H1)--(H3) retain
their distinct hypotheses. In particular this result does not negate
the known growing raw mean-zero gauge-vertex example; it estimates B,
Cbar and J, not that separately differentiated sandwiched vertex.

The compatible infinite or finite-periodic lattice conventions are
retained throughout. No periodic affine flux or regulator passage is
needed for the uniform bounded-input trajectory theorem.
