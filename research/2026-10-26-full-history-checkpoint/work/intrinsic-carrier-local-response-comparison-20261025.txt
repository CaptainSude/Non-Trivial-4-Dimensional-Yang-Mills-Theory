Scope. This treats the NEW elliptic response, its selected-current map,
and sampled hierarchical frames. The primitive coefficients can be
defined on local native field data without a globally regular extension.
It then gives a finite-prefix map comparison between TWO independently
admitted global backgrounds which agree in an interior region. No
globally regular interpolation between those backgrounds is required.
Normalized density comparison and intrinsic normalized source coefficients
remain separate operations. All variable reads below are complete reads.

2. A PRECISE INTRINSIC WORD DEFINITION

Fix the native geometric word catalogue, including every partition,
Dirichlet subproblem, private pivot, orientation and transpose. For each
primitive word omega, let Read(omega) be the UNION of its external
coefficient domains X_i^5, its endpoint/averaging paths, and its required
boundary collar. This may be larger than its kernel support. For a
carrier Y retain only words whose COMPLETE read is contained in Y.

Given (U,J) on Y in the smaller strict native direct subdomain, compute
each retained primitive factor on its own declared Dirichlet domain.
All required field values lie in Y. Its real reference/local gauge
tests are inherited from the representative on Y; the complex relative
field is also restricted there. The native Dirichlet bounds therefore
apply to this factor with the SAME constants as in the global word
majorant. No choice of U or J outside Y enters that calculation.

This is an important distinction from two incorrect replacements:
 - it is not the principal submatrix of a global inverse;
 - it is not automatically one inverse on all of Y with a newly invented
   boundary convention.
The retained complete-read series is its definition. Individual local
Dirichlet inverses are genuine inverses of their native finite problems.

For example, define the primitive G0,Y,s, Q_Y,s, W_Y,s, G'_Y,s and R_Y,s
by their PRE-EXPANDED local words. W_Y,s is the retained word family for
the coarse Schur inverse, not (Q_Y,s G0,Y,s Q_Y,s*)^-1. That last inverse
can be undefined at zero scalar parameters. All bilinear transposes
are taken after the declared word construction and retain their reads.

For fixed |s|<=S, the native word majorants absorb the scalar factors by
choosing the localization size after S. Absolute convergence of the
retained series follows from the same majorant as before: discarding
words cannot increase its positive bound. This proves holomorphy in
the local field/current variables and scalars. On overlapping field
charts the coefficients agree because their finite matrix formulas
and convergent inverse series agree; a compact-reference witness is
used only for estimates, not as extra data in their definitions.

Each local Dirichlet factor is gauge covariant on its full read, with
the correct endpoint representations. Products, inverses, transposes
and sums preserve these laws. Thus the definitions are intrinsic and
covariant under a gauge on Y; no gauge outside Y has to be chosen.
Norm bounds mean the native norms in an admitted regular representative.
They are not uniform raw matrix bounds after an arbitrarily large
complex gauge. Gauge-invariant scalar coefficients inherit the bound
on the orbit; covariant fields must retain their transformed external
arguments and their declared norms.

Noncontractible carriers cause no difficulty for this definition. Local
Dirichlet coercivity uses the mass/averaging and gauge-fixing structure,
not a globally defined small potential or a global flattening of U.
All local test cubes and all full reads must still fit in Y. A carrier
which is too thin for a particular word simply contains no such word;
its collar must not be borrowed from an unspecified exterior.

The current cap is chosen after S so the weighted Neumann ratio is

Hhat*J read. Then define, as factor-word series,

 G_c=sum_(n>=0)(G_Q E)^n G_Q,

The local averaging residual and the raw plaquette gradient are taken
with their full stencils. On the reserved field/C1 ball solve

 D(A')=C_U(A'-Hhat D(A')),
 a+G_c Vhat_raw'(KB+a)=0,
 Aresp=KB+a-Hhat D(KB+a),

where Vhat_raw is the previously audited raw remainder, including the

stencils. Their Picard series are uniformly convergent local read
series. Boundary cushions are retained: a global kernel term that

At zero exterior scalar parameters, every surviving primitive operator

4. THE ENDPOINT FRAME IS A LOCAL OUTER OPERATION

At step j, the stored input frame is Gamma_(j+1) on T^j and the stored
output is Gamma_j on T^(j-1). For x in T^(j-1) and its T^j ancestor y,

 Gamma_j(x)=Gamma_(j+1)(y) u_j(y)
              P_(j-1)(M^(j-1)U_j;y,x).             (1)

The lower paths in the full hierarchy are empty at this endpoint.
The order in(1) is literal. Its last path has at most d(L-1) links.
The anchor u_j(y) retains its whole depth-j read. The recursive ordinary
averages, all relative means and all collars occupy a fine region of
diameter C L^j, by the geometric sum of their scales. Include that full
region in the local protected seed.

Consequently the outer operation is intrinsic when its complete read
is in Y. Its longer dependence after substitution is exactly the
response-word dependence already accounted for. Its only old frame
read is the sampled Gamma_(j+1)(y). Joint B98 common-chart analyticity
gives local Cauchy bounds. This needs no fine C1 estimate on Gamma.

For auxiliary localization it is enough to tag the WHOLE finite outer
read, retaining its group operations as one analytic coefficient.
Alternatively use the previously declared noise-transport increment
construction. One must not clip a path or invert a zero-weakened frame.
The actual unmarked comparison below always uses genuine frames and
the original fixed real noise, with xsharp=Ad_(Gamma^-1)x.

5. INTRINSIC COEFFICIENTS GIVE A TWO-EXTERIOR MAP COMPARISON

Consider first two independently admitted REAL input states z,z' at
one grade. Their compact/gauge references may differ outside the
region being compared. Their original real noise array x is the SAME.
Use collared native packets with the fixed normalized field/C1,
current and sampled-frame norms of the first-jet audit.

Define a bounded local discrepancy d_q in[0,1] as follows. If the two
restrictions on the collared packet lie in a common reserved local
chart, take a fixed multiple of the relative-field/C1, independent-
current and local logarithmic frame difference, capped at1. If the
small common relative chart does not exist, set d_q=1. The comparison
chart is local and gauge covariant; an admitted compact representative
of the first restriction is sufficient when the relative difference
is small. Include cross-edge stencils in the collared packet norm.
The definition is used for an estimate, not to redefine either map.

Let f_X be one intrinsic complete-read coefficient in a component of
the response/current/frame map, with uniform cap m_X on a larger
reserved chart. The complete one-mark localization argument yields
a summable connected-carrier majorant for these coefficients and for
their one packet derivatives. Then

 ||f_X(z)-f_X(z')|| <= C m_X sum_(q meeting X)d_q.   (2)

Proof. If some discrepancy in X is outside the small common-chart
range, its capped value is bounded below by a fixed positive constant;
the two independent coefficient bounds prove(2). If every discrepancy
is small, the relative logarithms are consistently defined on the
full collared read X. Interpolate ONLY these local restrictions in
the reserved chart, including J and sampled frames. Their C1 norms
remain in the larger local direct caps; B109 p263 supplies the local
determining tests. Cauchy and the fundamental theorem of calculus give
(2). No field outside X occurs in this interpolation. In particular
there is no evaluation of a nonlinear equation at a field spliced
globally between two incompatible gauge references.

One can use the maximum discrepancy in the small case; the displayed
sum is convenient for the spatial kernel. Marking one packet in the
connected coefficient family only adds its summable one-mark count.
The native scalar radius and walk reserve pay that count once.

Summing(2), and including the direct local field/current copies, gives

 d_out(p)<=sum_q K_j(p,q)d_in(q),
 sup_p sum_q exp[mu dist(p,q)/(M L^t)]K_j(p,q)<=C_L. (3)

The same one fixed mu works at all j<=t. Native decay is on scale
M L^j and hence is stronger than this final-scale metric. The actual
packet/refinement count is the one proved in the first-jet audit.
Field output comparison is in relative normalized field/C1 coordinates:
the small product/log estimates convert response and old-field
differences to that distance. If the right side is not small, the
capped output distance is bounded trivially after increasing C_L.
Selected-current comparison uses all four slots and the P1 coefficient
branch. The frame outer operation has a fixed local derivative bound
and fixed read, so also satisfies(3). Its sampled interpolation has an
O(1) background derivative even at zero noise; this is included in C_L.

Equation(3) is a REAL two-input stability statement. The same argument
works for admitted complex inputs when the small local common charts
and their reserves are supplied, but it does not assert that arbitrary
large complex gauge orbits have comparable raw matrix norms.

6. FINITE PREFIX, ONE FIXED PROFILE, NO EXPONENTIAL t-SQUARED LOSS

Suppose the two terminal pairs agree on a filled region Y in one
common gauge, and use the same original noise array at every date.
Use the same fixed top coarse anchors and canonical hierarchy. The
top frame then agrees wherever its complete O(L^t) read is contained
in Y. Include this read and the packet collars once by replacing Y
by its fixed final-scale inner region Y0.

Set R_t=M L^t and

 h_Y(p)=exp[-mu dist(p,Y0^c)/R_t],

with h_Y=1 outside Y0. The initial capped discrepancy is zero on Y0
and at most1 elsewhere, so it is bounded by h_Y. Distance to a set is
1-Lipschitz, hence

 h_Y(q)<=exp[mu dist(p,q)/R_t] h_Y(p).

Applying(3) at successive dates proves

 d_j(p)<=C_L^(t-j) h_Y(p),                        (4)

with an additional fixed L^c per date only if the chosen packet norms
require that conversion. The total cost is therefore C^t L^(ct).
The spatial exponent is never reduced at intermediate dates; all
collars are inside the fixed weighted row estimate. No exp(C t^2)
or g^(-c t) cost has been introduced.

For an admitted analytic source with complete seed P and a local
Lipschitz bound supplied by its own reserved domain, (4) gives its
pointwise fixed-noise image comparison with the corresponding source
constant and exp[-mu dist(P,Y0^c)/R_t]. A merely bounded Borel source
does not acquire that Lipschitz bound; it still needs its separate
physical-source projection or aligned-coordinate comparison.

This proves the map comparison input needed for a normalized hybrid
argument. It does not compare normalized measures by itself. In
particular, the old complete Hamiltonians, their original cuts,
normalizers and scalar factors still need the separate density
comparison. No positive measure or denominator is altered here.

7. WHY A UNIVERSAL GLOBAL REGULAR COMPLETION IS NOT AVAILABLE

There is a concrete obstruction on general native carriers. Take a
wall-connected cubical annulus in two coordinate directions times a
filled transverse two-dimensional box, with all widths fixed multiples
of M. This is an allowed B109 localization domain (printed257). It
has a loop gamma around the hole which bounds a plaquette disk D in
the ambient lattice but not in the carrier.

Choose a compact flat connection on the annulus whose holonomy around
gamma is a fixed H!=1, in a one-parameter subgroup of SU(2), and take
J=0. One explicit construction assigns a closed lattice one-cocycle
with period1 around the hole and exponentiates it to H. Every plaquette
inside the carrier is identity. Every simply connected local test cube
contained in the carrier has a pure-gauge restriction, so its regular
potential and all test derivatives can be zero. The complex relative
field is zero. Thus(i)--(iii) hold for arbitrarily small positive
direct caps. B109 p263's direct-to-full implication supplies(iv), on
the maximal native determining domains of this carrier. We do not
assume that a determining domain fills an absent hole.

Any COMPACT extension preserving the data on gamma and having global
plaquette cap C alpha0 xi^2 must obey the lattice nonabelian Stokes
estimate on D:

 ||H-1|| <= sum_(p in D)||U(partial p)-1||
          <= C alpha0 xi^2 |D|.                    (5)

The first inequality follows by peeling plaquettes, expressing the
boundary holonomy as a product of unitarily conjugated plaquettes,
and telescoping the product. The physical area xi^2|D| is fixed by
the chosen annulus. For sufficiently small admitted alpha0, (5)
contradicts the fixed nontrivial H. Hence no universal reality-preserving
global completion with uniform cap inflation can exist on all native
local domains. Enlarging an exterior buffer does not remove the
obstruction because the same fixed disk still has to be filled.

This rules out a general completion theorem in the natural compact
real category, and hence a holomorphic completion with that reality
property. It is not a claim about a specially restricted class of
filled boxes or a completion allowed uncontrolled curvature in holes.
The direct intrinsic Dirichlet-word construction is unaffected by it.

8. THE EXISTING FILLED-BOX COMPLETION HAS A DIFFERENT, USEFUL SCOPE

The repository's 24 October curvature-completion note works on a
FILLED nonwrapping cube Q_(2R) of determining data V. Its fixed rooted
coordinate tree gives canonical links O(R delta). Cutting off their
small group logarithms gives curvature O(delta) when R^2 delta is
small. It agrees with the canonical input on Q_R and is flat outside.
It is holomorphic on a suitably small neighborhood and changes under
an input gauge only by constant root conjugation.

At a physical terminal minimum U_t(V_t), one may apply that construction
to V_t, then use B102 Theorem1 to obtain a globally admitted physical
minimum and physical current with O(delta) caps. With the running cap
delta=O(g log^p(g^-2)) and R=O(log^(2p)(g^-2)), R^2 delta tends to zero.
This is a useful auxiliary physical neighborhood class, and it avoids
the annular obstruction by supplying the entire filled input box.

It does NOT complete an arbitrary fine pair (U,J) while preserving
that U on its interior. Reconstructing U_t(M^t U) need not return U;
B109 condition(iv) only bounds this reconstruction. Even for a
physical minimum, changing the exterior determining data changes
its fine interior by a decaying tail, rather than by exact equality.
Use the native minimum/common-shell response comparison for that
tail before applying the two-exterior prefix estimate above.

Nor does a curvature-only cutoff of an arbitrary FINE U automatically
prove its native local C1 gauge tests. A coordinate-tree potential can
have large transverse first differences even when a different gauge
has small C1 norm. The 24 October theorem avoids this issue by applying
the native minimum theorem to the completed coarse determining field.
One must not transfer its conclusion to arbitrary fine inputs without
another argument.

An independent current can be transported by the same real tree gauge
and cut off with a scalar function, preserving its sup cap. This gives
an auxiliary independent-current extension once its fine FIELD has
been admitted. It does not make a reconstructed fine field equal to
an arbitrary original U. Thus the two clean interfaces are:
 (i) intrinsic local word coefficients for strict native fine-pair data;
 (ii) physical determining-data completions for a new finite neighborhood
      source class, with the actual minimum comparison and complete reads.
They should not be identified without that comparison.

9. LIMITS OF THE PRESENT RESULT

The intrinsic theorem is for the declared complete-read map coefficients
and their local nonlinear contractions, with fixed strict cap reserves.
It bypasses global regular completion and handles noncontractible
carriers without choosing a global small gauge. It supplies (3)--(4)
for two admitted global real prefixes using the same original noises.

It does not turn the fixed-Wbar cutoff shell into an intrinsic normalized
coefficient automatically. The normalized denominator and any inherited
interaction must also admit a complete local construction, or their
exterior dependence must be compared with an explicit remainder.
The source's tree/filling packet, its own analytic domain and all
original outer masses remain part of that separate interface.
