2. TWO LABELS RETAIN THEIR CONNECTING EXPONENTIAL

For supported distinguished coefficients A_H and B_K, keep H,K in the
retained conditioning set. In the normalized connected logarithm, a term
with both nilpotent labels has a connected union joining H to K. Its source
majorant contains exp(-kappa length(tree)). Use half of the unused tree
reserve to pay dist(H,K); sum the remaining weighted connected union as in
the one-root proof. All Gaussian moment factors are fixed-degree polynomials
of its carrier size and are paid by another fixed part of the reserve.
The result, before summing the two distinguished supports, is

 ||Cov(A_H,B_K)||_out
       <=C alpha_H beta_K exp[-kappa' dist(H,K)].          (2.1)

The same proof works for every fixed cumulant used below: retain a tree
joining its distinguished supports. Interpolating unmarked V_g adds a
rooted coefficient family with bounded norm; summing its support preserves
the exponential between the two external distinguished labels. It does
not introduce an ambient-volume factor.

If a distinguished old carrier contains external label z, its propagated
carrier is rooted near hz, h=L^-1. Distances from its internal field slots
to hz cost at most h times its old diameter. The input diameter reserve
pays these terms, so (2.1) gives the same connecting kernel between the
two external output roots. Regrouping strict coefficients never discards
the external labels. This is the extra content beyond an unweighted
fixed-degree cumulant estimate.

For broad packet sums this is exactly the overlap B_j of
broad_packet_connected_witness, Section 2. The one-root and two-root
coefficient profiles therefore remain available after the source expansion.

3. EXACT FIRST AND MIXED DERIVATIVES

Let A_j(W)(B) denote the normalized inner q-expectation of W evaluated

 X(z_1,z_2)=X(0)+z_1 W_1+z_2 W_2+z_1 z_2 W_12+... .

Direct differentiation of the ratio gives, exactly,

 W_a^+ = A_j(W_a),
 W_12^+ = A_j(W_12)+B_j(W_1,W_2).                       (3.1)

Subtract the value at B=0 from each output and keep that value in the
corresponding scalar source-vacuum ledger. Write Pi F=F-F(0). Thus the
nonvacuum maps are Pi applied to (3.1), while the mixed vacuum increment is

           [A_j(W_12)+B_j(W_1,W_2)](0).                 (3.2)

Source-free prefactors cancel in these ratios or remain in their exact
base law. Source-dependent prefactors do NOT cancel unless their
derivatives have explicitly been included in W_a,W_12 or the tail births.

The exact physical output is gauge invariant when the complete input is.
For an external label z, or pair z_1,z_2, the whole corresponding derivative

4. ONE-STEP ANALYTIC ESTIMATES WITH THE CORRECT WEIGHTS

Use a fixed block and suppress its index. Let mu_a=||W_a||<=M_a b^2,
mu_12=||W_12||<=M_12 g^2 b^2. The exact supported Taylor formula is

 Delta W_a := W_a(P(B)+noise)-W_a(P(B))
      = g L_a + g^2 Q_a + (g^3/b) C_a + (g^4/b^2) D_a,   (4.1)

where L_a is odd linear, Q_a even quadratic, C_a odd cubic, and D_a has
degree-four growth. Their coefficient bounds are respectively
C M_a b, C M_a, C M_a, C M_a. The notation in (4.1) denotes the actual
nonlinear chart Taylor expansion, not addition of link logarithms. The
radius margin is supplied by (a). All coefficients retain their external
root, analytic background norm, and complete support families.

The normalized interpolation d<t F>/dt=Cov_t(F,V_g), and its cumulant
versions, are applied only for 0<=t<=1 to the original stable V_g. For
example an odd Gaussian covariance is zero at t=0 and gains a g after one
interpolation. An even covariance differentiated against the odd O has
zero initial value and gains a second g after one more interpolation.
The degree ceiling is finite (at most 11 for the displayed covariance
comparisons), so input (b) applies with constants independent of T.

These formulas give

 ||Pi(A(W)-W(P))|| <= C (g/b)^2 ||W||,                  (4.2)
 ||B(W_1,W_2)|| <= C g^2 b^2 M_1 M_2.                 (4.3)

The second bound is the FULL-background size. At B=0 all L_a vanish
because the first jets of the whole marks vanish. Its vacuum instead
starts at g^4. Constant-color preparation permits use of this statement

 |A(W_12)(0)| <= C g^4 M_12.                           (4.4)

The same estimates hold with the external joining kernel in (2.1).
For (4.2), the odd g L term needs one interpolation and costs O(g^2 M b);

5. MATCH TO COMPLETED GAUSSIAN QUADRATIC MARKS

Let H be the actual linear stationary prefix at zero background and Cx its
flat centered Gaussian fluctuation, in the source trace/coarea normalization. For rooted quadratic
forms Q_1,Q_2,Q_12 define

 L_a^G(B,x)=D Q_a(HB)[Cx],
 Q_12^+ = Q_12(HB)+Cov_G(L_1^G(B,x),L_2^G(B,x)),        (5.1)

and the mixed scalar Gaussian increment

 c_12^G = g^4 [ E_G Q_12(Cx)
                         +Cov_G(Q_1(Cx),Q_2(Cx)) ].     (5.2)

Here the mixed nonvacuum reference is g^2 C(Q_12). With varying real g_j,
use the corresponding explicit ratios in (5.1)--(5.2), or compare all g_j
to one reference g_* as below. These are identities for quadratic Gaussian
marks, including the mixed conditional response. They are not obtained by
dropping the second derivative of the effective action.

The forms in (5.1) have the linear gauge Ward identity. H maps coarse
linear gradients to the corresponding fine gauge gradients; Q_a kills
those gradients. Therefore L_a^G vanishes for a pure-gauge background, and
the mixed quadratic term does too. Completion (e) consequently applies
to every reference form in this recursion.

For each fixed q,

 ||C(Q_a)(P(B))-C(Q_a^+)|| <= C M_a b^3.                 (5.3)

Both functions are genuine invariant whole marks and have the SAME
quadratic jet. Their cubic remainder bound follows from the fixed

The same statement applies to a mixed completed mark, with extra g^2.

For the completed pair, the full-background leading Gaussian covariance

O(g^2 b^3 M_1M_2). Indeed all mark completions, geometric chart coefficients,

6. FINITE-PREFIX SUM AND WHAT HAS ACTUALLY BEEN ESTABLISHED

Consider N=O(log gamma_*) admitted macro steps, with bounded real coupling
increments, b_j comparable to g_j(log gamma_j)^s, and gamma_j comparable
to gamma_*. Let epsilon_0 bound the initial completed-Gaussian residuals.
The direct-prefix classical identity and whole-age estimate (d) give a
variation-of-constants expansion with one direct classical transport from
each quantum/completion error birth to the final scale. A fixed static
constant is not iterated N times. Discrete Gronwall then gives first-mark
relative error

       C epsilon_* ,   epsilon_*=epsilon_0+
                         C sum_{j<N}(b_j+eta_j).        (6.1)

For amplitude-one broad packets, the actual connected mixed births have
the overlap B_j proved in broad_packet_connected_witness. Their transported
mass Q_j is (3.2) of that note. Equations (4.3)--(5.5) and the same
variation-of-constants expansion bound the sum of mixed vacuum errors by

                         C g_*^4 epsilon_* .           (6.2)

The geometric sums sum B_j and sum Q_j are finite. A second convolution
of Q_j can add an age factor to its L^-4 late tail, but that factor is
summable: sum_{n>=0}(1+n)L^-4n<infinity. At the endpoint it changes R_N^4
only by a fixed polynomial in N. No factor rho^8 or rho^-8 occurs in (6.2).

Comparing the Gaussian reference with fixed coupling g_* costs an
additional O(N/gamma_*) in (6.2): the independent Gaussian band covariances
have uniform relative precision changes O(N/gamma_*), and their rooted
mixed sums are the same finite B,Q sums. One may retain the varying
Gaussian reference instead until this final comparison.

Since N=O(log gamma_*), sum_j(b_j+eta_j) tends to zero when s>1/2
(and the retained tail is negligible). The rough retained-window analytic
alternative eta~(gT/b)^2 also suffices when 2(s-t)>1. The moment proof above
does not insert a T^2 into the natural mixed norm g^2 b^2.

This proves the INNER homogeneous finite-prefix comparison from the actual
kernel/source inputs (a)--(e), including same-family normalization, analytic
support reserves, invariant residual closure, two-root connection and every
mixed vacuum birth. It does not yet prove that a specified original Wilson
observable has the required initial marked bounds, or bound the omitted
outer/R marked births, or the final full endpoint expectation. Those are
precisely the three remaining interfaces for turning (6.2) and the positive
free packet covariance into an actual Wilson OS witness. The state-selection
and diagonal-reflection issue for continuous rotations is independent of
this inner comparison.
