TURN88 -- THE ACTUAL FLOWED SCALAR: INFLUENCE, COHERENCE AND WILSON WEIGHT
gauge47. 20 October 2026. New finite-lattice derivation; prior files unchanged.

RESULT

The declared bounded spatially flowed magnetic scalar has no cutoff-uniform
pointwise modulus in the unweighted original-link sup metric, even when
EVERY original plaquette satisfies beta h_p<=1. Two explicitly constructed
fields differ by O(a^2 beta^(1/4)) per link, but their physically calibrated
flowed scalars have different order-one limits. Away from the symmetric
configuration used in TURN65, this also gives a genuine FIRST-derivative
lower bound for the scalar, not only for its raw flow or its Hessian.

This is NOT an annealed counterexample. The existing exact Wilson
conditional-window estimate suppresses a whole neighborhood of these
fields by exp(-c a^-3). That cost beats the global flow derivative bound,
so their expected contribution actually vanishes. The remaining problem
is the original-law weighted influence on the complement and, in a
replacement proof, the law of the interpolation paths. Total energy
dissipation does not estimate either quantity.

The deterministic calculation below is new relative to TURN65's symmetric
Hessian test. The coherent-window probability theorem is explicitly an
OLD result, reused here with the new actual probe derivatives. No claim
of a positive original reflected channel or of full YM4 existence follows.

1. EXACT PROBE AND SOURCE NORMALIZATION

Use a nondegenerate four-dimensional periodic SU(2) Wilson lattice with
isotropic spacing a and original beta>=1. In one spatial slice let
 S_sp=sum_(spatial plaquettes) h_p, h(P)=1-Tr P/2,
 d Phi_s(U)/ds=-(4/a^2) grad S_sp(Phi_s(U)).                 (1.1)
The tangent metric is the Pauli/2 metric: i x.sigma/2 has norm |x|.
The spatial flow is applied separately to each original time slice.

For fixed physical s,alpha,g_*>0 and P12(Phi_s U)=p0 I+i p.sigma, put
 b_(s,a,x)(U)=exp[-alpha |2p/(g_*a^2)|^2].                  (1.2)
This is exactly the TURN63/87 radial family, with alpha denoting the
parameter called h in the minimal-source note. In particular g_* is FIXED;
it is not silently replaced by the vanishing bare coupling. For a
nonnegative smooth unit-mass physical profile f, let
 O_a(f)=sum_(t,x) w_(t,x) b_(s,a,x)(U(t)),
 w_(t,x)=integral_(cell(t,x)) f, sum w=1.                  (1.3)
All bounds below concern these original observables. Multiplying an
action source by a^4 is not involved.

2. EXACT HOMOGENEOUS FLOW AND ITS ASYMMETRIC RADIAL LIMIT

On a spatial cubic torus with n=(L/a)^3 vertices set
 U1=sqrt(1-w)I+i sqrt(w) n1.sigma,
 U2=sqrt(1-w)I+i sqrt(w) n2.sigma, U3=I,
 z=n1.n2, r=w/(1-w), I=zr, tau=8s/a^2.
The exact equations, including the full-link metric normalization, are
proved in radial_flow_hessian_turn65.txt Section1:
 h12=2w^2(1-z^2), h13=h23=0,
 dr/dtau=-(r^2-I^2)/(1+r), I constant,
 h12(s)=2(r^2-I^2)/(1+r)^2,
 tau=integral_r^r0 (1+x)/(x^2-I^2) dx.                    (2.1)
No continuum approximation is used to define this solution.

Take beta(a)->infinity with beta(a)=o(a^-2), and set
 w0=(2beta)^(-1/2), r0=w0/(1-w0), I=q/tau,
 z0=q/(tau r0),                                         (2.2)
where q is in a fixed compact subinterval of (0,infinity). For all small
a, |z0|<1 and the initial configuration exists. It has
 beta h12(initial)=1-z0^2<=1.                            (2.3)
Take the same spatial field at every original time and all temporal
links I; then every other original plaquette has energy zero.

Write R=tau r and M=tau r0. Equation(2.1) is exactly
 1=integral_R^M (1+y/tau)/(y^2-q^2) dy.                   (2.4)
Here M->infinity, r0->0. Uniformly for q in the fixed compact interval,
 R->q coth q, with convergence of the first q derivative. (2.5)
Proof: let J_M(R)=integral_R^M (y^2-q^2)^(-1)dy. Since
1<=1+y/tau<=1+r0 on the integration range, (2.4) implies
1/(1+r0)<=J_M(R)<=1. The omitted tail to infinity is O(1/M), uniformly
once M is larger than twice the largest q. Thus J_infinity(R)->1,
whose unique solution is R=q coth q. This also places R a fixed
positive distance above q and bounds it above. Differentiating(2.4)
now gives the claimed C1 convergence by dominated convergence: the
q derivative of the integrand is 2q(1+y/tau)/(y^2-q^2)^2, integrable
uniformly on these ranges, and the lower-endpoint derivative stays
bounded away from zero. M and tau are independent of q.

Consequently the actual finite-lattice scalar, as a function F_a(q),
obeys the following C1 limit:
 h12(s)/a^4 -> q^2 csch^2(q)/(32s^2),
 F_a(q) -> F(q)=exp[-A q^2 csch^2(q)],
 A=alpha/(4g_*^2s^2),
 F'(q)=2A F(q) q csch^2(q)(q coth q-1)>0.                (2.6)
The exact identity used here is |p|^2=2h12-h12^2. Thus the h12^2/a^4
term tends to zero with its first q derivative. The limit is the
calibrated nonlinear observable itself, not its small-field surrogate.

3. FIRST INFLUENCE AND A FINITE DISPLACEMENT OBSTRUCTION

Use n1(theta)=(cos theta,sin theta,0) and
n2(theta)=(sin theta,cos theta,0), so z0=sin(2theta).
Evaluate at theta_a=(1/2)arcsin(q/(tau r0)), holding w0 fixed.
The full spatial tangent J=dU/dtheta has
 ||J||_2^2=8n w0, ||J||_infinity=2sqrt(w0),
 dq/dtheta=2tau r0 cos(2theta_a).                       (3.1)
These are first derivatives; the path need not be a geodesic.
For the unit tangent j=J/sqrt(8n w0), at any final spatial point,
 |D b_(s,a,x)[j]|^2
   =tau^2 r0^2 cos^2(2theta_a) F_a'(q)^2/(2n w0)
   =[32s^2 F'(q)^2/(sqrt(2)L^3)]
                         (a sqrt(beta))^(-1)[1+o(1)].  (3.2)
The same holds for any normalized spatial average at that time, since
all its point responses agree on this homogeneous family. This diverges
on the displayed sequence. Gauge invariance annihilates the vertical
part of J; projecting onto the horizontal tangent can only reduce its
norm. Thus(3.2) is not produced solely by a gauge direction. No claim
that J itself is horizontal at nonzero z0 is needed.

Time smearing matters. Write W_t=sum_x w_(t,x). Independent slice
directions give only the lower bound
 ||grad O_a(f)||_2^2 >= RHS(3.2) sum_t W_t^2.             (3.3)
For a fixed smooth temporal profile, sum W_t^2=O(a), with the usual
nonzero continuum limit after dividing by a. Thus this particular
calculation is of order beta^(-1/2) after time smearing, NOT a divergent
four-dimensional squared-gradient lower bound. It must not be used
to disprove the time-smeared L2 influence target.

The SUMMED influence relevant to a uniform per-link perturbation is
different. Apply the same theta variation on every time slice in the
support of f and use duality between l-infinity and l1:
 sum_e |grad_e O_a(f)|
   >=tau r0 cos(2theta_a)|F_a'(q)|/sqrt(w0)
   =8s F'(q) a^-2 (2beta)^(-1/4)[1+o(1)].               (3.4)
Only spatial links enter this sum. The physical source weights sum to1;
they therefore do not remove this coherent response.

There is also a finite version with no differentiation. Choose two
fixed 0<q1<q2. Their initial configurations have per-link Pauli/2
distance at most
 2sqrt(w0)|theta_a(q2)-theta_a(q1)|
                      <=C a^2 beta^(1/4),              (3.5)
which tends to zero. Both obey(2.3), including all temporal plaquettes.
Yet their smeared observables differ by
 O_a(f;U(q2))-O_a(f;U(q1))->F(q2)-F(q1)>0.               (3.6)
Thus plaquette smallness alone does not give equicontinuity of this
physical scalar in the unweighted original-link sup metric. This
excludes any uniform Lipschitz or fixed positive Holder modulus of
that particular type on the entire energy sublevel. It says nothing
about the probability of this comparison under a native coupling.

4. THE ACTUAL WILSON LAW SUPPRESSES THIS WHOLE WINDOW

The following input is already proved in
coherent_energy_window_suppression_turn65.txt Sections1--2. Given all
links but one edge e, its original Wilson conditional density on S^3
is proportional to exp(z.q), |z|<=6beta. A cap lower bound for its
normalizer gives a density supremum C beta^(3/2). Haar h_p=1-a.q
has density (2/pi)sqrt(h_p(2-h_p)). Therefore
 P_W(|beta h_p-1|<=epsilon | outside e)<=C epsilon        (4.1)
for epsilon<=1/2, uniformly in beta>=1, exterior and volume.
Choose at least n/5 edge-disjoint 12-plaquettes in one spatial slice.
Repeated conditioning applies(4.1) without independence. For fixed
sufficiently small epsilon0>0,
 E_t={all spatial x: |beta h12(t,x)-1|<=epsilon0}
 satisfies P_W(E_t)<=exp(-c n).                         (4.2)
This is an ORIGINAL-law estimate, not an energy-MGF countermodel.

By(2.2)--(2.3), the entire family at any fixed compact set of q values
lies in the INTERIOR of E_t for all small a. The event includes a
gauge-invariant open neighborhood of those configurations. Thus the
new first-derivative obstruction has the same rare coherent geometry
as the old symmetric Hessian example.

For completeness the final radial function has the sharper elementary
terminal gradient bound
 ||grad_V b_(0,a,x)||_2 <=2sqrt(2alpha/e)/(g_*a^2).        (4.3)
Indeed the maximum gradient of exp(-alpha|X|^2) is sqrt(2alpha/e);
the Pauli/2-to-quaternion derivative of each of four links is1/2,
so the product-plaquette p map has l2 operator norm at most1, followed
by the factor2/(g_*a^2). Bounded local incidence in the exact flow
vector field gives ||D Phi_s||<=exp(Cs/a^2), as already proved in
the TURN65 window note. It follows that, for every fixed p>0,
 E_W[1_Et ||grad b_(s,a,x)||_2^p]
       <=C_p a^(-2p) exp(C_p s/a^2-c(L/a)^3)->0.        (4.4)
For the l1 influence insert at most (3n)^(p/2) on the right, by
Cauchy--Schwarz; its limit is still zero. Convex spatial averages
have the same l2 bound. The crude exponential derivative estimate
is useful HERE only because it is multiplied by the proved a^-3
probability cost; it is not a full-measure continuity estimate.

For a fixed physical time period, let E be the union of E_t over the
slices read by f. Its probability is at most N_t exp(-cn), N_t=O(a^-1).
The deterministic l2 gradient of O_a(f) is at most C a^-2 exp(Cs/a^2)
by convexity (or better by the disjoint-slice weights), and its l1
norm is at most sqrt(3n N_t) times this. Hence the analogue of(4.4)
for O_a(f), with E and either norm, also tends to zero. A polynomial
number of time slices is equally admissible. No arbitrary time-volume
limit is absorbed in this union bound.

This controls the stated coherent windows, not every large-influence
configuration. A covering of the entire bad derivative set by such
windows with a paid entropy has NOT been proved. Treating(3.2) as a
Wilson-average divergence would contradict the available estimate(4.4).

5. THE REMAINING ORIGINAL-LAW MARK, WITH THE ACTUAL PROBE AT ITS END

Here is the precise place where the existing energy estimates stop.
Let V_r=Phi_r(U) and let Y_r be the adjoint of the remaining tangent
flow applied to the final probe gradient:
 Y_r=(D Phi_(s-r)(V_r))^* grad b_(0,a,x)(V_s).
Then Y_0=grad b_(s,a,x)(U), Y_s=grad b_(0,a,x)(V_s), and
 nabla_r Y_r=(4/a^2) Hess S_sp(V_r)Y_r.                  (5.1)
The sign is positive because this is the backward adjoint variable;
its pairing with each forward Jacobi field is constant.

The exact plaquette Hessian calculation in
nonlinear_spatial_flow_energy_turn65.txt Section2 gives
 Hess S_sp[Y,Y]>= (1/4)||d_VY||_2^2
                      -C sum_e rho_e(V)|Y_e|^2,
 rho_e(V)=sum_(p containing e)sqrt(h_p(V)).
Integrating(5.1) BACKWARD and averaging under the SAME original law,
 E_W||grad b_(s,a,x)||_2^2
 <=E_W||grad b_(0,a,x)(V_s)||_2^2
    -(2/a^2)int_0^s E_W||d_(V_r)Y_r||_2^2 dr
    +(C/a^2)int_0^s sum_e E_W[rho_e(V_r)|Y_(r,e)|^2]dr. (5.2)
The last correlation is rooted at the actual final plaquette, through
Y_s, and includes the physical a^-2 terminal derivative(4.3).
Replacing it by E rho_e E|Y_e|^2 is unjustified.

The actual TURN64/65 consumers provide
 E S_sp(V_r)<=9n/(2beta),
 E int_0^s ||grad S_sp(V_r)||_2^2 dr<=9n a^2/(8beta),
 E rho_e(V_r)<=C beta^(-1/2).                            (5.3)
They give no marked bound on the last line of(5.2), nor an estimate
on its complement after removing E. Even a fictitious factorization
would produce the nonuniform rate C/(a^2 sqrt(beta)). The dissipated
force grad S_sp in(5.3) is different from this probe-dependent adjoint
Y. Ground-state-law entropy/Fisher identities from TURN67 must not be
substituted for the original isotropic Wilson law in(5.2).

An exact original-law integration-by-parts reformulation shows another
issue. Set c=4/a^2, F_r=b_(0,a,x) composed with Phi_r, and let S_4 be
the beta-free sum of ALL four-dimensional plaquette deficits, so the
original law is dmu_W=Z^(-1)exp(-beta S_4)dHaar. On the product of compact
groups, the transport equation is partial_r F_r=-c grad S_sp.grad F_r.
Weighted integration by parts yields
 d/dr E_W|grad_sp F_r|^2
 =-2c E_W Hess S_sp(grad F_r,grad F_r)
   +c E_W[(Delta_sp S_sp-beta grad_sp S_4.grad_sp S_sp)
                                      |grad F_r|^2].   (5.4)
All derivatives are finite-lattice derivatives, so this identity is
legitimate. The temporal plaquettes in grad_sp S_4 are essential.
Neither this score nor its correlation with |grad F_r|^2 is controlled
by(5.3). Dropping it, or replacing S_4 by S_sp, would change the law.
Formula(5.4) is a diagnosis, not an additional smoothing estimate.

6. WHAT A REPLACEMENT ARGUMENT WOULD ACTUALLY NEED

For any real path of original fields U_v joining U and U', at fixed
cutoff the fundamental theorem of calculus gives the exact bound
 |O_a(U')-O_a(U)|
 <=int_0^1 sum_e |grad_e O_a(U_v)| |dot U_(v,e)| dv.     (6.1)
It is valid for the complete probe and keeps the physical source
weights. To average(6.1) in a native/basic-R coupling one needs the
JOINT marked influence on the path's actual distribution. An endpoint
having original Wilson law does not make every U_v Wilson distributed.
Even a proved estimate E_W sum_e|grad_e O_a| would require a justified
change-of-measure or a different exact conditional transport consumer
before it could be inserted into(6.1).

The old coarse-preserving/half-local oscillation consumer in
turn75_full_law_tail.txt Section3 is exact at its own admitted kernels.
Replacing its deterministic oscillations by an original-law gradient
mean is not a formal improvement: the conditional replacement paths
and their marked laws must be supplied. Likewise TURN84 bounds for
cumulative ORIGINAL plaquette displacement do not directly bound the
flowed plaquette displacement; doing so requires precisely this
nonlinear influence estimate.

The actual comparison norm need not be stronger than an L1 error for
this specified source. For a coupling of two laws and bounded source
values O_+,O_-,O'_+,O'_- in[0,1], direct expansion gives
 |Cov(O_-,O_+)-Cov(O'_-,O'_+)|
       <=2 E(|O_--O'_-|+|O_+-O'_+|).                   (6.2)
Thus a reflection-compatible coupling with each source error at most
epsilon has connected two-source error at most4epsilon. This is the
elementary existing bounded-probe consumer, not a newly proved coupling.
It retains both means and requires the SAME calibrated physical source
on both sides. The influence problem is to bound its right side for
an actual original-law comparison, not all future bounded tests.

For the minimal TURN87 source, one only needs a sufficiently accurate
comparison of ONE connected reflected two-source coefficient, not an
arbitrary bounded-test norm. Equations(3.5)--(3.6) show why even that
bounded source cannot be handled by a universal energy-sublevel link
modulus. Equation(4.4) also shows why the pointwise failure does not
rule out a more selective original-law comparison. A useful next
estimate must control the rooted mark in(5.2), or directly the joint
coupling functional(6.1), on configurations outside the already paid
coherent window. No such bound is established here.

PROVENANCE AND EXACT LIMITS

The spatial-flow definition and calibration are TURN63. The homogeneous
ODE, exact Hessian and Wilson coherent-window theorem are TURN65. Their
reuse is explicit above. The new results are the C1 asymmetric radial
limit, its finite original-link displacement consequence, the source
smearing distinction and the application of the existing ORIGINAL-law
coherence bound to those actual probe influences. The adjoint and Wilson
IBP formulas are finite-dimensional derivations, not imported continuum
regularity theorems.

No claim is made about the original reflected covariance's lower bound,
nonlinear continuum-flow existence, tightness of a full gauge-invariant
YM algebra, O(4) invariance, uniqueness of vacuum, a mass gap, or YM4
existence. The bounded scalar's elementary compactness does not supply
any of those missing conclusions.
