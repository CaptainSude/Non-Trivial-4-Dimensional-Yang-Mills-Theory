  <= C_K min(1,a-T)^(-D) d(G) p_s(f),

Why exp(CG) is sufficient and available: all internal separated packet

Choose the finite spanning packet configurations with distinct relative

Replace p_s,p_(s0) by one fixed dominating seminorm p_S throughout.

The conclusion below is a compatible family of bounded heat-damped

2. PLACE ALL FINITE SCALES IN ONE SCALAR HILBERT SPACE

Choose G_n=2^n G0. At a finite bare regulator only its permitted finite

take the scalar continuum subsequence on its ordered core.

subsequences; use the fixed weak-band thresholds in the packet shapes,
not a varying exact gamma at first passage. The finite matching matrices
are bounded by P(G_n), so a subsequence converges. The fixed-G prefix

bounds may grow as exp(C_l G_n), harmless when G_n is fixed. A diagonal
Arzela--Ascoli/subsequence choice therefore gives their needed limits.

Do this countably for n, compact supports and test seminorms. The result

The matching may depend on N and the source-free trajectory before this

3. NORM CAUCHY CONVERGENCE AT EVERY POSITIVE PADDING

For fixed a>T choose n(a) so that(2) holds for all n>=n(a). The dyadic
sum is finite:

 sum_(k>=n) d(G_k)
 <= C[exp(-c'(log G_n)^sigma)+G_n^(B-K_native)] =: d_*(G_n). (4)

For the power term this is a geometric series. For the first term use
log G_k=log G_n+(k-n)log 2 and reduce c; its superlinear exponential
dominates both G_k^B and the remaining geometric sum. Thus B_(G_n,a)
converges in the norm of continuous linear maps from the test space

 ||B_(G_n,a)(f)-B_a(f)||
       <= C_K min(1,a-T)^(-D) d_*(G_n) p_S(f).          (5)

This is uniform on the unit ball of ONE finite seminorm. No extraction
depending on f and no unknown convergence rate is left in this step.

Every approximant is centered. Therefore <Omega,B_a(f)Omega>=0. Padding
composition also passes exactly: for b>a>T,

             B_b(f)=exp(-(b-a)H)B_a(f)exp(-(b-a)H).      (6)

Different admissible support collars give the same family after the

6. CONTINUOUS TRANSLATION COVARIANCE FROM THE SHRINKING PERIOD

Let p_n be a common causal prefix period for the n-th ACTUAL frame,
chosen on the same regulator subsequence. It satisfies
p_n<=exp(-cG_n)P(G_n), hence tends to zero. The constant matching matrix
has no additional spatial period. For h_n in p_n Z^4 the exact scalar
observable identity gives the corresponding translation of H_(G_n)(f)
as H_(G_n)(f_(h_n)), with the usual shifted test. This remains exact
after centering, since the scalar state is translation invariant.

Choose h_n->h with |h_n-h|<=C p_n. Spatial translations are the scalar
unitaries U(y). With heat slack delta>0,

 ||(U(y)-1)exp(-delta H)||<=C |y|/delta               (10)

by the scalar spectral cone. Thus conjugation of a bounded, more
strongly heat-damped form is norm-continuous uniformly in n by(7).
The test difference obeys p_S(f_(h_n)-f_h)<=C p_n p_(S+1)(f) on one
fixed support collar. Passing the exact identities to the limit gives
spatial translation covariance of q and B, with the appropriate test
translation.

For Euclidean time translations, do NOT use a unitary. On the ordered
scalar insertion core, shifting a whole finite fusion by t is its
Euclidean conjugation exp(tH)Fexp(-tH). The identity at a prefix-period
shift is an exact OS slab/padding identity. Its bounded interpretation,
if a>a0+|t|, is

                                      exp(-(a-a0+t)H).       (11)

All heat times are positive. The difference of(11) at t_n and t has

Consequently the limit has continuous translation covariance in this
heat-form sense although the finite prefix counterterms had only a
shrinking period. This argument does not apply to rotations: their

7. DOMAIN LIMIT: WHAT HAS NOT BEEN INFERRED

Polynomial bounds on exp(-tau H)Qexp(-tau H) alone do not prove that

formal matrix q_nm=1 has heat sandwich v_tau v_tau^*, v_tau,n=e^-tau n,
with polynomial norm O(tau^-1), but q e_m is the non-square-summable

operator on D_fin.

To obtain a tempered Minkowski field one must additionally construct
and identify its time-zero/translation distribution, using the exact
Euclidean translation/padding identity, then use energy transfer to
remove the heat damping after real-time test smearing. The existing
scalar-reconstruction argument has an explicit joint-tube identification
for the original scalar field; it cannot be invoked for these new
fusion forms merely by changing the field's name. One may develop an
analogous one-insertion construction from(8),(11), but that is a
separate domain/analytic-identification proof. No such step is assumed
in the convergence conclusion of Sections3--6.

In particular, this note establishes neither the Gram rank of the
