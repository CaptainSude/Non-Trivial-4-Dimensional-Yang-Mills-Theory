TURN76 -- COUPLED FIXED-CORE AND RUNNING-SOURCE STABILITY
20 October 2026. Root. Conditional sequence theorem with explicit native gates.

PURPOSE

The actual homogeneous q estimates of TURN75 and TURN76 do not require
an analytic extension of every inherited source to a fixed field ball.
A homogeneous running source can remain on a_j=g_j ell_j^p, with
ell_j=1+|log g_j|, provided
its neutral size is measured as t_j=||r_j||_(a_j)/a_j^2. Its quantum
error in that norm is delta_j=O(ell_j^(-2p)), which is small but is
NOT summable along the ultraviolet sequence by itself.

This note proves the coupled iteration needed to use that fact. The
smallness of a newly forced tag must be inserted BEFORE summing its
feedback into the marginal. No bound on an arbitrary R/B source, no
native all-history typing, and no continuum observable theorem is
assumed to have been established by this sequence argument.

1. EXACT BOOKKEEPING TO WHICH THE LEMMA APPLIES

Use fixed q endpoints, relabelled j=0,...,N. Scalars/vacua are retained
in a separate exact source ledger. The nonconstant one-mark state is

             X_j=alpha_j S_j+c_j+r_j.                         (1.1)

Here S_j is the same coupling-free physical reference, beta_j S_j=1;
c_j is a beta-zero complete homogeneous family on a fixed ball b;
r_j is a beta-zero complete homogeneous family on the running ball a_j.
Both families retain the source as a jointly covariant neutral variable
and retain complete carrier/frame reads. The finite Maxwell extraction
on both domains is the SAME actual functional. Projection obeys

 ||S_j||_(a_j)<=C a_j^2,
 |beta_j r|<=C a_j^(-2)||r||_(a_j),
 ||P_j||_(a_j -> a_j)<=C.                                  (1.2)

Representing a sum in (1.1) is bookkeeping, not a unique direct-sum
decomposition of functions. Fix the declared decomposition throughout
the exact map. In particular, small unmarked-base corrections may be
filed as running sources only after their actual complete family has
been admitted there. They are not assigned to the fixed core by size.

Assume the exact retained one-mark map, with all unmarked data frozen,
can be written in this declared decomposition as

 c_(j+1)=A_j c_j+f_j alpha_j,
 r_(j+1)=B_j r_j+F_j alpha_j+H_j c_j,
 alpha_(j+1)=(1+b_j)alpha_j+l_j(c_j)+m_j(r_j).               (1.3)

Any linear running-to-running correction is included in B_j. Every
source scalar produced by this equation is still retained elsewhere.
Equation (1.3) is an ATTACHMENT HYPOTHESIS, not a consequence of knowing
only the size of one isolated raw term. It must hold for the same
actual native source, and not for a selected substitute density.

Write C_j=||c_j||_b and t_j=||r_j||_(a_j)/a_j^2. Suppose uniformly

 ||A_j c||_b <= rho_c ||c||_b,       ||f_j||_b <= C g_j^2,
 ||B_j r||_(a_(j+1))/a_(j+1)^2 <= rho_r ||r||_(a_j)/a_j^2,
 ||F_j||_(a_(j+1))/a_(j+1)^2 <= C eta_j,
 ||H_j c||_(a_(j+1))/a_(j+1)^2 <= C eta_j ||c||_b,
 |b_j| <= C[g_j^4+epsilon_j g_j^2+eta_j],
 |l_j(c)| <= C[g_j^2+epsilon_j+eta_j] ||c||_b,
 |m_j(r)| <= C[delta_j+epsilon_j+eta_j] ||r||_(a_j)/a_j^2,   (1.4)

where rho_c,rho_r<1, 0<=delta_j<=delta_*, and 0<=eta_j<=eta_*<=1.
The period errors epsilon_j are bounded. Uniform contraction in (1.4)
is obtained by choosing q and the minimum final period first, then the
weak floor: rho_c bounds kappa_c+Cg^2 and rho_r bounds
kappa_r+Cdelta_j+Cepsilon_j+Ceta_j. This does not sum delta_j.

The same choice requires the actual source noise admission g_j T_j/a_j
to be small and the source-free occupied expansion on its separate
fixed ball b0. These are not consequences of contraction alone.

2. COUPLING COMPARISON AND TWO CONVOLUTIONS

Let gamma_j=g_j^(-2)>=Gamma>=1 and
|gamma_(j+1)-gamma_j|<=D. Fix K>2 and suppose

                         eta_j<=C_eta g_j^K.                (2.1)

Choose Gamma large enough that, for rho=max(rho_c,rho_r),

       rho(1+D/Gamma)^(max(2,K)/2)<1.                       (2.2)

For i<j, the bounded signed increment condition gives

 g_i/g_j=(gamma_j/gamma_i)^(1/2)
                    <=(1+D/Gamma)^((j-i)/2).

Thus for s=2 or K the geometric sum gives

 sum_(i<j)rho^(j-1-i)g_i^s <= C_s g_j^s.                   (2.3)

No monotonicity is needed. Constants depend on the fixed margin in
(2.2), not on N. At q endpoints D and all constants include that fixed q.

3. UNIT REFERENCE START: PROOF BEFORE GRONWALL

Take alpha_0=1 and c_0=r_0=0. Let M_j=max_(0<=i<=j)|alpha_i|.
Variation of constants in the first equation of (1.3), followed by
(2.3), gives

                        C_j<=C g_j^2 M_j.                  (3.1)

Insert this result into the running equation. Since g_j<=1,

 t_j <= C sum_(i<j)rho_r^(j-1-i)eta_i(|alpha_i|+C_i)
     <= C M_j sum_(i<j)rho_r^(j-1-i)g_i^K
     <= C g_j^K M_j.                                      (3.2)

Neither (3.1) nor (3.2) presupposes a bound on M_j. Substituting BOTH
in the exact marginal equation gives

 |alpha_(j+1)-alpha_j| <= C a_rate,j M_j,
 a_rate,j=g_j^4+epsilon_j g_j^2+(1+delta_j+epsilon_j)g_j^K.  (3.3)

For example, the potentially dangerous running contribution is
delta_j t_j <= C delta_j g_j^K M_j. Summing delta_j alone would be an
incorrect and much weaker estimate. The terms eta_j C_j and eta_j t_j
are absorbed into the displayed g_j^K term because g_j<=1 and eta_*<=1.
The factor C_eta is included in constants, and remains fixed before
the ultraviolet depth is varied.

Let A_N=sum_(j<N)a_rate,j. Discrete Gronwall and then the absolute
increment sum yield

 M_N<=exp(C A_N),
 C_N<=C g_N^2 exp(C A_N),
 t_N<=C g_N^K exp(C A_N),
 |alpha_N-1|<=C A_N exp(C A_N).                             (3.4)

In particular, if C A_N exp(C A_N)<1/2, the reference coefficient
cannot vanish. This is ONLY a coefficient statement, not a covariance
lower bound or a nontrivial quantum field theory.

4. DEPTH-UNIFORM SUMS AND UV TAILS

Suppose in addition the tuned trajectory has the lower envelope

 gamma_j >= G+d(N-j),  d>0,
 Q_j=L^(N-j)Q_end,     L>1,
 epsilon_j<=E exp[-c Q_(j+1)].                             (4.1)

The envelope is a separate input from the bounded signed increment
condition. The decreasing integral comparison gives

 sum_(j<N)g_j^4 <= 1/(dG),
 sum_(j<N)g_j^K <= G^(1-K/2)/[d(K/2-1)].                   (4.2)

Writing s=N-1-j and using L^s>=1+s(L-1) gives

 sum epsilon_j g_j^2
 <= E G^(-1) exp(-cQ_end)/[1-exp(-c(L-1)Q_end)].            (4.3)

Because delta_j and epsilon_j are bounded, (4.2)--(4.3) imply

 A_N <= C[G^(-1)+G^(1-K/2)+G^(-1) exp(-cQ_end)].            (4.4)

The constant in (4.4) is uniform over N, with a fixed positive minimum
Q_end. Thus the running feedback is summable despite the nonsummable
operator error delta_j~ell_j^(-2p).

For the strict fine prefix j<N-m, replace G in the first two terms
of (4.4) by G+dm. The finite-period contribution is bounded by

 C E [G+d(m+1)]^(-1) exp[-c L^m Q_end].                    (4.5)

These tails are for one coefficient recurrence with its fixed
decomposition. They do not compare different regulated measures,
prove compatibility of observable families, or construct a limit.

5. NORMALIZED VERSUS UNNORMALIZED RAW POWERS

The input eta_j in (1.4) is a NORMALIZED running-source bound. If a
typed forcing has the unnormalized norm O(g_j^P), then dividing by
a_(j+1)^2, with a_(j+1)/a_j bounded above AND below by fixed positive
constants (as follows from the signed-step profile) and ell_j>=1, gives

      eta_j<=C g_j^(P-2) ell_j^(-2p).                      (5.1)

Consequently P>4 is a simple sufficient condition to use the theorem
with K=P-2>2. Conversely, a desired normalized g_j^K forcing follows
from an unnormalized bound C g_j^(K+2) ell_j^(2p). Forgetting the a^-2
in (5.1) would falsely permit a much larger raw error.

The condition P>4 is sufficient, not necessary. At P=4 the comparison
series sum gamma^(-1)(1+log gamma)^(-2p) is convergent when p>1/2.
Using that profile in place of g^K and its corresponding convolution
also gives summability. This endpoint improvement is not needed for
an already proved arbitrary-power forcing; no such full sourced
forcing is asserted here.

6. WHICH NATIVE OBLIGATIONS THIS DOES AND DOES NOT REMOVE

TURN75 gives the fixed-core one-mark bounds and constant-reference
birth cancellation, with its exact finite-period leakage. TURN76's
running q estimate supplies the homogeneous B_j contraction and
delta_j feedback in (1.4), once r_j already has the required class.
The finite-radius normalization is therefore not a reason to force
every healed term to extend holomorphically to the fixed ball b.

What remains is an actual source attachment of (1.3)--(1.4):
the old/current mixed reads, boundary compensations, gauge and source
roots, complete age-dependent raw ownership, and the resulting
normalized forcing eta_j. The source-free C+D decomposition by itself
does not prove those assertions for a sourced D correction. Its
covariance interpolation is available only on the admitted running
domain; no larger unmarked domain has been assumed in this note.

The exact source vacua and two-source conditional covariance births
still need their full-law estimates. Reflection-positive reconstruction,
continuum covariance and nonzero original-law physical-time correlation
are not consequences of (3.4). This is a completed sequence calculation
for the explicit class above, not a completed Yang--Mills existence proof.
