3. A REUSABLE POSITIVE-NORMALIZER LEMMA, WITH THE CURRENT ATTACHMENT

Lemma 3.1. Let nu be a finite positive measure, Z=nu(1)>0 and
mu=nu/Z. Let G be an event. Suppose, for C>0 and a probability lambda,

  nu(G)=C integral_B (1+r) dlambda,
  |r|<=delta<1 on B,   lambda(B)>=1-epsilon_4,
  mu(G^c)<=epsilon_3<1.                                 (3.1)

Then

 C(1-delta)(1-epsilon_4)
      <= Z <= C(1+delta)/(1-epsilon_3).                  (3.2)

Proof. Positivity and (3.1) sandwich nu(G) between
C(1-delta)(1-epsilon_4) and C(1+delta). The full-law probability
assumption gives (1-epsilon_3)Z<=nu(G)<=Z. Combine.
For delta,epsilon_3,epsilon_4<=1/2,

  |log Z-log C| <= 2(delta+epsilon_3+epsilon_4).           (3.3)

The same proof permits a positive extra parameter theta by integrating
C_theta and the corresponding selected identities first. Uniform
delta and epsilon_4 suffice; epsilon_3 must concern the resulting
normalized full mixture, not each unverified conditional law.

4. WHY THE PRESENT GLOBAL-ALL-GOOD STRATEGY HAS A CUTOFF OBSTRUCTION

This section is a statement about the CURRENT proved admission
conditions and an exact reference model. It is not a no-go theorem
for YM4, nor a calculation of the full interacting probability.

Take n fixed-ratio RG steps, ratio R>1, at fixed final lattice volume.
There are m_n>=c R^(4n) ambient color blocks at the finest step.
The selected tuned trajectories have

  c_1 n <= g_0^-2 <= c_2 n                              (4.1)

for large n, with fixed terminal weak coupling and fixed block
constants. Our currently proved connected-expansion admission has

  g_0 T_0^3 <= c_3.                                     (4.2)

Therefore any threshold certified by (4.2) satisfies
T_0^2<=C n^(1/3).

Let X_i be independent standard fixed-dimensional color Gaussians,
and c_T=Pr(|X_i|<=T). Their all-good probability is EXACTLY
c_(T_0)^(m_n). For T>=1, a scalar component gives

  1-c_T >= c (T+1)^-1 exp(-T^2/2).                      (4.3)

For completeness, integrate the scalar Gaussian density over
[T,T+1/T]. Its length is 1/T and its exponent differs from -T^2/2
by a bounded amount. Decreasing the constant covers 1<=T<=2.
If T<1, a fixed positive lower tail is even stronger.

Combining (4.1)--(4.3),

  m_n(1-c_(T_0))
     >= c R^(4n) (1+C n^(1/6))^-1 exp(-C n^(1/3))
       --> infinity,

and hence

  c_(T_0)^(m_n) <= exp[-m_n(1-c_(T_0))] --> 0.           (4.4)

The projected-product construction gives exactly this selected mass
in the unrestricted flat quadratic reference with constant coarea
density, after the normal Gaussian of mass one has been appended.
With the admitted weak-chart
cutoff the product-ball numerator is still this exact Gaussian
integral whenever its image lies inside the chart. One must not
reinterpret (4.4) as the probability under the compact interacting
Wilson law or under a separately normalized finite chart.

To make an independent Gaussian all-good event overwhelmingly likely
among R^(4n) rows, thresholds must at least be of order sqrt(n).
The sufficient cubic admission (4.2), together with g_0~n^-1/2,
permits only order n^(1/6). Even a hypothetical replacement by only
g_0 T_0<c would leave a quantitative coefficient competition; it
would not prove the desired global event automatically.

WHY QED3 IS DIFFERENT HERE. Its fine charge is e_j=e sqrt(a_j).
At depth k-j the charge decreases exponentially. Thus cutoffs that
grow polynomially in k-j, as in T09, can defeat the exponential
coordinate count through their Gaussian squared exponent while
e_j times any fixed power of the cutoff remains small. The current
YM4 marginal trajectory does not provide this separation.

IMPORTANT LIMITATION OF THE OBSTRUCTION. Vanishing selected mass
does NOT rule out a common-pressure strategy. In the exact product
model,

  log(Z_good/Z_full)=m log c_T,

which is a pure bulk vacuum. For a j-fold cover in every direction,

  j^d m log c_T -(j^d m)log c_T=0.                       (4.5)

Thus a full bad-history resummation could produce a common extensive
pressure whose contribution cancels in a two-reference defect.
The missing ingredient would then be uniform boundary/winding and
source control of that resummation, rather than probability that
there are no bad labels anywhere. Bound (3.5) alone has no such
cross-volume cancellation or source structure.

5. A DIMENSION-INDEPENDENT NORMALIZED TRANSFER LEMMA

The useful algebraic content of T17 is not abelian or
three-dimensional. Here is a full proof with its hypotheses visible.

Fix d>=1 and an even reference cell count s0. Put N_ref=s0^d.
Let Z_s(O)>0 be the reflected-product numerator on the rectangular
torus with even cell counts s=(s_1,...,s_d). A zero numerator is
handled by a limit or directly by the corresponding zero
chessboard norm. Suppose:

(i) For each direction, with the other counts fixed, the
    reflected-product partition is Tr[A^(s_mu/2)] for some positive
    operator A independent of that count. The indicated traces
    are finite. The same holds for O=1.

(ii) The actual normalized rectangular measure satisfies
    the chessboard estimate

    |E_s product_(c in C) O_c^[c]|
       <= product_(c in C)
                [Z_s(O_c)/Z_s(1)]^(1/N_s),              (5.1)

    for distinct cells, where N_s=product_mu s_mu.

All partition functions use the SAME local action and bare parameters.
Retuning between volumes changes the function in (i) and invalidates
the deduction.

For integer j>=2 define

  D_j=j^d log Z_ref(1)-log Z_(j ref)(1),
  delta_j=D_j/[(j-1)^d N_ref],
  ||O||_ref=[Z_ref(O)/Z_ref(1)]^(1/N_ref).               (5.2)

Then D_j>=0 and, for every s with s_mu>=j s0,

  |E_s product_(c in C) O_c^[c]|
       <= product_(c in C) exp(delta_j)||O_c||_ref.       (5.3)

The reference torus itself satisfies (5.3) by (5.1), since delta_j>=0.

Proof. For a positive A, (Tr A^p)^(1/p) decreases with p and
log Tr A^p is convex in p. These follow from the l^p monotonicity
of its nonnegative eigenvalue sequence and Holder, respectively.
Consequently, increasing a side decreases
(1/N_s) log Z_s(O), for every reflected O. In particular,

  (1/N_s)log Z_s(O)<=(1/N_ref)log Z_ref(O).               (5.4)

Write t_mu=s_mu/s0 and f(t)=log Z_s(1). On the permitted rectangular
counts f is separately convex. No continuum lattice size is needed:
the trace inequality supplies convexity between any three permitted
side counts while the other counts are fixed.

Define

  h(t)=(product_mu t_mu) f(1,...,1)-f(t).                (5.5)

Root-density monotonicity gives h(t)>=0 whenever all t_mu>=1.
For fixed other variables h is concave in any single variable.
For t_mu>=j, concavity between 1,j,t_mu yields

  h(t_mu,hat t)
     <= [(t_mu-1)/(j-1)] h(j,hat t),                    (5.6)

because the omitted endpoint term h(1,hat t) is nonnegative.
Iterating (5.6) in the d directions gives

  h(t)<=D_j product_mu[(t_mu-1)/(j-1)].

Divide by N_s=N_ref product_mu t_mu. Since (t_mu-1)/t_mu<=1,

  (1/N_s)log Z_s(1)
     >= (1/N_ref)log Z_ref(1)-delta_j.                  (5.7)

Subtract (5.7) from (5.4), exponentiate, then use (5.1). This proves
(5.3). Taking s=j ref in root monotonicity proves D_j>=0.

7. WHAT THIS CHANGES IN THE RESEARCH ROUTE

The QED3 plan supplies a clean decision:

A. A direct small-complement route requires a full-law tail mechanism
   for all coordinates used by the selected construction. QED3's
   version is the projected slab trace plus a GLOBAL LINEAR Gaussian
   isometry. Our local moving-saddle covariance bounds do not supply
   this mechanism. The current marginal cutoff class moreover cannot
   make a globally all-good reference event likely as the UV depth
   tends to infinity.

B. A pressure route can tolerate many bad blocks. It requires a
   spatially organized full-law expansion or comparison whose bulk
   part is common at the same bare coupling and whose remaining
   two-reference and source errors are uniformly controlled. The
   exact product example shows why this is logically viable even
   when the selected fraction tends to zero. The present good-
   conditioned probability bounds are useful inputs but do not
   establish this organization.

Thus the new reusable mathematical output is the explicit positive
normalizer lemma and the dimension-four-compatible transfer lemma,
plus the cutoff diagnostic that prevents spending effort on an
unsupported global-all-good comparison. The missing premise remains
an estimate about the ORIGINAL full measure, not another local
selected-kernel identity. No complete nontrivial YM4 construction
follows from QEO1 or from the current project inputs.
