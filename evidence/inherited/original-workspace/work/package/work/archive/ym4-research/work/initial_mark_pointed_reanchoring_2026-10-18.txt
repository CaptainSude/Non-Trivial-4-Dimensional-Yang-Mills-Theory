2. LINEAR REANCHORING LEMMA

Let D_{H} be a strict, vacuum-subtracted scalar family with
sum_H D_H(A_H)=0 for every finite-support field A. Each H carries an
anchor c_H in H. Suppose its absolute norm has exp(a|H|) cardinality
weight and a reserve of log 3+epsilon over the target norm; alternatively
retain the indicated cardinality reserve and one spatial diameter
reserve. For a scalar profile f of Lipschitz constant lambda,

          sum_H f(c_H)D_H(A_H)                            (2.1)

has a strict representation of norm at most C lambda ||D||_reserved.
It vanishes identically for constant f.

Proof. For S subset H form the complete-cell Mobius component

 D_{H,S}(A_S)=sum_(T subset S)(-1)^(|S|-|T|)D_H(A_T),

where all other field slots are set to zero. Then ||D_{H,S}|| is at
most 2^|S| ||D_H|| and D_H=sum_S D_{H,S}; S empty vanishes. For fixed
nonempty S, applying the finite difference in its slots to the zero
total evaluated on fields supported in S gives

                     sum_(H contains S)D_{H,S}=0.         (2.2)

The interchange is justified by absolute local summability. Fix c_S
in S. Subtract f(c_S) times(2.2) to rewrite(2.1) exactly as

 sum_(S nonempty) sum_(H contains S)
                     [f(c_H)-f(c_S)]D_{H,S}(A_S).         (2.3)

Retain carrier H, even though actual field support is S. The profile
difference is at most lambda diam(H), and sum_(S subset H)2^|S|=3^|H|.
The stated reserve pays both factors. This proof counts complete
current cells, not fine coordinates. It applies on finite tori, then
passes to local limits by the same estimates.

Different anchor labels attached to the same H are retained as
separate summands; (2.2) sums them too. Thus it applies directly to
the difference of A_{z,H} and B_H, which need not have the same
anchor assignment.

If the input families have no field-linear terms coefficientwise,
neither does(2.3). Constant-color averaging supplies that property
without increasing the norm. No field-radius Cauchy loss occurs in
this linear reanchoring step.

4. GAUGE-INVARIANCE SCOPE: A REAL INTERFACE TO CHECK

The COMPLETE residual in(3.2) is gauge invariant: it is the actual
source derivative minus the actual gauge-invariant weighted Wilson
energy. However, neither B_H nor the Mobius pieces in(2.3) need be
individually gauge invariant. The construction retains constant-color

5. MIXED INITIAL TERMS: SOURCE CHI COSTS ARE PAID BY SEPARATION

For two disjoint amplitude-one profiles with separation c rho, every
actual mixed local coefficient of the sourced state has a connected

f_1(z)f_2(z)=0. The remaining localized coefficients retain their
connecting exponential, giving exp(-c' rho) after the fixed tree sum.

At witness scale s, the local source class has supremum radius O(r)
and Lipschitz radius O(r chi_s), chi_s=ell^(-(N-s)/2). The profile

       C gamma_s^C rho^C exp[-c rho+C(N-s)] .             (5.2)

gamma_s>=Gamma_*+c_0(N-s)-C_0, so N-s<=C(1+gamma_s).
