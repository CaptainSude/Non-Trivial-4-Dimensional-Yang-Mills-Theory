1. WHY THE INITIAL FUSION POWER MUST NOT BE CARRIED FOREVER

Let i be a fusion date, gamma_i=G, and normalize the COMPLETE actual
fusion frame to the fixed terminal marginal coordinates. Its physical
regular marginal at a later date j is

 c_j=P_(j,N)^(-1)v plus its explicitly controlled transverse correction.

Thus |c_j|<=C gamma_j^p |v|, with a fixed terminal weak band. The
normalization power G^B is needed at creation, but is not the right
bound for the continuing marginal. All finite matrices here are the
actual source-free ones; no extension is changed.

For the actual NEW source births, use M_u for the complete normalized
creation norm, including the new tensor coefficient before separating
its phase mean and zero-mean part. The difference recurrence and the
periodic_marginal_coboundary identity give a positive inequality of
the following form:

 M_(j+1)<=C delta_j[gamma_j^p+
             sum_(i<=u<=j) k_(j,u) M_u]+native_j,
 k_(j,u)<=C(1+j-u)^q min(1,P(gamma_u)e^(-beta(j-u))),       (1)

where P is a fixed logarithmic power and delta_j<=C(log gamma_j)^-nu,
nu>0. The source prepared scalar at NEW birth has the same creation
supremum; this does not give an h^4 gain to an old scalar. Causal
scalar extraction remains separate.

The row estimate in phase_kernel_positive_volterra also holds with
the DECREASING current weight gamma_j^p. Indeed bounded increments give

 gamma_u^p/gamma_j^p<=C(1+j-u)^p,

and the additional polynomial just increases q. Therefore the row
cost is at most C(1+log log gamma_j)^(q+p+1), absorbed by delta_j at a
sufficiently weak floor. The current forcing in (1) has the solution
bound C gamma_j^p. A creation term G^B at i remains a separate
resolvent transient; on every comparable-G interval it is bounded by

 C G^B exp[-c(j-i)/(1+log log G)].                        (2)

Across intervals, one uses the actual positive resolvent, not a new
G^B conversion at each date. In particular the original creation
polynomial is not the continuing current marginal bound.

2. A LARGE PREPARATORY CONSTANT ONLY LENGTHENS THE READ MEMORY

Here is an elementary estimate useful at a FIRST chronological read.
Let the read's actual proper layer be n, let t=n-u>=0, and suppose the
same compensated dated function has both the neutral bound and the
graded prepared bound, with their COMMON connection factor retained.
After the old root sum these bounds have the form

 k_read(n,u)<=C(1+t)^q min{1,L_n exp(-alpha t)}.          (3)

The neutral member must be taken before multiplying by L_n. This is
exactly the two-bound use in graded_collar_interpolation; it does not
assert a small original static function on the entire current tube.
Include any printed neutral ring/radius comparison in the polynomial
if it has the already established weighted kernel bound. An unbounded
ring factor not so controlled remains an extra input, not a constant.

For any p>=0, bounded coupling increments imply

 sum_(u<=n) k_read(n,u) gamma_u^p
   <=C gamma_n^p [1+log(2+L_n)]^(p+q+1).                (4)

Proof: gamma_u<=gamma_n+D(1+t). Split at
 T=ceil[2 alpha^(-1)log(2+L_n)]. For t<=T use the neutral
member and sum its fixed polynomial. For t>T,
 L_n e^(-alpha t)<=e^(-alpha t/2); the remaining polynomial
tail is uniformly summable. This proves (4) without an L_n factor.

The safe source preparation allowance is
 log L_n<=C(1+R_n)(1+log gamma_n)^C. It is polylogarithmic.
Consequently (4) is a fixed polynomial in gamma_n (in fact a power
of its logarithm in addition to gamma_n^p). No hierarchy demanding
q_n>log L_n is needed for this use. It is incorrect instead to apply
L_n to a native inverse power G^-K unconditionally.

The same argument treats a periodic marginal kernel with the neutral
member independent of its period. Its effective memory is log p,
not p. The masks, complete pointed sum and current companion must be
the SAME in the two bounds. This does not permit cancellation between
unrelated localized read maps.

For the transient (2), split the u sum at (i+n)/2. Its late part has
the exponentially small transient times the row bound in (4); its
early part uses L_n exp[-alpha(n-i)/2]. Thus it disappears once n-i
dominates both log L_n and log log G times log G. In the selected
fusion-to-observation regime n-i is of order G, so this condition is
satisfied even when gamma_n is only of logarithmic observation size.

3. REFERENCES AND CAUSAL SCALARS AT THE SAME READ

The current reference has coefficient c_n bounded by gamma_n^p.
Old references are combined with the SAME original fine clover.
At a fixed read the Abel terms retain their literal actual mask
change or their common-cut strip. A late common-cut strip of an old

 sum_(u<=n) gamma_u^p (1+n-u)^q ell^(u-n)<=C gamma_n^p.    (5)

For a genuine earlier actual stratum use its own index and original
occupation/attachment reserve, not the late strip formula. The old
phase means and oscillations are separated by the exact tent-balanced
identity, not by redating the full original pair function.

The universal scalar v_u is extracted AT BIRTH. Its deficit is kept
on that birth's eligibility history. For one actual own-n cell, only
births u>=n can have that cell in the birth complement. Their current-u
root count is fixed/polylogarithmic, never ell^(4(u-n)). The number of
dates until healing is at most polynomial in gamma_n; bounded-flow
comparisons give gamma_u<=C gamma_n. Thus a current creation bound
polynomial in gamma_u yields a polynomial in gamma_n for this causal
deficit census. This is a coefficient bound, not a claim that a full
macroscopic singleton deficit is small after normalization.

An original input with n<i has no neutral rescaling. Its polynomial
G creation bound is paid at that actual fine contact, where gamma_n
is at least comparable to G. The existing occupation reserve
exp[-c(log gamma_n)^s], s>1, pays each fixed polynomial without a
new inverse factor at every later operation. Multiple assignments
still require the original labelled cell/path census.

Equations (4)--(5) therefore identify a route to a polynomial
CURRENT first-read coefficient in its original analytic spectator
norm. They do not by themselves prove an arbitrary full-history
source disc: the reference and scalar qualifications above are
necessary, and the summed connected two-label history remains a
distinct step.

 ||T_q W_z||<=C ell^(-4q) ||W_z||                       (6)
