h(z)=(1-z/(2a))^(-1), and give two points weights

For real |z|<a these weights are positive, and their full normalizer

give a fixed analytic domain when a tends to zero, even when the source
itself and the full normalizer cause no difficulty. This example is
only a logical counterexample, not a claim about the actual YM kernels.
