1. AN EXACT POSITIVE SU(2) REDUCTION OF ALL MULTI-LOOP MOMENTS

Use w(A)=Tr(A)/2. Cayley--Hamilton for B in SU(2) gives

              w(A)w(B) = [w(AB)+w(AB^{-1})]/2.              (1.1)

This is [KZ](1.1) in normalized-trace convention. Transport the based
holonomies of any finite collection of loops to a common vertex by fixed
lattice paths. Each trace is unchanged. Apply (1.1) successively:

 product_(j=1)^n w(A_j)
     = 2^{-(n-1)} sum_(epsilon_2,...,epsilon_n in {+,-})
                        w(A_1 A_2^{epsilon_2}...A_n^{epsilon_n}). (1.2)

The formula is pointwise, not a factorization of expectations. It holds
under the SAME normalized Wilson law, any source-deformed positive law,
and indeed any probability law of these matrices. The weights have total
mass one. There is no normalization denominator to compare, no large-N
limit and no factorial cost from this trace reduction. Ordinary joint
moments determine cumulants by the usual partition formula if desired.

Complete reads matter. The single words contain the common-base paths,
their inverse segments and every original loop. The union may be much
larger than an individual loop. A product supported on separated sets
does not become a local one-loop observable in either set. The equality
is exact despite this path dependence of the individual terms.

[KZ](2.31) makes the SU(2) lattice loop equation linear on single-loop
expectations after this reduction. It is an INFINITE loop-space equation;
plaquette deformations increase word length. Their finite-length bootstrap
