1. ACTUAL METRICS AND THE NATIVE EXPONENT RESERVE

B109 printed251 explicitly chooses L odd and greater than11. Its
printed257 definition of d_j(X) is the shortest connected tree meeting

the birth mesh L^-j. Thus one unit represents physical length M a_j.
Under the next homogeneous L blocking, length in old units is L times
length in new units. Fixed complete map/read collars introduce only a
fixed additive error C_L (also depending on fixed M and stencil choices).

For source root read P and next coarse roots q,r, with their complete
old-scale footprints denoted by hats, the standard block comparison is

 d_in(P,hat q) >= L d_out(P_+,q)-C_L,
 T_in(P;hat q,hat r) >= L T_out(P_+;q,r)-C_L.             (1)

Here P_+ consists of all new cells met by the complete old P, enlarged
by the actual fixed collars. T(P;i,j) is the minimum of the three
two-edge trees connecting the collapsed set P and i,j. Each individual
edge obeys the first comparison, so taking the minimum proves the
second, after increasing C_L. P is never replaced by one convenient
point. This convention retains its size in the source amplitude.
