1. STRICT LOCALITY AND THE PHYSICAL CURRENT

The current must be constructed from the joint corrected minimizer
and multiplier, not by taking a rough outside divergence. The note
does this correctly. The identity

 E(A_*)=Q^T m+DR(A_*)^T m,
 J_phys(exp(i eta A_*))=T_(eta A_*)^(-T) E(A_*)

uses the corrected physical adjoint already controlled in the
absolute-row norm. The local analytic multiplication and ordinary
gauge transfer preserve the joint support algebra. The local sources
explicitly distinguish this corrected adjoint from the nonuniform
full ordinary adjoint, so no unavailable ordinary-adjoint estimate
has been used. Complete fine regularity remains inside one Banach
output norm per physical cell.

The resulting tail estimate is a maximum over output cells. It does
not require summing over all fine coordinates in X. Coarse sharp
truncation is legitimate because the coarse input is a supremum
product ball; the complete regular fine field is produced only after
that truncation by the minimizing map.

2. GEOMETRY AND SHELL SUMMATION

CMP109 p257 defines d_i(X) by a tree meeting every fixed localization
cube, with its length divided by the fixed block side. This controls
physical diameter and block count up to constants involving that
fixed side. The extra requirement that X be a union of larger birth
blocks only reduces the set of admissible domains. Consequently the
source exponent can pay fixed diameter weights and any fixed
polynomial support factors without an exp(C R_i^4) conversion loss.

The shell estimate C A_X exp(-kappa_cl n) follows from the independent
(A,J) Cauchy bound C A_X/r_i and the complete-field locality estimate
C b_i exp(-kappa_cl n), with b_i comparable to r_i. Its derivatives
are consistent derivatives of the same shell scalar. Cauchy at fixed
order k costs only r_i^-k; the sum of coordinate k-tuples costs
O(|H|^k), not an exponential number of monomials.

For an anchored site z, the factor

  1 <= exp[-delta d(z,x_X)] exp[delta(D_X+n)]

on z in H lets the source-domain sum and shell sum be performed before
the final exponentially convergent sum over anchor locations. The
polynomial support reserve pays both coordinate tuples and the |H|^3
needed for the response theorem's rooted cumulants. No factor depends
on fine-coordinate dimension, regulator volume or birth block side.

3. MATCHING THE REQUIRED REAL JETS

The joint supported scalar G(B,t) vanishes at t=0. Its exact Taylor
decomposition gives L=D_tG(B,0)x, odd in x for every real B, and

 Q_g=int_0^1 (1-s) D_t^2G(B,sgx)[x,x] ds.

After two B derivatives, Q has fourth mixed derivatives of G. One
x gradient differentiates both explicit x slots and the evaluation
point sgx. The latter term is g times a fifth mixed derivative and
is bounded by C g r_i^-5 ||x_H||^2. The stated bounded g/r_i condition
therefore gives C r_i^-4. This term was retained, not dropped.

Uniform coordinate L32 moments pay the L8 norms of all linear and
quadratic x factors. Minkowski gives |||x_H|||_32 <= C |H|^(1/2)
and |||x_H||^2||_8 <= C |H|. Component sums of gradients and ordered
B labels add only fixed powers of |H|. The resulting per-site
support-anchor bound is stronger than the required bound for W_r
formed by summing source labels with x_X=r: every such shell support
contains r. Reducing the positive spatial exponent if necessary
aligns it with the response theorem's kappa.

4. NECESSARY SCOPE RESTRICTIONS

(a) Corollary3 uses the retained-domain premise from Section4:
    g||x||_infinity must be less than a fixed fraction of the available
    t-radius c_2 r_i, uniformly on the actual retained support. The
    L32 moment assumption alone does not imply this domain condition.
    For old births at later couplings this must be obtained from a
    verified enlarged domain or a separate startup mechanism.

(b) The input gives norms under an actual hard-cutoff law. Application
    of the smooth-barrier covariance proof additionally requires the
    smooth extension/approximation hypotheses stated in the response
    theorem; those are not supplied by Corollary3 alone.

(c) Shell pieces need not individually be gauge invariant. The scalar
    sum has the original invariance. This is sufficient for the real
    rooted response estimate and for Ward identities of the complete
    family, but not a source E-sector preparation theorem for every
    separate shell piece or an independent-current decoupling norm.

(d) This pays a fixed entry cost epsilon_i r_i^-4. It is not a cost
    that may be charged anew at every step. The response theorem
    outputs a Hessian bound while its full input can require W^5;
    these two results do not form a closed finite-order iteration.

(e) Actual uniform convexity, weighted Hessian locality, Gaussian
    background-score bounds and O(g) jets for the entire inherited
    interaction remain separate. The birth localization does not
    imply them.
