dependence on the bare coupling. It tunes EVERY prescribed finite
depth. No clipped RG, derivative of beta, unique root, or independent
memoryless beta function is needed. The proof is in Sections2--3.

new non-Gaussian coupling increment differs from the complete native

required. The repository's proposed comparison is a useful clue but

No trajectory existence or all-history closure is promoted here.

2. FINITE SHOOTING FROM NO LARGE RECOVERY

Let a>0 be the inverse-coupling weak threshold. For j=0,1,... let

 x_(j+1)=x_j-b_(j+1)(x_0,...,x_j)                       (1)

be a causal recursion. The functions b_(j+1) are continuous on the
closed admissible history cone [a,infinity)^(j+1). It suffices that
they are continuous on the generated history family and have a
continuous one-step continuation at every finite boundary history
whose inputs are still >=a. Only genuine histories are used below.
The dependence may include a recursively propagated action state;
continuous finite-step state maps give the same proof by induction.

Assume a uniform B>=0 such that b_(j+1)<=B whenever the inputs to
that step are admissible. Assume also a uniform recovery bound C>=0:

 x_j<=x_i+C for every i<j whose steps have admissible inputs. (2)

The last output x_j need not be >=a for this assumption. Alternatively
it is enough that(2) extends continuously to boundary histories.
These assumptions concern a single family of actual step maps, not
different favorable beta choices made for different bare parameters.

THEOREM. For every N>=0 and target X>a+C, some initial t=x_0
generates N genuine admissible steps, x_N=X and all x_j>a. One may
choose t<=X+BN, and the chosen path has x_j>=X-C for every j<N.
No assertion of uniqueness or continuity of the chosen root in N
or X is made.

Proof for N>=1. Let U_N be the set of t>a for which the first N
INPUTS x_0,...,x_(N-1) exist and are strictly greater than a.
Causal continuity makes U_N open in(a,infinity). It contains the
ray t>a+BN: induction using b<=B gives x_j>=t-jB>a for j<N.
Let(t_*,infinity) be its unbounded connected component, allowing
t_*=a. The finite output x_N(t) is continuous on this interval.

At t_* the limits of the first N inputs are defined successively
using continuity on the closed admissible histories. To spell out
why this is not a compactness assumption on an infinite action
space: x_0 tends to t_*; if the first j inputs and their recursively
constructed states have finite limits, continuity of the next
finite step gives the next limits. All input limits are >=a.
If every input limit were >a, local continuity would extend the
same component to the left, contradicting its definition. Thus some
input x_l(t_*)=a with l<N, including l=0 when t_*=a.

The boundary version of(2) gives x_N(t_*)<=a+C<X. At
t_+=X+BN, the upper drop bound gives x_N(t_+)>=X and all its
inputs are >a; t_+ is in the unbounded component because the whole
ray above a+BN is. The intermediate value theorem on[t_*,t_+]
therefore supplies a root with t>t_*. Its first N inputs are >a and
its last output is X>a, so every step is original and admissible.
Applying(2) between j and N gives x_j>=X-C>a. N=0 is immediate.

Notice what is minimal here. Endpoint existence itself requires a
uniform bound on possible RECOVERY after an encounter with the
threshold, not necessarily a positive average drift. With b=0 it
still holds, but it yields no ultraviolet weakening of the bare g.

3. SIGNED STRETCH DRIFT AND ALL-DEPTH ULTRAVIOLET TUNING

Suppose more quantitatively that for some lambda>0,C>=0,B<infinity
every admissible generated stretch obeys

 lambda(j-i)-C <= x_i-x_j <= B(j-i),     0<=i<j.        (3)

The upper one-step inequality and(2) follow. For every N and
X>a+C, the root of Section2 obeys the exact bounds

 X+lambda(N-j)-C <= x_j <= X+B(N-j),  0<=j<=N,         (4)

with x_N=X and x_j>a. In particular

 X+lambda N-C <= x_0 <= X+BN.                          (5)

For g_j=x_j^-1/2 this tunes the bare coupling to a fixed terminal
g=X^-1/2 at every depth and gives g_0 of order N^-1/2. Formula(4),
not a claimed sign of each increment, keeps every earlier coupling
within the same weak interval. An individual step can increase x.
The endpoint map t->x_N(t) can oscillate; only continuity was used.
The maps may depend on N as a fixed regulator parameter, provided
the premises hold with the same constants for each N. No automatic
compatibility between those different finite constructions follows.

Continuity cannot simply be omitted. For N=1 and target X well
above a, take b_1(t)=2 for t<X+3/2 and b_1(t)=1 for t>=X+3/2.
All increments lie in[1,2] and satisfy positive drift, but t-b_1(t)
jumps from values below X-1/2 to values at least X+1/2. There is no
root with x_1=X. Discrete changes of an ACTUAL cutoff prescription
must therefore be treated, not hidden behind a formal beta series.

A convenient sufficient coefficient input is

 b_j=d_j+e_j, |d_j|<=B_G, |e_j|<=epsilon,
 sum_(l=i+1)^j d_l >= c_G(j-i)-C_G, c_G>epsilon.       (6)

Then(3) holds with lambda=c_G-epsilon, C=C_G and
B=B_G+epsilon. The d_j can depend on grade and individual d_j
can be negative. The stronger repository estimate
|sum_(1)^k d_j-kc_G|<=C_0 gives C_G=2C_0, but is not necessary.
A uniform positive average on fixed-length Gaussian blocks also
suffices after retaining the bounded endpoint fragments: for every
aligned block of q dates with sum>=b_*>0 and |d_j|<=B_G,
one may take c_G=b_*/q and C_G=2b_*+2q B_G. The constants are
fixed BEFORE the weak floor, and the terminal margin includes C_G.

6. FINITE CAUSAL CONTINUITY ON THIS HOMOGENEOUS LANE

Fix a finite number of steps and a compact set of positive real
coupling histories whose couplings do not exceed the weak ceiling.
Keep the actual continuous radii(7) and all auxiliary constants
fixed. Each current free integral can be changed to a fixed unit
product by x=R_j u. Its density, Jacobian and inherited finite
coefficients vary continuously with the positive couplings. The
complete real normalization is positive. At any fixed finite step
and regulator it therefore stays nonzero in a sufficiently small
real-parameter neighborhood. The gauge/minimum implicit maps use
the common strict tube already established; they do not acquire a
new branch merely by changing the real coupling.

For coefficient families one may apply the same reasoning to each
fixed localized integral/decoupling term. Products, finite sums and
the consistent logarithmic/connected expansion preserve continuity.
The common rooted carrier majorants of(9), with the fixed derivative
reserve used in(10), dominate the infinite sums locally uniformly
on that compact coupling set. Dominated convergence gives continuity
of the current beta coefficient. This argument is inductive in a
FINITE history length and supplies continuity at the closed weak
threshold after choosing the ceiling strictly inside the proven
common cap. It needs no modulus uniform in depth, no derivative in
g, and no continuity at g=0.

Thus the actual homogeneous recursion has the continuity needed
by Section2 on every admissible finite history. The construction
of beta on the infinite lattice uses the same locally uniform
carrier sums; it does not require an infinite-volume normalized
probability measure. No theorem about a discontinuous rounded
waiting schedule or moving mixed-history catalogue is included.
If an auxiliary rounded decomposition is used, its effect must
cancel in the same exact scalar family before invoking this proof;

The determinant localization itself proves boundedness and analytic

If the native Gaussian integral is
Z_j^G(theta)=a_j(theta)(2pi)^(m/2)det D_j(theta)^(-1/2),
with the ACTUAL Gaussian-order prefactor a_j, then

 partial_qr log Z_j^G
 =partial_qr log a_j
  +one half Tr[D_j^-1 D_(j,r) D_j^-1 D_(j,q)]
  -one half Tr[D_j^-1 D_(j,qr)].                        (13)

All physical minimum/constraint second jets belong to D_(j,qr);
they are not removed by writing a formal transverse propagator.
Applying the actual moment functional to(13), followed by a uniform
native infinite-volume limit, gives d_j. Formula(13) is an exact
computable producer, but its two terms have no immediate positive
sign. Field-independent determinants/volumes cancel only under the

Positive Gaussian curvature cannot replace the calculation: a local
precision D(U)=exp(lambda h_p(U))I remains positive on real fields

This is a diagnostic of the insufficiency of qualitative bounds,
not a counterexample to the actual Wilson precision.

The proposed native route to(12) is still useful: prove the exact

The exact universal one-loop coefficient is a stronger objective
