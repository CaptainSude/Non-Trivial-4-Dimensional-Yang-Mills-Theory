1. PRECISE CLASS AND JOINT DEFECT

Use one fixed fractional input/output b margin, b=g log gamma, g/b<1,
and g T<c b. All norms below are complete rooted supported-family norms;
the spatial reserve and q are fixed first. The actual chart Phi(B,t) is
holomorphic on a fixed geometric ball, Phi(0,0)=0, and consequently

 Phi(B,t)+Phi(-B,-t)=O((|B|+|t|)^2).                         (1)

This is coordinate Taylor algebra, not a purported symmetry under
inversion of nonabelian links. Take a WHOLE rooted mark W with

 ||W||_b <= M b^2,  ||W(B)-W(-B)||_b <= M b^3,  DW(0)=0.   (2)

The last condition follows from its constant-color invariance. Scalar
constants may be present. The even/odd algebraic pieces need not be gauge
invariant separately and are not transported separately as physical marks.

Write F(B,t)=W(Phi(B,t)) and

 D(B,t)=F(B,t)-F(-B,-t).

Using (1), the even W derivative bound O(Mb), and the odd W bound O(Mb^3),
gives ||D|| <= C M b^3 on the joint fractional b ball. Its Taylor jets of
total degree at most two vanish: the constant cancels, DW(0)=0, and the
quadratic jet of F is even under joint reversal. This is the exact point
at which the stronger input (2) is used. An arbitrary mixed norm Mb^2
alone gives no such conclusion.

2. SECOND-ORDER REMAINDERS SUFFICE

Subtract the static value before expanding in the ONE joint noise amplitude.
The exact Taylor formulas with integral remainder give

 Delta F(B,gx)=g L(B;x)+g^2 R_g(B;x),
 Delta D(B,gx)=g L_D(B;x)+g^2 R_D,g(B;x),                    (3)

where L,L_D are odd linear noise polynomials and the remainders have
degree-two growth on the retained set. Cauchy on the same fractional ball
gives coefficient bounds

       L: M b,       R_g: M,
       L_D: M b^2,   R_D,g: M b.                            (4)

Thus their fixed-degree moment sizes are Mgb and Mgb^2 respectively.
These estimates do not replace x by its cutoff supremum and contain no T.
They retain all external roots. The source fixed-degree mark estimate
applies to integral remainders with polynomial growth, not only polynomials.

This order-two proof avoids a possible fourth-remainder error. If using
the cubic Taylor formula instead, the fourth remainder must be taken
AFTER forming D: it is O(g^4 M/b), rather than the ordinary O(g^4 M/b^2).
For a mixed input M=M12 g^2 this is g^6 M12/b <= M12 g^4 b.

3. THE ACTUAL REFLECTED LAWS AND THEIR NORMALIZATION

Let mu_B be the actual normalized inner law. Change x to -x in mu_-B.
The original independent nonpivot radial windows are exactly preserved;
no whitening or moving cutoff is used. The reference Gaussians remain
centered. Their precision difference is a localized quadratic noise
score Psi_2 with coefficient norm O(b), by the fixed-geometric-radius
background operator bound. Vacuum determinant factors cancel in normalized
ratios, or equivalently have zero covariance with every mark.

After static value and static quadratic Gaussian extraction, the geometric
residual has coefficient envelope O(g) of fixed polynomial growth. The
actual inherited X obeys ||X||_b<=K b^2 and DX(0)=0. Its fluctuation score
has envelope C K [gb |x|+g^2 |x|^2], on the same prepared ball. This is
true without assuming ANY refined parity for X. The reflected difference
of the two residual potentials is therefore O(g) in fixed-growth norms.

Interpolate the two precisions inside their common strict coercivity margin,
and the two bounded local residual potentials inside the reserved source
budget. This is an analytic comparison of two admitted local integrals,
not a new physical flow and not an arbitrary tilted large-field law.
The actual complete-operator/localized Gaussian input and the original
absolute connected proof are uniform on this fixed smaller interpolation

For a normalized expectation, differentiation is exactly covariance with

4. COVARIANCE BIRTH

Static values cancel exactly from Cov(W1,W2), leaving Cov(Delta F1,Delta F2).
Replacing one increment by its reflected defect costs, by (4),

       C (M1 g b^2)(M2 g b)=C M1 M2 g^2 b^3.              (5)

The difference of the two laws costs O(b) from Psi_2 and O(g) from the
residual score. Applied to two increments of size Ma g b, the normalized
third-cumulant estimate gives the same bound. Hence

 ||Cov_B(W1,W2)(B)-Cov_-B(W1,W2)(-B)||
                         <= C M1 M2 g^2 b^3.               (6)

The original two-root connecting tree remains present. A fixed number of
noise-growth and attachment polynomials is paid by the already fixed
spatial reserve; no number-of-roots, number-of-ages, or volume factor occurs.

5. SHARP SINGLE-MARK QUANTUM ODD BOUND

Put QW(B)=<Delta F(B,gx)>_muB. Its reflected difference is the sum of
(a) the mean of Delta D under one law and (b) the change of law applied
to the other ordinary increment. For (a), the centered Gaussian mean of
L_D vanishes; one residual interpolation supplies g. Equations (3)-(4)
give C M[g^2 b^2+g^2 b] <= C M g^2 b.

For (b), the complete list of potentially largest terms is

 score / increment       normalized estimate
 Psi_2=O(b) / g L        centered covariance zero; one V=O(g)
                         insertion gives C M g^2 b^2
 Psi_2=O(b) / g^2 R_g    C M g^2 b
 Delta V=O(g) / g L      C M g^2 b
 Delta V=O(g) / g^2 R_g  C M g^3 <= C M g^2 b.

The first line is essential. A generic O(b) law-comparison bound on gL
would be too weak. The exact inherited interaction can also be interpolated

The fixed-degree connected estimates apply to every entry and preserve
the same rooted support norm. Therefore

       ||QW(B)-QW(-B)|| <= C M g^2 b.                       (7)

Together with the ordinary sharp estimate ||QW||<=C M g^2, this gives
operator loss C(g/b)^2 in the refined norm

       b^-2 ||W|| + b^-3 ||W(B)-W(-B)||.                   (8)

For mixed W12 put M=M12 g^2: its quantum odd part is at most

No claim that the entire static-plus-quantum map is a contraction follows.
