9. THE PRECISE FAILURE OF A NAIVE ARBITRARY-DEPTH ITERATION

Two distinct issues remain and should not be conflated.

(a) ANALYTIC TAIL DOMAIN. The inherited tail is known on an actual
datum ball of radius c_j g_j Lambda_j, not the fixed radius of the
local analytic family. Repeating the containment proof used here
would demand, with a fixed margin,

 Lambda_j >= A_j (g_(j+1)/g_j) Lambda_(j+1),      A_j>1.           (9.1)

The enlarged l2 domain removes the earlier sqrt(N_j) factor. It
does not remove A_j. Iterating this sufficient condition over k
levels requires Lambda_1 >= (g_k/g_1) Lambda_k product_(j<k) A_j.
Even fixed A_j=A>1 gives geometric cutoff growth. This is incompatible
with the polylogarithmic cutoff regime of the present estimates when
the number of scales grows along the expected weak-coupling trajectory.
It is a failure of THIS verification scheme, not a theorem that the
actual density has no larger useful domain or that YM4 is impossible.

Keeping every calculation in fixed-size blocks removes a possible
global-volume factor but does not by itself remove (9.1). The same
strict contour margin and inherited radius are still present. A
successful block reorganization must additionally give a local
energy-conditioned analytic domain stable under the actual maps,
or transport the small tails through a separate large-field/real
measure estimate without repeatedly imposing this nested ball
criterion. Neither follows merely by calling a box a block.

(b) UNIFORM LOCAL CLASS. The second KKT proof legitimately uses the
known local K_L. Repeating it indefinitely needs uniform locality
and a uniform nonlinear datum radius for the changing direct coarse
classical actions and their generated analytic families. At arbitrary
depth on a fixed-crossing box, TURN42 proves normal boundary screening
but leaves the along-boundary projection estimate open. Its exact
identity K_K-K_per=L^*(I-Pi_K)L does not bound distant shallow-boundary
entries of Pi_K. Positivity, the gap, and the normal screening bound
alone do not supply that missing spatial estimate.

The two-step substitutions also spend definite analytic-radius and
carrier-exponent reserves. A fresh uniform direct-scale theorem or
contraction/renormalization mechanism is needed before those losses
can be iterated without degeneration. This note supplies neither the
native carrierwise gauge identities with independently varied
background/current fields nor control of all relevant and marginal
terms. Nor does it cover interrupted/large-field histories or prove
a nonzero separated continuum observable.
