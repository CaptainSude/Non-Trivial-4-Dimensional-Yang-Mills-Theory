QUANTIFIED BUFFER CHOICE FOR THE FIRST MASKED-LAW BOUNDARY ERROR
24 October 2026. Root finite consumer; not a source transport theorem.

PURPOSE

The proved replica profile can supply BOTH a rare factor and any fixed
fourth-power target h^4 with a buffer polynomial in log(1/h). This note
records the actual finite census condition and complete-read restriction.
It does not identify an age-dependent finite comparison with the later
homogeneous propagation of a once-born source.

1. INPUT AND NUMERICAL CHOICE

For each admitted finite first fiber, the preceding theorem gives

 |mu_z F-mu_z' F|
 <=C osc(F)|P|(r^2+q) exp[-Phi_A(D)/2],
 Phi_A(D)=min(aD,sqrt(aAD)),  A=log(1/(Nq)),              (1)

provided Nq<=1/4. Here D is the distance to the actual complete boundary
mask AND score reads, not just to the nominal box boundary. All fibers
hold the same local coarse reference and geometry. Endpoints in the
guarded box may include a declared auxiliary z'=0 reference with its
normalized mask law defined there. This does not make that reference
an actual exterior atom or replace the original Z_D exterior weight.

Assume q<=C_q exp(-b r^2). Strengthen the weak floor once and suppose
the literal census on the chosen box satisfies

                  log(C_q N)<= b r^2/2.                 (2)

Then A>=b r^2/2 and Nq<=1/4 after one further fixed floor.
Let h=L^(-s), s>=0, with fixed L>1. For any requested rare exponent
k>0, choose a fixed K so

 c_K:=min(aK,sqrt(abK/2)) >= max(2k,8 log L).

A sufficient integer buffer is

       D >= K (r^2+s)^2/r^2
         = K(r^2+2s+s^2/r^2).                           (3)

For the census and complete-read conclusions below, make the CONCRETE
choice D=ceil[K(r^2+s)^2/r^2]+d_read, where d_read is the fixed native
read-collar allowance. Thus D also has the corresponding fixed upper
bound. An arbitrarily larger buffer would satisfy (4) but would not
automatically satisfy the census or image conditions below.

Indeed aD>=aK(r^2+s), while
sqrt(aAD)>=sqrt(abK/2)(r^2+s). Thus

              exp[-Phi_A(D)/2] <= exp(-k r^2) h^4.      (4)

For an original plaquette with fixed complete read P, (1) becomes

 |mu_z F_p-mu_z' F_p|
 <=C alpha_p epsilon0^2(r^2+q) exp(-k r^2) h^4.         (5)

All original microscopic amplitudes alpha_p=|c_a f_p| are retained.
Changing k changes the FIXED buffer multiplier K, not the original
cutoff hierarchy or source calibration. Equation (2) must then be
checked for this larger box; it is not dropped by choosing K.

2. WHEN A POLYNOMIAL CENSUS MEETS THE CONDITION

Suppose the native number of elementary masks on a box of diameter D
obeys N<=C G(g0)(1+D)^d, where G and the geometric parameters have at
most a fixed inverse-power growth in g0. Suppose also s<=C g0^(-t)
for one fixed t, and r is the registered fixed constant times
(log(1/g0))^p with p>1/2. These are EXPLICIT extra range assumptions.
Then (3) is at most polynomial in g0^(-1) and log(1/g0), so

 log(C_q N)=O(log(1/g0))=o(r^2).

Condition (2) follows at one weak floor uniformly in that stated
range of s. This is a finite-box argument. A physical fine-lattice
volume of order exp(c/g0^2) cannot be inserted for N in this proof.
Nor does the note establish the stated maximum-age relation for a
particular RG history: its actual date/coupling relation must be used.

If the box wraps around a torus or has insufficient physical room,
the required D is unavailable and (5) is not claimed. The distance
must be computed in the actual finite geometry with its read collars.

3. COMPLETE READ AND THE EARLY-DATE WINDOW

If a later real-field consumer needs a complete filled read of size
n(D)<=C(1+D)^m and the small-image condition n(D)h<=eta, it must be
checked in addition to (2). For fixed m, (3) and h=L^(-s) imply that
this condition holds for all

              s>=C_m log(2+r),                           (6)

after increasing the fixed C_m and weak floor. To check it directly,
take logarithms: log n(D)<=C+2m log(r+s), while the right-hand small
factor contributes -s log L. The latter eventually dominates, and
the ratio is decreasing beyond a fixed s threshold.

For earlier s, (1) with D=K r^2 still provides a rare estimate, but
no unproved current-image admission is inferred. On the finite
window s<=C_m log(2+r), a factor h^(-4) costs only a fixed power of r.
This is an arithmetic observation about a POSSIBLE position/date
consumer. The actual calibrated count, overlap constants, partner
source and contacts still have to be matched before it is spent.

4. EXACT SCOPE OF THIS PROGRESS

The boundary comparison can therefore be made smaller than a rare
h^4 target without assuming differentiability at coincident mask
corners. The earlier all-D stretched-exponential profile is sufficient;
a globally exponential profile is unnecessary for this particular
finite buffer choice.

What remains is substantial. The central auxiliary source mu_0 F
still needs its own complete coarse/background quadratic-curvature
estimate. The coarse reference, prior/coarse certificate complement,
and changed history must be treated in the original law. A box chosen
separately for each s is an age-dependent analytic decomposition, not
automatically the exact evolution of one previously emitted source.
Its changing baseline, normalizers, raw contacts and complete reads
cannot be ignored. No positive local analytic activity, R ownership,
root-density payment, or all-scale source bound is asserted by (5).
