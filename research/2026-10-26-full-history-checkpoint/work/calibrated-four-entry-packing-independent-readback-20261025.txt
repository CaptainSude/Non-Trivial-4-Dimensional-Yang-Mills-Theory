1. The exact original source is J_a(v)=c_a sum_p v(x_p)h_p with the SAME
frozen c_a=x_n/e_n^C for every profile, history and derivative. With ONE
holomorphic E_a(v), h_a=exp(-E_a)E_mu exp(J_a) and h_a(0)=1,

 Cov(J_a(u),J_a(v))=D^2E_a(0)[u,v]

This retains all first-mean/normalizer and exterior covariance terms.
No nonzero extension of h_a away from zero is needed to define this
second logarithmic germ. Profile-by-profile choices of E would invalidate
the common bilinear form, and are explicitly excluded.

2. A fixed endpoint cell can contain m>=c R^-4 disjoint translated
four-profile patterns of diameter O(R), R=tau/ell_T. Bare-lattice rounding
is harmless when a/tau is small; the cell buffer and shape constants are
fixed. Independent signs of disjoint compact smooth bumps give a union
with supremum O(1) and endpoint Lipschitz constant O(R^-1), independent
of m. The union includes all four pieces before applying the source norm.

The bidisc radius d_R=c r_* R is therefore valid under the explicit
common-source hypothesis. Two-variable Cauchy bounds D^2h by Q_*/d_R^2
and the product of first derivatives by Q_*^2/d_R^2. Averaging the SAME
sign in the positive and reflected profile sums cancels every off-diagonal
translated pair exactly, because the logarithmic germ is bilinear.

The original finite Wilson law is invariant under those bare translations.
The RG scalar E need not be. Thus the result is an average of its actual
translated second jets, not an assertion that each scalar jet is equal.
Dividing by m gives the R^2 factor in (9), with the physical tau^-8
normalization restored. A translated four-pattern need not itself be a
reflection pair about the original plane for this algebra: simultaneous
translation of both entries preserves their original covariance.

The other supplier, averaged D^2E_a minus the homogeneous covariance,

The current homogeneous contact calculation alone does not identify
