RESULT AND SCOPE

There is no proved uniform physical variance floor in this note.
There are two concrete conclusions on the ACTUAL finite-spatial
Hamiltonian, and an exact variational obstruction to using only its
known energy estimates:

(a) A deterministic smooth color displacement has a relatively cheap
    ground-state score, with cost bounded by C g^-2 sum_e |v_e|^2.
    Its mean response to EVERY gauge-invariant observable is exactly
    zero. Making the displacement horizontal by gauge projection
    does not change this response. A field-dependent equivariant
    displacement can have a nonzero response, but the inexpensive
    cross-site score cancellation no longer follows from gauge
    symmetry. The available localized kinetic estimate gives the
    explicit, more expensive bound (3.3).

(b) For the SAME bounded smooth gauge-invariant physical observable F,
    ground-state localization constructs actual smooth, strictly
    positive, gauge-invariant quantum states with arbitrarily small
    Var(F), energy at most E0+C D_Omega(F)/epsilon^2, and F-Dirichlet
    form at most C D_Omega(F). This keeps both the physical probe and
    its normalization fixed. No auxiliary reference variance is used.

In particular, if the desired bound D_Omega(F_a)=O(1) is established,
then at fixed physical spatial volume these states can have
Var(F_a)->0 while retaining the SAME leading extensive magnetic,
electric, and total-energy bounds from TURN66 and a uniform
F_a-Dirichlet upper bound. They are not asserted to be ground states.
This proves that those estimates alone cannot supply the missing
ground-state variance floor. The full ground-state equation, or an
additional actual response/nonconcentration estimate, must do work
beyond the extensive energy budget.

All results below are finite-lattice identities or inequalities.
No extension of a native reference conditional law to the original
ground state, and no Gaussian approximation, is made.

1. ACTUAL LAW, METRIC, AND LOCAL KINETIC BUDGET

Take the finite nondegenerate periodic cubic spatial SU2 lattice
with n sites, 3n positively oriented links, spacing a, and

 H = kappa K+V,
 kappa=g^2/(2a),  K=-Delta,
 V=4 S_sp/(g^2 a).                                      (1.1)

Use the Pauli/2 link metric throughout. The unique normalized positive
ground state Omega is smooth and gauge invariant; write

 rho=Omega^2,  mu(dU)=rho(U)dHaar(U).

The actual TURN66 trial-state argument proved

 E0<=9n/a,
 int |grad Omega|^2<=18n/g^2,
 E_mu S_sp<=(9/4)n g^2.                                 (1.2)

Cubic translations and orientation permutations preserve H, so by
uniqueness they preserve Omega. Consequently EACH link obeys

 int |grad_e Omega|^2 <=6/g^2,
 E_mu |s_e|^2<=24/g^2,
 s_e=grad_e log rho.                                    (1.3)

Here s_e is written in the left-invariant orthonormal frame at e.
For a deterministic set D of links,

 int sum_(e in D)|grad_e Omega|^2<=6|D|/g^2.              (1.4)

This is independent of the size of the exterior. It is an integrated
estimate, not a pointwise score estimate or a product conditional law.
On a rectangular spatial torus the per-orientation budget gives
the weaker kinetic constant18 and score constant72 in (1.3),
and constant18 in (1.4); nothing essential changes.

For real smooth F, define its actual physical Dirichlet form

 D_mu(F)=kappa E_mu |grad F|^2
       =<F Omega,(H-E0)F Omega>.                         (1.5)

The last equality is the ground-state transformation and is exact.
Subtracting E_mu F leaves both sides unchanged.

2. THE CHEAP DETERMINISTIC COLOR SHIFT HAS ZERO PHYSICAL RESPONSE

Let v_e be deterministic elements of su2, and let

 V_v=sum_e v_e . grad_e^L.

The vector field is divergence free for product Haar. Thus its
rho-score is

 sigma_v=-sum_e v_e . s_e,
 E_mu(V_v F)=E_mu(F sigma_v).                            (2.1)

For an edge e=(x,x+hat i), the left score s_e transforms in the
adjoint representation at x. Under an independent gauge rotation
at x, scores rooted at different vertices are unchanged. Adjoint
averaging therefore gives

 E_mu s_e=0,
 E_mu(s_e^A s_f^B)=0 if e,f have different initial vertices. (2.2)

This includes incoming links: right multiplication at their terminal
vertex does not rotate their left-frame scores. There are no self
loops in the admitted lattice.

There are three outgoing positive links per vertex. Cauchy--Schwarz
within each such triple, followed by (1.3), gives the uniform bound

 E_mu sigma_v^2 <=72 g^-2 sum_e |v_e|^2.                 (2.3)

The constant is deliberately not optimized. Adjoint isotropy can
reduce it. The important fact is the sum of squares instead of the
square of a sum across the spatial region.

However, for EVERY gauge-invariant F,

                         E_mu(V_v F)=0.                 (2.4)

Proof: F rho is gauge invariant. The vector grad_e^L F transforms
adjointly at the initial vertex, so its expectation is an invariant
vector in su2 and hence zero. Equivalently, average (2.1) over the
independent gauge rotation at that vertex.

The result applies, in particular, to the flowed radial magnetic
probe and to any prescribed gauge-invariant thick/coarse probe.
For a smooth continuum displacement with link coefficients
v_e=a f_i(x_e), supported in a fixed physical region,
sum |v_e|^2 is of order a^-1. The appealing Fisher cost
O((g^2 a)^-1) is real, but its physical-response numerator is zero.

Nor does orthogonally projecting V_v onto the horizontal tangent
space repair the numerator: the difference is vertical, and every
vertical tangent annihilates F. Thus V_v^hor F=V_v F pointwise
where the projection is defined, and (2.4) still holds.

This is a statement about this deformation, not an assertion that
all gauge-equivariant coarse deformations have zero response.

3. THE EXACT COST OF A FIELD-DEPENDENT EQUIVARIANT DEFORMATION

Let W be a real smooth vector field on the full compact link manifold.
It may be field dependent and gauge equivariant. Its exact score is

 sigma_W=-div W-W log rho,
 E_mu sigma_W=0,
 E_mu(WF)=E_mu[(F-E_mu F)sigma_W].                        (3.1)

The Haar divergence cannot be dropped. Cauchy--Schwarz proves

 Var_mu(F) >= |E_mu(WF)|^2 / E_mu sigma_W^2               (3.2)

when the denominator is positive. These identities hold for the
actual Omega^2 law, without any history or source normalization.

Suppose W is supported on a deterministic set D of m links, and set
v_2^2=sup_U sum_(e in D)|W_e(U)|^2. Then (1.4) gives

 E_mu sigma_W^2
 <=2 E_mu(div W)^2+8 int |W Omega|^2
 <=2 E_mu(div W)^2+48 m v_2^2/g^2.                       (3.3)

The first inequality follows from
W log rho=2 W Omega/Omega. The second is pointwise Cauchy--Schwarz
in the tangent slots, followed by the actual local kinetic budget.
If only an L-infinity divergence bound is known, it can replace its
expectation; a pointwise bound is not required in (3.3).

For a genuinely gauge-equivariant W, each scalar W_e.s_e is gauge
invariant. Independent site gauge rotations consequently do NOT
kill correlations between those scalars at different sites.
Applying (2.3) after substituting field-dependent W_e would be invalid.

For the natural amplitude of a physical connection displacement,
assume |W_e|<=C a on m<=C a^-3 links in a fixed physical region.
Then v_2^2<=C a^-1 and the kinetic term in (3.3) is O(g^-2 a^-4).
Even if a separate construction supplied a nonzero response bounded
below and a harmless divergence, this available estimate would only
certify a lower bound of order g^2 a^4. It would not give a uniform
physical variance floor. This is the scaling of the proved bound,
not a claim that the actual score grows at this rate.

The radial choice W=grad F does make E(WF)=E|grad F|^2 nonnegative.
For the nonlinear flowed probe its divergence and norm contain the
flow's first and second derivatives. The known TURN66 magnetic
energy estimate controls neither this score nor a lower bound on
its response. Calling W a coarse direction does not remove these
terms.

4. EXACT LOCALIZATION IN THE SAME PHYSICAL OBSERVABLE

This section gives a concrete variational test of what the known
energy estimates can imply. Let F be ANY prescribed smooth real
gauge-invariant function with0<=F<=1, and put

                         D=D_mu(F).

For0<epsilon<=1 choose a smooth partition of unity on the real line
in squared form

 sum_j chi_j(t)^2=1,
 supp chi_j subset (j epsilon-epsilon,j epsilon+epsilon),
 sum_j |chi_j'(t)|^2<=C_0 epsilon^-2.                    (4.1)

One explicit construction starts with a fixed nonnegative smooth
bump supported in(-1,1), positive on[-1/2,1/2], translates it at
integer centers, and divides every translate by the square root
of the sum of their squares. Only finitely many translates meet
the range of F. The constant C_0 is numerical and independent of
the graph, F, epsilon, a, and g.

Set

 psi_j=chi_j(F)Omega,  p_j=||psi_j||_2^2.

The exact ground-state identity gives, for p_j>0,

 E_j-E0
 :=<psi_j,H psi_j>/p_j-E0
 =kappa E_mu[chi_j'(F)^2 |grad F|^2]/p_j,               (4.2)

 D_j
 :=kappa int |grad F|^2 psi_j^2/p_j.

Summing before dividing yields

 sum_j p_j=1,
 sum_j p_j(E_j-E0)<=C_0 epsilon^-2 D,
 sum_j p_j D_j=D.                                      (4.3)

There is therefore an index j with p_j>0 for which BOTH

 E_j-E0<=(C_0+1)epsilon^-2 D,
 D_j<=(C_0+1)D.                                        (4.4)

Indeed, choose j for which
(E_j-E0)+epsilon^-2 D_j
does not exceed its p-weighted mean. Each summand is nonnegative.
This avoids selecting a low-energy window whose F-Dirichlet form
has secretly become uncontrolled.

The normalized gauge-invariant state psi_j/sqrt(p_j) is supported
where F lies in an interval of length2epsilon. Hence

              Var_(psi_j^2/p_j)(F)<=epsilon^2.          (4.5)

Equations(4.2)--(4.5) retain exactly the same F, including its
physical smearing/flow time and normalization. Only the quantum
state is changed. The proof uses no reference-field redraw,
selected-sector denominator, or classical configuration estimate.

5. STRICT POSITIVITY AND PHYSICAL-SPACING SCALING

If a strictly positive density is desired, replace psi_j by

 psi^+=(chi_j(F)+delta)Omega,
 delta=epsilon sqrt(p_j),

and normalize in L2. Since chi_j is nonnegative,
||psi^+||_2^2>=p_j. Its ground-energy excess is still bounded by
the first expression in (4.4), because grad(chi_j+delta)=grad chi_j.
Using (chi_j+delta)^2<=2chi_j^2+2delta^2 gives

 D_(psi^+) (F)<=2(C_0+1)D+2epsilon^2 D
               <=2(C_0+2)D.                            (5.1)

Choose the center c=j epsilon of the selected window. Since that
window meets[0,1], |F-c|<=2 for0<epsilon<=1, and on its chi_j
support |F-c|<=epsilon. Therefore

 Var_(psi^+)(F)
 <=E_(psi^+)(F-c)^2<=10epsilon^2.                       (5.2)

The normalized psi^+ is smooth, strictly positive, and gauge
invariant. Thus the examples do not rely on a singular measure,
gauge breaking, a chart boundary, or failure of elliptic regularity.
They are quantum states for the SAME Hamiltonian, not its claimed
ground states.

Now hold a physical cubic side L fixed, with n=(L/a)^3, and take
the SAME proposed physical family F_a. If

                         D_mu_a(F_a)<=D_*              (5.3)

has been established, choose epsilon_a=(a/L)^r for any0<r<2.
Equations(4.4)--(5.2) construct positive gauge-invariant states nu_a
such that

 Var_nu_a(F_a)<=10(a/L)^(2r) ->0,
 D_nu_a(F_a)<=2(C_0+2)D_*,

 <H_a>_nu_a <=9n/a+C D_* (L/a)^(2r)
             =[9+o(1)] n/a.                            (5.4)

Nonnegativity of the two Hamiltonian terms then also gives

 E_nu_a S_sp <=[9/4+o(1)]n g_a^2,
 int |grad sqrt(nu_a)|^2 <=[18+o(1)]n/g_a^2,
 I(nu_a)<= [72+o(1)]n/g_a^2.                            (5.5)

Here nu_a denotes its Haar density where used under the square root.
All error estimates are independent of the rate g_a>0, since it
cancels in comparison with the leading extensive budgets.
The construction works in particular on a logarithmic weak-coupling
sequence. No claim of convergence of nu_a to the ground-state law
is made. Its excitation energy can diverge, despite being negligible
relative to n/a.
Nor are these comparison states asserted to arise as stationary,
reflection-positive original Wilson Euclidean laws. The limitation
concerns the listed energy, state, and Dirichlet estimates; it does
not refute an argument that also uses such additional structures.

This is a decisive limitation on an inference from the currently
known bounds: extensive energy, magnetic energy, electric energy,
gauge invariance, positivity, and EVEN a uniformly bounded physical
F-Dirichlet form do not force a uniform Var(F) floor. A proof for
Omega must use additional information particular to the minimizer.

6. WHAT THIS LEAVES AS A CONCRETE GROUND-STATE TARGET

For an actual smooth equivariant physical deformation W_a, a
genuine uncertainty proof would establish, in the ORIGINAL ground
state and at the prescribed normalization,

 |E_mu_a W_a F_a|>=r_*>0,
 E_mu_a[-div W_a-2 W_a log Omega_a]^2<=C_*<infinity.      (6.1)

Then(3.2) would give the desired floor. Neither numerator nor
denominator is replaced by a variance in a native fresh reference
field. Equations(2.4),(3.3),(5.4)--(5.5) identify three specific
reasons the currently available estimates do not verify(6.1):
the deterministic response vanishes, the general equivariant
score bound has the wrong physical scaling, and the extensive
energy budget allows concentration of this same physical F.

This is not an assertion that every route requires(6.1). In
particular a direct lower bound on a separated ground-state
correlation, or a quantitative minimizer-specific coarse response,
could avoid it. The new Hamiltonian energy and local marked-energy
estimates remain useful for the upper-gradient task. They do not
yet constitute the nontriviality input for a YM4 existence proof.
