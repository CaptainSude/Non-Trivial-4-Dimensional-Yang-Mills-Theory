 ||E||_(N;a,b)=sup_i sum_(X contains i) exp(a|X|)||E_X||_b,

and quotient by equality of the normalized total
Phi_E(w)=sum_X E_X(w)-sum_X E_X(0) on the entire b polydisc.
Its invariant real subspace consists of totals with the local gauge,
translation, hypercubic and color identities. Individual coefficients
need not be invariant. The fixed weight a and inner radius b are the
ones in the archived classical contraction theorem.

TURN48's strict joint (w,xi) catalogue maps an admissible family at
this weight and radius to an output family at the SAME prescribed
weight and radius after the parameter choices stated there. Whitening
does not add undeclared reads: remote xi and datum reads are included.
The complete old argument remains strictly inside the old b ball.
This strict inclusion is the reason the following quotient descent
is an actual identity.

If Phi_E=Phi_E', their raw totals differ by a constant c throughout
that ball. Hence
E(U_pre(w,gKxi))-E(V_p(w)) is unchanged pointwise. The inherited
background changes by c. The base action, P,K,J and the window are
independent of the inherited input and stay fixed. The exact
integrals therefore differ by exp(c) only, and their normalized
logarithms agree. Thus the new F_j,g=T_j+G_j,g, its T_j and its G_j,g
descend to this finite quotient. Real and spatial/gauge identities
are precisely those already established for the actual TURN48 map.

For two classes x,y in a quarter of the family ceiling epsilon_bar,
choose near-minimal representatives Y of y and D of x-y, then use
X=Y+D. Their norms stay below epsilon_bar. Linear inherited
composition and the connected logarithm Cauchy estimate yield

 ||G_j,g(x)-G_j,g(y)|| <= C_Q(gT/b)||x-y||.              (10.1)

Sending the near-minimal error to zero proves the quotient bound.
No common or continuous choice of minimizing representative is
needed. All constants are uniform in M and compatible volume at
fixed R,J and radii.

The complete zero-input G_j,g(0)=Dphys+F_g has a uniform outer-radius
catalogue bound H_* for weak g. This uses the full log A0 catalogue
and (4.2). Subtract its constant and linear coefficient sectors
before restricting to b<=rho/2. The total linear sector vanishes
by semisimplicity. Therefore

                  ||G_j,g(0)||<=C_F b^2,
                  C_F=2H_*/rho^2,                     (10.2)

after an inessential fixed norm conversion. It is crucial that this
bound is O(b^2), not a claim that the full force is O(g^2).

10.4 Finite continuity for the new window.

Fix one finite regulator and one admissible input representative.
The new normalized expectation is a finite integral over the
FIXED compact product color balls with the fixed product Gaussian
measure muT. The base exponent, J and inherited arguments depend
continuously on positive real g, uniformly over the full output

margins; bounded holomorphy of the inherited input on b suffices.
No extension of an arbitrary activity beyond its original radius

Consequently the integral varies in H-infinity(b) by uniform

catalogue supplies a uniform finite-volume bound on its normalized
log. Its nonzero value at w=0 and that log bound give a positive
lower bound on the modulus throughout the entire b ball. Nearby

||[h]||<=exp(a N_cells)||h||_infinity. Thus the new F_j,g is

finite argument needs neither a volume-uniform continuity modulus
nor continuous representative choices. With continuous T(g), first
dilate the integral to unit color balls; the same proof applies.

10.5 Actual finite endpoint conclusion.

Choose q a multiple of q_a as in Section 8, then enlarge N0 and
decrease g_w to satisfy both the amplitude and positive-drift
requirements. Let B_-=delta/4, B_+=qD_abs, and choose
Gamma_*>=g_w^-2+qD_abs. For every finite m and compatible final
period N_end>=N0, the new selected local maps admit a real starting
inverse coupling in

 [Gamma_*+mB_-, Gamma_*+mB_+]

whose exact extraction trajectory ends at Gamma_* after mq steps.
All its physical baseline couplings remain weak and all activities
remain in the same fixed norm/radius envelope.

Proof. Run the continuous auxiliary evaluation at
g_eval(gamma)=max(gamma,g_w^-2)^-1/2, keeping the scalar decrement
unclipped. The amplitude induction applies to every auxiliary
history because every evaluated coupling is weak. At the lower
endpoint of the interval the total decrement is at least mB_-,
so the final gamma is <=Gamma_*; at the upper endpoint it is
>=Gamma_* by the upper bound. Continuity gives equality somewhere.
For this history, partition the future of any intermediate stage
into complete q-blocks plus fewer than q remaining steps. The
complete blocks have positive decrement by (8.3)-(8.4), which use
only the uniform intermediate envelope. The leftover decrement
is bounded below by -qD_abs. Hence gamma_j>=Gamma_*-qD_abs>=g_w^-2
at every stage. Clipping was inactive, so this is the actual update
(10.4). The same argument tolerates an initial extracted activity
inside the stated endpoint ball.

This is a finite, homogeneous, local selected-sector trajectory at
every requested finite length, with g_bare of order m^-1/2 at fixed
terminal inverse coupling. This section does not establish compatibility
or convergence of the tuned choices as m varies, preservation of a
uniform original-source disc, estimates for all label histories,
or a nontrivial continuum limit. The constant original-action source
disc is supplied separately by the companion note named above.
