1. EXACT INTERPOLATION ON NESTED GRIDS

First work on Z^d with an integer scale R>=2. For a coarse vertex
function f define its tensor multilinear extension to R^d by

  F(x)=sum_(z in Z^d) f(z) prod_(mu=1)^d hat(x_mu-z_mu),
  hat(t)=max(1-|t|,0).

At most 2^d weights are nonzero, all are nonnegative, and their sum
is1. Set

                    (I_R f)(m)=F(m/R), m in Z^d.              (1.1)

The same definition applies to a periodic torus by periodizing the
weights. It also applies to nested affine grids after using their
actual common aligned coordinates. Alignment is an assumption:
an arbitrary change of block centers is not silently identified
with a nested vertex hierarchy.

SUP NORM. Convexity of the complex modulus immediately gives
||I_R f||infinity<=||f||infinity. In particular this is valid for
complex source values and introduces no source-radius shrink.

SEMIGROUP. Suppose a grid of spacing1/S is inserted between the
integer grid and its spacing1/(RS) refinement. On each integer
unit cube F is affine in each coordinate separately. Every finer
aligned cube lies inside one such cube. Multilinear interpolation
from the values of F at that finer cube's vertices therefore
reproduces F throughout that cube. Evaluating on the finest grid
proves I_R I_S=I_(RS), with the order interpreted as coarse-to-fine
prolongation. The identity is unchanged on periodic compatible
grids, at grid faces, or under iteration.

SPATIAL SYMMETRIES. Tensor interpolation commutes with translations
by coarse vertices and with signed permutations of the coordinate
axes, including their induced action on the refined grid. Its use
therefore preserves joint scalar-source/field covariance under these
symmetries. Matching this aligned source grid to the actual ordinary
mean's chosen lattice is part of the geometric input; no independent
change of the physical mean or field lift is proposed.

2. EXACT HOLDER CONTRACTION AND THE ROOT COUNT

Use distance |x-y|_1. Suppose
|f(z)-f(z')|<=[f]_alpha |z-z'|_1^alpha on the coarse grid.
Let U_1,...,U_d be independent uniforms on[0,1], and put

  Z(x)_mu=floor(x_mu+U_mu).

Then E f(Z(x))=F(x). Coupling Z(x),Z(y) with the SAME uniforms
gives, coordinate by coordinate,

  E |Z(x)_mu-Z(y)_mu|=|x_mu-y_mu|.

This uses monotonicity of floor and E floor(x+U)=x. Hence,
by the triangle inequality and concavity for 0<alpha<=1,

 |F(x)-F(y)|
 <=[f]_alpha E|Z(x)-Z(y)|_1^alpha
 <=[f]_alpha |x-y|_1^alpha.                            (2.1)

Putting x=m/R,y=m'/R proves the Holder bound in (R1).
For a torus, choose shortest coordinate lifts and use periodic f;
the geodesic grid distance is no larger than the lifted distance.
Thus the same conclusion holds. No differentiability of f is needed.

Write w_R(m,z)=prod_mu hat(m_mu/R-z_mu). Besides the row identity,
one has exactly

  sum_z w_R(m,z)=1,       sum_m w_R(m,z)=R^d.           (2.2)

For the second identity in one dimension,

  sum_(m in Z) hat(m/R)
     =1+2 sum_(m=1)^(R-1)(1-m/R)=R.

Tensor products give (2.2), also on compatible periodic grids when
coincident labels are combined with their actual weights.

Consequences for source carriers:

* A term f_m E_(m,X) becomes sum_z w_R(m,z) f_z E_(m,X).
  The sum of coefficient absolute values does not acquire a factor
  2^d: the weights sum to1.
* Each new source root z is in a fixed coarse-cell collar of m/R.
  Adding it to a complete classical/quantum read carrier costs only
  that fixed collar and its declared connected hull.
* A supremum over coarse incidence roots still sees R^d fine cells.
  For example a spatially constant per-fine-vertex scalar density
  is multiplied by R^d. Thus an unweighted rooted activity norm
  is NOT contracted solely by source interpolation.

The final point is indispensable in d=4. The field gain/root balance
in history47's classical estimate remains a separate requirement.

3. A LITERAL FIXED PHYSICAL SOURCE, INCLUDING PLAQUETTE OFFSETS

             f_j=I_R^(n-j) f_n,        0<=j<=n.       (3.1)

No field-strength normalization or comparison with a different

This is not a replacement of a composed ordinary mean by a direct

5. A CONTROLLED SPATIAL CURRENT FOR AN ORDINARY NULL TOTAL

The remaining distinction between an ordinary and augmented quotient
can be made exact without an uncontrolled all-arity Taylor expansion.

Let a_(x,X)(w_X) be analytic on radius b_s, with a complete connected
carrier X containing source vertex x. Suppose

        sum_(x,X) a_(x,X)(w) is independent of w.     (5.1)

Assume its absolute rooted norm at exponent a_s is K. Let
0<b_w<b_s and 0<a_w<a_s be fixed. Each current cell has a fixed
finite number D of scalar background coordinates. For such a
coordinate q choose its cell vertex y(q). Differentiating (5.1)
gives sum_(x,X) partial_q a_(x,X)=0.

For arbitrary numerical f, including independent complex values,
radial integration therefore gives the exact identity

 sum_(x,X) f_x[a_(x,X)(w)-a_(x,X)(0)]
   =sum_(x,X) sum_(q in X)(f_x-f_(y(q))) I_(x,X,q)(w),

 I_(x,X,q)(w)=int_0^1 w_q partial_q a_(x,X)(t w)dt.    (5.2)

Cauchy gives ||I_(x,X,q)||_(b_w)
 <=b_w K_(x,X)/(b_s-b_w). Choose a deterministic path inside
the connected cell carrier, with a fixed collar if necessary,
from x to y(q). It has at most C_d|X| edges. Telescope
f_x-f_(y(q)) along this path and collect coefficients to obtain

       sum_(oriented cell edges e) (f_e+-f_e-) J_e(w). (5.3)

Every current coefficient retains the original carrier X and the
edge it uses. At most D|X| coordinates and C_d|X| path edges
are charged. The fixed exponent reserve pays |X|^2, proving

          ||J||_(a_w,b_w) <= C K.                   (5.4)

Constants depend only on fixed margins and local geometry, not
volume or original fine depth. Applying the Holder modulus
directly in (5.2) instead gives

 ||sum f_x[a_(x,X)(w)-a_(x,X)(0)]||_weak
                  <=C ell^alpha [f]_alpha K.         (5.5)

This is a bounded spatial-divergence representation of the
modulation of a null total. It retains the true f-dependent
vacuum separately. The proof differentiates once and integrates;
there is no hidden factorial growth from monomials of arbitrary
degree and no repeated source-radius loss.

Gauge scope is essential. The constructed J_e need not be
individually gauge invariant. If the original augmented total is
gauge invariant for every f, its divergence in (5.3) has that
invariance, but this does not prove invariant current coefficients.
Nor does it produce a bounded right inverse from the ordinary
gauge-invariant quotient to the augmented one. A topological
or gauge-local cohomology issue is not solved by this spatial
telescoping identity.

6. WHAT THIS CHANGES, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

The current-grid interpolation is an exact physical source choice,
not an approximation which accumulates with RG depth. It removes
the need to keep M^4 independent source arguments as independent
variables at every new scale, while retaining their actual
coefficients, offsets, and volume balance. A common complex source
sup disc is preserved. Constant-source evaluation protects the
scalar marginal without any ambiguous near-minimal modulation.

This supplies a concrete domain and normalization interface for
root's neutral-scalar-source finite-jet analysis and for the
classical strict-support estimates under development. The missing
step is still a uniform augmented classical/quantum induction on
the actual gauge-invariant source quotient, with the required
relocalization and carrier bounds. One-step O(g^2) response or
zero scalar marginal alone does not imply that induction.

Even an eventual selected-source induction would not automatically
give the full original-law regular-channel covariance, control its
remaining histories, or prove a nonzero continuum OS norm. Those
are separate requirements of the YM4 existence objective.
