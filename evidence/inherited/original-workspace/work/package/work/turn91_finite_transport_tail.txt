TURN91 -- FINITE ORIGINAL WILSON TRANSPORTS AND THE OUTER RESPONSE
21 October 2026. tail47. Finite original-law calculations.

STATUS

No physical variance floor or continuum Yang--Mills construction is
proved. Three concrete conclusions are established below.

(i) For EVERY finite-entropy change of the exact Wilson/HS joint law,
the response of the physical two-window average differs from its
interacting auxiliary-marginal response by at most sqrt(D_cond/n).
This includes deformations moving the auxiliary record, and not just
fixed-record moves. Bounded entropy cannot produce an order-one
response entirely from conditional link noise.

(ii) An explicit triangular, tree-framed finite SU2 transport has
exact Haar Jacobian one. It eliminates the action change on the tree
faces, but the actual Wilson conditional mixing of long transverse
chords gives a bulk entropy cost proportional to beta theta^2 times
the number of moved links. At theta=g a on a fixed physical torus,
this cost diverges like a^-2. This is a calculation for this particular
frame construction, not a prohibition of all adapted transports.

(iii) Full four-dimensional Wilson flow is a legitimate different
deformation: its temporal read is not confined to one slice, so the
slice-gradient score statements do not automatically apply. Its exact
Jacobian/action formula is given in Section5. Available unmarked
energy estimates do not yet control the required cancellation or the
response of the prescribed original probe.

The current target remains the same original bounded spatial-flow
observable and one common original Wilson sequence. This note does
not change its spatial flow, g_*, temporal calibration or RP support.

1. LAW, TARGET AND INPUTS

Let mu(dU)=Z^-1 exp[-beta S(U)]dHaar(U) on a nondegenerate periodic
four-dimensional SU2 lattice, all periods at least4, beta>0,
 S=sum_p h_p, h_p=1-w(U_p), w(V)=Re Tr(V)/2.
All four-dimensional plaquettes, including temporal plaquettes, occur
in S. The Pauli/2 basis i sigma_c/2 is orthonormal.

Let phi_t in[0,1] be the prescribed original gauge-invariant spatial-
flow slice observable. Its complete read lies on slice t, possibly on
the whole spatial slice. Set
 G_n=n^-1 sum_(0<=t<n)phi_t-n^-1 sum_(n<=t<2n)phi_t, 2n<=N. (1.1)
The physical window L=na remains in a fixed positive interval. The
unproved target is Var_mu(G_n)>=v>0. TURN89 supplies the physical-band
and buffered reflected-norm consumer for that target. No variance
floor is inserted in any calculation below.

Use the exact positive HS disintegration already proved in TURN81:
 Pi(dU,dxi)=mu(dU)kappa_U(dxi)=nu(dxi)k_xi(dU).             (1.2)
Given the entire real xi, ALL original links are independent. Thus
the 2n slice observables in(1.1) are independent under k_xi. Write
 m(xi)=C G_n=E_(k_xi)G_n.

The second source input, used only in Section4, is the actual original
private-edge conditional Haar theorem from
 physical_coarse_variance_turn66.txt Sections1--5 and
 temporal_filter_physical_probe_turn67.txt Sections2--3:
for an edge-simple loop and m sparse private edges, no two sharing a
plaquette, the conditional law of its holonomy, given all other links,
obeys
 ||Law(C|outside I)-Haar||_L1
 <=epsilon_m=C(1+beta)^(3/4) exp[-c(m-1)/(1+beta)].        (1.3)
The constants are uniform in the conditioned original links. A greedy
selection along z distinct edges gives m>=z/19. Outside tests may read
all other links but must not read I. No independent-plaquette model or
selected-history law is used.

Specifically, finite_volume_wilson_energy_turn64.txt(0.2) gives
 E exp(beta h_p/4)<=2^(3/4).
Jensen therefore gives E beta h_p<=3 log2<8. The harmless loose bound
implies, for EVERY orientation on the rectangular torus,
 c_p=E w(U_p) obeys 1-8/beta<=c_p<=1.                    (1.4)
No equality between different orientations on a rectangular torus is
needed below; translations give equality within each orientation.
We use beta>=16 when a positive lower bound for these means is needed.

2. FINITE ENTROPY: THE INTERACTING MARGINAL CANNOT BE OMITTED

Conditional Hoeffding, with 2n ranges of length1/n, gives exactly
 E_(k_xi) exp[s(G_n-m(xi))]<=exp[s^2/(4n)], s real.        (2.1)
This is uniform in xi and in the original cutoff, volume and beta.

Let Q be ANY probability absolutely continuous with respect to Pi,
with finite relative entropy. Disintegrate
 Q(dU,dxi)=nu_Q(dxi)q_xi(dU),
 D_cond=int D(q_xi||k_xi)nu_Q(dxi).
The chain rule reads
 D(Q||Pi)=D(nu_Q||nu)+D_cond.                             (2.2)
Apply the entropy variational inequality to q_xi versus k_xi, then
integrate, using(2.1), with a common s>0. Applied to both signs it yields
 |E_Q G_n-E_(nu_Q)m|<=D_cond/s+s/(4n).
Optimization proves
 |E_Q G_n-E_(nu_Q)m|<=sqrt(D_cond/n).                    (2.3)
For D_cond=0 this follows directly or by s downarrow0.

Consequently the ORIGINAL mean displacement decomposes as
 E_QG_n-E_muG_n
 = [E_(nu_Q)m-E_nu m] + error,
 |error|<=sqrt(D_cond/n).                                (2.4)
An order-one response under bounded total relative entropy therefore
requires an order-one response of the interacting conditional mean
under the changed auxiliary marginal. Formula(2.4) does not bound that
outer response by o(1). The general Pinsker bound, where applicable,
does not supply the missing lower bound either.

This applies to original finite transports and their fixed-innovation
lifts: xi may move, so nu_Q need not equal nu. It is strictly broader
than the fixed-xi score obstruction. Conversely, if a proposed finite
transport keeps nu unchanged, then
 |E_QG_n-E_muG_n|<=sqrt(D(Q||Pi)/n).                      (2.5)

There is also a literal finite-displacement statement for a map
 (U,xi) -> (T_xi U,xi)
whose output law Q has finite entropy D. The absolute-value MGF from
(2.1) is at most2 exp[s^2/(4n)]. Therefore
 E_Q|G_n-m|<=sqrt((D+log2)/n),
 E_Pi|G_n-m|<=1/sqrt(2n),
 E_Pi|G_n(T_xiU)-G_n(U)|
 <=sqrt((D+log2)/n)+1/sqrt(2n).                          (2.6)
The second inequality uses the actual conditional variance bound
E Var(G_n|xi)<=1/(2n). In particular, a fixed-record finite map of
uniformly bounded entropy cannot move G_n by a fixed positive amount
on a set of probability bounded below. This is a two-event restriction
on that transport, NOT an upper bound on Var_mu(G_n).

3. A SIMPLE EXACT FINITE ACTION COST

For orientation mu=1, multiply only the positively oriented mu-links:
 U_e -> exp(i theta_e sigma3) U_e,
where the theta_e are deterministic and |theta_e|<=1. All other links
are unchanged. This product translation preserves Haar and is invertible,
so its output law has exact relative entropy
 D(T_*mu||mu)=beta E_mu[S(TU)-S(U)].                      (3.1)

Independent original vertex-gauge averages at the two distinct starting
vertices of the mu-links of a mu-nu plaquette give
 E w(U_p(TU))=cos(theta_e)cos(theta_e') c_p.
This uses invariance of the ORIGINAL law. It does not replace a noisy
plaquette by its flat value. Therefore
 D=beta sum_(p containing mu)
                  c_p [1-cos(theta_e)cos(theta_e')].    (3.2)
For |theta|,|phi|<=1,
 (theta^2+phi^2)/6<=1-cos(theta)cos(phi)
                              <=(theta^2+phi^2)/2.
Each mu-link belongs to six such plaquettes. From(1.4), beta>=16 gives
 (beta/2)sum_e theta_e^2 <= D <=3beta sum_e theta_e^2.    (3.3)
Spatial smoothness of the deterministic angles does not turn this cost
into a difference-of-angles action: unframed color directions at
different vertices have been independently gauge averaged.

For theta_e=g_a a v(x_e), with a nonzero fixed smooth physical profile
and beta g_a^2 comparable to1, the cost is of order a^-2 in four
dimensions. This is not a bound on every gauge-framed transport. The
next section tests one such frame with its exact Jacobian retained.

4. A TRIANGULAR TREE FRAME: ZERO TREE COST, LARGE CHORD COST

Take periods N0,N1,R,R in directions0,1,2,3 respectively. For each
plane (t,x1), construct the comb tree on its transverse R by R torus:
from root(0,0), first follow direction2 along the bottom row without
wrapping, then direction3 up the column without wrapping. Denote the
root-to-x parallel transport by P_x. It uses ONLY links in directions
2 and3. Let H_(t,x1) be any fixed Pauli unit axis and put
 K_x=P_x^-1 exp(i theta H_(t,x1))P_x,
 T_theta U_1(x)=K_x U_1(x), other directions unchanged.  (4.1)
Use one common |theta|<=1. Frames depend only on unchanged links, so
this is an invertible triangular Haar-preserving transformation:
conditional on all direction2,3 links, it is a product of fixed left
translations of direction1 links. Thus(3.1) is still exact, including
every temporal plaquette and the full partition normalization.

4A. Independent root-axis averages are legitimate for this calculation.
Under an arbitrary original vertex gauge g,
 P_x(U^g)=g_root P_x(U)g_x^-1,
 T_H(U^g)=(T_(Ad(g_root^-1)H)U)^g.
The Wilson measure and action are gauge invariant. Distinct plane roots
are distinct vertices. Hence E[S(T_H U)-S(U)] is independent of each
root-axis direction. It may be computed by averaging these axes
independently and uniformly. This is an equality of expected costs for
the fixed-axis maps, not a lower bound on the entropy of their mixture.

4B. Exact tree and chord formula.
For a direction1-nu plaquette, nu=2 or3, with x and y=x+nu on the
same transverse plane, write its old holonomy as P, and set
 A=P_x P P_x^-1, C=P_x U_nu(x)P_y^-1.
Its new Wilson trace is
 w(exp(i theta H) A C exp(-i theta H) C^-1).
If (x,y) is a tree edge, C=I and the trace is unchanged pointwise.
For an arbitrary chord, uniform axis averaging in SU2 gives
 E_H[h_p(T_HU)-h_p(U)]
 =(4/3)sin^2(theta) [w(A)-w(AC)w(C^-1)].                 (4.2)
Indeed averaging conjugation by exp(i theta H) multiplies the vector
part of any quaternion by (1+2cos(2theta))/3. In particular the absolute
value of(4.2) is at most(8/3)sin^2(theta), without any weak-field event.

For a nonwrapping direction2 chord at height z, with x2<R-1, cancel
the shared bottom stem in C. The remaining loop is an edge-simple
rectangle of width1 and height z. The far vertical branch at x2+1 has
z edges, none read by A: A uses the near tree branch, the local
direction1-2 plaquette, and the shared stem. Select m>=z/19 sparse
edges on the far branch. Conditional on their complement, A is fixed
and(1.3) applies to the rectangle holonomy. The SU2 Schur identity is
 int w(A C)w(C^-1)dC=w(A)/4.
Conjugating by the fixed bottom stem does not alter the statement.
It follows, after the full original expectation, that
 E[w(A)-w(AC)w(C^-1)] >=(3/4)c_p-epsilon_m.               (4.3)
Choose an integer z0 so epsilon_m<=1/8 whenever z>=z0. One may take
 z0=ceil{19[1+(1+beta)c^-1 log(max(2,8C(1+beta)^(3/4)))]}.
Thus z0=O((1+beta)log(2+beta)), with the fixed constants of(1.3).
For beta>=16, the right side of(4.3) is at least1/4. Every such bulk
chord therefore has expected action cost at least sin^2(theta)/3.

4C. All other faces and the global entropy lower bound.
Nonwrapping direction3 edges are tree edges and cost exactly zero.
The direction2 chords of height z<z0, including any tree edges, may
be bounded below by -(8/3)sin^2(theta) each. There are at most
(R-1)z0 of them per transverse plane. The wrapping direction2 and
direction3 chords contribute at most2R further possibly negative
terms, with the same bound. There are (R-1)(R-z0) good chords.

Temporal direction0-1 plaquettes connect two distinct transverse
planes. Independent root-axis averaging gives cos^2(theta) w(P)
for their new trace; their expected cost is c_p sin^2(theta)>=0.
No temporal plaquette is discarded. Faces not containing direction1
are unchanged.

Summing the preceding bounds, the cost per plane is at least
 (sin^2(theta)/3)[R(R-1)-9(R-1)z0-16R].
In particular, if R>=128 and z0<=R/36, it is at least
 sin^2(theta)R^2/6. Together with(3.1),
 D((T_theta)_*mu||mu)
 >= (beta sin^2(theta)/6) N0 N1 R^2.                    (4.4)
The per-face absolute estimates also give
 D((T_theta)_*mu||mu)<=8 beta sin^2(theta)N0 N1 R^2.     (4.5)

For fixed physical side lengths, R,N0,N1 comparable to a^-1, and
polylogarithmic beta, z0/R tends to0. At theta=g_a a v with fixed
nonzero v and beta g_a^2 comparable to1, both bounds are of order
a^-2. The frame cancels every tree-face cost but its long transverse
chords are not uniformly coherent under the actual Wilson law.

This result excludes a bounded-KL certificate for this specified
triangular comb-frame move at that amplitude. It does not prove that
the move changes G_n by order one. It also does NOT bound the entropy
of a randomized-axis mixture from below, nor exclude gauge-covariant
transports using different frames, changes of transverse links,
field-dependent amplitudes, nontrivial Jacobians or auxiliary motion.
The private-loop estimate is used as an ORIGINAL conditional theorem,
not as a claim that the flowed probe itself is a thin Wilson loop.

5. FULL FOUR-DIMENSIONAL FLOW IS A DISTINCT DEFORMATION

This section is a precise scope check, not a claimed inexpensive
transport. Define the finite-lattice gradient flow Phi_tau by
 dU/dtau=-grad S(U).                                     (5.1)
It is a global smooth diffeomorphism for every finite positive or
negative tau on the compact product of groups. This convention fixes
all constants below; a physical flow time s corresponds, up to the
chosen fixed conventional factor, to tau=s/a^2.

The group Laplacian gives Delta h_p=3w(U_p), since each of the four
links contributes Casimir3/4. Therefore
 Delta S=3(P-S), P=number of plaquettes.
Liouville's formula and action dissipation give the exact identities
 log Jac_Haar Phi_tau(U)=-3 int_0^tau [P-S(Phi_r U)]dr,
 S(Phi_tau U)-S(U)=-int_0^tau |grad S(Phi_r U)|^2 dr,
 D((Phi_tau)_*mu||mu)
 =int_0^tau E_mu[3(P-S(Phi_r U))
                     -beta|grad S(Phi_r U)|^2]dr.       (5.2)
At tau=0 the integrand has expectation zero by the actual Wilson
integration-by-parts identity. The entropy is nonnegative as a whole;
one cannot retain the positive Jacobian term and drop the negative
action term as if that produced a sharp physical cost. Nor does the
unmarked action MGF estimate their cancellation at tau~a^-2.

More generally, let Q(U)=q(Phi_tau U), where q is a bounded smooth
gauge-invariant four-dimensional smoothed action functional. In
covariant differential notation J_tau=D Phi_tau,
 grad Q=J_tau^* grad q(Phi_tau U),
 Hess Q(v,w)=Hess q(J_tau v,J_tau w)
                    +<grad q,(nabla D Phi_tau)(v,w)>.   (5.3)
The genuine score of X=grad Q is beta<grad S,grad Q>-Delta Q,
including both terms in(5.3). Finite flow has a full spacetime read;
it is not a sum of independent slice-local vector fields. Thus the
slice-local score tridiagonality does not apply without a new argument.
The general TURN90 coarse Hessian certificate still applies, and is
still insufficient for the chosen G_n. Keeping its exact signed terms
instead needs actual marked bounds for J_tau, its second variation,
and their correlation with the ORIGINAL law and G_n.

The deterministic Hessian bound |Hess S|<=6 Id only gives
 ||J_tau||<=exp(6|tau|), which is unusable at fixed physical s as
a downarrow0. This does not say that typical derivatives grow at that
rate. It identifies what the existing bound fails to estimate.

A four-dimensional flowed generator can be used for a deformation
without changing the half-local original observable: the RP support
condition belongs to G_n and its reflected test, not to an auxiliary
integration-by-parts vector field. However, such a generator has not
yet supplied either a uniformly controlled true entropy/score cost
or an order-one response. By(2.4), any successful bounded-entropy
construction must in particular move the interacting mean channel.

6. CHANGE OF NEXT ACTION AND LIMITS

The conditional part of a finite transport is quantitatively settled
by(2.3): at physical n~a^-1 it contributes at most O(sqrt(a D_cond)).
One should calculate the OUTER response and its true cost for a
candidate that moves the auxiliary record, or retain cancellations in
the original exact score. Repeating fixed-record noise deformations
cannot produce the desired response at bounded entropy.

The tree calculation supplies a tested alternative to the cheapest
fixed-color move: exact gauge framing and Jacobian one alone do not
make its physical cost finite. The missing input is not repaired by
declaring a thin framed chord smooth. More adaptive frames or full
four-dimensional flow remain legitimate, but their marked correlated
cost is an additional estimate, not an unmarked energy consequence.

None of these statements is an upper bound on the full physical
variance, an obstruction to Yang--Mills existence, a spectral-band
lower bound, or a new scalar continuum construction.
