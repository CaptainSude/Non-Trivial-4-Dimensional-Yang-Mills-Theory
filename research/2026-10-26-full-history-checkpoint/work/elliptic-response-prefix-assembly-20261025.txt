2. STRONG RIGHT INVERSE

Use native cell-volume bilinear pairings. Normalize the coarse Q row to
include its L^j eta factor, so QH0=I. Set

 A=Delta_a=Delta_raw+DRD*+Q*a_Q Q,
 G0=A^{-1}, W=(QG0Q*)^{-1}, H0=G0Q*W,
 G_Q=G0-G0Q*WQG0.

Define E_U lambda(b)=lambda(b_-)+Ad_{U(b)}lambda(b_+), T=E_U G'RD*,
and, for independent J,

 2<X,S_JY>=<i[TX,Y],J>,
 S_JY=(i/2)DRG'E_U^*[Y,J].

The commutator has weight -4; DRG' maps it to weight -3. Thus
||S_JY||_(-3)<=C||J||_(-3)||Y||_(-1), without differentiating J.
Two-ended kernels have a fixed exponential majorant. For a small fixed
current cap the series

 Hhat=(I-G_Q S_J)^{-1}H0

converges in an exponentially weighted kernel norm, with both row and
column Schur bounds. The leftmost G_Q supplies all four strong slots:
field, covariant first difference, P1, and covariant Laplacian.
QHhat=I at s=1. For physical H, B102(137)--(138) gives
A H=Q*Lambda_phys+S_JH and QH=I. Applying G_Q proves the same contraction
equation, so Hhat=H on that physical graph. No off-graph Landau identity
is assumed.

3. BOUNDED RAW NONLINEAR REMAINDER

Set

 Lambda=W[I-QG0 S_J Hhat],
 L_Q=Q*Lambda-Q*a_Q+S_JHhat.

Exactly L_Q=(Delta_raw+DRD*)Hhat. Use the bounded displayed formula
to estimate and localize it; do not estimate its second-derivative
representation separately. Physically Hhat=H and RD*H=0, hence
L_Q=Delta_raw H.

Let C_U be the nonlinear averaging residual, starting at degree two.
For A' in the reserved field/C1 ball solve

 D(A')=C_U(A'-Hhat D(A')),
 Z=A'-Hhat D(A'), D3=D-C_U^(2).

The native contraction and derivative kernels give D=O(r^2),
D'^*=O(r), D3=O(r^3), D3'^*=O(r^2). Define

 Vhat(A')=-<Hhat D3,J>-<A',L_QD>
          +(1/2)<D,Hhat*L_QD>+V0(Z).                 (1)

B102 p297 explicitly uses the RAW-Delta version of functional(74)
in its alternative equation. At a physical background (1) agrees with
that raw remainder for ALL A', so its unrestricted derivatives agree.
Equality merely on the Landau subspace would have been insufficient.

Off-graph Hhat*L_Q need not be symmetric. The exact gradient is

 Vhat'=-D3'^*Hhat*J-L_QD-D'^*L_Q*A'
       +D'^*Sym(Hhat*L_Q)D+(I-D'^*Hhat*)V0'(Z).       (2)

Two-ended weighted kernels bound Hhat,L_Q and their transposes. The
local plaquette integration by parts B102(90)--(96) bounds V0' using
first derivatives only. Therefore, on ||A'||_{field,C1}<=r,

 ||Vhat'(A')||_(-3)<=Cr^2,
 ||Vhat'(A')-Vhat'(B')||_(-3)<=Cr||A'-B'||_{field,C1}. (3)

This avoids confusing the componentwise Laplacian with the raw action

4. CORRECTED SEED AND RESPONSE

Define the symmetric quadratic current operator E by polarization:

 <A',E A'>=2<Hhat C_U^(2)(A'),J>.

Its full read includes the Hhat*J branch. It is small in the native

The G0E Neumann series and coarse Schur inverse give strong and local
kernel bounds. Solve

 Aresp(B)=KB+a-Hhat D(KB+a).                          (4)

After choosing the B cap, (3) is a contraction in a strong ball of
radius C||B||^2. Thus a is holomorphic, a=O(||B||^2), and

 max(||Aresp||,||nabla_U Aresp||,
     ||P1 Aresp||,||Delta_U Aresp||)<=C||B||.         (5)

At s=1, QHhat=I, QK=I and QG_c=0 give exact averaging straightening.

elliptic, scalar, coarse inverse and correction coefficient into its

 G_c,s=sum_{n>=0}(G_Q,s E_s)^n G_Q,s,

unweakened Schur-complement equalities for independently weakened
