The actual R family retains its independent unmarked g^P bound at the
actual history. In the gradient ledger write exactly
R(actual)=R(homogeneous anchored)+Delta R and store Delta R among the

bookkeeping view of the same action, not an additional interaction.

On the enlarged tau disc Delta R need not retain a g^P bound. It may be
as large as the reserved E0 local-action budget. Hence the printed
R bound cannot simply be invoked there. What is needed is the absolute
component estimate for a vacuum-subtracted marginal family with a bounded
age-summed amplitude. Once that estimate is supplied, a healed component
has its original exp[-c p0(g_k)] reserve and its gradient birth costs
C(1+K)chi_k g_k^P. Dividing by chi_k E0 gives
C(1+K)g_k^P/E0, arbitrarily small at a sufficiently weak floor. There is
no recurrence K_R<=C K_R lacking a small coefficient.

       exp[-kappa' d_i(X)] exp[-sigma' D].              (8.1)
