\title{Toward a Constructive Existence Argument\\for Four-Dimensional Yang--Mills Theory\\[8pt]\large Results, proofs, and the remaining analytic interfaces}
\author{}
\date{18 October 2026}
\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
We consolidate a continuation of the public Alpoge--Sussman project
based on Ba{\l}aban's lattice renormalization group. The objective is a
nontrivial four-dimensional \(SU(2)\) Yang--Mills observable quantum field
theory. We present local constrained-field and measure identities,
homogeneous coupling reductions, positive finite-measure comparisons,
dated source estimates, and conditional reconstruction and fusion
arguments with their hypotheses explicit. Further results include a
local adjusted-average right inverse and physical-current estimate,
a dimension-independent complex-margin lemma, the full Gaussian
21-dimensional quadratic fusion span, and a refined fixed-prefix cubic
parity and normal-form analysis. The remaining attachment to the actual
large-field recursion is reduced to a common-gauge coarse-datum estimate
on preparation-changed complete footprints. We specify the additional
all-history, tensor-covariance and observable-identification theorems
required for existence. No complete continuum existence or mass-gap
theorem is claimed. The accompanying source archive preserves the full
derivations and the companion records counterexamples and parked routes.
\end{abstract}
