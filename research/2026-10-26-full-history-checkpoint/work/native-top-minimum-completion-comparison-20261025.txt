plus any separately declared terminal source-coordinate synthesis
constant. Thus for L^t=O(g^-1/2), a running rho of order g times fixed
logarithmic powers, and n polynomial in log(1/g), the expression tends
to zero. This is the quantitative phase-tube implication; admission
of the old density and of the source's own complete image is still
