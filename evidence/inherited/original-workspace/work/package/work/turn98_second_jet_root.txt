TURN98 -- THE GENERATED ACTION AND ORIGINAL SOURCE: AVERAGED TWO-JET
CONTROL THROUGH ARBITRARY ORIGINAL-LINK DECIMATION
21 October 2026. Root derivation.

RESULT

For any deterministic set B of integrated original Wilson links, the
exact generated marginal action has first exterior-coordinate derivatives
bounded in L^q by C_q sqrt(beta) and ordered second derivatives by C_q beta.
For an original local energy mark G=sum_p w_p h_p with W=sum|w_p|, its
exact conditional average P_B G has first derivatives bounded by
C_q W/sqrt(beta) and second derivatives by C_q W. Constants are
independent of the number of integrated links and of the lattice volume.
These are norms in the actual retained marginal of the original measure.

Both action and source, including all normalization scores, are present.
The conclusions also hold after any number of nested deterministic
original-link eliminations, by exact conditional-expectation composition.
They are per-coordinate and per-coordinate-pair estimates, not a bound
on a full derivative matrix operator norm or a decay estimate in the
distance between its coordinates. The original source weight W is not
removed. No change to a native constrained/history-conditioned law is
included. These qualifications matter for the remaining physical sum.

1. DEFINITIONS AND ALREADY PROVED MOMENT INPUT

Use the full finite periodic four-dimensional SU(2) Wilson measure with
periods at least four, beta>0, normalized link Haar measure,

 mu_beta(dU)=Z_beta^-1 exp[-beta S(U)]dU,
 S=sum_p h_p,       h_p=1-Tr U_p/2.

B is any deterministic subset of original links. Write
S=S_out+S_B, where S_B contains every distinct plaquette reading B once,
and S_out contains the remaining plaquettes. The exact partial partition
and conditional expectation are

 Z_B(xi)=int exp[-beta S_B(U_B,xi)]dU_B,
 P_B F=Z_B(xi)^-1 int F(U_B,xi)exp[-beta S_B(U_B,xi)]dU_B.

The exterior marginal has density proportional to exp(-H_B) with

 H_B(xi)=beta S_out(xi)-log Z_B(xi).                    (1.1)

Adding a field-independent constant to H_B has no effect below. The
measure used for every displayed L^q norm is the original mu_beta; for
exterior functions this equals its actual exterior marginal norm.

Let f and g be exterior links. X_f and X_g are fixed unit left-invariant
Lie-algebra derivatives on their original link coordinates. If the
links coincide, keep the indicated order of derivatives; no symmetry
of noncommuting vector fields is asserted. The generators are not
field-dependent and no differentiated history decision is hidden in them.

The full original-law energy bound from TURN64 and the derivative
calculation in work/turn98_largefield.txt give, for every finite r>=1,

 ||G||_r <= C_r W/beta,
 ||X_f G||_r <= C_r W_f/sqrt(beta),
 |X_g X_f G| <= W_fg,

 W_f=sum_(p reading f)|w_p|,
 W_fg=sum_(p reading both f and g)|w_p|.                (1.2)

For f=g, the last sum is over plaquettes reading f. The second derivative
of an individual plaquette defect has magnitude at most one under the
chosen unit generators: multiplication by unit quaternions is isometric,
and each differentiated factor or inverse factor has norm one. Each
original plaquette reads a given link at most once in this geometry.

Put K_f=X_f S_B, K_g=X_g S_B, and L_gf=X_g X_f S_B. Each K has at most
six plaquette terms. Therefore

 ||K_f||_r+||K_g||_r <= C_r beta^-1/2,
 ||L_gf||_infinity <= 6.                              (1.3)

The bounds are independent of |B|. L_gf vanishes when no touched
plaquette reads both f and g. K_f vanishes if f shares no plaquette
with B. The underlying moment bound is a full-measure theorem, not
a uniform bound under arbitrary frozen exterior data.

2. EXACT SECOND DERIVATIVE OF THE NORMALIZED ORIGINAL SOURCE

The first conditional response is

 X_f P_B G=P_B(X_fG)-beta Cov_B(G,K_f).                 (2.1)

For three functions, write the conditional third cumulant as

 kappa_B(A,C,D)=P_B(ACD)-P_B A P_B(CD)
               -P_B C P_B(AD)-P_B D P_B(AC)
               +2P_B A P_B C P_B D.

Differentiation of a normalized covariance gives exactly

 X_g Cov_B(A,C)=Cov_B(X_g A,C)+Cov_B(A,X_g C)
                       -beta kappa_B(A,C,K_g).        (2.2)

Apply (2.1) to its first term and (2.2) to its second term. The result is

 X_g X_f P_B G
  =P_B(X_g X_f G)
   -beta Cov_B(X_fG,K_g)
   -beta Cov_B(X_gG,K_f)
   -beta Cov_B(G,L_gf)
   +beta^2 kappa_B(G,K_f,K_g).                         (2.3)

All five terms are needed. This is an identity of smooth functions on
the compact exterior space at every finite regulator. In particular
the last term is part of differentiating the conditional normalizer.

For q finite with q>=1, conditional contraction and Holder give

 ||Cov_B(A,C)||_q <=2||A||_(2q)||C||_(2q),
 ||kappa_B(A,C,D)||_q
                 <=6||A||_(3q)||C||_(3q)||D||_(3q).   (2.4)

The factor six is the sum of the absolute cumulant coefficients.
For example ||(P_B A)(P_B(CD))||_q is bounded using exponents 3q and
3q/2, followed by contraction and Holder for CD. The three-single-factor
product is handled with three factors of exponent 3q.

Inserting (1.2)-(1.3) into (2.3)-(2.4) yields

 ||X_g X_f P_B G||_q
       <= C_q (W_fg+W_f+W_g+W).                       (2.5)

The beta powers cancel: in the third cumulant term they are
beta^2 times beta^-1 times beta^-1/2 times beta^-1/2. The other terms
obey the same bound. This estimate holds for every beta>0, with no
weak-exterior restriction or growth of the constant with |B|.
Together with TURN98's first-derivative theorem, it gives

 ||P_B G||_q <= C_q W/beta,
 ||X_f P_B G||_q <= C_q(W_f+W)/sqrt(beta),
 ||X_g X_f P_B G||_q <= C_q(W_fg+W_f+W_g+W).            (2.6)

For a source normalized as beta G, multiply these bounds by beta.
The coupling here is the original beta, not a substituted running
coupling. A deterministic beta-dependent source weight can be used at
each fixed beta; (2.3) differentiates exterior fields, not that weight.

3. THE ACTUAL GENERATED ACTION, IN THE SAME MARGINAL

Differentiate (1.1) while keeping all generated factors in Z_B. Since
S_out is exterior measurable,

 X_f H_B=beta X_f S_out+beta P_B(X_f S_B)
        =beta P_B(X_f S).                             (3.1)

Its next ordered derivative is

 X_g X_f H_B
   =beta P_B(X_g X_f S)
          -beta^2 Cov_B(X_f S,X_g S).                 (3.2)

To see the second score in this form, first differentiate using K_g.
Then X_g S-K_g=X_g S_out is a conditional constant, so replacing K_g
by X_g S does not change the covariance. This is an exact cancellation
of exterior-only terms, not an omission of the action outside B.

Each full X_f S still has only the six original plaquettes at f; each
X_g X_f S has at most six terms of magnitude at most one. The same
full-law moments and (2.4) therefore prove

 ||X_f H_B||_q <= C_q sqrt(beta),
 ||X_g X_f H_B||_q <= C_q beta.                        (3.3)

No assertion that H_B remains a Wilson action or a finite-range action
is used. Its generated interactions may have long range, and these
coordinate bounds do not constrain that range or imply convexity.
The exact scalar value of H_B, which may contain an extensive vacuum
term, is not bounded by (3.3).

4. NESTED ORIGINAL-LINK DECIMATION DOES NOT ACCUMULATE THESE CONSTANTS

Let B_1 subset B_2 subset ... subset B_m be any deterministic sequence
of eliminated link sets. At stage j, use the EXACT marginal obtained
from the original measure and integrate the additional retained links
B_(j+1) minus B_j. Fubini and the tower property give

 E_(stage j)[P_(B_j)G | original links outside B_(j+1)]
                           =P_(B_(j+1))G.             (4.1)

The resulting density is exactly exp(-H_(B_(j+1))) up to its scalar
normalizer. Thus (2.6) and (3.3) hold at every stage with the SAME
constants, for coordinates still retained. They do not accumulate an
error proportional to the number of stages. This is a true nested
integration statement; it is distinct from the original-state-space
single-link heatbath projections studied in TURN97 and TURN98 Section3.

The proof retains the source's identity as the conditional expectation
of the ORIGINAL G. It does not assert the same result for an arbitrary
new effective source supplied only through an unrelated norm. It also
does not include a change of variables to rescaled coarse connections,
adaptive large-field decisions, or replacing part of a density by a
fresh reference. Each such operation would need its own controlled
transformation and score.

5. WHAT THIS RESOLVES AND WHAT IS STILL MISSING

At the level of averaged coordinate derivatives, growth in the number
of exactly integrated original links is no longer an unproved source
of divergence for a fixed energy mark. Both the original source and
the exact marginal action have a controlled two-jet in the same
normalized marginal, and the statement survives nested integrations.

The estimates retain the original W=sum|w_p| and contain no spatial
decay when f and g are far apart. A smooth profile on a fixed physical
region can have W of order a^-4. The corresponding separated covariance
sum is not controlled by these bounds. Nor does boundedness of each
Hessian entry bound an operator norm on an increasing number of links.

The next useful strengthening must resolve spatial dependence or the
cancellation of local scalar contributions in the original connected
source sum, and must retain that gain under the actual coarse-field map.
An unqualified stronger pointwise estimate is not the target: TURN97
already exhibits why such an estimate can fail. The global pair-round
value theorem and the full-measure derivative theorem address different
norms and should be used at their proved scopes.
