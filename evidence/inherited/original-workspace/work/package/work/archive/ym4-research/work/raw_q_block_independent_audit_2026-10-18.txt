1. EXACT COEFFICIENT LEDGER

The raw identity is valid for the homogeneous universal BULK COEFFICIENTS.
It must not be read as an identity deleting the large-field/boundary sectors
from the full Wilson density. CMP119 pp278--279, around (3.47), identifies
interior E terms with their whole-lattice functions and identifies interior
R'' terms as independent of the surrounding Lambda. Their sum is the exact
decomposition of the conditional logarithm. CMP122-II (1.98)--(1.100)
exponentiates the subsequent R-operation; its interior second group is the
additive new bulk R' functional F. Thus, on a homogeneous compatible
periodic regulator, the exponential of the universal coefficients obeys

 rho_{r+1}^bulk = exp(F_r) K_r^outer rho_r^bulk

modulo the explicitly tracked vacuum scalar. Here F_r is the actual
coefficient produced by the full source R operation, then frozen; it is
not a claim that applying the R operation just to rho^bulk reproduces all
large-field sectors. R'' is already inside the logarithm of K and must
not be counted as another F insertion. Static old R is not removed from R.

The q-fold raw unrolling and formulas (12)--(15) then follow algebraically.
The source beta(E)=0 conditions apply to the endpoint regular coordinates,
not separately to each young R piece. All density-coordinate intermediate
classical factors cancel before introducing normalized Gaussian measures.

2. THE OUTER-WINDOW ISSUE AND ITS EXACT RESOLUTION

The independent identity-centered UF ball does NOT imply inclusion of every
original q-layer outer-window field. CMP109 (1.14) bounds actual curvature
and current, so changing the real reference does not remove this issue.
If p_cut exceeds q_min, a generic noise of size g log^p_cut can exceed the
proved common ball g log^q_min. No such inclusion should be asserted.

Section 5 of auxiliary_cutoff_comparison resolves this without that
inclusion. At each ACTUAL source state set

 h_r = log[(K_r^outer rho_r)/(K_r^inner rho_r)].

Its denominator and logarithm branch are supplied by the same connected
source expansion. The exact equality is

 rho_{r+1}=exp(F_r+h_r) K_r^inner rho_r.

Unroll these equalities with the h_r frozen at their actual output fields.
There is no cutoff-modified trajectory and no hybrid-state admissibility
assumption. Only inner kernels remain. The actual one-step prepared class
controls h_r; CMP119 p276 following (3.43) treats the full measure (3.27),
including young R, with the same preparation as E apart from its second
coupling subtraction. Fix the interpolation parameter s=1. The independent
source extension of h_r seed-converts to the full analytic activity norm.
Every fixed partial inner chart is bounded by C_q(b+g log^t), and hence fits
the true source radius g log^q_min when 1/2<t<s<q_min and b=cg log^s.
Its tail remains superpolynomially small after all fixed-q constants and
b^-2 extraction. This is an exact repair of the original Section 9.

For finite primitive containment use a DISTINCT h_r^reg, the same annular
ratio in the regular s=t=0 integral with E+R_old. Then

 E_{r+1}=P[T_1E_r+D_1+N_1(E_r+R_old)+h_r^reg].

The classical R_old remains in R; young interpolation creates R''. Thus
the primitive coupling increment needs only past E and R_old, not the R
birth at the output whose source bound is being established.

3. INNER q-LAYER PREPARATION

The action telescoping is exact because the coupling-free stationary
actions use the same nested ordinary minima. With the initial gamma
factored from the raw Wilson phase, summing the primitive action
differences yields the q-endpoint stationary action plus q quadratic
forms and remainders. At the zero-noise static suffix the quadratic form
is block-diagonal in the retained species. The changes from static to
noisy suffix are cubic (background difference O_q(gR) times x_r squared).
The triangular nested coordinate transformation has the product of the
primitive Jacobians; no extra cross-layer Jacobian occurs.

One precision is necessary in the written formula. A primitive source
Gaussian precision contains both Wilson and gauge-fixing quadratic
parts. With actual gauge parameters gamma_r and Wilson gamma_j, either
retain the mixed precision gamma_j A_r^Wilson+gamma_r A_r^gauge, or put
the difference from the uniformly gamma_j-scaled full source precision
in the O_q(g^2 R^2) interaction. Only after that choice may a scalar
gamma_j/gamma_r multiple of the entire source precision be used. The
determinant of the scalar gamma ratio is field independent; the remaining
static determinant is part of D_q, not a discarded denominator.

The coefficient derivatives in the noisy-minus-static precision do not
cost b^-1: these are the geometric source Hessians/propagators on fixed
small physical domains, and the actual fixed-q chart is on that domain.
Their complete localized operator bounds, not entrywise fine-coordinate
norms, are the inputs. Both explicit noise slots belong to the support.
The displayed cubic and Jacobian bounds are therefore of size
C_q(gR^3+D_q g^2R^2), small for logarithmic R.

4. SPATIAL AND GAUSSIAN RESERVES

The separate multihomogeneous expansion of an input activity, subtraction
at zero noise, and the connected-animal compression pay the factor
(1-u)^(-|H|-1), the marked |H|, and inherited carriers using separate
fractions of the old a exponent. New chart trees use the chosen A norm.
A factor counting old roots per new block is finite (at most O(L^4))
and belongs to C_q; uniformity as q tends to infinity is not claimed.
This supports the stated same-a Delta_q estimate for a fixed sufficiently
large q. Leaving w as a strict local spectator under covariance
decoupling is legitimate: include each mark support in Y_0 before the
retained/bad-set split, so its noise arguments remain inside its domain.

The direct-sum Gaussian extension uses the source random-walk and square-
root construction with a central domain containing the whole prescribed
carrier plus its collar (CMP116 p13, following (2.6)). In final physical
units, an error escaping that collar costs exp(-cJ/h_r), while the trace
coordinate count is a fixed power of J/h_r. Thus summing species is
bounded by C J^D sum_s ell^(Ds) exp(-cJ ell^s), uniformly for truncated
q sums. No J(q) enlargement is needed. Interior short walks are retained
in the exact conditional Gaussian, not bounded as errors.

In particular the potentially delicate square root in CMP116 (2.6) does
not introduce an unanchored exterior trace: it appears in the Gamma
product coupling Z0 to its exterior. The conditional covariance
determinant is restricted to Z0. Thus every remote decoupling change
contributing to the displayed G reaches Z0 through the collar. Short
walks solely in the exterior do not contribute to this difference.
The exp(-cJ/h_r) assertion concerns this Gaussian stage BEFORE physical
background substitution. The static-prefix dependence on final w has
only coarsest-scale locality. Its fixed exponent is paid by the chosen
J/support reserve and its q-dependent amplitude by the smaller fixed
background ceiling; it is not assigned the faster species exponent.

The remaining q-dependent finite local coordinate count is paid by a
smaller fixed geometric background ceiling and smaller Gaussian contour
margin. For annular marker comparisons the positive quadratic coefficient
may also be chosen smaller after q is fixed. These choices leave a>0 in
exp(-a log^(2t)) and do not impair superpolynomial smallness. There is no
claim of constants uniform in q. Under these explicit conventions, the
source conditional Gaussian estimates, disconnected-support factorization,
and connected logarithm arguments extend by finite direct sums; I found
no missing cross-species covariance or unnormalized determinant term.

5. REMAINING SCOPE OF THIS AUDIT

This verifies the inner joint integral mechanism and the actual-state
cutoff/coefficient ledger, not continuum existence. Finite primitive
containment and the positive-block-flow induction still have to be closed
without assuming their own future R estimates. For intermediate estimates,
shrinking the analytic radius alone need not preserve the same cardinality
exponent after one primitive step. A finite decreasing sequence of carrier
exponents, or an explicit finite spatial reserve, is allowed; the audited
q-endpoint compression restores the fixed endpoint exponent. All primitive
phase losses are fixed-q costs, not losses repeated along the whole flow.
