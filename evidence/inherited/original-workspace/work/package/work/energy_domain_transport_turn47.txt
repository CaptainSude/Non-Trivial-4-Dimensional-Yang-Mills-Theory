5. WHAT CAN BE SAID ABOUT THE TRANSPORTED TAIL NORM

Domain containment does not by itself bound a normalized complex
integral. The following exact distinction prevents that shortcut.

At real datum and zero source, with positive unsourced incoming
amplitude, a real inherited |D|<=epsilon obeys

 exp(-epsilon)<= [integral w exp D]/[integral w]
                                      <=exp(epsilon).           (5.1)

Hence its new real logarithmic contribution has magnitude at most
epsilon. This is an exact positive-kernel contraction, no smallness
of the unmarked amplitude being required.

For complex data define, after the common leading factor is removed,
the exact normalized contour kernel functional L_v. If its operator
norm on bounded holomorphic amplitudes on ALL comparison contours
is at most K_j, and the density without D is nonzero, then

 | L_v(exp D)-1 |<=K_j(exp(epsilon)-1).                 (5.2)

For K_j(exp(epsilon)-1)<1/2, the added logarithmic tail satisfies

 |log L_v(exp D)|<=2 K_j(exp(epsilon)-1).               (5.3)

The L_v normalization in (5.2) is by the complete density WITHOUT
D, so L_v(1)=1. An absolute value integral and a denominator lower
bound establish K_j. Under the existing TURN46 contour bounds and
auxiliary zero-free bound, K_j<=exp(CN_j), with the exponentially
small comparison terms included. This is an available finite-step
bound; it is not silently replaced by 1 in complex variables.

For the original two-step polylogarithmic construction, the stronger
generated-source hypotheses of TURN45 give its sharper stated
O(epsilon) perturbation estimate. Either estimate can be inserted
after the NEW domain containment has actually been checked.

A uniform-depth norm theorem must control these K_j, the accumulated
new comparison errors, and their carrier charges. Equation (4.4)
does not prove such a norm theorem by itself. It also does not put
the inherited tail on the independent large fluctuation-parameter
domain used for the local cluster; D remains separate.

For completeness, suppose the complete generated amplitude WITHOUT
D has the verified local cluster on the independent product domain,
has contour bound exp(CN_j), and has the corresponding auxiliary
lower bound exp(-CN_j). Applying the SAME linear/Morse/cube comparison
to (3.3) gives a new error

The real excess interval from 0 to e_j is the common core; after the

6. WHY A REAL POSITIVE-KERNEL BOUND CANNOT REPLACE ANALYTIC CONTAINMENT

Let the real conditional fibre be (v,y), with coarse map (v,y)->v
and independent Gaussian conditional y. Take a bounded smooth but
nonanalytic function r(v), and an incoming perturbation D(v,y)=r(v).
Then the exact positive-kernel image is

 log E_y exp D(v,y)=r(v).

Thus real smallness and conditional integration alone do not produce
analyticity in the preserved datum. The same example with r analytic
only on a finite disc shows that the kernel need not enlarge its
datum domain. One cannot avoid checking old complex domains merely
by invoking positivity of the real measure.

7. THE COMPLEX ACTION SUBLEVEL IS NOT A GEOMETRIC NORM

Already for s(u_1,u_2)=(u_1^2+u_2^2)/2, the points

 (u_1,u_2)=(R,iR)

have s=0 for arbitrary R. They satisfy every action-only sublevel
condition but leave every fixed analytic chart. The real quadratic
form is positive; its holomorphic extension is bilinear, not positive
Hermitian. Consequently the Omega_j intersections and actual contour
image requirements in Sections 1 and 4 are indispensable.

Using Re s alone is even less sufficient. The energy wedge is chosen
to control complex source phases, not to supply missing coordinate
coercivity. No independent-current or full complex gauge-orbit domain
is obtained from this lemma.

8. MANY-SCALE COST AND THE PRECISE NEXT OBLIGATION

Every new excess window must at least satisfy the cube reserve

 e_j>=C g_j^2 m_j T_j^2                                (8.1)

if its comparison is organized with its scale's g_j. For the literal
constant-source calculation above, g_j=g until an actual renormalized
rate extraction is proved; this notation alone does not create one.

On a hypothetical asymptotically free trajectory g_j^(-2)~x_0+bj,
with m_j>=1 and T_j>=1, the bare sufficient sum from (8.1) grows
at least like sum_j 1/(x_0+bj), hence logarithmically with depth.
It cannot fit indefinitely in one fixed initial E. This is a failure
of the stated sufficient energy-budget scheme, not a lower bound on
the true necessary reserve or a failure theorem for Yang--Mills.

In a single finite original box, the actual number of degrees of
freedom shrinks under coarsening, and the total sum can be explicitly
finite. That supplies a legitimate nongeometric finite-depth theorem
when the actual chart and norm inputs are checked. It must not be
confused with a uniform continuum construction in a fixed physical
region, where the number of original ultraviolet degrees grows.

To obtain a useful arbitrary-depth statement one still needs a proved
local reorganization, a resetting/renewal of the analytic remainder,
a scale-weighted energy budget justified by the actual maps, or
another mechanism that bounds (4.4) and (5.3) uniformly. Such a
mechanism is not postulated in this note.

CONCLUSION

The earlier fixed-fraction energy condition and its repeated
coordinate-ball losses were avoidable. A variable energy reserve and
frozen fibre-excess windows give the exact additive-domain formula
(4.4), with the original physical source and all normalization factors
retained. The first actual remainder is extended by a direct complex
comparison, not inferred from real smallness.

This changes a concrete next-step obstruction and supplies a revised
regular-sector construction worth testing. It does not complete the
uniform local class, the complete Wilson history expansion, or a
nonzero continuum Yang--Mills observable. No full existence theorem
is claimed.
