supports an auxiliary positive finite-g reference with a convergent local

For T>=1 fixed during the analytic argument, and gT^3 sufficiently small,

1. EXACT AMBIENT INPUT AND THE FIXED KERNEL SPACE

Use fixed L and the complete turn39--41 geometry. Let Q be the literal flat
ordinary derivative, E its bounded local right inverse, P=I-EQ, and
K=ker Q with its induced Euclidean measure. P is not orthogonal.

The exact ambient chart of finite_g_local_chart_turn42, Section2, solves

 x(b,u)=x_*(u)+P b+E tau(b,u),
 F_e(x(b,u))=F_e(x_*(u)), tau(0,u)=0.                    (1.1)

For the arbitrary-volume auxiliary theorem, x_* denotes the uniform
analytic LOCAL KKT branch. Its equality with the global compact-fiber
minimum is used only in regimes covered by a separate coverage theorem,
in particular the original intrinsic-window finite-box regime. The
auxiliary cluster proof itself needs only this local branch.

It agrees with the physical fiber on b in K, but is holomorphic on a common
sup ball for ALL ambient b and weak complex framed data u. Its implicit
equation has a fixed-range local nonlinear part and an exponentially local
inverse A_*^-1, A_*=DF_e(x_*)E. The any-leg tree argument of turn41 applied
to this equation gives common geometric Taylor bounds, including both
operator legs whenever the output is a matrix. The complete functions

 f(b,u)=S_e(x(b,u)), ell(b,u)=log J(b,u),
 J=c_E j_C(x)/[j_J(v) det(DF_e(x)E)]                     (1.2)

have convergent local analytic coefficient catalogues. This is the actual
ambient producer, not an inference from a finite-order physical Hessian.
In particular, with Taylor factorials included in F_d,L_d,

 f(b,u)=s(u)+.5 b^T H_u b+sum_(d>=3) F_d(b,u),
 ell(b,u)-ell(0,u)=sum_(d>=1) L_d(b,u).                  (1.3)

The linear phase term vanishes because the whole chart lies in the same
constraint fiber and x_* is constrained critical. On K, H_u is the exact
constrained Hessian. For real weak u its spectrum is in[c,C]. The complex
neighborhood is small enough to retain the same resolvent separation.
All constants in this note may depend on fixed L and color multiplicity.

2. EXPONENTIALLY LOCAL ANALYTIC COVARIANCE SQUARE ROOT

Let C(u) be the inverse of H_u on K, embedded in the full ambient space.
It has the exact formula obtained by applying P on both sides of the
physical top-left KKT covariance:

                  C(u)=P C_phys(u) P^T.                (2.1)

For real u it is symmetric positive, with kernel K^perp and positive
spectrum in[c,C]. For complex u it remains symmetric, has this SAME
kernel, and is analytic. The KKT inverse and P give exponential kernels
and any-leg analytic Taylor bounds, uniformly in volume.

Let Pi be the ORTHOGONAL projection onto K:

                 Pi=I-Q^T(QQ^T)^(-1)Q.                 (2.2)

The complete-row right inverse makes QQ^T uniformly positive. Its finite
range and gap give exponential inverse kernels, hence Pi is exponentially
local. It is fixed, independent of u. Set

                 D(u)=C(u)+(I-Pi).

D has spectrum uniformly separated from zero. At u=0 choose a fixed
complex contour surrounding its positive spectrum and avoiding the
negative real axis. The resolvent (z-D(0))^-1 has uniform exponentially
weighted row/column bounds on this contour: conjugating by a small
exponential weight changes D(0) by a small operator norm, so the usual
resolvent inverse persists. Constants are independent of volume.

Expand the perturbed resolvent with Delta D=D(u)-D(0):

 (z-D(u))^-1=R0(z) sum_(k>=0)[Delta D R0(z)]^k.          (2.3)

Every marked coefficient is an OPEN operator chain, with two operator
endpoints and its datum legs. The any-leg tree contraction norm bounds it
by products of the factor norms. Rooting at either an operator endpoint
or any datum leg is allowed; no unrooted volume is introduced. Summing
the geometric scalar majorant on a smaller common datum ball and then
integrating z gives the same common analytic tree bounds for D(u)^(1/2).

Since D commutes with Pi, define

                  K(u)=Pi D(u)^(1/2) Pi.                (2.4)

Then K(u) is symmetric, K(u)^2=C(u), range K(u)=ker Q, and, on real data, it is the positive
square root of the physical embedded covariance. Its row and column
exponential bounds and its common marked Taylor bounds are uniform in
volume. This construction avoids treating the zero eigenvalues of C as
points of an inadmissible scalar square-root branch.

3. THE EXACT INTERACTION UNDER WHITENING

              (K zeta)^T H_u(K zeta)=zeta^T Pi zeta.     (3.1)

Fix T>=1 and restrict each ambient coordinate to |zeta_i|<=T. Write
c_T=Pr{|Z|<=T}, and let mu_T be the NORMALIZED product of these truncated
one-coordinate Gaussians. All m coordinates, including redundant ones,

c_T^(dim K) and it is not one.

Introduce an auxiliary complex amplitude z, holding T, the physical

 V_z(zeta,u)= -[f(zKzeta,u)-s(u)-.5z^2 zeta^T Pi zeta]/z^2
                  +ell(zKzeta,u)-ell(0,u)
       =-sum_(d>=3) z^(d-2)F_d(Kzeta,u)
                    +sum_(d>=1) z^d L_d(Kzeta,u).        (3.2)

At z=g this is the EXACT centered nonlinear phase and complete log-density
correction. No finite Taylor truncation or added stabilizer is used.
For real u,z it is real. The actual nonlinear chart is valid on the
window since ||zKzeta||infinity<=C|z|T, uniformly in m.

Compose the any-leg coefficient catalogues of(1.3) with(2.4), and assign

 sup_i sum_(X contains i) exp(kappa0|X|) ||V_(z,X)||
    <= C[|z|T^3/(1-C|z|T)+|z|T/(1-C|z|T)]

4. BOUNDED-POTENTIAL CLUSTER EXPANSION WITH SMALL EXPONENT RESERVES

Here is the precise elementary reduction used for(3.3). It avoids an
unavailable fixed factor exp(4|X|) on microscopic supports. Choose
kappa>0 and delta>0 with kappa+3delta<kappa0. Suppose

        sup_i sum_(X contains i)e^(kappa0|X|)||V_X||<=epsilon,
                         2epsilon<=delta/4.             (4.1)

Put W_X=exp(V_X)-1, w_X=||W_X||<=2||V_X||. Expand
E_muT product_X(1+W_X). Group every finite family of overlapping X into
connected components. Product measure factorization on disjoint zeta
supports is exact. Shared datum sites may be declared incompatible too;
this only groups extra terms and still preserves factorization of the
remaining compatible components. The result is a hard-core polymer gas
with activities A_Y indexed by unions Y of overlapping carriers.

The absolute activity bound follows from a rooted overlap-tree sum.
For every connected family whose union contains i, select an X containing
i and overcount by all rooted spanning trees. After putting
a_X=w_X exp[(kappa+delta)|X|], the tree recursion is majorized by

              F_X=a_X exp(sum_(Y intersects X)F_Y).

The supersolution F_X<=a_X exp(delta|X|) is valid because

 sum_(Y intersects X) a_Y exp(delta|Y|)
 <=|X| sup_j sum_(Y contains j)w_Y exp[(kappa+2delta)|Y|]
 <=2epsilon |X|<=delta|X|.

The exponential generating tree recursion includes the factorials of
unordered offspring; repeated carriers only enlarge this majorant.
Also exp[(kappa+delta)|union X|]<=product exp[(kappa+delta)|X|].
Consequently

       sup_i sum_(Y contains i)|A_Y|exp[(kappa+delta)|Y|]
                              <=2epsilon.               (4.2)

Apply the hard-core cluster criterion with nonnegative comparison
activity r_Y=|A_Y|exp(kappa|Y|) and a(Y)=delta|Y|:

 sum_(Y intersects Y0) r_Y exp(a(Y))
                        <=2epsilon|Y0|<=a(Y0).          (4.3)

For a directly checked primary statement see Fernandez--Procacci,
Commun. Math. Phys.274 (2007),123--140, DOI10.1007/s00220-007-0279-2,
Section2 equations(2.7),(2.12)--(2.15), or their stronger Theorem1:
 https://webspace.science.uu.nl/~ferna107/papers/ferpro07.pdf
Their positive pinned expansion is bounded by exp(a(Y)); this supplies
absolute convergence and the analytic zero-free logarithm for the complex
activities satisfying(4.3). The original Kotecky--Preiss result is
CMP103 (1986),491--498, DOI10.1007/BF01211762. The former primary PDF was
read directly here, not inferred from the latter paper's abstract.

Pinning a log cluster at a coordinate i and summing over one of its
polymers containing i gives, using(4.2) and the pinned bound,

 log E_muT exp(sum_X V_X)=sum_Y F_Y,
          sup_i sum_(Y contains i)e^(kappa|Y|)||F_Y||<=2epsilon. (4.4)

All carrier functions are holomorphic in u and in any common complex
amplitudes admitted by(4.1); uniform convergence on inner balls preserves
that property. Equation(4.4) is uniform in m. No Gaussian density ratio
per fine coordinate enters, because mu_T is already a product probability.
The arbitrarily SMALL reserves delta, not a fixed order-one support
exponent, are essential when kappa0 itself is small.

7. WHAT CAN BE ATTACHED TO THE ACTUAL INTRINSIC REFERENCE

In the intrinsic-window finite-box regime established in turn41--42,

       +C g^2 N exp(-cT^2)+g^2 exp(CN-c Lambda^2).       (7.1)

This supplies an exact decomposition of the actual one-box action into

8. GAUGE, NATIVE-DOMAIN AND ITERATION LIMITS

The fixed product window in zeta is not automatically preserved by a
nonlinear change between coarse frames. Even if scalar color norms are
used in the cutoff, the induced transformation in these whitened ambient
coordinates need not be a product of sitewise orthogonal maps. Therefore
neither a_aux nor each cluster coefficient is asserted to satisfy the
native own-field gauge-invariance axiom. The actual intrinsic density's
gauge identity is restored by the COMPLETE difference in Section7 on its
weak domain; its arbitrary-volume/native decomposition remains separate.

Likewise, this auxiliary product-measure proof does not certify B116's
independent-current orbit domains, its original decoupling deformations,
or its later boundary/history rules. Its virtue is a concrete full finite-g
local analytic reference with volume-independent convergence, obtained
from the actual ordinary-map chart rather than from formal perturbation
theory. Repeated effective actions and the correct common geometry/domain
at later steps have not been identified. Full YM4 existence remains open.
