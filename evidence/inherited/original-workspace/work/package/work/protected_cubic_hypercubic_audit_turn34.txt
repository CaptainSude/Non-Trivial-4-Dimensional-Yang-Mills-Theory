PROTECTED ONE-LEG CUBIC SECTORS: EXACT HYPERCUBIC AUDIT
19 October 2026. public_argument_audit.

Use SU(2) adjoint curvature vectors F_ab=-F_ba in R^3, or their
complexifications. The color determinant is invariant under the
adjoint SO(3) action, so the following are genuine gauge-invariant
cubic polynomials. Distinct indices i,j,k,l run through the four axes.
All statements concern the WHOLE covariant homogeneous coefficient
catalogue. Fixed geometry/pivot summands need not have these symmetries.

1. A NONZERO PATH-DIFFERENCE WITH NO QUADRATIC TARGET

Define
 C_ij;kl=det(F_ik,F_kl,F_lj),
 D_ij;kl=det(F_il,F_lk,F_kj),
 X_ij;kl=C_ij;kl-D_ij;kl.

Under coordinate sign reflections epsilon_a=+/-1, each F_ab acquires
epsilon_a epsilon_b, so C,D,X have character epsilon_i epsilon_j.
The complete space of gauge-invariant quadratic curvature polynomials
with this character is spanned by

 Q_k=Tr(F_ik F_jk),              Q_l=Tr(F_il F_jl).

Indeed a product of two distinct 2-form components must share exactly
one index; its two unshared indices are i,j. Diagonal squares have
trivial reflection character, and disjoint pairs have all-odd character.
The unique SU(2) invariant bilinear color form is the dot product/trace.

Let tau swap i and j while leaving k,l fixed. Directly,

 tau C=det(F_jk,F_kl,F_li)
      =det(F_kj,F_kl,F_il)
      =-det(F_il,F_kl,F_kj)=D.

Similarly tau D=C. Thus tau X=-X. But tau Q_k=Q_k and tau Q_l=Q_l,
since the color bilinear form is symmetric. Any linear quadratic
extractor Pi that intertwines the complete hypercubic action therefore
satisfies Pi X=0. A constant/vacuum coefficient is also excluded by
the nontrivial reflection character. This applies to a ONE-LEG linear
transport of X under an equivariant scalar body: its entire transported
homogeneous family remains in the same symmetry sector.

For i=0,j=1,k=2,l=3,

 X=det(F_02,F_13,F_23)+det(F_03,F_12,F_23).

It is nonzero: take F_02=e1,F_13=e2,F_23=e3 and F_03=F_12=0.
Arbitrary antisymmetric Lie-algebra curvature values can be prescribed
at one point by a linear potential vanishing at that point, so this is
not an algebraically inadmissible curvature assignment.

There are six independent path-character directions, one for each
unordered pair {i,j}. Their distinct reflection characters prove their
linear independence. Swapping the complementary indices reverses X;
swapping i,j also reverses X. Axis permutations permute the six lines
with the corresponding signs, so their span is a hypercubic submodule.

2. THREE MORE PROTECTED STAR DIFFERENCES

Define

 S_i=(1/6) sum_(j,k,l) epsilon_ijkl
                         det(F_ij,F_ik,F_il).

Only the six permutations of the other three axes contribute. For an
ordered complement a,b,c this equals
epsilon_iabc det(F_ia,F_ib,F_ic). Under every coordinate reflection
it has the all-odd character product_a epsilon_a. Under a permutation
pi of the axes,

                         pi S_i=sgn(pi) S_(pi i).

The four S_i are linearly independent: turn on only the three edges
incident to i, with independent color vectors, to isolate S_i. Their
trace-zero coefficient subspace sum_i c_i=0 therefore has dimension 3.

The all-odd quadratic target is the three-dimensional disjoint-pair
space. Choosing oriented representatives

 F_01 dot F_23,    F_02 dot F_31,    F_03 dot F_12,

its permutation action is sign times the permutation of the three
pairings. There is a simple exact no-intertwiner proof without an
irreducible-character table. The Klein four subgroup of S4 consisting
of the identity and the three double transpositions fixes every pairing
and has positive sign. Thus it acts trivially on that target. On the
four-star permutation space it acts transitively on the four indices,
so its average is projection onto the constants. Its average is ZERO
on the trace-zero star subspace. For any intertwiner L from that
subspace to the pairing space, L=L average_(Klein)=0.

Hence the full quadratic extractor of each trace-zero star combination
vanishes. Their nontrivial reflection character also excludes vacuum.
The star trace itself is NOT protected by this argument.

3. COMBINED NINE-DIMENSIONAL SCOPE AND PARITY

The six path directions and three star differences have different
reflection sectors and together give a protected nine-dimensional
hypercubic module. It can be matched to the hypercubic transformation
rules of a pseudo-traceless symmetric rank-two array by assigning the
path with odd axes {i,j} to the complementary off-diagonal slot {k,l},
with an epsilon convention, and the stars to diagonal slots. This
does NOT alone prove that the polynomial span is invariant under the
full continuous O(4) action, or identify a physical spin-(3,3) field.

Full coordinate inversion -I fixes each F_ab and hence every cubic
above. Every dimension-five quadratic curvature word with one
covariant derivative is odd under that inversion. Therefore such
one-derivative FF terms cannot occur in the equivariant one-leg
homogeneous channel either. This is a symmetry selection rule, not a
new native h^6 or h^7 Taylor estimate and not a reconstruction theorem.

Two or more cubic insertions tensor these characters and can have
allowed quadratic and vacuum contacts. None of the above forbids
those mixed terms. Nor does it prove source admission, all-history
continuation, a separated limiting field, or an improved engineering
bound on original complex field/current contours. For native use,
Pi must be the previously matched universal full quadratic extractor
and covariance must hold for the complete catalogue with geometry
and pivot labels transformed together.

4. AN EXACT O(4) POLYNOMIAL REALIZATION OF THE NINE COMPONENTS

This additional algebra identifies the polynomial module, without
asserting rotation covariance of a limiting quantum field. Give R^4
the orientation epsilon_0123=1 and use (*F)_ab=(1/2)epsilon_abcd F_cd.
For each color a, regard F^a as a real antisymmetric 4 by 4 matrix. Set

 U_mn=epsilon_abc (F^a F^b (*F^c))_mn,
 T=Sym_0 U=(U+U^t)/2-(Tr U)I/4.                         (4.1)

The color epsilon makes this gauge invariant. For R in O(4),
*(RFR^t)=(det R)R(*F)R^t, hence

                         T(RF)=(det R)R T(F)R^t.       (4.2)

Thus T is an exact pseudo-symmetric-traceless polynomial tensor.
Its components are precisely the protected components above, up to
the following nonzero normalization and orientation choices:

 T_ii=-2(S_i-(1/4)sum_a S_a),
 T_kl=-epsilon_ijkl X_ij;kl       (i,j,k,l distinct).    (4.3)

These identities follow by direct matrix multiplication. For example,
write A=F01,B=F02,C=F03,D=F12,E=F13,F=F23. Then
S0=det(A,B,C), S1=det(A,D,E), S2=det(B,D,F),
S3=det(C,E,F), and

 U00=2(S1+S2+S3),        Tr U=6 sum_i S_i,
 U01=U10=det(A,C,D)-det(A,B,E)=-X_23;01.

Axis permutations give the other entries, with the epsilon sign in
(4.3). Consequently the nine-dimensional polynomial span actually
IS invariant under continuous O(4), refining the deliberately weaker
hypercubic conclusion of Section3. Nonzero follows already from
F02=e1,F13=e2,F23=e3, all other components zero: T23=T32=-1,
and all other entries vanish. A pointwise Bianchi constraint does not
force this example to vanish; curvature values at one point are free.

This is only an algebraic representation statement. It does not prove

5. THE PROTECTED COMPONENT STILL HAS A QUADRATIC TWO-LEG CONTACT

The preceding protection is strictly a one-leg statement. Let
B=F02,E=F13,C=F03,D=F12 be four independent centered color Gaussian
vectors with covariance sigma^2 I, and put F=F23=m+xi, where xi is
independent with the same covariance. The protected component is

                 T23=-det(B,E,F)-det(C,D,F).

Conditionally on F the two summands are independent and centered.
For one independent pair B,E,
E[(B cross E)(B cross E)^t]=2 sigma^4 I; therefore

 Var(T23|F)=4 sigma^4 |F|^2,
 Var(T23)=4 sigma^4 |m|^2+12 sigma^6.                  (5.1)

The background Hessian of this two-leg conditional coefficient is
8 sigma^4 I. Hence even this specifically protected component
generates an allowed quadratic channel when two legs meet. This is
a finite Gaussian contact calculation, not a statement about a
separated continuum limit or the size of an interacting native term.
