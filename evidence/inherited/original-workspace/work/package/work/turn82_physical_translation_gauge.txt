1. ACTUAL TRANSFER INPUT, INCLUDING GAUGE PROJECTION

Use a finite spatial lattice, beta>0, and a periodic temporal extent2N
steps of spacing a. Let X=SU(2)^(spatial edges), with normalized Haar. Write
P_G for normalized site-gauge averaging and H_phys=P_G L2(X).
Let V be the spatial Wilson action on one time slice, M=exp(-V/2),
and let Q be product convolution by
  q_beta(U)=exp[-beta(1-Tr U/2)]/c(beta),
  c(beta)=2 exp(-beta)I_1(beta)/beta.
On H_phys the transfer operator is T=M Q M; on all L2(X) retain
T=M Q P_G M. Its kernel is
  T(U,V)=M(U)M(V) integral dg
       product_(e=(x,y)) q_beta[U_e g_y V_e^-1 g_x^-1].   (1.1)
The g variables are the actual temporal links. The two endpoint M
factors give half of each spatial action per incident slab, and hence
the complete action on multiplying slabs. All gauge volumes are1.
Consequently
  Z_W=c(beta)^(2N |E|) Tr_phys T^(2N).                   (1.2)
No boundary potential, temporal gauge projection or partition scalar
is discarded in using the transfer trace.

This construction and its normalization were independently derived in

  https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01614090
Here the required pure-SU(2) positivity also follows directly: the
convolution eigenvalues are I_(2j+1)(beta)/I_1(beta)>0, summable with
representation multiplicities; M is positive, bounded and invertible
at this finite volume. Thus T is positive trace class, injective on

nonphysical states, which are not included in that spectrum.

Define the exact finite-spacing Hamiltonian and thermal normalizer

2. A BUFFERED BLOCK OBSERVABLE AS AN ACTUAL OS VECTOR

Let F be a bounded, gauge-invariant function of the complete ORIGINAL

Integrating the central block of length N-2q with insertion F defines

  |K_F(U,V)|<=||F||infinity T^(N-2q)(U,V).               (2.1)
Gauge invariance, including at the block endpoints, makes it an operator
on H_phys. Reflection of the whole block replaces K_F by K_F^dagger.
It follows by multiplying the two half-path kernels that the literal
original OS form is the Hilbert--Schmidt inner product of
  A_F=T^q K_F T^q/sqrt(Z_T):
  OS(F,G)=E_mu[overline(RF)G]=Tr(A_F^dagger A_G).         (2.2)
R is the LINEAR geometric reflection; conjugation occurs exactly once.
The equality holds for block observables, not just products of slice

projection are the transfer input; they are not consequences of Pbar.

Let tau_m shift F forward by m steps. The central kernel is translated

This is a physical lattice-time translation of all original reads.
Let F be centered in the ORIGINAL periodic law and put

3. A UNIFORM PHYSICAL-TIME CONTINUITY MODULUS

In the transfer eigenbasis, let L be the selfadjoint Hilbert--Schmidt
energy-difference generator
  (L A)_ij=(E_i-E_j)A_ij,
and let nu_F be its positive spectral measure for A_F:
  nu_F=sum_(i,j)|(A_F)_ij|^2 delta_(E_i-E_j).
Equation(2.3) says A_(tau_m F)=exp(-ma L)A_F. This identity is asserted
only on the buffered vector and allowed integer translations; it does
not claim that exp(-ma L) is bounded on the entire thermal OS space.

Set d_a=q a. The two endpoint bounds m=+q and m=-q in(2.4) imply
  integral cosh(2d_a lambda) dnu_F(lambda)<=v.           (3.1)
Both signs matter: the thermal L spectrum contains positive and negative
energy differences. No positivity assumption on L has been made.
In particular, since cosh(2x)>=2x^2,
  ||L A_F||_HS^2<=v/(2d_a^2).                            (3.2)
This is an UNNORMALIZED state estimate. It gives no normalized bound
without a lower bound on ||A_F||_HS^2.

For a physical lattice increment t=ma with |m|<=q/2,
  ||A_(tau_m F)-A_F||_HS^2
      =integral |exp(-t lambda)-1|^2 dnu_F(lambda)
      <=(8/e^2) v (|t|/d_a)^2.                          (3.3)
Indeed |exp(-t lambda)-1|<=|t lambda|exp(|t lambda|).
Writing x=d_a lambda, the ratio to cosh(2x) is bounded by
(t/d_a)^2 2x^2 exp(-|x|)<=(8/e^2)(t/d_a)^2. Integrating
uses(3.1). This proof includes all spectral sums by nonnegative summation.
It also defines a continuous interpolated vector exp(-tL)A_F for
|t|<d_a, although only integer t/a are literal Wilson lattice translates.

Thus a bounded family with a fixed positive physical buffer has a uniform
OS time-translation modulus along the actual lattice translations.
This can be retained in an extracted continuum OS family if that family
and its translation identification have otherwise been constructed.
It does not itself supply convergence or a nonzero state. In particular,
it concerns the centered OS state already at a positive separation from
the reflection planes, not the ordinary equal-time slice variance.
