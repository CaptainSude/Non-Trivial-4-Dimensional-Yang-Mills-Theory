RESULT

There is a useful source-level narrowing. The native construction DOES use
strictly localized curvature tests and a component-local normalized reference.
Thus a blanket claim that every native decision reads the global minimum is
incorrect. But its later fluctuation-coordinate branch and its compensating
changes of variables use the GLOBAL adaptive minimum. The supplied primary
paper makes both facts visible. A buffer around the reflection planes therefore
does not, by the already verified statements alone, produce an exact half-local
forward kernel of the ORIGINAL field.

This note proves an exact one-replacement buffer comparison and the quantitative
consumer for a prospective full-history coupling. It identifies the precise
native step still requiring a positive-law comparison. No signed localization
coefficient is treated as a probability, and no selected law is substituted for
the original Wilson law. No terminal filtered-density lower bound is supplied.

4. EXACT BUFFER REPAIR FOR ONE FIXED BASIC-R MODULE

Fix a complete old branch record w and its disjoint eligible component catalogue.
By the literal product of normalized reference quotients, realize all its new
draws by independent auxiliary seeds conditionally on w. This assertion concerns
the REFERENCE draws, not the old physical variables. Let K be this replacement.
Choose a reflection-symmetric frozen strip B around the two reflection planes.
Let K^B use the same seeds but skip every component whose prescribed complete
geometric read/write footprint intersects B. All other draws are unchanged.
Then K^B leaves the current field in B pointwise unchanged; in particular it
preserves its current marginal under every old input measure.

Let G be a bounded function of output current fields AND any output catalogue
entries used by its filter, with complete output read P. Under this coupling,

 |K G(w)-K^B G(w)|
 <= osc(G) 1_{some skipped component can change the read P}.      (4.1)

Proof: outside that event all coordinates read by G are identical, seed by seed;
on the event the difference is at most its oscillation. Integrate over the seeds.

In particular, if every footprint under consideration has diameter at most d_k,
and dist(P,B)>d_k, the right side is zero. No all-good probability enters.
The eligible100 M R_k size test, finite native collars and local reference data
give such a bound for the verified local module. A component too large to pass
that test is not redrawn by this module. A bounded-size component test can be
decided by exploration stopped at the size cap; rejection does not require
learning the full shape of an arbitrarily large component.

There are two important qualifications. First, here w is the SAME complete
input record for both kernels. Iterating(4.1) requires control of how earlier
changes affect later deciding reads. Section3 shows why exact finite read is
not already supplied for the physical inverse coordinates. Second, a local
terminal filter must include its component/collar decisions in P. A global
no-current-raw filter reaching the reflection planes is not a strict-positive-
time observable merely because the plaquette probe itself is distant.

5. WHAT A COMPLETE-READ COUPLING WOULD GIVE QUANTITATIVELY

Suppose two ACTUAL forward history constructions are coupled from the same
original Wilson configuration and use the same local seeds until a complete
read meets a discrepancy. The second construction is independent between the
two open halves conditional on the ORIGINAL configuration and reads only the
corresponding original half; the constructions are reflection related. Suppose
that for the full output reads of G and theta G their disagreement event D has
probability at most epsilon under this original-law coupling. If |G|<=1, then

 |E_native G-E_buffer G| <=2epsilon,
 |E_native (theta G)G-E_buffer (theta G)G| <=2epsilon,
 |Cov_native(theta G,G)-Cov_buffer(theta G,G)| <=6epsilon.         (5.1)

The last estimate follows by adding the product-of-means difference, bounded
by4epsilon. The exact half-local forward identity then identifies the buffered
covariance with the original covariance of F=K_+G. All normalization is the
original probability law, not a tiny component normalizer.

Unnormalized terminal-filter Gram entries satisfy the same bounded-observable
comparison. They must be divided only by the actual filter mass. In particular
a native filtered lower bound cannot be transferred without both its mass and
these joint read bounds. The finite Wilson benchmark and filter identity in
TURN76 retain their exact hypotheses; marginal density positivity is insufficient.
The source-free observable family must be fixed coherently as the cutoff varies.

If strict finite read at step j were established with physical radius
 r_j<=C a_j M R_j, a_j=ell L^(-(n-j)), and
 R_j<=C'[log(2+Gamma+n-j)]^r, then its total backward cone obeys

 sum_(j=0)^n r_j
 <= C'' ell M [log(2+Gamma)]^r.                              (5.2)

Indeed log(2+Gamma+s)<=log(2+Gamma)+log(1+s), the power is bounded
by a fixed multiple of the sum of those powers, and both geometrically
weighted series in s converge. Thus a sufficiently thick FIXED physical
buffer could separate the full cone. Equation(5.2) is the useful deterministic
geometry; Section3 is the presently missing exact physical-read attachment.

Alternatively, a geometric influence chain spanning distance D may be charged
by the native positive complete-history estimates at physical unit a_i M R_i.
At their admitted scope those estimates have the form exp[-c D/(a_i M R_i)].
They concern the FULL deciding/ancestral support, not merely a final small
carrier. To use them in(5.1), D must be an event of a positive joint history
with the original inverse records retained. A signed walk or a relocalized
coefficient support is not such an event. One cannot repeatedly spend the same
ancestral primitive credit; use one first-influence tag or distinct allocations.

At fixed terminal volume the retained normalization/census bounds yield, for
tags in their actual supported class, a bound of the schematic precise form

 epsilon <= C_V sum_i m_i P_i exp[-c D/(a_i M R_i)],               (5.3)

where m_i counts allowed birth roots/types, P_i is the actual permitted finite
polynomial/date cost, and C_V may be of order V exp(CV). A terminal raw-component
census applies only to tags carried by those components; healed positive tags
need their separate inserted census. Equation(5.3) does not suppress all UV
large fields. It has a uniformly summable fine-scale tail since a_i decreases
geometrically, even with polynomial multiplicity. It does not give a
thermodynamic-uniform estimate, since exp(CV) cannot be paid by exp(-cD) when
V grows like a fourth power. None of these bounds supplies the missing
positive discrepancy estimate for the global-coordinate branch in Section3.

6. WHY SIMPLY FREEZING THE STRIP DOES NOT FINISH THE ATTACHMENT

An independently defined buffered algorithm is legitimate once its local
probability kernels are specified. It leaves the original strip untouched and
can have the desired conditional half factorization. But retaining native
estimates for that modified algorithm is a mathematical obligation. If raw
regions in the strip are permanently exempted from R, their primitive credit
must control an arbitrarily long unrenormalized history; this is the very
large-field difficulty motivating R in B119's introduction. The published
renormalized ledger cannot be transferred merely because fewer redraws occur.

A different shortcut is to condition a crossing q on a newly drawn coarse
boundary B_c. Conditional factorization at fixed B_c is insufficient: averaging
in the two forward pullbacks separately loses

             E_original Cov_(B_c|original)(m_-,m_+).

B_c is not a retained original boundary variable unless it is deterministic
from that boundary, or this loss is separately bounded. Reflection commutation
alone does not remove it. The complete read of the probe AND its filter must
stay a fixed positive physical distance from both reflection planes.

8. EXISTING SHELL ESTIMATES DO NOT YET SUPPLY THE NEEDED POSITIVE COMPARISON

The relevant earlier estimates were checked rather than replaced by another
generic sufficient lemma:

     It is not a proved uniform conditional probability bound for a complete
     mixed native positive history.

 (ii) regular_conditional_marked_tails_turn11 proves real tail bounds inside
      the ACTUAL retained regular rectangle when the COMPLETE coordinate
      action is uniformly convex and its true minimum has an interior margin.
      Its denominator is obtained on that margin. The attachment is the bare
      fixed-block chart, or other explicitly admitted actions satisfying those
      assumptions. It does not cover arbitrary native rejection histories.

 (iii) nonflat_weak_chart_means_turn49 Sections2--3 controls a marginal radial
       boundary density under a complete strong-convexity hypothesis and a
       small mode/mean condition. Its positive hard-chart inputs are explicit;
       generated quantum histories are not all placed in that class. Bad-event
       conditioning is not an innocuous application of its good-domain result.

 (iv) coherent_energy_window_suppression_turn65 proves an ORIGINAL Wilson
      single-edge conditional shell estimate by its exact SU2 staple density
      and Haar radial volume. It applies to beta h_p with a genuine private
      edge. A flattened B_j in(3.2) is not that variable: it has the full
      constrained/global-minimum read. The private-edge conditional proof
      cannot be applied after treating that read as exterior-measurable.

There is a specific reason coercivity alone cannot fill this gap. A standard
real Gaussian conditioned on T<=B<=T+s remains a positive density with the
same quadratic action and the same coercivity. Its probability of the width-s
shell at T is1. Its normalizer is of order s exp(-T^2/2). This is exactly the
algebraic type of a retained good cut intersected with a rejected smaller cut.
It does not assert that every such arbitrarily thin annulus is realized by a
particular native history. It proves that the stated coercivity and cut-type
information alone cannot imply a conditional shell bound tending to0 with s.
An interior-margin/normalizer estimate or a direct UNCONDITIONED marked
positive numerator estimate is essential. A small raw mass for such a branch
can still be useful, but it must enter before dividing by that branch mass.

Finally, a per-root loss only exp(-R_j), R_j of order(log gamma_j)^r, is not a
replacement for distance decay. At a UV age s, gamma_j is of order Gamma+s,
whereas the number of current roots in a fixed physical region is of order
L^(4s). The elementary union upper bound with only this loss contains
 L^(4s) exp[-c(log(Gamma+s))^r], which does not vanish for fixed finite r.
This diagnoses insufficiency of that bound, not divergence of the actual
error. Complete distance-dependent localization exp[-c D/(a_j M R_j)],
a suitable influence weight, or a stronger collective cancellation is needed
before claiming a cutoff-uniform positive comparison from all these local
shifts. The fixed-loop/relative-coordinate branch issue remains source-specific.
