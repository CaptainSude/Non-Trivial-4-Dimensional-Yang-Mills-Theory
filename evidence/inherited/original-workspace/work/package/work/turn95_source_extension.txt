1. EXACT ORIGINAL FINITE-LATTICE OBJECT

For SU(2), write h_p(U)=1-Tr(U_p)/2 and

 Z_a[gamma]=integral exp[-sum_p gamma_p h_p(U)] dHaar(U),
 S_f(U)=sum_p f_p h_p(U).

All sums use the complete original finite lattice and its original
positive Wilson measure. For the homogeneous baseline gamma_p=gamma_0,
define

 W_a(s,t)=log Z_a[gamma_0-s f-t h]-log Z_a[gamma_0].       (1)

At every fixed regulator this is a real analytic function near zero,
and direct differentiation on the compact integration space gives

 d_s W_a(0,0)=E S_f,
 d_s d_t W_a(0,0)=Cov(S_f,S_h).                           (2)

No gamma_0 multiplies these insertions. This is a literal change of
the inverse coupling by an additive amount. A source for gamma_0 S_f
instead changes it by a relative amount. These are different choices
of source coordinate, and their two-jets differ by gamma_0 squared.

The proof of (2) needs only finite differentiation. Although the finite
partition function is entire in its finitely many coupling variables,
this does not give a cutoff-uniform zero-free domain for its logarithm.
No such domain is used here, and none is needed to state a fixed two-jet
problem. The exact R/T coefficient algebra 1,e,t,et with e^2=t^2=0
already retains all terms needed for that problem.

Every source-free deciding map, chart, covariance, window and coupling
used to represent the baseline integral can remain frozen while taking
these derivatives. This avoids differentiating geometric branch choices.
It does not turn a source-free estimate into a marked estimate.

2. THE PHYSICAL COUPLING JACOBIAN, AND WHY DISJOINT SUPPORTS HELP

Suppose a physical inverse-coupling coordinate theta is implemented at
cutoff a by a twice differentiable bare tuning function Gamma_a(theta).
This hypothesis is not asserted for the current complete construction;
at the baseline require Gamma_a(theta)=gamma_0.
Write q for the second test profile in this paragraph, to distinguish
it from the plaquette action h_p(U). At finite a consider the deformation

 gamma_p(s,t)=Gamma_a(theta+s f_p+t q_p).

The exact chain rule gives

 d_s d_t log Z_a[gamma(s,t)]|_0
  =Gamma_a'(theta)^2 Cov(S_f,S_q)
   -Gamma_a''(theta) E sum_p f_p q_p h_p(U).              (3)

For disjoint profile supports the second term is exactly zero. Thus the
physical mixed derivative is Gamma_a'(theta)^2 times the coupling-free
covariance. A derivative |Gamma_a'| uniformly bounded above and bounded
away from zero in the cutoff would suffice for uniform comparison and
nontriviality transfer; it need not grow as gamma_0.

This is an identity for any specified differentiable Gamma_a. It neither
constructs Gamma_a nor proves a bounded Jacobian for the full native map.
If physical coupling is parameterized by g_* instead of g_*^-2, there is
an additional fixed coordinate derivative; at a fixed positive g_* that
does not introduce a cutoff-diverging factor by itself.

For an observable centered by a source-linear scalar counterterm, the
mixed covariance is unchanged. More generally, local source-only terms
whose functional second derivatives are supported on the source diagonal
do not contribute when the closed supports have positive separation.
Finite derivatives of profiles do not change this support statement.
This does not eliminate field-dependent mixing or prove later contact
extensions for intersecting supports.

3. COUPLING-FREE IS NOT AUTOMATICALLY A VANISHING PHYSICAL NORMALIZATION

For smooth geometric connections with anti-Hermitian curvature F,
U_p=exp(a^2 F_munu(x_p)+O(a^3)) implies, in the displayed SU(2) trace
convention,

 h_p(U)=-a^4 Tr(F_munu(x_p)^2)/4+O(a^5).

Consequently S_f already has the classical dimension-four integral
normalization. There is no additional a^4 to insert in the sum.
This smooth-field calculation identifies the intended local insertion;
it is not a probabilistic approximation for typical ultraviolet fields.

At a fixed weak physical stop g_*>0, the geometric fluctuation field is
g_* times its canonical Gaussian normalization to first order. Hence a
coupling-free action insertion has leading quadratic term g_*^2 Q_f.
For nonnegative profiles f,h in separated regions its free connected
covariance is

 2 g_*^4 ||sqrt(f) P sqrt(h)||_HS^2,                     (4)

up to the fixed convention for Q, with P the relevant curvature
covariance/projection. The separated free benchmark in TURN30 proves
that the Hilbert--Schmidt quantity can be finite and strictly positive.
Since g_* is fixed, this signal does not vanish merely because gamma_0
tends to infinity. The required non-Abelian full-measure comparison is
still absent. In particular, (4) is not a lower bound for the Wilson
theory and does not certify an interacting continuum limit.

7. WHY SEPARATION ALONE DOES NOT CLOSE THIS GAP

A connected, exponentially localized one-step two-mark catalogue has a
small direct cross birth when its two complete reads are separated by
many current cells. This controls short-scale communications between
the two regions. It does not uniformly bound the separately evolved
one-mark coefficients at the scale where the regions communicate.

For example, a generic inherited-mark estimate can grow with the number
of earlier ultraviolet steps. At a fixed physical communication scale,
that number tends to infinity while the separation in current units is
fixed. A factor exp[-c distance/ell_j] at that final scale cannot pay an
unbounded earlier-age factor. Thus removing source-only contacts and
using separated supports does not dispense with one-mark renormalization.

Likewise a full source-free vacuum bound is not a bound on this mixed
derivative: differentiating requires uniform control in the appropriate
source directions, or direct finite-mark estimates. Real positivity
and finite-lattice analyticity alone do not supply a cutoff-uniform
derivative bound after the necessary local cancellations.

8. THE CLOSED TRACE HELPS AT ENTRY BUT DOES NOT BY ITSELF ESTIMATE THE
   INTEGRATED LIVE COEFFICIENT

On SL(2,C), inverse entries are polynomial because det U=1, and
Tr U=Tr U^-1. The original oriented plaquette trace is therefore a
holomorphic polynomial of its four original link matrices. Exact
complex gauge transformations telescope around the closed loop, so
their internal factors cancel before estimating that trace.

If, after that cancellation, the four actual reconstructed original
links and the needed inverses obey ||U_e||,||U_e^-1||<=exp(epsilon_e)
on a specified common complex contour, then

 |h_p(U)|<=1+exp(sum_(e in boundary p)epsilon_e).         (6)

This simple estimate is global in the polynomial; no small action chart
or infinite source series is needed. Its first partial-entry application
is already in TURN78 Section2. With c0=1 it has no gamma_0 multiplier.

The outstanding task is to establish an appropriate bound for the SAME
four reconstructed original links, or their closed holonomy directly,
on every later native localization contour after intervening regular
integrations, and to retain the supported derivative/read census of
the complete old integral. A current-link bound is not automatically a
bound for this old reconstructed holonomy. Gauge cancellation removes
pure gauge factors; it does not remove background reconstruction,
the integrated conditional weight, or derivatives of that weight.

Nor does a bounded entire original mark bound its normalized complex
posterior. A finite two-point example illustrates the logical issue:
take x=+/-1, positive real weights exp(n z x), and mark F(x)=x. For real
z its posterior is bounded by one, while its analytic continuation is
tanh(n z), with poles at z=i pi/(2n). The original mark is bounded and
independent of z, but the complex posterior radius shrinks. This is not
a counterexample to the native theory; it shows why (6) alone does not
prove the needed propagated complex response estimate.

The unnormalized route avoids dividing by that posterior denominator,
as the native programme already does. It still requires the absolute
localized marked-integral estimate, including all mixed products and
the actual ancestral/read/domain charges. If a uniform (6) on the full
history-localization polydomain can be proved, inserting it into that
unnormalized majorant is a promising direct route. This note does not
prove that history-polydomain bound or the ensuing complete census.
