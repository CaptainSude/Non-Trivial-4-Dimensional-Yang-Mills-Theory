VERDICT

The finite SU(2) Wilson lower bound

 Var_mu[c_a sum_p f_p h_p]>=C_f |c_a|^2 a^(-4)t_a^(-2)

is correct for the stated nonzero fixed real Lipschitz scalar profile,
its local coherent sampling convention, the ordinary periodic Wilson
law, sufficiently small a and t_a above a fixed weak-coupling floor.
The proof uses exact conditional-link laws and elementary finite-volume
estimates. It does not assume independence of plaquettes, a Gaussian
approximation, a gauge-fixing density, or an unproved correlation sign.

1. UNIFORM MEAN-ENERGY INPUT

An SU(2) Haar ball of radius 1/(4 sqrt(t)) has probability at least
c t^(-3/2), uniformly for t>=1. If every link lies in this ball, the
bi-invariant triangle inequality places every plaquette product within
distance 1/sqrt(t) of identity. Since h=1-cos(theta), h<=1/(2t).
Multiplying the link probabilities gives the stated partition lower
bound and -log Z(t)<=C N_p log(2+t).

For the convex function phi(t)=log Z(t), phi(0)=0 and
phi'(t)=-E_t H. Convexity gives phi'(t)>=phi(t)/t, with the correct
sign, hence E_t H<=-log Z(t)/t. Translation invariance at each fixed
plaquette orientation and nonnegativity of all h_p then yield the
uniform one-plaquette estimate. No rotation symmetry or equality of
the torus side lengths is needed. N_p/N=6 supplies the fixed factor.

2. CONDITIONAL INDEPENDENCE IS EXACT

Within one fixed link orientation, the conflict graph connecting two
links in a common plaquette has degree at most six. A greedy independent
set therefore has at least one seventh of the candidates. The physical
profile box contains a positive multiple of a^(-4) candidate midpoints.
At least four sites per direction prevents degenerate wrapping issues.

Conditioning on all links outside this set leaves no plaquette with two
unconditioned links. Both the Wilson action and the weighted energy are
therefore sums of one-link functions plus conditioned constants. The
selected links are conditionally independent even if their stars share
other, already conditioned links. This is sufficient for adding their
conditional variances.

3. STAPLE EVENT AND RADIAL VARIANCE

For each selected link, the six plaquette traces are exactly u dot v_p
with u in S^3 and unit outside-measurable quaternions v_p. Thus its
conditional law is exp(t u dot w), w=sum v_p, |w|<=6.
The event |w|<3 forces sum_(p contains link)h_p>3 for every u. The
unconditional mean-energy bound and Markov's inequality consequently
give P(|w|>=3)>=1/2 at a universal sufficiently large t. No energy
estimate conditional on arbitrary fixed exterior links is used.

For k=t|w|>=6, z=u dot (w/|w|) has density proportional to
exp(kz)sqrt(1-z^2). With y=k(1-z), the density is proportional to
exp(-y)sqrt(y)sqrt(2-y/k). Its normalizer is bounded above by
sqrt(2)Gamma(3/2). On [1/2,1] and [2,3], the last square root is at
least sqrt(3/2). Both interval probabilities therefore have fixed
positive lower bounds. The independent-copy variance formula gives
Var(y)>=p1 p2, since their distance is one. Thus Var(z)>=c/k^2.
All constants are independent of |w| in [3,6], volume and t.

4. PROFILE ERROR HAS NO DANGEROUS CROSS TERM

The local sampling condition implies
|w_f-f(x_l)w|<=6 C a Lip(f). On the chosen box f>=f_*>0.
Taking C a Lip(f)<=f_*/4 ensures

 alpha=w_f dot (w/|w|)>=(f_*/2)|w|

whenever |w|>=3. The algebra uses |w|>=3 and is uniform up to |w|=6.

The tilted S^3 distribution is invariant under rotations perpendicular
to w. Radial/perpendicular covariances vanish exactly, and perpendicular
variance is nonnegative. Consequently

 Var(u dot w_f)>=alpha^2 Var(z)>=c f_*^2/(4t^2).

This is stronger than a triangle estimate of the sampling error: no
assumption such as a sqrt(t)->0 or a t->0 is necessary. The proof only
requires a small relative to the fixed profile's Lipschitz data.

expectation of this independent conditional-variance sum. Each selected

Together with the positive-density independent set, this proves the
producer's (15). Outside-dependent conditional means cannot cancel it.

SUPPLEMENT: EXACT CONDITIONAL LOCALITY, SECTION 3F

Because a plaquette contains at most one selected link, the exact
decomposition is

 H_f=C_f^S(outside)-sum_(l in S)u_l dot w_(f,l).

The conditional law is a product over l. Cross terms with different
selected links therefore have zero covariance, leaving exactly (15a).
If no selected link star meets both actual sampled plaquette supports,

Rotational invariance of the single-link tilted Haar law gives

support independent of the profile.
