1. WHICH SOURCE PACKAGE IS BEING USED

Raw I 13546--13676 defines, on the admitted hierarchy,

 G0=Delta_a^-1, q0=(QG0Q*)^-1,
 P0=I-G0 Q* q0 Q, barG=P0G0=G0P0^T,
 H0=G0Q*q0 ell^-1.

It gives P0^T Q*=0, Q barG=0, Delta_a barG=P0^T and
Delta_a H0=Q*q0 ell^-1. Raw I 13723--13800 defines

 G=Gtilde^J=(I-barG Theta_J)^-1 barG,

with its weak C^1/Holder mapping, weighted bounds, and expanded
walks. K10, Raw I 15265ff, decorates the primitive/concatenated
walks, then forms the composites. Its exact algebraic identities
are asserted only at sigma=1. Its primitive estimates are the
quoted source inputs, including q0's published but not reproduced
walk theorem. They must not be silently promoted to Laplacian
estimates for every current-dependent operator.

The affine fixed point is

 A0=-G_sigma f + G_sigma[Theta_sigma H0_sigma b
                                -V'_sigma(A0+H0_sigma b)]. (1)

Here f is a current datum of weight -3, distinct from the background
current J and from the field datum j=G_sigma f. The contraction,
weighted response and holomorphy follow in the same weak chart norm
as the block-only equation, with size |f|_{-3}+|b|. The operator
coefficients and V' are precisely the existing K10 coefficients.
The companion below is measured from f. An arbitrary small C^1
field j alone does not control its inverse current, so that weaker
first-slot premise is insufficient.

At sigma=1, set P=A0+H0b, X=D^J(P), H=P-H^J X and

 Z=-f+Theta_J P-V'^J(P).

Then the forced representation identity is

 Delta_a P=P0^T Z+Q*q0 ell^-1 b.                           (2)

This follows by rewriting (1) as P=H0b+barG Z, without taking a
derivative of f. For all sigma define the substituted current by
the explicit weak formula

 K_sigma=P0_sigma^T[-f+Theta_sigma P-V'_sigma(P)]
       +Q*(q0_sigma-a)ell^-1 b
       -Q*k_sigma X-Lambda_sigma^T j_J(H_sigma^J X)
       -Delta' H+F(W,H).                                  (3)

All terms in (3) have weight -3 using the K10 weak mappings and
local Delta',F estimates. Thus

 N_beta(H)+|K_sigma|_{-3} <= C_sigma(|f|_{-3}+|b|),          (4)

with corresponding weighted Lipschitz bounds on a smaller common
input ball. C_sigma is bounded uniformly on the specified complex
decoupling polydisc, with the fixed e^{C kappa1} loss of K10.

At sigma=1 equation (2) gives the algebraic differential expression
for (3). It does NOT, at an arbitrary independent pair (W,J), imply
K_1=J(e^{i eta H}W)-J(W). Physical equality needs the true-pair
forced-chart match. In the unforced source proof this match uses
RD*P=0 and RD*H^J=0, Raw I 15798--15824. Restoring f=J(W) restores
the source Euler--Lagrange equation, but its gauge/critical-point
identification must be proved or imported with its actual premises.
This companion note does not assume it at independent currents.

There is a useful broader source match: Raw I 15541ff,
4.5:lem-true-pair-reduction, imports RD*H=0 from CMP99b(3.124)
for a real regular true carrier satisfying CMP99b(3.35),(3.36);
its statement does not require that carrier itself to be an old
minimizer. If the selected new critical configuration A'_1 exists
near that carrier with the source gauge RD*A'_1=0, then repeat
Raw I 15735ff but RETAIN P0^T J. The critical equation becomes

 P0^T[J+Delta_a A'_1-Theta A'_1+V'(A'_1)]=0,

and hence A'_1=H0b+barG[-J+Theta A'_1-V'(A'_1)]. Uniqueness
in the weak ball identifies it with (1) for f=J. The physical
current identification then follows by the same Landau identities.
This isolates the remaining nearby-critical-configuration and
determining-family premise; it is not an assumption that arbitrary
external forcing preserves gauge invariance.

2. LOCALIZING THE FORCING TERM WITHOUT A DIFFERENTIATED GREEN FUNCTION

Put A_sigma=P0_sigma^T. At sigma=1 the exact identity

 A_1 Q*=0                                                (5)

holds at every admitted independent pair: it is just QP0=0
transposed. It requires neither the physical-current Ward identity
nor the current-dependent Landau identity. A_sigma is order zero
and has the same supported walk-composite construction as K10.

Suppose a forcing f agrees on a deep region D with Q*lambda, using
the ACTUAL Q of this layer. Truncate lambda on whole determining
blocks inside D, with one block-locality collar removed. Denote the
truncation by lambda_D. Then

 f=f_out+r+Q*lambda_D,                                   (6)

where f_out is f outside D and r is supported in a fixed determining-
block collar of its boundary. The block-local bound for Q* and the
bound for lambda=(QQ*)^-1 Qf, when this is how lambda is chosen,
give |r|_{-3}<=C_Q |f|_{-3}. Scale weights across that collar differ
by at most a fixed L-power on an admitted graded hierarchy. This
is the same geometric truncation used in Raw I 92401ff.

Apply A_sigma and use (5):

 A_sigma f=A_sigma(f_out+r)
                   +(A_sigma-A_1)Q*lambda_D.              (7)

The last term MUST be retained. Annihilation at sigma=1 does not
license annihilation at arbitrary complex sigma.

Suppose the decoupling parameters differ from one only outside
Lambda. For the decorated walk expansion, write a term as
m_omega(sigma) A_omega. In A_sigma-A_1 its multiplier is
m_omega(sigma)-1. Every surviving term has a localization domain
meeting Lambda^c. The order-zero walk estimate, with one fixed
fraction of the spare exponential reserved, therefore bounds its
output in a block z in Lambda by

 C exp[-delta dist_sc(z,Lambda^c)/2] |Q*lambda_D|_{-3}.     (8)

This follows by splitting the anchored walk-length exponential:
one part pays the required trip from z to Lambda^c; the remaining
part pays the input-anchor and walk sums. The multiplier is bounded
by |m_omega|+1, both terms already admitted by the same decoupled
walk majorant. Constants do not count the number of input blocks.
The first term of (7) has the ordinary distance gain from the
support of f_out+r. Together they give the familiar source-distance
profile relative to the union of that support and Lambda^c.

No Delta A_omega is formed. This is the precise gain over trying
to infer a differentiated defect estimate from G_sigma-G_1. It is
the current-companion version of the retained correction in Raw I
92401ff, and controls the -P0_sigma^T f member of (3).

The geometric premise f=Q*lambda is justified by constrained
criticality only when the determining structure there is the same
as the Q of this layer away from the cut. If structures change,
their comparison collar must be included in r. Equation (7) is not
an assertion that every physical current is in the range of Q*.

3. THE CUTOFF COMPANION AND THE EXACT COMMUTATOR

Given H_sigma and K_sigma, define for a scalar cutoff chi

 K_sigma[chi]=chi K_sigma+[D_W*D_W,chi]H_sigma
                       +F(W,chi H_sigma)-chi F(W,H_sigma). (9)

This is a definition on the independent-current complex domain.
On any locus where K_sigma=D_W*D_W H_sigma+F(W,H_sigma), it gives
the exact differential current of chi H_sigma, by expansion.
In particular it preserves the physical current identity at
sigma=1 once the true-pair forced-chart match is established.
It does not require physical equality away from that locus.

The independent audit of work/cut_current_commutator_repair_2026-10-18.txt
passes. With T_mu the covariant shift, nabla_mu=(T_mu-I)/eta,
nabla_mu*=(T_mu^-1-I)/eta, a_mu=partial_mu chi,
b_mu=partial_mu^- chi, the exact identities are

 [nabla_mu* nabla_mu,chi]B
 =-(partial_mu^- partial_mu chi)B
                  -a_mu nabla_mu B-b_mu T_mu^-1 nabla_mu B;

 [nabla_mu* nabla_nu,chi]B
 =a_nu nabla_mu* T_nu B
  -(partial_mu^- partial_nu chi)T_mu^-1 T_nu B
  -b_mu T_mu^-1 nabla_nu B;

 nabla_mu* T_nu B
 =-T_mu^-1 nabla_mu B+T_mu^-1 nabla_nu B-nabla_nu B.

The last identity is valid for noncommuting transports. Summing
the curl components gives c_d=(2+4tau)(d-1), tau<=5/4, for the
first- and second-scalar-difference bound. Every field and gradient
lies in the Chebyshev-one stencil. Therefore, if

 C_chi=sup[|chi|+ell|partial chi|+ell^2|partial^2 chi|]

is bounded on the declared local-scale collar, (9) gives

 |K_sigma[chi]|_{-3} <= C(C_chi)(|K_sigma|_{-3}+N(H_sigma)), (10)

and the corresponding difference estimate for two small H,K at
the same base. Local analyticity of F gives its Lipschitz estimate
on the common smaller ball. Weighted versions use only comparability
of the weight on this fixed stencil. No Laplacian norm of H occurs.

Holomorphy follows from the explicit formulas and the bounded
contraction. Gauge covariance follows because scalar chi commutes
with the adjoint action and D_W transforms covariantly. Support
locality is preserved up to the one-fine-stencil collar. Exact
separation over decoupled components follows when their declared
domains include this collar, or when compatible zero boundary
extension is part of the kernel convention. A collar must not be
silently omitted if the component convention uses disjoint open
sets without the necessary local stencil.

4. FIXED-FAMILY BASIC CARRIER CONTAINMENT

Let W=e^{i eta a}U, with U the admitted real regular factor and
N_U(a)<=a1-m_a, |J|_{-3}<=gamma0-m_J. Use the same U after the
update and write

 W_new=e^{i eta chi H}W=e^{i eta a_new}U,
 J_new=J+K_sigma[chi].                                   (11)

On the common small logarithm ball the C^1 BCH estimate gives

 N_U(a_new-a)<=C N_U(chi H).                              (12)

For completeness, the zeroth-order estimate is the usual local
analytic Lipschitz bound of BCH. Apply the covariant first
difference to that same analytic map at neighboring bonds. Its
derivatives have fixed bounds on the smaller logarithm ball;
the difference of its derivative in the a slot is O(eta|H|).
Consequently the cross term is bounded after scaling by
ell^2 eta |H| |nabla a| <= (eta/ell)N(H)N(a), with eta<=ell.
This proves (12) with no fine-mesh loss. Adjacent hierarchy units
cost only their fixed L-comparison constant.

Hence (10)--(12) preserve the same basic carrier whenever
C N(chi H)<m_a and C(|K_sigma|_{-3}+N(H))<m_J. The real factor U
is unchanged, so its local gauge/regularity premise is unchanged.
Iterating a fixed number of such updates with a divided margin
gives holomorphic basic-carrier continuation. There is no product
over all historical levels. Exponential weighted input profiles
are retained by the weighted chart bound and local estimates.

5. EXACT SCOPE OF THE REPAIR

What is proved from the actual earlier primitive package:
  - the affine weak map and its substituted current use bounded
    current forcing f; they do not need a Laplacian response norm;
  - the P0^T forcing term has the exact localization identity (7),
    with an order-zero walk tail and annihilation used only at one;
  - the cutoff companion has the required C^1/current bounds,
    holomorphy and gauge covariance;
  - a fixed finite sequence preserves strict basic K109 margins
    on one eligible determining family.

What is not obtained merely by these algebraic operations:
  - eligibility of EVERY later graded determining family for the
    earlier propagator/averaging package. K10 is uniform over its
    admitted nested towers. Raw I 42950ff separately assumes the
    determining-set hypothesis and mixed-level averaging input;
    adjacent levels differing by one does not prove the R1 M

  - the true-pair identification of the forced chart on every real
    geometry and the exact first-slot convention of each T/P/L
    layer. These need the source variational/gauge match;
  - derived physical-current conditions such as Q4 or CMP109(iv).
    These evaluate currents of reconstructed configurations, not
    just the independent J slot of (11). The earlier basic class
    expressly does not impose or derive (iv), Raw I 13016ff;
  - equality to a preselected complex-decoupled differential
    partner. Equation (9) is a new holomorphic extension with the
    correct physical endpoint once matched; if replacing the

    this extension consistently.

The stronger graph-norm interface is therefore avoidable for the
located cut-current and basic-carrier consumers. The repaired

and composite-current obligations without assuming those away.

6. ADVERSARIAL CHECK: EXACT RAW-ROW PROJECTION

Let R_raw be the coordinate projection onto fixed raw fine bonds
and M=I-R_raw. Suppose the raw rows of the exact constraint Q are
the identity on those bonds. For a symmetric constrained Green
operator T_1, QT_1=0 implies R_raw T_1=0 and, by transpose,
T_1 R_raw=0. Therefore M T_1 M=T_1. Likewise the exact H_1 has
raw row ell_0^-1 b_raw, ell_0=eta. The replacements

 T_sigma^new=M T_sigma M,
 H_sigma^new=M H_sigma+L_raw,

where L_raw is the exact raw-row lift, preserve sigma=1 and have
the required raw rows for every sigma. If the nonlinear raw
constraint is the identity, its nonlinear remainder D_raw=0.
Using these operators in the fixed point gives P_raw=b_raw/eta
and H_raw=b_raw/eta. Thus b_raw=0 implies H_raw=0 termwise in
the new complex extension. This algebra is valid.

Preservation of the weak norms requires a multiplier check. The
exact first-difference formula contains (partial M) shifted(v),
and partial M can be eta^-1. A sufficient uniform condition is

 every block y whose enlarged norm stencil meets a jump of M
                         has ell_y <= C eta.              (13)

Then ell_y^2 eta^-1 |v| is bounded by C N_y(v), using the
comparable local input unit. The localized Holder member also
has a fixed bound: a sharp one-mesh jump costs eta^-beta,
paid by ell_y^beta under (13). The exact raw lift is bounded
by the same argument. Multiplication at an input forcing end is
harmless in the weight -3 sup norm; right-divergence members
require the analogous product-rule check in their Holder input
norms. Support and exponential decay are not enlarged.

Without (13), sharp post-projection is NOT a uniform C^1 map:
a field of size O(ell_y^-1) cut across one fine mesh produces
a scaled first derivative O(ell_y/eta). Adjacent-level grading
alone does not prove (13), since an enlarged coarse observation
block may reach across several graded layers. The actual tower
collars may prove it, but they must be used explicitly.

RAW CURRENT COUNTEREXAMPLE. Even exact H_raw=0 does not fix the
physical current on raw bonds. Work at W=1 in a commuting Cartan
subalgebra of SU(N), embedded in directions 1,2 of four dimensions.
Take the raw bond (0,eta e_1) with H_1(0)=0, and let the only
nonzero nearby field be H_2(eta e_1)=h T. Then

 (D*D H)_1(0)=-nabla_2* nabla_1 H_2(0)=h T/eta^2 !=0.     (14)

At small h the Wilson current has this nonzero linear term;
equivalently its commuting plaquette formula includes a nonzero
multiple of sin(eta h)/eta^3. The raw link stays exactly fixed,
but the adjacent plaquette changes. Taking h=t/eta keeps the
dimensionless local C^1 norm O(t), while the dimensionless raw
current changes by O(t). This is an actual local Wilson effect,
not a complex-decoupling artifact.

Consequently replacing the companion's raw slots by zero cannot
preserve its literal true-pair endpoint in general. It is valid
only where the whole current stencil is unchanged, or where an
additional exact cancellation has been proved. An open raw-current
ball with no available margin can be exited by an arbitrarily
small nonzero outward current increment; smallness alone does
not replace a strict margin. The raw real-background gauge premise
also is not created by projecting a response.

A different possible redesign would prove that all relevant
restricted-action consumers are insensitive to raw J, on a tangent
space whose fields and allowed gauge variations vanish at the raw
bonds. Then one could freeze an INDEPENDENT raw J slot while
matching only the restricted action. This is not preservation of
the full true pair and is not proved by M T_sigma M. It needs a
separate audit of every current-dependent primitive, gauge term
and consumer. No global closure is inferred here.
