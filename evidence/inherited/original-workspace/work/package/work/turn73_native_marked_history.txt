RESULT AND SCOPE

At a fixed COMPLETE outer datum of Large-II(1.72), two source reads
which are still literal private old variables have a stronger bound
than the global oscillation estimate of TURN72. Inserting them in
their unnormalized raw operations and applying the ACTUAL connected
expansion (1.90)--(1.99) gives a connected two-mark coefficient. Its
carrier retains both original read anchors and its raw scalar part.
It has a first-class small factor and the native relative tree decay.

In particular, for two bounded private-source families supported at
current-cell sets A,B outside the fixed retained components,

 |Cov_o(F_pr,H_pr)|
  <= C M_F M_H exp[-c p0(g_k)]
       min(|A|,|B|) exp[-sigma dist_k(A,B)].                 (0.1)

The constants have the admitted native parameter dependence, not
dependence on the total volume. The scale dist_k is in CURRENT
M-cell units, with complete read collars retained.

F_pr,H_pr below are precisely specified RAW-OWNED CONTRIBUTIONS
of the original observables. They are not their expressions on a
fresh reference field. They also are not the entire original
observables when other fine variables have already been regularly
integrated. Section 7 gives the exact residual terms in that case.
No estimate on those residual terms or on the outer mean covariance
is inferred from (0.1).

This is a consequence at the admitted native positive-representation
scope, with its existing absolute activity margins. It does not
independently reprove the full native induction or a continuum limit.
Sections 9--10 additionally prove a fixed-jet bound for canonical
original private action profiles, with beta_0^-K for each fixed K,
using the separately checked initial primitive-credit reserve.

2. WHICH ORIGINAL READS ARE COVERED

A private source mark f_X(o,xi_X) is permitted when:

 (a) all its unfixed arguments are OLD variables actually present
     in the private operation nu_X;
 (b) its full original read and every required reconstruction or
     gauge read belong to that operation's complete carrier X;
 (c) its selection/ownership predicate is determined by o and
     xi_X, rather than by a nonlocal choice among other components.

For instance, an original gauge-invariant plaquette trace whose
four initial link variables are still integrated in one raw
lineage is such a mark. The original trace is evaluated on those
old links, not on the redrawn V'_k. A plaquette whose fine links
have already been regularly integrated does NOT satisfy this
description merely because its original physical position lies
inside X. Its conditional source coefficient would need a
separate transport theorem.

The source class is nonempty and directly read from the finite
raw integrals: set the mark to zero on private histories lacking
the specified literal old slot. Such a history test changes no
external hard cutoff and is made inside the existing old integral.
There is no retrospective insertion of a test on a future draw.

For an actual original observable F with read A, choose a specified
outer-measurable baseline F_*(o). On a private history owning that
complete read set, put

 f_X = 1_(X owns the still-private read A) [F_old-F_*(o)],
 F_pr = sum_(X in eta) f_X.                                  (2.1)

Ownership is unique because distinct raw components have disjoint
private regions. A read straddling components is assigned to the
remainder, not arbitrarily to one of them. Define

 F_rem = F_old-F_*(o)-F_pr.                                  (2.2)

This is an exact decomposition of the original observable on its
positive joint lift. The baseline is retained. It is not assumed
to be the expectation of the old source or its value on the new
reference. For a plaquette action the fixed zero baseline is a
legitimate simple choice. The same definitions apply to H.

The initial bounded version of the theorem assumes

 |f_X|<=M_F,       |h_X|<=M_H,                              (2.3)

uniformly over admitted real outer data and all private histories,
and f_X=0 unless X contains a declared source anchor in A, similarly
for h_X and B. One fixed complete read uses one fixed anchor; sums
of bounded local reads can use a coefficient-sum family of anchors.
In that case its total absolute marked bound, not the supremum of
individual summands, must satisfy the input norm used below.

No holomorphic dependence of an arbitrary bounded mark on the
outer field is asserted. The estimate here concerns real outer
data and the conditional original-source covariance.

3. EXACT TWO-JET ALGEBRA, INCLUDING THE SCALAR TERM

Use commuting symbols e,h with e^2=h^2=0. The exact identities
below use the UNSCALED f_X,h_X and formal variables. For estimates

 ||a_0+e a_1+h a_2+eh a_12||=sum |a_i|.

It is submultiplicative. The exact source insertion at one raw

 exp(e f_X+h h_X)=1+e f_X+h h_X+eh f_X h_X.                  (3.1)

Apply the raw operation to each coefficient. The component masses
are never divided out. In particular the mixed coefficient is

It includes a field-independent scalar contribution if one is
present.

With sources inserted in every private raw factor, denote the

 [e] log Xi_o(e,h)=E_o F_pr,
 [h] log Xi_o(e,h)=E_o H_pr,
 [eh] log Xi_o(e,h)=Cov_o(F_pr,H_pr).                        (3.2)

The identity follows by expanding the finite positive integral:
the coefficients of Xi are Xi_o times 1,E F_pr,E H_pr,E F_pr H_pr,
respectively. Expanding the logarithm subtracts the product of
the two means. The constant Xi_o, including its empty-family

This four-coefficient argument requires only finite jets and

finite-source disc. This distinction matters for the canonically

10. WHAT THE GEOMETRIC SCALE SUM DOES AND DOES NOT PROVE

Suppose the complete physical source supports are bounded and
separated by d>0. Write ell_k for the physical CURRENT M-cell
mesh and include their declared collars before imposing the
separation. If |A_k|,|B_k|<=C_support ell_k^-4, (9.7) is bounded by

 C_K beta_0^-K ||f||||h||
         ell_k^-4 exp[-sigma' d/ell_k],                    (10.1)

where the harmless exp[-c'P_k]<=1 was dropped. On geometrically
spaced ell_k<=ell_* the sum of (10.1) is finite uniformly in
the number of finer scales. For epsilon<=d its fine tail obeys

 sum_(ell_k<=epsilon) ell_k^-4 exp[-sigma' d/ell_k]
        <= C epsilon^-4 exp[-sigma'' d/epsilon].           (10.2)

To verify (10.2), put ell_k=ell_top L^-m, with ell_top<=epsilon
the largest included scale. Exponential decay in L^m beats L^(4m);
extract half of the exponent and sum the remaining geometric
superexponential series. Enlarging C covers the first term and
the bounded ratio between epsilon and ell_top.

This is SUMMABILITY OF THE ONE-DATE CONDITIONAL ENVELOPES.
It is not an identity expressing the complete original covariance
as their sum. A private lineage can be seen at several dates.
No independent-increment or martingale decomposition has been
asserted for those repeated sightings. For an actual history sum
one must assign a unique owner/first-healing date and prove its
subsequent source transport; this note has not done that step.
The cross terms and outer means in (7.1)--(7.2) also remain.
