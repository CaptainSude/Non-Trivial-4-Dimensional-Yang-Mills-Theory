RESULT AND SCOPE

Uniform causal signed-stretch drift is enough to select actual finite
Wilson laws with ultraviolet mesh tending to zero and physical torus
size tending to infinity, while the native extracted coupling at a
specified stopping scale stays in a compact positive interval. Neither
continuity in the bare coupling nor a horizon-independent extracted
beta is required. This supplies a regulator selection mechanism for an
R^4 objective, conditional on the exact finite-law/native inputs below.

It does not prove convergence of observables, tightness, reconstruction,
nontriviality, or identify a physical renormalized coupling observable.
It does not give a fixed-physical-torus, exactly prescribed endpoint.

1. ACTUAL FINITE-LAW PREMISES

Fix L>1 and a compatible torus side T>0. For each predetermined integer
H, start the actual four-dimensional Wilson measure on the torus of
side T and mesh L^-H with bare inverse coupling t. The finite graph,
original Haar measure, action, cuts, scalar/vacuum allocations and all
RT state data are those of this original law. The recursion may depend
on H, T, earlier coefficients and all other causal state data. It may
be discontinuous in t. No universal infinite-volume beta is substituted.

Assume the actual step is available whenever its input inverse coupling
x_j>=Y and the explicitly retained native geometric/state conditions
hold. Assume that along every generated admitted weak prefix there are
constants B>0, lambda>0, C0>=0, independent of H and volume, such that

 |x_(j+1)-x_j|<=B,                                           (1)
 x_i-x_k>=lambda(k-i)-C0.                                   (2)

The last output in (1)--(2) need not be weak; only the inputs of the
steps used must be weak. The causal drift note proves precisely this
numerical conclusion CONDITIONAL on its native common-factor, complete
source/image/compiler and SAME Gaussian stretch premises. Those are
not re-proved or discharged by the present argument.

All needed finite-law steps up to the stopping date must preserve the
exact original law. A homogeneous coefficient comparison can establish
(1)--(2) for this purpose only after its equality to the relevant full
finite-law coupling, including all retained corrections, is registered.
An abstract homogeneous sequence alone is insufficient.

2. A PREDETERMINED HORIZON WITH A STOPPING WINDOW

Choose fixed constants

 X-B>=Y>0,       0<sigma<lambda,       t_H=X+sigma H.          (3)

Strict X-B>Y may be used for reserve. For each H, generate this original
finite recursion from x_0=t_H until its first passage

 n_H=min{j>=0: x_j<=X}.                                     (4)

The unmarked coupling/state recursion, and hence n_H, is deterministic
for each finite law. No gauge configuration is selected or conditioned
on a field-dependent stopping event.

Until this passage all previously generated states are >X>=Y, so no
weak-floor exit prevents the next step. We separately retain any native
geometric/state admission requirements from Section 1.

Set m_H=floor((sigma H+C0)/lambda)+1. If no passage occurred before
m_H, (2) applied to the generated stretch would give

 x_(m_H)<=X+sigma H-lambda m_H+C0<X,

a contradiction. For all sufficiently large H, (lambda-sigma)H>C0 and
m_H<=H. Therefore the passage exists within the predetermined horizon,
and

 n_H<=m_H<=(sigma H+C0)/lambda+1.                            (5)

Because x_(n_H-1)>X and the last drop is at most B,

 X-B<x_(n_H)<=X.                                            (6)

In particular the terminal state is weak, as are all earlier states.
There is no modified or clipped step. Summing (1) and using x_n<=X gives

 sigma H<=x_0-x_(n_H)<=B n_H,

so

 sigma H/B<=n_H<=(sigma/lambda)H+C0/lambda+1.                (7)

Consequently n_H tends to infinity and

 H-n_H>=(1-sigma/lambda)H-C0/lambda-1 tends to infinity.      (8)

The indices n_H need not be monotone. This proof uses neither a sign
for every increment, bare-parameter continuity, exact endpoint shooting,
nor compatibility of the extracted beta functions at different H.

For j<n_H, the first-passage definition gives x_j>X. In addition,

 X-B+lambda(n_H-j)-C0 < x_j <= X+B(n_H-j).                   (9)

The strict lower inequality can harmlessly be weakened to <= notation.
Thus the bare inverse t_H and the stopping depth are comparable, and
g_0=t_H^-1/2 has order H^-1/2 and order n_H^-1/2.

3. EXACT COORDINATE RELABELING OF EACH FINITE WILSON LAW

By (7)--(8), epsilon'_H tends to zero and T'_H tends to infinity.
After passage to a subsequence, both integer sequences n_H and H-n_H

The physical torus is NOT held fixed by this construction. It becomes
