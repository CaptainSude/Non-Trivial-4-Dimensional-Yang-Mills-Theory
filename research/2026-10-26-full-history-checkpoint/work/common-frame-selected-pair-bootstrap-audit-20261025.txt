1. REQUIRED CORRECTION TO COMPACT REFERENCE SCALING

The draft initially claimed that restriction of terminal real gauge cubes and
unit conversion give all compact tests with allowance
L^{-2(t-j)} kbar_t. This is too strong as stated. If in one terminal gauge

  Wbar=exp(i xi_t a_t),

then the same gauge gives a_j=L^{-(t-j)}a_t and
nabla^{xi_j}a_j=L^{-2(t-j)}nabla^{xi_t}a_t. Thus the potential norm shrinks
with one power, while its derivative and curvature shrink with two powers.
Obtaining two powers for the potential would need a new re-centering gauge
construction on each smaller cube.

No such stronger claim is needed. It suffices to assume the required compact
real gauge-cube tests on every complete grade read with ONE fixed small cap.
Terminal gauges restrict to smaller cubes and satisfy that fixed cap whenever
their local stencils/collars fit. Retain the exact L^{-2(t-j)} statement only
for curvature. The parent has accepted this correction.

2. UNIT CONVERSION

All group fields are on the same underlying fine bonds. With
xi_j=L^{-j}, xi_(j+1)=xi_j/L, and
U_(j+1)=exp(i xi_(j+1) B_(j+1))Wbar, set Y=B_(j+1)/L. Then

  |Y|=L^{-1}|B_(j+1)|,
  |nabla_Wbar^{xi_j}Y|=L^{-2}|nabla_Wbar^{xi_(j+1)}B_(j+1)|.

These are exact for the same native finite difference stencil and the same
parallel transport; no gauge comparison occurs in this unit conversion.
Since b is the maximum of these two norms, the old contribution is at most
L^{-1} b_(j+1), with coefficient exactly one.

3. COVARIANT BCH ESTIMATE

On a fixed logarithm neighborhood, write

  (1/(i xi))log(exp(i xi A)exp(i xi Y))=A+Y+R_xi(A,Y).

The analytic remainder vanishes on both axes. Its mixed derivative is bounded
uniformly for xi<=1, so |R_xi|<=C xi |A||Y|. For a covariant finite difference,
transport A and Y at the second point to the first with the SAME Wbar
transporter. Logarithm and BCH are equivariant under this conjugation. Integrate
the first derivative of R along the straight segment between the paired
arguments; within a slightly enlarged chart this gives

  |nabla R_xi|<=C xi (|nabla A||Y|+|A||nabla Y|).

This is a finite-difference proof, not an application of a continuum chain
rule. It requires only the stated branch reserve and fixed compact transporter
norms. Native Lie-algebra bond fields have their usual reverse-bond convention.

Replacing the U_(j+1) transporter by the Wbar transporter changes each fixed
stencil factor by O(xi|Y|). The xi^{-1} in the difference cancels that xi,
and hence

  |nabla_Wbar A|<=|nabla_U A|+C|Y||A|.

All nonlinear contributions contain at least one A factor. With the prior
iterate inside a FIXED cap, they are bounded by C delta_j, rather than by a
fixed multiplicative loss times the old b. This verifies

  b_j<=L^{-1}b_(j+1)+C_b delta_j.

4. CURVATURE ESTIMATE IN A COMPLEX BACKGROUND

Compare the two ordered plaquette products in the SAME corner frame. After
transporting the signed four A values to that corner, their oriented sum is
a covariant curl term plus a curvature commutator term. The former is bounded
by C xi |nabla_U A|, the latter by C |dU-1||A|. The leading exponential factor
adds xi; product remainders contribute C xi^2 |A|^2. Therefore

  |d(exp(i xi A)U)-dU|
    <= C xi^2[|nabla_U A|+|A|^2+k_direct |A|],

where xi<=1 and |dU-1|<=xi^2 k_direct were used. The finite transporter
norms/inverses are bounded on the declared common chart; unitarity of U is not
used. The triangle inequality comparing this difference to dU-1 has coefficient
ONE on the previous curvature. Dividing by xi_j^2 and using
xi_(j+1)^2/xi_j^2=L^{-2} verifies

  k_j<=L^{-2}k_(j+1)+C_k delta_j.
