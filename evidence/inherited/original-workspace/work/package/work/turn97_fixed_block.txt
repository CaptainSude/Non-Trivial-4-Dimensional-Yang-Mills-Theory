21 October 2026. Producer block97. Fixed finite block only.

PURPOSE AND STATUS

This note independently audits the concrete two-link construction of
two_link97 and computes its first nontrivial coefficients. It does not
present the familiar finite-dimensional Laplace method as a new general
RG theorem. TURN13 already proves full compact free-boundary finite-graph
Laplace concentration; TURN40 proves a real C2 constrained-window version.
The new calculation here concerns the ACTUAL generated compact two-link
action and source with exterior fields multiplied by the same large beta.

The exact finite-block statements below retain the complete compact Haar
integral. They are uniform on a sufficiently small fixed real boundary
tube. Constants depend on this fixed block and this tube. No native
coarse constraint, branch indicator, multiscale source census, or
cutoff-uniform history statement is supplied.

1. THE LITERAL WILSON TWO-LINK FIBER

Use unit quaternion coordinates x,y in S^3 after orienting/inverting one
of the two integrated links and absorbing the two exterior sides of
their shared plaquette. There are five remaining unit exterior staples
a_i for x and five b_j for y. The affected eleven-plaquette action is

 S_t(x,y)=11-x.y-sum_(i=1)^5 a_i.x-sum_(j=1)^5 b_j.y.
 Z_beta(t)=int exp[-beta S_t(x,y)] dHaar(x)dHaar(y).

All eleven plaquettes have the original homogeneous coefficient beta.
This is not a model with bounded external couplings as beta tends to
infinity. Near a common flat staple n, write a_i,b_j in tangent charts.
Put s(t)=min_(x,y) S_t(x,y), and A_beta(t)=E_beta,t S_t.

At t=0 all staples are e0=(1,0,0,0). The action is

 S_0=5(1-x0)+5(1-y0)+(1-x.y).

Every summand is nonnegative; equality forces x=y=e0. The complete
compact configuration space therefore has a positive action gap outside
every neighborhood of this point. Its Hessian on the six tangent
coordinates is H=[[6,-1],[-1,6]] tensor I_3, with eigenvalues 5 and 7.
The implicit function theorem, local strict convexity, and the compact
gap give a unique smooth global minimum throughout a sufficiently small
fixed boundary tube. Thus the full compact integral has the uniform
parameter-smooth Laplace expansion used below. This also supplies a
direct check that no alternative zero-action saddle or gauge zero mode
was omitted for this fiber.

2. CHECK OF THE MOMENT ARGUMENT, INCLUDING THE BETA DERIVATIVE

In parameter-dependent Morse coordinates centered at the true minimum,
S_t-s(t)=|z|^2/2. Let B(z,t) be the complete positive Haar density in these
coordinates. A smooth fixed radial cutoff separates the Morse core from
the compact complement. That complement and its first two t derivatives
are exponentially small after normalization; the fixed positive action
gap pays the polynomial beta factors produced by differentiation.

Odd monomials in the core have zero Gaussian integral. Taylor expansion
with parameter derivatives performed BEFORE estimation gives, in
dimension d=6,

 Z_beta(t)=exp[-beta s(t)] (2pi/beta)^3 B(0,t)
           [1+c1(t)/beta+O_C2(t)(beta^-2)],
 c1(t)=Delta_z B(0,t)/(2 B(0,t)).

For the source one should not simply differentiate an unspecified error.
Instead insert |z|^2/2 into the same Gaussian integral. Gaussian moments
give its numerator coefficients (3/beta)B(0,t) and
(4/beta^2)(Delta B(0,t)/2). Dividing by the denominator gives directly

 A_beta(t)=s(t)+3/beta+c1(t)/beta^2+O_C2(t)(beta^-3).

To obtain the stated source remainder, expand the smooth even part of B
through degree four, retaining its first two parameter derivatives, and
bound its next Taylor remainder by Gaussian moments. The omitted odd
terms vanish. All needed derivatives exist because the action and Haar
density are smooth and the saddle stays in the same strict chart.

Consequently, with the EXACT flat conditional value removed,

 A_beta(t)-A_beta(0)
   =s(t)-s(0)+[c1(t)-c1(0)]/beta^2+O_C2(t)(beta^-3).

The universal 3/beta term cancels because the physical dimension of the
integrated fiber is six at every boundary datum. The determinant factor
is field dependent but beta independent, so it contributes to the
generated action while disappearing from this constant-coupling source.
Neither statement permits dropping it from subsequent integrations.

3. WHY THE RELATIVE ERROR IS QUADRATIC IN STAPLE DISAGREEMENT

The action, Haar integral, classical minimum, and normalized source are
invariant under a simultaneous O(4) rotation of x,y and all ten staples.
On the common-staple diagonal a_i=b_j=n, the integral is exactly the flat
integral after a rotation, s=0, and A_beta-A_beta(0)=0.

At such a diagonal point the stabilizer O(3) rotates each of the ten
staple tangent spaces by its standard three-dimensional representation.
No nonzero invariant linear functional exists. Therefore all first
derivatives of the source, of s, and of their difference vanish at every
point of this diagonal. Taylor's theorem with the C2 bound from Section2
gives

 |A_beta(t)-A_beta(0)-s(t)|
       <= C beta^-2 dist(t, common-staple diagonal)^2.

The classical Hessian normal to that diagonal is positive. A direct
check is to write tangent staples u_i,v_j and minimize

 (1/2)|X-Y|^2 +(1/2)sum_i|X-u_i|^2
                         +(1/2)sum_j|Y-v_j|^2.

This quadratic form has zero minimum precisely when every u_i and v_j
equals the same X=Y. Its restriction normal to the diagonal is strictly
positive in this fixed finite dimension. The minimum and diagonal are
smooth, so, after shrinking the tube,

 c dist(t,diagonal)^2 <= s(t) <= C dist(t,diagonal)^2.

This proves the useful stronger comparison

 |A_beta(t)-A_beta(0)-s(t)| <= C beta^-2 s(t).

This reasoning applies to independent unit staples. Restriction to the
subset realized by actual Wilson exterior links preserves the inequality.
It does not require their independence as random variables. It also does
not yield a relative comparison of connected covariances.

4. EXPLICIT FLAT COEFFICIENT

Use north-pole tangent coordinates
x=(sqrt(1-|X|^2),X), y=(sqrt(1-|Y|^2),Y). The action and Haar amplitudes
have the expansions

 S_2=3(|X|^2+|Y|^2)-X.Y,
 S_4=(3/4)(|X|^4+|Y|^4)-(1/4)|X|^2|Y|^2,
 j_2=(|X|^2+|Y|^2)/2.

There is no cubic action term at this datum. With the Gaussian covariance
H^-1=[[6,1],[1,6]]/35 tensor I_3, put v=6/35, w=1/35. Then

 E|X|^2=18/35,
 E|X|^4=15v^2=108/245,
 E|X|^2|Y|^2=9v^2+6w^2=66/245.

Therefore

 c1(0)=E(j_2-S_4)=-39/490.

Normalized S^3 Haar density at the north pole is 1/(2pi^2). Thus the
complete leading coefficient, including both Haar measures, is

 A0(0)=2/(pi 35^(3/2)),
 Z_beta(0)=A0(0) beta^-3[1-39/(490beta)+O(beta^-2)],
 A_beta(0)=3/beta-39/(490beta^2)+O(beta^-3).

5. AN EXPLICIT CHANGED-ACTION FAMILY AND ITS SOURCE

Let n(theta)=(cos theta,sin theta,0,0). Set all a_i=n(theta) and all
b_j=n(-theta). This family can be realized with two opposite parallel
Wilson links in a plaquette: keep the two exterior sides of that
plaquette equal to identity and set the distinct opposite links in their
ten other plaquettes to realize these staples. Periods at least four
avoid the relevant identifications. The unused exterior links can be I.

The unique weak minimum is x=n(phi),y=n(-phi). Its stationarity equation
and small-angle expansion are

 sin(2phi)=5sin(theta-phi),
 phi=5theta/7+O(theta^3),
 s(theta)=11-cos(2phi)-10cos(theta-phi)
         =(10/7)theta^2+O(theta^4).

Use rotated north-pole charts centered at these two minimizers. Write

 C=cos(2phi), D=sin(2phi), P=5cos(theta-phi), k=P+C.

The Hessian consists of three two-by-two blocks [[k,-c_i],[-c_i,k]],
where c_1=C and c_2=c_3=1. In particular

 det H=(k^2-C^2)(k^2-1)^2,
 log[A0(theta)/A0(0)]=(206/343)theta^2+O(theta^4).

The cubic and quartic terms, with the same density j_2 as above, are

 S_3=(D/2)(|X|^2 Y_1-X_1 |Y|^2),
 S_4=(k/8)(|X|^4+|Y|^4)-(C/4)|X|^2|Y|^2.

Thus c1=E[j_2-S_4+S_3^2/2] in this centered Gaussian. Let
v_i=k/(k^2-c_i^2), w_i=c_i/(k^2-c_i^2), m=sum_i v_i. The first two
terms are exactly

 m -[(k-C)/4]m^2 -(k/2)sum_i v_i^2 +(C/2)sum_i w_i^2.

For the cubic contribution at order theta^2 only the flat Gaussian is
needed. Wick's formula gives

 E[(|X|^2Y_1-X_1|Y|^2)^2]=4/35,
 (1/2)E S_3^2=D^2/70+O(theta^4).

Here C=1-50theta^2/49+O(theta^4),
k=6-60theta^2/49+O(theta^4), D^2=100theta^2/49+O(theta^4).
The resulting covariance variations (coefficients of theta^2) are

 delta v_1=324/12005,  delta v_2=delta v_3=444/12005,
 delta w_1=-226/12005, delta w_2=delta w_3=144/12005.

Substitution gives the explicit finite-block coefficient

 c1(theta)=-39/490-(3123/84035)theta^2+O(theta^4).

Define the exact generated relative action
V_beta(theta)=-log Z_beta(theta)+log Z_beta(0). The coefficient of
theta^2 in its Taylor expansion is

 [theta^2]V_beta = 10beta/7 -206/343
                          +3123/(84035beta)+O(beta^-2).

The coefficient in the corresponding exact flat-subtracted source is

 [theta^2](A_beta(theta)-A_beta(0))
                 =10/7-3123/(84035beta^2)+O(beta^-3).

The source formula is obtained by the moment argument of Section2,
not by differentiating an unproved remainder. A parameter-smooth
expansion through the displayed order justifies extracting each
theta-quadratic coefficient.

6. WHAT THIS AUDIT ESTABLISHES AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

The two-link integration has a genuine field-dependent one-loop action
term: the coefficient -206/343 is nonzero. Constant-source protection
removes that term from the beta derivative, leaving a nonzero correction
only at order beta^-2. This explicitly verifies that both action and
source must be propagated. Replacing the generated action by the
classical minimum would alter the next conditional measure even though
the source has the stronger estimate.

The positive normal Hessian plus simultaneous rotation symmetry checks
the stronger relative C beta^-2 s bound without an independent constant
error per microscopic block. No global all-real-field gradient estimate
was proved here; only the quantified existence of a fixed weak tube and
its finite-block C2 control. The existing all-real one-link estimate is
not implicitly extended to this two-link block.

The remaining multiscale problem is to control the accumulated generated
action and transported sources with actual coarse constraints and bad
regions, while paying the physical source census. Fixed-block Laplace
constants and a positive fixed-block Hessian do not provide that result.

7. INDEPENDENT RADIAL CHECK OF THE NONFLAT COEFFICIENT

The exact flat radial law from two_link97, Section6, gives

 C_o=(1/3) E[(1-c^2)m(beta sqrt(26+10c))/sqrt(26+10c)],

where c has density proportional to
sqrt(1-c^2) exp(5beta c) z(beta sqrt(26+10c)). Simultaneous rotation
in the (0,i) plane gives the exact Ward identity C_d+C_o=M/(5beta).
Consequently the theta-quadratic coefficient for the opposing-staple
family is EXACTLY 50 beta^2 C_o at every beta>0.

Put h=1/beta and T=beta(1-c). Its leading normalized density is Gamma
with shape 3/2 and rate 35/6. Write r=sqrt(36-10Th). Relative to that
Gamma density, an unnormalized weight through h^2 is

 W(T,h)=(1-Th/2)^(1/2) (r/6)^(-3/2)
   *[1-3h/(8r)-15h^2/(128r^2)]
   *exp[-25T^2h/432-125T^3h^2/15552] +O(h^3).

The corresponding observable is

 F(T,h)=(2Th-T^2h^2)/(3r)
                *[1-3h/(2r)+3h^2/(8r^2)]+O(h^4).

These Bessel coefficients can also be obtained directly by expanding
the S^3 Haar radial integral against a Gamma(3/2,1) density. Restricting
first to a fixed small neighborhood of c=1 and controlling its compact
complement by the strict phase gap justifies the usual Laplace
remainders. The displayed expressions are sufficient for this
coefficient check.

Exact Gamma moments E T^j=(3/2)_j(6/35)^j give

 E W=1-39h/490-57h^2/6860+O(h^3),
 E(FW)=h/35-h^2/70+351h^3/240100+O(h^4).

Division yields

                         +3123/(4201750beta^3)+O(beta^-4).

Thus 50beta^2 C_o reproduces all three coefficients in Section5 by a
different, one-dimensional calculation. This is an algebraic
cross-check independent of the six-dimensional cubic/quartic Wick
expansion. It also checks the signs and factors in the finite-beta
quadratic source formula of two_link97.
