INTRINSIC SOURCE INTERFACE: GLOBAL INVARIANCE, LOCAL WORDS, FRESH LIFT
25 October 2026. Scoped deductions and remaining native interfaces.

MAIN FINDING

There are two coherent routes. A raw zero-weakening coefficient can be
defined intrinsically on a specified LOCAL representative chart by
evaluating only its surviving complete local words. This still needs
a local primitive-admission inventory; global numerical bounds alone
do not provide it. Alternatively keep the normalized global source
F_t, prove its gauge/anchor independence by exact real noise changes,
and use quasilocal tails plus compatible physical coarse completions.
A fresh lift E_new(U,J)=F_t(M^t U) is then an exact new source interface
on its declared running domain. Neither route identifies old and new
independent-current continuations by physical agreement.

2. AN INTRINSIC LOCAL-WORD LEMMA

Fix a carrier Y and a chosen zero-exterior-weakening pattern. Suppose:

(L1) Every surviving primitive term has all input/output and coefficient
reads, Dirichlet domains, inverse branches, gauge cubes, paths and collars
contained in Y. Terms whose tags vanish are discarded BEFORE evaluating
any unavailable exterior input.

(L2) For every input in a specified local pair chart D_Y, those very local
primitives exist, are holomorphic, covariant, and obey the uniform native
word estimates. The local compact reference and its gauge-cube tests are
part of D_Y; they need not be globally extendible.

(L3) The local field/current recursion and all its fixed-point equations
have the same uniform strict caps on Y. Boundary stencils are either
included with their full read or vanish by the registered collars/tag.

Then the surviving series and fixed points DEFINE an intrinsic
holomorphic function of the data in Y. Their majorants are exactly the
same ones used globally. On a restriction of an admitted global input,
this function equals the global zero-weakening evaluation term by term
and by fixed-point uniqueness. No exterior completion is needed.

Proof: each primitive is a function on D_Y by (L1)--(L2); uniformly
convergent series of them are holomorphic. The uniform contraction in
(L3) defines the same analytic fixed points. Equality on restrictions
is an algebraic equality of the same terms, not an identity-theorem
extension from physical data. This proves the lemma.

For the new whole-prefix family, L1 includes the top anchor/average
paths, every recursively substituted frame/noise branch, the private
pivots, P1, and complete selected-current stencils. L2--L3 still need
an explicit inventory on the LOCAL domain chosen for the application.
They are plausible from the local elliptic primitives, but have not
been supplied merely by the global-prefix numerical bound.

There is an additional orbit issue for RAW fixed-noise functions:
their covariance is joint in (U,J,x). A complex adjoint rotation does
not preserve the fixed real x product. Therefore a uniform bound on a
local regular representative chart cannot automatically be declared a
uniform raw bound on its entire G_C orbit with the same numerical x box.
Normalized gauge-invariant scalar functions are a different object and
can avoid this particular fixed-noise orbit problem.

3. EXACT GLOBAL MIXED-KERNEL GAUGE INVARIANCE

The following finite lemma isolates the actual hypotheses. Let the
state include its frame Gamma. Assume that the OLD density is evaluated
in its matching original frame and has the native covariance

 W_old(z^h,T_h x)=W_old(z,x)

under real compact gauge transformations, with the same identity for its
actual denominator. The original centered color-ball product is invariant
under T_h and its real Jacobian is1. Assume the NEW response and selected
current are covariant in the simultaneous background/current/noise
arguments. These are statements about all independent real pairs in the
admitted chart, not just physical minima.

For clarity take a canonical top frame with real compact anchors a_y:

 Gamma_a(U)(x)=a_y P(U;y,x),

where y is the fixed top-block root for x. For an external real fine
gauge g, path covariance gives the EXACT identity

 Gamma_a(U^g)(x)=c_g(x) Gamma_a(U)(x) g(x)^{-1},
 c_g(x)=a_y g(y) a_y^{-1}.                           (1)

c_g is block-constant at the top scale and real compact. The relative
frame increment R_j satisfies R_j(U^g,A^g)=g R_j(U,A)g^{-1}, so the
same identity propagates down the hierarchy. Thus

 N_j'=Ad_g N_j Ad_(c_g)^{-1},
 N_j' T_(c_g)x_j=Ad_g(N_j x_j).                      (2)

The free-noise action in (2) is evaluated at its actual grade-j sites;
no free/pivot color embedding is changed. By new-response covariance,
the common output fields/currents transform by g. In the ORIGINAL
frames the states transform by c_g, because

 (U_j^g)^(c_g Gamma_j g^{-1})=(U_j^Gamma_j)^c_g.

Make the REAL change x_j -> T_(c_g)x_j at every date. It preserves the
original products, Lebesgue/Haar/coarea factors and masks that belong
to the stated homogeneous gauge-covariant kernel. The old densities
and actual denominators match by their covariance. A gauge-invariant
terminal cavity/source is unchanged. Therefore the full finite mixed-
kernel output F_t is gauge invariant on the admitted real independent
pair chart.

The same argument proves independence of real compact TOP ANCHORS.
Changing a_y to h_y a_y gives Gamma' =h Gamma; rotate each original
noise by the corresponding real block-constant Ad_h. Common-frame
fields are identical and original-frame density states are h-related.
The normalized value is identical. This is an equality of the whole
integral; it need not be pointwise equality at unchanged x.

If anchors are changed successively using higher-date data only, a
triangular real rotation gives the same Jacobian/product argument, but
its actual conditional dependence must be registered. No such dependence
should be guessed for an arbitrary frame choice.

These statements require the ORIGINAL-FRAME density covariance, not
only covariance of the new field map. Arbitrary non-gauge-invariant
history selectors are not included. Complex noise rotations are not
performed. Once both sides are holomorphic on a common connected chart,
equality extends from the full real independent-pair chart to that
complex chart. Infinitesimal complex gauge invariance then gives the
usual local Ward identities. This does not license continuation through
zeros of a denominator or outside a response branch.

4. CONSEQUENCE FOR GLOBAL QUASILOCAL SOURCE CLASSES

The unweakened formulas depend on U,J and native geometric choices;
the compact reference used to prove estimates need not be an argument
of the function. Exact gauge/anchor invariance therefore allows one to
keep the global scalar F_t and its uniform local germs/weighted tails,
rather than force an immediate decomposition into functions on every
native U^c(Y). At flat data, constant-color invariance and semisimplicity
already imply vanishing of its linear color jet; scalar and quadratic
jets remain actual data and must be retained.

This is a possible change of SOURCE CLASS, not an automatic application
of Balaban's action consumer. A global quasilocal consumer must prove
the needed Taylor/Ward/marginal extraction and remainder estimates in
that class, with its actual shrinking tube radii and read counts. The
existing analytic-neighborhood consumer supplies a relevant framework
for a finite marked source, but does not give arbitrary quantum suffix
contraction or a full all-history construction.

5. FRESH LIFT FROM PHYSICAL COARSE DETERMINING DATA

Suppose the finite normalized output is defined as a function F_t(V)
of actual top coarse determining data: the top background is the native
minimum U_t(V), using the actual B109 averaging convention. Define the
NEW source lift

                  E_new(U,J)=F_t(M^t U).            (3)

It is intentionally independent of J. On the physical subfamily,
M^t U_t(V)=V in the registered endpoint convention, so (3) is exactly
F_t(V). This does not alter the microscopic observable or identify
an old offgraph source continuation with the new one.

Holomorphy and a source cap follow by ordinary composition on the
explicit domain

              { (U,J): M^t U belongs to D_coarse(F_t) }.

The full averaging read must be present. Endpoint covariance of M^t
and gauge invariance of F_t imply gauge invariance of the fresh lift.
No assertion J=J(U) is needed to define it.

The native averaging estimates must verify this domain inclusion. If
the required coarse cap is RUNNING, delta_t=O(g log^p(g^{-2})), the
lifted fine-field/curvature cap must be chosen accordingly, with all
fixed averaging/minimum constants retained. Enlarge auxiliary caps by
fixed factors where needed to contain the ORIGINAL physical success
family; do not silently discard that family to fit a smaller domain.

For complex neighborhoods, retain the actual derivative/branch price
of M^t and of the top minimum. At fixed derivative order, one-step
averaging composition gives at most exponential-in-t costs when its
one-step analytic constants have uniform reserves; for t=O(log(1/g))
these are fixed inverse-g powers. That reserve/composition estimate
must be checked, not replaced by the assertion that averaging is local.

6. COMPATIBLE COARSE COMPLETIONS AND FINITE EXACT TAILS

The existing curvature-preserving completion Vhat_n has the needed
structural properties: one consistent coordinate tree, exact inner
agreement, flat exterior, root-conjugation covariance, curvature
O(delta_t), and polynomial derivative costs, PROVIDED n^2 delta_t is
small and the filled nonwrapping read/collar is present.

Consequently F_n(V):=F_t(Vhat_n(V)) is gauge invariant and finite-read
whenever that completed datum is in F_t's actual admitted domain.
The existing FIRST-BARE projection theorem does not by itself prove
this admission/comparison for a whole finite prefix; its new kernel
and image estimates must be used. If those yield an exponentially
small tail, the finite identity

 F_t(V)=F_R0(V)+sum_(n=R0+1)^N [F_n(V)-F_(n-1)(V)]
                                      +[F_t(V)-F_N(V)]

is exact. Every original outer mass multiplies it at the original datum.
The auxiliary completion laws do not replace those masses. The last
bracket is retained, even if it has a stronger rarity bound.

At fixed positive delta_t, n cannot tend to infinity through this
completion theorem. On a running cap delta_t~g log^p(g^{-2}), a finite
range such as N=O(log^2(g^{-2})) satisfies N^2 delta_t ->0; larger
ranges need their actual check. This supports a finite-delay source
class with running/poly-radius charts, not an automatic fixed-radius
native E_j class or an infinite-radius expansion at fixed g.

7. WHY HAAR AVERAGING DOES NOT REMOVE THE DOMAIN ISSUE

An exterior Haar average of a global function can preserve real gauge
invariance, but its completions are typically not in the admitted
regular-field/zero-free chart. The source bounds cannot be applied
there. Averaging over 'regular completions' instead requires a genuine
completion map/kernel, a holomorphic parameter dependence, and fixed
admitted domains; a moving sharp regularity cutoff does not supply them.
Holomorphic partitions of unity do not provide a generic patching cure.

Such an auxiliary averaging operation would not change the original
measure if used only as an algebraic source decomposition with its exact
complement. It would change the observable if its error were discarded.
The intrinsic-local-word lemma or the compatible deterministic completion
route makes the necessary interface more explicit and avoids pretending
that an arbitrary exterior Haar completion is regular.
