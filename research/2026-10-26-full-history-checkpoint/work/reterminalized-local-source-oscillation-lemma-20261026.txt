RETERMINALIZED LOCAL SOURCE OSCILLATION: A CAUSAL CAUCHY LEMMA
26 October 2026. Root. Exact analytic lemma with explicit supplier hypotheses.

This is not a theorem that Balaban's source constructor meets the hypotheses.
It isolates the needed inference and its phase ordering. A single small
source ball adapted to a final date N does NOT imply the conclusion below.
Uniform bounds for the SAME coefficient on the larger endpoint-k shape
class are essential. No complex continuation of old private coordinates,
source-weighted component inverse, or scalar partition lower bound is used.

1. STATEMENT

Fix a finite lattice with metric measured in date-k units. Let H be the
COMPLETE source read of a localized coefficient F_H, with anchor z in H,
and put D=1+diam_k(H). The source is a complex scalar function. Write

  ||v||_(k,shape)=||v||_infinity+Lip_k(v).

Let s range over an inner constant-source disc. Assume a strictly larger
constant disc is available with margin at least 4r_k, and

  r_k=c(1+x_k)^(-d),   c>0, d>=0 fixed independently of k and depth.

Assume the following facts about F_H, including all its other parameters:

(A) On every source s+v with ||v||_(k,shape)<r_k the SAME F_H is
    holomorphic and |F_H(s+v)|<=M_H. This is a bound for this entire
    source shape class, not just constant v. Other complex field/current
    parameters remain on the same prescribed contours, and old private
    coordinates remain real parameters.

(B) F_H depends only on source values in H. Its finite construction is
    independent of a chosen final stopping label. In particular the
    endpoint-k and endpoint-N constructions agree wherever both are
    defined. All cutoffs, geometries and reference inverses are frozen.

(C) A source w admitted by the endpoint-N construction satisfies
    |F_H(w)|<=M_H, w(z)=s lies in the inner constant disc, and
    Lip_k(w)<=epsilon_k. (Only its restriction to H is needed.)

Then with a numerical constant C independent of the number of source
coordinates, diameter, lattice depth and volume,

  |F_H(w)-F_H(constant s)|
      <= C M_H min(1, epsilon_k D/r_k).                 (1)

In particular if epsilon_k<=C0 r_N chi_k^N, where
chi_k^N=L^(-(N-k)/2), then

  |F_H(w)-F_H(s)|
      <= C' r_N chi_k^N (1+x_k)^d D M_H.               (2)

If M_H=m(x_k) exp[-mu tree_k(H)], one reserved carrier moment pays D,
leaving the same stretched-log factor m(x_k) with only a FIXED polynomial
loss (1+x_k)^d. A rooted tree norm can be used in place of pointwise M_H
provided the same source shape class bounds that norm; apply the scalar
argument to each complete read and retain the reserve before summation.

2. PROOF INCLUDING THE EXTENSION AND SMALL-DISC CASE

Set v=w-s on H. Since v(z)=0,

  ||v||_(infinity,H)<=epsilon_k diam_k(H),
  Lip_(k,H)(v)<=epsilon_k.

Extend real and imaginary parts separately to the whole finite lattice
by the scalar Lipschitz extension formula. Clamp each extension to the
interval containing its original values. Clamping preserves those values
and does not increase its Lipschitz constant. The resulting complex v_tilde
equals v on H and obeys

  ||v_tilde||_(k,shape)<=C_ext epsilon_k D.

This extension is chosen for the FIXED w. It need not be holomorphic as a
function of w; only the affine line tau -> s+tau v_tilde is used. Its
complex tau dependence is entire. Define

  R=r_k/(2 C_ext epsilon_k D),
  phi(tau)=F_H(s+tau v_tilde).

When epsilon_k=0, locality gives the result directly. Otherwise (A) gives
phi holomorphic and bounded by M_H on |tau|<2R. If R>=1, Cauchy's formula
on any circle approaching radius 2R bounds the power series and gives

  |phi(1)-phi(0)|<=M_H/(2R-1)<=M_H/R.

Locality identifies phi(1)=F_H(w). For the borderline R=1 the radius-2
limit is legitimate because the domain is open and the bound is uniform.
If R<1, do NOT infer admission of the actual endpoint from this tau disc.
Use (C) and (A) separately to get |F_H(w)-F_H(s)|<=2M_H.
Since epsilon_k D/r_k=1/(2C_ext R), this is bounded by a constant times
M_H min(1,epsilon_k D/r_k) in the R<1 regime as well. This proves (1),
and substituting epsilon_k and r_k proves (2).

The same proof works for Banach-valued coefficient norms by the Banach
Cauchy inequality. For a localized word, D must measure its COMPLETE
source read; replacing it by the visible final support invalidates the
locality step. All source pieces may be included in w: the extension and
the constants do not depend on their number or on a chosen decomposition.

3. CAUSAL APPLICATION TO A FRESH HEALED BIRTH

To use this lemma in a primitive-prefix bootstrap, one must simultaneously
construct the source families for all terminal labels and constant anchors.
Their formulas must be identical; only their estimates use those labels.
At date k the required order is:

  (i) Have strict bounds for every earlier birth on the endpoint-k source
      class and on the endpoint-N source class under consideration.
 (ii) Construct the current T outputs needed by R using those strict
      earlier bounds, including actual new boundary responses and their
      dated private blocks. This does not use the yet-unborn R'_k gradient.
(iii) Use the admitted incoming arrays and the UNNORMALIZED selected
      component construction to bound the new R'_k VALUE by its fresh
      rare majorant on BOTH classes. Every old-private dependence remains
      in its original joint bracket. Hypotheses (A) and (C) are this
      output VALUE conclusion, not the desired output gradient conclusion.
 (iv) Only now define the R'_k gradient by subtracting its actual constant
      value at s=w(z), and apply (1). This supplies chi_k^N for its filing
      into the next gradient record.

The native input theorem in (iii) is still a required mathematical input.
In particular it must bound the whole complex source private block with
the actual dated cost sum and prove the fresh rare token on the enlarged
endpoint-k shape class. One cannot replace (iii) by real boundedness or by
an unsupported claim of generic source analyticity.

4. WHY THIS DOES NOT CREATE A CHI FACTOR FROM A SINGLE MASTER BALL

On a single endpoint-N ball whose gradient radius is already r_N chi_k^N,
the source oscillation has the same size as that radius. Ordinary Cauchy
therefore gives a relative factor of order one, not chi_k^N. The proof
above instead uses endpoint-k radius r_k with the SAME coefficient formula.
That independent supply is the entire reason the small chi survives.

For example a discrete derivative (w(z+h)-w(z))/h has bounded norm on a
fixed sup+Lip_k ball. Evaluating it on a source with Lip_k<=epsilon_k gives
an epsilon_k bound, in agreement with (1). A bound only on the much smaller
Lip_k radius epsilon_k would give no such extra factor by Cauchy alone.

5. FIXED POLYNOMIAL RADIUS AND RETERMINALIZATION

If the source supplier has r(x)=c(1+x)^(-d) with fixed c,d, then choosing
the same finite prefix and estimating it with k as stopping date changes
no finite integral, source source-zero subtraction, own beta, or scalar
normalization. Its estimates must be proved uniformly for floors >=Y;
they cannot use a terminal constant C(Y) as a local coefficient price.
The source-domain change is a change of the available estimate, not a
change of the observable, trajectory, source or field cutoff.

The conclusion is therefore a rigorous CONDITIONAL link needed by the
joint source bootstrap. Whether the actual simultaneous source constructor
satisfies (A)--(C) is to be checked in that bootstrap, with no circular use
of (2) in the preceding primitive.
