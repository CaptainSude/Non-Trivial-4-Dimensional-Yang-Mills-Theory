1. The coefficient that must be used

Let V=Lambda^2 R^4, with ordered orthonormal basis
(12,13,14,23,24,34), and let S=* be the Hodge involution for the
orientation 1234. Remove the nonzero common normalization constant
from the separated Maxwell covariance, and write

 K(x)=|x|^-4 Lambda^2(I-2xx^T/|x|^2),  x!=0.

Thus K(x) is real symmetric and SK(x)=-K(x)S. For k distinct points
define the symmetric six by six matrix

 A_k(x_1,...,x_k)=sum_{pi in S_k}
       K(x_pi1-x_pi2)...K(x_pi(k-1)-x_pik).             (1)

Set A_1=I. Reversing a path transposes its product, proving symmetry.
This is the actual coefficient of the quadratic background in the
Gaussian conditional logarithm, not a vacuum cumulant: if a scalar
quadratic source is placed at each point, the Schur complement
1/2 Y^T A(z)(1-C A(z))^-1 Y has coefficient
1/2 F^T A_k F when all slow background values are the same F.
Powers of the coupling and of the scalar matching coefficient multiply
(1) by nonzero scalar factors. Those factors matter for eventual OPE
normalization, but not for the span proved here. Colour is contracted
with its invariant inner product and does not change this matrix span.

For R in O(4), covariance of K gives exactly

 A_k(R x_1,...,R x_k)=Lambda^2(R) A_k(x_1,...,x_k) Lambda^2(R)^T.

2. Exact span theorem

THEOREM. The real linear span of the matrices (1), over configurations
of at most five distinct points, is all Sym^2(V), of dimension 21.
At most four points do not span it: their pseudoscalar component is zero.

Proof. In the self-dual/anti-self-dual splitting V=V_+ plus V_-,
the symmetric matrices decompose as

 R I plus R S plus Sym^2_0(V_+) plus Sym^2_0(V_-)
               plus Hom(V_-,V_+),
 dimensions 1+1+5+5+9.                              (2)

The last summand means symmetric off-diagonal blocks. A_1 gives I.
For k=2, A_2(0,n)=2 K(n), |n|=1. If P_n=n wedge i_n is the
orthogonal projection on n wedge n-perp in V, K(n)=I-2P_n.
The natural action S0^[2] of S0=nn^T-I_4/4 on two-forms is
P_n-I_6/2. Thus K(n)=-2 S0^[2]. The matrices nn^T-I_4/4 span
Sym^2_0 R^4, and the map S0 -> S0^[2] is injective: diagonalize
S0, and note that all pairwise eigenvalue sums can vanish only
when every eigenvalue vanishes. Its image has dimension nine and
is exactly the symmetric Hodge-odd summand of (2).

For k=3, take (0,e_1,e_2). Direct rational multiplication gives

 A_3=diag(3,-2,-2,-2,-2,3).                           (3)

It commutes with S. Its trace is -2, and after subtracting (-1/3)I
its restrictions to BOTH V_+ and V_- are nonzero traceless symmetric
matrices with eigenvalues (10/3,-5/3,-5/3). Under SO(4), the two
three-dimensional rotation actions on V_+ and V_- vary independently;
equivalently Spin(4)=SU(2)xSU(2) acts through SO(3)xSO(3).
Averaging the orbit over one factor kills that factor's traceless
matrix and leaves the other. The rotation orbit of any nonzero
traceless symmetric three by three matrix spans Sym^2_0 R^3:
diagonal permutations span the traceless diagonals and planar
rotations supply the off-diagonal entries. Hence the orbit of (3),
together with I, supplies both five-dimensional summands separately.
At this point the span is the 20-dimensional S-orthogonal subspace.

For k=5 take

 x_1=0, x_2=e_1, x_3=2e_2, x_4=3e_3, x_5=4e_4.

With exactly the convention (1), finite rational arithmetic gives

 Tr(S A_5)=313318719/648669531250000 != 0.             (4)

Equation (4) is independently reproducible without floating point:
for each edge vector x use the four by four rational matrix
I_ij=delta_ij-2 x_i x_j/(sum x_l^2); its exterior square entries are
I_ik I_jl-I_il I_jk; divide by (sum x_l^2)^2, multiply the four
edge matrices for each of the 120 permutations, sum, and contract
with S_(ij),(kl)=epsilon_ijkl. The exact computation is retained in
scalar_fusion_tensor_rank_check.py --exact. Subtracting from A_5
its already-spanned 20-dimensional component gives a nonzero multiple
of S. This completes the full span.

For the necessity of five points, any configuration of at most four
points lies in an affine hyperplane. Reflection in that hyperplane
fixes every point, so covariance implies A_k commutes with its induced
exterior-square reflection. That reflection conjugates S to -S.
Consequently Tr(S A_k)=-Tr(S A_k)=0. This also proves the missing
pseudoscalar conclusion without numerical rank arguments. QED.

3. Why this matters, and the exact unproved bridge

The established scalar continuum covariance is available before tensor
covariance. The theorem shows that scalar products have enough leading
quadratic information to calibrate every curvature-pair direction,
including the pseudoscalar which a total constant-source action misses.
It rules out a purely algebraic obstruction to obtaining this sector
from scalar fusion. It DOES NOT prove that the quantum field algebra
generated by the constructed scalar contains these point fields.

The needed statement is an actual fusion remainder: after identity
subtractions and the appropriate finite mixing, a family of localized
scalar products with diameter r is approximated by its 21-dimensional
renormalized quadratic projection, uniformly against arbitrary exterior
words, or in a common heat-damped / Hamiltonian graph norm. One useful
target, with all short-distance normalization factors included in F_r,
is

 || e^-tH [F_r(f)-sum_a C_a(r) Q_a(f)] e^-tH ||
       <= C_(t,f) r^epsilon (1+|log r|)^p,            (5)

for fixed t>0 and some epsilon>0; equivalent sufficiently strong
mixed correlation estimates could replace this norm. Finite-dimensional
mixing matrices and their inverses may grow polynomially in |log r|.
Thus a positive power of r pays their losses, but a merely o(1) vacuum
comparison does not give (5).

There is a concrete possible advantage over an independent tensor source
construction. These fusion marks are derivatives of the SAME scalar
source producer, so their literal original independent-pair extensions
and large-field ancestry already exist. Causal reterminalization at the
fusion date should give a fixed polynomial coupling bound for each fixed
number of marks. The regular full quadratic projection and its stable
graph can then be used on the resulting composite mark. What remains
to prove is compatibility of that projection with original-tube reentry,
the full mixed-history operation, and arbitrary endpoint estimates.
The scalar vacuum four-point comparison is insufficient for this task;
the five-point OPEN quadratic coefficient is also distinct from the
vanishing free odd vacuum cumulant.

Even after (5), a normalization issue remains: multiplying a covariant
family by an arbitrary noncommuting matrix can change its declared tensor
law. One must establish an invariant finite field space and its actual
rotation representation, and choose an intertwining basis, or prove
covariant fusion counterterms directly. The rank theorem alone does not
authorize conjugating away this issue. These are next proof obligations,
not assumptions labelled as completed Yang--Mills construction.
