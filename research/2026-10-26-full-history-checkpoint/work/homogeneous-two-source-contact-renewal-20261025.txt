HOMOGENEOUS TWO-SOURCE CONTACT RENEWAL
25 October 2026. Original roots, full contact values and finite-period order.

RESULT AND SCOPE

For the admitted homogeneous ordinary chain, the strong body bound gives
a complete two-source renewal estimate, including the scalar part of
every contact. At separated ORIGINAL source footprints its positive
contact majorant is uniform in the ultraviolet horizon, relative to the
two genuine initial-entry/core-growth factors. The original source norms
and their calibration are not removed. This is not a mixed-history,
retained-component, coincident-composite or continuum nontriviality theorem.

The proof uses the actual complete protected one-/two-mark columns, the
new augmented source map, the fixed-core replay, and the strong body on
the SAME common class D_j(b_j). It adds (i) a full-value scalar norm,
(ii) the running-radius core contact bound, (iii) the two-arm geometric
bookkeeping, and (iv) a summable separated-root contact recurrence.

1. OPERATORS, DOMAINS AND NUMERICAL INPUTS

Fix a finite admitted prefix i,...,n. Put x_j=g_j^-2,

 b_j=c_b c x_j^-1/2(log x_j)^q,
 |x_(j+1)-x_j|<=B,
 x_j>=x_n+lambda(n-j)-C0,       x_j>=Y.                  (1)

All original cuts, private pivots, field/current reads, localizer contours
and normalizers are those of the existing homogeneous source modules.
Let C_j be the normalized fixed-core source operator at the SAME actual
numerical coupling history, S_j its zero-noise map, and A_j the complete
actual homogeneous ordinary source operator. The latter contains every
centered R cohort, young as well as mature. Set E_j=A_j-C_j.

The strong body gives, with its fixed-prefix error retained,

 chi_j=C_K x_j^-s+e_(N,K)(P),
 q_j=C_bod b_j^2 chi_j,
 ||E_j H||_birth <= q_j ||H||_current.                  (2)

The constant includes preparation and the restored output norm. The
prepared body-amplitude ball also gives the two-root contact difference
bound in Section 3 below. The actual source family must be admitted;
boundedness of an arbitrary real observable does not supply this input.

Choose all exponents and arm margins in the native columns once. A useful
explicit sufficient allocation is

 kappa_hi+8 mu_* < a_L(kappa_lo-ell_2-epsilon_mom),       (3)

and the corresponding one-mark and base-cluster inequalities. Here
ell_2 includes the complete two-seed/exclusion/localizer costs,

All these constants belong to the fixed Pi before the target power K.
An inequality with no spare arm/moment margin would not suffice.

2. FULL VALUE PROPAGATION, INCLUDING THE SCALAR

Retain the original external source labels, including the two labels of
an already formed contact. Let sigma_j be evaluation at the flat pair
on this coherent homogeneous source germ, with its original root/profile
weights. This is a bounded linear functional on the positive coefficient
norm. Constants are coalesced only at fixed original labels, not summed
as a new current-lattice census. Because Psi_j(flat,0)=flat,

 sigma_(j+1) S_j F=sigma_j F,        S_j 1=C_j 1=A_j 1=1. (4)

Use the finite-horizon full norm

 ||F||_full=|sigma F|_profile+||F-(sigma F)1||_orbit.      (5)

The second term is the previously constructed homogeneous centered
static-orbit norm. The scalar profile norm retains the required original
endpoint/constructor-arm weights; in particular a scalar contact retains
its stored pair joining weight. Coalescing constants never resets those
weights or replaces them by an unweighted scalar sum. Thus S is
nonexpansive in (5). The original fixed-core
quantum column controls BOTH its scalar and centered outputs by
C g_j^2/rho^2 on the fixed-radius class. Consequently the full fixed-core
propagator obeys

 ||C_[m,u) H||_full <= C h(r_u) W_(m,u) ||H||_birth,
 W_(m,u)=(X_u/X_m)^p,  X_j=x_j+c_*,
 h(r)=max(1,(rho/r)^nu).                                (6)

Here p is a sufficiently large fixed exponent, including the scalar
output constant. It may be larger than the earlier centered-only p.
Pre-entry uses the already FULL value column K_col L^4, so
nu=log(max(1,K_col L^4))/log Lambda_s, Lambda_ratio<Lambda_s<L.
Conversion to (5) is made once at entry and does not change nu.

The linear column treats an already formed Gamma_H as ONE insertion.
Its two original labels are passive indices in the positive kernel,
which is summed before the profiles. There is one adjacent L^4 census,
not a new independent pair-root census at every step. Its entire joined
carrier is retained. Thus (6) applies to full contacts with the same
linear column constants. It is not a claim that two separate insertions
have the one-seed generation cost.

The exact one-mark Duhamel proof now applies in the full norm (5), since
(2) bounds the full output as well. For a source available on radius r_u
at date u (r_u>=b_u),

 ||A_[m,u)H||_full <= C h(r_u) W_(m,u) exp(Q_(u,m)) ||H||,
 ||(A_[m,u)-C_[m,u))H||_full
     <=C h(r_u) W_(m,u)[exp(Q_(u,m))-1] ||H||,
 Q_(u,m)<=C sum_(j=u)^(m-1) H_(j+1) q_j,
 H_j=h(b_j).                                            (7)

This does bound the homogeneous scalar ledger relative to the SAME
initial entry and core-growth benchmark. It does not give an absolute
UV bound for an uncalibrated initial source. A scalar depending on a
live exterior/history variable is not a constant for (4); such mixed
states are outside this theorem.

3. THE RUNNING-RADIUS CORE CONTACT AND THE BODY CORRECTION

Write F^n=F(Psi_j(b,g_j x)), F^0=F(Psi_j(b,0)), and Delta F=F^n-F^0.
Suppose F and G are available on radii r_F,r_G and their next radii have
ratio at most Lambda_s. The map theorem supplies noise balls c_nu r_F,
c_nu r_G on the same complete scalar contours. The enlarged ORIGINAL cut
satisfies lambda_cut g_j R_j<c_nu min(r_F,r_G)/2.

Radial Cauchy followed by the exact noise-line integral gives

 |Delta F_H|<=C (g_j/r_F) M_H X_H(x),
 |Delta G_J|<=C (g_j/r_G) M_J X_J(x).                    (8)

X_H is the complete prepared CURRENT-noise seminorm; it includes the
pivot and current branches. This uses a strictly larger noise ball and
introduces no fine-coordinate count. The marked variables stay inside
the protected real cuts before exterior Gaussian integration.

For the normalized core law,

 K^C_j(F,G)=Cov_C(F^n,G^n)=Cov_C(Delta F,Delta G).         (9)

Use the protected connected two-mark column on (8). Its Gaussian moment
degree is two; equivalently the earlier linear-plus-quadratic expansion
uses moments through degree four. The current-carrier polynomial is paid
by epsilon_mom in (3). Connected denominator cancellation is performed
before absolute values. This proves the complete positive bound

 ||K^C_j(F,G)||_birth,2
       <=C g_j^2/(r_F r_G) M_F M_G.                    (10)

The output lives on radius min(r_F,next,r_G,next). In particular two
fixed-radius cohorts generate a fixed-radius contact with cost
C g_j^2/rho^2. They are not intrinsically new fresh-radius sources.
No parity assumption is needed for the two oscillation factors in (8).
The actual old score and nonzero normalizer remain those used to admit
the protected moment/cluster column.

Let K^A_j be the same covariance with the actual normalized kernel.
The exact identity and the previously proved body-amplitude Cauchy give

 K^A_j(F,G)-K^C_j(F,G)
   =integral_0^1 kappa_(3,t)(F^n,G^n,Delta D_j) dt,
 ||K^A_j-K^C_j||_birth,2<=q_j M_F M_G.                 (11)

This latter contact is born on the common fresh output b_(j+1).
It retains its scalar, both source profiles and the connected carrier.
No third arbitrary mark is exponentiated: the quantitative estimate is
the uniform two-mark compiler on the prepared BODY amplitude ball.

4. ORIGINAL ENDPOINT ARMS: OVERLAPPING EVOLVED READS ARE ALLOWED

The separation estimate is NOT the distance between the current live
reads alone. For every initial source label p, retain its entire initial
footprint and its designated original anchor. A current coefficient has
its complete own read PLUS a comparison carrier joining that read to the
current coarse image of this footprint/anchor. Every propagated scalar
retains the same external anchor. For a contact both anchors are retained.

This bookkeeping is supplied by the COMPLETE positive source columns:
the protected seed contains the old complete source carrier before
localization/integration; an output construction carrier contains its
coarse image and all added operator, current, pivot and cluster reads.
One does not shrink this carrier after a cancellation makes part of it
independent of the live field. A two-mark connected construction contains
both seeds and their joining cluster. Thus its full comparison carrier
connects the two original endpoint images even when the live reads have
already spread and overlap.

Equivalently retain the arm histories explicitly. A stage-k arm of length
ell_k has length at most C L^(k-j) ell_k in grade-j units. The native
charged length ell_k is at least this rescaled length for k<=j, up to
fixed cover constants. The deterministic parent-copy displacements cost
at most C sum_(k<=j)L^(k-j), uniformly bounded. The union of the two arms
and their current joining cluster therefore has length at least a fixed
multiple of the distance between the ORIGINAL footprint images, minus
a fixed constant. Weighted convolution of the positive kernels uses
the spare exponent (3); it never discards a previous arm when a new
source date is assigned.

No extra fixed multiplier is charged at every static date: the total
copy-displacement exponential is bounded by this geometric sum.
Quantum/rare arms use their actual supported positive columns and keep
their g_j^2 or q_j factors. The fixed constants/exponents in (6)--(7) are
taken in these anchored spaces. This is the same complete-carrier
construction with a fixed unused arm margin, not a conclusion from an
unrooted supremum or a final visible support.

Consequently (10)--(11), for fixed original labels p,q, have an additional
factor

              exp[-mu D_(j+1)(p,q)],                    (12)

where D_(j+1) is their original footprint separation in output-grade
units, with the bounded parent/mother corrections included in constants.
The positive remaining norm still has the required future kappa_hi.
This follows by paying (12) from the extra mu_* in (3), rather than from
the exponent needed for subsequent transport.

This complete-seed convention is essential. If a proposed compiler
forgets the old endpoint arm, (12) is not supplied by a bound involving
only dist(current live read F,current live read G). No retained-core
shortcut from the outer R operation is used here.

5. EXACT CONTACT RECURRENCE AND TRUE COHORT BIRTH DATES

Let F_j=A_[j,i)F_i and G_j=A_[j,i)G_i. For a pre-existing logarithmic
mixed source Gamma_i the exact recursion is

 Gamma_(j+1)=A_j Gamma_j+K^A_j(F_j,G_j),
 Gamma_n=A_[n,i)Gamma_i
            +sum_(j=i)^(n-1) A_[n,j+1)K^A_j(F_j,G_j).   (13)

The first term and every contact in the sum contain their full scalar
values. Equation (13) is not a product of the two transported means.

For a more precise radius ledger write the exact one-source cohorts

 F_j=sum_(u=i)^j F_(u;j),
 F_(u;j)=C_[j,u) B^F_u,
 B^F_i=F_i,  B^F_u=E_(u-1)F_(u-1) for u>i,             (14)

and likewise for G. A fresh cohort u has radius
r_(u;j)=min(rho,Lambda_s^(j-u)b_u); an initially wider source uses its
actual initial radius. Each such radius contains the current b_j.
Then the core part of (13) is the finite sum of

 K^C_j(F_(u;j),G_(v;j)),       u,v<=j.                  (15)

The contact (15) is born at j+1, with source radii r_(u;j),r_(v;j)
in (10), and with output radius
r_c=min(r_(u;j+1),r_(v;j+1)). Its remaining entry cost is h(r_c),
not automatically H_(j+1). The actual-body difference (11) is a separate
fresh contact. Existing contacts propagate linearly by A and are not
regenerated as a product. Formulas (13)--(15) retain every true birth date.

The resulting sharper majorant is the sum over j,u,v of

 C exp(Q) W_(n,j+1) h(r_c)
   [g_j^2/(r_(u;j)r_(v;j))]
   ||F_(u;j)|| ||G_(v;j)|| exp[-mu D_(j+1)],             (16)

plus the analogous fresh term
C exp(Q) W_(n,j+1) H_(j+1) q_j ||F_j||||G_j||
exp[-mu D_(j+1)], and the initial-contact term. Every norm in this formula
is the positive anchored coefficient norm with its original weights.

6. A CLOSED COARSER BOUND AT FIXED PHYSICAL SEPARATION

For a uniform bound it is legitimate to restrict every (15) to b_(j+1)
and pay H_(j+1). This loses efficiency but not the exact scalar or roots.
Take p common to the linear one-source/contact bounds. Put

 Q= C sum_(j=i)^(n-1) H_(j+1) q_j,
 W_(n,i)=(X_i/X_n)^p,
 U_i=h(r_F,i) h(r_G,i) ||F_i|| ||G_i||.                 (17)

Equations (7),(10)--(13) give

 ||Gamma_n||_full,2
 <=C h(r_Gamma,i) W_(n,i)e^Q ||Gamma_i||
   +C U_i W_(n,i)^2 e^(3Q) [B_n^C(p,q)+B_n^D(p,q)],    (18)

where the first term is omitted if Gamma_i=0 and

 B_n^C=sum_(j=i)^(n-1) H_(j+1) [g_j^2/b_j^2]
                                      exp[-mu D_(j+1)(p,q)],
 B_n^D=sum_(j=i)^(n-1) H_(j+1) q_j
                                      exp[-mu D_(j+1)(p,q)]. (19)

Indeed W_(n,j+1)W_(j,i)^2/W_(n,i)^2
=(X_n/X_j)^p (X_(j+1)/X_j)^p is uniformly bounded by (1).
This is the precise two-leg core-growth cancellation. The estimate does
not claim a lower bound on the actual signed core covariance; B_n^C is
an explicit positive core CONTACT BENCHMARK.

For separated original compact footprints at physical distance d>0,
let ell_n be the final physical cell length and d_*=d/ell_n. Compatible
ordinary blocks give the bound below. Here d is their ACTUAL physical
torus/native distance at the finite regulator, or a common injective
placement whose distance agrees with it. An unwrapped Euclidean distance
across a shorter periodic seam cannot be used.

 D_(j+1)>=c d_* L^(n-j-1)-C.                            (20)

Let m=n-j-1. Bounded signed increments imply
x_j<=x_n+B(m+1). Using H_j<=C x_j^(nu/2) and
g_j^2/b_j^2<=C(log x_j)^(-2q)<=C gives the concrete bound

 B_n^C <= C X_n^(nu/2) Phi_(nu/2)(c d_*),
 Phi_A(t)=sum_(m>=0)(1+m)^A exp[-mu t L^m].              (21)

Phi_A(t) is finite for every t>0. It is at most
C[1+log_+(1/t)]^(A+1) as t decreases to zero, and decays exponentially
for t>=1 after enlarging a fixed constant. Thus when x_n is in the
fixed positive-coupling stopping window and ell_n is the fixed stopping
resolution, (21) is independent of the UV horizon. The bounds on root
profiles are applied before summing p,q. For separated profile supports,
their minimum gap supplies d; otherwise retain the kernel (19).

The body part obeys B_n^D<=Q. Its rare contribution is uniformly
summable already when s>nu/2, since

 H_(j+1)q_j^rare
  <=C x_j^(-1-s+nu/2)(log x_j)^[q(2-nu)].               (22)

Therefore the same fixed choice K>max(6,4+nu) closes (18), with the full
source-entry/core-growth benchmark retained. The contact separation
provides the additional summability of the nonrare Gaussian/core term.
This argument does not settle the coincident limit d_*=0.

7. ACTUAL-MINUS-CORE CONTACT STABILITY

Let Gamma^C be defined by (13) with C and the core-transported initial
marks, using the same Gamma_i. Let F^A-F^C=f and G^A-G^C=g. Bilinearity
gives the EXACT identity at every step

 K^C(F^A,G^A)-K^C(F^C,G^C)
             =K^C(f,G^A)+K^C(F^C,g).                  (23)

Subtract the two versions of (13). Apply (7) to the suffix difference
on every core-born contact and apply (23) to the two incoming leg
differences. The positive bounds above give

 ||Gamma^A_n-Gamma^C_n||_full,2
 <=C h(r_Gamma,i)W_(n,i)(e^Q-1)||Gamma_i||
   +C U_i W_(n,i)^2 e^(C Q)
                     [(e^Q-1)B_n^C+B_n^D].             (24)

Thus the new body controls a genuine two-source/contact comparison,
including scalar contacts, relative to the explicitly displayed core
benchmark. There is no replacement by Cov of products of separate means.

On a stronger permitted period diagonal with e_(N,K)(P)<=min_j x_j^-s,
define
 zeta=sup_j q_j/(g_j^2/b_j^2)
      <=C_K sup_(x>=Y) x^(-s-1)(log x)^(4q).            (25)

Then B_n^D<=zeta B_n^C. Both zeta and the rare Q tend to zero when the
fixed weak floor is increased. At arbitrary admitted finite P, retain
B_n^D and the weighted error sum instead of claiming (25).

8. PERIOD ORDER, ENTRY NORMS AND FINAL COVARIANCE

For each fixed prefix and fixed K the error e_(N,K)(P) tends to zero.
Choose P afterward so sum H_(j+1)b_j^2 e_(N,K)(P) meets the desired
renewal tolerance and all prior body/image repairs. The stronger
datewise choice used in (25) is also possible. No source-function limit,
rate uniform in N, or fixed-period-side statement is inferred.

Necessary initial data are the genuine complete own-domain norms of
F_i,G_i (including their original profile/calibration and root measures),
their actual starting radii, the original anchored/footprint geometry,
and the full two-root norm of any initial Gamma_i. To obtain an absolute
UV bound, not merely this relative one, one must also control
h(r_F,i)h(r_G,i)W_(n,i)^2 ||F_i||||G_i||. An arbitrary real bound by one
does not control that analytic entry quantity. Any required original
reflection-time locality must be verified for the actual initial probes.

For a final admitted positive terminal law nu, the physical connected
quantity is

      nu Gamma_n + Cov_nu(F_n,G_n),                     (26)

with conjugation inserted in the first source for the sesquilinear
version. The terminal covariance in (26) is not discarded or estimated
by (18) alone. It has the elementary full-value bound when that suffices;
any further terminal spatial decay or reflection/nonzero limit requires
its actual terminal law. The analytic estimates above use complex
bilinear cumulants and make no positivity assertion for complex kernels.

All conclusions are restricted to the homogeneous ordinary chain. They
do not transport new total cohorts through partial native mixed B sums,
retained T'' operations, or arbitrary exterior/history-dependent scalars.
