RESULT AND SCOPE

There is an exact, source-preserving finite-prefix construction on
the SAME admitted native T/R operations. Each original plaquette
source is inserted once, before the FIRST integration which removes
one of its original arguments. At a native basic-R replacement the
source remains in the OLD integral of Large-I(1.100); the normalized
reference is frozen. Therefore the original-source jets after that
replacement are the old conditional means/covariance, not the jets
of an observable evaluated on the new reference field.

The four raw coefficients and their logarithmic counterparts give an
exact finite two-jet recursion, including scalar and mixed terms.
The existing quantitative protected-coefficient module also extends
to two admitted coefficient marks on its same fixed typed domains.
This yields a concrete finite-step continuation of the TURN73
private first-healing output, without an inverse raw mass.

This does NOT supply a cutoff-uniform complete source norm. A source
which straddles the first regular integration needs its actual joint
prepared coefficient; it is not a TURN73 private mark. The existing
arbitrary-coefficient continuation has inverse-radius costs. An
improved fixed-core quantum estimate cannot automatically be applied
to a general healed R or live B input on its different domain.

2. THE BASIC-R STEP HAS AN EXPLICIT OLD-SOURCE FORMULA

Fix one actual component/choice branch. Let x be its integrated

u the retained data. Large-I(1.100) supplies the literal product

        d tau(u,x,xi) q_u(dy),        integral q_u(dy)=1,  (2.1)

remain in d tau. The exterior admission gate chi_(k,Lambda)

distinction is fixed by the primary formula.

If the old density at this step has logarithmic jet

       d tau exp[e X+h Y+eh Z],                            (2.2)

where X,Y,Z may depend on x,xi, then its new density is exactly

       q_u(dy) integral_(x,xi) d tau exp[e X+h Y+eh Z].    (2.3)

Condition on the entire u so no unread old argument is erased.
Write K_u for the normalized OLD integral in (2.3). Then the
new logarithmic jet is

 X'=K_u X,   Y'=K_u Y,
 Z'=K_u Z+K_u(XY)-(K_u X)(K_u Y).                         (2.4)

At fixed u, all three quantities are independent of the fresh y.
This is a concrete consequence of the actual R formula, stronger
than an unspecified total-mass identity. It prevents attributing
fresh reference fluctuations to an original source. A subsequent
classification of y is performed after q_u, at its actual date;
its future indicator is not pulled back into the old integral.
This statement is CHOICEWISE, or with the actual coordinate-choice
label included in u. If different choice branches with different
reference kernels are subsequently mixed and their label is
forgotten, the old posterior may depend on y through that mixture.
No fresh-y independence after such marginalization is asserted.

The normalization in K_u is used here to describe an exact
conditional expectation. It is NOT used to estimate a raw marked

proof continues to use unnormalized operations and connected logs.

For the full coordinate average, use the source's declared

3. FIRST ELIMINATION, FIRST HEALING, AND SOURCES CROSSING REGIONS

Use the actual chronological list of integration operations in
the finite admitted representation. An operation can integrate a
whole group of variables simultaneously. Keep the complete list
of original variables which remain literal before each operation,
including any inverse coordinate records required to reconstruct
their original values. Localization and algebraic exponentiation
alone are not an elimination of an original field argument.

For each original plaquette p, assign its emission time tau(p)
to the FIRST actual integral which removes any of its still-literal
four-link original read. If no such operation occurs before the
chosen endpoint, the original function remains a terminal read.
The elimination sets are already determined on the actual branch
before that integral; this is not a future-event test introduced
retroactively. A fixed order resolves any simultaneous operations
or the whole simultaneous integral can be treated as one operation.

At tau(p), insert the whole original plaquette function into that
integral, with EVERY remaining argument left in its real slot.
Afterward remove p from the uninserted-source list. Its conditional
source coefficient is now inherited by later operations. It is
never emitted a second time when a current carrier enlarges or is
assigned another birth/maturity label.

This is a deferred evaluation of(1.2), not a second insertion of
the source. Indeed its finite algebra factorizes exactly as
product_p exp[e F_p+h H_p]. Before tau(p) none of that factor's
arguments is being integrated, so it can be carried outside the
actual integral unchanged. At tau(p) it must remain inside it.
This algebraic/Fubini observation proves equivalence with inserting
all sources at the start. A gauge substitution carries its complete
old read as in Section1; it does not replace the factor by a new one.

This describes a pathwise partition of source ownership. It does
not assert that the random ownership pieces are independent.
In particular, for two sources, their product is not partitioned
as a sum of only equal-date products: all pairs of emission times
are retained by multiplication in the four-coefficient algebra.

There are three importantly different cases.

 It is a JOINT typed source coefficient. One cannot allocate

 (iii) A source already emitted in case(i) or(ii) subsequently
 meets a raw component. It enters as an INHERITED coefficient

For a fixed wholly private lineage the first-healing partition

4. THE EXACT FOUR-COEFFICIENT RECURSION ON THE FULL NATIVE MAP

For clarity, take a complete source-free retained state containing
every variable and discrete record still needed by future native
operations. At an actual positive integration or basic-R step,
write its fixed linear map as L_j. This is the map just identified
by the original integration slots, not a selected-sector map.
All internal changes of representation are exact identities for
the following four coefficients.
Only these actual positive maps are conditional kernels below.
A signed walk or Mayer localization label is not a stochastic
transition or a positive event; its expansion is an exact
representation of the same coefficient after the positive step.

Let rho_j>0 be the source-free density on its admitted support and
write the sourced density as

       rho_j exp[e X_j+h Y_j+eh Z_j]
       =rho_j[1+e X_j+h Y_j+eh(Z_j+X_jY_j)].              (4.1)

The raw four entries are sent linearly through L_j. With

 rho_(j+1)=L_j rho_j,
 K_j A=L_j(rho_j A)/rho_(j+1),                            (4.2)

on the positive output support, one obtains exactly

 X_(j+1)=K_j X_j,
 Y_(j+1)=K_j Y_j,
 Z_(j+1)=K_j Z_j+Cov_(K_j)(X_j,Y_j).                      (4.3)

At first emission one may instead keep latent sources and write
X_j=D_j+A_j, Y_j=E_j+B_j, where A_j,B_j are the newly emitted
original functions in that very integral. The third formula is

 Z_(j+1)=K_j Z_j+Cov_(K_j)(D_j+A_j,E_j+B_j).               (4.4)

Its four covariance terms retain old/new cross-source collisions.
In particular an original plaquette spanning the integration
boundary is not replaced by its exterior baseline before (4.4).
Any still-unemitted source sums R_j,S_j are measurable in the
retained output of this operation and pass through unchanged.
Thus the complete one-jets are R_j+K_j(D_j+A_j) and
S_j+K_j(E_j+B_j), respectively. They add zero conditional
covariance to(4.4), because they are constant in that conditional
integral; they are not deleted from the later source or its means.

Equations(4.1)--(4.3) also follow by applying the literal local
steps to the positive original-record lift. They give the actual
finite-prefix conditional means and covariance on the resulting
retained state. The native localizations and connected logarithms
compute representations of these quantities, not different
independently sourced kernels.

At the final integral the mixed log coefficient is consequently

 [eh] log Z_final(e,h)
   = E_final Z_n + Cov_final(X_n,Y_n)
   = Cov_original(F,H).                                  (4.5)

All original scalar normalizers are included. No field-constant
piece of Z_n can be dropped as a vacuum convention. Gauge fixing,
reference factors and source-free normalization do not change
(4.5) when their old-variable slots are treated as in Section2.

This construction is finite and exact at the admitted positive
prefix. It does not establish a uniform norm for the conditional
means. Supremum or L2 contraction of a positive conditional
expectation does not by itself estimate a local analytic carrier
norm, and does not remove a divergent original coincident norm.

7. WHAT FINITE CONTINUATION BOUNDS ACTUALLY FOLLOW

For a fixed number m of later steps, (6.4) iterates to a finite
bound in the same typed system. It includes the actual young-R
waiting/maturity dates and every relative-boundary read; none is
renamed a fresh initial source. The corresponding dated Volterra
response has the fixed polynomial/exponential kernels of TURN53.
Two marks can meet at any later step. Summing their collision date
adds a finite factor and multiplies two one-mark response chains.

For a source emitted at i and endpoint j, a coarse envelope of
the resulting two-jet costs is therefore

 C poly(2+gamma_i+gamma_j,1+j-i)
               exp[C(j-i) log(2+gamma_j+j-i)].            (7.1)

Increasing C includes the two response branches and their meeting
date. The elementary proof uses the same strictly increasing
dated chains as native_winding_propagation_turn53.txt Section1;
there are at most exponentially many compositions of an age,
and each visited factor is polynomial in the endpoint coupling
and age. It does not assert contraction of the actual map.

For the private class, multiply(7.1) by its first-healing input,
including beta_0^-K and its native current suppression. This is
a finite-depth bound on a genuinely source-attached continuation.
It is not the cutoff-uniform estimate required for existence.

In particular, no FIXED K makes beta_0^-K control (7.1) when
beta_0 is comparable to ultraviolet depth n and j-i is comparable
to n. Even retaining the stronger initial factor
exp[-c(log beta_0)^(2p)] does not control exp[C n log n].
The ratio of the latter logarithmic exponent to the former tends
to infinity. Taking K dependent on n would also change the
constants B_(m,K); TURN73 states no uniform such estimate.

This is a limitation of this upper-bound calculus, not evidence
that the actual native source is unstable. The positive posterior
and coefficient norm estimates measure different things. To
improve(7.1), an actual source-specific stable/marginal transport
estimate or a sharper quantum gain is required.

8. THE FIXED-CORE GAIN MUST NOT BE ASSIGNED TO EVERY HEALED INPUT

There is already a precise source distinction in the archive:
balaban_normalized_remainder_turn18.txt Section2 records B109's
fixed positive field radii, whereas B119(2.28), printed259, uses
running radii g_j C(log g_j^-2)^q. Its Sections3C--4 give explicit
locally gauge-invariant moving-ball examples. Thus analyticity
on the smaller native domain alone is not a fixed-radius bound.

An elementary instance shows why the distinction matters for a
proposed universal mixed quantum estimate. If a local analytic
norm only controls a ball of radius r_g=g L_g, consider a scalar
analytic germ D_g(A)=(A/r_g)^m, with fixed m>=1. For a centered
nondegenerate Gaussian fluctuation A=g xi,

 Var[D_g(g xi)]=L_g^(-2m) Var(xi^m).                       (8.1)

For polylogarithmic L_g this is not O(g^2) times its unit analytic
norm squared. Gauge-invariant analogues use powers of a closed
plaquette class function, as in TURN18 Section4A. This is a norm
implication example, not a claim about the actual original source.

The new fixed-core/native inner-window work may yield O(g^2)
quantum derivatives for its specifically controlled fixed-radius
class, with explicit polylogarithmic and annulus terms. That would
improve the corresponding entries of(6.4). It does not supply an
extension of an arbitrary healed R or mixed live B coefficient to
that larger domain. The static classical pullback, the finite low
jets, and boundary/interior feedback must also be controlled on
the same actual source family.

9. EXACT REMAINING ESTIMATE, AFTER THE CHRONOLOGY IS FIXED

The next estimate concerns the source already emitted into
regular or healed coefficients, on its actual original domains,
with the jointly source-labelled symmetries and all static jets.
One must control its complete chronological continuation without
the repeated worst-case inverse-radius products of Section7.
For two sources this includes the mixed collision coefficient
B_j(D,E) and its scalar part, as well as the one-mark paths.

The regular-origin source in case(ii) requires its own actual
prepared birth. Replacing it by a source on a fresh coarse or
reference field would change(1.2) and invalidate(4.5).
The private first-healing estimate does not cover that replacement.

Even an estimate of all conditional mixed increments leaves the
terminal term Cov_final(X_n,Y_n) in(4.5). It is the actual full-law
outer covariance. It cannot be removed by keeping the raw vacuum
normalization correct or by smallness of a subset of raw components.

This note therefore advances the exact native source attachment
and its finite two-jet continuation. It does not prove a complete
uniform two-source bound, continuum convergence, or a nonzero
physical continuum witness. Full nontrivial YM4 existence remains
unproved.
