ARBITRARY-DEPTH GAUSSIAN BOUNDARY SCREENING

RESULT AND LIMIT

On the actual aligned fixed-fine-crossing box, with ALL inherited incident
coarse rows retained, let K_K be its direct total-M ordinary-average
Maxwell precision. Compare it to the unrestricted periodic precision
K_per on an auxiliary larger compatible torus, restricted to these rows.
Then, uniformly in M=L^k>=L>=11 and box size,

 D:=K_K-K_per|J >=0,
 ||1_A D 1_B||_(2->2)
       <= C exp[-c(depth(A)+depth(B))].                 (0.1)

Depth is distance in FINAL coarse cells to the original fine boundary,
with a fixed collar subtracted and negative depths replaced by zero.
Consequently

 ||1_A K_K 1_B|| <= C { exp[-c dist(A,B)]
                      +exp[-c(depth(A)+depth(B))] }.     (0.2)

These are genuine arbitrary-depth boundary estimates, not consequences
of spectral comparison alone. The periodic minimizing-lift estimate used
below is reverified from its explicit canceled alias formulas in Sections
2--3; it is not merely inherited from an unaudited symbol assertion.

This does NOT prove exponential locality between two distant shallow
boundary sets. The precise remaining operator is an energy-orthogonal
projection in (5.2). Section7 gives a countertest to inferring its required
tangential locality from positivity, normal screening and spectral bounds.
No finite-g measure, nonflat minimum or nonlinear remainder is considered.

1. GEOMETRY, NORMALIZATION AND PROVENANCE

The final coarse spacing is one; fine spacing is eta=1/M. Fine potentials
are physical potentials, so d_eta includes eta^-1 and the fine energy
uses eta^d. In d=4 this is the rescaled quadratic Wilson energy with no
extra power of eta. Color coordinates are independent copies.

K is a cube made of complete aligned total-M blocks. A fine variation
is free only when BOTH endpoints belong to K. All crossing and exterior
fine variations are zero. The energy includes every plaquette reading a
free edge, including outward plaquettes. J consists of every coarse row
with at least one active endpoint. Its crossing rows are VARIABLES.
Extend fine fields and coarse data by zero outside these sets. A new
freezing of coarse crossing rows would change the assertion.

Embed K in a compatible periodic torus with side at least four times
its diameter plus a fixed collar. All distances below may first be read
in that torus. This torus is a comparison construction; it does not change
the conditional action or exterior values in K. Their tangent is zero.

R_M denotes the derivative at identity of the ACTUAL successive ordinary

2. REVERIFIED PERIODIC PRECISION AND MINIMIZING LIFT

Put I_M={-(M-1)/2,...,(M-1)/2}, p_r=z+2pi r, r in I_M^d. Define

 a_r=prod_nu [M^-1 sum_(s in I_M) exp(i p_(r,nu)s/M)],
 ell_(r,mu)=M^-1 sum_(s=0)^(M-1) exp(i p_(r,mu)s/M),
 omega_(r,mu)=a_r ell_(r,mu),
 u_(r,mu)^+=M(exp(i p_(r,mu)/M)-1),
 u_(r,mu)^-=M(exp(-i p_(r,mu)/M)-1),
 P_r=sum_mu u_(r,mu)^+u_(r,mu)^-,
 d_mu^+=exp(i z_mu)-1,  d_mu^-=exp(-i z_mu)-1.

The superscript minus on a or omega below means evaluation at -p_r,
not Hermitian conjugation at complex z. On real z it is the conjugate.
The physical normalization gives the exact identity

                  omega_(r,mu) u_(r,mu)^+=a_r d_mu^+.

Set A=a_0 a_0^-, g_mu=omega_(0,mu)omega_(0,mu)^-, and

 R_mu=sum_(r!=0) omega_(r,mu)omega_(r,mu)^-/P_r,
 D_mu=g_mu+P_0 R_mu, h_mu=D_mu^-1,
 E=A^-1-sum_mu d_mu^+d_mu^- R_mu/(g_mu D_mu),
 R_s=sum_(r!=0) a_r a_r^-/P_r, D_s=A+P_0 R_s.

Direct subtraction of 1/D_mu and 1/g_mu proves

 d^- h d^+=P_0 E,
 K_per(z)=P_0 h-h d^+d^- h/E.                          (2.1)

There is NO central denominator P_0 in this formula. This cancellation
is necessary because P_0 has a complex null cone arbitrarily near zero.

For input B put S=d^- h B. A straight-average minimizing right inverse
is defined by the following already canceled formulas:

 (H_0 B)_mu=omega_(0,mu)^- h_mu B_mu
       +(u_(0,mu)^+/a_0)(R_mu h_mu-R_s/D_s) S/E;

 H_r B=P_r^-1 {P_0 diag(omega_r^-)h B
       +[u_r^+a_r^-/D_s-diag(omega_r^-)h d^+]S/E},
                                      r!=0.            (2.2)

Here and below products are ordered as written. For real z!=0 these
are equivalently

 F_r=P_r^-1 diag(omega_r^-) K_per B,
 theta=S/(P_0 E),
 t_0=a_0^-/D_s, t_r=P_0 a_r^-/(P_r D_s),
 H_r B=F_r+u_r^+ t_r theta.

The identity sum_r a_r t_r=1 and d^-K_per=0 imply Q^st H=I
and u_r^- F_r=0. The transverse F part has the minimum Maxwell
energy; the other part is an exact fine gradient. Thus

 Q^st H=I,  ||d_eta H B||^2=<B,K_per B>.                (2.3)

One can verify the last equality by first minimizing the diagonal fine
Feynman form with blocked covariance diag(sum omega omega^-/P_r),
then minimizing the resulting coarse form over B+d theta. This yields
exactly(2.1). At z=0 take H_0=I and all high aliases zero. Constant
coarse fields have zero energy; harmonic modes have not been deleted.

3. WHY THE PERIODIC LOCALITY CONSTANTS ARE UNIFORM IN M

Here are the analytic estimates needed for(2.2), including the steps
that would fail if one simply counted M^d aliases.

Use an enlarged real zone |Re z_mu|<pi+delta_0<2pi and a sufficiently
small fixed imaginary width. For r!=0, direct sine estimates, including
the wrapped fine-zone endpoint, give

 |P_r(z)|>=c |r|^2,   |u_r(z)|<=C(1+|r|).

Discrete Fourier orthogonality of the scalar block weights gives
sum_r|a_r|^2<=C; multiplication by a bounded string average gives
sum_r|omega_(r,mu)|^2<=C. The same holds for the minus factors.
Consequently R_mu and R_s are uniformly bounded holomorphic functions.
All central factors are uniformly bounded as well.

On the enlarged REAL zone, a_0 and the central string factors are
uniformly separated from zero. Positivity then gives A,g_mu,D_mu,D_s
bounded above and below by positive constants. For z!=0,

 E=(sum_mu |d_mu|^2/D_mu)/P_0 >=c>0,

because sum|d_mu|^2>=c|z|^2 and P_0<=|z|^2. At zero E=1.
First shrink the complex tube to preserve the real gaps for A,g,D,D_s
using Cauchy derivative bounds; then use the displayed analytic formula
for E and shrink once more to preserve its gap. All shrinkages are fixed,
independent of M. Hence (2.1),(2.2) are uniformly holomorphic and bounded
in one common momentum strip.

For pointwise within-cell norms a stronger alias estimate is useful. Put
w(r)=prod_nu(1+|r_nu|)^-1. The finite geometric sums give

 |a_r|<=Cw(r),
 |omega_(r,mu)|<=Cw(r)/(1+|r_mu|).

Therefore (2.2), and the curl formula

 (d_eta H)_r=P_r^-1 u_r^+ wedge[diag(omega_r^-)K_per]

for high aliases, imply

 ||H_r||+||(d_eta H)_r||<=C w(r)/(1+|r|_infty).         (3.1)

The dyadic shell sum of w(r) is at most C(1+j)^(d-1); the extra
factor 2^-j makes(3.1) absolutely summable, uniformly in M. Even a
factor(1+|r|)^alpha is allowed for every fixed alpha<1.

Reconstruct at a fine location x=n+s/M by

 H_cell(z;s)=sum_r exp(i(z+2pi r).s/M)H_r(z).

On one cell or any fixed physical collar, the absolute sum proves
uniform potential and curl supremum bounds, and physical C^alpha bounds
if wanted. These are bounded holomorphic operator-valued cell symbols.
Under z->z+2pi e_mu aliases relabel modulo M and the reconstruction
phase cancels the relabeling; a wrapped alias has phase exp(i2pi s_mu)=1.
The formulas agree on real overlaps and hence glue holomorphically.
Fourier contour shifting gives

 ||1_x H 1_c||_(coarse component -> cell sup)
 +||1_x d_eta H 1_c||_(coarse component -> cell sup)
       <=C exp[-m dist(x,c)].                          (3.2)

Cell physical L2 follows. Formula(2.1) gives the corresponding exponential
kernel of K_per. Finite tori are exact periodizations; constants and
weighted row/column sums are uniform in their side lengths. This proves
the precise periodic locality input used below; it is more than the
spectral comparison from turn41.

4. LITERAL ORDINARY LIFT AND A BOUNDARY-RESPECTING TRIAL FIELD

The periodic lift above is for Q^st. The actual composed ordinary map
satisfies R_M=Q_M^st+d Psi_M. Besides the earlier exact identity, a
uniform LOCAL bound for Psi_M is needed here.

At an intermediate physical spacing h, the flat endpoint-contour
functional has norm at most C_L h and reads a fixed next-block collar.
Its input in the accumulated correction is the STRAIGHT partial average,
whose physical sup norm is at most one. The gradient covariance of the
ordinary next map simply restricts the previous scalar correction to
its next centers. Thus the correction is a sum of such terms, and
sum_(intermediate h) h<=C_L in final units. Its read radius is also a
fixed final-scale radius. Hence

 ||Psi_M A||_infty<=C_L||A||_infty

with fixed cellwise row/column locality. No C^k product is introduced.
This is also the linear version of the cumulative endpoint construction
in the turn16 proof. That proof supplies the sharper owner-star support
needed for E_M below.

Choose smooth scalar bumps beta_z supported strictly in their final
owner blocks, beta_z(z)=1, with uniform physical C2 bounds. The fine
gradient of lambda=-sum_z beta_z Psi_M(HB)(z) has zero curl and ordinary
average -d Psi_M(HB), by exact endpoint covariance. Therefore

 H_ord B=H B+d_eta lambda

is an ordinary right inverse with the same minimum energy. Its potential
and curl kernels satisfy(3.2), with changed fixed constants. It is NOT a
long rooted-forest representative. Conversely a rooted-feasible field
can be changed by a fine gradient to straight-feasible data of the same
coarse curl, so the two infima agree, as in turn41.

Choose a scalar fine cutoff chi which vanishes in a fixed physical
boundary collar and equals one more than a fixed distance inside K.
It has |d_eta chi|<=C uniformly in M. It is applied to oriented bonds
with one fixed endpoint/midpoint convention; the zero collar includes
their full stencils. Then A0=chi H_ord y belongs to the fixed-crossing
space. The defect

 b=y-R_M A0=R_M[(1-chi)H_ord y]

is zero except in a fixed coarse boundary collar of J. This follows
from the complete row footprint, not from the datum y alone. Rows outside
J vanish for both y and R_M A0. Use the compact whole-star inverse on
these actual rows and set

 A_trial=A0+E_M b,   h_y=A_trial-H_ord y,
                       L y=d_eta h_y.                 (4.1)

All original fine crossings remain fixed, and R_M A_trial=y exactly.
The lift E_M has physical potential/curl bounds and fixed cell overlap;
its owner blocks lie in a fixed boundary collar. Consequently L is
supported, as a curvature field, outside a fixed inner bulk K^deep.
Discrete product rule plus(3.2) and the bounded local R_M,E_M give

 ||1_x L 1_c||_(coarse component -> cell L2)
       <= C exp[-m dist(x,c)]                         (4.2)

and it vanishes when x lies in K^deep. The fixed collar may be enlarged
once to absorb all endpoint, repair and plaquette stencils. No M-dependent
fine-bond census appears: the target norm is physical cell L2.

In this calculation the path/endpoint functionals are applied to the
FINE-POINT SUP bounds of H_ord from the absolutely summable alias estimate,
not to an arbitrary fine L2 field. They are bounded from physical sup
norm because their path lengths are bounded in physical units. Likewise
d_eta(chi H_ord) is estimated by the discrete product rule using the
pointwise potential and curl on a fixed collar, then converted to the
physical cell L2 norm. No uniform arbitrary-L2-to-path trace bound is
assumed; such a bound would be false as M grows.

Define depth(A)=dist(A,(K^deep)^c), with the fixed support offsets
subtracted. Applying exponential row AND column sums to(4.2) gives

                 ||L 1_A||_(2->2)<=C exp[-m' depth(A)]. (4.3)

This is a Schur bound between coarse l2 and the direct sum of physical
fine curl cell spaces. It holds for arbitrary sets A, not merely one
point, after reducing m once to absorb polynomial shell counts.

5. THE EXACT ENERGY-PROJECTION IDENTITY

Work in the finite-dimensional fine CURL Hilbert space, so pure-gauge
null directions cause no inverse or gauge convention issue. Define

 W_K={d_eta a: a is fixed-crossing supported in K, R_M a=0},

and let Pi_K be its orthogonal projection. Every fixed-crossing feasible
field differs from A_trial by an allowed zero-datum field. Thus the
possible curvature corrections to the global minimum are exactly

                           L y+W_K.                   (5.1)

The global minimum curvature d H_ord y is orthogonal to d ker R_M.
In particular it is orthogonal to both Ly and W_K. Subtracting its
energy and minimizing(5.1) proves the EXACT matrix identity

       K_K-K_per|J=L^*(I-Pi_K)L.                       (5.2)

This is the promised place where the actual boundary problem enters.
It follows that D>=0 and, using only ||I-Pi_K||<=1 and(4.3),

 ||1_A D1_B||<=||L1_A|| ||L1_B||
          <=C exp[-m'(depth(A)+depth(B))].              (5.3)

The unique minimizing curvature also obeys

 ||d H_K 1_A-d H_ord 1_A||_(2->2)
            =||(I-Pi_K)L1_A||<=C exp[-m' depth(A)].     (5.4)

No representative-specific potential estimate for H_K is claimed.
Adding the periodic precision kernel bound proves(0.2). The bounds are
uniform over the chosen trial lift and auxiliary large torus. K_K is
the same restricted variational form in each embedding; K_per and its
comparison difference may depend on the auxiliary period. No equality
of those different periodic comparison kernels is claimed.

6. ACTUAL ITERATION CONTENT

The constraint in this result is the direct composition of the ordinary
averages. Exact nested infima from turn41 identify it with the generated
Gaussian precision after any number of those steps, provided intermediate
states retain ALL incident rows. Thus(5.3) is an actual hierarchy bound,
not an estimate for an unrelated straight-average surrogate.

It says that a datum supported deep inside the physical box has an
exponentially small boundary correction in precision and in total
minimum-curvature energy norm. Combined with the periodic locality this
gives a useful bulk comparison on every depth, uniformly in prior UV
refinement. It does not give a uniform exponentially weighted boundary
KKT inverse: boundary-to-boundary entries of Pi_K remain uncontrolled.
The one-step fixed-L finite-range KKT proof from turn40 cannot simply be
reused after many steps without that extra information.

7. A SHARP TEST OF WHAT THE REMAINING PREMISES CANNOT IMPLY

Take N disjoint coarse plaquettes along a boundary layer, spread over a
box of side R, and put u=N^-1/2 sum_p e_p in two-form space. Define

                    D_test=d_c^* u u^* d_c.

Then D_test>=0, D_test d_c(on scalars)=0, and
D_test<=d_c^*d_c. It has only boundary support, so it satisfies every
normal-depth screening statement above. But selected edge entries from
two remote plaquettes have magnitude 1/N, while their separation can be
of order R. With N polynomial in R this is not bounded by C exp(-cR)
uniformly in R. Adding D_test to a local Maxwell precision preserves
uniform energy comparisons and the gauge null space.

This is NOT a counterexample to the actual K_K. It demonstrates that
the already proved spectral, gauge and normal-screening properties do
not determine along-boundary locality. The precise additional task is
to establish a local approximation/weighted estimate for the ACTUAL
Pi_K in(5.2), or an equivalent boundary Schur complement. Its norm-one
property alone is insufficient. No missing boundary estimate has been
hidden in a choice of fine forest.
