3. WHAT REMAINS IF THE FUTURE TEST IS KEPT IN NEW COORDINATES

The exact operator with a test G_H specified in the new coordinates is

The right side shows precisely what is and is not cancelled. If
G_H=G o Psi_H, the integral is independent of this coordinate choice.
If G_H is instead kept numerically fixed, the remaining test changes
under the inverse map. This is relevant when later operations read the
new coordinate or when a proxy leaves their weights unchanged.

For a differentiable path Psi_t of admitted diffeomorphisms, let
v_t(b)=(D_b Psi_t)^-1 dot Psi_t(b). Differentiating the right side of
