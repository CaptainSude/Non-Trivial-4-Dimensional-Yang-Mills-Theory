PERIODIC PHASE KERNELS: A POSITIVE RECURRENCE WITH A SLOW MEMORY WINDOW
18 October 2026. Working lemma; the analytic kernel premise is separate.

Purpose. Periodic root coefficients need not have zero marginal at each
old root merely because the matured cohort has zero marginal. A root
divergence or period-block cancellation may give an age kernel with a
polylogarithmic prefactor. Bounding that prefactor outside every sum
would impose an unnecessarily strong smallness condition. The neutral
bound and the improved bound must first be combined by a minimum.

ASSUMPTIONS AND LEMMA

Let gamma_j>=Gamma, with gamma_u<=gamma_j+D(1+j-u) for u<=j. Work at
aligned endpoints so gamma decreases; fixed within-block comparisons
can be included in constants. Set t=j-u. Suppose the literal positive
kernel of one permanently dated birth satisfies

 k_(j,u) <= C(1+t)^q min{1, C(1+log gamma_u)^r exp(-beta t)}
              + C(1+t)^q exp(-beta t),                         (1)

where all exponents and constants are fixed, beta>0. In particular
the neutral member of the minimum has NO power of the period. Suppose
the actual fluctuation prefactor obeys

 delta_j <= C (log gamma_j)^(-nu),       nu>0.                   (2)

The prefactor delta is the original prepared-difference gain gT/a;
it is not identified with the future-projection parameter g^2/b^2.

Then, for L_j=1+log(1+log gamma_j),

                 sum_(u<=j) k_(j,u) <= C L_j^(q+1).             (3)

Proof. For t<=gamma_j, gamma_u<=C gamma_j. Put
T_j=C_0+C_1 log(1+log gamma_j), choosing C_1>2r/beta. For t<=T_j
use the neutral member; its sum is O((1+T_j)^(q+1)). For
T_j<t<=gamma_j use the improved member and a geometric tail.
For t>gamma_j, gamma_u<=C(1+t), so its improved member is bounded
by C(1+t)^q(1+log(1+t))^r exp(-beta t), whose sum is uniformly
bounded. This proves (3). Fixed additive oscillations change only C.

In particular sup_(gamma>=Gamma) delta(gamma)L(gamma)^(q+1) tends
to zero as Gamma tends to infinity. No condition of the form
delta(gamma)(log gamma)^r<<1 has been used.

POLYNOMIAL ADMISSION FOR AN ARBITRARY SINGLE COHORT

Suppose c_j<=A w_j, with w_j=(gamma_i/gamma_j)^p, and

 m_i<=A,
 m_(j+1)<=C delta_j[c_j+sum_(u=i)^j k_(j,u)m_u]+f_(j+1),         (4)

where |f_(j+1)|<=A w_(j+1), and all quantities in the inequality
are nonnegative majorants. Since w_u<=C w_(j+1), divide by
w_(j+1), use (3), and take the weak floor so the resulting
coefficient of sup_(u<=j)m_u/w_u is <=1/2. Induction gives

                        m_j<=C A w_j.                         (5)

Consequently the already used once-only date-count bound gives
sum_(u=i)^j m_u <=C A(1+j-i)w_j. This costs only a fixed power of
the initial inverse coupling when j-i<=C gamma_i. It neither
redates old activities nor bounds their static values by (5).

DECAY ON THE FUSION-TO-MIDPOINT WINDOW

Suppose on i<=j<=m the couplings lie in [cG,CG], and let
L=1+log(1+log G). For alpha=a/L with a>0 sufficiently small and
fixed, (1) gives the uniform exponential moment

          sum_(u=i)^j exp(alpha(j-u)) k_(j,u)<=C L^(q+1).        (6)

Indeed the neutral range has length O(L), where exp(alpha t)
is bounded by a fixed constant; the remaining geometric tail
has exponent beta-alpha>=beta/2. The same proof as (3) applies.

Assume the compensated physical coefficient obeys
c_j<=A[exp(-alpha_0(j-i))+zeta_G], alpha_0>0 fixed,
and the additional forcing is bounded by
|f_(j+1)|<=C A delta_j zeta_G. Set m_i<=A. Increase G so that
alpha<=alpha_0 and C exp(alpha)delta_* L^(q+1)<1/2, where
delta_*=sup_(i<=j<=m)delta_j. The induction in (4), first with
the weight exp(-alpha(j-i)) and then with the constant weight,
gives

       m_j <= C A[exp(-a(j-i)/L)+zeta_G].                       (7)

For j-i>=c_0G the first term is exp[-cG/(1+log log G)]. Any
fixed exp[C(log G)^d] preparatory loss is absorbed by this
term after reducing c. A native floor zeta_G=G^-K remains a
power-law floor and must be budgeted separately. Multiplying
it by exp[C(log G)^d] is NOT harmless for d>1; such a term must
use a direct native-error estimate without that read loss, or
have a stronger independently established suppression.

SCOPE AND CHECKS STILL REQUIRED

This is a proved scalar majorant lemma under (1)--(4). Applying
it to the actual recursion requires the phase cancellation on
the SAME fine clover and assigned analytic contours, the period
bound, and the neutral estimate without a period prefactor.
The phase mean must be the actual hat-weighted matured marginal.
Masks, profile differences and historical boundary strips stay
in their separate exact ledgers; they are not part of (1) unless
their stated estimates have been proved. Nonlinear mixed groups
still use the original connected expansion and prefix forest.

This lemma fixes the recurrence loss associated with a finite
phase-maturation window. It is not the copied pressure theorem
and is not a Yang--Mills existence theorem.
