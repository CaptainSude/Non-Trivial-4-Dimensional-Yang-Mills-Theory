3. ORIGINAL-SOURCE MIXED DERIVATIVE

Let F,H be real bounded observables on the original fields, interpreted
on the retained positive joint law. Write A=osc(F), B=osc(H), where
osc is essential supremum minus essential infimum on the full law.
The result also applies to bounded joint observables; originality
matters to the intended application, not to the probability algebra.

At every theta, set f=F-E_theta F and h=H-E_theta H. Differentiating
the covariance at this finite regulator gives

 d/dtheta Cov_theta(F,H)=-kappa_theta(N,F,H),
 kappa_theta(N,F,H)=E_theta(N f h)
                       -E_theta N Cov_theta(F,H).            (3.1)

Because N>=0, |f|<=A, |h|<=B and
|Cov_theta(F,H)|<=AB/4, it follows that

 |kappa_theta(N,F,H)| <= (5/4) AB E_theta N.                   (3.2)

Integrating (2.1),(3.2), and using bounded convergence at infinity,
proves

 |Cov_(P^0)(F,H)-Cov_P(F,H)| <= (5/4) AB Lambda_S.             (3.3)

In particular retain the actual ORIGINAL source likelihood

 p_S(s,t)= E_P[1_(N=0) exp(sF+tH)] / E_P exp(sF+tH).

All these finite-regulator expressions are positive near real zero,
and

 partial_st log p_S(0,0)=Cov_(P^0)(F,H)-Cov_P(F,H).            (3.4)

Equations (3.3)--(3.4) prove (0.1). F and H need not belong to a
complex native carrier class: the erasure estimate is applied to
the real count penalty, not to exp(sF+tH). The source derivatives
are then ordinary derivatives of finite bounded-observable
integrals. No source-dependent coupling is introduced.

The corresponding mean estimate is
 |E_(P^0)F-E_P F|<=A Lambda_S.
It follows from d E_theta F/dtheta=-Cov_theta(F,N) and
|Cov_theta(F,N)|<=A E_theta N.

A useful literal extension is a deterministic soft penalty
w=product_X r_X^(1_(X in eta)), 0<=r_X<=1. Interpolate
exp[-t sum_X tau_X 1_X], tau_X=-log r_X, first with r_X>0.
The same argument gives

 |Cov_(wP/Pw)(F,H)-Cov_P(F,H)|
    <= (5/4) AB sum_X barlambda_X(1-r_X),
 Pw>=exp[-sum_X barlambda_X(1-r_X)].                           (3.5)

Indeed E_t 1_X<=barlambda_X exp(-t tau_X), and

4. CUMULATIVE COUNTS: WHAT CAN BE SUMMED EXACTLY

Consider one complete finite positive native joint representation
through several dates. Suppose every later complete T/R integration
is the exact normalized/mass-preserving operation on the previous
integrand, with the previously born discrete counts retained as
passive records. This is the chronology required by I(1.100),(1.102)
and TURN60; it does not assert arbitrary future conditioning has
the same distribution.

The marginal law of a count already born at date k is then unchanged
by integrating the later complete continuation. Hence, for finite
sets S_k and A_*=intersection_k{N_(k,S_k)=0},

 delta:=P(A_*^c) <= sum_k E N_(k,S_k)
                <= sum_k |S_k| epsilon_k.                    (4.1)

This is a union bound, with no independence or multiplication of
conditional Laplace estimates. The all-date probability space and
its exact passive-record marginal are required. A theorem for each
separate conditional endpoint does not alone supply this joint law.

When P(A_*)>0, write P=(1-delta)nu+delta rho, nu=P(.|A_*).
The exact covariance mixture formula is

 Cov_P=(1-delta)Cov_nu+delta Cov_rho
        +delta(1-delta)(m_nu F-m_rho F)(m_nu H-m_rho H).

It gives

 |Cov_nu(F,H)-Cov_P(F,H)| <= (3/2) AB delta.                   (4.2)

If the right side of (4.1) is less than one, positivity of P(A_*)
follows. For a larger bound it must be known separately; one must
not multiply the one-date lower bounds (2.2) without a uniform
conditional admission theorem for all earlier penalties.

This supplies an exact cumulative first-class selection-source
bound wherever (4.1) is small. It says nothing about the event of
no second-class regions, nor about the cumulative projected windows
defining the selected regular trajectory of TURN70.

5. PHYSICAL SCALE AUDIT

The source's p0(g) is a power of log(g^-2), with fixed large exponent.
To state the relevant scaling explicitly, take an admitted weak
trajectory with age r from the terminal scale satisfying

 gamma_r=g_r^-2 comparable to Gamma+b r,
 ell_r=ell_* L^-r,
 p0(g_r)=A0 [log gamma_r]^p,  p>1.                            (5.1)

This section is a consequence for such a trajectory, not a new
proof of native bare-coupling tuning.

For one rooted current-cell lineage, even a fixed polynomial
weight gamma_r^q gives a summable series

 sum_(r>=0) gamma_r^q exp[-c(log gamma_r)^p] < infinity.       (5.2)

This follows by comparison with any inverse power of r for
sufficiently large r. Tails tend to zero.

A fixed four-dimensional physical region instead contains
O(ell_r^-4) current cells. The available bound (4.1) then contains

 L^(4r) exp[-c(log(Gamma+b r))^p].                             (5.3)

These terms do not tend to zero. Strengthening the raw penalty
from p0 to a fixed power of p0 does not change that conclusion.
This is a failure of the available census estimate to be useful,
not evidence that the actual probability or source error diverges.

For an original bare action source F=sum_p f_p h(U_p), h in [0,2],
the simple oscillation bound is O(sum_p |f_p|), typically O(a^-4)
for a fixed physical support. Subtracting any deterministic vacuum
does not reduce this oscillation. A fixed power of gamma cannot
compensate the cell census in (5.3). Bounded probes remove that
particular observable factor but still leave the count census.

Neither (3.3) nor (4.2) uses separation between F and H. To obtain
a useful separated physical source estimate one needs additional
local source sensitivity or cancellation. It does not follow from
the auxiliary count alone.

6. WHY THE SAME ARGUMENT DOES NOT CLOSE SECOND-CLASS REPLACEMENT

I(1.100) has a normalized new-reference quotient with the old
integrand still present. Schematically, at a fully resolved retained
datum e this is

 d tau_e(x,xi) q_e(dy),                                      (6.1)

where x,xi are the old private/original records and y the new
reference. If q depends on a displayed retained variable, that
variable belongs to e. Original source insertion is exp(sF(x,xi,e)
+tH(x,xi,e)), not exp(sF(y)+tH(y)).

For a complete first/second-class declaration G(e,y), let
p(e)=q_e(G=1). Conditional on fully resolved e, selecting G changes
only the y draw and leaves the old conditional source law unchanged.
But it changes the global e marginal by the factor p(e). Thus it
can change both the average old conditional covariance and the
covariance of the old conditional means. Source-independent q at
fixed e is not a vanishing global mixed selection derivative.

TURN64's exact identity, with birth indicator J and I^+=JG, is

 E[J a 1_(p>0)] = E[I^+ a/p]                                 (6.2)

for nonnegative pre-redraw marks a. It retains a zero-success term
and a potentially large inverse success. The raw mass estimate
II(1.89) applies to either class but does not remove these terms
from a NORMALIZED all-birth or second-class estimate.

The new result above avoids inverse success only for the literal
first-class absence penalty in the already prepared (1.72) gas.
It does not turn the first-class count into a bound on all R births.

This is decay AWAY FROM Lambda. It gives no small correction in

leading 2 epsilon_k is itself not a strict epsilon_k margin.
Shrinking the raw component by six MR_k layers does not, by this
formula, put every defining read far from Lambda.

The complete final chi_k is a test on the appropriate locally
reconstructed minima, not only on the plaquettes of the new

A definite zero-success mechanism would be a failed complete
defining read outside every freshly redrawn region, with all its
arguments fixed by an admitted retained record. Then p(e)=0

complete reads was located in the inspected propositions. The
precise missing estimate remains that theorem, with its actual
Lambda versus class-read geometry and the other fresh draws
accounted for. This is narrower than asserting the success bound
is impossible.

8. WHAT A LOCAL OSCILLATION REFINEMENT WOULD REQUIRE

The erasure proving (1.2) acts on the integrated component catalogue.
It is not a reconstruction map changing original fields only inside
X. Consequently osc(F) in (3.2) cannot be replaced merely by the
oscillation of F under changing a few newly drawn links.

Such a refinement would need an actual comparison of the retained
old-source conditional laws before and after component erasure:
the source defect, its full read collar, and the changed coarse
conditional means must all be bounded in that comparison. Alternatively
one can prove a connected source-mark estimate in the complete
representation. The existing unmarked erasure/potential increment
does not assert either property. In particular signed connected
coefficients cannot be treated as positive original events.

The concrete improvement proved here is (3.3), its deterministic
label-softening version (3.5), and the exact cumulative consequence
(4.1)--(4.2) at the stated original-positive chronology. The physical
scaling and complete second-class success gate remain unresolved.
