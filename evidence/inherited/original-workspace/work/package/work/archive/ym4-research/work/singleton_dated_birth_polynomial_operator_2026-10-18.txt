5. LIMIT OF THE LOCAL SPATIAL CONCLUSION

A uniform homogeneous-cohort norm does not by itself imply a local
positive propagator for arbitrarily many differently placed copies.
For a translation convolution K, the bound on constants controls
|sum_x K(x)|, whereas a local l-infinity bound needs sum_x|K(x)|.
These can be arbitrarily different. Likewise
sup_z sum_(S,delta) a_(S,delta)(z) cannot be replaced by
sum_(S,delta) sup_z a_(S,delta)(z).

For one universal translation-covariant unweighted template, taking
the supremum over its finitely many phases is harmless. After external
copy profiles are inserted, dropping those profiles before the sum
can cost the number of separated copies. A sum over relative
configurations therefore needs a retained root-to-output positive
kernel/tree majorant, or the independently proposed first-merger
estimate. Equations(1)--(7) solve the permanently dated singleton
budget once (1) applies; they do not manufacture that missing spatial
norm from a 21 by21 homogeneous matrix.
