2. UNIFORM PENALTY MOMENT INEQUALITY

and tending to infinity for each positive argument as t increases.
For example psi_t(u)=t(u_+)^4 is C2. Define

 H_t=H+sum_k psi_t(r_k-s0),       dmu_t=Z_t^-1 e^(-H_t)1_K dx.

The product outer boundary has X dot n>=0, since on its i-th spherical

This holds for arbitrary scalar f; it does not assume a boundary

There is no factor t, no count of all masks, and no inverse success
probability in(4). A function concentrated at the boundary is handled
by its gradient term, rather than by a false rare-mass trace bound.

   <=C B_L a E_t|grad v|^2
                  +C B_L(a^(-1)+r)E_t|v|^2.              (6)

containing domain has a nonnegative outer Neumann boundary term.

 >=[1-C g0 B_L a] E_t|D^2u|^2

5. LIMITS OF THE PASS

The baseline margin and native full-chart bounds concern the actual
guarded first fiber. After arbitrary additional coordinates are fixed,
that margin can disappear; this argument is not an automatic principal
conditional theorem. Likewise the weighted base covariance theorem
cannot simply be applied to H_t.

Specifically, the positive rank-one Hessian term in(5) becomes under
spatial diagonal conjugation

        psi''_t (W grad r_j)(W^(-1) grad r_j)^T.

Its symmetric part need not be positive when grad r_j has more than
one site and the weights differ. For the actual flat plaquette pattern
grad r proportional to (n,-n), unequal weights already give a negative
eigenvalue. Its magnitude grows with psi''_t, so a fixed small spatial
rate does not alone give a bound uniform in t. This is the soft-penalty
version of the hard multi-site tangent-space obstruction.

No exponential spatial decay, boundary-source cancellation, or fourth
power source-age estimate is claimed by this audit. The unweighted
uniform gap is the finite/native consequence that has been checked.
