SEPARATED LOCAL SOURCES: NORMALIZATION, AN EXACT CANCELLATION,
AND THE REMAINING FULL-MEASURE TERM
21 October 2026 — TURN95

STATUS

Nontrivial continuum four-dimensional Yang–Mills existence is not proved.
This pass implements the local-source reassessment and obtains a modest
finite-scale refinement. It does not establish a cutoff-uniform original
correlation estimate, a nonzero continuum response, or a local field theory.

The useful decisions are to use an additive inverse-coupling insertion,
to exploit separated supports before estimating the mixed coefficient,
and to retain only the actual generated native descendants in the next
preparation problem. The already completed selected one-source induction
is not reopened or treated as a new missing theorem.

1. THE INSERTION NEED NOT CARRY THE DIVERGING BARE INVERSE COUPLING

On a finite original SU(2) lattice set

 e_p(U)=1-(1/2)Tr U_p,
 S_f(U)=sum_p f_p e_p(U),
 Z_a[gamma]=integral exp[-sum_p gamma_p e_p(U)] dHaar(U).

The original normalized measure at homogeneous gamma_0 is denoted mu_a.
At a fixed regulator the exact identities are

 d_s log Z_a[gamma_0-s f]|_0 = E_mu S_f,
 d_s d_t log Z_a[gamma_0-s f-t q]|_0 = Cov_mu(S_f,S_q).       (1)

Proof: differentiate the finite compact-group integral and then its
logarithm. The first derivative is the normalized marked numerator;
the second subtracts the product of the two first moments. This uses
no source-dependent geometric choices or uniform analytic source disc.

A source gamma_0 S_f is a relative change of inverse coupling. S_f is
an additive change. The extra gamma_0 is therefore not compulsory.

Physical calibration still matters. Suppose a specified C^2 tuning
function Gamma_a(theta) implements a physical inverse-coupling coordinate
theta, with Gamma_a(theta)=gamma_0 at the baseline. Consider
gamma_p(s,t)=Gamma_a(theta+s f_p+t q_p). Directly,

 d_s d_t log Z_a[gamma(s,t)]|_0
   = Gamma_a'(theta)^2 Cov_mu(S_f,S_q)
       - Gamma_a''(theta) E_mu sum_p f_p q_p e_p.              (2)

Indeed the action has derivatives Gamma_a' S_f and Gamma_a' S_q,
and mixed derivative Gamma_a'' S_(fq). Differentiating log Z gives
the covariance of the first derivatives minus the expected second
derivative. If f and q have disjoint plaquette supports, S_(fq)=0
exactly. A derivative |Gamma_a'| bounded above and bounded away from
zero uniformly in the cutoff would therefore suffice for a uniform
comparison with the coupling-free covariance.

Equation (2) does not construct Gamma_a or bound its derivative for the
full theory. It prevents us from imposing the stronger normalization
gamma_0 S_f without a physical reason. The selected constant-source
theorem already uses exp(zeta S_0), with a protected marginal coefficient.
That selected result is not a differentiability theorem for the full
native tuning, whose geometric choices have not been controlled in this
way.

The classical normalization is appropriate to geometric curvature:
for a smooth anti-Hermitian connection,

 U_p=exp(a^2 F_munu+O(a^3)),
 e_p=-a^4 Tr(F_munu^2)/4+O(a^5).

Thus sum_p f_p e_p already has the classical dimension-four integral
normalization. This is a smooth-field calculation, not an approximation
valid for typical ultraviolet samples. At a fixed positive weak physical
coupling g_*, the free separated benchmark for this insertion is of
order g_*^4 and need not vanish. No corresponding Wilson lower bound
has been established.

Additive source-only counterterms in the connected functional, whose
second derivatives are supported on coincident source points, drop out
for separated supports. Field-dependent mixing and later contact
extensions remain necessary.

2. AN EXISTING REGULAR ESTIMATE BECOMES LESS DEMANDING

For the literal complete action source on one admitted homogeneous
native regular primitive, TURN83 already gives a separated envelope
of the form

 |C_j(f,q)| <= C min(N_f,j,N_q,j) exp[-c d/ell_j]
     [g_j^2 ell_j^(alpha+alpha') [f]_alpha [q]_alpha'
        +g_j^4 ||f||_infinity ||q||_infinity].                (3)

Here d is the distance between the actual interpolant supports at the
scales where these are separated, ell_j the current cell scale, and
N_f,j the corresponding source-cell count including interpolation/read
collars. The stated physical-graph,
complete-read, Gaussian-window and source-admission conditions remain.
For bounded physical supports N_f,j<=C_f ell_j^-4. The geometric sum
over fine scales is uniform, because exp[-c d/ell_j] dominates these
powers of ell_j^-1. With the gamma_0 S_f convention the right side
would instead carry gamma_0^2. The c0=1 choice removes that unnecessary
factor.

This is a useful application of an existing primitive estimate, not a
new bound on the complete original covariance. Previously transported
original marks contain more than the current primitive reference action.

3. NEW FINITE-SCALE REFINEMENT: ONE SMALL COMPONENT CANNOT OWN BOTH MARKS

Use the existing once-owned first-healing convention on the full positive
history representation. An original plaquette touching a raw component
is assigned nonnegative weights among its initial raw owners, totaling
one. Follow each owner through whole-component mergers to its first
healing. At scale k let F_X and H_X be the portions of S_f and S_q
whose owners first heal in the eligible first-class raw component X.

Let A and B be the original plaquette-center supports of f and q,
d_infinity their periodic physical sup-distance, a_0 the original
mesh, and ell_k=M a_k the current M-cell mesh. Then

 d_infinity > 100 R_k ell_k + 2 a_0
       implies F_X H_X=0 for every such X.                    (4)

X here is the eligible raw component itself, not a later enlarged
localization carrier or the complete analytic read of a posterior.

Proof. Large field renormalization I, printed page 177, restricts an
eligible component to a cube of side 100 M R_k in current lattice
units. Its ancestors remain inside it in physical coordinates: the
cover and layers contain the old component before rescaling, while
the lattice mesh increases by the reciprocal rescaling factor.
Whole-component mergers preserve this containment until first healing.

Each assigned original plaquette has at least one original raw link in
an ancestor. Its original center is therefore at most a_0 from X.
If X owned nonzero portions of both sources, two such centers would
be at distance at most 100 R_k ell_k+2a_0, contradicting (4).
Integrating some other links of a straddling plaquette does not move
this original anchor. This proves the claim also for those plaquettes.

The first-class X_i in Large field renormalization II, printed page 378,
are these same eligible components. The source locations and the
physical-containment step were independently checked against the
retained primary text and rendered formulas.

Write R_f=sum_X F_X and R_q=sum_X H_X at this date. In the earlier
first-healing moment estimate, the same-owner degree-two term is now
absent. The existing distinct-carrier erasure bound therefore gives

 E|R_f R_q| <= C_K^2 gamma_0^(-2K) ||f||_infinity ||q||_infinity
                                      Lambda_k(A)Lambda_k(B),
 |Cov(R_f,R_q)| <= 2 C_K^2 gamma_0^(-2K)
          ||f||_infinity ||q||_infinity Lambda_k(A)Lambda_k(B). (5)

All constants and admissions are those of the earlier once-owned
real-source estimate; fixed powers can be paid by its initial reserve.
The complete original denominator is retained. No independence of
the two components is assumed. If outer data are averaged, apply
uniform censuses to the absolute mixed and first moments separately;
global covariance is not the average conditional covariance.

This is an exact improvement, but (5) has no decay in the distance
between two distinct owners. Its remaining current-cell censuses can
grow as ell_k^-4 each. Along the logarithmic running-coupling trajectory,
the available weak-tail envelope does not by itself pay that growth.
Thus (5) is not a continuum clustering or summability theorem.

4. THE ONE-RAW / ONE-REGULAR TERM DOES NOT CANCEL

Decompose the literal original sources on the same complete history
space as F=F_rest+R_f and H=H_rest+R_q. Exactly,

 Cov(F,H)-Cov(F_rest,H_rest)
   =Cov(R_f,H_rest)+Cov(F_rest,R_q)+Cov(R_f,R_q).              (6)

Equation (4) improves the last term only. The first two can transmit
correlation through the exterior even when the original supports are
disjoint.

For a specified basic-R factorization, write the actual positive joint
with its remaining continuation as

 dPi = Z_0^-1 sigma(de) tau_e(dx,dxi) q_e(dy) W(e,x,xi,y).

The old slots x,xi include original records; q_e is the normalized
fresh reference from Large-I (1.100). All class indicators and remaining
weights belong in the stated factors exactly once. In particular, a
class depending on the new reference cannot be omitted when centering
or summing histories. The surviving term is

 Cov(R_f,H_rest)
   = Z_0^-1 integral sigma tau q
          r_f(e,x,xi) [H_rest(e,x,xi)-E H_rest] W.            (7)

The complete source/history sum is understood in this expression.
Equation (7) is an original marked numerator, not a variance of the
fresh reference. If the other mark is exterior-measurable, the old
integral gives a first posterior which can still correlate with that
exterior mark. If both marks were partially integrated, their joint
conditional coefficient cannot be replaced by the product of two
first posteriors.

Sibling summation and scalar vacuum cancellation preserve (7); they
do not estimate or annihilate it. A safe bound by absolute moments
still contains the number of original fine plaquettes in the distant
source. It therefore gives no uniform continuum estimate.

5. THE DIRECT RENORMALIZED-MEASURE FORK WAS TESTED, NOT ASSUMED

The finite reflection-positivity audit did not produce a factorization
or an actual counterexample for the full first native R operation.
That operation follows an admitted N-step prefix; an arbitrary
one-plaquette redraw would not test it.

For the complete positive old/new coupling, including all unreplaced
branches and every crossing component, let V_o,V_n be the old and new
current fields and F a bounded gauge-invariant positive-half cylinder.
Let Q_o(F),Q_R(F) be their reflected quadratic forms. Direct subtraction
and a product difference yield

 |Q_R(F)-Q_o(F)| <= ||F||_infinity E[
       |F(V_n)-F(V_o)|+|F(theta V_n)-F(theta V_o)|].          (8)

A replacement that writes no link of the complete current support of
F leaves F unchanged, even if its decision read is larger. Consequently

 |Q_R(F)-Q_o(F)| <= 2||F||_infinity^2
   [P(a replacement writes into supp F)
      +P(a replacement writes into theta supp F)].           (9)

For a reflection-invariant incoming law and equivariant complete lift,
this is 4||F||_infinity^2 times the first probability. The inequality
does not require incoming RP; using it as a lower bound for Q_R does.

The exact reflected kernel retains each actual old component mass and
the whole normalized reference integral. A crossing component has one
joint denominator, not separately normalized positive and negative
halves. Reflection invariance makes the kernel Hermitian, not positive.

No bound on all basic replacements makes (9) vanish in the continuum
at present. First-class counts alone are insufficient. Therefore this
audit supplies no established shortcut past (7). It also does not rule
out asymptotic reflection positivity by a future quantitative estimate.

6. THE NEXT REQUIRED ESTIMATE, WITH OLD RESULTS PRESERVED

TURN70 already closes the selected one-mark induction on its admitted
fixed domains: the protected constant channel controls its marginal,
and augmented contraction handles its inhomogeneous remainder. Do not
replace this achievement by a new demand for a general invariant
localization theorem.

The unproved preparation concerns the actual GENERATED NATIVE source
descendants on their running domains and the old integrated marks at
later localization contours. The original closed SU(2) trace is a
holomorphic polynomial; internal complex gauge factors cancel. But a
current-link bound does not automatically bound its reconstructed old
holonomy, or the marked integral's localization and source-cell costs.

The next substantive advance must estimate (7), or obtain the same
full original separated response by another construction. A complete
two-source estimate needs both the source's renormalized one-mark
transport and its connected contribution when the two regions can
communicate. Separation makes the early direct cross contributions
small; it does not control an unbounded inherited one-mark coefficient
at a fixed later physical scale.

No entire source functional, arbitrary-future-test comparison, or new
proof of the selected one-mark theorem is required for this target.
The physical calibration, local hierarchy, Euclidean symmetry, volume
limit and nontriviality obligations from TURN94 remain open.
