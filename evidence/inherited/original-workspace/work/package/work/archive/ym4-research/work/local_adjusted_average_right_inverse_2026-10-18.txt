1. Normalization and uniform-block finite matrix.

Let r=M eta, M=L^j, be an averaging scale. Q^M is normalized as an
average of the fine potential in physical units: the linear coarse link
ANGLE is r Q^M A. Thus the fine/coarse inner products carry eta^d and
r^d, respectively. We use the actual adjusted averaging of CMP98,
(124)--(125), composed as (89)--(92), not unadjusted path averaging.

At the trivial background, repeated flat block averaging telescopes to
the M-scale straight-string average. On variations in one r-block P,
the only nonzero rows in direction mu are the incoming and outgoing
mu-bonds incident to P. In normalized coordinates of P their two weights
are t_M and 1-t_M, where t_M=(k+1)/M, up to interchanging their names or
shifting k by one according to the centered-block convention. Explicitly,
their contribution is

 M^{-d} sum_{x in P} (t_M(x_mu),1-t_M(x_mu)) A_mu(x).

For clarity this identity also follows by the generating polynomial:
the transverse factor is the M-point uniform average and the longitudinal
factor is its convolution with itself. Composition of L-step factors
gives the same M-point factors, because M is a power of L.

Choose two nonnegative smooth longitudinal bumps, one supported near
1/3 and one near2/3, and a transverse bump supported away from every
face. Their discrete masses are normalized to fixed positive constants.
For M>=M0 the two longitudinal first moments lie in disjoint fixed
intervals. The resulting 2 by2 moment matrix has determinant bounded
away from zero, uniformly in M. Its inverse and the discrete C1 norms
of the resulting two shapes are bounded uniformly in M. Tensor each
shape with every Lie-algebra basis vector, and repeat for mu=1,...,d.
The resulting map Psi_P from 2d dim(g) row values to fine fields obeys

 Q_flat Psi_P = I on all incident r-rows,
 ||Psi_P v||_infty + r ||partial Psi_P v||_infty <= C |v|.       (1)

Its support is strictly inside P, a fixed fraction of r from its faces.
Rows not incident to P vanish EXACTLY. One may fix L>=M0 once; finite
small M exceptions can instead be handled by finite discrete matrices.
The raw j=0 identity is dealt with separately, not by a continuum bump.

Now assume the real background W admits ONE regular gauge with

 r ||A_W||_infty + r^2 ||partial A_W||_infty <= a

on the full endpoint-block footprint of these incident rows. In that
gauge, after the harmless fixed row endpoint-frame identifications,

 ||Q_W^M Psi_P-I|| <= C a,                                     (2)

uniformly in M. This is the finite-dimensional version of the adjusted
averaging comparison: CMP98(124) gives the fixed-step curvature error;
at intermediate scale s it is O_L(a(s/r)^2), while comparison of real
parallel frames costs O_L(a s/r). Both sums over s=L^p eta are geometric.
Products of the norms remain bounded. The exact endpoint-block support
is CMP98 p31, after(91); no Neumann correction is allowed to spread
outside those blocks.

For a<1/(2C), invert the FINITE matrix in(2):

 E_P = Psi_P (Q_W^M Psi_P)^{-1}.

Then Q_W^M E_P=I on the incident rows, all other r-rows vanish, and(1)
holds with partial replaced by the W-covariant difference. The inverse
acts on finitely many constant coefficients, so it does not enlarge
support or introduce derivatives of an inverse kernel. Choose one
endpoint owner block for each coarse bond and take the corresponding
column of E_P. These columns give Q_W E=I in a uniform region, with
uniform bounded overlap and range O(r).

2. Why the naive one-block graded extension is false.

A block P of tier j immediately outside Omega_{j+1} can be read by a
retained tier-(j+1) row. At the flat background Q_{j+1}=Q_step Q_j on
variations in P. Consequently prescribing all incident j-row outputs
can force a nonzero (j+1)-output. No matrix inverse using only variations
in P can in general prescribe that j-output AND independently set the
coarser output to zero. Adjacent-tier incidence alone is not a proof
of a one-block right inverse.

3. Exact two-tier triangular repair.

Use the literal terminal convention: a retained j-row has an endpoint
in the retained j-sites; every read is contained in its TWO endpoint
j-blocks. The Omega_j are unions of complete aligned j-blocks (indeed
of the source's larger fixed L-power cubes). All blocks and endpoint
conventions in this paragraph belong to the SAME determining family.

For a desired retained j-row choose an endpoint j-block P in
Omega_j\Omega_{j+1}. Build the column E_P^0 of Section1, controlling
ALL incident j-rows, including any rows not retained in the mixed
family. Choose its core distance from every face greater than the
largest endpoint-block footprint of a tier j-1 row; a fixed sufficiently
large L makes this compatible with the two interior bumps. Hence no
lower retained row reads its support. The source separation excludes
tiers j+2 and higher. Only tier j+1 outputs may remain.

If a retained (j+1)-row reads the core of P, one endpoint (j+1)-block
contains P and is outside Omega_{j+1}. Since the row is retained, its
other endpoint block P' is inside Omega_{j+1}. This is an exact crossing
edge: it follows from endpoint-block locality, not a neighborhood
estimate. The Omega_{j+2} gap keeps P' and a fixed enlargement outside
Omega_{j+2}. There are only a fixed number of such crossing rows.

For each residual crossing row c, use the uniform construction in its
INSIDE (j+1)-block P'. The bubble core is farther than C r_j from every
face of P', so no retained j-row or finer row reads it. The hierarchy
gap excludes (j+2)-rows: it suffices that R M_source>C_fp L with a fixed
footprint constant. These inequalities are compatible with the source
choice of a large fixed separation. Its column E_{P',c} controls every
incident (j+1)-row exactly, and consequently has no other retained output.

Set

 E_cj = E_P^0 - sum_{c at tier j+1} E_{P',c} (Q E_P^0)(c).       (3)

This is a finite triangular correction, not an iteration. Every
retained output is now zero except the prescribed j-row. Support is
within distance C L r_j of P; at most a fixed number of j+1 blocks
is used. Norm and covariant derivative bounds are C_L and C_L/r_j.
The proof needs weak C1 gauges on this COMPLETE finite two-tier patch.
It does not infer them from C1 on P alone. Adjacent-tier changes of
units cost fixed powers of L; choose the weak ceiling after these
constants. For a raw j=0 row use its fine-bond column first and the
same higher-tier correction; the elementary bound is then C/eta.

More precisely, EACH bubble needs ONE regular gauge on its full
incident-row footprint, including the neighboring endpoint blocks.
Unrelated good gauges on separate blocks do not imply the finite
matrix comparison(2). An admitted full source class cube can supply
the common gauge. Every column and the additional one-fine-step
collar needed by D_W must also lie in the variational FREE domain.
No boundary condition is changed by this construction.

This geometry was independently checked by minimal_route, specifically
using the exact two-endpoint-block support and aligned-domain identity.
It is not asserted for a determining family which fails those hypotheses.

4. Local current consequence with the physical transpose.

Let W be an actual real constrained critical point. The source
stationarity identity says Jphys annihilates ker Q, so Jphys=Q^*lambda
for some coarse multiplier. For a local column E_c v with Q E_c v equal
to v at c and zero elsewhere, integration by parts gives

 r_c^d <lambda(c),v>
   = <Jphys,E_c v>_fine = <F_W,D_W E_c v>_fine.                 (4)

The column has support volume at most C_L r_c^d and covariant derivative
at most C_L/r_c. Therefore

 |lambda(c)| <= C_L r_c^{-1} sup_{support E_c} |F_W|.

The physical transpose Q^* carries the coarse/fine measure ratio. Its
uniform sup bound is proved by FIXED-L composition, as detailed in
Section6 below; one must not assign an M^{-d} bound to an isolated
adjustment connector. Exact adjacent-tier incidence and bounded row
overlap then give, at a fine bond of local tier r,

 |Jphys(b)| <= C_L r^{-1} sup_{finite two-tier collar} |F_W|.    (5)

With curvature at most C a/r^2 there, this is C_L a/r^3. No derivative
of F, no preexisting physical-current bound and no q0 estimate is used.
This is local: arbitrary bad geometry far outside the finite collar
does not enter(4). It does require the exact stationarity/adjusted-Q
identity and the stated full weak-C1 collar for W.

The observation bond can read columns of tiers n-1,n,n+1. Each column
may in turn use its next-tier correction. Thus the final observation
collar has radius at most C L^2 r_n, still fixed in L and independent
of refinement depth; it is not literally confined to two tiers relative
to the observation. Stationarity and(5) apply on FREE fine bonds only.
They do not bound the current on a fixed Dirichlet boundary; columns
or their D_W collars touching such a boundary are excluded unless the
corresponding variational boundary statement is separately supplied.

5. Source-admission consequence and limitation.

The join formula transcribed from CMP119(2.13)--(2.14), Raw I92519--33,
replaces old blocks by finer blocks only in a graded boundary collar.
It is not a literal intersection of two complete nonlinear constraint
families. Neither this refinement fact nor local right inverses prove
that every mixed datum in CMP122-II(1.61)--(1.64) comes from one admitted
field on the entire new collar. That remains a distinct task.

A useful exact admission test is available: start with one admitted
real tower V0 satisfying CMP102(7), including its seams. A uniformly
small relative coarse-link perturbation V V0^{-1} preserves(7), with
the fixed enlarged constant transcribed at Raw I16779--85; complex
relative logarithms enter the source polydisc(172). Thus one need not
prove an exact nonlinear refinement identity if that SAME-reference
bound is verified on every actual modified datum slot.

Bare Y2 subset Y3 and the fact that Y3' intersects the boundary of Y2
do not put the entire Y2 seam in Y3^0: attachment on one face leaves
the opposite face unchanged. A possible weaker test is to reference
the preceding full admitted field E4. Boundary slots in the next
minimum are its averages already. For interior slots supplied as M(U),
one must show they are constraints retained by E4's baseline minimum,
so their difference is only the small localized H/gauge perturbation.
The artificial-boundary monotonicity helps where the underlying old
towers agree. Preparation cuts and Lambda-fill interfaces still need
their literal constraint/gauge identities; this note does not assert
them from final U'_k membership.

6. Adversarial support, transpose and gauge-tangent audit.

(a) The adjusted operator, not just its straight-string part, has the
asserted support. The pinned quotation of CMP98 p31 after(91), at Raw
I24396--24413, expressly states that the FULL nonlinear kth adjusted
average at c depends only on fine bonds in B^k(c_-) union B^k(c_+).
Therefore differentiation at W has that exact support. In(124) the
within-block mean/contour corrections and central crossing string do
not add a third endpoint block. Iterated normalization does not change
this statement: it is the operation covered by the quoted kth-order
locality assertion. I found no adjustment path escaping these blocks.

At flat background this also validates the finite 2d-row construction:
the physical derivative of the nonlinear adjusted mean is the source
Q, whose main term is(125), and every correction in(124) vanishes.
The two longitudinal moment weights include BOTH adjacent rows. A
single outgoing-row convention would miss the incoming constraint.

(b) The fixed-dimensional perturbation is uniform in refinement, not
in L. On one common regular gauge at final scale r, at spacing s the
real parallel-frame error is O_L(a s/r), and the fixed-step correction
in(124) is O_L(a(s/r)^2). The latter is the explicit source estimate
transcribed at Raw I25169--25174, using intermediate averaged-curvature
control from CMP98 Proposition2. Sum these errors over s=L^p eta.
Both sums converge geometrically, and the product of 1+error is bounded.
Applying the telescoped difference to the uniformly bounded finite
bump map proves(2). No inverse over all fine coordinates is taken.

For the transpose, each FIXED-L step includes the physical factor L^d.
The straight-string transpose has sup norm at most1: its two adjacent
row contributions have total scalar mass1 at a fixed fine bond. Real
parallel transports are isometries. All connector/root-bond terms have
fixed incidence at this step, so their physical transpose has norm
at most C_L a(s/r)^2, even if an individual connector is concentrated.
Consequently

 ||(Q_W^M)^*|| <= product_s [1+C_L a(s/r)^2] <= exp(C'_L a).

This avoids the false shortcut of bounding each M-scale connector
coefficient by C M^{-d}. Transposes of the fixed-step compositions use
the intermediate physical measures, so no uncancelled L^{dj} remains.
Restricting to the actual mixed terminal rows only removes terms; the
adjacent-tier incidence argument then gives the bound used in(5).

(c) Stationarity does not require the columns E_c themselves to obey an
axial gauge condition. CMP102(178), as transcribed at Raw I15765--68,
states <delta A',J>=0 for ALL delta A' with Q(U_k)delta A'=0. There is
no additional R D* delta A'=0 condition in that test class. The source
constraint-linearizing coordinate is L(A')=A'-H D(A'), with
D(A')=O(|A'|^2) (Raw I13996--14000), hence D L(0)=I. Thus the J in
that first variation is the physical current, not a different gradient
introduced by a linear gauge projection. The displayed gauge condition
R D* A'_1=0 characterizes the chosen solution; it does not narrow the
stationarity test class in(178).

Once this annihilation is available on the FREE ambient bond space,
finite-dimensional duality gives J=Q^*lambda there. Equation(4) may then
pair that identity against any free E_c; E_c is not a tangent vector
to ker Q and is not gauge-corrected. In particular, no nonlocal axial
projection is inserted into E_c, and its r^{-1} derivative estimate
is unchanged. If a different mixed problem supplies stationarity only
on ker Q intersect a fixed axial slice, the identity J=Q^*lambda must
first be justified by its actual gauge-orbit/boundary construction;
the local inverse alone does not supply it. For an intrinsic problem
to which CMP102(178) applies, this is already the asserted source test.

(d) The graded repair uses the actual complete endpoint-block convention,
not a larger vague neighborhood bound. An outside owner block can be
read by a retained next-tier row only if that row crosses to an inside
endpoint block. The correction's deep core excludes the finer rows;
the next admitted gap excludes higher rows. I found no further
geometric obstruction under these conventions. The one-block graded
construction in Section2 remains false, so the correction cannot be
