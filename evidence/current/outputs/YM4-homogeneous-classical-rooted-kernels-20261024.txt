2. FINE PLAQUETTE VALUE AND FIRST RESPONSE KEEP eta^2

For an oriented fine plaquette p, let P_p(B)=U_tilde(B)(partial p),
expressed at its chosen base vertex in the fixed U0 frame. Define
K_p(B)=(1/i)log P_p(B) on the small principal branch. Its complete
four-link and transported-gradient read is assigned to a current
packet a; moving to its fixed collar only changes constants.

The primary finite product identity is CMP102 printed282(24)--(25):
in its transported plaquette convention the signed first-order sum
is EXACTLY eta (D_U0 H)(p). The BCH form is printed283(34). Thus the
following cancellation is part of the literal native eta convention,
not an assumed continuum replacement of a discrete difference.

We claim uniformly on the complex tube

 |K_p(B)|<=C eta^2,
 |partial_q K_p(B)|<=C eta^2 exp[-mu1 d(a,q)].                 (9)

Here is the finite product argument that supplies the crucial factor.
Transport the four relative link fields eta H around p to its fixed
base vertex using U0. The plaquette is a product of four exponentials
of signed transported eta H, followed by U0(partial p). The signed
sum of their unscaled fields is bounded by

 C eta |nabla_U0 H| + C|U0(partial p)-1||H|.

The difference between the exponential product and identity is its
linear signed sum times eta plus an O(eta^2|H|^2) remainder. Together
with (5),(7), this bounds P_p(B)-1 by C eta^2. The same calculation
differentiated ONCE replaces one H by partial_q H: its signed linear
sum costs eta |nabla_U0 partial_q H|, and every nonlinear term has at
least two eta factors and one partial_q H. Equations(7),(8) then give

 |partial_q P_p(B)|<=C eta^2 exp[-mu1 d(a,q)].

All U0 transports are fixed while differentiating B. A possible
transport around the complete plaquette contributes its already
bounded (5) and is smaller. The derivative of the principal logarithm
is uniformly bounded on this fixed small holonomy neighborhood, so
(9) follows. This proof works for complex H by the same convergent
finite product/Taylor identities. It does not appeal to positivity
of a complex measure.

Using only |partial_q H| would give an erroneous O(eta) estimate.
The covariant gradient entry in (190), with its eta^-1 convention,
is exactly what produces the second eta. Conversely no raw gauge
return derivative is needed: the trace is already evaluated in (6).
We do not assert that the untraced K_p has the same kernel in every
parameter-dependent gauge.

3. HIGHER RESPONSES: CAUCHY, SYMMETRY AND THE ROOTED ARMS

Fix P=4 or5. Keep a fixed spare fraction of rho so all required
coordinate disks remain in the SAME native complex tube. Apply
coordinatewise Cauchy to the second estimate in (9), taking up to
p-1 additional derivatives. For every chosen index j among the p
external coordinates, mixed-derivative symmetry gives

 |partial_(q_1)...partial_(q_p) K_p|
   <= C_p eta^2 rho^(1-p) exp[-mu1 d(a,q_j)].                 (10)

Repeated coordinate/color indices are allowed; their factorial
Cauchy price is absorbed in C_p for fixed P. Since (10) holds for
EACH j, take the minimum of its right-hand sides. The inequality
max_j d_j >= (1/p)sum_j d_j proves

 |D_(q_1,...,q_p) K_p|
   <= C'_p eta^2 exp[-(mu1/p)sum_j d(a,q_j)].                 (11)

The fixed inverse rho powers are retained in C'_p and are independent
of depth. This is not an inference of joint root decay from a final
summed row: the complete action-root a is kept in (9)--(11) before any
external or fine-root sum. For p=0 use the value bound in (9).
