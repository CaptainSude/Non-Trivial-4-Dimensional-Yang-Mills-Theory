RESULT

There is a concrete native matched subclass for the normalized-law
variation: vary only the retained CURRENT ordinary free coordinates
while holding the ordinary geometry, background and older raw data
fixed. The full ordinary law is known on this slice, including all
action channels. Its real score and spatial response can be bounded
without differentiating an interior cut or a gauge delta.

This does NOT compare the arbitrary reset branches A and empty. Such a
comparison can change geometry, old histories, background-dependent
operators, coordinate charts and source reads. The exact missing
derivatives for that application are identified in Section 6 below.
Nor does a mere bounded-source cap supply the spatial source gradient
needed by the score estimate.

2. A FIXED-DOMAIN SCORE IDENTITY

Let a(t), 0<=t<=1, be a C1 path in the same admitted physical branch,
with psi(a(t)) inside the common strict slice. Its independent interior
coordinate set, domain K_I, background y and all native geometric
labels are fixed. For a differentiable pulled-back test F_t(x),

   =Q_(a(t)) dot F_t-Cov_(Q_(a(t)))(F_t,S_t),

 S_t(x)=partial_a H_pre(x,psi(a(t));y)
                              [Dpsi(a(t)) dot a(t)].       (3)

Differentiate numerator and denominator in their ORIGINAL ratio to
prove this. No lower bound for Z_a occurs. On the finite compact
interior domain, the strict smooth chart permits differentiation under
the integral; more general integrable tests follow by domination.

Every x-independent derivative disappears from the covariance. This
includes a derivative of log J_psi(a), of the exterior scalar allocation,
and of the retained Schur baseline. It is therefore unnecessary to
prove a joint outer score in order to prove (3). The earlier switch
note correctly did not establish the joint score, but that stronger
object is not needed for this fixed-fiber calculation.

The score gradient is exactly

 grad_x S_t=H_pre,I E''(x,psi(a(t));y) b_t,
                         b_t=Dpsi(a(t)) dot a(t).         (4)

This is where the mixed complete action Hessian enters. Bounding only
the inherited-B score would omit the Gaussian force D_IE b_t and the
other physical action channels.

B119 printed 251--252 (1.22) has, in physical variables z=g a,

where H is independent of the current noise x,a. The retained-current
switch is bondwise; outside its declared exterior set it is identity.
The native bounds give |H|<=C epsilon exp(-R) and delta=lambda epsilon
with fixed lambda>0. Choose the allowed smooth cutoff in (1.22) by a

              ||D_a chi||<=C_chi g/delta.                (5)

This is an explicit cutoff choice, not a consequence of the word
smooth for an arbitrary uncontrolled cutoff. The small strict annulus

On the fixed BCH chart, BCH(z,0)=z. Smoothness and the mean-value
theorem therefore give uniformly

 ||partial_z BCH(z,w)-I||<=C|w|,

Differentiating the native map and using (5) yields

 ||Dpsi-I||<=C|H|+C|H|/delta

The bound holds in each finite Lie-algebra block; Dpsi is block diagonal

4. A REAL NORMALIZED-LAW VARIATION BOUND

The full conditional weighted covariance theorem says

 |Cov(F,G)|<=c_nu^(-1)
             ||W^(-1)grad F||_(L2(Q)) ||W grad G||_(L2(Q)),

for positive block-scalar W with nu-Lipschitz logarithm. The complete
mixed-Hessian row/column bound and Schur's test give

 ||W_I grad_x S_t||_(L2(Q))
                     <=M_nu ||W_E b_t||_2.              (7)

Consequently

 |d/dt Q_(a(t))F_t|
 <=Q_(a(t))|dot F_t|
   +c_nu^(-1)M_nu(1+C_lambda exp(-R))
       ||W_I^(-1)grad_x F_t||_(L2(Q))
       ||W_E dot a(t)||_2.                              (8)

All means and norms use the same exact law (1). For a fixed test F,
the first term vanishes, giving a bound on (Q_a1-Q_a0)F itself.

If dot a is supported in T and F has a source arm based on P with

 L_F= sup_t ||exp[nu dist(.,P)]grad_x F||_(L2(Q_t))<infinity,

choose W(z)=exp[-nu dist(z,P)]. Integrating (8) gives

 |(Q_a1-Q_a0)F|
 <=c_nu^(-1)M_nu(1+C_lambda exp(-R)) L_F
       exp[-nu dist(P,T)] integral_0^1 ||dot a(t)||_2 dt.  (9)

For a straight supported path its length is ||a1-a0||_2, provided the
ENTIRE interpolation satisfies the stated admission conditions. Admitted
endpoints alone do not establish this. This is a finite one-ordinary-
kernel comparison with the original denominator.
For a literal smooth cylinder F(x_P), L_F<=sup||grad F||_2. More
generally it applies to a complete admitted native source whose OWN
rooted contour estimate supplies the stated gradient arm. It does not
assign an arm from a nominal source label.

The existing physical_boundary_source_transport_20261022, (2.6), gives
the unweighted bound ||grad F||_2<=C ell_j^(-Q) A_(j,F) for its separately
admitted complete source jets. Inserting that estimate in (8) with
W=1 supplies a concrete whole-law retained-coordinate response for that
source subclass. A distance gain additionally uses the actual arm
reserve of the input, not the unweighted estimate alone.

For an opaque bounded source |F|<=a, conditional Cauchy--Schwarz and
the unweighted variance bound for S instead yield

 |(Q_a1-Q_a0)F|
 <=a c0^(-1/2) M_0(1+C_lambda exp(-R))
                        integral_0^1 ||dot a(t)||_2 dt.  (10)

This bound requires no derivative of F. It carries NO source-to-change
distance and is not a volume-uniform comparison for arbitrarily many
changed retained coordinates. It cannot close the remote survivor
problem by itself.

5. WHAT HAPPENS TO MOVING CUTS AND GEOMETRY

The exact absence of a moving interior cut is part of this subclass:
the native independent set and K_I are fixed and the switch is exterior.
One may vary the exterior masks along the path only insofar as the path
stays on the same positive ordinary stratum and the conditional ratio
continues to be (1). Those masks cancel there, not in the outer law.

For a genuinely moving smooth interior domain K_t, the exact derivative
has, in addition to the score and test terms, the shape contribution

 Z_t^(-1) integral_(boundary K_t)
            (F_t-Q_tF_t) exp[-H_t] v_normal dS.           (11)

A varying smooth cutoff contributes its true density derivative
instead. A changing constraint/gauge chart requires its actual
pullback and coarea/Jacobian derivative. None of these can be omitted
by invoking convexity. A jump between geometric branches may not have
a common-dimensional differentiable path at all.
