1. FIXED EVENT AND ALREADY PROVED INPUT

Use the ACTUAL free current bonds of LI(1.100), including its gauge slice,
and keep every other current bond frozen at its actual exterior value.
The joined baseline v0 is V_Lambda on these free bonds and is the frozen
field elsewhere. Let R be the union of the COMPLETE raw current plaquette
reads of all the actual B119(2.16)--(2.17) final tests being considered.
Endpoint, virtual-edge, and helper-neighborhood reads are included.

Set epsilon=epsilon_k and define

 G(r) = {sup_(p in R) |v0(partial p)-1| <= epsilon/(8 B3)}.       (1)

Here r is the complete conditioning data. The reference q_r is precisely
the normalized fresh kernel in LI(1.100), with its original Haar measure,
gauge fixing, cutoff, and denominator. No older mass is absorbed into q_r.

The proof in work/final_test_sensitivity_20261022.txt and its independent
audit give, for the actual final failure indicator B(r,v),

       1_G(r) q_r B <= 1_G(r) delta_k,
 delta_k = C_M exp[-c_M (epsilon_k/(32 B3 g_k))^2].             (2)

The proof uses exact full-array retraction, unitary four-edge telescoping,
and the strict native parameter epsilon_1=epsilon/(3 B3): inside the
success ball every tested fine plaquette is strictly below
epsilon eta^2/3. It also admits the final branch on the whole actual q
support under G. Neither part is reproved here. With the native convention
epsilon_k/g_k=A0 p0(g_k), the exponent in(2) is a fixed multiple of
p0(g_k)^2, with A0 included in the fixed coefficient.

Equation(1), not a boundary-only replacement of it, is the event used in
this note. A large baseline plaquette on a free bond need not itself be
a plaquette of the sampled outer variable.

6. THE NEW CREDIT THAT IS ALREADY LEGITIMATE

For any positive old/raw measure d tau(r,x), with all old mass factors
retained exactly once, conditional Fubini and(2) give

 integral d tau 1_G(r) integral q_r(dv) B(r,v)
          <= delta_k integral d tau 1_G(r).                 (6)

This is a genuine new probability credit for a newly sampled final failure
on G. It does not reuse the old primitive chi record. It also holds with
an arbitrary nonnegative weight depending only on the conditioning data.

The actual raw R operation may additionally carry a cavity or source
weight W(r,x,v) depending on the newly drawn v. Equation(2) alone gives
only

 integral q_r B |W| <= delta_k sup_v |W|                    (7)

on G, not delta_k times integral q_r|W|. A repeated contraction therefore
requires an actual weighted row comparison or an admitted oscillation/
majorant estimate for that weight. It also requires positive normalized
intervening kernels, or an explicitly controlled replacement for them.
The raw R operation contains nonnormalized masses and cavity factors;
calling it a Markov kernel does not establish these requirements.
This note leaves the current common-reference/weighted-kernel registration
to its separate audit. It does not negate(6) when that registration holds.

Quantitatively, the new credit is strong enough for the already accepted
forecast IF that weighted registration succeeds. Endpoint/source volume
nu<=C R_k^4 costs exp(C nu), absorbed by exp[-c p0(g_k)^2] when p>2r.
The accepted augmented full forecast uses p>6r; in that hierarchy
p0(g_k)^2 dominates R_k^6 p0(g_k), hence can fund the full new lookback
and endpoint forecast. Thus a genuine final failure from a newly sampled
field on G can conditionally become a NEW primitive token for the funded
forest argument. Merely performing a reset is not this new event. This
comparison of scales is not a product-of-probabilities proof: the actual
weighted raw kernel must first support the conditional row estimate.

7. EXACT NEXT MATHEMATICAL PRODUCER

The exceptional sector needs the following two linked native statements,
for the actual eligible geometry and actual numerator after baseline
cancellation, with the old masses and crossing factors retained.

WITNESS / SINGLE-EVENT COST. From G^c produce either an already owned
native primitive bad record, or an actual-variable/action-packet witness

             E_a => S_a >= c_a (epsilon_k/g_k)^2.            (8)

The coefficient c_a may contain the fixed B3,B5,M losses in(5), but must
be uniform over the admitted geometry and histories. An actual coarse
plaquette witness of size theta epsilon_k would suffice only after the
native comparison S_a >= c gamma_k |dV-1|^2, its localization, and its
unspent positive fraction have been verified. An exceptional minimizer
plaquette alone does not provide that comparison.

ONE-TIME OWNERSHIP. Assign each newly charged action packet to one owner
at its first actual use. For every simultaneous/history collection of
such owners, prove

             sum_a S_a <= S_available,                     (9)

with enough remaining action for all still-required native coercivity
estimates. A bounded-overlap version is sufficient if the allocation
fractions are decreased accordingly. The exact elementary consequence is

 exp(-S_available) product_a 1_(E_a)
 <= exp[-sum_a c_a(epsilon_(k_a)/g_(k_a))^2]
                       exp[-(S_available-sum_a S_a)].        (10)

Repeated uses of the same packet can appear only once in this product.
A new scale/date label does not prove(9). No native construction checked
here yields(9) across repeated reads/resets of the exceptional exterior
sector. The existing lifetime recurrence cannot be substituted for(9)
