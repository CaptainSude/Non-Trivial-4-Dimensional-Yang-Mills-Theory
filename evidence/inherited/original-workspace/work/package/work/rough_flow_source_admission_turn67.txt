1. PRIMARY SOURCE AND EXACT ADMISSION

Ilya Chevyrev and Hora Mirsajjadi, "Local well-posedness of subcritical
non-linear heat equations with Gaussian initial data," arXiv:2410.11638,
version2 (21 August 2025), Assumption1.1 and Theorem1.2, pages2--3:
 https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.11638
 https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfa.2025.111160

Their theorem treats stationary centered Gaussian initial data in
spatial dimension n>=2 with even covariance C(x)=C(-x) and bounds
|D^k C(x)|<=K |x|^(2-d-|k|), |k|<=1, for a fractional parameter
2<d<4. This parameter need not equal n. The heat equation has the
specified bilinear A,DA and cubic A terms, with the allowed lower
order polynomial terms. It proves convergence of mollified nonlinear heat
solutions up to a positive random time, with inverse-time moments.
The critical endpoint d=4 is excluded. The discussion explains that
equation-specific structure would be needed there.

The theorem also requires Gaussianity. We have not proved the stated
covariance hypotheses or an appropriate non-Gaussian extension for
the interacting Hamiltonian ground law. These are separate issues
from the endpoint discussed next.

2. OWN FREE EQUAL-TIME CALCULATION

Take one canonically normalized free scalar component as the
ultraviolet scaling diagnostic. Integrating the four-dimensional
massless covariance in the Euclidean frequency gives

 int_R (dk0/(2pi)) [k0^2+|k|^2]^-1 =1/(2|k|).

In three spatial dimensions its inverse Fourier transform is

 C(x)=1/(4pi^2 |x|^2), x!=0.

One direct calculation inserts a radial regulator exp(-epsilon |k|):

 C_epsilon(x)
  =[1/(4pi^2 |x|)] int_0^infinity exp(-epsilon r)
                                      sin(r |x|) dr
  =1/[4pi^2(epsilon^2+|x|^2)].

Letting epsilon tend to zero proves the displayed formula away
from the origin, and the distributional limit is the same.
For transverse free Maxwell components the matrix projector changes
the angular tensor, while the homogeneity remains degree-2. For
example the spatial trace has rank factor2 and exactly the scalar
homogeneity above. Removing infrared modes on a torus does not
change this short-distance exponent.

Thus the equal-time free ultraviolet benchmark corresponds to
fractional d=4, even though the spatial slice has dimension3.
It cannot be admitted to Theorem1.2 merely by calling the slice
three dimensional. Indeed for every epsilon>0,

 |x|^-2 / |x|^(-2+epsilon)=|x|^-epsilon ->infinity.

A fixed logarithmic weakening does not repair this power admission:
for any p>=0 and epsilon>0,

 [|x|^-2 / log(1/|x|)^p] / |x|^(-2+epsilon)
       = |x|^-epsilon / log(1/|x|)^p ->infinity.

This comparison is an elementary test of the theorem's hypothesis,
not an assertion of the exact interacting short-distance covariance.
We have not replaced the interacting measure by this free law.

3. OWN HEAT-SMOOTHING DIAGNOSTIC

For the same scalar component, smoothing both factors by spatial
heat time s>0 gives at one point

 C_s(0)
  =int_(R^3) [d^3k/(2pi)^3] exp(-2s |k|^2)/(2|k|)
  =[1/(4pi^2)]int_0^infinity r exp(-2s r^2)dr
  =1/(16pi^2 s).

The bare small-time integral of this quantity is logarithmically
divergent. This identifies a critical scale in a prospective
nonlinear estimate. It does not calculate all nonlinear diagrams,
their gauge cancellations, renormalizations, or their final sum.
In particular it is not a proof that the actual gauge-invariant
flowed observable diverges.

Replacing the free multiplier by |k|^(-1-2epsilon), epsilon>0,
would change the fractional dimension to4-2epsilon. Such a
replacement changes the input. Uniform estimates as epsilon goes
to zero, and gauge compatibility of a corresponding construction,
would still have to be proved.
