FLAT PHYSICAL MINIMUM: PERIODIZATION, MOMENT TAILS, AND AFFINE MATCHING
25 October 2026. endpoint_return. Fixed finite homogeneous prefix.

RESULT AND LIMITS

The old physical flat conditional minimum, together with its actual Wilson
current, has a period-independent exponentially localized linear response
at every FIXED finite homogeneous depth. Its finite-torus response is the
exact image sum of the explicit infinite-lattice flat response, including
the chosen local gauge return. This gives uniform TAILS of second and
higher position moments, not just bounded moments. On affine determining
data its field is the native affine fine field modulo the recorded gauge,
and its linearized physical current vanishes. Consequently its current
Bloch symbol has a double zero, with the internal fine-cell coordinates
retained.

No limit of a new independent-current continuation or of its coefficients
is used. Constants below may depend on the fixed depth N and all frozen
local conventions, but not on the ambient period P. This is sufficient for
a fixed-prefix P -> infinity comparison. It is not a uniform-in-N estimate,
a proof of convergence of arbitrary coefficient germs, or a proof that an
independent-current AJ Hessian can be ignored solely because J(q)=O(q^2).
The latter still needs the appropriate pair Ward identity.

2. EXPLICIT FLAT OPERATORS

Write a>0 for the fixed homogeneous gauge/constraint penalty. At flatness
the scalar and vector operators are

 G'=(d^*d+Q'^*aQ')^-1,
 R=I-G'Q'^*(Q'G'^2Q'^*)^-1 Q'G',
 G=(K+dRd^*+Q^*aQ)^-1,
 W=(QGQ^*)^-1,
 H=GQ^*W.                                                     (1)

These are precisely the flat instances of B99(3.25),(3.26),(3.126),
with the same averaging and adjoint conventions. In particular

             QH=I,       Rd^*H=0.                           (2)

The real flat coercivity estimates and the kernel estimates cited above
construct these inverses on the infinite periodic block lattice. This
uses only explicit flat operators. They can equivalently be obtained by
the native convergent local inverse expansions; no interacting infinite
coefficient family is required.

At fixed N, put all fine sites, bond types and component indices in one
coarse cell into a finite fiber. After taking a smaller decay rate, the
native bounds imply for each displayed kernel and required finite
composition a weighted row/column estimate

 max{sup_x sum_y exp(mu_N |x-y|)|T(x,y)|,
     sup_y sum_x exp(mu_N |x-y|)|T(x,y)|} <= C_N.              (3)

Here distance includes the bounded internal endpoint displacement; sums
use the native measures or their fixed-N equivalent fiber weights. The
constants can absorb any fixed finite fine-cell census. No assertion of
uniformity as N increases is being made.

The physical current derivative is especially simple:

 D J(I)[A]=K A,       L_J=K H.                               (4)

Indeed differentiating D_U^* introduces a factor multiplying Im(dI)=0,
and eta^-2 Im d(exp(i eta A)) has derivative d_1 A. Thus there is no
additional current branch at flatness. This is a statement about the
ACTUAL physical current, not an off-graph selected-current formula.
Multiplication by the finite-range K stencil preserves (3), with a
possible fixed-depth factor C_N. This elementary proof avoids using a
general-background covariant-Laplacian estimate that Theorem3.12
explicitly excludes. It is enough for the present fixed-prefix purpose.

3. EXACT PERIODIZATION, INCLUDING SCHUR FACTORS

Let P be an integer number of coarse cells in each torus direction,
compatible with the fixed block hierarchy. For a coarse-translation
covariant fiber kernel define

 (Per_P T)(x,y)=sum_{n in Z^4} T(x,y+Pn).                    (5)

The sum is absolute by (3). Exact local primitives d,d_1,Q,Q' and their
native adjoints have the same periodic quotient. Absolute sums prove

       Per_P(ST)=(Per_P S)(Per_P T),  Per_P I=I_P.           (6)

The same algebra holds for kernels that are only P-periodic rather than
covariant under every coarse translation. This is the version used for
the frozen-P tiled source Hessian in Section6; its germs need not have
any extra translation symmetry.

Apply this first to the scalar inverse, then to Q'G'^2Q'^*, its inverse,
R, the vector augmented inverse G, QGQ^*, and its inverse W. An image sum
of an inverse solves both finite inverse equations; uniqueness identifies
it with the actual finite inverse. Thus every factor in (1), not merely
H as a formal symbol, has the correct finite counterpart. In particular

            H_P=Per_P H,       (KH)_P=Per_P(KH).             (7)

The scalar zero mode causes no exception: Q'^*aQ' controls it. The
unconstrained vector gauge modes are controlled by the SAME R and Q
system. Inverting a massless ghost or K alone would be a different and
invalid argument.

The native axial return has a linearization of the form A -> A+dT_N A
(with any explicit coarse endpoint record kept). T_N consists of the
same finite within-block/hierarchical endpoint reads at every period.
It commutes with (5); composition with H preserves (3), possibly with
new C_N,mu_N. Therefore (7) also holds for the actual ordinary physical
field representative. Its current is unchanged since Kd=0. One may
instead use the relative Landau representative in a gauge-invariant
Hessian, provided the corresponding fine gauge is applied in BOTH slots.

There is no freedom here to introduce a period-dependent global tree or
to forget a coarse gauge record. Such a choice would require its own
comparison. B102(181) uses the native block-based gauge convention.

4. CONSTANT AND AFFINE REPRODUCTION

Let A_mu(x)=c_mu+sum_nu B_munu x_nu, evaluated at the fine bond midpoint.
The coefficients may be in one color, or interpreted componentwise in
the linearized Lie algebra. Its curl is constant, hence

                         K A=0.                            (8)

For the corrected flat Q, the literal primitive formula is the average
of the straight parallel string, after its endpoint-gradient correction.
The centered offset has mean zero and the mean string midpoint is the
coarse bond midpoint. Hence Q A is exactly the affine coarse one-form
with the same physical coefficients c,B. Equivalently in raw unit-link
coordinates, a constant scales by L and curvature by L^2 each step.

For completeness the ordinary average has the same result. Write an
endpoint x=y+r. The two endpoint-contour linear means have translated
offset terms that cancel. The full path from y through y+r and its
parallel string to y+ell e_mu has affine integral

 ell A_mu(y+ell e_mu/2)
       + ell sum_nu (B_munu-B_numu) r_nu.                   (9)

The anchored relative logarithm in B109(0.12) averages the second term;
the centered r average is zero. This proves the literal ordinary result.
Iterate the PRIMITIVE average: balanced digits still have mean zero. It
is unnecessary and unjustified to assume nonlinear group-mean
associativity. For the transverse Maxwell test B_mumu=0, midpoint and
tail conventions give the identical displayed test.

The scalar projection satisfies R1=0. On a finite torus this follows
also from R projecting onto d^*d ker Q', which is orthogonal to constants.
Directly from (1), Q'1=Q'^*1=1 with the native measures, G'1=a^-1 1,
and (Q'G'^2Q'^*)^-1 1=a^2 1, so the subtracted term in R1 equals one.
All these equalities extend to the cover by absolute kernel sums.
Since d^*A is constant for an affine one-form,

                  Rd^*A=0.                                (10)

Thus if b=QA is the affine coarse datum,

 (K+dRd^*+Q^*aQ)A=Q^*a b.

The exponentially localized inverse identities extend to polynomial
growth sequences: multiplication by (1+|y|)^p is absorbed by a moment
of the exponential kernel, and double sums remain absolutely convergent.
Applying G and then Q gives A=GQ^*a b and QGQ^*a b=b. Applying W yields
a b=Wb, whence

                         Hb=A.                             (11)

This establishes the affine identity for the relative Landau lift with
the native units, not only an existence argument for a Maxwell solution.
After the ordinary axial return,

             H_phys b=A+d omega_b,    L_J b=0.              (12)

omega_b has at most polynomial growth with a fixed block-periodic
pattern. The polynomial is only a test of a Taylor kernel at zero;
an unbounded finite-amplitude field is NOT put in the nonlinear domain.

In particular the Landau H(0) maps constant coarse potentials to the
constant fine potential. The native axial representative can have pure
gauge cell aliases already at q=0; it need not literally be constant.
Any statement of a constant zero-mode in axial gauge must be understood
modulo that explicit gradient.

5. GAUGE INTERTWINING AND THE BLOCH FIRST JET

B99(3.115) gives Qd=d_c Q'. One explicit scalar extension is obtained
by applying the native Landau projection to dQ'^*phi:

 E phi=Q'^*phi-G'R d^*d Q'^*phi.

Its Q' average is phi, its gradient is Landau, and its curvature is zero.
The constrained uniqueness or (1) therefore gives

                    H d_c phi=d E phi.                    (13)

This identity holds on the cover and for every allowed torus momentum
after (5). In the native axial convention the corresponding identity
is the derivative of B102(181), using its block-constant extension of
the coarse gauge. It is not a claim that two arbitrary interpolations
of a coarse scalar gauge coincide.

Use Bloch fibers with ALL fine-cell indices. Input a coarse plane wave
b_mu(y)=v_mu exp(i q.(y+e_mu/2)), and keep the actual fine bond midpoint
in the output phase. Differentiating at q=0 produces exactly the
constant and affine polynomial inputs of Section4. Therefore in the
relative Landau representative the zeroth field jet is the constant
mode, and the first jet, with the output phase restored, is the native
fine affine field. In an axial representative these equalities hold
with the derivative of the recorded gauge return added.

Equations (8),(11) imply, in either convention,

              L_J(0)=0,   partial_q L_J(0)=0,
              ||L_J(q)|| <= C_N |q|^2                      (14)

on a fixed small real or complex momentum neighborhood. The analyticity
and Taylor bound follow from (3). This proves the double zero despite
the fine-cell aliases. Merely observing that a scalar lattice Laplacian
has a double zero would NOT suffice for the full internal matrix.

6. UNIFORM POSITION-MOMENT TAILS

For each joint response branch L=(H_phys,KH), each fixed p>=0, and
R>=1, (3) gives

 sup_x sum_{y:|x-y|>=R} (1+|x-y|)^p |L(x,y)|
       <= C_(N,p) exp(-mu_N R/2),                           (15)

and the analogous column estimate. The finite version uses the image
displacement BEFORE summing images. Its positive majorant is bounded by
the same cover sum, uniformly in P. Using minimal periodic displacement
only makes this estimate smaller. A winding image with a short periodic
endpoint is still detected if one keeps its image displacement.

Now take any family of fixed-P short own-carrier pair Hessians K_P^short
whose complete rooted exponential cell norm is bounded by M r^-2,
with the original source roots retained. The numerical short germs may
depend on P. Lift those finitely supported numerical germs and tile
them according to their declared root allocation; no convergence is
asserted. Define the physical Hessian on the cover by

              G_P=L^T K_P^short L.                          (16)

Use bilinear transpose and the native lattice measures. Combining the
three read lengths, each input or output separation is bounded by their
sum. Weighted Schur convolution, with a smaller exponent, gives a
complete rooted bound C_N M r^-2 for (16), and

 sum_{read length>=R} (1+length)^2 |G_P(root;read)|
       <= C_N M r^-2 exp(-c_N R).                           (17)

The same proof gives every fixed higher moment. This is the required
UNIFORM TAIL, not only a bounded total second moment. Each AA,AJ,JA,JJ
branch is included. Internal fine-cell coordinate conversions and any
fixed collar have already been included in C_N. If stronger depth-
uniform constants are needed later, the strong graded operator norms
must be used; (17) alone does not provide them.

By (6)--(7), the periodization of (16) is EXACTLY the actual finite
short physical Hessian formed from that same K_P^short and actual
physical lift. This is a comparison of one finite family with its
declared numerical cover lift, not a comparison of different P germs.

8. WHAT HAS AND HAS NOT BEEN SUPPLIED

Supplied for the actual flat physical minimum at fixed finite depth:
  - the explicit augmented/Schur operator and exact periodization;
  - physical current KH and its periodization;
  - exponentially small tails of all fixed position moments;
  - ordinary/corrected affine matching with the actual centered rule;
  - recorded gauge-return qualification, allowed-momentum gauge
    intertwining, and the Bloch current double zero;
  - the same finite Wilson reference normalization.

Not supplied by this operator lemma: convergence of any independently
chosen nonlinear source continuation across periods; a Ward identity for
an arbitrary noninvariant root allocation; disposal of wrapping scalar
functions; uniform-in-depth constants; or the full Ward/Maxwell tensor
classification. The fixed-P short coefficient norm and its gauge/root
registration must be checked by their own producer. Once those are
registered, (16)--(17) require no further source-germ convergence.
