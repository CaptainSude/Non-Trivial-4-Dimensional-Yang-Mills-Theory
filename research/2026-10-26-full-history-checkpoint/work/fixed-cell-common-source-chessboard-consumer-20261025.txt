FIXED-CELL COMMON-SOURCE CHESSBOARD CONSUMER
25 October 2026. Root. Exact conditional theorem, not a source producer.

PURPOSE AND STATUS

This records the precise local normalized estimate needed by
calibrated-four-entry-common-source-packing-20261025.txt. It shows that
there is no additional volume-dependent normalization problem once the
actual common source family has the four hypotheses below. It does NOT
assert that the full original Wilson-history source family has those
hypotheses. In particular finite homogeneous source jets do not supply H2.

The complex chessboard step uses conjugate reflected COPIES of the source,
not its real part. Replacing a complex source by its real part before
subtracting the ultraviolet vacuum would leave an uncontrolled counterterm
difference. No zero-free neighborhood for a partition function is used.

1. FINITE SETUP AND EXPLICIT HYPOTHESES

Let mu be the ORIGINAL positive Wilson probability measure on a compatible
finite four-torus of endpoint volume V. Tile it by m congruent cells D_c,
each of endpoint side ell, so V=m ell^4. Require the usual reflection-positive
cell algebra and the complex chessboard inequality for this tiling. The
original plaquettes in a source read must lie wholly in its open cell.
This is the bare Wilson algebra, not an effective history measure or a
conditioned homogeneous branch. Compatible reflection tilings may be chosen
cofinally; no arbitrary-torus chessboard assertion is needed.

Fix D_0 compactly inside one open cell D, at distance eta>0 from its faces.
The fixed ell, eta, D_0 are independent of UV depth and ambient volume.
For complex bare weights w with support in D_0, write

 J(w)=sum_original_positive_p w(x_p) h_p,
 Z_raw(w)=Z_raw(0) E_mu exp[J(w)],
 h(w)=exp[-E(w)] Z_raw(w)/Z_raw(0).

If a source-free vacuum normalization has already been inserted, use that
same normalization in both raw partition functions; it cancels in h.
The calibrated substitution w=c_a v will be made only at the end.

H1. There is ONE scalar family E(w)=sum_{j,X} e_j(X;w), E(0)=0,
holomorphic on the specified complex source norm ball. Each coefficient
depends only on the weight values in its COMPLETE source carrier X^+,
and vanishes if those values are all zero. Its definition is independent
of the way w is written as a sum of profiles. All completed regular and
healed universal scalars use this same family. History-dependent scalar
terms remain in the remainder. There is no decomposition into independently
chosen single-profile normalizations.

H2. The construction is admitted not only for a weight in D_0 but also
for its FULL reflected/conjugated chessboard weight w^ch on all cells.
For these weights, with the SAME E,

 |Z_tilde(w^ch)| <= exp(C_+ V),
 Z_tilde(0)=Z_raw(0) >= exp(-C_- V),
 Z_tilde(w)=exp[-E(w)] Z_raw(w).                         (1)

The constants are independent of UV depth and ambient volume. These are
unnormalized, full-history estimates before taking any root. The source
ball must admit arbitrarily many disjoint reflected copies without a
radius loss depending on their number. A global l1 norm on profile
coefficients does not have this property; the local supremum/Lipschitz
weight norm does, since the weight vanishes in a face buffer.

H3. Local coefficients are covariant under this fixed cell reflection/
translation group and satisfy e_j(X;conj(w))=conj(e_j(X;w)). It suffices
instead to have a bound C_cov on the SUMMED real bulk covariance defect
per cell, uniform in UV depth. No covariance under arbitrary bare
translations of the RG scaffold is requested.

H4. There is a rooted tree majorant with fixed exponential reserve. In
particular, for every w in the ball supported in the WHOLE D_0 (not just
one of its possibly many pieces), its face-crossing vacuum sum obeys

 |E^face(w)| <= C_face,
 m^-1 |E^face(w^ch)| <= C_face.                         (2)

These bounds follow from a complete-carrier tree norm as follows. At
date j let s_j be its physical cell scale and assume s_j<=s_max, fixed
by the terminal band. Root the coefficients at a scale-j cell meeting
D_0 or one of its copies. The number of possible roots is at most
C[1+diam(D_0)/s_j]^4. A carrier meeting both D_0 and a cell face has
tree diameter at least [c eta/s_j-C0]_+. Thus the total is bounded by

 C sum_j [1+diam(D_0)/s_j]^4 exp[-b eta/s_j]             (3)

with a harmless change of C for s_j comparable to eta. For the
geometric scales s_j<eta the exponential dominates the polynomial;
the sum is uniformly finite as the UV cutoff is removed. There are
only O(1+log(s_max/eta)) coarser scales. Copies multiply the root
count by at most m, canceled by m^-1 in (2). A carrier hitting several
copies may be overcounted, which is harmless for this upper bound.
The bound depends on the diameter/buffer of D_0, never on the number
of disjoint bumps used to describe w.

2. COMPLEX CHESSBOARD ESTIMATE AND EXACT BULK CANCELLATION

Set F=exp[J(w)] in the cell algebra of D. Repeated reflection-positive
Cauchy-Schwarz gives the standard complex chessboard estimate

 |E_mu F| <= [E_mu product_c F^[c]]^(1/m),              (4)

where a reflected odd copy is conjugated. The number in brackets is
nonnegative. The product is exactly exp[J(w^ch)], since every bare
plaquette in these supports belongs to one cell. This is an identity
before integrating any ordinary or large-field variable. Thus

 |E_mu F| <= exp{m^-1 Re E(w^ch)+(C_++C_-)ell^4}.       (5)

Equation (5) follows directly from (1). It does not estimate |E(w)|
by an extensive absolute bound. Taking a real part at this final
stage preserves the counterterm cancellations needed below.

Split E into bulk coefficients whose complete X^+ is inside one
open chessboard cell and face coefficients. Locality makes each
bulk coefficient depend only on the weight copy in that cell.
Reflection covariance and reality imply

 m^-1 Re E^bulk(w^ch)=Re E^bulk(w).                     (6)

For an odd cell the contribution is the conjugate of the original
bulk contribution and hence has the same real part. The zero source
on all other cells makes their original bulk terms vanish. With
the bounded-defect form of H3 the difference in (6) is at most C_cov.
Subtract Re E(w) from (5) and use (2). This proves

 |h(w)| <= Q_0,
 Q_0=exp[(C_++C_-)ell^4+2 C_face+C_cov].               (7)

The estimate is uniform in UV depth, ambient volume, arbitrary signs,
and the number of profile pieces inside D_0. It holds for complex w.
The normalization is the full original law. h(0)=1 exactly and h is
holomorphic by H1 and the original finite Haar integral; zeros of h
away from zero do not affect (7) or the two-jet consumer.

3. REFLECTION AND SOURCE DOMAIN DETAILS

The reflected weight uses w or conj(w) on alternate cells. This is
not holomorphic as a function of w, but it is used only as a pointwise
comparison weight at each fixed w. H2 is a bound on all weights in a
complex norm ball, so it applies to this comparison weight. h itself
remains a holomorphic function of the original source coordinates.

When w vanishes at distance eta from the faces, the copy operation
preserves its supremum and Lipschitz norms up to a fixed convention
constant. For two points in different cells, connect their segment
through a face where w is zero; the usual two estimates bound the
difference by Lip(w) times their separation. No sum over cells enters.
On the lattice, retain the plaquette diameter in the buffer: take
the fine spacing sufficiently small that the whole plaquette read
is in its assigned open cell. Finitely many earlier cutoff values
can be excluded when choosing the cofinal regulator sequence.

The possible large value of Q_0 at a fixed weak terminal band is not
a UV divergence. It is retained explicitly in the packing scale
choice. This proof gives no connected clustering estimate by itself.
