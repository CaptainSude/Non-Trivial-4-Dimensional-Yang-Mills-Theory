This is a diagnostic of an inference, not a Wilson-theory counterexample.

2. EXACT POSITIVE MARK ABSORPTION

Let nu be ANY positive finite measure, including a frozen positive
history-augmented Wilson representation. Let F,H>=0 be the literal old
marks, and set Z(s,t)=int e^(sF+tH)dnu. For lambda,eta>0 and integers m,n>=0,

 int F^m H^n dnu
 <= (m/(e lambda))^m (n/(e eta))^n Z(lambda,eta).          (2.1)

A zero exponent has factor1. Proof: x^m exp(-lambda x) is bounded by
(m/(e lambda))^m on [0,infinity), applied separately to F,H. Division by
Z(0,0) gives the FULL normalized moment bound. A bounded remaining test T
costs ||T||infinity on the right. Nothing is replaced by a current mark,
and neither the reference normalizer nor the original normalizer is lost.

For nonnegative plaquette profiles, the right side is precisely the
partition function with the specified local inverse couplings decreased
by lambda f_p+eta g_p. Signed profiles may be dominated using |f|,|g|.
Thus a sufficiently strong REAL bound on the corresponding absolute mass
ratio would give finite marked control without complex source discs.

However a physical-support energy contains O(a^-4) microscopic marks.
Its source-only vacuum/mean terms occur in this mass ratio. Suppression
of a native raw event and its once-owned polynomial reserve does not
remove that census. Subtracting a nonlinear local vacuum functional also
destroys the simple positivity premise used in (2.1). This estimate is
useful only when the required ratio is independently bounded with its
correct source calibration and supported census; it is not such a bound.

3. A COMPACT POSITIVE COUNTEREXAMPLE TO THE UNMARKED REAL-PRESSURE SHORTCUT

Take Z,W independent uniform variables on [-1,1]. For n>=2 put epsilon=1/n
and use the positive probability density

 dmu_n(z,w)=[1+epsilon sin z sin w] dz dw/4.              (3.1)

It lies between 1-epsilon and 1+epsilon times the product probability;
normalization and unchanged uniform marginals follow by oddness. Define
the POSITIVE compact marks F_n=n(1+Z), H_n=n(1+W), each in [0,2n].

Write M(u)=E exp(uZ), a(u)=E[exp(uZ)sin Z]/M(u). Direct integration gives

 Z_n(s,t)=exp[n(s+t)]M(ns)M(nt)
          [1+epsilon a(ns)a(nt)].                      (3.2)

The source-only terms cancel EXACTLY from the interaction pressure

 R_n(s,t)=log[Z_n(s,t)Z_n(0,0)/(Z_n(s,0)Z_n(0,t))]
         =log[1+epsilon a(ns)a(nt)].                    (3.3)

Since |a(u)|<=sin1<1, for ALL real s,t,

 |R_n(s,t)|<=-log(1-epsilon sin^2 1)<=C/n.               (3.4)

But a(0)=0 and a'(0)=E[Z sin Z]=sin1-cos1=:c>0. Consequently

 Cov_mu_n(F_n,H_n)=partial_s partial_t R_n(0,0)
                  =epsilon n^2 c^2=n c^2 -> infinity. (3.5)

This is not due to sign oscillation of the covariance. Under the tilted
one-variable law, a'(u)=Cov_u(Z,sin Z)>0: for independent copies Z,Z',
twice that covariance is E[(Z-Z')(sin Z-sin Z')], positive off the diagonal
because sin is increasing on [-1,1]. Therefore at ALL finite real s,t,

 partial_s partial_t R_n(s,t)
 =epsilon n^2 a'(ns)a'(nt)
   /[1+epsilon a(ns)a(nt)]^2 >0.                        (3.6)

It is a positive response concentrated in a narrowing real-source region.
For any fixed r>0, oddness of a yields the exact integrated response

 int_[-r,r]^2 partial_s partial_t R_n ds dt
 =2 log[(1+epsilon a(nr)^2)/(1-epsilon a(nr)^2)]
 =O(1/n),                                              (3.7)

despite the divergence at the baseline. Thus uniform control of connected
real finite differences does give a smeared estimate here, but cannot be
differentiated to a uniform pointwise mixed jet. The construction is fully
compact; no Gaussian approximation, truncation limit, complex posterior,
or unbounded original integration space is used.

The marks' ranges grow with n. This is precisely the regime relevant to
an unrenormalized sum over an increasing number of microscopic plaquettes.
This example does NOT assert that Wilson sources realize (3.1), nor rule
out a model-specific bound supplying the missing source regularity.

4. WHAT A REAL PRESSURE ESTIMATE ACTUALLY PROVES WITHOUT EXTRA REGULARITY

For any finite Wilson regulator and disjoint deterministic source profiles,
let R(s,t) be its interaction pressure as in (3.3). Then for compactly
supported smooth real test functions phi,psi,

 int Cov_(s,t)(S_f,S_g) phi(s)psi(t) dsdt
 =int R(s,t) phi'(s)psi'(t) dsdt.                        (4.1)

If |R|<=K on their support, the absolute value of the LEFT INTEGRAL is
at most K ||phi'||_1 ||psi'||_1. This is a signed, source-averaged bound;
without a separately proved sign it is NOT an integral of the absolute
covariance. If the mixed covariance is nonnegative on a rectangle, its
integral there is its four-corner pressure difference, bounded by4K.

Equations (3.4)--(3.7) prove that neither statement determines the value
at a prescribed baseline source. A pointwise bound therefore requires
additional quantitative source regularity or direct finite-mark estimates.
For an existence construction at a fixed physical coupling, averaging in
an artificial source parameter is a changed target unless an independent
argument recovers the baseline hierarchy and its axioms.
