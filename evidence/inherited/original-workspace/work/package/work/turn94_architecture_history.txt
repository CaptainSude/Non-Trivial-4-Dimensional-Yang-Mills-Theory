DECISION

Keep a source-preserving construction from the ORIGINAL Wilson law as
the primary route. Give priority to a genuinely local, finite-volume
marked theorem with the same original normalization and boundary data.
Keep the bounded time-window/entropy witness as a nontriviality diagnostic,
not as the definition or nearly completed construction of full YM4.

A direct theory built from the native RT laws is logically possible and
would not have to be proved equal to the original Wilson limit merely
to establish existence by another regulator. But that switch does not
remove the present difficulty for free: it abandons the already identified
original observable law and imports unproved reflection positivity,
scale compatibility, locality and Yang--Mills identification obligations.
The current archive does not justify preferring that much larger fork.
A limited literal one-R positivity audit is a sensible early feasibility
test if the fork is pursued; a new abstract positivity criterion is not.

1. THE ENDPOINT MUST BE KEPT DISTINCT FROM THE CURRENT WITNESS

The earlier full_hierarchy_strategy_audit_turn19.txt Section2 already
records the initial project target as SU(2) existence, with local
gauge-invariant observable content and Yang--Mills ultraviolet matching.
That is different from the all-compact-simple-groups mass-gap problem.
I rechecked Jaffe--Witten, Quantum Yang--Mills Theory, Sections3--4,
printed pp5--6:
https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/yangmills.pdf
Its existence formulation requires a relativistic local QFT, local
curvature/jet observables with renormalization mixing, and the prescribed
short-distance Yang--Mills behavior. The mass gap is an additional
requirement of the full Millennium problem. A positive-energy vector is
neither a mass gap nor identification of a Yang--Mills theory.

For the present existence target the following are distinct obligations.
They need not be completed in this chronological order.

 (I) A COMMON continuum observable theory. Construct compatible local
     Schwinger distributions/field operators, or an adequate algebraic
     local net and state. Joint observables, their domains/regularity,
     and any required contact extensions must refer to one theory and
     one physical normalization, not separately selected subsequences
     for mutually incompatible probes.

 (II) The reconstruction properties of that theory: positivity of the
      relevant OS forms or an equivalent positive-energy construction,
      Euclidean/Poincare covariance, locality and suitable continuity.
      Hypercubic covariance and a single time semigroup do not establish
      continuous rotations, relativistic locality or field domains.

 (III) Identification as the intended non-Abelian theory: a controlled
       physical coupling/scale trajectory and the required local
       gauge-invariant content and ultraviolet relations. Giving a
       bounded statistic a parameter named g_* does not identify the
       renormalized Yang--Mills coupling. Classical Taylor symbols alone
       do not prove renormalized composite matching or quantum dynamics.

 (IV) Nontrivial physical dynamics, and a vacuum/phase with the intended
      clustering properties. A nonzero time-filtered OS vector is an
      efficient sufficient witness for part of this obligation. The
      witness must belong to the SAME theory in (I)--(III). If a mass-gap
      theorem is later claimed, it requires additional quantitative work.

This does NOT require putting all renormalized local composites on one
ordinary classical probability space S'(R^4;V). Singular composite
contacts can prevent that representation even when a Schwinger hierarchy
or local quantum field is meaningful. In particular, tightness of bounded
flowed scalars is not a replacement for the contact-extension/OS-domain
problem for local curvature polynomials. The common-law hypothesis of
the TURN91 component theorem is one available sufficient input, not a
necessary format for every possible Yang--Mills construction.

A gauge-fixed pointwise connection, simultaneous construction of every
invariant jet before any useful theorem, a common entire source disc,
and one particular factorial norm are also not intrinsic requirements.
The earlier per-arity reconstruction and algebraic-net alternatives
remain available at their stated regularity hypotheses. Conversely,
relabeling a scalar subsector as a net does not prove those hypotheses.

2. WHAT THE CURRENT BOUNDED-SOURCE PROGRAM WOULD ACTUALLY FINISH

The ORIGINAL nonlinear spatial-flow probe phi_a(t) is bounded and is
measurable on its original time slice. Its two adjacent time averages
give G_a with |G_a|<=1, exact zero mean and a reflection symmetry which
negates it. The TURN89 finite thermal argument and TURN92 real-source
identity show how a uniform Var(G_a)>0 would yield a nonzero filtered
OS norm at fixed positive physical times. The thermal reduction does
not require taking a ground-state limit before removing other regulators.
These are substantial exact consumers of a lower bound, not that bound.

TURN93's entropy formulation is quantitatively useful only if a real
competitor is constructed: bounded entropy plus positive original
response gives the variance floor. Conversely the floor itself provides
a bounded-entropy competitor by conditioning. Thus the reformulation is
not an independent source of nondegeneracy.

Even success here would establish a nonstatic scalar sector, not (I)--(III).
There are three concrete reasons.

 * Fixed positive spatial smoothing reads the spatial slice, with an
   unbounded spatial read at finite volume or tails in infinite volume.
   It can be a useful composite of an eventual local theory. Its own
   one-axis RP does not give strict local four-dimensional observables.

 * Rotating the smoothing prescription gives several recipes. It does
   not automatically place their joint algebra in every reflected half
   of one common continuum theory. A spatial flow chosen for one time
   axis can cross a different mirror. The missing assertion is about
   the common algebra and support, not merely an equal scalar covariance.

 * The existing three-color Maxwell reference already supplies nonlinear
   bounded scalar statistics and positive physical reflected responses.
   Consequently a nonzero scalar variance, or non-Gaussian distribution
   of a nonlinear scalar of Gaussian fields, is not by itself evidence
   for a non-Abelian interaction. Yang--Mills matching is a separate task.

The currently proved component module makes this scope especially clear.
turn91_four_dimensional_components_root.txt assumes an actual common
translation-invariant field law with RP at sixteen coordinate/diagonal
mirrors. It then selects clustering components retaining a positive
filtered witness. It supplies only a coordinate spectral cone from those
finite mirrors, not full O(4). The current spatial-flow sector does not
supply that common-law premise. Component selection cannot manufacture
missing local fields, rotations or interaction, and need not preserve
arbitrary nonlinear matching data without a further argument.

The sixteen mirrors are required by THAT sufficient selection proof;
they are not an additional universal axiom independent of a full
O(4)-covariant RP construction or another proved phase-selection route.
Likewise existence can use a common subsequence and an appropriate
phase; uniqueness of every cutoff limit is a stronger optional theorem.

3. WHAT ORIGINAL-WILSON COMPARISON BUYS, AND WHEN IT IS OPTIONAL

There is no logical requirement that every possible construction of
Yang--Mills be obtained as a limit of precisely the Wilson regulator.
One can define another regulator or constructive measure and prove its
axioms and Yang--Mills short-distance identification directly. Equality
to the Wilson limit would then be a universality theorem, not the
definition of existence.

But two different uses of 'comparison' must not be conflated.

 (a) To claim a theorem about the ORIGINAL Wilson source F(U0), a native
     representation must retain that original source and denominator,
     or prove the required comparison. This is indispensable to THAT
     claim. An unsourced partition identity is not a substitute.

 (b) To define a new theory by the CURRENT RT field, comparison with the
     original F can be dropped. Then RP, locality, consistent observables
     at different physical stops, renormalization matching and the other
     reconstruction inputs must be established for the new law itself.

The archive contains a literal reason why these alternatives differ.
At a completed basic R the positive resolved joint is, with full mass
and exterior data retained,
  Pi(de,dx,dy)=Z^-1 sigma(de) tau_e(dx) q_e(dy).
The old record is x, the new current record is y, and q_e is normalized.
For an old source F(e,x), its conditional mean given (e,y) is tau_e(F)/
tau_e(1), independent of y. Fresh q_e variation is not old-field response.
For incomplete continuation the exact marked term instead includes the
actual remaining weight W(e,x,y): int tau_e(F-g)W. Nothing in the scalar
normalization removes this term. These facts are verified in TURN66,
TURN87 and turn93_native_source_history.txt, not hypothetical objections.

Large-I printed194 removes the interior coarse-average constraints before
the old variables are integrated; (1.100), printed201, inserts a new
reference determined by the exterior. Hence the new current field is
not the deterministic original coarse average. A terminal Borel theorem
for a retained ORIGINAL coordinate, as in the reviewed Abelian TPM1,
would be a different exact attachment. Native RT does not presently
have that attachment merely by having a terminal density estimate.

4. THE ACTUAL PRICE OF AN RT-FIRST CONSTRUCTION

4.1 Reflection positivity is not supplied by positive resampling.

For a frozen complete positive forward operation K and nu=mu K, the
relevant new-field form is
  nu[(Theta F)F]=mu[K((Theta F)F)].
It is not generally mu[(Theta KF)(KF)]. The latter identification needs
the actual half-local factorization established only for its stated
scope in TURN63/76/77: independent conditional half noises, reads confined
to the corresponding old half, and reflection-related kernels. Positivity
and reflection covariance of K alone do not prove it. A signed localized
polymer coefficient is not a positive conditional kernel at all.

This does not prove that the native RT law fails RP. It establishes that
the property must be checked rather than inherited without an argument.
Finite-regulator RP failure would also not, by itself, rule out RP in
the continuum; a vanishing negative-form error could be sufficient.

The exact finite native operation is sufficiently specified for a LIMITED
literal audit now. Fix a finite compatible torus, one first basic-R
operation, and the common algebra of bounded gauge-invariant cylinder
functions of its NEW current links supported strictly in a chosen half.
Use the actual old positive density, the complete branch/geometry sum,
the accepted identity branch, old rejected integration and normalized
q_e, including components touching/crossing the mirror. Inspect the
quadratic form above, or its reduced boundary kernel, on that algebra.

The source locators are LI(1.53)--(1.60), printed187--189; the removal of
interior constraints before(1.77), printed194; and(1.99)--(1.102),
printed201--202. These give the defining integrals, not a precomputed
nonnegative character expansion of the reduced boundary kernel. The
implicit adaptive minima and all exterior/gauge conditions remain part
of the operation. Testing q_e alone, a fixed selected homogeneous branch,
or an arbitrary replacement of the actual incoming density is not that
audit. Nor does a finite numerical Gram test prove positivity for the
whole algebra. This is a bounded feasibility task, not a completed input.

4.2 The source of the locality issue is now narrower than an old cut error.

The local deciding configurations in B119 printed246 and LI(1.20) have
the stated finite collars. In contrast LI(1.53) uses the global adaptive
minimum in a relative field, and(1.56)--(1.58) use its restriction in the
small global/local comparison. TURN87 proved that the first new cutoff
and the complete inherited cutoff product are carried exactly by the
native switch. That resolved identity must not be reintroduced as a
generic first-cut crossing error.

What remains unproved for the half-local program is the complete
old-record/current/source read and its quantitative physical influence
through continuation, including reference replacement. A nonlocal gauge
coordinate does not itself prove nonlocality of the gauge-invariant law:
complete gauge transport may cancel coordinate dependence. The necessary
test concerns the actual law and observable algebra, not the numerical
gauge convention. The old half-local note is useful for identifying the
read issue, not for undoing TURN87's exact cut repair.

4.3 Compatibility and scaling are independent of tightness at one stop.

A positive current law on a fixed finite physical stopping graph has
compact configuration space. A convergent subsequence there does not
construct fields at arbitrarily small physical separations. One must
relate observables at different stops and/or take a shrinking final mesh,
with controlled joint normalizations and appropriate regularity. Exact
deterministic projective consistency under one particular M is not
mandatory: a proved asymptotically compatible system could suffice.
But the stochastic history joint alone does not supply that assertion;
freshly inserted reference randomness need not describe the same local
holonomies at the different resolutions.

Current RT stability also retains the weak-trajectory hypothesis. It
does not by itself choose an original beta(a), identify a nonzero physical
renormalized coupling, restore O(4), or provide an infinite-volume local
theory. None of these obligations disappears because the current field
is declared fundamental. Partition stability and positive coarse variance
are strictly smaller conclusions.

5. WHAT IS OPTIONAL, WHAT HAS BEEN EXCLUDED, AND WHAT IS STILL OPEN

Optional methods, not prerequisites for every existence proof:
 all-future-test total variation; a fixed complex disc for every old
 observable; every independent tensor source at once; arbitrary-state
 slab operator bounds; a global gauge chart; literal equality to Wilson;
 uniqueness of every continuum subsequence; and a uniform mass gap as
 a prerequisite for the current nonstatic-existence witness.

Optional does not mean already avoidable within a specified proof. For
example Cauchy extraction from one analytic source theorem needs its
actual complex domain, while a direct finite-jet or real-source proof
may use another input. The chosen estimate must suffice for the stated
consumer, not just for a weaker unmarked norm.

Actual exclusions are also route-specific. TURN90 excludes using an
all-bare-boundary weak event of the stated radius on a growing physical
block. TURN92 excludes a particular all-microscopic-cap positive sector
under the specified scaling. TURN93 excludes the raw covariant spatial
heat trace and bounded-entropy competitors for that collapsing witness.
None excludes all local observables, renormalized composites, alternative
regulators or nontrivial YM4. Conversely a new name for the same raw
normalization does not evade any of these estimates.

The copy-datum calculation from TURN93 could complete a useful finite
CURRENT reference box/minorization gate after the exact B119 datum/test
identification. It is not an original posterior theorem, an RP theorem,
or a continuum compatibility theorem. It should be completed if cheap,
but not used to declare the strategic problem solved.

6. RECOMMENDED NEXT MATHEMATICAL WORK

Primary track: target a source-preserving LOCAL finite-volume theorem
for a specified finite collection of original gauge-invariant reads,
with one physical calibration and the full original normalizer. At the
first genuine R, write the actual marked old integral against the full
remaining density; prove a quantitative estimate of that integral, not
of q_e alone. Complete source footprints, original boundary data and
opposite-half weights remain explicit. The useful next theorem must
control the mark and its required mixed/contact term on the same law.
It need not first control every bounded future test or all source orders.

The exact first-R row is already identified in TURN87--93, and the
regular selected/native modules are available at their audited domains.
The new work is the marked original comparison across that row, with
an error small at a fixed weak physical stopping coupling and uniform
in prior UV depth. If a Gaussian comparison is used, it must use the
actual blocked precision q_M and matching boundary problem. TURN15's
explicit momentum calculation rules out silently substituting the bare
nearest-neighbor Gaussian for a fixed coarse-cell observable.

For the local-observable track, start with an explicitly stated finite
family and a limited finite-order result, not a claim that one scalar
generates all curvature/jet fields. The earlier local quadratic/oriented
cubic seeds are available, but their growing normalizations, mixing and
contacts still require estimates. A finite result is valuable precisely
when its role in the later compatible hierarchy is specified. If the
bounded flowed witness is pursued instead, label the result a witness
theorem and keep the local construction track independently active.

Secondary, time-bounded fork: perform the literal one-R RP audit in4.1
before investing in RT-current variance as an alternative existence
proof. A positive result would justify further work on a direct local
state construction. A finite negative result would require either a
quantified continuum-defect argument or a regulator change with renewed
stability estimates. Neither outcome is presently known. Do not replace
this actual finite calculation by another statement that half-local
kernels would preserve RP.

Finally, report progress along TWO separate ledgers: construction of the
common local theory and identification of a nonstatic physical vector.
A new entropy reformulation, finite strict positivity, or reference
variance is not progress on the former and may be only a consumer for
the latter. A theorem that fixes an actual marked source, full denominator,
uniformity regime and local algebra closes a named piece of the program.
This recommendation changes prioritization; it proves no new lower bound
and does not certify the public or private Yang--Mills construction.
