DIRECT CLASSICAL TRANSPORT OF INDIVIDUALLY INVARIANT CARRIERS
TURN70. history47. 20 October 2026.

STATUS

This proves a fixed-domain direct-endpoint bound for a specified invariant
carrier subclass, using the complete classical prefix theorem already
recorded in the project. It does not identify that subclass with the
near-minimal representatives of the quantum quotient. The output is an
ordinary analytic carrier family with invariant TOTAL; individual output
coefficients are not asserted invariant. Consequently this theorem alone
does not close the quantum recursion.

The separate scalar-source augmented Ward route being developed this turn
may avoid this representative issue. Section 8 isolates its quadratic
transport requirement; it is not silently imported into the theorem below.

1. INPUTS AND PRECISE GAUGE SCOPE

Use dimension four, a compact semisimple group, and compatible periodic
lattices. The primitive ratio is fixed. The endpoint ratio L is an arbitrary
admissible power, not a newly chosen primitive ratio. M denotes preceding
depth. Old carriers H are connected sets of J-current cells, k=|H|.
The final cells have side JL in old current units. Their projection has
fiber L^4. Scalar link-angle domains have radius b.

Each input E_H is holomorphic on its complete original link/read domain and
bounded there by e_H. It is invariant under local gauge transformations
along any path for which all those read links remain in that domain.
The reads include every link needed for this invariance. Global color
conjugations are included. This is the ordinary individually invariant
carrier hypothesis, not invariance of the sum alone, and not invariance
only under a smaller boundary-fixed subgroup that excludes the path used
below. If a native typed carrier has only that smaller invariance, the
theorem requires a separate identification of its allowed gauge paths.

Normalize E_H by subtracting E_H(0). This costs at most two in norm; retain
the vacuum sum separately. Write the normalized function again as E_H.
The input bound is

  sup_i sum_(H contains i) exp(a |H|) e_H <= epsilon.       (1.1)

Global color invariance and semisimplicity imply DE_H(0)=0: the derivative
at each link is an invariant linear functional on the Lie algebra.
Thus every normalized E_H has a full first-order zero.

The actual direct pre-gauge ordinary prefix a_(M,L)(w) supplies:

 (P1) A(w)=L a_(M,L)(w) is bounded in complete physical C^(1,alpha)
      norm by C_A b on each fixed final-unit collar.
 (P2) It has a coherent strict support expansion in that complete norm,
      with arbitrary prescribed finite cardinal reserve and a positive
      spatial exponent, bounded by C_A b. The bound is taken before
      counting output coordinates; C_A and its radius are independent
      of M,L and compatible volume.
 (P3) The complete total is the actual ordinary classical map. Equality
      of its successive endpoint substitutions follows from the
      classical semigroup theorem for gauge-invariant totals.

These are the results of pregauge_holder_prefix_research §§3–4 and
classical_background_semigroup_research. The construction below is an
explicit new consumer of P1–P2. It does not replace their admitted
ordinary-minimum/coherent-domain hypotheses by mere power counting.

2. STATEMENT AND ONE-TIME PARAMETER CHOICES

Fix a>0. Choose the classical cardinal reserve to pay the output exponent
and one fixed rerooting exponent. Choose a sufficiently large fixed J and
then b>0 below the complete-prefix radius and a fixed group chart radius.
There are L0,C<infinity, independent of M,L and volume, such that for every
admissible L>=L0 one can construct connected analytic output carriers with

  sum_K (T_(M,L)E)_K(w_K) = sum_H E_H(a_(M,L)(w)|H),
  ||T_(M,L)E||_(a,b) <= C epsilon.                         (2.1)

The SAME a,b work for all endpoint powers L>=L0. In particular the radius
is not reduced by a factor L at each successive endpoint.

Here is a permissible order. First fix the target support/reroot reserves;
obtain the constants in P2. Then choose J to convert the final-unit
spatial exponent to the desired J-cell support weight. Choose a finite
C0>4e C_A and a small c>0 with C C0 c<1/8 and C C0 c^2<1/8.
Take b small enough that rho=C0 b stays inside a fixed complete-field
holonomy chart and below the auxiliary bounds used in Section 3.
Finally enlarge L0 for the compression and ordinary composition bounds
in Section 5. All choices are independent of the eventual L.

3. SMALL CARRIERS: ACTUAL PATHS AND THE L^-4 GAIN

Call H small if J k<=c L. Lift it locally to the ordinary integer lattice.
A connected J-cell set has diameter at most C J k. Choose a root and, by a
spanning tree of its CELLS followed by fixed within-cell paths, paths P_v
from the root to each read vertex. Their lengths are at most C J k.
This does not use a fine-vertex spanning tree of length O(J^4 k).

For any physical complete field A with ||A||_(C1)<rho, put U_e=exp(i A_e/L).
The current plaquette holonomies obey

  ||U_p-I|| <= C(rho+rho^2)/L^2.                          (3.1)

This follows directly by multiplying four exponentials: first differences
of A over current distance 1 are O(rho/L), while commutators contribute
O(rho^2/L^2). All constants hold for complex fields on the stated ball.

Let g_v be the ordered holonomy of P_v, in the convention that the
transformed edge is g_u U_e g_v^-1. Each transformed read link is the
holonomy of a based loop of length at most C J k. The loop is contractible
in the chosen local lift. Commuting coordinate steps and cancelling inverse
pairs expresses it as a product of at most C(Jk)^2 conjugated plaquettes.
The conjugator lengths are O(Jk); all paths and plaquettes lie in a box of
diameter O(Jk), hence in one fixed final-unit collar.

For completeness, the product bound pays the norms of those conjugators
by exp[C rho Jk/L] and the accumulated plaquette errors by
exp[C rho (Jk/L)^2]. Both are uniformly bounded in this split. Therefore
the logarithms z_e of all transformed read links satisfy

  ||z||_infinity <= C rho (Jk)^2/L^2.                    (3.2)

This is a retained-path estimate: the actual path and filling data have
not been discarded just because the final endpoints are close.

The holonomies g_v have logarithms B_v with
||B_v||<=C rho Jk/L. Along g_v(t)=exp(t B_v), 0<=t<=1,
the transformed read-link logarithms are at most C rho Jk/L.
The choices in Section 2 keep the entire path inside the ORIGINAL b-ball.
Thus the individual invariance of E_H licenses this gauge transformation.
The same statement for complex A follows from local holomorphic gauge
invariance, or by continuation from the open real field chart.

Since E_H(0)=DE_H(0)=0, radial Schwarz on its original product ball gives
|E_H(z)|<=e_H (||z||_infinity/b)^2. Hence the scalar Banach functional

  F_H(A)=E_H(exp(i A/L)|H)

is holomorphic for ||A||_(C1)<rho and has the stronger bound

  ||F_H||_rho <= C e_H J^4 k^4/L^4.                     (3.3)

The use of gauge invariance in proving (3.3) does not change F_H itself.
In particular it is not a substitution of different gauges into an
arbitrary null family. The estimate is for an individually invariant
input coefficient.

There is no factor D(J)k from expanding individual fine field coordinates:
F_H is one scalar analytic functional of one COMPLETE local field.
The loops fill O((Jk)^2) fine plaquettes, but their final physical collar
has bounded diameter. Its read support is that collar, not a sum over
independent fine output variables.

4. SMALL-CARRIER SUPPORTED COMPOSITION AND REROOTING

Expand F_H in complete-field homogeneous multilinear coefficients.
Cauchy and polarization give the n-th coefficient bound

  C e_H J^4 k^4/L^4 * (e/rho)^n.

Substitute the strict complete-field family of A(w) from P2. Its weighted
sum is at most C_A b. As e C_A b/rho=e C_A/C0<1/4, the supported series
converges normally. Each union of supports is connected by the declared
rooted trees and retains the original collar and every field read.

Here are the fixed geometric weight costs. For a fixed collection of
final-unit collars, the joined J-cell tree obeys the projection estimate

  T_macro <= (2/J)[tau(collar roots)+sum tau(root union X)]
               +4[n0+sum |X|].                         (4.1)

The number n0 of collar roots needed here is fixed. Choose the unit
spatial exponent so that 2(a+nu)/J is below it and the classical cardinal
reserve at least 4(a+nu). Then exp(a|K|), as well as the rerooting
factor exp[-nu dist(z,pi_L root(H))], is paid in the same series.
Consequently the contribution of one small H at output root z is bounded
by

  C e_H J^4 k^4 L^-4
                exp[-nu dist(z,pi_L root(H))].          (4.2)

Choose one input-cell root for H. Sum (4.2), using (1.1) and
sup_k k^4 exp(-a k)<infinity. There are L^4 old J-cell roots over one
output J-cell, up to a harmless fixed boundary convention. The output
root convolution is bounded by its infinite-lattice exponential sum.
The L^4 root count cancels L^-4 exactly:

  ||T E_small||_(a,b) <= C epsilon.                     (4.3)

All constants may depend on the fixed J,a,nu and chart parameters.
They do not depend on L,M or periodic volume.

For k>cL/J, the input tail has the weaker-exponent bound

  ||E_large||_(a/2,b) <= exp[-a cL/(2J)] epsilon.        (5.1)

Use the ordinary core/tail composition of
high_jet_background_transport_research §§2–3 at lambda=1.

More explicitly, the core halo K0(H) has

The supported tail divided by the fixed Cauchy margin b/2 has norm

  t <= C J^4 L^-1 exp[-kappa0 ell_tail/2].

The coordinate multiindex sum is exactly (1-t)^(-D(J) k).

  (a+nu)c1(L)<=a/4,
  D(J) log(1/(1-t))<=a/4

  ||T E_large||_(a,b)

The right side is uniformly bounded in L>=L0. This proves (2.1) together

6. PERIODICITY, VACUUM, AND REPEATED USE

Small-carrier loops are constructed in a local lift of diameter O(cL).
Take the minimum final period larger than the fixed collar diameter.
Then all these paths and fillings admit that lift. A coordinate seam
crossing alone is harmless. A genuinely winding input carrier has size
at least c' L/J and belongs to the large-carrier estimate, after reducing
c if necessary. P2 uses periodic coherent supports; its root sums are
bounded by the same infinite-lattice exponential convolution.

The omitted sum_H E_H(0) is an exact vacuum, not zero or an error.
Equation (2.1) concerns normalized germs; restore this scalar for raw
partition/source identities.

The same a,b admit all direct endpoints L, including L=R^q for arbitrary
q. The actual semigroup identifies the TOTAL endpoint functional for
successive classical steps. However the construction of Sections 4–5
does not make every output carrier invariant. Therefore an iterated
argument must either retain the original individually invariant family
and evaluate the direct endpoint, or prove an invariant representative
theorem for the intervening quantum corrections.

7. THE REPRESENTATIVE OBSTRUCTION IS NOT REMOVED

The ordinary activity quotient minimizes over families with the same
invariant total. It does not require every coefficient to be invariant.
The proof in Section 3 cannot be applied to such a minimizing family.
A different gauge path for each H does not preserve a null TOTAL.

Likewise the TURN69 local source cancellation supplies a small ordinary
family, but individual coordinate derivative terms need not be invariant.
The companion invariant_source_relocalization_turn70 gives a legitimate
two-background invariant identity; removing its second background is
another operation and is not accomplished by setting it to I.
Its Section 9 additionally gives a direct covariant-projector repair
for the actual leading newborn source, at its explicit Q/Hessian inputs.
That special repair does not by itself make this theorem's supported
output coefficients invariant or supply arbitrary quotient representatives.

Thus the new theorem is a bounded endpoint consumer on a real, useful
subclass. It is not a proof that the all-scale physical-source recursion
has a bounded propagator in its current quotient.

8. AUGMENTED SCALAR SOURCE: THE PRECISE NEXT ADAPTATION

A neutral scalar leg f can be retained as part of a jointly covariant
source-field module. Nested tensor piecewise-linear interpolation I_L
has exact composition and sup contraction. Retaining that leg avoids
choosing a noncovariant modulation of arbitrary null representatives.

The homogeneous quadratic spatial-transport proof must nevertheless be
adapted. Its integration by parts transfers a difference onto the first
field. With a source factor f(x), that operation also differentiates f.
One cannot quote the old final formula unchanged.

A sufficient direct replacement is a source-rooted factorization

  K2(p,q)=sum_(rho,tau) d_rho(p) B_(rho,tau)(p,q) d_tau(q),

with independent field momenta, exponential coefficient norm, and
B(0,0)=0 (plus the relevant higher moment vanishing). Each difference
then acts on a field before source summation; no integration by parts
through f is needed. On the actual prefix, both field differences have
size O(b L^-2), and cancellation of the constant B moment leaves one
Hölder difference at gain L^-alpha. The source-root sum contributes
L^4, so this yields the desired L^-alpha remainder bound.

This paragraph identifies the required factorization and scaling; it
does not prove that the augmented Ward identities produce it with the
correct lattice phases and fixed exponential norm. Root is treating
that algebra separately. Cubic and quartic anchored Taylor expansions
have no analogous integration-by-parts issue: the scalar source can
remain attached to their common root while the field moment cancellations
are applied, provided the augmented kernel's moments are actually known.

Neither this direct endpoint theorem nor a future augmented contraction
would by itself prove the full native mixed-history source attachment,
the original-law separated limit, or a nonzero physical covariance.
