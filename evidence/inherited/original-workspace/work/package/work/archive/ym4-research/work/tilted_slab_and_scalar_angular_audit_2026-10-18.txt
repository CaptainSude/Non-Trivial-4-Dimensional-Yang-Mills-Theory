3. JOINED CHARTS, THE SECOND FRAME, AND DETECTION

The local rotation transport of coefficients follows by uniqueness
where a real small rotation remains in an ordered chart. Generic
configurations with no pair of perpendicular chords have their whole

failure of both orderings would require a horizontal and vertical
chord. On overlaps the kernels agree with the SAME original ordered
Schwinger sector, so coefficient transport continues around the orbit.

For an arbitrary distinct planar configuration choose an angle giving
distinct times, and transport its coefficients back. Two choices are
consistent by approximation with generic configurations while retaining

so the extension is real analytic. This defines a separated planar

been assumed.
