1. NORMALIZED HOMOGENEOUS SPACES AND TWO SCHUR BOUNDS

Work in one homogeneous current scale with coarse block side1 and
fine spacing xi=L^-j. Fine pairings include xi^d; coarse pairings
include coarse cell volume1. Let row and column Schur bounds always
use these measures. No count of fine sites may replace their weighted
volume. All complete native reads and collars are retained.

For a fine-to-fine kernel T, the two norms are

  sup_x sum_y xi^d ||T(x,y)||,
  sup_y sum_x xi^d ||T(x,y)||.

Use the corresponding mixed coarse/fine measures for H0,Hhat,L_Q,Q.
The same definitions with exponential block-distance weights give
decay norms at any fixed rate below the native rate. Their convolution
inequalities have fixed constants, independent of volume and depth.
These are the homogeneous versions of the native weighted Schur sums.

Both ends are needed. A bound of T from sup norm to sup norm alone
would not imply the bound for T^T used in the fluctuation gradient.
