1. EXACT REAL KERNEL

Let T_j F(z)=Q_(j,z)[F(Phi_j(z,x))], where Q has density
exp[-H_j(z,x)]/Z_j(z) on the ORIGINAL fixed compact noise product.
The state z includes its actual frame. The denominator is this same
positive integral. All images lie strictly in the source domain.
Retain canceled parameter-only factors in the outer physical law.
Assume C1 regularity and domination for differentiation of this finite
integral. In the common-frame implementation H includes the matching
noise transport, not just the field/current in another frame.

Index output state packets by u, input packets by q, and noise sites
by i. Packet derivatives use one unit vector in their declared norm;
they are not sums over fine coordinates inside a packet. Assume the
nonnegative derivative envelopes, uniform on the whole product:

 ||D_q Phi_j|_u||<=M_j(u,q),
 ||partial_(x_i) Phi_j|_u||<=B_j(u,i),
 ||partial_(x_k) D_q H_j||<=A_j(k,q).                    (1)

Color norms change only fixed constants. Assume the genuine gradient
covariance estimate for this same positive measure:

 |Cov_Q(f,h)|<=sum_(i,k) C_j(i,k)
                    sup|partial_i f| sup|partial_k h|. (2)

Positivity alone does not imply (2); hard-mask mixtures are not covered.
For sup||D_u F||<=a_u, exact normalized differentiation gives

 D_q T_jF=Q[D F(Phi_j)D_q Phi_j]-Cov(F(Phi_j),D_q H_j).

The chain rule and (1)--(2) prove

 sup||D_q T_jF||<=sum_u a_u P_j(u,q),
                  P_j=M_j+B_j C_j A_j.                (3)

These are ordinary products of positive envelope matrices. When
B_j=g_j Btilde_j, the noise derivative of the SOURCE contributes g_j;
a score bound of order R_j is paid as g_j R_j. No extensive score
supremum is used. Positivity also gives ||T_jF||<=||F||.

2. COMMON METRIC AND FIXED EXPONENT

Assign every packet/noise index at every grade a point in one common
metric, e.g. physical position in FINAL-grade lattice units. Keep all
actual collars in the derivative envelopes. Define

 ||K||_(mu,row)=sup_v sum_w exp(mu d(v,w))K(v,w).

Do not use distances between arbitrary footprints as a metric. With

where all four norms are the same weighted row norm. Iterating at ONE

                      <=A_birth product_(j<t)p_j,      (5)

There is no repeated exponent loss or polynomial of the growing read

3. REAL NEIGHBORHOOD TELESCOPE

Suppose the final real domain permits a truncation z^[n] keeping all
packets within distance n of P and replacing the rest by a baseline.
Require the line segments used in truncation to stay in the domain and
each changed packet to move by at most B. An admitted Cartesian box
suffices; a gauge orbit or constrained fiber does not automatically.
The fundamental theorem of calculus and (5) give

 |F_t(z)-F_t(z^[n])|
                  <=B A_birth product_j p_j exp(-mu n). (6)

Thus f_0=F_t(z^[0]), f_n=F_t(z^[n])-F_t(z^[n-1]) have complete

For canonical gauge charts, an admissible compatible tree/truncation

These are REAL bounds. A complex coefficient bound additionally needs
whole-prefix holomorphy on a proved tube and a quantitative continuation
