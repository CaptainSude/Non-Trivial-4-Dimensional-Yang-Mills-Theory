CALIBRATED FOUR-ENTRY PACKING: AN EXACT FULL-LAW CONSUMER
25 October 2026. Root lemma; its source hypotheses remain open.

PURPOSE

This proves a finite-dimensional consumer for the ENTIRE normalized
history remainder of the current calibrated original energy. It adapts
the archived Rademacher argument to the four entries and the actual
c_a=x_n/e_n^C. It does not import the archived one-run source theorem as
a proved statement for these marks.

1. EXACT COMMON SOURCE DATA

Let mu_(a,P) be the original positive finite Wilson law. For a dimensionless
bare weight v on its plaquette centers define

 J_a(v)=c_a sum_p v(x_p) h_p,
 Z_a(v)=E_mu exp[J_a(v)].                                (1)

All geometry, coupling/stopping labels and c_a are frozen. Let E_a(v) be
ONE holomorphic scalar function, E_a(0)=0, defined from v itself, and put

 h_a(v)=exp[-E_a(v)] Z_a(v),       h_a(0)=1.              (2)

No profile-by-profile choice of E_a is allowed. In the proposed RG use,
E_a is the sum of genuinely universal completed scalar births, with all
missing-region compensations and history-dependent scalar terms retained
inside h_a. That identification must separately be proved.

Define the bilinear second logarithmic GERM

 B_h(u,v)=D^2h_a(0)[u,v]-Dh_a(0)[u]Dh_a(0)[v].           (3)

Differentiating (2) at zero gives the exact identity

 Cov_mu(J_a(u),J_a(v))=D^2E_a(0)[u,v]+B_h(u,v).           (4)

There is no need for h_a to be zero-free away from zero. In particular,
the product of first derivatives in (3) is not dropped. It contains the
normalization and outer conditional-covariance terms that would be lost
by multiplying separate posterior first marks.

2. THE UNIFORM LOCAL HYPOTHESIS NEEDED

Fix a terminal physical length ell_T and an endpoint-unit cell D, with
an interior D0 separated by a fixed positive buffer from its faces.
Suppose there exist r_*>0 and Q_*<infinity, independent of a and the
ambient compatible period, such that

 |h_a(v)|<=Q_* whenever supp v is in D0 and
 ||v||_infty+Lip_(endpoint)(v) <= r_*.                    (5)

The weight is allowed to be complex. This is ONE bound for the union of
all its pieces, independent of their number or signs. The cell and the
constants may depend on a fixed terminal weak floor and fixed shapes.
They may not depend on the UV cutoff through c_a. It is enough to assume
joint holomorphy and the bound on the particular bidiscs used below.

A bare-source bound for sum v h_p does not automatically imply (5) for
J_a(v). Under (1) its admitted radius would be divided by |c_a|, and its
Cauchy bound would acquire c_a^2. The subsequently checked sharpened
constant-energy calibration gives e_n=1+O(1/X) and c_a=O(X), uniformly
in UV depth, but does not prove (5). An absolute bare radius r0(Y) at a
later fixed terminal weak floor Y therefore gives r_*=r0(Y)/(C X).

3. PACKING ALL FOUR PROFILES

Use the physical unit-mass f_0,f_1 from the checkpoint contract and set
u_i=tau^4 f_i, v_i=tau^4 theta f_i. These are amplitude-one fixed-shape
patterns up to constants depending on kappa. Their diameter, separation
and distance from their reflected partners are O(tau). In endpoint units
put R=tau/ell_T. For R sufficiently small and a/tau sufficiently small,
one can place m>=c_pack R^-4 translated FOUR-profile patterns in D0,
with all 4m supports disjoint. Use boxes of side C_shape R and round their
centers to exact bare-lattice translations; the buffer and packing density
remain fixed when a/tau is small. No scaffold-preserving translations are
required here.

Let t_l be those bare translations and epsilon_l independent signs. For
each i,j define

 U_i,epsilon=sum_l epsilon_l u_i(. - t_l),
 V_j,epsilon=sum_l epsilon_l v_j(. - t_l).

Disjoint support and the fixed shape margins imply

 ||U_i,epsilon||_infty+||V_j,epsilon||_infty <= C_shape,
 Lip_T(U_i,epsilon)+Lip_T(V_j,epsilon) <= C_shape/R.       (6)

These constants do not grow with m. Therefore
z U_i,epsilon+w V_j,epsilon obeys (5) for

 |z|,|w|<d_R=c_shape r_* R,

with c_shape small enough and R<=1. The two-variable Cauchy bound gives

 |B_h(U_i,epsilon,V_j,epsilon)|
                          <=(Q_*+Q_*^2)/d_R^2.          (7)

Because (3) is bilinear, averaging the SAME signs in the two sums cancels
every cross-pattern entry and gives exactly

 E_epsilon B_h(U_i,epsilon,V_j,epsilon)
                =sum_l B_h(u_i(. -t_l),v_j(. -t_l)).      (8)

The exact original Wilson measure is invariant under these bare lattice
translations and c_a is common. Thus the bare covariance in (4) is the
same for every l. No translation invariance of E_a or the RG scaffold is
used. Combining (4), (7), (8), and m>=c_pack R^-4 yields

 |Cov_mu(O_a(f_i),O_a(theta f_j))
   -tau^-8 m^-1 sum_l D^2E_a(0)
           [u_i(. -t_l),v_j(. -t_l)]|
             <= C_shape (Q_*+Q_*^2) r_*^-2 tau^-8 R^2.   (9)

The estimate holds for ALL FOUR entries with common constants. All
finite-history/private/exterior mixed terms are inside the exact B_h;
no rare-event probability or separate conditional mean is substituted.
The proof is purely finite-dimensional once (5) and the coherent E_a
have been supplied. It also works for each fixed periodic regulator
large enough to contain D, with the same bound.

4. THE OTHER NECESSARY SUPPLIER

To compare (9) with the saved homogeneous covariance one must prove

 |tau^-8 m^-1 sum_l D^2E_a(0)[u_i(. -t_l),v_j(. -t_l)]
                           - C_hom,ij| <= epsilon_vac.   (10)

The universal E_a may include healed large-field scalar births. A
homogeneous ordinary contact calculation alone does not identify or
bound those mixed derivatives. The true completed birth dates and both
outer-R source operations must be retained. An unmarked R' bound is
not a marked second-derivative bound without an admitted source ball
or an explicit two-mark producer with its prices.

If (5) and (10) are established, choose a sufficiently long but finite
weak continuation so R is small enough, while keeping r_*,Q_* fixed
at its terminal band. Then (9)+(10) gives the checkpoint estimate when
their sum is below its allocated allowance. This is a quantified route,
not a proof that those two suppliers hold for the present construction.

5. WHY THE CURRENT HOMOGENEOUS ESTIMATE DOES NOT SUPPLY (5)

It controls physical first-source functions and selected second jets on
the homogeneous core, and a separate actual/core error on running
domains. It does not bound a common nonlinear source functional on all
retained histories. Finite-jet control cannot be promoted to such a bound:
the elementary entire functions exp(N z^3) have the same value and first
two derivatives at zero for every N, yet have no uniform bound on any
fixed complex disc. This example tests the implication, not Yang--Mills.

Likewise, a finite-volume upper/lower stability quotient exp(C V_stop)
does not itself give the fixed-cell bound (5) as V_stop grows. The
archived proof uses a separately justified reflection/chessboard passage
and coherent vacuum cancellation. Those steps and the calibrated source
ball must be verified before invoking this consumer.
