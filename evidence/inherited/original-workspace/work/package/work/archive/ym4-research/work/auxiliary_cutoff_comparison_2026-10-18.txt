EXACT AUXILIARY CUTOFF COMPARISON IN THE PREPARED SOURCE INTEGRAL
18 October 2026. Working lemma and source audit, not a published artifact.

1. STATEMENT

Use the actual independent retained fluctuation coordinates x_e, the actual
covariance and normalization, and the actual prepared localized potential V.
In particular V includes the inherited regular action and the mature R
fluctuation differences absorbed into it by CMP119, pp270--271. Let

  Z_T(u)=int dmu_{C(u)}(x) prod_e chi_T(x_e) exp(sum_Y V_Y(u,x)),
  chi_T(v)=1_{|v|<T}.

All factors independent of the cutoff can be retained outside this formula;
they cancel in the log difference. Here u denotes exactly the source complex
background/current and decoupling variables. Assume the prepared hypotheses
of the source cluster proof hold on the ENTIRE original retained window
|x_e|<T, with its fixed strict Gaussian and geometric summability margins.
These are the hypotheses on actual source states, not the stronger auxiliary
small-b norm which is available only on a smaller window.

For T>=t>=t_min there are constants a,C>0, independent of T,t, volume and
scale, such that the connected logarithms have compatible local analytic
representations with

  ||log Z_T-log Z_t||_source <= C exp(-a t^2).                 (1)

The source norm can be the rooted sum of local analytic sup norms with any
fixed reserved fraction of the published tree-decay exponent. Constants and
t_min can depend on the fixed source geometric parameters. This is an exact
comparison of the two finite-dimensional integrals; it is not an estimate
of a global event probability and has no volume prefactor in the local norm.

2. PROOF BY A SINGLE CUTOFF MARKER

Define for each retained bond

  chi_z(v)=chi_t(v)+z[chi_T(v)-chi_t(v)],
  Z(z)=int dmu_C prod_e chi_z(x_e) exp(sum_Y V_Y).

For finite volume Z(z) is polynomial in z. Its integrals are defined since
every retained coordinate is restricted to the original window T. For any
a>0, choose the marker disk |z|<=R=exp(a t^2). Pointwise on real v,

  |chi_z(v)| <= exp(a|v|^2) chi_T(v),
  |1-chi_z(v)| <= 2 exp(-a t^2) exp(2a|v|^2).                (2)

The second inequality follows because 1-chi_z vanishes on |v|<t, is 1-z
on t<=|v|<T, and is 1 outside T. Thus its modulus is at most 2exp(a t^2)
on |v|>=t. No regularity of the sharp cutoff in v or in t is used.

Repeat the source Mayer expansion of exp(sum V_Y). If D is the selected
interaction family and Y0 its support union, retain prod_{e in Y0} chi_z
and expand the complementary cutoff as prod_{e outside Y0}[1-(1-chi_z)].
This is the exact source identity CMP116 (2.1)--(2.3), now with chi_z.
The potential is evaluated only where |x_e|<T on its own strict support;
therefore its actual original prepared bound, including all inherited terms,
remains valid. In particular no extension of that potential beyond T occurs.

For a complementary bad set P, (2) gives a factor

  2^{|P|} exp(-a t^2 |P|)

and extra positive quadratic forms with coefficients at most 2a on Y0 union
P. In the conditioned Gaussian proof these are absorbed in exactly the
same quadratic stability margin as the original bad-bond estimate. Choose
a first, small enough that adding O(a) to alpha5 preserves both the Gaussian
positivity and the source small-volume-cost inequalities. The determinant
cost is exp[O(a)|Z|], of precisely the same kind as the original alpha5
cost. The source coefficients/decoupling analyticity are otherwise unchanged.
Choose t_min last so that the remaining bad-bond gain pays the finite source
P-set entropy conditions, including the case of empty D. Constants such as
2^{|P|} are absorbed by increasing t_min. Thus the original connected cluster
series converges normally, uniformly for |z|<=R, and defines

  Z(z)=exp(sum_X H_X(z)),       ||H(z)||_source<=C.            (3)

As usual, a field-independent reference normalization may be factored out
before (3). It is z-independent. This proves zero-freeness on the marker
disk and fixes the logarithm consistently; no independently estimated
denominator or branch choice is being suppressed.

The same coefficientwise source construction makes H a Banach-valued
holomorphic family. On a slightly smaller marker disk if necessary, Cauchy
gives ||[z^n]H||<=C R^{-n}. Since Z(0)=Z_t and Z(1)=Z_T,

  ||H(1)-H(0)|| <= C sum_{n>=1}R^{-n}
                  =C/(R-1) <=2C exp(-a t^2).

This proves (1), after a harmless decrease of a if the proof uses an open
marker disk. Normal convergence justifies all coefficientwise operations.

4. USE WITH MOVING RADII; WHAT IS AND IS NOT CLOSED

Let q_min=min(q0,q1)>1 be the printed source exponents. Select

  1/2 < t_exp < s < q_min,
  t=(log gamma)^{t_exp},  b=c gamma^{-1/2}(log gamma)^s.

Then g t/b=c^{-1}(log gamma)^{t_exp-s}->0. The independent physical noise
window g t fits the moving field radius b, and the latter fits the original
source background/current domain after fixed chart constants are allowed.
The cutoff replacement error satisfies for every fixed A,K,

  gamma^A (log gamma)^K exp[-a(log gamma)^{2t_exp}] ->0.       (4)

In particular Maxwell extraction with cost O(b^-2) preserves an error
smaller than every fixed inverse power of gamma. The original threshold
T=A1(log gamma)^{p0}, p0>=5r, stays unchanged in the source RG. Only the
analysis splits its integral as an inner integral plus the exact analytic
log ratio (1). Consequently p0<s is unnecessary for this one-step split.

A q-step LITERAL JOINT kernel can use the same proof on all retained
coordinate copies if its exact joint preparation has the same stable
Gaussian/interaction bounds on the original outer window. This must be
checked by the joint-kernel construction; it does not follow by calling the
one-step estimate q times. Replacing an early cutoff changes intermediate
normalized states. An alternative finite-q telescope must control those
insertions with the actual fixed source counterterm schedule. Any fixed
inverse-g loss in that finite comparison is harmless by (4), but the exact
identification remains necessary. This note does not claim that joint
preparation or the moving regular-action induction has thereby been proved.

5. EXACT ACTUAL-STATE OUTPUT INSERTION: NO OUTER q-KERNEL IS NEEDED

There is a simpler exact way to use (1) in a finite block. Fix an already
constructed actual source prefix. Write K_r^o and K_r^i for the SAME raw
one-step kernel, with its actual geometric/gauge/coupling parameters frozen,
using respectively the original outer cutoff and the auxiliary inner cutoff.
For the actual incoming homogeneous source density coordinate rho_r define

  h_r(w)=log[(K_r^o rho_r)(w)/(K_r^i rho_r)(w)].             (5)

The source's zero-noise static factor and all cutoff-independent raw
normalizers cancel in this ratio. Formula (1) controls it on the source
output background/current domain. If F_r denotes the actual subsequent
homogeneous bulk R-operation contribution, the source identity becomes

  rho_{r+1}=exp(F_r+h_r) K_r^i rho_r.                       (6)

Equality is exact; if densities are represented modulo vacuum constants,
the field-independent part of h_r is retained in the corresponding scalar.
The function h_r is computed at the ACTUAL incoming rho_r, then frozen as
an output insertion. It is not redefined on a hybrid density. Substitute
(6) recursively through a fixed block of q steps. The result contains
only inner kernels, and the fixed functions exp(F_r+h_r) inserted at their
actual intermediate output fields. Every equality in this substitution
uses an actual source state. No assertion that a cutoff-modified state
belongs to the original source inductive class is needed.

The annular estimate covers the full inherited action here. CMP119 p276,
following (3.43), discusses the action defining the measure (3.27), and
treats the R_k contribution by the same preparation as E_k except for the
second coupling renormalization. Together with the old-R absorption at
p271, this includes BOTH old and young R fluctuation expressions. Fixing
the source interpolation at s=1 suffices. No analytic continuation in s
or comparison with the zero-R trajectory is required. The Mayer cutoff
marker only uses the actual prepared potential estimates and their
Gaussian margins; Euclidean symmetry of each young term is unnecessary.

Its localized h_r coefficients have the independent source (U,J) analytic
extension and the source fixed-M tree decay. Therefore the already audited
block-Banach analytic seed conversion applies to h_r just as to a new
small local family. On a physical moving radius b below a fixed fraction
of the true source radius r_source it gives

  ||h_r||_{a,b} <= C exp(-a t_r^2),                         (7)

with the necessary fixed support/decay reserves chosen first. This is a
conversion of the source analytic extension, not a bare Hessian estimate.

For insertion at an intermediate field of the inner q-kernel, its supported
chart has size at most C_q[b_out+g(log gamma)^t_exp]. The true source radius
at each of these finitely many comparable scales is at least a fixed
multiple of g(log gamma)^q_min. Since t_exp<s<q_min, this chart fits with
a strict analytic margin for all sufficiently large gamma. The complete
support substitution has a fixed finite-q bound, using the same seed
conversion/prefix support reserves; any finite partial-prefix constant
is paid once. Thus the h_r insertions remain superpolynomially small,
including after b^-2 Maxwell extraction. This replaces the q-outer-window
extension discussed at the end of Section4. It does not prove the separate
inner q-species cluster estimate, but it removes the need for an outer
q-species prepared domain or for hybrid-state stability.
