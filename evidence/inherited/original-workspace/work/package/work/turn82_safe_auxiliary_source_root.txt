TURN82 -- FIXED AUXILIARY SOURCE GERMS AND UNSAFE-CLUSTER GEOMETRY
20 October 2026. Root derivation. Actual finite SU(2) Wilson lift.

RESULT

Combining the ACTUAL auxiliary-current tails with the weighted current
kernel theorem gives a concrete improvement over TURN81. At every real
safe auxiliary record, an arbitrary bounded original finite-read probe
has a common complex AUXILIARY Hilbert-ball source germ. Its radius,
norm and fixed-order Frechet derivative constants are independent of
beta>=1, volume and the number of read links. This is not just a source
parameter disk or a domain in unrelated independent currents.

The real complement has a prescribed-set exponential bound in its
connected components. This proves a microscopic-hole geometry statement
at refining cutoffs. It does not show that an arbitrary physical source
is insensitive to those holes, or supply a normalized source expansion
under the full interacting auxiliary law.

1. SETUP AND THE TWO VERIFIED INPUTS

Use the standard positive-path, three-first/three-second HS convention
of turn82_auxiliary_tails_tail. The zero-link marginal is the actual
nu_beta, not its Gaussian reference. Its original conditional links are
independent tilted Haar variables with currents

 J_e=sum_(first h at e) sqrt(beta) R_h
                +sum_(second h at e) 2 R_h^dagger G_(p(h)).

The real four-vector a_e is defined by ReTr J_e^dagger U_e=a_e dot u_e,
u_e in S3. The real-linear projection has operator norm sqrt2 from
Frobenius matrices to R4. A standard link has six incident half-path
records, one from each of its six incident plaquettes.

Let S be the complete ORIGINAL-link read set of bounded probes B_i,
0<=B_i<=1. Let T contain every G_p and R_h entering J_e for e in S,
with each auxiliary coordinate counted once. The complete read set,
not just a spatial center or a nominal source root, is used.

For each e the safe event requires

 |a_e|>=2 beta,
 ||G_p||_F<=2 sqrt(beta),
 ||R_h||_F<=(9/4)sqrt(beta)

for its actual six G and six R records. These real events obey

 nu_beta(all e in A are unsafe)<=p_beta^(|A|/19),
 p_beta=min(1,192 exp(-beta/3200)),                       (1.1)

for every finite set A. The bound is even uniform conditional on U.
It follows from independent conditional Gaussian noises and disjoint
plaquette-star extraction, not independence of nu_beta coordinates.

The independent-current theorem states that, at any real current a,
on the complex weighted Hilbert ball

 ||zeta||_a^2=sum_(e in S)|zeta_e|^2/(1+|a_e|)<rho^2,
 rho<=1/2,

the normalized product-link kernel has total variation at most
exp(4 rho^2/3). In particular for R=sum|z_i|,

 C(z;a+zeta)=E_(k_(a+zeta)) exp(sum z_i B_i)
 satisfies |C-1|<=exp(4 rho^2/3)(exp(R)-1).              (1.2)

The source integral is the full conditional source, retaining all mixed
covariances. The proof uses centered characteristic functions and the
exact S3 covariance, not any estimate on the interacting nu measure.

2. COMPLEXIFICATION AND AN EXPLICIT CURRENT-INCREMENT BOUND

Complexify the REAL eight-dimensional Hilbert space of each complex
2-by-2 matrix, with its real Frobenius norm. Thus a formal dagger is
the complex-linear continuation of the real adjoint operation. It is
not conjugation of new complex coordinates. Use the Hilbert product norm

 d^2=sum_(p in T)||delta G_p||^2+sum_(h in T)||delta R_h||^2.

The real bilinear matrix product has norm at most one. Its complex
bilinear extension has norm at most two: expand real and imaginary
parts and apply Cauchy--Schwarz. If one input remains real its complex
linear extension still has norm at most that real input's Frobenius
norm. Adjoints and the physical-current projection retain their real
operator norms under Hilbert complexification.

Fix a real center xi=(G,R) for which EVERY e in S is safe. Write
g_p=||delta G_p||, r_h=||delta R_h||. For a first incidence the current
increment costs sqrt(beta) r_h. For a second incidence it costs at most

 4 sqrt(beta) r_h+(9/2)sqrt(beta) g_p+4 r_h g_p.         (2.1)

Here the first two terms use the safe record bounds, and the last term
uses the factor two for complex bilinear multiplication. Consequently

 |delta a_e|<=sqrt2 [5 sqrt(beta) sum_(h incident to e)(r_h+g_(p(h)))
                         +4 sum_(second h at e) r_h g_(p(h))].

The incidence matrix in the linear sum has row sum twelve and column
sum at most four: an R belongs to two original link currents, and a G
belongs to four. Its l2 operator norm is at most sqrt48. Hence the
linear term has l2(S) norm less than 50 sqrt(beta) d.

For the quadratic term there are at most three terms per row, and each
half-path has one second link. Therefore

 sum_e (sum_(second h at e) r_h g_(p(h)))^2
 <=3 sum_h r_h^2 g_(p(h))^2
 <=3 (sum_h r_h^2)(sum_p g_p^2)<=3 d^4/4.

After multiplying by 4sqrt2 this is less than 5 d^2 in l2 norm.
We have proved the explicit, dimension-independent estimate

 ||delta a||_l2(S)<=50 sqrt(beta)d+5d^2.                 (2.2)

For beta>=1,d<=1, the safe lower current bounds yield

 ||delta a||_a<=55 sqrt(beta)d/sqrt(1+2beta)<40d.        (2.3)

These estimates include all cross terms of the actual polynomial
current map. No uniform bound on arbitrary unbounded auxiliary centers
has been substituted for the safe-center hypothesis.

3. A COMMON SOURCE GERM IN THE ACTUAL AUXILIARY COORDINATES

Take d<1/80. Equations (2.3) and (1.2) give a weighted current radius
less than1/2, and hence

 |C(z;J(xi+delta xi))|<=exp(R+1/3),
 |C(z;J(xi+delta xi))-1|<=exp(1/3)(exp(R)-1).           (3.1)

For example R<1/4 is a fixed common zero-free source region: the second
bound is less than1/2 there. The normalized source logarithm is
holomorphic jointly in these auxiliary increments and source variables.
At z=0 it is exactly zero. The conditional mean m_B is holomorphic
on the same auxiliary Hilbert ball and bounded by exp(1/3)||B||infinity.

At the real center, Cauchy on radius1/160 gives for a unit auxiliary
direction v and fixed integer k>=1

 |D^k m_B(xi)[v,...,v]|<=k!160^k exp(1/3)||B||infinity.

Complex polarization gives the corresponding multilinear operator bound
with k^k in place of k!. Mixed source jets follow by Cauchy in the fixed
source region. All constants are independent of beta, |S| and volume.

This is a Hilbert-ball and operator-jet statement. It is not an l1 sum
of separate coordinate derivatives, a native analytic polymer norm, or
a fixed per-coordinate polydisc when the number of coordinates grows.
The probe B is merely bounded measurable in its original read set; no
derivative of B or of an original nonlinear flow was needed to obtain
this conditional analytic germ.

The real safe indicators themselves have not been extended analytically.
The assertion is a germ centered at a given safe real record. It does
not claim that cutting the real integral by Safe_S is a holomorphic
background operation. Averaging these germs under the interacting nu,
or continuing their centers through arbitrary RG records, is a separate
problem with its own measure/domain and locality requirements.

4. CONNECTED UNSAFE SETS AND THE PHYSICAL SIZE OF HOLES

Join two original links when they share a plaquette. This graph has
maximum degree D=18. The number of connected sets of m links containing
a fixed root is at most D^(2(m-1)): choose a spanning tree and its
depth-first walk of length2(m-1); the walk's visited set determines
the set, and a canonical choice gives an injection into these walks.

If an unsafe component at e contains at least m links, it contains a
connected unsafe set of exactly m links containing e. By (1.1),

 nu_beta(|component(e)|>=m)
 <=324^(m-1) p_beta^(m/19)
 =p_beta^(1/19) q_beta^(m-1),
 q_beta=324 p_beta^(1/19).                              (4.1)

This bound is useful for q_beta<1 and does not discard any normalization.
In a box or torus with N links, the probability of ANY such component
is at most N times (4.1). It is an explicit connected-set census.

For a fixed physical volume with N_a<=C a^-4, assume for illustration
beta(a)>=b log(1/a), b>0, for sufficiently small dimensionless a.
Then, for every fixed integer m,

 P(any unsafe component has >=m links)
 <=C_m a^(-4+b m/60800).                               (4.2)

Choosing m>243200/b makes this tend to zero. The numerical constants
are conservative. The physical diameter of component link centers is
then at most2a(m-1), and the union of their geometric links has diameter
at most2am, both tending to zero. Use the periodic metric on a torus,
or a common nonwrapping embedding. This is conditional on the displayed
coupling trajectory; it does not prove its renormalization prescription.
More generally if beta(a)->infinity, (4.1) with m proportional to1/a
shows that an unsafe component of any fixed positive physical diameter
has probability tending to zero in a fixed physical volume.

The statement concerns artificial auxiliary small-current/large-record
holes, not large-field Wilson regions or a Yang--Mills mass scale. It
is compatible with many isolated unsafe links and with failure of the
global all-safe union bound N_a p_beta->0. It does not assert that an
arbitrary bounded observable changes little when those components are
removed. Such an observable can read all of them; a needed locality or
stability estimate cannot be replaced by their small physical diameter.

5. ROLE IN THE EXISTENCE ARGUMENT

This closes a specified finite source-domain problem for the exact HS
family: bounded original probes have uniform analytic auxiliary germs
on real safe records, and a full-law connected-set estimate describes
the complement. Both results are proved under the actual Wilson lift.
There is no new assumption of global convexity of the zero-link action.

What is missing is a normalized, spatially controlled integration of
these marked germs and the unsafe components under the full interacting
nu, or a valid comparison to an existing Bałaban source class. The old
inverse-record analytic problem is avoided in this representation but
has not been replaced by a proved all-scale auxiliary RG estimate.
No nonzero physical reflected correlation, continuum limit, Euclidean
invariance or Yang--Mills reconstruction follows from (3.1) or (4.2).
