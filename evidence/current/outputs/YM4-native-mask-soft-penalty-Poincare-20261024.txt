RESULT AND STATUS

The complete first guarded real law, restricted by its actual earlier
P0 and averaged-gauge masks, has a volume-independent unweighted
Poincare constant. This includes coincident corner masks. The proof
uses the already matched complete first action and native finite-read
mask geometry. It does not require pair transversality or a uniform
lower bound on the probability that all masks pass.

This is a finite first-step theorem on the specified near-flat,
guarded certificate, not an all-scale Yang--Mills result. It does NOT
assert arbitrary-principal-section bounds, exponential spatial
covariance, background differentiability, or fourth-power source age.
In particular the word 'uniform' refers to active volume and the weak
floor in this explicitly matched family.

1. FINITE HYPOTHESES AND NATIVE MATCH

Let I be a finite subset of the first noise lattice, with fixed real
block dimension m. Let Omega=product_(i in I) B_S. The actual active
support is

with no added outer cutoff: the unique original chart already puts
the original support strictly inside Omega, enlarging its fixed
containing radius if necessary. Each w_j is smooth on a neighborhood
of Omega and reads only T_j. Read size, diameter and incidence are
bounded by constants v,d,B independent of I. D has positive measure.

Fix r>=2. Assume S/r, s_j/r, r/s_j bounded above by fixed constants.
On the whole containing product,

 |D w_j|<=C,  |D^2 w_j|<=C g0,
 |w_j(0)|<=theta s_j, theta<1.                        (2)

Take one weak floor such that C g0 S^2 <=(1-theta)s_j/4
for every j. Complete reconstruction reads, including private-pivot
inputs, are meant in (2). A mask with no active read is already held
and can be omitted if it passes; no failed held mask is divided out.

The base probability has positive density exp(-H), where H is the

It satisfies H''>=lambda I. For every mask j choose

2. OUTWARD FLUX ON THE ENTIRE PENALIZED REGION

For q_k(x)>=s_k, its gradient is well defined. The exact identity

 D w_k(x)x = w_k(x)-w_k(0)
              +integral_0^1 [D w_k(x)-D w_k(t x)]x dt

and (2) show

 x dot grad q_k(x) >= q_k(x)-|w_k(0)|-C g0 |x_Tk|^2
                    >=c s_k.                         (4)

This is valid throughout the outside-cut part of Omega, not merely
on a boundary level. For i0 in T_k, the fixed read diameter implies
|chi_i^j/chi_i0^j-1|<=C eta on T_k. Hence

 X^j dot grad q_k
 >=chi_i0^j[c s_k-C eta S] >=c' chi_i0^j s_k>=0      (5)

by a fixed choice of eta, independent of volume and g0. For k=j the
weights on T_j equal one, so X^j dot grad q_j>=c s_j.

At every product boundary, X^j has nonnegative outward flux, since
each block component is a nonnegative scalar times that block's
radial vector. Furthermore

 |X^j|<=C S,  div X^j<=C,
 div X^j-X^j dot grad H <=C(1+r^2).                  (6)

For the last bound integrate H'' along t x. With W=diag(sqrt chi),
weighted accretivity gives
sum chi_i x_i dot[grad_i H(x)-grad_i H(0)]>=c sum chi_i |x_i|^2.
Young's inequality absorbs the linear force using |grad_i H(0)|<=Cr.
No estimate on the unknown masked-law mean is used.

3. SOFTENING AND A UNIFORM PENALTY-TRACE BOUND

For t>0 put psi_t(u)=t(u_+)^4 and

 H_t=H+sum_j psi_t(q_j-s_j),
 nu_t(dx)=Z_t^-1 1_Omega exp(-H_t) dx.                (7)

The composed penalty is C^2 (indeed C^3) on Omega: q_j is smooth
where it is used, while the penalty is identically zero near w_j=0.
The choice of the fourth power is just a convenient C^2 approximation.
All finite t laws are positive and normalized; no lower bound on Z_t
relative to the base partition function is assumed.

For smooth real f, integrate div(f^2 X^j exp(-H_t)). The boundary
flux is nonnegative. By (5), every penalty term is nonnegative, and
the j-th one is at least c s_j psi_t'(q_j-s_j) f^2. Therefore

 c s_j E_t[psi_t'(q_j-s_j) f^2]
 <= E_t[2 f X^j dot grad f+(div X^j-X^j dot grad H) f^2]. (8)

This sign is essential: the nonnegative boundary term is SUBTRACTED
when the penalty drift is moved to the other side. It can be dropped
in the upper bound. Equation (8) uses the base drift H, not H_t.

Using (6), S/s_j<=C, (1+r^2)/s_j<=Cr, and Young, for every a>0,

 E_t[psi_t'(q_j-s_j) f^2]
 <=C a E_t|grad f|^2+C(a^(-1)+r) E_t f^2.            (9)

The constants are independent of t,j,I and the total mask count.
This is an expectation bound for the soft penalty, not a rare-event
bound for arbitrary boundary traces. It does not use a small global
failure probability or sum over exterior prior records.

4. BOCHNER ABSORPTION

For q_j>0 the FULL, not only tangent, Hessian formula is

 D^2 q_j[v,v]
 =(|D w_j v|^2-|n_j dot D w_j v|^2)/q_j
      +n_j dot D^2 w_j[v,v],  n_j=w_j/q_j.

Consequently D^2 q_j>=-C g0 P_Tj in quadratic form. The convex
psi_t'' term in the penalty Hessian is positive, so

 H_t''[v,v]>=lambda |v|^2
             -C g0 sum_j psi_t'(q_j-s_j)|v_Tj|^2.    (10)

Apply (9) to each real component of grad_i u for i in T_j and sum.
Bounded read incidence, rather than mask count, gives

 sum_j E_t[psi_t'(q_j-s_j)|(grad u)_Tj|^2]
 <=C B a E_t|D^2 u|^2
     +C B(a^(-1)+r) E_t|grad u|^2.                  (11)

For L_t=Delta-grad H_t dot grad, the integrated Neumann Bochner
identity on a smooth convex domain has nonnegative boundary term:

 E_t(L_t u)^2 >= E_t|D^2u|^2+E_t H_t''[grad u,grad u]. (12)

Thus with a=1 and one weak floor satisfying
C B g0<=1/2 and C B g0(1+r)<=lambda/2,

 E_t(L_t u)^2 >=(1/2) E_t|D^2u|^2
                         +(lambda/2) E_t|grad u|^2.  (13)

The actual hierarchy has g0 r=delta0 tending to zero. It therefore
admits this floor without changing the source normalization or any
native cutoff ratio.

5. DOMAIN REGULARITY AND THE LIMIT TO THE ORIGINAL LAW

There is no need to invoke a Bochner identity on the nonconvex masked
domain or to assert that corners have no contribution. First work
instead on the smooth convex inner exhaustion

 Omega_p={sum_(i in I)(|x_i|/S)^(2p)<1}, p=1,2,... .   (14)

For fixed finite I these increase to the open product Omega. Their
boundaries are smooth, and X^j dot n>=0 because all chi_i are positive.
Equations (2)--(11) remain valid pointwise there with the SAME constants.
For each finite t,p, the classical smooth Neumann problem for positive
C^2 density on a bounded connected convex domain has discrete spectrum.
Apply (13) to a nonconstant eigenfunction with eigenvalue kappa>0:

 kappa^2 E_t u^2 >=(lambda/2) kappa E_t u^2,
 so kappa>=lambda/2.

Expansion in this basis gives Var_(t,p) f<=2 lambda^(-1) E_(t,p)|grad f|^2.
First let p increase to infinity at fixed t. Finite normalized dominated
convergence yields the same inequality on Omega. Then let t increase
to infinity. The penalties converge to zero exactly on all successes
and to infinity if any mask fails. The equality surfaces have measure
zero for nonredundant native masks; redundant masks lie strictly below
their cut. D has positive volume by its admitted product core. Hence
the finite integrals converge to the ORIGINAL normalized restricted law

                    mu_D(dx)=Z_D^-1 1_D exp(-H) dx,

and for every smooth f on the containing product,

               Var_(mu_D) f <=(2/lambda) E_(mu_D)|grad f|^2. (15)

The original domain is a finite Lipschitz domain with a common strictly
inward radial direction, as proved in the native geometry note. Smooth
approximation then extends (15) to its Sobolev W^(1,2) functions. The
finite-dimensional approximation constants need not be volume uniform:
the inequality's constant is already uniform before taking the limits.

For an arbitrary finite subset of these masks, the proof is unchanged.
In particular no N sqrt(rho) restriction is needed for (15). This is
distinct from the small-total-activity condition in the replica spatial
comparison theorem. However fixing arbitrary additional x coordinates
may destroy (2); no arbitrary-section Poincare claim is made.

6. LIMITS FOR TRANSPORT AND BACKGROUND RESPONSE

The estimate is unweighted. Inserting a spatial diagonal matrix W in
the gradient equation does not leave the positive rank-one penalty
Hessians positive after similarity. Their psi_t'' factors need not
stay bounded as t tends to infinity. In the hard limit this is exactly
the multi-site normal/tangent problem identified in the geometry audit.
Thus (15) is not an exponential source-to-boundary covariance proof.

Nor does a spectral gap differentiate a moving coincident mask pair.
The actual boundary cusp obstruction survives. Spatial comparison
must use a separately proved mechanism (for example the exact replica
comparison currently under review), or a new weighted boundary form.
No age-four gain, source-owned R admission, all-scale closure, common
continuum state, or interaction witness is inferred from this note.
