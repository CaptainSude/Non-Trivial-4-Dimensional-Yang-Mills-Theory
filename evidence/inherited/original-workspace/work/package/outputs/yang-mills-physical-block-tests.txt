has norm |X_e|^2/4. The half real trace is at most operator norm. Each
original edge belongs to six plaquettes, so Hess S4<=6 Id as quadratic

the actual bound(0.2).

4. THE EXACT CONDITIONAL CONTACT OBSTRUCTION

Use the positive original HS joint of TURN82 and TURN89:

For ANY real square-integrable joint random variable sigma satisfying
C sigma=0, conditional Cauchy--Schwarz gives

 |E[G_n sigma]|^2
   =|E[(G_n-CG_n)sigma]|^2
   <=[E Var(G_n|xi)] E sigma^2
   <=E sigma^2/(2n).                                    (4.2)

In particular, fix xi and deform the original U variables by an arbitrary
smooth field X(U,xi). Its conditional score

             |E XG_n|^2 <=[m/(4n^2)]E sigma_cond^2.      (4.3)

Dependence of the coefficients of X on the remaining coordinates does

5. ORIGINAL VERSUS CONDITIONAL SCORES: THE EXACT FISHER PRICE

Return to a smooth ORIGINAL field X(U), independent of xi. Define the

It has E_(kappa_U) S_kappa=0 at every U. Taking the U derivative of the

 sigma_cond=sigma_X-S_kappa,

X(U). Also E[G_n S_kappa]=0 since G_n is an original function. Thus
both integration-by-parts numerators equal the same E XG_n. No derivative
of an auxiliary posterior is being thrown away.

 |E XG_n|^2 <=[E sigma_X^2+E I_U(X)]/(2n)

5A. THE EXACT FISHER MATRIX AND WHAT FIXED AUXILIARIES REMOVE
