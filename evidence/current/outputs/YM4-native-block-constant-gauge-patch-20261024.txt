6. A CHECKABLE SUFFICIENT CONDITION FOR PATCH ADMISSION

The existence clause(7) has a standard finite geometric sufficient
condition that can be proved without differentiating the minimum.
Take W inside a FILLED contractible coarse box containing every
previously specified determining and incident read. Choose one
root and coordinate-ordered tree paths in that filled box. Let
k(y) be the actual path transport from the root to y, with the
convention making the tree edges equal1 after transformation.

For each coarse bond c in W, its transformed value is the holonomy
of its tree loop, transported to the root. Give that loop an actual
plaquette filling inside the filled box. Let A_W bound the number
of plaquettes in any such chosen filling. The exact non-Abelian
plaquette-insertion identity expresses the loop as a product of
at most A_W conjugated plaquette holonomies (orientations may be
reversed). Unitarity and telescoping of the product give

 max_(c in W)|V^k(c)-1|
       <= A_W max_(p in filling)|V(dp)-1|.                (11)

At sufficiently small right side, the principal logarithm has
norm at most twice that bound. Hence

       2 A_W max|V(dp)-1| < a_box,                       (12)

with a strict reserve, is sufficient for the Cartesian admission.
For a rectangular filled box and these paths, one may use a safe
bound A_W<=C_d n^2 in terms of its side diameter n; a sharper area
bound is unnecessary here. All filling plaquettes must lie in the
actual complete filled read. The same conclusion follows with any
explicit finite filling bound in a different contractible geometry.

Small curvature on a patch with holes or noncontractible cycles
does not supply(12) for its holonomies. Nor does small curvature
without the area factor imply a fixed near-identity bond bound on
arbitrarily large W. These are genuine admission conditions, not
discardable constants. They can later be checked on a current
small-image family, but no such later scale conversion is proved
here. Tree-gauge construction alone does not guarantee the bound.
