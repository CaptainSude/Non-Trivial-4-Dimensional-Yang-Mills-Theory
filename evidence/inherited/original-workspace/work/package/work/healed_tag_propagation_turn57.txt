1. THE NEW HANDOFF AND ITS PRECISE SCOPE

TURN54 controls a designated winding tag which is still present in a
complete raw component history. TURN56 assembles that estimate when the
tag is in a last raw component. Neither operation alone controls a tag
which has already entered an exponentiated interaction coefficient.

The handoff below is made at the FIRST such entrance. It uses the actual
raw activity before the optional normalization of retained components.
A marked activity contains its raw component mass exactly once.
The connected logarithm produces a marked coefficient on the original
native complex domain. That coefficient can then be used by the
TURN53 protected-carrier source response.

The original tag suppression is

               p_i = exp[-c N_i/(M R_i)].                 (1.1)

Here i is the tag date, N_i the period in its current lattice units,
and M R_i the native block scale. All charged history/walk geometry,
weak-flow conditions, extra fixed census allowance, and hierarchy
feasibility are those of native_tagged_history_propagation_turn54.txt.
In particular the p0 threshold exponent must be chosen to dominate the
actual polynomial R costs. Raising a weak floor alone does not repair
an insufficient threshold exponent.

The result applies to a FIXED admitted outer catalogue: all regions,
cut domains, classical reconstructions, retained real variables,
reference normalizers and ancillary choices are held as in the native
coefficient construction. The exterior (U,J) variables are continued
on that catalogue's original typed domain. It does not assert global
holomorphy of the hard rule selecting Omega_k as U varies.

The input is one fixed charged witness supported in a raw component,
or a positive additive sum of such witnesses with actual multiplicities
retained. Its predicate is independent of the external complex (U,J).
Signed supported walk decorations may also be marked in their absolutely
convergent native catalogue, but these are not thereby positive events
of the original Wilson measure.

The imported modules are specific, not a new analytic-map assumption:

  (a) the complete native raw-history positive estimates and their
      once-weakened dated census of TURN54, uniformly for the original
      admitted ancillary data;
  (b) B122II's complex-to-real bound for the actual raw operator,
      its Mayer activities and connected expansion, with fixed spare
      spatial/root margins;
  (c) the original typed-domain substitution/localization inputs N1--N4
      recorded in native_marked_comparison_audit_turn53.txt, for later
      use of a coefficient which has already healed.

The uninspected joint G0,T0 law is not supplied here. Uniform fixed-choice
bounds do not assert an unknown average has analytic, local, or
source-independent weights.

The tag must preserve every own-gauge/typed symmetry used in its
later admission. This is checked by the native change of integration
variables: if that change preserves the fixed tag's geometric labels,
the same substitution proves the marked coefficient's covariance.
A tag referring only to gauge-invariant component geometry has this
property whenever the underlying fixed-choice module does. For a
decorated catalogue one must transform its labels too or sum the
required orbit. Holomorphy by itself supplies no gauge symmetry.
A mark lacking such a property remains a typed marked function only
where the later arbitrary-mark module explicitly admits that type;
it is not declared an ordinary invariant R coefficient.

3. WHY A FROZEN TAG HAS THE REQUIRED ANALYTIC RAW EXTENSION

For a fixed component X and outer catalogue write its native operator as

 T_X(zeta) F = sum_h int dmu_h(x)
                           K_h(zeta,x) F_h(zeta,x),       (3.1)

where zeta=(U,J), h retains the complete internal discrete history,
and x includes the integration variables which later factors read.
The shorthand includes the original reference denominator. It does
not replace that denominator by an independently normalized kernel.
Any subsequent V factors depending on h remain inside this expression;
this is the explicit warning after B122II (1.72).

For a fixed discrete predicate chi_i(h), put that predicate inside
(3.1). On the original typed complex domain, the source's comparison
(1.73) bounds the complete integrand by its positive real reference
with the bounded complex correction. All cuts and measure factors
which the comparison holds fixed are unchanged by chi_i.

Retain the tag through that absolute estimate and through every
history sum or entropy-weighted supremum. TURN54 then gives, with
the same fixed weaker ledger,

 |T_(ell,X;tag_i) F|
   <= C p_i exp[-2 a P_ell-kappa_* d_ell(X)]
                                      ||F||_admitted,    (3.2)

where a>0 is fixed, P_ell=p0(g_ell), and kappa_* includes the
fixed reserve needed below. Equivalently, one can use its full
dated envelope before the final component estimate (3.2).
If an exterior factor is part of the native integrand, its actual
prepared majorant is used rather than an unrelated global sup.

This estimate is uniform in zeta on the original admitted domain,
not just on its real part. Indeed the complex correction in the
source comparison is one of the fixed local costs already included
in the strengthened census. The bound is applied to the COMPLETE
continued history before its final integrations/sums; it is not
inferred by reusing an earlier small integrated mass.

The holomorphy assertion concerns F=1, or an externally holomorphic
prepared F_h with the uniform admitted majorant. These include the
actual prepared Mayer factors below. It does not concern an arbitrary
bounded test depending nonanalytically on zeta; the magnitude bound
still applies to such a test at each admitted zeta.

For finite cutoff and volume the resulting integrands in (3.1) are
holomorphic in zeta on that fixed domain. The positive estimate gives locally
uniform domination of the integration and of the remaining history/
walk series. Dominated integration, or Morera followed by Fubini,
therefore proves holomorphy of the tagged raw operator. No identity
theorem for a final unmarked coefficient is being used.

The same argument works for a supported analytic walk decoration
using its absolute all-support majorant. Its statistical positivity
is a separate question.

This argument would FAIL for a new hard test chi(zeta,h) which changes
with the external complex field, or for a history sub-sum known only
to be bounded on real zeta. A global background-dependent selection
of the outer catalogue is likewise outside (3.1). All three
distinctions are essential to the handoff.

4. LOCAL OWNERSHIP AND FIRST HEALING

Choose a fixed witness label (date, root and any native type labels).
While its charged support is carried by one raw component, insert its
indicator/count in that component's actual history. Local component
factorization is preserved. If summing labels, use an additive count

                      N = sum_w chi_w.                 (4.1)

For a finite collection of raw owners this is encoded to first order
by the product of (1+eta n_owner), with eta^2=0. Each witness has one
owner in this disjoint factorization. Actual witness multiplicities
are retained; they are not hidden in a uniform constant.

A generic global earliest-bad-event indicator need not factor over
raw components and is not used. For an event covered by the witnesses,
1_event <= N is enough for a later positive union bound.

For each witness, partition its complete continuation by the first
native activity/connected-coefficient step at which its raw owner is
integrated and its tag enters a coefficient. Before that date apply
(3.2). At that date apply Sections5--6. After that date propagate
the already marked coefficient. If it later enters another raw
component, it is an inherited coefficient insertion, not a fresh tag.

This is bookkeeping of a fixed witness on the actual factorization.
It does not assert that an arbitrary nonlocal event can be localized
in one component. A tag whose deciding support already spans several
independent raw components needs its own connected supported

Healing into a boundary coefficient touching a surviving large-field

It is not called an own-field bulk interaction prematurely.

6. THE MARKED CONNECTED SERIES AND ITS QUANTITATIVE BOUND

 log Xi(a) = sum_(n>=1) 1/n! sum_(C_1,...,C_n)

 D_ell^i = sum_(n>=1) 1/(n-1)! sum_(C_1,...,C_n)
             phi^T(C_1,...,C_n) b_ell^i(C_1)
                                      product_(r=2)^n a(C_r).

There is one marked polymer and no extra factorial. This follows
by differentiating the n identical choices in the absolutely
convergent series; equivalently it is the eta coefficient in
log Xi(a+eta b).

For completeness the bound can be obtained without any assumption
that the full renormalization map is analytic in a Banach ball.
In the following disc b_ell^i refers to ONE fixed witness: all root,
type and owner labels suppressed by the notation remain fixed.
For a sum over birth roots or witnesses, apply this bound separately
and sum the derivatives, retaining its multiplicity m_i. Equivalently
one can normalize the aggregate mark by that multiplicity as well.
There is no uniform disc for an unnormalized aggregate of arbitrarily
many witnesses.

Use the concrete activity family

                  a_z(C)=a(C)+z b_ell^i(C)/p_i.          (6.2)

Sections3--5 put the two summands in the SAME native absolute

 ||D_ell^i(C)|| <= C p_i M_ell,c(C).                    (6.3)

8. SCALAR AND QUADRATIC JETS, WITH FROZEN COUPLING

The entire D_ell^i is kept. In particular its zero-field value is
a marked vacuum scalar and need not vanish. At a legal own-field
extraction one may write

 D_ell^i = s_ell^i + L_ell^i + sum_a beta_(ell,a)^i S_a
                                           + D_(ell,rem)^i. (8.1)

Here L is any permitted linear jet; it is set to zero only when
the actual marked family's symmetry proves that it vanishes.
The quadratic basis is the one justified by the
actual invariances of the marked family. A fixed rooted witness
need not have every translation/rotation symmetry of the
unmarked action. In that case keep the full finite local quadratic
jet; do not assert that it is one scalar multiple of the
standard action or that it has a zero extracted curvature matrix.
It may be kept as a bounded bilinear tensor on its original
carrier rather than expanded into an asserted fixed-size global
basis. Its Cauchy operator-norm bound has the usual inverse-radius
square cost; any support/root allocation uses the fixed spatial
reserve. One can also keep D entirely unsplit, which is sufficient
for the arbitrary-mark response.

Finite Cauchy extraction and any local redistribution costs are
included in the dated constants of TURN53. The scalar is kept
once in the marked vacuum ledger. Every quadratic term is an
insertion at the FIXED baseline numerical coupling. It does
not reset g, the fluctuation window, or an infinite-lattice beta
prescription. No Ward gain is required to bound these static
insertions on their original finite domains.

A live boundary coefficient is not subjected to a bulk own-field
extraction until the native construction actually makes it one.
Its marked value and all admissible derivatives remain in the
relative/interior state in the meantime.

This also prevents losing a winding contribution to the vacuum.
If complete short lifts already match between two covers, taking

12. WHEN A GEOMETRIC WINDING TAG CANNOT HEAL

There is a useful simplification for a narrower class. The native
preparatory R eligibility requires a component to satisfy the
conditions (i),(ii) imported from Large-I. B122II p386 in the
first induction case and p387 before (1.88) explicitly use
containment in one cube of side 100 MR_j; the activity estimate
on p389 uses the corresponding bounded size of each integrated
first-class component. This is stronger than the unconditional
small-component hull convention in B119 (2.3).

Suppose a tagged geometric support has nonzero deck winding at
date i and remains a subset of its continuing raw carrier in
common physical coordinates. Before this carrier's removal,
the pure covering/layer operation S, hull enlargement and merger
are inclusions. They cannot remove that winding support. Thus,
as long as an eligible healing cube (with any prescribed finite
collar) has side strictly smaller than the torus period, this
carrier cannot satisfy the bounded-cube eligibility condition.
Equivalently a uniform N_j > C_h MR_j excludes its healing.
A genuine winding loop cannot lie in such a contractible cube.
A mere crossing of a coordinate seam is not winding.

For that purely geometric, support-preserved positive class,
the first-healing alternatives above are empty: it remains in
the last-raw class already controlled in TURN54--56. The present
argument does not create an additional prerequisite for that case.

There are two reasons this observation is not a dismissal of the
handoff. First, the complete charged support may include a long
walk, reconstruction read, or inherited coefficient outside the
compressed raw region. Its winding is not a winding subset of
the eligible raw carrier. The original all-support catalogue,
not the final region label, must then be retained; these can be
signed auxiliary marks. Second, a more general large-support
witness may actually become eligible at later scales, or may
have entered an interaction coefficient before any winding
predicate is imposed. The raw and coefficient estimates must be
used at their real chronological slots.

The set-inclusion observation establishes the narrow nonhealing
case under the explicit support-preservation premise. It does
not prove that every historical deciding/gauge support is a
subset of the current raw carrier. The distinction in TURN54
between geometric history regions and separately charged full
walk/read supports is retained.
