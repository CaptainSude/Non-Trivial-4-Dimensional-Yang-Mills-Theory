4. AN EXACT ORIGINAL WILSON EXTERIOR RESPONSE

This calculation establishes that the dependence needing treatment in
Section3 is not an artifact of a vacuum normalization. It does not
assert that a specified native W forgets the data below.

Consider one unfixed SU2 link U in a nondegenerate four-dimensional
Wilson lattice at coupling beta>0, conditioned on every other link.
Its conditional density is proportional to

              exp[ beta sum_(r=1)^q (U B_r)_0 ] dU,        (4.1)

where q=6 for an interior periodic link and X_0=Tr X/2. Choose a real
weak exterior with B_1=...=B_(q-1)=I and
B_q=exp(i theta sigma_3). This is realizable by changing a single
parallel outer edge unique to one of the incident plaquette staples,
with all other exterior links initially I. Other exterior action terms
are constants in this conditional integral.

The ORIGINAL first plaquette observable is h(U B_1)=1-U_0 in this
fixed exterior frame. Let

 R(theta)=[q^2-2(q-1)(1-cos theta)]^(1/2),
 c(kappa)=I_2(kappa)/I_1(kappa).

The Haar identity Z(kappa)=2 I_1(kappa)/kappa and rotational symmetry
on quaternion S^3 give exactly

 E_theta h(U)=1-[(q-1+cos theta)/R(theta)]
                              c(beta R(theta)).            (4.2)

Here c'(kappa)=Var_kappa(U_0)>0 and c(kappa)>0. Expanding at theta=0,

 E_theta h-E_0 h
 = theta^2 [ c(q beta)/(2q^2)
               +(q-1) beta c'(q beta)/(2q) ]+O(theta^4).   (4.3)

The displayed coefficient is strictly positive at every beta>0,
including arbitrarily weak real boundary angles theta. This is a
gauge-invariant closed plaquette observable; the frame choice B_1=I
