RESULT

Two actual finite-regulator advances are established here:

 (i) the new space-time action MGF gives a physical magnetic source
     amplitude domain independent of the cutoff, with a local
     extensive majorant and an explicit smaller zero-free disc;

 (ii) localizing the log-coupling source produces an exact electric
     local-energy remainder. Its ground Dirichlet form is bounded
     directly by spatial differences of the source profile, without
     a Fisher or marked-score term.

The first result does not give an O(1) connected, separated-time
source remainder. The second explains precisely why the global
coupling identity cannot simply be asserted for a local magnetic
source. The remaining term is identified and estimated on the
actual ground law, not introduced as an unspecified hypothesis.

1. ACTUAL LAW AND THE NEW SPACE-TIME INPUT

Use the finite-spatial SU2 Hamiltonian

 H=kappa K+vS,   kappa=g^2/(2a),  v=4/(g^2a),
 S=sum_p h_p,   h_p=1-Tr U_p/2,

with Pauli/2 link metric, positive ground state Omega, ground energy
E0 and normalized law mu=Omega^2 dU. At a fixed finite graph all
operators and derivatives below have their usual smooth compact
elliptic meaning. A=H-E0 and

              D(F)=<F Omega,A F Omega>
                  =kappa E_mu |grad F|^2.              (1.1)

A constant part of F does not affect(1.1).

The companion proof hamiltonian_spacetime_moments_turn68.txt gives
the following exact actual-ground-process bound. On its dyadic
closed spatial board with V sites per fundamental tile, temporal
cell length Delta, and

 X_c=(a g^2)^-1 int_(I_c) S_(B_c)(U(t))dt,
 c_Delta=9VDelta/(2a),

one has, for any finite distinct cell family,

 log E exp[sum_c t_c X_c] <= c_Delta sum_c t_c,
                         0<=t_c<=2.                  (1.2)

The proof retains the full thermal normalizer, uses full-trace
Brownian loops to prove space/time RP, and then takes the ground
limit. The full and physical ground states coincide. This is not
an assertion about a native RG reference measure or a flowed law.

For Delta=a, c_Delta=9V/2 is independent of a,g and volume.
The same proof supplies the sharper actual energy-curve expression

 log E exp[sum t_cX_c]
 <=(VDelta/n) sum_c [E0-E_(t_c/2)],                    (1.3)

where E_c=inf spec[kappa K+(1-c)vS]. Both kinetic and potential
coefficients are retained.

2. PHYSICAL LOCAL SOURCE: FIXED AMPLITUDES AND AN EXPLICIT LOG DISC

For a real profile f(t,p) supported in a finite time window define

 Y_f=int dt Q_f(t),
 Q_f(t)=4/(g^2a) sum_p f(t,p)h_p(U(t)).                (2.1)

Choose one containing closed spatial tile as the owner of each
plaquette. Let F_c be the supremum of |f| on the owned plaquettes
and times of cell c. This ownership enters only a pointwise upper
bound, not the chessboard construction. Then

                 |Y_f|<=4 sum_c F_c X_c.

Set

 r_* = 1/(2 max_c F_c),
 L_f = (18VDelta/a) sum_c F_c.                         (2.2)

If f=0, every conclusion is trivial and r_* may be infinite.
For |z|<=r_*,

 |Z_f(z)|=|E exp(zY_f)| <= exp(L_f |z|).               (2.3)

All expectations in this section use the SAME unsourced stationary
ground process and its complete normalizer. No selected sector,
sourced ground vector, or source-dependent probability denominator
has been substituted.

The radius r_* is independent of the cutoff for a bounded physical
profile. For fixed tile lengths, Delta=a, and fixed physical
space-time support, however,

                         L_f=O(a^-4).                 (2.4)

Thus(2.3) supplies a genuine local extensive bound for source
VALUES. It is stronger than the elementary stationary-Jensen bound,
which would require |z|=O(a/T) on a slab of fixed duration T.
It does not supply a uniform connected-log bound.

There is also a proved, but shrinking, zero-free disc. Positivity
and(1.2) give

 |Z_f(z)-1|
 <= E[exp(|z||Y_f|)-1]
 <= exp(L_f |z|)-1.

Consequently all such actual source generators have their canonical
logarithm on

 |z|<=min{r_*, log(3/2)/L_f}.                         (2.5)

This radius is O(a^4) on a fixed physical support. It removes the
extra g^2 loss in the elementary bound using the supremum of the
unflowed Wilson action, but is still not uniform as a->0.

At fixed regulator, Y_f is bounded and its generator is entire.
Subtracting its mean or any explicitly known affine-in-time vacuum
term multiplies Z_f by a nonzero exponential; it does NOT move its
complex zeros. One cannot infer a larger zero-free domain by that
subtraction alone.

For a finite family of cell sources, (2.5) is naturally an l1
budget on their coefficients. Individual cell amplitudes have
uniform moments, while a physical test source occupies O(a^-4)
cells. This distinction is relevant to source derivatives.

3. A LOCAL LOG-COUPLING DOES NOT EQUAL A LOCAL MAGNETIC SOURCE

For this section take a cubic isotropic spatial torus, so the actual
ground energy bound and cubic symmetry give

                         E_mu h_p<=3g^2/4              (3.1)

for every plaquette. The rectangular variant merely has the larger
constant9g^2/4. Let f_p be arbitrary real numerical plaquette
weights and put

 eta_e=(1/4) sum_(p incident to e) f_p,
 Q_f=v sum_p f_p h_p,
 T_eta=kappa sum_e eta_e K_e,
 H_f=T_eta+Q_f.                                       (3.2)

Every spatial edge has four incident plaquettes. The numerical
weights commute with all differential operators. Consider the
actual local elliptic coupling family

 H_f^loc(tau)=kappa sum_e exp(-tau eta_e)K_e
                   +v sum_p exp(tau f_p)h_p.           (3.3)

For real tau near0 it is a positive-coefficient elliptic
Hamiltonian; near zero in the complex plane it is an analytic
elliptic family on the common fixed-graph domain.

Here H_f^loc(0)=H; the weighted operator H_f in(3.2) is a
different object. At tau=0,

 (H_f^loc)'(0)=Q_f-T_eta=2Q_f-H_f,
 (H_f^loc)''(0)=kappa sum_e eta_e^2 K_e
                         +v sum_p f_p^2 h_p=:C_f.      (3.4)

Write q_f=Q_f-<Q_f>. Define the real smooth centered function r_f
by its ground vector

 r_f Omega=(H_f-<H_f>)Omega.                           (3.5)

Omega is strictly positive at this finite graph, so this definition
is unambiguous. It is gauge invariant. Its expression as a function
can read Omega globally: no finite local support for that function
is being claimed merely because H_f is a local differential
operator. Explicitly, r_f=Omega^-1(H_f-<H_f>)Omega. This ground
quotient is not a magnetic multiplication insertion already
present in the original Wilson action.

The centered derivative vector is exactly

 [(H_f^loc)'(0)-<(H_f^loc)'(0)>]Omega
                         =(2q_f-r_f)Omega.             (3.6)

For constant f_p=1, eta_e=1, H_f=H and r_f=0. That is the global
identity used in TURN67. For a spatially localized profile there
is no such cancellation in general.

With fixed UNSOURCED ground endpoints define

 K_f(T,tau)=log <Omega,exp[-T(H_f^loc(tau)-E0)]Omega>.

At the fixed graph its local source derivatives satisfy

 partial_tau^2 K_f(T,0)
   =2 int_0^T(T-s) C_b(s)ds -T<C_f>,
 b=2q_f-r_f,
 C_b(s)=<b Omega,exp(-sA)b Omega>.                     (3.7)

Taking the second forward time difference of step d>0 gives

 Delta_d^2 partial_tau^2 K_f(T,0)
       =2 int exp(-TE)[(1-exp(-dE))/E]^2 rho_b(dE).     (3.8)

The contact term -T<C_f> cancels, but the vector b remains.
For f=1, rho_b=4rho_Q and(3.8) becomes the established factor8
magnetic-window formula. For a general local f, (3.8) is NOT
eight times the magnetic Q_f window norm.

4. EXACT LOCAL ENERGY CURRENT AND ITS ACTUAL DIRICHLET BOUND

There is a useful estimate for the remainder r_f which avoids a
marked Fisher term. Put d_(e,p)=f_p-eta_e for p incident to e.
Since all K_e commute, and all h_p are multiplication operators,

 [H,H_f]
       =kappa v sum_(e,p incident) d_(e,p)[K_e,h_p].    (4.1)

The ground equation gives

 A(r_f Omega)=[H,H_f]Omega.                           (4.2)

For any real smooth F, integration by parts in the normalized Haar
measure gives the exact cancellation

 <F Omega,[K_e,h_p]Omega>
                      =E_mu grad_e F . grad_e h_p.   (4.3)

Indeed [K_e,h_p]Omega=-(Delta_e h_p)Omega
-2 grad_e h_p . grad_e Omega. Integrating the first term by parts
cancels the second term containing grad Omega. No pointwise or
weighted Fisher bound is used.

Using(4.2),(4.3) and the ground transform,

 E_mu grad F . grad r_f
   =v E_mu sum_e grad_e F .
                         sum_(p incident e)d_(e,p)grad_e h_p.
                                                               (4.4)

Thus grad r_f is the gradient-space projection, in L2(mu), of the
explicit local one-form on the right. Taking F=r_f and applying
Cauchy--Schwarz proves

 D(r_f)<=kappa v^2 E_mu sum_e
                       |sum_(p incident e)d_(e,p)grad_e h_p|^2.
                                                               (4.5)

For each link of a plaquette, in the Pauli/2 convention,

 |grad_e h_p|^2=(2h_p-h_p^2)/4<=h_p/2.

There are four terms at each spatial edge. Therefore

 |sum_p d_(e,p)grad_e h_p|^2
                   <=2 sum_p d_(e,p)^2 h_p.

Combining this with(3.1), kappa v^2=8/(g^2a^3), gives the explicit
actual-law result

             D(r_f) <= (12/a^3)
                         sum_(e,p incident) |f_p-eta_e|^2.       (4.6)

No unverified conditional density or source window is involved.
The averaging choice eta_e in(3.2) minimizes the numerical square
sum independently at every edge.

For comparison the same calculation, without the profile
differences, gives

                    D(q_f)<=(48/a^3)sum_p f_p^2.       (4.7)

This agrees with the magnetic-source quadrature coefficient
proved independently in separated_source_attachment_turn68.txt.

For a fixed smooth physical profile sampled at plaquette centers,
centers of plaquettes incident to the same edge are at mutual
distance at most a. Consequently

 |f_p-eta_e|<=a ||grad f||infinity.

If N_inc is the number of affected edge-plaquette incidences,
then(4.6) is at most12 N_inc ||grad f||infinity^2/a.
On a fixed physical support N_inc=O(a^-3), yielding O(a^-4).
It vanishes exactly for a constant profile, but it is not a
cutoff-uniform local-source estimate.

The bound controls the first spectral moment int E rho_r(dE).
It does not bound rho_r near energy0 or supply the inverse-energy
control required to discard r_f from a finite-time window. No
cutoff-independent spectral gap has been assumed.

5. WHAT THE NEW MOMENTS CANNOT IMPLY BY THEMSELVES

This subsection is a logical test of an inference, not a
counterexample to the SU2 Hamiltonian.

The joint upper bound(1.2), even with reflection positivity,
does not alone imply a uniform zero-free physical-source disc
or a separated covariance bound. To see the information loss,
fix Delta=a and any b with0<b<=c_Delta. Let all spatial cell
variables be bZ(t), where Z(t) is a stationary two-state reversible
chain taking values0,1 with equal weights and flip rate epsilon.
Define the cell variable as its temporal average

 X_(b,j)=(b/a) int_(I_j) Z(t)dt.

For a cell of duration a, 0<=X_c<=b pointwise. Hence, for every
finite nonnegative coefficient array,

 E exp(sum t_cX_c)<=exp(b sum t_c)
                                <=exp(c_Delta sum t_c).

This includes all simultaneous rare-cell upper bounds derived
solely from(1.2). The stationary process is temporally reflection
positive, and spatial reflection positivity is trivial because
every spatial cell reads the same variable. For epsilon>0 it has
a unique positive two-state ground state.

A source occupying N_sp spatial cells has instantaneous canonical
amplitude alpha_a Z(t), with

                      alpha_a=4b N_sp/a.

On a fixed physical volume N_sp is of order a^-3, so alpha_a is of
order a^-4. Its centered covariance is

          C(t)=(alpha_a^2/4)exp(-2epsilon|t|).

It can diverge like a^-8 at fixed separated times while all the
joint MGF upper bounds above hold. This is NOT a local Wilson
Hamiltonian: precisely that additional spatial kinetic/dynamical
structure must be used to improve the source estimate.

The zero-flip limiting diagnostic makes the source zeros explicit:
on a time slab of length T,

 Z_source(z)=(1+exp(z alpha_a T))/2,
 exp(-z alpha_a T/2) Z_source(z)
                         =cosh(z alpha_a T/2).

The nearest zeros have modulus pi/(alpha_a T). Centering does not
move them; nor does multiplication by any affine-in-T scalar
normalization. The zero-flip law is used only for this elementary
zero calculation. The preceding epsilon>0 model already supplies
the unique-ground, separated-covariance obstruction.

Thus an extensive absolute source majorant is real progress, but
cannot be relabeled a connected source remainder estimate.
