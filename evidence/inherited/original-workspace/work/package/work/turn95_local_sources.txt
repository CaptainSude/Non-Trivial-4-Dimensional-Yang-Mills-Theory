bound, and identifies the term it does not estimate. It does not prove a
full original separated covariance estimate or YM4 existence. No frontier

  F=S_f=sum_p f_p h(U_0,p),  H=S_h=sum_p hprof_p h(U_0,p),

2. WHAT c0=1 REALLY SAVES

The old source convention F_c=c0 S_f, H_c=c0 S_h has
Cov(F_c,H_c)=c0^2 Cov(S_f,S_h), exactly at every regulator. Thus c0=1
removes beta_0^2 from TURN83's native regular-primitive envelope:

 |C_j(f,h;W)| <= C min(N_f,j,N_h,j) exp[-c d/ell_j]
  [g_j^2 ell_j^(alpha+alpha') [f]_alpha[h]_alpha'
       +g_j^4 ||f||inf||h||inf].                         (2.1)

All scopes in TURN83 remain: actual homogeneous primitive, complete
stationary action, physical background, frozen even window, truncated
correlated covariance, and complete read support. For fixed separated
bounded physical supports N_f,j<=C_f ell_j^-4, and g_j<=g_*, this envelope
has a uniform geometric UV sum. Its tail at ell_j<=epsilon<=d is bounded
by

 C exp[-c' d/epsilon]
 [g_*^2 epsilon^(-4+alpha+alpha') [f]_alpha[h]_alpha'
       +g_*^4 epsilon^-4 ||f||inf||h||inf].             (2.2)

This is the existing reference-primitive estimate with the less demanding
observable normalization. It is useful and now has no divergent beta_0
prefactor. It is still not an additive decomposition of the full original
covariance.

In contrast, the once-owned raw theorem already pays every fixed beta_0
power from its initial primitive reserve. Removing one factor does not
change its decisive physical-cell census. For example TURN78(5A.3) gives

 E_o |F_heal,k| <= C_K beta_0^-K ||f||inf Lambda_k(A)     (2.3)

for every fixed K, after relabeling K, under the SAME full conditional
normalization. It already had this form for the canonical source. The

3. EXACT FINE-SCALE EXCLUSION OF A COMMON FIRST-HEALING OWNER

Use the TURN78 ownership convention on the complete original record.
An initial raw-touch plaquette p is assigned nonnegative weights w_p,D
over the initial raw components D containing one of its raw links, with
sum_D w_p,D=1. Follow each D through its actual union/merger lineage until
its FIRST healing. At date k and current first-class component X, let

 F_X=sum_(D first healing in X) sum_p w_p,D f_p h(U_0,p),
 H_X=sum_(D first healing in X) sum_p w_p,D hprof_p h(U_0,p).

No new ownership is assigned after that first healing. All assertions
below are before signed polymer decoration, on the actual positive joint.

Let A,B be the sets of original plaquette centers on which f,hprof are
nonzero. Let d_inf be their periodic physical sup-distance. Write a_0
for the original physical fine mesh, a_k for the current lattice mesh,
and ell_k=M a_k for the current M-cell mesh.

Claim. If

           d_inf > 100 R_k ell_k + 2 a_0,               (3.1)

then F_X H_X=0 IDENTICALLY for every eligible first-healing X. More
strongly, for each fixed geometric X at least one of F_X,H_X is identically
zero. If a slightly wider fixed plaquette-collar convention is used,
replace2 a_0 by its corresponding fixed C a_0; no age factor appears.
Here X means the eligible raw component in the first-class catalogue,
not a later localization carrier enlarged by its analytic source reads.

Proof. By native containment and whole-lineage merging, an initial
unhealed raw component D ancestral to X is contained in X in common
physical coordinates. This is the whole-component geometry used in
TURN62 and TURN78, not a claim that all reconstructed regular reads are
inside X. A plaquette assigned to D has at least one original link in D;
its center is at sup-distance at most a_0 from X. If both F_X and H_X
can be nonzero, the a_0-neighborhood of X therefore meets A and B.
Printed177's eligibility condition puts X in a cube of side100MR_k in
current lattice units. Any two such centers have periodic sup-distance
at most100R_k ell_k+2 a_0, contrary to(3.1). This proof works also for
straddling plaquettes: it uses only one actual raw link, not an assumed
pointwise reconstruction of their previously integrated regular links.

The same-owner term in TURN78(5A.4) is thus exactly absent under(3.1).
Write R_f=F_heal,k=sum_X F_X and R_h=H_heal,k=sum_X H_X. The existing
two-distinct-carrier erasure argument, with the same full denominator,
then gives the sharper ACTUAL positive bound

 E_o |R_f R_h| <= C_K^2 beta_0^-2K ||f||inf||h||inf
                         Lambda_k(A)Lambda_k(B).        (3.2)

Together with(2.3),

 |Cov_o(R_f,R_h)| <= 2 C_K^2 beta_0^-2K ||f||inf||h||inf
                         Lambda_k(A)Lambda_k(B).        (3.3)

The pointwise geometry is uniform in the admitted outer datum o. After
averaging o, the same estimate holds if the displayed Lambdas are replaced
by the uniform per-label censuses already used in TURN78: separately
average the absolute mixed-moment and first-moment bounds and then use
|Cov(A,B)|<=E|AB|+E|A| E|B|. Do not identify global covariance with the
average conditional covariance. No independence
of the carriers is assumed: the two-carrier erasure theorem supplies the
product upper bound. Every initial credit is spent once, just as before.

This sharpening removes the degree-two SINGLE-carrier term from the real
straddling-source bound at sufficiently fine physical scales. It is not a
spatial clustering theorem: the remaining product of censuses has no
distance decay. For ell_k=ell_* L^-r and polylogarithmic R_k, condition(3.1)
does hold for all sufficiently large r at fixed positive d_inf, provided
the usual rounded-grid/weak-flow regime used in the earlier notes holds.

4. THE EXPLICIT MIXED TERM WHICH STILL SURVIVES

At this date decompose the ORIGINAL sources exactly as

      F=F_rest+R_f,     H=H_rest+R_h.

Then

 Cov(F,H)-Cov(F_rest,H_rest)
  =Cov(R_f,H_rest)+Cov(F_rest,R_h)+Cov(R_f,R_h).          (4.1)

The common-owner exclusion only improves the last term. It does not
remove either one-mark term. The latter already has one original mark
on a first-healing component and its other original mark outside that
owner. Both source supports can be disjoint while that covariance remains.

Here is its literal native numerator rather than a proxy reference term.
On a specified complete real factorization write the basic-R measure

 dPi=Z_0^-1 sigma(de) tau_e(dx,dxi) q_e(dy) W(e,x,xi,y).

q_e is the ACTUAL normalized reference quotient from Large-I(1.100);
tau_e contains the old interior, old cuts, inverse records and whatever
old density factors have already been assigned to it. W is the remaining
factor in this specified factorization and must not duplicate those
factors. Summing all required histories/continuations gives the original
Wilson marginal. With r_f the original once-owned contribution and H_rest
its literal original complement, the surviving numerator is

 Cov(R_f,H_rest)
  = Z_0^-1 int sigma(de) tau_e(dx,dxi) q_e(dy)
       r_f(e,x,xi) [H_rest(e,x,xi)-E H_rest]
                         W(e,x,xi,y),                  (4.2)

with the complete component/history sum implicit in sigma,tau,W. It is
not correct to replace H_rest by its new-current formula, nor to delete W
because int q_e=1. In the fully completed positive augmentation the
remaining test can be1, but the old product still has to be retained.

If H_rest is genuinely measurable in the retained exterior, it pulls out
of the old integral and(4.2) becomes the covariance of the first original
posterior of r_f with that exterior observable. This does NOT vanish.
If it was partly integrated earlier, one must retain its old joint mixed
coefficient: E[r_f H_rest|current record] is generally not the product
of its two first posteriors. Thus disjoint ORIGINAL supports do not make
this an unmarked constant test of the current raw operator.

This is the smaller native term worth estimating next. An estimate of
two marked rare carriers alone, even(3.3), leaves this one-rare/one-regular
term untouched. Likewise source-only one-root constants disappear from
a mixed logarithmic derivative, while exterior-dependent one-root
coefficients in(4.2) can carry the whole long-range response.

5. WHY THE NEW REAL BOUND IS NOT YET SUMMABLE

For fixed physical profiles, the existing uniform census is of type

 Lambda_k(A) <= C_A ell_k^-4 epsilon_k,

where epsilon_k has native weak-tail form poly(log gamma_k)
exp[-c(log gamma_k)^p], p>1, along gamma_k comparable to Gamma+r.
Thus even the improved upper envelope(3.3) contains

       beta_0^-2K ell_k^-8 epsilon_k^2.                 (5.1)

No fixed K makes this envelope uniformly summable down the original
fine lattice: ell_k^-8 grows exponentially in r whereas the weak tail
and beta_0^-2K give only subexponential/logarithmic suppression along the
asymptotically free regime. This diagnoses this bound, not the true size
of the covariance. Setting c0=1 does not alter that conclusion.

For the first term of(4.1), the completely safe real bound uses
|H_rest|<=2 sum_p |hprof_p|, so(2.3) gives

 |Cov(R_f,H_rest)| <= 4 C_K beta_0^-K ||f||inf
            Lambda_k(A) sum_p |hprof_p|.                (5.2)

There is no useful continuum estimate in(5.2): the final sum is O(a_0^-4).
Original Wilson higher energy moments can improve calibration factors
but do not supply missing spatial influence. Repeatedly paying the
initial reserve, dropping this source census, or declaring the two
posteriors independent would be incorrect.

6. CONCLUSION FOR THE MAIN ROUTE

Use c0=1 if the constant-source matching confirms it as the intended
geometric-energy normalization. It removes an unnecessary beta_0^2 from
the proven regular separated envelope and avoids demanding a harder
observable without reason. It does not by itself repair the raw route.

At first healing, source separation really DOES remove a same-owner
original mixed moment at fine scales, even for initially straddling
plaquettes. After that cancellation the explicit surviving object is
(4.2), not a missed cutoff/Jacobian term. The next new estimate must
control the localization/transport of the OLD one-mark coefficient
against the distant original regular mark, with the full continuation.
The earlier fixed-jet and once-owned reserve machinery can then be used
without asking for an entire source disc or arbitrary future tests.

No cutoff-uniform bound on(4.2), nonvanishing separated response, or
complete continuum local hierarchy is established in this note.
