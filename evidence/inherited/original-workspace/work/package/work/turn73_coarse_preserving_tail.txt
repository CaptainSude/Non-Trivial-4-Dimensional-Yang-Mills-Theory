COARSE-PRESERVING REPLACEMENT AND ORIGINAL TWO-SOURCE ERRORS
TURN73. tail47. 20 October 2026.

RESULT

Preserving the full coarse marginal is a valid way to avoid the
selection-induced change of that marginal. It does not require a
global all-good probability close to one. The exact TURN37 replacement
already performs this operation for the original bare conditional
Wilson boxes, including arbitrary admitted boxwise coarse statistics.

A new consequence is a local chi-square bound with the complete
original denominator. Combined with the TURN64 original Wilson action
moments, it gives an actual two-source comparison for literal original
weighted plaquette actions, without an observable supremum bound.
Sections2--3 prove this quantitative extension.

It does NOT turn the TURN70 selected trajectory into a full-law
trajectory. Two precise obstructions remain:

 (a) even with the coarse marginal preserved, changes of conditional
     source means contribute an outer covariance; controlling just
     the conditional two-mark covariance is insufficient;
 (b) preserving the full coarse marginal makes the outgoing density
     rho_full=rho_selected/p(coarse). Its unknown acceptance factor
     has become a coarse log-density term. The existing selected
     analytic/source induction does not control that new term.

These are exact identities and a specific missing attachment, not a
new counterexample to Yang--Mills or a requirement that all-good mass
must be large. The new bound applies to the actual first bare-box
replacement at its retained TURN37 hypotheses. Its physical support
count does not yet give a UV-summable continuum estimate.

1. THE ACTUAL COARSE-PRESERVING KERNEL

For the separated bare cores of TURN37, write the original normalized
Wilson law as

 mu(domega,du)=mu_X(domega) product_i Q_i^omega(du_i).    (1.1)

The common exterior omega includes every boundary edge. No original
plaquette reads two active core interiors. A coarse statistic
Y_i=A_i(omega,u_i) may be any standard-Borel statistic whose complete
read uses only omega and core i. If a desired coarse map spans several
cores, it must factor through these separate recorded statistics for
the product statement below to apply.

Disintegrate Q_i^omega against Y_i:

 Q_i^omega=integral nu_i^omega(dy_i) R_i^(omega,y_i),
 Lambda(domega,dy)=mu_X(domega) product_i nu_i^omega(dy_i).  (1.2)

Thus the conditional law of all interiors given Z=(omega,Y) is
product_i R_i. The original joint law of Z is Lambda.

Let B_i be the exterior bad-boundary event, W_i the original window,
and suppose the retained bare theorem gives

 Q_i^omega(W_i^c)<=alpha on B_i^c,  0<alpha<1.           (1.3)

Put p_i(Z)=R_i(W_i^c), fix 0<theta<1, and set

 G_i={B_i^c,p_i<=theta},
 P_i=R_i(.|W_i) on G_i,   P_i=R_i otherwise.            (1.4)

The replacement probability

                     nu(dZ,dv)=Lambda(dZ) product_i P_i(dv_i)

preserves Lambda exactly. Its density against the SAME original law is

 M=product_i M_i,
 M_i=1_(G_i^c)+1_(G_i)1_(W_i)/(1-p_i).                 (1.5)

Each M_i has conditional R_i mean one. The denominator 1-p_i is the
actual original fiber probability. It is bounded below by 1-theta on
the modified fibers. Bad boundaries and bad coarse fibers are left
unchanged. Exceptional null fibers are irrelevant to these a.e.
measure statements; no new coarea version at every datum is claimed.

In particular, the probability that all boxes lie in their windows
can be extremely small. It does not appear as a denominator in(1.5).
All moments of functions of Z are preserved exactly.

2. NEW EXACT LOCAL CHI-SQUARE ESTIMATE

Let A be a finite set of active core labels, k=|A|. Retain the full
Z and only the interiors U_A. Integrating every other interior in
(1.5) gives the exact marginal density

                  dnu_(Z,U_A)/dmu_(Z,U_A)=M_A:=product_(i in A)M_i.

At a fixed Z, a direct two-value computation gives

 E_(R_i) M_i^2
      =1+1_(G_i) p_i/(1-p_i),
 E_(R_i)(M_i-1)^2
      =1_(G_i) p_i/(1-p_i).                            (2.1)

Conditional product and E_mu M_A=1 therefore imply

 D_A:=chi^2(nu_(Z,U_A)||mu_(Z,U_A))
    =E_Lambda[product_(i in A)(1+a_i)-1],
 a_i=1_(G_i)p_i/(1-p_i).                              (2.2)

This is an equality, not an estimate of two extensive partition
functions. At fixed omega, the Y_i are independent and a_i depends
only on omega,Y_i. From(1.3),

 integral a_i dnu_i^omega
       <=alpha/(1-theta) 1_(B_i^c).

Hence

             D_A <=[1+alpha/(1-theta)]^k-1.            (2.3)

No independent law for the bad boundaries is required: they simply
turn off the replacement. No original-law bad-boundary probability
estimate enters(2.3). Such an estimate is needed for other claims
about how completely the new law lies in a weak chart.

For a frozen weight 0<=w_i<=1 in place of the hard event W_i, one may
append a local uniform flag and define W_i={flag<=w_i}. The same
theorem applies on that mass-one augmented original space; forgetting
the flag can only decrease chi-square divergence. Directly,
E_R[w_i^2]/(E_R w_i)^2<=1/(E_R w_i) gives the same bound.
Its actual geometric reads and conditional product must still match
(1.1)--(1.2).

For theta=1/2 and k alpha<=1/2, (2.3) implies, for example,

                         D_A<=4 k alpha.              (2.4)

This is a finite-read estimate. There is no claim that the divergence
of the complete replacement law is small when the number of all
replaced boxes tends to infinity.

2A. Exact covariance consumer for original observables

Let F,H be ORIGINAL observables measurable in (Z,U_A), with finite
fourth moments under mu. Put

                 f=F-E_mu F,    h=H-E_mu H.

Since M_A is the exact marginal density,

 Cov_nu(F,H)-Cov_mu(F,H)
   =E_mu[(M_A-1)fh]
        -E_mu[(M_A-1)f] E_mu[(M_A-1)h].              (2.5)

Cauchy--Schwarz proves

 |Cov_nu(F,H)-Cov_mu(F,H)|
   <=sqrt(D_A)||fh||_(L2(mu))
                   +D_A||f||_(L2(mu))||h||_(L2(mu)).  (2.6)

This retains the mean-shift term. In particular, it is not enough to
estimate a marked product and silently discard the two mean changes.
Neither a selected normalization nor an auxiliary-reference observable
has been substituted. Divergent deterministic additive source constants
cancel by the displayed ORIGINAL centering.

The finite-regulator original generating functions may also be used:
nu has density M_A frozen at the unsourced baseline, so the two-source
covariance is the corresponding mixed logarithmic derivative. Retuning
M_A when sources are inserted would define a different derivative.

3. AN ACTUAL FULL-WILSON SOURCE APPLICATION

Take the full nondegenerate periodic SU(2) Wilson law at homogeneous
coupling beta, with h_p=1-Tr(U_p)/2. The retained TURN64 theorem gives
for each plaquette

                    E_mu exp(beta h_p/4)<=2^(3/4).    (3.1)

No conditional-on-exterior version is used here. Markov's inequality
and tail integration give, for every fixed r>=1,

 ||h_p||_(Lr(mu)) <= C_r/beta,
 C_r=4[2^(3/4) Gamma(r+1)]^(1/r).                     (3.2)

Indeed P(beta h_p>t)<=2^(3/4)e^(-t/4); integrating
r t^(r-1) times this bound proves(3.2). This does not differentiate
an inequality of moment-generating functions to infer higher
coefficientwise inequalities.

For arbitrary deterministic real weights, let

 F=sum_p f_p h_p,    H=sum_p htilde_p h_p,
 W_F=sum_p|f_p|,     W_H=sum_p|htilde_p|.

Suppose every non-exterior plaquette read by these two sources belongs
to the chosen read cores A. Plaquettes wholly in the preserved exterior
may be included. Minkowski and(3.2) imply

 ||F||_r<=C_r W_F/beta,   ||H||_r<=C_r W_H/beta.

Thus their original centered versions obey
||f||_r<=2C_r W_F/beta and ||h||_r<=2C_r W_H/beta.
Hölder in(2.6) gives the explicit original-source comparison

 |Cov_nu(F,H)-Cov_mu(F,H)|
  <=4 beta^(-2) W_F W_H
                  [C_4^2 sqrt(D_A)+C_2^2 D_A].        (3.3)

The replacement keeps every selected coarse statistic and the full
exterior marginal. Equation(3.3) nevertheless compares observables of
the ORIGINAL links and retains the complete original denominator.
It is an actual first bare-box consequence of TURN37 plus TURN64.
It does not require a uniform covariance estimate at every prescribed
coarse fiber, or probability near one for all box windows.

For canonically rescaled sources beta F,beta H, the beta^(-2) factor
is removed. For another cutoff-dependent multiplicative calibration,
both sides of(3.3) are multiplied accordingly. The l1 plaquette-weight
counts remain; the full-law energy moment bound does not remove the
contact contribution to those moments.

For fixed read cores and fixed weights, the polylog-box alpha from
TURN37 makes this error small. The same statement does not follow
for a fixed physical support containing order a^(-4) fine plaquettes.
If beta is of order log(1/a), then log(1/g) is of order log log(1/a).
The archived polylog-box alpha is of the form
exp[-c(log(1/g))^p] while the number of read cores is of order
a^(-4) divided by a polylogarithmic factor. The displayed bound
does not make k alpha small in that regime; W_F W_H can also grow
as a^(-8). This diagnoses the estimate's failure to prove a physical
UV limit, not the divergence of the actual covariance.

4. WHAT PRESERVING THE COARSE LAW DOES, AND DOES NOT, CANCEL

Consider sources of the form

 F=F_ext(Z)+sum_i F_i(Z,U_i),
 H=H_ext(Z)+sum_i H_i(Z,U_i).

Define their full conditional means m_F(Z)=E_mu[F|Z],
m_H(Z)=E_mu[H|Z] and the replacement means

 m_F^nu=m_F-delta_F,     m_H^nu=m_H-delta_H.

For example
delta_F=sum_i(R_i F_i-P_i F_i). Inactive fibers contribute zero.
The exterior contributions occur in both means unchanged.

Conditional product on BOTH laws gives the exact identity

 Cov_mu(F,H)-Cov_nu(F,H)
 =E_Lambda sum_i[Cov_(R_i)(F_i,H_i)-Cov_(P_i)(F_i,H_i)]
   +Cov_Lambda(delta_F,m_H)+Cov_Lambda(m_F,delta_H)
   -Cov_Lambda(delta_F,delta_H).                       (4.1)

This follows by expanding the two total-covariance identities with
the SAME Lambda. The change of coarse marginal has indeed disappeared.
Changes of the conditional means have not.

In particular, if F and H read disjoint core sets, their conditional
covariances given Z vanish in BOTH laws. The first line of(4.1) is
then exactly zero. The entire two-point correction is in the three
outer terms. A perfect fixed-fiber two-mark bound is not a substitute
for this conditional-mean information.

On an active fiber let a_i(s,t) be its ORIGINAL source-tilted window
probability, with the window and the active-fiber rule frozen. Then

 partial_s log a_i|0=P_i F_i-R_i F_i,
 partial_st log a_i|0=Cov_(P_i)(F_i,H_i)-Cov_(R_i)(F_i,H_i).
                                                               (4.2)

Thus the two necessary local inputs are its one-source AND two-source
responses, followed by the actual full coarse expectation/covariance.
The chi-square argument in Section2 bounds their combined effect
for the finite-read bare application without pretending that the
three outer terms vanish.

One can force mean matching by defining under nu

 F_sharp=F+delta_F(Z),       H_sharp=H+delta_H(Z).

Then

 Cov_mu(F,H)-Cov_nu(F_sharp,H_sharp)
    =E_Lambda sum_i[Cov_(R_i)(F_i,H_i)-Cov_(P_i)(F_i,H_i)]. (4.3)

This identity is valid, but F_sharp is a modified observable using
the UNKNOWN full conditional source means. Equation(4.3) is not a
comparison for the unchanged physical F,H unless those added
functions themselves have been computed and controlled.

Recording F and H themselves among the preserved coarse data likewise
makes their covariance invariant tautologically. It supplies no estimate
of that covariance and changes the constrained fibers. The TURN70
source/geometry theorem does not cover that augmentation automatically.

5. WHY THE KNOWN SELECTED RG CANNOT SIMPLY BE USED AS THE REPLACEMENT

This issue is already visible at one integration step. Let an actual
full incoming density rho(u) be disintegrated over a coarse value v,
with full positive coarea kernel q^0(du|v). Write

 rho_full,out(v)=integral rho(u) q^0(du|v),
 rho_sel,out(v) =integral rho(u)w(u,v) q^0(du|v),
 p(v)=rho_sel,out(v)/rho_full,out(v).                  (5.1)

Assume positive denominators on the considered fiber. Its original
conditional law is K_full and its w-selected law is

                   K_sel=w K_full/p(v).              (5.2)

There are two different joint measures:

 ordinary selection:
       coarse density proportional to rho_sel,out,
       conditional fine law K_sel;

 coarse-preserving replacement:
       coarse density proportional to rho_full,out,
       conditional fine law K_sel.                  (5.3)

On fibers not treated by (5.2), a hybrid must retain the original
kernel, as in Section1. Choosing K_sel without changing the outer
law is a legitimate probability construction, but it is not the
ordinary selected law. The two coarse marginals agree only when
p(v) is constant almost everywhere on that support.

If one writes the coarse-preserving output using the known selected
density, it is exactly

            rho_full,out(v)=rho_sel,out(v) exp[-log p(v)].       (5.4)

The acceptance function has not vanished. It is now an additional
coarse log-density term -log p, including its scalar and source
responses. If the density is written exp(-S), the corresponding
action relation is S_full=S_selected+log p. In the pure tangent
Gaussian reference, p=c_T^n is
independent of v; that special case explains why its coarse law
is unchanged. TURN72's nonlinear analysis does not establish this
constancy for actual generated constrained fibers.

At the next scale the selected TURN70 recursion uses the particular
incoming selected density and its proven analytic marked class.
Replacing it by rho_full,out inserts the factor in(5.4). Its class
norm, common complex domain, original source derivatives and bad-fiber
continuation have not been bounded by that induction. Therefore the
already proved selected one-mark and conditional covariance maps
cannot simply be iterated with the full marginal.

Equivalently, for a multi-level path one may retain the full terminal
marginal and choose selected backward kernels, but those kernels must
be the conditionals of a SPECIFIED path law at every stage. Kernels
formed from the repeatedly selected incoming densities are generally
not the conditional replacements of the repeatedly full densities.
The missing incoming factor is again(5.4), not a failure of basic
probability normalization.

The actual coarse variable may also lie outside every admitted selected
chart, with p(v)=0. A coarse-preserving hybrid must leave that fiber
unchanged or supply another kernel on the same fiber. A theorem only
on the selected weak domain does not bound those unchanged branches.
An arbitrary Borel completion can define a probability, but supplies
no new small-field/source estimate.

6. SOURCED NORMALIZATION DOES NOT REMOVE THE MISSING RESPONSE

The factors in(1.5) and(5.2) are frozen at zero source. Their sourced
integrals genuinely insert the original observables F,H, which is
why(2.5) and(4.1) are the relevant comparison formulas.

One might instead define at EVERY source a new balancing factor

 M_(s,t)=w/a_Y(s,t),
 a_Y(s,t)= E_mu[w exp(sF+tH)|Y]/E_mu[exp(sF+tH)|Y].

Where this is defined, its exact identity is

 E_mu[M_(s,t)exp(sF+tH)|Y]=E_mu[exp(sF+tH)|Y].          (6.1)

This preserves the tilted coarse marginal by construction. But
derivatives of M_(s,t) insert derivatives of -log a_Y(s,t), including
precisely the one-source and mixed responses in(4.2). Differentiating
(6.1) as though M were frozen would replace the physical source by
a different one. Thus this construction is not an estimate or a
cancellation of the unknown original-source correction.

TURN37 already gives a simple warning about coarse regularity:
p(y)=alpha(1+sin Ny) may have a small positive real range while
the derivative of -log(1-p(y)) grows like alpha N. The almost-everywhere
disintegration and the new real chi-square bound do not assert
uniform holomorphy or locality of(5.4).

7. PRECISE ATTACHMENT STATUS

Actual retained inputs now give:

  * the positive coarse-preserving bare replacement, with full
    exterior/coarse law and raw partition mass preserved;
  * the new exact finite-read chi-square and original two-source
    bounds(2.3),(2.6);
  * the explicit bare Wilson action consequence(3.3), using the
    original normalized energy moments.

They do not currently give:

  * the same product/disintegration/window estimates for every
    generated constrained fiber under chronological full histories;
  * a UV-summable local source norm replacing the extensive l1
    weight and read-core counts in(3.3);
  * control of the conditional mean errors against the full coarse
    law, or control of the new coarse term -log p in the TURN70 class;
  * the physical nonzero witness, RP/full-law continuum identification,
    or a completed YM4 construction.

The useful next target is consequently an original-source estimate
for the SAME full conditional kernels, including their one-mark
coarse response, rather than an estimate of a global no-bad
probability. Coarse-preserving normalization is a valid mechanism;
the present selected hierarchy cannot be identified with its output
without that additional actual model estimate.
