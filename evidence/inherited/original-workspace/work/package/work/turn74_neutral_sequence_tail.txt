TURN74 -- COUPLED NEUTRAL / MARGINAL SOURCE SEQUENCE
20 October 2026
Producer: tail47. Status: proved conditional sequence lemma; native attachment
requirements are stated in Section 7. This is not a full-law source theorem.

0. RESULT AND PURPOSE

For a unit, coupling-free classical action source, a full quantum birth of
size O(g_j^2) can coexist with a physical Maxwell birth of size O(g_j^4).
If the SAME native source class has a contracting neutral classical
propagator and O(g_j^2) quantum linear response, the neutral part remains
O(g_j^2) and its feedback into the marginal is summable O(g_j^4).
The marginal bound follows from the coupled equations; it is not an input.

The result allows signed primitive coupling increments. It does not need
an invariant quotient by arbitrary functions vanishing on the physical
minimizing graph. Such functions remain in the neutral class whose
contraction is an explicit hypothesis.

1. PRECISE FIXED-CLASS HYPOTHESES

Dates are j=0,...,n. Let V_j be normed source-coefficient spaces, with one
fixed prepared norm convention and uniformly bounded maps as below.
For this lemma only deterministic scalar vacua are factored out. Their
actual coefficients remain in the separate raw source ledger. In
particular this convention supplies no bound on a scalar mixed cumulant.

Let beta_j:V_j -> C be a uniformly bounded physical marginal functional,
and let S_j in V_j satisfy

 beta_j(S_j)=1,   sup_j ||S_j|| < infinity.

Set P_j=I-S_j beta_j, N_j=ker beta_j. Assume ||P_j|| <= C_P uniformly.
The real version is obtained by preserving real coefficients throughout.

L_j is the actual linear one-mark map at a fixed unsourced trajectory.
The source does not move g_j, the cutoff domains, or the Gaussian measure.
Suppose

 L_j S_j = S_(j+1) + B_j,
 L_j u   = T_j u + D_j u,       u in N_j.                 (1.1)

Here T_j is the SAME classical map/reference convention, and

 T_j N_j subset N_(j+1),
 ||T_j|| <= M,
 ||D_j|| <= C_D g_j^2,
 ||B_j|| <= C_B g_j^2,
 |beta_(j+1)(B_j)| <= C_b g_j^4.                          (1.2)

The first inclusion is the exact beta-transport assertion on neutral
inputs; for an extension to all V_j it is beta_(j+1) T_j=beta_j, with
T_j S_j=S_(j+1). Only (1.1)--(1.2) are used in the proof.

For one integer q>=1 and kappa<1, assume contraction of every complete
q-block in one fixed aligned partition of the trajectory:

 ||T_(kq+q-1)...T_(kq)||_(N_(kq)->N_(kq+q)) <= kappa.    (1.3)

Single-step boundedness covers the initial/final incomplete pieces of
any interval. One may instead assume (1.3) on all starting dates.
All constants, q, spaces, and norm margins are fixed before choosing
the weak floor. No bound on the source coefficient alpha is assumed.

Write gamma_j=g_j^(-2). In addition to gamma_j>=Gamma, assume the
geometric comparison (3.2) below, or one of its sufficient conditions.
Finally the sum S_* = sum_(j<n) g_j^4 is finite with the quantitative
bound needed in the intended application. Section 5 gives depth-uniform
bounds from a positive block-drift envelope.

2. EXACT COUPLED RECURRENCE AND PERTURBED CONTRACTION

Decompose X_j=alpha_j S_j+u_j, where beta_j u_j=0. Equation (1.1) gives

 alpha_(j+1)=alpha_j + b_j alpha_j
                          + beta_(j+1)(D_j u_j),
 u_(j+1)=A_j u_j + alpha_j f_j,                           (2.1)

 b_j=beta_(j+1) B_j,  f_j=P_(j+1) B_j,
 A_j=T_j+P_(j+1)D_j : N_j -> N_(j+1).

The beta(D_j u_j) term is essential. The O(g^4) birth alone does not
control the marginal if the complete neutral correction is ignored.

Put d_*=C_P C_D/Gamma. Choose the weak floor so that d_*<=1 and

 q (M+1)^(q-1) d_* <= (1-kappa)/2.                       (2.2)

Telescoping a product, replacing one factor at a time, proves

 ||A_(kq+q-1)...A_(kq)-T_(kq+q-1)...T_(kq)||
          <= q (M+1)^(q-1) d_*.

Thus every aligned A-block contracts by kappa'=(1+kappa)/2<1.
Let r=(kappa')^(1/q). Splitting any interval into two incomplete
pieces of length <q and complete aligned blocks gives

 ||Phi_(j,i)|| <= C_Phi r^(j-i),
 Phi_(j,i)=A_(j-1)...A_i,    0<=i<=j<=n.                 (2.3)

For example C_Phi=(M+1)^(2q)(kappa')^(-2), enlarged to at least one,
works. The empty product is included. Neither (2.2) nor (2.3) uses
alpha or u, so this is not a bootstrap assuming the desired conclusion.

3. NEUTRAL FORCING AND SIGNED MICROSTEPS

Let U_0=||u_0|| and M_j=max_(0<=i<=j)|alpha_i|. Variation of constants
in (2.1) gives the sharper, source-dependent estimate

 ||u_j|| <= C_Phi r^j U_0
       + C_Phi C_P C_B sum_(i<j) r^(j-1-i) g_i^2 |alpha_i|. (3.1)

In particular, if

 sum_(i<j) r^(j-1-i) g_i^2 <= C_conv g_j^2               (3.2)

with C_conv independent of n,j, then

 ||u_j|| <= C_0 r^j U_0 + C_1 g_j^2 M_j.                (3.3)

Two sufficient conditions for (3.2) are useful.

(a) Monotone coarseward growth: g_i<=g_j for i<=j. Then
C_conv=1/(1-r).

(b) Bounded signed primitive increments: gamma_i>=Gamma and
|gamma_(i+1)-gamma_i|<=D. Put B=1+D/Gamma. At each step,
gamma_(i+1)/gamma_i<=B, and consequently

 g_i^2/g_j^2 = gamma_j/gamma_i <= B^(j-i).

If rB<1, the left side of (3.2), divided by g_j^2, is at most

 sum_(m>=1) r^(m-1) B^m = B/(1-rB).                     (3.4)

Choosing Gamma once so that rB<=(1+r)/2 makes this constant uniform.
This uses no primitive monotonicity. Positive block drift is needed
for the long sum of g^4, not for this geometric convolution.

4. NONCIRCULAR MARGINAL BOUND

Uniform boundedness of beta and (2.1), followed by (3.3), gives

 M_(j+1) <= (1+C_2 g_j^4) M_j
                           + C_3 g_j^2 r^j U_0.         (4.1)

Define

 S_j=sum_(i<j)g_i^4,      H_j=sum_(i<j)g_i^2 r^i.

Iterating (4.1), and using 1+x<=exp(x), proves

 M_j <= exp(C_2 S_j) [|alpha_0|+C_3 U_0 H_j]
     <= exp(C_2 S_j) [|alpha_0|+C_3 U_0/(Gamma(1-r))].   (4.2)

This is a discrete Gronwall argument with the neutral feedback already
included. Substitution into (3.3) yields the simultaneous estimate

 ||u_j|| <= C_0 r^j U_0
   + C_1 g_j^2 exp(C_2 S_j)
                    [|alpha_0|+C_3 U_0/(Gamma(1-r))].    (4.3)

For the unit reference initial condition alpha_0=1,u_0=0,

 max_(i<=j)|alpha_i| <= exp(C_2 S_j),
 ||u_j|| <= C_1 g_j^2 exp(C_2 S_j),
 |alpha_j-1| <= C_4 exp(C_2 S_j) S_j.                    (4.4)

The last inequality follows by summing the absolute increments in
(2.1), not merely by bounding their maximum. If S_* is small enough,
the real marginal therefore stays within, say, 1/2 of one. This is
only a statement about a coefficient sequence. It is not a nonzero
reflection norm, a positive-energy spectral witness, or nontriviality
of a continuum Yang--Mills theory.

A neutral initial input need not stay marginal-free: beta(D_j u_j)
can generate alpha. Formula (4.2) retains the corresponding initial
transient, of order Gamma^(-1)U_0 in the general bound.

5. DEPTH-UNIFORM SUM AND ULTRAVIOLET TAIL

Suppose

 gamma_j >= Gamma_end + c(n-j) - C_h,    0<=j<=n,
 G=Gamma_end-C_h>0.                                     (5.1)

Use G in place of Gamma in the weak-floor and ratio hypotheses if it
is the available common lower bound. Since the steps run j=0,...,n-1,

 S_n <= sum_(s>=1)(G+cs)^(-2) <= 1/(cG).                 (5.2)

For m>=0, the prefix strictly before the last m coarseward steps obeys

 sum_(j=0)^(n-m-1) g_j^4
       <= sum_(s>=m+1)(G+cs)^(-2) <= 1/[c(G+cm)].        (5.3)

The bound is zero when the indicated prefix is empty. Also

 sum_(j=0)^(n-m-1)g_j^2 r^j
             <= 1/[(G+c(m+1))(1-r)].                   (5.4)

Thus (4.2)--(4.4) are uniform in the initial ultraviolet depth.
For the unit reference input, the total variation of alpha accumulated
in the fine prefix in (5.3) is at most

 C exp[C/(cG)]/[c(G+cm)].                               (5.5)

For general initial data add the transient controlled by (5.4).
This is a coefficient tail, not a comparison of physical source laws
at different regulators.

A positive fixed-block drift with bounded signed microsteps gives an
envelope of the form (5.1). For example, if on every q-step interval
gamma_j-gamma_(j+q)>=b>0 and |Delta gamma|<=D, write n-j=kq+s,
0<=s<q. Then

 gamma_j >= gamma_n + bk - Ds
         >= gamma_n +(b/q)(n-j) -(D+b/q)(q-1).

For drift given only on one aligned block partition, the two end
pieces instead cost another fixed constant. Retain that constant as
C_h; do not replace the signed trajectory by a monotone one.

6. WINDOW TERMS AND CALIBRATION

The native birth in turn74_regular_source_gauge.txt, Sections 5--8,
has the more literal bound

 |beta B_j| <= C g_j^4 + C g_j^2 tau_j,
 tau_j=exp(-c_0 T_j^2).                                 (6.1)

If tau_j<=C g_j^2 uniformly, it is absorbed in (1.2). This follows,
after a fixed weak floor, from
T_j>=a_0[log(gamma_j)]^p with p>1/2. Indeed
c_0 a_0^2(log gamma)^(2p)-log gamma tends to infinity.
The cutoff is frozen during source differentiation.

Without that absorption, the same proof has the Gronwall rate
g_j^4+g_j^2 tau_j, provided the full neutral birth remains O(g_j^2).
Its summability must then be supplied explicitly. An unspecified
small tail is not enough. Extra neutral forcing or finite-period
beta leakage likewise needs its actual convolution and summed
marginal budget; the exact theorem does not silently discard it.

The source normalization here is the literal unit coupling-free S_j.
For a source beta_0 S_original, linearity multiplies alpha_0,u_0,
and the complete one-mark sequence by beta_0. It does not convert the
unit bound (4.4) into a uniform canonically normalized source bound.
Two source derivatives carry beta_0^2. Scalar vacua and the eventual
two-mark collisions still require their own exact bookkeeping.

7. ACTUAL NATIVE ATTACHMENT GATES

The mathematical proof above is complete under (1.1)--(1.3), (3.2),
and the stated coupling sum. It does not establish those native
functional-analytic inputs merely from their names. In particular:

* The fixed prepared source spaces must be the SAME spaces for the
  actual native source lift, classical map, quantum response, beta
  extraction, and repeated re-entry. Shrinking native analyticity
  radii alone give inverse-radius response losses, not (1.2).
* Physical beta transport from the native quadratic calculation must
  match this exact ordinary-minimizer/reference composition. A
  selected-kernel statement cannot supply this identification.
* The q-block contraction has to hold on the complete beta-zero
  native class, including retained off-pair physical-null families.
  Neither a physical rank identity nor beta(Tu)=beta(u) proves that
  nonlinear contraction. The selected TURN70 theorem is not imported
  as a theorem for a different native kernel.
* All one-mark norms, margins, and constants used in (1.2) must be
  fixed before the source magnitude is bounded. Otherwise (4.2)
  would be circular. This lemma concerns the linear jet at a frozen
  unsourced trajectory, not a finite source disc whose radius is
  secretly chosen from a presumed bound on alpha.
* Native regular/healed/raw/boundary chronology, complete original
  reads, mixed-source scalar coefficients, and outer conditional-mean
  covariance are not supplied by a neutral one-mark sequence bound.

Consequently this result narrows the missing native source induction:
an O(g^2) complete birth is compatible with a summable physical
marginal once SAME-MAP neutral contraction and fixed-core response
are attached. It neither removes those attachment gates nor proves
full original-law separated correlations or Yang--Mills existence.
