18 October 2026. Scope: finite-volume fold and placement, fixed beta.

1. DIAGONAL FEATURE AND THE CLOSING TWIST

At an integer diagonal mirror, the only plaquettes crossing its strict
sides are the (1,2) squares bisected through their diagonal. Their two
two-link paths have common fixed endpoints. The displayed real symmetric
tensor feature has inner product exp[(beta/N)ReTr(AB*)] and constant
squared norm exp(beta). Thus the slab operator is Hilbert--Schmidt by
Minkowski on the finite compact link space. Sheet-contained plaquettes
receive two square roots; touching plaquettes stay entirely on one side.
Gluing integrates each shared boundary link once and contracts each
feature once. No independent corner weights or gauge-volume factor occur.

For a height-width b slab, reflection in the upper wall gives
S[Theta F]=U_(bv) S[F]*, with bv an INTEGER normal translation even
when the two boundary sheets have different tangential parity. Keep the
two Hilbert spaces distinct. On a sheared cylinder the original torus
integral is Tr(U_gamma^-1 S_J...S_1), with its actual closing unitary.
Schatten Holder removes that unitary from the bound; it does NOT say
that reflection is positive on the original sheared torus.

For even J, alternately reflected copies of a single slab give
U_((J/2)bv)(S* S)^(J/2). On the STRAIGHTENED torus the closing unitary
cancels exactly, yielding ||S||_J^J. The normal period is (Jb/2)v,
which is integral in every application. This proves the one-axis
comparison with its precise unnormalized partition functions.

2. REPEATED FOLDS REALLY RESTORE THE WILSON GRAPH

After the first one-axis gluing, all its auxiliary boundary variables
have been integrated/contracted and the result is an ordinary Wilson
torus with one weight per plaquette. The next axis therefore starts a
new exact graph classification; it does not split an uncontracted corner
factor again. Orthogonal affine reflections preserve the other height
coordinates and commute. A retained row of cells stays separated into
the next family of slabs. The four Holder exponents multiply to
1/(J_1J_2J_3J_4), with exactly that many original cell factors.

An important potential trap is harmless here: coordinate reflections do
NOT generally preserve Gamma_D. For example reflecting (a,-a) in x1
gives (-a,-a), which is not in Gamma_D. The second fold never assumes
that symmetry. It uses Gamma_D's twisted trace, compares to a NEW
straightened rectangular Gamma_T, and only then reflects there.

3. PERIODS, VOLUMES, AND SOURCE PARITY

For n=2a, the initial diagonal height periods (n,n),(-n,n) give the
brick [0,n) x [0,2n). Straightening the first height replaces the
closing vector n e1 by a(e1-e2), while its parallel period is
n(e1+e2). Thus

 Gamma_D=<a e_-,2a e_+,n e3,n e4>,  covol(Gamma_D)=n^4.

The second diagonal direction and transverse directions need no further
period change. For coordinate height x1, Gamma_D's zero-height subgroup
is <4a e2,n e3,n e4>; the actual closing vector is a e1-a e2. Its
straightening gives

 Gamma_T=<a e1,4a e2,n e3,n e4>,  covol(Gamma_T)=n^4.

With odd h,H,b=qh dividing a and w=b/2, the height counts are

 (n/h,2n/h,n/h,n/h),
 (a/w,4a/w,n/w,n/w)=(2A,8A,4A,4A), A=a/b.

All are even. The fundamental-domain reduction for Gamma_D in the
second frame uses index generators (2A,-2A),(4A,4A), and hence the
zero-first-coordinate generator (0,8A). It is bijective, not just a
volume count. Every original/adapted period is an even-cell translation
of the corresponding reflected array. Odd h therefore causes no
unlicensed normal half-height translation. All copies are made by
actual lattice wall reflections.

Consequently N=2n^4/h^4, N'=2n^4/H^4, N''=n^4/w^4 exactly.

4. PLACEMENT AND THE OPERATOR CUTS

The displayed parity vectors of the coarse image index kappa differ
by at most six in each coordinate, whereas H/w>=16. The index map is
injective. Its compatibility with Gamma_D translations shows that
exactly N' occupied coordinate cells remain after quotienting; no two
different coarse images collide through a period.

There are at most four source-center residues per relevant coordinate.
The half-integer wall grid modulo h/2 has h classes. Each source box
forbids at most 4R_O+2 classes, so h>16R_O+16 suffices. The placement
interval has length at least 2h, and the image-center error is <2h;
the strict cell margin includes that error. Thus neither a source box
nor an insertion box is cut by a cell wall.

Each original clean coordinate link plane maps to a clean coordinate
link plane under a coarse diagonal reflection. In a complete coordinate
fold the inserted cuts have alternating gaps 2d and 2(w-d), positive
integers with sum 2w. The number J of cuts is even. Their integer values
belong to a fixed finite set once h,w,H and the insertion are fixed.
At a cut the distinct spatial boxes have disjoint link sets, so the
tensor insertion norm is at most the product of operator norms; the
physical gauge projection does not enlarge it.

5. PRESSURE AND FULL-ARRAY POWERS

For the first comparison one must use a source copy in EVERY fine
diagonal cell. Each cell's complete fold is then exactly the SAME
array on D_n, and the powers sum to one:

 Z_C(exp(t V_C)) <= Z_D(exp(t V_D)).

This has matching pressure powers at t=0. It is not the erroneous
Z_C(exp(tO))^N comparison. The coordinate torus is invariant under
each lattice signed permutation used to reflect O, so
mu_C(V_C)=N mu_C(O) without assuming its diagonal RP.

After coarse amplification, exactly N' occupied second-frame cells
contribute an operator factor. Their total norm factor after Holder is
||X||^N'. If cell c contains m_c source copies, its complete fold has
N''m_c copies and receives exponent 1/N''. Since sum_c m_c=N, the
unnormalized source cost is exp(t N||O||). The SINGLE original
denominator contributes the same cost. The amplification root is
therefore exp[2t(H/h)^4||O||], as stated.

For a complete coordinate fold with time length T, cross-section Gamma
and gaps l_j, the comparison pressure is
(1/J)sum_j log Z_Gamma(J l_j). Its leading volume is exactly
p_beta |Gamma| sum_j l_j=p_beta n^4. Averaging over all N'' cells
with exponent 1/N'' retains one n^4 volume, not N'' volumes. Every
reference shape has bounded aspect ratios fixed by the finite gap set.
Thus its common fixed-beta pressure error is o(n^4), and the N' root
reduces it to o(1). No uniform ultraviolet estimate is required.

VERDICT. The old finite-volume fold proof survives this new adversarial
audit. The current straight-torus diagonal-RP counterexample does not
refute it: the proof explicitly changes the torus through unitary-twist
Holder bounds, and uses tilt removal plus other arguments only later.
Whether the complete same-state theorem follows still depends on the
separate operator-functional and paired local-reduction steps. Those
steps must not be replaced by a pressure-only argument.
