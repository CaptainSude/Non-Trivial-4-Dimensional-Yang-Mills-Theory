RESULT

It is sufficient to rewrite the COMPLETE physical static insertion
before LII(1.61), and then choose an admitted analytic extension of that
rewritten insertion. The new extension need not equal the previous
canonical Phi extension away from the physical current graph. All
localization, subtraction, scalar/reference bookkeeping and mixed raw
rows must then be constructed coherently from this one new insertion.

This permits a local-r minimum representation if its real physical
identity and full complex admission are proved. It does not authorize
replacing an arbitrary existing off-graph source germ halfway through
an expansion, or keeping old companions from a different extension.

1. EXACT PHYSICAL PREMISES

Fix a finite lattice/cutoff, an actual source-zero geometry/history
sector, its real old variables, and all source-zero transformations and
gauges. Let U_old be the ACTUAL physical field in the static insertion.
Let X^+ contain the complete source field/current read, including the
incident bonds and all collars needed to evaluate the physical current.

Suppose the proposed local operation is

       V_r=M_r U_old,
       U_loc=Min_r(B_loc,V_r; boundary datum),              (1)

with the literal determining tower, boundary datum and gauge. The
required physical identity is, for every allowed real configuration
and every allowed old-real spectator in this sector,

 (U_old,J_phys(U_old))|X^+
       = (U_loc,J_phys(U_loc))^g|X^+.                       (2)

The equality is in the correct source units. A gauge-invariant native
own-field F_i then gives

 F_i(U_old,J_phys(U_old))
        =F_i(U_loc,J_phys(U_loc)).                         (3)

The SAME F_i occurs on both sides. Equality of fields only on nominal
carrier endpoints, equality of coarse averages alone, or equality of
kernel masses does not imply (2). If a more general marked source has
old/private arguments, those complete arguments must also be carried
by the identity; gauge invariance alone does not remove them.

A valid route to (2) is an actual constrained-fiber refinement plus
uniqueness of the same local variational problem. It must show that
the new constraints and fixed fine boundary preserve every original
constraint affected by the variation, using the actual nested
averaging and gauge conventions. Merely placing a box in Omega_r is
not such a proof. B109(3.18) is a native example for its explicitly
compatible minimum/tower; this note does not extend it to every
joined determining array.

2. THE NEW ANALYTIC INSERTION

Let D_new be the domain on which the rewritten insertion will actually
be localized. Construct a holomorphic datum map V_r(z) and the local
minimum/current pair

       K_r(z)=(U_loc(V_r(z)),J_phys(U_loc(V_r(z)))).        (4)

Require the following on the ENTIRE occupied complex domain, uniformly
in all allowed old-real spectators:

 (a) V_r(z), its complete determining bonds, boundary values and
     gauge-return maps belong to the native local-minimum tube;
 (b) K_r(z)|X^+ belongs to the actual original domain of F_i;
 (c) the further localizer/Cauchy substitutions all stay in this tube;
 (d) the gauge/locality/component properties, bounds and spatial/column
     reserves needed by the subsequent native numerator/localization
     module hold there. Mere holomorphicity does not imply these types.

Then define the new extension by composition,

                     F_i,new(z)=F_i(K_r(z)).              (5)

It is holomorphic, and (2) proves agreement with the original physical
insertion on the real physical configurations. B109(3.24)--(3.27) and
Lemma4(3.53) illustrate the kind of complete datum/current admission
needed for (4)--(5). A statement only about the original physical
minimum does not establish (a)--(d). Nor does the old LII(1.65) inclusion
automatically apply to a newly chosen datum map: its value space must
be matched to this new composition.

The old canonical extension F_i,old(Phi(z)) need not equal (5) for
arbitrary independent (U,J). Equality on the physical current graph
does not imply that equality: the graph is not an open subset of the
independent-pair domain. For example, a gauge-invariant holomorphic
function built from (J-J_phys(U))^2 vanishes on the physical graph but
need not vanish off it. This is why an off-graph equality is neither
available by an identity theorem nor required for the ordering below.

3. FINITE-CUTOFF LOCALIZATION IDENTITY

Apply the native auxiliary localizers to (5), BEFORE taking any
absolute coefficient bounds. Their maps and gauges are the fixed

insertion itself; their endpoint at the physical value is (5).

The fundamental theorem of calculus/decoupling identity reconstructs

       F_new(s=1)=sum_nu L_nu F_new,                      (6)

with each L_nu using the SAME chosen insertion and complete determining
read. B116(1.9)--(1.10) is the native model. At finite cutoff the subset
interpolation is finite. Any additional random-walk, kernel or cluster
series must have the native absolute/normal convergence bounds on the
specified contours, so its rearrangement, differentiation and physical

normal convergence of an infinite analytic expansion.

Combining (3), (5) and (6) yields the original physical insertion after
the full localized sum is recombined. Where the native step also
integrates variables, the same assertion holds under the original
source-zero raw measure, with any integrable common continuation test.
It is an identity of the complete source numerator. It does not say
that one localized coefficient from the new expansion equals an old
coefficient, or that the old and new off-graph sums agree.

No action, source-zero minimum, coupling trajectory, physical mask,
normalizing partition function or input probability law is changed.
The local minimum in (4) is part of the source representation. If it
is instead used to modify the source-zero action or a physical map,
that is a different assertion and needs its own exact identity.

4. FIRST AND MIXED SOURCE JETS

On the same physical state write the complete formal source insertion

 W(t,u)=exp(t p+u q+tu r) mod (t^2,u^2)
       =1+t p+u q+tu(r+p q).                             (7)

Rewrite p,q and r by exact physical identities of the form above and
choose their jointly coherent new analytic representatives p',q',r'.
Then use

                 W_new=1+t p'+u q'+tu(r'+p'q').          (8)

On the physical graph (7)=(8), including the raw mixed row. The raw

replace r alone, discard a contact term, or combine p' with an old
off-graph q while silently claiming that all previous localizer
identities remain unchanged. Different source records may have their
own verified local maps, but those maps form one specified joint
insertion and all products use that specified joint construction.

Because the maps are source independent, coefficient extraction
commutes with the linear localization identity and the physical raw

No finite source radius or native smallness slot is inferred merely
from the formal polynomial (8); those are separate quantitative
requirements when an analytic cluster proof consumes it.

The physical source-zero denominator is unchanged. Thus the first
normalized response and the mixed connected response remain

       E[p],           E[r]+Cov(p,q),                    (9)

respectively, and have the same values using the new insertion. This
is not a comparison of old versus new normalized Q kernels at complex

5. SCALAR, REFERENCE, MASK AND COMPANION ORDER

An exact own-field split F_i=v_i+T_i Q_i+Z_i must be evaluated on the
SAME K_r(z) in (5). Its complete Ward dictionary is assembled before
the common linear localizer. The reference and scalar terms are not
left evaluated on the previous Phi while only Z_i is smoothed.
Geometry-dependent scalar values remain actual source rows.

If a claimed neutral cancellation uses several carrier/kernel summands,
they need the SAME complete near read for that cancellation. Separate
termwise physical identities with different local minima may preserve
the total physical expression but do not by themselves preserve its
whole-kernel off-graph Ward cancellation or its quantitative gain.

The same applies to physical carrier masks and completion baselines:
retain the exact complete expression on the chosen new read, then
apply any add/subtract identity with all its companions. This does
not identify different minima associated with different records or
justify a common-reference aggregation across them. Every separate
mask or changed-read companion that the chosen construction creates
must be retained and bounded by its actual consumer.

If a source is already inside a previously localized/collapsed old
kernel, this lemma cannot rewrite just its visible static fragment.
One must either reopen and rewrite the full relevant numerator by a
valid exact identity, or keep the existing extension. In particular
an arbitrary complex source germ with its already fixed companions
cannot be replaced solely because the new germ has the same physical
graph values.

 1. Prove the actual physical constrained-fiber/read identity (2).
