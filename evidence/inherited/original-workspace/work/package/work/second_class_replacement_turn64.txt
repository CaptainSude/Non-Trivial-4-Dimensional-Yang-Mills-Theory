RESULT

The primary raw bound (II1.89) does apply to the complete operator for
EITHER class. It is not, by itself, a normalized probability bound for
second-class components. Their current large-field tests remain outside
the first-class gas, so the fixed-new-field erasure from TURN62 does not
automatically extend to them.

There is nevertheless a new normalized consequence of the actual
forward joint and the now-proved first-class count:

 (A) Weight EACH basic-R replacement record by its conditional
     probability p of producing a first-class outcome. The resulting
     weighted ALL-replacement count has the SAME Laplace bound as the
     first-class count. Conditional Jensen proves this without any
     assumption of independence between output classes.

 (B) On records with p>=kappa>0, all replacements have a local count
     first-moment bound with intensity lambda/kappa. Second-class
     outcomes there have intensity ((1-kappa)/kappa)lambda. The stronger
     mixed factorial/Laplace versions additionally require that every
     COMPLETE class predicate reads only its own fresh reference draw
     and old fixed data. The references factor, but complete-predicate
     read separation is kept explicit pending the exact buffer check.
     A weaker all-birth Laplace bound needs no such premise.

 (C) The native reference Gaussian estimate supplies quantitative
     examples of p near1 when the COMPLETE class test has a verified
     strict margin at its reference center. A current-plaquette margin
     alone is NOT that test: native chi_k includes derived minima.

Thus all replacements on a specified reference-success stratum can
now be controlled without erasing a Y at fixed new current field.
What remains is a normalized estimate for selected records with small
p (or their inverse-success mark in the first-class numerator).
No full second-class or physical continuum bound is asserted.

2. THE ACTUAL PRE-CLASSIFICATION JOINT LAW

At one basic-R date let F_pre contain the complete variables and
positive branch records immediately BEFORE the new reference draws:
the selected eligible component family eta, all old current/private
variables, the admitted exteriors, and the chosen component gauges.
Gauge coordinate changes are lifted with their inverse records.

For a selected geometric label D, define
 J_D=1_{D belongs to eta}.
After the draw let G_D be its native FIRST-class indicator,
 I_D^+=J_D G_D,       I_D^-=J_D(1-G_D).

The superscripts + and - mean class, not time reflection.
The first-class family in TURN62 has occupancy I_D^+.
Define the actual success probability

       p_D=E[G_D | F_pre,J_D=1],                        (2.1)

and put p_D=0 on J_D=0 if a total definition is desired. Every formula
below contains J_D, so this convention has no probabilistic effect.
The probability includes exactly the normalized reference integration,
with no omitted cutoff or normalizer.

The retained Large-I(1.100) expression is the old positive integration
times the normalized reference. Its local reference for D uses the
actual admitted exterior in D and the component's fixed choices.
The component reference integrations factor after those data are fixed.
The class tests introduced in Large-II p378 are the displayed component
factors, before their integrated reverse-FP restoration. For the
product-odds theorem, impose the following additional read condition:

 (PR) The COMPLETE defining predicate G_D reads only the reference
      coordinates freshly drawn in D, and F_pre-measurable data.
      In particular, no derived-minimum read reaches another fresh
      component interior.

Under (PR), the simultaneous branch has

 E[product_(D in A) I_D^+ | F_pre]
        =product_(D in A) J_D p_D,                      (2.2)
 E[product_(D in A) I_D^- | F_pre]
        =product_(D in A) J_D(1-p_D)                    (2.3)

for DISTINCT selected geometric labels. This is independence of the
NEW component reference draws conditional on their complete old
data, not independence of the old histories or old physical fields.
Conditioning on a later selected class/current event generally destroys
these formulas.

Factorization of reference integrations by itself does not establish
(PR). The source's shrink/cube buffers suggest it, but the complete
B119(2.16) deciding footprint must be checked at the actual scales.
This note does not infer (PR) merely from the notation chi_k(D^{~-6}).
If a predicate reads another newly redrawn interior, (2.2)--(2.3)
must be replaced by the actual joint conditional law. The Jensen
theorem and every first-moment conclusion remain valid.

All these identifications are at the admitted positive native-prefix
scope used in TURN60/62. An arbitrary signed localization decoration
is not an event G_D or a positive old record.

3. SUCCESS-WEIGHTED ALL-REPLACEMENT COUNT: NO NEW MARGIN PREMISE

TURN62/63 give, at the same finite date and full normalization,

 E product_(D in A) I_D^+ <= product_(D in A) lambda_D,   (3.1)
 E exp(sum_D t_D I_D^+)
       <=exp(sum_D lambda_D(exp(t_D)-1)), t_D>=0.        (3.2)

Here lambda_D are the deterministic PER-CARRIER majorants obtained
from the complete raw mass, interaction cost and candidate census.
They are not formed by exchanging a supremum and a sum. Their rooted
sum is <=epsilon_k=exp[-c p0(g_k)] at the stated native hierarchy.

For deterministic nonnegative weights omega_D put

 W_+=sum_D omega_D I_D^+,
 W_p=sum_D omega_D J_D p_D.

The literal joint law gives the exact conditional identity

                    W_p=E[W_+|F_pre].                  (3.3)

Therefore conditional Jensen proves, for every t>=0,

 E exp(tW_p)<=E exp(tW_+)
       <=exp(sum_D lambda_D(exp(t omega_D)-1)).          (3.4)

This proof does not use (2.2), conditional independence, a lower bound
on p_D, or a new field-deletion comparison. In particular

 E W_p=E W_+<=sum_D lambda_D omega_D,
 E W_p^2<= (sum_D lambda_D omega_D)^2
                                      +sum_D lambda_D omega_D^2. (3.5)

This is a normalized bound on ALL basic-R birth records, with their
actual success weights, including records whose realized outcome is
second class. It is a new use of the first-class theorem, rather than
a reference-normalizer calculation returning an uncontrolled old mass.

For any bounded ORIGINAL observable F on the passive original record,
positivity immediately gives |E[F W_p]|<=||F|| E W_p.
The count need not be an original-link-measurable event.

4. UNWEIGHTED SECOND-CLASS AND ALL-CLASS COUNTS ON p>=kappa

Fix 0<kappa<=1 and set R_D=1_{p_D>=kappa} on selected records.
R_D is F_pre-measurable. Define
 J_D^rob=J_D R_D,    I_D^{-,rob}=I_D^- R_D.

Even without product independence, conditional expectation gives

 E J_D^rob <=kappa^-1 E I_D^+,
 E I_D^{-,rob} <=[(1-kappa)/kappa] E I_D^+.              (4.1)

Under (PR), for distinct D_1,...,D_q, use (2.2)--(2.3) and the
pointwise inequalities
 R_D<=R_D p_D/kappa,
 R_D(1-p_D)<=[(1-kappa)/kappa]R_D p_D.
After multiplication, expectation, and dropping the R restrictions on
the first-class side, (3.1) gives

 E product_i J_(D_i)^rob <= product_i(lambda_(D_i)/kappa),
 E product_i I_(D_i)^{-,rob}
      <=product_i([(1-kappa)/kappa]lambda_(D_i)).        (4.2)

The weighted Laplace bounds follow by the same finite nonnegative
product expansion as TURN63. For example

 E exp(t sum_D omega_D I_D^{-,rob})
 <=exp{[(1-kappa)/kappa]
                          sum_D lambda_D(exp(t omega_D)-1)}.      (4.3)

For all robust replacements replace the prefactor by1/kappa.
These have no volume-dependent denominator loss and no division by
the tiny raw mass T'_k(D)1. If q_D(G_D^c)<=delta<1 uniformly on a
chosen pre-record stratum, take kappa=1-delta. The second-class
intensity is then delta/(1-delta) times the first-class intensity.

These are normalized birth-date conclusions, with (PR) retained for
(4.2)--(4.3). Multiplying
by a general LATER outer event and asserting the same conditional
probability bound is not justified by this proof. A pre-draw restriction
can be retained in the conditional algebra, but the final unweighted
first-class bound supplies no additional factor equal to the probability
of that arbitrary pre-event.

The no-independence fallback is also explicit: W_p>=kappa W_rob for
the corresponding nonnegative weighted birth count, hence
 E exp(tW_rob)<=exp sum_D lambda_D(exp(t omega_D/kappa)-1).
Its first moment is the same as (4.1), but its higher moment statement
is weaker than the product-reference result(4.2).
It also bounds the robust second-class count by its domination by
the robust all-birth count. That weaker Laplace conclusion is available
without (PR), even though it lacks the improved second-class odds.

There is also a useful simultaneous-array bound WITHOUT (PR). Let
A be any finite collection of distinct labels and choose deterministic
0<kappa_D<=1. Conditional Jensen applies to the full Laplace functional:

 E exp(sum_(D in A) t_D J_D p_D)
       <=exp sum_(D in A) lambda_D(exp(t_D)-1), t_D>=0.  (4.4)

On the event J_D=1 and p_D>=kappa_D for every D in A, the left
exponent is at least sum_D t_D kappa_D. Chernoff optimization gives

 P(all D in A are selected and p_D>=kappa_D)
     <= product_(D in A) h(lambda_D,kappa_D),           (4.5)

 h(lambda,kappa)=1                         if lambda>=kappa,
 h(lambda,kappa)=exp[-kappa log(kappa/lambda)
                               +kappa-lambda]         if 0<lambda<kappa,
 h(0,kappa)=0.

Indeed the minimizing t is log(kappa/lambda) when positive and0
otherwise. In particular h<= (e lambda/kappa)^kappa on the
nontrivial branch. This retains a fractional rare-activity exponent
for every specified robust array even without class factorization;
the same bound applies to the event that all these records realize
second class as well. Converting (4.5) into a summed geometric count
requires a new census of h(lambda_D,kappa_D); one may not replace
that sum by the old sum of lambda_D. For fixed kappa>0 the native
per-carrier exponential bound can be rechecked with its exponent
multiplied by kappa and the same explicit label entropy.

7. THE EXACT REMAINING MASS, NOT AN UNSPECIFIED NORMALIZER

For the robust threshold kappa define
 N_low,S=sum_(D meets S) J_D 1_{p_D<kappa}.
At the same birth date,

 N_all,S=N_rob,S+N_low,S,
 N_second,S=N_second,rob,S
                         +sum_(D meets S)I_D^-1_{p_D<kappa}. (7.1)

Sections3--4 bound the robust terms with local intensity of order
epsilon_k/kappa. They do not bound N_low,S.
This is a precise pre-reference marginal of the actual positive
Wilson/native-prefix joint law, not a guess about a new coarse law.

There is a useful exact inverse-success identity. With zero terms
understood as0, for deterministic nonnegative weights,

 E sum_D omega_D J_D 1_{p_D>0}
            =E sum_D omega_D I_D^+/p_D.                 (7.2)

The right side is a first-class numerator with its actual
inverse-success mark. A bound on the unweighted first-class count
does not bound this singular mark. The selected p_D=0 records form
an additional, explicitly retained term. Higher distinct-label
versions follow from(2.2), under (PR), with the product of inverse
probabilities.

Thus a complete all-replacement bound would follow from a uniform
positive lower success probability, a normalized bound on the
small-success pre-records, or an appropriate inverse-success
marked estimate. The raw mass bound(1.1) alone supplies none of
those normalized statements.

8. DIRECT CONSEQUENCE FOR THE PHYSICAL TRANSFER

Suppose an actual current-field/reconstruction comparison satisfies
 |F_old-F_new|<=sum_D omega_D J_D
at this one basic-R step, with the proper inverse-gauge records.
Then the robust contribution is bounded by

 E sum_D omega_D J_D 1_{p_D>=kappa}
              <=kappa^-1 sum_D lambda_D omega_D.        (8.1)

The second-class part on that stratum has the additional
(1-kappa) factor. The remaining physical error is the explicitly
weighted low-success numerator
 E sum_D omega_D J_D 1_{p_D<kappa}.

If a model-specific influence estimate instead gives
 |F_old-F_new|<=sum_D omega_D J_D p_D, then Section3 controls the
ENTIRE step with no lower success assumption. This is a precise
observable-dependent alternative, not an assertion that the known
physical smear obeys it.

The improved class coverage does not repair the physical engineering
normalization or predictable drift identified in TURN63. It also
does not add arbitrary-date conditional independence or extend the
bound to an undeclared complex source law.

The new actual progress is the success-weighted normalized theorem,
the unweighted first-moment and no-independence Laplace bounds on
success strata, and their combination with the native innovation
estimate on a complete-margin stratum. The sharper product-reference
odds transfer is proved under the explicit complete-read condition(PR).
The full second-class physical error remains localized to
the exact small-success pre-record/marked-numerator problem(7.1)--(7.2).
