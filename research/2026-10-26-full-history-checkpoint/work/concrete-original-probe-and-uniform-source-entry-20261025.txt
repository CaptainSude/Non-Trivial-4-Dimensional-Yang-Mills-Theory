RESULT

There is a simple original bounded positive-time probe with an honestly
UV-uniform INITIAL analytic source norm: a probability-weighted average
of microscopic magnetic plaquette energies. Its normalization is explicit
below. However this probe tends to ZERO in every finite L^p along the
weak bare sequence, by an elementary uniform-volume estimate. It is a
useful exact marked-transport test, not a nontriviality witness.

A plaquette of a field blocked to fixed physical spacing is the better
candidate for a nontrivial physical-resolution source. On its admitted
coarse chart its source norm and source disc are uniform. Balaban's actual
mean/log averaging is only locally defined, so this is not by itself a
globally defined original full-law observable. An explicit Borel fallback
makes a bounded original probe, but the failed-branch/full-history analytic
entry estimate is still missing. Spatial-only flow is another legitimate
positive-time original observable with a different unresolved UV source
estimate. Thin Wilson loops are legitimate originals but real boundedness
does not supply their microscopic complex source norm.

1. AN UNCONDITIONAL UNIFORM INITIAL SOURCE

Let a be the spacing of the ORIGINAL periodic SU(2) Wilson law

 dmu_a=Z_beta^-1 exp[-beta sum_p h_p(U)] product_e dU_e,
 beta=g_a^-2,             h_p=1-(1/2)Tr U_p in[0,2].

Fix f>=0 smooth, compactly supported in a physical box K with time
coordinate at least d>0. Use only spatial 12-plaquettes, based at x_p.
For small a put m_a=a^4 sum_(p parallel12)f(x_p)>0 and

 w_(a,p)=a^4 f(x_p)/m_a,
 O_a(U)=sum_p w_(a,p) h_p(U)/2.                            (1)

This defines ONE original function with 0<=O_a<=1, gauge invariant,
supported in K enlarged by O(a), and using no temporal links. Reflection
maps it to the same construction on the reflected test. Thus it belongs
to the original positive-time observable algebra for small a; no RG or
R transformation is used to assert that support. Any reflection-positive
Gram inequality of the original Wilson law applies to these functions
without changing the law. This does not prove a continuum RP theorem.

The physical calibration is EXACTLY c_(a,p)=a^4 f(x_p)/(2m_a). It is NOT
the original energy calibration c_a=t_R or a renormalized F^2 insertion.
No extra microscopic census may be inserted after these weights.

On a product complex-link chart with ||U_e||,||U_e^-1||<=exp(r_*),
the four-link plaquette word satisfies |h_p|<=1+exp(4r_*). Therefore

 sup|O_a|<=M_*=(1+exp(4r_*))/2,
 sum_p sup|w_(a,p)h_p/2|<=M_*.                            (2)

Each complete initial read contains only its four links and the fixed
native mother/stencil closure. Its carrier/seed price is a fixed constant
C_* independent of a. Consequently the strong initial TOTAL profile norm
is <=C_*M_*, while a pointwise rooted density norm has the corresponding
w_(a,p) factor. Sum the actual original root labels once. The source
ignores J and is holomorphic on the larger complex chart, hence restricts
to every relevant smaller native birth chart without a radius price.
No Euclidean/Reynolds averaging of this specified magnetic probe is used.

The exact ORIGINAL generating function also has a uniform source disc:

 |E_mu exp(z O_a)-1|<=exp(|z|)-1.

It is nonzero for |z|<log2, so its logarithm and every fixed source jet
have UV/volume-uniform bounds on smaller discs. Joint probes have the

2. THIS UNIFORMLY ENTERING PROBE IS TRIVIAL IN THE WEAK BARE LIMIT

Here is a direct volume-uniform bound, requiring no perturbation theory.
Let V be the number of sites, N_b=4V, N_p=6V. For beta>=2 restrict every
link to a Haar ball ||U_e-I||<=c beta^-1/2. Its measure is at least
c1 beta^-3/2 per link. On that event h_p<=C/beta, because a plaquette
is a four-link word and h_p=(1/4)||U_p-I||_HS^2. Thus

                 log Z_beta >= -C V log beta.               (3)

The function phi(beta)=log Z_beta is convex and phi'=-E_beta sum h_p.
The tangent inequality between beta and beta/2 gives

 E_beta sum h_p <= (2/beta)[phi(beta/2)-phi(beta)]
                <= C V log beta/beta,                        (4)

using Z_(beta/2)<=1. Translation invariance makes E h_p equal within
each orientation; positivity of the other orientations then bounds the
chosen orientation by C log beta/beta. Therefore

 E O_a<=C log beta/beta ->0,
 E |O_a|^p<=E O_a ->0             for every fixed p>=1.        (5)

The result is uniform in the periodic volume and the positive weights.
It proves that this particular uniform-entry probe cannot witness a
nonzero limiting field. It is NOT a statement that all bounded probes
or the Yang-Mills theory are trivial. Multiplying (1) by beta destroys
the uniform real bound; nonlinear rescaling may retain a real bound but
requires a new analytic source estimate and is not covered by(2).

3. THIN FIXED-PHYSICAL WILSON LOOPS

For a spatial loop gamma of physical perimeter ell at time>d,
w_gamma=(1/2)Tr product_(e in gamma)U_e is a globally defined original
function, |w_gamma|<=1 and with exact positive-time support. But for
n~ell/a independently perturbed links,

                   |w_gamma|<=exp(n r)                      (6)

on a microscopic complex-link ball of radius r. This scale is real,
not merely a bad upper bound: take a simple loop and choose commuting
imaginary traceless perturbations so each ORIENTED traversal contributes
exp(r sigma3). Then w_gamma=cosh(n r). Real compact boundedness therefore
does not imply UV-uniform holomorphic norms in that chart. A fixed cap
requires r=O(1/n), incompatible with a naive uniform microscopic cap.

This example concerns the independent-link ball. It does not disprove a
better bound on a more restrictive graded regularity chart, or a future
renormalized loop estimate. The initial complete carrier also has order
ell/a microscopic cells, so its native exponential seed weight must not
be discarded. The current source compiler has no proved uniform thin-
loop perimeter renormalization that pays these costs.

4. A NATIVE-BLOCKED PLAQUETTE: GOOD COARSE ENTRY, GLOBAL GAP

Let Q be the ACTUAL ordinary average and consider formally

             O_(a,ell)^reg(U)=[1-tr(Q^m U)(partial p_ell)]/2. (7)

A precise globally bounded candidate is possible. For each elementary

5. SPATIAL FLOW: SAME SUPPORT ADVANTAGE, DIFFERENT OPEN ESTIMATE

Spatial-only Wilson gradient flow in a fixed spatial box at each time
