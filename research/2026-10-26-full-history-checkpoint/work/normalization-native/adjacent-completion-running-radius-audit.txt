ADJACENT PHYSICAL COMPLETIONS: NORMALIZED DERIVATIVE AND RUNNING TUBE
25 October 2026. Independent conditional synthesis audit.

This audits the proposed simplification for adjacent filled-box
completions. It is a finite neighborhood-family statement on running
coarse-link domains. It is not an arbitrary-carrier polymer theorem.

1. ADJACENT PHYSICAL PATH

Use one fixed root, coordinate-tree convention and canonical potential
A(V) on the largest filled box under consideration. All smaller
completions use restrictions of that same potential, not potentials
with changing roots or trees. Let

 Vhat_n=exp(i chi_n A),
 V_s=exp(i[(1-s)chi_n+s chi_(n+1)]A), 0<=s<=1.

The completion proof supplies real physical admission of this path
when n^2 delta is sufficiently small, uniformly in s. For coherent
radial cutoffs, ||chi_(n+1)-chi_n||<=C/n and ||A||<=C(n+1)delta.
Since the two Lie-algebra multiples at each link commute, the right
logarithmic tangent is exactly

 dot B_s=(chi_(n+1)-chi_n)A.

Thus ||dot B_s||<=C delta and its support lies outside the protected
inner Q_n (with the actual fixed cutoff/collar convention).

This is an admitted global physical path for THESE adjacent
completions. It is not a path between an arbitrary original exterior
configuration and its completion.

2. DIRECT NORMALIZED DERIVATIVE

Let mathcal F_t(U,J) be the unframed normalized finite prefix and set
F_t(V)=mathcal F_t(U_t(V),J_phys(U_t(V))). Use its actual source a_F,
the original old density, numerical couplings/cuts and normalizers.

Suppose the birth source has weighted derivative sum <=C eta/tau on
its reserved cavity chart. The registered normalized row transport
then gives

 sum_p exp[mu rho_t(P,p)] ||D_p mathcal F_t||
       <=C(eta/tau)L^-t exp[C_L sum_j delta_j].       (1)

The top physical minimum/current response must have a uniform
weighted row bound in the SAME normalized packet units. B102(190)
is the intended input, with its cell-volume derivative normalization,
the physical-current derivative reconstructed from all required
second-order slots, and a coherent relative Landau representative.
This top conversion is charged once. A raw axial gauge derivative
cannot be silently replaced by that Landau derivative.

If these conditions hold, gauge invariance of mathcal F_t annihilates
the pure-gauge tangent of a coherent real gauge lift. Composition of
the weighted rows with the top response and the support of dot B_s
gives, uniformly in s,

 |d/ds F_t(V_s)|
 <=C eta(delta/tau)L^-t exp[C_L sum_j delta_j] e^-mu n.

Consequently, with f_n=F_t(Vhat_(n+1))-F_t(Vhat_n),

 |f_n|<=eta P(g)e^-mu n,
 P(g)=C(delta/tau)L^-t exp[C_L sum_j delta_j].          (2)

Fixed collar displacements change only C. This proof needs no hybrid
Hamiltonian interpolation. The latter remains useful for arbitrary
two admitted exterior pairs, for which no such physical path exists.

3. FULL REAL COARSE-LINK BALL

For the analytic interpolation, the variables are the coarse
determining links V, not an independent (U,J) pair. Around each real
coarse center with curvature <=delta, use logarithmic raw-link
coordinates of radius a=c delta, with c fixed and small. Their real
cube is full dimensional in these variables. Curvature of every real
point is <=C delta. The completion and physical minimum theorem must
admit all of them with the SAME strict reserves and constants.

Under that verification, (2) holds on the WHOLE real cube. The fact
that its composed fine pair has J=J_phys(U) is harmless here: the
function being continued is a function of V, and all real V directions
in the cube have been covered. It would be invalid to use this as a
full independent-(U,J) real-ball statement.

4. ACTUAL COMPLEX TUBE AND ITS READ COST

Suppose the completed input has O(n^4) owned top packets and its
logarithmic completion derivative is O(n). The top minimum conversion
has its registered bounded two-ended norm. A sup raw-link perturbation
rho therefore has top ell2 size at most C n^3 rho. The sharp unframed
prefix amplification gives the sufficient phase radius

 h_n=c L^-t n^-3 exp[-C_L sum_j delta_j].              (3)

All constants include the actual completion/minimum synthesis and
read counts. A claim h_n asymptotic to sqrt(tau)/n^3 additionally
uses the chosen delay relation L^-t asymptotic to sqrt(tau).

The holomorphic completion, top minimum/current pair and all prefix
images must be admitted on a genuine tube of this radius around the
entire real cube. The old original normalizers are zero-free there,
with each normalized total variation <=2. The cavity cap eta must
hold on the whole complex image. Hence

 |F_t(Vhat_n)|<=2^t eta,
 |f_n|<=Q(g)eta,  Q(g)=2^(t+1).                      (4)

Different local real-reference constructions must glue on overlaps
as the SAME holomorphic gauge-invariant scalar. Equality on the real
coarse-link open set and the identity theorem can prove this once
their domains and original scalar definitions coincide. Merely
possessing separate small charts is not yet a full glued tube.

For all n<=N(g), sufficient quantitative running-range conditions are

 N(g)^2 delta <<1,
 delta N(g)^3 L^t exp[C_L sum_j delta_j] <<1,          (5)

with the small constants chosen to preserve the real and complex
reserves. The second condition makes h_n larger than a fixed multiple
of delta. These are finite-range requirements, not uniform statements
as n tends to infinity at fixed positive delta.

5. NO EXTRA n-DEPENDENT SHRINK AFTER THE LOGARITHMIC START

Assume t=O(log(1/g)), sum delta_j bounded, and delta,tau are fixed
powers of g times powers of log(1/g), so

 B_eff=max(0,log(Q(g)/P(g))) <=C0 log(1/g).

Take the real radius a=c delta and, using(5), a dimension-free slit
interpolation radius R=c1 delta. The previously proved full-tube
lemma gives, on the smaller tube of radius

 r_n=(R/64)[mu n/(mu n+B_eff)]^2,

 |f_n|<=eta P(g)e^(-mu n/2).                         (6)

Choose n0>=C1 log(1/g), with fixed C1 large enough. Then the square
bracket is uniformly bounded below for n>=n0, and a fixed radius
r=c2 delta works in(6) for EVERY n0<=n<=N(g). There is no remaining
n-dependent radius loss. The full-tube lemma includes points whose
real parts lie just outside the original real cube, so subsequent
Cauchy bounds have a genuine reserve.

Each fixed-order Cauchy jet on half this tube costs only C^m m!
delta^-m times the amplitude in(6), with the declared directional
norm/synthesis. It does not pay a coordinate-by-coordinate factor.

Lump the smaller radii into the base coefficient F_t(Vhat_(n0)).
Its true read is the complete filled box O(n0^4), and its proved
complex cap is 2^t eta. Then exactly

 F_t(Vhat_N)=F_t(Vhat_(n0))+sum_(n=n0)^(N-1) f_n.

The original F_t(V) still has the terminal remainder
F_t(V)-F_t(Vhat_N). The adjacent path argument does not estimate that
remainder for a general original exterior. It needs the separate
physical top-minimum/common-shell comparison or another justified
terminal identification.

6. WHAT THIS CLOSES AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

With the stated top-response, full-real-ball and common complex-image
checks, the construction gives a finite own-filled-box coefficient
family on a RUNNING coarse-link domain, independent of a frozen
exterior. Canonical completion depends only on those filled data;
under a real input gauge it changes by root conjugation, so the
gauge-invariant scalar coefficients are intrinsic to those data.

The real decay e^-mu n is distance decay. The full read has volume
O(n^4). It is NOT automatically a bound in a Balaban polymer norm
weighted by exp[kappa d(Y)] with d(Y) of order n^4. Such a conversion
needs the finite cutoff/rare amplitude to pay exp(C N^4), or a separate
thinner complete-carrier expansion. The base term has its actual
O(n0^4) carrier cost too.

The original terminal error, arbitrary connected carrier domains,
selected history masks, raw contacts, position/date/copy census and
all-scale source consumer remain separate. The finite family does
not itself prove the full Yang--Mills existence objective.
