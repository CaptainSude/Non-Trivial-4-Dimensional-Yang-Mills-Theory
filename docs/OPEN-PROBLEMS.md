**Updated 26 October 2026. The full continuum construction is unfinished.**

The target remains a nontrivial interacting local four-dimensional SU(2) Yang–Mills theory at positive renormalized coupling. The [new four-entry checkpoint](../research/2026-10-26-full-history-checkpoint/README.md) is internally reviewed under its named native estimates. It concerns fixed separated reflected energy probes under the original law; it is not a general source or collision theorem.

## Immediate work: a common observable contract

The next construction step is **G3 together with the compatibility part of G4**: specify the observable algebra and contact topology, then obtain the joint estimates needed for one common limiting state. An audit of R11–R15's imported estimates is also useful work; overview labels do not certify their full dependency chain.

The old G1a–G1d cavity route is retained below as a particular alternative interface. The new from-bare source construction addresses what its own checkpoint needs. It does not silently turn every earlier conditional source theorem into an unconditional one.

## Shared requirements

- State the exact original law, calibration, source class, field/current domains, cuts, normalizers and regulator sequence.
- Keep complete source reads, true birth dates, posterior terms, mixed contacts and original root counts.
- Distinguish exact algebra, published inputs, internally reviewed estimates, conditional applications and open claims.
- Link the producer and matching scoped audit; retain corrections rather than changing historical evidence.

## G2 — Full original mixed-history source law

**Reached at bounded scope:** the four separated energy entries retain the original law's histories, normalization, contacts and outer-R operations. See [R15](PROOF-STATUS.md#r15--complete-original-law-four-entry-checkpoint).

**Open beyond that scope:** obtain the joint full-law estimates for all observable collections used in the chosen limiting algebra, including required collision/contact regimes. Preserve retained private operators and history-dependent scalar records. Rare-history mass alone cannot replace marked covariance estimates.

**Definition of done for an extension:** state the source class and topology, match the actual coefficient/domain suppliers, and give uniform joint estimates for that same original law and normalization, with all regulator errors controlled.

## G3 — Observable algebra, calibration, collisions and volume control

**Open; immediate construction task.** The fixed calibration and four separated probes are now specified. A common observable algebra and its collision/contact prescriptions are not.

**Definition of done:** choose the algebra, test classes and topology; define its renormalized products or contact prescriptions; prove the joint and local volume estimates needed for convergence or compactness. The original raw energy's self-variance obstruction remains relevant. A separated covariance bound is not a coincident-point L² estimate.

Read the [new contract](../research/2026-10-26-full-history-checkpoint/work/full-history-four-entry-checkpoint-contract-20261025.txt), [draft, open obligations](../research/2026-10-26-full-history-checkpoint/Proof-draft.pdf), and [Wilson variance audit](../evidence/current/outputs/YM4-Wilson-variance-audit-20261023.txt).

## G4 — Common continuum/infinite-volume state and reconstruction

**Open.** Construct one compatible limiting state or correlation family for the tuned regulators and the algebra chosen in G3.

**Definition of done:** specify the joint cutoff/volume procedure, prove convergence or a common compatible subsequence, and verify consistency, reflection positivity, continuous Euclidean covariance, regularity and locality. Then check the precise reconstruction theorem hypotheses. Separate finite-horizon tunings are not a limiting family.

The [four-entry assembly](../research/2026-10-26-full-history-checkpoint/work/full-history-four-entry-assembly-20261026.txt) provides a uniform witness that must survive this same procedure. It does not supply the compactness or contact bounds itself.

## G5 — Yang–Mills identification, nontriviality and interaction

**Open.** Identify the dynamics of the same reconstructed state as the intended nonabelian Yang–Mills theory and establish genuine interaction.

**Definition of done:** carry a nonzero physical observable/reflection norm into the common limit, prove a stated dynamical identification criterion, and give an interaction criterion with justified contact prescriptions that excludes the relevant free alternatives. The original-law energy witness advances finite-regulator nontriviality; nonlinear observables of free fields can also have nonzero covariance and higher cumulants.

Mass gap, uniqueness, universality and extension to all compact groups are outside the stated target; they are not added here as completion conditions.
