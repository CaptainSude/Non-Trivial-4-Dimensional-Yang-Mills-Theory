12. WHAT A SECOND-MOMENT IMPROVEMENT REALLY REQUIRES

Count moments by themselves do not center physical replacement marks.
Here is an exact finite example. Let I_i be independent Bernoulli(p_i)
and let S be an independent common sign with probabilities1/2. Put
Delta_i=S w_i I_i, w_i>=0. All the count factorial/Laplace bounds hold
with lambda_i=p_i, and each individual mark has mean zero. Nevertheless

 E|sum_i Delta_i|^2
   =sum_i w_i^2 p_i(1-p_i)+(sum_i w_i p_i)^2.            (12.1)

The coherent second term is not removed by individual mean-zero
statements or by count rarity. This finite probability example is a
test of an inference, not a claim about Yang--Mills fluctuations.

There is also a positive exact reduction. Order a finite actual set
of component-update marks, with Delta_i measurable in a filtration
F_i and |Delta_i|<=w_i I_i. Define

 B_i=E[Delta_i|F_(i-1)],    M_i=Delta_i-B_i.

Orthogonality of martingale differences gives

 E|sum_i M_i|^2=sum_i E|M_i|^2
              <=sum_i E|Delta_i|^2
              <=sum_i bar-lambda_i w_i^2.              (12.2)

Only the marginal count estimates are needed for the last step.
But the physical error is sum_i Delta_i=sum_i M_i+sum_i B_i.
The drift has not disappeared. Count control alone gives at most
E|sum B_i|<=sum_i bar-lambda_i w_i. A valid application therefore
needs a quantitative bound/cancellation for this ACTUAL predictable
drift, or a verified zero conditional mean. In the common-sign example,
the first nonzero revealed mark makes later predictable marks coherent.

The normalized native reference is not the old conditional Wilson law.
Consequently subtracting its reference expectation from an old
observable does not automatically create the conditional centering
in(12.2). Keeping the old/reference mean difference is essential.
The martingale correction also need not preserve the desired spatial
support, gauge representation, or physical observable definition.

A useful scaling diagnostic is LINEAR spatial heat smoothing on a
three-dimensional time slice. If a bounded link-angle jump represents

 w_(a,b)<=C a^2 g_*^-1 |grad K_s(x-x_b)|,                (12.3)

where s>0 is a fixed physical smoothing time. This follows simply
from the convolution cell volume a^3 times the connection factor a^-1.
For marginal cell count intensity epsilon and fixed physical support,
Riemann summation gives the distinct scales

 sum_b epsilon w_(a,b)
        <=C epsilon a^-1 g_*^-1 ||grad K_s||_1,
 sum_b epsilon w_(a,b)^2
        <=C epsilon a g_*^-2 ||grad K_s||_2^2.           (12.4)

Finite carrier/collar multiplicities add their explicit polynomial
factors. The first line need not vanish; the second can vanish.
This explains why an observable-specific centered-mark/conditional
covariance estimate could be materially stronger than absolute
oscillation counting.

Equations(12.3)--(12.4) are a linear scaling illustration only.
