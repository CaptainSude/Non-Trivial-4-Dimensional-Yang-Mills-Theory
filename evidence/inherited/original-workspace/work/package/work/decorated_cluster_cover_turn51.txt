DECORATED BOUNDED-POTENTIAL CLUSTERS AND COVER COMPARISON
20 October 2026. TURN51. Root proof.

PURPOSE AND PRECISE STATUS

This proves the cluster step needed for a strengthened version of the
selected-kernel periodic comparison. Its hypothesis is a primitive
potential catalogue with a positive majorant retaining actual lifted
edge/path lengths. Ordinary final-support decay alone does not imply
that hypothesis. Primitive construction and inherited substitution are
checked separately in common_bare_two_volume_turn51.txt.

This result concerns the selected product-ball integral. It says nothing
about the omitted full-Wilson histories.

1. PRIMITIVE CATALOGUE AND THE LENGTH NORM

Let mu be a product probability law on finitely many coordinate blocks.
Coarse complex fields w range over one fixed common polydisc; they are
parameters, not integrated variables. Suppose

       V=sum_(D in catalogue) V_D(w,xi).

Each decoration D has a declared finite connected carrier C_D,
containing every xi and w read and an anchoring site, and a
nonnegative length l_D. That length includes any lifted propagator
path, intermediate vertex connection, or inherited-carrier connection
needed to reconstruct its geometry. Different decorations may have
the same projected carrier and the same functional dependence.
They must not be merged before their length is charged.

Fix kappa>0, delta>0, sigma>0. Assume

 sup_x sum_(D: x in C_D)
    exp[(kappa+3delta)|C_D|+sigma l_D] ||V_D|| <=epsilon, (1.1)
 2epsilon<=delta/4, epsilon<=1/4.

The norm is a supremum over the same coarse polydisc and product
xi support. Matrix/component multiplicities are already included.
Continuous auxiliary labels may be treated with their total-variation
integrals instead of sums, by finite approximation and domination.

The connected carrier can be supplied by a chosen finite path hull.
If a primitive estimate instead weighs read count and path length
separately, its own comparison must pay the added hull cardinality.
For J-cell hulls a path of fine length l visits at most C(1+l/J)
cells; the corresponding exponent must be reserved once.

2. LENGTH IS PRESERVED THROUGH THE NONLINEAR CLUSTER EXPANSION

Conclusion: the analytic branch at zero has an absolutely convergent
decorated connected expansion

   log E_mu exp(V)=sum_Gamma F_Gamma(w),
   C_Gamma=union of primitive carriers,
   L_Gamma=sum_D n_D l_D,

where n_D is the multiplicity of V_D in that term, and

 sup_x sum_(Gamma: x in C_Gamma)
     exp[kappa|C_Gamma|+sigma L_Gamma] ||F_Gamma||
                                                     <=2epsilon. (2.1)

Every Gaussian read, even if repeated in several factors, remains in
the geometry. Closed loops with no far external read are still charged
by L_Gamma. The right side is independent of volume.

Proof. Put a_D=exp(sigma l_D). Expand each factor

   exp(V_D)-1=sum_(n>=1) V_D^n/n!.

The absolute sum with the desired length charge is bounded by

   exp(a_D ||V_D||)-1<=2a_D||V_D||=:w_D,               (2.2)

since a_D||V_D||<=epsilon<=1/4. Thus nonlinear repetition charges
n l_D, not merely l_D.

Expand the product of these factors and group overlapping carriers
into connected families. Independence of disjoint xi supports makes
this an exact hard-core polymer gas. Declaring an overlap of coarse
reads or unused path sites incompatible only groups additional
terms; disjoint declared carriers still imply independent xi reads.
All field parameters w retain their full analytic functions.

The positive overlap-tree majorant, with
b_D=w_D exp[(kappa+delta)|C_D|], has recursion

   T_D=b_D exp(sum_(E: C_E intersects C_D) T_E).

The supersolution T_D<=b_D exp(delta|C_D|) follows from

 sum_(E: C_E intersects C_D)
       w_E exp[(kappa+2delta)|C_E|]
 <=2epsilon |C_D|<=delta|C_D|.

The exponential generating recursion includes unordered offspring
factorials; allowing repeats only enlarges the positive bound.
Since the cardinality of a union is at most the sum of cardinalities,
the length-weighted overlap activities A_P obey

 sup_x sum_(P contains x)
       exp[(kappa+delta)|P|] ||A_P||_(length) <=2epsilon.

Apply the same pinned hard-core cluster bound used in TURN42,
Section4: with comparison activity
r_P=exp(kappa|P|)||A_P||_(length) and a(P)=delta|P|,
the incompatibility sum is <=2epsilon|P0|<=a(P0).
Its positive pinned logarithmic expansion is bounded by exp(a(P)).
Pinning a final log cluster at x and summing over one polymer
containing x proves (2.1).

The entire argument uses positive absolute majorants. Retaining the
individual primitive-power labels throughout makes their total
length additive under every product and expectation. Integration
against a product probability cannot increase a supremum norm.
No cancellation is used to pay a length weight. Finite catalogue
truncations converge absolutely in this norm, proving the statement
for countable catalogues and preserving holomorphy.

The only general cluster theorem used here is the same pinned
hard-core bound already recorded in whitened_local_cluster_turn42,
Section4. The additional length bookkeeping and its nonlinear
multiplicity are proved above rather than inferred from a bound
on final supports.

3. WHAT SHORT-CLUSTER MATCHING REQUIRES

Consider a torus of current period N and its j-fold cover, j>=2.
All local rules and real product laws are the same. Assume the
primitive catalogues have an exact lift correspondence for short
decorated objects, including all operator image labels, integration
variables and symmetry/root factors.

In particular, let the TOTAL geometric length of a connected
primitive family dominate the length of a connected lifted graph
through every read, with its inherited carrier hull. Any inconsistent
identification around a closed path, or any identification of two
distinct lifted reads by reduction modulo N, then costs at least N.

Accordingly, families with total length <N/4 have a unique consistent
root-fixed lift to either torus. Their local coefficients and their
product-law moments agree. The same is true after overlap grouping
and logarithmic clustering. A connected log cluster links its
constituents through carrier intersections; all corresponding paths
must be part of the stipulated geometry.

This is a geometric premise on the actual declared catalogues.
It cannot be justified from the projection of their final support
alone, and it can fail if a global coefficient depends on the period.

Let R_j denote normalized cover restriction, averaging over the j^d
root lifts and restricting the coarse fields to repeated ones.
Assume its carrier norm is nonincreasing, as proved by carrier
projection in the companion note. Short clusters cancel termwise.
Each of the two remaining long-cluster sums is bounded by (2.1).
Consequently

  ||R_j F_(jN)-F_N||_kappa
                         <=4epsilon exp(-sigma N/4).  (3.1)

This is uniform in j and in the finite regulator. Factors associated
with a fixed spatial grouping can be absorbed by changing sigma by
a fixed factor; they must not depend on N or on the number of steps.

4. EVENNESS, COUPLING DERIVATIVES, AND A FIXED ANALYTIC DOMAIN

Suppose the primitive catalogue is holomorphic on one amplitude disc
|z|<r and has (1.1) uniformly there. If its parity is preserved by
the decoration assignment,

  V_D(-z,w,-xi)=V_D(z,w,xi),

and mu is even, then every integrated overlap activity and every
decorated connected logarithmic term is even in z. If V_D(0)=0,
the logarithm and its cover difference both vanish to second order.
Cauchy estimates in the SAME weighted length norm then improve
(3.1), for |g|<=r/4, to

 ||R_j F_(jN)(g)-F_N(g)||
           <=C epsilon (g/r)^2 exp(-sigma N/4).

For gamma=g^-2 this also gives

 ||partial_gamma F_N(g)||<=C epsilon g^4/r^2,

on a smaller fixed subdisc. These are derivatives of the actual
baseline product-ball integral with its real cutoff T fixed during
the analytic argument. They are not derivatives of a cutoff T(g)
or an identification with the frozen-window original-action source.

When applying this statement to repeated selected maps, every field
radius, length exponent, grouping and amplitude margin must be fixed
once. An additional shrinking of the field ball at each step would
not give the claimed arbitrarily deep comparison.

5. APPLICATION BOUNDARY

This theorem supplies the decorated CLUSTER step. A valid selected-map
application must also provide its primitive weighted norm, canonical
short lift correspondence, complete inherited substitution, leading
determinant comparison, and beta extraction comparison.
Those interfaces are addressed in common_bare_two_volume_turn51.txt.

The full Wilson law does not have the independent product-ball
reference assumed here after its omitted histories are restored.
No full-measure pressure, reflection, or continuum conclusion follows
from (3.1) alone.
