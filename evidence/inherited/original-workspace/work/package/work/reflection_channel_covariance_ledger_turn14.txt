REFLECTED CORRELATIONS UNDER POSITIVE CHANNELS: ONLY SHARED NOISE ENTERS
19 October 2026. Root finite-probability theorem; native attachment open.

PURPOSE

The ordinary covariance budget in positive_channel_innovation_ledger_turn14
can be unnecessarily strong for a quantum nontriviality witness. A refill
can create large ordinary variances independently in the two reflected
halves while creating NO correlation across their separating plane. The
correct consumer for an Osterwalder--Schrader (OS) witness is the reflected
cross-covariance. This note proves its exact ledger and a quantitative
criterion which pays only shared refill noise. No property of the actual
Balaban R is assumed without attachment.

1. EXACT REFLECTION LEDGER

Let X_0,...,X_N be actual probability-valued Markov states with transition
kernels K_j and laws mu_j. Each space has an involution theta_j. Suppose
K_j intertwines these involutions: K_j(f o theta_(j+1))=(K_j f) o theta_j.
Define the antilinear reflected function Theta_j f=conjugate(f o theta_j).
The kernels are real probability kernels, so K_j Theta_(j+1)=Theta_j K_j.
Assume mu_0 is reflection invariant; then every mu_j is reflection invariant.

For a finite vector F_N of complex L2 terminal observables, set

 F_j=K_j F_(j+1),
 G_j[a,b]=E_mu_j[(Theta_j F_j^a) F_j^b].                    (1.1)

All G_j are Hermitian, but need not be positive before RP is proved.
The exact conditional product identity gives

 G_N=G_0+sum_j E_j,                                        (1.2)
 E_j[a,b]=E_mu_j { K_j[(Theta_(j+1) F_(j+1)^a) F_(j+1)^b]
                  -(Theta_j F_j^a) F_j^b }.                (1.3)

Proof: integrate the first term against mu_j to obtain G_(j+1), and
the second to obtain G_j, then telescope. Unlike ordinary innovation
covariances, E_j need NOT be positive semidefinite. They are Hermitian
after integration because both adjacent G matrices are Hermitian.

For a fixed unit coefficient vector c, put f_j=sum_a c_a F_j^a.
Then c*E_j c is the integrated conditional cross-covariance of
f_(j+1) o theta_(j+1) and f_(j+1), using complex covariance
Cov(A,B)=E[conjugate(A)B]-conjugate(E A)E B. A bound

 sup_(|c|=1) sum_j |c*E_j c| <= epsilon                      (1.4)

implies ||sum_j E_j||_op<=epsilon. Hence a terminal reflected Gram
G_N>=c_* I transfers to G_0>=(c_*-epsilon) I. To interpret this as
an OS witness for the original field, the backward observables F_0
must actually belong to its positive-half algebra, and X_0 must contain
no auxiliary random seed hidden before the ledger starts. Otherwise
the extra initial disintegration is an additional channel and defect.
This bound controls only the integrated finite reflected Gram. These
signed cross-defects do not have the pointwise positive structure used
to obtain the ordinary ledger's L1 product-defect estimate.

Centering is compatible: the means are constant along the chain, so
subtract the terminal means at every date. This removes the vacuum
constant direction. An ordinary L2 Gram floor is NOT a hypothesis
G_N>=c_* I; Section6 gives the simplest obstruction.

2. PRODUCT REFILLS GIVE A SHARED-INFLUENCE BOUND

Fix the COMPLETE input x and a deterministic selection of refill blocks.
Suppose the output noises Y_1,...,Y_m are independent under K(x,.).
Let osc_i f be the supremum of |f(y)-f(y')| when only Y_i is changed.
For real f,h one has

 |Cov_K(f,h)| <= (1/4) sum_i (osc_i f)(osc_i h).               (2.1)

Proof: expose the independent variables in a fixed order and use their
Doob martingale differences d_i f,d_i h. Cross-dates are orthogonal,
so Cov(f,h)=sum_i E[d_i f d_i h]. At fixed prior variables, the function
of Y_i obtained by averaging later variables has range at most osc_i f.
Its conditional variance is at most (osc_i f)^2/4. Conditional Cauchy--
Schwarz gives the product bound and summation proves (2.1).

For complex f,h, a safe version is

 |Cov_K(f,h)| <= (1/2) sum_i (osc_i f)(osc_i h).               (2.2)

Indeed Var_C(Z)=E|Z-EZ|^2=(1/2)E|Z-Z'|^2 is at most one half the
squared diameter, followed by the same Doob argument. No sharp complex
constant is needed here. Apply (2.1) to real reference spans, or (2.2)
to a complex reference span, with h=f and the first argument f o theta.

Only noises affecting BOTH reflected source functions contribute. In
particular, if f reads a set S_+ and f o theta reads S_-, a refill block
which meets at most one read set has a zero product in (2.1)/(2.2).
If every block has diameter smaller than dist(S_+,S_-), and the output
read sets are literal link sets, all conditional cross-covariances vanish.
The two ordinary conditional variances can nevertheless be large.
Here changing noise Y_i must change ONLY its stated complete output
block. Independent seeds followed by a nonlocal reconstruction do not
automatically meet this locality condition.

These are the actual BACKWARD functions f_(j+1), not fresh bare source
functions at each date. Their full reads include long kernels, selection
data, normalization data and carriers. Geometric separation of only the
nominal source centers is insufficient.

3. RANDOM GEOMETRY AND INTERACTING REFILLS

If a random label L chooses the blocks or refill laws, total covariance is

 Cov(f,h|x)=E_L Cov(f,h|x,L)
             +Cov_L(E[f|x,L], E[h|x,L]).                      (3.1)

The second term can couple opposite halves even when every conditional
block is small. A shared coin choosing whether each half is refilled
provides a simple example. Either retain this term or expose L as its
own actual transition. Conditioning it away is invalid.

Likewise, a factorization of retained integration symbols before
interactions are multiplied does not prove product conditional output
laws. For an interacting refill, (1.2) remains exact, while (2.1) must
be replaced by a proved covariance inequality for that actual kernel.
Its sequential disintegration can have enlarged reads and cannot be
treated as independent small blocks by notation.

4. EXACT HALF-ALGEBRA CRITERION: RP AND AN OS ISOMETRY

For one transition let A_+^out and A_+^in be its output and input
positive-half algebras. Assume three actual properties:
 (a) reflection intertwining as above;
 (b) K maps A_+^out into A_+^in (including ALL input reads);
 (c) K[(Theta f)h]=(Theta Kf)(Kh) for f,h in A_+^out.

Property(c) follows if the reflected output halves are conditionally
independent given the input; shared boundary variables must then be
deterministic at this conditioning or included in a correct boundary
formulation. It does not follow merely from reflection covariance.

If the input law is RP, then

 E_(mu K)[(Theta f)f]=E_mu[(Theta Kf)(Kf)]>=0.                 (4.1)

Thus the output is RP. The backward map K induces an isometry from
the output OS quotient into the input OS completion. Null vectors map
to null vectors by equality of norms. It can erase independent noise
as ordinary random variables while preserving all reflected norms.
This is a sufficient construction criterion, not a claim that all
RP-preserving maps must satisfy (a)--(c).

If (c) is only approximate, (1.2)--(1.4) control a FIXED finite Gram
matrix. They do not by themselves prove positivity on the entire
positive-half algebra. Original bare Wilson RP can supply the ambient
positive form if property(b) and original observable identification
hold; a witness can then be transported within that already positive
form. If property(b) fails, the algebra assignment remains a separate
error even when the scalar covariance defect happens to vanish.

5. PURE GAUGE RANDOMIZATION HAS ZERO DEFECT ON INVARIANT SOURCES

There is one useful case requiring no spatial independence at all. If
a kernel changes a field only within its gauge orbit, then Kf=f and
K(fh)=fh for gauge-invariant f,h. All ordinary and reflected product
defects are exactly zero on invariant sources, even if the random gauge
choice uses shared randomness across the reflection plane. Reflection
of an invariant function is invariant for the usual reflected gauge
action. Pure gauge fixing/restoration can therefore be handled on the
gauge quotient or as a zero-defect substep.

This remains applicable to BACKWARD functions only if later channels
preserve gauge invariance. It does not identify a physical large-field
refill with a gauge transformation: a refill that changes plaquette
conjugacy classes is not orbit preserving. Integration-only equivalence
of two gauge-slice formulas also requires a correct joint/quotient
dictionary before it supplies any non-gauge replacement kernel.
The source must be an actual function of the orbit on the COMPLETE
state. Gauge invariance of its field argument does not erase dependence
on an auxiliary label changed by the same substep; such label changes
must be retained unless the observable is also constant under them.

6. WHY THE CURRENT MINORIZATION IS NOT THE TERMINAL WITNESS

Let the two reflected halves be independent identically distributed
compact variables under product Haar. Any nonconstant centered real
function f of the positive half has E|f|^2>0, and the full measure has
a positive density lower bound. Nevertheless

 E[(Theta f)f]=conjugate(E f)E f=0.                           (6.1)

The state is RP but the strictly half-supported centered functions are
OS null. Therefore the fixed-volume minorization of the RT law proves
ordinary coarse randomness only. It must not be promoted to a positive
reflected Gram matrix. Ordinary positivity order mu>=alpha nu also
does not imply RP order of the remainder mu-alpha nu.

7. REVISED NATIVE TARGET

For a nontrivial OS witness it is sufficient to establish:
 - a terminal centered separated reflected Gram lower bound;
 - actual positive-half support of its backward original-field sources;
 - a small complete sum of their shared-noise defects (1.3), or the
   exact half-algebra factorization in Section4.

This can be weaker than controlling the TOTAL innovation covariance
from every refill. Small independent refills remote from the opposite
read contribute exactly zero; only shared noise, crossing components,
and selection correlations require estimates. Native source long tails
and changing histories may still create those connections. No inspected
primary theorem currently supplies their required bound or the terminal
reflected witness. This is a narrower testable target, not a completed
continuum existence or nontriviality theorem.
