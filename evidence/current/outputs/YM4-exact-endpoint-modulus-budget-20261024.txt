EXACT ENDPOINT MODULUS BUDGET AND ITS ITERATION LIMIT
24 October 2026. audit_two_root. Intermediate proof note; no archive edits.

RESULT

The elementary minimum estimate in the existing endpoint proof admits
a sharper, explicit summation. It replaces an arbitrarily selected
Holder exponent by a quantitative spatial-rate/modulus tradeoff. At
zero spatial output rate it gives a logarithmic modulus loss. At any
fixed positive spatial output rate it still gives a positive power
loss. Neither version is a uniform RG closure. Original-root density,
physical gR, changing reference laws, and their actual admission all
remain in the recursion below.

This is a finite consumer of stated endpoint/shell estimates. It does
not assert that the actual native correction saturates its bound or
that its true regularity deteriorates. A better native joint estimate
could avoid this method's loss.

1. THE EXACT SUM IN THE EXISTING PROOF

Use a d-dimensional bounded-degree lattice with polynomial ball and
shell counts; d=4 in the intended application. F has an actual hybrid
coordinate profile A exp[-lambda d(i,P)]. The existing bounded-
interaction covariance consumer implies, for a bounded function G
supported in B_n(b),

 |Cov(F,G)| <= C A (1+n)^d osc(G)
                    exp[-lambda (d(P,b)-n)_+].                 (1)

Assume the difference of one correction rooted at b has an EXACT
shell decomposition, after removing its noise-independent scalar,
with

 osc(G_(b,n)) <= K min{u exp[-eta d(b,q)],
                         chi (1+n)^(d-1) exp[-eta n]}.         (2)

Here q is the changed determining coordinate. The first cap is its
actual endpoint modulus; the second is the complete current-noise
shell cap. No mixed physical derivative is assumed. The old endpoint
proof has u=T s and chi=gR. For a general input modulus these are
replaced as in Section4.

Choose a FIXED eta_bar<eta, absorbing the displayed shell polynomial
into its reserve. For 0<kappa<=lambda and 2 kappa<eta_bar, (1),(2),
the triangle inequality d(P,q)<=d(P,b)+d(b,q), and the root shell
count give

 sum_(b,n) |Cov(F,G_(b,n))|
 <= C A K exp[-kappa d(P,q)] S_kappa(u,chi),

 S_kappa(u,chi)
  =sum_(r,n>=0) (1+r)^(d-1)(1+n)^d exp[kappa(r+n)]
                min{u exp[-eta_bar r],chi exp[-eta_bar n]}.    (3)

This counts BOTH the correction roots b and their noise shells.
An initial family with density rho of labels per lattice cell costs
rho on the right. All constants in (3) are independent of volume.
The original full support may have extra fixed size powers; they
only change the explicitly finite polynomial powers below.

Write m=min(u,chi), M=max(u,chi), theta=kappa/eta_bar. For positive
u,chi,

 S_kappa(u,chi)
 <= C_(kappa,eta_bar) m^(1-theta) M^theta
                         [1+log(M/m)]^d.                     (4)

When either argument is zero the sum is zero. At kappa=0 separately,

 S_0(u,chi) <= C_eta_bar m [1+log(M/m)]^(d+1).                  (5)

Proof of (4): suppose u<=chi and put N=log(chi/u)/eta_bar.
Split the sum at n=r+N. Below this line the minimum is u exp(-eta_bar r).
The n-sum is at most C(1+r+N)^d exp[kappa(r+N)]. The remaining r-sum
has decay eta_bar-2kappa. Above the line, summing the exponential
n-tail gives the SAME factor u exp(kappa N), times the same polynomial.
This is u^(1-theta) chi^theta(1+N)^d. If u>=chi interchange r,n;
the two polynomial powers differ by one, so the same upper power d
is sufficient. At kappa=0 the lower finite n-sum has power d+1;
the reverse ordering has power d, bounded by the stated d+1.

The constants in (4) deteriorate as kappa approaches zero or
eta_bar/2, or as the earlier reserve eta-eta_bar vanishes. Formula
(5) is a different bound; it does not assert continuity of those
particular constants. Extra support powers can be handled by replacing
d in (4) and d+1 in (5) by the corresponding larger fixed powers.

2. WHAT THIS SAYS ABOUT MODULI AND RATES

For s tending to zero, chi>0 fixed and u(s) approximately s^alpha,
(4) has modulus

 C chi^theta s^[alpha(1-theta)] (1+log(1/s))^d.                (6)

Thus exact summation improves the quantitative budget but does not
remove the power loss when a positive spatial rate is demanded by
this proof. In old lattice units the output rate is kappa. If actual
blocking gives d_old>=L d_new-C, its rate in the new lattice is at
most L kappa before other reserves.

Consequently, if a proposed induction requires a rate floor nu_*>0,
while the available input/noise-shell exponents eta_bar are bounded
above by a fixed nu^*, then it must take

 kappa_j>=nu_*/L,   theta_j>=nu_*/(L nu^*).                    (7)

The orders supplied by this particular proof then obey
alpha_(j+1)<=alpha_j(1-theta_j); a fixed positive order cannot survive
arbitrarily many steps. Choosing summable theta_j instead gives up
the asserted rate floor, unless an ADDITIONAL estimate replenishes
the spatial exponent beyond these hypotheses. A fixed blocking
factor helps a finite step, but does not by itself refute (7).

At zero spatial output rate (5) preserves a power with logarithmic
loss: the majorant is omega(s)[1+log(B/omega(s))]^(d+1) when omega(s)
is the smaller argument. This does not produce a stable concave
modulus at fixed parameter contraction. Indeed if omega is nonzero,
continuous at zero and concave, omega(tau s)>=tau omega(s) for fixed
0<tau<1. The quotient of this TRANSFORMED MAJORANT at tau s by
omega(s) diverges as s tends to zero. This is an obstruction to
closing this majorant in the same modulus, not a lower bound on
the actual normalized response.

Moreover Dini continuity alone cannot transfer an exponentially
localized minimum response into an exponentially localized endpoint
response. For example omega(s)=[log(e/s)]^-p, p>1, is Dini near zero,
but omega(s exp(-sigma r)) decays only polynomially in r. A positive
upper scaling index, such as

 omega(t v)<=C v^alpha omega(t), 0<v<=1, alpha>0,              (8)

or a separate jointly spatial endpoint estimate, is needed for
that composition. Power-log moduli can satisfy weaker versions of
(8) with a rate reserve, but their constants are not uniform when
the logarithmic powers accumulate.

3. A SUFFICIENT MIXED ENDPOINT ESTIMATE, AND WHY IT IS NEW

If instead of (2) one proves the genuinely JOINT bound

 osc(G_(b,n)) <= K u(s) exp[-eta d(b,q)]
                         chi_n exp[-eta n],
 sum_n (1+n)^d exp(2kappa n) chi_n < C,                        (9)

then (1) directly gives C A K u(s) exp[-kappa d(P,q)] with fixed
spare rates and NO change to its modulus. This is a useful exact
interface: a mixed parameter/noise localization estimate supplies
what the two separate caps cannot. An appropriate block version
could carry the physical gR gain in chi_n.

The currently proved first derivative of ell supplies only separate
caps. Its elementary four-corner consequence is

 |Delta_q Delta_r ell_b|
 <=2K min{s exp[-eta d(b,q)],t exp[-eta d(b,r)]}
 <=2K sqrt(s t) exp[-eta(d(b,q)+d(b,r))/2].                   (10)

It is tempting to regard (10) as a stable mixed Holder class. There
is an additional closure issue. For a bilinear interpolation of the
four corner energies S and sources F on the SAME product domain,
ordinary auxiliary u,v differentiation gives the exact identity

 partial_u partial_v E F
 = E F_uv-Cov(F_u,S_v)-Cov(F_v,S_u)-Cov(F,S_uv)
                                      +Cum(F,S_u,S_v).        (11)

These are derivatives in auxiliary interpolation variables, so (11)
is valid for nonsmooth physical endpoints. The term Cov(F,S_uv)
requires the spatial CURRENT-NOISE localization of the mixed
endpoint difference S_uv. If only its amplitude is controlled by
(10), attaching F's distant root again requires a third mixed
parameter/noise difference or a separate joint estimate like (9).
A finite mixed-order hierarchy therefore does not close merely by
writing (10). No analytic or all-orders cluster estimate has been
proved here to remove that requirement.

4. AN EXPLICIT FINITE AMPLITUDE/ROOT RECURSION

This is a conditional consumer, not a native induction theorem.
Assume at grade j an original-label family of root density rho_j,
amplitude k_j, and a normalized concave modulus omega_j(1)=1 on
physical input radius a_j. Write delta_j=rho_j k_j, absorbing the
already paid fixed support norms into that quantity. Require the
small value-interaction hypothesis of the covariance consumer
uniformly for ALL partial insertion coefficients and the auxiliary
endpoint laws. Require complete reference score response S_j, actual
minimum/map response T_j, whole product admission, and the spatial
composition needed for (2). They are not consequences of delta_j.

For a change s in one next determining block define

 u_j(s)=omega_j(C T_j s/a_j),
 h_j=omega_j(C g_j/a_j),
 chi_j=omega_j(C g_j R_j/a_j).                               (12)

Arguments can be capped at a fixed domain-sized number with the
corresponding harmless constant. A concave modulus extended
constantly after that number is sufficient. Its subadditivity gives
omega_j(C g_j t/a_j)<=C(1+t) h_j. Thus k_j h_j is the incoming
test's HYBRID current-noise profile; chi_j is its full-noise cap.
The latter retains the PHYSICAL g_j R_j, not just the radius R_j.

The exact endpoint interpolation and (3)--(4) give the per-original-
label insertion response at a spare spatial rate

 m_(j+1)(s) <= C k_j {u_j(s)+h_j S_j s
                      +delta_j h_j S_kappa(u_j(s),chi_j)}.    (13)

The first term is the direct test change. The second uses the
COMPLETE normalized smooth-reference score. The last is the
covariance against the entire correction difference; its total
root density/amplitude is delta_j. No global total-variation
smallness, derivative of the nonsmooth score, or omitted denominator
is used. All raw flat scalar factors stay in the outer mass, as in
the exact sequential-insertion proof.

To turn (13) into next supported value interactions, telescope over
the NEXT physical Cartesian box, changing each coordinate by at
most C a_(j+1). Root and shell counts give, schematically but with
the actual scale factors displayed,

 delta_(j+1) <= C_geo,rate L^d delta_j
   {u_j(C a_(j+1))+h_j S_j C a_(j+1)
        +delta_j h_j S_kappa(u_j(C a_(j+1)),chi_j)}.            (14)

Here C_geo,rate includes the convergent shell sums and any declared
fixed polynomial support-size norm. The L^d is the actual maximum
number of grade-j roots entering a grade-(j+1) cell; in dimension
four it is L^4. If roots represent an older cohort, its accumulated
density must already be in delta_j. Renaming their sum does not
remove that density. Additional new emissions require an ADDED,
independently proved current-root term and their own cohort census.

For a Lipschitz input, k_j=a_j K, (12)--(13) recover the earlier
finite estimate, now with the exact minimum sum: direct term K T s,
reference K g S s, and correction term proportional to
K^2 g S_kappa(Ts,gR), with the appropriate label density. This
checks the scaling of both g and gR in (13).

The displayed constants must be uniform to use (14) repeatedly.
In particular the coefficient tubes, whole-cut physical margins,
complete smooth-reference gap and score, map response and lattice
conversion are additional later-grade hypotheses. The present
first-three-grade native proofs do not supply that uniform sequence.

5. NUMERICAL FEASIBILITY IS SEPARATE FROM NATIVE CLOSURE

Even granting all reference hypotheses, (14) would need a genuine
linear contraction or an independently stable renormalized sector.
For example if omega(t)=t^alpha and the physical determining ratio
T_j a_(j+1)/a_j is of order L^-1, its direct channel is of order
L^(4-alpha), before the other terms. It does not give a dimension-
four gain. This is a property of this envelope, not proof that the
actual YM correction has that size.

The exact independent-variable example in Section8 of the third-
closure critical review confirms that the abstract assumptions cannot
imply contraction: ell_i(beta)=delta+c beta, B_i=theta/L+g x_i,
N=L^4 independent fine variables give the exact field-dependent
next log ratio L^3 c theta. Positivity, small local values and all
reference gaps hold; the retained scalar is not the cause of growth.
That example is not a gauge-invariant native YM correction.

A numerically sufficient stable recursion would require, for example,
an actual norm with delta_(j+1)<=q delta_j+C delta_j^2+birth_j,
q<1, uniformly controlled source gains and rates, and controlled
birth accumulation. The present estimate establishes no such q.
An initially very small exp[-c(log gamma_0)^(2p)] tail alone does
not pay L^(4T) when gamma_0 is proportional to ultraviolet depth T.

Thus the exact min sum is a usable sharper finite consumer. A real
uniform advance would be a native estimate of type (9), or an
equivalent stable spatial mixed-modulus/smoothing theorem, together
with the dimension-four sector extraction and later-reference
induction. The existing bounded-interaction mechanism is compatible
with such inputs but does not manufacture them.
