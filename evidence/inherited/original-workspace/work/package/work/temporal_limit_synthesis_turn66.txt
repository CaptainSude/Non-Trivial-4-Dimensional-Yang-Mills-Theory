1. A PROVED BRIDGE TO THE ACTUAL NONLINEAR GRADIENT

Fix a finite spatial SU2 lattice, normalized Haar measure, the
Pauli/2 link metric, K=-Delta and a smooth bounded gauge-invariant
potential V>=0. Hold kappa>0 and this spatial model fixed.
For temporal spacing delta set

 beta_delta=2/(kappa delta),
 M_delta=exp(-delta V/2),
 T_delta=M_delta Q_beta_delta P M_delta.

The partition function is exactly
c(beta_delta)^(N|E|) Tr_phys T_delta^N.
No scalar normalizer is omitted. First taking N to infinity gives
the actual Wilson ground-state law, with leading eigenvector
Omega_delta and eigenvalue lambda_delta.

For H=kappa K+V, let Omega be its positive normalized ground state
and E0 its ground energy. The proof establishes

 Omega_delta ->Omega in L2,
 -log(lambda_delta)/delta ->E0,
 (T_delta/lambda_delta)^floor(t/delta)
                            ->exp[-t(H-E0)] strongly,

and, for every fixed real smooth physical observable F,

 lim_(delta->0) E_delta[(F1-F0)^2]/(2delta)
                      =kappa int Omega^2 |grad F|^2.    (1.1)

This last equality controls the genuine nonlinear derivative.
It does not replace it by a deterministic coefficient array.

The finite-spatial proof has three essential estimates.
First, exact electric eigenvalues and the uniform domination

 (I-Q_beta_delta)/delta <=kappa K

give convergence on smooth cores. Second, monotonicity in spin
gives spectral-tail compactness of the ground-state vectors.
Third, the difference between the exact pair form and its
arithmetic--geometric-mean upper bound is

 (4 lambda_delta delta)^-1 int Q_delta(U,V)
            (F(V)-F(U))^2 (w_delta(V)-w_delta(U))^2,
 w_delta=M_delta Omega_delta.

It vanishes by the one-link comparison
h q_beta<=C beta^-1 q_(beta/2), strong convergence of w_delta,
and the fixed finite number of links. No unproved bound on
derivatives of Omega_delta is used.

One may choose delta separately at each spatial cutoff, then choose
a sufficiently large finite temporal period. This approximates any
specified finite set of bounded observable words and the quotients
in (1.1) to a prescribed tolerance. It is a legitimate diagonal
choice, not a spatially uniform temporal convergence rate.

2. A DIRECT HAMILTONIAN ENERGY INPUT

For the usual SU2 normalization on a cubic spatial torus with n sites,

 H_a=[g_a^2/(2a)]K +[4/(g_a^2 a)]S_sp,
 S_sp=sum_(spatial p)(1-Tr U_p/2),

the normalized product trial vector

 psi_t(U)=product_e [exp(t u0,e/2)/sqrt(Z(t))]

gives an explicit upper bound. Sphere integration by parts yields
kinetic energy at most3t/16 per edge. Centrality and the elementary
Gamma-tilt moment bound yield magnetic energy at most6/t per
plaquette. There are3n edges and3n plaquettes, so

 E0(a) <= (n/a)[9g_a^2 t/32+72/(g_a^2 t)].

Taking t=16/g_a^2 proves, for every g_a>0,

 E0(a)<=9n/a,
 E_(Omega_a^2) S_sp <=(9/4)n g_a^2,
 <Omega_a,K Omega_a> <=18n/g_a^2.                      (2.1)

Although the trial vector is not gauge invariant, the full elliptic
ground state is uniquely positive and therefore gauge invariant.
Its energy is exactly the physical ground energy; the variational
bound is legitimate without an uncontrolled gauge-averaging
normalizer.

For the actual spatial flow -4a^-2 grad S_sp, (2.1) also bounds
the expected decreasing action and the time integral of its squared
current-action gradient. These statements concern the action along
the flow. They do not bound the derivative of the final magnetic
probe with respect to its initial field.

The corresponding Wilson regulator is genuinely anisotropic:

 beta_time=4a/(g_a^2 delta), beta_space=4delta/(g_a^2 a).

The energy estimate above is proved directly for its Hamiltonian
limit. It is not borrowed from the isotropic spatial-plaquette
bound, which degenerates when delta tends to zero at fixed a.

3. THE TWO PHYSICAL ESTIMATES NOW HAVE AN EXACT CONSUMER

For a real bounded smooth physical F_a set

 v_a=Var_(Omega_a^2)(F_a),
 D_a=[g_a^2/(2a)] E_(Omega_a^2)|grad F_a|^2.

Integration by parts gives the exact ground-state form

 <(F_a-EF_a)Omega_a,(H_a-E0(a))(F_a-EF_a)Omega_a>=D_a.

Spectral Jensen therefore gives

 Cov(F_a(0),F_a(t))>=v_a exp(-t D_a/v_a).               (3.1)

Thus v_a>=v_*>0 and D_a<=C would preserve a nonzero reflected
correlation at each fixed temporal separation, in any compatible
spatial limit. Equation (1.1) permits approximation by actual
finite-period anisotropic Wilson measures.

Neither estimate is established for the physically calibrated
nonlinear magnetic family. In particular:

 * The energy bound (2.1) does not bound the nonlinear flow Jacobian.
 * A variance bound for a current reference field need not be a
   variance bound for the original observable.
 * Even both estimates would not by themselves supply a continuum
   local algebra, spatial and Euclidean symmetry, unique vacuum,
   Yang--Mills identification, or a positive excitation energy.

The goal remains the complete nontrivial YM4 continuum construction.
This conditional consumer is not substituted for that goal.

4. A PHYSICAL-SIZE VARIANCE FLOOR THAT STILL FAILS REFLECTION

For an edge-simple thin Wilson loop, a set of at least length/19
loop edges shares no action plaquette. The original conditional
law on these edges is an exact product of compact tilted Haar
laws. Fundamental and adjoint representation contractions give

 |EW|<=eta,
 |EW^2-1/4|<=(3/4)eta,
 eta=exp[-c m/(1+beta)], W=Tr U(loop)/2.

These estimates hold conditionally on every exterior. Hence for
fixed physical loop size with a(1+beta_a)->0, Var(W_a)->1/4.
Nevertheless, any bounded test avoiding the selected edges has
vanishing covariance with W_a. In particular,

 E[(W_a(1)-W_a(0))^2]/2 ->1/4,
 centered separated reflected covariance ->0.

This is an actual example of nonzero ordinary variance without
the required nonzero reflected state. It does not apply to
flowed, thick, averaged or appropriately renormalized loops
without a separate argument.

On the literal admitted native replacement branch dJ=d tau q_e,
one can likewise choose a nonconstant current-loop test centered
under q_e. It has positive q_e variance but identically zero
conditional expectation given all original variables. This proves
that marginal preservation plus reference nondegeneracy alone
does not transport the desired variance floor.

5. LIMITS OF THREE NONLINEAR SHORTCUTS

The exact Gaussian temporal source theorem remains valid and has
a nonlinear Fourier consumer for every smooth finite-lattice F.
If nu is an ambient Fourier representation, then

 d(F)<=H_beta(nu)^2/2,
 H_beta(nu)=int sqrt[2(1-exp(-sum_e|b_e|^2/(2beta_e)))] |nu|(db).

However independent-site gauge averaging gives
Var_mu(F)<=C beta_max H_beta(nu)^2 under every probability mu.
Using this absolute norm alone to certify d=O(a) collapses variance
when a beta_max tends to zero. The actual increment may be much
smaller than this sufficient bound; that possibility is not excluded.

A different universal shortcut, [F,[F,T]]<=C T, fails already
for the physical plaquette character F=chi_spin1(W_p)/3 and the
actual Wilson transfer matrix. An explicit high-spin physical
Jacobi block has relative quadratic forms unbounded above.
Those test states are not the actual thermal remainder, so a
state-dependent Wilson bound is not refuted.

Finally, a single smooth background for the actual nonlinear
spatial flow has a derivative spread over order a^-3 links.
Its center-gauge orbit requires log M>=c a^-3 to cover by
fixed relative-error deterministic derivative templates.
This excludes a uniform small-entropy derivative net. It does
not exclude a consumer with a controlled original-law remainder,
because no Wilson mass lower bound for that spread class is
proved. Field-dependent gauge fixing also introduces a real
drift and does not inherit the centered deterministic-source
bound automatically.
