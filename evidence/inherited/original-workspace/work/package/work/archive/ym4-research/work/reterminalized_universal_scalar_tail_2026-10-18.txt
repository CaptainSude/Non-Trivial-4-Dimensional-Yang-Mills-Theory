1. WHICH COEFFICIENT IS BEING ESTIMATED

Use precisely e_j(X;w) in one_run_source_gradient_closure, Section16:
the field-independent value of a UNIVERSAL birth, minus its value at
w=0, recorded once at its actual birth date. This includes regular
births, universal healed R' births, and the corresponding source-gradient
births. It does not include a surviving T component's field-independent
coefficient. Missing-region compensations and history-dependent constants
remain inside the history law. Nothing below changes that convention.

All preceding source estimates are imported at their stated scope. In
particular this note does not independently certify the full one-run
producer. It proves a new consequence of its audited local source ball,
quantitative admission, causality, and uniform local vacuum bound. Unlike
a proposed long-time marked-history estimate, it never needs a normalized
conditional law inside a surviving large-field component.

4. PACKED SOURCES AND ONE LOCAL CAUCHY ESTIMATE

Fix the actual final date N and a fixed buffered region D in its unit
coordinates. In D pack m pairs (f_l,g_l), l=1,...,m, of translated copies
of a fixed separated packet pair. Each profile has amplitude O(1),
diameter O(R_N), and Lipschitz constant O(R_N^-1); all the individual
supports in the collection are disjoint with comparable gaps. Assume

  m >= c_pack R_N^-4.                                  (4)

The packing and any restrictions on allowed translations belong to the
packing lemma. In particular (4) must not silently lose a parameter-
dependent density factor when imposing a scaffold period.

For independent signs epsilon_l in {-1,1}, put

  F_epsilon=sum_l epsilon_l f_l,
  G_epsilon=sum_l epsilon_l g_l.

Disjointness gives uniformly in m and the signs

  ||F_epsilon||_infinity+||G_epsilon||_infinity <= C,
  Lip(F_epsilon)+Lip(G_epsilon) <= C/R_N.               (5)

At date j the same profiles have size
R_j=R_N ell^(N-j), and their union is contained in the rescaling D_j
of D, with |D_j^(+fixed collar)| <= C_D ell^{4(N-j)}.
On the bidisc

  |z_1|,|z_2| < c r_j min(1,R_j),                       (6)

the weight z_1 F_epsilon+z_2 G_epsilon belongs to the terminal-j source
class. Earlier source scales also fit: rescaling a Lipschitz bound from
j to i multiplies it by ell^{i-j}, which is at most chi_i^(j).
All fixed M and lambda_* factors are absorbed into c.

Let c_(j,l)=D_1 D_2 sum_X e_j(X;z_1 f_l+z_2 g_l)|_(0,0).
Cauchy on (6), then sign averaging, gives

 |sum_l c_(j,l)|
 <= C r_j^-2 max(1,R_j^-2)
        sum_(X reading D_j) C_e exp[-kappa_e d_j(X)].    (7)

Indeed E_epsilon epsilon_l epsilon_l'=delta_ll'; differentiation is
bilinear at zero. No logarithm or zero-free radius is used in (7).
The coefficient already IS an analytic vacuum coefficient.

By exact source locality e_j(X;w)=0 when w vanishes at every point read
by X. A nonzero derivative in (7) therefore has a complete source
carrier meeting D_j. Root its tree at such a point and sum the reserved
exponential. This includes carriers extending outside D_j and yields

  sum_(X reading D_j) exp[-kappa_e d_j(X)]
       <= C_D ell^{4(N-j)}.                            (8)

The source tree is measured on the fixed source cells, as in the
locality/anchor convention of Section16; there is no ambient torus-volume
factor. The finite collection of R-domain carrier shapes is included
in that already used rooted tree sum.

Combining(4),(7),(8) proves the averaged bound

 |m^-1 sum_l c_(j,l)|
 <= C r_j^-2 R_j^4 max(1,R_j^-2)
 <= C (1+gamma_j)^(2d_r) max(R_j^4,R_j^2).               (9)

For the late scales R_j<=1, the right side is precisely
C(1+gamma_j)^(2d_r) R_j^2. This is an AVERAGED signed mixed coefficient
bound, not a bound on the absolute value of every translated coefficient.
That is exactly the quantity the independent-sign normalized consumer uses.

5. THE WHOLE LATE UNIVERSAL TAIL IS SMALL

Let s be the weak witness date, gamma=gamma_s, and choose an early
comparison endpoint n_0>s with R_(n_0)<=gamma^-A. Positive macro drift
and bounded primitive oscillations imply

  gamma_j <= gamma+C_q,             s<=j<=N.            (10)

Hence (9), applied to EVERY universal birth after n_0, gives

 |m^-1 sum_l sum_(j>n_0)c_(j,l)|
 <= C(1+gamma)^(2d_r) sum_(j>n_0)R_j^2
 <= C'(1+gamma)^(2d_r) R_(n_0)^2.                      (11)

There is no factor N-n_0: the packet radii decrease geometrically.
Taking A>d_r+1, with any fixed strict exponent reserve, makes(11)
o(gamma^-2)=o(g_s^4). If a polynomial C_e was retained, add half its
exponent to this condition. All these exponents are fixed before gamma
is made large.

For the broad witness packet rho=gamma^B in s-unit coordinates, one
may take n_0-s=ceil(log_ell rho)+ceil(A log_ell gamma), plus the fixed
macro/phase buffer. This is O(log gamma), so the previously established
initial, burn-in, Gaussian, R-deletion, and genuine-new-tail estimates
still apply to the early scalar through n_0. The remaining actual run
to the fixed terminal weak band has length of order gamma, but(11)
does not transport marks or multiply constants for that length.

6. WHAT THIS BYPASSES AND WHAT MUST STILL BE MATCHED

The late estimate treats fresh regular and healed vacuum births alike,
using the same universal coefficient formula on its larger, re-terminalized
source domain. It does not assert that an old R'' descendant is a fresh
stretched-log tail, or that live histories contract like regular marks.
It also does not move history-dependent constants into e_j.

The normalized remainder for the whole packed family is handled separately
by the fixed-cell bound for h=e^-E_UV Z/Z(0), h(0)=1. Its sign-averaged
mixed logarithmic coefficient is h12-h1*h2 at zero, requiring only a
modulus bound on h. Thus no new same-exterior fill or normalized component
denominator is needed by the combination of THAT consumer with(11).

For an actual witness conclusion the early positive c12 comparison must
hold uniformly over the translated pair positions admitted by(4), or
directly for their average. The vacuum coefficients need not themselves
be translation invariant on a fixed scaffold. Bare translation invariance
is used only AFTER the universal vacuum has been restored. A proof that
packs only translations of a very large terminal-compatible scaffold can
lose (4); it cannot be substituted without redoing the count.

The remaining logical dependencies are therefore the audited one-run
producer including its quantitative radius (1), coherent causal universal
vacua, the fixed-cell normalized modulus estimate, and the uniform early
regular comparison. No further lifetime contraction or RF lower filling
inequality is asserted here.
