TURN93 -- A CONTRACTIVE ORIGINAL HEAT PROBE AND ITS UV COLLAPSE
gauge47. 21 October 2026. New finite original-Wilson result.

RESULT

The normalized diagonal heat trace of the original spatial covariant
Laplacian is a bounded gauge-invariant forward observable. Its exact
finite-change influence is bounded by FREE random-walk bridge occupation,
with no exponential nonlinear-flow stability factor. For a physical
spatial smear, each link influence is O(a), their sum is <=6s/a^2.

However this particular unrenormalized probe collapses under the ACTUAL
original Wilson law at fixed positive physical heat time. More precisely,
if spatial periods have physical lengths bounded below, then

 E_W Phi_(a,s)(x) <= C(s/a^2)^(-1/5)
                      +exp[-c s/(a^2(1+beta))].             (0.1)

Constants can depend on s and the positive lower size bound, but not on
the cutoff, beta or a growing spatial volume. Thus a^2(1+beta)->0 forces
its variance, reflected covariance and bounded two-window witness to
vanish. The argument uses many singly traversed edges of a three-
dimensional random-walk bridge and the retained exact conditional Wilson
link damping. Repeated/backtracking walks are not treated as simple loops.

This excludes this specified raw heat probe in the intended logarithmic
coupling regime. It does not exclude renormalized heat ratios, other
gauge-invariant smoothing, native RG, or Yang--Mills. A scalar-shift-
invariant spectral ratio is recorded in Section7 as an unestimated option.

1. EXACT FORWARD OBSERVABLE AND POSITIVITY

Take a finite periodic spatial three-torus with N_i>=4 sites per direction,
lattice spacing a, and original SU2 spatial links on one time slice.
The full probability is the original four-dimensional homogeneous Wilson
law exp[-beta sum_ALL4Dplaquettes h_p]/Z, beta>=0, h_p=1-ReTr(U_p)/2.
Every temporal/spatial period of that original law is at least4.
Let V_3 be the number of spatial sites. On C^2-valued site functions set

 (L_U f)(x)=a^-2 sum_(i=1)^3
   [2f(x)-U_(x,i)f(x+i)-U_(x-i,i)^dagger f(x-i)].          (1.1)

Its quadratic form is a^-2 sum_(x,i)|f(x)-U_(x,i)f(x+i)|^2,
so L_U>=0. Write H_s^U=exp(-s L_U), and let p_s(x,y) be the
scalar heat kernel for U=I. Translation invariance makes p_s(x,x)=p_s(0).

 Phi_(a,s)(x;U)=Tr_C2 H_s^U(x,x)/(2p_s(0)),  s>0.          (1.2)

Uniformization gives a direct path formula. Define the lazy covariant
step Q_U=(1/2)I+(1/12)sum_(six oriented neighbors) U_step T_step.
Then L_U=12a^-2(I-Q_U). With lambda=12s/a^2,

 H_s^U=e^-lambda sum_(m>=0) lambda^m Q_U^m/m!.             (1.3)

The underlying scalar walk stays with probability1/2 and moves along
each oriented edge with probability1/12. Each path contributes its actual
ordered original-link holonomy, with inverse links on reversed steps.
The conditional scalar path law given return to x at time s is denoted
P_br. Equation(1.2) becomes exactly

 Phi_(a,s)(x;U)=E_br[ReTr U(walk)/2].                      (1.4)

The block kernel satisfies ||H_s^U(x,y)||op<=p_s(x,y) by the positive
path weights. Since H_s^U is a positive operator, its diagonal block is
positive semidefinite. Consequently 0<=Phi<=1, even though individual
loop traces in(1.4) can be negative. Gauge covariance of H gives exact
gauge invariance of Phi. It is a function of original links on this slice,
not an auxiliary or new reference field. It reads the whole spatial slice;
its spatial influence can decay, but it has no strict finite spatial read.
At strictly positive times it is admissible for the original temporal RP
form. This is not a common O(4)-covariant local observable construction.

For nonnegative weights w_x with sum_x w_x=1 define phi_s=sum_x w_x Phi_s(x).
A physical smear can have w_x<=C_w a^3. Pure-gauge link fields give Phi=1
exactly. This deterministic calibration is not an original-law response
floor; limits beta->infinity at fixed lattice and a->0 are different.

2. EXACT LOCAL INFLUENCE WITHOUT AN EXPONENTIAL STABILITY FACTOR

For arbitrary original link fields U,V on the SAME slice, Duhamel and
the preceding scalar domination give

 |Phi_s(x;U)-Phi_s(x;V)|
       <=sum_e I_e(x) ||U_e-V_e||op,                      (2.1)

where e=(y,z) is an unoriented nearest-neighbor link and

 I_e(x)=[a^-2/p_s(0)] integral_0^s
   [p_(s-t)(x,y)p_t(z,x)+p_(s-t)(x,z)p_t(y,x)]dt.           (2.2)

Both off-diagonal changed blocks of L are included. The normalized color
trace is bounded by the operator norm, so no missing color factor occurs.
This is a finite-change identity/inequality; it needs neither a weak chart
nor differentiability of a nonlinear flow. I_e is the free bridge's edge
occupation weight. Summing over all unoriented spatial links and using
the scalar semigroup gives

 sum_e I_e(x)= [2s/(a^2 p_s(0))]sum_i p_s(e_i)
                                      <=6s/a^2.           (2.3)

The last inequality follows, for example, from positivity of the scalar
heat operator and Cauchy--Schwarz: p_s(x,y)<=p_s(0).
For I_e^w=sum_x w_x I_e(x), w_x<=C_w a^3, another semigroup contraction
gives the genuinely dimension-free per-edge bound

 I_e^w<=2 C_w s a,
 sum_e I_e^w<=6s/a^2,
 sum_e (I_e^w)^2<=12 C_w s^2/a.                           (2.4)

Thus the sensitivity is polynomial rather than exp(Cs/a^2). It is still
not a uniform summed influence. Using TURN63's existing first-replacement
census with this envelope retains the a^-2 price and requires the actual
same-original-read displacement. No later native reconstruction estimate
is inferred from(2.1).

3. ORIGINAL WILSON DAMPING FOR A WALK WITH SINGLE EDGES

For a closed spatial walk let N_1 be the number of its unoriented lattice
edges traversed EXACTLY once, in either orientation. Select among these
edges a set I with no two in a common four-dimensional action plaquette.
The conflict degree is at most18 (six plaquettes, three other edges), so
|I|>=N_1/19. This selection is deterministic given the scalar walk.

Condition on every original Wilson link outside I. Its conditional density
is exactly a product of tilted-Haar laws with strengths kappa_e<=6beta.
Each selected link occurs once in the walk word. All other segments,
including any repeated/backtracking portions, are fixed under this
conditioning. The fundamental representation integral replaces each
selected link or inverse by its unitary staple center times a scalar
<=exp[-c/(1+beta)]. Therefore

 |E_W ReTr U(walk)/2|<=exp[-c_0 N_1/(1+beta)].             (3.1)

This uses the original full partition function; there is no weak exterior,
selected history, conditional normalizer estimate or independent-plaquette
approximation. It is the TURN66 proof applied ONLY to genuinely single
edges. Applying it to the total length of a repeatedly traversed walk
would be false.

4. MANY SINGLE EDGES IN A THREE-DIMENSIONAL CLOSED WALK

Lemma. Let the lazy walk of(1.3) be conditioned to return after m steps on
a periodic three-torus, with N_min=min_i N_i. Fix C0<infinity and assume
m<=C0 N_min^2. There are q>0 and C=C(C0)<infinity such that, for all
sufficiently large m,

 P_br,m{N_1<q m/2}<=C m^(-1/5).                          (4.1)

The constants are uniform in larger individual periods; no bounded aspect
ratio is needed. Here q can be any sufficiently small fixed lower bound
constructed in the transience argument below.

4A. Finite Fourier estimates and the exact bridge ratio

On this torus the lazy symbol is
 qhat(theta)=1/2+(1/6)sum_i cos(theta_i),  0<=qhat<=1.
On [-pi,pi]^3, 1-qhat is comparable to |theta|^2. Gaussian grid sums give,
for 1<=l<=C0 N_min^2,

 sup_z p_l(z)<=C(C0)(l+1)^(-3/2),
 p_l(0)>=c(l+1)^(-3/2),
 (1/V_3)sum_theta |theta|^2 qhat(theta)^l
                                      <=C(C0)(l+1)^(-5/2). (4.2)

For completeness the upper bounds follow from qhat^l<=exp(-c l|theta|^2)
and the one-dimensional sums bounded by C(l^-1/2+N_i^-1); insertion of
theta_i^2 costs l^-1. Since l<=C0 N_min^2, each N_i^-1 is bounded by
sqrt(C0) l^-1/2. The lower bound follows by lifting walks to Z^3:
p_l^torus(0)>=p_l^Z3(0), and the latter Fourier integral over
|theta|<=c/sqrt(l+1) is >=c(l+1)^(-3/2), with nonnegative integrand.

If k<=m/2 increments, at any specified distinct times, are fixed with
total displacement z in Z^3, their bridge density relative to the product
increment law is EXACTLY p_(m-k)(-z)/p_m(0), with z read modulo the torus.
Evenness of the Fourier sum, 1-cos(theta.z)<=|z|^2|theta|^2/2 and
1-qhat^k<=k(1-qhat) imply

 |p_(m-k)(-z)/p_m(0)-1|<=C(C0)(k+|z|^2)/m.              (4.3)

This controls full finite-increment patterns, not just their marginals.
It is also valid for sets of times crossing the cyclic bridge seam.

4B. Local uniqueness and transience

On the two-sided infinite lazy Z^3 walk, let I_i^R indicate a nonzero
central step whose unoriented edge is not traversed at any other time
within cyclic/ordinary temporal distance R. It depends on at most2R+1
increments. Let q_R=E I_0^R in the two-sided infinite product-increment
law. There is a uniform q_R>=q>0, as follows without a range theorem.

The Green sum G(0)=sum_l p_l^Z3(0) is finite: its Fourier integral has
integrand (1-qhat)^-1, integrable at0 in three dimensions. The probability
of never returning to one's starting vertex after time0 is zeta=1/G(0)>0
by the geometric return decomposition. Given a central nonzero edge,
the independent forward and reversed-past walks each have probability
at least(5/6)zeta to leave their endpoint in one of the other five
directions and never return to that endpoint. Then neither can traverse
the central edge again. Since the central step is nonzero with probability
1/2, q_R>=q:=25 zeta^2/72>0 for every R.

On the torus bridge use cyclic time distance and the same definition.
Take R with 4R+2<N_min and 4R+2<=m/2. Local edge equalities then agree
with the lifted Z^3 window. Equation(4.3) gives

 |E_br,m I_i^R-q_R|<=C R^2/m.

For two disjoint increment windows, their joint density is within
C R^2/m of the infinite product law, uniformly in their positions.
There the two events are independent. At most O(Rm) pairs of windows
overlap, including at the seam. Hence

 Var_br,m[(1/m)sum_i I_i^R]<=C R^2/m.                    (4.4)

4C. Long repeats and the choice of window

Let I_i indicate that the i-th edge is nonzero and is traversed exactly
once in the ENTIRE closed walk. Then N_1=sum_i I_i and I_i<=I_i^R.
If an edge repeats at lag l, its starting vertices differ by either0 or
one unit vector. For |z|<=1 the exact bridge position law is

 P_br,m(S_l-S_0=z)=p_l(z)p_(m-l)(-z)/p_m(0).

For R<l<=m/2, (4.2) bounds this by C(l+1)^(-3/2); the other half is
symmetric. Summing the seven possible z and both halves gives

 E_br,m[(1/m)sum_i(I_i^R-I_i)]<=C R^(-1/2).               (4.5)

Choose R=floor(m^(2/5)). The two window restrictions above hold for
large m, uniformly under m<=C0 N_min^2. The local mean is then at least
7q/8. Chebyshev using(4.4) bounds the chance that the local average is
below3q/4 by C R^2/m. Markov using(4.5) bounds a lost fraction aboveq/4
by C R^-1/2. Their sum proves(4.1). This proof explicitly handles the
torus, cyclic bridge seam and all repeated edges.

5. POISSONIZATION AND ACTUAL ORIGINAL-LAW COLLAPSE

Assume N_min a>=ell0>0, s fixed positive, and a->0. Thus
lambda=12s/a^2<=C0 N_min^2. The continuous-time return bridge has length
law proportional to Pois_lambda(m)p_m(0). Its scalar return probability
is >=c lambda^-3/2, by(4.2) and the Poisson mass near lambda. Hence the
conditional chance of m outside[lambda/2,2lambda] is at most
C lambda^(3/2)exp(-c lambda). On that interval the walk lemma is uniform
with the slightly enlarged C0.

Combine(1.4), (3.1), (4.1), and 0<=Phi<=1. On the good walk event,
N_1>=q m/2>=q lambda/4. The bad event has probability <=C lambda^-1/5.
Therefore

 0<=E_W Phi_s(x)
 <=C lambda^-1/5+exp[-c lambda/(1+beta)],                 (5.1)

after absorbing the exponentially small Poisson tail. This proves(0.1).
The original Wilson lattice may have arbitrary temporal length>=4; the
walk uses just one spatial slice while conditional link integration uses
the FULL four-dimensional action.

For any nonnegative normalized spatial smear, E_W phi_s obeys the same
bound. Since 0<=phi_s<=1, Var(phi_s)<=E phi_s. By Cauchy--Schwarz its
centered reflected covariance tends to0. If G_n is the usual difference
of two time-window averages of phi_s, write the two averages as A,B in
[0,1]. Then (A-B)^2<=A+B gives

 E_W G_n^2<=2 E_W phi_s ->0                              (5.2)

whenever a^2(1+beta)->0, uniformly in the window length. A deficit probe
1-phi_s has the identical centered/two-window collapse. Any fixed
Lipschitz scalar transform also tends to its value at0; an amplification
depending on a is a different source with new sensitivity/calibration
requirements. No ordinary-variance-to-OS lower inference is made.

6. AN EXACT FREE CARTAN DIAGNOSTIC AND THE NORMALIZATION ISSUE

The same effect is already visible in a precisely specified Gaussian
reference; it is not merely a nonlinear-flow instability. Let A_e be
independent-direction real Gaussian link-coordinate fields on the full
four-torus with covariance C=Delta_lat^dagger, where Delta_lat is the
dimensionless scalar nearest-neighbor Laplacian and constants are
removed. Set U_e=exp(i g A_e sigma3/2). This is an Abelian Cartan Gaussian
diagnostic, not the interacting SU2 measure or a proved YM scaling law.

For any closed walk, let J_e be its signed net edge count and J0 its
constant-mode-free part. Commutativity gives exactly

 E_A ReTr U(walk)/2=exp[-g^2 <J0,CJ0>/8].                 (6.1)

Since the nonzero eigenvalues of Delta_lat are <=16,
<J0,CJ0> >=||J0||^2/16. Every singly traversed edge contributes1 to
||J||^2, whereas removing constants subtracts at most m^2/V_4. Thus

 <J0,CJ0>>=[N_1-m^2/V_4]/16.                            (6.2)

In the same spatial physical-size regime, V_4>=4N_min^3 and
m=O(N_min^2), so m/V_4->0. On the typical single-edge event the right
side is >=c m for large m. The same bridge proof therefore gives a
Gaussian expectation bound C lambda^-1/5+exp(-c g^2 lambda). If
g^2 is comparable to1/beta and a^2 beta->0, this diagnostic also
collapses. It retains exact path correlations; it is not an independent
per-step Gaussian approximation.

At g=0 the probe equals1 identically. Its fixed-lattice quadratic response
is obtained by expanding(6.1). Its magnitude is bounded below by a
constant times lambda, using the typical single-edge event; no matching
upper bound is asserted here. Thus a naive small-g expansion is
not uniform at fixed physical heat time lambda=12s/a^2. This identifies
the ultraviolet/perimeter problem which a physical heat observable must
renormalize or cancel. The original-law theorem(5.1), not this diagnostic,
is what excludes the raw bounded probe in the stated Wilson regime.

7. A SURVIVING OPTION IS NOT COVERED BY THE RAW-TRACE PROOF

For the finite spatial covariant spectrum define
 Z_s(U)=Tr exp(-s L_U)>0,
 P_s(U)=Z_(2s)(U)/Z_s(U)^2.
It is gauge invariant, reads the original time slice, and satisfies
1/(2V_3)<=P_s<=1. It is invariant under a scalar shift L_U->L_U+m^2 I,
because numerator and denominator acquire the same factor. Thus the
collapse of Z_s relative to its free value does NOT prove collapse of
P_s: both its numerator and denominator can vanish simultaneously.

For a differentiable Hermitian variation of L, the exact identity is
 d log P_s=2s[<dL>_s-<dL>_(2s)],
 <B>_t=Tr(B exp(-tL))/Z_t.
Consequently |d log P_s|<=4s||dL||op. This elementary bound does not
recover the free local occupation envelope(2.2); the spectral Gibbs
weights now depend on the original field. Localization, degeneracy,
physical-volume normalization and a nonzero original response remain
unestimated. A deterministic flat-reference value is not such a response.
No extensive investigation or successful renormalization of this option
is claimed here.

CONCLUSION

There is a new exact polynomial sensitivity bound for a forward original
gauge-invariant heat observable. There is also a stronger, actual-law
reason that its simplest bounded normalization is not the desired source:
it vanishes because closed diffusive walks carry many independent single
Wilson link factors. The proof is specific to this heat trace and the
regime a^2(1+beta)->0. It leaves renormalized ratios and other forward
observables open, and supplies no nonzero physical channel or full YM4
existence claim.
