1. CYLINDER CUMULANT AND SET GEOMETRY

For bounded real f,g,h the identity

 cum(f,g,h)=Cov(f,(g-Qg)(h-Qh))

is exact. The product's oscillation is at most a fixed multiple
of osc(g)osc(h), and its determining read is the union of their
two reads. Applying the assumed two-cylinder covariance estimate
to each of the three isolations gives decay by the maximum of
the three pairwise minima. For any three nonnegative distances,
that maximum is the middle value; the sum of the two smallest
is the proposed three-set tree length. Thus the factor1/2 in
producer(5) is valid. This argument never requires the false
triangle inequality for distances between sets.

Subtracting Qg or Qh is a scalar operation at the fixed parameter
value, so it does not enlarge the noise read. Nor is independence
of the three factors presumed. Complex-valued tests can be split
into real and imaginary components with fixed constant changes.
