1. WHAT THE EXISTING PREPARATION BOUND HAD DISCARDED

Fix one original pair-only near source, with original record R, born
scale i, preparation grade r>=i, protected source x, complete invariant
coefficient groups and original z/role measures. Write

This is exactly the amplitude in guarded_complete_real_source_cap (1).
In particular A_R does NOT explicitly include the actual proper-r field
amplitude E_r; any factor already in M_i is retained independently.

buffered_static_neutral_preparation, Sections 1--3, gives the complete
near value by using a controlled proper-r frame and a fixed cutoff pair
(A_c,J_c). In born-i units its actual bounds are

 |A_c|<=C E q_s,       |nabla A_c|<=C E q_s^2,
 [nabla A_c]_alpha<=C E q_s^(2+alpha),
 |J_c|<=C E q_s^3.                                          (1)

E=C_beta(epsilon_r+a_r) is supplied by the ACTUAL complete local-read
admission. The original proof bounds each occurrence of E by a fixed
small constant in its Ward argument. We retain that amplitude here.

The two facts used below are part of the existing source definition:

 - The complete own-field source has zero vacuum and zero full pointed
   curvature tensor, with its original whole-kernel Ward identities.
 - EACH near/far coefficient group is an analytic gauge-invariant scalar
   of the pair (U,J), with no unsummed adjoint-valued spectator. Hence its
   first joint pair jet at (1,0) vanishes by global color invariance.

The second fact is essential. A source depending on a fixed adjoint
spectator could contain Tr(B A), and is outside this statement.

2. RADIAL CORE ADMISSION AND A QUADRATIC SCHWARZ ESTIMATE

Let G_R denote the complete near sum with the fixed original geometry
and family prescription, temporarily without the 0/1 selector. For one
admitted endpoint use its completed pair (A_c,J_c) from (1). Choose a
fixed c_*>0, depending only on the ORIGINAL core hierarchy and fixed
cutoff/box constants, so that for every complex z with |z|E<c_*,

              (U(z),J(z))=(exp(i eta_i z A_c), z J_c)       (2)

is in the same born-i core domain, including its derived tests.

Here is why that is a matched core-domain statement, not an assumed
large source disc. The linear potential, first and Holder norms in
(1) scale by |z|. The exponential curvature and covariant-derivative
conversion have additional products bounded by fixed multiples of
(|z|E)^2 times the appropriate q_s powers. The actual q_s<=1 and the
fixed finite cutoff collar permit one sufficiently small c_* to absorb
those products. The independent current scales linearly and is allowed
to differ from the physical current in the cutoff annulus. The smaller
core criterion following B109 (1.16), used in Section 2 of the existing
preparation proof, then supplies all further derived tests. The cutoff
is 1 on every near read, so the endpoint z=1 agrees with the original
near source in its good frame. No kernel/action/normalizer is deformed.

The pointwise frame need not be chosen holomorphically as the endpoint
varies. For this argument the endpoint and its frame are fixed; (2) is
one honest analytic z-path of the fixed original pair germ. Its flat
endpoint is (1,0) in every frame. Pair gauge invariance makes its value
independent of that estimating frame. No current-held spectator is
silently transformed.

Define

 H(z)=G_R(U(z),J(z))-G_R(1,0).

There is a bound on the WHOLE disk, uniform in E and original grades,

            |H(z)| <= C M_i q_s^(4+alpha), |z|E<c_*.     (3)

To prove (3), use precisely the full-Ward/far-completion identity, now
with its flat value subtracted:

 H(z)=[E_(i,x)(U(z),J(z))-E_(i,x)(1,0)]
          -sum_far [E_H(U(z),J(z))-E_H(1,0)].             (4)

Here E_H includes the original native expansion-root and role sums
with their measures. The complex parameter z is independent of those
original root labels. No source dictionary changes.

The first term has zero vacuum and the full neutral tensor. The existing
Ward proof gives C M_i q_s^(4+alpha) because every field bound now has
amplitude at most the fixed c_*. The far group's complete rooted tree
reaches beyond the protected near box, so its summed ORIGINAL majorant
is <=C M_i exp(-c/q_s), at both z and 0. This is <=C M_i
q_s^(4+alpha). The possible near vacuum is NOT assumed separately zero;
it is cancelled exactly by the displayed subtraction. Thus (3) is a
bound for H, not an assertion that a truncated near tensor vanishes.

Each invariant pair-only group has zero first joint jet at the flat
pair. Therefore H(0)=H'(0)=0. The order-two Schwarz estimate on the disk
of radius c_*/E, with E<c_*/2, gives

 |G_R(U(1),J(1))-G_R(1,0)|
             <= C M_i q_s^(4+alpha) (E/c_*)^2
             <= C_* A_R E^2.                             (5)

For E=0 the conclusion is immediate. The analytic z-disc is a FIELD
amplitude device; it does not shrink or replace an original source
parameter radius. All original calibration/radius prices in M_i and
A_R remain exactly where they were.

The comparison works because the complete pair source is gauge

5. THE ACTUAL FINITE CONSUMER AND ITS LIMITS

For an actually matched pair of endpoints, (6) is a deterministic cap if
the native allowances give deterministic E_0,E_1. If they are instead
retained as measurable actual read amplitudes, the valid estimate is

 sd_(mu_(e+1))(1_good c_(e,R))
 <= ||1_good c_(e,R)||_2
 <= C_* A_R[||1_good E_0||_4^2+||1_good E_1||_4^2].        (8)

Here good is the ACTUAL output-measurable qualified condition, all
expectations are under the original physical output law, and the
complement (1-good)c remains in the exact source decomposition. No
conditioning or division by P(good) occurs. A source selector varying
under a later Y-closing operator is still retained there.

Once emitted, this recenter piece can be transported by all later
ACTUAL kernels. The new actual-kernel energy theorem pays all its later
mixed covariances and terminal covariance against an actual source g
by (8) times sd(g at that emission stage). Thus this is an analytic
improvement with a complete finite consumer, not a new static-invariance
assertion. A finite sum of genuinely distinct emissions uses the sum
of their standard deviations, retaining their original labels once.

The E_j in (6)--(8) is the endpoint's ACTUAL proper-r amplitude. It is
not refreshed by renaming r or a retained old noise as the present date.
Where the matched LI domain theorem actually gives
E_j<=C g_e(log gamma_e)^P, one gets a conditional fixed-record cap
C A_R g_e^2(log gamma_e)^(2P). This may be used only on that actual
native sector; the unsimplified E_j bound is the general conclusion.

Even under gamma_e comparable to gamma_T+T-e, the date series
sum g_e^2(log gamma_e)^(2P) need not converge. Original M_i can contain
an additional born g_i^2, but it remains its original price, and no
claim that it controls the full position/date sum is made. In particular
no fixed polynomial coupling gain pays the physical cell density
L^[4(T-e)] by itself. The improved cap has no source-separation factor.
Occupation, cancellation, two-source locality, or another actual
consumer is still needed for a full uniform sum.
