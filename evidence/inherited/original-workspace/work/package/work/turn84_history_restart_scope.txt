TURN84 -- THE FIRST STOPPED/RESTARTED ORIGINAL SOURCE: EXACT SLOT TEST
TURN84. history47. Primary-source scope audit and concrete diagnostic.

RESULT

The finite admitted-prefix source theorem is already present in TURN74
Sections6--7 and TURN78 Sections2--3. Recasting it as a new restart theorem
would not improve the proof. The useful distinction exposed here is more
specific: the native inclusion in Large-II(1.65) controls the old analytic
BACKGROUND arguments (U,J). The live A arguments of the B class are real
integration parameters. An original partially integrated source can use
that inclusion if its restart changes only the former arguments. The
inspected formulas do not establish that condition for every raw slot
which was stopped, retained, and subsequently reconsidered.

For a background-only restart Section3 computes the requested four
integrated coefficients, including the mixed scalar and all cross terms.
It shows exactly which comparison terms follow from an already admitted
old source coefficient. It does not infer those terms from the unmarked
sigma in(1.73), and it gives no better quantitative bound than the earlier
finite typed module.

Section4 identifies the additional read at a genuine live-slot restart.
Section5 computes the cancellation on summing the accepted/rejected
siblings of(1.55). That cancellation removes the NEW cut, but leaves the
earlier cut(1.27). In the final representation the two alternatives need
not belong to the same fixed outer datum or component catalogue. Thus
neither a universal failure nor a successful source-resolved aggregation
is proved by the sibling identity alone. This is not a counterexample to
the native unmarked theorem or to Yang--Mills existence.

 d_f=I10/I00,  d_h=I01/I00,

These are the log jets of THAT integrated original numerator. Equivalently

the mixed scalar is part of d_fh. No scalar is deleted to normalize the
source, and d_fh is not generally zero.

TURN78's first-partial entry applies to a complete original closed trace,

3. THE BACKGROUND-ONLY RESTART COMPARISON, COMPUTED EXPLICITLY

Here is the valid finite comparison when the actual restart read changes
only the analytic background slots. It is a useful test of a proposed
slot dictionary, not a new uniform transport theorem.

Write psi_z for the ACTUAL composite background read(1.61)--(1.65), and
xi for all real private variables, choice labels and live old parameters.
Assume the source families from(2.2) are evaluated on the same old mixed
domains as the corresponding B terms, and that these xi slots stay real
and unchanged as independent slots in this comparison. This is exactly
the background-only case; Section4 discusses the omitted live-slot case.

Let nu(dxi) be the real positive expression used by(1.73), including the
placement of all continuation factors required for the operator under
consideration. At complex z the unmarked density is exp(sigma0(z,xi))nu.
Set d_f(z,xi)=d_f(psi_z,xi), and similarly d_h,d_fh. Then the four raw
coefficients, tested against a remaining bounded test G, are literally

 A00(z;G)=int nu exp(sigma0) G,
 A10(z;G)=int nu exp(sigma0) d_f(z,xi) G,
 A01(z;G)=int nu exp(sigma0) d_h(z,xi) G,
 A11(z;G)=int nu exp(sigma0)
                    [d_fh(z,xi)+d_f(z,xi)d_h(z,xi)] G.   (3.1)

If G itself has a complex external argument, its own admitted norm is
also required; it is not supplied by this formula. For a fixed bounded
real-record test, (3.1) gives holomorphic functions by the actual domain
inclusion and the same dominated integration as the native operator.
There is no source dependence of a reference q or of its denominator:
the baseline has been frozen. All previous normalization derivatives
already present in(2.2) remain in d_f,d_h,d_fh.

For clarity choose the real background z0 in the comparison and write

 m_f=d_f(z0,xi), m_h=d_h(z0,xi),
 m_fh=d_fh(z0,xi)+m_f m_h,
 sigma_f=d_f(z,xi)-d_f(z0,xi),
 sigma_h=d_h(z,xi)-d_h(z0,xi),
 sigma_fh=d_fh(z,xi)-d_fh(z0,xi).                        (3.2)

Substitution in(3.1) gives exactly

 A11=int nu exp(sigma0)G
       [m_fh+m_f sigma_h+m_h sigma_f+sigma_f sigma_h+sigma_fh]. (3.3)

The first-jet formulas contain m_f+sigma_f and m_h+sigma_h. Thus the
comparison coefficients proposed in TURN83 are computable DIFFERENCES
of the actual old typed jets in this case. They are not defined by an
unknown sourced partition ratio, and m_fh has not been replaced by the
product of the first moments.

If their old-domain sup bounds are M_f,M_h,M_fh, the direct bounds are

 |A10(z;G)| <= exp(sup|sigma0|) M_f int nu |G|,
 |A11(z;G)| <= exp(sup|sigma0|)(M_fh+M_f M_h) int nu |G|. (3.4)

The raw mass occurs once and is never divided out. A fixed reserved old
background margin also gives the usual first-difference Cauchy bound for
sigma_f, with the actual read displacement and inverse old radius. A
smallness claim for sigma_f is not needed for (3.4), since the source
variables are nilpotent. Source roots and all actual localization paths
are retained before any carrier norm or spatial sum is taken.

4. THE EXACT EXTRA READ AT A LIVE-SLOT RESTART

Suppose a live argument a of an old sourced coefficient must actually be
re-expressed as a=Phi_H(b) in the later step. Even if every background
read has the inclusion(1.65), the required source value is now

              d_f(psi_z, Phi_H(b), xi_rest),             (4.1)

not d_f(psi_z,b,xi_rest). Their exact difference on real data is

 Delta_live d_f = d_f(psi_z,Phi_H(b),xi_rest)
                              -d_f(psi_z,b,xi_rest).     (4.2)

For a real C1 coefficient it can be written as a real path integral of
D_a d_f against the displacement, whenever that path is in the actual
domain. That is not a holomorphic continuation of(4.1). If the earlier
coefficient contains an old smooth switch s(a), the latter expression
contains s(Phi_H(b)). The published analyticity in BACKGROUND arguments
and the value bound |s|<=1 do not define it at nonreal Phi_H(b).

The first partial integration can make this distinction relevant even
though the original h(U_p) is entire in its four matrix entries: its
conditional numerator also retains the earlier frozen cuts, changes of
variables and their normalizers. They must be integrated or transformed
with the insertion. An entire bare observable is not a proof that the
resulting coefficient is holomorphic in each live real parameter.

Fixed source degree two does not remove this issue. It restricts
derivatives in e,t, but a holomorphic external-background norm controls
arbitrarily high derivatives in that background. A merely Ck live-slot
bound, even for large fixed k and a superpolynomially small H, is not the
same conclusion. The real-source route of TURN81 concerns that different
real norm and should not be silently substituted for the analytic class.

There are three source-level resolutions of(4.1), any one of which would
be substantive: (a) a literal coordinate dictionary showing this a is
always a distinct fixed real inner slot, so later maps act only on its
analytic background; (b) an actual complete-source extension in the
moved a slot on a sufficient common tube, with its norm; or (c) an
integrated source identity eliminating the nonanalytic substitution.
This note proves none for all stopped/restarted histories.

Within ONE basic-R block the new switch of(1.59) is only on the rejected
alternative, which is removed from the subsequent accepted strips.
Moreover old A_j and new B_j are distinct there. Consequently the same-
block accepted sequence is not an example of repeated switch substitution.
The unresolved question is the later reconsideration of the retained
raw proper-scale record. The source's phrase about generalizing stopped
boundary domains, and the final unmarked representation, do not by
themselves display the required source-resolved slot dictionary. Neither
is there an exhibited unavoidable native substitution proving failure.

A plateau argument needs the SAME coordinate and threshold. B119's
switch equals one on an outer shell and zero on an inner ball. A later
accepted small-field condition is imposed after the relative-field and
constraint-flattening changes; it is not automatically a bound on that
earlier switch argument. Thus no constant-plateau simplification is
asserted from the two cut labels alone.

5. WHAT SUMMING THE SIBLINGS REALLY CANCELS

Fix all prior choices and retain a common pre-(1.55) real integral. Let
W(z,b,xi) include its density and remaining factors, C_j(z,b,xi) the
earlier(1.27) cut, and chi(b) the new(1.55) accepted test. Let
M00=1,M10=F_old,M01=H_old,M11=(FH)_old be the CORRECT old four entries,
possibly already partly integrated; M11 is not changed to M10 M01.
On real external data define

 Aab^+(z)=int db dxi W C_j chi Mab,
 Aab^-(z)=int db dxi W C_j(1-chi) Mab.                    (5.1)

Then, for each of the four coefficients separately,

           Aab^+(z)+Aab^-(z)=int db dxi W C_j Mab.        (5.2)

If the rejected term is written after the actual invertible real change
b=Phi_H(c), its Jacobian and EVERY factor, including Mab, must be pulled
back. Undoing that change gives(5.2). Changing the source only on one
branch would destroy this equality.

Equation(5.2) cancels chi, not C_j. To remove C_j one must also include
the sibling rejected earlier at(1.27), and then the corresponding prior
choices if their domains/cuts are to be removed. This is why cancellation
of one new smooth-coordinate correction does not by itself recover an
analytic original numerator on a fixed native component domain.

Nor can(5.2) be applied after fixing incompatible descendant outer data.
The rejected component leaves current Z; the accepted component undergoes
the remaining strips, changes the determining set, and may proceed to
the basic replacement. Large-I201 says the remaining-component T_k
contains the sum over its possible forms, whereas successful component
integrations are factorized separately. Large-II(1.72) fixes a NEW
Omega_k determined by the current U_k and places remaining raw regions,
second-class regions and first-class components in different positions.
Its internal sum(1.71) also acts on the interaction exponential. Nothing
in those displayed formulas identifies all complementary preparatory
siblings with one unchanged T'_X at one fixed descendant outer datum.

At the common ancestor, summing a complete finite family of exact
changes/integrations is of course source preserving. This may be a
useful alternative ORGANIZATION, but its estimate must retain the
common-ancestor variables and all original source coefficients. The
accepted channel cannot simply be added inside a fixed rejected-channel
operator while leaving its outside factors unchanged.

Smallness is equally specific. Summing chi+(1-chi) removes the suppression
attached to THIS rejected test. It need not remove an untouched earlier
primitive bad-field factor. Therefore this audit does not rule out a
common-ancestor aggregation paid by that older reserve. It identifies
what such a proof must actually show: a common analytic sourced ancestor
integral on the final external domain, an unchanged surviving primitive
credit, and the complete support/outer-variable identity after the
aggregation. These are not consequences of the algebraic partition of
unity alone.
