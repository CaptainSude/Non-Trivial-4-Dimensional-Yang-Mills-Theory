(a) A one-witness slot lemma.

                 D_old(c)=M_Bminus(W)^u(c).

Then use M_Bminus(E) as reference. Its difference from D is exactly zero

 |log[D(c) M_Bminus(E)(c)^(-1)]|
       <= C r_c sup_(complete footprint of c) |H|,             (14.1)

with the corresponding fixed complex-adjoint factor on the reserved
