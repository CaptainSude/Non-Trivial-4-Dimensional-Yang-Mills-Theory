1. THE INPUT PROPERTY THAT IS ACTUALLY NEEDED

An incoming coefficient is recorded with its creation date, original
determining-set/history restrictions, full localization domain H,
all native collars, and any private source/noise labels. The value
is an analytic local rule on that recorded data, not just a function
whose external-field argument happens to be supported in H.

For two embeddings of the SAME completed record into compatible
tori/covers, with no identifications inside the record and identical
local field/history data, the rule is the same. Truly common numerical
coupling/beta/cut labels and structural LOCAL normalizations are
frozen and equal in this comparison. Period-dependent flat vacuum
or determinant contributions remain actual local primitive records
through their cancellations; their global finite sums are not assumed
equal. Any chosen canonical scalar subtraction retains its finite
difference explicitly. This is the incoming common-recipe property.
The quantitative input also includes a POSITIVE completed-primitive
majorant with one fixed length charge theta, as described in Section6.
A bound only on already grouped final coefficient values is not
this majorant. Native coefficient norms may be used for an exact
common local core only after its full read has been certified and
its ambient independence proved. The input spatial/activity bounds
are the actual native ones on their original domains.

For B use its unresummed coefficients with full localization domains,
as in B119 printed261(2.41)--(2.42). Do not use the optional p262
resummation into a whole large-field component if it forgets the
internal geometry. Preserve its mixed native domain; this note does
not extend B to the new homogeneous fresh-radius source class.

The property is stronger than equality of numerical g/cut labels.
For example an arbitrary R_P=c_P times a one-cell invariant function
need not have it, despite satisfying every rarity/support bound.
The induction has to construct the actual c_P from the same local
rules, not discard this issue by applying a final norm estimate.

5. WHY THESE ARE COMMON LOCAL RULES, NOT MERELY LOCAL ARGUMENTS

The localization invoked above is the explicit B116(1.6)--(1.10)
construction. Its propagators are expanded using B99(3.107), with
each factor a local Dirichlet operator on a union of a fixed small
number of localization cubes, with its enlarged dependency collar.
B99(3.98)--(3.108) constructs these factors; B116 printed3 states
their dependence and kernel-support properties. These matrices and
their boundary conditions are identical under an injective lift.

B116 attaches s(Delta) to EVERY cube meeting the FULL union of these
factor domains. With s=0 outside a component, all operator kernels
vanish between different components. Its nonlinear constraint and
minimum equations then separate exactly on those components; the
solution vanishes in the boundary cushion and is the same unique
small solution of the same local equations. This is proved on
printed4--5, before (1.10). It is not localization by setting only
external-field arguments outside the component to a reference value.

Consequently, before regrouping terms, keep each Dirichlet factor
domain, each scalar derivative label, each inherited carrier, and
every averaging/return/current path. On an injective completed read,
each ingredient is the same by the incoming property and the local
matrix/solution rule. Products, scalar derivatives, local integrals,
the exact denominator, and local history sums preserve equality.
Summing equal absolutely convergent terms preserves it as well.

Local Dirichlet inverses may be retained as exact common local
functions once this has been established. They have no unrecorded
periodic image winding outside their own domain. One may NOT perform
that resummation on an ambient global Green kernel and retain only
its endpoints. For any still-periodic image edge or trace loop keep
its displacement and domains until it has either a unique short
lift or a charged long/winding record. Distinct private noise sites
are likewise not identified until their actual periodic incidence
has been recorded. These rules do not change the coefficient sum.

(b) Nonlinear localization. Use the same slightly strengthened

(c) The actual three-map reconstruction. Large-II printed376(1.66)

(d) Large cores: a DISTINCT-COMPONENT charge. The metric d_(k,Z) omits the inside of Z. It must

This span is the geometry of the ENTIRE current-coordinate union

8. BOUNDARY FILING AND WHAT THIS DOES NOT CLOSE

At printed390(1.98)--(1.100), connected records meeting the retained
large region or a Y_i are filed in B'; disjoint records form R'.
Only for the second class does the relative metric become the
full d_k(X) and the rare factor exp(-p0(g_k)) appear. The exact
RT action is the one reconstructed in(1.101), based on A'_k.
The new connected R' is not added relative to an unreconstructed
T action without its explicit local additions/subtractions.

The same local-rule equality holds for completed boundary records
on their actual native mixed domains. Their norm uses the native
boundary bookkeeping. No global torus-decay assertion for an
arbitrary fixed large boundary region follows from the bulk bound.
In particular one cannot erase B when feeding the next induction.

At one step this proves preservation of the incoming common-recipe
property, with an explicit positive complete-read reserve, for the
ACTUAL Large-II reconstruction/exponentiation. The local Wilson
denominator and its normalization are part of this proof. There
is no extra ambient translation average or full inherited-action
denominator hidden in the final R' primitive.

To complete the cross-period construction one must still supply
the incoming property for the actual ordinary-T E/R''/B catalogues
and close the joint induction from the bare local action. Common
counterterm labels should be chosen by that common primitive
construction, or their actual differences must remain explicit
marginal inputs. Freezing numbers in a comparison is not a proof
that an arbitrary foreign finite RG history is admissible.

If the incoming coefficients only satisfy numerical native bounds,
this note yields no matching: their mismatch propagates through
the same positive one-mark local comparison. That alternative is
the epsilon_in term in the finite-affine audit, not zero.
