1. THE COMMON SOURCE FUNCTION AND ITS FIXED-CELL BOUND

Fix the final weak stopping band [Gamma_*,Gamma_*+C_q] FIRST. Keep one
source-free trajectory, its frozen maps/thresholds/reference denominators,
and the common local vacuum family of one_run_source_gradient_closure,
Section16. At a finite regulator put

 h(v)=exp[-E_UV(v)] Z(v)/Z(0),    h(0)=1.                 (1)

Every coefficient in E_UV is a function of the weight values before
choosing any profile decomposition. In particular this is ONE h, not
separately normalized functions for different packets. Its polarized
second derivative at zero is the bilinear form

 B_h(f,g)=D^2 h(0)[f,g]-Dh(0)[f]Dh(0)[g].               (2)

The identity

 Cov_bare(O(f),O(g))=D^2 E_UV(0)[f,g]+B_h(f,g)          (3)

is exact. Equation(2) requires no zero-free disk for h. One may call it
the second logarithmic germ at zero because h(0)=1; no logarithm is used
away from that germ.

Choose one fixed compatible chessboard cell C, enlarged once if necessary,
and an interior subcube C_0 a FIXED positive distance d_0 from its faces.
For every complex weight v supported in C_0 with

 ||v||_infty+Lip_endpoint(v)<=c r_*,                    (4)

the existing source/chessboard consumer gives |h(v)|<=Q_*. Here r_*>0
and Q_*<infinity depend on the fixed terminal band, cell, source geometry
and source majorants, but not on the number of separate pieces of v,
their signs, UV depth, or total torus volume.

This last independence is essential. Apply chessboard to the whole source
in ONE occupied cell. Do not apply a many-profile inequality and multiply
its constant by the number of pieces. For the face remainder, root every
carrier meeting the source and the cell boundary at a source hit in C_0.
At localization length a its anchor count is at most C(1+a^-4), while its
tree length is at least c d_0/a-C. Hence its scale sum is bounded by

 C sum_a (1+a^-4) exp[-c d_0/a]<infinity.               (5)

The finitely many a comparable to or above d_0 have a fixed contribution.
The same bound handles fixed enlargements and sufficiently small bare
mesh. It depends on the enclosing cell and its outer buffer, not on the
total number or total boundary area of the pieces inside C_0. The bulk
vacuum cancellation uses the common family(1), exactly as in Section16.

2. PACKING AND THE EXACT RADEMACHER IDENTITY

Let (f_R,g_R) be one fixed-shape pair of amplitude-one smooth profiles,
with each diameter and their mutual separation comparable to R. The two
shapes are reflected copies, with compact disjoint supports. For R small
one can choose m>=c_pack R^-4 bare-lattice translations t_i such that all
2m supports of

 f_i(x)=f_R(x-t_i),    g_i(x)=g_R(x-t_i)                 (6)

are pairwise disjoint and lie in C_0. Use boxes of side C_shape R; if
the bare mesh is sufficiently smaller than R, rounding their centers to
the bare mesh preserves the support margins and the bound on m.

For independent signs epsilon_i in {-1,1}, put

 F_epsilon=sum_i epsilon_i f_i,
 G_epsilon=sum_i epsilon_i g_i.                         (7)

Disjointness and the fixed profile shapes give

 ||F_epsilon||_infty+||G_epsilon||_infty<=C,
 Lip(F_epsilon)+Lip(G_epsilon)<=C/R,                   (8)

uniformly in m and the signs. In particular v=z_1 F_epsilon+z_2 G_epsilon
obeys(4) on |z_a|<d_R=c_0 r_* R, with c_0 fixed sufficiently small.
All earlier source classes are also satisfied: at endpoint-relative
length a<=1, the ratio of the scale-a Lipschitz norm to chi_a=sqrt(a)
is at most C sqrt(a)/R<=C/R.

Cauchy applied directly to h gives

 |B_h(F_epsilon,G_epsilon)|
 <=(Q_*+Q_*^2)d_R^-2.                                (9)

Bilinearity and E(epsilon_i epsilon_l)=1_{i=l} imply EXACTLY

 E_epsilon B_h(F_epsilon,G_epsilon)=sum_i B_h(f_i,g_i).

The bare periodic Wilson measure is translation invariant, so all the
bare covariances in(3) for (f_i,g_i) equal the same number C_bare(R).
No translation covariance of the fixed RG scaffold or of E_UV is used.
Writing c_i=D^2 E_UV(0)[f_i,g_i], equations(3),(9) give

 |C_bare(R)-m^-1 sum_i c_i| <= C_* R^2,                (10)
 C_* = C (Q_*+Q_*^2) r_*^-2 / c_pack.

This controls the ENTIRE normalized endpoint remainder, including every
surviving-history coefficient and all denominator cross terms. It does
not estimate a bad-event probability, an individual component ratio, or
the sign of any large-field term. The exact common source function and
the averaging of its complete logarithmic second germ do the work.

3. LOCAL RE-TERMINALIZATION OF A UNIVERSAL VACUUM BIRTH

The following interface is checked separately in the late-universal-
vacuum audit. It is recorded here to make the combined argument explicit.

The universal birth e_j(X;v) dated at its COMPLETED endpoint j depends
only on the actual source-free prefix through j. Regular maps and
localizations are causal. The large-field preparation at endpoint j
uses past levels h+1,...,j; it does not integrate j+1,...,j+N. All
reference choices and the homogeneous scalar disc remain fixed.
Re-terminating the proof at j changes chi_i and the allowable source
radius, not the coefficient function or its original birth date.

Section15.4 therefore permits an explicit radius

 r(j)=c(1+gamma_j)^-d                                  (11)

for a fixed finite d, chosen as the maximum of all fixed polynomial
source-admission losses. If one retains the stronger original R-slot
condition r E_src g^-C_b<=g^P, choose d>=(P+C_b)/2. This only changes
the later fixed exponent A. Section16.2 gives

 |e_j(X;v)|<=C_e exp[-kappa_e d_j(X)]                  (12)

on the local W_j for the shortened run, where chi_j=1. Remaining fixed
geometric/logarithmic costs can be absorbed into a finite polynomial
of gamma_j. This is an analytic estimate for the SAME e_j; it is not
a new sourced trajectory or a different source normalization.

In this note ell is the PRIMITIVE spatial scale. The sum below includes
EVERY primitive vacuum birth, not merely q-block endpoints. Let
a_j=ell^{j-N} in endpoint units, R_j=R_N/a_j, and use the packed
profiles(6). At date j their scale-j source norm is at most
C max(1,R_j^-1), uniformly in m and the signs. Hence Cauchy for(12)
on |z_a|<c r(j) min(1,R_j) bounds its mixed coefficient by

 C r(j)^-2 max(1,R_j^-2) exp[-kappa_e d_j(X)].          (13)

There are at most C a_j^-4 anchors in the fixed enclosing endpoint
cell. Carriers extending outside that cell are counted by rooting at a
source hit and summing their retained exponential tree reserve. Divide
the sign-averaged sum by m>=cR_N^-4. Since
R_N^4 a_j^-4=R_j^4, for every late scale with R_j<=1,

 |m^-1 sum_i D^2 E_j(0)[f_i,g_i]|
 <=C(1+gamma_j)^D R_j^2.                              (14)

The sign average is taken BEFORE the absolute value; cross-pair mixed
coefficients disappear. No estimate for an individual late pair is
needed. One may use a direct localized derivative bound instead, but
the packed proof needs only the original local analytic majorant.

If gamma_j<=C gamma_s on the remaining weak trajectory and t_0 is a
date after packet scale, then the geometric decrease of R_j gives

 |m^-1 sum_i sum_{j>=t_0} D^2 E_j(0)[f_i,g_i]|
 <= C gamma_s^D R_{t_0}^2.                            (15)

No factor N-s occurs. In particular this step does not propagate a
marked norm through the entire long trajectory and does not require
sum_j eta_j to be small there. Static vacua already extracted at earlier
births are not counted again.

4. PARAMETER ORDER AND THE NONZERO COVARIANCE

Fix all source geometry, q, source/lifetime powers and analytic reserves.

 R_{t_0}<=C Gamma_w^-A.                               (16)

Choose A>(D+2)/2, with a fixed extra strict margin, and also larger than

finitely many primitive phases affect only fixed constants. All couplings in this
prefix remain comparable to gamma_s. The existing regular two-source

 c_i,early=q_s^2 g_s^4 K_Maxwell(f,theta f)+o(g_s^4),  (17)

Here equality means the displayed uniform error estimate, not exact

The packing contains a huge but FINITE number of profiles after this

5. SCOPE OF THE CONCLUSION

The new exact consumer calculation is(2)--(10). The new late-tail
calculation is(11)--(15), conditional only on the SAME causal universal
birth formula and its re-terminated source-class estimate, not on any
normalized large-field probability or arbitrary boundary quotient.
Combined with the already developed regular early-prefix comparison,
these formulas remove the previously isolated normalized large-field
obstruction to this particular nontriviality witness.

They do not independently prove the original one-run source producer,
the sharp regular marked theorem, or the Gaussian normalization inputs.
Those remain explicit imported modules with their working proofs. The
present result is a nontriviality implication of those modules and the
existing compactness construction, not a completed four-dimensional
Yang--Mills existence proof or a mass-gap argument.
