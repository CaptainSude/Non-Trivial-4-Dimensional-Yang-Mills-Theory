SOURCE DOMAIN INCLUSION AND THE LIMIT OF SHRINKING-DOMAIN SCORE BOUNDS
18 October 2026. Primary-source bridge and independent mathematical audit.

RESULT

The uniform-fraction input UF in Notebook 42 can be discharged for the
common-radius, identity-centered physical (A,J) product ball that its
homogeneous specialization actually uses. This follows from a quantitative
estimate printed in CMP109, not just from its qualitative smaller-constant
sentence. The radius is c min(alpha0,alpha1,a_cap); c and a_cap are independent
of the radii, refinement, local restriction and volume in the published
geometric class. This also supplies straight independent (A,J) segments.

This result does NOT turn shrinking radii into fixed radii. A separate
gauge-invariant quartic example proves that the printed bounded analytic
class, even with vanishing vacuum and Maxwell jets, does not imply the
O(g) interaction-jet hypothesis of the new real-response theorem. This is
an obstruction to an inference from those assumptions, not a claim that
the actual recursively generated E has that bad behavior.

2. UNIFORM COMMON-RADIUS INCLUSION

PROPOSITION 1.
There are a_cap>0 and c_src>0 depending only on the fixed source constants
such that, for any positive target alpha0,alpha1 below the source ceilings,
put

             a = min(alpha0,alpha1,a_cap).

Any pair satisfying (i)--(iii) with the common smaller parameters
alpha0'=alpha1'=c_src a and gamma0'=c_src a belongs to the full target
space U_j^c(X,alpha0,alpha1). The constants are uniform in j, X and the
admissible finite regulator.

Proof. Choose a_cap inside every fixed smallness requirement of the
reconstruction estimate (1). Choose c_src <= min(1/4,1/(4 B3)). The first
three target conditions follow by monotonicity of their bounds. Formula
(1) gives all four reconstructed bounds <= a/2 <= alpha0/2, strictly
inside (iv). This proves target membership. The choices do not depend
on either target radius. QED.

The conclusion is deliberately a common-radius statement. It does not
assert that arbitrary independently prescribed shrinking fractions of
unequal radii are available without checking their other compatibility
conditions. This common-radius result is sufficient for our use.

PROPOSITION 2 (independent physical product ball).
Let eta be the physical fine spacing in the source convention. Write
U=exp(i eta A), take the real reference Ubar=I, and let J be INDEPENDENT
of A. There is c>0, uniform as in Proposition 1, such that

 ||A||_infinity + ||nabla_eta A||_infinity < c a,
 ||J||_infinity < c a                                  (2)

implies (U,J) in U_j^c(X,alpha0,alpha1). The field and derivative bounds
are imposed on the source restriction with every collar needed to form
its plaquettes and reconstructed interiors. The norm and J are the
physical ones used in source (1.8), (1.12)--(1.14).

Proof. The real reference I satisfies (i) with zero left sides. The
complex perturbation A supplies (ii). Multiplying four exponentials
around a plaquette, canceling the constant first-order potential terms,
and using the first differences gives

 ||U(partial p)-I|| <= C eta^2
       (||nabla_eta A||_infinity + ||A||_infinity^2).       (3)

Here eta<=1 and all matrix exponentials lie in a fixed chart; C depends
only on dimension, the matrix norm and that chart. This elementary bound
is uniform in mesh and volume. For a_cap<=1, choose c small enough that
the right side of (3), divided by eta^2, and both bounds in (2), lie
strictly below c_src a. This verifies the smaller conditions (i)--(iii).
Proposition 1 supplies (iv). QED.

The domain (2) is a convex product ball in the INDEPENDENT variables A,J.
Consequently straight segments between any two points in a smaller
fraction of (2) remain inside it, with a fixed margin available for
Cauchy estimates. No constraint equation is imposed on J in this fact.

COROLLARY 3 (actual homogeneous specialization).
Use the ordinary homogeneous minimizing field and physical Wilson
current of work/birth_radius_growth_2026-10-17.txt, Proposition 1 and

 ||A_ord(B)||_{C1,eta} + ||J_phys(U_ord(B))||_infinity
                                      <= C_h ||B||_infinity.

Thus every source R_X or E_X bounded by A_X on its stated source domain

It does not prove domain inclusion for arbitrary mixed-boundary

For t_j=log(g_j^{-2}) large, a_j is comparable to g_j t_j^q,

For a scalar function f bounded by A on ||u||<r, a fractional interior
has ||D^k f|| <= C_k A r^{-k} in Banach multilinear norm. For a fixed
analytic fluctuation chart u=Phi(B,gx), with physical-coordinate first
and second chart derivatives bounded independently of r and g, the
chain rule gives, on an admitted fractional interior,

 ||D_x f(Phi(B,gx))|| <= C A g/r,
 ||D_x^2 f(Phi(B,gx))||
               <= C A (g^2/r^2 + g^2/r).                 (4)

These are directional operator bounds. A weighted local Hessian Schur
bound requires the separate support summation and cannot be inferred
from (4) alone. With r comparable to g t^q, the dominant bounds in (4)
are A t^{-q} and A t^{-2q}, respectively. Background differentiation
can cost additional powers r^{-1}. Hence the analytic-radius argument
by itself supplies inverse logarithms, not the requested O(g) score
or O(g) jets through two background derivatives.

4. A GAUGE-INVARIANT QUARTIC OBSTRUCTION TO AN O(g) INFERENCE

The following example is a test of the stated function class. It is NOT
asserted to arise from the Wilson bare state under the actual recursion.

On an oriented plaquette p, let P=U(partial p) and use the fixed
normalized trace tau. Define the holomorphic, gauge-invariant scalar

       Q_p(U) = tau(2I-P-P^{-1}).

The matrix identity P+P^{-1}-2I=(P-I)^2 P^{-1} implies
|Q_p(U)| <= C alpha0^2 eta^4 on the source curvature domain. Fix a
nonzero sufficiently small lambda and set

 E_{r,p}(U) = lambda Q_p(U)^2/(C_* alpha0^4 eta^8),       (5)

with C_* chosen so |E_{r,p}|<=|lambda| throughout that domain.
This function is independent of J. A fixed source localization domain
containing p carries it with the corresponding fixed decay factor.
Summing the same construction over plaquettes supplies Euclidean
covariance; it does not change any local estimate. Constant factors
depending on the fixed localization convention are absorbed in lambda.

The Taylor expansion at I begins at fourth order. Thus the vacuum,
first and second jets vanish, and its Maxwell extraction is zero.
It is gauge invariant, analytic and bounded with the printed type of
uniform local envelope, despite its quartic coefficient alpha0^{-4}.

Choose a commuting local potential direction whose plaquette holonomy
is P(t)=exp(i eta^2 t H), with tau(H^2)>0. Such a direction is obtained
by assigning commuting link potentials around one plaquette. For fixed
eta, Taylor expansion gives

       E_{r,p}(t) = c_H lambda (t/alpha0)^4
                         + O(lambda eta^4 t^6/alpha0^4), (6)

where c_H>0 is fixed. Equivalently one may work on the unit mesh, which
already suffices to disprove a purported uniform implication. On a
physical fluctuation line t=B+gx subtract the static value at t=B.
Writing V_g(B,x)=E_{r,p}(B+gx)-E_{r,p}(B), its leading jets at B=0 are

 V_g(0,x)       = c_H lambda g^4 alpha0^{-4} x^4 + ...,
 D_x V_g(0,x)   = 4 c_H lambda g^4 alpha0^{-4} x^3 + ...,
 D_B V_g(0,x)   = 4 c_H lambda g^3 alpha0^{-4} x^3 + ...,
 D_B^2 V_g(0,x) = 12 c_H lambda g^2 alpha0^{-4} x^2 + ... . (7)

The dots have extra powers g^2 on any fixed x interval. Take
alpha0=C0 g (log g^{-2})^{q0}. The first two lines of (7) are of size
(log g^{-2})^{-4q0}, rather than O(g); the last two have additional
factors g^{-1} and g^{-2}. Nonzero Gaussian moments give the same
failure in any fixed local L^p norm. On a fixed compact x interval the
failure is already pointwise, so no tail issue is involved.

At a nonzero background B=theta alpha0 in an admitted real direction,
the leading linear fluctuation score is of order g/alpha0. The flat
example (7) is sufficient and avoids needing that larger real path.

Consequently vacuum subtraction, Maxwell subtraction, Ward symmetry
and the shrinking analytic envelope alone do not prove the O(g)
finite-order interaction-jet input used in the real-response theorem.
This example has zero quadratic jet, so subtracting a Maxwell term
cannot repair it. Additional generated-state structure or an explicit
fixed-radius plus small-mark decomposition is required.

5. EXACT ONE-MODE NORMALIZED RESPONSE TEST

PROPOSITION 4.
Uniform real convexity and locality, together with a bounded shrinking
analytic interaction and vanishing interaction Maxwell jet, do not by
themselves imply an O(g^2) static-subtracted response Hessian for a mark
of fixed analytic norm.

Proof. Fix lambda>0 and take the exactly normalized probability measure

 dmu_B(x)=Z_B^{-1} exp[-x^2/2-lambda(B+gx)^4/r^4] dx,
 W(u)=u^2,   R(B)=E_muB[(B+gx)^2-B^2].                    (9)

This is a one-mode model, not a Wilson-generated state. Its real
fluctuation potential has Hessian
1+12 lambda g^2(B+gx)^2/r^4 >=1. There are no off-diagonal locality
conditions to verify. The interaction lambda u^4/r^4 is bounded by
lambda on |u|<r and has zero quadratic jet. The mark W has fixed norm
on any fixed physical u-ball.

Put epsilon=lambda g^4/r^4 and m_n=E_mu0 x^n. The measure mu0 is even.
Differentiating its NORMALIZED expectations gives

 (E_muB x)'|_0 = -(4 epsilon/g) m_4,
 (E_muB x^2)''|_0
   =-(12 epsilon/g^2)(m_4-m_2^2)
      +(16 epsilon^2/g^2)(m_8-m_2 m_6).

Since R(B)=2Bg E_muB x+g^2 E_muB x^2, it follows exactly that

 R''(0)=-epsilon(28m_4-12m_2^2)
                  +16epsilon^2(m_8-m_2m_6).             (10)

As epsilon decreases to zero, dominated Gaussian integration gives
m_2=1+O(epsilon), m_4=3+O(epsilon), and bounded m_6,m_8. Thus

                   R''(0)=-72epsilon+O(epsilon^2).       (11)

With r=g(log g^{-2})^q, this is of order (log g^{-2})^{-4q},
not O(g^2). All normalization terms are included in (10). QED.

The sign in (11) is not an amplification assertion: this example
specifically disproves the proposed absolute O(g^2) estimate from the
weaker premises. It does not disprove the finite-order response theorem,
whose O(g) interaction-jet hypothesis fails here. The plaquette example
in Section 4 supplies the independent gauge-invariance diagnostic.

6. WHY A WEAKER INVERSE-LOG PER-STEP LOSS IS NOT ENOUGH

Let gamma=g^{-2}. Suppose one could prove only a multiplicative
propagation majorant 1+C/(log gamma)^a, a>0, at every later step.
Such a majorant cannot, by itself, be paid by a birth factor
exp[-c(log gamma_i)^nu] for any fixed finite nu.

Proof. Consider the exact decreasing scalar schedule gamma_{k+1}=gamma_k-1
until a fixed terminal gamma_*>1, and take an initial gamma_i=T.
This schedule has bounded drift and strictly positive blocks. At the
steps gamma_k in [T/2,T], log(1+C/(log gamma_k)^a)
>=c_a/(log T)^a for T large. There are at least T/3 such steps, hence

 product_{k>=i}(1+C/(log gamma_k)^a)
                     >= exp[c'_a T/(log T)^a].            (12)

Multiplication by exp[-c(log T)^nu] still diverges as T->infinity.
This is a failure of that proposed majorant as a proof method; it is
not a lower bound on the actual RG amplification. A genuine g^2 times
polylog factor is qualitatively different and is covered by the
logarithmic-transport lemma already proved on 17 October.

Finite startup intervals of O(log gamma_i) steps can be treated
separately: a polynomial-in-g_i^{-1} factor per step costs at most
exp[C(log gamma_i)^2]. Fresh birth suppression with exponent nu>2
can pay that cost, subject to the actual source parameter hierarchy.
The proof still needs a verified stronger norm after startup; small
birth size alone does not establish analytic descendant control.

7. SUPPORT METRIC CHECK FOR THE PARALLEL LOCALIZATION PROOF

B109 p257 defines d_j(X) using a shortest tree meeting every fixed-M
cube in X, with lengths divided by M. It is not the graph distance
after grouping into MR_j cubes. B119 (2.30)--(2.31) restricts R supports
to unions of MR_j cubes but retains d_j(X).
