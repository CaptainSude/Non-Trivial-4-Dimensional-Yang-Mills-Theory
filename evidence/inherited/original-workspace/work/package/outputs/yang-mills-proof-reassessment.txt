YM4 PROOF REASSESSMENT
21 October 2026 — TURN94

This is a research decision and dependency map, not a new existence
theorem. Nontrivial continuum four-dimensional Yang–Mills existence
remains unproved in this project. The mathematical frontier remains
TURN93; none of its conditional estimates is promoted to an unconditional
model estimate here.

DECISION

Keep the original Wilson lattice theory as the primary regulator, but
redirect the next substantial estimate toward local observable insertions.
The bounded spatial-flow/entropy program remains a possible diagnostic
of physical nontriviality. It cannot stand in for the construction of a
local four-dimensional theory.

The immediate target should be a theorem for two separated local sources
in a fixed physical box, with the actual running coupling, original full
normalization and all large-field histories. This should be designed as
the first member of a common local correlation hierarchy. It is a more
direct investment in existence than another abstract sufficient criterion
for one bounded scalar to fluctuate.

WHAT THE EXISTENCE ARGUMENT STILL NEEDS

1. Physical scale and coupling.
   Attach the existing selected-kernel tuning and conditional native
   continuation arguments to the actual construction. A controlled
   trajectory and an observable definition of the physical scale must
   refer to the same model. Ultraviolet stability at an assumed weak
   trajectory does not discharge this attachment by itself.

2. Compatible local observable content.
   Construct gauge-invariant local Schwinger correlations, or an adequate
   local observable algebra and state, with the required renormalization,
   operator mixing and contact extensions. The limits must be jointly
   compatible; different scalar subsequences do not define one theory.
   A single energy observable is an initial sector, not the whole local
   invariant algebra. Tensor/orientation-sensitive observables must enter
   the eventual construction.

   Do not impose an unnecessary classical probability representation on
   all renormalized composite fields such as Tr F^2. Their coincident
   singularities must be treated in the appropriate Schwinger/Wightman
   or algebraic formulation. Tightness of bounded smoothed scalars does
   not solve this issue.

3. Actual normalized continuum and volume estimates.
   Prove cutoff-uniform estimates for the original local insertions,
   including the large-field contributions and the full denominator.
   Use these to obtain common continuum limits, with enough local volume
   control to reach R^4. A finite physical torus is a legitimate first
   milestone, but is not the final spacetime theory.

4. Reconstruction and vacuum properties.
   Pass reflection positivity on the relevant common local algebra,
   regularity, translations and full Euclidean covariance to the limit;
   establish the remaining hypotheses of a reconstruction theorem or
   supply an equivalent direct construction. A single time semigroup
   and lattice rotations are insufficient. Obtain a suitable vacuum or
   phase with the needed clustering properties without losing the other
   construction properties.

5. Physical nontriviality and Yang–Mills identification.
   Show that at least one observable in that SAME local theory has
   nonzero positive-energy content, and establish the intended
   non-Abelian short-distance normalization and relations. Neither
   fresh coarse-reference fluctuations nor a non-Gaussian scalar
   statistic suffices: nonlinear functions of free fields can be
   non-Gaussian. A constant mixture can also fluctuate without dynamics.

For calibration of the intended endpoint, see Jaffe–Witten, Quantum
Yang–Mills Theory, Sections 3–4:
https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/yangmills.pdf
Our initial SU(2) existence target omits the additional all-groups and
mass-gap requirements of the full Millennium problem.

WHAT TO RETAIN FROM THE PREVIOUS WORK

The finite-regulator identities, normalization checks, source and
regular-region modules, conditional trajectory arguments and filtered
OS witness implications are reusable. Their exact hypotheses matter.
In particular, TURN93's entropy criterion is useful if an actual
competitor with positive original response and bounded relative entropy
can be constructed. It is not itself such a competitor.

The exclusions also save effort. Do not return to the excluded raw
covariant spatial heat trace, the specified all-microscopic-good event,
or the inference from fresh reference variance to original-field
variance. These are exclusions of particular methods and normalizations,
not a no-go theorem for YM4 or for renormalized local observables.

The remaining copied-datum source match can finish a useful current
reference normalization bound. Even its successful completion would
not give the original marked response, local fields or continuum RP.

THE NEXT CONCRETE MATHEMATICAL TARGET

Start with an explicitly chosen lattice representative of a local
gauge-invariant energy insertion, and smooth physical profiles f,h with
separated supports. Denote the regulated insertion by O_a(f), allowing
the renormalization constants and mixing that must subsequently be
determined. At finite regulator the exact connected source jet is

 C_a(f,h) = d_s d_t log E_W exp[s O_a(f)+t O_a(h)] at s=t=0.

All expectations here are under the complete original Wilson law.
This finite formula does not assert that a continuum real exponential
generating function exists.

The useful next theorem would:

 - carry this literal mixed derivative through one complete R/T step,
   with all sibling histories, boundary terms and normalizers;
 - estimate the resulting marked term in a norm whose errors can be
   summed through the ultraviolet scales;
 - determine its physical normalization and compare its separated
   response with the actual controlled blocked reference;
 - yield uniform upper bounds and a nonvanishing reflected response
   for an appropriate local, time-filtered insertion.

Additive source-only counterterms in the connected generating functional,
supported at coincident source points, have no mixed contribution when
the source supports are disjoint. This simplifies the first calculation;
field-dependent operator mixing and the later contact-extension task
still require control.
The comparison must use the actual blocked precision and boundary
problem, not an unproved replacement by a bare nearest-neighbor model.

The decisive missing estimate occurs where an original marked interior
is integrated and a fresh reference is inserted. The mark remains in
the old integral against the full continuation weight. A scalar
partition-function identity does not remove it. This is the place to
prove a quantitative model estimate, rather than another abstract
transfer implication. The proposal changes prioritization; it does
not claim that this marked estimate is already proved.

ALTERNATIVES AND THINGS WE CAN DEFER

Wilson convergence is a route choice, not an axiom of every possible YM
existence proof. A theory built directly from native RG states is a
legitimate alternative if its own local observable compatibility, RP,
physical calibration, symmetries and Yang–Mills identification are proved.
At present this exchange introduces more unproved structure than it
removes. A literal first-R RP audit, on the actual new-link half-space
algebra including crossing components, is a sensible limited feasibility
test. Positivity and reflection covariance of individual kernels are
not that audit. Finite RP failure would rule out exact inheritance, not
necessarily continuum RP after a vanishing-defect estimate.

We can defer a mass gap, uniqueness of every subsequential limit and
universality for every discretization. We need not solve every compact
simple gauge group in the first SU(2) construction. We also need not
control arbitrary future tests or entire source functions when finite
jets suffice for a precisely stated local theorem.

Track two distinct outcomes in future work: construction of the common
local theory, and certification of its physical nontriviality. Count a
conditional reduction as a reduction, and a proved full-measure bound
as a bound. There is no justified estimate here of how close the final
existence proof is.
