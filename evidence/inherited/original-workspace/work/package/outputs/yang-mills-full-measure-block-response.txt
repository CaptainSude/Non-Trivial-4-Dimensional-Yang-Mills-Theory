MAIN ADVANCE

An arbitrary deterministic block of original SU(2) Wilson links can now
be integrated with quantitative bounds for both its exact generated
action and its transported original energy source. The bounds include
first and second exterior-coordinate derivatives, use the full original
normalized measure, and have constants independent of the eliminated
block size and surrounding volume.

For G=sum_p w_p h_p, W=sum_p|w_p|, conditional expectation P_B over
the eliminated links, and any fixed finite q>=1, the simplified bounds are

 ||P_B G||_q <= C_q W/beta,
 ||X_f P_B G||_q <= C_q W/sqrt(beta),
 ||X_g X_f P_B G||_q <= C_q W.

X_f and X_g are fixed unit derivatives in retained original-link
coordinates. The exact marginal action H_B satisfies

 ||X_f H_B||_q <= C_q sqrt(beta),
 ||X_g X_f H_B||_q <= C_q beta.

All generated interactions and normalizer derivatives are included.
Exact nested original-link eliminations preserve these constants by
Fubini and the conditional-expectation tower property. The second
source derivative includes the conditional third cumulant; it is not
obtained by freezing the measure during differentiation.

These are averaged per-coordinate estimates. They do not assert uniform
pointwise bounds over exteriors, a bound on the full Hessian operator
norm, spatial decay, or control after a new coarse-field coordinate map.
The weight W remains explicit. For a profile of fixed physical support,
its microscopic plaquette sum can have W of order a^-4. Removing that
loss in connected correlations is now the precise next requirement.

1. MODEL, DERIVATIVES, AND MOMENTS

Use the finite periodic four-dimensional SU(2) Wilson law with periods
at least four, normalized link Haar measure, and homogeneous beta>0:

 mu_beta(dU)=Z_beta^-1 exp[-beta S(U)]dU,
 S=sum_p h_p,                 h_p=1-Tr(U_p)/2 in [0,2].

For a deterministic link f and fixed unit Lie-algebra generator T, let
X_f act by U_f -> exp(tT)U_f, holding every other original link fixed.
The norm convention identifies SU(2) with the unit quaternion sphere,
so this flow has unit speed. No field-dependent coefficient, stopping
decision, source-dependent update order or auxiliary record is used.

For a plaquette p containing f, quaternion differentiation gives

 |X_f h_p| <= sqrt(2 h_p).                              (1.1)

Indeed the scalar component derivative has magnitude at most the length
of the vector part of U_p, which is sqrt(1-(1-h_p)^2)<=sqrt(2h_p).
Left/right placements and inverse link orientations preserve the norm.
If p does not read f the derivative is zero.

TURN64 proves, for every finite plaquette set E,

 E_mu exp[(beta/4)sum_(p in E)h_p] <= 2^(3|E|/4).       (1.2)

In particular, for every real r>0, integration of the exponential tail
gives

 E h_p^r <= 2^(3/4) Gamma(r+1) (4/beta)^r.              (1.3)

The constants in (1.2)-(1.3) do not depend on the torus volume. Define
C_r as any finite constants covering these moment bounds; all constants
below may depend on q but never on beta, the torus or an eliminated
block's size. For r>=1, Minkowski therefore gives

 ||sum_p w_p h_p||_r <= C_r W/beta,
 W=sum_p |w_p|.                                       (1.4)

Likewise for q>=1, including q/2<1 by (1.3),

 ||X_f G||_q <= C_q W_f beta^-1/2,
 W_f=sum_(p reading f)|w_p|.                           (1.5)

The weights are arbitrary deterministic real numbers on the finite
torus. W is explicit and may be large. We are not asserting bounded W
for a fixed physical support as the microscopic mesh tends to zero.

2. ANY SIMULTANEOUS BLOCK: THE EXACT NORMALIZED COMMUTATOR

Let B be ANY deterministic set of original links. Denote by P_B the
conditional expectation given all links outside B, under the original
mu_beta. Let S_B be the sum of all distinct original plaquettes reading
at least one link of B, counted once. The literal conditional density is

 q_B(dU_B|U_out) = exp[-beta S_B]dU_B / int exp[-beta S_B]dU_B.

Every exterior-only action term cancels from this conditional ratio.
For f outside B, differentiating this finite compact positive integral
gives the exact identity

 X_f P_B G = P_B(X_f G) - beta Cov_B(G,X_f S_B).          (2.1)

All normalization and action-score terms are present. It is not a
derivative of a frozen conditional measure. Set

 H_B,f=X_f S_B=sum_(p reading f and at least one B link)X_f h_p.

There are at most SIX terms in H_B,f, independently of |B|. Equations
(1.1)-(1.3) give ||H_B,f||_(2q)<=C_q beta^-1/2.

Conditional expectations are contractions in L^r(mu) for r>=1. Holder
and these contractions give, without a conditional pointwise bound,

 ||Cov_B(G,H)||_q
 <= ||P_B(GH)||_q + ||(P_BG)(P_BH)||_q
 <= 2 ||G||_(2q) ||H||_(2q).                            (2.2)

Substitute (1.4)-(1.5) into (2.1)-(2.2):

 ||X_f P_B G||_q <= C_q beta^-1/2 (W_f+W).              (2.3)

This holds for every beta>0 and finite 1<=q<infinity, every deterministic B and exterior
link f. In particular its constant is uniform in both block and torus
size. Its norm is the FULL original mu norm, equivalently the actual
exterior marginal norm. That marginal contains the exact conditional
partition function; it is not replaced by Haar or a prescribed exterior.

If B touches no plaquette containing f, H_B,f=0 exactly and only the
first term in (2.1) remains. The estimate is an averaged first-derivative
theorem for an original energy mark. It does not say the generated
effective action is Wilson, or that its higher derivatives satisfy the
same norm, or that an arbitrary effective/history-conditioned measure
has the moments (1.3).

3. ARBITRARILY LONG SINGLE-PASS ORIGINAL HEATBATH SEQUENCES

Let e_1,...,e_n be a deterministic sequence of original links with
e_k != f for every k. Repetitions are allowed for the first statement.
Write P_e for the actual original Wilson heatbath projection and

 G_0=G,             G_k=P_(e_k)G_(k-1).

These are smooth functions at every fixed beta and finite regulator.
Original-law contraction gives

 ||G_k||_r<=||G||_r<=C_r W/beta                         (3.1)

for every r>=1. Let n(e,f) be the number of plaquettes containing both
distinct links e and f. It is zero unless they share a plaquette.
The one-link version of (2.1) gives

 X_f G_k=P_(e_k)X_fG_(k-1)
              -beta Cov_(e_k)(G_(k-1),H_(e_k),f),
 H_e,f=sum_(p containing e and f)X_f h_p.                (3.2)

Each H_e,f satisfies ||H_e,f||_(2q)<=C_q n(e,f)beta^-1/2.
Using (2.2) and (3.1) at each step, then iterating the real inequality,
proves

 ||X_fG_n||_q <= C_q beta^-1/2
             [ W_f + W sum_(k=1)^n n(e_k,f) ].          (3.3)

If every updated link occurs at most R times, then

 sum_k n(e_k,f) <= 18R.                                (3.4)

Indeed f lies in six plaquettes and each has three other links. Counting
these eighteen incidences also handles any repeated neighbor across
plaquettes; no assumption about their being distinct is needed. Thus
for a SINGLE PASS (R=1), the right side of (3.3) is independent of the
number and order of updated links. The bound permits an arbitrarily
large integrated region for a fixed mark G and an exterior derivative.

A later P_e can reintroduce an earlier coordinate, exactly as TURN97
showed. No nested-sigma-algebra identity is asserted. Deterministic
composition and the actual original law suffice for (3.2)-(3.3).
If F reads none of the updated links, the usual conditional identity
also retains Cov(F,G_n)=Cov(F,G). This observation does not itself give
decay or a summable estimate on that covariance.

For a field-dependent order or decisions depending on updated links,
the differentiation would also read those decisions. For a native
record, the conditioning density need not be q_e. Those cases are not
included in (3.3). For canonical marks beta G, multiply all displayed
source and response bounds by beta; the response scale is then sqrt(beta).

4. THE TWO-LINK EXCEPTIONAL EXTERIOR HAS A FIXED ENERGY COST

Use the actual TURN97 pair of opposite parallel links, and its ten

 S_pair(x,y;t)=11-x.y-sum_i a_i.x-sum_j b_j.y,

All eleven summands are nonnegative. For every original configuration,

 0<=D(U_out)<=S_pair(U)<=sum_(p in pair stars)h_p(U).    (4.1)

Choose epsilon>0 small enough that all ten staples within epsilon of
their first staple a_1 is inside TURN97's fixed weak-exterior tube.
Let E_bad be the exterior event

 max_i |a_i-a_1| >= epsilon OR max_j |b_j-a_1| >= epsilon.

This is a statement about their common conditional coordinate frame,
not a raw-link identity window. A change of that common orthogonal
frame leaves all the distances invariant. For any minimizing x,y,

 |a_i-a_1|^2 <=2(|a_i-x|^2+|x-a_1|^2)<=4D,
 |b_j-a_1|^2 <=3(|b_j-y|^2+|y-x|^2+|x-a_1|^2)<=6D.

 E_bad implies D>=d_epsilon:=epsilon^2/6.              (4.2)

Equations (1.2), (4.1) and exponential Markov now give the actual

 P_mu(D>=d) <= min(1, 2^(33/4) exp[-beta d/4]),
 P_mu(E_bad) <= min(1, 2^(33/4) exp[-beta epsilon^2/24]). (4.3)

This is uniform in the surrounding volume. No arbitrary conditioned
exterior theorem has been used. The bound applies to this exterior

For the TURN97 obstruction, a=sum_i a_i=I, b=sum_j b_j=-I.
Maximizing x.y+x_0-y_0 first over x gives max_(s in [-1,1])

Moreover D is 1-Lipschitz separately in the aggregate vectors a,b,

 min(1, 2^(33/4)exp[-beta(19/2-eta)/4]), 0<eta<19/2.     (4.4)

Thus the explicit order-beta pointwise response is compatible with

The harmless flat constant has zero covariance. Equation (5.4) has no

There is also a full-measure first-gradient upgrade for a fixed exterior

 |X_f(A_beta-D)|<=C beta^-2 sqrt(D).                    (5.6)

Common frame rotations contribute zero; the quotient slice bounds are

The gradient conclusion is a weak/a.e. derivative statement for D.

RESULT

For any deterministic set B of integrated original Wilson links, the
exact generated marginal action has first exterior-coordinate derivatives
bounded in L^q by C_q sqrt(beta) and ordered second derivatives by C_q beta.
For an original local energy mark G=sum_p w_p h_p with W=sum|w_p|, its
exact conditional average P_B G has first derivatives bounded by
C_q W/sqrt(beta) and second derivatives by C_q W. Constants are
independent of the number of integrated links and of the lattice volume.
These are norms in the actual retained marginal of the original measure.

Both action and source, including all normalization scores, are present.
The conclusions also hold after any number of nested deterministic
original-link eliminations, by exact conditional-expectation composition.
They are per-coordinate and per-coordinate-pair estimates, not a bound
on a full derivative matrix operator norm or a decay estimate in the
distance between its coordinates. The original source weight W is not
removed. No change to a native constrained/history-conditioned law is
included. These qualifications matter for the remaining physical sum.

1. DEFINITIONS AND ALREADY PROVED MOMENT INPUT

Use the full finite periodic four-dimensional SU(2) Wilson measure with
periods at least four, beta>0, normalized link Haar measure,

 mu_beta(dU)=Z_beta^-1 exp[-beta S(U)]dU,
 S=sum_p h_p,       h_p=1-Tr U_p/2.

B is any deterministic subset of original links. Write
S=S_out+S_B, where S_B contains every distinct plaquette reading B once,
and S_out contains the remaining plaquettes. The exact partial partition
and conditional expectation are

 Z_B(xi)=int exp[-beta S_B(U_B,xi)]dU_B,
 P_B F=Z_B(xi)^-1 int F(U_B,xi)exp[-beta S_B(U_B,xi)]dU_B.

The exterior marginal has density proportional to exp(-H_B) with

 H_B(xi)=beta S_out(xi)-log Z_B(xi).                    (1.1)

Adding a field-independent constant to H_B has no effect below. The
measure used for every displayed L^q norm is the original mu_beta; for
exterior functions this equals its actual exterior marginal norm.

Let f and g be exterior links. X_f and X_g are fixed unit left-invariant
Lie-algebra derivatives on their original link coordinates. If the
links coincide, keep the indicated order of derivatives; no symmetry
of noncommuting vector fields is asserted. The generators are not
field-dependent and no differentiated history decision is hidden in them.

The full original-law energy bound from TURN64 and the derivative
calculation in work/turn98_largefield.txt give, for every finite r>=1,

 ||G||_r <= C_r W/beta,
 ||X_f G||_r <= C_r W_f/sqrt(beta),
 |X_g X_f G| <= W_fg,

 W_f=sum_(p reading f)|w_p|,
 W_fg=sum_(p reading both f and g)|w_p|.                (1.2)

For f=g, the last sum is over plaquettes reading f. The second derivative
of an individual plaquette defect has magnitude at most one under the
chosen unit generators: multiplication by unit quaternions is isometric,
and each differentiated factor or inverse factor has norm one. Each
original plaquette reads a given link at most once in this geometry.

Put K_f=X_f S_B, K_g=X_g S_B, and L_gf=X_g X_f S_B. Each K has at most
six plaquette terms. Therefore

 ||K_f||_r+||K_g||_r <= C_r beta^-1/2,
 ||L_gf||_infinity <= 6.                              (1.3)

The bounds are independent of |B|. L_gf vanishes when no touched
plaquette reads both f and g. K_f vanishes if f shares no plaquette
with B. The underlying moment bound is a full-measure theorem, not
a uniform bound under arbitrary frozen exterior data.

2. EXACT SECOND DERIVATIVE OF THE NORMALIZED ORIGINAL SOURCE

The first conditional response is

 X_f P_B G=P_B(X_fG)-beta Cov_B(G,K_f).                 (2.1)

For three functions, write the conditional third cumulant as

 kappa_B(A,C,D)=P_B(ACD)-P_B A P_B(CD)
               -P_B C P_B(AD)-P_B D P_B(AC)
               +2P_B A P_B C P_B D.

Differentiation of a normalized covariance gives exactly

 X_g Cov_B(A,C)=Cov_B(X_g A,C)+Cov_B(A,X_g C)
                       -beta kappa_B(A,C,K_g).        (2.2)

Apply (2.1) to its first term and (2.2) to its second term. The result is

 X_g X_f P_B G
  =P_B(X_g X_f G)
   -beta Cov_B(X_fG,K_g)
   -beta Cov_B(X_gG,K_f)
   -beta Cov_B(G,L_gf)
   +beta^2 kappa_B(G,K_f,K_g).                         (2.3)

All five terms are needed. This is an identity of smooth functions on
the compact exterior space at every finite regulator. In particular
the last term is part of differentiating the conditional normalizer.

For q finite with q>=1, conditional contraction and Holder give

 ||Cov_B(A,C)||_q <=2||A||_(2q)||C||_(2q),
 ||kappa_B(A,C,D)||_q
                 <=6||A||_(3q)||C||_(3q)||D||_(3q).   (2.4)

The factor six is the sum of the absolute cumulant coefficients.
For example ||(P_B A)(P_B(CD))||_q is bounded using exponents 3q and
3q/2, followed by contraction and Holder for CD. The three-single-factor
product is handled with three factors of exponent 3q.

Inserting (1.2)-(1.3) into (2.3)-(2.4) yields

 ||X_g X_f P_B G||_q
       <= C_q (W_fg+W_f+W_g+W).                       (2.5)

The beta powers cancel: in the third cumulant term they are
beta^2 times beta^-1 times beta^-1/2 times beta^-1/2. The other terms
obey the same bound. This estimate holds for every beta>0, with no
weak-exterior restriction or growth of the constant with |B|.
Together with TURN98's first-derivative theorem, it gives

 ||P_B G||_q <= C_q W/beta,
 ||X_f P_B G||_q <= C_q(W_f+W)/sqrt(beta),
 ||X_g X_f P_B G||_q <= C_q(W_fg+W_f+W_g+W).            (2.6)

For a source normalized as beta G, multiply these bounds by beta.
The coupling here is the original beta, not a substituted running
coupling. A deterministic beta-dependent source weight can be used at
each fixed beta; (2.3) differentiates exterior fields, not that weight.

3. THE ACTUAL GENERATED ACTION, IN THE SAME MARGINAL

Differentiate (1.1) while keeping all generated factors in Z_B. Since
S_out is exterior measurable,

 X_f H_B=beta X_f S_out+beta P_B(X_f S_B)
        =beta P_B(X_f S).                             (3.1)

Its next ordered derivative is

 X_g X_f H_B
   =beta P_B(X_g X_f S)
          -beta^2 Cov_B(X_f S,X_g S).                 (3.2)

To see the second score in this form, first differentiate using K_g.
Then X_g S-K_g=X_g S_out is a conditional constant, so replacing K_g
by X_g S does not change the covariance. This is an exact cancellation
of exterior-only terms, not an omission of the action outside B.

Each full X_f S still has only the six original plaquettes at f; each
X_g X_f S has at most six terms of magnitude at most one. The same
full-law moments and (2.4) therefore prove

 ||X_f H_B||_q <= C_q sqrt(beta),
 ||X_g X_f H_B||_q <= C_q beta.                        (3.3)

No assertion that H_B remains a Wilson action or a finite-range action
is used. Its generated interactions may have long range, and these
coordinate bounds do not constrain that range or imply convexity.
The exact scalar value of H_B, which may contain an extensive vacuum
term, is not bounded by (3.3).

4. NESTED ORIGINAL-LINK DECIMATION DOES NOT ACCUMULATE THESE CONSTANTS

Let B_1 subset B_2 subset ... subset B_m be any deterministic sequence
of eliminated link sets. At stage j, use the EXACT marginal obtained
from the original measure and integrate the additional retained links
B_(j+1) minus B_j. Fubini and the tower property give

 E_(stage j)[P_(B_j)G | original links outside B_(j+1)]
                           =P_(B_(j+1))G.             (4.1)

The resulting density is exactly exp(-H_(B_(j+1))) up to its scalar
normalizer. Thus (2.6) and (3.3) hold at every stage with the SAME
constants, for coordinates still retained. They do not accumulate an
error proportional to the number of stages. This is a true nested
integration statement; it is distinct from the original-state-space
single-link heatbath projections studied in TURN97 and TURN98 Section3.

The proof retains the source's identity as the conditional expectation
of the ORIGINAL G. It does not assert the same result for an arbitrary
new effective source supplied only through an unrelated norm. It also
does not include a change of variables to rescaled coarse connections,
adaptive large-field decisions, or replacing part of a density by a
fresh reference. Each such operation would need its own controlled
transformation and score.

5. WHAT THIS RESOLVES AND WHAT IS STILL MISSING

At the level of averaged coordinate derivatives, growth in the number
of exactly integrated original links is no longer an unproved source
of divergence for a fixed energy mark. Both the original source and
the exact marginal action have a controlled two-jet in the same
normalized marginal, and the statement survives nested integrations.

The estimates retain the original W=sum|w_p| and contain no spatial
decay when f and g are far apart. A smooth profile on a fixed physical
region can have W of order a^-4. The corresponding separated covariance
sum is not controlled by these bounds. Nor does boundedness of each
Hessian entry bound an operator norm on an increasing number of links.

The next useful strengthening must resolve spatial dependence or the
cancellation of local scalar contributions in the original connected
source sum, and must retain that gain under the actual coarse-field map.
An unqualified stronger pointwise estimate is not the target: TURN97
already exhibits why such an estimate can fail. The global pair-round
value theorem and the full-measure derivative theorem address different
norms and should be used at their proved scopes.
