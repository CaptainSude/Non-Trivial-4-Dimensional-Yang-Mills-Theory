TURN82 -- EXACT PULLBACK COST OF THE NEW ORIGINAL-LINK SOURCE DOMAIN
20 October 2026. history47. Consumer analysis, not a native admission claim.

RESULT
The new HS source germ is a genuine uniformly normalized ORIGINAL-link
analytic germ. Its available norm does not by itself give the earlier
native coarse/pair source norm. The loss can be quantified: a four-
dimensional refinement h=M^-1 has fine link potential O(h b) on O(h^-4)
links per current cell, giving an original-link Hilbert cost O(h^-1 b).
The source-independent Gaussian bound then costs exp[C beta0 h^-2 b^2]
per current cell. A nonflat abelian refinement attains this order for
the absolute Gaussian kernel norm. This is a sharp limitation of that
particular majorant, not a proof that a gauge-invariant source jet grows.

There is a second, separate issue: the new annealed derivative estimate
is averaged under the original Wilson law. Evaluating it on a deterministic
minimum does not preserve that measure. The exact chain factors and the
needed measure change are stated below; neither can be omitted.

1. THE TWO ACTUALLY PROVED NORMS

The new input is turn82_global_smoothed_source_history.txt, Sections3--4A.
For F_n=P^n B, 0<=B<=1, n>=1 (also for the reflected mixture), it proves

 |F_n(U^0 exp z)| <= exp[C(beta0+1)||Im z||_2^2],
                       ||z||_infinity<r0,                (1.1)

with local exponential-coordinate conventions as in that note. It also
proves, for EVERY fixed k>=1,

 E_mu ||D^k F_n(U)||_op^2
          <=C_k(beta0+1)^k Var_mu(B)/(2n).                (1.2)

The derivative norm is the operator norm for k Hilbert directions in
the ORIGINAL independent links; it is not the sum of coordinate jets.
The complete source package is P^n exp(sum s_i B_i), not exp(sum s_i F_n,i).

The previous target is instead the strong source-family norm

 sup_I sum_(H contains I) e^(a|H|)
                          sum_x ||E_(H,x)||_(native domain). (1.3)

See turn75_classical_native_history.txt Section2,
augmented_source_classical_contraction_turn70.txt Section1, and
turn77_neutral_native_preparation_history.txt Section2. Every term of
(1.3) retains complete field, source, current, gauge and determining reads.
Its analytic norm is a supremum on a current-link or independent-pair
domain, before the carrier and source-label sums. The actual TURN70
argument only converts FIXED degrees<=4 into coefficient-sum kernels;
it handles higher degree as a whole analytic function. Therefore the
issue here is not an invented all-arity Wiener conversion. It is the
different background domain, the actual carrier localization, and the
measure of the annealed estimate.

2. EXACT CHAIN FACTORS ON A COMMON ADMITTED RECONSTRUCTION

Let Psi(w) be an ACTUAL holomorphic reconstruction into original links
on a declared native/current domain, and choose a common chart about
the real center Psi(w0). Write z(w) for its original link coordinates.
All correction gauges and current dependencies belong to this map.
An arbitrary independent (U,J) continuation is not defined by its
physical restriction; that separate old issue is unchanged.

The available analytic estimate composes literally as

 |F_n(Psi(w))| <= exp[C(beta0+1)||Im z(w)||_2^2],          (2.1)

provided EVERY z_e(w) is in the indicated chart. In particular, using
the uniform Hilbert-ball estimate to get a fixed bound M_* requires

 (beta0+1) sup_w ||Im z(w)||_2^2 <= C^-1 log M_*.         (2.2)

This is the admission condition for THIS MAJORANT. It is not a necessary
condition for the actual source to have a better holomorphic bound by
some other argument (for example a source-specific gauge cancellation).

For real-coordinate jets put

 L_r(w)=||D^r z(w)||_(native directions -> original l2), r>=1.

Faà di Bruno gives the exact partition sum

 D^k(F_n o Psi)[v_1,...,v_k]
  =sum_(pi a partition of{1,...,k})
    D^|pi| F_n(Psi(w))[
          D^|A|z(w)[v_i:i in A] : A in pi].              (2.3)

Equation(2.3) is first read at w0 in the fixed original chart centered at
Psi(w0), then recentered at each real w. Coordinate charts must be
composed consistently; if one differentiates a moving-chart expression,
its transition derivatives are included in z.
Thus the deterministic pullback factor is

 B_k(w)=sum_pi C_|pi| (beta0+1)^(|pi|/2)
                                   product_(A in pi) L_|A|(w). (2.4)

The first two cases are sqrt(beta0+1)L_1 and
(beta0+1)L_1^2+sqrt(beta0+1)L_2, up to fixed constants.
There is no permission to keep only L_1^k for a nonlinear minimum.

The stronger pointwise version of the new proof says

 ||D^k(F_n o Psi)(w)||_op^2
     <= B_k(w)^2 V_n(Psi(w)),
 V_n(U)=Var_(kappa_U)(C P^(n-1)B).                       (2.5)

The SAME V_n can be used for every derivative order, because the
normalized Gaussian density jets have mean zero. We know

 E_mu V_n=<B,P^(2n-1)(1-P)B> <=Var_mu(B)/(2n),            (2.6)

and pointwise V_n<=1/4. Consequently (2.5) is a valid pointwise
pullback bound, but using the factor1/n after averaging over a different
coarse law rho requires control of

                 E_rho B_k(w)^2 V_n(Psi(w)).             (2.7)

For example an actual pushforward domination Psi_*rho<=A mu and a
uniform B_k would give A sup B_k^2 times (2.6). A deterministic minimum
map from fewer coarse coordinates has image of Haar measure zero on
the fine configuration space and does NOT have such domination. Thus
(1.2) cannot simply be restricted to its image. An exact random inverse
record with original-law pushforward would retain the right measure,
but then its complete reconstruction derivatives and their correlation
with V_n remain in (2.7). None is supplied by (1.2) alone.

3. ACTUAL REFINEMENT GRADING AND THE HILBERT COUNT

The existing ordinary-prefix estimate is an estimate of individual fine
potentials before spatial summation. In current units, with h=M^-1,

 |a_e(w)|<=C h b,       |D a|<=C h^2 b,                  (3.1)

on a bounded complete current collar. The strict family and its Hölder
refinement are explicit in augmented_source_spatial_transport_turn70.txt
(1.2)--(1.3), and the native homogeneous source-only chart comparison is
in turn75_classical_native_history.txt Section3. A current carrier H
has O(h^-4 |H|) fine link reads. Therefore the implication of (3.1)
for the NEW norm is only

 ||a(w)||_l2(fine) <= C h^-1 b sqrt(|H|).                 (3.2)

This is the four-dimensional count h^-4 against TWO powers of h; it
is not the four-power curvature/action cancellation of the classical
Wilson reference. Inserting (3.2) in (2.1) gives

             exp[C(beta0+1) h^-2 b^2 |H|].              (3.3)

For a fixed H, a fixed O(1) bound by this estimate needs
b=O(h/sqrt(beta0+1)). A fixed native core radius does not satisfy that
as M grows. A current running radius g_j(log g_j^-2)^Q also has no
automatic such extra factor h. Its own-domain admission in TURN79 is
a different theorem and does not identify (3.3) with a small source
activity. Even for fixed H, a prefactor growing as exp(c M^2) is not
paid by a fixed decrease of the carrier exponent in (1.3).

For finite derivatives, fixed-radius Cauchy applied to the original
strict prefix gives a bound of the form

 L_r <= C_r h^-1 sqrt(|H|) b0^(1-r)                     (3.4)

when coarse directions use the UNSCALED coordinate supremum norm on
the radius-b0 domain. They are not normalized by division by b0.
This displays the factors entering (2.4); its product of r=1 terms is
already of order [(beta0+1)^(1/2) h^-1 sqrt(|H|)]^k.
Equations(3.2)--(3.4) are norm consumers at the declared classical map,
not a reconstruction theorem for unresolved mixed/raw histories.

4. A SHARP NONFLAT EXAMPLE FOR THE ABSOLUTE KERNEL MAJORANT

The h^-2 exponent in (3.3) is attained by the Gaussian TV norm itself.
Take a unit four-dimensional box subdivided into M^4 cells, and use
commuting links, with T=sigma_3/2,

 U_2(x;t)=exp(i T t x_1/M^2),
 U_mu(x;t)=Id for mu!=2.                                 (4.1)

Here x_1 is the integer fine coordinate between0 andM. For REAL t the
elementary12 plaquette has holonomy exp(iT t/M^2), so this is a genuine
nonflat constant-Cartan-curvature field, not a pure gauge. Its links
have size O(t/M), its curvature O(t/M^2), exactly the grading (3.1).
The complete boundary values can be retained. The real field is
stationary for interior Wilson variations: opposite12 plaquette
forces are equal and cancel, and the other forces vanish. This
observation is not an identification of arbitrary Bałaban boundary
data with this particular field, nor a needed uniqueness theorem.

Continue t to i b, b>0 small and fixed. For a12 plaquette at x, its two
length-two path matrices contain respectively U_2(x+e_1;i b) and
U_2(x;i b). The complexified REAL quaternion-vector coordinate of
their mean m_p is

 (sqrt(beta0)/2) i [sinh(b(x_1+1)/(2M^2))
                         +sinh(b x_1/(2M^2))].           (4.2)

The Frobenius real-coordinate norm of a quaternion matrix carries only
a fixed factor2. On c M^4 plaquettes with x_1>=M/2, (4.2) has magnitude
at least c sqrt(beta0)b/M. Consequently

                 ||Im m||^2 >= c beta0 b^2 M^2.         (4.3)

The exact Gaussian TV formula(2.3) of the new producer has determinant
factor>=1 and nonnegative further exponent. Hence

           ||kappa_(i b)||_TV >= exp(c beta0 b^2 M^2).   (4.4)

All per-link imaginary angles are O(b/M); the small per-link Gaussian
domain is satisfied. Thus the volume-independent l2 majorant cannot be
upgraded to a fixed coarse b supremum by dropping the fine read count.

This lower bound is for the absolute auxiliary kernel. A particular
gauge-invariant C B can cancel large complex contributions, so (4.4)
does NOT prove that F_n o Psi, its physical derivatives, or a Wilson
correlation diverges. It sharply identifies the loss of the available
source-independent proof. Indeed pure complex gauge examples would be
misleading here because gauge-invariant sources may cancel them exactly;
the field(4.1) deliberately has nonzero curvature.

5. PHYSICAL UNITS: FOUR-DIMENSIONAL VERSUS SINGLE-SLICE READS

Fix a smooth physical profile A on a bounded d-dimensional support,
sampled at spacing a. In the physical-potential convention its fine
link angle is z_e=g_a a A_mu(x_e). Riemann summation gives

 ||z||_2^2 = O(g_a^2 a^(2-d)||A||_L2^2),                 (5.1)

with a matching lower order for a fixed nonzero profile. Assume beta0
is bounded below by a fixed positive constant (in particular this holds
in the intended beta0->infinity regime), and beta0 g_a^2 is bounded
above and below by positive constants. Then (beta0+1)g_a^2 is also
uniformly comparable to1, and (1.1) costs
exp[C a^(2-d)||Im A||_L2^2]. A uniform physical Hilbert radius from this
estimate is O(a^((d-2)/2)): O(a) for d=4 and O(sqrt(a)) for d=3.
This calibration is explicit; omitting g_a would change the scaling.

For a finite-dimensional span of fixed smooth physical profiles the
linear sampling map has operator norm O(g_a a^(1-d/2)). Applying the
new annealed estimate to these DETERMINISTIC ORIGINAL-law directions,
at U still distributed according to mu, gives

 E_mu ||D_A^k F_n||_op^2
       <= C_k (beta0+1)^k g_a^(2k) a^(k(2-d))/n.
                                                               (5.2)

With n of order a^-1 allowed by a fixed temporal buffer, this is
O_k(a^(1-2k)) for d=4 and O_k(a^(1-k)) for d=3, up to the displayed
coupling constants. Already the four-dimensional first-derivative
upper bound grows as a^-1; a three-dimensional first-derivative bound
is only O(1). These are UPPER-BOUND scaling failures, not lower bounds
on the actual source jets. The d=3 statement is for variations confined
to one original slice. After buffered smoothing the observable reads a
time block; a general physical variation on that block is d=4.

Equation(5.2) keeps the original-law average. Replacing U by a minimum
Psi(A) is additionally subject to (2.7). For nonlinear physical or
native reconstructions, the higher L_r in (2.4) also remain. Linear
sampling alone cannot certify those terms.

6. WHAT WOULD HAVE TO BE SUPPLIED TO ENTER THE NATIVE PROOF

There is a concrete sufficient entry for a GIVEN native coefficient:
an actual common reconstruction on its entire admitted domain whose
fine-link imaginary l2 distortion satisfies (2.2), plus a supported
coefficient expansion having the strong sum(1.3). The new theorem
then supplies its normalized source supremum and fixed-order jets.
These are specific quantities; no auxiliary current cutoff is needed.
But the available generic refinement bounds give (3.3), not (2.2).

The sharp classical reference succeeds by another mechanism: its
per-fine-plaquette norm is O(h^4 b^2), and h^-4 plaquettes per current
cell cancel that cost. This is explicitly proved in
augmented_source_classical_contraction_turn70.txt Section2. Gauge/Ward
and curvature structure, not an l2 norm of all fine potentials, supply
that improvement. A bounded nonlinear probe smoothed by P has not yet
been given an analogous source-specific supported curvature estimate.

Nor does finite read support alone supply (1.3). A single coefficient
on a read of diameter n can be filed on its connected hull, but its
weight is e^(a|H|); for n=O(a^-1) buffered rounds that hull can contain
O(a^-4) fine cells. A bound on the total analytic function, or on its
Hilbert operator jets, does not furnish exponentially summable
carrier/root coefficients. Existing near/far native preparation starts
with that source family; it does not create it from (1.1).

Finally, a physical coarse restriction does not prescribe independent
outer J dependence or simultaneous complex old-record contours. Those
interfaces are unchanged. The new analytic theorem is useful as a
genuine starting source on the original real configuration and as a
dimension-free smoothing estimate, but these explicit pullback and
localization costs prevent declaring it an already admitted Bałaban
all-scale source. No frozen producer or authoritative output was edited.
