RESULT

For the actual SU(2) Wilson measure and the original coherently weighted
Wilson-energy sources, there is an explicit finite nested conditional
hierarchy in which every arriving source is its EXACT compiled conditional
mean and has a geometrically bounded boundary read. The conditional mixed
covariance is identically zero until one integrated block meets BOTH
original source supports. This is a nonempty original-source class with
all normalization and source-mask factors explicit, not a replacement by
a newly selected static near source.

The kernels constructed here are block-interior conditionals of the
original Wilson law. They are not identified with Balaban's B119 ordinary
or LII reset kernels. They provide an alternative exact covariance/source
decomposition for the same original observables. No uniform bound on the
remaining coarse-source variance, boundary mixing, continuum limit or
interacting theory is proved.

1. ORIGINAL LAW, ORIGINAL SOURCES, AND DETERMINISTIC BLOCKS

Use a finite four-dimensional periodic lattice of spacing a, with at
least four sites in each direction, normalized independent SU(2) link
Haar measures, and

 h_p(U)=1-(1/2)Tr U_p in [0,2],
 dmu=Z^(-1) exp[-t sum_p h_p(U)] product_l dU_l, t>=0.

For two fixed actual source profiles put

 O_f=c_f sum_p f_p h_p,          O_g=c_g sum_p g_p h_p.       (1)

The source weights are the original deterministic coherent plaquette
weights, including their actual sampling collar; no new current-grid
sampling or a^4 factor is inserted. The original c_f,c_g calibrations
stay explicit. The argument permits any finite deterministic real or
complex weights; real weights give the sharp elementary oscillation
bound below. There are no random source selectors or RG history masks
in this class.

Choose deterministic nested cubical partitions with side lengths
m_0=1, m_1,...,m_J in fine-lattice units, each m_(j+1) an integer
multiple of m_j. For simplicity the periods are multiples of all m_j,
with at least two level-J blocks in each periodic direction. This is a
nonempty class at every arbitrarily fine compatible lattice. More general
rectangular nested partitions work with their actual diameters retained.
Write ell_j=m_j a. A fixed common grid origin suffices; no field or source
value chooses a boundary.

For a level-j block B define I_j(B) to be the original links both of
whose endpoints are STRICTLY inside B. Put

 I_j=disjoint union_B I_j(B),      S_j=all links minus I_j.    (2)

Thus S_j consists of links with an endpoint on the level-j cubical
grid boundary; S_0 is the full original link set and

                         S_(j+1) subset S_j.

Every elementary plaquette contains interior links of at most ONE
level-j block. To see this, a plaquette crossing a grid hyperplane has
all its links incident to that hyperplane in S_j; any other interior
links are on only one side of it. Unit plaquettes cannot cross two
parallel grid hyperplanes when m_j>=2. The m_0=1 case has no interior
links. The same reasoning applies at intersections of hyperplanes.

Let P_j(B) be plaquettes containing at least one link of I_j(B), and
let P_j^S contain the remaining plaquettes (all links in S_j). These
sets partition the ORIGINAL plaquettes without repetition. Each
p in P_j(B) lies in the closed geometric cube B. Define the complete
boundary read

 partial_j B = {links in S_j occurring in a p in P_j(B)}.     (3)

It lies in the closed cube and includes the necessary boundary-to-
interior spokes. It is not merely the tangential boundary links.

2. EXACT BLOCK CONDITIONALS, INCLUDING THEIR NORMALIZERS

For fixed skeleton U_(S_j)=v define

 H_j,B(u;v)=sum_(p in P_j(B)) h_p(u,v),
 Z_j,B(v)=integral_(SU(2)^I_j(B)) exp[-t H_j,B(u;v)] du,
 Q_j,B(v,du)=Z_j,B(v)^(-1) exp[-t H_j,B(u;v)] du.          (4)

Here du is the ORIGINAL product Haar measure, and Z_j,B reads only
partial_j B. It is finite and strictly positive for every boundary:
exp[-2t|P_j(B)|]<=Z_j,B<=1. This elementary lower bound is used ONLY
to establish definition, never as a purported useful volume-uniform
estimate for the source.

The exact conditional law of all interior links, given S_j, is
the product of the Q_j,B. Their boundary reads may overlap; those
shared coordinates are HELD and do not spoil conditional independence.
Gauge fixing, an empty-branch replacement and a new reference law are
unnecessary. This follows directly by splitting the original plaquette
action according to P_j^S and the P_j(B).

The actual skeleton marginal is therefore

 dmu_j(v)=Z^(-1) exp[-t sum_(p in P_j^S)h_p(v)]
                         product_B Z_j,B(v) dHaar_(S_j)(v). (5)

Each block mass occurs ONCE. The same global Z is retained. Equation
(5) is generally correlated; the product of local masses does not
make the skeleton links independent.

3. COMPILED SOURCES HAVE AN EXACT FINITE SPATIAL READ

Write O_f=O_f,j^S+sum_B O_f,j,B using the same plaquette partition,
with ORIGINAL f_p and c_f in each part. Define

 f_j(v)=E_mu[O_f | U_(S_j)=v]
       =O_f,j^S(v)+sum_B Q_j,B O_f,j,B(v).                (6)

This is the actual compiled mean, including the full local conditional
normalizer in (4). It is not an evaluation at a zero-noise field or a
minimum. A direct finite definition of every local source term is

 Q_j,B O_f,j,B
   = d/ds log Z_j,B(s;v)|_(s=0),

 Z_j,B(s;v)=integral exp[-t H_j,B+s O_f,j,B]du.            (7)

The derivative is legitimate on compact integration space at finite
cutoff. The physical normalization remains Z_j,B(0;v). No global
source-zero-free complex partition assertion is used; finite formal
jets or differentiation at real zero suffice.

Let K_f be the union of full geometric plaquettes with f_p!=0. An
explicit complete read of f_j is

 R_j(f)= {links of p in P_j^S with f_p!=0}
          union union_(B:P_j(B) meets supp(f)) partial_j B.  (8)

It follows pointwise that v=v' on R_j(f) implies f_j(v)=f_j(v').
Every read in (8) is within L-infinity distance at most ell_j from K_f;
for Euclidean distance the corresponding block diameter is 2ell_j.
No fine-depth factor enters this physical radius.

The number of retained coordinates is NOT uniformly bounded. For a
cube of side m>=2, its boundary/spoke link count is at most

 4[m(m+1)^3-(m-2)(m-1)^3] <= C(m+1)^3.                  (9)

Hence |R_j(f)| is bounded by C(m_j+1)^3 times the number of source-
touched blocks, plus the actual skeleton-source plaquette reads. This
is the explicit spatial/read cost. At fixed physical ell_j it can
grow like a^(-3); it is not a finite collection of Balaban coarse
variables or a uniform analytic norm.

There is no nonempty-source matching gap here: (6) applies to EVERY
nonzero original coherent profile, including profiles supported in
two disjoint physical boxes. It follows from the original Wilson
action and its actual source, before any retired-source catalogue.

4. AN EXACT NORMALIZED RECURSIVE CONSTRUCTOR

Let T_j,B=S_j intersect I_(j+1)(B) be the fine-skeleton links internal
to one level-(j+1) block B. Condition the level-j marginal on S_(j+1).
Different T_j,B are independent under that conditional law.

Here is its complete unnormalized density, rather than an implicit
appeal to a conditional measure. Put

 R_j,B^P=P_(j+1)(B) intersect P_j^S,
 W_j,B(w;v)=exp[-t sum_(p in R_j,B^P) h_p(w,v)]
                 product_(b level-j block inside B) Z_j,b(w,v),

 K_j,B(v,dw)=W_j,B(w;v) dHaar_(T_j,B)(w) / Z_(j+1),B(v). (10)

Indeed P_(j+1)(B) is the disjoint union of R_j,B^P and the P_j(b)
of its fine subblocks. Fubini gives the EXACT normalization

       integral W_j,B(w;v) dw = Z_(j+1),B(v).             (11)

The original action on P_(j+1)^S is held outside. Every fine subblock
mass in (10) is integrated once, and every source-zero plaquette is
present once. There is no inverse probability of a survival event.

Define F_j=product_B K_j,B, acting on level-j tests and producing
level-(j+1) tests. Equations (5),(10),(11) imply

 mu_(j+1)F_j=mu_j,              F_j f_j=f_(j+1).          (12)

Thus (6) is CLOSED UNDER EVERY FINITE STEP of this deterministic
hierarchy. This is a genuine compiled-source locality statement with
its actual state-dependent normalizers. Its read grows from (8) at
level j to (8) at level j+1. No static source replacement or class
qualification of an opaque conditional mean is involved.

5. THE MIXED ROW VANISHES UNTIL THE ORIGINAL SUPPORTS JOIN

For fixed j+1, decompose the level-j means according to their ORIGINAL
plaquettes in the coarse partition:

 f_j=O_f,(j+1)^S+sum_B f_j,B,
 f_j,B=E_mu[O_f,(j+1),B | S_j],                          (13)

and analogously for g. Formula (6) shows that f_j,B reads only T_j,B
and the HELD boundary partial_(j+1) B. In particular it is identically
zero if no original f-weighted plaquette belongs to P_(j+1)(B).

Conditional independence under (10) gives the exact identity

 C_j:=F_j(f_j g_j)-(F_j f_j)(F_j g_j)
      =sum_B Cov_(K_j,B)(f_j,B,g_j,B).                    (14)

Cross-block terms vanish even when the two local functions share held
boundary links. Each summand of (14) is zero unless that coarse block
contains original plaquettes from BOTH source supports. Therefore

 no B with P_(j+1)(B) meeting supp(f) AND supp(g)
                         ==> C_j=0 POINTWISE.            (15)

In particular dist_infinity(K_f,K_g)>ell_(j+1) suffices. In Euclidean
distance, dist_2(K_f,K_g)>2ell_(j+1) suffices. These distances use the
actual weighted plaquette supports including any source-sampling
collars, not only the nominal continuum support points.

The actual total-covariance telescope is

 Cov_mu(O_f,O_g)=sum_(j=0)^(J-1) mu_(j+1) C_j
                              +Cov_(mu_J)(f_J,g_J).      (16)

All early terms excluded by (15) vanish exactly, regardless of the
individual source variances. This is an exact merging-scale covariance
decomposition. It retains the terminal covariance and does not replace
it by an estimate for a fresh near row. One may equivalently eliminate
directly to any level k with blocks smaller than the separation and get

                   Cov_mu(O_f,O_g)=Cov_(mu_k)(f_k,g_k).   (17)

The covariance in (17) can be taken after subtracting each deterministic
mean, but no local or global variance bound follows just from doing so.

6. RAW SOURCE JETS, LOCAL CONTACTS, AND THEIR EXACT SUPPORT

At a fixed level j, introduce two affine ORIGINAL log-source marks:

 Z_j,B(s,u;v)=integral exp[-tH_j,B+sO_f,j,B+uO_g,j,B]du_B.

The exact conditional source germ is

 E[exp(sO_f+uO_g)|S_j]
 =exp(sO_f,j^S+uO_g,j^S)
                  product_B Z_j,B(s,u;v)/Z_j,B(0,0;v).  (18)

Its first rows are f_j,g_j. Its logarithmic mixed row is

 h_j(v)=sum_B Cov_(Q_j,B)(O_f,j,B,O_g,j,B).               (19)

Thus the raw mixed row is h_j+f_j g_j, with no second copy of the
product. From (12),(18) one recovers exactly

 h_(j+1)=F_j h_j+C_j.                                   (20)

Every block in (19) must meet both original source supports. If no
such block exists, h_j is identically zero. Independent contacts in
a different initial source would have to be inserted explicitly in
(18); none is manufactured or discarded for the original affine
Wilson source. The same formal-log argument at any fixed higher order
shows that a conditional connected block cumulant involving several
marks vanishes unless ONE block meets every participating original
source support. This is finite exact support, not a higher-moment bound.

The scalar part can be separated in an equally exact way. Fix a single
deterministic boundary reference v_B^* for each block, for example all
its boundary links equal to the identity. Replacing the local first
row (7) by

 d/ds log[Z_j,B(s;v)/Z_j,B(s;v_B^*)]|_0

subtracts a deterministic scalar only. It preserves every connected
covariance. It does NOT show that the remaining boundary-dependent
source is uniformly bounded, and this optional subtraction is not
silently performed in (6),(14), or (16).

7. EXPLICIT FINITE BOUNDS AND THE REMAINING ANALYTIC TARGET

For real source weights, the oscillation of O_f,j,B is at most
2|c_f| sum_(p in P_j(B))|f_p|. Conditional averaging does not enlarge
this interval. Hence the coarse-block terms in (14) obey the valid
but usually very crude bound

 |Cov_(K_j,B)(f_j,B,g_j,B)|
 <= |c_f c_g| [sum_(p in P_(j+1)(B))|f_p|]
                  [sum_(p in P_(j+1)(B))|g_p|].          (21)

For complex weights, replace the interval estimate by the elementary
sup/variance bound and its corresponding fixed factor. Equation (21)
keeps the actual original plaquette count. No new normalization or
contraction removes its a dependence. The induced boundary factors
Z_j,B are highly nontrivial functions and need not be weak interactions
under arbitrary boundary conditions.

The proved gain is that microscopic conditional variances and contacts
at scales below separation are NEVER paid against the other source:
they are exactly absent from (16). The next sufficient analytic input
is a bound on the surviving LOCAL block-boundary source functions and
their actual boundary law, or a separation-sensitive response/mixing
bound for the kernels (10), with their real coordinate count and full

8. A FULLY MATCHED BOUNDARY RESPONSE INTERFACE FOR THIS SOURCE CLASS

Unlike the counterfactual geometry-changing native reset comparison,
the block conditional (4) has one fixed compact Haar integration domain
for EVERY real boundary field. Let v(s) vary a set D of boundary links
smoothly, with fixed block, original plaquette assignment and profiles.
Differentiation under this finite bounded integral gives exactly

 d/ds[Q_j,B(v(s)) O_f,j,B(v(s))]
   =Q_j,B dot O_f,j,B
                 -t Cov_(Q_j,B)(O_f,j,B, dot H_j,B).      (22)

The score dot H_j,B is the literal sum of derivatives of the original
plaquettes in P_j(B) incident to D. The centered covariance includes
the derivative of the exact Z_j,B; no partition-denominator response
is omitted. If the f-weighted plaquettes miss D, the direct source
term is ZERO. The remaining response is a source-to-boundary mixed
correlation under this specific original block law, with no moving
cuts, constraints, private histories or unmatched source extension.

For one boundary link l with velocity X=(d v_l/ds)v_l^(-1), choose the
standard fixed SU(2) Lie-algebra norm. Each incident plaquette obeys
|dot h_p|<=C||X||, and at most six original plaquettes are incident.
For real weights the elementary oscillation/Cauchy bound therefore
gives the explicit finite estimate

 |d/ds Q_j,B O_f,j,B|
 <= C |c_f| ||X|| [sum_(p in P_j(B), p contains l)|f_p|
                            +t sum_(p in P_j(B))|f_p|].  (23)

Sum the right side over varied links for a general D and integrate
along a real boundary path. This proves ordinary finite boundary
continuity with a cost local to the actual block, uniformly in the
surrounding volume. It is intentionally not advertised as a useful
cutoff-uniform bound: its source plaquette count and t remain visible.

An improvement replacing the second sum in (23) by a calibrated
source-to-D spatial kernel for the ACTUAL covariance in (22) is a
concrete remaining analytic target. The coordinates, source, action,
normalizer, support and direct-derivative term are all fixed here.
One does not need to establish that an arbitrary compiled B or neutral
prefix secretly belongs to this class before using that target.
