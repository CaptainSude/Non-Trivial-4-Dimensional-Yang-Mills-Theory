STRATEGIC RESET: PROTECTED SCALAR SOURCES AND SEPARATED REAL PRESSURE
19 October 2026. Nontrivial four-dimensional Yang--Mills remains unproved.

DECISION

Do not make closure of the full 21-component, arbitrary-profile source
catalogue the next prerequisite for every existence attempt. Preserve
turns1--29, including their scoped proofs and counterexamples, as an
available route. The new immediate test is a physical scalar response
in the ORIGINAL Wilson probability law, written as a cross ratio of
real locally deformed partition functions.

This is a change of proof target. It is not a claim that source-response
estimates have become unnecessary, or that the scalar sector alone is
the complete nonabelian Yang--Mills theory.

1. THE CONCRETE CLUE IN THE PUBLIC REPOSITORY

The current public tree contains the zeroth draft and three raw accounts.
Its scalar action source is treated as a shift of the inverse coupling.
Its driven scalar-flow argument gives bounded renormalized response
when the corresponding beta/forcing derivative errors are summable.
That protection is stronger than the generic polynomial bound used in
our arbitrary tensor calibration. It does not require all 21 directions
to be simultaneously invertible just to construct a scalar witness.

The E(w)+log h representation also separates local vacuum contributions
from the remaining normalized factor. For two separated supports, the
mixed pressure difference cancels every term confined to either support.
The useful idea is to retain that cancellation before taking norms.

PUBLIC PROOF STATUS MATTERS. The elementary driven-flow lemma is proved
in the raw account, but its complete-source/domain inputs are separate.
The public correlation theorem expressly depends on preparatory-window
and decoupling-holomorphy propositions. The later draft refers to an
on-request companion for their completion. No unavailable proof has
been imported here. This is an architectural clue, not verification
of the announced construction.

2. AN EXACT SMALLER NONTRIVIALITY TARGET

Choose one real gauge-invariant positive-half action observable F_a,
with support a fixed positive physical distance from its reflected
partner theta F_a. Its normalization must be the actual controlled
scalar coupling normalization, common across all correlations.
Set, in the ORIGINAL normalized finite Wilson law,

 Z_a(s,t)=E_a exp(sF_a+t theta F_a),
 p_a(s,t)=log Z_a(s,t),
 D_a(t)=p_a(t,t)+p_a(-t,-t)-p_a(t,-t)-p_a(-t,t).

All four partition functions are real positive. Reflection positivity
gives D_a(t)>=0. Every additive term depending on just s or just t
cancels exactly. If C_a(s,t)=partial_s partial_t p_a(s,t), then

 D_a(t)=integral_[-t,t]^2 C_a(u,v) du dv.

If ||Hessian C_a||<=M on a fixed real square, uniformly in the cutoff,
then the proved finite Taylor identity gives

 |D_a(t)/(4t^2)-Cov_a(F_a,theta F_a)|<=M t^2/3.       (2.1)

Thus finite-t separation plus a fourth-order REAL response estimate
can establish a scalar two-point lower bound without first proving a
common complex disc for all marked tensor descendants.

An explicit Gaussian-comparison target is the following. Let C_G>0 be
the reflected free comparison covariance with the SAME physical/source
normalization. Prove, for one t independent of the UV cutoff,

 D_a(t)/(4t^2)>=3 C_G/4,       M t^2/3<=C_G/4.        (2.2)

Then Cov_a(F_a,theta F_a)>=C_G/2. This criterion is not circular: it
asks for a finite real partition-function comparison and a quantitative
Taylor remainder, rather than assuming the desired covariance itself.
Neither estimate in (2.2) has yet been proved for the interacting YM law.

There is an equivalent useful physical direction: at a site-reflection
plane, the normalized half partition function is a random amplitude
R_t(sigma) under the true boundary law. Its relative variance equals
exp[p_a(t,t)-2p_a(t,0)]-1. A nonzero response of one bounded boundary
test under a one-half tilt supplies a lower bound. It uses the actual
positive boundary law, not a signed RG history or reference Gaussian.

Proofs: reflection_pressure_route_turn30.txt.

3. THE NEW TARGET PASSES AN EXACT FREE ULTRAVIOLET TEST

For a Gaussian curvature covariance with ||C||<=1, separated nonnegative
quadratic sources have covariance blocks A,B,D, with ||Ctilde||<=M.
Their cross-pressure G satisfies the exact determinant formula

 exp G(s,t)=det[I-4st(I-2tB)^-1 D*(I-2sA)^-1D]^(-1/2).

For |s|,|t|<=rho, rho M<1/4,

 |G(s,t)|<=2|st| ||D||_HS^2/(1-4rho M).

For s,t>=0 also G(s,t)>=2st||D||_HS^2. In four dimensions the curvature
projection kernel between separated compact regions is square-integrable,
so ||D||_HS is finite; a nonzero kernel gives a strict lower bound.
Strong diagonal and Hilbert--Schmidt off-diagonal convergence imply
trace-norm convergence of the determinant argument.

Meanwhile the ordinary centered F^2 variance diverges like a^-4. The
cross-pressure therefore avoids a norm requirement that was already
false in the free comparison model. This is a proved benchmark for a
different target, not a Yang--Mills comparison estimate in disguise.

Proof: separated_gaussian_defect_pressure_turn30.txt.

4. WHAT THE BROADER LITERATURE DOES AND DOES NOT SAVE

The algebraic Euclidean-net route can postpone constructing all point
fields. Schlingemann's continuum-state theorem still needs a nontrivial
normalized correlation input, and its weak Euclidean conclusion does
not automatically supply rotations. Reconstruction also has regularity
and time-zero requirements. The route is available if a suitable bounded
physical observable can be controlled; it is not supplied by ultraviolet
partition stability alone.

Two explicit checks prevent attractive false shortcuts:
 (a) full spacetime heat flow can violate reflection positivity already
     for a free Gaussian field; commuting with reflection is insufficient;
 (b) translation averaging a thin four-dimensional Abelian Wilson loop
     can still tend to zero in L2, because of its perimeter self-energy.

Spatial-only smoothing with the actual half-space support property does
preserve reflection positivity at finite cutoff. It is a legitimate
backup family, but its nonzero continuum reflected norm is unproved.
No nonperturbative four-dimensional theorem was found that supplies it.

Primary sources and full counterproofs:
alternative_continuum_routes_turn30.txt.

5. WHY THIS IS NOT YET AN EXISTENCE PROOF

Local vacuum cancellation does not cancel source-dependent field
counterterms, prove the running-coupling assumptions, or establish the
needed interacting normalized estimate. Source-free pressure asymptotics
and convexity allow models with completely vanishing separated response.

Even a scalar covariance lower bound requires compatible joint limits
and Euclidean reconstruction before it supplies a continuum scalar
theory. Identification of the nonabelian Yang--Mills observable algebra
and its short-distance behavior is a further obligation. The earlier
SU(2) Gram/oriented-cubic counterexample still applies.

The next substantive calculation should attack (2.2), or its exact
boundary-amplitude version, for the true Wilson law at a fixed physical
weak-coupling scale. A proof may reuse parts of the old source work, but
we should not declare the entire old catalogue necessary by default.
If only the same unproved full complex-source theorem can produce this
bound, record that honestly instead of counting a new formulation as
a solved obstacle. Preserve both successes and counterexamples.
