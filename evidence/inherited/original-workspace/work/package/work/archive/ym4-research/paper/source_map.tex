\subsection{Imported results and verification limits}

The local source input is taken from \cite{BalabanMinima,BalabanRGI,
BalabanRGII,BalabanConvergent,BalabanLargeI,BalabanLargeII}. Their domains,
ordering of parameters and normalization are part of each invocation.
The study of the changed preparation slots used primary text extraction
of the later large-field paper, together with pinned transcriptions of
earlier formulas. The full primary display and margins of
\cite{BalabanLargeI}, (1.73), and the cited local use of (1.89), were not
verified in that audit. They are not silently asserted as checked inputs.
Source transcription and source application are different obligations.

The Gaussian sum-rule coefficient cited from \cite{HarnettSteele} is a
scalar quotient calculation, not the required unintegrated local OPE
with all spectators. The operator classification in \cite{Gracey} is
used with its equation-of-motion and derivative conventions. The
invariant theory in \cite{DomokosDrensky} concerns polynomial symbols;
it does not construct their quantum-field realizations.
