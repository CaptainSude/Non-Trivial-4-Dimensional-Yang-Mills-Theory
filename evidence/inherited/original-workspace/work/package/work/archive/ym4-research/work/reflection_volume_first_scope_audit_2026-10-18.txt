FIXED-COUPLING REFLECTION ASSEMBLY AND THE ACTUAL STOPPING REGULATORS
18 October 2026. Independent scope/limit audit. Working note only.

Verdict: the fixed-beta, volume-first scope of reflection_assembly_audit.md
CAN be matched to the actual first-passage regulators. This does not
require volume-independent RG coefficients. The tilt-removal and fixed-
target convex-separation steps also pass the audit, CONDITIONAL on the
full-array fold, operator insertion bound, and paired local reduction.
Those three substantive inputs are being audited separately. No diagonal
RP of a finite straight torus is assumed here.

1. FINITE PREFIX, THEN VOLUME, THEN ULTRAVIOLET

Fix the source scheme, odd blocking factor L, grouping q, weak floor,
uniform primitive increment bound D, and aligned decrement d>0. Choose
the terminal threshold H>Gamma+2qD. The finite-phase theorem supplies,
on every sufficiently large compatible coordinate torus, the actual
homogeneous flow starting at gamma_0=A>H. Its first aligned crossing N
of gamma_N<H satisfies

  H-qD <= gamma_N < H,
  (A-H)/D < N <= q[1+(A-H)/d].                         (1)

The strict endpoint inequalities can be replaced by weak ones without
affecting the construction. Put n_max=q ceil(1+(A-H)/d).

At this FIXED A, consider original coordinate tori of side

  n_M=2 L^M,             M -> infinity.                (2)

Choose the first M large enough to support all source partitions and
collars through n_max, not only the as-yet unknown stopping date. The
source flow remains the actual finite-torus flow; its coefficients and
stopping date may depend on M. Since N(A,M) lies in the finite integer
set {q,2q,...,n_max}, an infinite subsequence has

  N(A,M)=N_A.                                           (3)

Assign physical mesh epsilon_A=L^{-N_A} on this subsequence. It is fixed
while M tends to infinity, and n_M epsilon_A tends to infinity. By
compactness of the compact-group product space, a further subsequence
of the ORIGINAL Wilson measures has a local weak limit nu_A. The bare
Wilson coupling is fixed throughout this limit: beta is the fixed
normalization multiple of A used by the chosen Wilson convention.

Consequently any fixed-beta theorem applying to EVERY local limit of
the coordinate tori (2) applies to nu_A. It is irrelevant that the RG
coordinates used to establish the source estimates varied along this
subsequence. No RG density replaces the physical Wilson measure in
this step.

The source bounds at the terminal band are uniform on these regulators
by the finite-prefix theorem's hypotheses. A smeared observable with
fixed compact physical support is, at fixed epsilon_A, a bounded
function of finitely many links. Its moments, centered covariances,
and finite polynomial RP tests therefore pass to nu_A by local weak
convergence. If an argument also uses the separate analytic universal
counterterms, their bounded finite-source germs may be extracted on
smaller polydiscs. This is an auxiliary normal-family extraction; it
does not require their equality at different volumes. Bounds proved
directly for the restored physical moments require no counterterm
limit at all.

Now take A_l -> infinity, AFTER constructing each nu_{A_l}. Equation
(1) implies N_{A_l}->infinity, hence epsilon_{A_l}->0. The terminal
inverse couplings stay in [H-qD,H]. The established continuum moment
compactness can then be applied to this volume-first family. All
reflection inequalities already valid for each nu_{A_l} pass through
the appropriate test-function convergence. No estimate uniform in A
is needed from the fixed-beta reflection proof itself.

This construction establishes admissible regulators and limit order,
not the source flow estimates or the reflection theorem on its own.

2. SOURCE DIVISIBILITY AND THE ODD-L GEOMETRY

The relevant source partitions have sides that are powers of the SAME
L: M_1,M_2,M and the rounded R_i are L-powers. The repository's literal
torus-floor definition is Raw II, Definition 5.1, lines 682--715 and
the subsequent divisibility paragraph: N_T is an arbitrary positive
integer, the terminal site count is N_T L^m, and the floor is a lower
bound on the L-power m. The side 100 M R_i is a component size bound,
not a divisibility condition. Thus at fixed A and finite n_max all
required powers divide 2L^{M-j} once M is sufficiently large. Bounded
primitive increments give a deterministic finite range of gamma_i
through this prefix, so the rounded radii also have a deterministic
finite upper bound before any volume subsequence is chosen.

For the old reflection geometry, h,b,H_aux are fixed powers of L,
w=b/2, and the adapted period lattice is

  <(n_M/2)e_-, n_M e_+, n_M e_3, n_M e_4>.

For large M, h|b|H_aux|n_M/2. The oddness of b makes w a strict
half-integer, so the coordinate wall family alternates site and link
planes. All slab counts in two_frame_geometry_research.md (7),(19)
are even. No requirement that 4 divide n_M is introduced. The source
partitions are used ONLY on the original coordinate tori; the adapted
and straightened comparison tori are used only in a fixed-beta Wilson
fold and pressure comparison. They require no source RG theorem.

3. THE WITNESS CAN BE KEPT AT ONE PHYSICAL SCALE

Choose a fixed large witness threshold Gamma_w>H and its compact
first-crossing band. At fixed A, the witness date s(A,M) belongs to
another finite set, so the volume subsequence can also make it
constant. The integer offset k=N-s is bounded above and below by
constants depending only on Gamma_w,H,q,d,D, since both the witness
and terminal couplings lie in fixed bands and aligned drift is
positive. Thus after A_l->infinity another subsequence makes k fixed.

A witness packet with radius rho=Gamma_w^B in witness lattice units
then has the SAME physical radius rho L^{-k} along this UV subsequence.
Its universal positive lower bound is uniform over the witness coupling
band, fine refinement and lattice phase. Fix the packet profile before
these extractions. One need not choose a new physical observable at
each ultraviolet cutoff. The same finite-offset extraction applies to
the early-prefix endpoint and the finite set of regulator phases used
by the Gaussian comparison.

4. TILT REMOVAL: THE ORDER OF QUANTIFIERS IS ADEQUATE

Fix A and an ORIGINAL coordinate-torus cluster limit nu, then a real
gauge-invariant local polynomial O. For each t>0 first take the adapted
tilted volume limit lambda_t along a subsequence of the SAME original
volume sequence. The full-array comparison gives

  nu(O) <= lambda_t(O).                                (4)

The pressure defect vanishes because all auxiliary lengths are fixed
before the volume limit. At finite A the interaction is bounded and
finite range. Removing plaquettes crossing a boundary changes log Z
by at most a fixed beta-dependent constant times their number. This
also explains why the required finite bounded-aspect family has a
common qualitative pressure limit; a UV-uniform rate is unnecessary.

The amplification conclusion for each fixed admissible operator X is

  |lambda_t(G_X)| <= exp(C_X t) ||X||.                  (5)

Choose t_l down to zero and a weak cluster limit lambda of lambda_{t_l}.
For EACH fixed X, (5) holds for every cluster subsequence at its t_l,
and C_X is finite. Therefore the SAME lambda satisfies the norm bound
for all X. There is no illicit exchange of a support-dependent constant
with a supremum over X. One may instead extract a countable dense test
family and extend, but that is not essential to this pointwise logic.

The paired local reduction has error epsilon+C_epsilon t. Keep epsilon
and the corresponding finite operator approximants fixed until AFTER
t->0. Then take the approximation limit and epsilon->0. This order
permits divergent operator norms along the approximation sequence.
It gives invariance at the eight fixed clean link planes of the source
array, provided the two-layer operator conclusion and paired reduction
are valid. Their adjacent-plane compositions give 2Z^4 invariance.

The source array need not be invariant at a clean coordinate plane:
the paired reduction is applied to both reflected observables with the
same tilted source law and an O(t) error. Invariance at the target
diagonal wall, and positivity there, come from the adapted construction.

5. THE FIXED TARGET IN CONVEX SEPARATION REALLY STAYS FIXED

Fix one lattice-preserving diagonal reflection theta. Let R_theta be
the weakly compact convex set of gauge-invariant laws that are invariant
and RP at theta. Positivity inequalities are closed affine conditions
for each fixed continuous local half-space observable. One may impose
extra closed conditions, but they are not needed for separation.

Given O, choose an EVEN translation a so that the support of tau_a O
lies on the required side of theta, then choose the cell width and the
other wall offsets to enclose it. This is possible for any finite
support. Construct lambda in R_theta for the TRANSLATED observable.
The original nu is fully translation invariant and lambda is even-
translation invariant. Hence

  nu(O)=nu(tau_a O) <= lambda(tau_a O)=lambda(O).        (6)

One does NOT translate lambda back; doing that could move its RP plane.
The state in (6) remains in the fixed set R_theta. Although lambda may
depend on O, (6) for all O implies nu belongs to R_theta by compact
convex separation. Continuous cylinder functions are uniformly dense
on the compact product; finite endpoint gauge averaging and then
Peter--Weyl approximation reduce any separator to a real local gauge-
invariant polynomial. Thus testing only this class loses nothing for
the gauge-invariant probability laws at issue.

The same argument can be repeated for each lattice diagonal plane.
It does not average states reflected in different planes and does not
infer RP from equality of free energies alone.

6. SCOPE AND DEPENDENCY VERDICT

PASS: first-passage volume subsequence, original 2L^M arithmetic,
source/witness limit order, support-dependent tilt removal, and fixed-
target convex separation. There is no new scope obstruction between a
correct fixed-beta same-state theorem and the actual source regulators.

STILL TO BE ESTABLISHED BY THE PARALLEL AUDITS: the full-array twisted
fold; its operator insertion/Schatten implementation; and the local

does not certify those inputs. If they hold, the finite straight-torus
nonseparating-mirror observation does not contradict the resulting

the regulator construction nor pressure equality repairs that failure.
