1. PRO1: WHAT ITS POSITIVE PROBE DOES

The construction is for a massive doubled THREE-dimensional free Dirac
operator and a real Abelian connection. Its stated large-mass expansion
is, with S_M=(M+gamma.dot partial)^-1 and a smooth compact vector field,

 q_(4,M)(a)=-1/4 Tr(S_M i gamma.dot a)^4
          =(240 pi M^5)^-1 int |curl a|^4 + O_a(M^-7).    (1.1)

The full remainder proof is assigned to the unavailable PRM1. The
outline specifies a mass derivative, a five-propagator diagonal,
local gauge cancellation and a constant-field heat-kernel coefficient.
Its displayed derivative leading coefficient -1/(48 pi M^6) is
consistent with differentiating (1.1). This consistency check does
not verify the complete trace or remainder argument.

The dilation consequence CAN be checked directly, conditional on (1.1).
For a_l(x)=l a(x/l), unitary dilation sends the inverse to l S_(Ml)
and the multiplication operator to l i gamma.dot a. Their product
therefore gains l^2 and its fourth trace gains l^8. Since the ordinary
fourth log derivative is b=24q_4, it follows that

 b(f_l)=l^3/(10 pi M^5) int |curl a|^4 + O_a(l M^-7).

For a fixed nonzero seed curvature, this is positive for sufficiently
large l. The seed is chosen before the interacting comparison; a
positive reference coefficient is not itself a comparison estimate.

The chamber approximation is a separate branch. It retains the
positive free mass, its precise 192-component regulator, antiperiodic
spinors, source sampling and all phase contact terms. The claimed
mesh comparison has error O(eta^(1/4)); the volume error is Yukawa
exponential. The second coefficient needs the cancellation of a
two-vertex term with its contact term before either is bounded.
Ordinary and logarithmic coefficients obey c4=b_ch+3 c2^2; only the
combination c4-3 c2^2 is compared with the continuum fourth log trace.

Useful YM4 lesson: select a concrete positive physical reference
signal and compare the EXACT normalized cumulant, keeping contacts.
Limitation: a linear Abelian field-strength probe and its fermion
determinant are not the non-Abelian pure-gauge observables in our
project. No valid substitution turns (1.1) into a positive SU(2) YM4
continuum channel. Even a nonzero local composite cumulant would need
its relation to a nonzero reflected physical state established.

2. VTO1: WHAT THE COMPONENT ARGUMENT REQUIRES

The component conclusion is conditional on a prescribed continuum
parent law, moment convergence along the SAME sequence, uniform
products of translated Wick errors at every finite collection of
distinct points, and component Gaussian domination. The last input is
explicitly separate. A positive fourth cumulant of a mixture alone
does not imply that its components have positive fourth cumulants.
Likewise a diagonal subsequence may not silently replace the parent.

The general lemma on p11 is independently checkable. Let T_y be a
probability-preserving Z^d action and G real in every finite Lp. Suppose
for every finite distinct y_1,...,y_J,

             |E prod_(a=1)^J T_(y_a) G| <= u^J.         (2.1)

The absolute value is present in the rendered statement; text
extraction can omit it. For this conclusion, just the upper bound
for even J would in fact suffice. Let Q_n be a cube with N=n^d sites,
and A_n=N^-1 sum_(y in Q_n) T_y G. In E A_n^J, the distinct-index
terms contribute at most u^J. At most binomial(J,2) N^(J-1) tuples
have a repeated index. Holder bounds the absolute expectation of each
such term by ||G||_J^J. Thus, for even J,

 E|A_n|^J <= u^J + binomial(J,2) N^-1 ||G||_J^J.

The L2 mean ergodic theorem gives A_n -> E[G|I_Zd] in L2. An almost
sure subsequence and Fatou imply ||E[G|I_Zd]||_J<=u for every even J.
Let J increase to obtain |E[G|I_Zd]|<=u almost surely. If this is a
restriction of an R^d action, I_Rd is contained in I_Zd and the tower
property gives the same conclusion for E[G|I_Rd]. This needs no
assumption that the lattice subaction is ergodic. Identifying that
conditional expectation with expectations under ergodic components
requires the stipulated disintegration.

The lemma is a useful general interface; it supplies no missing
finite-regulator product estimate. VTO1 obtains the latter through
model-specific flux, reference-response and pressure-defect inputs.
Its small-charge error must survive the normalization loss before the
component argument is applied. Those QED3 inputs have not been proved
for our non-Abelian four-dimensional Wilson construction.

3. COMBINED CONSEQUENCE FOR THIS PROJECT

The three new outlines sharpen the architecture:

 (i) an exact ORIGINAL sourced identity, including scalar terms;
 (ii) a normalized connected response bound with each source support;
 (iii) a positive reference signal and a controlled comparison error;
 (iv) full-law volume/moment passage preserving the chosen parent;
 (v) a separate argument identifying a nonzero physical continuum
     channel, and the required reconstruction hypotheses.

They do not remove these obligations. RRO1 explicitly imports the
source-dependent Borel identity and supported operator derivatives.
The TURN83 native calculation supplies only a one-primitive part of
(ii). The TURN83 HS theorem supplies a complete initial conditional
source family, not (iv) or a full-history version of (ii).

The useful narrowing is to the exact integrated first and mixed source
coefficients at the first large-field entry, retaining all external
tests and original source anchors. A finite two-jet formulation is
enough for that covariance gate and is weaker than an entire-source
holomorphy theorem. It still has to be proved on the actual continued
native domain. No newly inaccessible paper has been identified as a
necessary missing input; the current obstruction is mathematical.
