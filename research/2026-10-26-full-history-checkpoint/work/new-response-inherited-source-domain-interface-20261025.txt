MAIN CONCLUSION

A GIVEN inherited E_old on its full independent-pair domain can be
composed directly with the NEW selected-pair response. This is a rigorous
analytic/preintegration source interface and requires no equality of old
and new internal response extensions. It can be used with an unchanged
old weight W_old as a separately defined function.

The native near determining-cube expression is a DIFFERENT independent-
current continuation. It cannot generally be identified offgraph with
that direct composition. Lemma4 remains useful as a domain theorem for
its own specified reconstruction pair, without assuming that the INPUT
J is physical, but it is not an equality theorem for arbitrary selected
currents. Original near/far Taylor gains cannot be imported just from the
new whole-response bound. A direct localized-source route is available,
provided the complete selected-current read, especially P1, is localized
along with the response.

1. THREE PAIR TYPES WHICH MUST NOT BE IDENTIFIED

(a) Incoming pair z=(U,J): J is independent. Its certificates include
the full old-domain/current-domain intersection (3.16), on every actual
read/collar. The RAW INPUT J must meet both current allowances.

(b) Direct selected pair, the literal B109(3.13) map:

 Phi_A(z)=(exp(i xi A)U,
        lambda^3 J+Delta_U^xi A-P1_U A+F1(U,A)),    (1)

where lambda^3 is the printed factor (L^(j-1) eta)^3. Use the actual
grade-j conventions; do not replace this by a physical current at complex
or independent-pair inputs. In adjacent-grade normalized prefix units
the old-current coefficient is L^{-3}.

(c) Determining reconstruction pair Pi_{j,box0}(U,A,B_extra):
the pair displayed in B109(3.53), whose field is the native U_j on the
prescribed nested determining cubes and whose displayed J_j is the
specified reconstructed current. This is a particular analytic map of
the determining data. It is not a freely selectable output current.

B109 p275 expressly retains incoming J as an independent variable when
the background U is replaced in the response by the local completion
U_{k+1}(box0,M'(U)). P276 says to transport J AND B under both local
gauges, not merely U. By p277, once the response argument is replaced
by an arbitrary A, the determining expression in (3.34) is a function
of U,A only. Lemma4's arbitrary B_extra gives a disc in determining
data; it does not supply an independent-current output disc.

2. DIRECT INHERITED-SOURCE COMPOSITION THEOREM

Fix a carrier X and an inherited function E_X holomorphic on the FULL
space U_j^c(X,alpha0,alpha1), satisfying

                  |E_X| <= M_X := E0 exp[-kappa d_j(X)].

Use a complete strict input domain D contained in (3.16), and a strict
response radius a less than the alpha2 of (3.14). Denote its four-slot
response norm by

 N_X(A)=max(|A|,|nabla_U^xi A|,|P1_U A|,|Delta_U^xi A|)_X.

Let A(z,w) be ANY declared jointly holomorphic response with N_X(A)<a
on D and on the specified auxiliary polydisc w. Then

                 F_X(z,w)=E_X(Phi_{A(z,w)}(z))       (2)

is jointly holomorphic and |F_X|<=M_X on that same domain.

Proof. B109 (3.13)--(3.14) is exactly the inclusion/analyticity statement
for (1), not a theorem requiring that A be the old H_j. Compose this
map with E_X. The independent J is used literally. All conditions (iv)
and all collars are inherited through the FULL input certificate, not
replaced by a first-order small-field test without its direct-to-full
proof. This proves the assertion.

The audited new response qualifies after choosing its noise cap small
enough that its uniform four-slot bound lies inside a. The same is true
for every simultaneous operator-weakening value in its admitted domain.
This theorem identifies a NEW composition; it does not identify it with
an older offgraph composition merely because both agree physically.

At a native physical constrained-minimum background, s=1 and actual real
free noise, the new response equals the native response pointwise. Its
Landau condition on that subfamily makes (1) the actual output current.
Thus (2) equals the original physical inherited-action evaluation there.
Equality also extends in the response/noise variables within their shared
analytic chart at that fixed physical base. No identity theorem in the
independent J direction is invoked.

For ALL older-grade copies on the same original fine bonds, keep their
different operator types. If eta_new is the present mesh in its own
units and xi_old is an old copy's mesh, put rho=eta_new/xi_old<=1.
The identical group perturbation is represented by A_old=rho A_new.
With the same background parallel transporters, exactly

 nabla^(xi_old) A_old=rho^2 nabla^(eta_new) A_new,
 Delta^(xi_old) A_old=rho^3 Delta^(eta_new) A_new.

The old copy's P1_j is generally DIFFERENT from the present P1; its
bound must use its own native bounded kernel and complete read. Its
uniform field-to-source bound gives the sufficient estimate
|P1_j A_old|<=C rho |A_new| in old unit normalization. Do not infer
a rho^3 estimate by silently replacing P1_j with the present operator.
Thus a fixed small new-response cap admits all these old four-slot
copies when their complete input charts are reserved. Their selected
currents are copy-specific offgraph; they agree with the appropriately
scaled physical current only on the matched physical subfamily.

3. CAUCHY BOUNDS, ALL COPIES, AND WHAT FAR BOUNDS REQUIRE

If N_X(A0)<=a/3 and h is a variation, then

 zeta -> E_X(Phi_{A0+zeta h}(z))

is bounded by M_X on |zeta|<=a/(3N_X(h)). Consequently

 |d/dzeta E_X(Phi_{A0+zeta h}) at 0|
                         <=3 M_X N_X(h)/a.          (3)

The zero direction is treated by continuity. Similarly, if N_X(A)<=a/3,
the difference from A=0 obeys

 |E_X(Phi_A)-E_X(Phi_0)| <= C M_X N_X(A)/a.          (4)

For (4), integrate the derivative along tA and use the same fixed spare
radius around every t in [0,1]. Neither bound assumes physical J.

For simultaneous probes h_l of the SAME evaluation copy the legitimate
polydisc condition is

 N_X(A0)+sum_l r_l N_X(h_l) < a.

Then the full mixed derivative is bounded by
alpha! M_X product_l r_l^{-alpha_l}. One cannot allocate a/3 to every
probe independently and silently ignore their sum. If there are genuinely
separate evaluation copies, each has its own pair map and its own complete
certificate; shared background/noise dependence must still satisfy all
certificates simultaneously. Product bounds multiply the M_X of the
explicit copies. This is a typed statement, not a source-copy census.

Equation (3) proves the functional step behind B109(3.17) for ANY h with
the required four-slot local bound. Hence if a new marked response obeys
the explicit spatial estimate (3.9), its far inherited-source bound
follows unchanged, with its registered constants. A global bound
N(A)<=C||B|| does NOT itself imply (3.9): spatial decay of the marked
response on the actual source read still has to be proved from the
complete kernels. A large old carrier may instead supply decay from
M_X, as in B109's second far case. These alternatives cannot be merged
without retaining their separate geometric hypotheses.

4. WHY NEAR/DIRECT IDENTIFICATION IS FALSE OFFGRAPH

At U=1 and A=0, a zero determining reconstruction has reconstructed
current zero. Thus Pi_{j,box0}(1,0,0) is independent of incoming J.
By contrast Phi_0(1,J)=(1,lambda^3 J).

Take the bounded, gauge-invariant holomorphic local test
E_X(U,J)=c tr(J(b)^2), with c chosen for the desired cap. On the direct
pair its value is c lambda^6 tr(J(b)^2); on the zero determining pair
it is zero. Both agree on the physical flat point J=0, but differ on
the admitted independent-current chart. Therefore a blanket offgraph
identity between these two source continuations is mathematically false.

The statement does NOT accuse Lemma4 of assuming physical INPUT J.
Rather, Lemma4 certifies its particular reconstructed OUTPUT pair.
It is valid to feed a new analytic A(U,J) to that map while retaining
the same reconstruction and current. It is invalid to replace its
displayed J_j by an arbitrary independent selected current without a
new inclusion argument, or to call the resulting function equal to
the direct source (2) by analytic continuation.

The local cutoff products have fixed corresponding norms. Gauge

The incoming independent J still needs its transformed cap in those

6. CRITICAL CARRIER ISSUE: THE SELECTED CURRENT'S P1 READ

Even if A_s|X depends only on a finite carrier Y, the term P1_U A_s
can depend on U outside Y. P1 is the nonlocal DPD* coefficient built
from native propagators. Localizing A alone is therefore insufficient
to assert that (2) is a Y-local source. Every P1 kernel/current branch,
boundary collar and field needed to certify the pair must be included.

One concrete declared localizer family is

 Phi_{A,s}(U,J)=(exp(i xi A_s)U,
       lambda^3 J+Delta_U^xi A_s-P1_s A_s+F1(U,A_s)), (5)

where P1_s is its complete native weakened word series, using the SAME
fixed convention and support bookkeeping as the response. It agrees
with (1) at s=1. Native P1 word bounds give
||P1_s A_s||_(-3)<=C_kappa||A_s||_(-1). Thus the independent-output-
current allowance and the complete field certificate admit (5) after
a fixed smaller response cap. This uses the freedom of the J coordinate
in U_j^c; it invokes no offgraph Ward identity. It is a specified source
localizer extension, not an identity with a version leaving P1 unweakened.

7. PREINTEGRATION CARRIER LEMMA AND ITS EXACT HYPOTHESES

Let protected seed S contain X and every local current stencil/collar.
Suppose the chosen primitive word families, including P1_s, have the
native B116 separation property: after setting s=0 outside a union Y,
their kernels and coefficient reads are confined to components of Y
with the native boundary cushion. Suppose all local nonlinear stencils
fit in that cushion. Then uniqueness of the D/a fixed points makes
the response restricted to the seed component depend only on that
component's data/noise. The same is true of (5), and hence of E_X(Phi).
Other components cannot affect it. This is the direct support proof;
no near determining-source identity is needed.

On the simultaneous polydisc |s_Delta|<=S0>1, its value bound is M_X.
For the noise-dependent difference subtract its A=0 value and use (4),
giving M_X C r/a. The multivariable FTC expansion at real s in [0,1]
has terms indexed by sets sigma of active exterior cubes. Cauchy on
the circles |z_Delta|=S0 gives the explicit per-set bound

 |F_sigma| <= M_X (C r/a) [S0/(S0-1)^2]^{|sigma|}. (6)

If a component of sigma misses the protected seed, the corresponding
mixed derivative vanishes by the separation argument. Hence only
connected-to-seed components contribute. Repeated scalar factors in
the factor-word convention are handled by the same analytic polydisc
bound; they do not permit discarding their support multiplicities.

This is a preintegration localized source bound once the stated native
separation/read conditions are checked for the chosen primitives.
Conversion of (6) into a particular graded polymer norm still requires
its actual connected-set count, scale geometry and seed/read convention.
It is not automatically B116(1.36), and it is not a normalized bound.
For a full prefix, all deterministic frame/noise-argument reads must
additionally be localized or retained; the one-step carrier proof does
not erase those earlier dependencies.

8. INTERFACE WITH AN UNCHANGED OLD WEIGHT

It is legitimate to DEFINE

 T_new F(z)= [integral W_old(z,x) F(Phi_new(z,x)) dx]/Z_old(z)

on a common holomorphic domain, keeping W_old and Z_old as their own
given functions. Theorem(2) supplies the source-factor domain and bound;
it does not require the old internal H extension to equal Hhat offgraph.
At an admitted physical input all factors must be matched to the original
law, including original x cuts and masks, giving physical equality there.

This note supplies no denominator nonvanishing, normalized operator norm,
integration estimate, or complete root/copy count. The old weight's own
complex phase/normalization theorem must apply to this SAME common input
domain and the actual new source factor. Keeping it unchanged avoids one
unnecessary comparison but does not make those estimates automatic.
