WHY A RUNNING ANALYTIC BOUND DOES NOT CONSTRUCT THE UNMARKED CORE
25 October 2026. Root. Counterexample to an inference, not to Balaban's
actual renormalization trajectory or a proposed existence theorem.

The missing unmarked C+D producer cannot be replaced merely by saying
that the actual correction is analytic and small on a running domain,
even if its low field jets have already been removed. The following
finite-dimensional estimate makes this precise.

Fix b>0 and M<infinity. For 0<a<b let

                 F_a(z)=a^2(z/a)^6.

This is entire, even, bounded by a^2 on |z|<a, and all jets through
degree five vanish. Suppose C_a is holomorphic on |z|<b and bounded
there by M, independently of a. Cauchy's coefficient bound gives
|[z^6]C_a|<=M b^-6. Applying the same coefficient inequality to
D_a=F_a-C_a on the a-disc gives

 ||D_a||_a >= a^6 |a^-4-[z^6]C_a|
           >= a^2-M(a/b)^6.

Hence ||D_a||_a>=a^2/2 for sufficiently small a. In particular no such
uniformly bounded fixed-domain C_a can produce a remainder o(a^2).
The conclusion holds even though F_a itself is entire: the obstruction
is to the UNIFORM fixed-domain bound with the requested tiny remainder.

For a lattice gauge analogue at a fixed local regulator, the analytic
SU(2) plaquette class function h(U)=1-(1/2)Tr U has restriction
h(diag(e^(iz),e^(-iz)))=1-cos z. The gauge-invariant local expression
a^-4 h(U)^3 has first nonzero field degree six and size O(a^2) on a
small local a-chart. Its leading one-variable coefficient is
1/(8a^4), so the same estimate applies with changed fixed constants.
Summing its reflection/permutation-related plaquettes preserves those
scalar symmetries; low-degree neutrality does not remove the example.

This example is not asserted to satisfy every native coupling-dependent
inductive assumption, nor to occur in the actual Wilson trajectory.
It shows only that running-domain analyticity, a value bound and low-
jet/Ward neutrality do not by themselves prove a fixed-core split with
remainder a^2 g^P plus still higher powers of g. A proof of that split
must use additional information about the ACTUAL renormalization flow,
its coupling dependence, its prepared action difference and its exact
vacuum/coupling counterterms.

The source-image and value-column theorems do not supply this missing
unmarked information. They become applicable once a complete prepared
difference and uniformly admitted interpolation have been constructed.
