RESULT

For fixed geometric private pivots and the native positive-bond Lie-algebra
coordinates, define the FREE transformation by

 (T_g x)(b)=Ad_(g(b_-))x(b),  b in I_j,                    (A)

where I_j is the set of positive grade-j bonds after deleting b0(c) for
every grade-(j+1) bond c. Here g is restricted from the fine lattice to
grade-j sites. This is the actual transformation on free coordinates,
not a presumed transformation of a constrained field.

For the literal map

 B'_U(x)=lambda C_U x-h_U Dtilde_U(lambda C_U x),

where lambda=g_j is the scalar coupling, one has exactly

 B'_(U^g)(T_g x)=R_j(g) B'_U(x),                         (B)

with (R_j(g)A)(b)=Ad_(g(b_-))A(b). The independent current transforms
simultaneously as J->Ad_g J, but is a spectator in this local pivot map;
it is not recomputed. Thus (B) is precisely the requested independent-
(U,J) map identity. Below the native pivot operator h is unrelated to
any large gauge transformation also sometimes denoted h.

1. FULL NONLINEAR CONSTRAINT

Set V=M^j U. Ordinary covariance gives V^g=M^j(U^g), using the restriction
of g to grade-j sites. B109(2.4) defines the nonlinear grade-j constraint by

 exp(i Qcal_V(A))=M(exp(iA)V)(MV)^-1.

The ratio at a coarse bond c transforms by conjugation at c_-, so on the
common logarithm branch

 Qcal_(V^g)(R_j(g)A)=R_(j+1)(g) Qcal_V(A).               (1)

Decompose its Taylor expansion in the literal native convention as

 Qcal_V(A)=L Q_V A+N_V(A),   N_V(A)=O(A^2).

Differentiating (1) at0, then subtracting the linear parts, gives

 Q_(V^g) R_j=R_(j+1) Q_V,
 N_(V^g)(R_j A)=R_(j+1) N_V(A).                          (2)

No minimum identity or current equation is required for (1),(2).

2. THE PRIVATE PIVOT AND ITS ACTUAL ROW

B109 printed267 chooses p(c)=b0(c), the grade-j bond lying in c and in
the corridor connecting its two endpoint blocks. The set of pivots is
fixed geometrically; a site gauge changes neither bonds nor labels.
Endpoint paths in each averaging row stay within the two endpoint blocks,
so that row's interface pivot is its own p(c). B109 explicitly specifies
h by its pivot-only support and L Q_V h_V=I.

Write A_c(V) for the coefficient of A(p(c)) in (Q_V A)(c). The displayed
native formula is

 (h_V d)(p(c))=h_c(V)d(c),   h_c(V)=L^-1 A_c(V)^-1.

Extracting that coefficient in (2) gives the concrete transformation

 A_c(V^g)=Ad_(g(c_-)) A_c(V) Ad_(g(p(c)_-))^-1,
 h_c(V^g)=Ad_(g(p(c)_-)) h_c(V) Ad_(g(c_-))^-1.           (3)

Thus, including the factor L^-1,

                  h_(V^g) R_(j+1)=R_j h_V.              (4)

This checks the native row formula, rather than assuming that a chosen
right inverse would automatically transform covariantly.

3. THE LITERAL FREE EMBEDDING C

Let E inject the free coordinates as zero on pivot bonds, and let P_free
be restriction to the free bonds. Both are geometric and obey
R_j E=E T_g and P_free R_j=T_g P_free. The embedding obtained by the
published elimination of delta(Q_V A) is exactly

                  C_V=E-h_V L Q_V E.                    (5)

It is characterized by P_free C_V=I and Q_V C_V=0, and uniqueness follows
from the invertible private pivot coefficient. Using (2),(4),(5),

 C_(V^g)T_g
 =E T_g-h_(V^g)L Q_(V^g)E T_g
 =R_j E-R_j h_V L Q_V E
 =R_j C_V.                                              (6)

In particular the free variables transform by the explicit (A), even
though each eliminated pivot depends on a whole finite block read.

4. THE NONLINEAR PIVOT SOLUTION

B109 printed267 defines Dtilde as the unique small analytic solution of

              Dtilde_V(A)=N_V(A-h_V Dtilde_V(A)).         (7)

Let d=Dtilde_V(A). Equations(2),(4) imply

 N_(V^g)(R_j A-h_(V^g)R_(j+1)d)
 =R_(j+1)N_V(A-h_V d)=R_(j+1)d.

Hence uniqueness on the native branch gives

       Dtilde_(V^g)(R_j A)=R_(j+1)Dtilde_V(A).           (8)

Substitute A=lambda C_V x and use(4),(6),(8). This proves(B), including
the nonlinear correction. Since C,h,Dtilde here depend on V=M^j U and
the local averaging constraint, they have no additional J dependence.
Any later minimum-response operation depending on J is a distinct map
and requires its own simultaneous-current covariance.

5. THE PUBLISHED FREE CUTOFF REALLY IS PRESERVED

B109(2.9) cuts precisely the nonpivot coordinates of the original physical
increment B'. On those coordinates C_V is the identity and h_V vanishes,
so the full nonlinear reconstruction satisfies

                      B'_U(x)(b)=lambda x(b), b in I_j.

The original cutoff therefore becomes the literal centered product
|x(b)|<epsilon1/lambda (or the alternative radius specified after(2.9)).
For real compact-group g, T_g is orthogonal in the invariant Lie-algebra
inner product. It preserves these individual balls, including varying
fixed radii, and its absolute real Jacobian is1. For a connected compact
gauge group its determinant is+1. No cutoff at a pivot has been invented,
and no pivot bound has been substituted for the actual free cut.

6. SCALARS, JACOBIANS AND GAUGE FACTORS

The scalar factors are not discarded. Their matching can be checked:

- Linear coarea coefficients A_c transform by the two adjoints in(3).
  Their absolute determinants are unchanged for real compact g.
- Differentiating(8) gives Dtilde'_(V^g)(R_j A)=
  R_(j+1)Dtilde'_V(A)R_j^-1. Consequently I-h Dtilde' transforms
  by similarity under R_j. The actual determinant and its analytic
  logarithm near identity in B109(2.12) are unchanged.
- The Haar density of each reconstructed exponential is a conjugation-
  invariant analytic density, so it has its same value under(B).
- B109 printed266 explicitly defines the gauge variable as
  P(exp(iB')V;y,x)P(V;y,x)^-1, immediately before(2.5).
  Thus the necessary relative-gauge form is ALREADY native. Under
  simultaneous transport of V and exp(iB')V this ratio conjugates by
  g(y), preserving the real trace gauge term and its invariant-norm cut.
  Replacing it by a new absolute gauge functional would be unjustified.

These facts agree with the printed covariance (2.16), which expressly
uses (R(u)B')(b)=Ad_(u(b_-))B'(b) and says that the characteristic
function is preserved by the local orthogonal transformation.

The complete gauge-covariant quadratic and covariance consequently
transform in free coordinates by T_g and T_g^-1. For an active/retained
split T_g is block diagonal and does not mix the two sets. Both retained
data and conditional mean must be transformed; the principal determinant
and Schur scalar are then unchanged. This does not delete a retained
field or change the outer conditional mass.

Every additional mask/mark not explicitly shown to be invariant is
kept as its literal pullback. The map identity itself gives no right
to remove it, redefine its event, or identify it with a native mask
written in a different untransported frame.

7. COMPLEX EXTENSION: AN IDENTITY, NOT A CONTOUR CHANGE

For fixed native branches, ordinary averaging, the pivot inverse and
Dtilde are holomorphic in the independent coefficient variables. The
same derivation works algebraically for complex g whenever both sides
belong to their common analytic domains: logarithms are conjugation
equivariant, (3)--(6) are algebraic, and(8) follows from uniqueness of
the same analytic implicit solution. Equivalently the real identity
extends analytically to a neighborhood of every admitted real gauge.

For real g, transformed small-noise norms are identical. For complex g
near a real gauge they are inflated by the actual Ad norm, so spare
noise/chart margins must be checked. The identity does not by itself
provide a common prefix radius, preserve a real ball under a complex
adjoint, or authorize any complex change of integration contour.

8. TRIANGULAR USE

If a grade-j gauge is determined only by higher-grade data/noises and
is applied to grade-(j-1) free variables, (A) is an adapted real block
rotation. The full sequence of such rotations is triangular, its
diagonal blocks have determinant modulus1, and recursive inversion
preserves the original product noise integration exactly. Its complete
reconstructed arguments match by(B), not by an assumed transformation
of pivot coordinates. All scalar and mask bookkeeping from Section6
remains attached. This closes the literal local free/pivot map matching;
common-frame analytic prefix admission is a separate theorem.
