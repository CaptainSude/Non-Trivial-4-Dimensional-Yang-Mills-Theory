3. ONE-MARK LOCALIZATION LEMMA

Here is a convenient way to obtain map sensitivity without a nonlinear
three-noise tree theorem. Let an output packet p of a map F have an
auxiliary complete localizer family F_p(s), holomorphic and bounded
by M_F on |s_Delta|<=S0>1 and the same fixed input packet discs.
Assume its native component-separation property with a fixed boundary
cushion and a protected seed of uniformly bounded current-scale size.

Expand F_p(1) by the multivariable FTC, setting exterior scalar
parameters to zero. A term with sigma differentiated cubes is bounded
by M_F q0^|sigma|, where

  q0=S0/(S0-1)^2.

If an input packet q misses the complete component of that term,
the derivative in a direction supported in q is zero. Otherwise
Cauchy on its fixed input disc costs only r_in^(-1). A connecting
component must join p to q. If the connected-set count is at most
C_seed nu^n and nu q0<1, summing gives

  ||D_(q,v) F_p|| <= C M_F r_in^(-1)
                          exp[-mu d(p,q)] ||v||_q. (3)

The geometric constants and protected seed enter C and mu. Choosing
S0, then the native M needed for its operator bounds, is permitted;
both remain fixed. This proof retains factor multiplicities through
the uniform analytic bound. It neither flattens them nor counts
individual fine coordinates inside a packet.

Only ONE input derivative is taken. The argument is different from
claiming a summable trilinear noise Taylor norm for a global action.

5. ADJACENT ELL2 SENSITIVITY AND GRADE REPLICATION

Let B_j be the extended packet state at grade j. For fixed original
real x_j, let B_j=Psi_j(B_(j+1),x_j). The one-mark bound gives a row
Schur sum at most C_L and a column Schur sum at most C_L L^4 when
coarse packets are represented on the next finer unit packet lattice.
The L^4 is the actual four-dimensional replication count. Any fixed
packet overlap or larger fixed native seed is included in C_L.

Hence, in unweighted packet ell2 counting norms,

  ||D Psi_j||_(2->2)<=A_L,   A_L=C_L L^2,             (4)

after enlarging C_L if needed for the fixed grade-unit conversions.
It is enough to retain any proved fixed power C_L L^c; no contraction
claim is necessary. Strong coefficient slots use their normalized

By the chain rule, uniformly over the entire ORIGINAL outer-noise
product and the strict complex domain,

  ||D_(B_t) B_j||_(2->2)<=A_L^(t-j).                (5)

Thus a terminal perturbation eta supported in h packets, each of norm
at most rho, has

  ||B_j(u+i eta)-B_j(u)||_(packet,2)
                  <=C_syn A_L^(t-j) sqrt(h) rho.   (6)

Integrate the derivative along the straight terminal parameter path.
The baseline B_j(u) is formed with the SAME fixed real noises. All
finite-dimensional logarithmic relative coordinates are taken in their

baselines; they need not be the same baseline for every noise record.

6. FINITE PREFIX NORM AND SOURCE CAP

Let eps be the minimum of the extended phase allowance, the strict
pair/frame reserve, and every specifically required source-domain
reserve after its actual sensitivity constant. A sufficient terminal
radius is

  rho<=eps/[C_syn sqrt(h) max_(j<t) A_L^(t-j)].      (7)

More generally use the actual K_jt in place of A_L^(t-j). Formula(7)
ensures that every intermediate input belongs to its OLD density's
phase tube, uniformly in all outer original real noises.

If the terminal source F0 is holomorphic on its OWN full domain and
its entire nested new-map image has cap M0 there, each conditional
norm is at most2. Finite iteration therefore gives

  |T_(t-1)...T_0 F0|<=2^t M0.                       (8)

The source-domain/cap hypothesis is not supplied by a density estimate.
For an additional source interface with reserve sigma_F and sensitivity
K_Ft, intersect(7) with rho<=sigma_F/(C_syn sqrt(h)K_Ft).
No old mask is analytically continued by this argument.

For t<=c0 log(g^(-1)), 2^t<=g^(-c0 log2), and A_L^t is also a fixed
power of g^(-1). Thus the radius loss and the prefix norm cost are
polynomial in g^(-1) and sqrt(h), with no ambient lattice-volume
factor. Fixed-order Cauchy jets retain the explicit inverse powers
of (7). This does not bound a sum over arbitrarily many carrier labels.

If a separately admitted source has an exp[-c log^(2p)(g^(-1))]
bound with p>1/2, these polynomial costs can be absorbed by reducing
c. Neither that source bound nor its complete carrier admission is
proved here.

7. LIMITS OF THE RESULT

The OLD density/interaction theorem is an explicit import. The NEW
primitive phase theorem remains conditional on its separate nonlinear
marked-kernel theorem. The present argument does not use it.

The phase baseline must be real: unmarked s=1 is covered. Arbitrary
complex weakening contours cannot be inserted while retaining the
same real baseline unless their induced extended-state imaginary norm
is ALSO bounded by (7). Holomorphy of the map on a large scalar
polydisc is not enough for that phase assertion.

The one-mark map argument requires actual complete component-separated
word families and the local native return reads. These conditions are
identified above; they should be checked against any later changed
gauge or source-localizer convention. No claim is made for macroscopic
determining-cube gauges or histories outside the homogeneous sector.

This is a finite local complex-tube theorem with explicit radius loss.
It does not close a fixed-radius connected source catalogue, erase
read/root multiplicities, or construct the continuum theory.
