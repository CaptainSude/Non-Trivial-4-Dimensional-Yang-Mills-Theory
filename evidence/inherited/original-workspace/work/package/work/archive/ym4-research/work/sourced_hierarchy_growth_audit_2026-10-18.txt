SOURCED HIERARCHY: ORDER DEPENDENCE AND THE OS GROWTH OBSTRUCTION
18 October 2026. Research audit, not a publication document.

This note audits the repository's actual displayed envelopes. Its
references are to the checked local copy of Ethan Sussman and Levent's
theorem_0_complete_account_raw_{I,II,III}.tex. It does not promote their
conditional correlation theorem to an unconditional source theorem.

1. WHAT THE EXISTING CAUCHY STEP REALLY COSTS

Raw III, around line53471, Lemma9.8:lem-derivative-closeness assumes a
normalized sourced partition h holomorphic on a norm ball of fixed
radius R0 with |h|<=Q_w and h(0)=1. The smaller radius R1 is fixed by
(Q_w+1)R1<=R0/2. For p observables it obtains a factor (2p/R1)^p.
All Q_w,R0,R1 are fixed at a fixed terminal torus. This p-dependence
is factorial: p^p<=e^p p!. A radius allocation proportional to 1/p
is not an obstruction to the OS factorial-growth requirement.

The precise same phenomenon occurs in Raw I, Theorem3.1(iii-a),
lines2600ff:

 C_n(d)=[C_UV(d)+log2 (2Q+2)^n](n/varrho)^n,
 Q=exp[(E_+^sharp+C_low) N_T^4].

At a FIXED torus, N_T and Q do not depend on n. Therefore, for n>=2,

 C_n(d)<=C(d) A_T^n n!,

with A_T finite and independent of n. The statement remains conditional
on the antecedents of the repository correlation theorem. Its own
Theorem3.1 scope paragraph lists the weight lemma, sixth-family lemma,
and further source uniformity inputs as incomplete or stated inputs.

2. THE ACTUAL VOLUME-FREE BOUND IS NOT FACTORIAL

Raw II, around lines62682--62780, (6.2:eq-cell-covariance-4)--(9),
and its explicit diagnosis in lines68088--68171, give

 |h(z)|<=Q_infty^n,
 log Q_infty=(E_+^sharp+C_low) ell(n,R,g)^4+2C_face(R)+C_cov,
 ell(n,R,g)>=8n(R+1),

and then the connected n-point bound contains

 log2 * mathfrak_r^n (2Q_infty^n+2)^n (n/r)^n.                 (1)

The terminal bulk and face constants are independent of n. For fixed
R, (1) grows at least exp(c n^6) when E_+^sharp+C_low>0. The rounded
power ell is also bounded above by a fixed multiple of the displayed
floor, so the dominant envelope is exp(C n^6), up to factorial and
fixed-parameter factors. This is not an exp(exp n) loss, but it is
already much larger than C^n (n!)^beta for every fixed beta.

This is a lower bound on the SUPPLIED UPPER-BOUND CONSTANT, not a lower
bound on the actual correlations. It diagnoses an insufficient bound,
not a no-go theorem for Yang--Mills or even for this construction.

The dependence on separation is much better:

 C_UV(d;R)<=C [1+(R+2)/d]^4 [1+log_L^+(32M/d)].              (2)

Thus the published-in-the-repository calculation already gives a
polynomial ultraviolet separation cost. It is the remaining normalized
last-scale partition factor, not the 1/n Cauchy allocation or (2),
that prevents the all-order OS estimate.

3. EXACT LOCATION OF THE LOSS

The chessboard argument first gives a modulus bound

 log|h|<=C n ell(n,R,g)^4+O(n)=O_R(n^5)

on a source norm ball of fixed radius. The bounded-logarithm lemma
(6.2:lem-bounded-logarithm), Raw II lines62399--62490, then uses only
h(0)=1 and |h|<=Q to infer zero-freeness on a radius of order Q^-1.
For Q=Q_infty^n this is exp[-O_R(n^5)]. Taking n derivatives produces
the exp[O_R(n^6)] cost.

There is no generic analytic improvement of this step from those exact
hypotheses. For example h(z)=1+A z^2 has h(0)=1, is positive for real z,
is bounded by 1+A on the unit disc, and has zeros at +/-i A^-1/2.
For n=2k, (d/dz)^n log h(0)=(-1)^(k+1)n! A^k/k. Taking
A=exp(c n^5) shows the scale of the allowed loss. This is a test of the
printed analytic assumptions, not an example of a Wilson-generated
partition function or a coherent field hierarchy.

It is not legitimate to apply a positive-mgf cumulant improvement
directly to h: the source normalization is

 h(z)=exp[-E_UV(w_z)] Z(w_z)/Z(0).

Z/Z(0) is a positive-measure moment-generating function for real sources,
but h after the source-dependent UV subtraction need not be one. The
subtraction is essential: diagonal smeared moments have divergent UV
pieces. Positivity of the original measure alone does not give a
cutoff-uniform bound for these unrenormalized moments.

4. THE SMALLEST QUANTITATIVE IMPROVEMENT THAT WOULD SUFFICE

Suppose one proves directly for the normalized remainder a zero-free
connected logarithm on the source norm ball of radius c n^-p, with
|log h|<=C n^d there, where p,d are FIXED. Allocating that radius to
n distinct sources and applying Cauchy gives

 |partial_1...partial_n log h(0)|
 <=C n^d (C' n^(p+1))^n product_i ||f_i||_sharp
 <=C''^n (n!)^(p+1+d) product_i ||f_i||_sharp.                 (3)

The final exponent is intentionally crude; n^d can be absorbed by a
fixed exponential, so p+1 plus a small fixed reserve also works. No
source radius uniform in n is necessary. An order-polynomial radius
already removes the actual obstruction.

A direct localized connected-source cluster estimate could give this;
alternatively an exact terminal positive-kernel representation with a
bounded local source log-weight and polynomial source-support cost
could give it. Neither is contained in the currently displayed
chessboard modulus estimate. One must track normalized denominators
and source counterterms; repeating the zero-source ultraviolet
stability theorem does not supply (3).

5. FLAT TESTS AT COINCIDENCES AND WHAT THEY DO NOT FIX

Restricting to Schwartz functions flat on the union of coincidence
diagonals can remove the need to CHOOSE contact terms. It does not
change the n-dependence (1). The polynomial separation estimate (2)
is compatible with such a flat-test construction at each fixed n:
Whitney cubes away from the diagonals have separated coordinate
projections; the tensor C^1 bound, a blockwise Fourier expansion, and
Taylor vanishing on the diagonals give a finite Schwartz seminorm of
order O(n). This requires writing the tensor-to-general-test extension,
not simply asserting that separated tensors are all Schwartz tests.
Even after that extension, the prefactor inherited from (1) remains
exp(C n^6), so it does not prove the all-order growth axiom.

At fixed torus the n-growth issue is absent as in Section1, but passing
to R^4 reintroduces the volume problem. A fixed torus constant may
grow exponentially with the torus volume; it cannot be substituted
for a position-uniform Schwartz bound in infinite volume.

Full Euclidean rotations remain a logically separate obligation.
Theorem3.1 and Raw I lines3183ff explicitly make O(4) invariance
conditional on the far-sphere rotation-current flux hypothesis.
Neither factorial estimates nor contact-term avoidance prove that
hypothesis, the forward-cone spectrum condition, or nontriviality.

Initial audit status: the repository's fixed-torus source derivative envelope
has acceptable factorial order dependence. Its actual volume-free
envelope does not. The decisive missing quantitative source statement
is a polynomial-in-order zero-free logarithmic radius (or an equivalent
connected-source bound), with the printed UV polynomial separation
cost and source hypotheses retained explicitly. Sections6--9 below give
a different consumer that avoids this zero-free-log requirement for
FULL centered moments. They supersede any inference that the printed
n^6 envelope is an intrinsic obstruction.

6. A SHARP FACE ESTIMATE WITH A SHRINKING BUFFER

Assume the repository's source producer supplies its common local
functions e_j(X;v), with the locality, holomorphy, exponential tree
bound, covariance/reality, and last-scale stability hypotheses used
in Chapter6. This is a CONDITIONAL consumer of those hypotheses;
their outstanding producer proofs are not filled by this section.

Let epsilon=L^-K' be the bare spacing, s_j=ML^(j-K'), and let each
source piece be supported in a ball of radius r_b at distance at least
eta from the fixed chessboard faces. Suppose r_b<=C0 eta and
epsilon<=eta/4. The '+2' in Raw II's anchor count is only the bound
diam(plaquette)<=2epsilon<=2. Keeping the sharper first inequality,
the anchor count is

 a_j=[2(r_b+2epsilon)/s_j+6]^4.

A domain X with X^+ meeting both that source and a cell face has

 d_j(X)>=[eta/(8s_j)-3]_+.

Indeed the plaquette-to-barycentre discrepancy is at most2epsilon,
so the actual distance remains at least eta/2; the source diameter
lemma then gives this bound. The original tree sum consequently gives

 C_face(r_b,eta,epsilon)
 <=C sum_{j<=K'} [2(r_b+2epsilon)/s_j+6]^4
                   exp{-c[eta/(8s_j)-3]_+}
 <=C'[1+log^+(M/eta)].                                      (4)

Proof of the last bound: for s_j>=eta each bracket is bounded by a
constant depending only on C0, and there are O(1+log(M/eta)) such
scales. For s_j<eta put u=eta/s_j. The summand is bounded by a fixed
polynomial in u times exp(-c'u); u grows geometrically by L, so this
tail is bounded uniformly. The constants are independent of n, K',
the torus volume, and the positions. Minimal_route independently
checked this refinement against the source anchor and diameter proofs.

The source chessboard proof needs vanishing near faces to maintain the
bare plaquette insertion support; it does not need a fixed macroscopic
buffer. Thus its face step remains valid with (4).

7. FIXED CELLS FOR ARBITRARILY MANY SMALL SOURCE PIECES

Choose once an allowed cell side ell, a power of L above the fixed
last-scale volume floor. Let eta=c_ell/n, with c_ell sufficiently
small and fixed, and r_b<=eta. In one coordinate the excluded shifts
modulo ell have total length at most 2n(r_b+eta)<=4c_ell<ell/2.
The complement of these at most n intervals contains an interval of
length at least ell/(2n). Therefore, for epsilon<=c eta, a common
shift a in epsilon Z^4 moves ALL n source pieces to eta-buffered
interiors of cells of the SAME fixed chessboard grid.

This is the same argument as Raw II6.2:lem-place-balls, lines61420--
61508, with its unnecessarily fixed buffer replaced by eta. It does
not require moving the RG scaffold: translate the entire selected
source tuple by a, keep the scaffold and aligned cell-reflection group
fixed, and use exact bare Wilson translation invariance. Fully centered
moments are also invariant under this simultaneous translation. The
UV counterterms are evaluated on the translated tuple and restored
algebraically below, so no counterterm covariance under a fractional
last-lattice shift is being assumed.

There are at most n occupied cells. Using (4), the source chessboard
inequality with its exact bulk covariance cancellation gives on the
original fixed source norm ball

 |h_n(z)|<=B_n,
 B_n=exp{C n[1+log(1/eta)]+C n ell^4}
     <=C1^n n^(C2 n).                                      (5)

For a SINGLE active profile of the same tiny radius, the same argument
has one occupied cell and gives

 |h_i(z_i)|<=C eta^-C3.                                     (6)

Here h=e^-E_UV Z/Z(0), h(0)=1, as in the repository. The same source
producer is restricted to the coordinate axis in (6); no independently
chosen or inconsistent counterterm scheme is substituted.

8. FULL CENTERED MOMENTS: EXACT SQUARE-FREE RESTORATION

Let m_i=partial_i h(0). Cauchy using (6) implies

 |m_i|<=C r_source^-1 eta^-C3 ||f_i||_sharp.

Put h_c(z)=exp(-sum_i z_i m_i) h(z). On the smaller source norm ball
sum_i |z_i|||f_i||_sharp < c r_source eta^C3, one has
|h_c|<=e B_n. This is only a polynomial radius shrink in n.

Work temporarily in the finite algebra C[z_1,...,z_n]/(z_i^2).
Write E_mix for the square-free part of E_UV after deleting its linear
terms. It contains only monomials z_S with |S|>=2. Single-profile
terms of degree>=2 vanish in this algebra; this is why they need not
be interpreted as contact-renormalized probability-field moments.
Since the ACTUAL mean is

 <O(f_i)>=partial_i E_UV(0)+m_i,

the exact full centered n-point moment is

 [z_1...z_n] exp(E_mix(z)) h_c(z).                           (7)

This identity restores all mixed UV counterterms. It removes only the
actual one-point means; it does not discard multi-source terms or
replace the physical measure by the UV-subtracted h.

Let D(d)=max(1,C_UV(d;1)). The repository UV lemma applied to every
subset S of size m>=2 gives

 |[z_S]E_mix|<=D(d) (m/r_source)^m product_{i in S}||f_i||_sharp.

Choose equal normalized polydisc radii

 rho_i=rho_*/[n ||f_i||_sharp],
 rho_*=c r_source min(eta^C3,D(d)^-1/2).

The sum of absolute monomials of E_mix on this polydisc is bounded by

 D(d) sum_{m=2}^n binom(n,m)(m/n)^m (rho_*/r_source)^m
 <=D(d) sum_{m=2}^n (e rho_*/r_source)^m<=1

after choosing c small. Thus |exp E_mix|<=e there, and |h_c|<=e B_n.
Applying Cauchy to the actual analytic polynomial E_mix and analytic
h_c (whose square-free product equals (7)) yields

 |S_n^centered(f_1,...,f_n)|
 <=e^2 B_n (n/rho_*)^n product_i ||f_i||_sharp
 <=C^n (n!)^B D(d)^(n/2) product_i ||f_i||_sharp.             (8)

B is a fixed finite constant. We used eta=c_ell/n and
n^n<=e^n n!. The separation envelope (2) gives, for 0<d<=1,
D(d)^(n/2)<=C^n d^(-A n) for a fixed A, for example any A>5/2
after harmless enlargement. No zero-free logarithmic radius for h
has been used. No claim that h is itself a positive mgf is needed.

9. RETURN FROM TINY PIECES TO UNIT-BALL PROFILES

For any Lipschitz f supported in a ball of fixed radius1, use a smooth
partition of unity on mesh c eta. It has O(eta^-4) nonzero pieces,
each supported in an eta-ball, with

 sum_a ||f_a||_sharp<=C eta^-5 ||f||_sharp.

Apply (8) to every selected n-tuple of pieces. Each tuple has its own
good simultaneous baremesh translation from Section7; the exact
centered moment being bounded is translation invariant, so these
different proof translations cause no incompatibility. Piece supports
remain subsets of the original supports, and hence retain their
pairwise separation d. Multilinearity and the triangle inequality
multiply the right side by at most (C eta^-5)^n. This is another fixed
factorial power. Consequently the conditional source hypotheses imply

 |S_n^centered(f_1,...,f_n)|
 <=C^n (n!)^B' d^(-A n) product_i ||f_i||_sharp               (9)

for unit-ball profiles at arbitrary locations, uniformly in the torus
volume and in sufficiently small cutoff epsilon<=c/n. For a continuum
subsequence and each fixed n this cutoff floor is harmless.

Unlike the original exp(C n^6) consumer, (9) has acceptable factorial
order growth and a diagonal cost linear in n. A Whitney/tensor
decomposition on Schwartz functions flat at coincidences can now spend
O(n) derivatives to pay d^(-A n), with n^(C n) bookkeeping absorbed
in B'. That final extension still requires an explicit construction
and compatibility with the OS test space. The source PRODUCER and
continuous Euclidean rotations remain separate outstanding inputs.

Revised status: factorial growth of separated FULL centered moments
can be deduced from the repository's stated source-locality,
chessboard, covariance, and stability hypotheses by Sections6--9.
The previous n^6 bound was a nonoptimal consumer. This does not prove
those source hypotheses or complete the continuum existence theorem.
