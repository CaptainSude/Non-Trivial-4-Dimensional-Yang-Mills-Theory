The minimum is then unique by TURN97. Conversely the complementary

uses the actual nonnegative plaquette decomposition, not a conjectured

3. UNIFORM FULL-COMPACT PARTITION AND ENERGY-EXCESS BOUNDS

Let X=S_t-D and use its actual Gibbs probability. From (3.3),

 P_beta,t(X>=s) <= min(1,c^-1 beta^3 exp(-beta s)).

Integrating this tail on [0,infinity) gives

The upper integration limit can be enlarged because X is nonnegative.

 Z_beta(a,b)<=sqrt[Z_beta(a,a)Z_beta(b,b)].

By rotation invariance Z_beta(a,a) depends only on |a|. Simultaneously

 S_original(U)=S_out(xi)+sum_P S_P(U_P;xi).                 (7.1)

The Haar-preserving coordinates of TURN97 transform each S_P into

9. EXACT SEPARATED-SOURCE COVARIANCE TRANSPORT

For arbitrary original additive plaquette sources S_f,S_g, conditional

 Cov_original(S_f,S_g)

If no pair has affected plaquettes in both source supports, the last

 Cov_original(S_f,S_g)=Cov_nu(M_round,f,M_round,g).         (9.2)

10. WHAT THIS CHANGES AND THE REMAINING OBSTRUCTION

The prior two-link statement required weak exterior staples. Equations
(0.1) and (8.2)--(8.4) remove that restriction for one simultaneous
positive-density round, at explicitly weaker logarithmic and profile-
oscillation gains. No exceptional-exterior probability is needed for
THIS round. The full original conditional law is used everywhere.

The new marginal contains exact finite-range multi-link potentials
V_beta(t_P(xi)), in addition to the untouched Wilson plaquettes. The
potential for a subsequent eliminated link therefore includes those
changed factors. It is not the original Wilson one-link or two-link
fiber. Bounded factor supports and overlap do not prove a contraction
or an invariant source/action norm. Repeating this theorem unchanged
on the new marginal would be invalid.

Likewise S_cl,round is still a sum over microscopic positions. Bounds
by its total value do not pay a physical source census, yield separated
correlation decay, prove a nonzero continuum response, or identify
Yang--Mills interactions. The global logarithmic gain is weaker than
the weak-tube beta^-2 source gain; no summability over an unbounded
number of scales is asserted. The complete-block and iterated native
source assembly remain substantive work.

Originality within this project: independent conditional factorization
and finite-dimensional Laplace calculations already occur in earlier
turns, including TURN40/TURN74. The additional results here are the
all-exterior relative continuation using small defect versus a compact
