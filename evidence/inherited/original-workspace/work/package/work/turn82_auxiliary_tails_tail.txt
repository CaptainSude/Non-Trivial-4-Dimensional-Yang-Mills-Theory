TURN82. ACTUAL ZERO-LINK AUXILIARY TAILS AND NONZERO CURRENTS
20 October 2026. tail47. Finite original Wilson law, no selected sector.

RESULT

The exact two-HS construction gives useful quantitative bounds under
its ACTUAL interacting auxiliary marginal nu_beta. At every link of
the standard four-dimensional lattice, its physical quaternion current
has magnitude at least 2 beta except on an event of probability at most
min(1,192 exp[-beta/3200]). This estimate is uniform even conditional
on an ARBITRARY original configuration U. It therefore needs no
small-plaquette assumption. It comes from the concrete conditional
Gaussian means in the HS construction, not Gaussian domination of nu.

The same local event controls the real auxiliary records entering that
current. Prescribed sets of unsafe links have a uniform joint rarity
bound, with exponent |S|/19. Using the actual Wilson energy estimate
additionally proves concentration of a(J_e)/beta around 27 u_e in the
joint law, and hence of |a(J_e)|/beta around27 in nu_beta. Volume-uniform
exponential moments of G, R and J are also proved, with their explicit
beta scalings. These conclusions do not bound the interacting marginal
by a free Gaussian law or prove a physical reflected lower bound.

1. EXACT LAW, INDEPENDENT NOISES, AND CURRENT CONVENTIONS

Use a nondegenerate periodic hypercubic SU(2) lattice in d=4, with all
periods at least4. The original Wilson law has h_p=1-Re Tr U_p/2 in[0,2]
and E_p=beta h_p. Keep the two positive half-paths A_p=U_h V_h and B_p
from turn81_external_source_history, Sections1--5. For either half-path
h, denote the OTHER full half-path of the same plaquette by C_h.

The exact positive joint law can be generated as follows:

 U ~ mu_beta,
 G_p=m_p+Z_p,         m_p=sqrt(beta)(A_p+B_p)/2,
 R_h=sqrt(beta)U_h/2+G_p V_h^dagger+W_h.                 (1.1)

Given U, all Z_p and W_h are independent standard complex 2x2 matrix
Gaussians gamma_1, with four complex entries of variance one. This is
the actual normalized conditional law obtained by completing the HS
squares. Its (G,R) marginal is nu_beta with the SAME D_beta as TURN81.
The noises are not independent of U by an informal approximation:
(1.1) is an exact generative representation of the joint probability.

For a positive link e, its current is

 J_e=sum_(h:first(h)=e) sqrt(beta)R_h
        +sum_(h:second(h)=e) 2R_h^dagger G_(p(h)).        (1.2)

There are exactly three first-link and three second-link incidences.
They belong to six distinct plaquettes. Each incident plaquette uses
one of its two half-path R_h in this particular current. The conditional
U law given all (G,R) is the product normalized tilted Haar law with
kernel exp(Re Tr J_e^dagger U_e)/I(J_e); this remains the TURN81 exact
normalization, not a reference-kernel substitution.

Write U=u_0 I+i sum_(a=1)^3 u_a sigma_a with u in S^3. Define the REAL
physical current a(J) in R^4 by

 Re Tr J^dagger U=a(J) dot u.

Then a(cU)=2c u for real c, and |a(J)|<=sqrt(2)||J||_F. The latter
follows because I,i sigma_1,i sigma_2,i sigma_3 form an orthogonal
real basis of the quaternionic matrix subspace, each with norm sqrt2.
All current magnitudes below refer to this normalization.

2. EXACT DRIFT AND A UNIFORM NONZERO NOISE-FREE CURRENT

Put r_h^0=sqrt(beta)U_h/2+m_p V_h^dagger, and let J_e^0 be (1.2)
with G=m and R=r^0. For SU(2),

 m_p^dagger m_p=beta(1-h_p/2)I,
 ||m_p||_op<=sqrt(beta),       ||r_h^0||_op<=3sqrt(beta)/2. (2.1)

These use A^dagger B+B^dagger A=Re Tr(A^dagger B)I. The two noise-free
incidence contributions are exactly

 J^0_(p,first)=beta U_h+(beta/2) C_h V_h^dagger,
 J^0_(p,second)=(5beta/2-beta h_p)V_h+(beta/2)U_h^dagger C_h.
                                                               (2.2)

Taking the aligned half trace with the appropriate original link,

 (1/2)Re Tr U_h^dagger J^0_(p,first)
                         =beta(3/2-h_p/2)>=beta/2,
 (1/2)Re Tr V_h^dagger J^0_(p,second)
                         =beta(3-3h_p/2)>=0.             (2.3)

Thus FOR EVERY original U, without a weak-field condition,

                 a(J_e^0) dot u_e>=3 beta.              (2.4)

For clarity, J^0 is not the exact conditional mean. Since
E Z_p^dagger Z_p=2I, each second incidence gains 4U_e on averaging,
whereas each first incidence has mean J^0_(p,first). Consequently

 E[J_e|U]=J_e^0+12U_e.                                  (2.5)

At a flat configuration these means are 3beta U_e/2 per first
incidence and (3beta+4)U_e per second incidence. The total noise-free
current is (27beta/2)U_e, with a(J_e^0)=27beta u_e.

3. ACTUAL EXPONENTIAL MOMENTS, INCLUDING REPEATED AUXILIARIES

Let b=1+beta. The exact noncentral Gaussian transform gives

 E[exp(t||G_p||_F^2/b)|U] <= (1-t)^(-4) exp(2t),       0<=t<1,
 E[exp(t||R_h||_F^2/b)|U] <= (1-2t)^(-4) exp(9t/2),   0<=t<1/2.
                                                               (3.1)

For the second formula, R_h conditional on U is a complex Gaussian
with covariance 2I and mean r_h^0, whose norm square is at most9beta/2.
For the first, ||m_p||_F^2<=2beta. The formulas are uniform in U;
averaging over the original interacting law preserves them.

To handle joint moments without pretending R variables are independent,
define the complete local record

 L_p=||G_p||_F^2+||R_(p,A)||_F^2+||R_(p,B)||_F^2,
 c_0=2+sqrt3,
 K(t)=(1-c_0 t)^(-12) exp(11t),       0<=t<c_0^(-1).

After unitary right multiplication of the two centered R matrices, the
fluctuations in one plaquette block are (Z,Z+W_A,Z+W_B). Their covariance
matrix is

       [1 1 1; 1 2 1; 1 1 2] tensor I_(C^4),

with largest eigenvalue c_0. The combined mean norm square is at most
2beta+9beta/2+9beta/2=11beta. The noncentral complex Gaussian formula
therefore proves

 E[exp(t sum_(p in A) L_p/b)|U] <= K(t)^(|A|)            (3.2)

for EVERY set A of distinct plaquettes. The different complete blocks
are independent conditional on U; no independence under nu_beta is
used. The same bound consequently holds in the actual marginal.

For one link, sqrt(beta)r<=beta/2+r^2/2 and 2rg<=r^2+g^2 imply

 |a(J_e)|<=sqrt2[3beta/2+sum_(p incident to e) L_p].

Hence

 E_nu exp[t |a(J_e)|/b]
    <=exp(3sqrt2 t/2) K(sqrt2 t)^6,  0<=t<(sqrt2 c_0)^(-1).
                                                               (3.3)

For any link set S, each plaquette enters at most four incident link
stars, and |star(S)|<=6|S|. A conservative joint consequence is

 E_nu exp[t sum_(e in S)|a(J_e)|/b]
   <=[exp(3sqrt2 t/2)K(4sqrt2 t)^6]^(|S|),
                         0<=t<(4sqrt2 c_0)^(-1).        (3.4)

These are actual scaled tails and moments, not an assertion that the
zero-link auxiliary potential is convex. TURN81's explicit nonconvex
direction at beta>4 remains consistent with all these estimates.

4. A LOCAL SAFE RECORD WITH EXPONENTIAL LOW-CURRENT TAIL

Fix a link e. Let N_e(t) be the event that all six Z_p and all six
W_h actually entering its current obey

              ||Z_p||_F, ||W_h||_F <=t sqrt(beta).

For a first incidence, the current error has norm at most
sqrt(beta)(||Z||_F+||W||_F). For a second incidence, expand

 2(r^0+Z V^dagger+W)^dagger(m+Z)-2(r^0)^dagger m.

Using (2.1) gives the bound

 5sqrt(beta)||Z||_F+2sqrt(beta)||W||_F
                  +2||Z||_F^2+2||W||_F||Z||_F.

Summing the three incidences of each type proves on N_e(t)

 ||J_e-J_e^0||_F <=beta(27t+12t^2).                    (4.1)

Take t=1/40. Since sqrt2(27/40+12/1600)<1, (2.4) implies

 |a(J_e)|>=a(J_e) dot u_e>=2 beta.                     (4.2)

The same event gives, for the actual records entering J_e,

 ||G_p||_F <=(sqrt2+1/40)sqrt(beta)<2sqrt(beta),
 ||R_h||_F <=(3/sqrt2+1/20)sqrt(beta)<(9/4)sqrt(beta).
                                                               (4.3)

Define Safe_e to require ONLY |a(J_e)|>=2beta and all the upper
bounds on the right of (4.3), for these six G and six R records.
The U-dependent aligned inequality in (4.2) is used in the proof,
not in the definition of Safe_e. Thus Safe_e is an event of the REAL
auxiliary marginal alone. N_e(1/40)
implies Safe_e for every U, although N_e itself uses the conditional
noise coordinates.

For one standard complex matrix, ||Z||_F^2 is Gamma(4,1), and

 P(||Z||_F>x)=e^(-x^2)(1+x^2+x^4/2+x^6/6)
                            <=16 exp(-x^2/2).

A union bound over the twelve noises therefore gives the EXACT
normalized probability estimate

 P(Safe_e^c|U) <=p_beta:=min(1,192 exp[-beta/3200]),
 P_nu(Safe_e^c)<=p_beta.                                (4.4)

In particular (4.4) bounds the low-current event |a(J_e)|<2beta.
It is uniform in volume and in every original configuration, including
large plaquettes and balanced original staples. The auxiliary current
is a different conditioning statistic from an original fixed exterior.
There is no contradiction with the earlier balanced-staple Haar example.

If a construction needs BOTH half-path R records for every incident
plaquette, use eighteen noises instead. The same deterministic bounds
hold, with p_beta replaced by min(1,288 exp[-beta/3200]). This enlarged
ownership convention must not be silently paid by the twelve-noise count.

4A. PATH-CONVENTION VARIANT FOR REFLECTION-ADAPTED SPATIAL READS

The exact three-plus-three count is tied to the standard positive
half-path convention. It must not be silently reused after choosing
different basepoints/path orientations to adapt the HS map to a time
reflection. There is a simple version with its convention dependence
visible. Suppose m of the six half-path incidences of a link traverse
it as a first link and 6-m as a second link. An occurrence may traverse
the independent link as U_e^dagger; converting its current back to U_e
then takes a matrix adjoint. The aligned real trace, Frobenius bounds,
and Gaussian norm estimates above are unchanged by that conversion.

Equations (2.3) and the same expansion give

 a(J_e^0) dot u_e>=m beta,
 ||J_e-J_e^0||_F
    <=beta[(42-5m)t+4(6-m)t^2] on the twelve-noise event. (4.5)

If m>=2, use t=1/80. Since sqrt2(32/80+16/6400)<1, the
resulting safe-record statement is

 |a(J_e)|>=beta,
 ||G_p||_F<=2sqrt(beta), ||R_h||_F<=(9/4)sqrt(beta),
 P(unsafe_e|U)<=min(1,192 exp[-beta/12800]).              (4.6)

Its graph/count version below is unchanged, with this new probability.
For a spatial link in d=4, keeping the canonical positive half-path
choice on purely spatial plaquettes already supplies two first and
two second spatial incidences. Altering the temporal plaquette paths
to match reflection cannot remove these two first incidences. Thus
(4.6) applies to such a reflection-adapted spatial-link construction.
Any other convention must verify its own value of m. In particular
the numerical flat value27beta in Section6 remains a statement about
the standard three-plus-three convention, not all adapted conventions.

5. PRESCRIBED-SET RARITY AND INVERSE-SCALE CONSUMERS

Connect two positive links if they share an incident plaquette. This
graph has maximum degree at most6*3=18. Every finite set S therefore
contains a subset S_0 of size at least |S|/19 whose incident-plaquette
stars are disjoint. Conditional on U, the unsafe events for S_0 are
independent, because they read disjoint Z,W blocks. Their conditional
probabilities have the same bound (4.4). Thus

 P(all e in S are unsafe | U) <=p_beta^(|S|/19),
 P_nu(all e in S are unsafe) <=p_beta^(|S|/19).           (5.1)

The same result holds for the eighteen-noise definition with its own
prefactor. It also bounds any specified set of low-current links.
No independence of the original U, or of overlapping auxiliary
records, is asserted. A spatial connected-set census may now use
(5.1) with the appropriate lattice animal entropy; a merely unmarked
one-link probability was not substituted for a joint estimate.

For each fixed k>0 another useful actual marginal consequence is

 E_nu (1+|a(J_e)|)^(-k)
       <=(1+2beta)^(-k)+p_beta
       <=C_k(1+beta)^(-k).                              (5.2)

The final constant is finite since an exponential beats every fixed
power. This is a REGULARIZED inverse moment, suitable for bounded
conditional-noise coefficients. No claim of all singular moments
E|a|^(-k) is made near the four-dimensional current origin.

 |a(J_e)/beta-27u_e|

 P_joint(|a(J_e)/beta-27u_e|>epsilon)

7. WHAT THESE ESTIMATES CAN AND CANNOT PAY

Equations (4.3)--(5.1) supply real safe records with bounded scaled
auxiliaries and strong nonzero physical currents. They can be combined
with an independently proved weighted current-response theorem on those
same records. They do not alone prove such a complex response theorem,
or promote a real source germ to all complex auxiliary fields.

For a read set S, the probability that ANY record is unsafe has only
the immediate union bound |S|p_beta. On the physical logarithmic Wilson
trajectory beta~b log(1/a), this is a power-law suppression in a with
the displayed constant, not automatic domination of a read count of
order a^(-4). No assertion that the complete lattice or every UV
history is safe follows. The prescribed-set estimate (5.1) is useful
for local/connected expansions if their actual interaction bounds and
normalization are separately supplied; it is not such an expansion.

Strong auxiliary currents also do not directly imply a nonzero
reflected original observable. The exact conditional product-link law
has small one-link noise at large current, but all collective original
interactions remain in nu_beta. TURN81's original/auxiliary channel
identities and contact corrections continue to be required. This note
proves actual probability and moment estimates, not YM4 existence,
continuum nontriviality, or comparison with a selected RG law.
