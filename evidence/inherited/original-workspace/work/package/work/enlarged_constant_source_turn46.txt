RESULT

The enlarged-window comparison of enlarged_energy_window_audit_turn46
admits the full constant-source disc |g^2 zeta|<c_s, even when its
general source-profile disc c/(rho+gT)^2 is much smaller. Every charged
action term must carry the SAME constant source. The cutoff, ordinary
map, chart, classical saddle and all exterior data conventions remain
frozen as the source varies.

The decisive identity is lambda z^2=1, where lambda=g^-2-zeta and
z=lambda^-1/2. It keeps the normalized quadratic phase exactly real
and positive on the complex linear contour. A small bound g Lambda
is sufficient to absorb the cubic phase error into the Gaussian
decay. There is no requirement g Lambda^3 or N g Lambda be small.

This note rechecks the complex source contours and the zero-free
normalization. It does NOT automatically upgrade the second-step
theorem, establish a generated-action class on larger domains, or
identify the entire unwindowed Wilson history density.

1. PRECISE HYPOTHESES AND TWO DIFFERENT DATUM DOMAINS

ambient Gaussian dimension, and Pi its orthogonal projector onto K.
The nonorthogonal coordinate-chart projector is not substituted for Pi.

Use the uniform saddle/Hessian/Morse chart hypotheses (1.1)--(1.2)
of enlarged_energy_window_audit_turn46. In particular, in a fixed
small joint neighborhood,

 f(B_*+b,u)-s(u)=b^t H(u)b/2+R3(b,u),
 |R3(b,u)|<=C||b||_2^3,
 |log[J(B_*+b,u)/J_*(u)]|<=CN||b||_2,
 b_M(y,u)=H(u)^-1/2 y+O(||y||_2^2),

The complete Morse amplitude A(y,u)/A0(u), including its Jacobian,

Require all of the following:

2. LARGE CONSTANT-SOURCE DISC AND THE COMPLETE AUXILIARY DENSITY

Fix c_s<1/8. On |g^2 zeta|<c_s define

 lambda=g^-2-zeta,
 z=g(1-g^2 zeta)^-1/2,
 z^d=g^d exp[-(d/2)log(1-g^2 zeta)].                              (2.1)

Branches are analytic and take z=g at zero source. They satisfy

The auxiliary product-Gaussian theorem, evaluated at this z, gives

 a_aux(u;zeta)=exp[-lambda s(u)]A0(u)(2pi)^(d/2)z^d
                                      c_T^m exp F(z,u),          (2.2)

The source disc maps inside |z|<c/T^3 after reducing the fixed
cluster smallness bound gT^3. Each F_X is even in z and vanishes at
z=0; the exact interaction contains all its convergent terms. The
factor c_T^m uses the FULL ambient dimension m, not d.

Normalize actual and auxiliary densities by the same leading factor

 A_lead(u;zeta)=exp[-lambda s(u)]A0(u)(2pi)^(d/2)z^d.

Call the resulting quantities R_E and R_aux. Then
R_aux=c_T^m exp F. This normalization is essential: zeta s(u) may
be large on the enlarged window, and is retained exactly rather
than estimated as a small quantum correction.

3. LINEAR CONTOUR AND THE AMBIENT-CUBE COMPLEMENT

Let xi be REAL in the full ambient space, r=|Pi xi|, and set
b=z H(u)^-1/2 Pi xi. The bilinear symmetry and analytic square-root
identity give EXACTLY

 lambda b^t H(u)b=|Pi xi|^2.                                    (3.1)

Thus, for r<=C0 Lambda,

 Re[lambda(f(B_*+b,u)-s(u))]
     >=r^2/2-C|z|r^3>=c r^2,                                   (3.2)

provided g Lambda is small relative to fixed C0. This uses no
Hermitian positivity for a complex H. The quadratic term becomes
real by (3.1), and the cubic error is absorbed into it.

The Jacobian factor is bounded by exp(CN |z|r)<=exp(CN). Including
the untouched orthogonal Gaussian directions, the absolute normalized
integrand is bounded by

 exp(CN)(2pi)^(-m/2)
       exp[-c|Pi xi|^2-|Pi_perp xi|^2/2].                        (3.3)

Replace it by an isotropic Gaussian with a smaller fixed rate. Its
normalization costs another exp(Cm)<=exp(CN). A union bound for the
m ambient coordinates then bounds its mass off [-T,T]^m by

                         exp(CN)m exp(-cT^2).                   (3.4)

No product structure for Pi xi is assumed. No source factor is
bounded separately by exp(|zeta|E0): it has already been incorporated
EXACTLY into lambda and the whitening (3.1).

Take the linear K-ball radius R=Lambda/2. The ambient cube lies in
its cylinder by mT^2<=Lambda^2/16. Its integrand is identical to the
auxiliary integrand there, including the K-perpendicular Gaussian.
Therefore the normalized linear-ball integral L_R obeys

                 |L_R-R_aux|<=exp(CN)m exp(-cT^2).                (3.5)

This is a fixed-domain holomorphic integral estimate in u and zeta,
not a complex positivity argument. The real positive submeasure
interpretation at zeta=0 additionally retains the cube-in-plateau
condition from the preceding audit; the direct action bound and the

4. THE SOURCE-DEPENDENT STOKES CONTOUR

On real xi in K, |xi|<=R, use

 lambda[f(B_*+bt,u)-s(u)]=R^2/2+O(|z|R^3).                      (4.2)

Hence the real part of the last expression is at least R^2/4.
The requirement is |z|R small, not |z|R^3 small.

The normalized top-degree holomorphic form is

           exp[-(f(B_*+b,u)-s(u))/z^2]J(B_*+b,u) db_1...db_d.

Morse integrands, with the SAME chosen complex z^d branch. The form

All determinant, Jacobian and weakened-Gaussian costs are paid by

5. FIXED REAL ENERGY CUTOFF AND THE COMPLEX RADIAL DEFORMATION

Let a=d/2>0 and define the angular Morse amplitude

 h(v,u)=average_theta A(sqrt(2v)theta,u)/A0(u).

Put t0=E0/2 and q=t0-s(u). The exact normalized radial expression is

 R_E=lambda^a/Gamma(a) {
   q^a integral_0^1 exp(-lambda q t)t^(a-1)h(qt,u)dt
   +integral_(t0)^(2E0) exp[-lambda(t-s)](t-s)^(a-1)

The cutoff is a function of the FIXED REAL outer variable t. The
real equality follows from complete supported-fiber coverage. The

Change variable w=lambda v in the inner integral. Since |s|<E0/8,

 Re(lambda q)>=(3-5c_s)Lambda^2/8>Lambda^2/8.                    (5.2)

The source-dependent Morse ball in (4.1) ends at

 v=z^2 R^2/2,             w=lambda v=R^2/2=Lambda^2/8.

Its radial integral is therefore the EXACT common positive-real

7. LOCAL MARKS, DATUM DERIVATIVES, AND THE LARGE-WINDOW REGIME

Define Hloc(u,zeta)=F(z(zeta),u)-F(g,u) independently on the FIXED

 ||partial_zeta^n Hloc(.,0)||_kappa
                   <=n! C^n g^(2n+2)T^6.                       (7.1)

In particular its first marked Hessian has exponentially weighted

This bound is unchanged by enlarging Lambda because the auxiliary

For the actual tail, Cauchy instead gives

 ||D_u^k partial_zeta^n [D(u,zeta)-D(u,0)]|_(0,0)||
    <=k! n! C^(k+n) rho^-k g^(2n) delta_const,       n>=1,        (7.2)

in the corresponding sup-direction norm. For k=2,n=1, this is
C N Lambda^-2 delta_const, with fixed constants depending on c0.
A full-box carrier still pays exp(kappa C_L N). Choosing T^2 and
Lambda^2 above fixed sufficiently large multiples of N+log(1/g)
and retaining the cube inclusion makes that charge and any specified

For the enlarged-window choice

The omitted-energy part of (6.1) is now
