RESULT AND SCOPE

For a bounded source with a fixed thin physical read, the finite local
projection can be averaged under the ORIGINAL whole first-bare success
law. Its good-boundary restriction has a quantitative probability bound
under that very law. A constant replacement on the boundary complement
preserves constants and linearity. The resulting exact split T=A+E has

 |E_beta f| <= C osc(f) (|I|+|B|) exp(-c r^2),             (1)

with no ambient-volume factor in this REAL error. I is a complete local
active free-coordinate set and B its complete finite free-coordinate
boundary read. This improves the full-torus union price in the preceding
thin-source projection. It is still a first-bare thin-source theorem.
The analytic radius proved below may depend on ambient volume. No
uniform analytic norm on a volume-independent tube, supported carrier,
thick-read smoothing, or later source induction is asserted.

1. ORIGINAL LAW AND THE PRECISE LOCAL FIBER

Here J is the complement of I in the free-coordinate index set;
K_I and K_J are products of the SAME original radius-r balls.
The original raw outer mass remains C_out(beta) Z_beta. All earlier
source-independent record factors remain in that original mass.
The theorem does not identify (2) with the pre-Q1 masked D_y law.

Choose I to contain all physical source arguments and all old/new pivot
rows and reconstruction stencils needed for its source-compatible chart.
Affected boundary pivots remain solved variables. B subset J contains

through z_B. The background minimum can depend on the complete beta;
it does not thereby create an additional free-noise read. This distinction

This is a linear evaluation functional with c(1)=1. A fixed probability

There is no division by a selected atom or a lower bound on its prior

3. EXACT FULL-KERNEL SPLIT AND ITS REAL BOUND

Define

                 [1_G(z) A_beta,z f+1_(G^c)(z)c(f)],

                 [1_G(z) E_beta,z f
                  +1_(G^c)(z)(T_beta,z f-c(f))].         (7)

This uses the ORIGINAL exterior marginal (3). The identity T=A+E is
exact. Both operators are linear, A1=1 and E1=0. On the real family A

domain, then |T_beta,z f-c(f)|<=osc(f), for real or complex-valued f.
Equations (5),(6) give

 |E_beta f|<=osc(f)(epsilon_I+epsilon_B),                (8)

and hence (1) after taking the smaller fixed exponent. No rarity of
independent events is asserted; the two probabilities are simply added.
For f with sup norm eta this is at most C eta (|I|+|B|)exp(-c r^2).

The same identity multiplied by C_out(beta)Z_beta, and by any actual
held record indicators, is the corresponding RAW identity. Neither

4. FINITE HOLOMORPHY AFTER THE ORIGINAL OUTER AVERAGE

This step does not differentiate the z-dependent auxiliary domain.
For each fixed REAL z in G, the preceding projection gives A_beta,z f

The original complete H_beta(x,z) is holomorphic in beta on a spare
native chart for every (x,z) in the fixed ORIGINAL K_I x K_J. On a
sufficiently small tube about a real beta_c, its finite pointwise

 |exp[-H_beta(x,z)+H_beta_c(x,z)]-1|<=q<1/2.             (10)

A sufficient radius is c/(1+M_global), where M_global bounds the sum
of all coarse-parameter derivatives of H on that common chart. This
bound is finite and may depend polynomially on ambient volume and
inverse chart margins. Take the minimum with the uniform local

Using (3), the FIRST line of (7) is exactly

 A_beta f = [integral_(K_I x K_J) exp[-H_beta(x,z)]
                 {1_G(z) A_beta,z f+1_(G^c)(z)c(f)} dx dz]
                    /[integral_(K_I x K_J) exp[-H_beta(x,z)] dx dz]. (11)

All outer integration sets in (11) are fixed real sets. No f is

5. ACTUAL ADVANCE AND REMAINING TASK

For a fixed eligible thin original source, choose I as the finite union
of its complete reconstruction/pivot reads, and B as the finite first-
bare action collar. Their sizes do not grow when the ambient torus is
enlarged, provided this neighborhood embeds without wrapping. The real
projection error (1) is therefore uniform in ambient volume. The local
boundary window is quantitatively typical under the exact original full
first-success law, so it need not be left as an unestimated complement
at this scope.

This does not remove the complete-row rank obstruction for a thick hybrid,
does not prove an iterated remainder keeps its original thin read, and
does not replace the source-free original first-success density by a
different all-history law. The resulting A can depend on the full coarse
datum. Its complex radius in (10) can shrink with volume. A gauge-invariant
local or quasilocal analytic carrier and a uniform native scale recursion
remain required before this can enter a continuum existence proof.
