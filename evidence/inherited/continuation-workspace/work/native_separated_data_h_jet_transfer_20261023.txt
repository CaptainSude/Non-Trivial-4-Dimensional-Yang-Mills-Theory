2. METRIC ESTIMATE INCLUDING FINE CHANGED INPUTS

For any admissible contour from output root y of grade a to z in K,
let p be its largest visited grade. A subcontour reaching that grade
costs at least G(p-a-1), by the native grade-crossing argument. Native
incident-root conventions can add a fixed endpoint cost; include it in
C_inc below. Also every grade on the contour is <=p, hence its weighted
length is at least its physical length divided by ell_p. Therefore

 length_d(Gamma) >= G(p-a-1),
 length_d(Gamma) >= r L^(h-p)-C_inc.

Put t=h-a and q=h-p. Averaging these bounds gives

 length_d(Gamma) >= (G/2)t
        + [r L^q-Gq-G-C_inc]/2.

The supremum S(G,r,L)=sup_(q in R)[Gq-rL^q] is finite: its expression
tends to minus infinity in both directions. If desired its maximizing
point is q=log_L[G/(r log L)]. Thus, with

 C_* = max(0,[G+C_inc+S(G,r,L)]/2),

                 d(y,z) >= (G/2)(h-a)-C_*.             (1)

The proof allows fine changed rows, paths which leave the h patch, and
paths visiting grades above h. It does not assume the input grade >=h.
Finitely many native incident-root corrections alter C_*, not the slope.

Spend half of the rate beta on (1) and the other half on that Schur sum:
