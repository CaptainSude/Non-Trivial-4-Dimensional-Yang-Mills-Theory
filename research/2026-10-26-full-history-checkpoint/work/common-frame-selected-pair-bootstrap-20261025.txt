COMMON-FRAME SELECTED-PAIR BOOTSTRAP
25 October 2026. New deterministic lemma; independent review pending.

PURPOSE AND BOUNDARY

This note proves a simultaneous direct-domain statement for a specified
Landau-pair recursion. It isolates a way of avoiding a repeated B3 loss.
It does NOT identify this recursion with the complete original ordinary
prefix law. In particular, an exact choice of real noise coordinates and
the covariance of every private-pivot/return/gauge factor must still be
matched before this can be a producer for the project's G1 gate.

The recursion uses the independent selected current, NOT the physical
current recomputed from its output field. All complex localizer parameters
at all dates can vary simultaneously. The result is finite dimensional
and uniform in the number of dates and the lattice volume at fixed L.

SETUP

Fix a finite original fine lattice and grades j=0,...,t. In grade-j units
the original fine mesh is xi_j=L^{-j}. All fields below are on the SAME
original fine bonds. The spacing in a covariant difference changes with
the grade; the group-valued field itself does not change under this
conversion of units.

Fix a compact reference Wbar on the complete fine array. Its curvature
obeys the terminal grade-t direct bound with allowance kbar_t. Require
its local compact gauge-cube tests at EVERY grade on the same fixed
small cap, and retain the complete read of every later primitive,
localizer, and determining tower. The curvature allowance converts to
L^{-2(t-j)} kbar_t at grade j. The potential in one fixed terminal gauge
only converts with L^{-(t-j)}, whereas its first difference converts
with L^{-2(t-j)}; no stronger potential estimate is inferred by mere
restriction. Smaller-cube re-gauging could supply such a stronger
estimate, but is not required or asserted here. The construction never
changes Wbar. Each grade may use its own UNITARY compact-reference
gauge to verify the stated local test.

Let the top pair be

 U_t=exp(i xi_t B_t) Wbar,              J_t independent,

with b_t=max(||B_t||,||nabla_Wbar^{xi_t} B_t||),
 k_t=xi_t^{-2}||dU_t-1||,              c_t=||J_t||.

All matrix norms are a fixed submultiplicative norm; color dimension is
fixed. Restrict to a uniform logarithm neighborhood for the relative
fields. Covariant differences use the same native finite stencils at all
grades. The formulae below are interpreted on complete reads, including
their boundary collars. We do not extend an arbitrary field outside a
carrier by declaration.

ONE-STEP RESPONSE INPUT

At grade j>=1 let A_j be a holomorphic function of the current pair
(U_{j+1},J_{j+1}), the fixed free-noise coordinate x_j, and all of that
step's source-localizer variables s_j. Its literal intended realization is

 B'_j=g_j C_j x_j-h_j Dtilde_j(g_j C_j x_j),
 A_j=H0_j(s_j) B'_j+A0_j(s_j,H0_j(s_j) B'_j)
       -H_j(s_j)D_j(s_j,H0_j(s_j) B'_j+A0_j(...)).       (1)

Here C_j, h_j and Dtilde_j are the private-pivot objects. H_j in the last
line is the linear propagator, not the whole response. This distinction
is essential. Require each coefficient in (1) on its complete admitted
current domain.

The usable response bound is

 max(||A_j||,||nabla_{U_{j+1}}^{xi_j} A_j||,
     ||P1_j A_j||,||Delta_{U_{j+1}}^{xi_j} A_j||)
                   <= C_H delta_j.                    (2)

It holds on one FIXED small direct input domain, for all original
||g_j x_j||<=delta_j and |s_j(Delta)|<=exp(kappa1).
Constants and the input domain do not depend on j or volume. No t_D
old-action Cauchy circle is included in (2).

B116 (1.11)--(1.21), with B109 (3.13)--(3.17), are the native candidates
for (1)--(2). Their independent-field/current and simultaneous-localizer
uniformity is explicit. A concrete prefix application must still match
the operator conventions and all four norms of (2), including P1.
The present lemma also applies to any fully verified responses with (2).

Set A_0=B'_0 on the original finite fine-noise stencil. For j=0 use the
corresponding finite-stencil bound (2), which has xi_0=1; no depth-zero
minimum response is invoked. This bottom map must be checked separately
when identifying a concrete native prefix.

RECURSION AND CLAIM

Define, in this common frame,

 U_j=exp(i xi_j A_j) U_{j+1},
 J_j=L^{-3}J_{j+1}+Delta_{U_{j+1}}^{xi_j} A_j
                   -P1_j A_j+F1_j(U_{j+1},A_j).        (3)

This is the adjacent-grade B109 (3.13) selected current. In particular,
at zero response J_j=L^{-3}J_{j+1}. No stationarity identity is imposed
on J_j, and no weakened response is replaced by a new physical current.

On a fixed small direct cap, the finite current expansion gives

 ||F1_j(U,A)|| <= C[k_direct r+r^2+r^3],               (4)

where r is the four-slot norm in (2). This is B109 (3.11)--(3.14), with
the local nonlinear remainder retained. All inverse matrices are
holomorphic inverses, not Hermitian conjugates at complex configurations.

There are fixed C_b,C_k,C_c such that the recursively defined pairs obey

 b_j <= L^{-1} b_{j+1}+C_b delta_j,
 k_j <= L^{-2} k_{j+1}+C_k delta_j,
 c_j <= L^{-3} c_{j+1}+C_c delta_j.                    (5)

Here U_j=exp(i xi_j B_j) Wbar on a common logarithm branch, and b_j,
k_j,c_j have the same meanings as at the top. Therefore

 b_j <= L^{-(t-j)} b_t
             +C_b sum_{ell=j}^{t-1} L^{-(ell-j)}delta_ell,
 k_j <= L^{-2(t-j)} k_t
             +C_k sum_{ell=j}^{t-1} L^{-2(ell-j)}delta_ell,
 c_j <= L^{-3(t-j)} c_t
             +C_c sum_{ell=j}^{t-1} L^{-3(ell-j)}delta_ell. (6)

The constants use fixed strict caps, not the unknown values of later
iterates. Choose those caps first and choose the top allowances and
delta_* afterwards. If their geometric bounds (6), with delta_j<=delta_*,
fit strictly inside the response domain of (2), descending induction
constructs the entire family. The statement is uniform for the Cartesian
product of all original noise balls and all indicated localizer polydiscs.
For holomorphy use strict enlarged bounds around the closed polydiscs.

PROOF OF THE THREE ESTIMATES

At step j write Y=L^{-1}B_{j+1}. Exactly,

 U_{j+1}=exp(i xi_j Y)Wbar,
 ||Y||=L^{-1}||B_{j+1}||,
 ||nabla_Wbar^{xi_j}Y||
                   =L^{-2}||nabla_Wbar^{xi_{j+1}}B_{j+1}||. (7)

The local logarithm gives

 B_j=A_j+Y+R_{xi_j}(A_j,Y).

BCH analyticity and its vanishing mixed remainder on either coordinate
axis imply, uniformly for xi_j<=1 on the fixed cap,

 ||R_xi(A,Y)|| <= C xi ||A||||Y||,
 ||nabla R_xi(A,Y)|| <= C xi
      (||nabla A||||Y||+||A||||nabla Y||).              (8)

For the covariant finite-difference version of (8), parallel transport
the two endpoint arguments to the same base point and use conjugation
equivariance of the logarithm. Apply the integral first-derivative formula
for R to that finite difference; no continuum chain rule is assumed.

The difference between the U_{j+1} and Wbar covariant derivatives obeys

 ||nabla_Wbar^{xi_j}A_j||
 <= ||nabla_{U_{j+1}}^{xi_j}A_j||+C||Y||||A_j||.       (9)

Indeed each changed parallel transporter differs by O(xi_j ||Y||),
which cancels the single xi_j^{-1} in the difference operator. The
number of links in this transporter is fixed by the native stencil.
All remaining transporter norms are uniformly bounded on the fixed cap.

Equations (2),(7)--(9) give b_j<=L^{-1}b_{j+1}+C_b delta_j.
The terms involving Y or its derivative multiply delta_j, and Y lies
inside a previously fixed cap. They contribute to C_b delta_j, not to
a constant larger than one multiplying the old b_{j+1}.

For curvature, compare one complete plaquette of exp(i xi_j A_j)U_{j+1}
and U_{j+1}. Transport the four A values to one corner. The oriented
linear sum is bounded by

 C xi_j ||nabla_{U_{j+1}}^{xi_j}A_j||
     +C||dU_{j+1}-1||||A_j||.

Multiplication by xi_j and the exponential product remainder give

 ||dU_j-dU_{j+1}|| <= C xi_j^2
      [r_j+r_j^2+k_direct r_j] <= C_k xi_j^2 delta_j. (10)

Finite complex transporter norms are bounded as above; their unitarity
is not needed. The difference (10) compares the two plaquette matrices
in the SAME corner frame, so the triangle inequality has coefficient
one on ||dU_{j+1}-1||. Divide by xi_j^2 and use
xi_{j+1}^2/xi_j^2=L^{-2}. This proves the second estimate in (5).

The third estimate follows directly from the exact L^{-3} coefficient
in (3), (2), and (4). It does not require a physical-current identity.
Solving the three scalar recurrences gives (6).

ONE-TIME DIRECT-TO-FULL ADMISSION

Choose the fixed direct caps small enough that the B109 p263 sufficient
criterion places every grade-j pair with those caps into its full
U_j^c space, for each actual carrier and its prescribed maximal tower.
In particular choose k_direct<=alpha0/(8 B3) and the relative potential
cap below the corresponding native comparison fraction. The compact
factor is still Wbar, so its real gauge-cube and derived tests also hold.
The bound on independent J_j is imposed separately by the third line
of (6). This criterion is applied once to each constructed pair. The
factor B3 is NOT inserted into the recursion (5) or multiplied t times.

This supplies the full (iv) tests only on those complete reads to which
the B109 criterion applies; it does not identify a source localizer's
read with a smaller original cavity packet. All reads must be present
in the fixed complete array. Restrictions to carriers retain every
required gauge cube and determining collar.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT YET ESTABLISH

The actual native minimum is related to the Landau field by a return.
B98 controls the small relative return u; a separate relative axial h
can occur in the total h^{-1}u. Coarse normalization h|T^j=1 controls
neither h nor its restriction to the NEXT finer lattice. Therefore (3)
cannot simply be asserted to equal the original fixed-noise prefix.

There are two possible matching routes. One can prove covariance and
smallness of the full literal frame/noise arguments without changing
coordinates. Alternatively, one can prove an exact triangular REAL
change of free-noise coordinates, including original cuts, Jacobians,
soft gauge factors, coarea factors, masks and normalizers, and then
define a holomorphic extension using those new fixed REAL coordinates.
The latter never permits rotating a complex integration contour or
declaring an arbitrary predicate invariant. Either route requires its
own complete proof. No complex prefix law is claimed here.

Even after that matching, source denominator bounds and catalogue/root
summations remain separate. A uniform field/current image does not by
itself prove a normalized source norm or a Yang--Mills limit.
