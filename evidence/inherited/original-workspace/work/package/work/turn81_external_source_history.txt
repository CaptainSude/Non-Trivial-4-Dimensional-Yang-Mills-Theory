RESULT
There is an exact full-original-law source representation that does not
evaluate an inverse RG record on complex backgrounds. A first
Hubbard--Stratonovich (HS) transformation replaces each ORIGINAL canonical
plaquette source by an explicit function of the squared norm of a complex
2x2 Gaussian auxiliary field. Its determinant factor, scalar exponential,
literal Wilson partition function, and coincident-plaquette contact terms
are all retained. A second HS transformation integrates every compact
link exactly, giving the same source family on a positive zero-link
auxiliary measure.

This is an adaptation of the known plaquette HS construction, not a new
decoupling method. The useful addition here is the complete original
source dictionary and its precise obstruction to a simple Gaussian
domination argument: the canonical radial auxiliary variance grows as
4 beta+O(1), and the resulting zero-link density is actually nonconvex for
beta>4 along an explicit direction. Neither fact rules out more refined
estimates. In particular the auxiliary conditional noise is not a
physical variance witness. The same source family and contacts must be
used when testing any proposed continuum or reflection-positive consumer.

1. ORIGINAL LAW, CONVENTIONS, AND PROVENANCE

Use a finite nondegenerate periodic hypercubic SU(2) lattice, with periods
at least4 for the TURN64 energy estimate used below. Every independent
oriented link is integrated with normalized Haar measure. Write
  h_p(U)=1-Re Tr U_p/2 in[0,2], E_p=beta h_p, beta>0,
  Z_beta=integral exp(-sum_p E_p) dU,
  mu_beta(dU)=Z_beta^-1 exp(-sum_p E_p)dU.                 (1.1)
All identities through Section5 hold on any finite graph with the stated
plaquette path decomposition. No selected sector, reference redraw, or
thermodynamic limit is introduced.

For a plaquette based at x with positive directions mu,nu, choose paths
  A_p=U_(x,mu) U_(x+mu,nu),
  B_p=U_(x,nu) U_(x+nu,mu), U_p=A_p B_p^dagger.           (1.2)
Both A_p and B_p run from x to z=x+mu+nu. Thus
  ||A_p+B_p||_F^2=8-4h_p.                                (1.3)
The standard complex matrix Gaussian is

Its four complex entries have E|G_ab|^2=1. Direct completion of squares
gives, for a fixed complex matrix X and real c,

2. FIRST HS TRANSFORM AND A POSITIVE ORIGINAL-LAW LIFT

Equations(1.3)--(1.4) give the literal identity
  exp(-beta h_p)=exp(-2beta)
     integral exp(sqrt(beta) Re Tr G_p^dagger(A_p+B_p))
                      gamma_1(dG_p).                    (2.1)
Consequently the following probability measure has U marginal exactly
mu_beta:
  J_beta(dU,dG)=Z_beta^-1 exp(-2beta|P|)dU
      product_p gamma_1(dG_p)
      exp[sum_p sqrt(beta) Re Tr G_p^dagger(A_p+B_p)].    (2.2)
The conditional G_p given the COMPLETE original U are independent, with
  G_p=m_p(U)+Z_p,
  m_p=sqrt(beta)(A_p+B_p)/2,
  ||m_p||_F^2=beta(2-h_p)=2beta-E_p, Z_p~gamma_1.         (2.3)
This is an explicit positive augmentation, with no inverse RG record.

Gauge covariance is literal. Under the original gauge transformation,
A_p,B_p and G_p transform as g_x (.) g_z^-1. The Frobenius norm, Gaussian
measure and coupling in(2.2) are invariant. The chosen path convention
can be transported under lattice symmetries, including reflections;
it should not be silently fixed asymmetrically when claiming such a
symmetry. Reflection-positive half-local assertions require the kernel
to read only the corresponding original half, as in the separate root
OS-quotient argument. No such assertion follows merely from positivity.

3. FULL ORIGINAL SOURCE FAMILY, INCLUDING ITS NORMALIZER

Set r_p=||G_p||_F^2. For Re t<1, direct Gaussian integration gives
  E[exp(t r_p)|U]=(1-t)^-4
             exp[t(2beta-E_p)/(1-t)].                    (3.1)
For Re s<1 take t=-s/(1-s). Define
  T_beta(s,r)=exp(2beta s)(1-s)^-4
                         exp[-s r/(1-s)].               (3.2)
Then, exactly and pointwise in the original real U,
  E[T_beta(s,r_p)|U]=exp(s E_p).                          (3.3)
For an arbitrary finite plaquette source array s=(s_p), conditional
independence gives the FULL normalized source identity
  M_beta(s):=E_mu exp(sum_p s_p E_p)
     = exp(sum_p 2beta s_p) product_p(1-s_p)^-4
          E_J exp[-sum_p s_p r_p/(1-s_p)].                (3.4)
This expectation uses the SAME source-zero probability J_beta and its
literal Z_beta. None of its scalar factors can be discarded. Near zero,
the log version contains sum_p[2beta s_p-4 log(1-s_p)] plus the logarithm
of the last expectation. The branch of log is the one at zero.

The literal auxiliary integral is absolutely convergent and holomorphic
on Re s_p<1 for every p: its Gaussian precision has real part
Re[1/(1-s_p)]>0, and the remaining fixed-U linear coefficients are bounded.
Compactness of U also proves local domination after U integration. The
left side of(3.4) is entire on all of C^|P|. Continuing it beyond that
half-plane does not assert absolute convergence of the displayed
auxiliary integral there. At every finite regulator Z_beta>0.

For plus sources F_f=sum_p f_p E_p and F_k=sum_p k_p E_p, put
s_p=z f_p+w k_p. This retains the complete original canonical calibration.
For the alternative fixed physical reference gamma_R sum_p f_p h_p,
one must instead set s_p=z gamma_R f_p/beta. The factor beta has not
disappeared: changing this substitution changes the observable. This
dictionary therefore applies to both earlier calibration discussions
without declaring them equivalent or assuming a trace-anomaly identity.

4. EXACT JETS AND THE COINCIDENT CONTACT TERM

Let Y_p=2beta+4-r_p. Differentiating(3.2) gives
  T'_beta(0,r_p)=Y_p,
  T''_beta(0,r_p)=Y_p^2+4-2r_p.                           (4.1)
Therefore
  E[Y_p|U]=E_p,
  E[Y_p^2+4-2r_p|U]=E_p^2.                              (4.2)
All higher finite jets are obtained by differentiating the same T.
Distinct plaquettes multiply their T factors; repeated derivatives at
one plaquette act on its SINGLE T factor. Replacing every source by Y
and then taking arbitrary powers loses(4.1)'s contact subtraction.

For real profiles f,k, define R_f=sum_p f_p r_p. Total covariance and
the conditional independence in(2.3) yield
  Cov_mu(F_f,F_k)
    =Cov_J(R_f,R_k)
           -sum_p f_p k_p E_mu[4+4beta-2E_p]              (4.3)
    =Cov_J(R_f,R_k)+4 sum_p f_p k_p-2 E_J R_(fk).
For DISJOINT plaquette supports the contact vanishes. This identifies
their mixed original covariance exactly with an auxiliary radial mixed
covariance, but supplies no decay or positive lower bound for it.
The equality concerns the actual original law, not a conditional
reference covariance. It includes vacuum/scalar response automatically.

5. SECOND HS TRANSFORM: EXACT ZERO-LINK SOURCE REPRESENTATION

There are two half-paths h=(U_h,V_h) per plaquette, A_p and B_p. Introduce
one further standard complex matrix R_h for each half-path. The identity
  ||sqrt(beta)U_h/2+G_p V_h^dagger||_F^2
     =beta/2+r_p+sqrt(beta) Re Tr G_p^dagger U_h V_h
gives
  exp[sqrt(beta) Re Tr G_p^dagger U_h V_h]
   =exp(-beta/2-r_p)
      integral gamma_1(dR_h)
        exp Re Tr R_h^dagger[sqrt(beta) U_h+2G_p V_h^dagger]. (5.1)
The conditional R_h given G,U is CN with mean
sqrt(beta)U_h/2+G_p V_h^dagger. Every original link now appears only
linearly. Write its complete coefficient J_e as the sum of
  sqrt(beta) R_h       when e is the first link U_h,
  2 R_h^dagger G_p     when e is the second link V_h.      (5.2)
Indeed Re Tr R_h^dagger G_p V_h^dagger
equals Re Tr G_p^dagger R_h V_h. The positive oriented convention(1.2)
makes all these links original positive links. Other conventions require
the corresponding inversion/conjugation, not a changed identity.

Let
  I(J)=integral_SU(2) exp(Re Tr J^dagger U)dU>0,
  gamma_3(dG)=(3/pi)^4 exp(-3||G||_F^2)d^8G,
  D_beta=integral product_p gamma_3(dG_p)
                product_h gamma_1(dR_h) product_e I(J_e).
The two occurrences exp(-r_p) in(5.1), together with gamma_1(G_p),
produce 3^-4 gamma_3(G_p). All constants are consequently
  Z_beta=exp(-3beta|P|)3^(-4|P|)D_beta.                   (5.3)
This is finite and strictly positive by the exact previous identities.
The auxiliary law nu_beta is D_beta^-1 times the integrand defining
D_beta. Its G marginal is the same G marginal as J_beta. Therefore
  M_beta(s)=exp(sum_p2beta s_p)product_p(1-s_p)^-4
       [integral product gamma_3(G)gamma_1(R)
          product_e I(J_e) exp(-sum_p s_p r_p/(1-s_p))]
       /D_beta.                                         (5.4)
Both numerator and denominator use the SAME beta and the SAME J_e.
No hidden retuning of the link kernel or removal of D_beta occurs.

For completeness, identify a real four-vector a(J) by
Re Tr J^dagger U=a(J) dot q for U=q0 I+i sum_a q_a sigma_a.
Then I(J)=2 I_1(|a|)/|a|, with value1 at zero understood by its power

6. WHAT THE AUXILIARY LAW DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY CONTROL

The conditional moments of r_p are
  E[r_p|U]=4+2beta-E_p,
  Var(r_p|U)=4+4beta-2E_p.                                (6.1)
TURN64's actual finite-volume Wilson energy estimate gives
  E_mu E_p<=3/2,
  E_mu exp(E_p/4)<=2^(3/4),
  E_mu E_p^2<=C_2:=32*2^(3/4).
Thus the SAME full auxiliary law satisfies
  Var_nu(r_p)=Var_mu(E_p)+4+4beta-2 E_mu E_p,
  4beta+1<=Var_nu(r_p)<=4beta+4+C_2.                      (6.2)
This rules out a beta-uniform sub-Gaussian variance constant for the
unrescaled centered radial auxiliary, and a beta-uniform absolute bound
for its centered MGF on a fixed complex disk: Cauchy's second-derivative
estimate would contradict(6.2). It does NOT show that physical canonical
energy fluctuations grow. The contact in(4.3) cancels precisely this
conditional Gaussian noise. It is also not a physical OS-norm witness;
conditional noises can be null in an induced reflection quotient.

There is an additional exact failure of a naive globally convex
Gaussian-potential argument. Relative to Lebesgue measure the zero-link
density has potential
  V_aux=3 sum_p||G_p||^2+sum_h||R_h||^2-sum_e log I(J_e)+constant.
Take all G=0 and all R=0 except one R_h=t I. Only its first link has
J_e=sqrt(beta)t I. Consequently
  V_aux(t)=2t^2-log I(sqrt(beta)t I)+constant,
  V_aux''(0)=4-beta.                                    (6.3)
The last equality uses Haar Var(Re Tr U)=1. Hence this ACTUAL transformed
Wilson density is nonconvex for beta>4. No positive uniform Hessian
bound relative to this unmodified Gaussian reference is available.
This example neither disproves a different domination/cluster method
nor identifies the transformed law with a free Gaussian law.

7. OTHER ORIGINAL PROBES AND COMPACT-GROUP SHIFTS

For any bounded original gauge-invariant probe Phi(U), including a fixed
finite-lattice flowed probe, its normalized source E_mu exp(z Phi) is
already entire. The transformations above preserve it exactly by keeping
  E[exp(z Phi(U)) | G,R]
in the zero-link integral. The remaining U conditional factorizes by

covariance. The simple formula(3.2) is special to the plaquette energy.
No uniform locality or complex auxiliary-domain norm for the flowed

For comparison, an arbitrary smooth vector field V on the original
compact link manifold satisfies the exact integration-by-parts identity
  E_mu[(div V-V S_beta)F]=-E_mu[VF].                      (7.1)
Transporting the WHOLE action and Haar Jacobian by the flow of V leaves
Z_beta unchanged. Its score is not the original plaquette source unless
one separately solves an appropriate mu-dependent divergence/Poisson
equation. Dropping V S_beta or the Jacobian drops real terms. Earlier
TURN45/69 force/Fisher work already studies these issues; (7.1) is not
a new uniform source estimate. Likewise the four-partition and pressure
comparison identities in TURN30/31 are not replaced by a new estimate
merely by making the source external.
