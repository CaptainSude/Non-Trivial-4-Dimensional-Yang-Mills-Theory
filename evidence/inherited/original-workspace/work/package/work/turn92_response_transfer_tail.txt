5. AN EXACT ONE-REAL-SOURCE TARGET FOR THE ORIGINAL WILSON PROBE

5A. Reflection symmetry of the actual finite source.
Keep the homogeneous original Wilson torus and the exact probe
 G_n=n^-1 sum_(0<=t<n)phi_t-n^-1 sum_(n<=t<2n)phi_t,
 0<=phi_t<=1, 2n<=N.
Each phi_t is the same spatial-flow and spatial-smear function of the
original spatial links on its slice. Define the lattice time reflection
 r(t)=2n-1-t modulo N.
It swaps the two sets of n slices bijectively, even if2n<N. Spatial
links keep their spatial orientation and are time relabeled; temporal
links are reflected with reversed orientation. Product Haar and every
Wilson plaquette trace are preserved. Spatial flow on each slice and
its common spatial weights commute with this relabeling. Thus
 G_n(rU)=-G_n(U), |G_n|<=1, E_mu G_n=0.                 (5.1)
This uses time-reflection INVARIANCE only, not diagonal RP. The later
strict positive-half placement still requires its ordinary physical
buffer; the reflection used to prove(5.1) need not be that OS plane.

Let
 Z_a(s)=int exp[-beta S(U)+s G_n(U)] dHaar(U),

There is one fixed bare action/normalizer; no source-dependent tuning.
By(5.1), M_a(s)=M_a(-s)=E cosh(sG_n). For any s!=0,
 (s^2/2) Var_mu G_n <= M_a(s)-1
                   <=(cosh s-1) Var_mu G_n.            (5.2)
Proof: expand cosh(sx)-1 in even powers. For |x|<=1 its first term
is s^2x^2/2, and every x^(2k)<=x^2. Average. All constants are
cutoff/volume independent.

Thus a lower bound at ONE fixed positive real s suffices:
 M_a(s)>=1+q, q>0  ==> Var_mu G_n>=q/(cosh s-1).        (5.3)
Conversely a variance floor v gives M_a(s)>=1+s^2v/2.
No source derivatives, shrinking complex radii, fourth cumulants or
all-cell hierarchy are needed for this particular parent witness.

For a declared symmetric bounded calibrated reference X_ref in[-1,1]

For example epsilon<=s^2 v_ref/4 gives the positive floor
