NATIVE PRIVATE SOURCE JETS AND THE FULL-LAW REMAINDER
TURN73. 20 October 2026.

THE NONTRIVIAL CONTINUUM YM4 EXISTENCE PROOF IS STILL INCOMPLETE.

This continuation improves a concrete part of the original-source
problem. It does not identify a restricted source contribution with
the full physical observable. Its main result is a fixed-order bound
for canonically weighted ORIGINAL fine plaquette sources still owned
by a raw large-field component, followed by a connected two-mark
estimate at one native date. A separate original-law bare-box
comparison clarifies why preserving the coarse marginal does not
by itself close the remaining induction.

The proofs and their checks follow as appendices. The earlier
complete-history, positive-representation, and native parameter
premises remain explicit imports. Internal checks of these new
deductions are not external certification of the whole archive.

1. THE ORIGINAL PRIVATE SOURCE THAT IS NOW CONTROLLED

Use SU(2), h(U_p)=1-Tr(U_p)/2 in[0,2], with original inverse
coupling beta_0=g_0^-2. In a current raw component X with complete
history h, let P_private(X,h) contain only fine plaquettes whose
four ORIGINAL link reads still belong to its literal private
integration slots. Define

 F_X=beta_0 sum_(p in P_private(X,h)) f_p h(U_original,p),
 H_X=beta_0 sum_(p in P_private(X,h)) hprof_p h(U_original,p).

The selection of those plaquettes and their ownership is local to
the complete private history. The full original source also contains
the variables already integrated in regular regions, previously
healed components, and nonprivate reads. Their responses have become
coefficients and scalar terms. They are not in F_X.

At bare initialization, the exact initial geometric cover and tree
count in Large-II imply

 N_initial(h) <= C M^4 R_initial^4 Q_initial(h),
 Q_initial(h)=sum_(initial fresh births F)[1+d'_initial(F)].

The initial absence of an arbitrary preceding raw region matters.
Equation(1.76) at a general date has such a preceding-region term;
the actual bare base case on printed385 supplies the needed
initial cover. Arbitrary incoming raw operators would need their
own genuine ancestry credits.

The retained fixed-fraction primitive-credit argument from TURN54
reserves exp[-c P_initial^2 Q_initial] ONCE before the terminal
history sum. The weaker ledger still pays the future costs. This
does not spend an already committed prospective account twice.

Writing ell=1+log beta_0, with P_initial bounded below by c ell^p
and R_initial bounded above by C ell^r, the inserted fixed-order
source cost is bounded by

 [C beta_0 ell^(4r) Q]^j exp[-c ell^(2p)Q],  Q>=1.

For every fixed j>=1 and every fixed K>0 its supremum is at most
B_(j,K) beta_0^-K. This follows by splitting the exponential,
maximizing Q^j exp[-c ell^(2p)Q/2], and then using 2p>1 to
bound exp[(j+K)ell-c ell^(2p)/2] times its fixed power of ell.
The stronger admitted native hierarchy supplies these inequalities.
When Q=0 the private source is absent.

Consequently, for m+n>=1 and a bounded remaining private test G,

 |T'_k(X)[F_X^m H_X^n G]|
 <= B_(m+n,K) beta_0^-K ||f||_infinity^m ||hprof||_infinity^n
       envelope_k(X) sup|G|.

The weaker current envelope has exp[-c P_k] and, in the corresponding
general-region version, the retained spatial factor. The estimate
is taken on complete histories before their labels and source
ownership are erased. It is not a domination inferred from a small
final component mass.

The improvement is substantive: the profile norm replaces a count
of all fine plaquettes in the geometric preimage of X. Only the
actual initial private region is charged, using its own birth
credits. Constants depend on the fixed derivative orders and K,
not on the number of later renormalization steps.

This is a FINITE-JET result. The direct exponential-source argument
would only allow |s| of order P_initial^2/(beta_0 R_initial^4),
which shrinks with the cutoff. No fixed nonzero complex source
disc has been proved from this reserve.

2. HOW THESE JETS ENTER THE NORMALIZED CONNECTED EXPANSION

Fix the complete real outer datum o of Large-II(1.72), including
the variables of all retained second-class components. Leave the
actual first-class gas, its interactions and its raw private
operations intact.

The algebra with e^2=h^2=0 encodes precisely the required jets:

 exp(e F_X+h H_X)=1+e F_X+h H_X+eh F_X H_X.

Its coefficient-sum norm is submultiplicative. Applying each raw
operation to all four coefficients and following the absolute
native activity proof retains every scalar contribution. The
mixed logarithmic coefficient is exactly

 [eh] log Xi_o(e,h)=Cov_o(F_private,H_private).

There are two kinds of connected terms: one activity with both
marks, and two activities with one mark each. Neither is removed
as a vacuum term. Every marked term contains at least one raw
first-class factor, so it retains a current factor exp[-c P_k].

The fixed-fraction ledger changes the numerical native mass
exponent. The proof therefore uses a smaller positive constant
c throughout; it does not reuse the unweakened numerical
-3P_k/2 estimate. Fixed jet constants and the polynomial factors
for choosing marked roles are absorbed by once-fixed margins.

For source supports at current-cell sets A_k,B_k, outside the
fixed retained components, the resulting bound has the form

 |Cov_o(F_private,H_private)|
 <= C_K beta_0^-K ||f||_infinity ||hprof||_infinity
      exp[-c P_k] min(|A_k|,|B_k|)
      exp[-sigma dist_k(A_k,B_k)].

All original read collars remain in the supports. The source's
relative size charges the ordinary length of a tree visiting
the outside cubes. Travel through a retained component is not
free. Two private factors are not declared connected merely
because both see the same fixed retained component. These are
essential features of the native grouping and incidence proof.

For bounded separated physical supports, the associated geometric
envelopes are summable over geometrically spaced block widths:

 sum_(ell_j<=ell_*) (1+ell_j^-4) exp[-c d/ell_j] < infinity,
                                                        d>0.

Thus the fine-cell census obstruction in TURN72 is absent for
these separated private conditional bounds. This fact does not
make the covariances at different conditioning dates additive.
No full-law telescoping identity with all other terms estimated
has been supplied.

3. THE EXACT TERMS THAT CANNOT BE OMITTED

On the original positive joint lift write, exactly,

 F_original=F_*(o)+F_private+F_remainder,
 H_original=H_*(o)+H_private+H_remainder,

where the baselines are outer-measurable. At fixed o the complete
conditional covariance contains FOUR terms:

 Cov_o(F_original,H_original)
  =Cov_o(F_private,H_private)
   +Cov_o(F_private,H_remainder)
   +Cov_o(F_remainder,H_private)
   +Cov_o(F_remainder,H_remainder).

The original full covariance also contains

 Cov_outer(E[F_original|o],E[H_original|o]).

Only the first of the four displayed conditional terms is
controlled by the new private theorem. In particular, small
private corrections do not prove a positive physical covariance:
the part which should carry the continuum signal is still in
the regular/coarse source coefficients and their correlations.

Closed private plaquettes can retain outer holomorphy when their
actual gauge-coordinate identities leave the old compact source
independent of the continued outer parameters. This produces
marked outgoing coefficients at that native step. An all-scale
theorem for their subsequent regular transport, the regular
fine-source births, all cross terms and the outer covariance
does not follow merely from the one-step bound.

4. A SEPARATE FULL-COARSE-MARGINAL COMPARISON

For the actual separated bare Wilson cores already admitted in
TURN37, condition on the exterior and separately read coarse
statistics. On fibers whose window rejection probability p_i
is at most theta<1, replace the conditional law R_i by its
window conditioning; leave all other fibers unchanged. Every
replacement factor has conditional mean one, so the complete
coarse marginal is preserved.

If the annealed conditional rejection probability is at most
alpha on acceptable boundaries, the exact local chi-square
divergence for a read set of k cores satisfies

 D_A <= [1+alpha/(1-theta)]^k-1.

For original F,H measurable in these read cores and preserved
data, and f=F-E_mu F, h=H-E_mu H, the covariance comparison is

 |Cov_nu(F,H)-Cov_mu(F,H)|
 <= sqrt(D_A)||fh||_(L2(mu))
                  +D_A||f||_(L2(mu))||h||_(L2(mu)).

The last term is the product of the mean shifts; it is required.
The original finite-volume Wilson moment estimate from TURN64
then supplies a concrete bound for weighted original plaquette
actions. The physical plaquette-weight and read-core counts
remain. This bare estimate alone is not UV-summable for fixed
physical supports.

The precise iteration obstruction is an identity. If p(v) is
the full conditional acceptance of the next window, then

 rho_full,out(v)=rho_selected,out(v)/p(v).

Preserving the full coarse marginal thus inserts -log p(v)
into the coarse log-density (or +log p into the action in
the convention rho=exp(-S)). Its locality, complex domain,
source derivatives and exceptional fibers require control.
The already established selected recursion cannot simply
be run on this different density.

Preserving the coarse marginal also does not preserve the
conditional source means. For sources in disjoint cores,
their conditional covariances vanish and the entire comparison
can be a covariance of those means. Source-dependent balancing
preserves tilted marginals only by inserting the unknown
derivatives of its balancing factor.

5. THE CURRENT MATHEMATICAL FRONTIER

The next required source theorem must control the complete
original marked regular coefficients and their chronological
transport on the SAME full-law native or constrained kernels.
It must retain the scalar mixed coefficients, private/regular
cross terms, and final covariance of coarse conditional means.
The private estimate above is a usable input to such a theorem;
it is not that theorem.

Even after a complete original-source bound is established,
the following still need proof:

 - compatible renormalized continuum observables and products;
 - a nonzero witness at fixed physical scale;
 - a common limiting law with the required positivity,
   continuum symmetry, locality and reconstruction properties.

No completed Yang--Mills existence theorem or mass-gap theorem
is asserted.
