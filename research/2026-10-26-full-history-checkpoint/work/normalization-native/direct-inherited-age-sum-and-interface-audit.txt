1. INTERFACE VERDICT

The finite mixed-kernel lemma is correct under its explicitly stated
common-domain, compact-image and nonzero-denominator hypotheses. No
identity between the old weight's internal response and the new source
map is needed. Physical matching is a pointwise statement, and does
not use an identity theorem in independent J.

For a framed input, the weight must be evaluated at the original-frame
pair, or at the gauge-covariant equivalent with transported noise. The
new paragraph in the mixed-kernel note correctly retains this issue.
W_old(U,J,x) is not a substitute for W_old(U^Gamma,Ad_Gamma J,x).

The direct inherited composition theorem is also correct: B109
(3.13)--(3.16) provides the actual inclusion on the complete old/current
intersection, for an arbitrary sufficiently small four-slot A. Its
Cauchy estimates retain the simultaneous sum of probe radii.
The near/direct counterexample correctly disproves a blanket independent-
current identity. The near-domain reuse requires its stated extra
Holder-gradient slot and the actual reconstructed current.

The carrier estimate S0/(S0-1)^2 per differentiated scalar follows
directly by Cauchy on |z|=S0 around a point s in[0,1]. Its support
conclusion needs the stated separation/cushion property. Neither this
estimate nor direct composition alone sums old ages or copy labels.

2. ABSTRACT SUFFICIENT AGE SUM

Let a be an old age, rho_a=L^(-a), in four dimensions. There are at
most C rho_a^(-4) old unit roots per current unit block. Let n_a be
the ACTUAL number of evaluation/copy families per old root; it must
be inserted, not silently bounded by one.

Assume an old rooted polymer norm epsilon_a, for example

  sup_u sum_(X containing u) M_(a,X) (1+|X|)^m
                     exp[kappa' diameter_old(X)] <= epsilon_a,

where kappa' uses a strict part of the given positive carrier exponent.
The finite m pays the finite marked/copy incidences actually used.
The familiar bound E0 exp[-kappa d_old(X)] gives epsilon_a<=C E0
after the old-lattice animal count, but supplies NO age decay by itself.

Let beta_a^(1), beta_a^(2), beta_a^(1,b) be respectively the complete
age-converted, marked response prices after dividing out g, g^2 and g
for one noise derivative, two noise derivatives, and a noise/packet
derivative. These prices include every old P1 operator, copied current,
frame/return and actual read. Suppose the fixed-domain Cauchy argument
and marked kernels give each old-root contribution its epsilon_a times
the corresponding price, with a summable current-scale root tree.

Then the sufficient constants for inherited estimate(10) of the new
density note are

  B1 =sum_a n_a rho_a^(-4) epsilon_a beta_a^(1),
  B2 =sum_a n_a rho_a^(-4) epsilon_a beta_a^(2),
  B1b=sum_a n_a rho_a^(-4) epsilon_a beta_a^(1,b).    (A)

If all three are uniformly finite, the inherited force, weighted
Hessian and mixed packet force are bounded by Cg B1, Cg^2 B2 and
Cg B1b respectively. This conclusion follows by summing rooted
carriers first, then the old roots in each current block, then ages.
Exponential distances are preserved with a smaller fixed exponent.
Fine-carrier diameters cost only rho_a times the old diameter in
current units, so the reserved old exponent pays the conversion.

3. WHAT THE CURRENT DIRECT INTERFACE ACTUALLY GIVES

The direct copy has A_old=rho_a A_new. Its first difference and
componentwise Laplacian improve to rho_a^2 and rho_a^3. But the
old P1_j is a different operator; the interface currently certifies
only the safe rho_a field-to-source estimate for that slot. A complete
first-mark bound therefore has, at best from these stated inputs,

  beta_a^(1)<=C rho_a.

A fixed-radius current-noise Cauchy step applied to this first-mark
bound gives a sufficient beta_a^(2)<=C rho_a, not automatically
rho_a^2: the response's second derivative can carry only the same
single age factor. A fixed packet Cauchy step similarly gives
beta_a^(1,b)<=C rho_a. Additional powers need an actual marked
identity or moment cancellation, not an extra use of the word 'copy'.

With n_a bounded and epsilon_a=E0 at every age, (A) is consequently
bounded only by a constant times

  E0 sum_(a<=t) L^(3a).

This is a failure of the available estimate to be uniform, not a
claim that the actual Yang--Mills force has that size. It proves that
the present analytic/direct-domain and value-cap argument does not
yield the desired uniform inherited bound. An abstract bounded local
linear analytic activity saturates the one-age-factor bookkeeping.
Actual gauge symmetries can improve it, but must be used explicitly.

For example, even a gauge-invariant unremoved Wilson marginal has
engineering dimension four: the L^(-4a) gain per old cell is canceled
by its L^(4a) density, giving a contribution per age rather than a
summable age tail. This explains why actual running-coupling/marginal
extraction is indispensable; an E0 cap at each age does not replace it.

A simple sufficient route, WITH the currently proved rho_a marked
price, is

  sum_a n_a L^(3a) epsilon_a < infinity.             (B)

For bounded n_a this follows from epsilon_a<=C E0 L^(-(3+sigma)a).
Alternatively, a completed Ward bracket with value/marked-root bound
epsilon_a beta_a<=C E0 P(a)L^(-(4+sigma)a) yields the familiar
summable sum P(a)L^(-sigma a), with its actual copy multiplicities.
These are sufficient bounds with explicit losses, not inferred
properties of the new direct independent-current continuation.

4. CONCRETE NEXT ESTIMATE FOR THE NEW DENSITY

One must prove (A), or a stronger completed-bracket estimate implying it,
for the NEW direct-source/current maps. The old near determining-source
Ward gain cannot be transferred merely by physical equality; the two
off-graph current maps are distinguishable as the interface note shows.

There may be additional cancellation in old P1_j acting on a smooth
new response. That would be a useful estimate to prove, but the current
four-slot bound does not certify a rho_a^3 P1 price. Native derivative
or moment cancellations and the complete current branch have to be
retained. Even improving that slot would not remove the need to sum
old marginal/completed activity contributions with their actual counts.

Thus the finite mixed-kernel route legitimately bypasses the need to
prove (A) for a rebuilt density if it explicitly imports an OLD density
theorem already providing its normalized/phase bounds. It does not
make (A) true for the new density by definition.
