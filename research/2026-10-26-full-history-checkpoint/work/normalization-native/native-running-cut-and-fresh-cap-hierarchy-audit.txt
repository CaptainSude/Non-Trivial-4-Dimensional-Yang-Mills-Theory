Fix all native geometric/operator parameters first: L,M, the current

Let K_ref,K_rel,K_J be the fixed conversion constants from the new

 C_noise lambda_noise delta_j <= theta_noise a_j,

 K_ref a_j <= theta_dom alpha0,j,

4. A COMPATIBLE EQUAL-POWER CHOICE

One explicit choice of the displayed primary form is

 q=q0=q1=p0,               a_j=c g_j ell_j^p0.                   (5)

It is allowed by the displayed integer condition p0>=5r>=10>1.
Fix the coefficients in this order:

 (i) Keep the ORIGINAL A_epsilon,A_delta and their ratio fixed.
     Choose c greater than the two left coefficient thresholds in
     the first two lines of (4), with their desired strict margins.

 (ii) Choose C0 so large that K_ref c and K_J c are below the
      corresponding theta_dom C0, and so that c2 C0 also exceeds
      every fixed scalar/localizer/enlarged-noise multiple of
      A_delta required by the inherited fluctuation-radius test.

 (iii) Choose C1 large enough that C1>=K01 C0 and
       K_rel c<=theta_dom C1. Choose c2,c3 sufficiently small fixed
       ratios as in Section2, independently of the running date.

 (iv) Finally increase the weak floor Y until all absolute caps and
      perturbative products meet the native upper smallness bounds:
      fixed constant times g ell^p0 is as small as required for x>=Y.

The ordering has no conflict with A0>>A1: no physical characteristic
function is changed, and C0,C1 are analytic-domain proof parameters.
It also does not demand a g-dependent enlargement factor. Every ratio
in (4) is a fixed coefficient ratio, while all absolute caps tend to zero.

For (5) the normalized quantum parameter is still

            g_j^2/a_j^2=c^-2 ell_j^(-2p0) ->0.                  (6)

Thus the improved fresh-radius bootstrap does not need a logarithmic
gap to obtain its small quantum factor. The commonly suggested choice
p0<q<min(q0,q1), with fixed current exponent included, is another
sufficient hierarchy if those native exponents have been chosen that
way. It is unnecessary to assume that additional freedom merely to
establish (4).

5. WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT REGISTER

At the numerical and matched domain-image level, (5) together with the
explicit coefficient ordering registers (4) and all the imported
relative/absolute restrictions listed in Section2. The fixed source
geometry does not introduce a hidden R_j^geom factor into M_ref.
If a different mother convention grows as (R_j^geom)^s, replace the
second condition of (4) accordingly; equal powers would then generally
fail and an extra sr logarithmic power would be required.

For an already FROZEN native parameter choice one must instead test
(4) directly. If min(q0,q1,q_gamma)<p0, no weak-floor increase can
satisfy both physical inclusion and the corresponding analytic upper
cap. At equality, fixed coefficient separation is indispensable;
small g does not repair a bad ratio. Neither the numerical theorem
nor analytic restriction enlarges the old functions to fix that failure.

Finally, this is not a stand-alone proof of B119 Theorem1 for arbitrary
new parameters. Its imported full induction and R-operation hypotheses
must be used with a parameter choice satisfying this certificate and
the theorem's other restrictions. The source-level cut/cap obstruction
is resolved by the displayed hierarchy; a bare citation of (2.28) with
unspecified original constants would leave that application unregistered.
