  ||a||_C1 <= C_A (1+D) h^2 a_k,    ||j|| <= C_A h^3 a_k.

hD is fixed small and the source weak-coupling ceiling is fixed after A.

product-ball constant c_ball. The displayed constant c_src of Proposition 1

constant correction was communicated to the author.

This invokes the already proved common-radius inclusion, including source
condition (iv); no new unequal-radius version of Proposition 9 is used.
For h<=c_A m the disc radius is at least two. Global color invariance of the

The proof-selected gauges need not depend holomorphically on the input.

5. FINITELY YOUNG PREPARED TERMS: A UNIFORM NOISE DISC

Section 9's young-term construction also passes in the original homogeneous
source geometric class. It uses actual published slack, not openness of a
domain. CMP109(3.13)--(3.17) starts the old background in a half-radius domain
and allows a separate fluctuation ball. CMP116(1.19)--(1.22) bounds all the
admissible response norms uniformly throughout its complex decoupling discs,
and the paragraph following (1.30) gives the dated-domain inclusion into the
enlarged new tube. The near-group domain construction precedes the eventual
Maxwell subtraction and fifth-order summation, so those operations are not
needed for a fixed number of young ages.

Here is explicit varying-radius bookkeeping. Fix the source beta>0. At a
step k to k+1 set a*=alpha0,k+1/(1+beta). For a fixed young-age threshold a0,
bounded coupling increments and a sufficiently large weak floor give

  alpha0,k+1 / min_{k-a0<i<=k} alpha0,i <= 1+beta/2.

Hence a* is smaller than every relevant birth radius. The actual U-part of
the new tube fits the published enlarged new domain with radii
((1+beta)a*,(1+beta)A a*). Its inclusion in the old half-radius domains leaves
a fixed margin inside each ORIGINAL birth domain.

The independent current needs a separate argument: the printed enlarged new
tube has current bound a*, not (1+beta)a*. But a term i<=k is evaluated on
the output k+1 background, so its OLD current is h^3 J_out,
h=ell^{i-(k+1)}<=ell^-1. Its magnitude is at most
2 ell^-3 alpha0,i, strictly below alpha0,i/2. The primitive response operators
are analytic and uniformly bounded on CMP116's larger fixed alpha-prime
envelope, not just on the shrinking current ball. Allowing the actual new
current therefore does not consume an unprovided operator radius. The full
current increment in CMP109(3.13), rather than an isolated rough D*D term,
is bounded by C_dec g_k |t B| in its physical units.

The prepared chart size can be chosen alpha2=c_a0 a*. The near-group source
restrictions (3.49)--(3.52) require only fixed linear upper bounds on alpha3
by alpha1 and beta L^-2 alpha0, while Q'(A)=O(A). The far-group defining
regularity increments are likewise linear in the controlled A,P A,nabla A,

Thus the allowed size is a fixed multiple of a*, not an unspecified smaller

Consequently the actual inner retained field |B|<=T_aux admits

  |t| < c_a0 alpha0,k/(g_k T_aux)
