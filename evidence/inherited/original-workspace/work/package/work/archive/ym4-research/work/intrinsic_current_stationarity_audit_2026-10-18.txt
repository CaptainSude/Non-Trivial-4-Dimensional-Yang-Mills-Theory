1. Exact normalization and algebra.

Use the physical lattice pairings of the source. Its H0 satisfies

 Q H0 = ell^{-1},   E0 := H0 ell,   Q E0 = I,
 P0 := I-E0 Q,     P0^T Q* = 0.                         (1)

These are Raw I4.5, lines13640--13665 (CMP102b129,131), not QH0=I.
Let W be an actual real constrained critical configuration, J its actual
Wilson-action derivative, and F its plaquette force, with exact first-variation
identity J=D_W* F. If its adjusted constraint tangent is Q, then

 P0^T J=0,
 J=Q* E0* J=Q* (D_W E0)* F.                            (2)

Equivalently, lambda=E0*J and J=Q*lambda. No inverse fine derivative of F is
taken. The normalization matters: H0 has order -1, D H0 order -2, whereas
E0=H0 ell has order0 and D E0 order -1. Thus (2), with the source kernel
bounds and bounded Q*, has exactly curvature weight -2 to current weight -3.

This is a conditional algebraic implication, not yet a bound for every
modified mixed minimum used by CMP122-II.

2. Why the adjusted tangent can be used, and its remaining boundary check.

CMP98(92), transcribed in Raw I24239--24380, expresses the adjusted average
as the ordinary relative average conjugated at its two retained endpoints by
the explicitly normalized contour mean. Hence a curve whose adjusted average
is identically the background average has ordinary average in the same
coarse gauge orbit. Extending that retained endpoint gauge to a fine gauge
restores the ordinary constraint, and gauge invariance preserves the action.
Differentiation gives criticality against ker Q. The adjusted logarithmic
derivative at zero is ell Q, so ker(ell Q)=ker Q.

For a specified mixed problem one must still check that this extension is
identity on the fixed external boundary and assigns compatible values at all
retained sites. Compact variations with the full averaging stencil inside
the admissible domain have this property. Equality of only a local scalar
functional does not replace that constraint/boundary verification.

3. A new elementary Q* bound on a uniform own-tier collar.

The explicit CMP98(124), transcribed Raw I24339--24380, gives, for one fixed
odd-L block step, Q=Q0+E. Here

 (Q0 A)_c = sum_{x in B(c_-)} L^{-(d+1)}
             (R_{0,c_-}A)([x,x']),                    (3)

and every coefficient in E vanishes when Y=Y_x=0. The connectors and the
last root-bond term in that formula are part of E; they are not discarded.
For real W all R factors are isometries. The fixed one-step paths have a
bounded number of bonds and incidences. Consequently

 ||E*||_{infinity->infinity} <= C_L kappa,             (4)

if all the background loop logarithms Y,Y_x have size <=C_L kappa. The
adjoint here includes the physical coarse/fine measure ratio L^d. Since L
is fixed, that ratio and every possible concentration at a root only change
C_L. There is no claim uniform in L.

The pinned source cross-check is stronger than an inference about the
auxiliary letters: Raw I25169--25174 explicitly records that CMP98 bounds
the operator coefficients in the correction terms of (124) by
O(L^2 alpha0), with alpha0 the curvature constant of CMP98(52).
Only this real-background statement is used here.

The main term has the sharper bound

 ||Q0*||_{infinity->infinity} <= 1.                    (5)

Indeed a fixed positively oriented fine bond occurs in the straight strings
in its own direction with total scalar coefficient L^{-d}; multiplication
by the coarse/fine measure ratio L^d gives one. The sum includes the two
possible adjacent coarse starting blocks; treating each separately would
give a false factor2 per step. Parallel transports only decrease the norm
of the weighted sum. Different directions do not mix in Q0. Boundary
restriction can only remove summands.

Now take final physical block scale r and initial spacing eta, and suppose
the complete uniform averaging collar has plaquette defect <=a(eta/r)^2.
CMP98 Prop2 controls the intermediate background at spacing h_p=L^p eta by
C_L a(h_p/r)^2. The same fixed geometric loop estimate yields
kappa_p<=C_L a(h_p/r)^2. The corrected higher averages are compositions of
the one-step corrected operations, as in CMP98(88)--(92). Their linear
derivatives therefore compose, and their physical transposes compose in
reverse order. Equations(4)--(5) give

 ||Q_{eta->r}*|| <= product_p[1+C_L a(h_p/r)^2]
                 <= exp[C_L a sum_p(h_p/r)^2]
                 <= exp[C'_L a].                     (6)

Thus the nonflat connector concentration does not by itself force an
ultraviolet loss for the ADJUSTED average. The geometric curvature factors
pay it. A hypothetical O(a) remainder independent of p would not suffice.
The proof also gives block-local weighted versions on this uniform collar.

The displayed source formula is known here through the pinned transcription;
its original CMP98 PDF has not been independently recovered in this pass.

3A. Adjacent-terminal-level assembly, checked with published_density.

Assume the literal determining rows at level j have coarse roots in
Omega_j\Omega_(j+1), and their read support is the union of their two
j-blocks (a fixed C ell_j neighborhood of those roots). Let a fine bond b
have own tier n. If j<=n-2, b is in Omega_n subset Omega_(j+2), whereas a
level-j root is outside Omega_(j+1). The admitted gap between Omega_(j+2)
and Omega_(j+1)^c exceeds the row footprint. If j>=n+2, the row footprint
stays in Omega_(j-1) subset Omega_(n+1), which does not contain b. Thus
only j=n-1,n,n+1 can contribute, with a fixed enlargement for endpoint and
bond conventions. The large fixed gap constant pays that enlargement.

More explicitly, for the C ell_j footprint choose the admitted gap with
R mathfrak M > C L plus the fixed endpoint allowance. This constant
condition was checked independently by constrained_fiber. There are
O_d(1) coarse rows per contributing level through a fixed fine bond.

For any one such row its entire double block has own tiers at least j-1;
the curvature bound in j units therefore controls all intermediate averages
used in (6), with a fixed adjacent-radius factor. Each fixed-level operator
is block-local with fixed incidence. Summing only these adjacent terminal
levels gives the weighted order0 bound for Q and Q*, with fixed L factors,
not a depth factor. Their support also has bounded graded block distance,
so the same statement holds with the admissible exponential weights.

This proves the proposed incidence reduction under the displayed terminal
row convention. It does not identify a different truncated composite

4. What is not supplied by (6).

(a) A mixed determining family stops the iterated averages at different
levels. One must verify bounded incidence of the terminal double blocks, or
the corresponding source K0 bound for Q*. The word 'block-local' alone
does not imply it. An unrestricted sum of all nested terminal levels could
grow with depth. Original admissible grading should exclude that sum, but
the exact endpoint convention must be checked.

(b) To estimate E0*D_W* in (2), the localized first-derivative kernel bound
for E0 is required. The source primitive package gives this by composing
the first-derivative G0 bound, Q*, q0, and ell; transposition uses the
physical pairings and the graded kernel algebra. A bare unweighted N(E0b)
operator estimate does not by itself control its transpose on sup data.

(c) An own-tier a_m estimate needs either local curvature bounds with the
same weighted kernel envelope or a controlled ratio of the radii a_n/a_m.
Replacing all a_n by an uncontrolled global maximum would lose the needed
dated smallness. Polynomial age ratios can be absorbed into the exponential
graded kernel reserve once those ratios are actually established.

(d) Raw I128912ff explicitly treats the multilevel extension of the
CMP109(1.16) current bound as hypothesis(b). That paragraph is not an
independent proof of the desired mixed current estimate. Likewise the
generic independent pair class S expressly omits CMP99b(3.36),(3.38).
