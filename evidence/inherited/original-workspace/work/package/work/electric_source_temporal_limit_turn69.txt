TURN69. 20 October 2026. Root derivation; submitted for audit.

RESULT AND SCOPE

At a fixed finite spatial SU2 regulator, the temporal source-jet
attachment extends from bounded magnetic multiplications to analytic
changes of BOTH the electric and magnetic couplings. The original
Wilson source normalizer is retained explicitly. Its divergent
temporal contribution is exactly affine in slab length and cancels
from the second length difference.

The proof uses a uniform ADDITIVE complex-Bessel estimate, rather
than an invalid complex-probability bound or a uniform relative
large-spin asymptotic. The estimate gives stable transfer powers
on a common complex source disc. Real temporal convergence and
holomorphic compactness then give every fixed source derivative.

This closes the fixed-spatial electric/coupling derivative gate
left open in TURN68. It does not give any estimate uniform in
spatial cutoff, an isotropic-to-anisotropic RG theorem, or a
nontrivial continuum correlation.

1. UNIFORM COMPLEX-BESSEL ELECTRIC STABILITY

Fix eta>0 and consider complex beta with
Re beta>=eta|beta| and |beta| large. Let the square root use the
branch positive on the positive axis. For every integer nu>=0,

 I_nu(beta)=e^beta/(sqrt(2pi beta))
       [exp(-nu^2/(2beta))+epsilon_nu(beta)],
 sup_(nu>=0)|epsilon_nu(beta)|<=C_eta/|beta|.            (1.1)

The error is ADDITIVE inside the brackets, uniformly in nu.
It is not a relative error against the possibly tiny Gaussian
factor at large nu.

PROOF. The exact integer Fourier formula is

 e^-beta I_nu(beta)
 =(1/(2pi)) int_(-pi)^pi
           exp[beta(cos theta-1)] exp(-i nu theta)dtheta.

For real theta near0, put d(theta)=cos theta-1+theta^2/2.
Then 0<=d(theta)<=theta^4/24. Using the integral formula for
e^(beta d)-1 and taking a fixed sufficiently small theta0 gives

 |exp[beta(cos theta-1)]-exp(-beta theta^2/2)|
       <=C |beta| theta^4 exp(-c_eta |beta|theta^2)

on |theta|<=theta0. Its integral is O_eta(|beta|^-3/2).
Outside that interval the original integrand and the Gaussian
tails have exponentially small L1 norm. Because
|exp(-i nu theta)|=1 on the real integration line, the same
absolute error is uniform in every integer nu.

The full real-line Gaussian Fourier integral is exactly

 int_R exp(-beta theta^2/2-i nu theta)dtheta
       =sqrt(2pi/beta) exp(-nu^2/(2beta)).

Multiplying the L1 error by sqrt(beta) proves(1.1).

For large |beta|, the nu=1 bracket in(1.1) is bounded away
from zero. Also Re(1/beta)>0. Dividing by that bracket yields

 sup_(nu>=1) |I_nu(beta)/I_1(beta)
           -exp[-(nu^2-1)/(2beta)]|<=C_eta/|beta|,      (1.2)

and hence

                  sup_(nu>=1)|I_nu(beta)/I_1(beta)|
                                 <=1+C_eta/|beta|.    (1.3)

The normalized one-link SU2 Wilson convolution has precisely
the spin-j eigenvalue I_(2j+1)(beta)/I_1(beta), by the retained
Peter--Weyl/character computation. This identity extends
holomorphically wherever its denominator is nonzero.
Thus its L2 operator norm is bounded by(1.3).

It also follows that

 c(beta)=2e^-beta I_1(beta)/beta
        =sqrt(2/pi) beta^-3/2 [1+O_eta(|beta|^-1)]      (1.4)

is nonzero in this sector for sufficiently large |beta|.
All source discs below are chosen so that this condition holds.

2. ANALYTIC ELECTRIC AND MAGNETIC FAMILIES ON A FIXED GRAPH

Fix a finite spatial graph X=SU(2)^E, its physical gauge-invariant
Hilbert space, and baseline positive coefficients kappa_e(0),
v_p(0). In a complex disc |z|<=r let kappa_e(z),v_p(z) be
holomorphic, real and positive for real z near0, and satisfy

 Re kappa_e(z)>0,
 beta_e(delta,z)=2/[delta kappa_e(z)]
     in a fixed right sector with |beta_e|>=c/delta.    (2.1)

For the potential V_z=sum_p v_p(z)h_p, it is enough to have
a uniform finite bound on its negative real part. Shrinking
the disc around positive v_p(0) makes Re V_z>=0 if desired.
All constants in this note may depend on this FIXED graph.

Define

 H_z=sum_e kappa_e(z)K_e+V_z,
 M_delta,z=exp(-delta V_z/2),
 Q_delta,z=product_e q_(beta_e(delta,z))*,
 T_delta,z=M_delta,z Q_delta,z P M_delta,z.             (2.2)

P is the normalized Gauss projection. Each central edge
convolution and each gauge-invariant potential preserves the
physical sector, so P is harmless there.

From(1.3), each electric factor has norm at most1+Cdelta.
There are finitely many links. Including the two magnetic
multipliers gives

                 ||T_delta,z||<=exp(Cdelta),          (2.3)

uniformly on the closed source disc for small delta.
Consequently

            ||T_delta,z^n||<=exp(Cn delta).            (2.4)

These are complex OPERATOR bounds, not bounds on the total
variation of a complex one-step path weight. The latter
would give a fixed loss per step and would not prove(2.4).

The kernels are holomorphic in z and their electric denominators
are nonzero by(1.4). Their locally bounded operator families,
products and fixed-vector matrix elements are holomorphic.

For each real z near0, the real temporal theorem applies with
these coefficients:

       T_delta,z^floor(T/delta) -> exp(-T H_z)

strongly, uniformly for T in compact intervals. For completeness,
its generator verification is unchanged: on smooth physical
vectors the product electric derivative is sum kappa_e(z)K_e,
the magnetic derivative is V_z, and the real transfer products
are contractions. The finite Peter--Weyl core and contraction
product theorem give the stated convergence. No sourced
ground-state limit is required here.

3. FIXED UNSOURCED ENDPOINTS AND ALL SOURCE JETS

 F_T(z)=<Omega,exp[-T(H_z-E0)]Omega>.                  (3.1)

The locally bounded scalar holomorphic family has a unique

4. ORIGINAL FINITE WILSON SOURCE NORMALIZER

Set S_delta=T_delta,0/lambda_delta and
W_delta,n(z)=(T_delta,z/lambda_delta)^n.
For an original finite time period N>n, define

 F_delta,N,n(z)
 =Tr_phys[W_delta,n(z)S_delta^(N-n)]/Tr_phys S_delta^N.
                                                               (4.1)

The original Wilson source expectation is EXACTLY

 Z_orig,delta,N,n(z)
 =exp[n L_delta(z)] F_delta,N,n(z),

 L_delta(z)=sum_e log[c(beta_e(delta,z))
                              /c(beta_e(delta,0))].    (4.2)

Each logarithm is the branch vanishing at z=0. The source
uses n modified transfer intervals and the symmetric spatial
weights at their two boundaries. Thus the spatial action has
half the new weight on each boundary slice and the full new
weight on each interior slice. Temporal plaquette coefficients
are changed on exactly those n intervals.

Equation(4.2) does not discard the original electric scalar.
It isolates it explicitly. There are no extra source-dependent
Gauss or Haar normalizers. Baseline factors on all unmodified
intervals remain those of the original Wilson measure.

For |z| on a fixed smaller disc, (2.4) bounds ||W_delta,n(z)||
by a constant M for n delta in the finite duration list.
The positive baseline spectral remainder gives

 sup_z |F_delta,N,n(z)-F_delta,n(z)|
             <=2M[Tr S_delta^(N-n)-1].                (4.3)

The proof is the trace-norm ground-projection argument of
TURN68: subtract its rank-one projection in S_delta^(N-n),
then retain the denominator Tr S_delta^N>=1. Its additional
error is no larger because baseline eigenvalues lie in[0,1].

Choose N AFTER delta, along any required cofinal integer
period class. The remainder tends to zero uniformly on the
source disc. This proves finite original Wilson realization
of every prescribed renormalized source jet.

The normalizer term nL_delta(z) generally diverges as
delta->0 at fixed physical slab length. For example, for
beta_e(delta,z)=beta_e(delta,0)exp(z),

                   L_delta(z)=-(3|E|/2)z+O(delta)

on a fixed small disc. For additive inverse coupling it has
nonzero higher derivatives as well. None is omitted.

5. EXACT REMOVAL OF THE TEMPORAL SCALAR BY LENGTH DIFFERENCES

At the SAME baseline finite period N compare slab lengths
n,n+h,n+2h, with N>n+2h. Since nL_delta(z) is exactly linear
in n,

 Delta_h^2 log Z_orig,delta,N,n(z)
                  =Delta_h^2 log F_delta,N,n(z).       (5.1)

This cancellation is exact at finite cutoff, before any
derivative or limit. It holds for every fixed source jet.
Take n delta->T, h delta->d, with T,d>0. The source theorem
then gives

 partial_z^m Delta_h^2 log Z_orig,delta,N,n(0)
                    ->partial_z^m Delta_d^2 log F_T(0) (5.2)

after the ordered period selection.

Thus the source-dependent temporal electric vacuum term is
not an obstruction to the separated slab observable. It
cancels by its literal length dependence. This is stronger
than knowing only unsourced partition convergence.

For real analytic H_z put

 B=(H'_0-<H'_0>)Omega,   A=H_0-E0,
 C_B(s)=<B,exp(-sA)B>.

At a fixed regulator Omega is smooth, so the differential
operator insertions on it are well defined. Fixed-endpoint
Duhamel differentiation gives

 J(T):=partial_z^2 log F_T(0)
   =2 int_0^T (T-s) C_B(s)ds -T<H''_0>.               (5.3)

Both the scalar in(4.2) and the contact term in(5.3) cancel
from the second length difference. Consequently

 (1/2)Delta_d^2 J(T)
   =int exp(-TE)[(1-exp(-dE))/E]^2 rho_B(dE).          (5.4)

Here rho_B is the spectral measure of the centered vector B.
It has no ground atom. The continuous value of the bracket
at E=0 is understood.

The original source need not be linear in z. Its second
derivative also contains the expectation of the literal
second action derivative. Translation invariance makes that
term exactly affine in n, so(5.1) removes it. The remaining
second difference is the triangular sum of actual centered
first-source-derivative covariances.

Temporal transition sources live at interval centers, rather
than on a single time slice. Choose the appropriate parity

8. CONSEQUENCE AND REMAINING REQUIREMENTS

For any countable spatial regulator sequence, one may choose
delta and then a finite period to approximate a finite
increasing list of these coupling-source jets, mixed
windows and length differences to prescribed tolerances.
All normalizers, endpoint weights and source contact
operators are retained. A spatially uniform temporal

The new theorem closes a precise source-attachment gate.
It does not supply the spatially uniform connected source
estimate, a nonzero separated covariance, a calibrated
local operator algebra, or Euclidean rotation symmetry.
It does not show that Bałaban's isotropic native expansion
applies to this anisotropic family.

In particular, uniform coupling-source analyticity at each
fixed spatial regulator is not a uniform continuum source
disc. The full nontrivial YM4 existence proof remains open
in this project.
