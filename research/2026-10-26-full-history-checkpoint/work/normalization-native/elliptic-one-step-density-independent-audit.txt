The sentence 'differentiating the convergent fixed-point expansions

result of the assembly. Uniform finite-dimensional holomorphy and
||r'||<=C||q||_infinity^2 alone give an O(gR^2) coordinate force. They
do NOT give the moment-closing, spatially summable force
