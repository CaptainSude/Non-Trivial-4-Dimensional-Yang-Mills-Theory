POSITIVE RG CHANNELS: AN EXACT INNOVATION COVARIANCE LEDGER
19 October 2026. Root proof; native kernel attachment remains separate.

WHY THIS IS THE RELEVANT CONSUMER

A completed coarse-state measure can have a uniformly positive reference
Gram matrix even if part or all of its variance was introduced by a
stochastic replacement operation. The published complete sequence is
(RT)^k, not automatically the deterministic native average of the bare
law. This note isolates exactly the additional quantity needed to transfer
coarse nondegeneracy to cutoff observables on the original field.

The ledger does not require rarity of each historical component. It
requires a bound on actual conditional replacement variances of the
BACKWARD transported observables. That may be a weaker target, but it is
not supplied merely by a scalar stability theorem.

1. EXACT FINITE MARKOV IDENTITY, INCLUDING ALL HISTORY VARIABLES

Let X_0,...,X_N be a finite Markov chain with actual probability kernels
K_j(x_j,dx_(j+1)), and laws mu_j. Any history needed to make a transition
Markov is part of X_j. There is no assertion that a signed localized
coefficient is such a kernel. Scalar normalization/vacuum constants must
first be separated so that these are the true positive normalized laws.

Let F_N be a finite real vector in L2(mu_N). Define the backward functions

 F_j(x)=K_j F_(j+1)(x),      j=N-1,...,0.                 (1.1)

For the natural filtration H_j=sigma(X_0,...,X_j),

 M_j=F_j(X_j)=E[F_N(X_N)|H_j]

is a vector martingale. Its increments have zero conditional mean and
are orthogonal in L2 at distinct dates. Therefore

 Cov_(mu_N)(F_N)
 =Cov_(mu_0)(F_0)+sum_(j=0)^(N-1) D_j,                  (1.2)

 D_j=E_(mu_j) Cov_(K_j(X_j,.))(F_(j+1)) >=0.             (1.3)

The formulas hold componentwise, including cross covariances. For a
scalar c.F_N they follow from the variance decomposition; polarization
proves the full matrix identity. The means are identical at every j.

In particular, if Cov_(mu_N)(F_N)>=c I and

                   ||sum_j D_j||_op <=c/2,              (1.4)

then Cov_(mu_0)(F_0)>=c I/2. This gives a genuine nonzero variance for
the specified BACKWARD cutoff observable F_0. Its physical observable
identification, support, symmetry and normalization remain obligations.
It need not equal the bare deterministic block-average observable.

For a claim about the ORIGINAL bare field U, X_0 must consist only of
U and deterministic data. If X_0 already includes auxiliary randomness,
set F_bare(U)=E[F_0(X_0)|U] and add
D_init=E Cov(F_0(X_0)|U) to the innovation sum. Otherwise an initial
random geometry or seed could itself supply the alleged bare variance.

For a deterministic blocking transition X_(j+1)=q_j(X_j), D_j=0 exactly.
Thus the loss in (1.2) comes only from stochastic steps in a fine enough
decomposition of the chain. They may be split into their actual substeps
without changing the identity. A positive operation preserving total
mass is useful here only after its genuine Markov representation is
identified; formal positivity of an unrelated majorant is insufficient.

2. WHY A POSITIVE COARSE GRAM MATRIX ALONE DOES NOT TRANSFER

Take X_0 to be one deterministic point and let K_0 replace it by an
independent variable with a smooth positive density on a compact group.
Many nonconstant real class functions F_1 then have positive variance.
Nevertheless F_0=K_0F_1 is constant and has zero variance. Here D_0 is
the ENTIRE terminal variance. A pointwise lower density bound for mu_1,
or its positive finite Gram matrix, does not constrain D_0.

The exact product defect of a backward positive channel is

 K(fh)-(Kf)(Kh)=Cov_(K(x,.))(f,h).                        (2.1)

Thus the same ledger measures a finite-source product defect. More
precisely the composite backward channel P satisfies
P(fh)-(Pf)(Ph)=Cov(f(X_N),h(X_N)|X_0). Its L1(mu_0) norm is bounded
by the square root of the two integrated conditional variance budgets.
This controls the fixed reference span and its specified products;
it is not a uniform operator-algebra or L-infinity embedding theorem.
Joined source coefficients cannot be discarded just because the
singleton images remain bounded. The forward normalized conditional
source map and the backward channel used here are distinct adjoints
of the same actual joint law; their formulas must not be interchanged.

3. CONDITIONAL PRODUCT REFILLS: A DETERMINISTIC OSCILLATION BOUND

Suppose that, conditional on the full input state x, one transition
draws variables Y_1,...,Y_m independently in disjoint active regions
B_1,...,B_m and leaves the exterior fixed. Their laws can depend on x
and need not have densities, Gaussian tails or small bad-event weights.
Assume the ACTIVE REGIONS and their read/selection data are already
fixed at this conditioning. For any scalar output observable f,

 Var_(K(x,.))(f)<=1/4 sum_i osc_(B_i)(f)^2,              (3.1)

where osc_(B_i) is the supremum difference obtained by changing only
Y_i, with all other output variables held fixed. It can be restricted
to the actual conditional supports if those are known. Tensorization
of variance gives Var f<=sum_i E Var_(Y_i)(f|Y_(not i)); the elementary
range variance bound Var Z<=(sup Z-inf Z)^2/4 gives (3.1).

Consequently, if for every unit reference vector c

 sum_i osc_(B_i)(c.F_(j+1))^2 <=v_j(x),

then ||D_j||_op<=E_mu_j[v_j]/4. A summable estimate on these quantities
is sufficient for (1.4). This pays the actual source influence rather
than the probability of every separately labeled history.

Random choices of the partition or other auxiliary labels require their
own conditional variance term. Conditioning on that label does not
remove Var(E[f|label,x]|x). Interacting output blocks do not satisfy the
product tensorization merely because their geometric centers are apart.
The real refill kernel must supply the factorization or a separately
proved conditional variance inequality with the required constant.

4. A CONCRETE GLOBAL OSCILLATION ESTIMATE FOR AVERAGED WILSON LOOPS

The oscillation bound has a useful geometric gain in dimension4, without
any small-field assumption. Work on a fixed physical periodic box of a
d-dimensional cubic lattice of mesh a. Choose a fixed rectilinear loop
shape of physical length bounded by ell, resolved at mesh a, with at most
C(1+ell/a) fine edges counted with multiplicity. Let w(U_gamma) be a real
normalized character with |w|<=1. Let f be a bounded weight supported in
a fixed physical volume. Define

 O_a(U)=a^d sum_(lattice translations x) f(ax) w(U_(gamma+x)). (4.1)

Changing one fine link can affect at most C(1+ell/a) translated loops:
each occurrence of an edge of gamma determines at most one translation
that puts it on that fine link, with the finitely many orientation
conventions included. Therefore its oscillation is at most

             C ||f||_infty (ell+a) a^(d-1).             (4.2)

Changing a block of at most v fine links gives at most v times this
bound by telescoping over links. If a product refill step uses at most
C_V a^-d disjoint blocks, each with at most v links, then

 Var_K(O_a)<=C_V ||f||_infty^2 (ell+a)^2 v^2 a^(d-2).   (4.3)

For d=4 this is O(v^2 a^2). This bound is exact for arbitrary compact
group link values and arbitrary independent block refill laws. It uses
bounded characters and support counting, not a Taylor approximation.
If v grows as a fixed power of log(1/a), a^(d-2) v^2 remains summable
over geometric meshes in d>2. The same proof works for a fixed finite
linear combination of loop shapes, with its actual coefficient norms.
Only the UV tail of this sum, over a<=epsilon, becomes small as
epsilon tends to zero. All later stochastic dates still contribute;
summability alone does not show their total is below a covariance floor.

For variable block sizes a useful version is

 Var_K(O_a)<=C ||f||_infty^2 (ell+a)^2 a^(2d-2)
                                  sum_i |B_i|^2.        (4.4)

An expectation bound on this squared-size sum suffices. A deterministic
bound on the total number of active links alone is insufficient if a
single component can have macroscopic size.

5. WHAT (4.3) DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY ESTIMATE

The f in (3.1) for the martingale ledger is F_(j+1), the observable AFTER
all later transitions have been averaged backward. It is not an
arbitrarily chosen bare loop function at date j+1. Equation(4.3) closes
the ledger only if these actual transported functions retain an estimate
of type (4.2), with a summable quantitative enlargement. Neither a
pointwise terminal density bound nor finite-field analyticity proves
that propagation. A discontinuous group average or a data-dependent
large refill can destroy a small individual-link influence.

Also, unrenormalized averaged Wilson loops need not have a nonzero
continuum covariance. Equations(4.1)--(4.4) prove an influence bound;
they do not choose the physical renormalization or establish a positive
terminal covariance witness. The path-holonomy obstruction of turn13
is not repaired by citing the boundedness of w.
Multiplying O_a by a cutoff-dependent normalization Z_a multiplies its
variance bound by |Z_a|^2; that factor can destroy the UV summability.

Lastly, (1.4) must be quantitatively compared with the available terminal
Gram floor. A minorization constant which becomes exponentially small
in the entire coarse volume cannot be compared with a merely geometric
UV variance tail by suppressing its volume dependence. Fixed-volume
nondegeneracy and volume-uniform physical nondegeneracy are different.

6. A PRECISE ALTERNATIVE NATIVE TARGET

For the actual positive R/T construction retain its full auxiliary state
and prove both:
 (a) the terminal invariant reference has a calibrated covariance floor
     at the chosen physical scale;
 (b) its backward functions obey a conditional influence estimate whose
     complete innovation covariance sum is smaller than that floor.

The deterministic T steps contribute no innovation once their actual
map is fixed. In the R steps the common-frame supports, random choices,
joint refill distribution and long carriers must all be accounted for.
The sufficient product-refill bound above is available if those actual
hypotheses hold. It does not assert that Bałaban R has them.

This target does not demand a separately normalized rarity theorem for
every ancient component. It can in principle pay a large component by
a small physical source influence. Whether the native maps have the
needed bounds is the substantive next question. No continuum existence
or nontriviality transfer is claimed without that attachment.
