REAL-SLICE PROPAGATION OF A FIRST-CONTACT GAIN
18 October 2026. Working mathematical lemma, not a completed YM application.

1. A dimension-independent two-constants lemma

Let X be a complex Banach space with an isometric conjugation, X_R its
real part, and Y any complex Banach space. Let F:B_X(0,3r+eta)->Y be
holomorphic, with eta>0. Suppose

 ||F(w)||<=M on ||w||<=3r,
 ||F(v)||<=epsilon on v in X_R, ||v||<=3r,

where 0<=epsilon<=M. If w=x+i y, x,y real, ||x||,||y||<=r, then

 ||F(w)|| <= M^(1-theta) epsilon^theta,
 theta=(2/pi) arctan(3/4)>0.                         (1)

Proof. For a norm-one functional l in Y*, set
 f(z)=l(F(x+2z y)). This is holomorphic on a neighborhood of the
closed unit disk. On the diameter [-1,1] it is bounded by epsilon,
and on the upper semicircle by M. The two-constants inequality for
the subharmonic log|f| bounds its value at z=i/2 by
log M + omega log(epsilon/M), where omega is the harmonic measure
of the diameter as seen from i/2 in the upper half unit disk.
The conformal map z -> -(z+1/z) sends that domain to the upper
half plane and i/2 to 3i/2. The diameter maps onto the complement
of [-2,2] on the real axis. Thus

 omega=1-(2/pi)arctan(4/3)=(2/pi)arctan(3/4)=theta.

Taking the supremum over l proves (1). At epsilon=0 use the identity
theorem, or let epsilon decrease to zero. No coordinate-by-coordinate
continuation is used, so the exponent is independent of the number
of field variables, localization parameters, volume, and cutoff.

The same proof works on any parameter domain containing all the
disks z -> x+2z y needed above, with real diameters in a specified
real admissible set. An infinite-dimensional ambient ball is not
required for a finite-cutoff application.

2. Consequence for an original compensated read

Suppose one WHOLE compensated Pi-zero bracket F_h, as a function of
all source/background/localization variables, has on such disks

 ||F_h||<=A h^4,
 ||F_h||_(real diameter)<=B h^(4+beta),  0<h<=1.

Then the target complex read obeys

 ||F_h|| <= A^(1-theta) B^theta h^(4+theta beta).    (2)

If B h^beta>A one may retain the neutral estimate instead. A common
retained connection factor in A and B survives with its full power.
If only A contains that factor, interpolation retains only its
(1-theta) power: any such exponent loss must be charged explicitly.
Finite-degree radius polynomials and exp(polylog gamma) preparatory
constants stay of those respective types after taking these powers.

For graded collars d=r_m/r_n, if the real improved estimate is
B(h/d)^(4+beta) while the complex neutral estimate is A h^4, (1)
instead gives

A^(1-theta)B^theta h^(4+theta beta) d^(-theta(4+beta)).

Its minimum with A h^4 still has a positive large-age power after
the usual boundary-strip optimization. It is also bounded by the
weaker same-shape expression C(h/d)^(4+theta beta) when d<=1 and
the constant A^(1-theta)B^theta is retained. The first, sharper
formula should be used when checking the actual collar sum.

In fact it preserves the ORIGINAL optimized boundary exponent. Set
sigma=theta(4+beta); since theta>1/4, sigma>1. With K>=0 and
0<h<=1, a codimension-one strip sum/integral obeys

 integral_0^1 min(1,K h^(theta beta) d^(-sigma)) dd
 <=[1+1/(sigma-1)] (K h^(theta beta))^(1/sigma).      (3)

For delta=(K h^(theta beta))^(1/sigma)<1, split at delta.
The first part is <=delta; the second is at most
K h^(theta beta) delta^(1-sigma)/(sigma-1).
If delta>=1, the integral is <=1<=delta. Thus its h power is
theta beta/sigma=beta/(4+beta), exactly the former shell exponent.
A geometric discrete shell sum has the same estimate up to a fixed
constant. This does not prove the actual patch/face census, which
must supply the codimension-one measure and its retained constants.
It shows that analytic propagation need not worsen that exponent.

Independent check: minimal_route verified the conformal calculation,
Banach dual argument, and normalized graded ratio on 18 October.

3. What this does NOT establish

This lemma replaces a COMPLEX smoothing claim only if a uniform
improved bound is available on the FULL real diameters in the
actual parameter chart. Equality or regularity at s=1 alone does
not suffice. Neither does restriction to the physical graph
J=Jphys(U), which need not contain the required real diameters in
the independent-(U,J,s) domain.

In the proposed minimum-plus-response application, an independent
real J can remain a response datum; it is never inserted into a
physical Euler equation. Every minimizing factor on the real
diameter must instead be an actual admitted real constrained
minimum, with its complete averaging stencil and local regularity
bound. Localized responses must preserve the needed norms there.
This real-domain admission has not been verified for all nested
modified coarse slots of CMP122-II(1.61)--(1.64).

Nor has the required factor-three parameter reserve been checked
for the actual simultaneous source contours. One must work inside
the existing larger analytic domain, or establish a larger domain
before reducing the read contours. Merely shrinking the contours
in an already fixed Cauchy/localization argument is not an identity.

Thus (1) is proved, and (2) is a proved implication. The actual
large-field bridge remains conditional on its real admission and
contour hypotheses. This offers a weaker sufficient target than
complex C1,beta regularity of every mixed minimizing field.

4. Any fixed positive analytic margin suffices (root extension)

The factor three in Section1 is convenient, not necessary. Let 0<r<R,
F holomorphic on a neighborhood of the closed complex ball B_R, with
norm <=M there and norm <=epsilon on its ENTIRE real ball. Put lambda=R/r.
Let D_lambda be the upper half of the ellipse

 |z-i|+|z+i|<2 lambda,

whose real diameter is (-sqrt(lambda^2-1),sqrt(lambda^2-1)). Let theta_lambda
be the harmonic measure of that diameter, viewed from i in D_lambda.
The point i is strictly interior for every lambda>1, hence 0<theta_lambda<1.
For every ||w||<=r,

 ||F(w)|| <= M^(1-theta_lambda) epsilon^theta_lambda.        (4)

Proof. Write w=x+i y with x,y real. The isometric conjugation gives

 x+z y = ((1-i z)/2)w + ((1+i z)/2)bar(w),
 ||x+z y|| <= (|z-i|+|z+i|)r/2.

Thus f(z)=l(F(x+z y)) is holomorphic on D_lambda and bounded by M, while
its real diameter maps into the real ball B_R, where the bound is epsilon.
The two-constants inequality at z=i and duality prove (4). Open-domain
versions use any intermediate lambda'<R/r, avoiding boundary assumptions.
The exponent depends only on the fixed margin, never on dimension, volume,
or refinement. A smaller fixed margin may make the exponent very small;
it cannot be treated as bounded below if R/r tends to one with the cutoff.

For the graded ratio a d^(-sigma), now a=K h^(theta_lambda beta) and
sigma=theta_lambda(4+beta), the collar estimate has THREE cases. For 0<a<1,

 integral_0^1 min(1,a d^(-sigma)) dd
 <= C_sigma a^(1/sigma)                 if sigma>1,
 = a[1+log(1/a)]                       if sigma=1,
 <= [1+1/(1-sigma)]a                   if 0<sigma<1.

The proof splits at a^(1/sigma). Thus the resulting large-age power is
min(theta_lambda beta,beta/(4+beta)), with a logarithm at equality.
Every FIXED positive analytic margin therefore gives a positive gain.
Section2's unchanged beta/(4+beta) exponent is specific to sigma>1.

Source margin check, limited scope. CMP116 pp5--7,(1.11)--(1.23), gives
simultaneous localization analyticity |s(Delta)|<=exp(kappa1), with kernel
norm B0 exp(16 kappa1), provided the fixed M and smallness constants are
chosen in the displayed order. Repeating that primitive construction with
kappa1+log(lambda) gives radius lambda exp(kappa1) while retaining the
original Cauchy circles at exp(kappa1); its extra kernel constant is
lambda^16. This spends a fixed parameter choice, not a loss per fine scale.
The paper's external-field target also has a fixed (1+beta) margin versus
the preceding (1+2beta) domain in its Section1 summation. Either type of
margin may support (4) once represented by ONE actual real parameter chart.

This source observation does not prove that EVERY mixed large-field read
and every external field coordinate share that reserve. It does show that
a factor-three enlargement is not intrinsically required. The remaining
application is the uniform chart/margin and real-diameter admission, with
the literal nested minima and complete averaging collars retained.
