TURN98 -- FULL-WILSON AVERAGED RESPONSE THROUGH ARBITRARY ORIGINAL BLOCKS
21 October 2026. largefield98. New finite-regulator estimates.

RESULT

For a fixed original energy mark G=sum_p w_p h_p, arbitrary simultaneous
integration of original links preserves an L^q exterior derivative bound
of size C_q beta^(-1/2) sum|w_p|. The constant is independent of the
number of integrated links and the surrounding periodic volume. All
exteriors are included under the same original normalized Wilson law.

An arbitrarily long deterministic single-pass sequence of original
heatbath projections also preserves this bound, at an exterior link
which is never updated. Only the at most eighteen links sharing one of
its six plaquettes contribute to its derivative commutator. This does
not claim that a heatbath sweep is simultaneous decimation.

The particular bad exteriors in TURN97 are exponentially rare under the
full original law. Combining that fact with TURN97's weak-exterior
theorem upgrades its centered two-link source/classical-minimum comparison
to a full-measure L^q error O(beta^-3), and its exterior gradient error
to O(beta^-5/2). These constants are volume-uniform for that fixed pair.

Source weight W=sum|w_p| is retained throughout. No spatial decay,
physical source census, native history attachment or continuum existence
claim follows merely from these estimates.

1. MODEL, DERIVATIVES, AND MOMENTS

Use the finite periodic four-dimensional SU(2) Wilson law with periods
at least four, normalized link Haar measure, and homogeneous beta>0:

 mu_beta(dU)=Z_beta^-1 exp[-beta S(U)]dU,
 S=sum_p h_p,                 h_p=1-Tr(U_p)/2 in [0,2].

For a deterministic link f and fixed unit Lie-algebra generator T, let
X_f act by U_f -> exp(tT)U_f, holding every other original link fixed.
The norm convention identifies SU(2) with the unit quaternion sphere,
so this flow has unit speed. No field-dependent coefficient, stopping
decision, source-dependent update order or auxiliary record is used.

For a plaquette p containing f, quaternion differentiation gives

 |X_f h_p| <= sqrt(2 h_p).                              (1.1)

Indeed the scalar component derivative has magnitude at most the length
of the vector part of U_p, which is sqrt(1-(1-h_p)^2)<=sqrt(2h_p).
Left/right placements and inverse link orientations preserve the norm.
If p does not read f the derivative is zero.

TURN64 proves, for every finite plaquette set E,

 E_mu exp[(beta/4)sum_(p in E)h_p] <= 2^(3|E|/4).       (1.2)

In particular, for every real r>0, integration of the exponential tail
gives

 E h_p^r <= 2^(3/4) Gamma(r+1) (4/beta)^r.              (1.3)

The constants in (1.2)-(1.3) do not depend on the torus volume. Define
C_r as any finite constants covering these moment bounds; all constants
below may depend on q but never on beta, the torus or an eliminated
block's size. For r>=1, Minkowski therefore gives

 ||sum_p w_p h_p||_r <= C_r W/beta,
 W=sum_p |w_p|.                                       (1.4)

Likewise for q>=1, including q/2<1 by (1.3),

 ||X_f G||_q <= C_q W_f beta^-1/2,
 W_f=sum_(p reading f)|w_p|.                           (1.5)

The weights are arbitrary deterministic real numbers on the finite
torus. W is explicit and may be large. We are not asserting bounded W
for a fixed physical support as the microscopic mesh tends to zero.

2. ANY SIMULTANEOUS BLOCK: THE EXACT NORMALIZED COMMUTATOR

Let B be ANY deterministic set of original links. Denote by P_B the
conditional expectation given all links outside B, under the original
mu_beta. Let S_B be the sum of all distinct original plaquettes reading
at least one link of B, counted once. The literal conditional density is

 q_B(dU_B|U_out) = exp[-beta S_B]dU_B / int exp[-beta S_B]dU_B.

Every exterior-only action term cancels from this conditional ratio.
For f outside B, differentiating this finite compact positive integral
gives the exact identity

 X_f P_B G = P_B(X_f G) - beta Cov_B(G,X_f S_B).          (2.1)

All normalization and action-score terms are present. It is not a
derivative of a frozen conditional measure. Set

 H_B,f=X_f S_B=sum_(p reading f and at least one B link)X_f h_p.

There are at most SIX terms in H_B,f, independently of |B|. Equations
(1.1)-(1.3) give ||H_B,f||_(2q)<=C_q beta^-1/2.

Conditional expectations are contractions in L^r(mu) for r>=1. Holder
and these contractions give, without a conditional pointwise bound,

 ||Cov_B(G,H)||_q
 <= ||P_B(GH)||_q + ||(P_BG)(P_BH)||_q
 <= 2 ||G||_(2q) ||H||_(2q).                            (2.2)

Substitute (1.4)-(1.5) into (2.1)-(2.2):

 ||X_f P_B G||_q <= C_q beta^-1/2 (W_f+W).              (2.3)

This holds for every beta>0 and finite 1<=q<infinity, every deterministic B and exterior
link f. In particular its constant is uniform in both block and torus
size. Its norm is the FULL original mu norm, equivalently the actual
exterior marginal norm. That marginal contains the exact conditional
partition function; it is not replaced by Haar or a prescribed exterior.

If B touches no plaquette containing f, H_B,f=0 exactly and only the
first term in (2.1) remains. The estimate is an averaged first-derivative
theorem for an original energy mark. It does not say the generated
effective action is Wilson, or that its higher derivatives satisfy the
same norm, or that an arbitrary effective/history-conditioned measure
has the moments (1.3).

3. ARBITRARILY LONG SINGLE-PASS ORIGINAL HEATBATH SEQUENCES

Let e_1,...,e_n be a deterministic sequence of original links with
e_k != f for every k. Repetitions are allowed for the first statement.
Write P_e for the actual original Wilson heatbath projection and

 G_0=G,             G_k=P_(e_k)G_(k-1).

These are smooth functions at every fixed beta and finite regulator.
Original-law contraction gives

 ||G_k||_r<=||G||_r<=C_r W/beta                         (3.1)

for every r>=1. Let n(e,f) be the number of plaquettes containing both
distinct links e and f. It is zero unless they share a plaquette.
The one-link version of (2.1) gives

 X_f G_k=P_(e_k)X_fG_(k-1)
              -beta Cov_(e_k)(G_(k-1),H_(e_k),f),
 H_e,f=sum_(p containing e and f)X_f h_p.                (3.2)

Each H_e,f satisfies ||H_e,f||_(2q)<=C_q n(e,f)beta^-1/2.
Using (2.2) and (3.1) at each step, then iterating the real inequality,
proves

 ||X_fG_n||_q <= C_q beta^-1/2
             [ W_f + W sum_(k=1)^n n(e_k,f) ].          (3.3)

If every updated link occurs at most R times, then

 sum_k n(e_k,f) <= 18R.                                (3.4)

Indeed f lies in six plaquettes and each has three other links. Counting
these eighteen incidences also handles any repeated neighbor across
plaquettes; no assumption about their being distinct is needed. Thus
for a SINGLE PASS (R=1), the right side of (3.3) is independent of the
number and order of updated links. The bound permits an arbitrarily
large integrated region for a fixed mark G and an exterior derivative.

A later P_e can reintroduce an earlier coordinate, exactly as TURN97
showed. No nested-sigma-algebra identity is asserted. Deterministic
composition and the actual original law suffice for (3.2)-(3.3).
If F reads none of the updated links, the usual conditional identity
also retains Cov(F,G_n)=Cov(F,G). This observation does not itself give
decay or a summable estimate on that covariance.

For a field-dependent order or decisions depending on updated links,
the differentiation would also read those decisions. For a native
record, the conditioning density need not be q_e. Those cases are not
included in (3.3). For canonical marks beta G, multiply all displayed
source and response bounds by beta; the response scale is then sqrt(beta).

4. THE TWO-LINK EXCEPTIONAL EXTERIOR HAS A FIXED ENERGY COST

Use the actual TURN97 pair of opposite parallel links, and its ten
exterior staples a_i,b_j in S^3 after the exact Haar changes. Its eleven
distinct plaquettes have local action

 S_pair(x,y;t)=11-x.y-sum_i a_i.x-sum_j b_j.y,
 D(t)=min_(x,y) S_pair(x,y;t).

All eleven summands are nonnegative. For every original configuration,

 0<=D(U_out)<=S_pair(U)<=sum_(p in pair stars)h_p(U).    (4.1)

Choose epsilon>0 small enough that all ten staples within epsilon of
their first staple a_1 is inside TURN97's fixed weak-exterior tube.
Let E_bad be the exterior event

 max_i |a_i-a_1| >= epsilon OR max_j |b_j-a_1| >= epsilon.

This is a statement about their common conditional coordinate frame,
not a raw-link identity window. A change of that common orthogonal
frame leaves all the distances invariant. For any minimizing x,y,

 |a_i-a_1|^2 <=2(|a_i-x|^2+|x-a_1|^2)<=4D,
 |b_j-a_1|^2 <=3(|b_j-y|^2+|y-x|^2+|x-a_1|^2)<=6D.

Each squared edge equals twice its corresponding plaquette defect.
Therefore, globally on compact staple space,

 E_bad implies D>=d_epsilon:=epsilon^2/6.              (4.2)

Equations (1.2), (4.1) and exponential Markov now give the actual
original-law exterior bound

 P_mu(D>=d) <= min(1, 2^(33/4) exp[-beta d/4]),
 P_mu(E_bad) <= min(1, 2^(33/4) exp[-beta epsilon^2/24]). (4.3)

This is uniform in the surrounding volume. No arbitrary conditioned
exterior theorem has been used. The bound applies to this exterior
marginal precisely because the deterministic D lower bound forces
the original energy on eleven actual plaquettes for EVERY x,y.

For the TURN97 obstruction, a=sum_i a_i=I, b=sum_j b_j=-I.
Maximizing x.y+x_0-y_0 first over x gives max_(s in [-1,1])
sqrt(2+2s)-s=3/2 (attained at s=-1/2). Hence D=19/2 exactly.
Moreover D is 1-Lipschitz separately in the aggregate vectors a,b,
so |a-I|+|b+I|<=eta implies D>=19/2-eta. The probability of this actual
aggregate-staple neighborhood is at most

 min(1, 2^(33/4)exp[-beta(19/2-eta)/4]), 0<eta<19/2.     (4.4)

Thus the explicit order-beta pointwise response is compatible with
(2.3)-(3.3). It occurs on exteriors with a fixed large action cost.
Those bounds concern the full original average, not the conditional
Haar transition layer with exterior frozen.

5. FULL-MEASURE UPGRADE OF THE TURN97 SOURCE COMPARISON

Let A_beta=P_pair S_pair, let a_beta=A_beta(flat), and retain D from
Section4. Jensen, (1.3), and (4.1) give for each r>=1

 ||A_beta||_r+||D||_r <= C_r/beta,
 0<=a_beta<=C/beta for beta>=1.                         (5.1)

The last inequality follows from TURN97's flat expansion (and a compact
beta interval if needed). On E_bad^c, TURN97 proves

 |A_beta-a_beta-D|<=C beta^-2 D.                        (5.2)

On E_bad use |A_beta-a_beta-D|<=2A_beta+a_beta. Holder with (5.1), (4.3)
therefore gives, for q>=1 and beta above the TURN97 threshold,

 ||A_beta-a_beta-D||_q
 <= C_q beta^-3
    +C_q beta^-1 exp[-beta d_epsilon/(8q)]
 <= C'_q beta^-3.                                     (5.3)

The exponential prefactor 2^(33/(8q)) is absorbed into C_q. This is a
full original-law estimate, not a weak-sector conditional estimate.
The constants depend on the fixed weak tube and pair, but not on the
surrounding volume. In particular, for any original test F in L^(q')
with 1/q+1/q'=1, the norm yields the absolute covariance error

 |Cov(F,A_beta)-Cov(F,D)|
       <= 2||F||_(q') ||A_beta-a_beta-D||_q.             (5.4)

The harmless flat constant has zero covariance. Equation (5.4) has no
spatial decay and no covariance sign or multiplicative comparison.

There is also a full-measure first-gradient upgrade for a fixed exterior
link f and unit X_f. The compact minimum D is globally Lipschitz in the
exterior variables; where differentiable, the envelope bound at a
minimizer and (1.1) give

 |X_fD|<=sqrt(12D),                                    (5.5)

since at most six pair-star plaquettes read f. At points with multiple
minimizers the same bound holds for every directional envelope slope;
the ordinary derivative exists Haar-almost everywhere by local
Rademacher, hence almost everywhere under the smooth positive exterior
marginal. No chosen minimizer needs to be differentiated.

In the good tube, TURN97's invariant quotient gradient bound, and the
uniformly smooth finite staple maps, give

 |X_f(A_beta-D)|<=C beta^-2 sqrt(D).                    (5.6)

Common frame rotations contribute zero; the quotient slice bounds are
uniform after covering the common S^3 frame. The original staple maps
are finite products of unit quaternions, so their first derivatives
are bounded independently of beta and volume. On the bad set apply
Holder to (2.3), (5.5) and (5.1). This proves

 ||X_f(A_beta-D)||_q
 <=C_q beta^-5/2+C_q beta^-1/2 exp[-beta d_epsilon/(8q)]
 <=C'_q beta^-5/2.                                    (5.7)

The gradient conclusion is a weak/a.e. derivative statement for D.
A_beta itself is smooth everywhere. Neither (5.3) nor (5.7) reclassifies
all nonflat exteriors as weak; their actual original-law weight is paid.

6. VARYING ELEVEN-PLAQUETTE WEIGHTS

This optional extension uses the already independently audited TURN97
weighted weak-tube estimate, with wbar the eleven-weight mean and
omega=max_p|w_p-wbar|. Let M_w=P_pair S_w. To define a global classical
weighted value even at nonunique baseline minimizers, use

 C_w(t)=min{S_w(x,y;t): S_pair(x,y;t)=D(t)}.              (6.1)

It agrees with TURN97's S_w,cl on the good tube. It is measurable: it
equals the right derivative at s=0 of the continuous compact-minimum
value min_(x,y)[S_pair+s S_w]. To verify the derivative, compare to a
baseline minimizer with smallest S_w, choose minimizing points for
s>0, use compact subsequences, and note their limiting S_pair is D.
This is also gauge/frame invariant. Pointwise |C_w|<=||w||_infinity D.
No source-dependent minimizer is substituted for the baseline one in
the good tube.

Since |M_w|<=||w||_infinity A_beta and its flat value has the same bound,
the bad-sector argument of Section5 applies. On the good set TURN97
gives |M_w-M_w(flat)-C_w|<=C[|wbar|beta^-2+omega beta^-1]D.
Consequently

 ||M_w-M_w(flat)-C_w||_q
 <=C_q[|wbar|beta^-3+omega beta^-2]
   +C_q||w||_infinity beta^-1 exp[-beta d_epsilon/(8q)]
 <=C'_q[|wbar|beta^-3+omega beta^-2].                    (6.2)

Here beta is above a fixed threshold, and ||w||_infinity<=|wbar|+omega.
The final absorption uses exponential decay; it does not discard an
unpaid per-pair constant. A varying-profile gradient theorem is NOT
claimed globally: C_w can have worse regularity where minimizers split.

7. WHAT THIS CHANGES AT THE PROOF FRONTIER

The TURN97 exceptional-exterior probability is now quantitatively paid
for its actual fixed pair, including its explicit bad aggregate-staple
neighborhood. Its source approximation holds in the full original law.
Separately, an actual arbitrary original block and an arbitrary-length
single-pass heatbath sequence admit first L^q response bounds uniform
in eliminated-link count for a fixed original local energy mark.

These are not another assumed sufficient criterion: (2.3), (3.3),
(4.3), (5.3), and (5.7) follow directly from the actual original density
and the already proved original moment theorem.

What remains is sharply visible. The estimate retains W for a source
summed over microscopic sites, gives no distance decay between distinct
marks, and does not prepare the generated action/source for another
scale with a new running domain. An actual RG history may read updated
links and changes the conditional score. The exact conditional formulas
here cannot be applied after such conditioning without its additional
scores. A fixed-tail factor exp(-c beta) alone is not asserted to pay
the a^-4 physical root census along an unspecified trajectory beta(a).
