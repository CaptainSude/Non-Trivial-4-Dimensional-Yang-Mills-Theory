3. ACTUAL TWO-WORD CONDITIONAL COVARIANCE

Under (1), the new ordinary-local-marginal theorem gives for two real
bounded local tests on reads R_u,R_v

 |Cov_Q(f_u,g_v)| <= C_ker b_u b_v exp[-nu d(R_u,R_v)],   (4)

after enlarging C_ker>=1 to cover overlapping reads. Here nu>0 can be
chosen no larger than both the full-law decay exponent and sigma.
For complex Cauchy words, apply the real bound to real/imaginary parts;
a fixed factor at most four suffices. Nothing depends on |I|, |R_u| or
|R_v| through this kernel constant. All source-size and read costs are
in b and D. The full Hamiltonian's inherited c0,c_nu,M_nu determine
C_ker; the native weak floor/profile assumptions are not removed.

Since P is contained in X_u and Q in X_v,

 d(R_u,R_v) >= d(P,Q)-diam(X_u)-diam(X_v).               (5)

When one read is empty its covariance is zero, so no convention for
distance to the empty set is needed. Multiply (4) by exp[-D_u-D_v],
use (5), and sum with (3). This proves the positive rooted column

 sum_(u,v) exp[-D_u-D_v] |Cov_Q(f_u,g_v)|
       <= C A_p A_q exp[-nu d(P,Q)].                    (6)

This is the new finite source application. It controls the actual next
ordinary connected two-first row. It has no ell^(-2Q) source Cauchy-
