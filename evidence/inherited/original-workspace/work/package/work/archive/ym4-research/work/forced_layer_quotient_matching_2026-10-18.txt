1. Exact invariance of the physical-endpoint chart.

Use the operators and corrected action of forced_global_chart_2026-10-18.txt.

Delta_a T0=P0^T. Let f and f' be two current forcings at the same fixed
background (W,J) and with the same block datum b. Suppose

                  T0(f-f')=0.                         (1)

Then G(f-f')=0. The fixed-point equations for P are identical, so uniqueness
gives P_f=P_f', X_f=X_f', H_f=H_f'. Also P0^T(f-f')=Delta_a T0(f-f')=0.
The only remaining explicit forcing term in the companion is -P0^T f.
Consequently K_f=K_f'. This proves quotient invariance of BOTH the field and
the current, not just of the Euler equation.

In particular (1) holds when f-f'=Q*lambda, since T0Q*=0. Conversely, on
the finite eligible domain the identity Delta_a T0=P0^T and
range P0=ker Q imply that (1) gives f-f' in range Q*. Here transpose and
orthogonal complements are interpreted first on the real finite-dimensional
spaces; complexification gives the same algebraic equality of ranges.

Thus the physical-match condition for a forcing is

                  T0 f=T0 Jphys(W),                   (2)

rather than literal f=Jphys(W). On the affine constraint QP=ell^(-1)b,
the extra linear action from f-Jphys(W)=Q*lambda is constant. It therefore
does not affect the gauge-orbit stationarity proof. The Landau cancellation
and eligibility requirements of the forced-chart note are still required.

CMP122-II, pp370--371, (1.54)--(1.55), localizes the current J_{1,2} by
replacing it with a supported forcing inside its constrained Green operator.
The displayed identity is equality AFTER applying that Green operator. The
repository names the resulting supported current mathsf J_3 and its field
datum j_3=T0(sigma)mathsf J_3 (Raw I73108--73114,77060--77068).
When the Green operator is the T0 of the matching determining family, its
physical-endpoint identity is precisely (2). Section1 is the algebraic

No analogous identity is asserted at arbitrary complex sigma. For those
parameters use the full-walk decoration convention of the forced-chart note.
The extension must be used consistently in the source consumers; independently
decorated inverse identities must not be substituted for physical identities.
