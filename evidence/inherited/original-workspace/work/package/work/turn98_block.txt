TURN98 -- GLOBAL COMPACT SOURCE/ACTION CONTROL AND A VOLUME-UNIFORM
SPARSE INTERACTING ELIMINATION OF ORIGINAL WILSON LINKS
21 October 2026. block98. No shared frontier changes.

RESULT

The fixed weak-exterior two-link theorem from TURN97 has an elementary
GLOBAL continuation with a weaker gain. Let D be the actual classical
minimum of the eleven affected plaquettes, A_beta their exact conditional
mean, and V_beta their generated action relative to flat exterior data.
For ALL ten exterior unit staples and beta>=1,

 |A_beta(t)-A_beta(flat)-D(t)| <= C q_beta D(t),
 |V_beta(t)-beta D(t)| <= C log(2+beta) D(t),                 (0.1)
 q_beta=log(2+beta)/beta.

Constants depend only on this fixed two-link fiber. Near the zero-defect
manifold the stronger bounds are respectively C beta^-2 D and C D.
No large-field exterior has been discarded. This is a VALUE theorem;
it asserts no global derivative norm and does not contradict TURN97's
conditional-response obstruction.

A positive-density original-lattice packing then gives one exact
normalized elimination round, with both the generated action and the
old source controlled by the same classical eliminated energy. Constants
are independent of the number of eliminated pairs and of the volume.
The round eliminates 1/24 of all links in the explicit geometry below.
It leaves the generated multi-link interactions intact. It does not
prove an invariant class under subsequent rounds, a complete-block
estimate, or a continuum source-census bound.

1. THE FIXED COMPACT FIBER

Retain TURN97's exact original Wilson fiber on S^3 x S^3:

 S_t(x,y)=11-x dot y-sum_(i=1)^5 a_i dot x-sum_(j=1)^5 b_j dot y,
 a_i,b_j in S^3,
 D(t)=min S_t,
 Z_beta(t)=int exp(-beta S_t) dx dy,
 A_beta(t)=Z_beta(t)^-1 int S_t exp(-beta S_t) dx dy.          (1.1)

All measures are normalized Haar. Each of the eleven displayed
plaquette defects is nonnegative. Write t0 for all staples equal to a
common n, and put

 V_beta(t)=-log Z_beta(t)+log Z_beta(t0).                    (1.2)

This does not replace the changed second density by a Wilson kernel.
Equations (1.3)--(1.6) of work/turn97_two_link_source.txt remain its
exact sequential representation. In particular

 partial_beta V_beta(t)=A_beta(t)-A_beta(t0).                (1.3)

We use two proved TURN97 facts only in a fixed weak neighborhood:

 |A_beta(t)-A_beta(t0)-D(t)| <= C beta^-2 D(t),              (1.4)

 and the parameter-C2 compact Laplace expansion for Z_beta after
 factoring out exp(-beta D) beta^-3. This is used below for the action.
The fixed dimension and universal dimension cancellation are older
modules; no new general finite-dimensional Laplace principle is claimed.

2. SMALL DEFECT FORCES THE REQUIRED WEAK EXTERIOR GEOMETRY

Choose any global minimizer (x_*,y_*). Every nonnegative plaquette
summand in S_t(x_*,y_*) is at most D. Since |u-v|^2=2(1-u dot v)
for unit quaternions,

 |x_*-y_*|, |a_i-x_*|, |b_j-y_*| <= sqrt(2D).                (2.1)

Consequently

 |a_i-a_1| <= 2 sqrt(2D),
 |b_j-a_1| <= 3 sqrt(2D).                                 (2.2)

There is a fixed delta>0 such that D<delta puts every staple in
TURN97's weak tube after a simultaneous SO(4) rotation sending a_1 to
n. Coordinate and compactness constants are uniform in that rotation.
The minimum is then unique by TURN97. Conversely the complementary
region D>=delta has a fixed positive classical cost. This conclusion
uses the actual nonnegative plaquette decomposition, not a conjectured
probability that typical exterior links are close to identity.

3. UNIFORM FULL-COMPACT PARTITION AND ENERGY-EXCESS BOUNDS

Give each S^3 its round metric and their product the product metric.
Along a product geodesic of velocity (X,Y), the absolute second
derivative of S_t is bounded by

 6(|X|^2+|Y|^2)+2|X||Y| <= 7(|X|^2+|Y|^2).               (3.1)

Indeed the five private staples have total norm at most five at each
spin, and the shared dot product contributes one to each diagonal
bound and the displayed cross term. At a global minimum the first
derivative is zero. Thus at round distances r_x,r_y from a minimizer,
using the minimizing product geodesic,

 0<=S_t(x,y)-D(t) <= (7/2)(r_x^2+r_y^2).                   (3.2)

For beta>=1 use the product of caps r_x,r_y<=beta^-1/2. On it the
excess exponent is at most seven. A normalized S^3 cap of radius r<=1
has mass

 (2/pi) int_0^r sin^2(s) ds >= (8/(3pi^3)) r^3.

Therefore, with c=e^-7[8/(3pi^3)]^2 in (0,1),

 c beta^-3 <= I_beta(t):=exp(beta D(t)) Z_beta(t) <= 1       (3.3)

for every exterior t and beta>=1. Nonuniqueness or degeneracy of the
minimum causes no difficulty. This lower bound is for the FULL compact
integral, with its actual normalizer.

Let X=S_t-D and use its actual Gibbs probability. From (3.3),

 P_beta,t(X>=s) <= min(1,c^-1 beta^3 exp(-beta s)).

Integrating this tail on [0,infinity) gives

 0<=A_beta(t)-D(t)
   <= [3 log beta+log(c^-1)+1]/beta.                       (3.4)

The upper integration limit can be enlarged because X is nonnegative.
This elementary estimate is deliberately logarithmic. A uniform C/beta
claim is NOT inferred merely from finite dimension: changes of saddle
degeneracy could invalidate an unproved uniform sharper claim.

4. GLOBAL RELATIVE ORIGINAL-SOURCE BOUND

At t0, (3.4) applies with D=0. For D>=delta, it follows immediately that

 |A_beta(t)-A_beta(t0)-D(t)|
 <= 2[3 log beta+log(c^-1)+1]/beta
 <= C delta^-1 q_beta D(t).                               (4.1)

For D<delta use (1.4) and beta^-2<=C q_beta. Combining the two proves
the first assertion of (0.1), with constants independent of t. Thus for
all sufficiently large beta,

 (1-C q_beta)D <= A_beta(t)-A_beta(t0)
                         <= (1+C q_beta)D.                (4.2)

This statement includes arbitrary frustrated and large exterior data.
It does not assert that A_beta(t)-A_beta(t0) has a uniformly bounded
exterior derivative. It does not identify A_beta(t0) with the one-point
expectation in the full Wilson measure.

5. GLOBAL RELATIVE GENERATED-ACTION BOUND

For D>=delta, (3.3) gives

 V_beta(t)-beta D(t)=-log I_beta(t)+log I_beta(t0),
 |V_beta(t)-beta D(t)| <= 3 log beta+log(c^-1)
                     <= C log(2+beta) D(t).                (5.1)

For D<delta, the parameter-uniform Laplace calculation in TURN97 gives

 Z_beta(t)=exp(-beta D(t)) beta^-3 K_beta(t),
 ||log K_beta||_C2 <= C,

on a fixed weak tube, with K_beta bounded above and away from zero.
For large beta this follows by division of the parameter-C2 Laplace
expansion; for beta in a bounded interval it follows from compactness,
positivity and smoothness of the exact integral. Put

 L_beta(t)=-log K_beta(t)+log K_beta(t0).

After rotating a_1 to n, L_beta(0)=0. The SO(3) stabilizer of n forbids
an invariant linear functional of the remaining nine tangent staples,
so its first jet also vanishes exactly. TURN97's positive transverse
classical Hessian then gives

 |L_beta(t)|<=C |w_slice|^2<=C D(t).                        (5.2)

Combining (5.1)--(5.2) proves the second assertion of (0.1). In the
weak tube the determinant correction is bounded relative to D with no
log beta. Its nonzero value was computed in TURN97; it is not removed.

There is also exact positivity V_beta(t)>=0 for every beta>=0. To see
this, put a=sum_i a_i, b=sum_j b_j and f_a(x)=exp(beta a dot x).
The kernel K_beta(x,y)=exp(beta x dot y) is positive semidefinite:
each term (beta^k/k!)(x dot y)^k is a tensor-power Gram kernel, and
the series converges uniformly on the compact spheres. Cauchy--Schwarz
for its positive bilinear form gives

 Z_beta(a,b)<=sqrt[Z_beta(a,a)Z_beta(b,b)].

By rotation invariance Z_beta(a,a) depends only on |a|. Simultaneously
changing x,y to -x,-y rewrites its field factor as
cosh(beta |a| n dot (x+y)), which is nondecreasing in |a|>=0 pointwise.
Since |a|,|b|<=5, both diagonal factors are at most Z_beta(t0). Hence
Z_beta(t)<=Z_beta(t0). This fact does not require an asymptotic estimate.

5A. AN ACTUAL DEGENERATE EXTERIOR SHOWS WHY THE GLOBAL GAIN WEAKENS

Choose the five a_i to be the five equally spaced unit quaternions in
one fixed circle, and do the same for the b_j. Their sums are a=b=0.
These exterior staples are realizable on the original lattice: set the
transverse links in the pair's eleven plaquettes to the identity, and
assign the ten distinct exterior direction-1 middle links the desired
staples (or their inverses, according to orientation). None is one of
the two integrated links. Other, exterior-only plaquettes contribute
only to S_out and impose no hard constraint on this conditional fiber.

Then exactly

 S_t(x,y)=11-x dot y,  D(t)=10,
 Z_beta(t)=exp(-11beta) z(beta),
 A_beta(t)=11-m(beta),
 z(s)=2I_1(s)/s, m(s)=I_2(s)/I_1(s).                       (5A.1)

The one-sphere compact Laplace expansion, or the same Bessel formulas,
gives z(beta)=c_1 exp(beta) beta^-3/2[1+O(beta^-1)] and
m(beta)=1-3/(2beta)+O(beta^-2), with c_1>0. TURN97 gives
Z_beta(t0)=c_2 beta^-3[1+O(beta^-1)] and
A_beta(t0)=3/beta+O(beta^-2), with c_2>0. Therefore

 V_beta(t)=10beta-(3/2)log beta+O(1),
 A_beta(t)-A_beta(t0)-D(t)=-(3/2)/beta+O(beta^-2).           (5A.2)

The logarithmic global action cost is therefore real, and a global
C beta^-2 D source bound is false. The source estimate in (0.1) has
an extra logarithm not proved necessary by this example; no optimal
global source rate is claimed. The example concerns a legitimate fixed
exterior, not a claim about its probability in the full Wilson measure.

6. DETERMINISTIC VARYING SOURCE WEIGHTS

Let w_l be arbitrary real weights on the eleven plaquettes, wbar their
mean, omega=max_l|w_l-wbar|, and

 S_w=sum_l w_l h_l,
 M_beta,w(t)=E_beta,t S_w,
 S_w,cl(t)=S_w(x_*,y_*),                                   (6.1)

where (x_*,y_*) is ANY minimum of the BASELINE UNWEIGHTED S_t. A
minimum exists by compactness; no uniqueness is asserted globally.
For every such choice,

 |M_beta,w(t)-M_beta,w(t0)-S_w,cl(t)|
 <= C[|wbar| q_beta+omega] D(t).                           (6.2)

Proof. Split w=wbar*1+d. The constant part is Section4. For D<delta
the exact weighted TURN97 estimate gives C omega beta^-1 D. For
D>=delta, use |S_d|<=omega S and |S_d,cl|<=omega D to obtain

 |M_beta,d(t)-M_beta,d(t0)-S_d,cl(t)|
 <= omega[A_beta(t)+A_beta(t0)+D(t)]
 <= omega[2D+C q_beta]
 <= C omega D.                                           (6.3)

Here q_beta is bounded on beta>=1. This proves (6.2), including the
case of a nonunique baseline minimum. No global omega/beta improvement
is claimed. The choice-free target wbar D also satisfies

 |M_beta,w(t)-M_beta,w(t0)-wbar D(t)|
 <= C[|wbar| q_beta+omega]D(t),                            (6.4)

because |S_w,cl-wbar D|<=omega D. For a scalar Lipschitz physical
profile sampled at the plaquette centers, omega<=2a Lip_infinity(f)
up to the chosen norm convention. Orientation-dependent profiles use
their actual oscillation, not a fictitious spatial derivative gain.

7. AN ACTUAL POSITIVE-DENSITY PACKING, WITH NO PAIR INTERACTION

Take a periodic four-dimensional hypercubic lattice with side lengths
N_1>=4, N_2>=6 divisible by three, and N_3,N_4>=4 even. Use positively
oriented direction-1 links. For every

 x=(x_1,3k,2l,2m),

select the pair P_x consisting of the links (x,1) and (x+e_2,1).
No selected links from different pairs share a plaquette: any plaquette
contains at most two direction-1 links, and their starting points differ
by one unit in a transverse direction. The selected starts have isolated
adjacent pairs in direction2 and spacing two in directions3,4. Changes
of x1 do not couple two direction-1 links in a single plaquette.

There are V/12 pairs and V/6 eliminated links, where V=prod_i N_i.
Since the lattice has 4V positively oriented links, the eliminated
fraction is 1/24. The periods above also avoid accidental short-torus
identifications in the eleven-plaquette fiber.

Let E be the union of selected links and xi all retained links. Every
affected plaquette belongs to exactly one pair. Write P(P) for that
pair's eleven affected plaquettes. Then, exactly,

 S_original(U)=S_out(xi)+sum_P S_P(U_P;xi).                 (7.1)

The Haar-preserving coordinates of TURN97 transform each S_P into
(1.1) using only retained links. They may depend on common retained
links, but never on another eliminated pair. The conditional law given
xi therefore factors into the actual compact pair laws. Shared reads
do not spoil conditional factorization.

Each S_P and its exact Z_P read at most 32 retained links: the ten
private plaquettes each have three exterior link occurrences, and the
shared plaquette has two. Repetitions only reduce this count. Each
retained link is in at most six affected plaquettes, hence in at most
six pair read sets. Generated pair interactions thus have bounded
support and bounded overlap, uniformly in the volume. The read sets
include the two transverse links used in the Haar coordinate change.

8. EXACT NORMALIZED MARGINAL AND UNIFORM ACTION/SOURCE COMPARISON

Integrate all selected pairs simultaneously. The retained marginal is
exactly

 dnu_beta(xi) proportional
       exp[-beta S_out(xi)] prod_P Z_beta(t_P(xi)) dxi.

Remove only the field-independent product Z_beta(t0)^(#pairs) and set

 V_round(xi)=beta S_out(xi)+sum_P V_beta(t_P(xi)),
 S_cl,round(xi)=S_out(xi)+sum_P D(t_P(xi)).                 (8.1)

Then dnu_beta is the normalized density proportional to exp(-V_round)
with respect to the original retained Haar product. In particular all
generated potentials and the full normalizer are retained, exactly.
Sections4--5 sum without any volume-dependent constant:

 |V_round-beta S_cl,round|
   <=C log(2+beta) sum_P D_P
   <=C log(2+beta) S_cl,round,                            (8.2)

 A_round:=partial_beta V_round
          =S_out+sum_P[A_beta(t_P)-A_beta(t0)],

 |A_round-S_cl,round|
   <=C q_beta sum_P D_P<=C q_beta S_cl,round.              (8.3)

Here the original full-action conditional mean is A_round plus the
constant (#pairs) A_beta(t0). Its removal is exact for connected
correlations. These inequalities have no additive per-pair defect
bound. They hold on the entire retained configuration space, and
constants are independent of N_i and the number of pairs.

For arbitrary deterministic original plaquette weights w, define

 M_round,w=S_out,w+sum_P[M_beta,w_P(t_P)-M_beta,w_P(t0)],
 S_cl,round,w=S_out,w+sum_P S_w_P,cl(t_P).

Then

 |M_round,w-S_cl,round,w|
 <=C sum_P [|wbar_P| q_beta+omega_P] D_P.                  (8.4)

The exact conditional source equals M_round,w plus a deterministic
sum of flat-pair constants. Measurable choices of pair minima can be
made if the classical weighted expression is used inside an integral;
the choice-free version with wbar_P D_P avoids this issue entirely.

9. EXACT SEPARATED-SOURCE COVARIANCE TRANSPORT

For arbitrary original additive plaquette sources S_f,S_g, conditional
factorization and the law of total covariance give

 Cov_original(S_f,S_g)
   =Cov_nu(M_round,f,M_round,g)
      +E_nu sum_P Cov_(beta,t_P)(S_f|P,S_g|P).             (9.1)

If no pair has affected plaquettes in both source supports, the last
sum vanishes identically. In particular, it vanishes when the two
supports of plaquette centers have l-infinity separation greater than
2a: the eleven plaquette centers of one pair have l-infinity diameter
at most 2a. Then

 Cov_original(S_f,S_g)=Cov_nu(M_round,f,M_round,g).         (9.2)

This transports the ORIGINAL source into the SAME exact retained
marginal, with no unproved history-label measurability. It does not
replace the right side by a covariance of S_cl,round,f and S_cl,round,g;
pointwise errors (8.4) alone do not establish such a comparison.

10. WHAT THIS CHANGES AND THE REMAINING OBSTRUCTION

The prior two-link statement required weak exterior staples. Equations
(0.1) and (8.2)--(8.4) remove that restriction for one simultaneous
positive-density round, at explicitly weaker logarithmic and profile-
oscillation gains. No exceptional-exterior probability is needed for
THIS round. The full original conditional law is used everywhere.

The new marginal contains exact finite-range multi-link potentials
V_beta(t_P(xi)), in addition to the untouched Wilson plaquettes. The
potential for a subsequent eliminated link therefore includes those
changed factors. It is not the original Wilson one-link or two-link
fiber. Bounded factor supports and overlap do not prove a contraction
or an invariant source/action norm. Repeating this theorem unchanged
on the new marginal would be invalid.

Likewise S_cl,round is still a sum over microscopic positions. Bounds
by its total value do not pay a physical source census, yield separated
correlation decay, prove a nonzero continuum response, or identify
Yang--Mills interactions. The global logarithmic gain is weaker than
the weak-tube beta^-2 source gain; no summability over an unbounded
number of scales is asserted. The complete-block and iterated native
source assembly remain substantive work.

Originality within this project: independent conditional factorization
and finite-dimensional Laplace calculations already occur in earlier
turns, including TURN40/TURN74. The additional results here are the
all-exterior relative continuation using small defect versus a compact
Gibbs tail, its action counterpart, and their literal volume-uniform
assembly at positive lattice density with exact changed marginal and
separated original-source covariance identity.
