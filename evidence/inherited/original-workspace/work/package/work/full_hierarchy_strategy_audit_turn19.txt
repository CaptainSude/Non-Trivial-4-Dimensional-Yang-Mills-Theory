3. SU(2) REMOVES THE EXACT C-ODD NO-GO, NOT THE CUBIC TASK

For SU(N>=3), charge conjugation fixes spacetime points, preserves the
Wilson measure, and fixes the action density. A nonzero d_abc F^aF^bF^c
is odd and cannot lie in its C-fixed scalar subnet. This is an exact
internal-symmetry obstruction, recorded in the turn18 scope note.

For SU(2), C is inner and d_abc=0. The actual oriented cubic is instead
epsilon_abc F_A^a F_B^b F_C^c; it is C-even. The free colored-Maxwell
reference has an extra improper-color O(3) symmetry fixing every dot
product and reversing this cubic, but the interacting nonabelian action
breaks that accidental symmetry. Therefore no exact C argument forbids
interacting SU(2) scalar fusion from producing the cubic. It simply must
detect and control an interaction-dependent coefficient if that route
is used. The original scope already permits a direct independent source.

4. AN EXACT FINITE PRIMITIVE LATTICE FAMILY

Use Hermitian generators t_a=sigma_a/2 and ordinary matrix trace.
At a vertex x choose the six oriented plaquettes based at x and set

 B_A(U)=(U_(p_A)-U_(p_A)^dagger)/(2i),
 Q_AB(U)=2 Tr[B_A B_B],                                  (4.1)
 T_ABC(U)=-2i Tr[B_A [B_B,B_C]].                          (4.2)

For SU(2), B_A is Hermitian and traceless. All factors transform by the
same conjugation at x, so (4.1)--(4.2) are real gauge-invariant local
functions. Their complete read has a fixed finite number of links.
Reflected/orientation companions can be retained as part of the finite
frame. These definitions are on the compact group itself, not only a
weak-field gauge chart.

Write B_A=b_A^a t_a. The exact trace identities give

 Q_AB=b_A dot b_B,
 T_ABC=b_A dot (b_B cross b_C).                           (4.3)

Sign check: [t_b,t_c]=i epsilon_bcd t_d and Tr(t_a t_d)=delta_ad/2,
so -2i Tr(t_a[t_b,t_c])=epsilon_bca=epsilon_abc. In contrast,
Im Tr U_p vanishes identically for SU(2), as does the imaginary trace
of every SU(2) word. The SU(N>=3) single-plaquette C-odd seed in the
turn18 note is therefore NOT this SU(2) cubic. If curvature samples
are based at different vertices, first transport each to x along its
specified finite path. The common conjugation and leading sign are
unchanged; the higher germ terms acquire the corresponding covariant
derivatives. These are smooth-field germ statements, not uniform
quantum-field estimates.

For a smooth canonical curvature in the stated convention,
U_(p_A)=exp(i a^2 g F_A+O(a^3)) and B_A=a^2 g F_A+...
Thus the leading germs are a^4 g^2 times the dot product and a^6 g^3
times the oriented triple. In particular the cubic seed has a nonzero

5. WHY FINITE SEEDS ARE NOT AUTOMATIC FULL-JET COMPLETENESS

There is a direct algebraic obstruction to finite differential generation.
Let a finite collection of invariant polynomial generators have maximum
engineering dimension D0. Expand a proposed identity to quadratic field
degree around the zero connection. There are no invariant linear terms,
so products of two nonconstant generators cannot contribute quadratically.
Only ordinary derivatives of the original quadratic terms contribute.
On two external momenta p,q, those derivatives multiply by p+q. Setting
q=-p kills every proper descendant; the surviving relative momentum
degree is bounded by D0.

But invariant bilinears tr[(D_1^m F_12)(D_1^m F_12)] have arbitrarily
high relative degree and nonzero linearized on-shell forward symbols.
For example take complex null p=t(1,0,i,0), q=-p and transverse
polarizations e_2 in the same Cartan color. The leading symbol is a
nonzero multiple of t^(2m+2). Linearized EOM terms vanish on this test,
and total derivatives vanish at p+q=0. Therefore sufficiently high m
cannot arise from the finite family by ordinary polynomial operations.
This is the existing exact argument in local_observable_ope_route_turn6,
restated here to settle the proposed finite-seed shortcut.

There are two valid ways forward, neither automatic:

 (i) For every fixed engineering degree D, use the FINITE set of required
     covariant-jet color vectors and their dot/triple invariants, and prove
     a compatible joint construction as D increases.
 (ii) Start with the small primitive family and prove renormalized
     point-splitting/OPE limits that recover relative derivatives and
     products, in the actual joint distribution topology.

At the symbol level, the second is plausible: a single free contraction
of Q(x)Q(y) produces a bilocal color dot product; its relative Taylor
coefficients generate differentiated bilinears. A cubic gives oriented
analogues. But a formal Wick-sector extraction is not an identity for
the complete interacting product, whose higher-field sectors and lower
subtractions must remain. The turn17 complete angular construction is
a genuine partial example, not yet this all-label OPE theorem.

Classical reconstruction of a color frame by inverting a nonzero Gram
determinant also does not bypass this issue. It excludes degenerate
configurations, uses rational rather than polynomial operations, and
has no automatic meaning for coincident quantum distributions.

6. THE N-LEG MIXING ISSUE HAS TWO DIFFERENT ANSWERS

The exact theorem in tensorized_leg_calibration_turn19 says the following.
If a fixed finite one-leg calibration produces an integrable connection
K(g), then n independent local calibrations produce exactly the Kronecker
sum K^(n)=sum_a K_a. The norm is at most n||K||, fundamental matrices
cost at most C^n, and their endpoint error costs C^n n times the one-leg
tail. The spin-six turn18 finite correction therefore works at every n
with the SAME local scheme; no perturbative order proportional to n is
required just from tensoring the local frame.

This remains true for complete correlated moments: a linear change of
each inserted field acts tensorwise by multilinearity, regardless of
whether the probability law factorizes. What does NOT follow is that
the entire scale evolution of an n-point correlation is a Kronecker sum.
Genuinely joint forcing and connected source/contact terms remain.

For those terms a bound with one inverse-running norm per external leg
can have a factor j^(alpha n). A positive physical-scale power r_j^delta
absorbs this with a fixed-factorial cost. The parent's separate theorem
spatial_gain_joint_os_growth_turn19 proves the exact sum. A mere fixed
g_j^p remainder cannot do so for all n, and even
exp[-c(log j)^nu], nu>1, can produce constants exp[C n^(nu/(nu-1))].

If perturbative order N(n)=O(n) must instead grow for a joint remainder,
that is not intrinsically fatal: a WHOLE-correlation estimate
C^(n+N)((n+N)!)^B is still fixed-factorial at N=O(n). By contrast
independently paying (N!)^B at each of n legs can yield
exp[C n^2 log n], which is not such a bound. Unspecified fixed-order
constants C_N prove neither conclusion. Most importantly, N(n) may
change an estimate or a truncation used in its proof, not redefine the
physical field counterterms separately at every arity.
