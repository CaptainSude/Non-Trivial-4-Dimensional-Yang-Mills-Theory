2. PROJECTED MEAN AND THE CONSTANT IN EQUATION (5)

Fix tau and let C=I\R. Write w_i=exp[-nu d(i,R)], so w=1 on R. On a
conditional fiber x_R=r put

 A(r)^2=E_r |grad_R G|^2,
 B(r)^2=E_r |w_C grad_C G|^2.

For every vector v on R, exact differentiation of the conditional ratio is

 D_v m_R=E_r[v.grad_R G]-Cov_r(G,v.grad_R H_tau).

Use the complementary weighted covariance theorem with its weight w_C^-1.
The complete row/column bound and Schur's test give

 ||w_C^-1 H_CR'' v||_2 <= M_nu ||v||_2,

because w_R=1. Therefore

 |D_v m_R| <= ||v|| [A(r)+(M_nu/c_nu) B(r)]
 <= ||v|| sqrt[1+(M_nu/c_nu)^2] sqrt[A(r)^2+B(r)^2].

Take the vector dual norm BEFORE integrating over r. The tower property
gives exactly

 ||grad_R m_R||_(L2(q_tau))
 <= sqrt[1+(M_nu/c_nu)^2] ||w grad G||_(L2(q_tau)).

Thus equation (5) has the correct constant, no extra square-root read-size
factor, and no missing direct-gradient term. If C is empty the covariance
term vanishes and the same bound is immediate. An empty R is a trivial
constant-observable case and may be separated before introducing distance
weights; it has zero response and zero marginal TV.
