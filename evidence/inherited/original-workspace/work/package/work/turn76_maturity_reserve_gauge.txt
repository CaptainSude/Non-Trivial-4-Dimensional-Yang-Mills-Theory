TURN76 -- A FRESH HEALING RESERVE PAYS THE NATIVE MATURITY WAIT
20 October 2026. gauge47. Narrow quantitative corollary.

RESULT AND INPUT SCOPE

The native MR_birth partition prevents immediately calling every newly
healed R term an ordinary homogeneous source. Its prescribed finite
maturity delay, however, can be paid by the retained fresh-healing
logarithmic reserve. This uses the ACTUAL finite typed continuation
bound from turn74_native_transport_history Sections6--7, not the
homogeneous contraction estimate whose admission is still at issue.

The corollary applies to a source family already admitted at each of
these young/native preparatory steps, with its complete dated carrier,
source and scalar ledger. It does not prove that admission, an identity
with an ordinary coarse lift, or a full mixed-state closure.

1. WAITING LENGTH AND COUPLING COMPARISON

Use gamma_j=g_j^(-2), ell_j=1+log gamma_j, gamma_j>=Gamma>1.
Replacing ell by 1+|log g| only changes fixed constants. Suppose the
native partition size satisfies R_j<=C_R ell_j^r, with fixed M,L>1.
The B119 maturity criterion L^[-m]MR_j<=L is met by

 m_j=max(0,ceil(log_L(MR_j/L)))
                  <=C_0+C_1 log ell_j.                     (1.1)

Retain the signed-step input |gamma_(k+1)-gamma_k|<=D on the actual
admitted prefix. For 0<=m<=m_j and sufficiently large fixed Gamma,

        gamma_j/2 <= gamma_(j+m) <= 2 gamma_j.               (1.2)

Indeed D(C_0+C_1 log ell_j)=o(gamma_j). Thus coupling/log radii over
this wait are comparable. No monotone coupling flow is needed.

2. ACTUAL FINITE-JET COST

For one fixed finite source derivative order, TURN74's admitted typed
continuation supplies a coefficient cost envelope of the form

 A(j,m)<=C poly(2+gamma_j+gamma_(j+m),1+m)
             exp[C m log(2+gamma_j+gamma_(j+m)+m)].          (2.1)

Its engineering factors and fixed-degree Cauchy losses have already
been included; it is not a uniform contraction. For two jets the
transported size is bounded by such a cost multiplying the SUM of
the initial mixed size and the PRODUCT of the two initial singleton
sizes. The collision branches and their finite meeting-date sum are
included by changing the fixed C and polynomial. For a fixed higher
order, retain the corresponding finite polynomial in initial jets.
This requires the actual complete typed state and, when present,
its original raw-history test envelope.

Equations(1.1)--(1.2) imply

                    log A(j,m_j)<=C ell_j log(2+ell_j).     (2.2)

Any additional FIXED power of the current inverse field radius, or
current coupling, costs O(ell_j) in this logarithm. In particular the
normalization by a_(j+m_j)^2 with a_k=g_k ell_k^p is included. So are
fixed-degree marginal extractions and their already recorded carrier
moments, provided the finite prepared norm in(2.1) is the one used.
No arbitrary all-arity conversion or new uncounted geometry is hidden
in this sentence.

3. RESERVE ABSORPTION

Suppose the freshly healed source envelope is

             epsilon_j <= C_* B_* exp[-c ell_j^s],          (3.1)

where s>1, c>0, and B_* is the passive factor for the declared jet.
For the one-mark statement B_* is arbitrary and retained linearly.
For the finite multiple-jet statement normalize the profiles first
and assume the complete initial jet array obeys(3.1) with 0<=B_*<=1;
at the weak floor its size is at most1, so every positive-degree
product of those initial sizes is bounded by a constant times the
same size. For the original private canonical jets of TURN73,
B_* may be its established beta_0^(-K_0)<=1 factor. Restoring separate
source amplitudes uses their correct degree in each jet; unrestricted
cutoff-dependent B_*>1 is not silently treated linearly through mixed
collisions. An independent large inherited mark needs its own product
cost. All constants are fixed before varying UV depth.

For every fixed K>0 and every fixed preparatory normalization power,
there is a weak floor (and a finite constant for its bounded remainder)
such that

 A(j,m_j) [normalization cost] epsilon_j
 <= C_K B_* gamma_j^(-K) exp[-(c/2)ell_j^s].                (3.2)

Proof: by(2.2) the logarithm to be paid is bounded by
C ell_j log(2+ell_j)+K log gamma_j. Since s>1, its ratio to
ell_j^s tends to zero. At the chosen floor it is at most
(c/2)ell_j^s. Exponentiating proves(3.2).

For the ACTUAL current first-healing envelope use
P_j=p0(g_j)=A0(log g_j^(-2))^(p0_exponent), so s=p0_exponent.
The native condition p0_exponent>6r>1 suffices. This exponent is
not identified with the running source-radius exponent p in Section2.
The squared initial birth credit P_in^2 Q_in used in TURN73 to pay
original private-coordinate counts is a different reserve; no squared
CURRENT healing power is asserted here. The retained current bound
exp[-c P_j] alone proves the claimed absorption.

Thus the finite maturity delay preserves an arbitrary fixed inverse-
coupling gain and a nonzero portion of the full healing reserve.
Taking merely an arbitrary-power estimate and discarding its original
exponential reserve BEFORE the wait would obscure this conclusion:
the exp[O(ell log ell)] loss is larger than any fixed gamma power.
The complete reserve must be retained until after the wait.

4. WHAT THIS REMOVES AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

This calculation removes the quantitative objection that the native
young-R delay by itself destroys a freshly retained log-power-small
source. It applies equally to its scalar jets. It does not permit
discarding those scalars or creating a new independent raw mass at
each intermediate date.

It only covers the actual O(log ell_birth) maturity interval. It does
not pay arbitrary later bulk/relative-boundary transport or arbitrary
old-row age costs by reusing this one reserve. If a term encounters a
new large-field event or changes native type, keep the actual history
and its applicable module; (2.1) cannot be presumed unchanged.

After maturity the remaining problem is geometric and analytic:
identify the actual complete physical coefficient with an ordinary
coarse source plus a quantitatively owned boundary difference, on
the same native domains and matched constraints. Section3 of
turn76_fresh_source_typing_gauge states that exact chronological
comparison. This reserve calculation does not establish it.
