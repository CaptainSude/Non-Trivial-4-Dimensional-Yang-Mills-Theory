RESULT

A small terminal TV error is not necessary for the present bounded
original-source target. A finite reverse entropy cost, however large
but uniform in the cutoff, and one positive averaged ORIGINAL posterior
response suffice: the real source can be chosen large but fixed. The
competitor can retain the exact original conditional fine law, integrating
all fine defects with ZERO additional conditional entropy. Neither it
nor its construction has to be reflection positive or half-local; those
requirements belong to the original witness and its eventual OS use.

The actual native stability sandwich gives a corresponding finite
entropy estimate for its CURRENT RT law, with the mass of the complete
lower reference kept explicit. It does not identify the original
deterministic coarse marginal. Forcing a literal R redraw to preserve
such a marginal introduces exactly the old constrained partition ratio.
That ratio is not removed by its normalized reference or scalar vacuum.

The new concrete test of a competitor is also quantitative: the TURN92
original-Wilson microscopic-cap bound forces every bounded-entropy
competitor to give that all-fine-cap event vanishing probability. This
applies even to soft competitors which assign only a fraction of their
mass to it. A single all-small fine history cannot supply this route.

No physical response floor is proved. The entropy algebra is elementary
and is NOT reported as a new model theorem. Its use here changes the
comparison actually needed and pins the original constrained-ratio
obligation in the published native operation.

1. A FINITE-COST COMPETITOR, WITH NO SMALL-ERROR REQUIREMENT

Let mu be the actual normalized finite Wilson law and G the fixed
two-window observable of TURN92. Thus |G|<=1, EG=0, and its law is
symmetric under the stated lattice time reflection. Put M(s)=E_mu e^(sG).
For ANY Q<<mu of finite entropy D(Q||mu), Jensen gives

             log M(s)>=s E_QG-D(Q||mu), s real.        (1.1)

Indeed integrate e^(sG)/(dQ/dmu) against Q and use log Jensen; the
possibly unrepresented complement only increases M. The same statement
holds on a complete positive lift Pi with original G passive. If a
cutoff sequence admits E_QG>=d>0 and D(Q||mu)<=C<infinity uniformly,
choose s=(C+1)/d. TURN92's exact cosh bound then gives

       Var_mu G >= (e-1)/(cosh((C+1)/d)-1)>0.          (1.2)

The constant can be very small. It need not beat C perturbatively.
The original bounded-source family exists for every fixed real s;
there is no shrinking complex source radius to respect in (1.1).
An s depending without bound on the cutoff would NOT give (1.2)'s
uniform conclusion. No competitor estimate is asserted here yet.

1A. Actual fibers remove all conditional fine entropy.
Let A be any genuinely identified record of the SAME original positive
law/lift, with marginal rho, and write
 Pi(domega,dA)=rho(dA)K_A(domega),
 m(A)=E_Pi[G|A].
The fine conditional K_A includes all original configurations/histories
on that fiber, not just the Gaussian small-field branch. For gamma<<rho
and any gamma-measurable event B with p=gamma(B)>0, define

 Q_B(domega,dA)=gamma(dA|B)K_A(domega).                 (1.3)

Then exactly
 D(Q_B||Pi)=D(gamma(.|B)||rho),
 E_Q_B G=E_gamma[m|B].                                (1.4)
Taking the original omega marginal can only decrease the entropy;
the original-source expectation is unchanged. There is no fine-volume
entropy term in (1.4), because the conditional law was left EXACTLY
unchanged. Knowing or estimating that conditional law is still needed
to calculate m; its formal existence is not a response estimate.

If D(gamma||rho)<=C0, the partition identity for entropy is
 D(gamma||rho)=p D(gamma_B||rho)
             +(1-p)D(gamma_Bc||rho)-H(p),
 H(p)=-p log p-(1-p)log(1-p),
with the evident endpoint convention. Nonnegativity of the second
relative entropy therefore proves

        D(gamma_B||rho)<=(C0+H(p))/p.                  (1.5)

Thus a reference contrast set of fixed positive mass and one positive
AVERAGED old posterior suffice. Pointwise source reconstruction, small
discarded conditional variance and TV closeness are unnecessary for
this particular use. For example, g(A)>=2u on B and
E_gamma[|m-g| | B]<=u imply E_gamma[m|B]>=u. Only this one integrated
first-posterior comparison is needed; it is not supplied by gamma alone.

For comparison, if |m|>=u rho-almost everywhere on the same retained
measurable event B, binary entropy/data processing gives
 rho(B)>=exp[-(C0+H(p))/p], hence
 Var_mu G>=E_rho m^2>=u^2 exp[-(C0+H(p))/p].           (1.6)
The absolute-value version need not select a sign. To check the binary
constant, D(gamma||rho)>=-H(p)+p log(1/rho(B)); the omitted term
(1-p)log(1/(1-rho(B))) is nonnegative. These are finite-cost, not
small-cost, estimates.

2. THE EXACT CONSTRAINED PARTITION QUANTITY

For a deterministic original coarse statistic A=C_a(U), suppose its
unnormalized pushforward has density Z_a(A) relative to a declared
coarse reference volume lambda; singular parts require the equivalent
Radon--Nikodym formulation. Then
 rho_a(dA)=Z_a(A)lambda(dA)/Z_a,
 Z_a=int Z_a(A)lambda(dA),
and for a reference gamma of density g,

 D(gamma||rho_a)=log Z_a+int log g dgamma
                              -int log Z_a(A)dgamma.  (2.1)

This requires positive constrained density gamma-almost everywhere and
integrability of its negative logarithm. An upper bound on Z_a alone
does not bound the last term. All coarea, gauge, old-component masses
and actual constrained normalizers belong in Z_a(A).

If the SAME ultraviolet vacuum is extracted as
 Z_a(A)=e^(E_a)e^(-S_ref(A))r_a(A),
 gamma(dA)=e^(-S_ref(A))lambda(dA)/Z_ref,
then the vacuum cancels EXACTLY and

 D(gamma||rho_a)=log E_gamma r_a-E_gamma log r_a.       (2.2)

Thus one does not need r_a close to1, or a pointwise score bound. A
uniform bound on this arithmetic/geometric mean gap would be enough.
But the geometric-mean term is a real constrained-partition input.
A field-dependent extracted term cannot be renamed the common vacuum;
it remains inside r_a. Re-running different constrained constructions
with different scalar conventions would not prove (2.2) for the
original law.

                 [dLambda_e/dlambda_e](D_e(z)).        (4.1)

Let A_r be the event that every fine plaquette of one fixed orientation

The same applies to a complete positive lift by marginal data

Thus the finite-cost route cannot put any fixed positive fraction of
