1. FAMILIES, UNITS AND RESULTS

Use d=4, K=L^p, mu=K^-1, current counting pairings, old grade m, and
the actual effective classical action S_m. Set U(A)=exp(i mu A),
W(A)=M_L^p U(A), with literal ordinary averages. All Hessians below
are symmetric LEFT-RELATIVE Hessians, including their current/BCH terms.
The primitive moving-reference products define J(A), Cbar(A). Put

 B_s(A)=D(A)^T D(A),
 B_v(A)=S_m''(U(A))+D(A)D(A)^T,
 T(A)=B_s(A)+mu^2 J(A)J(A)^T,
 V(A)=B_v(A)+Cbar(A)^T F(A) Cbar(A),
 F(A)=aI-S_(m+p)''(W(A)),          a>=R_q+1.                (1)

At flat all four families are positive semidefinite, and T,V have a
gap comparable to mu^2. Superscript T is the analytic bilinear transpose,
equal to the real Hilbert adjoint at the real expansion point.
Let P_0 project onto one final K-block, assigning bonds by initial site.
Each block has a growing fine fiber, which is never estimated by its
dimension after an operator bound. The coarse output fiber is fixed.

The affine source means a POLYNOMIAL TEST of Taylor kernels:

 A_alpha(x)=x_nu H/K, alpha!=nu, tr(H^2)=1,
 U(tA)_alpha(x)=exp(i t x_nu H/K^2).                       (2)

It is not a nonzero unbounded input to a global small-field ball and
not a continuous magnetic-flux family on a finite torus. Infinite
native kernels and their specified periodizations are distinguished.

For E=T,V, respectively B=B_s,B_v, define the affine block coefficient
h_E,K(s)=[t^2]Tr(P_0 exp(-s E(tA))). Then, uniformly in m,K,

 integral_(0,1) |h_B,K(s)| ds/s <= C,                     (3)
 |h_E,K(s)-h_B,K(s)| <= C s/K^2, 0<s<=K^2,               (4)
 integral_(K^2,infty) |h_E,K(s)| ds/s <= C.               (5)

In particular both soft families also satisfy the short-band bound(3).
All estimates use complete smooth physical source collars and rooted
kernel sums. No claim about a separately differentiated raw mean-zero
ghost or an extensive unnormalized trace is made.

3. THE BASE SHORT-TIME BAND

At a fine diagonal root x, a commuting Cartan gauge subtracts the
constant value of the affine potential there. Its source becomes
t(z_nu-x_nu)H/K^2. Each diagonal color trace is invariant under
that gauge. This is justified first by compact truncation of its
formal jets and then by exponential moment summation.

The flat bases and their first-two background jets have uniform
exponential fine-coordinate Schur kernels. The semigroup power
series therefore has weighted norm at most C exp(Cs) for 0<=s<=1.
One degree-two insertion contributes K^-4 times a polynomial in
the joining distances, and two first-order insertions contribute
the same K^-4. The rooted kernels absorb these polynomial factors.
The ordered heat insertion formulas give

 |[t^2]tr_color(exp(-s B(tA)))_(x,x)|
                    <=C K^-4(s+s^2)exp(Cs).                (6)

There are K^4 original scalar roots, or a fixed multiple for bonds,
in the final block. Summing them cancels K^-4 and proves(3).
No gap is used in this short-time argument. The eventual proof of(4)
then transfers the same conclusion to T,V.

4. PHYSICAL SEMIGROUP JETS FOR TIMES AT MOST ONE

Write X_B=K^2 B and X_E=K^2 E. Their difference factors as

 X_E-X_B=C(A)^T F(A) C(A),
 (C,F)=(J^T,I) for the scalar,
 (C,F)=(K Cbar,aI-S_(m+p)''(W)) for the vector.             (7)

Both C and its first-two source derivatives have uniformly bounded
whole-block operator norms and finite physical reads. F has uniform
exponentially rooted coarse kernels. These claims retain the final
theta_p chain, including its second derivative.

Let H=I+K^2 Delta and nabla=K nabla_0. For either family X=X_B,X_E,
the flat shifted operator Y=I+X_0 satisfies cH<=Y<=CH. On the
sectorial contour at fixed angles +/-pi/4, closed to the left of
the positive spectrum at a fixed radius below its lower bound,
R_z=(Y-z)^-1 obeys

 ||R_z||<=C(1+|z|)^-1,
 ||nabla R_z||+||R_z nabla^T||<=C(1+|z|)^-1/2,
 ||nabla R_z nabla^T||<=C.                                (8)

These follow from the spectral bound relative to Y+|z| and energy
comparison. The previous weighted flat estimate, now relative to
H+|z|, proves(8) under small physical weights by Neumann inversion.
Restricted block kernels acquire a common exponential in their
separation. No sharp block projection is commuted through nabla.

Rescale(d). Every first or mixed-second physical vertex has form

 X_j=nabla^T Zbar_j+Zbar_j^T nabla+Ebar_j,
                  ||Zbar_j||+||Ebar_j||<=C product b_i,    (9)

with complete rooted block bounds. Each summand contains at most
ONE unbounded physical derivative. This fact, not just H-relative
boundedness, controls the following contour powers.

Differentiated resolvents contain one X_j insertion, one X_jk
insertion or two ordered first insertions. By(8)--(9), one first-
order insertion costs (1+|z|)^-3/2, while its zero-order part costs
(1+|z|)^-2. Two insertions cost at worst (1+|z|)^-2: if both
derivatives face the middle resolvent, it costs O(1) and both
external resolvents cost (1+|z|)^-1; the other placements have
two half-power legs and one full resolvent. All powers are
integrable on the contour at infinity, uniformly as u decreases
to zero. Combining weighted resolvents and rooted vertices gives

 ||delta_j exp(-uX)_xy||<=C b_j exp[-c tree(x,y,j)],
 ||delta_j delta_k exp(-uX)_xy||
                  <=C b_j b_k exp[-c tree(x,y,j,k)],
                                        0<u<=1.           (10)

The undifferentiated semigroup has the analogous endpoint decay,
by the weighted numerical-range estimate and exp(Cu) norm bound.
Source directions are complete smooth physical collars; source
norms are b_j=||A_j||_phys,1. A quadratic polynomial family with
the same X_0,X_1,X_2 suffices to justify functional differentiation;
no uniform full nonlinear operator domain is added here.

5. PENALTY REMOVAL THROUGH A FIXED COARSE OUTPUT FIBER

Decompose the affine A in(2) with a smooth physical partition into
collars j. Its direction norms grow at most C(1+|j|). The rooted
sums in(10) converge with these polynomial weights. Consequently
the first two affine semigroup jets have block norms bounded by
C(1+|x|+|y|)^b exp[-c d(x,y)], uniformly for 0<=u<=1.
The same polynomial/exponential bounds hold for C,F and their jets.

Use the exact Duhamel trace with the fine root projector retained:

 Tr_f P_0(exp(-uX_E)-exp(-uX_B))
  =-integral_0^u Tr_c[F C exp(-vX_B) P_0
                           exp(-(u-v)X_E) C^T] dv.         (11)

The right-hand side sums ALL coarse diagonal blocks. It is not a
cyclic rearrangement of an unmarked partial trace. For compact source
truncations it follows from ordinary finite-rank trace cyclicity;
the following absolute sums justify the infinite continuation.

Each degree-two term has finitely many ordered differentiations,
and both coarse endpoints are linked to block zero through(10).
The sum of their absolute trace bounds is at most

 C sum_(Y,Z)(1+|Y|+|Z|)^b
                exp[-c(|Y|+|Z|+d(Y,Z))] <= C'.            (12)

The trace is in the fixed coarse color/site or color/bond fiber.
The large fine block merely occurs as an intermediate Hilbert
space in an operator product; its dimension is not a trace cost.
Equation(12) is uniform at v=0,u, since the semigroup jets there
are the identity or zero. Integrating over length u proves

 |[t^2]Tr P_0(exp(-uX_E)-exp(-uX_B))|<=Cu, 0<u<=1.

Substitute u=s/K^2 to obtain(4), including 0<s<1.

6. UNIFORM LOCAL SCHATTEN ESTIMATE FOR THE INFRARED

For X=K^2 T or K^2 V, X_0>=cH, H=I+K^2 Delta. The elementary
flat lattice heat sum, integrated against t^2 exp(-t)/2, gives

                         Tr(P_x H^-3 P_x)<=C.             (13)

Indeed the per-final-volume heat sum is at most
C{K^4/(1+K^2 t)^2+N^-4} on a declared torus; its indicated integral
is bounded. On the infinite lattice the zero-mode term is absent.

Compression is not interchanged with taking powers. For each
eigenvector u of P_x H^-1 P_x, scalar spectral Jensen gives
<u,H^-1u>^3<=<u,H^-3u>. Summing yields

 ||P_x H^-1/2||_(S_6)^6
   =Tr[(P_x H^-1 P_x)^3]<=Tr(P_x H^-3 P_x)<=C.

Thus Schatten Holder with exponents6,infinity,6 gives

 ||P_x H^-1/2 A H^-1/2 P_y||_(S_3)<=C||A||.               (14)

This is the dimensional gain: three such localized resolvents form
a trace-class product uniformly, rather than costing the fine rank.

7. MARKED RESOLVENTS AND THE CUBIC HEAT FORMULA

Choose 0<lambda_0<c/4 and fixed 0<phi<pi/2. Let Gamma have rays
z=lambda_0+r exp(+/-i phi), enclosing the positive spectrum to
their right with the positive orientation. Close beyond the finite
spectrum first, then take the closure to infinity. For
R_z=(z-X_0)^-1, energy comparison gives

                   ||H^1/2 R_z H^1/2||<=C_Gamma.          (15)

The bound follows by sandwiching the scalar spectral bound for
X_0^1/2 R_z X_0^1/2 with the bounded energy comparison factors.
Relative physical-weight perturbation extends(15) uniformly to
W R_z W^-1, W=exp(theta f). The constants are independent of z
on Gamma, K and m.

The reviewed source vertices have both conjugated and two-sided
marked bounds. In particular, for W_j the exponential of physical
distance to the complete collar j,

 ||H^-1/2 W_j V_j W_j H^-1/2||<=C b_j,
 ||H^-1/2 W_j V_jk W_j H^-1/2||<=C b_j b_k,                (16)

and the latter holds with j replaced by k. Here V denotes source
derivatives of X, not the soft family name. The factorization(9)
proves(16) directly: commute W_j through the outer gradient, whose
commutator is bounded O(theta), and use two-sided rooted Zbar/Ebar
bounds. No fractional power of H is commuted through a weight.

Derivatives of R_z are R_z V_j R_z and, at second order,
R_z V_jk R_z+R_z V_j R_z V_k R_z+R_z V_k R_z V_j R_z.
All normalize to H^-1/2 A H^-1/2 with bounded A. For example

 W_j(R V_j R V_k R)W_j
  =(W_j R W_j^-1)(W_j V_j W_j)(W_j^-1 R W_j)
                     (W_j^-1 V_k W_j)(W_j^-1 R W_j).

Equation(16) controls the marked vertex and the conjugated bound
controls the other. Applying(14) gives S_3 bounds with a marked
exponential at j or k. The geometric mean of these two estimates
connects both sources and both endpoints. Thus, for n<=2,

 ||P_x delta_(j_1)...delta_(j_n) R_z P_y||_(S_3)
 <=C product b_j exp[-c tree(x,y,j_1,...,j_n)].             (17)

For n=0 a one-sided distance weight suffices. Bounded truncations
of the distance weights justify these identities before the limit.

Functional calculus now gives exactly

 exp(-uX)=(2/u^2)(1/(2pi i)) integral_Gamma
                                      exp(-uz)(z-X)^-3 dz. (18)

The third-order residue is u^2 exp(-u lambda)/2, fixing the sign
and coefficient. Differentiate the THREE resolvent factors through
degree two, insert block resolutions between them, and keep P_0
at the two trace ends. Schatten Holder with exponents3,3,3 and(17)
gives an absolutely summable local trace bound

 |delta_(j_1)...delta_(j_n) Tr(P_0 R_z^3 P_0)|
 <=C product b_j exp[-c' tree(0,j_1,...,j_n)].              (19)

All intermediate block sums converge after a fixed exponent loss.
The root projector is not removed or cycled away. Since
integral_Gamma |exp(-uz)||dz|<=C u^-1 exp(-lambda_0 u),
(18)--(19) imply, for u>=1,

 |delta_(j_1)...delta_(j_n) Tr(P_0 exp(-uX) P_0)|
 <=C u^-3 exp(-lambda_0 u) product b_j
                    exp[-c' tree(0,j_1,...,j_n)], n<=2.   (20)

The same absolute majorants justify the contour, source and block
sum exchanges. Affine reconstruction with b_j<=C(1+|j|) is summable,
so |[t^2]Tr(P_0 exp(-uX(tA)))|<=C exp(-c u). Substituting
u=s/K^2 and integrating du/u proves(5) for EACH soft family.

8. EXACT CONSEQUENCE AND WHAT IS STILL NEEDED

The operator work proves(3)--(5) for the explicit native families,
uniformly in old grade and K. The native affine-multiplier theorem
identifies their first-two polynomial affine jets with the actual
soft pair once its exact gauge/coarea reduction is established.

For that matched logarithmic Gaussian, if the coarse Schur factors
have uniformly bounded Maxwell coefficients as separately proved,
the integral formula for log and beta(E)=2[t^2]rho_0 give

 beta(G_(m,p))=integral_1^(K^2) h_base,m(s) ds/s+O(1),      (21)

where h_base,m is the vector-minus-two-scalar affine heat coefficient
of S_m''+DD^T and D^T D with the UNSCALED current affine probe t x_nu H.
The sign follows from log D=-integral(exp(-sD)-normalizer)ds/s:
log T-(1/2)log V has heat integrand (1/2)heat_V-heat_T;
the factor2 in beta cancels the one half.

The source in(2) scales each fine diagonal affine coefficient by
K^-4, while the original final block has K^4 roots. Gauge covariance
and translation remove the constant potential at each root, so this
census converts the base block coefficient exactly to h_base,m.
Equation(21) is conditional ONLY on the stated determinant/coarse
identification, not on an omitted endpoint heat estimate.

The nonlinear Ward/base comparison to Wilson and the fixed Wilson
large-time coefficient must still be checked before any signed drift
is claimed. No full continuum construction, mixed-history source
estimate, or interaction conclusion is obtained here. Native beta is
an infinite-lattice coefficient; a uniform varying-depth winding
