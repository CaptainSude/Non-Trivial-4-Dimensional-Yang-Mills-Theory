TURN75 -- THE MARGINAL SEQUENCE WITH FINITE-PERIOD CLASSICAL LEAKAGE
20 October 2026. Root calculation. Conditional sequence theorem.

PURPOSE AND SCOPE

TURN70 proves contraction after projecting the output neutral, and
P_out T P_in=P_out T because the chosen classical reference transports
exactly. This identity DOES NOT prove beta_out T=beta_in on all inputs.
TURN74's sequence lemma explicitly assumed the stronger identity.
Here we remove that unnecessary exactness assumption. This is a precise
extension of that conditional lemma, not a full original-law source bound.

Work at fixed q-block endpoints if that is where the actual native
same-radius source norm is available. No bounded same-radius primitive
map is assumed merely to interpolate between endpoints. Scalar vacua
are removed only from this coefficient norm, with their actual source
coefficients retained in the separate exact ledger.

1. ASSUMPTIONS AT THE ENDPOINTS

Let V_j be normed spaces with uniformly bounded beta_j:V_j->C,
S_j in V_j, beta_j S_j=1, and P_j=I-S_j beta_j. Put N_j=ker beta_j.
All constants below include the fixed norms of these objects. Suppose
the actual linear one-mark map has

 L_j S_j = S_(j+1)+B_j,
 L_j u = T_j u+D_j u,                           u in N_j,
 T_j S_j=S_(j+1),
 ||P_(j+1)T_j|N_j|| <= kappa < 1,
 ||D_j|| <= C g_j^2,
 ||B_j|| <= C g_j^2,
 |beta_(j+1)B_j| <= C[g_j^4+epsilon_j g_j^2],
 |beta_(j+1)T_j u| <= C epsilon_j ||u||.           (1.1)

The epsilons are nonnegative. They are finite-period errors, not a
declaration that every masked/raw/boundary coefficient has an epsilon
Ward defect. A native Gaussian-window birth g_j^2 tau_j can be included
in (1.1) by adding tau_j to epsilon_j, or absorbed in g_j^4 when
tau_j<=C g_j^2. All couplings, windows and unmarked bodies are frozen
when defining L_j. The birth assumption and projection contraction
have to hold for the SAME prepared source family.

Assume g_j^-2=gamma_j>=Gamma and choose Gamma so large that
r:=kappa+C/Gamma<1. If the inequality is strict with any smaller
uniform r, fix that r once. Also require the convolution bound

 sum_(i<j) r^(j-1-i)g_i^2 <= C_g g_j^2.           (1.2)

Monotone g_i<=g_j suffices. Alternatively bounded SIGNED increments
|gamma_(j+1)-gamma_j|<=D suffice when
r(1+D/Gamma)<1, by the calculation in TURN74 Section3. At q-block
endpoints D and the forcing constants already include that fixed q.

2. EXACT RECURRENCE AND PROOF

Write X_j=alpha_j S_j+u_j with u_j in N_j. Put

 A_j=P_(j+1)(T_j+D_j)|N_j,
 f_j=P_(j+1)B_j,
 b_j=beta_(j+1)B_j,
 ell_j=beta_(j+1)(T_j+D_j)|N_j.

Then the two exact equations are

 u_(j+1)=A_j u_j+alpha_j f_j,
 alpha_(j+1)=alpha_j+b_j alpha_j+ell_j u_j.        (2.1)

In particular ||A_j||<=r, ||f_j||<=Cg_j^2, and
||ell_j||<=C(epsilon_j+g_j^2). No invariant neutral subspace for T_j
has been assumed. Repeated output projection is part of A_j.

Let U_0=||u_0|| and M_j=max_(i<=j)|alpha_i|. Variation of constants,
(1.2), and (2.1) give

 ||u_j|| <= r^j U_0+C g_j^2 M_j.                 (2.2)

Substitute this bound into the alpha equation BEFORE Gronwall. With

 a_j=g_j^4+epsilon_j g_j^2,
 h_j=(g_j^2+epsilon_j)r^j,

one obtains

 M_(j+1) <= (1+C a_j)M_j+C h_j U_0.

Writing A_N=sum_(j<N)a_j and H_N=sum_(j<N)h_j yields

 M_N <= exp(C A_N)[|alpha_0|+C U_0 H_N],           (2.3)
 ||u_N|| <= r^N U_0+C g_N^2 exp(C A_N)
                                  [|alpha_0|+C U_0 H_N].

For alpha_0=1,u_0=0, summing the absolute alpha increments also gives

 |alpha_N-1| <= C exp(C A_N) A_N.                 (2.4)

This is noncircular: contraction and (1.2) precede any marginal bound.
The exact finite-period leakage appears multiplied by the small
neutral amplitude g_j^2. Deleting it would give a stronger statement
not justified by the projected intertwining identity alone.

3. ACTUAL BACKWARD PERIODS DO NOT COST N TIMES AN ENDPOINT ERROR

Let the fixed endpoint scale ratio be L>1, and suppose the periods
are Q_j=L^(N-j)Q_end. A block-output error of the form

 epsilon_j <= E exp[-c Q_(j+1)]                  (3.1)

has uniformly summable date weights. Indeed write s=N-1-j. Since
L^s>=1+s(L-1),

 sum_(j<N)epsilon_j
 <= E sum_(s>=0)exp[-c L^s Q_end]
 <= E exp[-c Q_end]/[1-exp(-c(L-1)Q_end)].        (3.2)

Choose Q_end above a fixed positive lower bound. The denominator in
(3.2) is then bounded away from zero, and

 sum_(j<N)epsilon_j g_j^2 <= C E Gamma^-1 exp[-c Q_end]. (3.3)

If also gamma_j>=G+d(N-j), G,d>0, then
sum_(j<N)g_j^4<=1/(dG). Consequently the rate in (2.3) is at most

 A_N <= 1/(dG)+C E G^-1 exp[-c Q_end].           (3.4)

The initial-transient quantity satisfies
H_N<=1/[Gamma(1-r)]+sum epsilon_j, a sufficient depth-uniform bound.

For the fine prefix j<N-m, m>=0, its period contribution obeys

 sum_(j<N-m)epsilon_j g_j^2
 <= C E [G+d(m+1)]^-1 exp[-c L^m Q_end],         (3.5)

where the denominator from the geometric bound is bounded by the same
fixed constant as in (3.2). Its g^4 contribution is at most
1/[d(G+dm)]. These are coefficient-tail statements. They do not compare
different regulated observable laws or supply a continuum limit.

4. REQUIRED NATIVE ATTACHMENT

The classical error should be established on the actual finite-period
Maxwell prescription with the reference normalization retained. The
bound expected from finite lifted Ward identities is
C_(L,J)b^-2 exp[-c_(L,J)Q_(j+1)] times the source norm. Fixed b,L,J
are absorbed in E, not silently allowed to depend on depth.

Even with that classical estimate, (1.1) still requires the actual
same-radius fixed-core quantum map and the small physical marginal
birth. A q-fold normalized kernel cannot be replaced by a selected
Gaussian law to meet that requirement. Running-domain tags, inherited
boundary/null differences, tensor/profile low modes and raw histories
need their own coupled transport. They are not members of V_j merely
because their amplitudes are small.

Finally, alpha bounded away from zero is a statement about a source
coefficient. It is NOT a reflected covariance floor, positive energy,
interaction, or a nontrivial continuum Yang--Mills theory. All exact
one/two-source scalars and the final outer conditional means remain
required in the separate full-law construction.
