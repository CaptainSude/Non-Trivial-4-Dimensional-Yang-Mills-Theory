HEREDITARY OWN-CARRIER DOMAINS: THE COMPLETE RESPONSE IMAGE
25 October 2026. Endpoint/response audit of the proposed new source class.

Scope. This gives an own-domain MAP image lemma for the unframed NEW
elliptic response on the declared scalar localizer contours. It uses a
new hereditary compact-reference domain, deliberately stronger than
the single-grade native domain. It does not prove a normalized marked
cluster column, restore its carrier exponent, or identify arbitrary
complex-gauge orbit witnesses for a normalized integral. Those remain
separate source/normalization tasks.

1. WHY THE HEREDITARY CONDITION IS NEEDED

The earlier intrinsic complete-read theorem constructs operators from
local Dirichlet factors and needs no globally regular completion. That
fact alone does not prove the nonlinear output passes every smaller-
grade source test near a finite carrier's boundary. A raw compact base
connection is copied even at zero noise. If its input gauge test is
known only on larger mother cubes, an output test cube near the boundary
may have no such mother cube inside the input carrier. The vanishing
response in a boundary cushion does not repair this missing base test.

There are two legitimate repairs. One can protect a larger parent-scale
neighborhood of the old mark, including all mother cubes, and pay its
full carrier/seed cost. Alternatively one can strengthen the domain
to retain the compact-reference tests at all lower grades. The latter
is the convention used here, as chosen by the root synthesis. It is a
NEW class, not an assertion of equivalence with Balaban's U_j^c(Y).

2. DEFINITION OF THE WITNESS CLASS

Work on the fixed original fine array, with xi_j=L^-j and the actual
native block, orientation, private-pivot and incident-stencil conventions.
A complete carrier means its recorded data include every bond,
plaquette, current stencil and endpoint/averaging collar required by
the functions being considered. No undefined values are borrowed
across a naked carrier boundary.

A regular witness for D_j(Y) is a pair (U,J) on that complete data
region together with a compact reference Wbar on the same region,
such that

 U=exp(i xi_j B)Wbar,
 max(|B|,|nabla_(j,Wbar) B|)<=b_*,    |J|<=c_*,
 xi_j^-2 |curv U|<=kappa_j<=a_core.                 (1)

The constants b_*,c_*,a_core and the compact-reference caps are fixed
strictly inside the required OLD density and NEW response domains.
The independent-current allowance is the actual smaller allowance
needed there, not a current bound inferred from curvature.

The analytic domain uses STRICT inequalities in(1); the displayed
non-strict inequalities denote its uniform bounds and smaller closed
subcaps. With Wbar fixed, the small relative-log chart is complex open,
and the actual-curvature/current inequalities are open by continuity.
Taking the union over witnesses and then their fixed complex gauge
translates preserves openness. A holomorphic choice of Wbar as a
function of U is neither assumed nor required.

The HEREDITARY reference condition is: for every r with 0<=r<=j,
Wbar satisfies the fixed compact-reference curvature and local real
gauge tests at grade r on EVERY required complete test cube contained
in Y. Include the special second-difference/Holder reference tests
when the imported old theorem requires them. The gauge on one cube
need not equal the gauge on another; these are the native existential
tests. Its complete neighborhood/stencil must be part of the data.

The reference curvature cap is fixed; it is NOT required to be
kappa_j. The extra small condition in(1) concerns the ACTUAL complex
field U. Native direct tests and the source curvature budget are
different pieces of the definition.

One may define the gauge-invariant source domain as the union of
complex gauge orbits of these witnesses. The map norm proof below is
made in a regular witness. Its covariance is algebraic. Single-valued
normalized scalar functions on overlaps of arbitrary complex-gauge
witnesses still require the separate orbit/normalization registration;
we do not infer that property from a norm bound in one witness.

For a real completed determining field with curvature cap C delta,
let Wbar be its actual terminal physical minimum. Fix mother terminal
cubes, including their native enlarged neighborhoods, covering every
required smaller-grade test cube and full collar. Their fixed size
M_ref is chosen once, not at each grade or each completion radius.

B102 Theorem1, printed279(9)--(10), provides the real gauges when

 M_ref <= R1 M1 a1/epsilon_real,  epsilon_real<=C delta. (2)

The nonwrapping geometry must accommodate those fixed charts. In the
running regime(2) holds at a uniform weak-coupling floor. Restricting
a terminal gauge gives A_r=L^(r-t)A_t, with first, special second and
Holder derivative factors L^(2(r-t)), L^(3(r-t)) and
L^((2+beta)(r-t)), respectively. Thus every lower-grade fixed reference
test follows, with no C^t loss and no two-power claim for the potential.

This verifies physical inclusion of the stronger reference class. It
does not assert that every element of the old single-grade local domain
has such a hereditary witness.

4. INTRINSIC SCALAR-WEAKENED RESPONSE ON Y

and K_s with the previously fixed factor-word convention. Repeated
scalar factors are retained. After choosing the localization block
size and then the fixed current cap against S, every weighted Neumann

Keep the local averaging and raw plaquette-force formulas on their
complete stencils. Their component locality follows from the boundary
cushion. There is no need to impose a new discontinuous output mask
on a fine derivative. If a local forcing is itself tagged, it must
be the already bounded COMPLETE covariant forcing stencil; separately
tagging uncancelled xi^-2 plaquette terms, or differentiating a new

The D and a contractions then give, on |s|<=S,

 max(|A_s|,|nabla_U A_s|,|P1_s A_s|,|Delta_U A_s|)
                                  <=C(S) delta_j,  (3)

in the normalized output-grade units, with delta_j=g_j R_j on the

map and Dtilde equation in this statement. No pivot is made into a

For the fixed-cut moment-tensor orbit lemma, prove this same estimate

epsilon>0. Replace delta_j throughout the image budget by
(1+epsilon)delta_j, or absorb this ONE fixed factor into C(S).
The source-domain/noise slack must actually permit that enlargement;

Every surviving nonlocal operator word stays within a component at

It is NOT necessary to assert that a nonlinear field has been defined

 U_out=exp(i xi_(j-1) A_s)U,
 J_out=L^-3 J+Delta_U A_s-P1_s A_s+F1(U,A_s).        (4)

All conversions in(4) are the literal adjacent-grade ones. Near a
zero-component boundary the response correction vanishes with the

The SAME Wbar restricted to H is a compact reference for U_out. It

       <=L^-2 kappa_j+C_k(S)delta_j.               (6)

The coefficient of the old curvature is exactly its scale conversion;

Therefore the following numerical conditions prove the own-source

7. THE SMALL ACTUAL CURVATURE IS ENOUGH FOR THE CAVITY ONLY WITH ITS

8. AN ESSENTIAL SCALAR-CONTOUR BUDGET

Conditions(7) must hold for the ACTUAL scalar radius needed by the
marked column. In particular the lowest output grade requires

                 C_k(S)delta_1 <= O(tau)            (8)

with indices adjusted to the convention in which the first noise is
called delta_0. Making g smaller does not automatically prove(8) if
delta_first/tau tends to a fixed nonzero ratio and C_k(S) is enlarged.
One needs a registered original cut/cavity constant margin, a strict
logarithmic exponent advantage of tau over that noise cap, or a scalar
radius whose actual C_k(S) fits the original allowance. No original
cut is reduced or cavity domain enlarged by this lemma.

There is also an elementary strictness issue with the shorthand
kappa_j approximately L^(2j)tau. Exact equality kappa_j=L^(2j)tau
has ZERO room in(6), since L^-2 kappa_j=kappa_(j-1).
Choose a budget with positive noise slack, for example

 kappa_j=L^(2j)[kappa_0
             - sum_(r=1)^j L^(-2(r-1)) C_k(S)delta_r],         (9)

for the downward j->j-1 convention used here, as long as the bracket
stays positive and kappa_j<=a_core. Then(6) is exactly budgeted.
Equivalent strict inequalities with a spare constant are preferable
when source/domain margins are also reserved. The weighted noise sum
must be checked against the ORIGINAL kappa_0 allowance.

own-H map image and the zero-component locality, including P1,
independent current, raw base copies and actual small curvature.
Physical completed-family witnesses exist by(2). There is no new
global-completion assumption and no missing fine C1 gauge-return input
in the unframed map.

Still separate are:
 - the exact OLD normalized marked-column theorem on these scalar
   contours, with component denominators and full inherited activities;
 - gauge-orbit witness consistency for its scalar analytic functions;
 - the actual contour/noise/cavity inequality(8) and a slack budget(9);
 - carrier-size/exponent restoration, all contact/scalar contributions,
   and the subsequent recursion on THIS stronger source class.

In particular no equivalence with the usual single-grade native domain
or full standard source closure is claimed. The stronger hereditary
domain and the running actual-curvature cap must be preserved by every
later consumer, not silently replaced by a larger domain.
