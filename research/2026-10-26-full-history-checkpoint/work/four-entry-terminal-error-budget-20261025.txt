1. THE FOUR-ENTRY TARGET

Let phi be a fixed nonnegative smooth function supported in the unit 4-ball,
with integral1. For 0<epsilon<=tau/1000 set

 f_0(x)=epsilon^-4 phi((x-tau e_0)/epsilon),
 f_1(x)=epsilon^-4 phi((x-2tau e_0)/epsilon).

Use exact lattice translates at each cutoff and reflect them by the actual
lattice reflection. Their limiting four reflected supports have gap at least
d0=2tau-2epsilon; small lattice rounding is absorbed by replacing d0 with,
for example, d0/2 in estimates. Let A_M=9/pi^4. The correctly normalized
Maxwell energy covariance is

 K0(f,h)=A_M integral f(x)h(y)|x-y|^-8 dx dy.

The signed four-entry difference for f_0-f_1 has the checked lower bound

 D0 >= A_M/(400 tau^8).                                    (2)

Suppose the comparison amplitude has lambda_ref>=lambda_*>0. An error of
at most

 B_ent=lambda_*^2 A_M/(3200 tau^8)                          (3)

in EACH of the four separated entries leaves a signed lower bound at least
lambda_*^2 A_M/(800 tau^8). This is a sufficient numerical criterion, not
a result of this note. One can use a regulator-dependent lambda_ref with a
common positive lower bound; a subsequential amplitude limit is separate.

For definiteness allocate B_ent/5 to each of: (i) the pre-comparison UV
contacts, (ii) the final boundary remainder, (iii) the finite-shell signed
comparison including initial matching, (iv) lattice/free/finite-period
reference errors, (v) the full-history correction. Other allocations work.
Every contribution must be assigned once. A source-zero pressure estimate
does not supply item(v).

3. PRE-COMPARISON CONTACTS: THE ORIGINAL CELL COUNT

                    exp(-c_ct d0/ell_*).                  (5)

All fixed shape, collar, source and weak-recovery constants are in C_ct.
The proof uses the convergent series

               exp[-c(d0/ell_*)L^r].
It keeps the original root census and all contact scalars. Whole enlarged

Put kappa=epsilon/tau, t=tau/ell_*, a_ct=c_ct d0/tau. Then

 tau^8 E_UV <= C_ct X_+^2 kappa^-4 t^4 exp(-a_ct t).        (6)

An explicit sufficient condition for E_UV<=B_ent/5 is as follows. Define

 t >= t_geom AND
 t >= (2/a_ct)[2 log X_+ + log C_R+C_a]_+.                (7)

t_geom includes ell_*<=epsilon and all fixed collar/gap requirements.
Compatible geometric rounding can enlarge t by at most a fixed L factor.

4. WHY A DIRECT TERMINAL SUPREMUM ESTIMATE DOES NOT FINISH THE TASK

For centered actual terminal marks m=Q+R and n=H+S, Cauchy--Schwarz gives

 |Cov(m,n)-Cov(Q,H)| <= r_f q_h+q_f r_h+r_f r_h,            (9)

where r_f=||R-E R||_2 and q_f=||Q-E Q||_2 under the ACTUAL terminal law.
For q_f,q_h<=Q_* and r_f,r_h<=R_*, the right side is 2Q_*R_*+R_*^2.
For example R_*<=min(B_ent/(15Q_*),sqrt(B_ent/15)) pays B_ent/5.
Neither these actual moments nor their Gaussian values follow from a
holomorphic source bound.

The fixed-radius neutral cone before calibration is O(e_n^C rho_core^2/X).
Multiplication by X/e_n^C leaves O(rho_core^2), not O(1/X), per cell on that
whole fixed domain. A support sum can cost ell_*^-4. On a genuinely admitted
small physical chart, zero first jets improve the calibrated neutral to
O(g^2 B^2), but uniform actual moments of B and local chart/tail control
remain necessary. The nonlinear Wilson/minimum remainder is generically
O(g B^3+g^2 B^4), not merely quartic. A profile error restricted to a running
cut is O((log X)^(2q) ell_* grad f) per cell, still with an ell_*^-4 count.
Thus taking ell_* small alone does not make a direct terminal replacement
small. Even the free energy's same-source variance is cutoff divergent;
its finite separated covariance is not an ordinary continuum L2 bound.

These observations motivate the finite extra-shell boundary reduction.

 O(X^-1/2(log X)^(q+1)),

respectively. Thus q>1/2 suffices for the moment-based second term. The

constants/exponents; it is not replaced by a new cutoff. If only a crude
