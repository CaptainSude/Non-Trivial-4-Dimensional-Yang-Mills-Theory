RESULT AND LIMIT

Large-II (1.50)--(1.65) supplies a specific change-of-read construction,
not just exponential locality of two separately defined functions. For
one already admitted native coefficient it changes the OLD background
to the NEW background, retains all boundary gauges, and localizes every
nonzero difference onto a complete connection to the actually changed
region. The same construction applies linearly to a finite source jet,
with its source roots and scalar coefficients retained. Section4 below
spells out this extension and its norm. For a fresh fixed-core forward
lift u(M_i U), the required input is its original full native-domain
norm, obtained by the already retained averaging admission; no Sobolev
bound on an arbitrary independent current and no all-arity Wiener
conversion are required.

This is a usable native boundary transport operator. It does NOT yet
prove that an old prescribed independent-(U,J) extension and a newly
chosen forward lift agree on every new read. In particular, (1.65) is
an inclusion of domains, not a statement that its image lies on the
homogeneous minimizing graph. Section6 isolates this remaining term
without evaluating any coefficient outside its actual domain.
