RESULT AND LIMIT

On the actual guarded first pre-Q1 fiber, the earlier P0 and averaged-
gauge masks are not product-convex restrictions. Nevertheless their
effect on a smooth local source can be estimated through the SAME
convex-product reference already constructed in the original-source
entry. Refining the masks into their individual finite-read tests,
using joint rare-event bounds, and expanding their finite product
gives a source comparison without the former active-volume prefactor:

 |mu_z F_p-q_z F_p| <= C alpha_p g0 epsilon0 exp(-c r^2)
                       <= C alpha_p g0^2 r exp(-c r^2).       (A)

Here alpha_p=|c_a f_p|, r=delta0/g0, epsilon0/g0 is a fixed
multiple of r, and all fixed-L constants are kept in C. The local
box still has to satisfy N exp(-c r^2)<=a fixed small constant,
where N is its number of affected elementary masks. Polynomial
buffers from the preceding entry meet this condition. Thus (A) is
not a theorem about an infinite-volume mask product at fixed coupling.

The comparison has an exact decomposition with a marked mask read j
and a bound exp[-eta dist(H_p,T_j)] exp(-c r^2). No derivative of
an indicator or regularity of its moving boundary is required. The
decomposition is a finite reference-law identity; its coefficients
still read the full conditional reference and normalizer. It is NOT
a connected polymer expansion, a newly localized analytic activity,
or a matching to an original large-field/R birth. It supplies neither
the fourth-power age gain nor the all-scale source transport theorem.

1. FINITE REFERENCE HYPOTHESES

Let I be a finite subset of the noise lattice, with fixed coordinate
block dimension m=3 and lattice metric d. Let

 nu(dx)=Z^-1 exp[-H(x)] product_(i in I)1_(|x_i|<=S) dx.

On the complete containing product and its neighborhood assume the
uniform Hessian lower bound lambda I, the weighted Hessian row and
column bounds, and weighted accretivity used in the already audited
convex-product covariance lemma. Use a sufficiently small fixed eta>0.
The same constants hold on every principal conditional restriction.
An affine force is allowed; the additional input needed here is

                    |nu x_i| <= M r+1.                       (1)

Choose R=K r with K>M+3 (enlarged for the fixed color convention).
The guarded first reference nu_z has exactly these properties, by
Sections 1--2 of local-original-source-entry. Its complete first
action includes current, Haar and private-pivot terms. The mean bound
is proved there from interpolation and the complete boundary score.

Let E_1,...,E_N be measurable elementary failure events. Each E_j
depends only on x_(T_j), where T_j is a nonempty finite active read,
with |T_j|<=v, and each noise block belongs to at most B such reads.
Retained parameters, including all supported guarded exterior values,
are fixed here. Assume the literal implication

            E_j subset union_(i in T_j){|x_i|>R}.             (2)

This is a local redundancy condition, not a differentiability
assumption on the masks. Define D=intersection_j E_j^c, and let
nu_D=nu(.|D). Empty/identically passing tests can be discarded.

2. JOINT RARITY UNDER THE NORMALIZED REFERENCE

For every real vector t supported on any coordinate set, the affine
tilt of nu has the same Hessian and convex product support. Poincare
therefore bounds the second derivative of its log moment generating
function by lambda^-1 |t|^2. Integrating twice gives

 nu exp[t dot (x-nu x)] <= exp[|t|^2/(2 lambda)].             (3)

If Q is any set of k distinct noise blocks, integrate (3) against a
standard Gaussian vector of dimension mk. For 0<s<lambda/2 this gives

 nu exp[s sum_(i in Q)|x_i-nu x_i|^2]
                  <=(1-2s/lambda)^(-mk/2).                  (4)

Take s=lambda/4. By (1), all |x_i|>R imply
|x_i-nu x_i|>a r, with a>0 fixed after K. Hence

 nu(all i in Q have |x_i|>R)
      <=2^(mk/2) exp[-lambda a^2 r^2 k/4]
      <=exp[-c_1 r^2 k]                                    (5)

at one weak floor. For a prescribed collection J of n mask labels,
choose one witnessing i in T_j for each label. There are at most
v^n choices. Each coordinate can witness at most B labels, so the
number k of distinct witnesses is at least n/B. A union bound yields

 nu(intersection_(j in J)E_j) <=rho^|J|,
            rho=v exp[-c_1 r^2/B] <1.                       (6)

All probabilities in this argument use the normalized reference.
No factor inverse to a global all-good event has been introduced.
In particular nu(D)>=1-N rho. We will use N sqrt(rho)<=1/4;
this implies nu(D)>=3/4 and keeps the finite subset sum bounded.

3. A BOUNDED-LOCAL COVARIANCE WITH THE RARITY KEPT

Let F be a C^1 source on the containing product. Write
L_i=sup |grad_i F|, and suppose L has finite support H_F.
For any event A reading only x_T, put f_T=nu(F|x_T). Exact
conditioning gives Cov_nu(F,1_A)=Cov_nu(f_T,1_A).

For j in T, differentiating the fixed-product complementary integral
gives

 grad_j f_T=nu(grad_j F|x_T)
             -Cov_(I\T)(F,grad_j H|x_T).                   (7)

The weighted conditional covariance estimate, with weight centered
at j, and the complete weighted Hessian column bound imply

 |grad_j f_T| <= L_j+C[sum_(i in I\T)
                                L_i^2 e^(-2 eta d(i,j))]^1/2.

After summing j in T and spending a fixed amount of spatial exponent,

 sup |grad_T f_T|^2
       <=C sum_i L_i^2 e^(-2 eta' d(i,T)),   0<eta'<eta.      (8)

Indeed sum_(j in T)e^(-2 eta d(i,j)) is at most a fixed lattice
shell constant times e^(-2 eta' d(i,T)). The same estimate absorbs
the direct L_j term. Apply the Poincare inequality of the FULL nu
to f_T(x_T), whose gradient is supported on T. This avoids any
unproved assertion about a marginal Hessian. Cauchy--Schwarz, with
sd(1_A)<=sqrt(nu A), now proves

 |Cov_nu(F,1_A)|
 <=C sqrt(nu A) [sum_i L_i^2 e^(-2 eta' d(i,T))]^1/2
 <=C sqrt(nu A) sum_i L_i e^(-eta' d(i,T)).                  (9)

The event may have corners, redundant inequalities, or merely
measurable boundary. Only F and the reference action were
differentiated. Approximation admits measurable events directly.
For a complex-valued source apply the real estimate to its real
and imaginary parts, changing only a fixed numerical constant.

4. EXACT FINITE MASK EXPANSION AND SOURCE LOCALIZATION

Let E_J=intersection_(j in J)E_j, with read T_J=union_(j in J)T_j.
The exact product identity and normalization give

 nu_D F-nu F
   =nu(D)^-1 sum_(empty!=J subset {1,...,N})
                      (-1)^|J| Cov_nu(F,1_(E_J)).            (10)

No log expansion is used. For each i,
e^(-eta' d(i,T_J))<=sum_(j in J)e^(-eta' d(i,T_j)).
Use (6),(9) and the elementary finite subset identity

 sum_(J containing j) (sqrt(rho))^|J|
                  =sqrt(rho)(1+sqrt(rho))^(N-1).

It follows that

 |nu_D F-nu F|
 <=[C sqrt(rho)(1+sqrt(rho))^(N-1)/nu(D)]
                   sum_j sum_i L_i e^(-eta' d(i,T_j)).       (11)

Finite overlap gives
sum_j e^(-eta' d(i,T_j))
 <=sum_j sum_(k in T_j)e^(-eta' d(i,k))<=B V_(eta'),
where V_(eta') is the fixed lattice shell sum. Consequently

 |nu_D F-nu F|
 <=C B V_(eta') ||L||_1 sqrt(rho)                           (12)

under N sqrt(rho)<=1/4. C is independent of the active volume.
If all affected tests are at distance at least d_* from H_F,
the unreduced formula (11) keeps that separation; a second strict
exponent reserve gives the usual exp(-eta'' d_*) bound with a
fixed shell constant, not N times that exponential.

There is also a literal one-mark decomposition. Order mask labels
by increasing dist(H_F,T_j), resolving ties deterministically.
Assign each nonempty J in (10) to its first member j, and call the
resulting signed sum R_j(F). Then

 nu_D F-nu F=sum_j R_j(F),
 |R_j(F)|<=C ||L||_1 sqrt(rho)
                     exp[-eta' dist(H_F,T_j)].              (13)

For the bound, dist(H_F,T_J)=dist(H_F,T_j), use the first line
of (9) or its L1 version, and sum the remaining subset choices.
The factor exp[N sqrt(rho)] is bounded as above. R_j is linear
in F and R_j(1)=0, provided the source support/ordering is fixed.
The retained full reference and nu(D) enter R_j; (13) does not
claim its physical/background read is just H_F union T_j.

7. WHAT THIS ADVANCE DOES AND DOES NOT PAY

The earlier total-variation estimate cost |I_C| because it bounded
every test by the probability of at least one failure anywhere.
For actual differentiable plaquette sources, (9)--(12) keep the
source derivative and spatial response before summing local failures.
That removes this volume prefactor despite the nonconvex earlier
hard masks. It also gives a source-zero marked-read decomposition.

The event product was expanded only on the finite containing
reference, with N sqrt(rho) small. No uniform infinite-volume
normalizer ratio, conditional rarity under arbitrary original
exteriors, or complex continuation of the hard masks is asserted.
The chosen source buffer and native grids are polynomial in r and
log(1/g0), so this explicitly displayed size condition is available.

All coefficients R_j still contain the full reference integration
and scalar normalization. Their estimate is not a tree bound joining
all the other J labels, and no such tree is inferred from a sum of
one-root distances. Thus a native rare-source/R consumer cannot be
invoked merely by calling R_j an activity. The real h^4/age gate,
arbitrary-record entry and full-source transport remain open.
