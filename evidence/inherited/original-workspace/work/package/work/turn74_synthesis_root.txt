1. THE EXACT ORIGINAL SOURCE SURVIVES THE R REPLACEMENT

e,h with e^2=h^2=0. The original density is multiplied by

                 1+eF+hH+ehFH.

Fix the entire unmarked construction, including all kernels, reference
normalizations, couplings, gates and coordinate choices. At a basic-R
branch the fresh normalized reference is a source-free factor q_u(dy).
The old source stays in its old integral:

 q_u(dy) integral rho_old(u,x) exp[eX(u,x)+hY(u,x)+ehZ(u,x)] dx.

Thus the source jets are old conditional means and covariance. They
cannot be replaced by an observable of the newly drawn reference field.
With the choice label retained, these old jets are independent of fresh
y at fixed u; this independence need not survive forgetting that label.

Assign each original plaquette factor to the first actual integration
which eliminates one of its original arguments. This is deferred
evaluation of the original product, not a new insertion. All its other
arguments and inverse-coordinate records remain. A plaquette crossing
regular and raw regions produces a joint coefficient, not a whole-private
mark. Reassigning later carrier or maturity dates never inserts it again.

For every actual positive elimination map, the four raw coefficients
give the exact logarithmic recursion

 X'=KX,              Y'=KY,
 Z'=KZ+Cov_K(X,Y).

Every old/new cross term and every scalar remains. Signed localization
or Mayer labels are algebraic representations, not positive kernels.
At the endpoint,

 Cov_original(F,H)=E_endpoint Z+Cov_endpoint(X,Y).

The existing protected native coefficient module extends to two finite
marks on its explicitly admitted typed domains. It gives finite-depth
continuation with inverse source-radius costs. It does not give a
cutoff-uniform estimate. The worst resulting majorant can grow like

2. THE NATIVE REGULAR STEP HAS A BETTER FIXED-CORE ESTIMATE

Keep the actual correlated Gaussian, its coarse constraints, retained
window and the full incoming prepared action. On the fixed native core
domain let u be a complete prepared coefficient and write its noisy
minus static value as

 Delta u(g,x)=g L_u(x)+g^2 Q_u(g,x).

L_u is odd and linear. Its and Q_u's complete carrier envelopes have
fixed-degree polynomial growth in x. The reduced exponent is V_g=g U_g,
with U_g of uniformly bounded degree-three growth in the admitted class.
The Gaussian and window are even. Interpolating exp(lambda V_g) gives
the exact identity

 <Delta u>=g^2[<Q_u>+integral_0^1 Cov_lambda(L_u,U_g) d lambda].

The native occupied-support connected expansion bounds these fixed
polynomial marks on its actual complex contours. Consequently

 ||D_E log I_g[u]|| <= C g^2 ||u||,
 ||D_E^2 log I_g[u,v]|| <= C g^2 ||u|| ||v||.

These are estimates for the quantum part. The static classical
pullback is also present. Source-independent prefactors are not
discarded; all source-dependent scalar derivatives remain in the log.

The fixed-radius qualification matters. If only a shrinking radius
a_j is controlled, the corresponding estimate is of order (g_j/a_j)^2.
An arbitrary healed or boundary coefficient cannot be assigned a fixed
radius merely because it is small. Prior moving-radius estimates help
in their compatible homogeneous class, but do not establish arbitrary
mixed-domain re-entry or cancellation of static low-mode leakage.

3. PHYSICAL EQUIPARTITION REQUIRES REMOVING GAUGE DIRECTIONS CORRECTLY

At the actual constrained minimum, let V be its pivot-free tangent,
Q the Hessian of the physical classical action restricted to that
constraint, and G the tangent of gauge transformations anchored at the
next coarse sites. Q includes the constraint-curvature/current term.
Let D linearize the soft gauge coordinates. Then

 QG=0,       Q>=0,       A=Q+D*D>0.

The anchored gauge coordinates give a square isomorphism D|G. Split
V=H direct-sum G and shift the gauge variable to remove D_H h from
the gauge square. The normalized Gaussian then satisfies

 E exp[t x^T Q x/2]=(1-t)^(-d_phys/2),
 d_phys=dim V-dim G.

The gauge-fixing quadratic form is not part of the original action
source. Sourcing the entire Gaussian energy would count the wrong
dimension. The above identity holds on the actual physical minimizing
graph, not for arbitrary independent values of the auxiliary current.

With the explicit analytic extension having zero constant-action first
noise jet, the order-g^2 coefficient off that graph is

                    A2(U,J)=1/2 Tr[C(U,J)Q(U,J)].

It has an explicit bounded rooted covariance-walk representation.
This is a specified birth representative, not an assertion that every
independent-current extension of the source has the same coefficients.
For a different extension, any nonzero first-jet terms and their
contractions must remain in its analytic formula.
Its trace is extensive; its bound is per current cell. The native

For a UNIT COUPLING-FREE constant classical action insertion, the first

For a varying profile, the first constrained source derivative generally

4. A PRECISE STABILITY CRITERION, STILL WITH A NATIVE INTERFACE TO PROVE

The useful scalar/neutral decomposition retains the off-pair order-g^2

Combine this with the quantum bounds
above and the improved physical constant-source marginal birth. Also
require sum_(i<j) r^(j-1-i) g_i^2 <= C g_j^2 for the contracted
propagator r<1. Bounded signed inverse-coupling increments and a fixed

For the pure-reference initial source, variation of constants then

be made small on the stated positive weak-coupling profile.

5. AN IMPROVED ORIGINAL-LAW COMPARISON FOR SEPARATE BARE CORES

For the actual bare Wilson conditional product, preserve the exterior
and each separately read coarse statistic. In a core replace its fiber
by its weak-window conditioning only when the rejection probability
is at most theta<1. Let alpha bound the annealed conditional rejection
on admitted boundaries. For an additive source the mean error splits
into a fiber-centered part and an exterior-dependent drift.

For canonical plaquette weights with absolute sum W_i in core i, the
full Wilson all-order energy estimate and optimized Holder bound give

 ||fiber mean error_i||_2 <= C sqrt(alpha) L_alpha W_i,
 ||exterior drift_i||_2 <= C alpha L_alpha W_i,
 L_alpha=2+log(1/alpha).

Fiber-centered errors are orthogonal conditional on the exterior.
For disjoint source core sets they cancel completely from the
covariance comparison. Only the exterior drifts remain, giving
O(alpha L_alpha W_F W_H) with the explicit quadratic drift term.
There is no exponential factor in the number of read cores.

This is a statement about the original bare Wilson law and its exact
normalizer. It is not extended to arbitrary generated kernels. It
contains no physical separation decay. With the stated canonical
physical weights W_F,W_H of order a^-4, the available superlogarithmic
alpha does not absorb a^-8. The improved comparison therefore still
does not close the physical continuum source estimate.
