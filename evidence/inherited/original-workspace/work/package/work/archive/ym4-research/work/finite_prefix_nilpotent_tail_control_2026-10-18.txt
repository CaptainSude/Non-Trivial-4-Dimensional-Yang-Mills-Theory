3. EXPLICIT FINITE-PREFIX GROWTH

 M_a,j <= C^j M_a,0,
 M_12,j <= C^j M_12,0+C^(2j) C' M_1,0 M_2,0.            (4)

The second inequality follows by summing
sum_{l<j} C^(j-l) C^(2l), a geometric sum. Thus if

No factor a_j^-p is multiplied at every regular step. If a proposed

 M_12,0 <= poly(rho,gamma) exp(C gamma-c rho).            (6)
