NEW-LEDGER PRE-INTEGRATION VALUE PREPARATION
25 October 2026. Homogeneous source-to-interaction lemma; no Gaussian step.

PURPOSE

For an explicitly declared continuation W_C^old exp[Delta_new D], this
lemma bounds the COMPLETE prepared interaction difference by the actual
current tag-catalogue norm. It removes an unspecified M_D preparation
claim from that new-ledger construction. It is not an identity with an
unverified old off-graph preparation, and not an all-history bound for
the catalogue itself.

1. INPUT AND PREPARED NORMS

Fix the original current noise lattice at grade j and its original
free-coordinate/pivot conventions. The background is at grade j+1.
Represent its cells on the same mixed read graph by their owned grade-j
cells; one background cell owns exactly L^4 grade-j cells in four
dimensions. Incidences at collars carry the declared fixed convention.
Native root measures are retained; fine-coordinate counting is not a
replacement for those measures.

Let D_j=sum_(a,H,z) F_(a,H,z), with a the actual birth/age/role index,
z the original marked/root label and H a complete CURRENT carrier.
Every source is bounded by M_(a,H,z) on its own analytic domain. Define

 B_j=sup_v sum_(H contains v) exp[a_hi d_j(H)]
                                     sum_(a,z) M_(a,H,z). (1)

The source labels and their actual calibration/amplitudes are summed
outside each coefficient supremum. Any source profile is inserted only
after this complete positive envelope is formed. There is no hidden
supremum over a combined profile which suppresses old roots or dates.
The same proof works for mature fixed-type tags as well as young ones,
provided their actual domains and catalogue norms are retained.

Assume a fixed feasible exponent allocation a_hi>a_mid>0 in the native
protected column: a_hi pays scalar-localization seed/collar/entropy costs
and leaves a_mid for the prepared interaction; the ordinary integrated
column's native compression reserve restores the declared output weight.
This is the actual B116(2.36)--(2.41) allocation with complete carrier
conventions, not a statement for arbitrary exponents or arbitrary L.
If a source was not previously given a complete carrier, its missing
collar/seed price must first be included in(1).

2. FULL VALUE IMAGE, INCLUDING STATIC COPY

Fix S0>S>1. On a common output/background domain B_out, suppose the
declared new noisy map Psi_(j,s) and its SAME zero-noise map Psi_(j,s)^0
send every complete H into that source's own domain, for |s|<=S0 and
the specified enlarged ORIGINAL complex noise product. Keep all field,
current, P1, transpose, private-pivot, gauge-return and stencil reads.
The weighted-reference all-cap image theorem is one way to prove this
hypothesis for young sources. For several ages, B_out is a common
intersection; a new birth domain must be shown to lie in that intersection.

For every coefficient put

 f_(a,H,z)(s)=F_(a,H,z)(Psi_(j,s)),
 f^0_(a,H,z)(s)=F_(a,H,z)(Psi_(j,s)^0),
 h_(a,H,z)(s)=f_(a,H,z)(s)-f^0_(a,H,z)(s).              (2)

Both terms use the same complete scalar conventions and current units.
Their bounds are M,M and 2M respectively on S0. The static term is
localized and reblocked too; it is not an old-date baseline silently
renamed as a current function. At s=1 the sum of h is exactly

        Delta_new D_j=D_j(Psi_j)-D_j(Psi_j^0).           (3)

3. THE PRE-INTEGRATION COMPILER

Protect H with its declared full collar before scalar localization.
The multivariable fundamental-theorem expansion differentiates each
active scalar once. Cauchy on the larger fixed polydisc gives

 |partial_A h_(a,H,z)|
                   <=2 (S0-S)^(-|A|) M_(a,H,z).         (4)

No field, current, noise or source-radius derivative is taken. The
image hypothesis already paid the source-domain margin. A changing
rho or kappa does not enter the denominator in(4).

The complete-word component separation removes added components which
do not connect to the protected seed. The fixed scalar tree/attachment
enumeration therefore yields a positive coefficient kernel K(Y,H),
including its TRUE exponential seed cost. At a slightly reduced spatial
rate, the native exponent allocation has the rooted-column form

 sum_(Y contains v) exp[a_mid d_j(Y)] K(Y,H)
       <=C exp[a_hi d_j(H)] sum_(w in H)exp[-mu dist(v,w)]. (5)

Polynomial attachment multiplicities in |H| are paid by the spare
input exponent before(5). All paths and finite cushions are in Y.
Formula(5) is the scalar/local-read portion of the already established
direct protected value column; it does not use a Gaussian integral,
a covariance, a pressure expansion or a replacement of the cut.

To see how it controls the norm, multiply by M_(a,H,z), sum all labels,
and exchange positive sums. The right side is bounded by
C sum_w exp[-mu dist(v,w)] B_j<=K_prep B_j. This gives a complete
current family Delta_new D_j=sum_(Y,a,z) d_(Y,a,z), with

 p_j^fine:=sup_v sum_(Y contains v)exp[a_mid d_j(Y)]
                             sum_(a,z)||d_(Y,a,z)||
                         <=K_prep B_j.                 (6)

The same argument gives separate prepared families for f and f^0.
Their static/noisy correspondence and the raw scalar values remain
exact. The coefficient assignment can be chosen once and then restricted
to overlapping read collars; no incompatible rowwise definitions are used.

If the prepared norm is instead rooted at a grade-(j+1) background cell
q, union over its L^4 OWNED grade-j cells to obtain

                p_j^coarse <=L^4 K_prep B_j.             (7)

This is the explicit lattice/root conversion. Fixed collar-incidence
constants are in K_prep. If a later column already includes this same
conversion in its K_col L^4 convention, do not charge it a second time.
The native geometric inequality compares the two carrier distances for
the final restored exponent; it is not an additional root census.

4. CONSEQUENCES FOR THE RECONSTRUCTED UNMARKED LAW

Use the new continuation

              W_(C+D)^new=W_C^old exp[Delta_new D_j].    (8)

It is defined from the declared ledger. Physical matching must hold
for every retained real noise in consistent frames, as in the companion
unmarked same-coordinate module. Old/new off-graph equality is not used.

If K_prep B_j (with whichever explicit root convention is chosen) is
below the actual old cluster spare budget, the Mayer estimate
|exp(u d)-1|<=|u|exp(|u| ||d||)||d|| and the original compiler give
uniform admission for u near[0,1]. The exact one-step identity then is

 R_j(D)=T_C^new D+N_j(D),
 N_j(D)=integral_0^1(1-u)Cov_u(Delta_new D,Delta_new D)du.

The protected connected two-mark estimate gives

                    ||N_j(D)||<=C B_j^2,                (9)

with the chosen intermediate/final exponent allocations and root

this VALUE estimate. The full linear value column bounds T_C^new D,
including the static part. It still needs its actual output domains.

The new quadratic birth can be assigned a running own-domain only after
that domain is shown contained in B_out. The growth budget often makes
older cohort domains larger, but this must be checked with the actual
running rho_0 at each date; it is not implied just by calling a tag old.

5. HOMOGENEOUS MARGINAL VALUE COST VERSUS COUPLING COST

For a COMPLETE jointly covariant homogeneous family with the actual
translation and signed-permutation/color symmetries needed by the native
scalar extractor, and with its actual normalized Wilson reference, the
projection of an ELIGIBLE regular-to-renormalize part may be controlled
without an inverse-radius loss in the resulting VALUE norm. On a source
domain with scaled actual curvature <=rho, each fine plaquette term
is <=C eta^4 rho^2 in the traceless action convention. There are O(eta^-4)
plaquettes per physical current cell, so the rooted reference value norm
is <=C rho^2. Its complete local plaquette reads are retained.

The flat physical pullback on a coarse-field ball c rho gives the actual
marginal extractor estimate |beta(F)|<=C rho^-2 ||F||. Therefore

                   ||beta(F) S||_own <=C ||F||.          (10)

The numerical delta_beta itself still carries rho^-2. The vacuum and
marginal coefficients must be filed exactly, with the signs in the
one-step module. Equation(10) neither asserts a universal profile
reference identity nor projects an arbitrary tensor/source family onto
one Wilson scalar. Unmarked f=1 by itself is insufficient: a young
partition or partial catalogue may break the current stabilizer symmetry.
The complete joint covariance/maturity and actual native filing must be
registered before scalar extraction is used. In particular B119 young R
terms receive only their prescribed vacuum renormalization, not a new
beta subtraction. Apply(10) only to the actual selected regular part
P_E F, with its true scalar extractor and reference normalization.
The right side is then C||P_E F||. Replacing it by C||F|| requires the
actual bounded filing/reblocking operator estimate; this lemma does
not assume that P_E is a norm-one selector after preparation.

6. WHAT REMAINS TO CLOSE A QUEUE

Equation(6) is a prepared-difference PRODUCER from an actual CURRENT
catalogue, not from a raw birth probability. To make B_j uniformly small
requires the real forcing, finite-age linear propagation, original
support/root costs and later mature-tag propagation. Mature tags may
remain as fixed-type D entries; that avoids changing their declared
preparation merely by relabeling them C. Absorbing one into the old-read
core needs its source-to-action interface, since a mixed old-C/new-D
continuation is initially ledger-dependent off graph.

This lemma establishes the pre-integration step and its radius-uniform
constant under the explicit image and exponent hypotheses. It does not
prove the final mixed norm or all-history renewal inequality, and it
does not enlarge the actual old density domain by fiat.
