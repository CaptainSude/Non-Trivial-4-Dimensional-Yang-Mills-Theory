FIXED REAL CUTS: COMPLEX GAUGE INVARIANCE BY MOMENT TENSORS
25 October 2026. Independent finite-dimensional lemma.

1. STATEMENT

Let E=product_(i in I) g_i be a finite product of real color spaces,
with invariant positive inner products, and K=product_i B(0,R_i).
Lebesgue measure on each ball has its original normalization. Let the
complexified compact group act on E_C by a block diagonal T_v, with
each block the corresponding complexified adjoint representation.

For each admitted background witness b let h_b(x) be holomorphic on
the product of complex balls ||x_i||<(1+epsilon)R_i, epsilon>0.
For two admitted witnesses b and b'=v.b on the same complex gauge
orbit suppose their germs at zero satisfy

             h_(b')(T_v x)=h_b(x).                 (1)

Then exactly

               integral_K h_(b')(x)dx
                        =integral_K h_b(x)dx.     (2)

No requirement says T_v K lies in h_b's holomorphy domain. No complex
change of integration variables or contour deformation is used.

The same result applies to a numerator with a covariant mark and to
its unmarked denominator. If the denominator is nonzero at admitted
witnesses, their normalized ratio is consequently well defined on
the orbit. Bounds are evaluated in any admitted witness.

2. PROOF

Write h_b=sum_(m>=0) P_(b,m), where P_(b,m) is its homogeneous Taylor
polynomial of TOTAL degree m at zero. For fixed x in K, use one scalar
lambda in h_b(lambda x). A circle |lambda|=1+epsilon/2 lies in the
holomorphy domain uniformly over x in K. Cauchy therefore gives

 sup_(x in K)|P_(b,m)(x)|<=M_b(1+epsilon/2)^(-m),

where M_b is finite on that compact enlarged product. Thus the sum of
these homogeneous polynomials converges absolutely and uniformly on
K. This suffices for termwise integration. There is no need to take
absolute values of every individual coordinate monomial.

For every polynomial P on E_C, the moment identity

            integral_K P(T_u x)dx=integral_K P(x)dx

holds for every real compact u, by the ordinary orthogonal change of
variables. For fixed polynomial degree it is a polynomial identity
in the finite matrix entries of T_u. It therefore holds for the
complexified group as well. Equivalently the odd moments vanish and
the even moments are invariant contractions of the complex bilinear
color form; products of balls give the tensor product of those moments.

Equation(1), valid as a germ on the nonempty sufficiently small
intersection of the two noise domains, implies the polynomial identity
P_(b',m)(T_v x)=P_(b,m)(x) for every m. Integrating these polynomials
and using the invariant moments proves equality at each degree.
Uniform convergence then proves(2).

The proof's M_b is used only to justify equality. It is NOT inserted
in the marked-cluster norm estimate. In particular this proof introduces
no exp(C|I|), no extra seed cost and no ambient-volume norm factor.
