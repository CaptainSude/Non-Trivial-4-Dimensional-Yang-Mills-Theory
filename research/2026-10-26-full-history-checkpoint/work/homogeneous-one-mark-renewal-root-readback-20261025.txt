RESULT

The finite Duhamel identity, scalar quotient, cocycle weight and discrete
Gronwall proof pass. This is a theorem for already admitted homogeneous
one-source families under the explicit parameter inequality, not a full
original-observable or contact theorem. In particular its conclusion
does not remove the initial ultraviolet norm or the core growth factor.

CHECKED POINTS

1. The operators preserve genuine constants as germs on the same
admitted domains, hence descend to the quotient. E=A-C annihilates
constants, so its full birth value can be bounded by the infimum over
representatives. A constant depending on a later history/exterior datum
is not a constant in that larger algebra. The exact scalar recursion
is retained; no uniform scalar-sum estimate is asserted.

2. A fresh cohort pays H_i=max(1,(rho/(c_b a_i))^nu) once. An unfinished
entry is bounded by that envelope without constructing post-horizon
steps. A fixed-radius initial source does not pay H_i. Every genuinely
new E birth does pay its own H_(j+1). The larger birth domain contains
the same marked c_b a_n class at later times, not necessarily a_n.

3. The exact product identity is
 A_[n,i)-C_[n,i)=sum_(j=i)^(n-1) C_[n,j+1) E_j A_[j,i).
Thus every feedback term contains the actual earlier source. No doubled
insertion or replacement by an unperturbed source occurs.

4. With X_j=x_j+c_*, W_(n,i)=(X_i/X_n)^p, the cocycle computation is
 (X_(j+1)/X_n)^p W_(j,i)/W_(n,i)=(X_(j+1)/X_j)^p.
Bounded increments control this adjacent ratio uniformly. Hence the
kernel is C H_(j+1) a_j^2 chi_j. No long-age power p remains in it.
The product proof of Gronwall gives exp(sum k_j), not 1/(1-sum k_j),
and needs no contraction condition sum k_j<1.

5. Since a_j=c x_j^-1/2(log x_j)^q, the rare summand is
 x_j^(-1-d)(log x_j)^r, d=s-nu/2, r=q(2-nu).
For Y>2C0 the stretch implies x_j>=x_n/2+lambda(n-j).
The decreasing integral comparison gives the stated uniform bound
 C x_n^-d(log x_n)^r when d>0. At d=0 the integral converges exactly
when r<-1. These are criteria for this envelope, not impossibility
results for better estimates. The strict convenient condition is
 kappa0>max(6,nu+4), because s=(kappa0-4)/2.

6. For independent absolute births one cannot conjugate an unknown
incoming source by W. The direct sum contains x_j^p and requires the
stronger s>p+nu/2. Confusing this with relative operator stability would
either impose an unnecessary condition or claim nonexistent uniform
ultraviolet control.

7. At finite period the error is sum H_(j+1)a_j^2 e_j(P), not sum e_j.
For each fixed finite horizon the weights are finite, so the existing
canonical diagonal period choice can meet its prescribed budget. This
does not supply any convergence rate in the horizon or a source-function
limit. The other native admission and repair budgets remain in force.

UNRESOLVED PARAMETER REGISTRATION

The algebra establishes the consequence of the inequality; it does not
show that the literal native full R'' remainder admits arbitrary kappa0
while the parameters defining nu stay fixed. The primary theorem's
quantifiers must not be reversed. A separate native response/re-filing
proof is needed if the geometry changes with kappa0. Neither this note
nor the independent derivation claims that proof has already been made.

Two-source contacts, exact accumulated scalars, selected and retained
outer R operators, mixed boundary domains, original source entry and
nontrivial continuum covariance are not covered by this scoped pass.
