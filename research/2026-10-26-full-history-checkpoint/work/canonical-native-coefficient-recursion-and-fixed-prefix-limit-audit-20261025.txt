7. ADVERSARIAL FAILURE CONDITIONS

The argument fails if any of the following is substituted for its
stated input:

* final support decay without common completed primitive rules;
* a B coefficient that secretly includes an ambient retained T(Y)
  integral while its declared carrier is only a small intersection;
* erasing a previous removed component's long read after resummation;
* a global component-name/selection rule when only local masks are
  retained, without the bounded certificate from Section2;
* a finite beta convention different from B109's canonical limit,
  silently identified with it;
* analyticity of a sharp cut inferred from continuity of a minimum;
* expansion of exact native cancellation groups into a divergent
  positive majorant of their separate algebraic summands.

In the original unresummed boundary convention and the completed
local construction audited above, none of these substitutions is
required. The local primitive closure is the actual mathematical
input. If its ordinary-T or R proof is later revised, the canonical
recursion must inherit that exact revised scope.
