 ||partial_k^r E_m(t,k)||
     <= C_r rho^-r (1+t rho^2)^r exp(-c t rho^2), r<=2.    (12)

The sharper first/second prefactors are Ct rho and

 E_m(t)-E_W(t)=-integral_0^t E_m(t-u)(B_m-B_W)E_W(u) du

and(11) give

 ||partial_k^r(E_m-E_W)(t,k)||
 <= C_r t rho^(4-r)(1+t rho^2)^r exp(-c t rho^2).          (13)

These estimates hold for EVERY t>=0, including t=0. In particular
(13) retains its factor t. No inverse of a Duhamel time or of a
spectral splitting is introduced.

For compact source directions the first vertex and mixed second

  +s^2 integral_0^1 Tr(exp(-s(1-v)B)B'[a]

  +s^2 integral_0^1 integral_k tr(E_m(s(1-v),k+p)

The normalized Brillouin measure is dk/(2pi)^4. Fixed-s exponential

No sign, half-factor or diagonal partial-trace cyclicity is hidden.
