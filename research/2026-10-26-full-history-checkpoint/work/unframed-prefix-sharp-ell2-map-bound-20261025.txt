SHARP FINITE-PREFIX MAP BOUND AFTER REAL FRAME REMOVAL
25 October 2026. Endpoint/response norm audit.

Scope. This proves the deterministic sensitivity estimate for the
UNFRAMED new response/current map. Its use for an original normalized
gauge-invariant prefix requires the separately audited exact real
noise-rotation identity, with every old density factor, original cut,
private pivot and scalar term retained. The estimate does not itself
prove that identity or justify a complex change of integration variables.

1. EXACT ZERO-NOISE MAP

Fix one compact reference Wbar on the original fine lattice, used at
EVERY grade of the finite prefix. In the common chart write

 U_(j+1)=exp(i xi_(j+1) B_(j+1))Wbar,
 U_j=exp(i xi_j B_j)Wbar,       xi_j=L^-j.

The unframed map uses the original free noise x_j directly in its
literal private-pivot input. That input is zero at x_j=0. The new
response satisfies Aresp(0;U,J)=0 IDENTICALLY for every admitted
independent pair, since all its D and a contractions have zero seed.
This remains true for every declared factor-word weakening.

The selected-current polynomial therefore has the exact zero-noise
value, not just an estimate,

 D0_j(B,J)=(L^-1 B, L^-3 J).                       (1)

The branch of the common logarithm is fixed and small. The first
component follows from xi_(j+1)/xi_j=L^-1. No minimum/current relation
is needed for (1); J is independent.

Since Wbar is fixed, its covariant first difference has the exact grade
conversion

 nabla_j(L^-1 B)=L^-2 nabla_(j+1)B.                (2)

Curvature and other admission tests are constraints on the chart, not
additional independent coordinates in this norm. The four strong
response slots are retained to bound the current correction.

2. OWNED-TAIL PACKETS GIVE BASE NORM L EXACTLY

Choose nested nonoverlapping cells: each grade-(j+1) cell has exactly
L^4 grade-j children, with the native homogeneous torus alignment.
Assign every original fine bond and every first-difference component
to the cell containing its tail. A first-difference stencil may cross
the cell boundary; its OWNERSHIP does not. Use the packet seminorm

 r_q(B,J)=max{ sup_owned |B|,
              sup_first-difference-with-owned-tail |nabla_j B|,
              sup_owned |J| }.

Use its induced counting ell2 norm, (sum_q r_q^2)^(1/2). The same
definition applies to tangent directions. If redundant jet coordinates
are used, restrict to jets of actual field directions; no independent
jet-coordinate variation is assumed.

Every child's owned set is contained in its parent. Equations(1),(2)
give r_child(D0_j v)<=L^-1 r_parent(v), with the current and gradient
slots actually smaller. Therefore

 ||D0_j v||_2^2
 <=sum_parents L^4 L^-2 r_parent(v)^2
 =L^2 ||v||_2^2,

so ||D0_j||_(2->2)<=L. No collar-overlap or partition-of-unity constant
is multiplied into this base estimate. This is the reason for using
owned tails rather than taking a new enlarged-cube norm at each grade.

3. THE ENTIRE CORRECTION IS NOISE-PROPORTIONAL

Write the actual unframed pair map as Psi_j=D0_j+R_j. With
delta_j=g_j Rnoise,j the uniform original physical noise cap, the
complete new response, covariant BCH field update, and selected-current
polynomial give, on the larger reserved chart,

 ||R_j||_field/C1/current <=C_L delta_j.            (3)

This includes the full P1=DPD* coefficient/argument branches, the local
Delta_U A and F1 stencils, and the actual private-pivot inverse and
Dtilde. Each term in the selected-current correction contains Aresp.
The pure old-current term is exactly the second component of (1).
The field correction is the covariant BCH expression with its exact
L^-1 B term subtracted. Hence no old-field O(1) term is hidden in(3).

The scalar-localized correction has the SAME zero-noise identity:
retain D0 unweighted, and use the declared complete factor words only
in the correction. Its uniform cap on the fixed scalar polydisc is
C_L(S) delta_j. Cauchy in the reserved BACKGROUND/current chart and
the complete one-mark localization lemma consequently give a kernel
for E_j=D Psi_j-D0_j with weighted row sum C_L delta_j and weighted
column sum C_L L^4 delta_j. Here the finite constants may depend on
the fixed L, localization block, scalar radius and chart reserve.

A bounded-overlap C1 partition is used ONLY to synthesize local field
directions when estimating E_j. Its constants belong to C_L in the
small correction, not to the exact base map. Marking a current factor,
an inverse word or a transpose is included in that same complete bound.
The selected independent current is never replaced by a physical
current off its graph.

Schur's inequality, with the fixed powers of L absorbed in C_L, yields

 ||E_j||_(2->2)<=C_L delta_j,
 ||D Psi_j||_(2->2)<=L+C_L delta_j.                (4)

There is no frame interpolation term in(4). If sampled frames remain
as state variables, their zero-noise interpolation has an O(1)
background derivative and the sharper proof here cannot simply be
reused for that larger state.

4. FINITE PREFIX AND PHASE-TUBE PRICE

Chain rule and 1+s<=exp(s) give

 ||D(Psi_j ... Psi_(t-1))||_(2->2)
 <=L^(t-j) exp[(C_L/L) sum_(ell=j)^(t-1)delta_ell]. (5)

The estimate is uniform over the original fixed real noise product
and over the stated common complex chart. A terminal perturbation in
h owned packets, each of norm at most rho, therefore has intermediate
packet norm at most

 L^(t-j) exp[(C_L/L)sum delta] sqrt(h)rho,          (6)

up to a fixed ONCE-only synthesis constant if the external terminal
parameters are different coordinates. Integrate the derivative along
the admitted terminal parameter segment and bootstrap against the
strict chart reserve. Conversion from Wbar coordinates to the real
intermediate input's relative chart costs another uniform ONCE-only
constant at that phase test, not a constant at every map composition.

If the old normalized density has a fixed unframed phase allowance
eps_phase in this packet norm, a sufficient terminal radius is

 rho <= c eps_phase h^-1/2 L^-t
               exp[-(C_L/L)sum delta].             (7)

Any initial completion/minimum map has its own derivative and support
price; multiply that price into(6) once. No such map is declared to
have norm1. For t=(1/2)log_L(1/g)+O(1), L^t=O(g^-1/2). Consequently
a running terminal radius g times a fixed power of log(1/g), with a
polylogarithmic number of affected packets and a polynomial-in-log
completion derivative, fits (7) for sufficiently small g. This is a
quantitative implication of (5), not a proof that a particular completed
input/source image satisfies every other phase or source condition.

5. WEIGHTED ROW VARIANT

In the common final metric dist/(M L^t), the exact base row is L^-1
times its child-parent displacement weight. That displacement is
O(L^(j+1)/(M L^t)); summed over all dates it is bounded by a geometric
series. Thus its metric cost over the whole prefix is a fixed constant,
not an unavoidable C^t. The correction retains its C_L delta_j weighted
row bound. This observation may sharpen a later normalized first-jet
estimate, but only after the actual score/covariance contribution has
also been shown to be O(delta_j) on the same chart. The present proof
only asserts the map estimate(5).

The exact real frame-removal identity, inherited density admission,
normalization, source-domain matching and intrinsic carrier gluing
remain separate requirements. No full Yang--Mills construction is
claimed by this finite-prefix sensitivity refinement.
