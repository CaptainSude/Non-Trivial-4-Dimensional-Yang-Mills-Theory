FINITE THEORY
 G = SU(2), normalized Haar measure on links. A fixed plaquette orientation
 gives U_p as an ordered nonabelian product with inverses on reversed links.
 h_p = 1 - (1/2) Tr(U_p) = (1/2)||U_p-I||_op^2 on SU(2).
 beta in exp[-beta sum h_p] is the positive Wilson inverse-temperature
 parameter. Its relation beta=C_W/g_0^2 retains the chosen normalization.
 beta(F) in the coupling notes is a Maxwell-coefficient EXTRACTOR; it is
 not the same object as the inverse-temperature parameter.
 c_a denotes the actual observable calibration. It may depend on cutoff.
 It is retained, not replaced by an invented microscopic volume factor.

SCALES AND COUPLING
 L > 1 is a fixed block factor; constants such as B3 may depend on L.
 j labels an ordinary scale, with fine mesh eta_j=L^-j in the real-prefix
 comparison. Some source notes also use xi for a particular old mesh.
 g_j is the running coupling; q_j=g_j^2 and x_j=g_j^-2 in trajectory notes.

 notes. Native epsilon_0=A0 g0(log g0^-2)^p0 and noise

 tau_j in some regular-source estimates instead denotes a Gaussian-window
 error. It is not the cavity threshold.

SOURCES, LAWS AND CONTACTS
 F_e or Q_e is an actual normalized conditional kernel with all original
 cuts, Jacobians, constraints, action and scalar normalizers retained.
 mu_(e+1) F_e = mu_e is the corresponding stage disintegration identity.
 f_(e+1)=F_e f_e is the arriving conditional mean, generally a compiled
 source rather than a fresh microscopic source with unchanged reads.
 c_e=F_e(f_e g_e)-(F_e f_e)(F_e g_e) is a covariance emission.
 h_(e+1)=F_e h_e+c_e updates an independent logarithmic contact source.
 h_e in this formula is not the plaquette action h_p above.
 s_e is a chosen baseline; F_e s_e-s_(e+1) is its exact emission.
 A source-zero operation fixes the original physical measure; sources
 are differentiated observables, not permission to change that operation.

NATIVE ANALYTIC DOMAINS
 (U,J) is an independent field/current pair. On the physical graph J
 equals the appropriate current, but holomorphic source extensions treat
 it independently. Localized current slots must use the selected map.
 The schematic selected current is theta^3 J + Delta A - P1 A + F1(U,A).
 Even at A=0 its value is theta^3 J, not J.
 D_E and related domains include direct curvature/potential/current tests,
 complete real-reference gauge data, and reconstructed tests (iv).
 A small plaquette bound alone is not a certificate for all these tests.
 s-localizers weaken complete source arguments. The action's auxiliary
 t_D response circle need not be admitted by the source and is kept apart.
 Carrier means the complete set of blocks/reads required by a coefficient.
 It is not automatically the apparent support of its initial observable.

MINIMA AND CAVITIES
 M_j is the literal recursive ordinary average through j scales.
 U_j(v) is the regular original-Wilson constrained minimum on the original
 fine grid with M_j U=v. It is not an arbitrary effective-action minimum.
 W_j=U_j(V_j) is the real-prefix minimum. Nested fibers imply gauge
 equivalence at the actual center under the stated uniqueness hypotheses.
 The ordinary-average return and background-relative-Q return are distinct
 representatives; the missing complex endpoint record cannot be dropped.
 a_p(B) is the raw marked cavity numerator divided by its original cavity
 denominator. The outer partition weight remains in the reassembled law.
 The discontinuous rare indicator stays on the real numerator contour.
 Dividing by its event probability would change the source and is excluded.

STATUS LANGUAGE
 Exact: a finite algebraic or disintegration identity at stated domains.
 Published input: an external result invoked with its actual hypotheses.
 Internally reviewed: a scoped local deduction plus an internal audit.
 Conditional: conclusions depend on explicit premises not all registered.
 Open: unavailable as a theorem input.
 Historical: preserved as provenance; later scoped corrections may govern.

A weak-coupling gain, an inverse-radius price, a root census, a carrier
