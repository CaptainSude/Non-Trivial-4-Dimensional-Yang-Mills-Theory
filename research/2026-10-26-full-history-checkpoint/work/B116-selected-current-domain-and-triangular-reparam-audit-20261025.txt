5. A USABLE ONE-STEP RESPONSE CONSEQUENCE

The displayed bounds furnish dimension-free directional Cauchy estimates

direction v and any of the genuinely proved output norms N,

 ||D_B[H_k(s,B'(B))]v||_N <=2 K_kappa g_k.

This follows by the complex line B+zv of radius epsilon1/(2g_k), using
the cap K_kappa epsilon1. Mixed fixed-order noise/weakening Cauchy

ambient dimension multiplier. They do not imply rooted spatial response

If a full original cut approaches the LIMIT of the only certified analytic

choosing the original constants with reserve and proving the same estimates
on a larger analytic cap; no real cutoff is enlarged by doing so.

6. WHAT THIS DOES NOT YET PROVE FOR G1b/G1c

Uniformity in k of a one-step theorem is distinct from stability under an
arbitrary number of already-noisy compositions. B116 treats each step with
one admitted external pair and one fluctuation B, and all incoming E^(j)
belong to its inductive analytic class. It does not prove that every marked
source/history can be represented by that class, nor that arbitrary
simultaneous weakened prefix images satisfy (3.14),(3.16), the specific
determining towers, and all old-source collars with spare margins.

Its output-domain closure suggests an alternative to composing every old
field map: rebuild marked source coefficients into a closed analytic class
after each step. The one-step domain proof is then already available for
members of that class. But arbitrary sources may lack the Ward, support,
relevance and norm structure used in the action proof, and a marked
coefficient-sum/normalization theorem remains to be supplied.

7. EXACT TRIANGULAR REAL FRAME REPARAMETRIZATION

There is a valid measure-theoretic mechanism behind the proposed change of
frames. Order free-noise blocks by descending grade. Suppose g_j is a REAL
compact-group site gauge determined by outer data and noises at grades
strictly above the block being changed. Set

 y_l(b)=Ad_(G_l(b_-)) x_l(b),

where G_l is the ordered product/restriction of those higher-grade gauges.
The map is triangular and recursively invertible. Each diagonal block is
orthogonal in the invariant Lie-algebra inner product, so its absolute
Jacobian is1. Every original centered color-ball product is preserved
exactly. Dependence of G_l on higher variables creates off-diagonal
Jacobian entries only. Dependence on x_l itself would invalidate this
argument and must be excluded in the actual construction.

Private pivot ownership is geometric and unchanged. Let R_full,R_free,
R_coarse be the adjoint actions on the complete, free and coarse tangent
fields. Covariance of the literal constraint gives

 Q_(U^g) R_full=R_coarse Q_U.

The native C_U is uniquely characterized by Q_U C_U=0 and free projection
P_free C_U=I. Since P_free R_full=R_free P_free, uniqueness gives

 C_(U^g) R_free=R_full C_U.

The private-pivot right inverse transforms in the same way by its support
and right-inverse characterization. Equivariance and uniqueness of the
nonlinear implicit solution give covariance of Dtilde and hence of the
FULL reconstruction g_k C B-h Dtilde(g_k C B). This proves the needed
coordinate matching; one must not freeze C or the nonlinear pivot.

8. GAUGE FACTORS AND CONDITIONAL COVARIANCE UNDER THAT CHANGE

The absolute soft gauge G(F)=sum[1-Re tr P(F;y,x)] is NOT invariant under
arbitrary gauges away from coarse centers. However, at an actual averaged
axial baseline W, P(W;y,x)=I. Therefore its exact value is also

 G_rel(F,W)=sum[1-Re tr(P(F;y,x) P(W;y,x)^-1)].

Under simultaneous transport of F,W the relative ratio conjugates by
g(y), so G_rel is invariant. This is an exact rewriting on the original
axial baseline, followed by a covariant coefficient continuation. The
original gauge cutoff has the same property IF it is precisely the norm
of this relative ratio minusI. No claim is made for other predicates.

Wilson differences and the simultaneous-current term transform covariantly.
Haar and nonlinear private-pivot factors are preserved by the corresponding
orthogonal coordinate transformation. Consequently the transported complete
quadratic is D_new=R_free D_old R_free^-1. Its covariance transforms the
same way; the positive Gaussian normalization is unchanged.

For an active/retained split, the block-diagonal R preserves both sets.
The principal covariance, the conditional mean and the retained vector
must ALL be transported. The conditional mean transforms by R_active;
the determinant and Schur scalar stay unchanged. This does not set any
nonzero retained noise to zero. These identities are for the transported
relative-gauge kernel, not a new absolute gauge functional substituted
without explanation.

Every arbitrary earlier/nonlocal mask or mark can always be retained as
its literal pullback through the triangular inverse map. This preserves
the original real law and all normalizers exactly, but does NOT prove
that the pullback has the required analytic source representation. Thus
the change of frames is a legitimate exact real reparametrization and
may remove large gauges from covariant analytic factors. To assert that
only small B98 returns remain in the complex proof requires an actual
factor-by-factor census, adaptedness at every grade, and common domains
for the resulting selected fields/currents and retained predicates.

No contour rotation, complex-noise substitution, mask deletion or
replacement of an original denominator is used in the real identity.
