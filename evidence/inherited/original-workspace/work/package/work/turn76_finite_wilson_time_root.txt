TURN76 -- A STRICT TIME-DEPENDENCE BENCHMARK ON A FIXED WILSON LATTICE
20 October 2026. Root.

This strengthens only the finite Wilson benchmark in
turn76_original_channel_tail.txt Section3. The graph and temporal period
stay fixed. It does not establish the missing comparison to a native
terminal density, a half-local pullback, or a continuum time family.

1. SETTING

Take the exact finite SU2 Wilson transfer operator of that note,
T=M C M on H_phys, with strictly positive temporal couplings and
M=exp(-V/2) for the real bounded gauge-invariant spatial action V.
Let f be a real bounded gauge-invariant slice function that is not
Haar-almost everywhere constant. Set A=f-E_W f, as multiplication,
and Z=Tr_phys T^N, for an even period N>=4.

The exact centered slice correlation is

 C(m)=Z^(-1) Tr_phys(T^(N-m) A T^m A),  0<=m<=N.            (1.1)

For 0<m<N it is strictly positive by the preceding finite benchmark.
We prove additionally

                C(m)>C(m+1),  0<=m<N/2.                   (1.2)

This is a finite-time statement, stronger than a positive ordinary
variance or a positive reflected norm at only one separation.

2. EXACT SPECTRAL DIFFERENCE

Let {e_i} be a complete physical eigenbasis of the positive injective
trace-class T, with eigenvalues lambda_i>0. Since A is self-adjoint,
the paired eigenbasis expansion is

 Z C(m)=sum_i lambda_i^N |A_ii|^2
   +sum_(i<j) 2(lambda_i lambda_j)^(N/2)
       cosh[(N/2-m) log(lambda_i/lambda_j)] |A_ij|^2.        (2.1)

All terms are nonnegative and the trace is finite for bounded A;
at m=0 it is Tr(T^N A^2). The countable positive sum can be handled
by finite partial sums and monotone convergence. For integer m<N/2,
each term in the difference of (2.1) at m and m+1 is nonnegative.
It is strictly positive whenever lambda_i!=lambda_j and A_ij!=0.
It remains to show that at least one such pair exists.

3. WHY NONCONSTANT MULTIPLICATION CANNOT COMMUTE WITH T

On the full kinematic Haar space extend T as T0=M C P M, where P
is normalized site-gauge averaging. Since C and M commute with P,
T0 agrees with T on H_phys and vanishes on its orthogonal complement.
Its Haar integral kernel is

 K0(U,V)=M(U) M(V) integral_gauge dg
                     product_e q_(beta_e)(U_e (V^g_e)^(-1)), (3.1)

with the inverse convention adjusted equivalently by Haar invariance.
The normalized Wilson q functions are everywhere strictly positive.
Thus K0(U,V)>0 everywhere. It is invariant separately under endpoint
site-gauge changes after the averaging, as required for T0=P T0 P.

Suppose A_ij=0 whenever lambda_i!=lambda_j. Then the bounded A
commutes with T on H_phys. Because f is gauge invariant, A commutes
with P and preserves both H_phys and its orthogonal complement.
Consequently [A,T0]=0 on the full Haar space. Its Hilbert-Schmidt
kernel is exactly

                   [f(U)-f(V)] K0(U,V).                   (3.2)

The zero operator has zero kernel almost everywhere. Strict positivity
of K0 implies f(U)=f(V) for Haar-almost every pair (U,V), so f is
Haar-almost everywhere constant, a contradiction. This proves existence
of the strict pair in Section2 and hence (1.2).

No quantitative lower bound on the eigenvalues, no spectral gap uniform
in the graph, and no differential Hamiltonian approximation is used.

4. FIXED COARSE PHYSICAL-SEPARATION TARGET

For N>=8, the two reflected slice separations m=2 and m=4 obey

                        C(2)-C(4)>0.                      (4.1)

They are obtained by placing the positive observable at times1 and2
relative to reflection in time0. Each lies strictly between the two
fixed slices0 and N/2. The second separation can equal N/2 when N=8;
the strict comparison still follows from (1.2).

On any fixed compact set of strictly positive ordinary Wilson
couplings, both C(4) and C(2)-C(4) are continuous and strictly positive.
They therefore have positive minima on that set. Continuity follows
directly from the finite compact-group integrals with positive
denominators. This remains a fixed graph and fixed period conclusion.

This supplies a benchmark for both positive reflected size and actual
time dependence if a future comparison controls the same translated
observable family at both separations. A separate forward filter at
each time need not be a translation of the same original observable.
Its own filter means, supports and native kernels may change; the
positive difference in (4.1) cannot be transferred without checking
that coherence and the two comparison errors.

In particular this note does not rule out a zero-energy continuum
limit of an as-yet-uncontrolled family, and does not supply any missing
continuum reconstruction or Yang--Mills identification.
