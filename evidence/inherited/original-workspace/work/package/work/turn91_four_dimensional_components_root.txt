1. PRECISE CONDITIONAL INPUT AND CONCLUSION

Let lambda be a translation-invariant probability law on S'(R^4;V),
V a finite-dimensional real orthogonal O(4) representation. Invariance
of lambda under O(4) is NOT assumed. The representation only defines
the geometric action on fields and tests. Assume reflection invariance
and reflection positivity on the full positive-half-space observable
algebra at the following SIXTEEN mirrors:
 x_j=0, 1<=j<=4;
 x_j+x_k=0 and x_j-x_k=0, 1<=j<k<=4.
RP for finite sums of compact-test characters suffices by L2 density.
Translation invariance then supplies each translated mirror.

Write U(a) for the unitary translation action in L2(lambda), I for its
invariant sigma-algebra, and P=E_lambda[.|I]. Then:
 (a) <A,U(a)B> -> <A,PB> as |a|->infinity, for all A,B in L2(lambda).
 (b) Almost every translation-ergodic component lambda_xi inherits
     all sixteen reflection invariances and RP forms, at every real
     height, and has ordinary clustering in all translation directions.
 (c) Its coordinate OS Hamiltonians have only the vacuum at zero energy.
 (d) A positive bounded time-difference reflected norm in the parent
     survives in a positive-measure set of these components, with the
     explicit quantitative estimate in Section5.

This is qualitative clustering and a zero-energy statement. It is not
a positive spectral gap, not full O(4) invariance, not nonabelian
interaction, and not existence of the input continuum law. The finite
mirror group is the hypercubic group, not the full orthogonal group.

2. RECONSTRUCTION AND THE TWO-DIRECTION SPECTRAL ARGUMENT

For a coordinate j, the ordinary RP quotient of positive-half-space
L2 observables gives the contraction semigroup exp(-t H_j), H_j>=0.
Transverse translations give a strongly continuous commuting unitary
group with generators P_i, i!=j. Strong continuity follows first for
compact-test characters from samplewise distributional continuity and
dominated convergence, then for all L2 by density. The contraction
construction is the same stationary RP argument used in TURN88.
The quotient norm is at most the original L2 norm.

The following calculation uses only coordinates i,j. All other two
coordinates are spectators, so no three-dimensional step is being
silently reused. For an observable A localized in
 B={0<x_j<D, |x_i|<D}, set psi=[A]_j. Let rho be the positive finite
joint spectral measure of (H_j,P_i) in psi, and z=E-i p_i.
Here R_j is the linear reflection action on observables and
Theta_j A=conjugate(R_j A) is its antilinear version.
For a,c>=0 the correlation
 K(a,c)=E[(Theta_j A) U(2D e_j+a(e_j+e_i)+c(e_j-e_i)) A]
has representation
 K(a,c)=int exp(-2DE) exp(-az-c conjugate(z)) rho(dE,dp_i).

RP at the diagonal normal (e_j+e_i)/sqrt2 gives a second quotient.
Translate both observables by -y, y=D(e_j+e_i)/2. The corresponding
two positive-side observables are
 A_1=R_+ U(-y) R_j A, A_2=U(2D e_j-y)A.
Their supports lie in the required diagonal half-space, and their L2
norms equal ||A||2. The displacement in K is positive time a along
e_j+e_i and transverse translation c along e_j-e_i. Reconstruction
therefore gives a complex measure rho' with E'>=0 and total variation
at most ||A||2^2 such that
 K(a,c)=int exp(-a E'+i c p') rho'(dE',dp').
Here sqrt2 has been absorbed into the spectral variables. Changing
coordinates to reconstruct this diagonal quotient does not assume
rotation invariance of the original law.

For every h in L1(0,infinity), integrating against h(a)conjugate(h(c))
and comparing the two formulas yields
 int exp(-2DE)|h_tilde(E-i p_i)|^2 rho
 <= ||A||2^2 sup_(u>=0)|h_tilde(u)| sup_(v real)|h_tilde(iv)|. (2.1)
The left side is nonnegative. Absolute integrability follows from the
finite spectral measures and the L1 hypothesis on h.

For s=+1 or -1 and integer m>=1 take
 h_tilde_m(z)=exp(-m) z^(1-4ism/pi)/(1+z)^3, Re z>=0,
with the principal power and value0 at z=0. Its two axis suprema are
at most exp(-m) and exp(m). It is genuinely the Laplace transform of
an L1 function: with nu=1-4ism/pi, factor
 z^nu/(1+z)^3=[1-(1+z)^-1]^nu (1+z)^-(3-nu).
The binomial coefficients of the first factor are absolutely summable
(their magnitude is O_m(k^-2)); each term is a finite-measure Laplace
transform. The second factor is the transform of
 a^(2-nu)exp(-a)/Gamma(3-nu), which is integrable. Convolution proves
the assertion, including the boundary values. No uniform-in-m L1 norm
is required for (2.1).

On s arg(z)>=pi/4+epsilon, epsilon>0, |h_tilde_m(z)|^2 is at least
 exp(8 epsilon m/pi)|z|^2/|1+z|^6.
Equation(2.1) forces rho of this sector to vanish. Both signs and
countably many epsilon yield E>=|p_i|. Compact-test character classes
localized in such strips are dense in the quotient. Thus
 H_j>=|P_i| for every i!=j.                              (2.2)
This is a coordinate cone: in four dimensions it also gives
H_j>=|P_transverse|/sqrt3. It does NOT give the Euclidean-norm cone
H_j>=|P_transverse| without further arguments.

3. FROM THE CONE TO ALL-DIRECTION RELATIVE CLUSTERING

Let P_j be the projection in the ORIGINAL L2(lambda) onto observables
fixed by U(t e_j), all t. Any such observable is measurable in either
j half-space: approximate it by compact-test character sums and shift
each approximant far into that half-space. Its approximation error is
unchanged because the limiting observable is invariant in direction j.

Reflection R_j restricted to ran P_j is a self-adjoint involution.
Its quadratic form is nonnegative by RP and half-space measurability,
so it equals the identity there. The quotient map is consequently an
isometry on ran P_j. Averaging the reconstruction identity in t>=0
shows that this isometry maps ran P_j ONTO ker H_j, with
 1_{0}(H_j)[B]_j=[P_j B]_j.
The range is closed, and contains the dense averaged quotient classes.

By(2.2), every transverse momentum vanishes on ker H_j. If A is in
ran P_j, [U(y)A-A]_j=0 for y perpendicular to e_j. This difference is
again in ran P_j, where the quotient is isometric, so U(y)A=A. Hence
 P_1=P_2=P_3=P_4=P.                                    (3.1)

For A',B on the positive side, spectral convergence gives
 |<[A']_j,V_j(y)exp(-tH_j)(1-1_0(H_j))[B]_j>|
 <=||[A']_j|| ||exp(-tH_j)(1-1_0(H_j))[B]_j|| ->0,
uniformly in ALL transverse y. Moving local supports into opposite
half-spaces and then using their L2 density extends this statement to
arbitrary A,B; a fixed support shift only replaces t by t-O(1).
The adjoint relation supplies negative t. Thus
 lim_(|t|->infinity) sup_(y perpendicular e_j)
 |<A,U(t e_j+y)(1-P)B>|=0.
For |a|->infinity at least one of four coordinates diverges along a
further subsequence. This finite coordinate argument proves(a).

4. DISINTEGRATION AND REFLECTION POSITIVITY

The evaluation sigma-algebra on S' with finite component space is
standard Borel; a countable dense set of compact tests determines it.
The continuous translation action admits its ordinary ergodic
disintegration lambda=int lambda_xi nu(dxi).

For each of the sixteen mirror normals, every bounded fully invariant
observable has a representative in its positive half-space, by the
same support-shifting argument. On that invariant subspace the
reflection is a positive involution, hence is the identity. For any
nonnegative bounded invariant weight h and positive-side A,
 E_lambda[h conjugate(R A) A]
 =E_lambda[conjugate(R(sqrt(h)A)) sqrt(h)A]>=0.          (4.1)
Disintegration gives the conditional RP form almost everywhere. Test
invariance against invariant weights similarly to obtain reflection
invariance of the conditional laws.

A countable determining character algebra, rational coefficients,
finite mirror family and rational heights put these assertions on one
common full-measure set of components. L2 density, distributional
continuity, component translation invariance and real-height limits
extend them to the stated full algebras and every height. Applying
Sections2--3 within each component yields ordinary clustering and
ker H_j=C Omega_j, because its invariant sigma-algebra is trivial.
No spectral gap is inferred from strong semigroup convergence.

5. A FILTERED WITNESS SURVIVES COMPONENT SELECTION

Suppose in addition that B is a bounded observable in a coordinate
positive half-space and s>0 translates farther into that half-space.
Put F=B-U(s e_j)B. Assume the ACTUAL parent reflected norm satisfies
 r:=E_lambda[conjugate(R_j F)F]>0.                      (5.1)
This hypothesis is exactly the kind of original-law lower bound that
our current construction has NOT established. It is not a consequence
of the preceding upper bounds or component theorem.

For almost every component, stationarity gives E_xi F=0 and RP gives
 r_xi:=E_xi[conjugate(R_j F)F]>=0.
Let C=||F||_infinity^2. Reflection invariance and Cauchy--Schwarz give
 r_xi<=E_xi|F|^2<=C, and disintegration gives int r_xi dnu=r.
In particular r<=C and
 nu{xi:r_xi>=r/2}>=r/(2C-r)>0.                         (5.2)
Indeed, if that set has probability p, r<=r/2+(C-r/2)p.
The weaker bound p>=r/(2C) is also available without optimizing.

For every selected component its OS vector is
 [F]_j=(1-exp(-sH_j))[B]_j.
It is orthogonal to the ENTIRE zero-energy subspace and has squared
norm at least r/2. Thus component selection does not erase this
positive-energy witness. It does not establish any connected fourth
cumulant or nonabelian interaction. Such properties need their own
componentwise argument; cumulants do not generally disintegrate linearly.

6. ACTUAL STATUS OF APPLICATION TO OUR YM4 ATTEMPT

The current bounded spatial-flow scalar sector has an established
time-axis RP limit. Its spatial flow reads across other reflection
half-spaces. We have not constructed a common local field law with
the sixteen RP hypotheses above, nor proved that this sector is a
measurable observable of such a law with the necessary locality.
Therefore this theorem is presently a verified conditional structural
module, not a theorem about our existing scalar limit's clustering.

If a suitable full continuum law and a positive filtered norm are
eventually constructed, a clustering ergodic component with that
witness can be selected without separately proving a uniform mass gap.
The construction, lower bound, O(4) covariance, physical scale/coupling
identification and nonabelian interaction remain separate obligations.
The full nontrivial Yang--Mills existence goal remains incomplete.
