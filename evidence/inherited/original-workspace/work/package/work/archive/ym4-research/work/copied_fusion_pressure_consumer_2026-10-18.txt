COPIED FUSION: THE EXACT NORMALIZED PRESSURE CONSUMER
18 October 2026. Conditional consumer theorem and independent audit.

This proves the pressure/reflection implication from stated input budgets.
It does not prove post-midpoint invariance of those budgets. In particular
it does not resolve the original-pair/static-representative question in
copy_weighted_variable_radius_feedback Section7.

1. PRECISE INPUTS: ONE GLOBAL ALGEBRA BOUND AND THE VACUUM LEDGER

Fix the bare cutoff and a finite periodic Wilson torus T. Its unmarked
normalized expectation is mu_T and its partition function is Z_T(0).
A copy is an ACTUAL local polynomial F_a in scalar Wilson observables,
including its finite fusion coefficients, all lower Appell terms and
its identity coefficient. Copies may be translated, reflected and
complex conjugated. Their complete bond supports, including plaquette
stencils, are separated by the prescribed physical padding tau. All
geometry and frozen RG maps are those of the same source-free run.

For a finite copy set I use eta_a^2=0 and the scalar coefficient norm

 |P|_t = sum_(S subset I) |P_S| product_(a in S)t_a.                  (1)

The local input norm has the label sum INSIDE the rooted spatial sum,
as in copy_weighted_history_input_audit (4a). This local convention is
what may make the following constants independent of |I|. It is not
replaced by a sum of the global norms of all separate copies.

Assume the exact finite-volume identity

 mu_T product_a(1+eta_a F_a) = exp(U_T(eta)) H_T(eta),                 (2)
 U_T(0)=0, H_T(0)=1.

Here U_T is the sum of the UNIVERSAL marked vacuum coefficients at
their actual birth dates. Every history-dependent constant remains
inside its chronological expression and hence inside H_T. No division
by T(X)1 is made. Setting labels to zero in (2), including in U_T,
must give the same construction for the corresponding subfamily.
Thus u_a=[eta_a]U_T depends on that one copy, not on which other copies
were included in I. This restriction consistency is indispensable.

The conditional producer outputs needed by this consumer are:

 (P) |H_T|_t <= exp(C_p V_T),                                        (3)

where V_T is the number of terminal-lattice cells. It suffices to have
the unnormalized algebra upper bound exp(C_+ V_T), together with the
SAME unmarked Z_T(0)>=exp(-C_- V_T); then C_p=C_++C_-. There is no
local relative-boundary partition function in this hypothesis.

 (V) |U_T - sum_a eta_a u_a|_t <= C_v |I|.                           (4)

The constants are uniform in UV depth, torus volume, number of copies
and their allowed phases/orientations, once the common input budgets
are satisfied. A bound C_v|I|+C'_v V_T would work equally well. In (4)
the entire weighted coefficient sum is bounded BEFORE extraction.
A fixed-order estimate with an unspecified C_|I| does not suffice.

Finally the literal universal construction is covariant under the
translations and face reflections of a fixed scaffold-compatible
chessboard cell. A cell of fixed terminal volume v_0 can contain any
single permitted copy with a fixed buffer. Its side is a common
multiple of all causal past partition periods. The source period
bound makes v_0 independent of UV depth at a fixed terminal weak band.
It can depend on that band, the fixed compact support and stencil.
No covariance under arbitrary bare translations of one UV subtraction
is required.

Copies in full disseminations must remain in the input class. Buffers
on the complete read supports ensure separation from reflected copies.
The weights t_a are reflected with the copies and can depend on their
type, but not on the clone number. These are explicit hypotheses of
the pressure input, not consequences of the word 'small'.

2. A SINGLETON RESIDUAL MEAN IS CONTROLLED WITHOUT A LOCAL RATIO

Put D=F-u, where u is the singleton coefficient in (2) for F on T.
Choose one of the compatible cells just described. Disseminate D to
all B cells of T using the actual chessboard reflections. Compatible
covariance and restriction consistency imply that its clones are
exactly F_b-u_b, with u_b=[eta_b]U_T for the disseminated family.
Conjugate reflections act on both F and u. Therefore, exactly,

 mu_T product_(b=1)^B(1+eta_b D_b)
       = exp(U_mix) H_T,     U_mix=U_T-sum_b eta_b u_b.              (5)

Submultiplicativity of (1), (3) and (4) give

 |mu_T product_b D_b| <= t^(-B) exp(C_p V_T+C_v B).                 (6)

The full reflected product defining the chessboard seminorm is
nonnegative by reflection positivity. Its B-th root and generalized
chessboard Holder applied to D in one cell and 1 elsewhere yield

 |mu_T F-u| <= ||D||_CB <= t^-1 exp(C_p v_0+C_v).                   (7)

Thus C_0=exp(C_p v_0+C_v) is a uniform singleton residual-mean bound.
It is a statement about the complete scalar expectation, not about
the value of a chronological operator on 1. The argument does not
need a zero-free disc or a logarithm of H_T. Equivalently an already
proved compatible fixed-cell holomorphic H bound and H(0)=1 can
supply (7) by Cauchy, but that extra local bound is unnecessary here.

An arbitrary-phase copy is enclosed in a suitable compatible cell
with the same bounded side and buffer. Its reflected clones preserve
the same scaffold. Hence (7) is uniform in phase. Using arbitrary
bare-cell reflection directly on F-u before this step would be wrong:
u need not have that covariance.

3. EXACT FULL CENTERING AND ARBITRARY BARE-CELL ARRAYS

Set m_a=mu_T F_a, F_a^c=F_a-m_a and d_a=u_a-m_a. Nilpotence gives

 mu_T product_a(1+eta_a F_a^c)
    = exp(U_mix + sum_a eta_a d_a) H_T.                             (8)

By (7), t_a|d_a|<=C_0. Therefore

 |mu_T product_a(1+eta_a F_a^c)|_t
       <= exp(C_p V_T+(C_v+C_0)|I|).                               (9)

For any I, extraction of its top coefficient costs exactly
product_a t_a^-1. There is no factorial, Bell number or new inverse
radius for each internal scalar label of a complete copy. The latter
losses, if present, must already be in the admitted copy weights.

The centered ACTUAL polynomial transforms under every bare lattice
isometry of mu_T, including reflected complex conjugation. Consequently
(8)--(9) can now be used on an arbitrary bare-site cell grid. Its u_a
are evaluated for their own phases on the same scaffold; their separate
covariance under that grid is neither asserted nor needed. This is the
point of taking the exact finite-T mean before the bare-grid argument.

In particular replacing full centering by only a universal singleton
subtraction on a noncompatible grid is not justified. Nor can a live
history-dependent scalar be absorbed into U_mix to help (4).

4. THE ARRAY CELL, VOLUME DIVISION AND ENDPOINT FACTORS

Place m copies in a time chain, each in a slab with its fixed compact
transverse support, with the prescribed empty gaps. Choose the array
cell C to contain the complete chain, with its first and last supports
strictly inside its time faces. Its spatial cross-section depends only
on the copy test support/stencil, never on an endpoint vector. For
0<tau<=1 the array cell can have

                         v_C <= C_K m,                             (10)

in terminal units. All faces pass through bare sites, and rounding is
uniform for a bare mesh sufficiently smaller than the fixed padding.
The reflected full dissemination of C still consists of separated
copies. If T has B such cells, it contains mB copy labels and
V_T=B v_C. Put W=product_(a=1)^m F_a^c in C. Apply (9) to its complete
dissemination and extract its mB labels. The B-th root gives

 ||W||_CB <= exp[C_p v_C+(C_v+C_0)m] product_(a=1)^m t_a^-1
           <= product_(a=1)^m(C_K' t_a^-1).                         (11)

This is the exact 'divide volume by number of cells' step. One must
disseminate the actual array cell across the full torus BEFORE taking
the root. An ambient bound for one isolated local insertion, without
that reflection step, would still contain the ambient volume.

Let A_left,A_right be fixed-UV polynomial endpoint words, whose full
bond supports lie strictly before and after the array time faces.
Expand them into finite sums of link-matrix-entry monomials. Assign
each bond factor to a closed cell containing its endpoints; bonds on
faces are allowed. None is assigned to the array cell because of the
time separation. Site-plane Wilson RP holds for these functions even
when a separate bond factor is not gauge invariant. Its generalized
chessboard inequality gives each endpoint cell factor a seminorm at
most its sup norm. The finite expansion therefore yields

 |mu_T[A_left product_a F_a^c A_right]|
       <= C_(bare,A_left,A_right) product_a(C_K' t_a^-1).            (12)

The endpoint prefactor is independent of m and total volume. It may
depend on the fixed bare regulator and the endpoint coefficients.
Enlarging the transverse array cell to contain the endpoints would
instead give exp(C_A m); this invalid shortcut is not used.

Complex arrays are kept intact. No absolute value is moved inside
their expectation and no complex source is replaced by its real part.
Reflected conjugate copies are separate formal labels until their
actual values are inserted. The argument uses ordinary Wilson RP,
not positivity of a sourced/modified RG density.

5. ORDER OF LIMITS, PHASES AND THE SLAB CONSEQUENCE

At each fixed bare regulator choose ONE cubic torus sequence with free
integer side factors divisible by 2k! and the fixed RG period. Every
fixed finite bare-cell grid then divides every sufficiently late torus
with even cell counts. Take a joint thermodynamic subsequence for the
state and the finitely/local-countably many required coefficients.
The same state serves every finite m. Formula (12) passes to that
state. Exact finite-volume means pass with the actual local polynomial
expectations; no identification of separately scaffold-dependent U,H
under arbitrary translations is made.

Only AFTER this thermodynamic limit perform arbitrarily many reflection
doublings. If a_r is the norm after 2^r copies and b the norm of the
endpoint vector, RP gives a_r^2<=b a_(r+1). Formula (12) bounds a_r^2
by a fixed endpoint prefactor times the per-copy rate to power 2^(r+1).
The 2^(-r) root removes that prefactor. The resulting padded slab
operator satisfies

                    ||D_tau(F^c)|| <= C_K' t^-1.                   (13)

Null vectors descend by the same RP inequality. Afterwards one may
take the bare-continuum limit on the already identified ordered scalar
core and extend by (13). Thus the bound is not obtained by assuming a
continuum probability field or by comparing normalized T components.

If variable-copy feedback supplies

          t_(a,N)^-1 <= C_Gamma epsilon_a G^B p_s(f_a),             (14)

with one fixed finite test seminorm p_s, (12)--(13) give precisely that
per-copy loss. Normalize p_s(f_a)=1 before the input proof and restore
it by exact multilinearity. Constants must be uniform on that unit
ball. Arbitrary fixed-f constants do not establish distribution order.
Likewise C_K' must be uniform once the early separation threshold
G>=A[1+log_+(1/tau)] holds, or have an explicitly fixed polynomial
padding loss. A hidden exp(exp(1/tau)) is not harmless.

Coordinate slabs need only coordinate RP. A tilted-frame use requires
RP of the SAME limiting state in that frame plus an appropriate array
placement. The already audited diagonal_chain_coordinate_chessboard
construction supplies the pressure for its packet geometry using
coordinate bare cells; it does not introduce a tilted source theorem.

6. WHAT THIS CLOSES, AND WHAT REMAINS IN THE PRODUCER

Given (P),(V), literal restriction/covariance and the admitted full
disseminations, the normalized array consumer is proved. It does not
require a normalized local large-field probability, a T/T1 score,
local relative-Z stability, all-order Gaussian cumulant constants,
or an additional same-phase assertion for arbitrary bare grids.

The scalar vacuum budget (V) is a real part of the producer theorem.
Early mixed universal births must keep their complete connecting tree
and pay the scalar fine-root/engineering factor r^-4; scalar identity
coefficients do not inherit neutral h^4. Late mixed births require the
current copy-weighted local norm/density estimate. Nonempty labelled
universal births must be entered only at their actual birth dates.
If their rooted connected sum is bounded by C per copy, summing roots
gives (4) immediately, without a copy-order combinatorial loss.

Likewise (P) concerns the FULL nilpotent coefficient norm after all
universal marked vacuum coefficients have been removed, divided by
the original Z_T(0). A scalar modulus estimate for a different family,
or bounds merely on T_H1, are not replacements. The original absolute
operator/Mayer majorants lift to this norm only when the literal
bulk and chronological inputs satisfy their original budgets on every
date and every test function. That post-midpoint producer-class issue
remains separate. No pressure-consumer gap is hidden by it here.
