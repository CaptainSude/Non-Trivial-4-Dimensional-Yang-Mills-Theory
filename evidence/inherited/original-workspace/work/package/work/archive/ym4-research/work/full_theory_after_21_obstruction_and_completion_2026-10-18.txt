0. DEFINITE ANSWER

Even granting the copied producer, the heat/domain/locality bridges,
and a nondegenerate 21-dimensional quadratic-curvature Gram matrix,
a complete SU(2) Yang--Mills existence theorem does not yet follow.
There are two mathematically different missing conclusions:

 (a) continuous Lorentz/tensor covariance of the new local fields;
 (b) construction and Yang--Mills short-distance identification of the
     higher gauge-invariant local operator hierarchy.

The scalar Hilbert-space route DOES remove a possible unnecessary
requirement: after actual common-domain operator estimates are proved,
one need not reconstruct a new Hilbert space from an independently
controlled all-order Euclidean hierarchy for every new label. Section 4
below gives the precise sufficient completion theorem and its proof.
It does not remove (a), (b), or the actual source-admission gap.

The official target is the local-polynomial hierarchy, its axioms,
and Yang--Mills ultraviolet behavior, not just the existence of a
non-Gaussian scalar field. The official statement also asks for every
compact simple group and a mass gap; the present audit isolates only
SU(2) existence. See Jaffe--Witten, Section 4 and its footnote:
https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/yangmills.pdf

1. AN EXACT ALGEBRAIC OBSTRUCTION ALREADY AT DIMENSION SIX

Use Hermitian generators t_a=sigma_a/2 of su(2), so
tr(t_a t_b)=delta_ab/2 and [t_a,t_b]=i epsilon_abc t_c.
At a spacetime point write the six curvature components as

                    F_A=x_A^a t_a, A=1,...,6.

Adjoint gauge transformations act by SO(3) on the six real vectors
x_A. The 21 proposed quadratic fields have classical symbols

                    Q_AB=tr(F_A F_B)=x_A dot x_B/2.       (1)

These determine the color Gram matrix. They determine the vectors up
to O(3), not up to SO(3). More precisely, equality of Gram matrices
defines an isometry between the two vector spans; extend it to an
orthogonal transformation of R^3. If the span has rank three, the
orientation of this transformation need not be positive.

For example, take x_1=e_1,x_2=e_2,x_3=e_3 and x_4=x_5=x_6=0, and
replace x_3 by -e_3. All 21 quantities (1) are identical, but no SO(3)
map makes this replacement while fixing e_1,e_2. The local invariant

 C_ABC=-i tr(F_A[F_B,F_C])
      = (1/2) epsilon_abc x_A^a x_B^b x_C^c                (2)

changes sign. It is a genuine gauge invariant of engineering dimension
six. These curvature values are not prohibited as point data: a smooth
connection with prescribed curvature at the origin is obtained by
A_mu(x)=-(1/2) F_mu,nu x_nu in anti-Hermitian conventions, with the
corresponding factors of i in Hermitian conventions.

This is an algebraic obstruction to claiming that the 21 quadratic
symbols already exhaust the gauge-invariant local algebra. It is NOT
a proof that the scalar-generated quantum net cannot contain (2).
An actual OPE/fusion theorem could produce it. That theorem is an
additional construction, not a consequence of a 21 Gram lower bound.

The same distinction persists for jets: each covariant derivative of
F is another adjoint color vector. Pair contractions and oriented
triple contractions distinguish the SO(3) color-orientation data.
Products and the differential/Bianchi identities must be included in
the chosen filtered operator basis. Merely taking ordinary spacetime
derivatives of (1) does not constitute that construction.

A useful elementary orbit-separation supplement is the following.
The full Gram matrix together with all triple determinants separates
SO(3) orbits of any finite list of vectors. If their span has rank 3,
one nonzero triple fixes the sign of the orthogonal extension. If the
rank is at most 2, one can reverse the extension on the orthogonal
complement without changing any vector, so an SO(3) extension exists.
This orbit statement is proved here; no polynomial-generation theorem
is being imported implicitly.

There is a sharper obstruction to extending the CURRENT Gaussian
fusion mechanism without a new calculation. In the free color-Maxwell
reference, the joint law is invariant under global O(3) color rotations.
The scalar F^2 and every Q_AB are O(3)-even. Products, Gaussian
conditional expectations, Wick contractions, derivatives, and
deterministic linear combinations of those observables remain
O(3)-even. The symbol (2) is odd under an improper color rotation.
Consequently NO purely Gaussian fusion of the present scalar or the
21 quadratic fields can have a nonzero projection onto (2), regardless
of the number of scalar insertions. This follows directly by applying
an improper color rotation to the conditional polynomial coefficient.
It is stronger than failure of a particular finite fusion span test.

In the actual nonabelian theory, interaction vertices contain the
color epsilon tensor, so an interaction-order contribution could
produce (2). A coefficient of order g would only require a polynomial
inverse power of gamma for normalization, potentially compatible with
the present method. But its NONZERO value and remainder have to be
proved from an interaction-sensitive marked expansion. The existing
Gaussian rank computation cannot establish this sector. The spacetime
pseudoscalar F wedge F in the dimension-four 21-frame is still
quadratic and O(3)-even in COLOR, so its construction does not remove
this obstruction.

2. BOUNDED WILSON LOOPS DO NOT BY THEMSELVES REPAIR THE GAP

There is an explicit counterexample to the implication

 bounded loop algebra + Euclidean invariance + reflection positivity
           => a nontrivial local continuum gauge QFT.

On every finite embedded analytic graph assign independent Haar SU(2)
variables to independent oriented edges. Reversing an edge takes its
inverse. Subdividing an edge replaces its variable by a product of
independent Haar variables; this product is Haar. Adding unused edges
also preserves old integrals. Thus the graph integrals consistently
define the generalized-connection product-Haar state. It is gauge
invariant and invariant under Euclidean transformations (indeed under
the graph-preserving analytic changes of coordinates).

For a simple loop C, let W_C=(1/2)tr U_C. Haar character orthogonality
gives

                 E W_C=0,       E W_C^2=1/4.             (3)

Choose arbitrarily small nonzero translations a for which C and C+a
are disjoint (a transverse translation of a planar simple loop does
this). Their holonomies are independent, so

               ||W_(C+a)-W_C||_L2^2=1/2.                (4)

Translations of these bounded variables are not strongly continuous.
Likewise a contractible small loop has Haar holonomy at every size:
there is no small-loop curvature Taylor expansion.

For the strictly positive-time cylinder algebra, the reflected graph
and original graph are disjoint. Hence

                  E[(Theta F)F]=|E F|^2 >=0.             (5)

Its OS quotient is one-dimensional: every centered positive-time
cylinder is null. Thus nonzero Euclidean loop variance (3) is not an
OS positive-energy witness. No claim about the beta->infinity Wilson
trajectory is made: this is a counterexample to the proposed abstract
boundedness shortcut, not a candidate limit of that trajectory.

The projective Haar construction and holonomy C*-algebra used here
are given in Ashtekar--Lewandowski--Marolf--Mourao--Thiemann,
J. Math. Phys. 38 (1997), 5453--5482, Sections 2 and 6:
https://arxiv.org/html/hep-th/9605128v1
Their complete two-dimensional result should not be confused with a
general four-dimensional reconstruction theorem. In Section 6.2 they
explicitly leave the appropriate general loop regularity conditions
open. Equations (3)--(5) above are our direct calculation.

Loops can detect the missing oriented invariant, but only through an
unbounded small-loop extraction. For U_A=exp(i a^2 F_A), etc.,

 tr(U_A U_B U_C)-tr(U_C U_B U_A)
                      = a^6 C_ABC + O(a^8).              (6)

Indeed all terms below the three-linear term cancel by cyclicity;
(i a^2)^3 tr(F_A[F_B,F_C])=a^6 C_ABC. The denominator a^6 required
to extract (2) destroys the raw loop sup bound. In a quantum theory
it also requires operator mixing, and a specified distributional
small-loop limit. A loop route is valid only after supplying precisely
such ultraviolet estimates; it does not bypass them.

3. WHY A 21 GRAM LOWER BOUND IS NOT A COVARIANCE THEOREM

The counterexample in full_ym_identification_frontier Section 4
already shows that hypercubic covariance, two-frame positivity, and
good fixed-component bounds do not fix the correct tensor action.
The Gram theorem supplies linear independence, a LOWER bound on the
field space. It does not say that the full Lorentz orbit of these
fields remains in that space, nor identify its representation.

There are two exact ways to add the missing conclusion:

 (i) Prove a rotation/boost calibration defect tends to zero for the
     actual finite fusion fields in their energy-bound topology.
     Equation (8) below is one sufficient form.

 (ii) Prove an intrinsic finite-dimensional local-field completeness
     theorem, with a covariant ultraviolet symbol map onto the
     quadratic-curvature space. Invariance of the intrinsic field
     space plus equivariance/injectivity of that symbol map would
     identify the tensor representation. A Gram lower bound supplies
     neither completeness nor symbol equivariance.

For comparison, the microscopic phase-space condition of Bostelmann
does produce finite-dimensional, symmetry-invariant point-field
spaces and nonperturbative OPEs:
https://arxiv.org/html/math-ph/0409070
https://arxiv.org/html/math-ph/0502004
Its hypothesis controls finite-rank approximation of ALL bounded
local observables after energy damping. The present copied estimate
controls selected fusion families. It is not that phase-space
condition. Moreover, an abstract OPE still needs its Yang--Mills
coefficients and field-content identification. Invoking that theorem
without its approximation estimate would only move the gap.

4. A PRECISE SUFFICIENT COMPLETION THEOREM ON THE SCALAR HILBERT SPACE

This theorem deliberately avoids a second all-order OS reconstruction.

Let (H,U,Omega) be the already constructed scalar vacuum theory in a
chosen nontrivial vacuum phase. U is a strongly continuous Poincare
representation with forward-cone spectrum; Omega is its unique
invariant unit vector. Put D_infty=intersection_s Dom(1+H)^s.

Choose increasing finite-dimensional spaces V_D indexing a consistent
basis of gauge-invariant SU(2) curvature/jet polynomials of engineering
dimension <=D, with the declared algebraic identities, derivatives,
adjoints and allowed renormalization mixing. Include the scalar field
already generating the scalar Hilbert space. Assume, for every D:

 A. ACTUAL FIELD LIMITS. The actual scalar-fusion or Wilson-observable
    construction gives linear operator distributions Phi_D(v,f) on
    D_infty, compatible as D increases. They and their adjoint
    companions preserve D_infty. For every integer r>=0 there are
    finite p,s and C such that

 ||(1+H)^r Phi_D(v,f)(1+H)^(-p)|| <= C |v| p_s(f).       (7)

    Here p_s is a Schwartz seminorm, and each finite set of labels
    uses common constants. The fields are jointly local on D_infty.
    These are exactly the type of conclusions targeted by the heat
    inversion/domain and shrinking-scalar-stencil locality notes;
    heat sandwiches alone are not substituted for (7).

 B. COVARIANCE CALIBRATION. Phi_D intertwines U with the specified
    finite-dimensional tensor representation rho_D. It suffices to
    prove this by the following actual approximant estimate, uniformly
    for Lambda in compact subsets of the connected Lorentz group:

 ||(1+H)^(-p)[ U(Lambda) Phi_(D,G)(v,f) U(Lambda)^-1
       -Phi_(D,G)(rho_D(Lambda)v,f o Lambda^-1)]
                                  (1+H)^(-p)|| ->0.      (8)

    The same approximants converge in the compatible energy-form
    topology to Phi_D. Since the forward cone gives comparability of
    transformed energies on compact Lorentz sets, the conjugations in
    (8) are legitimate on D_infty. Euclidean O(4) rotations are NOT
    being represented as unitary operators on the physical Hilbert
    space. One can instead prove the equivalent ordered-Euclidean
    covariance before analytic continuation, if its domain is checked.

 C. YM IDENTIFICATION, NOT JUST GAUSSIAN RANK. The maps are nondegenerate
    on the chosen operator quotient and respect derivatives/adjoints.
    For every finite collection, their short-distance correlation/OPE
    coefficients have the SU(2) Yang--Mills perturbative expansion in
    the SAME running coupling and mixing convention, with a controlled
    remainder at every requested finite perturbative order. In a
    fixed nonexceptional rescaled configuration this means, after the
    declared canonical scaling and logarithmic mixing, for every K,

       S_rescaled - sum_(k<=K) g(lambda)^k S_k^YM
            = O(g(lambda)^(K+1) |log g(lambda)|^q_K),     (9)

    in the specified distribution/energy topology. The corresponding
    local OPE remainder and the stress-tensor Ward normalization are
    included when asserting the full Jaffe--Witten identification.
    One scalar two-point asymptotic or a one-loop beta coefficient is
    not asserted to imply (9).

 D. NONTRIVIALITY. At least one centered field has a vacuum vector
    with nonzero component outside the translation-invariant sector.
    The existing derivative-packet scalar witness is sufficient if
    preserved in this phase. A 21 field Gram condition is stronger
    than needed for this part alone.

Conclusion: these data form a nontrivial local SU(2) Yang--Mills
observable QFT in the stated identification sense, with the already
chosen vacuum and spectrum. This conclusion does not assert a mass
gap or extension to other compact simple groups.

Proof of the QFT part. On D_infty define every finite product of
fields. Repeatedly apply (7), taking the energy exponent needed by the
next factor, to obtain

 |<Omega,Phi(v_1,f_1)...Phi(v_n,f_n)Omega>|
                    <= C_(D,n) product_j p_(s_j)(f_j).   (10)

The tensor-product Schwartz kernel theorem gives tempered vacuum
distributions. Hilbert-space positivity is exact; no limiting
reflection-positivity argument is needed here. Covariance follows
from (8), the spectrum condition from U, and local commutativity from
A. The scalar generating subfamily makes Omega cyclic on the same
Hilbert space. The adjoint companions give the Wightman adjoint
relations and closability. Thus these are Wightman fields in the
already constructed representation. C is the additional YM
identification, and D gives genuine dynamics. There is no need for
constants C_(D,n) uniform in n to prove that each Wightman distribution
is tempered: the Hilbert space and common invariant domain have
already been constructed. This statement does NOT say that arbitrary
fixed-order Euclidean limits alone can be reconstructed; that would
repeat the known OS-growth mistake.

The proof shows what has been saved: adding a verified field of each
finite engineering degree does not demand another independent
all-copy Euclidean reconstruction after (7) and joint locality are
available. It also shows what has not been saved: all the actual
field limits, their continuous covariance, and their UV matching
remain analytical theorems, not consequences of bounded loops or a
finite-dimensional Gram matrix.

5. IMMEDIATE RESEARCH CONSEQUENCE

After the presently open source admission and the 21 Gram step, the
smallest identification test with actual new content is:

  - calibrate continuous rotations of the dimension-four frame;
  - construct one oriented triple curvature field (2), jointly with
    that frame, from actual scalar fusion or a renormalized same-base
    three-plaquette product, with its nonzero UV symbol and energy bound.

The first prevents an incorrect tensor label; the second tests whether
the scalar fusion algebra reaches the nonabelian color-orientation
sector rather than only its even Gram subalgebra. Neither test alone
finishes the whole hierarchy, but together they directly probe two
remaining logical gaps. A claim that producer + 21 Gram already proves
the full theory should therefore be rejected. The exact completion
criterion is Section 4, with no new normalized large-field requirement
inserted into the consumer merely for convenience.
