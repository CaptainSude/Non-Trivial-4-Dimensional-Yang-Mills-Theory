from the full anisotropic Wilson ground chain to the proved Hamiltonian
ground process. ALL finite source derivatives converge. The normalized

then be chosen to approximate the same derivatives.

The proof uses the literal original Wilson normalizer, not an assumed

which grows arbitrarily fast along the spatial cutoff sequence:

        c(beta_delta)^(N|E_sp|) Tr_phys T_delta^N.        (1.1)

2. LITERAL MAGNETIC SOURCE INSERTIONS

   =<Omega_delta,(D_delta(z)S_delta)^n Omega_delta>

3. UNIFORM ENTIRE BOUNDS AND FINITE-WORD CONVERGENCE

The literal probability representation implies for ALL complex z

 |Z_delta(z)|, |Z_delta,N(z)|
                              <=exp(|z|T_delta M).      (3.1)

These functions are entire. This bound is uniform in delta and N
whenever T_delta is bounded. The corresponding continuum generating
function is

 Z(z)=E_pi exp[z int_0^T Q(X_s)ds]
     =<Omega,exp[-T(H-zQ-E)]Omega>,                     (3.2)

with the SAME unsourced ground endpoints. It is entire and obeys
|Z(z)|<=exp(|z|TM). The equality is the finite compact
Feynman--Kac/Dyson identity; no new ground state at source z is used.

We prove convergence of moments without differentiating a merely
pointwise limit. A k-point time-ordered ground expectation is a
word of bounded Q multipliers and the normalized semigroups.
For fixed k, its continuum intermediate vectors as the ordered
times range over a compact simplex form a compact Hilbert set.
Section1's compact-vector uniformity, boundedness of Q and
Omega_delta->Omega imply UNIFORM convergence of these words
over their mesh times to the continuum words.

The continuum words are continuous also when adjacent times
coincide, since the semigroup is strongly continuous and Q is
bounded. Their Riemann sums therefore converge. It follows that

                  E I_delta^k -> E I^k                 (3.3)

for each fixed k, including the ordered coincidences in a
discrete moment expansion. One can equally use an unordered
k-dimensional Riemann sum; time ordering is piecewise continuous
and agrees at the coincidence faces. Rounding a finite number
of window endpoints changes each integral by O(delta M).

The bounds |I_delta|<=T_delta M and |I|<=TM now justify
termwise summation of the exponential series, uniformly on any
compact source set. Consequently

 Z_delta -> Z locally uniformly on C,
 partial_z^k Z_delta -> partial_z^k Z
                  locally uniformly for every finite k.         (3.4)

For several Q_l the same argument applies to every mixed moment,
with domination by the product of their time-support bounds.
It proves locally uniform entire convergence on C^m and convergence
of all fixed mixed source jets. No uniform bound in k is needed
to identify any prescribed finite jet.

4. EXPLICIT FINITE-WORD AND SOURCE ERROR CONSUMERS

The preceding argument gives a useful error decomposition without
claiming an unavailable spatial rate. Fix a bound A on both
T_delta M and TM, with A>0. Suppose the first K moments have
the actual error

 e_delta,K=max_(0<=k<=K)
                 |E I_delta^k-E I^k|/A^k.

The k=0 difference is0. On |z|<=r, exponential-series tails give

 sup |Z_delta(z)-Z(z)|
  <=exp(rA)[e_delta,K+
                         2(rA)^(K+1)/(K+1)!].          (4.1)

For fixed spatial regulator, choose K to make the explicit tail
small, and then delta so that the finite list of word/Riemann errors
is small. This produces the claimed locally uniform convergence
without any implicit derivative-limit exchange.

For actual finite time periods there is an additional explicit
spectral remainder. Let Pi_delta be the rank-one ground projection
and

 R_delta(m)=Tr(S_delta^m)-1.

For m>=1 it is finite, nonnegative and tends to0 as m->infinity.
Writing the source word in S_delta units and using the trace norm
of S_delta^(N-n)-Pi_delta gives, for N>n,

 |Z_delta,N(z)-Z_delta(z)|
       <=2 exp(rA) R_delta(N-n),       |z|<=r.          (4.2)

The denominator Tr S_delta^N is at least1 and remains present.
If r_delta is the next eigenvalue of S_delta, then for m>=m0>=1,

 R_delta(m)<=r_delta^(m-m0) R_delta(m0).

All these constants refer to the exact fixed-regulator transfer
operator. They are not assumed uniform as a->0 or delta->0.
One chooses N AFTER delta. Prescribed even periods and a physical
time period tending to infinity can also be imposed.

6. DIRECT SEPARATED-WINDOW ATTACHMENT WITHOUT LOGARITHMS

Let q=Q-E_pi Q and use a strictly positive window[t,t+d],
t,d>0. The Hamiltonian centered reflected norm is

 R_Q(t,d)=int_t^(t+d)dr int_t^(t+d)ds C_Q(r+s),
 C_Q(u)=<q Omega,exp[-u(H-E)]q Omega>.                  (6.1)

At the discrete regulator choose a positive-time mesh window
and its EXACT reflected copy, with identical real weights delta.
Use the original Wilson centering E_delta,N Q, or E_delta Q in
the ground chain. The corresponding mixed second source cumulant
is exactly the centered reflected norm of this discrete window.
It is nonnegative by the original reflection positivity admission.

Convergence follows directly from the finite one- and two-point
words, their Riemann sums, and then(4.2). Thus this important
second-order attachment does not require any zero-free argument.
Both ways compute the same actual quantity.

There is also a quantitative quadrature version. Define at the
ground-chain stage

 epsilon_delta(U)=sup_(m delta<=U)
                 |C_delta(m delta)-C_Q(m delta)| ->0.

For complete mesh intervals approximating the window with endpoint
error at most delta, let d_delta be their total length, so
|d_delta-d|<=2delta. Since |C_Q|<=M^2 and

 |C_Q'(u)|<=D_Q:=kappa E_pi|grad Q|^2,

one obtains, for delta small compared with t,d,

 |R_delta(t,d)-R_Q(t,d)|
  <=(d+2delta)^2 epsilon_delta(2t+2d+4delta)
     +2delta(d+2delta)^2 D_Q
     +(4d delta+4delta^2)M^2.                           (6.2)

The middle term is the change of the covariance argument by at
most2delta when each integration time is replaced by its mesh
point. The final term bounds the symmetric difference in window
rectangles. A harmless smaller choice of rectangles improves
constants but is unnecessary. This estimate explicitly separates
the temporal word error from the elementary quadrature error.

For actual cubic Hamiltonian magnetic insertions
Q=sum_p w_p h_p, TURN67's exact form estimate gives

 D_Q<=3g^4 a^-1 sum_p w_p^2.                            (6.3)

In particular for the bare physical magnetic-energy insertion

On a fixed physical region this is O(a^-6), not a uniform physical

7. WHAT THIS NOW ATTACHES, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

Take any sequence of finite spatial regulators and any finite
list at each regulator of already specified/calibrated smooth
magnetic observables, source jets, and positive-time windows.
Their norms, source radii and constants may depend on that
spatial regulator. Sections1--6 prove:

 first choose delta_a sufficiently small to approximate all the
 prescribed Hamiltonian moments/source jets to a chosen error;
 then choose a sufficiently long finite original time period
 N_a to make all the corresponding finite-period errors small.

Because the list is finite at each stage, the choices can enforce
an increasing list of observables by a usual diagonal selection.
One can impose delta_a<=a^2 and N_a delta_a->infinity.
No spatially uniform temporal convergence rate is necessary for
this ordered construction. The literal observables and their
physical normalizations stay the same during each approximation.

This closes the fixed-spatial temporal/source-SECOND-DERIVATIVE
gap for bounded multiplication magnetic sources, including
insertions whose supremum diverges only when a is subsequently
varied. It is stronger than convergence of unsourced scalar
partition functions or pointwise source values.

It does not prove the central physical estimate

 0<c<=R_Qa(t,d),             R_Qa(t-s,d)<=C,

at fixed positive physical times, nor the operator renormalization
and mixed local compatibility needed for a continuum field.
It does not identify the required running g(a), extend isotropic
Balaban admissions to the anisotropic family, or restore Euclidean
symmetry.

Finally, the log-coupling source changes Q_beta_delta itself and
its scalar normalization. Its naive finite-Wilson action bound
is not the bounded time-integral bound(3.1). No electric-coupling
jet convergence is smuggled into this magnetic result. The exact
Hamiltonian identity Delta_T^2 partial_tau^2 K=8R_Q remains true
at that level, but its direct temporal transfer needs its own
analytic normalization argument.
