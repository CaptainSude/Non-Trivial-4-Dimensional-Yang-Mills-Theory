19 October 2026. Exact homogeneous theorem; compact-boundary and

RESULT

For the homogeneous sharp straight-string/box average, fine spacing
eta=1/R and coarse spacing one, there is a coarse-translation-covariant
operator K_(R,T) from coarse plaquette cochains to fine plaquette
cochains such that

             d_eta exp(-T P_eta) H_R = K_(R,T) d_c.         (0.1)

Here P_eta=d_eta^*d_eta on potentials, T>0 is fixed in coarse physical
units, and H_R is the actual flat constrained minimizing extension.
For every finite k, K_(R,T) has an exponentially weighted coarse-cell
kernel norm into fine physical C^k, uniformly in R. It therefore gives
the missing FREE curvature-slot source-space re-entry after heat.
The factorization does not divide by the massless Laplacian.

A periodic analytic Koszul division proves the algebraic factorization.
There are two safe ways to retain derivatives: divide in physical cell
L2 and then apply fine heat, or divide on overlapping physical output
patches and glue with smooth weights. Direct division in a single
Bloch-twisted C^k cell space is not justified; Section 4 explains why.

For later RG steps the relevant stationary precision is the actual
blocked precision q_i, not automatically P_eta. Section 8 incorporates
the independently checked matched_free_precision_heat_turn9 theorem
for the homogeneous blocked family. No replacement of q_i by the
nearest-neighbor Wilson precision is made.

1. COCHAIN AND NORM CONVENTIONS

Use forward cochains anchored at their lower-left lattice site. At
coarse momentum p, put

 delta_mu(p)=exp(i p_mu)-1,
 (d_c B)_(mu nu)=delta_mu B_nu-delta_nu B_mu,  mu<nu.

Fine derivatives include eta^-1, and the physical fine-cell norm is
eta^4 sum_(x in cell) |v(x)|^2. Colors are independent copies in this
free theorem. Other centered/cochain phase conventions are obtained
by fixed shift matrices; these preserve the estimates.

Let Q_R be the homogeneous ordinary flat straight-string average and
let H_R satisfy Q_R H_R=I and minimize ||d_eta A||^2. The established
flat symbol theorem gives a periodic reconstructed symbol

 A_R(p)=d_eta H_R(p): C^4 -> physical cell L2(two-cochains),

holomorphic and bounded on a strip |Im p_mu|<rho with rho and the
bound independent of R. This is the CURL statement in
archive/ym4-research/work/flat_minimizer_locality_research.md,
Sections 4--6 and 8. It is independent of the minimizing gauge because
all minimizing representatives have the same curvature. The explicit
source-gauge version in Section 10 has the same curl.

In kernel notation an estimate into a full fixed physical collar means

 sup_(x in cell c) sum_z exp(kappa |z-c|_1)
                    |D_eta^alpha K(x,z)| <= C_alpha.       (1.1)

All finite differences crossing a coarse-cell face are included. The
constants may depend on alpha,T and the fixed strip, but not on R,
the volume, or the number of earlier blockings.

2. EXACT GAUGE ANNIHILATION, INCLUDING ZERO MOMENTUM

The ordinary average obeys the chain identity

                       Q_R d_eta=d_c Q_R^(0),

with the scalar block average on the right. A coarse gradient therefore
has a zero-energy fine lift. Every minimizing extension has zero curl
on that input. Thus

                        A_R(p) delta(p)=0.                 (2.1)

The identity first holds on real momenta and extends holomorphically.
Fine Maxwell heat fixes gradients, and

 d_eta exp(-T P_eta)=exp(-T Delta_eta^(2)) d_eta,             (2.2)

where Delta_eta^(2)=d_eta d_eta^*+d_eta^*d_eta is the componentwise
positive cochain Laplacian. Hence the heat-smoothed map has the same
annihilation property.

At p=0, H_R maps a constant coarse one-form to a constant fine
one-form, so A_R(0)=0. This also follows from the first Taylor
coefficients of (2.1). A harmonic one-form on a periodic torus is
therefore killed exactly; no inverse zero mode has to be selected.

3. PERIODIC ANALYTIC KOSZUL DIVISION

Here is a bounded factorization usable with Banach-valued outputs.
Let a=(a_1,...,a_d) be a row of holomorphic, 2pi-periodic functions on
a fixed complex strip, taking values in a Banach space V, and assume

                       sum_j a_j delta_j=0.                (3.1)

Then there are antisymmetric k_(ij)=-k_(ji), holomorphic and periodic
on a fixed smaller strip, with

               a_j=sum_i k_(ij) delta_i,                  (3.2)
               ||k||_(rho') <= C_(d,rho-rho') ||a||_rho.

Explicit recursion. In dimension one (3.1) forces a_1=0. For d>1,
restrict p_d=0. Apply the (d-1)-dimensional recursion to the first
d-1 components, obtaining k'_(ij)(p_1,...,p_(d-1)). For j<d set

 b_j=a_j-sum_(i<d) k'_(ij) delta_i,
 c_j=b_j/(exp(i p_d)-1),
 k_(ij)=k'_(ij) for i,j<d,
 k_(dj)=c_j,       k_(jd)=-c_j.                            (3.3)

Each b_j vanishes when p_d=0, so c_j is holomorphic. The original
identity gives a_d=-sum_(j<d)c_j delta_j, proving (3.2). Antisymmetry
then gives, for every B,

                  sum_j a_j B_j=sum_(i<j) k_(ij)(d_c B)_(ij).

Uniform division bound. For a periodic f vanishing at p_d=0, divide
by exp(i p_d)-1. On a fixed neighborhood of p_d=0, Cauchy's derivative
bound and the integral formula for f give a quotient bounded by
C(rho-rho')^-1||f||. Outside that neighborhood the denominator is
bounded away from zero on a chosen smaller strip. Representing real
p_d in [-pi,pi] covers one period. All norms are Banach-valued, and
the finite recursion uses finitely many strip losses depending only
on d. There is no alias-count or R-dependent constant.

This is a periodic Laurent/Koszul homotopy. It avoids the expression
a d_c^*/|delta|^2, whose apparent singularity on the complex null
cone would not be controlled by a real transverse estimate.

4. HOMOGENEOUS CONSTRUCTION AND THE CELL-SEAM ISSUE

Apply Section 3 to the columns of A_R with V equal to physical
fine-cell L2(two-cochains). The reconstructed symbol is genuinely
periodic: a shift of coarse p permutes fine aliases and is compensated
by the integer fine-coordinate reconstruction, as proved in Section 8
of the archived locality note. Obtain K_R^0 with

                      d_eta H_R=K_R^0 d_c.                (4.1)

Fourier contour shifting on a further fixed strip gives

 ||K_R^0(c,z)||_(C^6 -> L2(cell c))
                    <= C exp[-rho_1 |c-z|_1].             (4.2)

The operator is defined on the GLOBAL fine lattice by its coarse-cell
kernel. It need not have uniformly bounded fine derivatives; it can
have cell-face seams. Define instead

              K_(R,T)=exp(-T Delta_eta^(2)) K_R^0.          (4.3)

Equations (2.2) and (4.1) prove (0.1) exactly.

Why heat repairs the norm. For fixed T>0, the fine heat kernel has
uniform exponentially weighted physical L2-to-C^k bounds between
coarse cells. One direct proof shifts the fine Fourier contour by a
fixed physical imaginary vector. Its derivative multiplier has size
|u|^k, while

 |exp[-T P_eta(p+i v)]|
                  <= exp[-c T |u(real p)|^2+C T |v|^2].

The physically normalized Fourier integral of any fixed polynomial
times this Gaussian is uniformly finite for 0<eta<=1. Contour shifting
gives exponential cell off-diagonal decay. Convolve this block estimate
with (4.2), sacrificing a fixed part of the exponent. This proves
(1.1) for every finite k, including differences across cell boundaries.

A tempting but invalid shortcut would apply the recursion directly to
a one-cell Bloch C^k norm. Evaluating p_d=0 changes the boundary twist
of that fine cell. Subtracting the two symbols can then make a jump
under the ORIGINAL twist, whose first fine difference costs eta^-1.
The L2 construction followed by GLOBAL fine heat avoids this issue.

5. A GENERAL PATCH VERSION, INCLUDING NON-WILSON PRECISIONS

There is a second construction which does not require the scalar heat
commutation (2.2). Let A be a coarse-translation-covariant map from
coarse one-forms to fine two-cochains. Assume:

 (a) A d_c=0;
 (b) on every fixed physical output patch W, its input Fourier symbol
     is periodic and holomorphic on a common strip with a uniform
     norm into the ORDINARY physical C^k(W), including the full patch
     collar; this holds for every desired finite k;
 (c) these patch bounds are uniform in R and under coarse translation.

Then A=K d_c with the weighted derivative estimate (1.1).

Proof. Choose a smooth coarse-scale partition of unity
sum_c chi(x-c)=1, with each chi supported inside a fixed physical
patch W+c, bounded overlap, and bounded physical derivatives of every
fixed finite order. Sample it on the fine lattice. On W+c use the
symbol

                   A_c(p;x)=sum_z A(x,z) exp[i p.(z-c)].

The target is now functions on a FULL output patch, not a twisted
cell quotient. Apply the recursion (3.3) in C^k(W+c). It commutes
with physical output differences and gives a kernel K^c there.
Define

                        K(x,z)=sum_c chi(x-c) K^c(x,z).    (5.1)

Only finitely many terms occur for each x. Every local factorization
equals A on its patch, so K d_c=A exactly. Leibniz' rule, fixed overlap,
and the analytic strip bounds give (1.1). Coarse translation covariance
holds because both the patches and their deterministic homotopy are
translated together. The partition creates no artificial cell-face
jump. Its derivatives are on the fixed coarse scale.

This theorem also explains why a full-patch formulation is sufficient
for a general actual precision. It does not assume that an arbitrary
transverse precision commutes with the fine Laplacian.

For the homogeneous Maxwell map A=d_eta exp(-T P_eta)H_R, the needed
patch bounds follow directly from the established alias-L2 estimate:

 sup_p sum_l ||H_(R,l)(p)||^2 <= C,
 |u_l|^k exp[-T Re P_l] <= polynomial_(k)(|l|) exp(-cT|l|^2).

Cauchy--Schwarz in the aliases gives all physical derivative sup norms
on any fixed patch, with constants uniform in R. Fine reconstruction
on the whole patch retains the actual phases on both sides of every
coarse face. This is also an independent proof of the hypothesis used
in the patch construction.

6. RE-ENTRY OF THE FREE n-SLOT KERNEL SPACE

The intertwiner does more than bound a polynomial on smooth fields.
It returns it to a positively normed curvature-kernel space.

Let the old rooted source have n curvature slots and kernel J_x(y),
where y=(y_1,...,y_n) are fine integer offsets. Suppose its complete
slot moments of total degree <r vanish at every x. Write the output
fine curvature as

 f(x)=sum_(z,A) K_(R,T)(x,z)_A F_c(z)_A,
                         F_c=d_c B.

Define the transported kernel by substituting this expression in
EVERY slot, keeping all tensor indices. Group fine roots x into a
coarse cell c only after that substitution; any old root mask remains
a multiplier of its whole root and is never differentiated.

The key is the Banach-valued version of the rootwise Taylor estimate.
For fixed root c use the tensor product of the weighted l1_z spaces
with weights exp[kappa sum_a |z_a-c|]. Equation (1.1) bounds every
physical difference of the product

       tensor_product_a K_(R,T)(x+eta y_a,z_a)

in this norm. Along the discrete Taylor paths the bound changes by at
most exp[C kappa eta sum_a |y_a|], because the centers of the kernel
weights have moved by those physical distances. Complete moment
cancellation removes the Taylor polynomial. Therefore

 ||J'_(single root x)||_(weighted l1 in all z slots)
 <= C_(n,r,T) eta^r
    sum_y ||J_x(y)|| (sum_a |y_a|)^r
                              exp[C kappa eta sum_a |y_a|]. (6.1)

For r=0 omit the power and the Taylor step. The discrete Taylor formula
uses Newton polynomials and forward/backward integer paths; its
polynomial span through order r-1 is the same as that of the stated
moments. Thus no continuum interpolation with an uncontrolled spatial
tail is being assumed.

Choose kappa small compared with the original fine-offset exponential
decay. The right side of (6.1) is bounded by the original exponential
kernel norm with a constant depending only on n,r,T and the fixed
decay reserve, uniformly in R. Restoring eta^(2n), summing at most
C eta^-4 fine roots in a coarse cell, and multiplying by R^(D-4)
gives

       ||J'_c||_kappa
             <= C_(n,r,T) R^(D-2n-r) sup_x ||J_x||_old.     (6.2)

This is an ACTUAL output kernel norm, not only a physical field-value
bound. With r=max(0,D-2n+1), it is O(R^-1). The bounded Newton projector
of free_multileg_flow_moment_bound_turn8 Section 5 can now be applied
on the COARSE output lattice, producing the next curvature-slot high
kernel and its exact finite low-reference remainder.

The source root may be arbitrarily masked in (6.1)--(6.2). There is no
integration by parts in the root. Constants are fixed for each finite
n,D before selecting the block ratio R. Overcomplete curvature
references and Bianchi-null directions still require honest quotient
bookkeeping if a physical independent low-coordinate matrix is wanted.

7. BIANCHI, HARMONIC SECTORS, AND COMPACT BOUNDARIES

Bianchi. K is defined on all coarse two-cochains, but (0.1) is asserted
on F_c=d_c B. Different Koszul homotopies may differ on two-cochains
outside this exact subspace. No bounded inverse curl is being asserted.
On exact inputs the output is an exact fine curvature automatically.
One need not require K to map arbitrary nonclosed inputs to closed
ones. On a finite periodic torus, closed two-cochains also have a
harmonic flux sector. That finite-dimensional sector is separate from
the globally periodic-potential sector and must be specified if used.

Harmonic potentials. Constant coarse one-forms have zero output
curvature exactly, as Section 2 shows. The construction therefore has
no obstruction from them and no gauge choice at p=0. Exact finite-volume
operators are obtained by periodizing the exponential infinite-lattice
kernel, with uniform bounds using periodic distance.

Compact flow with matched exterior. Fix an active fine box K and its
full Wilson action collar K^+, and let

                   E_T^K=exp[-T M_K P_eta].

Take ALL initial/fixed exterior values from the SAME global H_R B.
Then A^K=d_eta E_T^K H_R still annihilates coarse gradients: P_eta
kills their fine-gradient lifts even where M_K changes. On a protected
interior K0, the finite-box curvature energy and interior heat argument
from free_finite_ratio_Langevin_prototype_turn7 Section 4 give uniform
C^k(K0) bounds on each fixed complex input strip. The original H_R
exponential kernel bounds control the complete initial collar.

Apply the patch Koszul theorem on K0, with the active box fixed before
the factorization. It gives an exact protected-interior curvature
intertwiner, exponentially local in the coarse input, with constants
depending on T and the fixed buffer but not eta. The complete action
collar stays part of the source's read carrier. Heat is not claimed to
have finite propagation.

Independent boundary data. The preceding statement is false if one
discards independent exterior slots and asks for a function of interior
coarse curvature alone. A transverse one-dimensional reduction already
shows this: on [-1,1], fix a_2(-1)=a_2(1)=1 and constrain its block
mean to zero. The constrained energy minimum is
a_2(x)=3x^2/2-1/2, with nonzero F_12=3x, even though the sole interior
mean datum is zero. The discrete version is the analogous quadratic
spline. Finite-time compact flow has a boundary-forced contribution.
Such a problem needs the full boundary lift/term, not (0.1) with an
interior B and missing exterior. No fine boundary term is being dropped
in the matched-exterior theorem.

8. THE ACTUAL BLOCKED GAUSSIAN LAW AT LATER STEPS

At a later free RG stage the datum covariance is generally the blocked
Maxwell covariance, with precision q_i. A stationary OU replacement
for that law must use q_i (or a proved compatible mobility). Exact Wick
intertwining cannot be justified by substituting a nearest-neighbor
Wilson precision just because both have the same low-momentum limit.

The re-entry theorem itself has a clean abstraction. Let H_(i,R) be
the ACTUAL constrained extension for the current covariance and set

               A_(i,R,T)=d_eta exp(-T p_i) H_(i,R),

where p_i is expressed in FINAL physical units (p_i=R^2 q_i when
q_i is the symbol in old unit-link coordinates).

If it has gauge annihilation and the common-strip, complete physical
patch C^k bounds of Section 5, uniformly in i,R, then the same explicit
Koszul/partition construction gives a uniformly local curvature
intertwiner and the kernel re-entry estimate (6.2). No further special
nearest-neighbor identity is needed for that implication.

For the EXACT homogeneous blocked Maxwell family, that input is now
proved in matched_free_precision_heat_turn9.txt and was independently
checked for this note. The essential details are:

 (i) The explicit blocked Feynman covariance is diag(D_mu/P0), with
     D_mu uniformly above and below zero on real momenta. Minimizing
     its inverse quadratic form modulo coarse gradients gives
     c lambda_c Pi_T <= q_M <= C lambda_c Pi_T.

 (ii) G_M=q_M+delta delta^* is uniformly elliptic on real momenta and
     uniformly holomorphic on a fixed strip. Since it is Hermitian
     on the real torus, the linear term of G_M(xi+i zeta) is
     anti-Hermitian. The uniform second derivative bound therefore
     gives Re G_M(xi+i zeta)>=c lambda_c(xi)-C|zeta|^2.
     An energy estimate for its matrix ODE yields, without any
     normality assumption,
       ||exp[-T R^2 G_M((p+2pi l)/R)]||
                  <=exp[C T rho^2-cT|Re p+2pi l|^2].
     The gauge term does not change the curvature of the evolution.

 (iii) For the specified normalized flat straight-string averages,
     Q_R Q_M=Q_(MR) exactly on aligned grids: both the scalar
     start-box filter and the directional-string filter telescope
     separately. Thus H_(M,R)=Q_M H_(MR) is the ACTUAL between-stage
     constrained minimizing lift. Physical Jensen/counting bounds
     for Q_M and the original H_(MR) alias estimate give its uniform
     L2 bound on the same coarse strip. Any conditional-mean gauge
     difference is a fine gradient and is killed by curvature.

The heat alias estimate in (ii), multiplied by this L2 input bound,
proves every fixed full-patch output C^k bound uniformly in M,R.
Section 5 therefore applies to A_(M,R,T), and Section 6 gives genuine
curvature-kernel re-entry for the matched successive FREE measures.

These checks require the literal flat nested averaging convention.
They do not prove a comparable semigroup identity for nonlinear
path-ordered averaging. A compact active-region version for the
nonlocal q_M precision also remains separate: its full exterior
interaction tail and its boundary smoothing must be retained, as
the matched-precision note explicitly states. Global Fourier
locality alone does not make that compact flow finite-range.

This remains a free curvature-polynomial/Wick-kernel result. It is not
an interacting Wilson-score theorem, an independent-current analytic
extension, or a proof of the full local Yang--Mills observable hierarchy.
