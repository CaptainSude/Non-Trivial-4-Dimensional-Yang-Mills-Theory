STATUS

This is a finite, explicit conditional integration theorem, with a fully
proved Gaussian instance and an exact source-transport identity. It is
not yet an all-grade native Yang--Mills source theorem. The native gates

The useful point is that the incoming source need only be bounded and
measurable. An analytic rechart which holds its physical arguments fixed

All probability kernels and original scalar normalizers remain in the

1. FINITE KERNEL AND SOURCE-COMPATIBLE RECHART

Let theta range over a real neighborhood of0 in a finite-dimensional
parameter space. Write the ACTUAL positive normalized kernel as

 T_theta f = Z(theta)^-1 integral_K f(Y_theta(x)) w_theta(x) dx,
 Z(theta) = integral_K w_theta(x) dx.                         (1)

Here K is the original fixed product of free balls, w_theta includes
the complete original density, and Y_theta is the physical source
argument. Outer parameter-only factors may be factored from both
numerator and denominator of(1), but must still be retained in the
outer density. f is a bounded real or complex Borel function defined
on every real source argument used below. No derivative of f is assumed.

Assume a source-compatible real analytic rechart on a larger noise
neighborhood, holomorphic in complex theta near0,

 t=Psi_theta(x)=(Y_theta(x),z_theta(x)),
 x=X_theta(t),   D_theta=Psi_theta(K), D_0=Psi_0(K).           (2)

The source may depend on a lower-dimensional list of physical arguments;
these must be part of a genuine independent coordinate list in(2).
If a constraint fixes a combination of the chosen source arguments,
such a chart need not exist. A nonzero inverse determinant is a premise,
not a consequence of finite dimensionality. If redundant source data
are represented by functions of the independent y coordinates, that
representation must be independent of theta as well.

Define the transformed density on the FIXED real domain D_0 by

 wtilde_theta(t)=w_theta(X_theta(t)) det D_t X_theta(t),
 a_f(theta)=integral_(D_0) f(y) wtilde_theta(t) dt,
 A_theta f=a_f(theta)/a_1(theta).                            (3)

Choose the real orientation so the determinant is positive on the
real connected chart. Its holomorphic determinant, not an absolute
value applied at complex parameters, is used in(3). The formula also
works with a positive analytic reference volume form in place of dt.

For complex theta, f is evaluated ONLY at the real integration variable
y. It is never evaluated on a complex field. All complex dependence
in(3) lies in the analytic density. This is what permits bounded or
nondifferentiable incoming sources.

2. ANALYTICITY AND A QUANTITATIVE SHRINKING TUBE

Suppose wtilde_theta(t) is holomorphic for theta in a complex polydisc
D_rho, for a.e. t in D_0, with a measurable common integrable majorant.
A convenient sufficient quantitative premise is

 |wtilde_theta(t)/wtilde_0(t)-1| <= q < 1
                   for all theta in D_rho, a.e. t in D_0.    (4)

Then, putting z_0=a_1(0)>0,

 |a_1(theta)-z_0|<=q z_0,  |a_1(theta)|>=(1-q)z_0,
 |A_theta f|<=(1+q)/(1-q) ||f||_infinity.                    (5)

Dominated holomorphic integration proves analyticity of a_f, and the
first inequality proves its denominator is zero-free on the SAME
tube. In particular A f is holomorphic there. This argument does not
use a complex probability measure or infer zero-freeness merely from
positivity on the real axis.

One way to verify(4) is a common analytic logarithm of wtilde with

 sup_t |log wtilde_theta(t)-log wtilde_0(t)|<=c_0,
                 exp(c_0)-1<=q<1.                         (6)

If the full sup-direction logarithmic derivative is at most M on a
larger tube, take rho<=c_0/M, along with its actual inverse-chart and
action-domain restrictions. M includes the NUMBER of independently
varied parameter coordinates. This gives polynomial inverse-radius
prices when M and the inverse chart margins have polynomial bounds.
It is not automatically independent of ambient volume.

On an inner half tube, Cauchy's estimate gives, for any fixed multiindex
alpha,

 |partial^alpha A f|<=C_q alpha! (2/rho)^|alpha| ||f||_infinity. (7)

For m parameter coordinates, a sup-direction derivative of order k
can cost a further m^k under componentwise polydisc Cauchy estimates.
Retain that finite read price. No source derivative has entered(7).

3. THE ORIGINAL REAL KERNEL MINUS THE ANALYTIC REFERENCE

For real theta let

 K_theta=X_theta(D_0).

Change variables BACK in(3). Its normalized reference is exactly

 A_theta f = [integral_(K_theta) f(Y_theta(x))w_theta(x)dx]
                         /[integral_(K_theta)w_theta(x)dx]. (8)

Assume the following ACTUAL containing-law geometry on the real box
where the remainder estimate is to be used:

 K and K_theta are subsets of one K_plus,
 K_minus subset K intersection K_theta,
 nu_theta^+(K_plus minus K_minus)<=epsilon<1,
 nu_theta^+(dx)=w_theta(x)1_(K_plus)dx/Z_plus(theta).        (9)

No convexity of K_theta is needed. A common sufficient arrangement is
that K_plus enlarges each original free-ball radius R by a fixed small
fraction, while K_minus reduces it by that fraction. If the containing
law has coordinate means below R/4 and a uniform Gaussian concentration
bound, its finite union bound gives

 epsilon<=C |I| exp(-c R^2).                              (10)

This mean/concentration premise and the actual two-sided domain
inclusion must both be proved. Moving one cut to a nearby location is
not itself a probability estimate.

The conditional restrictions nu^+(.|K) and nu^+(.|K_theta) have common
mass at least1-epsilon before normalization. Their total variation
distance (half the total variation mass) is at most
epsilon/(1-epsilon). Therefore, for real f,

 E_theta f := T_theta f-A_theta f,
 |E_theta f|<=osc(f) epsilon/(1-epsilon).                  (11)

For complex f, the safe bound 2||f|| epsilon/(1-epsilon) applies.
The argument is simply comparison of the two normalized restrictions
of ONE positive containing measure; it does not divide by a rare atom.
It retains the original Z(theta) in T and Z_plus only in this proof.

Equations(1),(3),(11) give the EXACT decomposition T=A+E as linear
operators on bounded sources. Also

 A1=1, E1=0,  A_0 f=T_0 f,  E_0 f=0.                      (12)

The new scalar A_0 f is the true quantum average T_0 f, not f at the
zero-noise field. It must be recorded. Unlike a decomposition made
with an unnormalized reference numerator, this normalized A preserves
constants exactly. Its normalization has not changed the original
outer density: the entire correction remains E in the equality T=A+E.

For an incoming source with ||f||<=B eta, (10),(11) yield

 ||E f||<=C B eta |I| exp(-c R^2).                         (13)

This is a PRODUCT of the incoming amplitude and the new tail price.
It does not require independence of two rare events: it follows from
an amplitude bound multiplying the measure of the domain difference.
The output E may be merely measurable. No two-jet bound for E has been
claimed or used. A subsequent application of this theorem would still
accept it if its physical read has a new source-compatible chart.

5. A COMPLETE GAUSSIAN EXAMPLE AND THE NECESSITY OF SPARE VARIABLES

Let X_1,...,X_n be independent centered real Gaussians of variance g^2
and condition their average to equal theta. For a bounded measurable
f of the first k coordinates, with k<n, the conditional marginal is

 Y~N(theta 1_k, g^2 C), C=I_k-(1/n)1_k 1_k^T,
 C^-1=I_k+1_k 1_k^T/(n-k).                               (18)

Thus T_theta f is entire in theta by integrating f(y) against the
complex Gaussian kernel over fixed real y. Completing the real part
of the square gives the exact bound

 |T_(u+iv) f|<=||f|| exp[ k n v^2/(2(n-k)g^2) ].           (19)

For vector colors, replace v^2 by its Euclidean norm squared. A tube
rho<=c g sqrt[(n-k)/(k n)] therefore has bounded holomorphic norm
C_c||f||. Its Cauchy costs are polynomial inverse g and a quantified
inverse spare-coordinate margin. Analyticity has been obtained without
any regularity of f, because the unobserved variables randomize every
observed direction compatible with the conditioning.

If k=n, choose f(x)=h(n^-1 sum_i x_i) for an arbitrary bounded
nonsmooth h. Then T_theta f=h(theta), which need not be continuous.
There can be no general analytic-smoothing theorem for complete-row
sources. A source avoiding a solved pivot is a genuine rank condition.

This unbounded Gaussian example has no moving-domain correction. In a
truncated or nonlinear version, Sections1--3 state exactly the added
chart, complex-norm and tail premises; Gaussian conditioning alone does
not verify those premises for the native nonlinear kernel.

6. LOCAL GAUGE INVARIANCE IS A FURTHER MATCH, NOT AUTOMATIC

where the f, chart and density family are transported consistently.
The right side obeys(11) only where the canonical argument is in its

A shrinking analytic tube narrows this certificate. Its original-law
complement is retained, not assigned a small mass by this argument.
A finite conditional integration region does not automatically imply
a finite outer parameter read: inherited minimum/action kernels may
depend on an entire outer field. The word COMPLETE in this section
includes those reads, or a separately proved localized coefficient
expansion. Global holonomy and wrapping are not curls on a finite box.

7. WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH FOR THE YM4 PROGRAM

The theorem supplies a rigorous mechanism with potentially useful
rates: bounded incoming remainder -> analytic emission plus a remainder
smaller by a new exp(-c R^2), with no differentiation of hard source
selectors. It also identifies a genuine obstruction when the source
contains an entire constrained averaging row.

For native Q1s, the following are still required before claiming this
mechanism closes the original source induction:

 (i) an actual physical source-compatible private-pivot/rechart on the

The current canonical hybrid has a complete filled read W which can
contain all available pivots of a row; (i) is therefore not automatic.
Later coarsening or a supported decomposition might create spare rows,
but neither is proved by this finite theorem. Existing local quasilocal
two-jets do not on their own verify (iv) or (v).
