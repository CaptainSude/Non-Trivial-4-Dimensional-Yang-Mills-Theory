FORCED GLOBAL RESPONSE CHART: AFFINE DATUM, CURRENT PARTNER, AND PHYSICAL MATCH
Research note, 18 October 2026. Working proof, not a new published-source theorem.

All I: references refer to the pinned upstream Raw I. The construction below uses the
actual primitive and nonlinear coefficient estimates in Raw I4.5, not the later
unrecovered global-layer theorem. In particular the quoted inverse-block covariance

The determining family must be one on which those primitive estimates apply. The

1. Domains and the precise forcing convention

Raw I12935--12970 defines S by real local C1 regularity (CMP99b3.35), a complex C1
twist (3.37), and an independent current |J|_{-3}<a_gamma. Raw I110264--110283
defines K109 by the same three analytic conditions, on a determining family, with
M alpha0<=a0 and alpha1 below fixed ceilings. Thus, after decreasing the common
ceilings, these analytic background conditions are exactly those needed by I4.5;
no derivative of the independent current is added. Eligibility of the determining
family for the primitive propagator construction is a separate geometric matter.

Use the following exact primitive names:
  T0=P0 G0=G0 P0^T,  G=G^J=(I-T0 Theta)^(-1)T0,
  H0=G0 Q* q0 ell^(-1),  HJ=Gpi^J Q* qJ ell^(-1).
E is the potential C1/Hölder space N_beta, F the forcing space |.|_{-3}, and B the
coarse block sup space. All transposes are bilinear, including at complex fields.
The source formulas and estimates are I13583--13676,13723--13977 and13980--14080.

Let f in F be an independent FORCING and b in B independent block data. Define
  P=H0 b + G[-f+Theta H0 b - V'(P)],                 (1.1)
  X=D^J(P),  H=P-HJ X.                              (1.2)
Equivalently A0=P-H0b satisfies the same equation with H0b removed on the left.
At sigma=1, (1.1) is EXACTLY
  P=H0 b+T0[-f+Theta P-V'(P)].                       (1.3)
Indeed multiply (1.1) by I-T0 Theta, or use its convergent Neumann identity.
There is no change of higher-order action in this rearrangement.

The affine potential datum of (1.3) is j0=T0 f. The affine datum after resummation
is j_res=G f=(I-T0 Theta)^(-1)j0. These are not interchangeable. In particular,
inserting j0 directly into P=-j_res+(I+G Theta)H0b-GV'(P) changes the equation.

At complex decoupling sigma, use the EXACT K10 primitive convention I15265--15339:
expand the complete primitive walk with all side branches first and decorate each
distinct visited cube once. Define (1.1) from those decorated G_sigma,H0_sigma,
Theta_sigma,V'_sigma. Do not claim that decorated G_sigma is a resolvent recomputed
from decorated T0_sigma,Theta_sigma. Accordingly (1.3) is asserted only at sigma=1.
This is an explicit definition of a forced extension of K10; no missing decoupled
inverse identity is required. At f=0 it is exactly the K10 block-only chart.

2. Uniform existence, response kernels and analyticity

The earlier coefficient lemmas supply constants, at any fixed allowed sigma-disc,
  ||G||_{F->E}<=g0, ||H0||_{B->E}<=h0,
  ||Theta||_{E->F}<=theta0, |V'(P)|_F<=c0 N(P)^2.
Constants grow by the explicitly allowed fixed exp(C kappa1) factors in K10.
They are independent of volume, depth and forcing. Let
  L=(I+G Theta)H0, l0=h0(1+g0 theta0),
  d=g0 |f|_F+l0 |b|.
Take 2r inside the V' analytic ball, 8g0 c0 r<=1, and d<=r/2. Then (1.1) has a
unique solution in N(P)<=r, is jointly holomorphic, and N(P)<=2d, N(H)<=4d.

Proof. The equation is P=-Gf+Lb-GV'(P). Cauchy on the radius-2r ball gives
||V''(P)||<=4c0r on the radius-r ball. The right side is a contraction of ratio
at most1/2 and maps that ball into itself (in fact d+g0c0r^2<=5r/8). Picard
iteration proves holomorphy on every strict parameter subdomain. Its uniform
limit obeys N(P)<=d+N(P)/2. The nonlinear correction bound gives N(H)<=2N(P).

The same argument in every allowed exponential weight rho uses the WHOLE-BALL
weighted Hessian estimate proved in K8, I14841--14866 and14952--14969. It yields
  N_rho(delta P)<=C_rho(|delta f|_{F,rho}+|delta b|_{B,rho}),
  N_rho(delta H)+|delta X|_rho<=C_rho(|delta f|_{F,rho}+|delta b|_{B,rho}). (2.1)
Writing R_P=(I+GV''(P))^(-1) and T_P=I-HJ D'(P), the exact data kernels are
  D_fP=-R_P G,              D_bP=R_P L,
  D_fH=-T_P R_P G,          D_bH=T_P R_P L.           (2.2)
The weighted Neumann series for R_P converges with ratio at most1/2, so these
are actual exponentially localized data-response kernels, not merely unweighted
analytic derivatives. Higher data derivatives follow by Cauchy on a fixed smaller
data ball. Their n dependence is at most C^n n!, with the radius displayed in
the estimate; there is no volume factor. Gauge covariance and component
factorization follow term by term in Picard iteration from the same properties
of the K10 coefficients and the data.

3. A bounded current companion, using no second derivative of a datum

Put k=(qJ-a)ell^(-1), Delta'=Delta-D_W*D_W and Xi=Theta P-V'(P). Define
  Lambda_f=P0^T(Xi-f)+Q*(q0-a)ell^(-1)b
             -Q*kX-Lambda^T j_J(HJ X),
  K_f=Lambda_f-Delta' H+F(W,H).                     (3.1)
Every coefficient in (3.1) is one of I4.5's bounded first-order/lower-order
operators. In particular Delta' is local E->F of norm O(a0+a1), and F is the
current remainder of I14083--14130. The only new direct term is -P0^T f, an
order-zero localized operator on F. Thus
  |K_f|_{F,rho}<=C_rho(|f|_{F,rho}+|b|_{B,rho}),     (3.2)
and the corresponding two-data Lipschitz bound holds. No D* f or Delta HJ is
estimated or assumed. Formula (3.1) defines the companion also at complex sigma.

For completeness, define the bounded E->F derivative operator
  M_P=P0^T(Theta-V''(P))-Q*kD'(P)
       -Lambda^T j_J HJ D'(P)-Delta'T_P+F_H'(W,H)T_P.
Then its exact data kernels are
  D_fK=-P0^T+M_P(-R_PG),
  D_bK=Q*(q0-a)ell^(-1)+M_P R_PL.                  (3.3)
These have the exponential weighted bounds just proved. Formula (3.3) also
shows explicitly why merely adding a first forcing slot does not require a
Laplacian estimate for the free-current HJ propagator.

4. Exact sigma=1 identities and the physical-current defect

At sigma=1, QP=ell^(-1)b and applying Delta_a to (1.3) gives
  Delta_a P=P0^T(Xi-f)+Q*q0 ell^(-1)b.              (4.1)
Consequently (3.1) becomes
  K_f=(Delta_a-Q*aQ-Delta')P
        -{Q*kX+Lambda^T j_J(HJX)-Delta'HJX}+F(W,H). (4.2)
All these identities hold for independent complex (W,J,f), with the supplied
primitive inverse identities, and do not use a physical-current interpretation.

Let Jphys(W) be the actual action current. CMP109(3.11), p272, gives
  Jphys(e^(i eta H)W)-Jphys(W)=D_W*D_WH+F(W,H).     (4.3)
The following exact defect is useful and has not been dropped:
  K_f-[D_W*D_WH+F(W,H)]
   =D_W R D_W*H+k_J(Lambda HJX)+Lambda^T m_J(Lambda HJX). (4.4)

Proof. Subtract (4.3)'s increment from (4.2). The result before simplification is
D_W R D_W*P+Delta HJX-Q*kX-Lambda^Tj_J(HJX). Use
(Delta_a-Theta'_J)HJ=Q*qJ ell^(-1), QHJ=ell^(-1), and
Theta'_J=k_J Lambda+Lambda^Tj_J+Lambda^Tm_JLambda. The Q*k and Lambda^Tj_J
terms cancel, and D_WRD_W*(P-HJX)=D_WRD_W*H. This proves (4.4).

In particular the companion is the physical current increment whenever
  R D_W*P=0 and R D_W*HJ=0.                        (4.5)
It is not legitimate to impose (4.5) at arbitrary independent complex pairs.
Off the physical branch K_f is simply the bounded independent current rider
required by the analytic source domain. Identity (4.4) states exactly what would
have to vanish for physical matching; it prevents a hidden second-order claim.

5. Physical matching: which source must be restored

At a true real pair J=Jphys(W), the actual constrained physical action in the
linearized P variable has expansion
  S(e^(i eta L(P))W)=S(W)+<J,P>
       +1/2<P,(Delta-Theta)P>+V(P).                (5.1)
This is the true-pair reduction I15575--15673, using the corrected higher-order
functional V^disp+W^J (I14155--14201), not V^disp alone. For a published small
Landau critical point P_* with QP_*=ell^(-1)b, criticality on ker Q gives
  P0^T[J+Delta_aP_*-Theta P_*+V'(P_*)]=0.
The same algebra as I15746--15788, but WITHOUT setting P0^TJ=0, gives
  P_*=H0b+T0[-J+Theta P_*-V'(P_*)].                (5.2)
Hence the forced chart with f=J equals P_* by uniqueness in its small ball.
The published Landau properties of P_* and HJ then give (4.5), so J+K_J is
exactly Jphys(e^(i eta H)W). This identifies both field and current, not just the
Euler equation. On the old special background where J lies in range Q*, GJ=0
and this reduces to the block-only chart's diagonal exactness.

The matching uses a small physical critical point satisfying the published
Landau/regularity hypotheses, or a proof that the gauge-fixed fixed point itself
has those properties. For a general real regular carrier which is not already
a background admitted by CMP102, it is not enough to say 'J is a true current'.
The remaining physical issue is precisely whether the selected global layers'
actual real carriers and determining families meet that published hypothesis,
or whether the gauge-orbit argument establishes (4.5) for the new forced chart.
This note does not infer it from the independent-pair class K109 alone.

The natural physical forcing is f=J, not an arbitrary independent f. A different
f corresponds at most to an additional linear source in (5.1); that sourced
functional need not be gauge invariant, so the Landau conclusion cannot simply
be reused. At general sigma exact physical identification is neither needed nor
asserted; the complex companion (3.1) is a controlled analytic extension.

6. A lower-order cutoff companion

Once physical matching has been proved at sigma=1, define for the scalar cutoff
chi used by the source
  K_sigma[chi]=chi K_sigma+[D_W*D_W,chi]H_sigma
                  +F(W,chi H_sigma)-chi F(W,H_sigma). (6.1)
It is holomorphic, covariant, component-local and bounded in |.|_{-3} using only
the proved C1/Hölder field and combined-current bounds. The exact lattice
commutator estimate is proved in work/current_interface_research.md, (9): it
requires one field derivative and two scalar cutoff differences, no second
field derivative. At a true sigma=1 pair, (4.3) makes (6.1) exactly
Jphys(e^(i eta chi H)W)-Jphys(W). Thus this consumer can use the new independent
current companion without reinstating the missing graph norm.

If the earlier global definition of the cut rider is retained literally, its
equality with (6.1) away from sigma=1 has not been established. Replacing that
definition by (6.1) is legitimate only after checking the source consumers use
holomorphy, locality, bounds and physical matching, rather than a separate
off-diagonal algebraic identity. The old deposited theorem is not silently
declared proved by constructing a different extension.
