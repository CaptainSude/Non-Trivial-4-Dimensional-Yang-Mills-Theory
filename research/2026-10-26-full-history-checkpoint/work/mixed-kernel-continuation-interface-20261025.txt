FIXED DENSITY / NEW SOURCE MAP: EXACT CONTINUATION INTERFACE
25 October 2026. Root derivation; independent review requested.

PURPOSE

A new source-map extension does not logically force replacing every
off-physical formula inside an already proved density extension. It does
force declaring the joint kernel precisely and proving a common domain.
This note removes a potentially unnecessary equality requirement without
importing any missing normalization, locality, or source-domain estimate.

1. FINITE ONE-STEP LEMMA

Let Dplus,Dminus be open finite-dimensional complex state domains (possibly
native field/current slices). Let K be the ORIGINAL compact real noise
product with its ORIGINAL measure dx. Suppose

 w(b,x) is continuous in (b,x), holomorphic in b, locally uniformly
 dominated for b in Dplus;
 Z(b)=integral_K w(b,x)dx is nonzero on Dplus;
 Phi(b,x) is continuous and jointly holomorphic in its declared parameters;
 Phi(Dplus x K) is contained in Dminus, with compact images lying strictly
 inside Dminus over every compact parameter subset used in the proof.

Then for a holomorphic source F on Dminus define

 T F(b)=Z(b)^(-1) integral_K w(b,x) F(Phi(b,x)) dx.       (1)

This is holomorphic, T1=1, and its denominator is EXACTLY Z. The proof is
finite compact integration and local uniform domination, followed by
division by the specified nonzero holomorphic Z. There is no requirement
that the construction of w internally use the same response formula as
Phi. They constitute one jointly DECLARED extension by (1).

If, on a specified admitted real physical family, w is the original
unnormalized density and Phi is the original pointwise reconstruction,
then (1) is exactly the original conditional source expectation there.
Equality on this physical family is asserted directly, not used as an
identity theorem between arbitrary independent-current extensions.

All scalar/coarea/Haar/pivot factors assigned to w stay in w. A factor
depending only on b cancels inside (1) but remains in its original outer
mass. No marked-event probability or replacement denominator is introduced.

2. FINITE PREFIX

Let (w_j,Z_j,Phi_j) satisfy the same statements on the actual adjacent
domains, and define F_(j+1)=T_j F_j. If the full nested image and every
denominator are admitted simultaneously, finite iteration is holomorphic
and agrees with the original physical prefix by induction.

One may write it as an integral over the original product of all x_j,
with each b_j=Phi_j(b_(j+1),x_j) and factor
w_j(b_(j+1),x_j)/Z_j(b_(j+1)). Fubini is justified on compact admitted
parameter subsets. No off-physical telescope of the scalar factors is
needed or asserted. T_j1=1 at each step is enough for the normalized
recursion; the literal physical scalar telescope, where used, retains
its separate original-law proof.

For a fixed admitted parameter one always has

 |T_j F| <= M_j sup_K |F(Phi_j)|,
 M_j=integral_K |w_j| dx / |Z_j|.                      (2)

A useful uniform prefix norm requires ACTUAL bounds for these M_j on
the actual nested images, or a stronger connected-insertion theorem.
Holomorphy or zero-freeness alone supplies no volume-uniform M_j.
The previously proved phase criterion M_j<=2 applies only when its
specific real-part gap, coefficient continuation and imaginary packet
norm hypotheses have been checked on those images. It must not be
assumed throughout an arbitrary fixed native polydisc.

4. SOURCE DOMAINS AND AN OFF-GRAPH NONIDENTITY

For a bounded holomorphic old source E(U,J), the DIRECT source input is

 Phi_A(U,J)=(exp(i xi A)U,
       theta^3 J+Delta_U A-P1_U A+F1(U,A)).             (3)

On its full B109(3.16) old/current intersection and the four-slot A ball,
the direct composition E(Phi_A) has its original cap. It can be the
declared off-physical source extension in (1).

A near-source determining reconstruction from B109 Lemma4 is a different
candidate extension. It has a specifically reconstructed current, not an
arbitrary free output J. At fixed U and A=0, the direct current is theta^3J,
while the determining pair's current is fixed by its reconstruction.
For instance a scaled bounded analytic source tr(J(b)^2) distinguishes
the two as J varies. Thus physical agreement cannot supply their
off-graph equality. No such equality is required if (3) is retained as
the chosen direct source, but the near Taylor/irrelevance estimates do
not then transfer automatically.

5. COMPLETE LOCALIZER READS

Localizing only A is insufficient to localize (3). P1_U contains the
nonlocal D P D* branch and can read the background outside the response's
apparent carrier. The entire P1 kernel must either remain in the read
or receive its own native complete-word weakening. A possible new source
family replaces P1 by P1_s together with all other responses and frames;
it equals (3) at s=1 and needs its own domain/locality check. The native
bounded P1_s map can preserve the O(delta) current estimate on fixed
weakening caps. This is a source-localizer choice, not permission to
discard that branch.

Likewise a fixed original cavity read of at most 60 exterior links does
not remain the full read after propagation. Return paths, private pivots,
all selected-current words and each earlier noise dependence are part of
the propagated source read. Scalar smallness does not prove a connected
coefficient-sum bound over those carriers.

6. MASK AND HISTORY SCOPE

The lemma requires an actually holomorphic w on its domain. It does not
turn a parameter-dependent sharp history predicate into such a function.
For the homogeneous analytic reference, equality to the original law is
asserted only on its declared admitted physical sector with the original
cuts. Every nonredundant old/history mask and its mass remains in the
physical decomposition. A nonanalytic masked quotient cannot be declared
covered by (1) solely because its smooth density is covered.

7. THE FRAME IS PART OF THE STATE

For the common-frame prefix the state b includes its actual frame Gamma.
If x is the ORIGINAL free noise, source evaluation uses
xsharp=Ad_(Gamma^-1)x. The matching density must therefore be written
as the original-frame w_old(U^Gamma,Ad_Gamma J,x), or by its proved
covariance as w_old(U,J,xsharp), with the original scalar allocation.
It cannot silently be replaced by w_old(U,J,x). At a real unitary
frame this distinction can be removed in an estimate by an orthogonal
change of noise coordinates preserving the product of color balls.
At complex Gamma it is a deterministic complex argument, NOT a contour
change. The phase theorem must then include the complete Gamma reads
and its noise-transport score, or prove that its own pair chart covers
the original-frame coefficients. A bound on Gamma alone does not give
a fine C1 gauge bound. The abstract lemma above permits this enlarged
state, but does not supply the extra phase or locality estimate.

This is an exact interface lemma and a route choice. It does not prove
the missing full normalized catalogue or global Yang--Mills construction.
