NATIVE R: OUTPUT SUPPORT, BACKWARD READS, AND THE REAL LOCALIZATION SHIFT
19 October 2026. Independent admission/proof audit, turn 15.

RESULT

The reconstructed fixed-branch reference formula gives a precise local
replacement candidate. Its changed field region is Lambda_i, but its
backward data read is the larger component X_i (including any genuine
boundary slots). The old branch mass m_tau is NOT an extra input to this
fixed-branch backward kernel. The reference denominator has exactly the
same parameter reads as its numerator.

This does not yet attach the complete native R evolution to the strict
half-algebra criterion. In particular, B119 explicitly removes a nonlocal
background dependence of its characteristic functions by a change of
fluctuation variables. An observable must be pulled through that same
change. The remaining H-dependent read is exponentially small on the
specified changed region, not identically zero. A concrete unitary loop
bound for this change is proved below, with the coupling factor correct.

The original Part-I prebranch normalization and full local-minimum datum
dictionary remain source-verification requirements. The modified run's
iterated geometry selection also needs a local input-read certificate;
an unbounded iteration count alone is NOT a proof that no such certificate
exists. No full native Markov/half-algebra theorem is asserted here.

2. THE FIXED-BRANCH BACKWARD OPERATOR

Fix a component, geometry, gauge graph and all other prebranch labels
h=(X,Lambda,G,T,...). Write e for the complete retained datum outside
Lambda. The reconstructed reference quotient has the following form:

 s_h(dv';e) = Z_h(e)^(-1) delta_G(v') chi_Lambda(v')
   exp[-g_k^(-2) A(zeta,U_(k,X)(v' V_Lambda(e)))] dv',       (2.1)

where Z_h is the integral of the numerator over the active v' variables.
The field written on Lambda is v' V_Lambda(e); elsewhere the field is
unchanged. Let Phi_h(e,v') denote this complete output field. For fixed h,

 (K_h f)(v) = integral s_h(dv';e(v)) f(Phi_h(e(v),v')).      (2.2)

The exact source assertion awaiting the full datum check is that all
parameters in (2.1), including V_Lambda, read only V_k|_(X\Lambda).
If a boundary convention requires an additional collar, replace X by its
ACTUAL complete read X^read; do not suppress that collar in (2.2).

The locality conclusion is an equality statement, requiring no estimate:
if two inputs coincide on X^read and on the part of the support of f left
unchanged, and have the same h, their values under K_h f coincide. Thus

 read(K_h f) subset (read(f)\Lambda) union X^read.          (2.3)

If read(f) misses Lambda and f ignores any changed auxiliary label, then
K_h f=f exactly, even if the reference has extensive exterior dependence.

Why m_tau is absent. Disintegrate the old branch as
tau(de,dx)=m_tau(de) q_e(dx), with q_e normalized. The replacement
tau'=m_tau(de) s_h(dy;e) is realized by delta_e(de') s_h(dy;e).
The old q_e and m_tau are part of the input law; neither is an argument
which the fixed-branch sampler must recompute. In particular there is
no extra global denominator from m_tau in (2.2). This corrects an overly
pessimistic reading of the mass-preservation formula.

Conversely, after FORGETTING labels, the posterior formula
K(v,dy)=sum_h w_h(v) K_h(v,dy) has w_h=d tau_h/d(sum tau_h).
Those weights can have wider reads. A joint local construction should
retain the original chronological labels, rather than infer local
posterior weights from a signed or fully resummed density expansion.

3. NORMALIZATION DOES NOT ENLARGE EXACT READS, BUT DOES NOT ERASE THEM

If the unnormalized reference weight and its integration domain depend
only on e|D, then Z_h and the normalized reference depend only on e|D.
No locality is lost just by doing that integral. However, an interior
output observable can read EVERY part of D through the normalized law.

This is already visible for the exact finite Gaussian reference

 w(y;e) = exp[-(y^T A y+2 y^T B e+e^T C e)/(2g^2)], A>0.

Its normalized mean is -A^(-1)B e. For a symmetric quadratic mark D,

 K(y^T D y)
  = g^2 tr(D A^(-1))
    + e^T B^T A^(-1)D A^(-1)B e.                          (3.1)

Thus opposite-half exterior data affect a positive-half output whenever
the displayed response is nonzero. A concrete elliptic illustration is
the chain action (1/2)sum_(j=0)^n(a_(j+1)-a_j)^2 with a_0=e,a_(n+1)=0.
Its interior mean is e(1-j/(n+1)); the mean square of an interior discrete
curvature a_(j+1)-a_j contains e^2/(n+1)^2. This is an illustration of the
reference's boundary-response mechanism, not a separately verified
counterexample for every nonabelian native R branch.

In particular, a refill confined to the positive half may have zero
conditional reflected NOISE covariance while its backward expectation
reads negative-half INPUT. These are different obligations in the
reflection ledger. A bound on the changed region Lambda cannot establish
positive-half membership; the full X^read must lie in the permitted half,
or its crossing response must be estimated and compensated.

4. WHAT CAN BE BUNDLED INTO ONE COMPONENT NOISE

Suppose that, conditional on the complete prebranch state, component i
has a finite choice theta_i with nonnegative weights p_i(theta_i;e),
sum_theta_i p_i=1, followed by its normalized reference draw. The pair
(theta_i,v'_i) is then one component noise. There is no additional
selection-covariance term outside this noise.

For a product of such component kernels the complete input reads of
component i must avoid the active variables of component j, or else a
simultaneous product is not the stated kernel. Disjoint X_i suffice if
X_i are genuinely the complete reads; disjoint Lambda_i alone do not.
Sequential updates remain possible but their backward reads must be
composed in the actual order.

The outer reconstructed factor (1/N_i)sum_(...,G_i,T_i)chi_(k,Lambda_i)
is not automatically such a probability distribution. The required
pointwise identity for its weights must come from the original preceding
partition of unity, including any discarded/ineligible branch. Equality
of the final integrated mass is weaker. This is the precise branch
definition check passed to the source agent.

Deterministic component selection has no random-selection innovation
once the complete input is fixed. It can nevertheless have a large
input read. Pure random gauge restoration is free on true gauge-orbit
observables; a random physical reference choice is not free for that
reason alone.

5. AN ACTUAL SOURCE OF NONLOCAL BACKWARD READS

The native regular step itself supplies a warning stronger than a
generic example. B119(3.21) is initially nonlocal through V^(k), which is
obtained from the minimizing background. The source localizes the
characteristic by changing its fluctuation coordinates using H^(k).
The same type of operation is repeated during R preparation (secondary
locator Raw I80918--80924).

This creates the following exact alternative for a joint interpretation:

 * Keep the old physical coordinates. Then the original characteristic
   and its label can retain the nonlocal minimizing-background read.
 * Use the localized characteristic and the new integration coordinates.
   Then pull every old observable through the H-dependent coordinate
   change, and retain the actual Jacobian and action change in the joint
   law.

One cannot choose the localized label from the second description and
the unchanged observable from the first. A change of variables preserves
the integral with the transformed insertion; it is not an observable
identity obtained by dropping the transformation.

The source's bound on H on the changed fluctuation region gives a route
to QUANTITATIVE quasilocality. It does not give exact zero dependence
outside a fixed set of current coarse datum links: even a local read of
the global minimum U_(k+1)(V_(k+1)) must still be pulled back to V_(k+1).
An alternative strictly local joint realization is not ruled out here,
but it is not supplied by the displayed localization identity alone.

6. EXACT REAL WILSON-LOOP BOUND THROUGH THE CHANGE

Use a separate letter psi for the cutoff function to avoid confusion
with the coupling. The literal real group identity of B119(1.22), as
transcribed at Raw I80881--80886, is

 exp(i g_k A_e)
   = exp(i g_k A'_e) exp[-i psi(A'_e) H_e],  0<=psi<=1.      (6.1)

On the other changed bonds psi=1. This identity, not a truncated BCH
formula, controls a bounded Wilson insertion. In a fixed unitary
representation rho, Duhamel gives for real Hermitian generators

 ||rho(exp(-i psi H))-1|| <= |psi| ||d rho(H)||
                         <= C_rho ||H||.                  (6.2)

Multiplication by unitary surrounding links does not change this bound.
The potential difference has leading size H/g_k, but the link contains
g_k A. Hence (6.2) has neither an extra factor g_k nor a factor g_k^(-1).
If another lattice convention literally inserts eta H, that literal
angle must be used; an eta factor cannot be inferred from (6.1).

Let gamma be an oriented loop, counting repeated edges with multiplicity,
and w_gamma=d_rho^(-1)tr product_(e in gamma)rho(U_e), with or without
taking the real part. Telescoping the ordered product yields

 |w_gamma(U^H)-w_gamma(U^0)|
   <= C_rho sum_(e in gamma intersect S_changed) ||H_e||.   (6.3)

Reversed edges obey the same bound because inversion is an isometry on
unitaries. No commutativity between different edge factors was used.

If the same native frame also gives V_global,e=exp(iH_e)V_local,e, then
combining this identity with (6.1) gives the COMPLETE physical link

 exp(i g_k A_e)V_global,e
   = exp(i g_k A'_e)exp[i(1-psi(A'_e))H_e]V_local,e.         (6.4)

The residual is (1-psi)H; it vanishes where psi=1. Equation (6.4) is
used only when those are the actual matched background factors in the
insertion. Otherwise retain the separate exact background read. It is
not permissible to infer this cancellation for a separately gauged or
separately reconstructed background.

If h_k bounds H only on S_changed, (6.3) gives C_rho n_gamma,S h_k.
No bound on H elsewhere is needed or asserted. In the source situation
where h_k<=C epsilon_k exp(-R_k) on that set, a loop with a fixed number
of native edges has precisely this small error times its edge count.

For O_a=a^d sum_x f(ax) w_(gamma+x), the exact consequence is

 |O_a(U^H)-O_a(U^0)|
 <= C_rho a^d sum_x |f(ax)|
       sum_(e in (gamma+x) intersect S_changed) ||H_e||.    (6.5)

This retains the location of every changed edge. The coarser bound is
C_rho ||f||_(l1,a) |gamma| h_k. A fixed physical length can have
|gamma|=O(a^(-1)); therefore superpolynomial smallness in log(g_k^-2)
alone does not prove a UV-uniform sum over dates. A renormalization
factor Z_a multiplies the error by |Z_a|. Products of finitely many
bounded normalized loops obey the corresponding sum of their errors.
Already-backward functions require their own influence bounds; (6.3)
does not prove that they remain bare Wilson loops.

7. A NORMALIZER-FREE SENSITIVITY IDENTITY ON ONE ADMITTED BRANCH

There is a useful reduction for (2.1), but its domain conditions matter.
Suppose the branch is written on a COMMON parameter-independent slice
and fluctuation window, with weight w_e(z)>0 and output Phi_e(z).
Along a differentiable admitted parameter path,

 d_e K_e f
   = E_s[d_e(f o Phi_e)]
     + Cov_s(f o Phi_e, d_e log w_e).                     (7.1)

This is obtained by differentiating numerator and denominator together.
An e-dependent scalar in log w_e cancels from the covariance exactly;
in particular the large background action extracted in the reference
normalization is not a separate denominator loss.

For real f, the second term is bounded by
(1/4) osc(f o Phi_e) osc(d_e log w_e), with the safe constant 1/2 for
complex functions. Thus an actual exponential bound on the OSCILLATION
of the remote score would directly control normalized reference reads
without an additional lower bound for the old history mass.

The reference's fixed native fluctuation coordinates in B122-II(1.2)
are favorable for this calculation. However, (7.1) does not differentiate
through changes of the exterior gate chi_(k,Lambda), component geometry,
or first/second-class selection. Moving domains need their exact boundary
terms or a common-coordinate reformulation. Analyticity inside a branch
does not bound a jump of a sharp selector near its threshold. No uniform
remote-score estimate for all branches is established by this audit.

8. THE PRECISE LOCAL-SELECTION CERTIFICATE STILL NEEDED

The reconstructed eligibility rule (Raw I50478--50504) bounds the size of
the component acted on and also consults recent creation and waiting
history. A valid local-selection certificate would say:

 For every source read S, all branches that can change S, including their
 eligibility, preparation gates, full minimum data, and physical output
 maps, are determined by the input augmented state in
 S^{+C M R_k r_k}, with a fixed C and the same complete chronological law.

The component diameter condition alone proves only the output part of
this statement. The modified creating recursion in Raw I50316--50383
explicitly replaces the original decomposition by repeated bond/rim tests;
its stopping count has no bound there. The stabilized boxing definition
at 50087--50097 likewise supplies no uniform iteration bound. This shows
that a bounded local certificate has not yet been proved by those
definitions; it does NOT show that a clever certificate is impossible.
The original and modified operations must remain distinguished.

If the certificate were established and all reconstructed primitive
averaging reads were included, its physical backward-cone cost would be
manageable. For r_i=L^(i-n)r_n and
R_i<=C[1+log(gamma_n+D(n-i))]^r,

 sum_(i<=n) r_i R_i <= C_(L,r,D) r_n [1+log gamma_n]^r.    (8.1)

The proof is the convergent geometric series with the remaining age
dependence bounded by a fixed power of 1+log(1+age). This is uniform in
the UV starting date. Buffered source algebras could then be transported
while shrinking their buffer by this finite amount. It does not prove
the full unbuffered half algebra is preserved, and the actual H-dependent
read in Section 5 is one input which would instead need a tail estimate.

9. PRESENT CONCLUSION

At fixed labels the reconstructed R reference is compatible with a
local backward kernel on its COMPLETE component read, and componentwise
choice/refill noise can be bundled when its pointwise normalization and
cross-read exclusions are verified. The fixed-branch old mass is harmless.

The complete original-field half-algebra attachment is not established.
The explicit native localization shift is a concrete nonlocal read which
cannot be discarded with the characteristic functions. Its effect on a
bounded Wilson loop has the exact real bound (6.3), and a matched
background can improve it to the residual (1-psi)H in (6.4). What remains
is a uniform propagated influence/tail estimate for the actual backward
sources, together with the literal prebranch/selection joint-law check.
Neither localized action coefficients nor absolute large-field history
weights alone imply that normalized observable estimate.
