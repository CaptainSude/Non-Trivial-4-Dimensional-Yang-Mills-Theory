1. THE EXACT NORMALIZED OBJECT

original positive branch measure and Z=nu_y(1). For any bounded f,h,
in the commutative ring with t^2=u^2=0,

  nu_y[(1+t f)(1+u h)]/Z

Its logarithm has tu coefficient E(fh)-E f E h. The coefficient
therefore is the actual conditional covariance at the complete Z.
The unnormalized numerator, a product of separately normalized defects,
or a numerator missing tu fh would all be different objects. The
producer uses the correct one. Subtracting f(empty),h(empty) changes
neither covariance nor this mixed logarithmic coefficient.

3. BOOLEAN COEFFICIENTS AND THE 'NEITHER' ASSIGNMENT

  Delta_I f=sum_{J subset I}(-1)^(|I|-|J|)f(J),
