6. WHAT SPATIAL CONCLUSION DOES AND DOES NOT FOLLOW

The exp(-lambda s_pq) in (6),(9) is for the connected D_11 column.
It does NOT multiply the entire right-hand side of (12). Outer
covariances can connect the sources through the remaining random fields
at a different scale. Global deterministic scalar neutral values give
b_p=b_q=0, but the D/D covariance still requires its own estimate.

The elementary countercheck p_0=a_p Z, D_q=b Z for a centered random
sign Z shows why a small first defect and a bounded neutral value alone
give an outer covariance a_p b, independent of any nominated distance.
This is a logical test of the consumer, not a claimed native field
configuration. It rules out obtaining a connected-distance theorem by
attaching the two-root D_11 decay to all terms of (11).

The result is for a jointly admitted fresh p/q/r constructor. It does
not prove that every once-retired cohort, compiled neutral prefix,
full actual-prefix catalogue, or history flag has that presentation.
No all-date ultraviolet-uniform bound, neutral-law identification, or
continuum/existence conclusion is asserted. The independent contact
cannot be recovered by declaring every bounded raw mixed row to have
a bounded single-seed logarithmic contact.
