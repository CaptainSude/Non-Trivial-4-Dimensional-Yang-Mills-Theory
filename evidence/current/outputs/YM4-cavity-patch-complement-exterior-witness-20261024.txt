2. EXPLICIT FILLINGS USING NO EDGE OF C

Let D be the vertex box

For every loop gamma of B we construct a disk word using only
plaquettes in D that have NO edge in C, with at most12 length(gamma)

First lift each vertex vertically in direction4 to height x4=2.
Each oriented edge not in direction4 sweeps a strip with at most
three plaquettes. Direction4 edges give exact path cancellation and
need no plaquette. Normally the strip contains no cavity edge.

The only forbidden case is a direction1 or2 edge at height x4=-1
whose projection to x4=0 is an edge e0 of C. The strip then includes
the TWO adjacent (e0,4) faces at heights[-1,0] and[0,1]. Their common
edge is e0. Replace this two-square rectangle by the other FIVE
rectangular faces of the prism formed by extruding it from x3=0 to
x3=1. The replacement has eight unit plaquettes:

 - two on the opposite (direction(e0),4) face at x3=1;
 - one on each (direction(e0),3) end face at x4=-1 and x4=1;
 - two on each (3,4) face at the two endpoints of e0.

None has an edge in C. The first group has x3=1, the second has
x4=+-1, and the final groups have only direction3/4 edges. The
remaining strip between heights1 and2 also avoids C. Hence the
entire replacement of a lifted edge costs at most9 exterior faces.

This is a cubical disk replacement with the SAME perimeter. Its
orientation may be reversed when the original edge orientation is
reversed; curvature norms do not change. It is not a commuting or
Abelian Stokes argument. Elementary face moves write the loop as a
product of conjugated plaquette holonomies, with signs determined
by the disk orientation.

The lifted loop lies at x4=2, in the three-dimensional rectangle
[-1,2]^2 x [-1,1]. Contract it with ordered coordinate paths1,2,3
from the specific root o=(0,0,0,2). For a direction1 edge commute it
past at most |x2|+|x3|<=3 unit steps; for a direction2 edge commute
past at most |x3|<=1; a direction3 edge cancels directly. All these
plaquettes lie at x4=2 and avoid C. The top filling therefore costs
at most3 per loop edge. Together with the lifted strips this proves
