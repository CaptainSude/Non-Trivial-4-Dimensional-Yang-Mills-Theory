the physical inverse Wilson coupling while keeping the gauge width, original
cuts, independent field/current charts, pivots, P1/response definitions and
localization conventions fixed. The full independent-pair Gaussian extension
is constructed below; physical gauge identities are used only for its physical
Gaussian beta. No analyticity in an arbitrary already-existing coupling history
is assumed. The input coefficient ledger is held fixed and q NEW primitive

With the actual growing native windows, the resulting q-step beta satisfies

 |partial_x beta_q(x;E)|<=C_q x^-2,
 ||partial_x C_q(x;E)||_(fixed-cap coefficient norm)<=C_q/x.   (1)

All derivatives in this note mean the derivative of the explicitly FROZEN-
AUXILIARY Wilson-coupling continuation. Retuning a rounded logarithmic cut or
an auxiliary gauge width at the same time is a different derivative. The scalar
gauge normalizations and powers of the coupling are retained. C_q denotes the
neutral output coefficient ledger, or its prescribed finite-q assembled norm;
it is not a claimed uniform norm of an arbitrarily long unprepared old action.

The proof uses the already registered complete native Gaussian covariance and

       ||C||<=B_C,  ||K||<=B_K,

 D_E log I_(lambda,E)[h]=<Delta_old h>_(lambda,E).        (13b)

6B. THE REMAINING COMMON-NORM REQUIREMENT FOR NEUTRAL q-CONTRACTION

Equations (13a)--(13e) do not by themselves prove that the FULL neutral state
map is a contraction in its own input norm. In the literal stored-ledger norm,
the old dated coefficients remain stored unchanged under static transport.
That norm can therefore have static norm one, regardless of how their current
physical evaluation has decayed. A strict contraction requires a different,
coherent current static-orbit norm with BOTH of the following properties:

 (i) the literal OLD prepared fluctuation map obeys (13a) with B(h) bounded
     by that same state norm on its full independent-pair domains;
 (ii) its literal static state extension obeys the registered neutral
      classical contraction, with the same source quotient, all currents,
      own-domain caps, root allocation and high/low exponent reserves.

If these two properties are registered, the exact static-plus-newborn
decomposition and (13e) give
||D_E C_q|| <= C_static L^(-alpha q)+C_q/x,
so a fixed q and then a weak floor yield a number below one. This last
display is an APPLICATION FORMULA, not an unconditional conclusion of this
file: the existing projected source theorem uses OLD density with a NEW source
map, whereas (13b) differentiates the OLD unmarked preparation. Their equality
on physical minima alone does not identify their complete offgraph source
extensions or norms. The Holder/raw-copy source contraction cannot silently
be inserted for (ii) while (i) is still measured in the larger native ledger
norm. A new coherent unmarked continuation may solve this interface, but must
be explicitly constructed and checked.

Thus beta_E and newborn finite-difference estimates are supplied on the native
coefficient ball. A closed nonlinear neutral q-contraction in the requested
single current-state norm remains precisely the common preparation/static
norm interface above. No full complex-history or original-source ball is
claimed from these tangents.

7. WHY CO-SCALING THE GAUGE WIDTH IS NOT SILENTLY SUBSTITUTED

If one also changes the auxiliary gauge width, the literal B109 precision in
the same g0 coordinates is lambda D. Its independent-pair inverse is simply
lambda^-1 C, and its logdet change is a scalar. This is a convenient DIFFERENT
continuation. The accompanying normalized gauge insertion z(lambda), all
coupling powers and original masks must still be retained.

On an uncut physical gauge quotient the fixed-width and co-scaled-width
Gaussians differ only by their exactly computed gauge/scalar factors. With the
original free-coordinate cuts present, varying the gauge width changes a
conditional distribution. Equality of the full cut-conditioned maps does not
follow from (8). A separate tail/matching proof would be needed to replace one
map's derivative by the other's.

The theorem above avoids this substitution: (4), (6) and (9) construct the
Wilson-only fixed-gauge-width family directly, and all derivatives in (1)
refer to that family. At zeta=0 it is the actual old native core. If a consumer
defines its gamma derivative by simultaneous gauge-width or cut retuning, it
must either use this explicit frozen-label convention or supply that comparison.
No such extra comparison is asserted here.
