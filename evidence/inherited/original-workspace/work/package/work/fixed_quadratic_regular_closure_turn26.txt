8. WHAT THIS DOES NOT REMOVE

The following obstruction survives even for a finite family of fields.
A regular step creates functions, not merely 21 numbers. The fresh
fixed-radius core and running-radius tag are admitted in their original
regular prepared class. At first R contact they must be read on actual
old retained rows and possibly glued/interpolated fields, with colored
spectators, missing/restored terms and the original contour margins.
Physical equality on regular minimizing fibers does not supply those
values or their full native bounds.

The literal current clover has the one-date small-R insertion of turn24.
That does not re-enter all quantum-born functions or sum all old dates.
Nor do fixed-arity source derivatives, the real energy domination of Q,
or a polynomial regular forward bound control the normalized mass of
surviving histories. The source-bearing unnormalized operators, their
normalizers and actual available lifetime debit remain to be handled.

Consequently the improvement is concrete but limited: the homogeneous
regular one-linear-source calculation need not be confined to the weak
delta marginal estimate solely because its unmarked base contains tiny
running-radius R descendants. A fixed core plus the correctly retained
observable core can give (7.5). The full marked R/boundary producer and
the complete physical observable hierarchy are not proved here.
