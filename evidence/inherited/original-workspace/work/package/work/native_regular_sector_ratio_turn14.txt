NATIVE STABILITY AND A NORMALIZED REGULAR-CYLINDER MINORIZATION
19 October 2026. Fixed stopped volume; original weak-coupling hypothesis retained.

0. RESULT

The following normalized consequence does NOT require
proving that a surviving large-field history has small conditional mass.
Assume the original stability lower bound, interpreted as a bound of
positive measures, and the FULL integrated upper stability bound have the
SAME initial vacuum normalization, and assume the actual current cutoff
contains the common coarse box specified in Section2. On a fixed final
coarse torus these inputs imply

                  mu_eta >= alpha(T,g) nu_box,        (0.1)

where nu_box is product Haar restricted and normalized to a common small
coarse-link box, and alpha(T,g)>0 is independent of the preceding fine
refinement eta. The coupling at the stop is fixed, or belongs to a fixed
weak band bounded away from zero. The constant deteriorates exponentially
in the TOTAL stopped volume and may deteriorate as g tends to zero.

Consequences include a uniform lower probability for every cylinder with
positive nu_box mass; a lower bound for every fixed coarse-reference Gram
which is nondegenerate on that box; and preservation of these properties
in subsequential limits on this fixed coarse configuration space.

The measure-theoretic deduction is proved. Its attachment to the original
RT effective-state law remains conditional on the published weak-coupling
induction, the measure/volume interpretation in Section1, and the exact
localized cutoff-tower check isolated in Section2. The homogeneous action
bound does not by itself prove that last check. These are not statements
identifying that state with the deterministic native average of the bare
Wilson field. They give neither regular-sector dominance nor Gaussian
closeness, local conditional estimates in arbitrary exterior fields, or a
thermodynamic limit. A simple RP countermodel below shows why those stronger
conclusions cannot follow just from the stability sandwich.

1A. WHY THE SAME VACUUM FACTOR MATTERS

Write M_eta=C_eta Mhat_eta for the physical unnormalized effective measure,
where C_eta>0 is the source-independent scalar vacuum factor. Its possibly
large cutoff dependence cancels exactly in

          mu_eta=M_eta/M_eta(X_T)=Mhat_eta/Mhat_eta(X_T).

The regular numerator and the full denominator must use this same C_eta.
Renormalizing a selected history separately is not legitimate. The action
constants attached to different histories need not coincide; their
comparison is already part of the source theorem proving (1.1),(1.2).

A lower bound can alternatively be obtained before any signed localization
by retaining a positive regular contribution and bounding its complete real
action. It is NOT obtained by dropping negative Mayer/log terms. The
published lower inequality avoids that termwise-positivity ambiguity.
Moreover an empty CURRENT bad-domain label can contain healed histories
through the completed E/R activities. It is not identified here with the
microscopic event that no earlier field ever crossed a threshold.

2. A COMMON REGULAR COARSE BOX

The necessary geometric test is particularly small: it is homogeneous and
real. No independent-current complex contour or mixed-history retraction
is used. The source cutoff is built from uniformly localized minima; see
B119(2.11)--(2.17),pp256--257, especially the eta^2 plaquette threshold in
(2.17). The ordinary averaging constraints in (2.11) are the same ones
used in the homogeneous minimum construction.

The uniform homogeneous minimum estimate needed here is the following:
for a sufficiently small fixed coarse logarithm radius r_0, V=exp B with
||B||_infinity<=r_0 implies

 sup_p |U_eta(V)(dp)-1| <= C eta^2 ||B||_infinity,     (2.1)

and the same estimate holds for each localized minimum read in the
CURRENT small-field cutoff, with its fixed full read collar. Constants
are independent of eta and volume, after fixing the original localization
parameters. This is a classical native homogeneous minimum bound, not
a conclusion about typical configurations under the full law.

The archived classical_coarse_action_research.md and the ordinary-minimum
construction supply a complete physical C1-alpha bound O(||B||_infinity)
on a common ball. This gives the GLOBAL part of (2.1): each fine
plaquette is O(eta^2) times the physical first-derivative/commutator bound.
The global homogeneous bound alone does NOT identify the source's
localized minimum. In B119(2.16), the latter uses the minimal determining
tower based on the four-layer enlarged cube, with datum obtained from
the copied field Q^k V; (2.17) tests an inner enlarged cube. To deduce
the localized part of (2.1), one must check that this exact full datum,
including its graded boundary rows, satisfies the native minimum theorem
with parameter at most C||B||, uniformly in refinement, and that the
entire tested plaquette set lies in its top proper domain. Raw I
155039--155083 gives the intended application of CMP102 Theorem1(7)--(8),
but imports that datum regularity. This pass has not independently
verified the full primary datum hypothesis. A uniform analytic local
minimum map at the identity would also suffice, after verifying this
same input map and full tested collar. The localized part of (2.1)
therefore remains an explicit attachment premise here, not a consequence
of the global homogeneous estimate by renaming the minimum.

Since there are O(eta^-4 V_T) fine plaquettes and the Wilson plaquette
penalty is bounded by a fixed multiple of |U_p-1|^2 near identity,
(2.1) gives directly in dimension four

                  A_eta(exp B)<=C V_T ||B||_infinity^2. (2.2)

The fine eta^4 penalty per plaquette cancels the fine plaquette census.
No minimum Hessian inverse, scalar quantum estimate, or R-history norm
is used for this elementary action upper bound.

At a fixed weak stop coupling g, choose delta=c_0 g so small that
delta<r_0 and C delta is below EVERY current small-field threshold.
For thresholds g times a fixed positive logarithmic power this is
possible with fixed sufficiently small c_0. Define

 B_delta={V: each positive coarse-link logarithm has norm<delta}.

Then, uniformly in eta,

 chi_eta=1 on B_delta,
 A_eta(V)/g^2<=C c_0^2 V_T on B_delta.                (2.3)

The extra attachment between the source stability formula and this
box is exactly the localized part of (2.1) on its actual cutoff reads.
It is not certified in this pass merely by the published weak-trajectory
hypothesis. Once that native datum/top-domain check is supplied, no
additional mixed-history or normalized-surgery estimate is needed for
this deduction. If a modified scheme uses
other cutoffs, (2.3) must be checked again. We do not transfer (2.3) to a
different one-shot integration convention by renaming its radius.

For a stop band g in[g_-,g_+] with g_->0, take a common delta=c_0 g_- and
uniform source constants on that band. This yields the same argument.

3. THE NORMALIZED MINORIZATION, INCLUDING ALL POWERS

Let e_T be the number of independent positive coarse links and d_G=dim G.
Let h(delta) be Haar mass of the one-link exponential ball of radius delta.
For a fixed sufficiently small delta,

 c delta^(d_G)<=h(delta)<=C delta^(d_G),
 beta_delta=lambda_T(B_delta)=h(delta)^(e_T).          (3.1)

Define the genuine probability measure

        nu_box=beta_delta^(-1) 1_(B_delta) lambda_T.

Combining (1.1),(1.2),(2.3) gives, as an inequality of measures,

 mu_eta >= exp[-(a(g)+b(g)+C c_0^2)V_T]
                       1_(B_delta) lambda_T
        = alpha(T,g) nu_box,                         (3.2)

 alpha(T,g)=exp[-(a(g)+b(g)+C c_0^2)V_T] h(c_0 g)^(e_T)>0.

Replace the displayed alpha by min(alpha,1/2) if a harmless universal
probability upper cap is wanted. The formula itself is independent of eta.
For e_T<=C_d V_T,

 log alpha >= -[a(g)+b(g)+C_1+C_2 |log g|]V_T,         (3.3)

after absorbing the fixed c_0 and Haar constants. We do not replace a(g)
or b(g) by O(log g^-1) unless that stronger source dependence is separately
verified. At fixed g their finiteness is enough.

The proof uses no normalized individual live component and no global
complex zero-free function. Its denominator is the actual full positive
partition mass, bounded ABOVE, while its numerator is a genuine positive
measure lower bound. This direction of comparison is important.

The same calculation gives

 Mhat_eta(X_T)>=exp[-(a(g)+C c_0^2)V_T] beta_delta.    (3.4)

Thus the full normalized measure exists with an explicit denominator
floor in the same vacuum convention. It does not follow that the fraction
of the measure outside B_delta is small: (3.2) gives only a positive share.

4. CYLINDERS, FIXED COARSE GRAMS, AND FIXED-RESOLUTION LIMITS

For a set J of coarse links, write pr_J for the coordinate projection.
Pushing (3.2) forward gives

        (pr_J)_*mu_eta >= alpha(T,g) (pr_J)_*nu_box.   (4.1)

In particular, for every measurable local cylinder C_J,

 mu_eta(pr_J^-1 C_J)
 >=alpha(T,g) h(delta)^(-|J|)
           lambda_J(C_J intersect B_delta^J).        (4.2)

This is uniform in prior refinement, but alpha still contains the
regularization cost of ALL stopped links, not only J.

Let F_1,...,F_s be fixed bounded coarse functions. For c in C^s, (3.2)
implies the positive quadratic-form inequality

 E_mu |sum_a c_a F_a|^2
       >=alpha E_nu_box |sum_a c_a F_a|^2.            (4.3)

If their restrictions to the box are linearly independent in L2(nu_box),
their uncentered Gram matrices have a uniform strictly positive lower
eigenvalue. For centered covariances use the exact variational identity

 Var_mu(F)=inf_z E_mu |F-z|^2
       >=alpha inf_z E_nu_box |F-z|^2
       =alpha Var_nu_box(F).                         (4.4)

Thus independence modulo constants gives a uniform centered Gram floor.
This proof retains the identity direction and does not mistake a vanished
singleton mean for absence of a contact/variance term. Gauge-invariant
smooth functions of finitely many Wilson loops, nonconstant on the box,
are legitimate examples. A local faithful Wilson plaquette function is
nonconstant on a sufficiently small product box, whenever the chosen
coarse graph contains that plaquette. It therefore has a strictly positive
coarse-state variance floor, uniform in prior refinement at fixed T,g.

The configuration space X_T is compact. Any sequence of these positive
probability measures has weakly convergent subsequences. Their limits
retain (3.2): test against nonnegative continuous functions and use the
Riesz measure order. All fixed bounded continuous reference Grams also
pass to the limit. This proves a nondegenerate finite-resolution coarse
STATE, conditional on the actual source trajectory and stability inputs.

It is not continuum reconstruction. In particular no compatibility of
different stopping resolutions, original Wilson observable identification,
reflection positivity of the RT coarse law, local field normalization,
Euclidean covariance, or asymptotic freedom follows from this compactness
argument.

5. WHY THERE IS NO VOLUME-UNIFORM LOCAL CONCLUSION YET

The cost in (3.3) is extensive in total stopped volume. It tends to zero
as that volume tends to infinity, even for a cylinder on one fixed link.
One cannot cancel the exterior by dividing two unrelated extensive bounds.

A simple exact countermodel demonstrates the missing logical step. On
{0,1} times an exterior probability space with formal volume V, set

 rho_V(x,eta)=exp(cV) 1_(x=0)+exp(-cV) 1_(x=1).

Relative to product probability counting measure, this obeys the stronger
pointwise sandwich exp(-cV)<=rho_V<=exp(cV), but

                  P_V(x=1)=1/(1+exp(2cV))->0.        (5.1)

This is not a Yang--Mills counterexample or a uniformly bounded local
interaction model. It disproves inference of a local volume-independent
floor from the extensive stability bounds ALONE.

For a genuine local conditional estimate one needs a common exterior
factor, for example after integrating a complete hull D,

 M_reg(dV_D | eta_ext)>=L_D(eta_ext) nu_D(dV_D),
 M_full(X_D | eta_ext)<=C_D L_D(eta_ext),              (5.2)

with C_D depending on D, not total volume or prior refinement. The same
boundary constraints, coarea/reference normalizers and UV vacuum factors
must appear in both members. Source B122II(1.2)--(1.11) estimates a refill
normalizer; it is not by itself (5.2) for the actual full conditional law.
The unresolved normalized surgery comparison in
rooted_normalized_history_mass_turn3.txt is exactly of this relative type.

Arbitrary exterior data are another obstruction: the global lower cutoff
chi_eta can vanish there. No positive conditional cylinder floor follows
on those fibers merely from a lower bound on globally regular fields.

6. THE SANDWICH DOES NOT GIVE REGULAR DOMINANCE OR A FLOW LAW

There is a particularly sharp fixed-graph countermodel. On a compact
reflection-compatible Wilson lattice with Haar probability, let

                    rho_g(W)=exp[-S(W)/g^2]+1.       (6.1)

It satisfies exp[-S/g^2]<=rho_g<=2. Its unnormalized law is reflection
positive, being the sum of the Wilson and Haar RP laws. Yet, when the
Wilson zero set has Haar measure zero, its Wilson partition mass tends
to zero as g->0 by dominated convergence. The normalized law (6.1)
therefore tends to Haar, and every fixed nonzero-energy event of positive
Haar measure keeps positive probability.

Thus even positivity, RP and a pointwise energy-lower/constant-upper
sandwich do not imply Gaussian concentration, small bad-field probability,
or that the normalized regular part approaches one. This is a logical
countermodel to a proposed implication, not a statement that the source
density actually has the extra term1.

Likewise the measure lower bound does not supply a local smooth density
or a Hessian estimate. It permits singular extra sectors. Even bounded
upper and lower densities permit arbitrarily rapid oscillations, such as
1+epsilon sin(Nx) in a local coordinate example, whose score derivatives
grow with N. Consequently the real conditional-flow theorem still needs
an actual relative action/score estimate for the complete law. Minorization
alone cannot be used as that analytic input.

7. WHICH LAW IS CONTROLLED: T VERSUS RT

B119 pp243--244 explicitly states that T averages and R changes the
effective density in large-field regions. Therefore the source theorem
controls the law with state density generated by (RT)^k. It does not
automatically identify it with the deterministic native block average
(M^k)_* of the original Wilson law.

The native construction uses real reference normalizers and integrated
equivalence operations, including singular gauge-slice measures. These
facts alone do not certify a positive normalized Markov realization of
the COMPLETE R operation on one specified common configuration space.
The Part-I operation/measure dictionary is still conditional in the
current source audit. If such a kernel realization and exact total-mass
identity are separately supplied, it may be augmented by history
variables; even then its dual fixes1, not every observable. To infer a
statement about F(M^k U_bare), its exact
transported insertion must be retained through R, including all changed
supports and normalizers. The bounded coarse function F(V_k) occurring
in (4.3) is a different observable unless that matching is proved.

No positivity is assigned here to an individual localized Mayer term,
and no signed source coefficient is treated as a conditional probability.
The nilpotent upper-stability lift controls coefficient norms when its
joint input assumptions hold; it neither strengthens (3.2) into (5.2)
nor identifies a bounded coarse-state function with its microscopic
observable precursor.
