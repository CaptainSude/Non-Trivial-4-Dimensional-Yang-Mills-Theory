6. LOCALITY, CERTIFICATE AND INDEPENDENT-PAIR LIMITS

Tree gauge does not reduce the read of A. If A is proved supported on
a finite complete W, (14) is supported on that same W with its tree
paths. The first bare local principal kernel, however, can depend on
the entire coarse field through its background minimum. A finite
active noise region is not by itself a finite outer read. The
currently proved safe instance is therefore a FINITE full outer
datum (possibly the whole torus), or a separately registered truly
finite-data kernel. No ambient-volume-free carrier norm is obtained.

Nor does (16) prove a quasilocal positive carrier decomposition. A
global tree can turn a local edge derivative into a long path read.
To produce local coefficients one needs an actual localized source
or minimum/action expansion before the tree construction, with its
source-to-read connectors and positive norms paid. The finite
complex price P_I may depend on ambient volume and is retained.

The patch complement remains an original source/event component.
The smooth canonical cutoff used for a real C1,1 rare source cannot
be imported into an analytic coefficient: a nonzero holomorphic
function cannot have compact support in a connected complex chart.
Multiplying by a smooth chart selector destroys the stated
holomorphy. The analytic emission is used on its declared domain;
the exact complementary row must be retained separately.

Finally, beta holomorphy along the physical map
beta -> (U_min(beta),J_phys(beta)) does NOT give a holomorphic native
coefficient on independent (U,J). A proposed occupied-body treatment
of a bounded REAL source may legitimately tolerate nonanalytic noise
marks (as in the completed annulus expansion), but it still needs:

 (a) one common source-compatible inverse/fixed-real integration
     formula with the COMPLETE cancelled off-graph action, current,
     constraints, pivots and coarea holomorphic in independent U,J;
 (b) its same-domain zero-free/holomorphic norm bound, with the
     actual tube/read prices and all raw scalar factors retained;

     determining-data dependence before the native positive tree sum.

Analyticity on a physical constrained submanifold neither determines
such an extension nor bounds its transverse derivatives. The first
bare formulas suggest a finite small-tube construction, but (a)--(c)
are not proved by the present gauge argument. Gauge invariance is
one necessary interface, not the full independent-pair attachment.

7. ONCE-BORN SMALL-RADIUS PRICES

Suppose the incoming amplitude already has eta_in=exp(-c r_g^2),
r_g=ell_g^p, ell_g=log(g^-2), p>1/2. A fixed m-th Cauchy/Ward
operation on(17) costs at most a fixed power of rho_can^-1 and
the finite read size. If its actual total price is bounded by

 C g^-Q ell_g^A (1+read size)^B                           (18)

with fixed exponents, the inverse-g and logarithmic part is absorbed
while retaining the displayed polynomial read factor. If additionally
read size<=g^-Q0 ell_g^A0 for fixed Q0,A0 (or that factor has a
separate proved positive-tree reserve), the entire price is paid:
eta_in times(18) is <=C exp(-c'ell_g^(2p)) for any c'<c after a
weak floor. More generally log(read size)=o(ell_g^(2p)) suffices.
This is the elementary comparison
Q log(g^-1)+A log ell_g=o(ell_g^(2p)). A g^2-size tube merely
increases the fixed inverse-g exponent. Up to a fixed fifth-order
analytic Ward operation, this is still a once-born finite price.

This absorption is conditional on the actual read price in(18).
It does NOT absorb an arbitrary ambient-volume factor, a read growing
exponentially in g^-2, an unbounded differentiation order or a product
of ordinary constants over arbitrarily many dates. It also cannot
assign the NEW tail of E to A: if the incoming bounded f has no rare
factor, A has only its original amplitude. Its Cauchy price must be
paid by the original analytic source theorem or another genuine bound.

The native Ward theorem additionally requires the supported own-pair
coefficient class, complete tensor/flat marginal extraction and the
current-image certificate. On that class a narrow birth tube can be
paid once; repeated re-projection would create new emissions with
their own distinct prices. No uniform source induction follows from
the finite gauge construction alone.
