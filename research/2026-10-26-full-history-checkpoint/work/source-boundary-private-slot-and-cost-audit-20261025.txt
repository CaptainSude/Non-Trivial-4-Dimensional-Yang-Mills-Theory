1. WHICH PRIVATE VARIABLE IS ACTUALLY CHANGED

At an ordinary step k->k+1, the old tuple in B_k(U_k,A_old) consists of
retained A_i from earlier operations. Equation(3.15) introduces the NEW
current A_k through the reconstructed U_k argument. The change(1.22),
used again after(3.18)--(3.19), changes this new A_k, not the tuple A_old.
Consequently ordinary old-B Cauchy is in the actual U,J slots with A_old
held fixed. No analyticity in a stored A_i is implied or needed here.

The smooth cutoff g(A') in printed252 is explicitly C-infinity, with a
transition at the scale delta_k/g_k. It is not a holomorphic function of
A'. In the cutoff-removal transformation the new real integration variable
A' is fixed while the small H parameter is analytically varied. The factors
g(A') and its actual Jacobian derivatives are retained. A generic complex
Ad rotation or complex translation of this cutoff-decorated variable is
not justified by the pair-analytic B axiom(2.42).

The analogous preliminary step is equally explicit. Large-I187(1.53)
introduces a NEW B'_j from V_j, then linearizes and rescales it to B_j.
Printed188 states that there is ALSO the integration over the OLD A_j,
the first integral in(1.25). The cutoff-removal change(1.56)--(1.59) acts
on the new B_j coordinate. It does not translate the old A_j. A failed
branch retains its new coordinate as a private parameter in later steps.

Thus no generic complex-A domain is necessary for these literal coordinate
changes. If a proposed alternative actually changes an old cutoff-decorated
A_i, it needs a separate derivative/extension proof and is outside this
conclusion.

2. THE OLD-A INTEGRAL IS STILL NOT A SMALL U/J RESPONSE

The unchanged-coordinate fact does not imply a delta'/alpha gain for the
whole preliminary source logarithm. Large-I(1.60) has the static action
A_k^(n)(U_k^(n+1),A_j=0), while the additional real integral still acts on
the full A_j dependence. A bounded smooth nonconstant function of A_j,
independent of U,J and placed on an admissible boundary carrier, satisfies
the stated pair-analytic value axiom. Its Gaussian expectation minus its
value at A_j=0 can have order-one size, regardless of the new B_j noise.
Multiplying it by a small source amplitude gives the same counterexample
to an automatic delta'/alpha gain for the source variation.

This is a logical limitation of the generic axiom, not a counterexample
to the literal native constructor. A proof may either establish additional
scaled-A structure for that constructor or retain the whole old-A source
dependence as an exact private integral. It must not infer the missing
gain from the U/J Cauchy estimate alone.

With A_old fixed, split exactly

 B_i(Phi_noise,A_old)=B_i(Phi_0,A_old)+Delta B_i.       (1)

The prescribed direct/far B109(3.13) extension has H_s(0)=0, so Phi_0 is

For Delta B_i, the support to be estimated is the UNION of the complete
old boundary carrier and the response path to current noise. Either the

it. The mixed-layer distance therefore pays the grade separation before
Cauchy and root counts are taken. On a historical layer n the margin is
beta 2^{-(j-n)}alpha_n. A conservative bound pays 2^{j-n}, the determining-
root census L^{4(j-n)}, and polynomial radius/date factors. Choose the
fixed mixed geometric decay larger than their exponential rate, retaining
separate output-carrier reserve. Summing layers then gives

 p(Delta B_old)<=C(delta_j/alpha_j)
       sum_(i<=j)(1+j-i)^P exp[-c(j-i)] b_i.          (2)

There is no derivative in A_old in(2). A new deterministic H^(k) shift
is a different boundary insertion with its actual forced e^{-R_k} read

Equation(2) is the appropriate ordinary difference bound in the joint

4. A PRECISE PRIVATE-BLOCK ALTERNATIVE

The old-A source factor can be kept in one unnormalized component block
through the preliminary integrations, with every original cut, reference
denominator, retained exterior parameter and gauge return unchanged.
Composing the literal operators retains the old factor once; it does not
require a new logarithm/normalization of that factor at each stage.
Every genuine additional response/cutoff insertion is counted once at
its actual birth. Interrupted components retain the corresponding partial
block. The final Large-II(1.61)--(1.72) localization acts on that same
complete block, including all outside connections.

For actual histories the safe cost convention is DATED. If a is a genuine
primitive component/stage record, write
 W(a)=M^d R_(i(a))^(d+1)[d'_(i(a))(X_a)+1].
Keep the actual native determining-root incidence at its date. The bound
needed by the source block is

       |G_block|<=C r C_src(Y) sum_(a in live records) W(a). (3)

This is precisely a fixed increase in the chronological coefficient of
the native live-reserve induction. Every previously retained block has
its original identifier/debit; it is not added again at every new stage.

For ONE minimal chain with a separately verified per-stage root count,
N<=C R_k and the slow variation over that polylogarithmic window reduce
the last-N part of(3) to C r C_src M^d R_k^(d+1). This simplification is
NOT valid for arbitrary branching/merging histories merely because the
final component is small in current units: many fine-date components can
fit inside it. No such unproved current-scale conversion is used here.
Keep the dated sum in(3) unless the actual component incidence theorem
supplies the stronger conversion. The native lifetime proof is designed
to pay the dated sum with its actual ancestral tokens and forecasts.

An already stored old source block is not added N times to this sum.
Its existing whole-action/component bound is retained once. The source
Wilson mismatch is kept separately as deltaGamma[S_new-S_old]; its real
coercive part is not bounded by a small analytic sup on all old variables.

If the exact block carries the pointwise bound(3) on the actual complex
exterior image, its exponential is bounded before private integration by
the corresponding native cost tilt. The larger positive lifetime induction
then applies. No C^N product of normalized posterior norms is needed.
The complete source block must be exhibited in the action identity: merely
asserting boundedness of each successive normalized logarithm is not this

This is a concrete sufficient repair, not an inference that the original

5. POLYNOMIAL SOURCE RADIUS: REQUIRED QUANTIFIERS

For the packing consumer an arbitrary finite C_src(Y) is insufficient.
The local supplier must prove, for fixed D0 independent of the later
rarity target,

             C_src(Y)<=C(1+Y)^D0.                    (4)

The mixed small-R admission already asks for a master radius at most
c E_src^-1(1+Y)^(-kappa_R/2). Combining it with(4) permits

 r_abs(Y)=c E_src^-1(1+Y)^(-d),
 d>=kappa_R/2+D0,                                   (5)

with d fixed BEFORE any desired rare power or source packing. This radius
is bounded below by the displayed fixed polynomial after c,d are fixed.
The live cost coefficient is reserved before Y; only r_abs is chosen
after Y. Terminal partition constants may be arbitrary finite functions
of Y because they are not inserted into the lifetime forecast.

The following native prices do not cause a nonpolynomial C_src:
 fixed scalar-localizer circles and finite protected cluster constants;
 historical 2^age margins paid by the completed mixed decay in(2);
 primitive g/alpha ratios and finite fixed-order inverse-alpha extractors;
 dated preliminary root counts, charged as in(3), not exponentiated once
 per integration; and actual H-shift e^{-R} times finite g/log powers. A generic
inverse full partition function or arbitrary old-A analytic extension
would not have this property and is not available as a shortcut.

Rare sourced families should be handled on a prescribed polynomial MASTER
disc. Their uniform stretched-log bound gives a derivative price at most
the inverse master radius on a fixed smaller disc, hence a fixed power of
Y. Do not introduce an arbitrary inverse of a radius that is itself still
being chosen, or require every rare value bound to contain a factor r.
Its uniform small rare cost can be reserved before the final small source
radius is selected. This prevents a spurious circular inequality.

The completed joint proof still has to list which finite primitive
inverse-radius powers occur and verify the private-block pointwise bound
on its actual mixed images. The discussion above isolates the two genuine
requirements rather than labeling the whole source family closed from
ordinary U/J preparation alone.

6. A CAUSAL CHI SUPPLIER FOR A FRESH HEALED COEFFICIENT

A master value bound plus Cauchy in an arbitrary source direction gives
a slope, not a spatial chi_k. The following stronger formulation supplies
the required factor without using a bound on the coefficient being born.

Construct the strict source induction uniformly for EVERY finite stopping
date n and EVERY constant anchor s in a smaller fixed constant disc. At
endpoint n its nonconstant radius is
 rho_n=c E_src^-1(1+x_n)^(-d), with d fixed as in(5),
and the older profile weights are chi_i^n=L^{-(n-i)/2}. The same literal
source constructor is used for all these parameter choices. Its functions
therefore agree on intersections of the admitted source classes; changing
the stopping index changes an estimate, not the observable or the germ.

At current date k, first prove the fresh completed R'_k VALUE estimate
on the endpoint-k class around s. Its inputs are strict prior births and
the current ordinary-T outputs. The new healed V_k^R has not yet been
filed and is not an input to this value estimate. The entire old-private
source factor remains unnormalized as in Section4. Suppose this gives

 |R'_k(H;u)|<=m_k exp[-kappa d_k(H)]                  (6)

on that class and all the original scalar-localizer contours, with the
same bound on the actual endpoint-N class. This is the fresh rare VALUE
step supplied by the enlarged-cost real component majorant and its native
connected exponentiation, not a source derivative estimate.

Let the complete source read of H have diameter D in CURRENT k units,
and let z be its source anchor. For an actual profile w in the endpoint-N
class put s=w(z). Its values on that read satisfy

 |w(p)-s|<=C r_abs(Y) chi_k^N D.

The fixed direction v=w-s on the read can be extended to the finite source
lattice with sup/Lipschitz norm at most
C r_abs(Y) chi_k^N(1+D) in endpoint-k units. For example extend the real
and imaginary components as Lipschitz functions and clip outside the
read's amplitude range. This is used only for a bound on the already local
coefficient; it is not its definition. The fine-grade Lipschitz norms gain
L^{-(k-i)}, which is stronger than the required chi_i^k. There is no count
of microscopic source coordinates in this extension estimate.

Consequently the single coherent source path u_tau=s+tau v is admitted on
the endpoint-k class for

 |tau|<=c rho_k/[r_abs(Y) chi_k^N(1+D)].              (7)

All old Gamma, beta, vacuum and cohort source arguments vary on this SAME
path. None is frozen at its actual-w value while another is set to s.
Since the construction is source-local, u_1 and w give the same H
coefficient. Its source holomorphy is compatible with old A remaining real
parameters; no A-contour change or private source normalization is used.

If the radius in(7) is at least2, Cauchy applied to(6) yields

 |R'_k(H;w)-R'_k(H;s)|
 <=C r_abs(Y) chi_k^N (1+x_k)^d (1+D)
                         m_k exp[-kappa d_k(H)].     (8)

If that radius is smaller, use the separate value bounds at w and s.
Their sum is at most2m_k exp[-kappa d_k(H)], which is bounded by the
right side of(8) after changing its fixed constant. Thus no large-read
case is omitted. The source-read moment 1+D is paid by one fixed spare
carrier exponent, not by a volume or a new exponent loss at every step.
Subtracting the field identity value to file V_k multiplies the bound
by at most2; its scalar companion has the same bound and is retained.

Because d is fixed and m_k is stretched logarithmic, the coefficient in

fresh-healed chi forcing needed by the V recurrence. It is NOT obtained

For an unhealed private block use its value/cost bound, not a fictitious
