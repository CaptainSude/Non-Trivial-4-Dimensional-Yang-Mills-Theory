RAW-COPY TRANSPORT DOES NOT GIVE A NEUTRAL AGE GAIN ON THE C1 PAIR DOMAIN
25 October 2026. Exact finite lattice counterexample; scope correction.

Claim refuted. The noiseless new map copies the same raw bond field U
and regrades the independent current. Its full independent-pair output
norm on the present hereditary class does NOT in general have a strictly
decaying neutral age kernel. Higher Holder tests on a COMPACT REFERENCE

This does not contradict native minimum reconstruction, the one-step

1. DOMAIN AND SCALING

Fix integer L>1 and age n. In current units take the four-torus of side4,
fine mesh h=L^-n, N=4/h fine sites per direction. In birth units the same
torus has mesh1 and sideN. A link is unchanged by raw-copy transport.
The current is zero, so every regrading of that independent slot is zero.

Use the flat compact reference I. It satisfies every lower-grade weighted
reference test, including special second and Holder tests. Let eps>0 be
a fixed sufficiently small number, below the current fixed source caps.
With k in (Z/NZ)^4 and s(k)=sin(pi k_1/2), put

 U_2(k)=diag(exp(i eps h^2 s(k)), exp(-i eps h^2 s(k))),
 U_1(k)=U_3(k)=U_4(k)=I,               J(k)=0.          (1)

This is periodic because N is divisible by4. Its current-unit logarithm
relative to I is B_2(k)=eps h s(k) diag(1,-1). Consequently

 |B|<=eps h,  |nabla_h B|<=2 eps,
 h^-2 |plaquette(U)-I|<=C eps.                         (2)

Thus (U,J) is an admitted independent pair in the fixed-radius class
D_n(rho_core) for eps sufficiently small, uniformly in n. Earlier output
grades are admitted as well, since their normalized field and derivative
are smaller under the exact engineering rescaling. The possible size of
the ACTUAL current of U is irrelevant: the domain expressly admits an
independent J. Setting J=0 does not assert that this field is a minimum.

The difference of adjacent values of nabla_h B is O(eps). Therefore its
Holder alpha seminorm grows as eps h^-alpha for every alpha>0. The
domain's Holder tests concern I, not B, and do not exclude (1).

2. A LOCAL, GAUGE INVARIANT SCALAR NEUTRAL SOURCE

Let P_12(k) be the oriented 12 plaquette based at k. Transport the next
plaquette to the same base point by

 Q(k)=U_1(k) P_12(k+e_1) U_1(k)^-1.

Define the fixed local formula, with no age-dependent coefficient,

 e_k(U)=1-(1/2)Tr[P_12(k) Q(k)^-1].                    (3)

Both matrices transform by the same conjugation at k; hence (3) is
gauge invariant. It is holomorphic in complexified links, with a fixed
finite complete carrier and a uniform birth-domain coefficient norm.
It is independent of J. Its vacuum value and first derivative vanish.

For a fully scalar lattice family, average (3) over the finite signed-
permutation orbit of its complete oriented loop geometry. Call the
result e_sym,k and denote the number of terms by m. Translation of the
root and the full orbit average give joint translation/reflection/axis
covariance. On real SU(2) every term is nonnegative, since it is
1-(1/2)Tr of an SU(2) matrix. The orbit contains (3), so the total averaged
source is at least 1/m times the total of (3).

The quadratic field kernel of (3) is the square of the first difference
of linearized plaquette curvature. In momentum notation the unaveraged
quadratic form has the factor

 |exp(i p_1)-1|^2 |d_1 A_2-d_2 A_1|^2.                (4)

Its total momentum degree is at least4. Equivalently it vanishes on
every affine-potential test at quadratic order, because linearized
curvature is then constant. Its Maxwell/degree-two scalar is zero. This
property is retained by the orbit average. In physical source variables
with independent leg momenta the same two curvature differences give
the requisite higher-order vanishing. This is a genuine neutral scalar
source, not an arbitrary nonneutral coefficient called neutral.

Define F(f,U)=sum_k f_k e_sym,k(U), retaining its original numerical
root measure. At birth mesh1 this measure is the ordinary site sum.
Each rooted carrier has bounded diameter, a bounded number of roles,
and age-independent analytic norm; the complete birth norm is uniformly
bounded in N. There is no telescoping cancellation making F(1,U) zero.

3. EXACT VALUE AND ROOT CENSUS

For (1), P_12(k) is diagonal with angle eps h^2(s(k+e_1)-s(k)).
The sequence of first differences is 1,-1,-1,1, repeated. Its next
difference has values -2,0,2,0. Exactly half of the sites therefore give

 e_k(U)=1-cos(2 eps h^2),

and the remaining half give zero. With N^4=256 h^-4,

 sum_k e_k(U)=128 h^-4[1-cos(2 eps h^2)]
                  -->256 eps^2  as n-->infinity.      (5)

The symmetric source sum is bounded below by 1/m times (5). In
particular it stays bounded away from zero for a fixed nonzero eps.
If the fixed native collars require a larger current torus, choose side
4Q with a fixed integer Q large enough to admit them. Then N=4Q/h and
both sides of (5), including the limit, are multiplied by Q^4. The
positive lower bound per unit current volume is unchanged.
The factor h^-4 is the ORIGINAL rooted-source census per fixed current
volume; it must remain in the norm. Dividing by that census would change
the family and would not be an estimate for the declared source.

Any complete current-carrier norm controlling the source absolute sum
on a current torus of volume4^4 bounds |F(1,U)| by a fixed multiple of
that volume times its norm (assign each nonempty carrier to one current
cell). Hence a uniform claimed bound

 ||S_[n,0) F||_current <=C L^-alpha n ||F||_birth,
                    alpha>0,                          (6)

would force (5) to tend to zero. This is impossible. More generally,
there is no uniform neutral static age bound tending to zero for this
general C1 pair class and the literal raw-copy zero-noise map.

4. EXACT CONSEQUENCES FOR THE RESEARCH LEDGER

The all-cap image theorem still proves where sources can be evaluated;
it never gives decay by itself. The flat physical coarse-A pullback
still controls its stated smooth minimum family. It does not control
the supremum over arbitrary pairs such as (1). The native TURN77 age
gain uses an actually regular reconstructed field, including its
C1,alpha control; it cannot be imported merely from equality at the
physical minimum or from a raw-copy semigroup identity.

The one-step fixed-core quantum estimate may also remain valid: it
bounds T_C-S, while (6) concerns S. Combining that estimate with an
unproved decaying S bound would nevertheless be invalid. Therefore
the long-age source consumer and the mixed-current renewal assumption
remain OPEN on the current domain.

Two concrete repair routes remain:
 (a) strengthen the actual RELATIVE field domain by a uniform
     [nabla B]_alpha bound and prove all original-noise/scalar response
     images in that stronger domain; then prove the neutral static
     estimate with its full gauge/current/support bookkeeping;
 (b) use a genuinely smoothing native static preparation, proving the
     corresponding original-cut offgraph source identity and all
     independent-current/localizer images.

Neither route is a mere rewording of the existing C1 image theorem.
The present counterexample is excluded by (a). Establishing its
response-image and source-consumer claims remains a distinct proof.
