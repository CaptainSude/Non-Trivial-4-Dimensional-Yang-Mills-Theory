2. EXACT DESCENT WITH SOURCES AND A SEPARATE INHERITED TAIL

Fix a finite source polydisc and a source-independent real sector,
including its maps, exterior data, charts and cutoff weights. Suppose
on the full input field polydisc

  E_raw(u,zeta)-E'_raw(u,zeta)=c(zeta).                (2.1)

Here c is holomorphic and independent of u. Let D(u,zeta) be an
additional inherited factor, perhaps defined only on a proper open
subset Omega of that polydisc. Keep D IDENTICAL for the two inputs.
Assume every real integration field and every complex contour used in
the asserted continuation belongs to both the E domain and Omega.

Write the actual finite integral schematically as

  I_E(v,zeta)=integral W(u,v)
       exp[-gamma(zeta) S(u)+E_raw(u,zeta)+D(u,zeta)] dnu_v(u).

All measure factors are included in dnu_v. Then exactly

  I_E(v,zeta)=exp[c(zeta)] I_E'(v,zeta).               (2.2)

Proof: (2.1) holds at EVERY admitted input argument; pull out its
constant exponential. Where the real sector proves the identity, use
the same zero-free branch and analytic continuation to the asserted
connected domain. Equivalently it holds directly on each admitted
fixed contour. Normalized output logarithms have the same quotient
class. No positivity of a complex kernel is required.

This is the finite descent lemma WITH the original source. It does
not require D to have a connected-carrier decomposition, or to be

3. EXACT SOURCE-ANALYTIC REPRESENTATIVE SELECTION AT A FIXED RADIUS

The pointwise choice of a near-minimal representative for every source
value need not depend holomorphically on that value. This can be repaired
without a selection theorem and without spending radius at each step.

For rho>0 and a Banach space B, define

  W_rho(B)={x(zeta)=sum_(n>=0) x_n zeta^n:
                       sum_n rho^n ||x_n||_B<infinity},
  ||x||_W=sum_n rho^n ||x_n||_B.                      (3.1)

It is a Banach space of holomorphic functions on |zeta|<rho. Scalar
W_rho is a Banach algebra. Products or bounded multilinear maps obey
the corresponding coefficient-convolution norm bounds. For finitely
many sources use multi-indices and rho^alpha; the proof is unchanged.

THEOREM 3.1. If q:A->Q=A/N is a Banach quotient with its quotient norm,
then coefficientwise q gives an isometric quotient

        W_rho(A)/W_rho(N) = W_rho(Q).                (3.2)

In particular for every x in W_rho(Q) and epsilon>0 there is ONE
source-holomorphic family E in W_rho(A) with q(E)=x and

        ||E||_W <= ||x||_W+epsilon.                  (3.3)

Proof. The quotient map is norm-nonincreasing coefficientwise, so the
left infimum is at least ||x||_W. Choose lifts E_n of x_n with

  ||E_n|| <= ||x_n|| + epsilon 2^(-n-1) rho^(-n).

Then their weighted norm sums to at most ||x||_W+epsilon and their
power series converges in A on the source disc. The kernel consists
exactly of series with every coefficient in N. Equality of normalized
totals as functions of field and source is equivalent to these
coefficientwise null identities, since finite-regulator evaluation is
continuous and Taylor coefficients are unique. This proves (3.2).

For real data, a conjugate-reflection average makes the lift real and
does not increase its norm. Every coefficient has the required total
gauge and spatial identities if x does; the representatives themselves
need not be coefficientwise invariant. A field-independent raw
constant c(zeta) obtained by comparing two lifts is holomorphic, since
finite raw evaluation is a continuous functional.

No field radius, carrier weight, or source radius changes in (3.3).
There is no accumulating approximation: q(E)=x is EXACT. The small
lift tolerance is a norm allowance, not an error in the density.

For initial admission, if a B-valued function is bounded by H on a
larger disc |zeta|<rho_plus, Cauchy gives

  ||x||_(W_rho(B)) <= H/(1-rho/rho_plus),             (3.4)

and if x(0)=0 the right side improves to
H (rho/rho_plus)/(1-rho/rho_plus). This one initial restriction is
not repeated by (3.2). With p independent source variables the bound
from a supremum polydisc norm instead costs the product of p such
factors. Hence (3.4) alone is not a uniform many-local-source theorem.

4. THE ACTUAL FIXED-COUPLING QUANTUM ESTIMATE LIFTS TO THIS SPACE

The finite descended G_j is analytic, not merely continuous. Indeed

This also proves analyticity of the quotient map without choosing a

Here is a quantitative lemma sufficient to reuse the old induction.
Suppose G:B_Q(a)->Q' is analytic and L-Lipschitz there, G(0)=G0.
Write its expansion G(x)-G0=sum_(n>=1) A_n(x,...,x). One-variable
Cauchy and the diagonal bound ||G(x)-G0||<=L||x|| give

  sup_(||x||<=1)||A_n(x,...,x)|| <= L a^(1-n).

Complex polarization gives

  ||A_n|| <= (n^n/n!) L a^(1-n) <= e^n L a^(1-n).

For x,y in W_rho(Q) with norms at most r<a/e, coefficient convolution
and the n-term telescoping difference of each multilinear expression
give the absolutely convergent estimate

  ||G(x)-G(y)||_W
    <= [e L/(1-e r/a)^2] ||x-y||_W.                 (4.1)

In particular r<=a/(4e) gives a factor less than 5L. Every linear
classical map, beta functional and extraction projection acts
coefficientwise with its ORIGINAL norm. The exact reference telescope
(1.2) holds coefficientwise, with the SAME theta_q. Thus the old
finite induction runs on W_rho(Q_j) after replacing nu by 5nu and
choosing b small enough that its full O(b^2) intermediate envelope
lies within a/(4e). Couplings can then be decreased as before. The
source radius rho remains unchanged through every permitted step.

This proves an actual extension for a small inherited analytic source
activity at fixed real couplings and the original fixed windows of the
old local theorem. It is not just an assertion of a new conditional
recurrence: (3.2) constructs exactly equivalent analytic inputs and
(4.1) proves the norm estimate needed to use them repeatedly.

If the base kernel also depends on a prescribed holomorphic source
parameter t(zeta), use the same construction in the product Banach
