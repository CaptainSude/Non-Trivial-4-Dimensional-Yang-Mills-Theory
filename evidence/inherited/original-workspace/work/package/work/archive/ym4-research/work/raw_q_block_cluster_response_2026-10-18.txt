10. PARITY REFINEMENT OF THE SAME HOLomorphic MACRO RESPONSE

The crude estimate (9) suffices for scalar positive block drift. A
stronger estimate, useful for sourced coupling shifts, follows from
the already established bounded whole-family analytic construction.
No Gaussian moment regulator or exponentiation of an unbounded cubic
mark is needed. Write mu=||X||_{a,b}, delta=gR/b, and

 epsilon_base=C_q(gR^3+D_q g^2 R^2).

 ||L_X||_pre<=C_q delta mu,
 ||Q_X||_pre<=C_q delta^2 mu.                                (19)

One can obtain (19) by expanding X(Bhat_q(w,z t))-X(P_qw) in z:
its prepared norm is <=C_q mu for |z|<c_q/delta, and its constant
coefficient is zero. Summing terms of degree at least two gives the
second estimate. The coarse-carrier compression and support reserves
are spent once for this larger noise ball; no derivative of X is
introduced. Every retained characteristic function is invariant under
the simultaneous reversal x_r -> -x_r for all r.

Let A(V) be the source localized connected log of the centered static
q-Gaussian with the actual inner retained windows and bounded prepared
potential V, including the cutoff-only log. On a fixed prepared ball
||V||<sigma the construction in Sections5--6 gives a holomorphic map

For the pure linear mark, A(z L_X)=A(-z L_X). Indeed the centered
Gaussian, its complex centered continuations, the retained/bad-set

simultaneous noise reversal. In every localized term the first
derivative at zero therefore integrates an odd function and vanishes.
Equivalently the localized output family may be symmetrized in z;

 ||A(L_X)-A(0)||<=C_q ||L_X||_pre^2.                         (20)

The two-parameter Cauchy formula, on the fixed whole-family budget,

 ||A(V+L_X)-A(V)-A(L_X)+A(0)||
                   <=C_q ||V||_pre ||L_X||_pre.              (21)

For example use radii sigma/(4||V||) and sigma/(4||L_X||) for
the two amplitudes; the mixed difference keeps only terms containing
both amplitudes. Their absolutely convergent geometric sums prove

 ||A(V+L_X+Q_X)-A(V+L_X)||<=C_q ||Q_X||_pre.                 (22)

Using V=V_base,q in (20)--(22) proves the full holomorphic estimate

 ||N_q(X)||_{a,b}
 <=C_q [delta^2 mu + delta^2 mu^2 + epsilon_base delta mu].   (23)

For mu<=K b^2, the mu^2 term is absorbed into the first term. Thus
the logarithmic small factor can be delta^2 rather than delta. This
is a bound in the same analytic activity norm as (9), not just a
second-background jet. The gauge quadratic mismatch is even; the
possibly odd cubic, Jacobian, and noisy-background terms are all in
V_base,q and are accounted for by (21). No term has been omitted from
the normalized ratio.

 1/2<t<s<q_min,        2(s-t)>1.

s=1+2e, and t=1/2+e/2. Then s<q_min, t>1/2, and

 epsilon_base/delta=C_q[b R^2+D_q g b R] ->0.

Hence (23) is bounded by C_q delta^2 mu for sufficiently weak
coupling on the admitted moving envelope. Fresh births F_r and the
