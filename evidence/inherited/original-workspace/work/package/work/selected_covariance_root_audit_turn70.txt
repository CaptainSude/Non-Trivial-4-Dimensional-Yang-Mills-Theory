1. THE LAW AND ITS REVERSE CONDITIONAL KERNELS

Keeping all factors in rho_(j+1)=int rho_j q_j makes
K_j=rho_j q_j/rho_(j+1) a probability kernel. With the
actual terminal restriction chi_n, the product of these kernels
and the true terminal density telescopes to
chi_n rho_0 product q_j/Z_sel. No raw determinant, ball mass,
coarea factor or source vacuum is discarded in that identity.

This is a restriction of the actual positive selected construction.
It does not require a lower bound on its probability under the
full Wilson law. Such a lower bound would be a different issue.
The explicit terminal restriction and all state-local regular
data are part of the theorem. Future native factors that reread
an eliminated variable would require an enlarged state and
are not silently admitted.

2. SOURCE CHRONOLOGY AND CONDITIONAL COVARIANCE

The relation f_j=I_R f_(j+1) makes every transported X_j
the conditional mean of one fixed original insertion. Merely
resampling an unrelated smooth profile at each stage would
not give this exact identity.

The decreasing sigma-fields of the backward path give
orthogonal conditional increments. Their cross-products
give precisely the sum of conditional covariances and
the retained terminal covariance. This is an application
of the law of total covariance to an actual finite law,
not a claim that connected diagram coefficients are positive.

Input source-only constants cancel from these covariances.
They still belong to the raw source-density ledger.

3. WHY THE ONE-STEP RAW BOUND IS THE NEEDED INPUT

D_E^2 log E_muT exp V is the covariance of the two inherited
insertions under the same normalized selected density.
Subtracting the saddle values from each insertion changes
neither covariance nor its scalar value at flat background.
Normalizing the OUTPUT field functional would remove that
scalar covariance and would give the wrong observable.

Each inherited difference vanishes at auxiliary g=0.
Two marks therefore start at order g^2; the retained even
analytic expansion gives the same conclusion. Fixed g and
inherited-input margins control the coefficient uniformly.

The proof marks the actual coefficient occurrences before
summing the absolute connected majorant. Every resulting
carrier contains both source roots and all physical reads.
Its strong coefficient norm is bounded by the product of
the two strong one-mark norms. This spatial conclusion
does not follow from scalar analyticity alone.

The once-fixed weaker exponent is used only in estimating
and averaging covariance contributions. It is not fed back
into the RG induction, so there is no accumulating loss.

5. WHAT THIS DOES NOT IMPLY

Fine conditional increments have a vanishing ultraviolet tail
inside each regulator. Coarse transported observables and
coarse probability laws can still vary with the cutoff, so
this is not a cross-regulator Cauchy argument.

Off-diagonal covariances can have either sign. No positive
surviving lower bound follows. A positive, reflection-invariant
selected law need not be reflection positive.

The literal operator is the four-corner weighted original
action. Any additional cutoff-dependent calibration must
be inserted into the estimates explicitly.

Finally the full Wilson law contains omitted histories.
The new selected upper bound does not control their
contribution or establish a full-law comparison. These
limits are explicit in both producers.
