TURN95 -- ONE NATIVE R AND THE ACTUAL REFLECTED BOUNDARY KERNEL
21 October 2026. rp95. Bounded feasibility audit; no authoritative edits.

VERDICT

Exact reflection positivity of the first native R on new-current,
gauge-invariant half-space cylinders is UNDECIDED by this audit. I did
not obtain a literal half-space factorization, nor an actual native
counterexample. In particular, neither a hard cutoff in one reference
nor positivity of that reference answers the question for the full R.

The finite problem can, however, be reduced without any invented
transition kernel. Equations (3.1)--(3.5) below specify the complete
reflected boundary measure from Large-I (1.99)--(1.102), including the
unreplaced branch, actual old masses, normalized new references, graph
averages and components crossing the mirror. The subtraction formula
(4.1) is an exact identity for THIS operation. It also gives a useful
one-step defect estimate depending on actual writes into an observable's
support, not every variable read when choosing the branch.

This is not a new cutoff-uniform RP estimate. The missing quantitative
input is the complete basic-replacement event/oscillation estimate;
first-class counts alone do not supply it. The calculation gives no
reason to abandon the original-law route in favor of RT as the primary
construction, and it does not rule out asymptotic RP for a direct RT
construction.

2. THE COMPLETE POSITIVE ONE-R ROW

Fix a finite compatible torus and a reflection theta preserving the
coarse grid. Choose the coordinate/graph and cutoff prescriptions in
reflection-related families, with the normalized positive average
specified after (1.100). The source has a reciprocal notation typo in
the printed N_i convention; use the declared average, with weights
summing to1, as already checked in TURN60. No individual graph is
silently identified with its reflection.

Resolve the COMPLETE positive input representation, before localization
into signed connected coefficients, by H. A label H records the full
component/history catalogue and the native gauge/coordinate choices.
It includes the n=0/no-replacement alternatives and every failure or
retained branch of the intermediate partitions of unity. The index is
not only the first-class catalogue introduced later in Large-II.

For H with replaced components X_i and interiors Lambda_i, write the
corresponding actual old measure as

           sigma_H(de) tau_H,e(dx,dxi).                    (2.1)

Here e contains the retained current exterior and all outer integration,
choice and inverse-coordinate data needed for this resolved positive
row. The variables x are the OLD current interiors to be removed, and
xi are the older/private slots. Formula (2.1) is the positive resolved
version of the old integrals in (1.76),(1.99),(1.100), with their common
complete exp A_k'' and any remaining outer operators still included.
It is NOT a product of independent component old masses. Let

        A_H(e)=tau_H,e(1),
        Z=sum_H int sigma_H(de) A_H(e).                    (2.2)

The exterior admission gate chi_(k,Lambda_i) of (1.75), the other old
cuts, old tree insertions delta_(T_i), old V_h integration and T_h
operations, and chi'_i belong in (2.1) exactly once. In particular,
the exterior gate is not the new relative-link window in (1.101).

At these fixed data the actual component reference from (1.100) is

 w_i(e,y_i) = delta_(G_i)(y_i)
    1_{|log(y_i(b))/i| < M_0 epsilon_k for b in Lambda_i}
    exp[-g_k^(-2) A(zeta_i,
          U_(k,X_i)(y_i V_(Lambda_i)(e)))],

 z_i(e)=int_(Lambda_i) w_i(e,y_i) dy_i,
 q_H,e(dy)=product_i [w_i(e,y_i)dy_i/z_i(e)].             (2.3)

Both numerator and denominator use the identical actual slice/window.
The function V_(Lambda_i)(e) is the exterior-determined minimum in
Proposition1, printed194, not a function of the removed old x_i.
The localized minimizing interpolation U_(k,X_i), cutoff zeta_i and
all its determining data remain in the displayed action. They are not
replaced by a bare nearest-neighbor Wilson action or a free Gaussian.

The factorization of the NEW references at fixed complete e does not
factorize the old action, old histories, the exterior marginal, or the
two reflected halves of a single crossing component.

Let V^o_H(e,x,xi) be the reconstructed old CURRENT configuration and
V^n_H(e,y) the output current configuration. The latter is y_i
V_(Lambda_i)(e) on the new relative slices, with the actual gauge
transport restored. On gauge-invariant observables the current field
outside the union of Lambda_i is unchanged. Keep inverse gauges if
working in coordinates; treating y_i itself as the physical link is
incorrect. The actual complete coupling is

 Pi_H = Z^(-1) sigma_H(de) tau_H,e(dx,dxi) q_H,e(dy).     (2.4)

For unreplaced rows use V^n=V^o, or a Dirac identity current kernel.
The sum of (2.4) has old marginal equal to the incoming law and new
marginal equal to the basic R' law, on the gauge-invariant algebra.

The preceding switches and boundary expansions do not introduce an
extra cutoff error: Large-I printed193 explicitly states that they
preserve rho_k. TURN87 already verifies the first-new-cut invariance
and the exact complete old-cut pullback. Those facts are not undone
by this audit. The first density-changing operation is integration of
V_k|Lambda, followed by the displayed normalized reference insertion.

Large-II's subsequent first/second-class split must be summed in full.
Localization/exponentiation represents the same unmarked expression;
the first-class reverse Faddeev--Popov insertion preserves complete
gauge-invariant tests when its actual gauge record is retained. This
does not license replacing T'_k(X) by its mass before its remaining
exponential in (1.72), as the text immediately below (1.72) stresses.

3. THE REDUCED REFLECTED KERNEL

Let E_+ be current links strictly in the chosen positive half and let
X_+ be their compact gauge-orbit space. Write pi_+(V) for the restriction
followed by quotient. Closed half-space spin networks give a separating
algebra of continuous gauge-invariant functions on this space. For a
configuration V set

      P_theta(V)=(pi_+(theta V), pi_+(V)) in X_+ x X_+.

The reflected boundary measure is the pushforward of the complete
current law by P_theta. Its exact post-R expression is

 K_R = Z^(-1) sum_H int sigma_H(de) A_H(e)
                 int q_H,e(dy) delta_(P_theta(V^n_H(e,y))).       (3.1)

This is a measure-valued boundary kernel; no unsupported density or
absolute-continuity claim on a gauge quotient is needed. Formula(2.3)
is its actual component integrand. In particular a component crossing
the plane contributes ONE joint integral over its whole relative slice,
with its ONE denominator z_i(e), not two normalized half integrals.

For every bounded gauge-invariant positive-half cylinder F=f o pi_+,

      Q_R(F)=int conjugate(f(u)) f(v) K_R(du,dv).        (3.2)

RP on the stated algebra is exactly positivity of these quadratic forms.
Equivalently, for every finite collection of closed half-space spin
networks S_alpha, the matrix

 G^R_(alpha,beta)
 = Z^(-1) sum_H int sigma_H(de) A_H(e) int q_H,e(dy)
     conjugate(S_alpha(pi_+(theta V^n_H(e,y))))
                    S_beta(pi_+(V^n_H(e,y)))             (3.3)

must be positive semidefinite. Uniform approximation and the finite
measure then pass this to continuous invariant cylinder functions;
bounded measurable versions require their corresponding L2 closure.

The old boundary kernel is

 K_o = Z^(-1) sum_H int sigma_H(de)
           int tau_H,e(dx,dxi)
                    delta_(P_theta(V^o_H(e,x,xi))).       (3.4)

Subtracting gives the actual reduced correction

 K_R-K_o = Z^(-1) sum_(H with replacement) int sigma_H(de)
   int tau_H,e(dx,dxi)
      [int q_H,e(dy) delta_(P_theta(V^n_H(e,y)))
                      -delta_(P_theta(V^o_H(e,x,xi)))].  (3.5)

All unreplaced branches cancel in (3.5), because they are the same
measure on both sides. They are still present in K_o and therefore
in the complete K_R. Removing them from an RP test would be invalid.
Likewise isolating a single crossing component of (3.5) and proving it
has a negative direction would not establish failure of the sum(3.1).

If the complete incoming resolved measure is reflection invariant and
the full construction is reflection equivariant, pairing H with theta H
makes this kernel Hermitian. This requires the compatible homogeneous
prefix symmetry as well as the reflection-related graph average; graph
averaging alone does not repair an asymmetric incoming law. Hermiticity
is not positivity.
No source formula produces a factorization of (3.1) as an integral of
outer products with a nonnegative coefficient.

4. AN ACTUAL ONE-R DEFECT BOUND

Write B=||F||_infinity and Delta_+F=F(V^n)-F(V^o),
Delta_-F=F(theta V^n)-F(theta V^o), with expectation in the complete
coupling sum_H Pi_H. Direct subtraction, with old mass retained, gives

 Q_R(F)-Q_o(F)
   =E[conjugate(F(theta V^n)) F(V^n)
                    -conjugate(F(theta V^o)) F(V^o)].    (4.1)

Using a product difference once, rather than separately estimating
three expanded terms, gives

 |Q_R(F)-Q_o(F)| <= B E[|Delta_+F|+|Delta_-F|].          (4.2)

Let S be the complete current-link support of a gauge-invariant
cylinder F and let W_H be the union of replaced current interiors,
including their actual boundary-bond convention. By(2.4), modulo the
complete gauge transports,

       W_H intersect S = empty  ==> Delta_+F=0.          (4.3)

Thus

 |Q_R(F)-Q_o(F)|
  <=2 B^2 [P(W_H meets S)+P(W_H meets theta S)].          (4.4)

For a reflection-covariant complete lift, both probabilities agree
and the bound is4 B^2 P(W_H meets S). If Q_o is RP, its negative
post-R part is bounded by the same expression. No independence of
the old source, branches or new references is used.

This is a useful support distinction: an adaptive decision reading
across the plane does not itself change an observable whose current
links were not rewritten. A crossing component farther from a buffered
support than its entire write domain has exactly zero contribution
to(4.1) for that support, even though it can contribute to the density
and normalizer representations. Exact half-locality of the remaining
selection kernel does not follow from this support fact.

When deterministic coordinate oscillations d_b(F) are available on the
full product link space, or on a domain containing every hybrid
configuration used in the link-by-link interpolation, telescoping gives
the weighted version

 E|Delta_+F| <= sum_b d_b(F) P(b in W_H).                (4.5)

This is still a one-current-step statement. It does not identify an
earlier original observable with this current cylinder, and it does
not bound the accumulated effect of every earlier or later replacement.

No cutoff-uniform bound for the right sides of(4.2)--(4.5) has been
established here. In particular the first-class insertion bounds from
Large-II do not count all basic-R redraws: both eventual classes have
already received a reference, and the low-success second-class issue
from TURN64 remains. An unweighted cell census times a weak-coupling
tail envelope is not automatically summable over microscopic scales.

5. WHY THE SOURCE DOES NOT YET DECIDE THE SIGN

The unreplaced, accepted and crossing branches are now explicit enough
to state a correct finite test. The still-unevaluated objects are:

 (a) the actual complete old mass A_H(e), including the N-step input
     history and coupled old exp A_k'';
 (b) the reference action evaluated on the nonlinear minimizing map
     U_(k,X_i)(y_i V_(Lambda_i)(e));
 (c) the exterior-dependent normalization z_i(e), used before the
     boundary integration;
 (d) the complete sum over retained and replaced histories.

For a crossing X_i, (b) and(c) couple the two halves. For a component
on one side, its reference is component-local at fixed data, but the
actual complete selection/old-integral row is not shown to be a
positive-half measurable kernel of the original input. Global adaptive
coordinates alone are not a proof of physical nonlocality: cancellations
must be checked after the complete gauge/source transport. Likewise,
the exact first-cut repair is not a proof of this whole factorization.

The source's small external exponent in Large-II(1.73)--(1.75) controls
stated common-slot integrals. It does not give a nonnegative character
expansion of (3.1). An entrywise small perturbation of a positive Gram
matrix also does not establish positivity on the entire infinite
spin-network algebra; no uniform positive spectral floor is available.

6. FEASIBILITY AND WHAT WOULD ACTUALLY SETTLE THE AUDIT

The formula is a finite-dimensional, completely specified native
integral once an admissible RG prefix, its geometric constants and
coupling trajectory are fixed. It is feasible in principle to evaluate
selected matrix elements, but it is not a one-plaquette calculation.
The implicit minimizers, retained old histories and very large minimal
component cannot be replaced by arbitrary test densities to simplify it.

A literal positive conclusion would need a factorization or an operator
positivity argument for the FULL kernel(3.1). A literal negative
conclusion would need one finite half-space spin-network combination
with Q_R(F)<0, with errors controlled against all other branches. A
numerical finite Gram test without certified remainder can suggest a
candidate but cannot establish positivity for the whole algebra.

A weak-coupling Gaussian calculation might be a bounded next diagnostic,
provided it uses the actual blocked precision and boundary problem,
not the nearest-neighbor Gaussian excluded in TURN15. Even a positive
leading matrix is insufficient for all tests, and a negative leading
replacement term alone is insufficient against the full incoming form.
No such calculation is claimed in this note.

Finally, exact finite RP is stronger than what a direct continuum
construction needs. A bound making the negative part of(4.1) vanish
for each fixed physical test family could suffice when combined with
common limits and the other reconstruction inputs. Formula(4.2)
shows precisely why that alternative returns to an actual normalized
observable-transport estimate. Finite-R failure, if later established,
would reject exact inheritance only; it would not be a YM4 no-go.
