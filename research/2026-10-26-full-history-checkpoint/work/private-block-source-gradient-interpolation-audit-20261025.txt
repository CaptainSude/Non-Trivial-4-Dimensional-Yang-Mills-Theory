3. WHERE CHI MUST ALREADY OCCUR

Fix one complete opened selected history H at date k, an anchor z and a
constant comparison source w_z=w(z). The read H must include ALL original

The bare source satisfies the actual physical Lipschitz inequality

This is a BEFORE-Cauchy factor. Subsequent input ledgers must preserve the
corresponding dated gradient estimate. In a first-exit proof it is a
stopped inductive bound on the full coefficient/private-block difference,
not a conclusion from its source-zero value.

In particular, mere analyticity and a uniform bound on a sup+Lip source

The actual input to the fresh-R interpolation is the already registered

7. STRONGER SIMULTANEOUS RETERMINALIZED-PREFIX ROUTE

There is a stronger alternative to Section4's coefficient-ledger input
gradient. It obtains the factor from PREVIOUS-PREFIX source domains and
does not assume the gradient of the fresh output being estimated.

Keep ONE literal from-bare construction, independent of a stopping label:
same input functions, cuts, field domains, geometry, counterterm prescription
and connected-log branch. Prove the prefix bounds simultaneously for all
admissible terminal labels J, on source balls W^J_s around every constant
anchor s in a fixed interior constant-source disc. Their shape norm is
 sup |v|+Lip_J(v)<=r_J, with r_J>=c(1+x_J)^(-d), d fixed.
The stopping label changes this proof norm, not the coefficient functions.
All primitive phases, including preliminary and partial R phases, must
be included in that simultaneous induction.

Suppose the prior phases for the fresh output at k have already been
constructed and bounded on all these balls, hence on W^k_s. Take an actual
w in W^N_0, N>=k, and a complete source-read H. Put s=w(z) at an anchor
z in that read. In the date-k metric,

 sup_H |w-s|+Lip_k(w|H)
    <=C r_N chi_k^N [1+diam_k(H)].                     (2)

This is just physical metric conversion: Lip_k=chi_k^N Lip_N. Choose s
inside the fixed constant-source margin. Apply the real McShane extension
separately to Re(w-s) and Im(w-s) on H, then clip each extension to the
corresponding supremum range. The resulting fixed complex direction v
equals w-s on H and has global sup+Lip_k norm bounded by the right side
of(2), with at most a fixed factor. The construction is on the actual
periodic metric; no unwrapped distance across a short seam is used.

Now evaluate the FRESH primitive at s+tau v. For

 |tau|<c r_k/[r_N chi_k^N(1+diam_k H)],                 (3)

 <=C (r_N/r_k) chi_k^N(1+diam_kH)
                    exp[-c p0(g_k)]                  (4)
