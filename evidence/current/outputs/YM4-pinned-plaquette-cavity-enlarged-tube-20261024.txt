1. EXACT FOUR-DIMENSIONAL CAVITY GEOMETRY

Each fine link belongs to six unoriented plaquettes in dimension four.
Among plaquettes containing an edge of p, the plaquette p itself contains
all four such edges. No other elementary plaquette contains two edges of p:
two adjacent edges determine p uniquely, and the opposite edges have the
same unique elementary square. The nonwrapping assumption avoids small-
torus identifications. Therefore there are exactly

                  1+4(6-1)=21                                  (1.1)

incident plaquettes: p and twenty others. Each of the latter reads one
interior link and three exterior links, so |B|<=60, including all repeated
incidences only once. Orientations can invert an interior link, but h(U^-1)
=h(U). With normalized trace tr=Tr_fund/2 and h(U)=1-tr U, the exact action
at identity exterior is consequently

 H_I(U_1,U_2,U_3,U_4;1)
       =5 sum_(i=1)^4 h(U_i)+h(U(partial p)).                    (1.2)

Every summand is nonnegative on SU(2); h(U)=0 iff U=1. Thus the identity
quadruple is the UNIQUE global minimum. There is no residual interior
gauge orbit to divide out: the exterior physical links are held fixed.
Global conjugation fixes the minimum and does not create zero directions.

Write U_i=exp(i A_i) near identity, with A_i in the real three-dimensional
Hermitian traceless Lie algebra and its fixed Euclidean norm. Then
h(exp i A_i)=1-cos|A_i|=|A_i|^2/2+O(|A_i|^4). The Hessian in these twelve
coordinates is

                  D_*=5 I + L_p^T L_p,                         (1.3)

up to the explicitly fixed color norm convention; L_p is the oriented
sum giving the linear plaquette curvature. In particular D_*>=5I in
this convention. Globally, h(U_i)>=c dist(U_i,1)^2 for the geodesic
distance in its principal range. Formula (1.2) gives a uniform pinning
gap away from every fixed small neighborhood of the minimum.

For an explicit normalization check take A_i=q_i.sigma with
tr(sigma_a sigma_b)=delta_ab. Then L_p(q)=q_1+q_2-q_3-q_4 after choosing
the four orientations consistently. D_* has eigenvalues 9 with
multiplicity3 and 5 with multiplicity9. Normalized SU(2) Haar has
coordinate density 1/(2pi^2) at q=0, so J(0)=(2pi^2)^-4. The leading
flat coefficient below is therefore exactly
(2pi)^6 (2pi^2)^-4 / sqrt(9^3 5^9), multiplying beta^-6.
