1. AN ALREADY PROVED NONABELIAN INPUT

The archived classical_coarse_action_research.md, Section1, constructs
the actual homogeneous constrained Wilson action V_M(B) using the
nonlinear minimizing extension with its complete nonlinear averaging
constraint. Its fixed-slice multiplier m_M=DV_M obeys, on a common
complex coarse-field ball,

 m_M(0)=0,  Dm_M(0)=q_M,
 ||Dm_M(B)-q_M||_(infinity->infinity)<=C ||B||_infinity.   (1.1)

The constant is independent of the fine refinement M and volume.
The action is real on the real field space, and Dm_M is its symmetric
ordinary-coordinate Hessian with the stated physical pairing. These
are classical statements; the cited archive explicitly excludes the
fluctuation determinant and quantum effective action. The prior source
gauge/minimizer provenance qualifications remain in force.

The physical fine plaquette count does not destroy (1.1): its action
density carries eta^4, and each fixed physical cell contains O(eta^-4)
plaquettes. The archived classical_local_vertex_bounds_research.md,
Section4.2, proves this normalization before taking vertex derivatives.

2. A FIXED FINITE GAUGE/HARMONIC SLICE

Fix a finite coarse block/periodic regulator before taking M large.
Use a FIXED LINEAR flat transverse slice and remove or fix the harmonic
coordinates. Denote its isometric embedding by J_R. On that slice put

 P_(M,R)=J_R^T q_M J_R.

The uniform ellipticity comparison of matched_free_precision_heat_turn9
gives lambda_R I<=P_(M,R)<=Lambda_R I, with lambda_R>0 independent of M.
The finite regulator and its boundary/quotient convention are part of R;
no volume-uniform infrared gap is asserted. If an actual chosen boundary
does not give these inequalities, this argument requires its own gap.

Restrict the classical action to B=J_R x. Symmetry and the row-norm
bound in (1.1) give

 ||J_R^T[D^2V_M(B)-q_M]J_R||_(2->2)
                                      <=C_R ||B||_infinity.

Here a fixed component/norm-equivalence factor is allowed; it is
independent of M. Now y=P_(M,R)^(1/2)x and

 U_(M,R)(y)=V_M(J_R P_(M,R)^(-1/2)y).

The constant and linear jets vanish and its quadratic jet is |y|^2/2.
For |y|<=2b, ||B||_infinity<=C_R b. Hence

 ||D_y^2[U_(M,R)(y)-|y|^2/2]||<=C_R b.                  (2.1)

All constants are uniform in M for fixed R. Choosing b small puts the
whole ellipsoid inside the original common analytic branch. Higher
derivatives on a reserved fraction follow by ordinary finite-dimensional
Cauchy estimates; their constants may depend on R and derivative order.

Thus local_convex_completion_exit_turn10 applies to this ACTUAL
homogeneous nonabelian classical reduced action with delta=C_R b.
In particular a real convex completion agrees with it on the smaller
ellipsoid, and has the quantitative exit bounds stated there. The
classical perturbation is not an arbitrarily chosen toy potential.

3. A QUANTITATIVE REGULAR-CORRECTION CRITERION

Suppose, additionally and on the same actual finite chart, a regular
quantum density has the literal form

 exp[-V_M(B)/g^2+E(B)] dB,

with all relevant Jacobian factors included in E. Let E be holomorphic
on a coordinate domain of radius a, and suppose its local analytic bound
on the COMPLETE block and collar is K(g). On a fixed smaller domain,
Cauchy gives ||D^2E||<=C_R K(g)/a^2. Its contribution to the reduced
action U=V_M-g^2E therefore has relative Hessian size

                   delta_E<=C_R g^2 K(g)/a^2.           (3.1)

If a=g(log g^-2)^q and K(g)<=C(log g^-2)^P, this becomes
delta_E<=C_R(log g^-2)^(P-2q), which tends to zero if P<2q.
The smaller b=g(log g^-2)^s ellipsoid fits in the reserved a domain
when s<q, beyond a fixed floor. This is enough for small high-sector
operator errors after choosing R. It is NOT an O(g^2) marginal-flow
estimate, and summation over scales needs its own reference transport.

A general E also has constant and affine jets. The constant changes
normalization only. Its affine jet must be retained and its actual
critical point/shift controlled before using an origin-centered exit
estimate. Gauge-invariant total actions may supply a zero linear jet
at the zero field in some homogeneous settings, but fixed exterior or
individual localized components do not acquire it by declaration.

The preceding P<2q condition concerns the REAL small-Hessian/finite-jet
route. It does not imply that the completed law's GLOBAL density ratio
to the Gaussian tends to one. That stronger comparison uses
delta_E b^2/g^2=O((log g^-2)^(P-2q+2s)) and requires P+2s<2q.
The real conditional-jet argument in
interacting_finite_block_jet_stability_turn10 instead translates each
fiber to its actual constrained critical point. The critical-action
factor cancels between the conditional numerator and denominator,
leaving a Gaussian quadratic remainder of size O_R(delta_E). Hence
that finite-jet result does not require the stronger density-ratio
condition. Its finite scaled higher derivatives, common chart and
affine-force hypotheses still have to be checked.

Section3 is a criterion, not a checked assertion about the complete
native E or T history sum. The specific K(g), original domain, matching
volume/Jacobian, and all read supports still have to be supplied.

4. PRECISE LIMIT

Section2 attaches the classical part of the proposed finite-block
perturbation theorem to an existing actual Wilson-minimizer result.
It does not show that restricting the action to a flat linear slice
already constructs the physical nonlinear gauge-quotient measure.
It does not integrate fluctuations, prove their Jacobian bounds, or
control the surviving large-field histories. Those are measure inputs,
not consequences of classical convexity. The new completion is an
auxiliary real construction and does not alter old frozen native kernels.
