PUBLISHED COMPLETE DENSITY: THE MINIMAL IMPORT ROUTE
17 October 2026. Focused source audit and elementary reductions.
Authoritative output files and the public checkout were not edited.

DECISION

The potentially shortest route through the full-density obstacle is to use
the original complete-density construction, with its own homogeneous coupling
recursion, and prove positive cumulative drift for that recursion. This can
avoid transferring our entire local map to arbitrary mixed histories.

Our Notebook 37 does not currently prove that source-specific assertion.
Its positive coefficient and shooting theorem concern our actual local map.
The word "homogeneous" alone does not identify two integrals. A different
cutoff, contour gauge, retained window, or extraction convention requires a
comparison theorem. The candidate below therefore is a change in proof
strategy with a sharply defined missing input, not a claim that H2 is closed.

2. THE EXACT SMALL TARGET WITH THE LARGE PAYOFF

Fix ONE original source scheme: averaging, real cutoff, gauge normalization,
vacuum convention, Wilson normalization, extraction, and finite/thermodynamic
convention. Use the homogeneous action sequence defining the couplings of
its completed RT construction. Do not quietly replace its cutoff by ours.

Write gamma_j=(g_j)^(-2) and let e_j denote the complete inherited homogeneous
state, including whatever history the source definition reads. Define d_j^B
to be its ACTUAL inverse-coupling decrement:

  gamma_(j+1)=gamma_j-d_j^B(g_j,e_j).

The useful new target is the following source-specific finite-block assertion.

SOURCE BLOCK DRIFT (missing model-specific proposition).
There exist a fixed q, delta>0, D<infinity, and weak ceiling g_w>0 such that
every q-step update of the original homogeneous action map in its admitted
induction class, evaluated at ANY prescribed couplings in (0,g_w], satisfies

  |d_j^B| <= D,
  sum_(j=k)^(k+q-1) d_j^B >= delta.

This includes the adaptively chosen clipped evaluations in Section3: the
evaluated couplings need NOT satisfy the original scalar coupling recursion.
Both state invariance AND the displayed drift bound must hold on these
evaluated histories. Positivity only on self-consistent trajectories does
not justify this clipping argument.

The statements must hold uniformly in the starting depth, permitted history,
ultraviolet horizon, and the regulators required by the original scheme.
The state class must remain admissible under the prescribed-coupling runs
used to establish the estimates. Its finite updates and initial Wilson curve
must depend continuously on their positive evaluated couplings.

This target does NOT demand a positive decrement at every single step, an
exact universal coefficient at each step, a full asymptotic expansion, a
unique tuned trajectory, or a new mixed-history density construction.

The assertions about prescribed-coupling states and continuity are explicit
interfaces, not consequences we infer just from the title of B109/B116.
The source induction has to be read with those parameter conventions. If it
is formulated only on self-consistent runs, use a first-passage argument
restricted to that domain instead of pretending an arbitrary prescribed run
has been constructed.

3. ELEMENTARY CONSEQUENCE: A FINITE SOURCE TUNING THEOREM

The first-passage variant in Section3a below is stronger for the present
application: it requires positivity only along self-consistent weak runs.
The clipped lemma retained here is valid but has unnecessarily strong
prescribed-history hypotheses.

Assume the preceding proposition, including prescribed-coupling induction
and continuity. Put Gamma_w=g_w^(-2) and fix Gamma_*>=Gamma_w+qD.
For every horizon N=mq there exists an original Wilson bare inverse coupling
in

  [Gamma_*+m delta, Gamma_*+m qD]

whose actual source recursion stays in the weak range and ends at Gamma_*.

Proof. For the argument only, evaluate the state update and decrement at
g_eval(gamma)=max(gamma,Gamma_w)^(-1/2), while updating the real scalar by
gamma_next=gamma-d_j^B. The state stays in the admitted class. The terminal
scalar is continuous in its initial scalar. At the left end of the interval
it is at most Gamma_*; at the right end it is at least Gamma_*. The intermediate
value theorem selects a clipped trajectory ending at Gamma_*.

Summing future q-blocks backward gives

  Gamma_*+(m-r)delta <= gamma_(rq)
                       <= Gamma_*+(m-r)qD.

Within any block the scalar can lose at most qD. Therefore every selected
gamma_j>=Gamma_*-qD>=Gamma_w. Clipping was absent on the selected trajectory;
it is exactly the original recursion. Its bare g is of order N^(-1/2).
This is the same general shooting mechanism used in Notebook37, now stated
for the source recursion with its hypotheses retained. It proves no new
source-specific coefficient estimate.

Once the actual source constants, initial density, and all geometric
conventions match, weak containment allows the completed source density
theorem to be invoked for this same trajectory. This would discharge the
unmarked complete-density portion of an existence route based on that
scheme, without first rebuilding it for our one-shot retained-window map.
It would not identify our different local densities with the original ones.

3a. SHARPER TUNING BY A FIRST-PASSAGE COMPONENT

Assume that the original scalar/state recursion is continuous wherever
gamma_j>=Gamma_w, including the boundary, and preserves its admitted state
class there. Assume |d_j|<=D along every such actual run, and every completed
q-block contained in such a run has total decrement at least delta>0.
No extension to prescribed or clipped scalar histories is required.

For every finite N and every Gamma_*>Gamma_w+qD there is an actual weak
run of length N with gamma_N=Gamma_*. For N=0 take gamma_0=Gamma_*.

Proof. For N>=1 take A=Gamma_*+(N+1)D+1. The run from A is strictly weak
through N, since gamma_j>=A-jD>Gamma_*. Let S be the set of initial a in
[Gamma_w,A] whose actual runs remain weak through N. S is closed: if a_n
converges in S, apply continuity of each successive update, and the closed
inequality gamma_j>=Gamma_w, inductively through N. Let [a_*,A] be the
connected component of S containing A. It is an interval with a_*<A,
because the run from A is strictly weak and hence persists nearby.

If a_*=Gamma_w, the run touches the boundary at j=0. Otherwise a boundary
touch occurs at some j<=N: if every gamma_j were strictly larger than
Gamma_w, finite-step continuity would extend the component to its left.
If j+q<=N, the block estimate gives gamma_(j+q)<=Gamma_w-delta, contradicting
membership in S. Thus j>N-q, and the remaining one-step estimates imply
gamma_N<=Gamma_w+(N-j)D<=Gamma_w+qD<Gamma_*.

The terminal value is continuous on [a_*,A], is below Gamma_* at its left
endpoint, and above Gamma_* at A. The intermediate value theorem gives
the desired self-consistent run. QED.

For N=mq, summing its q-blocks backwards gives
Gamma_*+m delta<=gamma_0<=Gamma_*+mqD, as in the clipped lemma. The final
argument does not require monotonic dependence of gamma_N on gamma_0.

This sharper lemma was proposed by the parent agent and checked here.
It does not assert the source-specific positive block estimate itself.

4. A BOUNDED COUPLING-COORDINATE CHANGE IS ENOUGH, IF IT IS REAL

Here is a second elementary theorem isolating the precise transfer needed.
It is derived here, not attributed to the papers.

Suppose two genuine homogeneous RG systems O and B have state maps Phi_j^O
and Phi_j^B. Suppose maps H_j intertwine them on a class of actual histories:

  H_(j+1) o Phi_j^O = Phi_j^B o H_j.

Require H_0 to map the initial Wilson bare-state curve of O into the initial
Wilson bare-state curve of B. Suppose its scalar component is

  gamma_j^B=gamma_j^O+h_j(state_j^O),   |h_j|<=C.

The bound is uniform in old depth, horizon, regulator, admitted inherited
state, and all weak couplings used. The intertwiner also has to land in the
actual B admissible domain; scalar smallness alone is insufficient.

Then the actual decrements obey the coboundary identity

  d_j^B=d_j^O+h_j-h_(j+1).

Consequently for any n consecutive q-blocks,

  sum d_j^B >= n B_- - 2C,
  |d_j^B| <= D_O+2C,

if O has lower q-block drift B_->0 and |d_j^O|<=D_O.
Choose n with nB_->2C. The first bound follows by telescoping; the second
by the triangle inequality. No small per-step difference is required.

If a tuned O history satisfies gamma_j^O>=Gamma_B,w+C, its image is weak for
B. If its O endpoint is Gamma_*, its B endpoint is in
[Gamma_*-C,Gamma_*+C]. With Gamma_*>C this is a fixed, finite positive-coupling
interval independent of the cutoff. An exactly prescribed B endpoint needs
the corresponding continuous endpoint shooting, not an assertion that h_N
vanishes. Such a fixed interval is already adequate for weak containment.

Approximate scalar comparison variant. If genuine paired trajectories obey

  d_j^B=d_j^O+h_j-h_(j+1)+r_j,  |h_j|<=C, |r_j|<=epsilon,

then sum over nq steps is at least nB_--2C-nq epsilon. This is positive for
some n when epsilon<B_-/q, with n(B_--q epsilon)>2C. Merely bounding r_j
does not control the states or create the paired trajectories. An approximate
operator matching needs its own stability/shadowing theorem.

What is NOT sufficient:
* a formal perturbative change of renormalization scheme;
* the common one-loop coefficient;
* separately constructed weak scalar lists;
* a bound C_j growing linearly in j;
* a small error on one fixed finite regulator;
* equality of averaging constraints without equality of the integration
  windows and weights;
* defining gamma^B by the displayed formula without verifying the B update.

5. HOW TO ATTACK THE MISSING SCALAR ESTIMATE WITH EXISTING WORK

The most economical comparison may be DIRECT on the source homogeneous
recursion, rather than proving the exact intertwiner of Section4. A sufficient
decomposition is

  d_j^B=b_j^B+r_j^B,
  sum_(j=k)^(k+q-1) b_j^B >= delta_0,
  |r_j^B|<=epsilon, q epsilon<delta_0.

Here b_j^B must be the Gaussian coefficient of the SOURCE integral, including
its constraint/gauge determinant and measure conventions. The existing
actual determinant calculation supplies methods and comparison targets. It
does not identify b_j^B without checking its hypotheses for that family.

Concretely, try to verify only the following source objects:

(a) The exact leading Gaussian determinant and its primitive composition
    law, with its correct finite/thermodynamic extraction. Show the block
    coefficient grows as a positive constant times q up to a depth-uniform
    bounded end term. The soft-penalty and heat-kernel arguments of Notebooks
    35--37 may apply after their classical vertex and projection assumptions
    are matched. Re-proving their numerical coefficient is unnecessary if a
    strict positive lower bound already follows.

(b) A uniform second-background-derivative bound for the source remainder,
    on one fixed complex neighborhood, sufficient to make its extracted
    scalar contribution small. It is enough to control the exact weighted
    quadratic moment used by the source beta; matching every local activity
    representative of our construction would be excess work.

(c) The admissible-state, continuity, and regulator conventions needed for
    the elementary finite tuning lemma. The source's infinite-volume beta
    cannot silently be substituted by a finite short-lift functional, or
    vice versa. If its couplings are defined thermodynamically, retain that
    definition and establish continuity/uniformity there.

If either (a) or (b) already follows from the exact published formulas after
a bounded extraction, write that short proof and import it. If not, expose
that exact estimate as the task. There is no reason yet to reconstruct every
mixed-region minimizer as our first priority.

6. OBSERVABLES AND NONTRIVIALITY DO NOT COME FOR FREE

Even a successful Section3 import would give a full-density/stability result
for the original construction. To use it for original Wilson correlations,
one must propagate their insertions through the same transformations, retain
all normalization and R-operation effects, and prove bounds at physical
scales. A normalized operator fixes constants; it need not fix every
observable. This elementary distinction is precisely why an unsourced
partition-function theorem alone is insufficient.

An effective density estimate can be strong enough to give compactness at
one fixed coarse resolution, but without an identified compatible family of
physical observables this is not a continuum quantum Yang--Mills theory.
The existence route still needs sourced estimates, a common continuum
hierarchy, Euclidean covariance/reflection positivity and reconstruction,
and a nonzero physical normalization with Yang--Mills identification.
None of those follows from matching the weak coupling list alone.

STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATION

Before extending Notebook39 to arbitrary growing bad components, attempt
the original-scheme scalar block-drift theorem. This uses the largest already
published theorem with the smallest new interface. The output to demand is
one explicit proof for the source beta, not another general proof roadmap.
Our local work remains valuable as a source of determinant and remainder
estimates; it should be transferred only as far as that scalar task requires.
