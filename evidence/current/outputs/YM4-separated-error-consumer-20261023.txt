RESULT

On the already matched real ordinary product-cut kernel, a bounded
error with a genuine local coordinate read can be paired with a
distant source without using the divergent variance of the complete
original observable. No derivative of that local bounded error is
needed. The key operation is to project the OTHER source onto the
error's coordinate set before applying Poincare and the complete
normalized score identity.

The same argument proves exponential covariance decay for TWO bounded
local measurable functions, and gives a weighted gradient arm after
one ordinary integration of a strictly interior-local bounded error.
An exact early-local / late-energy split then states which quantities
are sufficient for the actual martingale remainder consumer.

These results require real locality in the common free coordinates.
A source label, ancestry anchor or bounded compiled-source cap is not
that locality. Arbitrary reset branches and retained-history closing
kernels are not turned into this ordinary kernel by these estimates.

1. ACTUAL FINITE KERNEL AND NOTATION

Fix a native ordinary geometric branch, background, old raw data,
coupling and coordinate chart. The full-law matching note identifies
the conditional law on its independent interior coordinates I as

 dQ(x)=Z^-1 1_K(x) exp[-H(x)] dx,    K=product_(i in I) K_i.  (1)

Each K_i is the native convex coordinate ball. H is the complete
source-zero action, including the actual Gaussian precision and all
P/B/E/R/variable-coupling channels, on their registered real slice.
Assume the SAME full-law hypotheses already used in
survivor_normalized_score_20261023, including the R lower profile:

 H_II''>=c0 I, c0>0;
 complete weighted Hessian row and column bound M_nu;
 |Cov_Q(u,v)|<=c_nu^-1
       ||W^-1 grad u||_L2(Q) ||W grad v||_L2(Q), c_nu>0,  (2)

for block-scalar W with nu-Lipschitz log W. The same estimates hold
for a principal coordinate subset conditioned on its complement,
with unchanged constants: the cut remains a product, its Hessian is
a principal restriction, and its absolute weighted rows/columns are
no larger. All differentiation below holds on the common strict
physical slice; the imported real extensions justify the hard-cut
covariance estimate, without changing the actual density on K.

For any coordinate subset P let d(i,P) be its native distance and
write W_P(i)=exp[nu d(i,P)]. An empty source set is simply a constant
and has zero covariance. Let

 C_nu=sqrt(1+(M_nu/c_nu)^2),
 K_nu=M_nu+M_nu^2/c_nu >= M_nu C_nu.                    (3)

All statements are conditional on the complete held output state;
uniform estimates can then be integrated against the actual output
law. No outer law is replaced by a reference law.

2. PROJECTED-MEAN GRADIENT LEMMA

Take P subset I and J=I\P. For a smooth test g define

                         h(x_P)=E_Q[g|x_P].              (4)

The conditional law on J has a fixed product domain. For any vector
v in the P coordinates, differentiation of its original normalized
ratio gives

 Dh[v]=E[partial_P g[v]|x_P]
                -Cov_(Q(.|x_P))(g, partial_P H[v]).      (5)

There is no moving-cut term. The second argument has J-gradient
H_JP'' v. For any permitted W, weighted Schur gives

 ||W_J H_JP'' v||_2 <= M_nu ||W_P v||_2.                 (6)

Let A(x_P) and B(x_P) be the conditional L2 norms of
W_P^-1 partial_P g and W_J^-1 grad_J g, respectively. Equations
(2),(5),(6), duality in v and conditional Jensen imply pointwise

             ||W_P^-1 grad_P h||_2 <= A+(M_nu/c_nu)B.

Taking L2 over x_P and using Cauchy--Schwarz on the two components
gives the useful complete bound

 ||W_P^-1 grad_P h||_L2(Q)
                         <= C_nu ||W^-1 grad g||_L2(Q). (7)

Here W_P denotes the restriction of the selected weight W, not the
particular distance weight until it is chosen. For W(i)=exp[nu d(i,P)],
W equals one on P, so (7) becomes

 ||grad_P h||_L2(Q)
          <=C_nu ||exp[-nu d(.,P)] grad g||_L2(Q).       (8)

The argument controls a vector norm, not a sum of componentwise
suprema. There is no extra number-of-coordinates factor.

3. ONE-SIDED LOCAL ERROR COVARIANCE: NO GRADIENT OF THE ERROR

Let r be a bounded measurable function of x_P alone. Choose any
constant r_0 with |r-r_0|<=b; for real r take b=osc(r)/2.
Because r is P-measurable,

                         Cov_Q(r,g)=Cov_Q(r,h).          (9)

Apply Cauchy--Schwarz to r and h, and Poincare ONLY to h under Q.
Using Var_Q(r)<=b^2, (8), and H''>=c0 I proves

 |Cov_Q(r,g)| <= b C_nu/sqrt(c0)
                     ||exp[-nu d(.,P)] grad g||_L2(Q).  (10)

This is the central asymmetric consumer. The complete source's global
variance is absent. The rough/local error never gets differentiated.
For a source arm based at T with

 L_g=||exp[nu d(.,T)]grad g||_L2(Q),

the triangle inequality d(i,P)+d(i,T)>=d(P,T) gives directly

 |Cov_Q(r,g)| <= b C_nu/sqrt(c0)
                                      L_g exp[-nu d(P,T)]. (11)

No smallness of the normalizer Z was used. A complete native source
gradient estimate supplies L_g only when its actual current coordinate
read and weighted arm have been proved. Its unweighted norm alone
supplies (10) with no asserted separation.

4. ONE ORDINARY INTEGRATION REGULARIZES A LOCAL BOUNDED ERROR

Now expose retained current coordinates a in E, with fixed old data,
and use the already matched law

 Q_a(dx)=Z_a^-1 1_K(x) exp[-H_pre(x,psi(a))]dx.            (12)

Let r(x_P) be a fixed bounded interior-local test, with |r-r_0|<=b.
It has NO direct retained-coordinate read. Put m(a)=Q_a r. For a
retained direction v, the exact score formula is

 Dm[v]=-Cov_(Q_a)(r,S_v),
 grad_x S_v=H_pre,IE'' Dpsi(a)v.                        (13)

The chosen smooth native cutoff gives the bondwise bound
||Dpsi-I||<=epsilon_sw=C_lambda exp(-R). It commutes with spatial
weights. Applying (10) to S_v and then weighted Schur gives

 |Dm[v]| <= b C_nu M_nu/sqrt(c0) (1+epsilon_sw)
                             ||exp[-nu d(E,P)]v||_2.

Duality therefore yields a genuine output source arm:

 ||exp[nu d(E,P)]grad_a m||_2
                 <= b K_nu/sqrt(c0) (1+epsilon_sw).     (14)

This bound is uniform on the stated admitted retained-coordinate
slice. It creates an arm for the conditional mean of a bounded local
test, even if that test is discontinuous. It does not create an arm
for a nonlocal compiled test from its cap alone.

For a path supported in T, with the ENTIRE path and its switched
image admitted, integrating (13)--(14) gives

 |Q_a1 r-Q_a0 r| <= b K_nu/sqrt(c0) exp[-nu d(P,T)]
                        integral ||Dpsi(a(t)) dot a(t)||_2 dt. (15)

An explicit direct a-dependence of the source adds Q_a partial_a r.
A bounded retained selector with a jump cannot be silently assigned
that derivative. Background, older history, geometry and moving-cut
changes also require their separate complete scores/domain terms.

5. TWO BOUNDED LOCAL TESTS, WITH NO DERIVATIVES OF EITHER

Let r(x_P),s(x_T) be real bounded measurable tests on DISJOINT actual
coordinate sets, with |r-r_0|<=b_r and |s-s_0|<=b_s. Condition on x_T
and set h(x_T)=E_Q[r|x_T]. Apply (15)'s infinitesimal bound to the
principal conditional kernel on I\T, with T held and identity switch.
It gives

           ||grad_T h||_2 <= b_r K_nu/sqrt(c0)
                                             exp[-nu d(P,T)].

Apply full-law Poincare to h and Var(s)<=b_s^2. Exact projection as
in (9) proves

 |Cov_Q(r,s)| <= b_r b_s (K_nu/c0) exp[-nu d(P,T)].       (16)

For overlapping sets use the elementary bound b_r b_s instead;
the proof of (16) assumes no direct x_T read in r. The universal
bound b_r b_s may also be combined with (16) by taking their minimum.
For real tests b_r b_s=osc(r)osc(s)/4.

This is a covariance statement for the actual fixed ordinary kernel,
not a conditional covariance under an additional rare event. In
particular literal local hard indicators are allowed. Their actual
rarity can be combined with (16) without conditioning on the event
or dividing by its probability. Such an event must first be matched
to the current coordinate law; a removed-owner history event is not
automatically one of these indicators.

6. QUASILOCAL APPROXIMATION IS AN EXPLICIT, WEAKER INPUT

For a bounded test r, let r_R be an actual P_R-measurable approximant,
where P_R is the radius-R neighborhood of an original source seed P.
Suppose

 |r|,|r_R|<=A,       |r-r_R|<=eta_r(R)                  (17)

on the complete actual fiber. Define s_S similarly with cap B and
source seed T. Conditional projection E[r|x_(P_R)] is one possible
choice: its cap is <=A, and (17) follows whenever the supremum of
the difference of r over pairs agreeing on P_R is <=eta_r(R).
This is a pointwise oscillation input, not an assumption on an
unknown covariance.

For disjoint P_R,T_S, combine (16) with bilinearity and the elementary
variance bounds to obtain

 |Cov_Q(r,s)|
 <= A B(K_nu/c0) exp[-nu d(P_R,T_S)]
                           +B eta_r(R)+A eta_s(S).       (18)

Indeed subtract Cov(r_R,s_S) as Cov(r-r_R,s)+Cov(r_R,s-s_S).
The errors are paid by their uniform caps, never by the variance of
the entire summed physical observable. If eta_r,eta_s decay
exponentially or stretched exponentially in the radius, choose
R,S as fixed fractions of d(P,T) to retain the corresponding
separation gain, with its actual source amplitudes.

This can accept a native common-shell/local-response theorem AFTER
its raw coordinate and same-law match has supplied (17). A geometry
deletion truncation in a reset-component law is not automatically a
coordinate-local approximant for (1). The source-centered seed,
original selectors, all held/current slots and complete source
normalization must be carried through that match.

7. EXACT EARLY-LOCAL / LATE-ENERGY MARTINGALE CONSUMER

Let F_e and mu_e be the original actual kernels and matching marginals.
An error r_a is emitted once and then propagated homogeneously:
r_(e+1)=F_e r_e. Let g be an actual conditional-mean source sequence.
Choose any later ACTUAL stage tau, a<=tau<=T. Total covariance gives

 Cov_mu_a(r_a,g_a)
 =sum_(e=a)^(tau-1) mu_(e+1)Gamma_e(r_e,g_e)
                                      +Cov_mu_tau(r_tau,g_tau). (19)

The already proved energy lemma on the tail gives the stronger
absolute-tail control

 sum_(e=tau)^(T-1) mu_(e+1)|Gamma_e(r_e,g_e)|
      +|Cov_mu_T(r_T,g_T)| <=sigma_tau(r_tau)sigma_tau(g_tau).

Thus if |r_a|<=b and L_e is any genuinely proved bound on the early
integrated absolute conditional covariance, then

 sum_(e=a)^(T-1) mu_(e+1)|Gamma_e(r_e,g_e)|
      +|Cov_mu_T(r_T,g_T)| <=sum_(e=a)^(tau-1)L_e
                                             +b sigma_tau(g_tau). (20)

At a matched ordinary stage L_e may be supplied by (10),(11),(16)
or (18), summed over an EXACT admitted source decomposition before
the actual output integration. At an independent-link elimination,
the earlier original-Wilson locality theorem gives L_e=0 for the
properly separated actual local pair. At a qualified physical reset,
use the separately proved two-near-source connected bound when its
actual source hypotheses match. Other stages retain their own terms.

For finitely many errors delta_j emitted at a_j<=tau, actual linear
transport and the triangle inequality give the corresponding bound
with sum_j sum_(e=a_j)^(tau-1)L_(j,e) and

                  [sum_j sigma_tau(r_tau^j)] sigma_tau(g_tau)
              <= [sum_j ||delta_j||_infinity] sigma_tau(g_tau). (21)

Each emission is counted once. There is no square-sum of injection
variances without orthogonality. If emissions occur after tau, apply
the same tail estimate from their own actual birth stages.

Taking tau at a fixed physical coarse scale changes the needed norm
from the divergent original sigma_a(g_a) to sigma_tau(g_tau). This
is a weaker and potentially useful target, but it is not automatically
bounded: the full state at tau can still carry old retained fine-scale
data. Its actual variance or a further localized substitute must be
proved. The coarse-scale name alone supplies no such theorem.

8. WHAT THIS ADVANCES, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

The new gain is derivative-free spatial control of a bounded local
error, obtained from the already matched full ordinary score and
weighted Hessian estimates. Equation (14) also shows an explicit
one-step mechanism creating a current-coordinate gradient arm.
This is a concrete producer/consumer bridge, not an assumption that
the desired mixed covariance is already small.

For the surviving compiled defect, the fixed-record capture amplitude
alone supplies neither exact locality nor (17). A retained ancestry
anchor is not the full read of its conditional mean. Likewise a new
baseline survival indicator may change the source's admitted law.
The presently missing inputs are an actual local/approximate-local
read for the emitted or propagated error on the stage being estimated,
and either full continuation of that input or a genuinely bounded
actual coarse-tail source norm. Y/q closing and independent contacts
remain separate exact stages/rows.

The position/date census must use the separation factor BEFORE source
positions are collapsed into one full-observable norm. Once an actual
pair bound of the form A_R A_S exp[-c dist_e(P_R,P_S)^beta] is obtained,
the existing near-current census can be reused with its original
calibrations and proper-grade window. Equations (10)--(21) alone do
not prove that every compiled mixed source satisfies such a bound.
