3. THE EXACT QUADRATIC DOUBLE-INCREMENT IDENTITY

Define

By Q2b the ell_r times ell_s term cancels, so the exact residual is

  P_2(f,a)=1/2 sum_(x,r,s) f(x) K_2(r,s)
       [ell_r,R_s]+[R_r,ell_s]+[R_r,R_s].              (3.2)

inside each of the three terms. Moment cancellations are applied to the
