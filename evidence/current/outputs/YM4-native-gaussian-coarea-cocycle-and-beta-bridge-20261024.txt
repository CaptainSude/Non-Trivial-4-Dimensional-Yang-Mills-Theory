1. A FINITE NONGAUGE THEOREM WITH ALL DENSITIES

Let X,Y,Z be finite smooth spaces with positive smooth densities
mu_X,mu_Y,mu_Z. Let f:X->Y and h:Y->Z be smooth submersions in a
neighborhood of the points in question. In local coordinates put
C=Df(x_*), D=Dh(y_*). Assume a smooth conditional minimum section

is a strict local minimum of s on h(y)=z. Write x_*=u(y_*).
Assume positive definite constrained Hessians at both minima. The
statement concerns these local branches; no global minimum selection
or regulator-uniform neighborhood is required.

Define a_f(y) as the leading Gaussian coefficient of the local
pushforward of exp(-S/tau)mu_X through f, relative to mu_Y, after
removing exp(-s(y)/tau)(2*pi*tau)^((dimX-dimY)/2). Define a_h(z)
similarly for s and h using mu_Y, relative to mu_Z. Let a_hf(z) be
the corresponding coefficient for h composed with f. Then

             a_hf(z)=a_f(y_*(z)) a_h(z).                    (1)

These a's are raw leading density coefficients. They contain all

The equivalent version at a nonzero base uses y_*(z_base) in the
inner denominator. This scalar remains in the exact normalization.

                     L_f H=C^T q.                          (3)

Thus, for any basis K of ker C,

 K^T L_f H=0,      H^T L_f H=q.                            (4)

The second identity includes the second derivative of the constraint;
it would generally be false with S_xx in place of L_f.

The complete composed constraint satisfies

    =sum_l D_al f_l''+C^T h_a'' C.

Therefore its Lagrangian Hessian is

 L_hf=S_xx-sum_a nu_a D^2(h composed with f)_a
     =L_f-C^T(sum_a nu_a h_a'')C.                         (5)

Choose a basis R of ker D. Every vector in ker(DC) has a unique
decomposition K xi+H R eta. Indeed its C image is in ker D and
subtracting H times that image leaves ker C. Using (3)--(5),

       =diag(A,B),
