AN EXPLICIT REFLECTED WITNESS IN THE FULL FIXED BARE WILSON LAW
19 October 2026. Fixed graph, free boundary, no prior-refinement claim.

0. RESULT

There is an exact gauge-invariant observable supported strictly on the
positive side of a reflecting four-dimensional box for which the FULL
bare Wilson measure has a strictly positive centered reflected Gram.
Use the box

              K={-1,0,1} x {0,1}^3,

with every nearest-neighbor edge and every elementary plaquette. All
edges are integrated with Haar probability; boundary conditions are
free. Reflect time t to -t. Choose any spatial plaquette p_+ at t=1,
and let p_- be its reflected plaquette at t=-1.

For G compact connected semisimple, faithful unitary representation rho,
and

 s(u)=dim(rho)-Re Tr rho(u),
 S(U)=sum_p s(U_p),           dmu_g=Z(g)^(-1)e^(-S/g^2)dU,

put q_(+,g)=g^(-2)s(U_(p_+)) and

                 F_g=q_(+,g)-E_mu_g q_(+,g).

This is a real gauge-invariant function of only the four positive-side
plaquette links; its centering constant adds no field read. With
Theta F=conjugate(F o theta),

 E_mu_g[(Theta F_g) F_g]
       = (dim G)/2 (37/1890)^2 + O_K,G,rho(g).           (0.1)

Consequently it is at least (dim G)(37/1890)^2/4 for sufficiently small
g. For SU(2) in its fundamental representation, the leading constant is

                         1369/2381400.

All configurations of the compact Wilson measure occur in (0.1), not
only its perturbative window. The error and the smallness threshold are
fixed-graph quantities. This result does not give the same witness for
the native RT state or for a growing/refining graph.

2. EXACT GRAPH, GAUGE, AND REFLECTION

The graph has 24 vertices, 52 edges and 42 plaquettes. Its cubical
two-skeleton is simply connected. Choose the root on t=0. A fine spanning
tree consists of all 16 time edges and any seven-edge spanning tree of
the spatial three-cube at t=0. These 23 edges connect every vertex and
have no cycle. The remaining 29 chord variables parametrize G^29.

The tree can be oriented away from the root and is reflection invariant
as an unoriented set. Exact rooted tree gauge sets all its links to one;
Haar disintegration is product Haar on the 29 chords times product Haar
on the 23 site gauge variables. Residual root gauge acts by simultaneous
conjugation and is NOT divided out as a singular quotient.

Faithfulness of rho makes s(u)=0 exactly at u=1. Simple connectedness
then makes the zero-action point in the rooted quotient unique. Its
Hessian is positive; there are no flat torus holonomy modes. These are
exactly the hypotheses of the turn13 full-law theorem, with
n=29 dim G real quotient coordinates.

The observable remains the original local plaquette function when using
this gauge. We do not introduce a cutoff depending on all tree chords:
that would obscure strict positive-half support.

For completeness, reflection positivity is direct on this graph. Let
sigma be the twelve spatial links at t=0, x the remaining positive-half
links and y the negative-half links. The Wilson action is

 S(U)=S_0(sigma)+S_+(x,sigma)+S_+(theta y,sigma).

Every plaquette lies in the fixed plane or one closed half; no plaquette
joins t=-1 directly to t=1. Product Haar therefore yields

 E_mu[(Theta H)H]
   =Z^-1 int d sigma e^(-S_0/g^2)
                  |int dx e^(-S_+(x,sigma)/g^2)H(x,sigma)|^2 >=0.

Thus both the law and the observable have the exact required reflection
and support conventions. No assertion about RP of a gauge-fixed density
is needed; the displayed identity is on the original link space.

3. THE GAUSSIAN QUOTIENT AND ITS SPATIAL MODES

Use the invariant Lie inner product

             <X,Y>_rho=-Re Tr(d rho(X)d rho(Y)).

Choose a real orthonormal color basis. In the tree chart U=exp(g a),
the leading action is

       (1/2)sum_p |(d_1 a)_p|_rho^2.

Each color is an independent copy of the same real Maxwell Gaussian.
All time links are tree links and hence have a=0. Write A_t in R^12
for the spatial edge field of the unit three-cube at time t. The remaining
linear gauge condition sets the chosen spatial tree at t=0 to zero.
The quadratic action per color is

 (1/2) sum_(t=-1,0,1) ||D A_t||^2
       +(1/2)||A_0-A_-1||^2+(1/2)||A_1-A_0||^2,        (3.1)

where D:R^12 -> R^6 is the oriented spatial curl.

For computing curls one may instead quotient the common spatial-gradient
mode orthogonally. This is just a linear change of gauge with constant
Jacobian; curvature expectations are unchanged. The spatial gradient
and its orthogonal complement decouple in (3.1). On the latter,
K_s=D^T D is positive. Therefore no inverse on a gauge nullspace is used.

Order the spatial faces as xy(z=0), xz(y=0), yz(x=0), xy(z=1),
xz(y=1), yz(x=1), with their ordinary positive orientations. Then

 C=D D^T =
 [ 4  1 -1  0 -1  1 ]
 [ 1  4  1 -1  0 -1 ]
 [-1  1  4  1 -1  0 ]
 [ 0 -1  1  4  1 -1 ]
 [-1  0 -1  1  4  1 ]
 [ 1 -1  0 -1  1  4 ].                               (3.2)

Its characteristic polynomial is z(z-4)^3(z-6)^2. Direct multiplication
also gives C(C-4I)(C-6I)=0, with kernel dimension one, the oriented
cube Bianchi relation. Cube symmetry is transitive on the unoriented
faces, and acts by signed permutations in this orientation convention.
Consequently every diagonal entry of the spectral projector E_4 is
3/6=1/2, and every diagonal entry of E_6 is 2/6=1/3.

4. EXACT CROSS CURVATURE COVARIANCE

The three-time-site free-boundary Laplacian is

                    [ 1 -1  0 ]
              L_t = [-1  2 -1 ].
                    [ 0 -1  1 ]

For each spatial positive eigenvalue lambda, inversion of the 3x3
matrix gives

 [(L_t+lambda I)^(-1)]_(-1,+1)
                    =1/[lambda(lambda+1)(lambda+3)].   (4.1)

This follows by the (1,3) cofactor, which equals one, and determinant
lambda(lambda+1)(lambda+3). Applying D in both spatial slots multiplies
this eigenvalue by lambda. Thus the six-by-six cross covariance of the
spatial curls at times -1 and +1 is

            K_-+ = (1/35) E_4 + (1/63) E_6.            (4.2)

For ANY fixed face p, its corresponding diagonal entry is

 k=E_G[(D A_-1)_p (D A_1)_p]
       =(1/2)(1/35)+(1/3)(1/63)
       =1/70+1/189=37/1890>0.                          (4.3)

All quantities here are real, per orthonormal color component, and use
the SAME action normalization as the Wilson Taylor expansion. The spatial
plaquette orientation is preserved by time reflection. Even if an
opposite orientation were chosen on one half, the energy calculation
below contains k^2 and is unchanged.

The exact rational matrix calculation was also independently checked
by constructing D from its edge incidences; no floating-point estimate
enters (3.2)--(4.3).

5. WICK ENERGY GRAM AND FULL-LAW CENTERING

Let f_(+,a), f_(-,a) be the two Gaussian curls in color a. Their colors
are independent, with E f_(-,a) f_(+,b)=delta_ab k. The leading polynomial
of q_(+,g) is

                 P_+=(1/2)sum_(a=1)^(dim G) f_(+,a)^2.

For centered real Gaussian variables, Cov(X^2,Y^2)=2(E XY)^2. Hence

 Cov_G(P_-,P_+)=(dim G)/2 k^2 =:kappa_0>0.             (5.1)

The full turn13 weighted moment theorem applies to the two GLOBAL
smooth gauge-invariant plaquette functions R_+=s(U_p+) and R_-=s(U_p-).
Each vanishes to order two at the unique rooted zero and has quadratic
jet P_+ or P_-. It gives, under the full normalized compact law,

 E_mu q_(+,g)=E_G P_++O(g),
 E_mu q_(-,g)=E_G P_-+O(g),
 E_mu[q_(-,g) q_(+,g)]=E_G[P_-P_+]+O(g).              (5.2)

The Gaussian means and the full normalized means are uniformly bounded
for this fixed graph. Subtracting their products therefore changes the
answer by only O(g). Reflection invariance identifies the two actual
means. Equations (5.1)--(5.2) prove (0.1) with the EXACT full-law
centering, rather than centering by a Gaussian or partial-history mean.

For clarity about excluded regions: the turn13 proof controls them by
g^(-29 dim G-D) exp[-Delta/g^2] for a fixed degree-D normalized mark.
The two-mark product here has D=4. This is smaller than O(g), after the
full normalizer Z(g)~c g^(29 dim G) is proved. Nothing outside the chart
has been discarded or assigned a favorable sign.

6. WHAT WOULD TRANSFER THIS TO A TERMINAL RT STATE

The theorem supplies a bare fixed-graph witness. It does NOT identify
the native stopped state with this bare Wilson law. In particular:

 (i) the regulator, boundary convention, action and coupling must match;
 (ii) the full RT state can contain retained or healed histories;
 (iii) the lower stability branch need not have this bare precision;
 (iv) the source insertion must be the actual transported observable;
 (v) RP and strict positive-half support of that transported algebra
     require their own verification.

An actual complete-law FINITE MOMENT comparison would suffice. Write
nu_g for the bare reference law above and F_g for its centered mark.
For a reflection-invariant terminal law mu on the same observable
algebra, if

 |E_mu[(Theta F_g)F_g]-E_nu[(Theta F_g)F_g]|<=epsilon,
                         |E_mu F_g|<=epsilon,

then its correctly recentered reflected Gram is at least

                       kappa_0-O(g)-epsilon-epsilon^2.

Only these particular complete moments are needed; no claim that every
history is separately rare is logically necessary. However scalar
pressure/stability bounds do not furnish this comparison.

Another sufficient interface is REFLECTION-FORM domination:

 int (Theta H)H dmu >= alpha int (Theta H)H dnu_g
                        for all H in span{1,q_(+,g)}.   (6.1)

Apply it to H=q_(+,g)-E_mu q_(+,g). The reference expression is its
centered reflected Gram plus the nonnegative squared difference of
means. Thus (6.1) supplies at least alpha(kappa_0-O(g)). Full RP order
is more than needed; the displayed two-dimensional form order suffices.

Ordinary measure minorization is NOT (6.1), since (Theta H)H need not
be pointwise nonnegative. An exact finite counterexample makes this
distinction clear. Let x,y=+/-1 be the two reflected halves, let mu be
the uniform independent law, and let dnu_a=(1+a xy)dmu, 0<a<1.
Both are RP. Pointwise, mu>=(1+a)^(-1)nu_a. Nevertheless

 E_mu xy=0,                  E_nu_a xy=a>0.

Thus even ordinary measure domination BY AN RP reference, together
with RP of the target, does not transfer a centered reflected witness.
The conditional box minorization of turn14 is weaker still: product
Haar on strictly disjoint halves has zero centered reflected Gram.

The abstract reflected-channel ledger of turn14 would transfer a
terminal witness backward if its actual channel and complete source
support hypotheses and small cumulative signed-defect bound hold.
This note supplies a terminal witness only when its law is the bare
fixed-graph law above, or when an additional comparison such as (6.1)
has been established. It does not silently fill the RT-law interface.

7. NO UNIFORM UV OR THERMODYNAMIC CONCLUSION

The graph, tree, Hessian gap, action complement gap and the number of
coordinates have been fixed before taking g small. Their constants may
deteriorate under subdivision or volume growth. There are no torus
zero modes because the graph is a free contractible box; replacing it
by a torus would change that argument. The path-holonomy precision
obstruction of turn13 remains valid and is not evaded by this witness.

This is a complete fixed bare reflected-correlation theorem. It is not
a construction of continuum fields, an RT terminal-law theorem, or a
proof of nontrivial YM4 existence.
