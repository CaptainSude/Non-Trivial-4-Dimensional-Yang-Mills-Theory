4. WHAT THIS DOES NOT FIX

the source box by that constant does not cure an unbounded n or
age loss in a complement-mass estimate. The original guard-pressure
theorem still has its stricter delta0 scope until separately proved
otherwise. A claim about typical gauge-patch admission, all-record
certificate probability, or later source transport requires another
argument. These limitations must accompany any updated domain
wording in the hybrid/curvature composition notes.
