3. NONLINEAR WARD ORDERS: CHECKED, INCLUDING THE CUBIC GATE

Let R=S_M-S_W. Its Maxwell coefficient is zero. The exact logarithmic
gauge generator has linear and quadratic field parts L and Q. The

  R_3(A,B,d lambda)=R_2(A,L_lambda B)+R_2(B,L_lambda A),

and the quartic identity is the sum of the three R_3(...,L...) terms
plus twice the three R_2(...,Q...) terms. In midpoint Fourier variables

sine factor of the gauge momentum.

Full spatial inversion gives Gamma_n(-p)=(-1)^n Gamma_n(p).
The linear Ward identity and hypercubic symmetry first give R_2=O(p^4).
For the cubic kernel, the possible degree-one tensor P_1 must satisfy
p_mu P_(mu nu rho)=0 and q_nu P_(mu nu rho)=0 with p,q independent.
Writing P=A p+B q, the mixed coefficients p_mu q_sigma in the first
identity force B=0; those q_nu p_sigma in the second then force A=0.
This eliminates all degree-one cubic tensors, including potential
antisymmetric ones. Inversion eliminates degree zero and two. Thus
R_3=O(p^3).

In the quartic identity the right side now starts at degree three.
Its possible constant tensor P_0 contracted with the arbitrary last
gauge momentum must vanish at degree one. Hence P_0=0, and inversion
gives R_4=O(p^2).
