ONE ORDINARY NORMALIZED KERNEL: COST-WEIGHTED LOCAL RESPONSE
23 October 2026. Corollary of the audited fresh numerator transfer and
the finite rooted exclusion theorem. No original file is changed.

This records the consequence for the term that originally motivated
the calculation: the change of normalized conditional law. It is a
one-step, matched-complete-read statement, not an arbitrary physical
reset or an all-prefix estimate.

1. MATCHED INPUTS

Take two admitted ordinary-step data sets eta=0,1 for the fresh reset
source construction in neutral_prefix_one_ordinary_numerator_transfer.
Use that theorem's full domains, parameter/reserve allocation and
complete positive output words

               Q_eta g_eta = sum_w v_eta(w).

Suppose they have a common canonical index/read assignment. In
particular, a word's X_w contains its complete registered source,
operator, constraint, cutoff and old-record reads and their actual
connectors. Equality outside a changed region D is required at these
primitive reads, so that

               X_w intersect D=empty => v_1(w)=v_0(w).       (1)

This is the primitive projectivity supplied by the native localized
construction, once the two data sets have been matched. Merely holding
the final current fields equal is insufficient for (1). Absent series
words may have coefficient zero; undefined source evaluations may not.

Let B_eta,w majorize |v_eta(w)|, and let D_eta,w be its actual compatible
met-island/read debt. The audited one-step estimate is

 sum_w exp[kappa_out d(X_w)] exp[-D_eta,w] B_eta,w
                    <= C_T A_eta.                           (2)

The d convention is the theorem's rooted numerical full-read cover,
not a relative metric that erases island-crossing paths. Any conversion
from its original relative estimate has already retained its debt.
Choose a common nonnegative D_w>=max(D_0,w,D_1,w). Doing this is a
numerical majorant, not evidence that this union of two histories can
be paid under either physical law.

2. THE POSITIVE RESPONSE COLUMN

Let P be the common nonempty root read, and assume the actual cover
comparison gives

          dist(P,H) <= c_geom[1+d(X_w)]                     (3)

whenever X_w meets H. Fix lambda>0 with lambda c_geom<=kappa_out.
Write D as the union of finitely many changed owners H. Assign each
nonzero word difference to its first intersected owner in a fixed
order, BEFORE taking absolute values. Denote this owner by H(w).
Then (1)--(3) give

 sum_H sum_(w:H(w)=H)
   exp[lambda dist(P,H)] exp[-D_w] |v_1(w)-v_0(w)|
          <= exp[lambda c_geom] C_T (A_0+A_1).              (4)

Proof: the spatial weight is at most exp[lambda c_geom]
exp[kappa_out d(X_w)]; exp[-D_w] is no larger than either
exp[-D_eta,w]; apply the triangle inequality and (2) on each side.
Every difference word is assigned once, so there is no owner-subset
sum. Formula(4) retains its debt inside the same word.

If D_w<=D_* for every contributing word, the complete difference has
the pointwise estimate

 |Q_1 g_1-Q_0 g_0|
 <= exp[lambda c_geom] C_T(A_0+A_1)
           exp[D_* - lambda dist(P,D)].                   (5)

The empty changed region gives zero. In the compatible sector with no
met-island/read debt, D_*=0. In general (4) is the valid conclusion;
replacing D_w by zero is not. A uniform D_* need not exist in a general
history sum, and the inequality does not assert it does.

3. WHY THIS ADDRESSES THE KERNEL TERM

On a genuinely common raw chart with a common held source f,

          Q_1 f-Q_0 f = (Q_1-Q_0)f.

The native distinguished numerator and its matching exclusion ratio
were used on EACH side of (2), so (4) includes this centered change
of conditional law. It has not compared only the source arguments or
the Gaussian references. With different pulled-back sources, split

 Q_1 f_1-Q_0 f_0 = Q_1(f_1-f_0)+(Q_1-Q_0)f_0

only when every expression is defined on the common admitted chart;
otherwise retain the complete word comparison (1) with its actual
discrete read changes.

This is an estimate for the specified analytic source and its complete
constructor. It is weaker than total variation for all bounded tests
and does not require that stronger result. It is also separate from
matched_gaussian_local_marginal_comparison, which treats only the real
Gaussian reference.

4. PRECISE REMAINING MATCH

No bare owner rarity is supplied by (4). To use it in the physical
F-minus-N sum, the changed owners and the common word/debt record must
be the ACTUAL counterfactual pair at the next reset; its complete LI
preparation must be controlled; and the resulting debt needs its real
cost or clipped-tail consumer. Choosing a large common union debt for
algebraic convenience does not establish that actual slot match.

The new one-step consequence is therefore (4), with (5) on its stated
bounded-debt sector. Full original-source census, arbitrary-prefix
admission, the all-date law estimate and continuum construction remain
unproved.
