2. THE ACTUAL REFRESHED SET, INCLUDING GAUGE-TREE BONDS

Work on the current lattice of spacing1. Define

 F_Lambda={oriented current bonds b: b intersects Lambda},
 I_Lambda={current plaquettes p: p intersects Lambda}.        (1)

For these native aligned cubes, this is equivalently the endpoint/corner
convention: a bond has an endpoint in Lambda, or a plaquette has a corner
in Lambda. There are no arbitrary cuts through lattice cells.

F_Lambda is the complete REFRESHED bond set in LI(1.100). The graph G_ax
of the gauge fixing lies in this set. The independent Haar coordinates
are the non-tree coordinates, but delta_(G_ax)(V') fixes V'=1 on the
tree. Therefore tree-bond joined values equal V_Lambda, not separately
frozen old V_k. In discussing an exterior witness, the frozen set is
the bond-set complement of F_Lambda, not the complement of the independent
Haar coordinates.

Let v_ext denote the actual current field on that frozen bond set. The
joined baseline used by the already proved final-tail lemma is exactly

 v0(b)=V_Lambda(b) for b in F_Lambda,
 v0(b)=v_ext(b)   for b not in F_Lambda.                      (2)

Gauge fixing chooses a representative of the same orbit. All estimates
below are plaquette defect norms and are unchanged by the allowed gauge
representative choices or the finite native averaging over axial graphs.

For elementary plaquettes the following implication is exact:

 partial p contains a bond of F_Lambda => p in I_Lambda.     (3)

Indeed an endpoint of that bond belongs to Lambda and is a corner of p.
Conversely, a plaquette in I_Lambda has boundary bonds in F_Lambda.
Thus every p outside I_Lambda has all four bonds frozen, and

                   v0(partial p)=v_ext(partial p).          (4)

3. THE LOCAL MINIMUM ESTIMATE COVERS ALL CROSSINGS

Lambda has a collar inside the selected component Z. To see the needed
fact, use Lambda subset (Omega_(k0+1)^(~4))^c and
Lambda_k subset Omega_k subset Omega_(k0+1). A path leaving a component
of Lambda_k^c must cross Lambda_k before reaching another component.
The four M-layers in the definition of Lambda therefore separate Lambda
from the exterior of its selected component by much more than one
current plaquette. In particular, every plaquette intersecting Lambda
and all its boundary bonds lie in the domain on which the local minimum
in LI(1.77)--(1.78) is defined. The intersection with Z in(1.73) does not
create a fresh exposed face through Lambda.

Apply LI Proposition1 with exterior regularity parameter epsilon_b.
Its conclusion p' IN Lambda, with the registered domain convention,
is precisely a bound on the set I_Lambda:

       |v0(partial p)-1| < B5 M^5 epsilon_b,
                                  p in I_Lambda.           (5)

On a crossing plaquette, V_Lambda in(1.78) is evaluated using the joined
connection: free/incident bonds take the minimum values, and its other
bonds retain the prescribed exterior values. This is exactly the
extension made explicitly on LI printed195 before(1.79). It is not a
bound on an independent interior-only four-edge product.

The input of the proposition is regularity of the actual prescribed
exterior connection. LI printed193 states this concretely for

For definiteness the criterion below controls the larger set of all

It imposes no nonexistent condition on an unknown crossing product at

4. COMPLETE EXTERIOR SET AND THE SUFFICIENT CRITERION

Let R be the complete raw current plaquette read used in
work/final_test_sensitivity_20261022.txt: the union over every actual final
B119(2.16)--(2.17) test under consideration, including full helper,
endpoint and virtual-edge reads. No assertion that R is contained in Z
is made or needed.

Define the finite, explicit exterior set

 E_ext = {p: partial p has no bond in F_Lambda and p intersects Z}
                         union (R minus I_Lambda).          (6)

Every plaquette of E_ext is an actual frozen-current plaquette by(3).
The first set is a conservative complete input to LI Proposition1.
The second adds all fully frozen final-helper reads, in particular every
such read outside Z. No claim that the first set is contained in R is
needed. Consequently the witness below may lie in the minimum's input
collar rather than in the final helper's own raw read.

Put
 t_k = epsilon_k/[8 B3 max(1,B5 M^5)],
 rho_ext(r)=max_(p in E_ext)|v_ext(partial p)-1|.              (7)

For admitted real conditioning data and sufficiently weak coupling,

                     rho_ext(r) <= t_k => G(r),             (8)

where G is the previously proved joined-read event

       max_(p in R)|v0(partial p)-1| <= epsilon_k/(8 B3).     (9)

PROOF. First take rho_ext<epsilon_b<=t_k. On R intersect I_Lambda,
apply(5), obtaining a bound at most B5 M^5 t_k<=epsilon_k/(8 B3).
On R minus I_Lambda, apply(4) and(6), obtaining a bound at most
t_k<=epsilon_k/(8 B3). These two sets exhaust R, including all crossing
and exterior helper reads. This proves(8) with strict exterior bound.

For rho_ext<=t_k, apply the proposition for every epsilon_b>t_k
sufficiently close to t_k. The actual boundary data and their unique
minimum are unchanged when only this bounding parameter is increased.
Let epsilon_b decrease to t_k in the bound(5). The resulting non-strict
bound proves(8) as stated. No limit in the conditioning data or change
of integration kernel is being taken. All epsilon_b used here are below
the fixed native regularity cap at sufficiently weak coupling. QED.
