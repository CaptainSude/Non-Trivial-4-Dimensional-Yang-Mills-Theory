LOCAL OBSERVABLE ROUTE: PUBLIC SCOPE AND A PRECISE RECONSTRUCTION TEST
19 October 2026. public_argument_audit. Turn38.

RESULT

There is no overlooked public theorem constructing the full tensor
hierarchy or its Wightman fields. The public zeroth draft expressly
restricts its observable result to the scalar action density and
separately excludes the relevant tensor and reconstruction conclusions.
Its separate rotation note contains a useful, concrete exponential-slab
argument, but that argument requires a value estimate before taking
source derivatives. A normalized scalar pressure or a finite list of
tensor jets is not that input.

The new mathematical check below isolates one exact danger: even in a
Gaussian model, complete single-block vacuum normalization can make all
normalized complex partition amplitudes equal to one while their
ordinary absolute majorants diverge with the number of ultraviolet
modes. Thus the public exponential-slab proof cannot simply consume a
normalized composite-source pressure estimate after inserting arbitrary
boundary states. This does NOT refute the local Schwinger-source route,
which needs no such arbitrary-state bound.

3. THE CONCRETE PUBLIC SLAB BRIDGE

Primary separate note:
https://github.com/ethanwsussman/Yang--Mills-LLM-experiment/blob/785a68fdeef78a1da854c4807fe1f17b597d05e7/QED3_slop/YM_Rotation_Invariance_simplified.pdf

Theorem A assumes a probability law of linearly closed two-forms with
a full Gaussian exponential bound for all real L2 tests. AppendixA.1,
pp13--14, proves an exponential slab bound by repeated RP
Cauchy--Schwarz; AppendixA.4 then differentiates a common holomorphic
source ball to obtain a bounded insertion operator. This is an actual
argument under its assumptions. It is not a theorem for nonabelian
curvature bilinears or their normalized source generating functions.

The exact reusable part can be stated without assuming a Gaussian law.
Assume a time-translation-invariant RP law on arbitrarily long time
intervals, for example an infinite-time law at fixed ultraviolet cutoff,
or a compatible volume limit with the following bounds uniform in time
length. A SINGLE fixed periodic time box is not enough for the limit
m->infinity below. Let a be a bounded complex amplitude in a time slab
of width ell. Let a# be its translated reflected-conjugate product,
in a slab of width L=2ell. It is reflection symmetric with conjugation.
Let K_m be m adjacent copies of a#. Suppose one has the ACTUAL bound

       E |K_m| <= exp(m p + o(m))                        (3.1)

along dyadic m, with p uniform in the cutoff. Then the slab operator

              T_a[A]=[a U_ell A]

on bounded positive-time observables obeys ||T_a||<=exp(p/2).
Here all normalizing scalar factors are part of a and remain in K_m.

Proof. Set b=||[A]|| and b_m=||[K_m U_(mL) A]||. Reflection and a common
translation give b_m^2<=b b_(2m). Also

  b_m^2 <= ||A||_infty^2 E |K_(2m)|
          <= ||A||_infty^2 exp(2m p+o(m)).

Iterating the first inequality, taking the 1/m power of the second,
and sending dyadic m to infinity gives b_1<=b exp(p). A final reflected
identity for a gives ||T_a A||^2<=b b_1. Null vectors are killed by the
same inequalities. This proves the assertion.

If (3.1) holds uniformly for a holomorphic amplitude a_z on a common
complex source disc, then operator-valued Cauchy estimates give bounded
source insertions. It suffices to control copied versions of the chosen
slab profile; one need not begin by postulating an operator norm on all
states. However (3.1) really is an ordinary absolute estimate in the
last step with A. Replacing it by |E K_m| is not justified.

4. AN EXACT COMPOSITE-NORMALIZATION OBSTRUCTION TO THAT SUBSTITUTION

Let xi_1,...,xi_N be independent standard real Gaussians and put

  F_N=sum_(j=1)^N (xi_j^2-1),
  Z_N(z)=E exp(z F_N)=exp(-Nz)(1-2z)^(-N/2),
  a_N(z)=exp(z F_N)/Z_N(z),                |z|<1/2.       (4.1)

Use the analytic logarithm continued from zero. These are centered
quadratic marks and their COMPLETE one-block normalization, not a
missing mean subtraction. For independent blocks,

           E product_(blocks b) a_N(z_b)=1              (4.2)

for any complex sources in the disc. Thus every such normalized
partition amplitude has a perfect cutoff-uniform value bound. Its
cross pressure is zero.

Nevertheless for real t!=0,

 |Z_N(it)|=(1+4t^2)^(-N/4),
 |a_N(it)|=(1+4t^2)^(N/4),
 E |product_(b=1)^m a_N(it)|
                   =(1+4t^2)^(Nm/4).                   (4.3)

The per-copy pressure in the absolute bound grows linearly in N.
Reflected pairs with conjugate parameters do not fix this: the absolute
cost doubles per pair. For real z, by contrast, a_N(z) is positive with
mean one and independent-copy pressure zero. Therefore real normalized
pressure bounds do not provide the holomorphic absolute bound needed
to apply Cauchy after the proof in Section3.

This is NOT a counterexample to a uniform actual OS insertion norm:
in a completely independent block model the operator may collapse to
its constant expectation. It is a counterexample to the proposed
positive-majorant inference. The cancellation is precisely what taking
absolute values before the appropriate conditional integration loses.
It explains why the free off-diagonal determinant formulas in turn37
must not be promoted to arbitrary-boundary Schur bounds merely because
their normalized partition amplitudes are uniformly holomorphic.

4A. REAL ARRAY BOUNDS DO NOT CONTROL EVEN ONE SOURCE DERIVATIVE

There is a separate exact RP countertest with genuinely unbounded
derivative norm, rather than merely a failed majorant argument. Take
one symmetric Rademacher variable sigma and the stationary process
sigma(t)=sigma for all real times. Its positive-half RP form is the
ordinary L2 form on this two-point space. Time translations are the
identity, with positive generator H=0. Thus arbitrarily many adjacent
time copies are available in one common stationary RP law.

For integer N define F_N=N sigma and the fully normalized amplitude

 B_N(t)=exp(tN sigma)/cosh(Nt)=1+sigma tanh(Nt).

For EVERY real t, 0<B_N(t)<2. Consequently every real array of m copied
amplitudes, with arbitrary choices of the real source values, satisfies

 E |product_(a=1)^m B_N(t_a)|<=2^m,

uniformly in N. In particular the reflected two-copy kernel is

 E[B_N(s)B_N(t)]=1+tanh(Ns)tanh(Nt)<=2.

Yet B_N'(0)=N sigma has OS norm N. The complex poles of the normalized
amplitude occur at z=i pi(2k+1)/(2N), approaching zero. This proves that
a real interval of positive, uniformly bounded normalized arrays plus
RP cannot replace the common COMPLEX germ required to differentiate
the slab bound. No uniform lower bound on opposite-sign two-copy
partition values is claimed or needed for this countertest.

The finite two-half version has law
P(sigma_+,sigma_-)=(1+rho sigma_+sigma_-)/4, 0<rho<=1,
and RP Gram diag(1,rho). Its derivative has reflected square rho N^2.
The constant-time version rho=1 additionally verifies the arbitrarily-
long-time assumption used in Section3. Neither example claims the
full spatial/field hypotheses of a four-dimensional gauge theory.

5. CONSEQUENCE FOR THE ACTUAL LOCAL TENSOR TARGET

The local Schwinger-source route is strictly less demanding than the
arbitrary-state slab route just tested. Its complex chessboard estimate
can use complete reflected source partition functions with their actual
vacuum catalogue. It does not insert an arbitrary bounded boundary A
and need not establish (3.1). The failure in Section4 is therefore no
obstruction to that route, and no reason to reopen the scalar proof.

What the public scalar construction does NOT provide is the corresponding
local, total-volume-uniform tensor source estimate after its own complete
tensor/mixed vacuum subtraction. Fixed-order coefficients on a finite
stopped volume do not give a common complex value disc, and O(4)
covariance does not supply its missing coefficient estimates. The
published/public rotation note uses a stronger full-value assumption,
not a finite-order replacement for it.

Thus the productive next action remains a direct estimate of the actual
local tensor source coefficients or source amplitudes, with the complete
same-law vacuum/copy cancellations retained. There is no need to prove
arbitrary-state Schur bounds first. If a per-arity reconstruction route
is pursued instead, (2.1), rather than another real-moment bound, is the
additional estimate that must be established. No primary theorem read
here derives (2.1) solely from the current real fixed-order bounds plus
full rotations.

This audit neither strengthens the full YM conclusion nor turns the
scalar subtheory into a claimed complete gauge-invariant observable
algebra. It identifies a precise public scope and a concrete failed
normalization inference, with the original simpler local-source route
left intact.
