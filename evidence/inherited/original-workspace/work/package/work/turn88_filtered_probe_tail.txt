2. WHEN THE TEMPORAL DIFFERENCE REALLY MEANS POSITIVE ENERGY

The following elementary reconstruction uses an INFINITE-time stationary reflection-positive probability law for the bounded scalar distribution b. Spatial-flow nonlocality within a time slice does not invalidate temporal positive-half measurability.

Let A_+ be the cylinder-polynomial algebra of smooth compact smears strictly in t>0. Define the OS form
 (F,G)_OS=E[overline(F(theta b))G(b)].
Time shifts sigma_t, t>=0, move every read later into the same half. Stationarity and reflection imply
 (F,sigma_t G)_OS=(sigma_t F,G)_OS,
 ||sigma_t F||_OS^2=(F,sigma_(2t)F)_OS.                (2.1)

The ordinary L2 norms of all translates are equal and bound their OS norms. Apply OS Cauchy--Schwarz repeatedly to(2.1): if a(t)=||sigma_t F||_OS, then
 a(t)^2<=a(0)a(2t),
 a(t)<=a(0)^(1-2^-n) a(2^n t)^(2^-n).
Since a(2^n t)<=||F||_L2, letting n->infinity gives a(t)<=a(0). This also sends null vectors to null vectors. Therefore sigma_t descends to a symmetric contraction T_t on the OS Hilbert completion. The semigroup identity is inherited from shifts. Strong continuity holds on the dense polynomial algebra from the L1 translation continuity of the test functions and |b|<=1, then extends by contraction. Finally T_t=T_(t/2)^2 is positive, so

This proves the scalar semigroup claim being used; no identification of -log of an isotropic lattice transfer matrix with a differential Hamiltonian is made.

Let v=[O(f)] and w=[O(f)-O(tau_delta f)]. Then

The constant part of v and every other invariant component are killed. A nonzero w has spectral support in (0,infinity), independent of component ergodicity. This is positive-energy CONTENT, not a spectral gap above the vacuum.

3. THE FREE RADIAL COVARIANCE AND ITS COMPLETE MONOTONE EXPANSION

Use three independent canonical Maxwell colors with spatial heat flow. The variance of one flowed12-curvature component and its covariance are

This strictness is not obtained by subtracting separate lower bounds for the three D terms. The positive spectral series is what controls the cancellation. Complete monotonicity in tau is asserted here at r=0, not for arbitrary nonzero spatial separation.

Thus the surviving filtered state has weight in a fixed finite, strictly positive physical energy band. A norm floor cannot be hidden entirely at zero energy or at arbitrarily large energies.
