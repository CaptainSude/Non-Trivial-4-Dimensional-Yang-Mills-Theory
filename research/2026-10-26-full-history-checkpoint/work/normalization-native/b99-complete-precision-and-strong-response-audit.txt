B99 COMPLETE PRECISION AND STRONG RESPONSE AUDIT
25 October 2026. Primary-source audit; no repository changes.

Scope
-----
This audits the independent-current precision import in
strict-independent-pair-packet-phase.txt and the response input N1 in
homogeneous-complex-prefix-map-20261025.txt. It does not assert source
catalogue closure, a denominator bound, or a Yang--Mills construction.

Primary sources are user-supplied B99 Propagators, paper5.pdf (printed
389--434), B102 Variational, paper3.pdf, B109, paper2.pdf, and B116,
paper4.pdf. Text and selected rendered pages were inspected. In particular
B99 pp419 and422 and B102 pp298 and306 were visually checked.

1. DEFINITE PRIMARY FACT: THE COMPLETE CURRENT BRANCH IS PRESENT

Let G0 denote the first, strongly regular inverse

  G0=(Delta + D R D* + Q* a Q)^(-1).

B99 Theorems3.3--3.4 supply complex first-order-background analyticity and
all the stated strong norms for G0, including the covariant Laplacian.
Here * in a complex continuation denotes the continued bilinear transpose,
not conjugation of the complex parameters. The native averaging scale
factors must be retained.

The later propagator G in B99 SectionD is different:

  G=G0 (I-Delta'_pi G0)^(-1),                         (B99 3.130)

where Delta'_pi is the explicitly current-dependent quadratic form in
(3.120). The next current correction is

  <A,Delta^(2) A>=2 <H C^(2)(A), J>,                 (3.134)
  Delta_pi^(2)=S* Delta^(2) S,
  S=I-DG'RD*,                                      (3.135)

and G1 is expanded in (Delta'_pi+Delta_pi^(2))G0 in (3.138).
The coefficient in Delta^(2) is H*J; (3.136)--(3.137) show that it is
small in the proper weighted local norms. This is a block-local operator
in A, but its coefficient is not local in the external background.

B99 p427 explicitly requires expanding the H in (3.134), as well as
the two main-line chains. The resulting walks have a main line and
additional branches, and obey the SAME (3.108) bound with shortest-tree
length in place of walk length. It is therefore incorrect to retain only
the endpoints of G1 or only the current label J in the complete read.

The unit-lattice quadratic form is

  <B,Delta_k B>=<B,(QG1Q*)^(-1)B>-a<B,B>
                  -2<H1 D^(2)(B),J>,                (3.156)

where the last D^(2) is the unit-block nonlinear-average coefficient,
not the previously defined Delta^(2). Eliminating the literal private
pivot coordinates gives

  D_free=C* Delta_k C,
  C_free=D_free^(-1),                               (3.158)

with the almost-local C described on p428. Every factor C, H1, and the
H branch inside G1 belongs to the complete read.

2. INDEPENDENT-CURRENT PROMOTION OF THE PRECISION WORDS

This is a valid inference from the actual displayed estimates, not an
extra claim that B99 itself formulates its main theorem with independent J.

Use one strict complex direct chart with compact real reference Ubar,
U=exp(i eta A') Ubar. The real reference satisfies the native local-cube
first-order tests. The relative A' satisfies (3.37), with a strict cap.
Replace every occurrence of the physical current in Delta'_pi and
Delta^(2) by an independent J satisfying the corresponding weighted
(-3) bound. In a homogeneous grade this is the usual small unit-scale
current cap. Require these conditions on every complete localizing cube
and its prescribed collar. No arbitrary extension outside a read is used.

The proof of (3.131) does not differentiate J: the derivatives are assigned
to the adjoining propagator/test factors. The estimate is therefore
unchanged when its small current coefficient is an independent J.
Likewise the derivation of (3.137) uses only the weighted size of H*J
and the local averaging coefficient C^(2). The H endpoint and first
derivative estimates suffice for this step. Consequently the two Neumann
expansions (3.130),(3.138), together with the inverses QGQ* and QG1Q*,
converge on a fixed small independent-current chart, uniformly in volume
and homogeneous grade. They give a definite holomorphic continuation of
the displayed precision formulas. No physical stationarity identity or
D*J=0 is required for these convergence estimates.

This conclusion agrees with the independent U,J operator continuation
explicitly invoked in B109 p261 and B116 pp6 and15. At (Ubar,0), the latter
paper explicitly uses symmetric operators and a positive Gaussian
measure. B99 p428 supplies the uniform positive lower bound for the free
precision on the original real chart, and B116's (Ubar,0) comparison is
the applicable baseline for this independent-current continuation.
Perturbative operator-norm bounds, with a strict enough complex/current
cap, retain a positive real-part gap. One must use the same precision
continuation in the action, Gaussian split and denominator.

For a normalized background/current packet direction v_q in the strong
relative-field/current units, fixed on its complete packet and collar,
the complete local words yield

  |D_{v_q} D_free(i,k)| <= C exp[-mu tree(i,k,q)].

Proof: a localized word depends on U,J only in the union of its native
localization domains. Its directional derivative vanishes if that union
misses the packet. On words that meet it, use Cauchy on a fixed-radius
packet disc contained in the strict chart, then sum the native tree
bound. The path from the main line to q is already part of that tree.
No fine-coordinate census occurs. Constants include the packet's fixed
strong norm; repeated nonorthogonal packet coordinates still require
the explicit synthesis bound stated in the packet-phase note.

Thus the flagged COMPLETE D_free current-branch/root import in that note
can be discharged for this precise literal algebraic continuation.
This does not identify different off-physical-graph continuations of an
action or response merely because they agree on physical minima.

3. THE STRONG H0 ROUTE AND THE CORRECTED K

B102 (129)--(130), pp297--298, use H0 constructed from G0, and prove
the Laplacian bound for H0 in addition to the bounds (3.133) of B99.
The covariance tilde-G in B102 is the Q-constrained covariance. With
G=(Delta_a-Delta^(2))^(-1), it is

  tilde-G=G-GQ*(QGQ*)^(-1)QG.

This is the G used in the corrected equation B102(180):

  A0 - tilde-G Delta^(2) H0 B
       + tilde-G V'(A0+H0 B)=0.                     (180)

It is not the G1 with Delta'_pi+Delta_pi^(2) in its inverse.
Put

  K=(I+tilde-G Delta^(2))H0,
  a=A0-tilde-G Delta^(2)H0 B.

Then the equation is exactly

  a+tilde-G V'(a+K B)=0,
  response=a+K B-H D(a+K B).

If V'=O(A'^2), a=O(B^2), and the derivative of this response at B=0
is K. In particular one must not omit the linear Delta^(2) term while
keeping the unredefined original H0 and simultaneously claim the
correct physical derivative H1.

Strong K bound on an independent-current chart:

Let X be the native strong norm consisting of the field, first covariant
derivative, covariant Laplacian and P1 slots, with their native weights.
Let Y be the native (-3) source norm. The actual estimates imply

  ||G0||_(Y -> X) <= C,
  ||H0||_(coarse -> X) <= C,
  ||Delta^(2)||_(X -> Y) <= C gamma,

for independent current cap gamma. The last estimate follows from
B99(3.136)--(3.137); its use does not require Delta_U H. The P1 slot
is the bounded DPD* part of B109's second-order decomposition and
uses B99(3.49). Its native scale weights cannot be discarded.

For gamma small, expand G=G0 sum_n(Delta^(2)G0)^n. Every term begins
with a strongly regular G0, so the four-slot estimate is retained.
The same conclusion for the Q-constrained covariance follows from its
Schur-complement formula and the corresponding coarse inverse bounds.
Therefore ||K||_(coarse -> X)<=C, uniformly in grade and volume.

This proof also supplies a legitimate B116-type localization of K:
expand all factors, including the H*J branch in Delta^(2), into their
native local words, group the complete composed words, and attach scalar
weakening parameters to those words. The leftmost strong factor and
native cutoff bounds retain the four slots; the tree decay pays the
finite localizer exponential as in B116(1.11). Constants depend on the
fixed localizer radius and fixed L,M. At s=1 the sum is exactly K.
It is a newly specified localization and must not be silently identified
with the printed B116 H0(s) if that symbol has not been redefined.

4. IMPORTANT NON-IMPLICATION: THE LAST H D TERM

B99 Theorem3.12 p423 explicitly EXCLUDES the covariant Laplacian
bound for G,G1. Its H,H1 conclusion is (3.133), which contains field,
gradient and Holder-gradient bounds, not a Laplacian bound.

B102 pp298--299 improves Delta_U H by using

  RD*H=0,                                           used in (137),(138)

and the cancellation in Delta'_pi H, before deriving (139),(140).
These identities are available for the physical constrained response.
They are not automatically identities of the literal independent-current
continuation of the final B99 resolvent formulas.

The precise issue is visible on B99 p419. Formula (3.119) defines the
gauge-invariant projected quadratic form S*Delta(U)S. Formula (3.120)
rewrites it as Delta(U)-Delta'_pi(U,J_physical), using the physical Ward
identity (3.117). If J is replaced independently only in the right-hand
side of (3.120), it is no longer legitimate to identify that expression
with S*Delta(U)S. In particular the proof of RD*H=0 in (3.124)--(3.125)
does not transfer by an identity theorem from the physical-current graph.

This is distinct from external gauge covariance. Under a simultaneous
external gauge transformation of U and J, the formal independent-current
words intertwine correctly. That observation helps N2, but does not
prove additive gauge invariance of the quadratic form or the cancellations
needed for the stronger Laplacian slot.

There is also a localization issue: an identity between full operator
sums need not survive weakening each constituent separately. A proof
using RD*H=0 must either preserve that identity under the chosen
localization or localize already grouped operators with the proven
strong bounds. B116's phrase 'all admissible norms' cannot by itself
resolve a mismatch of H versus H0 versus K conventions, especially in
view of its printed omission of the linear term of B102(180).

Accordingly the proof of K above does not yet establish all four slots
for the full independently continued localized response

  a(s)+K(s)B-H(s)D(s,a(s)+K(s)B).

The remaining concrete N1 task is a bound for

  Delta_U [H(s)D(s,A')]

on the SAME strict independent-pair direct domain and all fixed s
polydiscs, using the SAME actual response and action continuation.
A sufficient precise input is a complete localized strong H bound on
that domain. A weaker sufficient input would exploit the special range
of D(s,A') to prove the displayed composed bound directly.

5. CONSEQUENCES FOR THE PREFIX MAP AUDIT

The deterministic direct-domain recurrences in the common-frame note
are reasonable conditional consequences of the stated four-slot input.
The one-time smaller-direct-to-full use of B109 p263 is correctly
separated from the descending recursion; B3 need not be multiplied at
each date. The independent current contracts by L^-3 at zero response.

However the current synthesis draft cannot treat N1 as fully discharged
by B116(1.11)--(1.21) plus B109(3.13)--(3.17) without resolving the
H0/K convention and the final H D issue above. B109 p273 explicitly
requires the strong alternative representation (179),(180), not an
arbitrary first-order-regular current-dependent propagator.

Conditional on a corrected, matched N1 and the literal covariance N2,
strict margins at every step do give finite-prefix holomorphy: a finite
composition of holomorphic maps is holomorphic, and compact closed
noise/localizer products lying strictly in the domains have an open
neighborhood. This provides no dimension-independent Cauchy radius
for an arbitrary coefficient parametrization unless its synthesis
norm is separately controlled, and no normalization estimate.

The original fixed real-noise variables can remain fixed while their
already-evaluated arguments are conjugated as prescribed by N2. That
is an evaluation identity, not a change of integration variables.
No claim about cuts, determinants or conditional normalized laws is
obtained merely from that identity. Existing masks and denominators
still have to be evaluated with the same chosen continuation.

Verdict: complete precision/root import VERIFIED on the explicit
independent-current coefficient continuation; corrected K strong bound
PROVABLE with the displayed native G0/Delta^(2) route; full N1 response
and the unconditioned simultaneous native prefix theorem NOT yet
certified by these sources alone.
